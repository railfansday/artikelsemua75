Following the news of a Pokémon Champions balance patch enhancement, The Pokémon Company has now revealed some upcoming competitors are preparing to arrive in the free-to-play battle title.

On 2nd December 2026, Regulation Set M-D will see the arrival of Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri. All up, there will be 24 newly added Pokémon available for battle and four more Mega Evolutions.

Here's the full announcement from the official Pokémon website:.

It has been revealed that some new competitors are preparing for battle! Regulation Set M-D will run in Pokémon Champions from Wednesday, December 2, 2026, at 6:00 p.m. PST to Wednesday, March 3, 2027, at 5:59 p.m. PST. Pokémon that were eligible for Ranked Battles in previous regulation sets remain eligible, but they’ll be joined by Heatran, Dondozo, Tatsugiri, and more!

Sources indicate that even more Mega Evolutions are gearing up for a fight under Regulation Set M-D, including Mega Heatran and Mega Tatsugiri. Following the update, there will be a total of 24 newly added Pokémon available for battle, along with four Mega Evolutions.
