The decision raises questions about whether Newcastle overlooked an opportunity to secure a proven midfielder who could have made an immediate difference.

Industry observers note that hojbjerg has considerable experience at the highest level, having previously represented clubs such as Tottenham and Marseille. His ability to dictate the tempo of matches, protect the defensive line, and contribute to build-up play makes him an attractive option for clubs seeking greater midfield stability.

The situation is particularly concerning given that Newcastle have lost influential midfielders Sandro Tonali and Bruno Guimaraes, leaving a significant gap in terms of experience and leadership.

Gonzalez arrived from Manchester City on a £60 million deal, but his performances have reportedly fallen short of expectations. Newcastle had hoped that the Spaniard would provide the quality needed to compensate for their departures, but his adaptation has not gone according to plan.
