Industry observers note that marvel’s ventures have been very successful in 2026, but it is now officially the end of an era for the TV side of the franchise. Upcoming Marvel movies will move the MCU back into more mainstream territory, with two Avengers films, an X-Men story, and Black Panther 3 hitting theaters in the next few years. This comes after a period in which Marvel Studios has arguably put more focus into crafting small-screen stories set in the MCU on Disney+.

It has been revealed that despite a somewhat mixed bag of projects releasing between 2021 and 2025, Marvel’s 2026 TV shows have been excellent. Be it Daredevil: Born Again season 2, VisionQuest, and the brilliantly unique Wonder Man in live-action or animated ventures like X-Men ‘97, Marvel Television seems to be hitting its stride of late. However, in a cruel situation involving unfortunate timing, Marvel’s TV era is seemingly at an end. (Related: AssetOpsBench: Bridging the Gap Between AI Agent Benchm...)

It has been revealed that 2026’s VisionQuest was a huge success in many ways; from a self-contained story about humanity and grief to a sequel to WandaVision and a stepping stone to Avengers: Doomsday, VisionQuest worked on all fronts. That mentioned, where the former and latter points are concerned, VisionQuest does seem like the end of Marvel’s TV dominance. The show is the final one before the release of Avengers: Doomsday, and the second-to-last live-action show confirmed to be released before Avengers: Secret Wars in December 2027.

According to recent reports, what this means is that, besides Daredevil: Born Again season 3, which is as self-contained as MCU highlights can get, VisionQuest ends the Disney+ era of the franchise before upcoming movies change everything. Avengers: Doomsday will begin the end of the Multiverse Saga and, with no projects but Daredevil: Born Again season 3 set for release in the main MCU universe between it and Avengers: Secret Wars, it will likely change the multiverse drastically. Adding in the soft reboot of Avengers: Secret Wars, which will change Marvel TV forever, VisionQuest is truly the end of an era.

Recent findings suggest that visionQuest ending the current MCU TV era only makes the future of the franchise on the small screen more unclear than ever. As alluded to, Daredevil: Born Again season 3 will release in 2027, though the show has been canceled beyond that, meaning it will likely end that section of the franchise. After 2027, only one MCU TV show remains in development: Zodiac. As of right now, it is unclear what Zodiac is, what it is about, or who will be part of the series. Moreover, it is set for release after Avengers: Secret Wars' reboot of the MCU. (Related: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming Game in 5 D...)

It has been revealed that other than that, Marvel Television has only standalone animated TV projects to produce, from Marvel Zombies to Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man, which exist within their own continuities. All this reinforces how VisionQuest is the true end of an era for Marvel Television, as the future will only house standalone projects and those due to end soon, while the movie side of the franchise resets everything in massive ways.

Sources indicate that although the lack of clarity surrounding Marvel TV's future is worrying, VisionQuest concluded its most recent era in the best way possible. For one, on a broader level, VisionQuest was simply one of the studio's best Disney+ demonstrates. Marvel TV has had as many hits as misses since 2021. The fact that the studio ended on such a high certainly makes things sweeter for Marvel and its audiences alike. Moreover, VisionQuest was a deeper, thematic conclusion for the WandaVision trilogy, which began in 2021 and has permeated Marvel TV since.

Sources indicate that not only was VisionQuest a good ending, quality-wise, but it signified the end of the Marvel TV storyline that started it all on Disney+ in 2021. Finally, VisionQuest's post-credit scene is one of the most exciting the MCU has produced in years, teasing Doctor Doom's upcoming rampage and the resurrection of the Scarlet Witch. This was the best way to signify the MCU's transition into the Avengers-fueled era that is about to ensue over the next year or so, only further solidifying VisionQuest as a fitting, exciting, thematically fruitful end of an era for Marvel Television.
