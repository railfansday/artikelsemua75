The best Apple Watch deal I’m seeing for Black Friday? The Apple Watch SE 3, which just returned to a record-low price of $199. It’s the most budget-friendly smartwatch in Apple’s lineup, but the S10 chip-powered device doesn’t skimp on premium features. There are tons of handy health insights to help you reach your fitness goals, from workout monitors that’ll count those 10,000 steps to sleep scores and even period cycle tracking.

It has been revealed that this watch is available in 40mm or 44mm sizes, making it a great option if you prefer a sleeker look that won’t compete with your outfits. It also boasts up to 18 hours of battery life, with a fast-charging mode that’ll get you from 0 percent to 80 percent battery in just 45 minutes. Basically, this is your best option for a no-frills, entry-level smartwatch that won’t break the bank.

If you’re looking for more in-depth fitness insights and faster processing, the brand-new Apple Watch Series 12 is also worth adding to cart while it’s on sale for its lowest price ever. This S11 chip-powered model boasts a longer battery life than the SE 3 at up to 24 hours, and a slightly larger screen size at 42mm or 46mm. It boasts the same safety features and fitness trackers as the SE 3, plus upgraded insights like a Blood Oxygen app and hypertension notifications.
