Following testing, Upcoming England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye reportedly suffered a fractured right fibula in Week 10, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter and Mike Reiss.

Maye will not require surgery and there is a small chance he could be back for the Patriots late in the regular season if they are contending for an AFC playoff spot.

In a recent development, maye sustained the injury during the team's 35-10 loss to the Detroit Lions on Sunday. It happened on the team's first drive, which ended after Maye was sacked by Lions star Aidan Hutchinson. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

Maye's injury actually wasn't the focus initially. Following the sack, Hutchinson sustained a torn ACL while celebrating the play. He'll miss the rest of the season due to the injury. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

In a recent development, the true extent of Maye's injury came to light on the Patriots' next offensive drive. Backup Tommy DeVito entered the game in place of Maye, who was spotted on the broadcast walking back to the team's locker room. Maye was unable to return to the game, and the team was tight-lipped about the nature of the injury.

Later Sunday, reports started to emerge suggesting Maye could miss "a while." Those reports received more clarity Monday, when it was revealed Maye remained in Germany to receive further evaluation. By Monday morning, it was reported the Patriots feared Maye was dealing with a "significant lower leg injury," possibly a fracture.

According to recent reports, the injury could end Maye's third NFL season prematurely. While the 24-year-old got off to a tough start, turning the ball over seven times in the team's first three games, he proved last year's excellence was not a fluke. In the six games leading up to Week 10, Maye had 17 passing touchdowns against five interceptions. He was on pace for yet another strong year before the injury.

While Maye's long-term outlook remains strong, the Patriots' short-term outlook appears disastrous. Following the team's loss in Week 10, the Patriots sit at 5-5 through 10 games. The playoffs are still very much in the picture, but only if the team can rally around its new quarterback. That didn't happen in Week 10, as the team looked listless with DeVito under center.

But DeVito will have the bye week to get fully up to speed. When the Patriots return to action, they'll take on the 1-8 Los Angeles Chargers, giving DeVito an opportunity for a substantial game in his first start.

After that, though, things get much harder for the Patriots. During the last six weeks of the regular season, New England will take on the Buffalo Bills, Minnesota Vikings, Kansas City Chiefs and Denver Broncos. The team very likely needs to win at least two, possibly three of those games to secure a spot in the postseason.

Getting that done without Maye will be extremely difficult.
