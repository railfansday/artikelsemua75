Sources indicate that “The Microsoft incident has nothing to do with what we saw with the FAA. The FAA has nothing to do with what we saw with the banks a few days ago. These concerns that we’ve seen from a technical standpoint are completely unrelated one from another,” Rubio said […].

The statement was backed by Senator Mark Warner.

Sources indicate that democratic Sen. Mark Warner, vice chair of the Senate Intelligence Committee, also mentioned on Tuesday that there’s no indication these incidents are the result of an attack.
