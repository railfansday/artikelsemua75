Tottenham boss Roberto De Zerbi was not surprised by a dazzling full debut display from “special” talent Luca Williams-Barnett and compared the 18-year-old to Barcelona’s Lamine Yamal after Saturday’s 2-0 win over Ipswich.

De Zerbi turned to the highly-rated Spurs academy graduate after two bright cameos and he delivered with aplomb, scoring on his first Premier League start after 15 minutes before a mazy run set up Dominic Solanke at the start of the second half. After Williams-Barnett made his senior debut at the age of 16 last season, the clamour for the teenager to play has only increased during a difficult last 12 months and De Zerbi decided 10 days ago to include him in the starting line-up against Ipswich.

During a chat on Thursday with the skilful England Under-19 international, De Zerbi asked Williams-Barnett to play with freedom and work hard without the ball “like Raphinha, like Yamal” before he agreed when asked if the teenager could reach similar heights. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

After De Zerbi revealed Williams-Barnett prepared for the match on the team bus by watching YouTube clips of Ronaldinho, Eden Hazard and Neymar, he stated: “Luca is a special player.

Industry observers note that “I’m not surprised, because his talent is bigger than you think. He played very well. I think he’s lucky, because he’s playing in a top club, with top team-mates.

“I decided to play today with Luca from 10 days and in my plan, it was (James) Maddison and Luca very close to speak the same language, to play together and the experience of Maddison I thought could be vital.

Industry observers note that “The behaviour of Maddison could help him to play with freedom. And Thursday, I spoke with Luca and I asked him two things. Just two things. To play like he plays on the park with his friends, with the same freedom.

“And to play and to work hard without the ball, like (Ousmane) Dembele, like Raphinha, like Yamal, because football is going in a different way and the first defenders are the attackers.

“Even if they are Ballon d’Or winners, or any way the players with talent, they have to work on the pitch.”.

Asked if Williams-Barnett can reach the level of Yamal, De Zerbi noted: “Yes, I think so.

“And one of the best things in my career… I’ve seen today in the bus from the training ground to the stadium, because he was very close with me, while we were coming in the stadium, he was watching on YouTube Ronaldinho, Hazard, Neymar.

Sources indicate that “I thought he was a special guy, because if one hour and thirty minutes before you are debuting in the Premier League, you are watching Ronaldinho, Hazard, Neymar, all these types of talent. The football is magic, no?”.

Gary O’Neil watched Ipswich’s winless run extend to five matches.

It has been revealed that “Many of us weren’t quite able to get to the level that we’re used to. A tough afternoon. I didn’t enjoy it but I did enjoy watching the lads dig in and find a way to keep going together in a tough moment because that’s what we need,” O’Neil noted.

According to recent reports, “Between the goals and after the goals, we were OK and we arrived in the final third enough to cause problems but we didn’t. Disappointing but it was a substantial task coming here and we needed things to be perfect.”.
