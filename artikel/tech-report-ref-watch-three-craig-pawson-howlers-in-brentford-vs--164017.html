Craig Pawson hit the headlines on Monday after several controversial decisions in Brentford's 4-0 destruction of Everton.

Keane Lewis-Potter scored twice before Vitaly Janelt and Fabio Carvalho added found the net at the Gtech Community Stadium, but the headlines were dominated by another incident. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

James Tarkowski was sent off before half-time, essentially taking away any chance David Moyes' side had after the break.

Pawson initially gave a yellow card for the challenge, but upon review, changed his mind and awarded a red card, in a moment that has split opinion.

The dismissal is Tarkowski's first in over 350 Premier League games and will now see him suspended for Sunday's Merseyside derby, as well as games against Aston Villa and Fulham.

Here, Football Insider has analysed three of Pawson's most controversial decisions as Brentford beat Everton.

James Tarkowski is sent off! 🟥 pic.twitter.com/s5ta4f74np.

Tarkowski's challenge on Monday did not look pretty, and his studs were certainly raised.

The experienced centre-back had already got the ball and should have known better - he has played in the Premier League long enough to know that just getting the ball is no longer a good enough defence.

However, Monday's incident was a classic case of the slow-motion replay looking worse than the actual foul.

The real footage highlighted that there was really not much force at all in Tarkowski's lunge and that a yellow card would have been appropriate - Pawson should have recognised that.

On any night, Michael Kayode should have been booked when he got nowhere near the ball and kicked Jack Grealish high up the shin.

But especially on Monday night, when Tarkowski had already been dismissed for a challenge not massively worse.

Had his foot been turned slightly more, the Italy international likely would have been sent off, but Pawson did not even hand him a yellow.

Everton fans should have known what sort of night they were in for when Vitalli Mykolenko was left down needed treatment just seconds into the game.

The left-back had been elbowed in the face by Dango Ouattara, who had his arms raised as he jumped.

In a recent development, but there was no yellow card coming from Pawson. In fact, he did not even award a free-kick for the challenge.
