Jake O'Brien comes back into the starting XI as the only change for Everton's Premier League clash away to Brentford (8pm GMT).

With the Blues bidding to build on their three-game winning streak ahead of the recent international break, manager David Moyes – on his record-breaking 593rd game in charge of the Club – names the same line-up that triumphed 1-0 at Newcastle United on 2 November. That sees O'Brien start at right-back, with Merlin Röhl moving forward to right wing in place of Tyler Dibling. The rest of Everton's backline is made up of keeper Jordan Pickford, central defenders James Tarkowski and Jarrad Branthwaite, and Vitalii Mykolenko. The Toffees' central midfield three is once again formed of James Garner, Harrison Armstrong and Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall, with Tyrique George on the left flank and in-form striker Thierno Barry up top.

The Blues' bench includes former Brentford captain Christian Nørgaard and Jack Grealish.

Everton XI: Pickford, O'Brien, Tarkowski, Branthwaite, Mykolenko, Garner, Armstrong, Dewsbury-Hall, Röhl, George, Barry.

Subs: Travers, Maitland-Niles, Keane, Grealish, Dibling, Johnson, Nørgaard, Alcaraz, Hackney.
