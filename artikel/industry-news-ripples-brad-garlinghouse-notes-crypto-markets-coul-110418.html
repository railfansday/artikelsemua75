Industry observers note that ripple CEO Brad Garlinghouse believes the recent crypto market sell-off stems largely from investors rotating capital into high-profile initial public offerings. The Ripple boss and crypto billionaire anticipates a recovery once Anthropic’s massive listing is fully absorbed.

In a recent development, speaking to CNBC on November 19th, Garlinghouse drew a direct parallel to SpaceX’s public debut earlier in the year. He explained that when major IPOs generate intense demand, investors often sell liquid assets such as Bitcoin and XRP to free up cash for share subscriptions. The same pattern, he said, unfolded ahead of Anthropic’s offering. After SpaceX completed its listing, crypto markets staged a notable rebound. Garlinghouse expects a similar bounce this time.

Anthropic, the artificial-intelligence company behind the Claude chatbot family, has priced its IPO at $83 per share, above the previously marketed range of $70 to $78. (Related: Breaking: Obsessed fan breaks into YouTuber’s home dema...)

The deal ranks among the largest IPOs in history, raising tens of billions of dollars and valuing the firm in the trillion-dollar range, and shares are set to begin trading on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

Such blockbuster offerings typically attract enormous institutional and retail interest, temporarily diverting capital from other risk assets. Once the primary allocation is complete and secondary-market trading stabilizes, some of that capital often flows back into crypto and other liquid markets.

Bitcoin had slipped near $62,000 on Wednesday before recovering above $63,000 early Thursday. Garlinghouse framed the dip as a temporary capital-rotation effect rather than a fundamental loss of confidence in digital assets. He also expressed confidence that Anthropic’s shares would trade strongly given the strong demand and pricing above the range.

Looking further ahead, Garlinghouse remained optimistic about blockchain’s long-term role. He predicted that within roughly a decade, blockchain technology will be deeply embedded across global financial infrastructure, much as artificial intelligence has already transformed multiple industries.

His comments highlight how large technology IPOs can create short-term pressure on crypto while potentially reinforcing longer-term narratives around innovation and capital markets.
