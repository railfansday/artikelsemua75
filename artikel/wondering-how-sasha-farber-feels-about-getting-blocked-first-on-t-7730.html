📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that getting blocked first was not the plan. Standing in Sasha Farber’s temporary home on November 18, hours before his blocking on Hulu’s The Circle is revealed to the public, the former Dancing With the Stars pro is still in a state of disbelief. “Never in a million years did I think I was going to come on and be off first,” he tells me from his couch, where—as he threatens several times—he aims on staying until production forces him out. “On Dancing With the Stars, I think the earliest I’ve ever been eliminated was week seven.”.

During his 12-season run as a pro, he’d actually been eliminated twice during week six (once with Mary Lou Retton and again with Jenn Tran), but reminding him of this would only add insult to injury. Because not only did he get eliminated first—he got blocked by DWTS superfan Alyssa Khan. Thankfully, he doesn’t hold it against her...though I’m not sure the same could be noted for her fellow influencer Justin Hinsley.

Does he have any regrets? “I didn’t get to chat with the fellas, the guys. I wish I got to have a chance to speak to the guys,” he tells me. “I only spoke to Hannah [Brown] and Alyssa, and Hannah I already worked with [on DWTS]. I just wish I had that chance to build a bond with the fellas.”.

But as far as how he presented himself: “I’m like, what would I do different? I don’t think I would do anything [different]. I couldn’t do anything different.”. (Related: Breaking: How to generate text: using different decodin...)

I ask him about the first thing he’s going to do once he gets his phone back—and surprisingly, it’s not check social media (or find out who went home during the latest episode of Dancing With the Stars). “Calling mom and dad,” he says. “Oh god, I’m so scared to tell them I got eliminated.”.

According to recent reports, he adds, “People were like, ‘Oh, it’s probably going to be really hard to get offline and be in a room.’ But, I mean, look at this place. I was having the time of my life. I feel like the kitchen turned into my dance floor. I was having so much fun.”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

At this point, Sasha doesn’t know yet who’s real and who’s a catfish, but when I ask him if he has any suspicions, he immediately points to Lisa Vanderpump (who, as viewers know, is being played by Vanderpump Villa star Dom DeAngelis).

According to recent reports, “I know Lisa very well, and she doesn’t say ‘bloody hell’ very often,” he explains. “That’s like me saying, ‘Hey, guys, welcome to the Circle. Let’s put another shrimp on the barbie.’ So I was like, okay, I know Lisa is a catfish. I’m just gonna put that in my back pocket and use it when I need to. But I didn’t have a chance to use it.”.

It has been revealed that the good news? At least we’ll see Sasha in the ballroom for the Dancing With the Stars finale next week.

In a recent development, as he jokes, “There was so much support for me, and now I’m gonna be there for the finals, like, ‘I’m back, guys.’ You should be happy for that. The whole team is gonna be there and they’ll be like, ‘Wait, I thought you were [on The Circle],’ but it really is, like, how this show goes. You can’t really anticipate anything, which is which makes it really exciting. I don’t think there’s any show like it.”.
