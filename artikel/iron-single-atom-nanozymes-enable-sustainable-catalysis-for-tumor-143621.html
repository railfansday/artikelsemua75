Iron single-atom nanozymes enable sustainable catalysis for tumor therapy

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (68 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A research team led by Professor Wang Hui at the Hefei Institutes of Physical Science (HFIPS) of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), in collaboration with Professor Fan Kelong's group at the Institute of Biophysics (IBP) of the CAS, has found that the coordination number of the metal center governs sustained catalysis in single-atom nanozymes through a balance between substrate adsorption and catalytic turnover.
