Serie A has always been one of Europe’s most competitive football leagues, with established clubs, talented players and managers who place great importance on tactics.

Inter, Juventus, Milan and Napoli regularly compete for major honours, while other teams are constantly looking for opportunities to challenge them. Transfers, injuries, managerial decisions and individual performances can all influence the league standings.

This level of competition also means that expectations can change throughout a season. The best sports betting odds reflect these developments, taking into account recent results, player availability, squad changes and the strength of upcoming opponents. For this reason, betting on Italian clubs is one of the most common choices among those who frequently engage in sports analysis.

Recent findings suggest that however, few could have predicted how quickly Como would establish themselves among Italy’s leading clubs. After returning to Serie A in 2024, they finished fourth just two seasons later and qualified for the Champions League. Under Cesc Fàbregas, the club has developed an ambitious style of play and recorded remarkable results against established European opponents.

Their progress raises an key question: what has made Como so successful, and do they have what it takes to remain competitive at the highest level?

Sources indicate that como’s transformation becomes even more impressive when considering their recent history. The club competed in Serie D in 2019, following years of financial difficulties and instability.

The arrival of Indonesian investors Robert and Michael Hartono provided the financial support needed to rebuild the organisation. Rather than focusing exclusively on immediate results, the newly introduced ownership invested in recruitment, infrastructure, and the club’s long-term development. Their approach delivered results. Como earned promotion to Serie A for the 2024/25 campaign, ending a 21-year absence from Italy’s highest division.

The team subsequently finished tenth in its first season back before securing fourth place in 2025/26.

Fàbregas has been central to Como’s development. The former midfielder brought extensive European experience when he moved into coaching.

His approach places considerable importance on possession, positioning and intelligent movement. Rather than simply keeping the ball, Como try to create situations where players have space to receive passes and advance towards goal.

The team frequently operates in a 4-2-3-1 formation, with two central midfielders providing support during attacks and helping protect the defence.

One crucial feature is their willingness to build attacks from the goalkeeper. Defenders are encouraged to remain composed under pressure, while midfielders provide passing options.

Nico Paz has become one of Como’s most influential footballers since joining from Real Madrid in 2024.

The Argentine attacking midfielder combines close control, passing accuracy and an ability to identify space between opposing defenders.

According to recent reports, his development has been particularly impressive. After recording six goals and nine assists in his first Serie A campaign, he improved to 12 goals and seven assists in the following season.

Como’s European performances have provided particularly convincing evidence of their improvement.

Their 4-1 Champions League victory over Manchester United demonstrated how effectively they could compete against a major European club.

An even more extraordinary result followed against Lens, when Como secured a 9-1 away victory.

The victory equalled the largest winning margin in Champions League history and made Como the first away team to score nine goals in a match in the competition.

Beyond the scoreline, the performance highlighted several notable qualities.

Como created opportunities through coordinated passing, exploited defensive weaknesses and maintained their attacking organisation throughout the match.

The involvement of younger players also demonstrated the squad’s growing depth.

Industry observers note that nevertheless, exceptional results should be assessed carefully. Scoring nine goals in one match is highly unusual, and such performances cannot reasonably be expected every week.

The more notable measure is whether Como can consistently perform well against strong opposition.

The club has built a promising foundation, but staying among Serie A’s strongest teams presents different challenges than reaching that level.

It has been revealed that one major concern is squad depth. Competing in domestic and European competitions increases the demands on players, particularly those regularly involved in high-intensity matches.

Injuries and fatigue can affect performance, making reliable alternatives essential.

It has been revealed that another challenge involves retaining vital personnel. Players such as Paz are likely to attract interest from larger European clubs, while Fàbregas’ coaching achievements could generate opportunities elsewhere.

According to recent reports, tactical flexibility will also matter. Opponents will increasingly study Como’s methods, making it necessary for Fàbregas to develop alternatives when possession-based attacks become less effective.

Como’s development is not simply the result of significant financial investment, nor is it just another lucky streak of a team that was treated like an underdog by betting sites. Their achievements reflect effective recruitment, coaching consistency, and a clear understanding of how the team should play.

The victories over Manchester United and Lens show their potential, but long-term success will depend on delivering consistent performances over entire seasons.

Maintaining Champions League qualification will be difficult, particularly against established Italian clubs with greater experience and resources.

Even so, Como have already shown that a carefully managed club can improve rapidly without abandoning its footballing principles.

If they retain their key personnel, keep developing young players, and strengthen the squad intelligently, Como could remain among Italy’s leading teams rather than enjoy only a brief period of success.
