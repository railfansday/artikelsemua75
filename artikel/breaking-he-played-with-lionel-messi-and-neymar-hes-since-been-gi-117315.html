Playing for Barcelona, particularly during an era when Lionel Messi spearheaded an attack that also featured Luis Suarez and Neymar, is about as good as it gets for a footballer's career.

Yet, one star who was part of that squad between 2015 and 2020 saw his career completely spiral off course during his time in Catalonia, and is now nowhere near as revered as his Champions League, La Liga and Ballon d'Or-winning counterparts. The player in question was once considered a wonderkid at Galatasaray, with many fans drawing comparisons between his playing style and that of the great Luis Figo.

A winger and number 10 hybrid who didn't rely on raw pace, but instead used his exceptional ball control and footballing intelligence to become an elite dribbler, he was a rare breed. However, being surrounded by greatness doesn't necessarily mean it will rub off on you.

Having become one of Diego Simeone's most trusted lieutenants following his move from Galatasaray early in his career, 100-cap Turkish international Arda Turan saw his career go up in flames following a substantial-money move to Barcelona in 2015.

According to recent reports, fresh off the back of a historic treble the previous season, Turan was expected to be the perfect attacking midfielder to slot in behind the iconic MSN frontline. However, due to the club's ongoing transfer ban, Barcelona were unable to register him immediately. Consequently, he didn't make his debut until January 6, 2016 - almost six months after arriving - meaning he was playing catch-up from the get-go.

In a recent development, unsurprisingly, he struggled to find his feet, and talk of a potential exit emerged towards the end of the season. Luis Enrique, who had personally pushed for his signing, was nevertheless keen to persevere with the midfielder.

According to recent reports, turan even became the sixth player in Barcelona's history to score a Champions League hat-trick in December 2016, before bagging another treble later that month in a Copa del Rey thrashing of Hercules. However, what appeared to be a turning point ultimately proved to be a false dawn.

As soon as Enrique departed and Ernesto Valverde took charge ahead of the 2017/18 season, Turan's fate was effectively sealed. Out of favour with his new manager, the Turkish international later revealed to beIN Sports Turkey that he felt insignificant under Valverde, having failed to play a single minute during his tenure.

He subsequently left Barcelona on loan to return to Turkey with Istanbul Basaksehir, before making a permanent return to Galatasaray in 2020. He retired just two years later, aged 35, bringing the curtain down on a career that had promised so much. His Barcelona spell ultimately yielded just 15 goals and 11 assists in 55 appearances across five years.

Now, however, Turan is embarking on a new chapter in football, having swapped the pitch for the dugout and taken charge of Shakhtar Donetsk in 2025.

There are no better examples of Turan's downward spiral than the fact he was given two separate prison sentences in the latter stages of his career.

Industry observers note that in 2018, Turan became embroiled in a nightclub altercation that left Turkish pop star Berkay Sahin with a broken nose. According to Sahin's wife, Ozlem Ada Sahin, the former Barcelona midfielder had made inappropriate advances towards her before turning on her husband. In an even more bizarre twist, Turan later arrived at the hospital where Berkay was undergoing surgery, armed with a gun. He reportedly begged the singer for forgiveness, even telling him to shoot him if he genuinely believed he had harassed his wife.

The incident ultimately landed Turan with a suspended prison sentence of two years and eight months for firing a gun with the intention of causing panic, illegally possessing firearms and intentionally injuring someone, as reported by BBC News.

His second sentence came in March 2024 when he was slapped with a one-year suspended prison sentence and fined roughly €630,000 by a Spanish court after pleading guilty to two counts of tax fraud covering the 2015 and 2016 tax years during his time with Barcelona.
