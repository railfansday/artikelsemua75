It was a game of varying fortunes for Lewis Hall against Arsenal.

In a first half when Newcastle United struggled to even get out of their own half, the England left-back was one of the very few prepared to take responsibility and try to keep the ball, even when under pressure.

According to recent reports, when dealing with a difficult ball and facing his own goal just after the half hour mark, Lewis Hall attempted to get the ball down and keep possession. It backfired though with Kai Havertz stealing possession and played in Eze for the opening goal.

Like the rest of the team, a far better second half from Lewis Hall.

Especially as with Newcastle United pressing higher as a team AND doing it well, it allowed Hall to play a lot more in the opposition half and use his attacking skills on the ball to great effect.

Just as an absorbing game had gone into the nine minutes of added time, Lewis Hall was left limping after he had blocked a cross and having tried to carry on, was then having to limp off a minute or so later and be replaced by Sven Botman.

It has been revealed that following the match, Matthias Jaissle asked whether Lewis Hall was now an injury worry: “Hopefully it is only just a cramp that meant Lewis Hall had to come off. That is what we are hoping.”.

Lewis Hall, along with others, had put so much into this match, it could well have been cramp. I know when I played football (at a lowly level!), towards the end of the match that can easily happen, you are fatigued and then when you are stretching the blocking of a cross/pass can impact you painfully and set off a cramp. Here’s hoping.

Matthias Jaissle also asked about his other England full-back.

Tino Livarmento had only managed 33 Premier League minutes this season as once again impacted by injuries, in that second half though we were reminded just how good he can be, both in defence and attack.

Matthias Jaissle: “Tino can definitely give us another dimension to our play but we also have to be careful and smart. He was really on the limit of what he could do. I was really proud that he could deliver 45 minutes with that physicality in such an intense match. It was really well played, a top performance, and also something that he can build on.”.
