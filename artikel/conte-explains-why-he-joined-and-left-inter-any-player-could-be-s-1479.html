Antonio Conte has explained why he originally decided to join Inter and why he decided to leave immediately after winning the Serie A title in 2020-21: ‘We want to break Juventus’s dominance in Italy’.

Conte has made headlines in Italy with his upcoming interview with Cronache di Spogliatoio, which has been teased on social media over the last few days. In a clip released on Wednesday, Conte admitted that he would one day like to return to Juventus to ‘close the cycle’ and named several instances where he had been close to returning to Turin.

Another teaser was released on Thursday, with Conte explaining why he decided to join Inter in 2019 and why he decided to leave the Nerazzurri immediately after winning the Serie A title.

Conte had admitted that he had been available and willing to return to Juventus before moving to Inter, but the Bianconeri went with Maurizio Sarri instead, which was part of the reason why Conte decided to join the Nerazzurri instead.

Industry observers note that “There have been moments in the past where [Juventus and I] could have reunited, but it’s been difficult because two things have to align. I have to be available and Juventus have to be looking for a coach who isn’t currently employed. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

“There have been two instances. Once, when Juventus decided to hire Maurizio Sarri, I was available and later ended up at Inter. Then there was a second time when I was free, just as Giuntoli arrived. Giuntoli decided to go for Motta and I went to Napoli.”. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

In the latest clip of Conte’s Cronache di Spogliatoio interview, he explained further: “[Beppe] Marotta and [Steven] Zhang came to me and said ‘we want you to bring us back to winning ways, we haven’t won the league in 10 years, we want to break Juventus’s dominance in Italy and impose ourselves abroad’.

According to recent reports, “The goal from the club was clear, so we started on a upcoming journey together. What you see is what you get with me in every situation. If we are heading in a certain direction and we’ve agreed on it, then that is where we must go.”.

Conte feels that he was let down by the Inter management and claims that he left after learning that his title-winning squad could have immediately been dismantled.

Leading goalscorer Romelu Lukaku and star wing-back Achraf Hakimi were sold during the following transfer window.

“I left Inter because we had just won the Scudetto and we had a meeting where I was told that practically any player could be sold in the summer.

“The agreement was different to what it was before: Let’s build ourselves up and tear down Juventus’s dominance and then create a strong and long-lasting structure that will make us competitive in Europe.”.

The full interview with Conte is set to be released on Friday.
