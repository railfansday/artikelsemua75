Elden Ring Meets Minecraft In Huge Latest Free RPG

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Elden Ring fans already have a multiplayer game for those who want to play together in Elden Ring Nightreign. The spinoff is meant to be played either alone or with a team of three, making it the most cooperative game in the FromSoft Soulslikes catalog. That said, it definitely could be even more multiplayer, and it's not enough for some fans who want a truly massive cooperative experience.

One upcoming game sets out to make this dream a reality. The team behind it has created what they claim is "the first MMORPG in the Souls-like genre," finally letting fans live out their dream of brutally dying over and over again... together. Dark Craft is an upcoming free MMO from Take Top Entertainment, a small studio based in Kazakhstan that has previously only made mobile games. The title looks adorable, with an almost Lego-like Minecraft aesthetic. The muted colors and darkness might clue you in on the fact that this isn't as cute as the character models suggest. (Related: Tech Report: We Got Claude to Build CUDA Kernels and te...)

The title is planned to be every bit as challenging as a typical Souslike. The game is set in a grim dark fantasy world where monsters roam the world and evil forces threaten to take over. Players will be able to work together to roam the open world however they choose, fighting monsters, collecting artifacts and valuable loot, and managing resources. The world continues to exist without the player's intervention, making it feel alive and real.

It has been revealed that on top of that, Take Top aims to have "Game Masters" looking over the game, similar to the system used by Helldivers 2. These will have the power to start surprise boss battles, unique quests, and one-time special events.

The Souslike element doesn't just extend to the look and world of Dark Craft. Combat will heavily rely on stamina management, timing, and carefully watching enemies and bosses for attack tells. There's a lot at stake here, too, with valuable loot being rewarded for winning battles—or taken away from losing them.

As players explore a vast world of castles, forests, abandoned villages, dungeons, and more, they may encounter fellow adventurers. These can become allies to aid them on their journey, or they can turn into clashes for survival as adventurers turn on each other.

Industry observers note that the idea of a true Soulslike MMO is enticing, especially since Dark Craft is planned to be a free-to-play experience. And while the screenshots and trailer look fantastic, it's hard to know at this point what the game will actually feel and play like. Not much is known about the skills or world apart from what the screenshots and trailer reveal.

Recent findings suggest that the trailer reveals some impressive locations and atmosphere, with creepy enemies attacking players, backstabbings, a massive wandering castle, and the making of an epic story. Fans of Elden Ring and Minecraft who wish for a Soulslike MMO should keep an eye on this upcoming title. Though no release date has been revealed yet, you can wishlist Dark Craft on Steam to keep an eye out for the game's eventual release.
