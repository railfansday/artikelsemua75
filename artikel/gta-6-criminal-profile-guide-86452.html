Grand Theft Auto 6 has pulled plenty of mechanics from previous Rockstar titles, mostly focusing on revamping systems from previous GTA titles to make them feel incredibly in-depth. Even with this clear focus, some standout parts of other Rockstar titles have made the jump, including a revamp of Red Dead Redemption’s Honor System.

Titled the Criminal Profile, both Jason and Lucia have distinct mental and social states based on how they handle their criminal and civilian lives around Leonida. More so than Honor, the Criminal Profile affects various story moments, the game’s ending, and central mechanic functions from fight to fight. According to recent reports, grand Theft Auto 6’s Criminal Profiles are split into 5 different categories, with 4 that you can freely move between over the course of the game with little consequence. These 5 tiers include Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho, and Irredeemable. (Related: Industry News: These are the Senate Democrats backing S...)

Industry observers note that the profiles split down the middle: Professional and Hardened lean toward more controlled, non-lethal play, while Violent and Psycho reward wild aggression and quick thinking. Depending on your Criminal Profile, Jason and Lucia will act differently both toward each other and the events of the story.

Leaning one way on the scale or the other will also alter how you regain Focus in combat, allowing you to more accurately land shots and handle threats.

Professional and Hardened characters regain extra Focus via nonlethal gun takedowns, knockouts, and stealth takedowns.

Violent, Psycho, and Irredeemable characters gain boosts of Focus while landing headshots, lethal takedowns, or finishing off downed opponents.

Either way, your Criminal Profile rewards you for leaning into the identity of the profile, with extra Focus gain if you’re further along the scale (Professional or Psycho/Irredeemable).

Recent findings suggest that your Criminal Profiles are also a major factor in your eventual ending, heavily altering how Jason and Lucia act during major story events. In short, play how you want Lucia and Jason to act, either as professionals with an outer goal in mind, or as menaces who love the chaos they cause.

In a recent development, irredeemable is an outlier, as there is no way to leave it once you achieve it. As the name implies, becoming Irredeemable requires repeated acts of incredible, heinous violence for the sake of it. An Irredeemable Criminal Profile is deliberately impossible to achieve by accident, as you’re given repeated warnings and chances to bring yourself back.

To earn each different Criminal Profile, you’ll need to play in the ways that each profile rewards, making the process of earning and benefiting from the profiles rather natural. For example, if you want to be seen as a Professional, play professionally. If you want to be a Psycho, walk around Leonida lashing out and kicking puppies, you sicko.

Taking on drug dens, robberies, and optional missions is a great way to earn points either way, especially if you want to correct your Criminal Profile before you continue the main story.
