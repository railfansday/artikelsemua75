Adrien Rabiot believes Milan are growing as a team after he set up two goals against Frosinone. Ruben Amorim, meanwhile, has made it clear he wants the Frenchman’s contract renewal sorted soon.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, Rabiot spoke after the 5-1 win at San Siro, where he assisted Gonçalo Ramos and Ardon Jashari and controlled the game from start to finish.

“Our problem is that we need to be focused for 90 minutes,” Rabiot said. “Today it went well, even if we conceded a bit at the end, but that’s normal.

“We’re growing as a team: in the quality of our play, in our spirit and in our ability to make sacrifices for the whole game, all of us.”.

Sources indicate that he also had a word for Ramos, who was taken off as a precaution: “He finally scored after a long time. Today I saw he had a bit of pain in his thigh. I know he’s a very important player for us and we have a lot of games, so I asked the coach to rest him a little.

“Everyone’s attitude today was very positive, and we have to keep going like this.”.

According to recent reports, gerry Cardinale was at the club’s headquarters this week to plan for the January window. According to Sky Sport, Rabiot’s renewal was among the topics discussed, with the club aiming to tie him down until 2031.

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, however, Rabiot would like to stay and extend, but only until 2029.

Amorim didn’t hide where he stands.

“I watched France’s game and I think he’s one of the best players in that team, so his importance is huge,” he mentioned. “France have so much talent and there’s a lot of rotation, but Rabiot is always there. “With us, he’s the player who has to push the team forward. He’s the decisive element. With France, he has a different job to do, which indicates how well he adapts to the team he’s in.

“For me, he’s one of the best midfielders in Europe. I hope his renewal comes soon.”.
