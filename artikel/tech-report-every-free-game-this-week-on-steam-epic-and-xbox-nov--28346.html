Sources indicate that every week, there are limited-time offers that allow players to enjoy games for free, whether as temporary or permanent additions to their digital libraries. Here's everything that frugal and curious gaming fans can enjoy between Nov. 20 and Nov. 26, so they can make the most of their week without spending a dime.

Mark of the Deep is free on the Epic Games Store until Nov. 26. It is a Soulslike adventure game where players take on the role of a pirate named Rookie and explore a mysterious islandto find the rest of their crew. According to recent reports, hypnospace Outlaw, a 90s internet simulator, was free to play last weekend on Xbox. And this week, gamers can try a spin-off called Slayers X: Terminal Aftermath: Vengance of the Slayer, a game created by Hypnospace Outlaw's Zane. There are a handful of weapons to try out and lots of fun music to listen to as you blast through enemies in this 90s-themed shooter. Sources indicate that halloween may have already ended this year, but that doesn't mean the paranormal fun has to. In Polterguys: Possession Party, eight players must possess and sabotage their way to victory by luring opponents into the hands of a deadly monster. It's a unique multiplayer game that guarantees a night of spooky fun among friends.

Last year was all about extraction games. Players who had a good time then should try Mistfall Hunter during its free weekend on Steam — whether with friends or by themselves. Until Nov. 23, the extraction action RPG is totally free of cost, and if players end up falling in love with it, they can get it for 20% off.

Industry observers note that crusader Kings 3 has been free to try on Steam before. Gamers who missed out on these opportunities have another chance to give it a go, as it's available for free until Nov. 23. In Crusader Kings 3, it's up to players to manage their royal bloodline and make their own legacy as they encounter chaos, drama, war, and much more. How the story goes is entirely up to them.
