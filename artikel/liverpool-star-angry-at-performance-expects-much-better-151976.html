According to recent reports, liverpool played Manchester United off the park on Sunday and while that's not quite the statement that it used to be, this was a fantastic day out for the Reds. Florian Wirtz and Rio Ngumoha, in particular, stole the show as United just couldn't handle either of them.

Both finished with a goal and an assist. Both created chances and openings at will - their movement on and off the ball was simply too much for United side with 11 players, let alone 10.

According to recent reports, dominik Szoboszlai and Victor Munoz also got on the scoresheet here. Two fantastic finishes, the former lashing in a free-kick directly after the red card. Really, it's hard to ask for more from Liverpool - and yet it really could have been more.

United were lucky in the end. Lucky that more than one Red had an off day.

With what should have been a fantastic opportunity to show what he can really do, the Frenchman instead looked a level behind when it came to actually scoring goals. And that's a shame. Because Liverpool spent an awful lot of money and there's no question that Barcola is an elite player. He's just one who hasn't found his finishing in Red.

Recent findings suggest that barcola himself recognises this, too. He spoke to Canal Plus after the match, as relayed by Fabrizio Romano.

In a recent development, “I’m not happy with how I played," he remarked. "I know I can do much better and I know what I’m capable of”.

“I came away with an assist, but I could’ve scored three or four goals.".

He's right, there's no question there. The important thing from a Liverpool point of view is that the player doesn't need reminding of the standards - he expects to be better than that and expects to put teams to the sword.
