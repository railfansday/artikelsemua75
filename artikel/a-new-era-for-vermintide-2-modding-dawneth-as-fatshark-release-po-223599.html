"A new era" for Vermintide 2 modding dawneth, as Fatshark release powerful new tools for custom levels, models and animations

Pick up your feet, mayflies! Fatshark's co-op rat-whacker Warhammer: Vermintide 2 is getting some latest mod tools. It already has a public SDK and modding framework, you say? Ah, but the new mod tools are better. They are a public-facing incarnation of the very same digital spellcraft Fatshark use to create the official levels and characters.

"While the Vermintide 2 Software Development Kit and the Vermintide Modding Framework have been out for years, these mostly only facilitated the development of purely code-related mods," the devs write on the official site. "There was no support for any other type of mod, and so creating mods that involved creating new assets has been either extremely difficult or impossible. But no more! (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Industry observers note that "The Mod Tools is a public release of our internal Stingray developer tools for Vermintide 2!" they continue. "This powerful set of tools will allow modders to work their magic on both editing and creating anything from custom levels, models, textures, particles, animations, and more! While it will probably take them a little while to cook exciting new mods, this marks a new era for Vermintide 2 modded content!". (Related: Tech Report: One Piece Chapter 1200: Release date & maj...)

OK, that's enough exclamation marks. The new mod tools are available to all owners of the game. You'll be able to find them in your Steam library, listed as "Modding Tools" and "Modding Tools content".

In a recent development, fatshark have had to make a few "compromises" to get the latest mod toolkit finished. "The weapon pack and the level that we mentioned in the past has been put on hold," the devs write. "We still want to get back to that, especially the weapon pack, but for now there is no active development on that. It’s important for us that we have transparency here, and while we are not working on these things, we do stick with our games! We have prepares both near and far for Vermintide 2.".

Recent findings suggest that the Mod Tools rollout accompanies a change in server support. Hitherto, the Vermintide 2 modding scene has been split between the game's "Official Realm", where only officially sanctioned mods are allowed - "typically reserved for QoL mods that did not affect the experience much", the devs say – and the howling Chaos Wastes of the Modded Realm, which lets you install whatever grievous warpstone chicanery you please, at the cost of having no progression.

The lack of progression in Modded Realm has resulted in these servers "not seeing much use outside of niche communities", Fatshark claim. They're addressing that by, as you may guess, adding a Modded-only offline progression system, allowing players to scoop up loot and XP while performing weird experiments on the simulation.

Fatshark say they no longer have the capacity to keep up with mod approvals in the Official Realm, to say nothing of problems resulting from mods that have stopped receiving updates. They don't seem to have a solution for this, yet. They do, however, have a solution for an on-going lack of players in the dedicated Versus servers: shut down those servers! Let them crumple to dust!

"Versus is still playable, but it will be through community hosted games found through the Custom Game or the Lobby Browser," the devs assure. "This is already how most games of Versus are played, so we don’t think it will be a huge shake up in any shape or form, and it puts more of a focus on the Custom Game format where players have more control over the ruleset.".

The shutdown will happen alongside the game's next revision – Gifts of the Wolf Father, due in early December, which will rework the game's talent balance, update the weapon icons, and make the glow of different runes easier to distinguish from afar. Look out for more details down the line. In the meantime, please thrill my pointy ears with talk of Vermintide 2 mods.
