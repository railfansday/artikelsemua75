Being stranded at the airport is no fun. You can only eat so many Auntie Anne's pretzels and play Candy Crush for so long; eventually, you need to get to your destination. But what if that destination is 1600 miles away, every flight in the country is grounded, and there are no rental cars or trains available?

It has been revealed that well, you could take an Uber to a local Ford dealership and drop $48,000 on a new Ford Bronco. And according to a report from NBC News, that is exactly what Tara Harding did.

According to NBC, Harding and two of her employees were in Charlotte, North Carolina for training, and were set to fly back home to Bismarck, North Dakota last Friday. That is, until the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) grounded all flights across the country. Thousands of flights were cancelled or delayed as a result. The three women began to look at alternate transportation options, but none of them had any luck. There were no rental cars, buses, trains, or ride shares that could get them home. They were stuck in Charlotte. Harding called her husband to let him know, which is when her mother-in-law made a suggestion: “Why don’t you guys go buy a car?”.

Harding decided to investigate that possibility. Being what she described to NBC as a self-proclaimed “Ford girl,” Harding texted a local Ford dealer. The salesman remarked he would wait until the women arrived, which they did around 7:45 pm. Two hours and $48,000 later, Harding was the proud owner of a new Ford Bronco. The road trip was on.

It has been revealed that harding is a nurse practitioner, and she owns her own clinic; she had patients scheduled for Monday, and she noted she wanted to get her employees back home to their family. So, they covered the 1600 miles by driving 27 hours straight. The women took turns behind the wheel, sleeping while the others drove, while gas stations and drive-thrus provided the necessary fuel for both the Bronco and its occupants. Originally, Harding noted she planned to sell the Bronco once she got home. However, she added that while she was catching up on sleep, her husband took it for three drives and decided he wanted to keep it. So, the nationwide flight may have cost Harding $48,000—but at least she now has a cool newly introduced Bronco, and one heck of a story to tell.
