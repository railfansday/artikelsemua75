For Alpha Bank, a 147-year-old Greek financial institution with roots stretching back to 1879, modernizing that relationship required more than just slapping a fresh coat of blue paint over the ATM lobby.

According to recent reports, to tackle the overhaul, Alpha Bank brought in Pentagram for the visual identity and Dalziel & Pow for the spatial retail experience. Pentagram redrew the historic symbol, preserving the subtle asymmetry of the ancient coin while sharpening its central axis for digital screens. Backing up the refreshed mark is Alpha Sans, a clean custom typeface engineered in both Greek and Latin scripts, paired with a saturated revival of the institutionâs signature Alpha Blue.

Over in Athensâ Kolonaki district, Dalziel & Pow translated that graphic system into a pilot branch that feels remarkably welcoming. Digital queue systems and self-service kiosks handle routine transactions, while acoustic felt panels and warm banquette seating create private consultation corners without closing off rooms. In the double-height atrium, a sculptural ceiling light abstracts the Aegina coin symbol, and the upper floor borrows cues from boutique hospitality for Alpha Gold clients.
