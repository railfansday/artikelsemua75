Famous Japanese game director and producer Katsuhiro Harada, known for the Bandai Namco series Tekken, has revealed a Pokémon he wanted to add to the Pokémon fighting game Pokken Tournament but it didn't make the cut.

In a recent development, harada, who worked as a producer on both versions of the game, wanted the fighting / dark-type Pokémon 'Pangoro' from Generation VI. As for why he wanted to include it, Harada thought it had a "really cool" design and almost looked like it was wearing a traditional Japanese school uniform.
