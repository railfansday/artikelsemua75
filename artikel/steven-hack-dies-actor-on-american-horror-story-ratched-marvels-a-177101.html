According to recent reports, steven Hack, an original member of Broadway‘s Cats and A Chorus Line who went on to gain numerous credits on stage as well as in such TV series and films as Thor: The Dark World, American Horror Story: 1984, Criminal Minds, Scrubs, Fresh Off The Boat, General Hospital and many more, died November 19 at his home in Los Angeles following a sudden and unexpected cardiac event. He was 68.

His death was confirmed by Los Angeles’ Interact Theatre Tech giant, of which Hack was a founding member.

Born on April 20, 1958, in St. Louis, Hack eventually studied at the California Institute of the Arts (CalArts) and the American Academy of Dramatic Arts (A.A.D.A.), making his professional debut as a teenage dancers in the 1978 film Grease, just before making his Off Broadway debut in the musical Coolest Cat in Town.

In 1982 Hack joined the original production of the Andrew Lloyd Webber musical Cats as an understudy, over five years with the show playing the roles Mungojerrie, Carbucketty, Coricopat and others. From there he took the role of Greg in the national tour of A Chorus Line, and was with the organization when it returned to Broadway for its celebratory and record-breaking 3,389th Broadway performance on September 29, 1983. Stack performed in numerous other stage productions outside of Broadway, including Off and Off Off Broadway productions of Counsellor-at-Law with John Rubinstein, which also ran for seven months in Los Angeles. A founding member in 1989 of Los Angeles’ acclaimed Interact Theatre Tech giant, Stack performed in many of the company’s productions including Death of a Salesman, Our Town, The Laramie Project, A Little Night Music; he was nominated for Ovation and A.D.A. awards for his one-man play Willie’s Son. A member of the Television Academy for more than 20 years, Hack’s many TV credits include of Perry Mason, Pam & Tommy, American Horror Story 1984, Ratched, Marvel’s Agent Carter, Criminal Minds, The Newsroom, The Mentalist, Bones, Weeds, The New Adventures of Old Christine, Desperate Housewives, Days of Our Lives, Scrubs, The West Wing, Everybody Loves Raymond, General Hospital, Seinfeld, Mad About You, and many others.

Film credits include, among others, Minority Report, Fantastic Four, Thor: The Dark World, The Happytime Murders, and Intolerable Cruelty.

In announcing his death, Interact Theatre Firm artistic director Barry Heins and executive director Walter Ware III wrote, “As we remember Steven, we celebrate not only his many contributions to our company, but also the joy of knowing and working with him.
