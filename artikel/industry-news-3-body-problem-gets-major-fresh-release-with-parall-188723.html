In recent years, the sci-fi trilogy series The Three-Body Problem has evolved into a full-fledged transmedia franchise. The Netflix adaptation, which premiered in 2024, was a key factor in boosting the work’s prominence and bringing the story to a broader international audience. However, while 3 Body Problem Season 2 is slated for January 2027, a new series based on Liu Cixin’s work is set to premiere this year. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

It has been revealed that the Three-Body Problem novels have been adapted into both live-action and animated series. One of the earliest adaptations was a 15-episode Chinese animated series released in 2022, which primarily covered the second book of the trilogy, The Dark Forest. Now, four years later, The Three-Body Problem series just got a upcoming animated spin-off that will expand the franchise's universe.

On November 22nd, the Chinese video platform Bilibili held its annual event, the Bilibili Chinese Animation Works Release Conference, where it unveiled its slate of animation projects for the 2026–2027 period. One of the major announcements was a spin-off series set within the universe of Liu Cixin’s literary saga, titled "The Three-Body Problem Short Story Collection: Parallel Realms." Rather than a retelling of the original plot, as seen in previous adaptations, this version takes the form of an anthology, consisting of six independent animated short films that focus on the lens of several individuals exploring different concepts seen in the series. The announcement was accompanied by a trailer on the Bilibili platform, revealing that The Three-Body Problem: Short Stories Collection - Parallel Realms is scheduled for release on December 23, 2026.

The Three-Body Problem: Short Stories - Parallel Realms follows an episodic format, with each short film featuring a distinct visual style, an approach seen in series like Love, Death & Robots. The series is co-produced by Wanweimao Animation, and during the press conference, animation director Wang Yuren, who has been involved in the production since early 2024, noted that nearly a hundred artists have contributed to the project. Given the ample production time and the involvement of various animators, it suggests a high-quality work that will showcase the best of the medium while remaining faithful to the spirit of the source material.

Industry observers note that since it is being produced by Bilibili, which underwent a relaunch this past August, it is possible that The Three-Body Problem: Short Stories - Parallel Realms will be available outside of China with multi-language support on their global platform. Bilibili also distributes its series via its YouTube channel, so fans will have to wait until the release date approaches to find out more.

The fact that this series has a strong team behind it, combined with its accessibility outside of China, gives it an advantage over the earlier 2022 3D animated adaptation of The Three-Body Problem, which received mixed reviews due to its animation quality and pacing. Nevertheless, the arrival of a new series that explores more of Cixin Liu’s universe is great news for fans, giving them something to watch while they wait for the release of 3 Body Problem Season 2 on Netflix.
