The kid responsible for this one is Reeve Thompson, a cadet trying to find his brother after a cyberattack traps thousands of soldiers on a top-secret military base. Whoever breached security has turned the place into a playable fantasy world, complete with castles to clear and a dragon that can burrow underground. Reeve has plenty of people to recruit for a rescue mission. Getting them to follow a cadet through the first castle is another matter, especially once something starts following them. 47North will publish Castle Crawl, the first book in a newly introduced LitRPG series from game writer Justin Sloan and Disney shorts director Zack Petroc, on April 13, 2027. The 316-page paperback introduces Realm One, where Reeve and his squad have to fight their way through a series of castles to level up. Sloan has described the game as beginning to corrupt the world around the base, which gives Reeve another reason to figure out who debuted the attack while there’s still a chance to contain it.

Before he wrote for Battlefield 6, Sloan was a Marine Corps non-commissioned officer working in signals intelligence. He also wrote for Telltale’s Game of Thrones and The Walking Dead, two games that gave players plenty of opportunities to discover what picking a side could cost them the next time they needed somebody’s help. Reeve runs into a version of that problem as his squad starts to fracture, and he needs these people to help him reach his brother. The trick is that finding one missing soldier is his priority, while everyone else on that base has a stake in getting out, too. Petroc came to the project through Disney Animation, where he worked on Wreck-It Ralph and supervised the modeling team on Big Hero 6.

The publisher has been a little more forthcoming about what wants to kill Reeve than about the animal that wants to help him. So far, the one thing 47North will say about that baby unicorn is that it’s keeping a secret.

Castle Crawl releases April 13, 2027.
