📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The market for Claude and François-Xavier Lalanne’s irreverent creations has been on a high lately. In April, a single wooly sheep sculpture by François-Xavier fetched $2.3 million. Over the summer, a quartet of his frog fountains smashed their $3.5 million estimate to achieve nearly $6.3 million. And the record for most expensive collectible design work belongs to a set of flower-adorned bronze mirrors crafted by Claude: They netted $33.5 million earlier this year, eclipsing François-Xavier’s $31.4 million hippo bar.

Industry observers note that now, ELLE Decor can exclusively share that Sotheby’s will host the first-ever auction dedicated to Les Lalanne, featuring pieces sourced from various collections. On December 8, more than 30 works by the late French artist duo will hit the block in “Les Lalanne: Beyond Imagination.” It is expected to fetch in the region of $25 million. It has been revealed that “Les Lalanne have become a global phenomenon, changing the way generations of collectors think about design,” notes Jodi Pollack, chairman of 20th century design and major collections for the Americas. “A sheep can be a seat, a crocodile can be a console, and both can hold their own alongside the most important works in a collection.”.

The collection spans everything from a gold-patinated bronze gorilla (estimated at $8–12 million) to a white marble owl (estimated at $4–6 million), and a flock of five sheep, including two lambs ($300,000–500,000 each). Highlights also include lesser-known motifs, such as a playful pair of bronze monkeys with deep-green patination that makes them almost look like jade ($800,000–1.2 million each), and two matching crocodile consoles ($700,000–1 million) that complement the famous chairs Claude created after obtaining a deceased crocodile from the Paris Zoo.

Those hoping to get an in-person glimpse at the creatures can see the menagerie starting December 2 at the Breuer building, Sotheby’s upcoming Manhattan headquarters. To accompany the show, the auction house enlisted Milan-based artist Gaia Alari to devise custom illustrations for the catalogue, which reads like a fairy tale. Industry observers note that “The story begins in the catalogue and comes to life at the Breuer, where visitors will step into an enchanted winter garden and meet the cast,” indicates Pollack. “It’s our way of celebrating these works and the spirit in which they were conceived. We want visitors to look at familiar things as Claude and François-Xavier did and to see the extraordinary in the ordinary.”.

Sources indicate that “Les Lalanne: Beyond Imagination” will be on view at Sotheby’s New York from December 2 to 7. The live auction will take place on December 8, 2026, at Sotheby’s New York at 11 a.m..
