Recent findings suggest that the recalled midfielders Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain and Luke McCowan both scored as Celtic beat St Mirren 4-1. The champions brushed off the loss of midfield pair Mika Baur and Benjamin Nygren to injury to consolidate their place at the top of the Premiership.

Sources indicate that the centre-forward Camilo Duran was also on target as Celtic killed the game late in the first half with three goals in nine minutes. Callum McGregor grabbed his first league goal of the season before Killian Phillips scored a consolation from the spot. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

With Baur facing up to 10 weeks out with an ankle injury and Nygren nursing a heel problem, Martin O’Neill would have been delighted with how his midfield functioned.

Oxlade-Chamberlain made his fifth start of the season and McCowan his second. The pair had never started a game together before. O’Neill also introduced the former PSV Eindhoven midfielder Joël van den Berg for a debut in the second half and the 19-year-old showed some good touches. It has been revealed that celtic started with a new-look front three of Duran flanked by Jota and Yang Hyun-jun. Duran was a constant threat and had two goals disallowed for offside in the opening quarter.

Yang was instrumental in the 33rd-minute opener, racing past Declan John and drilling a low pass which ultimately fell for Oxlade-Chamberlain to stroke into the bottom corner.

Industry observers note that the South Korean soon passed up a brilliant chance, opting to look for Duran when played clean through by the goalkeeper Sam Johnstone. The pass was under-hit and Alex Gogic cleared.

It has been revealed that duran was on the end of a far better ball in the 40th minute, cushioning home a volley after a superb whipped cross by Kieran Tierney. Duran soon set up Jota, who was denied by Jacob Chapman, before finding Tierney, who provided another assist as McCowan drilled a first-time finish.

In a recent development, celtic went four ahead in the 63rd minute when the ball broke for McGregor on the edge of the box. The captain rapidly shifted the ball on to his right foot and swept it into the bottom corner.

According to recent reports, johnstone blew his chance of a clean sheet when he crashed into Calvin Ramsay as he came off his line to punch the ball. Phillips converted the resulting 70th-minute penalty and St Mirren kept pushing with Johnstone saving well from Ismeal Kabia and Samuel Ramos coming close.

Hearts returned to winning ways in spectacular style as they destroyed 10-man Dundee 8-0. They went into the game on the back of three straight defeats but they ran riot, scoring four in the first half thanks to Sabah Kerjota, a Pierre Landry Kaboré double and an own goal by Ryan Finnigan.

Dundee were reduced to 10 men after the break with Owen Bevan shown a straight red card before Claudio Braga, Sabri Guendouz, Kerjota with his second and Stuart Findlay blew Dundee away with four more goals.

According to recent reports, mario Frick enjoyed a dream start to life as Aberdeen manager by edging a six-goal thriller with a 4-2 win at Kilmarnock. Kevin Nisbet’s double along with Brad Lyons and Findlay Marshall strikes secured three priceless points to leave the Rugby Park side rooted to the bottom.

The visitors climbed to eighth spot after a remarkable afternoon where the Pittodrie side hit a much-needed scoring streak. Aberdeen opted to dispense with the services of Stephen Robinson after a run of one win from seven which saw them sink to second bottom and Frick was appointed as his replacement last Sunday.

Dundee United recorded their biggest ever win at Easter Road with an impressive 4-0 thrashing of Hibernian. Zac Sapsford’s first-half double laid the foundations before Lyall Cameron and Jesse Randall found the net after the break.

Industry observers note that the visitors were well worth the three points as they cemented their place in the top six and picked up their first win in Leith since 2021. It was a humbling afternoon for Hibs, who thought they had turned a corner under their new head coach, Marink Reedijk, after back-to-back victories before the international break.

Falkirk clung on to clock up six top-flight wins in a row for the first time since 1966 with a 2-1 home victory against St Johnstone. John McGlynn’s team scored early in each half to remain third.

Jacob Murrell’s eighth goal of a superb debut season sent Falkirk on their way inside six minutes. Finley Barbrook’s second goal in as many games on 48 minutes doubled the advantage. Josh Campbell nodded in a late reply to serve up a scare but the hosts held firm.
