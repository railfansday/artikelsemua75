For a mattress to be considered a good option for anyone with back pain, it needs to perform well in three specific areas; pressure relief, temperature regulation, and edge support.

In a recent development, in order to select only the beds that offer meaningful relief to people with a bad back, we've combed our test data to ensure each of the mattresses featured here have scored a minimum of 3.5 out of 5 in our pressure relief, temperature and edge support tests. Here's how the Helix Midnight Luxe, Saatva Rx, Plank Firm and Brooklyn Bedding CopperFlex Essential performed and compare across these crucial metrics:.

Pressure relief score (out of 5).

Temperature regulation: Reliable temperature regulation is an vital feature of a mattress for back pain. Waking up hot and sweaty can lead to more movement (like kicking off the covers), which can cause further injury. Pressure relief: This is a term to describe how much cushioning there is along the touch points of the mattress. For example, for side sleepers, this will be your shoulders, hips and knees. For back sleepers, this will likely be your lumbar region and upper back and shoulders. Minimal pressure relief can lead to a build-up of pressure, which can exacerbate existing pain. Edge Support: This describes how sturdy a mattress is at its edges and corners. This is an important feature for any mattress, but especially those with back pain who may need to sit comfortably and safely before standing.
