Sources indicate that if you replayed The Witcher 3 in its shiny fresh remastered form and got absolutely bodied by a couple of werewolf fights you thought were easier back when you had more youthful reflexes, don't worry—the problem wasn't your aging brain and hands after all. It was a bug that's been caught and exterminated in patch 5.02. "Fixed an matter where werewolves could regenerate health too promptly on higher difficulty settings," the patch notes say, "making them impossible to defeat.".

According to recent reports, another problem some players had with The Witcher 3 Remastered was its lighting, with interiors in particular looking too dark while some other locations were washed-out flashbangs. Good news: "Fixed various lighting issues across multiple locations and cutscenes," the patch notes say, "including overly bright or dark areas, inconsistent lighting transitions and flickering." They've also "Fixed various lighting issues with hair when Path Tracing is enabled" and "Made various Path Tracing and Ray Reconstruction optimizations and visual improvements, including lighting, shadows, and water rendering.". It has been revealed that that's all positive stuff, but the balance changes to various signs and mutations will be more controversial. The Remastered version released with buffs to Witcher signs that made them useful even if you didn't specialize in them, but it may have overdone things a little. This patch walks back some of the potential for you to dominate the entire game with an Aard build, though changes like reducing the Catalyst skill's bonus to Aard and Igni intensity by 10% don't seem like they'll totally change the way you play. (Related: CD Projekt Red says The Witcher 4 will be as big a leap...)

Recent findings suggest that I get that people don't like skill nerfs in singleplayer games, but I can also see CD Projekt anticipating next year's Songs of the Past DLC. They don't want to hear a bunch of players say, "It sucked because I trivialized the whole thing with a broken build I got by copying a YouTuber." If some skill nerfs and a reduction to stamina regeneration in combat mean you have to drink more potions to get your signs off, it probably won't ruin your fun.

Oh, and if you couldn't complete your Gwent collection because Zoltan Chivay's card didn't show up, the patch notes say it'll now appear in the correct place under the Hanged Man's Tree in Velen. Time to go back and get that 100% achievement at last.
