Benzinga News, Company Logos and Market Data Now Available Through Alpheya's AI-Powered Wealth Platform for Financial Institutions.

In a recent development, DETROIT, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Benzinga, a leading provider of real-time financial news and market intelligence, today announced an expanded data relationship with Alpheya, a wealth technology company headquartered in Abu Dhabi. Through this relationship, Alpheya will make Benzinga's real-time news and broader suite of data products available to banks and wealth management institutions.

Sources indicate that alpheya works with banks, family offices, and asset and wealth managers. Its platform gives these institutions a single cloud-based, AI-powered system for client apps, portfolio management and trading. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

It has been revealed that many banks and private banks are now modernizing the digital experience they offer clients. To meet that shift, institutions want Benzinga market content inside their own mobile apps, so clients can follow their holdings without leaving the bank's platform. The agreement gives Alpheya's clients a direct path to that content.

Benzinga's news coverage includes earnings, analyst ratings, corporate events and breaking market headlines, giving investors context on the stocks they hold and trade. Beyond news, Benzinga's broader suite of structured market data products can support watchlists, portfolio views, research tools and trading workflows across Alpheya's platform.

According to recent reports, "Banks are building wealth apps that compete with the best digital investing experiences anywhere, and the content inside those apps matters," mentioned Clint Rhea, Manager of Institutional and Channel Partnerships at Benzinga. "Working with Alpheya gives banks and wealth managers a simple way to add Benzinga content through a platform they already rely on.". "Wealth clients don't just need market news and data. They need to know what it means for their portfolio. With Benzinga inside the Alpheya platform, financial institutions can deliver trusted market intelligence, interpreted by Alpheya AI, so clients can act with confidence," said Deepak Khanna, Chief Product Officer at Alpheya.

The agreement also builds on growing interest in Benzinga's data across the Middle East, adding a upcoming channel to the brokerages, trading platforms and fintech companies that already use Benzinga globally.

Through its news platform, APIs, and data products, Benzinga provides traders, financial institutions, and fintech platforms with the insights they need to make smarter investment decisions. From breaking news and analyst ratings to corporate events and alternative datasets, Benzinga's tools help market participants stay ahead of the information that drives price movement.

About AlpheyaAlpheya is a cloud-native, AI-powered wealth management platform headquartered in Abu Dhabi and built for financial institutions worldwide. Its modular B2B and B2B2C solutions support financial institutions' investors, relationship managers, advisors and portfolio managers, covering the full wealth journey from client onboarding and financial planning to portfolio management, trading, robo-advisory and performance reporting. At the heart of the platform are Alpheya's award-winning AI capabilities, including fully conversational AI journeys. Alpheya connects directly to an institution's back-end systems and to a wide ecosystem of brokers, market data and news providers.
