Luxury US Startup Bets Significant On 10 Airbus A220s With A Private Suite And Just 54 Seats

🌐 Source: Simpleflying • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (56 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Airbus has confirmed Florida-based luxury airline startup Magnifica Air as the mystery buyer of ten ACJ220-300s, the corporate/private jet version of the A220-300. The carrier intends to equip each jet with just 54 seats, including a private suite. This is FAR less than the roughly 150 seats in a typical airline configuration.
