What exactly could keep investors from maintaining their conviction in TRUMP?

For context, U.S. President Donald Trump is set to host an exclusive dinner for top holders of his TRUMP memecoin on Sunday. The timing is interesting. Politically, the Democrats’ victory in the midterm elections adds another layer of uncertainty for TRUMP holders. According to recent reports, another glitch to note is that 10 wallets make up 80.6% of the overall token supply. So, while there are over 691k holders of TRUMP, a large percentage of these tokens are concentrated within a few wallets. (Related: Top Democrat asks Trump administration to stop using di...)

Against this backdrop, it’s no surprise that President Trump is hosting this dinner.

According to recent reports, the logic is simple: If any large holders (there are 10 holders holding >80% of TRUMP) begin to sell TRUMP, it may cause panic selling. This risk is increased as TRUMP is already facing macroeconomic uncertainty.

So, is the dinner a “strategic” attempt to bring back interest in the memecoin?

The speculative capital in this cycle isn’t flowing into the memecoin market.

According to recent reports, this marks a key difference from previous cycles, when investors often turned to memecoins during volatile market conditions to chase quick gains. But this time, the trend looks different. Despite broader market weakness, the memecoin market cap has continued to decline, with over $20 billion wiped out in Q4 so far.

But could President Trump’s dinner initiative change that? From a technical standpoint, TRUMP is deeply oversold, trading 99% below its all-time high and recently breaking below the key $1 level. If the dinner sparks fresh momentum, traders could look to take advantage of these lower price levels ahead of the event.

According to AMBCrypto, this could bring some speculative capital back into memecoins.

Industry observers note that however, sustainability is a concern here. Traders often come in and out of memecoins, so there may be little sustainability in this position in the long-term. Furthermore, as 80% of Official Trump [TRUMP] supply is concentrated in only 10 wallets, traders may be cautious of building up spot exposure.

Alternatively, they could favor short-term speculative trades to capitalize on  any event-driven price action. This could lead to increased speculation. However, due to weak market conditions, it may cause a newly introduced wave of de-leveraging and put more pressure on the larger crypto market.

TRUMP’s supply concentration and political uncertainty could weaken investor confidence, increasing the risk of sudden sell-offs.

Trump’s dinner may revive short-term memecoin speculation, but weak market conditions could trigger fresh deleveraging.
