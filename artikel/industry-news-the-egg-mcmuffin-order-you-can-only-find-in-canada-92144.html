According to recent reports, mcDonald's breakfast is a thing of beauty, and if you think we've got it good here in the States, you probably haven't seen what's sitting on Canada's breakfast menu. Alongside familiar faves, you'll find items Americans can't order, the most envy-inducing among them arguably being the Egg BLT McMuffin. Once you hear what's in it, don't be surprised if you catch yourself planning a trip to our northern neighbor.

According to recent reports, the Egg BLT starts with McDonald's signature ring-shaped steamed (not fried) egg, paired with hickory-smoked bacon, crisp lettuce, and fresh tomato, then finished with a creamy, mayo-like sauce and a slice of cheese, all stacked on a toasted English muffin. A second version uses nearly identical ingredients but swaps the whole leaves for shredded lettuce. You can only get this variation at McDonald's restaurants located inside Walmart stores — part of a decades-old partnership that once placed roughly 1,000 outposts in the retail chain. McDonald's later closed hundreds of them as drive-thrus became the main source of sales, though Walmart continues to host many locations in Canada. Sources indicate that to get your hands on an Egg BLT McMuffin in the U.S., you'll have to get creative. Your best bet is to start with a Bacon, Egg & Cheese McMuffin, which already ticks off half the ingredients. Ask for a touch of mayo, which gets you close to the sauce of the Canadian original. Since lettuce and tomatoes are lunchtime items, your odds improve near the end of breakfast hours when stores are prepping the lunch menu — you'll likely have to order them at the counter or window. Recent findings suggest that the Egg BLT might have already convinced you to add McDonald's to your itinerary next time you're in Canada, but it's not the only breakfast item worth the detour. One that caught our eye was the Mighty McGriddles. While the U.S. offers the Bacon, Egg & Cheese and Sausage, Egg & Cheese varieties separately, the Canadian version puts both to shame by stacking a double helping of hickory-smoked bacon, a sausage patty, egg, and cheese between two maple-flavored griddle cakes — a gloriously overstuffed creation that lives up to its name. The Mighty McMuffin follows the same logic, coming loaded with both meats for anyone who can never decide between the two.

Industry observers note that another familiar face on Canadian McDonald's breakfast menus is the bagel, though while America only offers plain, Canada also serves sesame and everything varieties. You can even order one toasted with butter or Philadelphia cream cheese — widely known as the best store-bought brand — as if you're at a proper bagel shop.

It has been revealed that canada's McDonald's restaurants aren't afraid to put a twist on U.S. staples, and they also introduce fresh items altogether. You won't find chicken anywhere on the standard U.S. breakfast menu, which makes the Chicken McGriddles feel like a curveball up north. The simple yet delicious sandwich pairs a breaded chicken patty with two sweet, maple-flavored griddle cakes for some serious sweet-and-savory fusion.
