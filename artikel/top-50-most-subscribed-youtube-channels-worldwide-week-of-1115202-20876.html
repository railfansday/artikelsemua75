According to recent reports, netflix is counting on Alan’s Universe to have a major impact. The streamer has spent freely to build partnerships with creators who first got significant on platforms like YouTube, and Alan Chikin Chow is one of the biggest names of that ilk who has come to terms with Netflix. Chow’s sprawling agreement with Netflix includes a collaboration with HYBE America that will incubate a K-pop group that can thrive across multiple continents.

Chow’s substantial Netflix play arrived on November 14, when fans met BOYNEXTDOOR, the K-pop outfit tied to the Alan’s Universe…universe. For this particular column, we’re not interested in the project’s performance on Netflix. We’re looking at YouTube, where both Chow and Netflix are benefitting from their mutual agreement.

Industry observers note that alan’s Universe, however, isn’t the only YouTube channel that has benefitted from the creator’s Netflix arrival. The streamer itself is active on YouTube, where an official channel promotes newly introduced and upcoming releases.

It has been revealed that that channel didn’t crack this week’s Global Sub Top 50, but it has been rising over the past six months. As Netflix has inked more deals with creators, it has increased its presence on the platform many of those creators call home. Over the past two months, Netflix has consistently hauled in at least 25 million views per week, and during some seven-day periods, its traffic has been much higher than that.

Recent findings suggest that many of those upticks have been powered by creator-adjacent media. A trailer for Salish Matter’s upcoming Netflix original has been a popular viewing option, and content related to K-pop continues to pick up hits as well.

If K-pop is the favorite topic of Netflix’s YouTube base (and, given the success of Kpop Demon Hunters, why wouldn’t it be?), then a team-up with Alan Chikin Chow and HYBE America is a wise choice. Those chefs have come together to cook up BOYNEXTDOOR, and the fresh group figures to make a major impact across multiple platforms.
