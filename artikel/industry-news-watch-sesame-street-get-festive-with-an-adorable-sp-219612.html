The Muppets of Sesame Street don’t want a lot for Christmas — there’s just one thing they need: literally U!

“If we had no U for Christmas, there’d be no U words you see,” the quartet sings jovially, trading off lines as a green letter U with a Santa hat bounces around to the jolly beat. “We could not unwrap the presents underneath the Christmas tree/ Without words that start with U, the holiday would not feel right/ We couldn’t even untie ribbons/ Or untangle Christmas lights/ Want to unbox Christmas but we can’t/ Won’t you help us understand/ Don’t know what we’ll do/ All we need for Christmas is U!”. Industry observers note that pop superstar Carey’s Christmas staple has so far topped the Hot 100 for 22 weeks since it first reached No. on the Dec. 19, 2019-dated chart, 25 years after the song was originally released. The holiday classic broke the Hot 100’s record for most weeks at No. 1 in December 2025, when it took the summit for a 20th week; it currently boasts 22 weeks at the top spot. (Ella Langley took the record from the Queen of Christmas in September, when “Choosin’ Texas” nabbed its 23rd week at No. 1 on the Hot 100.).
