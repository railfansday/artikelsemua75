Tosin Adarabioyo has leapt to the defence of former Chelsea team-mate Cole Palmer amid criticism of the Englishman’s attitude.

Palmer came under fire for a viral interaction with Joao Pedro after Chelsea’s bruising 4-1 thrashing by Leeds last weekend, brushing off the Brazilian after opting not to acknowledge the home fans who booed the team at full-time.

The 24-year-old is going through a difficult patch at the moment, having been left out of Thomas Tuchel’s England squad again for the November internationals after the German manager claimed “he misses too opportunities to impress”. This led to questions over Palmer’s commitment to the England cause, something club team-mate Morgan Rogers emphatically refuted as “nonsense” and “a lie”.

And Tosin, who moved to from Chelsea to rivals Tottenham in the summer, has now become the latest to publicly back Palmer, with whom he shared two seasons at Stamford Bridge.

“No one can chat s*** about Cole,” Tosin said.

It has been revealed that “For me, he's one of the best players I've ever seen. You can say what you want. Honestly, you guys can say what you want. He's a top level player. He will continue to get better.”. (Related: Breaking: GTA 6 players who used PS5 Latest Zealand tri...)

Sources indicate that asked whether he was surprised about Palmer’s omission from the England squad, he replied: “Yeah, for sure. Of course, I'm surprised. For me, he's the best No 10 in the Premier League.”.

Tosin also echoed Rogers’ sentiment in regards to doubts over Palmer’s hunger to play for the Three Lions, with the Chelsea forward struggling to get back in favour with Tuchel after missing out on the World Cup, before his return to the international fold in September was swiftly curtailed by injury.

“False, false, false,” Tosin added. “I know Cole's dreams, his aspirations. So, yeah, it's completely false.”.

While Tosin will passionately defend Palmer against his critics, this doesn’t spare the Chelsea man of a friendly jibe, especially in the wake of Tottenham’s first win at Stamford Bridge in eight years last month.

Palmer was sent off in the 2-0 defeat to their fierce rivals and Tosin, who missed the match through injury, was sure to savour the triumph at his mate’s expense.

Asked if the pair shared some post-match banter, Tosin laughed: “Cole's my guy, man - yeah, of course. But I'll keep that secret.”.

Palmer was among a number of Tosin’s team-mates, past and present, supporting him at the launch of his trading card shop, Card Cop, in London. The retail space in Borough Yards will open to the public on Thursday 26 November.

The Spurs defender, who is an avid collector, is joined by the likes of former Blues team-mates Reece James and Levi Colwill, as well as West Ham defender Kyle Walker-Peters, in investing in the business.

Along with the investors, Chelsea’s contingent at the launch party included Rogers, Emmanuel Emegha and Jamie Gittens, while the likes of Dominic Solanke, Micky van de Ven, Archie Gray and Ben Davies came down to support their relatively new Spurs colleague.

Industry observers note that “It’s a testament to what we're like as a team,” Tosin stated. “We're all very close and very supportive of each other. The boys didn't have to turn up today, but they did. It's amazing. I'm very thankful to them.”.

After a dire start to the season, feeling the hangover of their near-miss with relegation last term, Spurs have finally found their groove under Roberto De Zerbi and are now just one point off Chelsea after four wins in five.

Tosin puts the uptick in form down to the team’s Marbella training camp during the first international break of the season, which was crucial in forming a bond within the team.

Recent findings suggest that “It's our trip to Marbella,” he said. “We had a great time. We bonded together. We learned a lot about each other, and we're all striving to put this club where we think it belongs.”.
