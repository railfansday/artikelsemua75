In a recent development, hedera [HBAR] has rallied 8.96% in 24 hours, alongside a modest 2.6% Bitcoin [BTC] bounce from its $57.3k low on Wednesday. Hedera’s token price bounce was one of the biggest among the top 35 crypto assets by market capitalization.

Sources indicate that there was no direct catalyst or coin-specific development that helped Hedera post its heavy short-term gains. Rather, the price could be moving from one pocket of liquidity to another, aided by Bitcoin’s bounce.

It has been revealed that the 2-week liquidation heatmap highlighted $0.0480-$0.0493 as a nearby cluster of long liquidations. The selling pressure saw HBAR decline from its $0.056 Monday peak.

After sweeping the $0.048 liquidity pocket, which was also around the $0.048 local low HBAR token made last week, the altcoin has rallied 11.3%.

It has been revealed that this was a strong price reaction. CoinMarketCap data revealed a 26% daily trading volume increase, showing demand backed the short-term gains.

Industry observers note that to the north, the $0.057 zone was a strong magnetic zone. A denser cluster of liquidity gathered around $0.06, the local supply zone. It appeared likely that HBAR was preparing to test these resistance zones. (Related: Industry News: Train a Sentence Embedding Model with 1B...)

Sources indicate that while the short-term momentum and sizeable bullish relative strength against Bitcoin were tempting, buyers should remain cautious. The price trend on the 1-day timeframe was bearish.

In particular, the magnetic zones highlighted earlier at $0.057 and $0.06 were close to the local swing points from earlier this month, at $0.0548 and $0.0594.

It is highly likely that an HBAR foray into these zones would result in a setback as underwater and short-term holders rush to exit their positions.

According to recent reports, the OBV was in a deep downtrend and revealed steady selling pressure in recent weeks. A pause in this downtrend would be a good sign for investors looking to buy.

Until $0.059 is breached and the OBV begins to reverse, swing traders and investors can maintain a bearish bias.
