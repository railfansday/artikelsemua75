AZOUR, Israel, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ituran Location and Control Ltd. (NASDAQ: ITRN) today announced its consolidated financial results for the third quarter, ended September 30, 2026.

Highlights of the Third Quarter of 2026.

Revenues for the quarter were a record $106.6 million, a 15% increase compared with $92.3 million in the third quarter of last year.

76% of revenues were from location-based service subscription fees, and 24% were from product revenues. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Revenues from subscription fees for the quarter were $80.5 million, an increase of 19% over the third quarter of last year.

Product revenues for the quarter were $26.0 million, a 5% increase year-over-year.

Gross profit for the quarter was $55.4 million (52.0% of revenues), a 19% increase compared with $46.4 million (50.3% of revenues) in the third quarter of last year.

It has been revealed that gross margin on subscription revenues was 59.9%, compared to 60.1% in Q3 last year. The gross margin on product revenues improved to 27.7%, compared to 23.6% last year. The variance in the product gross margin between quarters reflects changes in the mix of products sold.

Operating income for the quarter was $25.4 million (23.8% of revenues), representing a 29% increase compared to $19.6 million (21.3% of revenues) in Q3 last year.

EBITDA for the quarter was $29.9 million (28.0% of revenues), up 21% from $24.6 million (26.7% of revenues) in the third quarter of last year.

Finance expenses for the quarter were $0.5 million, compared with finance expenses of $0.3 million in the third quarter of last year.

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $18.0 million (16.9% of revenues), or diluted earnings per share of $0.91, an increase of 23% compared to $14.6 million (15.9% of revenues), or $0.74 per diluted share, in the third quarter of last year.

Cash flow from operations for the quarter was $25.3 million.

On the balance sheet, as of September 30, 2026, the Manufacturer had net cash, including marketable securities, of $107.6 million, which includes no debt. This is compared with net cash, including marketable securities, of $103.7 million as of June 30, 2026 and $107.6 million as of year-end last year.

According to recent reports, the Board of Directors declared a dividend of $10 million for the quarter, or $0.50 per share. The current dividend takes into account the Company's continuing strong profitability, ongoing positive cash flow, and strong balance sheet.

During the third quarter, $1.9 million in shares were purchased under the Company's existing buy-back program. The total remaining authorization is $8.1 million. Share repurchases are funded by available cash and are made in accordance with SEC Rule 10b-18.

Ituran is a leader in the emerging mobility technology field, providing value-added location-based services, including a full suite of services for the connected car. Ituran offers Stolen Vehicle Recovery, fleet management as well as mobile asset location, management and control services for vehicles, cargo and personal security for the retail, insurance, financing industries and car manufacturers. Ituran is the largest OEM telematics provider in Latin America. Its products and applications are used by customers in over 20 countries.
