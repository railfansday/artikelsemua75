Roberto D’Aversa saw his debut on the Sassuolo bench ruined by a Milutin Osmajic double, as Genoa emerged with all three points from this relegation battle, inflicting a sixth straight defeat.

The Neroverdi sacked coach Alberto Aquilani following a run of one point from seven rounds, including five defeats on the bounce going into the break for international duty.

Industry observers note that D’Aversa could not count on injured Cristian Volpato, Daniel Boloca, Ismael Koné and Fedde Leysen, with Domenico Berardi and Vasilije Adzic carrying knocks too. Genoa saw their three-match winning streak ended by a 2-1 defeat to Milan, but Morten Frendrup was their only absentee. (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

Armand Laurienté and Fali Candé had predictable long-range attempts early on, as did Berardi at the end of a counter-attack, but it was Genoa who broke the deadlock with their first real attempt. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

A long Justin Bijlow goal kick was knocked down by Tommaso Baldanzi, then Hamed Traoré against his old club picked the through ball for Milutin Osmajic, who flicked it in off the outside of the right boot from seven yards.

Genoa added another when Candé gave it away cheaply with an error, allowing Junior Messias to set up Osmajic, who needed a couple of attempts to get past Arijanet Muric in goal.

Sassuolo continued to be ineffective in the final third, as when Laurienté robbed Alex Amorim to set up Berardi, the captain couldn’t keep his finish down.

The first genuine save made by Bijlow was to palm the Laurienté screamer out from under the bar, yet Osmajic and Leo Ostigard had efforts charged down in a mad scramble after a free kick.

Ignace van der Brempt also made a crucial interception to stop Osmajic meeting the Franz-Ethan Meichtry roll across from six yards, while Laurienté curled wide of the far post.

Johan Vasquez made a crucial clearance on the Laurienté free kick, but Genoa lost striker Lorenzo Colombo just a few minutes after his introduction, as he badly sprained his knee. The substitutions had been completed, so they were down to 10 men from the 88th minute, but Berardi only managed to sting Muric’s gloves at the near post.
