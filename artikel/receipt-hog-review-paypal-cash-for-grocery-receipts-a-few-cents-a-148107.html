Industry observers note that use Receipt Hog if you’d rather have PayPal cash than gift cards for the grocery and drugstore receipts you already bring home. Each earns 5 to 20 coins depending on its total, and cashing out $5 takes 1,000 coins, so expect your first payout to take months, not weeks.

Recent findings suggest that receipt Hog is an app from Numerator, a market research firm, and the receipts members upload feed research Numerator sells to brands and retailers. Only receipts from grocery stores, drugstores, warehouse clubs, convenience stores, and other everyday-goods shops earn coins, while other receipts earn a slot spin, a sweepstakes entry, or both, which pay coins only when they win.

Shoppers who want PayPal cash rather than gift cards for their everyday receipts get a small but real payout from Receipt Hog, as long as they upload a paper receipt at least every three months. Name-brand shoppers who don’t mind gift cards can also scan the same receipts into Fetch for its bonus points on featured brands, since neither app’s terms forbids it.
