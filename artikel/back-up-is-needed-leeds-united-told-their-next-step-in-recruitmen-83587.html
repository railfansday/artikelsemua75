Premier League icon Michael Owen believes the signing of a forward should be the “next step” for Leeds United.

Leeds signed Dominic Calvert-Lewin on a free transfer from Everton in the summer of 2025 and he has since led the line to widespread acclaim. His form has pushed him back into the England fold and he recently picked up his 15th cap in a 3-2 Nations League win over Spain.
