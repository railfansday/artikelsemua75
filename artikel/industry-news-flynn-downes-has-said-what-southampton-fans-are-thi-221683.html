Southampton midfielder Flynn Downes ensured that his side are "ready to go" ahead of the first clash with Middlesbrough since last season's play-off semi-finals on Friday night.

The first Spygate derby is upon us, as Kim Hellberg and Tonda Eckert lock horns under the lights at St Mary's, and anticipation is high, not only among fans of the respective sides, but also among neutrals, too. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

Back in May, these two sides were set to meet in the play-off semi-finals. Before the first leg, Middlesbrough officials caught a Southampton analyst illegally spying on their training sessions. (Related: Latest Update: F1 star Max Verstappen played 40 hours o...)

The games still went ahead, and Saints came out on top after extra time, prevailing 2-1 at St Mary's. They didn't progress to the final, though, as the EFL would expel them from the play-offs due to the spying incident, reinstating Middlesbrough in their place to go on and face Hull City, which they ended up losing.

Since that defeat at Wembley, the two meetings between Southampton and Boro were immediately marked as two of the must-watch games in the division, and Saints players are clearly excited to welcome their latest rivals to St Mary's.

Those Southampton players who were part of the squad last season will have felt cheated out of being able to become Premier League stars once more when they were expelled from the play-offs.

Industry observers note that saints fans will have felt the same, too, as it hardly was their fault, either. Because of that, there's been a bit of an edge online between both sets of supporters, as they felt Middlesbrough didn't deserve to take their place. Some may play it down, but deep down they'll want to win a little more than normal on Friday night.

Sources indicate that downes played a part in both semi-final legs back in May, coming off the bench in the second leg and playing through extra time. He admitted to Southampton's channels that the dressing room's mindset is a little similar to the fans' heading into the Boro clash.

After being asked how the players feel about a Middlesbrough rematch, the 27-year-old chuckled and said, "Probably similar to the fans, because we are all fans at the end of the day.

In a recent development, "I know we play, but we love the club, so probably similar to them. We're all up for it, and we're all ready to go.

"I know we've got to look at it a little more subjectively, like it's just another game that we have to go and win, but yeah, I'm sure there'll be a bit more needle in it.".

Sources indicate that the tough-tackling midfielder will relish games like these and will be desperate to come out on top. Not only would it see them get one over their new rivals, but it would close the gap between the two sides to four points.

Ultimately, this is a rivalry that is growing mainly off the field. Both Kim Hellberg and Tonda Eckert have done their best to play down the incident back in May, and as Downes alluded to, the focus initially will be on winning a game of football.

But in the stands, the atmosphere will be grittier than normal at St Mary's, as both sets of fans will be looking to get one up over each other. That's started online ahead of the game.

Saints supporters loved what Downes had to say ahead of the game. One fan commented on X, "What a legend. This is one man we can count on when the games matter - he understands." "Give him the armband," another wrote.

Recent findings suggest that middlesbrough fans have gotten their hands on the interview, too, though. As is always the case when a club puts importance on a game, there were plenty of "cup final" comments among them.

"Southampton trying to manufacture a rivalry because Portsmouth won’t give them enough attention," one Boro fan commented. "300 mile away and we’re still their cup final," another said.

In a recent development, there are plenty of layers to this game. Not only regarding the fallout from the Spygate and how both sides will approach that part, but also the fact that these are two of the best sides in the Championship going head-to-head.

Industry observers note that middlesbrough top the league for a reason. Kim Hellberg's side have bounced back from that play-off final loss in May spectacularly, and are starting to properly hit their stride in the second-tier once more.

In a recent development, opposite them are a Saints team who are imperious at home. Only Bolton Wanderers have come away from St Mary's with any points this season, and Tonda Eckert's side are 18 unbeaten on their own patch (W14 D4). The last side to beat them there was Hull back in January.

Both teams play attractive football, led by two of the more exciting, up-and-coming coaches in the game right now. When you add to that all the Spygate fallout, this has all the makings of a Friday night classic in the Championship.
