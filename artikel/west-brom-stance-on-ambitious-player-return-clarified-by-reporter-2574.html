Industry observers note that there has been tentative online speculation that the defender could be on West Brom’s radar ahead of the January transfer window. WBA will get the chance to bring in some reinforcements as they eye promotion from the Championship to the Premier League.

Heggem, 27, is under contract at Bologna until 2028 meaning he still has two years left to run down on his deal in Serie A. He made the switch to Italy from the Baggies back in August 2025 and he has since made 45 appearances in all competitions, seven of which have come this season.

Reporter Hermon has claimed on X that there is ‘no truth’ to rumours linking him with a surprise return to The Hawthorns anytime soon. James Morrison’s side will need to look elsewhere for targets as they aim to cement their place in the play-offs over the coming weeks and months.

West Brom beat Millwall 3-0 at home last time out before the international break after goals by Isaac Price, Jayson Molumby and Felix Horn Myhre. They take on Swansea City away next up this weekend.

Industry observers note that west Brom are well stocked up with options in the heart of defence. WBA’s main defender is George Campbell, who has recently been rewarded with a fresh deal running until 2029. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

The Baggies also have experienced pair Nat Phillips and Chris Mepham at their disposal. Therefore, they have sufficient depth and although Heggem would have been an eye-catching and exciting acquisition, they don’t particularly need him.

Morrison should target other positions to strengthen in such as central midfield. Ousmane Diakite has sustained an injury and will need replacing in the short-term.

Further up the pitch, another striker or winger should also be on the agenda if possible. West Brom will also have the opportunity to let some players leave to help free up space and funds to pave the way for arrivals.

West Brom could reignite interest in Heggem next summer if they want to. If WBA go up, they will have more financial power to play with. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Heggem has seen his game time dry up at Bologna over recent times and a change of scene may do him good. He was a hit during his time with the Baggies and played 46 matches altogether.

A January won’t happen, as per Hermon, probably due to finances. That isn’t to say he won’t return one day down the line though.
