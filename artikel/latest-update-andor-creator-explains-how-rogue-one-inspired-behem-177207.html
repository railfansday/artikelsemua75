Recent findings suggest that tony Gilroy's Behemoth! tells the story of Alex (Pedro Pascal), a professional cellist who is reliving moments in his life after returning to Los Angeles to care for his ailing mother. Using the score as a way of transporting Alex back into his own memories, the film jumps around through 20+ years of his life and career and indicates Gilroy's ability to tell a story that spans any number of years within a character's life. To celebrate the film's release on December 4, Collider is thrilled to bring you an exclusive behind-the-scenes look at one of the most fascinating parts of the film: its composers.

Recent findings suggest that the film has nine unique composers bringing the world of Behemoth! to life, including the likes of Michael Giacchino, Michael Abels, Emily Bear, Alan Silvestri, Lukas Frank, James Newton Howard, Henry Jackman, Nami Melumad, and Brandon Roberts. Throughout Behemoth!, Alex is working on a sound stage with other musicians to help score a movie, something that Gilroy has talked about finding fascinating in the lead-up to the film's release. More often than not, a film's score ends up being something fans love, but we rarely get to see how it is composed, until now. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

The featurette points to Gilroy's inspiration, including his fascination with Giacchino's process when scoring his film, Rogue One: A Star Wars Story. Gilroy talks about how he was a musician for a large portion of his life, even sharing with some of the composers of Behemoth! how he was trying to get a record deal by the time he was 25, before finding that same passion in creating film and television. Even Pascal shared how he used to go to movies as a young man based on who the composer was, making Behemoth! something special for him. It has been revealed that certain movies are made all the more iconic by their score. Fans can pick films out by score alone. It highlights how deeply a score can matter to the audience. With Behemoth!, you're part of the process. Alex is taking the music a composer was inspired to make and bringing it to life, live in the studio, and we get the joy of then seeing how that score paints his life. It's an incredibly meta story and one that every movie (and music) fan will love.

Recent findings suggest that you can see Behemoth! on December 4. Stay tuned at Collider for more.
