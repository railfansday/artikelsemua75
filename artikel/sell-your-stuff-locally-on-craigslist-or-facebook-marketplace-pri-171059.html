📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that list furniture, appliances, and other things too bulky or cheap to ship on both Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist, priced 10% to 20% above the least you’ll take so there’s room to haggle. Add daylight photos and measurements, answer messages rapidly, and get paid before anything leaves your hands.

Buyers on local sites expect to haggle. Price a sofa at the least you’ll take, and the first offer you get is one you have to turn down.

According to recent reports, find the same item, or the closest match, listed near you on both sites. Set your floor, the least you’ll take, near the lower asking prices you find, since an asking price is only what a seller hopes to get. Then list 10% to 20% above that floor.

Say your floor on a sofa is $300. List it at $350, and when the first serious buyer offers $300, you can say yes and set a pickup for the next evening.

Write a headline that starts with the brand and model, the words buyers search for. Take the photos in daylight, give the length, depth, and height so buyers can tell whether it fits their doorway and their car, and name the evenings or weekend hours you’re free for pickup. Post the same listing on Facebook Marketplace and on Craigslist, which charges nothing to list furniture or appliances you sell yourself, and delete both once it sells. Industry observers note that answer messages fast, and focus on buyers who name a price or a pickup time. Book the first one who commits to a time, and keep the next in line as a backup. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

For anything that fits in a car, meet in public. Craigslist’s safety page points to a bank or a police department as the place to meet, and some departments set aside areas for these sales. Hand over a sofa or a fridge in your garage or on your porch instead, with a friend there, since Craigslist also advises against inviting strangers inside.

Count the cash, or check that the payment indicates in your own app, before the buyer loads anything.
