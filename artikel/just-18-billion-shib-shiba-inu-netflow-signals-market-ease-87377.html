Although the current positive balance in the SHIB netflow shows that the number of SHIB tokens moved to exchanges for sell-off attempts is about 18 billion higher than the number purchased, its price movement is already turning bullish.

Latest market data shows that Shiba Inu has surged by 1.51% over the last 24 hours, bringing its price to $0.000003625 as of the time of writing.

According to recent reports, this suggests that demand is gradually returning to the SHIB ecosystem after multiple weeks of prolonged volatility. However, it remains uncertain whether the sudden price reversal is just a brief relief rally or a major recovery.
