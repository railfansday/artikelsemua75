According to recent reports, google is announcing some significant changes to its smart home software. Starting next year, the tech giant will shut down the Nest app in favor of the Google Home app and retire Google Assistant on its smart home devices.

The Nest app shutdown takes place on January 25th, 2027, meaning you’ll have to manage your Nest products, including Nest thermostats, cameras, and Nest Protect alarms, from the Google Home app instead. If you get the Google Home app set up, Google will move over your Nest devices, including cameras and video history, to the Home app on that January 25th date. The tech giant will also automatically transfer Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions to Google Home Premium. Google says it will begin deleting Nest account data on March 25th, 2027.

In a recent development, the changes for Google Assistant will take place later: On August 2nd, 2027, the Gemini for Home voice assistant will replace Google Assistant on smart speakers and smart displays, the manufacturer indicates. Google argues that Gemini for Home allows consumers to ask more complex questions and speak naturally instead of needing to use dedicated commands or specific language. Gemini for Home was confirmed last year, so it’s not exactly a shock that Google is going to move on from Google Assistant in the smart home (especially when the tech giant also killed Google Assistant on phones earlier this year). The company notes it plans to share more updates about the changes ahead of the August 2027 transition.
