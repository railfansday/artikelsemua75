📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nvidia shares plunged 14.01% on Friday to close at $125.53, a fresh 52-week low and nearly half their record of $243.37. Trading volume hit 633.4 million shares, almost four times the usual level, as the stock touched an intraday low of $124.50. Nvidia’s market value has fallen to about $3.03 trillion. The slide has pushed its valuation to its cheapest point in more than ten years. The stock now trades at about 16 times trailing earnings and under 10 times expected profits, according to a Motley Fool analysis, even though Nvidia produced $43.8 billion in free cash flow last quarter and is expected to nearly double sales this year. Three concerns are weighing on shares: fallout from Jensen Huang’s resignation and possible export curbs, questions over Nvidia funding customers to absorb chip supply, and fears that rival chips will squeeze margins. Long-term demand still looks strong. Apollo Global Management says on-demand GPU capacity is effectively sold out, while PwC expects data centre spending of $20 trillion to $50 trillion through 2050.
