West Ham United believe that selling striker Taty Castellanos in January is a move that will "suit all parties," sources have told Football Insider.

Recent reports have suggested that the Argentine wants out of the London Stadium, and the Hammers are not prepared to stand in his way this winter.

The 28-year-old has scored just one goal from 15 games in the Championship this season, and is performing way below the level West Ham expected from him.

Sources say that the East London outfit are prepared to sell Castellanos at a loss this winter, knowing that it will be "very difficult" to recoup the £26million they paid for him.

The player himself is also understood to be keen to play at a higher level than the second tier. (Related: Industry News: GTA 6 player hit with 30-day PSN ban aft...)

Castellanos netted six goals in 18 Premier League games after joining West Ham last January, but he has struggled to find any sort of form this term.

West Ham have endured a difficult start on their return to the Championship, and are now sitting outside the play-off places in tenth. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

They have brought in Scott Parker as manager in an attempt to galvanise the squad, and several changes in their group are expected in January.

Former West Ham scout Mick Brown recently told Football Insider that Parker is planning an "overhaul during the winter window.

Castellanos is expected to be one of the first names out the door, as the club try to clear out the group that got them relegated last season.

With Castellanos' performances leaving a lot to be desired, there is little to point towards him seeing out the remainder of the campaign at West Ham.

Brown also told Football Insider last week that West Ham are keeping tabs on a potential move for Sheffield United midfielder Sydie Peck.

The ex-recruitment man pointed out that the 22-year-old is widely regarded as being among the "best talent in the Championship.".

Brown believes that Peck could become available next summer, and would tick "plenty of boxes" as a project for them to work on long-term.

However, Newcastle and Sunderland are also tracking Peck at the moment, which is set to make it difficult for West Ham to get a deal done.
