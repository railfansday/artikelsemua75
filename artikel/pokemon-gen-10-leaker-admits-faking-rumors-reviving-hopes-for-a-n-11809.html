A Pokemon Winds & Waves leaker has admitted to faking rumors about the Gen 10 games, giving fans fresh hope for voice acting and Eevee’s first new evolution in more than a decade.

Sources indicate that moot, who posts as @Mootmonthly on X, had built a reputation for sharing information about the Pokemon anime. Centro Leaks had publicly vouched for him as a reliable anime source, and his claims about an internal delay for Winds & Waves made it into Dexerto’s November 9 coverage.

Meanwhile, a separate account called @kurwashibamaste had been feeding Centro Leaks rumors about the games since July. It claimed a development build appeared to lack fresh evolutions for Eevee and Tropius. It also claimed voice acting had been planned but had disappeared from recent builds.

On November 17, an X user posted a screenshot appearing to show Moot and @kurwashibamaste in the same account switcher. Moot responded with a GIF captioned “How it feels to spread misinformation and lies.” Centro then remarked Moot had admitted to running the alternate account and faking many of its Winds & Waves rumors.

Others were happy to see those rumors fall apart. Pokemon creator SoulSilverArt stated a Tropius evolution, a new Eeveelution, and voice acting were “back on the menu.” Eevee’s last new evolution was Sylveon, which debuted in Pokemon X and Y in 2013.

Centro mentioned some real details may have been mixed in. It pointed to the rumor of around 300 new Pokemon and forms as one it had heard separately, adding that development plans could have changed.

According to recent reports, the Pokemon Organization has yet to announce voice acting or a upcoming Eevee evolution. It has confirmed a 2027 release on Nintendo Switch 2 and revealed Gen 10 starters Browt, Pombon, and Gecqua.
