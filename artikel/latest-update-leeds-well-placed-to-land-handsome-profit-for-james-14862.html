Leeds United know that they will be able to secure a hugely lucrative fee for starting goalkeeper James Trafford in the coming years.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 24-year-old’s valuation will have soared after his latest display for England.

Trafford was given the nod by Thomas Tuchel to start the Three Lions’ decisive Nations League game away to Spain last Sunday, and made some eye-catching saves. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Wyness believes that Leeds have no plans to sell the ex-Manchester City star any time soon, but know they will be in for a “handsome profit” when the time comes.

The Whites shelled out £45million to secure the gloveman’s services this summer, and he has already made that look like a bargain.

Trafford made six saves to help England to a surprise win in Madrid, including a stunning double stop at the death to help his team get over the line.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Leeds boss Daniel Farke will be delighted with the confidence boost that Trafford has just received. (Related: Hawaiian Interisland Awards — Hop Between Islands...)

The youngster is in a battle with Everton’s Jordan Pickford to be England’s number one, and may have now emerged on top, having been handed the start in the decisive game.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Leeds are relaxed over Trafford’s future right now.

“It’ll be difficult to double their money to make it £90m. I don't see him going for that. But I certainly see a good profit coming in for Trafford should he be sold,” he mentioned.

Recent findings suggest that “Hopefully he won't be sold from Leeds’ point of view. Look, he did have a good performance. It's established great competition between him and the incumbent number one, Mr Pickford.

“Normally Pickford responds to this sort of thing by producing worldies for the next few weeks and reclaiming his position. Look, I think that Trafford is definitely a talent.

“He seems to have all the confidence. This will have done him the world of good. Farke will be happy with him coming back, full of confidence. There could be a handsome profit there in future.”.

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider earlier this week that winger Dan James is likely to push to leave Leeds in January if his gametime doesn’t improve.

The 29-year-old has played just 117 minutes of Premier League football so far this season, with Farke rarely trusting him on the pitch.

Brown outlined that the Welshman is “not happy” with his role at Elland Road, and isn’t prepared to sit on the bench for the remainder of the season.

The ex-recruitment man outlined that James’ athleticism still makes him a useful option from the bench, even if there are doubts over his ability at Premier League level.
