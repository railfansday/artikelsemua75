Sources indicate that planning is underway for the 2027 transfer windows at clubs around the world. The first stop is January, of course, but with it always being a difficult window, focus on the summer usually takes up the bulk of the work.

Recent findings suggest that for Liverpool, that means sorting out some contract situations. The substantial two are unquestionably Virgil van Dijk and Alisson Becker, though it's completely unclear what they strategy to do.

According to recent reports, the pair have been at Anfield so long that we imagine proper talks will be delayed until the end of the season. There's no enormous rush, after all, even if them being able to talk to non-English clubs from January 1st is a bit stressful.

But they're not the only decisions that need to be made. At least one other contract choice appears close to being made - and with that comes a substantial follow-on effect.
