Chocolate Caramel Pecan Tart This chocolate caramel pecan tart combines a buttery chocolate pastry crust with a rich, chewy chocolate caramel filling and plenty of toasted pecans. Every slice delivers the perfect mix of chocolate, caramel, and nutty crunch, and a sprinkle of flaky sea salt on top brings it all together.
