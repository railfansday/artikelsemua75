Skanska announced on November 26, 2026 that it had received an additional $275 million award from an existing client to build a new 48 MW data center in Georgia, USA.

Industry observers note that the award is worth about SEK 2.8 billion and will be included in Skanska’s US order bookings for the fourth quarter of 2026. That classification matters: the announcement adds contracted work to the order book, rather than reporting the full amount as revenue already earned. Sources indicate that the main facility will total 244,730 square feet, approximately 22,700 square meters. Skanska’s scope covers construction of the shell and interior fit-out for technical spaces, support areas and office functions. (Related: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using Gradio...)

It has been revealed that an 860-square-foot, approximately 80-square-meter, ancillary water-treatment building is also included. Although small beside the main facility, it places supporting utility infrastructure within the award. Construction began in November 2026, and Skanska expects completion in October 2028, meaning work was already underway when the additional award was declared.

Skanska’s September 22, 2026 announcement described an $84 million contract with an existing client, assigned to US order bookings for the third quarter. It listed the same 48 MW capacity and 244,730-square-foot footprint as the latest project, while specifying five colocation data halls and administrative space.

The September project was scheduled to begin in November 2026 and finish in the third quarter of 2028; the latest release gives October 2028 as the expected completion month. September’s delivery scope included civil, structural, utilities, electrical, mechanical, security, interior amenities and fit-out work, plus commissioning of major equipment through completion.

An April 9, 2026 release disclosed a $75 million contract, booked in the first quarter, for a 22,700-square-meter, 244,090-square-foot facility. Its scope included associated site work, underground utilities, administrative space and five data halls. Construction began in March 2026, with completion scheduled for the first quarter of 2028.

A June 23, 2026 announcement followed with a $255 million supplemental contract, booked in the second quarter. That project covered approximately 245,000 square feet, associated site work and underground utilities, administrative space and five data halls, with the same stated March 2026 to first-quarter 2028 construction schedule.

According to recent reports, these releases establish a sequence of Georgia data-center awards during 2026, but not one aggregate project value. Skanska identifies the customers only as existing clients and does not explicitly connect every announcement to the same facility. The values should therefore be read as separate awards, not as a documented cumulative cost for a single project.

Sources indicate that the latest award’s significance is split between two timelines: a fourth-quarter 2026 booking for Skanska’s US business and a construction program extending to October 2028. The first measures new work secured; the second will track how that booked work becomes a completed 48 MW facility with its supporting water-treatment infrastructure.
