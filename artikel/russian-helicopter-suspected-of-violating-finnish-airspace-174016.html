A Russian helicopter is suspected of violating Finnish airspace, the Nordic country's Ministry of Defense said Saturday.

The incident happened a few hours after a drone attack left a Russian cargo ship ablaze in the Gulf of Finland, the easternmost part of the Baltic Sea.

Recent findings suggest that according to Finland's Defense Ministry, the incursion happened at 12:34 p.m. local time (10:34 UTC/GMT) off the town of Loviisa, on the southern coast. (Related: Latest Update: British Airways Avios — Dublin Short-Hau...)

"This alleged airspace violation may be linked to rescue or surveillance operations carried out by Finland following damage sustained by a cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland," public broadcaster YLE cited Defense Minister Antti Hakkanen as saying.

The ministry added the helicopter could have penetrated up to 4.1 kilometers (2.5 miles) into Finnish territory. (Related: Turkish Miles & Smiles — Hawaii for 7,500 Miles...)

Earlier Saturday, Finland — one of NATO's newest members — remarked a Russian cargo ship heading for Russia's Baltic exclave of Kaliningrad caught fire after being struck by drones in the Gulf of Finland.

The Finnish military stated the Sparta II was damaged ⁠west ⁠of Russia's Gogland island, about 40 kilometers (25 miles) from Finland's coast, and later drifted into Finnish territorial waters.

Finnish ‌Border ​Guard Commander Ilja Iljin later told YLE that the vessel, which is known to have links to the Russian military, was struck by Ukrainian  drones while in Russian waters.

"At the moment, ​there is no confirmed information ⁠about ​what cargo ​is on board, ​but it ‌cannot be ruled out ​that ⁠there is military materiel on ⁠board," ​Iljin mentioned.

The ship is on a US sanctions list, according to a search of the Open Sanctions website.

The Border Guard later remarked the crew of the Sparta II was evacuating into a life raft and rescue boat.

"The [Ukraine] war is spilling over into our neighbouring areas," wrote Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo on X.

Ukraine has the right to fight against the aggressor," he added.

The Finnish government has put temporary curbs on aviation and maritime traffic in the eastern Gulf of Finland.

The Defense Ministry said Finland had informed its allies about the incident.
