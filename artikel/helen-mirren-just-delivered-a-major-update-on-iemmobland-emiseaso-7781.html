📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

MobLand season 2 hasn't even finished airing, but Paramount seems to be getting well ahead of the game, Slow Horses-style. Things are much further along the line than we may have imagined, and perhaps the third season is closer than we thought.

Recent findings suggest that previously, Pierce Brosnan had stated that filming would likely be starting to take place this month, November, but we'd had no official production update to confirm it. Until now, that is.

On the red carpet at the premiere for her new film A Talent for Murder last week, Helen Mirren told Radio Times: "We’re starting work on a third season. We start next week," though she refused to divulge any further details.

At the moment, it might be a little early to speculate what will happen in season 3, since, well, season 2 isn't even over, but we have got some hints about what we could expect to see.

Last month, at Paramount UK's "Upfront" event in London, actor Peter Sarsgaard, Kenneth Branagh and MobLand's executive producer David Glasser held an in-conversation event mostly about their new show, Lord. However, Glasser couldn't help but share some MobLand news. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

It has been revealed that he revealed that season 3 will see "someone challenge Tom Hardy," but he didn't say anything more, and refused to speak about MobLand beyond that. Hardy's Harry Da Souza hasn't exactly had an easy run of it so far, especially thanks to the addition of Johnny Flynn's delightfully unpredictable Frankie Cooper this season. Hopefully, we won't have to wait too long to see what happens next.
