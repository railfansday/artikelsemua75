Netflix has kicked off production on the second season of “Elite Force,” its hit French action thriller starring Tomer Sisley (“Largo Winch”).

In a recent development, the second season will see David and Max, after being dismissed from the GIGN – France’s elite counterterrorism and hostage rescue unit — find themselves at the center of an incident that raises suspicions within their unit and threatens its very existence. It has been revealed that “We’re starting filming on Season 2 of ‘Elite Force’ today. Audiences followed us throughout the first season, and it’s with great excitement that we’re embarking on a fresh storyline with everything dialed up: intrigue, emotion, action… Everything is in place to deliver!” noted Leclercq.

Netflix has a track record with French action thrillers, notably “Under Paris,” directed by Xavier Gens, and “AKA,” starring Alban Lenoir and Eric Cantona.

According to recent reports, “Elite Force” marks a return to familiar territory for Leclercq, who previously chronicled one of the GIGN’s most high-profile operations in his 2011 film “The Assault,” based on the 1994 hijacking of an Air France plane. He has since become a regular collaborator of Netflix, directing “The Wages of Fear,” a remake of Henri-Georges Clouzot’s 1953 classic, and co-creating “Ganglands,” a crime series inspired by his 2015 heist thriller “Braqueurs.” The latter ran for two seasons on the streamer.
