Fox News' Madeline Rivera provides details on a reported cyberattack after a string of outages with the FBI at the center of this attack. Rivera indicates this is the second breach in recent months.

Hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC), three sources familiar with the matter told Fox News, marking the second cyberattack against the bureau in months.

The FBI is investigating the hack to determine the unauthorized entry point, but, for now, it is unclear what information was compromised, sources remarked.

The NCIC database is a critical information-sharing tool for federal, state and local authorities, helping them track fugitives, monitor registered sex offenders, check whether someone has failed a gun background check, among other information.

In an interview with Fox News' Kayleigh McEnany Saturday morning, President Donald Trump said he had "just heard" about the hack.
