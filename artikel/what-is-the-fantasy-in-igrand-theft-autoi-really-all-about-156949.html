In a recent development, I will not be playing Grand Theft Auto VI. The limited flexibility left in my wrists after decades of typing and mousing and gaming must be reserved for a game I started three years ago and haven’t finished. I’m stuck in a shed in the rain, low on ammo, and there is a major Nazi robot outside. I have to find the time and desire to return to this dangerous situation and beat the robot before the red bar over my head that reads CARPAL TUNNEL goes fully red. But the main reason I won’t play GTA: I don’t wish to inhabit the life of an uninhibited sociopath.
