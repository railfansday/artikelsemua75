Bruno Mars is mourning the death of his father, Peter Hernandez. He was 74.

“Last week I got to hug my father one last time,” Mars, 41, wrote via Instagram on Thursday, November 19, alongside a photo of the musician embracing Hernandez in a hospital bed. “If you’ve ever seen me talk about him I’ve always stated that he’s the man that taught me everything I know, especially when it comes to music.”.

According to recent reports, mars continued, “He raised my four beautiful sisters, my brother and me. In my eyes he was the greatest dad in the world, and I will miss him everyday until we meet again. I love you, Pops. Kiss mom up there for me.”.

In a recent development, “My dad was just the king of finding these little spots for us to stay that we should never have been staying at,” Mars recalled on a 2016 episode of 60 Minutes. “We’d all sleep in one bed.

We had each other and it never felt like it was the end of the world.”.

Mars noted that his childhood leant itself to the determination and tenacity it took to pursue a music career in Hollywood.

Mars’ passion for music was apparent to Peter, who also was a performer.

Industry observers note that “[Bruno] was a ham,” Peter mused to Midweek in a 2014 profile. “As soon as he could walk and express himself, he, like, had to be in the middle of everything and wanted to be a part of whoever he’d seen on TV.”.

Even with all the success Mars has achieved, Peter still saw the “Uptown Funk” crooner as his “baby boy.”.
