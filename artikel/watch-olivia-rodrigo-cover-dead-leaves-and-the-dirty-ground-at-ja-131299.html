Halfway through her live-to-acetate set at Jack White’s Third Man Records in Nashville on Nov. 22, Olivia Rodrigo had a confession to make.

“Anybody that knows me at all knows that I’m kind of the biggest Jack White fan ever,” Rodrigo told the crowd at the intimate venue, gathered to watch her perform a tight set of songs at the label’s venue, the Blue Room, recorded for Third Man Records. “I was contemplating whether or not I’d say this, but given that we’re settling in, I thought I would come clean and admit that when I was 15, I did have a Jack White fan account called Third Fan Records. It’s kind of embarrassing in concept, but all this is to say that I think 15-year-old Olivia would be pretty stoked if you told her what she was doing tonight.”. In a plaid skirt and shirt with “Latest York Confuses Me” printed across the front, Rodrigo seemed completely at home at the tiny 250-capacity venue that’s just a touch smaller than the 20,000 or so seats she fills at arenas, the consummate rock star at both.
