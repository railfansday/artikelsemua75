A wedding guest at Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s Palm Springs nuptials found herself at the center of social media chatter after opting for a pop of color despite the event’s strict all-black dress code.

Recent findings suggest that belgica Vargas wore a bright yellow number while joined by her husband, music producer Shawn Everett, for the romantic ceremony held on Saturday, November 21. She completed her look with a pair of black sunglasses, although that didn’t stop photos showing Belgica in her vibrant ensemble from going viral and sparking conversation online, leading her sister, Grecia, to speak out and set the record straight.
