Liverpool's midfield has been a big problem for them for quite a while now.

For the moment, Trey Nyoni and Alexis Mac Allister seem to be Andoni Iraola's preferred options for the middle of the park despite others such as Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch also being available. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

This is rather concerning as, it means the two players that Liverpool handed long-term contracts to may not have much of a future left at Anfield. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

Obviously, when fit, Szoboszlai has been called upon but, if he can't keep his fitness levels up, there will be no reason for Iraola to keep him around, especially with the amount of money he's earning now.

All of these ideas are the main reasons why Liverpool's fanbase expected them to sign at least one new midfielder in the summer.

But now, it seems like the club are going to do that in January instead.

Liverpool have made a €70m bid for Lamine Camara.

Lamine Camara was one of the most-linked central midfielders to Liverpool last season.

The Senegal international was on top of the Reds' shortlist with others such as Alex Scott and Adam Wharton closely behind him.

Of the three, Camara was the cheapest but, thanks to Scott's connections with Iraola from his time at Bournemouth, the Englishman was the most likely player to join.

In the end, none of them were welcomed through the Anfield doors with the club choosing to focus on their attack and defence instead.

The signings of Bradley Barcola, Victor Munoz, Ronald Araujo and the arrival of Jeremy Jacquet have all been welcomed with open arms and now, it's time for the Reds' midfield to be given the same treatment.

That is, if this latest revision from Ekrem Konur is to be believed.

"aving tracked AS Monaco's 22-year-old Senegalese midfielder Lamine Camara for months, Liverpool have tabled a massive €70 million bid to land their top target," he wrote.

"Following the deadline-day collapse of his proposed summer move to Arsenal, Manchester United and Chelsea, the Reds have made strong progress in talks with the player's representatives and are close to reaching an agreement on personal terms.".

Lamine Camara is the perfect midfielder for Liverpool.

Even before Liverpool chose against making a move for another midfielder, it was made more than clear that they needed a upcoming central midfielder.

Obviously, Iraola has been fortunate enough to have Nyoni step-up into a regular first-team role but, without him, the Reds would have been screwed for the first half of the season.

The 19-year-old has come on leaps and bounds since the appointment of Iraola and, right now, it's clear that he's going to be a difference maker.

At the moment, he's in talks over a new Liverpool deal and, even though he can help cover central midfield, Liverpool are still going to need a new midfielder or two.

In a recent development, that's where Camara comes in. The Senegal international is a natural number six and, under Filipe Luis' guidance, he's never stopped running and has promptly become one of their best players.

As made clear by Konur's report, a deal wouldn't come cheap but, if Camara even has half as much of an impact as Mamadou Sangare has since joining Brentford, Liverpool will be onto a winner.

And, since he's only 22 years old and has been compared to N'Golo Kante, it's clear that he's ready-made for the role.
