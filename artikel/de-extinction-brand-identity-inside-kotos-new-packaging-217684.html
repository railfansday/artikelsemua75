Koto's De-extinction brand identity turns compostable packaging into a protest, with loud dinosaurs, acid green and hot pink.

It has been revealed that the dinosaurs are back as warnings. Koto draws them in thick black outlines and fills them with acid green and hot pink, then sets them loose across kraft cups, stickered boxes and poster walls. The palette is deliberately loud: acid green, hot magenta, black and off-white, with the brown of the compostable board keeping the whole system grounded. The posters shout in stacked capitals, Sweet packaging without the sour aftertaste and Re-package your future, while the product photography drops the cups into a lush jungle world of flowers, rock and mist. Koto calls the idea Once Upon A Time, and even created the De-extinction name, a reminder that extinction is real unless we act. (Related: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to transformer...)

Industry observers note that the type does the shouting. A heavy grotesque runs at poster scale in tight stacked lines, with single words reversed out of black bars: FEED and NOURISH picked out in white on black, PLANET highlighted in pink. The de-extinction wordmark stays small and lowercase beside a round dino mark, letting the headlines carry the volume. The same bluntness holds where the system has to be practical: a catalog page lists cold cup sizes in a plain table under a giant COLD CUPS header, with a small dino doodle at the foot. (Related: Introducing Optimum: The Optimization Toolkit for Trans...)

Recent findings suggest that the packaging is the proof. Kraft cups carry the small dino mark, boxes arrive covered in dino stickers, and a paper bag sits in the jungle photography with the same mark. Nothing is coated in gloss: the board stays brown, the print stays flat, and the dinosaurs do the talking. Koto took its cue from protest culture and global movements, building the brand to provoke action rather than nod at it, and the identity runs the same way across packaging, posters and communications. The approach landed: the identity helped the young manufacturer stand out with key customers, including meetings with Sweetgreen.
