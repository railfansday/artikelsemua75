Wusongkou Forum Unveils River-Sea Cruise Initiative and Consumption Index

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The 2026 Wusongkou Forum took place on Nov. 8 at the Shanghai International Cruise Tourism Resort in Baoshan. It brought together regulators, port authorities, cruise operators and industry experts to discuss what organizers called “three-cruise synergy,” which is the coordinated development of ocean cruise ships, river cruise vessels and yachts.

According to recent reports, hosted by Shanghai Wusongkou Culture & Tourism Investment (Group) Co., the event included the launch of a “Three-Cruise Synergy · A New Voyage from River to Sea” program. According to recent reports, the first is a “Cruise Plus” brand renewal blueprint. It targets four consumer segments: seniors, weddings and romance travel, sporting events and younger travelers. The goal is to turn passenger volume into more onboard and shoreside spending.

The second is a river-sea linkage project that uses Wusongkou’s location at the mouth of the Yangtze to connect inland river cities with ocean itineraries. Its flagship product is the Yangtze No. 3, which has begun sailing between Chongqing and Shanghai from the Wusongkou International Cruise Terminal. It is the first river-sea cruise service to cover the full length of the Yangtze. (Related: Breaking: Japan launches a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t con...)

Industry observers note that third, China Huaxia International Cruise Co. released October 2026 data for its China Cruise Consumption Vitality Index. The manufacturer described it as the country’s first regular data tool for the cruise market.

The fourth initiative links the cruise terminal with Shanghai Baoshan railway station, which is due to open next year as the meeting point of the Shanghai–Chongqing–Chengdu high-speed line and the Shanghai–Nantong railway. Intends include combined rail and cruise ticketing, a direct shuttle, baggage transfer from car door to cabin door and shared VIP benefits.

On forum day, the Adora Magic City and the Adora Flora City, China’s first two domestically built large cruise ships, were berthed together at Wusongkou for the first time.
