Upcoming chemical route delivers first synthesis of plant compound with cough-suppressing potential

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Neotuberostemonine, a complex plant-derived alkaloid with promising antitussive activity, has remained a challenging target for chemical synthesis for nearly 30 years. Researchers have now achieved its first total synthesis, alongside the synthesis of neostenine and the putative structure of tuberostemonine L. The findings also reveal how the nearly planar geometry of nitrogen atoms contributes to the oxidative instability of these alkaloids, offering new insights and synthetic strategies for natural-product chemistry and future drug discovery.
