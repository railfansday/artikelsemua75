Just over a week ago, we released Granite Switch 4.1, showing that our modular Switch architecture lets you configure a language model with specialized, cache-efficient adapters — adding newly introduced expertise on a single endpoint.

Granite Switch 4.1.1 is a patch release. The version number means what it means in software: fully backwards compatible with 4.1, no migration, incremental improvements under the hood.

While we are excited about these upcoming features, what is notable is the speed and efficiency with which our teams could release this patch. Apart from training the new ASR model and Decision adapter, nothing was recomputed: the base model is untouched. You compose the latest adapters you want and deploy.
