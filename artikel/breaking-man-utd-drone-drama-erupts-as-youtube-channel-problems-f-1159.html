Recent findings suggest that mauricio Pochettino's first training session at Manchester United was interrupted by an uninvited visitor on Thursday – a drone circling above, recording the entire thing. The Argentine manager stepped onto the training pitch with his latest squad for the first time on Thursday, only a day after arriving at Manchester Airport aboard a private plane. A remarkably clear 12-minute recording of Pochettino's session has emerged on YouTube, uploaded by an account titled 'CC Overview', thought to be operated by an individual named Colin.

The footage highlighted Pochettino instructing his new players, accompanied by running narration from the drone operator.

The footage appears days ahead of Manchester United's encounter with fierce rivals Liverpool in a highly anticipated fixture at Anfield on Sunday, marking Pochettino's fourth Premier League club following spells at Southampton, Tottenham, and Chelsea.

In the footage, the drone pilot justified the "legality" of the recording, contending that as "it is unrestricted airspace, anyone can fly over it.".

The channel stated: "The drone remained in line of sight at all times, it didn't fly over anyone or even near anyone so was a perfectly legal flight going by the CAA rules that regulates drone usage.

Industry observers note that "The drone is correctly labelled, and I hold all the relevant licences, public liability and insurance required. As for leaking team news/tactics, it was half an hour into the managers first training session, there are many media there so certainly won't be the only footage of training released.". In a recent development, "It was around 30 minutes into the session so presumed it would not reveal any tactics or hints at team selection and just wanted the give United fans that first look. I could have stayed longer, I wasn't on United's property and at no point flew over it but had captured all I had intended to so wrapped up filming.".

However, the channel removed the footage this morning and published an apology. The statement read: "I would like to speak about yesterday's incident.

"A total error of judgement on my part led to me filming some of the Manchester United training session at Carrington, whilst the flight was legal in terms of drone laws I fully accept it was unethical on my part.

"My aim was to picture Mauricio Pochettino in his United training kit for the supporters to see for the first time. I had wrongly presumed that with it being close to the start of the session that no meaningful training sessions would have started so would have been some harmless warming up shown.

"I also wrongly assumed that the training session would have been open to various media outlets. I would like to apologise to Manchester United, Mauricio Pochettino and the players for my actions and sincerely hope those actions do not affect the outcome of any match.

"I would also like to state that Liverpool Football Club or any other football club were not involved in my decision to film at Carrington yesterday.

In a recent development, "The video has been removed from my channel. I have spent the past four years building up my YouTube channel and were well regarded in the fields I film and would now like to get back to building up my damaged reputation and making well received responsible videos. Thank you.".

Manchester United have declined to comment. The BBC reports that Civil Aviation Authority guidelines contain the following caution: "If you use these devices where people can expect privacy, such as inside their home or garden, you're likely to be breaking data protection laws.".
