Recent findings suggest that no matter your makeup aesthetic, the holiday season is the time to embrace shimmery, lustrous looks. For minimalists, a luminous complexion and a glassy lip offer a subtle way to glow, while maximalists can fully commit to the sparkle with glitter-flecked metallic eyeshadow and liner.

The best part? You don’t have to drain your gift budget to make your dream makeup look a reality. From glitter mascara to body mists that gleam, SHEGLAM has everything you need to create all things merry and bright without maxing out your credit card. Here are our top four holiday makeup must-haves from the brand, plus more under-$10 finds to round out your glow.

According to recent reports, available in festive shades like gold, rose gold, and silver, this multi-use, cooling jelly stick delivers a high-shine, glassy finish on the eyes or as a highlighter on the cheekbones and nose. Sweep it onto skin and diffuse with your fingers for a touch of sparkle or layer it for a mirror-like chrome effect. For a more pared-back look, try the Lunar Frosty Eyeshadow Stick, which features a soft shimmer and matte base.

This silver mascara imparts light-catching sparkles onto lashes while lengthening and separating them for a doe-eyed effect. Wear it on its own for a wintry touch or layer it over the Lashlighter Mega Boost Mascara in Black for high-impact shine. If you’re feeling extra bold, swipe some onto your brows for a holiday look that feels uniquely you. True shimmer enthusiasts can also spray the Go Go Glow Highlighting Body Mist onto skin and hair for an allover, prism-like shine.

According to recent reports, ice out your lips with this frosty gloss that comes in shades like gold, rose gold, and bright bubblegum pink for a high-impact glassy finish. The formula is transfer-proof, so you can nibble and sip your way through the holiday party without constantly reapplying. For even more frost, sweep the Enigma Sparkly Eyeshadow in Such a Prize onto your lids and glide the Boldline Longwear Multi-Function Gel Liner in Pearl on your waterline for a complete look that sparkles like freshly fallen snow.

Industry observers note that if your holiday-party ‘fit is doing all the sparking, luminous skin might be all you need. This smooth and buttery highlight stick glides onto skin to deliver a dewy shine that feels totally weightless. Apply it on the high points of the face, such as the cheekbones, Cupid’s bow, bridge of the nose, and browbone. Diffuse with your fingers for an ethereal glow.
