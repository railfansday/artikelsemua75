We reckon this is the best one so far though - a secret portal that takes you into a nightclub from the original 2002 version of Vice City for the PS2 (which technically means it takes you back to its 1986-inspired setting).

The process of getting here is long-winded, and detailed in full in the video from Dan Allen Gaming up above. In a nutshell, you have to go to the Badger R&D Radio Tower in Key Lento and use the power switch and press a button to make the Malibu logo appear on the TV. From there, visit 12 LPL substations around the map without fast-travelling, and press the correct buttons in the specific order (from the video) when you reach them. You'll then get a text, at which point you head to Reactor 2 at the LPL Turtle Head Power Station between 7pm and 6am to find the portal. It seems someone called Memelo was the first to discover this trick:.

Sources indicate that another really cool part of this Easter Egg is that when you combine all the substations together, the wording on the outside of them adds up to "Life is Passing Me By", which references a classic cheat code from the original Vice City. You actually have to use the buttons to enter this cheat code and make the portal appear.

As mentioned, you get two free items for your trouble as well, which are Tommy Vercetti's classic shirt and bike from the 2002 game. These appear as soon as you're thrown out of the portal and back into the so-called real world!

According to recent reports, finally, we'll also highlight that although pretty much all the footage we've seen of this Easter Egg has been exclusive to the Jason character, it does appear to work with Lucia as well. That said, we've also come across many players who claim the portal isn't showing up for them, so we're not sure what's causing it to fail in those situations.
