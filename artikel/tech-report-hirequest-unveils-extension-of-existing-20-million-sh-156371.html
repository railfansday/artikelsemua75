Industry observers note that GOOSE CREEK, S.C., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- HireQuest, Inc. (NASDAQ: HQI) ("HireQuest" or the "Firm"), a global franchisor of on-demand staffing and executive search services, today announced that its Board of Directors has approved an extension of its existing share repurchase program (the "Repurchase Program") that authorizes the Company to repurchase up to an additional $17.2 million of its outstanding shares of common stock through November 16, 2027 (the "Amendment"). HireQuest entered into a corresponding amendment to its existing 10b5-1 plan in accordance with the Amendment.

According to recent reports, "Repurchasing our shares is an excellent use of our excess capital today," remarked Rick Hermanns, Chief Executive Officer of HireQuest. "At current levels, buying back stock is a compelling investment in our own business. We have ample liquidity, and the Board is confident we can repurchase shares while continuing to fund organic growth and pursuing acquisitions as they arise.".

Under the program, HireQuest may repurchase shares from time to time through, among other methods, open market purchases, privately negotiated transactions, block purchases, and/or pursuant to Rule 10b5-1 trading intends, subject to applicable securities laws and other legal requirements and relevant factors. The number of shares purchased and the timing of any purchases will depend upon a number of factors, including the price and availability of the Company's common stock and general market conditions. The Repurchase Program may be modified, suspended or terminated at any time, without prior notice.
