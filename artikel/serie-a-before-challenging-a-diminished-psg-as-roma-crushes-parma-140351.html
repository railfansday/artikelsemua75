AS Roma perfectly negotiated their trip to Parma by winning largely (1-5), a few days before their European meeting against PSG in the Champions League at the Parc des Princes, during the 12th day of Serie A. Gian Piero Gasperini’s men were however shaken up at the start of the match, with several dangerous situations conceded to the locals. But Mile Svilar kept his team afloat thanks to several decisive interventions, before Donyell Malen broke the deadlock in the 39th minute. The Dutch striker perfectly exploited a serve from Lulli to score his ninth goal of the season in Serie A.

Recent findings suggest that after Elphege’s equalizer after returning from the locker room (55th), the Giallorossi quickly regained control of the match. Malen scored twice in the 68th minute, before Rodrigo Mora widened the gap two minutes later on a new action initiated by Wesley. The Dutchman finally completed his performance in the 77th minute, scoring a hat-trick then a quadruplet later and taking his league tally to double figures. A convincing victory for Roma, which arrives rolled out before meeting PSG on Wednesday evening in the Champions League who will be deprived of Désiré Doué in addition to Khvicha Kvaratskhelia.
