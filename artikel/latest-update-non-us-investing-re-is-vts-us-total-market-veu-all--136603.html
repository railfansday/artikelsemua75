Industry observers note that from prior posts, alex123711 is probably an Australian investor.VTS & VEU are both Vanguard ETFs listed on Australia's ASX. The former is US stocks, & the latter is World ex US stocks.They're both US-domiciled funds. Apparently there are potential disadvantages for Australian investors who own these US-domiciled funds - in terms of how US estate taxes (death taxes) treat non-Americans -- see Fund domicile and avoidable US taxes (passiveinvestingaustralia.com).Have a read through & see if those downsides are significant for you. There's not significant for my situation (an Australian investor) & would not stop me owning some VEU for low fee ex-US international exposure.Statistics: Posted by pseudoiterative — Sun Nov 22, 2026 10:58 pm.
