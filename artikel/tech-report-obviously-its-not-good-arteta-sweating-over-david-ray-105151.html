Industry observers note that “Obviously it’s not good David had to come off but at the moment I don’t know [the prognosis],” stated Arteta, explaining that a keeper who kept seven clean sheets in the season’s opening 10 games will have the joint scanned on Sunday. “He will have an MRI tomorrow. We will have more information tomorrow. But we know how important David is to us.”.

Arsenal were 1-0 up at half-time at St James’ Park thanks to a goal from Eberechi Eze during an opening 45 minutes dominated by the visitors. Newcastle, though, improved significantly after the break and took the lead thanks to goals from Harvey Barnes and Malick Thiaw before Noni Madueke equalised.

In a recent development, “My feeling is we have lost two points,” said Arteta. “We were exceptional in the first half but we needed to score three or four goals and we didn’t. We knew there’d be a response but the way we gave Newcastle’s first goal away was completely unacceptable. We came back and scored a second goal but we lacked the moment of magic to kill the game. I don’t think we switched off in the second half; sometimes you have to praise the opposition.”.

Matthias Jaissle was left smiling after that second-half performance rescued a game watched by Newcastle’s chair, Yasir al-Rumayyan. He had flown in from Riyadh to witness his first match of the season and is believed to have complimented his head coach afterwards.

“What you saw in the second half was Newcastle’s DNA par excellence and that’s something we can build on,” mentioned Jaissle. “But we’ve got to be humble and realistic. The first half was not good and the second half was excellent. I’ve got mixed feelings because a little more was possible but a point was probably fair.
