Inter start Djed Spence, Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram for the trip to troubled Atalanta, with Charles De Ketelaere and Nikola Krstovic.

It kicks off at the New Balance Arena in Bergamo at 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET).

The pressure is on the reigning Scudetto and Coppa Italia holders, who need a victory to keep up after Lazio and Roma wins already on Saturday.

Lautaro Martinez is back in the squad, but not risked in the starting XI after more than a month out with a thigh concern, so Pio Esposito hopes to build on his excellent Italy form in partnership with Thuram. (Related: We Got Claude to Build CUDA Kernels and teach open mode...)

Ange-Yoan Bonny and Aleksandar Stankovic are out of action, with Piotr Zielinski not at 100 per cent after a knock to the foot playing for Poland.

Curtis Jones has not featured since the former Liverpool midfielder’s injury against Roma on September 19, and is only ready here for the bench along with John Stones, as Henrikh Mkhitaryan steps in.

Atalanta had a disastrous run of results with two points from four rounds, but went into the break for international duty with a positive 1-0 victory away to Monza.

The injury crisis is beginning to finally calm down a little bit, leaving only Isak Hien and Odilon Kossounou on the treatment table, although Gianluca Gaetano and Thomas Kristensen are not ready to start yet.

There was a new problem in the warm-up, as Marco Carnesecchi seemed to sprain his knee and walked off shaking his head, replaced by Marco Sportiello.

Franck Kessie is a boost in midfield with Ederson, while Eljif Elmas and Nicola Zalewski are preferred to Jonathan Rowe and Giacomo Raspadori.

De Ketelaere and Krstovic impressed on international duty, so will be eager to keep that form going here.

Atalanta have a famously awful record against Inter, as the 1-1 draw in March ended a run of nine consecutive defeats in all competition.

La Dea haven’t beaten the Nerazzurri since a 4-1 result here in November 2018, scraping just six draws and 11 losses.

Atalanta: Sportiello; Bellanova, Scalvini, Kolasinac, Zappacosta; Kessie, Ederson; Elmas, De Ketelaere, Zalewski; Krstovic.

Inter: J Martinez; Pavard, Bastoni, Carlos Augusto; Spence, Barella, Calhanoglu, Mkhitaryan, Dimarco; M Thuram, P Esposito.
