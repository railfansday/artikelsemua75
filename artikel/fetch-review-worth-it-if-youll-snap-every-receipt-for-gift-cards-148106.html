Recent findings suggest that use Fetch if you’ll snap every receipt, since any store’s receipt earns points toward gift cards, and buying the brands its offers feature earns more. Expect small rewards, because each receipt is guaranteed only 25 points, about 2.5 cents, and Fetch states even its top users average $275 a year.

Recent findings suggest that fetch is a free app that pays points for photos of paper receipts and for eReceipts, the digital receipts it pulls from email and store accounts you connect. It suits shoppers who keep every receipt and don’t mind trading their purchase data for gift cards.

Industry observers note that at Fetch’s own value of about $1 per 1,000 points, 30 receipts a month at the 25-point minimum earn about 75 cents, so it pays best for shoppers who also buy the brands its offers feature. Shoppers who want cash do better with Ibotta, which pays cash back on offers they add to its list and lets them cash out to PayPal at $20.
