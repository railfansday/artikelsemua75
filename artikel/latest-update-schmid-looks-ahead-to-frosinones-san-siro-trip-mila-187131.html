Frosinone forward Romano Schmid believes that his side will have to be more ‘proactive’ in order to get a positive result against AC Milan.

Having been promoted from Serie B last season, Frosinone have been the surprise package so far in Serie A barring perhaps the league-leaders Lazio, as they sit sixth in the table having won six of their opening 11 games so far.

They were heavily tipped to be in a relegation battle by pundits and the bookmakers, and yet they have scored 24 goals and allowed just 15, making them not just an effective team but also an entertaining one too.

Recent findings suggest that the summer arrival Schmid – who signed from Werder Bremen – has been a part of that. He spoke at a press conference two days before Frosinone’s trip north to face Milan at San Siro. The questions and his responses were relayed by TuttoFrosinone. After Cagliari, what will the match against Milan be like?

Sources indicate that “The match in Milan will be completely different from the one in Cagliari. Milan are obviously the favourites, their game is different from Cagliari’s, and we’ll need a more proactive Frosinone.”. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

How are you finding Serie A as a league?

Industry observers note that “It’s different from the Bundesliga, I like it. You have to always be on top of things otherwise you lose the duels, especially in midfield.”.

Did the fact that they play with a 4-2-3-1 like with Austria influence your choice to come to Frosinone?

“It’s not the main reason. With the national team, we don’t play man-to-man like here against Frosinone. It’s a similar game because we definitely play deep, we attack high.”.

Why did you miss Austria’s last match?

“My son had a problem, it wasn’t serious but he wasn’t feeling very well and since we had already qualified I preferred to stay with the family”.

You are one of the most crucial signings in Frosinone’s history, and it’s no coincidence that you started playing immediately under Alvini. How do you feel?

Recent findings suggest that “My past performances in the Bundesliga were crucial; that’s one of the reasons Froisnone chose me, given my previous performances. They’re similar to what I played under coach Alvini. I’m enjoying myself and I’m happy.”.

Where do you prefer to play, in the attacking midfield role or in the pivot?

Recent findings suggest that “My favourite position is number 10, an attacking midfielder behind the centre-forward. I feel comfortable playing there, I’ve also tried playing further back, but my favourite position is still number 10.”.
