According to recent reports, grand Theft Auto 6 has a huge tapestry of characters, and the array of memorable inclusions goes well beyond the cast that was showcased prior to release. While fans who followed the marketing will already be very familiar with characters like Brian and Cal before encountering them in-game, others, like Vicki, might be more surprising.

According to recent reports, vicki shows up at the motel in Chapter Two, where Lucia starts to look around for some painkillers to help out Jason after his injuries. The character has a distinctive in-game appearance, rocking bangs, space buns, and plenty of tattoos, but it might be her voice that rings a bell. Vicki is portrayed by the actress Taryn Manning, and there's a reasonable chance you've seen her in some live-action work in the past. Taryn Manning has several big credits to her name, but the most notable might be Orange is the Newly introduced Black, as she had a major role in the hit streaming series. Manning played Tiffany "Pennsatucky" Doggett across 87 episodes of the show, ultimately appearing in all but four episodes. While her character there could easily fit into the world of GTA 6, it's very different than Vicki, with Tiffany initially taking on an antagonistic and highly judgmental role. Manning also appeared in the FX show Sons of Anarchy, portraying Rita "Cherry" Zambell across seven episodes, and the CBS reboot of the procedural drama Hawaii Five-0, playing Mary Ann McGarrett in nine episodes.

In a recent development, television isn't Manning's only claim to fame. She's appeared in plenty of films over the years, with several mid-2000s works stand out as major career highlights. Manning had a central role in Hustle & Flow as Nola and revealed up in 8 Mile as Janeane. Fans of Britney Spears might also recognize Manning from Crossroads, a 2002 road trip movie where her character, Mimi, is at the center of a surprisingly heavy plotline.

GTA 6 is an impressive addition to that repertoire, with the game shattering day-one sales records and receiving glowing critical acclaim (despite a more contentious audience reception). While plenty of fans may not have gotten to Vicki quite yet, as Chapter One alone takes quite a few hours to complete, she steals the show in the early stages of Chapter Two. Along with the potential to casually encounter her after the initial meeting in "Just Moved In" and excursion in "Girls Trip," she also highlights back up for another mission later in the chapter.

White Oleander, Cold Mountain, and A Lot like Love are several more notable films that Manning has starred in over the years.

At the time of this article's publication, Manning hasn't yet spoken publicly about her role in Grand Theft Auto 6, but she'll doubtless have plenty of opportunities to do so in the future. Thanks to the massive cultural footprint of the game, millions of players will be meeting Vicki for years to come, making it the kind of role that will be remembered for a lifetime.
