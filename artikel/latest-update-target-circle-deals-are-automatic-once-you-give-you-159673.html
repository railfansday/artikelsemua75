Before each trip, open the app to activate your bonuses and add manufacturer coupons, which never apply on their own.

Others pay Target Circle Rewards, credit you can spend on a later trip, once you reach a spending total or use a service like Order Pickup. Industry observers note that the coupons are worth adding because they come off on top of the automatic deals. Target’s coupon policy allows one manufacturer coupon per item alongside its own offers, so a bottle of detergent with a 20%-off Circle deal and a $1 manufacturer coupon you added gets both discounts at checkout.
