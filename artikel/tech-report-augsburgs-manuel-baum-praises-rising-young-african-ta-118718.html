According to recent reports, FC Augsburg head coach Manuel Baum has praised the contributions of his younger players following Sunday’s 3-1 Bundesliga victory over Mainz 05. Two of Augsburg’s promising young African talents have now scored their first Bundesliga goals on successive matchdays, with Manchester City loanee Juma Bah and Nigerian striker Uchenna Ogundu both making their mark in recent weeks.

Sources indicate that bah, 20, scored Augsburg’s second goal against Mainz, while fellow youngster Tim Schnitzer, 18, impressed on his first Bundesliga start. Ogundu, also 20, nearly added a fourth goal after entering as a substitute, striking the inside of the far post in the 81st minute. Asked about Bah’s performance at the post-match press conference, Baum took the opportunity to highlight the youthful composition of his team.

“If you look back there, it looked like a child’s sweet shop today with Hennes [Behrens, 21], Tim Schnitzer [18], Juma Bah [20],” Baum responded. “Even Yannick Keitel [26] is still young. Marius [Wolf] was the most experienced player.”.

“The nice thing about seeing players like Juma and others back there is that, despite their age, they’re able to gain experience with us,” Baum continued. “And one sees what youngsters can achieve when they have a certain level of confidence. Uchi [Uchenna Ugundu, 20] also came in today and hit the post.”.

Bah’s goal represented an important personal milestone for the Sierra Leone international, who returned from suspension to help Baum contend with the absences of Jeffrey Gouweleeuw, Chrislain Matsima, Noahkai Banks, and Calvin Brackelmann.

According to recent reports, as Get German Football News reported when Bah completed his loan move from Manchester City in September, Augsburg secured a purchase option reportedly worth €21.5m for the young defender. Bah arrived with considerable senior experience from previous spells at Lens and Nice, but has faced competition for playing time in the Bavarian Swabian defence. Sunday’s performance offered another indication of what the club hopes to gain from the arrangement. Bah bundled home following Fabian Rieder’s 36th-minute corner to double Augsburg’s advantage and helped the makeshift back-three protect the lead.

Sources indicate that ogundu’s first Bundesliga goal came a fortnight earlier, with the Nigerian polishing off a Fabian Rieder through ball in second-half injury time of Augsburg’s 5-2 defeat to RB Leipzig. Speaking in an interview published in Augsburg’s matchday programme ahead of Sunday’s Mainz encounter, Ogundu described the goal as a proud personal moment, albeit one tempered by the disappointing result.

It has been revealed that the 20-year-old identified his speed as his greatest asset and expressed satisfaction with his technical and tactical development since arriving in the Bundesliga. Ogundu also spoke of the support he has received from his teammates, singling out Keitel, Michael Gregoritsch, and Behrens as particularly helpful in making him feel at home in the Fuggerstadt.

Recent findings suggest that the Nigerian, whom GGFN covered extensively following his expensive winter transfer from Alanyaspor, arrived in Augsburg amid stiff competition for places in attack. Our 2026/27 Augsburg tactical preview identified Ogundu as one of the winners of preseason preparations, while cautioning that his opportunities would remain limited by the presence of more established attacking options.

Recent findings suggest that his first Bundesliga goal and lively substitute appearance against Mainz nevertheless suggest that Ogundu is beginning to make a stronger case for playing time. Baum’s comments suggest that the FCA trainer remains encouraged by the progress of his younger players. With Schnitzer also delivering an impressive starting XI debut, Sunday’s victory provided Augsburg with more than just a welcome three points.
