Accused of ‘emptying the coffers’, Macron mulls spending caps for future French governments

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (63 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Right-wing candidates vying to succeed French President Emmanuel Macron have welcomed his suggestion of a “golden rule” to cap government spending and keep deficits in check, even as they mock the profligacy of his decade in power. Critics of the proposal denounce a budgetary straitjacket imposed on future governments and a distraction from the president’s growing impotence at home.
