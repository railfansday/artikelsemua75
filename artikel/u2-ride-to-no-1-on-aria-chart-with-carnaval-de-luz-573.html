U2’s Carnaval De Luz (via Island/Universal) enjoys the brightest possible start on the Australia’s albums chart, as it opens at No. 1.

With that fast opening, Carnaval De Luz becomes the Irish rock legends’ 12th chart leader in these parts. The Rock And Roll Hall of Fame inductees bagged their first No. 1 album with The Unforgettable Fire in 1984, a year after the ARIA Charts rolled out, and kept the hits coming with Rattle and Hum (1988), Achtung Baby (1991), Zooropa (1993), Pop (1997), The Best Of 1980-1990 (1998), All That You Can’t Leave Behind (2000), The Best Of 1990-2000 (2002), How To Dismantle An Atomic Bomb (2004), U218 Singles (2006), and No Line On The Horizon (2009).

According to ARIA, U2’s dozen No. 1s places the foursome equal fourth on the all-time leaderboard, tied with Madonna, and behind only The Beatles and Taylor Swift (14 each) and leader Jimmy Barnes with 16. Including Cold Chisel recordings, Barnes has 22 ARIA No. 1s. The latest ARIA Chart, published Friday, Nov. 20, is something of a birthday party for a bunch of veteran acts. Those celebrating include Aphex Twin, whose 25th anniversary edition of Drukqs (Warp/Inertia) drops in at No. 14, for the British electronic music great’s highest-charting album, bettering the No. 45 for Selected Ambient Works Volume II.

In a recent development, also, the late king of pop, Michael Jackson, returns to the chart at No. 21 with his former leader, Invincible, following its 25th anniversary release. And Australian punk rock act Trophy Eyes bounces in at No. 24 with 10th anniversary edition of Chemical Miracle.

It has been revealed that the latest frame welcomes latest releases from German DJ and production duo DJ Heartstring (No. 17 with Sentimental Ultra via Steel City Dance Discs), Sydney pub rockers The Johnnys (No. 40 with Rarehide – Outtakes, B-sides And Rarities via Cheersquad Records & Tapes) and Queensland sibling act Vixens of Fall (No. 48 with the independently-released Fox Hollow).

Over on the ARIA Singles Chart, Welsh pop artist Jamie Miller is crowned as “Babydoll” (Universal) rises 4-1. The former Voice U.K. contestant becomes not just the first artist to hit No. 1 in 2026 with his first charted single, he’s the only solo male artist to top the singles tally this year. The last time a solo male artist led the ARIA Chart was Justin Bieber, back in July 2025 with “Daisies.”.

Recent findings suggest that finally, the buzz surrounding the release this week of Grand Theft Auto VI drives “Flower In A Hurricane” (Ed Banger Records/Universal) to a new high. A collaboration of French electronic pair Justice with U.S. R&B star SZA and Aussie artists Tame Impala and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, the sountrack standout roars up the chart 48-17. It’s been a massive week for Kevin Parker’s Tame Impala, which collected trophies at the 2026 ARIA Awards on Wednesday night. Tame Impala has two Deadbeat tracks on the latest ARIA Top 50 Singles Chart: “Dracula” (down 9-12) and “Loser” (unchanged at No. 22).
