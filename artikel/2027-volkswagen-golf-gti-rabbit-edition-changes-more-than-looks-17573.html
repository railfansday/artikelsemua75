Do you ever find yourself yearning for the feeling you got the first time you drove the Mk 1 Volkswagen Rabbit? The diminutive VW helped usher the hot-hatch segment into the United States. Volkswagen can’t very well sell you a Mk 1 Rabbit any longer, so instead, the company unveiled a limited-production Rabbit Edition for the 2027 Golf GTI lineup.

Recent findings suggest that specifically, the Rabbit Edition is arriving for the 2027-½ model year as a sort of midyear patch to the GTI lineup. The special edition will slot between the S and the SE in the GTI’s roster and will come with a handful of performance upgrades generally limited to the Autobahn trim. The Rabbit version adds DCC adaptive damping, rides on 19-inch wheels, and wears a set of summer tires, rather than the all-season tires that generally come with the S. As far as cosmetic changes go, the special edition opts for a blacked-out logo design over the Dark Moss Green Metallic paint, which includes a set of front and rear badges that VW pulled from the Golf R Black Edition’s parts bin. The logos are joined by a black grille, black headlight elements (which are typically red), black wheels, black mirror caps, and a black roof. The one badge that isn’t black is the chrome Rabbit badge on the rear, which serves as an homage to the original. (Related: Breaking: Democrat Hakeem Jeffries nominated to be firs...)

The Rabbit Edition will be limited to the United States when it goes on sale in the second quarter of next year. Exact pricing hasn’t been revealed, but the Rabbit should cost somewhere between the $36,230 starting price of the S and the $40,420 starting price of the SE.
