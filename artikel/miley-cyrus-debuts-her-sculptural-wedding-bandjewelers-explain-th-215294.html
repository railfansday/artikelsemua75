Miley Cyrus Debuts Her Sculptural Wedding Band—Jewelers Explain the Design

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, miley Cyrus did more than give fans a first look at her wedding dress when she shared photos of her nuptials on Sunday. Zoom in and the singer’s thick wedding band is visible, stacked with her gold bombé cushion-cut diamond engagement ring.

She later shared another look at her bridal jewelry, including her band, which appears to be in a black round ring box:.

Industry observers note that the singer’s band choice is fashion-forward, The Clear Cut’s founder Olivia Landau tells ELLE. “Thick wedding bands have been trending, whether they are larger diamond eternity bands or wider solid gold bands.”. (Related: Industry News: Train a Sentence Embedding Model with 1B...)

It also really suits her, claims Lauren Boc, founder and CEO of Hera Fine Jewelry: “Miley’s wedding set is a beautiful reflection of her personal style—it has some rock and roll edge but still feels upscale.”.

Here, jewelers share their first impressions of the piece with ELLE.

Steph Mazuera, who founded her own namesake brand, says Cyrus’s ring has “a slightly curved silhouette” that “appears to follow the contours of her engagement ring.”.

Recent findings suggest that “When working with larger, bolder rings, introducing a soft curve or slight bend into the stack can make the overall combination feel more organic,” she adds. “It brings a sense of balance and softness without taking away from the impact of the individual rings. Everything should be designed to flow around the center stone.”. (Related: Introducing 🤗 Accelerate...)

Based on the photos she shared, Cyrus’s engagement ring and wedding band appear to be different metals. Her engagement ring is yellow gold.

It has been revealed that her wedding band, meanwhile, “looks to be a half-round band, around 4 mm, in white gold or platinum,” Boc says. “It appears that there’s a small east-west oval diamond bezel-set on top of the band, similar to our Amir ring. Raising the diamond slightly above the band like this creates dimension.”.

Several factors contribute to the value of a ring like Cyrus’s. “A custom band like this one could range quite a bit depending on the maker, gold weight, and craftsmanship,” Mazuera explains. “I would price a similar custom design in my studio in the $3,000–$5,000 range.”.

Cyrus’s wedding band is a statement piece that can be worn with or without her engagement ring. “I like that her wedding band feels substantial enough to hold its own alongside her engagement ring without competing with it,” Mazuera says. “Both rings have a strong sculptural presence, but the slight curve of the wedding band helps the two sit together more naturally.”.

Recent findings suggest that worn together, the two pieces have “a really bold look that’s both edgy and luxurious,” Boc says. “It’s maximalist, with a lot of metal on the hand. I love it!”.

Cyrus’s wedding band is anything but minimalist—but that’s exactly why it fuels multiple jewelry trends.

“I think we’re seeing a continued move toward more sculptural, substantial wedding jewelry, moving away from the idea that wedding bands need to be delicate or understated,” Mazuera notes.

Boc agrees. “Miley’s wedding set definitely reflects the trends we’re seeing at Hera. Chunky, bolder looks with a lot of gold weight have become really popular with our clients. When I first founded Hera in 2021, the massive engagement ring trend was really thin, delicate-looking bands. As a jewelry designer who cares a lot about durability, I’m so happy to see people turning toward more substantial designs.”.

Less traditional ring designs like Cyrus’s are also becoming more popular. “Our clients are often more concerned with having a ring that feels distinctive and uniquely like them,” Boc states.

Boc notes that while Cyrus’s photos give a limited view of her engagement ring and wedding band together, the apparent mix of metals is right on trend.

Industry observers note that “I think there’s something so chic and effortless about a mixed metal stack,” she adds. “Like everything else about Miley’s stack, mixing metals is the kind of design decision that makes your wedding set really feel curated and chosen around a strong understanding of your personal style. She certainly didn’t just pick this up pre-designed at a significant box store; a stack like this belongs to someone who’s confident in her personal style and chose something that feels uniquely her.”.
