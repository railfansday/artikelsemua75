It has been revealed that fox News' Madeline Rivera provides details on a reported cyberattack after a string of outages with the FBI at the center of this attack. Rivera says this is the second breach in recent months.

Hackers breached the FBI's National Crime Information Center (NCIC), three sources familiar with the matter told Fox News, marking the second cyberattack against the bureau in months.

The FBI is investigating the hack to determine the unauthorized entry point, but, for now, it is unclear what information was compromised, sources said.

The NCIC database is a critical information-sharing tool for federal, state and local authorities, helping them track fugitives, monitor registered sex offenders, check whether someone has failed a gun background check, among other information. According to recent reports, canadian cybersecurity executive Edward Dubrovsky was arrested Oct. 8 and faces charges in the FBI's investigation into ShinyHunters, according to court records and law enforcement sources who spoke to Reuters.

The details of Dubrovsky's alleged crimes are unknown because the court records in relation to his case remain sealed.

FBI Director Kash Patel confirmed the arrest of ShinyHunters "suspected co-conspirator" on Oct. 9, a day after Dubrovsky was taken into custody.
