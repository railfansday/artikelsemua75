Nvidia Stock Rises Nearly 4% Premarket as AI Shares Rebound on Black Friday: What’s Driving NVDA?

Recent findings suggest that nvidia shares are moving higher ahead of Friday’s Black Friday trading session, with the stock gaining nearly 4% in premarket activity as investors return to technology shares following a volatile week. The rebound comes after sharp swings triggered by leadership uncertainty, concerns over AI infrastructure spending and tighter US chip export controls.

It has been revealed that broader US futures are also pointing higher, suggesting the recovery extends beyond Nvidia. However, the latest rise faces an notable test: whether buyers can sustain momentum after the stock’s recent reversal or whether the rally will fade as investors reassess risks surrounding the AI trade. Nvidia shares rose approximately 3.6% in premarket trading on November 27, according to market data. The move followed a sharp decline on Wednesday, November 25, when NVDA closed at $149.30, down 3.91%, after touching an intraday low of $141.17. Recent findings suggest that the recovery is part of a broader rebound across US technology stocks. Nasdaq 100 futures advanced more than 2% as investors returned from the Thanksgiving holiday, while S&P 500 and Dow futures also moved higher. Semiconductor companies, including AMD and Broadcom, joined the advance.

According to recent reports, black Friday brings a shortened US trading session, with exchanges scheduled to close at 1 p.m. ET rather than the usual 4 p.m. The holiday shopping event also places consumer spending in focus, although retail sales are not the direct catalyst for Nvidia’s premarket move.

According to recent reports, nvidia’s recent volatility has been closely linked to the reinstatement of co-founder Jensen Huang as CEO on November 23. The announcement triggered a historic rally, with shares jumping more than 31% in a single session before reversing part of the gains over the following two trading days.

Sources indicate that president Donald Trump remarked he had spoken with Nvidia’s board and helped secure Huang’s return. Trump has also indicated that he aims to speak with Huang, while raising the possibility of a US government stake in Nvidia. However, no government investment agreement has been announced.

Leadership uncertainty is only one part of the challenge. Nvidia is also facing scrutiny over its China-related business, export-control compliance and the economics of the broader AI infrastructure boom. The reported pause in AI server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries has added another layer of uncertainty for the company and its manufacturing partners.

Investors will also be watching upcoming congressional scrutiny involving Huang. His return has eased one immediate leadership concern, but it has not resolved the regulatory and governance questions surrounding the firm.

Industry observers note that nvidia shares closed near $149.30 after falling as low as $141.17. The stock price has broken below its previously marked demand zone and remains under pressure from declining moving averages. The latest premarket rebound towards the $155–$156 region offers an initial recovery signal, but buyers need to reclaim nearby resistance before the broader technical structure improves.

The first hurdle is around $155–$157. A sustained move above this region could bring the $160–$165 area into focus, which is important after the sharp reversal from the recent rally. On the downside, $149 remains an immediate reference level, while the $141 area marks the recent intraday low.

It has been revealed that the Black Friday session may provide an early indication of whether investors are willing to rebuild positions in Nvidia. However, lighter holiday trading volumes can make price movements less reliable.

A sustained recovery will require stronger buying beyond the initial premarket reaction. For now, NVDA’s nearly 4% premarket gain signals renewed interest in the AI trade, but the firm’s export restrictions, leadership scrutiny and broader AI investment concerns remain unresolved.
