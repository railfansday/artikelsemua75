Ariana Grande’s Eternal Sunshine Tour Is Getting a Concert Film and Documentary

Ariana Grande’s Eternal Sunshine Tour may have wrapped in September, but fans will soon get another chance to experience it. Following an initial teaser on November 23, the singer announced on November 24 that both a concert film and a documentary for the tour are set to premiere on Peacock.

The news comes nearly three months after Grande concluded her first concert tour in seven years, an experience she has previously described as a significant chapter in her career. (Related: Industry News: huggingface_hub v1.0: Five Years of Buil...)

Here’s everything we know about the projects so far.

According to recent reports, the documentary—titled The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour —follows Grande “during the 30 days leading up to the opening of her sold-out tour as she and her team race to bring the ambitious production to life,” according to Peacock. “At the same time, Grande is putting the finishing touches on her eighth studio album, giving audiences an intimate window into her creative process during a pivotal moment.”.

The Eternal Sunshine Tour concert film “journeys through the collapse of her reality, a cosmic rebirth, a reconnection with her inner child, and the memories that shaped her. She transforms the Eternal Sunshine Tour into a cinematic celebration of healing, connection, and the music that has defined her life.”.

Her Eternal Sunshine Tour began in Oakland, California, on June 6 and concluded on September 1 following 10 performances at London’s O2 Arena. The reveals featured music from across her discography, including Eternal Sunshine, Positions, and her latest album, Petal, which was released in July.

The tour also marked Grande’s return to the concert stage following her 2019 Sweetener World Tour. Throughout the run, she performed in custom looks from designers including Alexander McQueen, Givenchy, and Vivienne Westwood, styled by Law Roach.

Grande shared the first teaser on Instagram on November 23, in collaboration with Peacock and Team Ariana.

The video features footage of Grande performing the title track from Eternal Sunshine. As the performance ends, the screen reveals the message: “Coming soon to… Peacock.”.

During an August interview with Vosk in the City, Grande reflected on how her latest tour differed from her previous experiences performing live.

“I think my last tour I wasn’t necessarily in the same place in life, and I wasn’t able to be as present as I am this time,” she explained, describing the Eternal Sunshine Tour as “such a beautiful corrective experience.”.

Grande also spoke about reconnecting with longtime fans through songs spanning more than a decade of her career.

Industry observers note that in August, her representative confirmed that the singer planned to step away from public-facing work following the tour. Grande later addressed the decision during a concert in Chicago, explaining that it had been made well in advance. (Related: Industry News: Apriel-H1: The Surprising Key to Distill...)

“It is something that I have decided; it’s a plan that I had quietly made a long time ago, and it’s a decision that was made from a thoughtful and empowered place,” she told the audience.

The Eternal Sunshine Diaries: The Making of the Tour documentary premieres on Peacock on December 11. The Eternal Sunshine Tour concert film arrives on December 12.
