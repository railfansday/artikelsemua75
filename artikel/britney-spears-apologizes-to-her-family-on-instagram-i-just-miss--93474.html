She described her own conduct as “childish and embarrassing,” adding: “Thoughts on the past, I’m obviously human, and there actually might be something to those things I remarked psychology-wise; either way, I’m sorry to my family for bringing up those memories.”.

Spears remarked the conversation had never taken place directly. “I’ve never actually spoken to them face to face on it,” she wrote. “I just miss them like crazy! And I’m very sorry!” She also called herself “blessed to have such a beautiful family.”.

Spears is the eldest child of Jamie and Lynne Spears, who are divorced. She has a brother, Bryan, and a sister, Jamie Lynn Spears.

The relationship has been publicly strained for years. Spears spent 13 years under a conservatorship, established in February 2008 with her father as conservator, which gave others control over her finances, career and personal decisions. After an explosive court hearing in June 2021 in which she testified against the arrangement, a Los Angeles judge suspended Jamie Spears that September and terminated the conservatorship on Nov. 12, 2021.

Industry observers note that in the years since, Spears has repeatedly addressed the fallout. “Honestly my family embarrassed me and hurt me deeply,” she wrote in December 2021, a month after the termination. Her 2023 memoir The Woman in Me detailed her account of the arrangement, including her view that it “stripped me of my womanhood, made me into a child,” and addressed tensions with her sister.
