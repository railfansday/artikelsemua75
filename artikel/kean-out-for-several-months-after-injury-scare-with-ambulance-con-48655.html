Italy international Moise Kean ‘will be out for several months’ after the latest injury scare, confirmed Como coach Cesc Fabregas, with the player whisked away in an ambulance.

The striker has been struggling with a recurring right leg glitch since the end of last season, carrying it on with the transfer from Fiorentina to Como, and was ruled out for five weeks following specialist consultation.

However, just as he was about to return to full training, he had another setback and now surgery may well be the only option. (Related: Latest Update: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for ...)

“Kean is doing well, he is calmer now,” revealed Fabregas in a press conference this afternoon.

“He had this treatment two or three weeks ago in Barcelona. We know that the crevice in the bone has closed up a bit. He felt something and got really scared, crumpled to the ground, and the rest of us were frightened for him too.

It has been revealed that “The ambulance came to pick him up, we thought it was really serious. It’s true that he will be out for several months, not weeks, but we want to resolve the situation definitively.

In a recent development, “It is a problem with the bone. We need to just be by his side now.”.

Kean had been a big investment for Como and has not played since September 20, with only four games for the club before then.

Problems with the tibia have gone back a very long way, and were even behind a failed medical with Atletico Madrid in 2023/24.

If this is indeed a long-term absence, it will also force Como to invest in a replacement, as Tasos Douvikas is at the moment the only available centre-forward in the squad.
