📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the Sang et Or must wash away the affront. Three weeks after the historic slap received by Como in front of its Bollaert crowd (1-9), RC Lens travels to the pitch of RB Leipzig, this Tuesday (9 p.m.) on behalf of the 5e day of the league stage of the Champions League.

This heavy defeat against Como led to the dismissal of Yannick Cahuzac, who was replaced by Didier Digard. The former Le Havre coach will thus experience his very first in C1 on the sidelines. And a victory is almost imperative for Florian Thauvin and his partners to hope to secure a place in the play-offs, especially since they will then face Bodo/Glimt, Manchester City and Liverpool.

In a recent development, opposite, Leipzig has so far achieved exactly the same course as the Artésiens, with three defeats and a small victory on the clock. On the other hand, the Germans occupy 4th place in the Bundesliga and have the best attack in their championship (32 goals in 10 days). They also remain on a large 5-1 success on the pitch of Union Berlin last Saturday. (Related: Breaking: Large Language Models: A New Moore's Law?...)

Exclusive and full holder of the broadcasting rights for the European Cups in France, the Canal+ group will not broadcast this Leipzig-Lens on its main (premium) channel. The match will be broadcast in full on the Canal+ Foot channel. Kick-off will be at 9 p.m. at the Red Bull Arena.
