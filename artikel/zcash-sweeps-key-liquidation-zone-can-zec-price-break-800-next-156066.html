Sources indicate that zcash [ZEC] has bounced 15.3% from the local market low of $679.27 that the leading privacy altcoin registered on Friday, the 20th of November. At the time of writing, ZEC was trading at $788, just under the $800 round-number resistance.

The majority of the 15% price rebound from the low occurred on Saturday. For the majority of the last 24-36 hours, Zcash has traded between $780 and $800. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

Sources indicate that coinalyze data showed that the Open Interest has slowly dwindled from $900 million to $861 million over the weekend. The Funding Rates had briefly dropped into negative territory on the 22nd of November, but had closed in on +0.01% since then. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

Led by a Bitcoin [BTC] bounce back above $60k, Zcash and the rest of the altcoin market saw some gains from the deep correction they endured last week.

Can these gains last, or are they just a brief respite within a broader bearish trend?

Zcash has made steady technological advances in 2026, as has the rest of the privacy sector, despite the bear market conditions in the crypto sphere this year.

According to recent reports, the price correction in recent months was part of a higher timeframe downtrend. At the time of writing, ZEC was trading just above a key demand zone from $516-$752, derived from the weekly price chart.

It has been revealed that the dense cluster of long liquidations that had built up around $720-$770 from September has been swept. It is possible that the privacy token will react bullishly following this sweep.

However, sustained wider market bearishness and increased BTC sell pressure could drag ZEC lower.

The 4-hour chart revealed a bearish price trend in progress. The recent bounce toward $800 could continue as high as $910, the 78.6% Fibonacci retracement level.

Recent findings suggest that the RSI was below neutral 50, and the OBV was in a downtrend. Both technical indicators highlighted prevalent bearish conditions despite the recent short-term bounce.

Swing traders can maintain a bearish bias, unless the price manages to climb back above $910.
