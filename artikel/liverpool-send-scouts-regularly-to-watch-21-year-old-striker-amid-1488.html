Liverpool are reportedly keeping a close eye on Monza striker Gustavo Varela as they consider strengthening their attacking options, with the Portuguese youngster emerging as one of the most exciting prospects in Italian football.

In a recent development, according to a TEAMtalk report, Liverpool have been sending scouts to monitor Varela’s performances weekly. The forward has made an excellent start to his career in Italy, registering nine goals and an assist in his first ten league appearances.

His remarkable goalscoring form has attracted interest from several clubs, with Tottenham, Brentford and Sunderland also enquiring about his availability. Italian giants Juventus and Napoli are also monitoring the situation.

The Merseyside outfit have struggled to produce consistent attacking numbers this season, and bringing in another goalscorer could help address that concern.

Industry observers note that alexander Isak remains an notable figure in their forward line, while Hugo Ekitike is another option when fully fit. However, Liverpool could benefit from more competition in the final third, particularly if they want to challenge for major honours. Varela’s age, potential and impressive performances make him an attractive long-term investment. Monza reportedly value the youngster at around €40 million, although his valuation could increase considerably if he maintains his current trajectory.

The Reds will need to assess whether he can consistently reproduce his remarkable goalscoring form, but his development in Italy has certainly made him a player worth monitoring ahead of future transfer windows.
