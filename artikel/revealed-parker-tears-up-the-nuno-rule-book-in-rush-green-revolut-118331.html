Training ground videos from the Nuno Espírito Santo era usually featured someone else taking training sessions at Rush Green: The former Hammers boss was usually nowhere to be seen when the customary pre-game PR videos were released with one of his assistants running the session.

His preparation was also rumoured to be perfunctory with short, superficial pre-match meetings, none of which produced the results on the pitch that would have helped him keep his job and West Ham keep their Premier League status.

As revealed by Roshane Thomas, writing in the NYTimes:.

Recent findings suggest that during the international break, he [Scott Parker] has focused on high-intensity training. It has been his preferred approach, rather than team bonding exercises away from their training hub in Rush Green.

The players, however, have noticed a massive difference under Parker. With help from assistant coaches Mark Noble, Jonathan Hill, Steve Potts, Gerard Prenderville and Billy Lepine, Parker has prioritised working on pressing structure and shape with and without the ball.”.

Which begs the question “Just what were the squad working on under Nuno”?

In a recent development, according to their new Manager, the changes he’s made have kicked in and there’s been a noticeable upturn. Which, lets face it, was long overdue, and after the new head coach’s first two games in charge was clearly, sorely needed.

Sources indicate that “My general feeling is this is a group that are desperate to do well and work hard,” said Parker. “I’ve sensed that in the training sessions and what we’ve been doing. It’s still early days, I understand that, but there’s been some real improvement over the last two weeks”.

We’ll all see for ourselves tomorrow when Preston North End visit: After his tough two-game baptism of fire, Parker got his two week break to prepare for a home tie against the bottom side. Which is as easy as it’s going to get this season.
