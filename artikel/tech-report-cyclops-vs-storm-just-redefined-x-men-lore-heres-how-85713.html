Sources indicate that thanks to the Marvel Cinematic Universe finally casting the X-Men, new fans are learning more and more about the lore of Marvel’s Mightiest Mutants. Chief among them is the Weather Witch, Storm (who will be played by Maya Boyd). Ororo Monroe has been a popular character for decades, but there’s a lot of people may not know. For example, she once challenged Cyclops for leadership of the X-Men and won.

You may be thinking that the woman who’s mastered her control of the weather, and also has magic in her bloodline, should easily beat Scott Summers. Even with her incredible abilities, it wouldn’t be an easy battle. However, when the two of them fought in Uncanny X-Men #201, Storm was depowered. This made his defeat more impressive. (Related: Tech Report: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons i...)

This wasn’t a fight between two enemies. It was just time for Cyclops to move on and be with his family. Ororo and Scott remained close and, to this day, are like siblings. Thanks to Marvel’s newest venture, the Midnight Universe, that is going to change. (Related: Obsessed fan breaks into YouTuber’s home demanding marr...)

Recent findings suggest that midnight X-Men #2's previews showed that Storm had disappeared for a week, but in seeking her out, something far more sinister transpired. Her team wasn't worried until her emergency beacon went off. That led Cyclops to put a team together (Beast, Kitty Pryde, Angel, Jean Grey and Iceman) to rescue their teammate. Unfortunately, after arriving at her location, the X-Men walked into a trap. Each of them was separated, and only Cyclops escaped.

The next couple of pages didn’t have any dialogue, but the images spoke plenty enough. Scott Summers is shown talking to both Charles Xavier and Magneto, and it’s easy to assume that he’s asking for help. On the final page in the preview and confirmed by the release, as it turns out, Vlad Dracul has Storm mesmerized. The newly introduced matter confirmed that he’s the one who turned her, with her turning more of the X-Men in turn.

In the debut issue of Midnight X-Men, it was revealed that there’s a newly introduced war brewing on Earth. It isn’t just humans vs. mutants. It’s now humans vs. mutants vs. vampires vs. empyres (mutant vampires), with Storm in charge. On the other end is Scott Summers, who's fighting back on behalf of the mutants.

Sources indicate that during a confrontation with Cyclops in the first issue of Midnight X-Men, Gambit joked that Scott hasn’t done well protecting people. Remy also mentioned that he couldn’t live with himself if he didn’t take a shot at killing the last X-Man. It was a shot he shouldn’t have taken because Scott blasted the ceiling and unleashed the sun on the Empyres before escaping.

The encounter isn't a big deal, but Cyclops being the last of the X-Men is. Matter #2 brutally highlighted just what came of Cyclops' first rescue attempt on Storm, as with his other attempts, as he soon was under Jean Grey's control. Regardless of the reason, Cyclops did fail at protecting his people. Among the people he failed was his wife, Jean Grey.Jean Grey was bitten by Storm, but her role an empyre has been striking. She's able to bend Cyclops to her will while not allowing him to join her as a fellow empyre, with the help of the Phoenix to draw him back in. That and Jean's telepathy could prevent her from being controlled. Even if that's the case, Scott won't forgive Ororo for what she's done, and won't forgive himself for his failures anytime soon.

Readers shouldn’t forget that it isn’t just Jean and Storm who are vampires. Iceman, Psylocke, and the aforementioned Gambit are only three of the many mutants who are now empyres, with a total of 37 victims Cyclops blames himself for allowing to be turned. If Storm is responsible for the end of the X-Men, readers can expect that a showdown is coming between the two former leaders.

Scott Summers took his job as leader of the X-Men seriously. It wasn’t just a job. He believed in Charles Xavier’s dream, and he failed to uphold it. Now, he doesn’t believe in anything. The introduction of Hope could be the one thing that rekindles the fire he lost. If that happens, he and Storm are on a collision course that will cost humans, mutants, and Empyres their lives.
