Latest Update: How AI Is Reshaping China's Industrial Policy

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (118 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Can Chinese tech companies help the country achieve technological self-reliance while keeping up in the AI arms race? On this episode, we speak with Kyle Chan, a fellow at the Brookings Institution’s John L. Thornton China Center and author of the High Capacity newsletter, about Beijing’s response to the global AI boom and the tensions between Chinese companies’ commercial ambitions and the government’s long-term aims. He explains how companies draw on shared expertise and supply chains to expand across industries including smartphones, robotics, EVs and semiconductors — and we also discuss Huawei’s efforts to compete with Nvidia and why technological success doesn’t necessarily translate into strong returns for stock investors in China.
