It has been revealed that "I'm a bit bored because in my last 237 press conferences I always praised him," remarked Farke. "And right now the whole country is praising him.

"I'm surprised that everyone is so surprised that my goalkeeper is capable of delivering a top performance. This is why he wears some gloves – I expect him to catch some balls and make some saves.

"For that, I can stay a bit away from praising him when the whole country is praising him. I'm not surprised he was there with a good performance.

"Since he came back and everyone is singing his praises, he's got just stick from me. He's a young lad and we need to make sure he stays humble and grounded.".

Trafford returns to domestic duty on Saturday when Leeds travel to old foes Chelsea.

Farke has reported a clean bill of health ahead of the trip to Stamford Bridge following a positive international window at home and further afield.

Anton Stach and Noah Okafor have both benefited from the break, while Joe Rodon and Gabriel Gudmundsson came through unscathed after starting for their countries despite fitness concerns.
