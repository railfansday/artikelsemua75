📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that ATEEZ‘s GOLDEN HOUR: Part.6 puts K-pop back in a familiar spot — No. 1 on the all-genre Billboard 200 chart. It’s the 27th album or EP by a K-pop act to reach No. 1 on Billboard’s flagship albums chart in just over eight years. The first was BTS’ Love Yourself: Tear, back in June 2018.

This is the fifth K-pop album or EP to top Billboard 200 in 2026, which ties 2023 as the year with the most K-pop leaders.

According to recent reports, here’s another fun fact: ATEEZ’s EP opened with 195,000 equivalent album units earned in the United States. That’s the highest one-week total for any release (from any genre) since another K-Pop act, Stray Kids, opened with 369,000 equivalent album units of This & That on the chart dated Aug. 22.

Sources indicate that stray Kids has amassed nine No. 1 titles on the Billboard 200, which puts the group out front among K-pop acts. BTS is second, with seven No. 1 titles. ATEEZ is next in line with four No. 1s. SuperM, BLACKPINK, TOMORROW X TOGETHER, NewJeans, TWICE and ENHYPEN have all notched one No. 1 to date. In addition, one multi-artist K-pop soundtrack, KPop Demon Hunters, has reached No. 1.

BLACKPINK was the first female K-pop act to land a No. 1 album on the Billboard 200. They scored with Born Pink in 2022. They were followed by NewJeans and TWICE.

Sources indicate that K-pop groups tend to be large ensembles. TWICE has nine members, one more than Stray Kids and ATEEZ. BTS has seven members, as did SuperM, which went on hiatus in mid-2021. ENHYPEN has six members. TOMORROW X TOGETHER and NewJeans each have five members. BLACKPINK has four members. To date, no K-pop solo artists, duos or trios have topped the Billboard 200.
