Scott McTominay not only scored the winner in Napoli’s win over Torino, but he was also the undisputed Man of the Match at the Stadio Maradona last night: ‘He played with fire in his belly,’ wrote Corriere dello Sport.

McTominay was the MOTM in Napoli’s 1-0 win over Torino on Saturday. The former Manchester United midfielder scored the winning goal with a powerful shot from the edge of the box, helping the Partenopei win their second match from the last four. Italian newspapers on Sunday are not short of praise for the 29-year-old who has now netted six goals in 12 appearances across all competitions this season.

McTominay was rated 8/10 by Gazzetta dello Sport and Tuttosport and 7.5/10 by Corriere dello Sport.

This is McTominay’s third season at Napoli, and he has now scored 33 goals in 92 matches across all competitions with the Partenopei, winning the Scudetto as the league’s best player in the 2024-25 season. (Related: GTA 6 player hit with 30-day PSN ban after sharing nude...)

In a recent development, here’s what Italian newspapers remarked after his winning goal vs. Torino.

According to recent reports, gazzetta: Hurricane Scott keeps Napoli alive almost single-handedly, ultimately in his long-distance duel with Perri. Physicality, quality and leadership. Simply irreplaceable.

It has been revealed that tuttosport: He caused panic in the opposition defence, forcing Perri into several saves. He eventually beat the Brazilian goalkeeper in the second half with the goal that sent the Maradona crowd into raptures.

Industry observers note that corriere dello Sport: He played with fire in his belly. Three shots in the opening 16 minutes; seven in total—three on target— and a decisive goal (his fourth against Torino, his favourite Serie A opponent, along with Inter), plus the charisma of a leader.
