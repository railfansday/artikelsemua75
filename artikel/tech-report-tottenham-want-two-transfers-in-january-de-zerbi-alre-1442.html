Tottenham are ready to complete two transfers in the January transfer window - one signing and one departure, according to Sky Sports.

Spurs were one of the biggest spenders in the summer, splashing out more than £300million on ten upcoming signings, while also allowing 17 players to leave the club, either permanently or on loan. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

But they are likely to be active in the winter window too, with one key area in need of further investment.

Sources indicate that tottenham have scored just ten goals in ten Premier League matches this season, the joint third fewest in the division. Indeed, it took them until the fifth game of the campaign to even find the back of the net.

Savio and Omar Marmoush were attacking investments in the summer, both arriving from Manchester City, but they have scored just one Premier League goal between them so far, with Dominic Solanke only netting once too.

GIVEMESPORT understands it is up front where head coach Roberto De Zerbi would like to strengthen when the January transfer window opens, if the right players are available and, in fact, the two transfers are both likely to be in the forward area, according to Sky Sports.

In a piece looking ahead to the January transfer window, they said: "Goals have been an issue for Roberto De Zerbi's side this season, with only two Premier League sides scoring fewer after 10 matches, and Spurs are open to bringing in another forward if the right player becomes available.".

Bournemouth star Eli Junior Kroupi was heavily linked with a move to N17 in the summer, but the Cherries were reluctant to sell and the Frenchman then picked up an injury which kept him out of action for the first few months of the season.

The 20-year-old scored his first goal for the France senior side during the international break, notching the winner after coming off the bench with 27 minutes to go against Turkiye.

Bournemouth remain reluctant to sell, particularly mid-season, but Spurs may well test their resolve with an offer when the window opens, although it would have to get towards the £100million mark.

Sources indicate that tottenham are also keen to let another player leave in winter, with Richarlison surplus to requirements at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. The Brazilian had a falling out with De Zerbi and as a result was left out of the club's 25-man Premier League squad when it was revealed in September. He was close to a move to Everton and Trabzonspor in Turkiye, before the summer window closed, but a move did not materialise.

He has since been training with the youth team and playing with the U21s and Spurs will be looking to move him on come January, with his contract at the club expiring in the summer, when he will be allowed to leave on a free transfer.
