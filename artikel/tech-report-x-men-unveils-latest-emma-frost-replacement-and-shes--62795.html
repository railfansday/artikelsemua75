According to recent reports, marvel establishes its latest psychic powerhouse as the one X-Men character who could render Emma Frost obsolete. Overshadowing Emma Frost is no easy task, as the White Queen ranks among the most skilled and powerful telepaths on Earth, an Omega-level mental force capable of matching Charles Xavier and Jean Grey in sheer potential, and also benefiting from a secondary mutation that shields her body from physical injury and her mind from psychic attacks. Emma Frost has also cemented herself as one of mutantkind's best leaders, having gone from one of their many enemies to a permanent guide to the X-Men and other mutant squads.

Alongside psychic heavyweights like Professor X, Jean Grey, Psylocke, Kwannon, Rachel Summers, Cable, Nate Grey, Quentin Quire, Legion, and Dani Moonstar, Emma Frost belongs to one of the most common mutant mutant archetypes in the Marvel Universe, which overflows with powerful telepaths and telekinetics. Naturally, mutantkind’s rogues' gallery mirrors the heroes' psychic abundance with telepaths of their own, from ancient astral parasites like the Shadow King and Cassandra Nova to the Mastermind family, Mesmero, Exodus, Selene, Stryfe, Madelyne Pryor, Gamesmaster, and the psychic entity Onslaught. As if this quantity of psychic X-Men characters weren't enough, Marvel has also introduced mutants with the overpowered ability to copy superpowers, which naturally entail the replication of Professor X, Jean Grey, and Emma Frost's signature abilities. In the pages of X-Men United, Justina LaGuardia aka Superior steps out of the shadows to confront Emma Frost threaten her status as mutantkind's foremost psychic. Justina’s unique mutant gift lets her replicate and immediately surpass any mutant power she encounters, which means that merely standing near the White Queen automatically elevates Justina’s telepathy far above Emma’s own Omega-level power. As flashbacks in X-Men United #9 reveal, Justina actually psychically overpowered Emma in the 1990s, secretly implanting the mental suggestion that drove Emma to host the fateful Hellfire Club truce dinner where the original Hellions were slaughtered.

The main cover for X-Men United #9 is an homage to Astonishing X-Men #2, where Emma places her hands over Cyclops' naked eyes.

Now targeting Emma Frost’s newly introduced Graymatter Lane school, Justina LaGuardia intends to tear down the White Queen’s authority and expose how easily mutantkind’s proudest telepath can be outclassed. Justina stayed hidden for decades after she rejected Charles Xavier’s original invitation to become a founding X-Man because she despised Xavier’s naive idealism and saw superheroes as reckless vigilantes. After decades of amassing wealth and secret influence, Justina’s antagonistic initiative is to assemble a private arsenal and army to wage a war against mutantkind and tear the X-Men apart.

Recent findings suggest that justina LaGuardia's ability to replicate and surpass any mutant power is so broken that Marvel will probably need to introduce a clear limitation to keep future X-Men battles dramatic. In that regard, Justina runs straight into the same narrative roadblock as Isca the Unbeaten, whose Omega-level mutant gift literally prevents her from ever losing. Since an automatic-victory power removes all tension from a fight, Marvel had to sideline Isca on Arakko and exploit her compulsion to switch sides whenever victory probability changes, thus preventing one of the most effective mutant fighters from becoming a regular fixture.

It has been revealed that without a similar weakness, Justina’s "Superior" mutation inevitably flattens every confrontation, not only with psychic mutants like Emma Frost, but also with all mutantkind as a whole. Marvel can resolve this problem by establishing strict rules for her power, such as forcing her to endure overwhelming mental feedback when copying multiple Omega-level minds, limiting her to one mirrored ability at a time, or leaving her completely vulnerable to non-mutant threats like human technology, magic, and baseline fighters whose skills fall outside mutant genetics. Emma Frost herself once got a weakness to balance her power, with her diamond form preventing her from using her psychic skills.
