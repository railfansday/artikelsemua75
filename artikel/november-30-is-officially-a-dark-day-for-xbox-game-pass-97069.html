📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Xbox Game Pass is going to take a significant hit on November 30, making it a dark day for the subscription service. This is a pretty busy month for Microsoft’s gaming service, with tons of new additions, as well as several departures. For example, 13 games were removed from Xbox Game Pass on November 15, including titles like Moonlighter, Relic Hunters Legend, Winter Burrow, and the amazing 1000xResist. To balance it all out, the service also gained tons of different games and will continue to do so throughout the rest of November. However, things will take a dark turn soon.

Microsoft made an official announcement for the Wave 2 updates to Xbox Game Pass in November via a post on Xbox Wire, and while the post mostly highlights the upcoming additions that will arrive during the second half of the month, it also reveals which games will be removed from the service. In total, Xbox Game Pass is losing eight games on November 30. The blow is huge, mainly due to the fact that all games are removed simultaneously, whereas additions happen spread out during the month. Nevertheless, there’s still time to play some of them if you want.

Most of the eight games that are being removed from the service on November 30 have been available in the catalog for some time. For example, the benefit of having Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden on Game Pass is coming to an end over a year after the title’s addition to the library. There are several other games that have been available for free via the service for even longer. This is the last opportunity to play them without having to pay anything extra, but there is good news for players interested in any of the titles leaving the service.

In case none of these games speak to you, you can instead look for some of the November additions to Xbox Game Pass, as there have been tons of them over the past few weeks. Sources indicate that if that doesn’t do it for you, an alternative is to blueprint ahead for some of the first December games on Xbox Game Pass, both of which are co-op experiences. Microsoft will certainly announce more titles to join the catalog throughout the next month, but the first two ones have already been announced during the Wave 2 updates for November.

November 30 may not be as grim as November 15 was for the subscription service, but the removal of eight games at once will hit it hard. Even so, it seems like Xbox Game Pass has a bright horizon ahead of it, so be sure to check out the catalog for any recent updates and look forward to the new free releases.
