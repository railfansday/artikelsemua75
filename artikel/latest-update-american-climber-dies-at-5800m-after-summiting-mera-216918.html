Richard Carlos Skidmore, 66, of the United States, was found dead in his tent on Mera Peak on Sunday, November 22, after summiting the 6,476m mountain the previous morning.

According to Solukhumbu district police, he reached the summit at approximately 10am on Saturday, November 21, and descended to the high camp at around 5,800m, where he spent the night.

Industry observers note that he was found dead the next morning. Past Mera Peak climbers have suggested on social media that it was unusual to overnight in the high camp after summiting so early in the day. (Related: Industry News: Train a Sentence Embedding Model with 1B...)

Industry observers note that his body was evacuated by helicopter to Kathmandu for an autopsy. The cause of death remains undetermined. An initial report raised the possibility of altitude-related illness or a cardiac event, but neither has been established medically. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Skidmore previously summited Lobuche Peak in 2024, according to the Everest Chronicle and the Italian Alpine Club’s publication Lo Scarpone. So he had previous Himalayan climbing experience above 6,000m.

It has been revealed that the Himalayan Database does include Mera Peak, but its coverage of popular trekking peaks is selective. However, it notes that since 2018, four people have died on Mera Peak, not counting Skidmore.
