Stablecoin giant Tether [USDT] has been making some moves lately. Here’s the rundown.

Sources indicate that tether has backed Argentine fintech app Lemon, with the former not putting a number on it when it declared the deal. Lemon did, however, saying that the package is worth $14.5 million.

Recent findings suggest that according to Lemon CEO Marcelo Cavazzoli, only $5 million of that is an actual investment in the company’s shares. The other $9.5 million is an incentive proposal linked to products the two will build together.

In a recent development, there’s a lot that’s unclear right now though. Neither side has shared Lemon’s valuation or how massive a stake Tether is getting. There’s also no word on how or when the $9.5 million gets paid out. Tether is after more people for USDT and Tether Gold [XAUT] across Latin America. Lemon gives it a decent shot at that. Worth noting, however, that Lemon’s not the only home for Tether this month.

On the 9th, D3 debuted a USD vault for Tether’s stablecoin. Consumers can deposit USDT, keep an eye on their position, and withdraw without leaving the platform.

Sources indicate that sentora built the system using its Earn OS and Upshift tools, which decide where deposited funds go. Money moves with the market, within a set of risk rules. It is noted that the setup has already handled more than $2 billion. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

Domain buyers usually come in, make a deal, and leave. Earning on stablecoins gives them a reason to keep their money on the platform.

It has been revealed that just don’t mistake automated for guaranteed. Returns depend on the lending markets the vault uses. So, consumers are still trusting a whole stack of infrastructure with their dollars.
