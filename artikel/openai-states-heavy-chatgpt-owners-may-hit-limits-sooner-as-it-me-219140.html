While the rollout has started for a small group of consumers, OpenAI aims to bring shared usage limits more broadly in December, per its support page.

Similar to OpenAI, rival Anthropic also moved to bring its conversational chat service Claude and work-focused Cowork under one interface in September.

Industry observers note that the changes come as AI companies face mounting infrastructure costs and growing demand for increasingly capable models, putting pressure on their computing resources. Bringing conversational and work-related tools into a single interface could also make AI chatbots simpler to use, allowing consumers to move between chats and more complex tasks without switching tabs.
