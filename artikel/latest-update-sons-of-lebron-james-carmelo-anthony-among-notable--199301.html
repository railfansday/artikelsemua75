Sources indicate that one of the best parts about Thanksgiving is reuniting with family, eating good food and watching sports. Whether it's watching a massive NFC North showdown on CBS between the Lions and Bears or tuning into one of the best college basketball games of the week between No. 11 Michigan State and Arkansas, Thanksgiving will have something for everybody.

It has been revealed that michigan State star Jeremy Fears Jr. was named Preseason Player of the Year by CBS Sports and has looked the part through the first three weeks of the season. Fears, who is the No. 35 ranked player on CBS Sports Prospect Rankings for the 2027 NBA Draft, is also improving his draft stock ahead of a wide-open draft with his play.

If Fears is drafted in the first round this summer, he will join exclusive tech giant with his brother Jeremiah Fears, who was selected No. 8 overall by the Pelicans in 2025. The Fears brothers could become the latest siblings to both get drafted in the first round of the NBA Draft if Jeremy is selected on the first day.

Here is a list of siblings who were both drafted in the first round of the NBA Draft.

Recent findings suggest that fears isn't the only notable sibling playing college basketball this season. Bryce James, the son of LeBron James, started the first three games of the season for Arizona. Kiyan Anthony, the son of NBA legend Carmelo Anthony, has been a key rotation piece for Syracuse this season. Andrej Stojaković, the son of one of the greatest shooters in NBA history (Peja Stojaković), has played like an All-American this season.
