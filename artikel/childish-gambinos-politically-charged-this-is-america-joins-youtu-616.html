Childish Gambino has joined the club. YouTube’s Billion Views Club, that is, for his 2018 hit “This Is America.”.

The official music video for the actor and rapper’s global smash is his first to pass the streaming milestone.

“This Is America’ was a politically-charged shot that rung out around the globe, a song that explores gun violence, racism and discrimination in the United States. It was, of course, powered by its confronting, one-take music video, directed by Hiro Murai and starring Gambino. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

The recording debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 chart (via mcDJ/Wolf + Rothstein/RCA), bowing as the most-streamed and top-selling song of the week. It also arrived atop the U.S. Streaming Songs and Digital Song Sales charts, and topped sales and consumption charts around the globe, including Australia. The single was the first and only Hot 100 leader for Donald Glover’s musical alter ego, who took the stage to unveil it as the musical guest on the May 5, 2018 episode of NBC’s Saturday Night Live, which he also hosted. Industry observers note that on the red carpet of the 2018 Met Gala, Glover spoke briefly about the song and its meaning. In a comment dripping with irony, he remarked that, with “This Is America,” he simply wanted to make a song folks could play on the Fourth of July.

He elaborated in a 2023 interview for a GQ cover story, in which he said the idea for the song “started as a joke,” and that its title was “all we had” at the embryonic stage. “It started as a Drake diss, to be honest, as like a funny way of doing it. But then I was like, this s— sounds kind of hard though. So I was like, let me play with it.”.

Recent findings suggest that it was no joke with critics and fans. Glover and co-writer Ludwig Goransson went on to win song of the year with “America” at the 61st Annual Grammy Awards, where it became the first rap song to ever win the prestigious “Substantial Four” category, which recognizes songwriters. “This Is America” went on to snag a total of four Grammys, including record of the year, best music video, and snagged the MTV Video Music Award for best direction.

The song and its video have enjoyed several afterlives through covers, mashups and parodies.
