According to recent reports, marvel and video games are a huge focus for Lego right now, so it was really only a matter of time before all three of those worlds combined to create some Marvel Rivals Lego sets. It was also inevitable that once those worlds collided, Jeff would be the first one called up for Lego duty.

No official announcement from Lego just yet, but as is often the case when it comes to new sets tied to popular IP, an image of Jeff’s set has made it out into the world before an official reveal.

It has been revealed that shared by various accounts that leak Lego and collectibles news, the set will simply be a Lego version of Jeff modeled after his default skin in Marvel Rivals, so just the little Land Shark wearing a pink collar with his name engraved on the gold tag. Lego Jeff has a massive smile on his face with his tongue sticking out, and in a world where Lego doesn’t always nail builds where you recreate beloved characters in Lego form, it seems to have nailed the assignment.

Industry observers note that it’s reasonably priced too at just $54.99. There’s a Pokemon set out there that costs $650, and Lego’s latest Death Star carries a $1,000 price tag, so Rivals fans can count themselves lucky that this first set isn’t going to leave them wondering whether they need to trade in whatever they use to play Rivals just to be able to afford it. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Industry observers note that you don’t have long to wait for the set’s release either, which likely means an official reveal is coming soon. The Jeff Lego set will be released on January 1, 2027, which tends to be the busiest day of the year for Lego. We already know about the seven new Star Wars sets releasing that day, and there will almost certainly be more announcements made between now and then. (Related: Industry News: Welcome spaCy to the Hugging Face Hub...)

The one thing that hasn’t been revealed via the Jeff set leak is how many pieces it will take to rebuild the Land Shark. We can make an educated guess based on the price of other Marvel sets, though. Taking similarly priced sets into account, it seems likely Lego Jeff will fall somewhere between 400 and 600 pieces.

In a recent development, there’s no guarantee that this is the only Marvel Rivals set we’ll be getting on January 1. It might simply be the only one to have leaked. As is the case with Rivals’ Funko Pops, Lego has an almost endless list of options from which to pull if it wants to make a series of Rivals Lego sets. Not just because of the game's growing roster, but because each of its heroes and villains also has several skins. Lego could focus on Jeff alone, releasing fresh sets where he’s sporting different looks, releasing one every few months, and be set for years.
