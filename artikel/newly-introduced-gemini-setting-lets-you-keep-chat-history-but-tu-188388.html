Google is rolling out new privacy settings for the Gemini app that will let you “keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training.”.

Upgrade 11/24: This new setting is now widely rolling out. On gemini.google.com, tap the settings gear icon to find a new “Gemini History” item at the very bottom. This opens myactivity.google.com/product/gemini with an On/Off dropdown. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

It has been revealed that under “Help train generative AI models with,” you’ll find the fresh “Chat content including text & attachments” setting. It’s enabled by default. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

Your future chats won’t be used to train generative AI models, unless you provide feedback.

Training on “Audio and Gemini Live videos & screenshares” remains an opt-in preference.

Original 11/16: Gemini’s existing Keep Activity setting “controls how long your chats are saved and whether they are used to train generative AI models.”.

Google is now separating that preference into two new options.

This is huge. One of the major things Siri-AI users on iPhone like to point out is that while their assistant is a less capable portmanteau of Siri and Gemini, their queries are not used for AI training. Gemini on Android, on the other hand, offers the superior AI platform with the caveat that there is no way to opt out of training and still retain your chat history. Until now. Now users of Gemini on Android get the best of both worlds - the best of Google's AI models powering their AI experiences and their chat history siloed from AI training.

The first setting is called “History” and lets you “control how long your history is saved.” If disabled:.

“…future chats will be saved for around 2 months and used to respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its consumers, and the public, with help from human reviewers.”.

The duration is at the moment 24 hours for chat context, and 72 hours to help protect Google, consumers, and the public.

The second setting allows you to “separately control whether your chat content, including text and attachments, is used to train generative AI models.”.

With this change, you will be able to keep your history while independently controlling generative AI model training.

If Keep Activity is currently off, the two “new settings will both be off.” These changes are rolling out over the “coming weeks,” and Google is notifying people via email.
