A Home Alone sequel is reportedly in development with original star Macaulay Culkin set to return.

According to Puck’s Matthew Belloni, Culkin met with Disney executives earlier this year with a pitch for a follow-up and now the project has been met with a green light. The new Home Alone film will bring the 46-year-old back to the franchise as Kevin McCallister for a comedy that, according to Deadline, will be from the father’s point of view. The film will also recruit Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg as writers and producers and they will work with Culkin to craft the script. Rogen and Goldberg recently worked with Disney on a Muppets TV special.

Home Alone was a major box office hit upon release in 1990, grossing $476m worldwide and becoming the year’s second-biggest movie. Culkin returned for the sequel Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, which made $359m two years later. (Related: Latest Update: Two weeks later, Senate vote count is co...)

The franchise continued without the actor for three further movies and then in 2021, after Fox had been bought by Disney, there was a straight-to-streaming reboot.

Culkin later reprised the role in a 2018 commercial for Google and this year has once again returned as Kevin for a new McDonald’s advert.

Last year, he was asked about a sequel at an anniversary event.

“I kind of had this idea,” he said. “I’m either a widower or a divorcee. I’m raising a kid and all that stuff. I’m working really hard and I’m not really paying enough attention and the kid is kind of getting miffed at me and then I get locked out.”.

In his pitch, the character’s son won’t let him in and would be the one setting the traps with the house as “some sort of metaphor for our relationship”.

The original film’s director, Chris Columbus, who later went on to direct the first two Harry Potter adventures, has spoken out about a possible sequel. “I think it’s a mistake to try to go back and recapture something we did 35 years ago,” he noted. “I think it should be left alone.”.
