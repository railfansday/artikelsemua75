Breaking: Trump tells CNBC ‘I made the deal’ to reinstate Nvidia’s Jensen Huang

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

President Donald Trump told CNBC on Monday morning that he spoke directly to Nvidia's board and urged them to reinstate CEO Jensen Huang.

"I spoke to them, let me put it that way," Trump told CNBC's Eamon Javers in a phone interview shortly after Nvidia's shock announcement that Huang was returning to lead the tech giant.

"And I convinced them," the president stated. "I made the deal.".

It has been revealed that nvidia's decision to reinstate Huang came less than two weeks after he resigned. Huang had stepped down after admitting he cheated on his wife and amid claims his affairs included one with an alleged Chinese spy and two Nvidia employees.

Huang's relationship with the suspected spy, Alice Huang, and her role as an executive at the Singapore-based manufacturer Megaspeed raised concerns that she was facilitating the diversion of Nvidia's most advanced semiconductor chip to China. Trump said Nvidia's board "did ask for assurance on the national security threat," Trump said.

"And we gave them assurances at least with everything we know, and we know a lot," the president said.

Trump noted that after he spoke with Nvidia's board, "when the deal was finished," he called Huang.

In a recent development, "I called him up, and I said: 'I ask you to do one thing, Jensen. Work hard,'" Trump said.

"I didn't talk about other things because he understands that. He understands it better than we do," the president said. "He knows what to do and what not to do. But I just said work hard because if you work hard, there's nobody that can capture what you've done. Nobody, nobody close.".

Trump also told CNBC that Huang "did nothing wrong.".

"I think the [Nvidia] board acted really responsibly" in deciding to bring Huang back.

"And we gave them assurances at least with everything we know, and we know a lot," Trump said.

"I'm very, very happy that the tech giant had the courage and the board had the courage to do the right thing, and they suffered for two weeks, and the company suffered greatly," the president noted.

Asked whether the broad stock market selloff last week after Huang's resignation from Nvidia factored into his decision to push for the CEO's reinstatement, Trump stated, "Probably so, but the bigger impact was the long term.".

"The bigger impact was, you know, he's got other chips in his brain that nobody else knows about," Trump said.
