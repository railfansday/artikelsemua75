Sporting head coach Paulo Fonseca believes predicting Manchester United's tactical approach will be difficult for their Champions League clash in Lisbon, though he anticipates a highly "motivated" display from Mauricio Pochettino’s team. The English side travel to Portugal after suffering a 4-0 Premier League defeat against Liverpool on Sunday, a fixture that saw defender Leny Yoro red-carded early on, leaving fresh uncertainty surrounding the club following Pochettino’s opening game at the helm. Fonseca, who stepped into the Sporting role on 4 November after departing Lyon to succeed Rui Borges, maintains that United will present a significant challenge to his team's European ambitions at the Estadio Jose Alvalade.

"It is hard to get the measure of this Manchester side," remarked Fonseca, who has achieved victories in both of his matches so far, overcoming Moreirense in domestic league action and Uniao da Serra in the Taca de Portugal.

"They changed coach and with the sending-off after eight minutes in (against Liverpool), it became impossible to carry out any kind of analysis.".

Speaking at a press conference, Fonseca stated: "It is hard to predict, but this will be a very strong Manchester team, featuring very strong players. We expect a motivated opponent under a latest manager.".

Ahead of the next round of fixtures, Sporting sit 10th in the Champions League standings on nine points, having secured wins over Galatasaray, Lens and Shakhtar Donetsk in their previous European match.

"Obviously, United are a team packed with top-class talent, and Bruno (Fernandes) stands out, but we don’t prepare for a match based on a single player," Fonseca added.

"We prepare based on the team as a whole and we have tried to prepare for various scenarios.".

United have only won three matches all season, beating Ipswich in the Premier League, while overcoming Sabah and Roma in the Champions League.

They will be hoping to bounce back from their Anfield drubbing, and did receive some good news on Tuesday as it was confirmed that Yoro’s one-match ban has been overturned following his red card.

The French defender was deemed to have denied a clear and obvious goalscoring opportunity when he brought down Alexander Isak, but United’s appeal against the decision has proved successful.
