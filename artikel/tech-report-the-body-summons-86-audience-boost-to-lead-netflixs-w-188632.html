It has been revealed that the Body is summoning some interest on Netflix. The YA drama tallied 9.5 million views from November 16 to 22, taking No. 1 on the English TV list for its first full week on the streamer.

It has been revealed that that’s an 86% increase from the 5.1 million views it managed in its first few days of availability after premiering on November 11. In The Body, a group of badly-behaved Catholic school girls begin having prophetic visions that set off mass hysteria in town after a dance-team initiation goes wrong on Halloween night in 2004.

According to recent reports, its premiere sparked some controversy when a petition emerged criticizing the “blasphemous anti-Christian series,” but, if anything, that buzz seems to have only led to more intrigue and a bigger audience.
