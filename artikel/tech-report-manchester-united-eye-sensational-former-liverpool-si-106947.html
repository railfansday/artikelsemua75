With the Glazers interested in the appointment, such a move would be a major shift from the current arrangement at Old Trafford, where Sir Jim Ratcliffe and INEOS are at the moment responsible for overseeing the football department.

Edwards is regarded as one of the most influential executives in modern English football, having played a major role in building Liverpool’s squad under Jurgen Klopp.

According to recent reports, michael Edwards joined Liverpool in 2011 before being appointed as their sporting director in November 2016. He oversaw player recruitment bring in some of the biggest names at Liverpool including Mo Salah and Sadio Mane. Liverpool saw one of their most successful years in modern history in the Premier League and Europe under Jurgen Klopp while Edwards oversaw the footballing operations.

Manchester United have previously attempted to bring Edwards to Old Trafford before, but were unsuccessful.

In a recent development, following his departure from Liverpool in 2022, Edwards turned down approaches from several clubs, including Manchester United and Chelsea. He then returned to Fenway Sports Group in March 2024 to oversee FSG’s wider football operations.

His second spell with FSG came to an end in July 2026. That exit has reopened the door for many interested clubs including Manchester United. However, there is no confirmation that Edwards is considering another move to United.
