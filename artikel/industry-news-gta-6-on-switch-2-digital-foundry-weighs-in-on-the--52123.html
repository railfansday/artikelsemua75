According to recent reports, could Grand Theft Auto 6 come to Switch 2? That has been the substantial question ever since Nintendo officially revealed its console last year, and it's something that we have mused over ever since (as unlikely as it may seem at an initial glance). Now that the game is out, the tech experts over at Digital Foundry have weighed in on the port possibility, and, perhaps unsurprisingly, they don't rate its chances.

In the outlet's latest rundown, it touches on the "compromised" Xbox Series S performance. It isn't all that, with DF noting "frame-rates routinely lingering in the mid to high 20s during ordinary traversal and combat," but it's the system that regularly lands the most comparisons to Switch 2, so DF states that it would be "interesting" to see if it would be "possible for a further cut back version to ship on Nintendo's hybrid console". (Related: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to transformer...)

However, the experts point out that a Switch 2 port "feels extremely unlikely at this stage".

"Unless there are massive changes made," DF's John Linneman claims around the 53:24 mark in the above video, "I don't see how this could possibly work on Switch 2".

Recent findings suggest that the main reasoning seems to mostly come down to the sheer size of the damn thing. This is a game that is pushing even the highest-end consoles to their limit — "the huge CPU burden that restricts GTA 6 to 30fps on even PS5 Pro" — so how would the comparatively little guy Switch 2 ever stand a chance? (Related: Industry News: The Reformer - Pushing the limits of lan...)

Sources indicate that now, we're never willing to rule anything out completely. Switch 1 got its fair share of 'impossible ports' in its lifetime, and Switch 2 has already landed a handful of beefy boys that we previously never could have imagined making the jump intact. However, this one might be a step too far. Please do prove us wrong, Rockstar!

It has been revealed that it's a shame, because by all accounts, the game is fantastic. Our sister sites Push Square and Pure Xbox both gave it 10/10 reviews, praising it as one of the greatest open worlds of all time. It shot right to the top of Metacritic's ratings, too, tying with Ocarina of Time as the best of the best.
