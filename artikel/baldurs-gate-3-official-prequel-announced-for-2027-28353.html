The world of Baldur’s Gate 3 is expanding beyond video games once again, with a latest official prequel novel set to explore the darker corners of the Forgotten Realms. The upcoming book will take readers beneath the streets of Baldur’s Gate, where a murder threatens to expose the secrets of a secretive religious order.

Titled Baldur’s Gate 3: Shadowheart, the upcoming fantasy novel comes from Tasha Suri, the World Fantasy Award-winning author of the Burning Kingdoms trilogy. It is scheduled to arrive on June 8, 2027, and is available to preorder now at major retailers.

As the title suggests, the story centers on Shadowheart, the cleric of Shar who became one of the most popular companions in Larian Studios' hit RPG.

In a recent development, according to the official book description, Shadowheart is a devoted follower of the goddess of darkness and loss. Living beneath Baldur’s Gate in a secretive underground church, she has embraced the belief that sacrificing memories is the key to gaining strength.

Sources indicate that however, when a member of her religious order is murdered in the city's wealthy Upper City, Shadowheart is tasked with investigating alongside several fellow acolytes. Among them are an ambitious Griefguard named Mirie, a cynical propagandist named Caldan, and Nocturne, a charming quartermaster whose intentions Shadowheart has reason to question.The investigation soon reveals that Baldur’s Gate's wealthiest district has plenty of secrets of its own. As Shadowheart encounters unfamiliar temptations and begins questioning the comforts of her faith, she must solve the murder before her devotion is called into question.
