Harry Potter fans are counting down the days for HBO's highly anticipated upcoming adaptation, which will finally bring back the iconic fantasy story after more than a decade since the original film series concluded. It's difficult to overstate the cultural impact of J.K. Rowling's original novels, which have sold hundreds of millions of copies worldwide since the publication of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone in 1997.

For nearly 30 years, generations of readers have grown up alongside Harry, Ron, and Hermione, with the books inspiring countless young readers to fall in love with fantasy literature. While there's plenty to look forward to with HBO's upcoming adaptation, there's also something special about discovering an entirely new magical world for the first time.

Anyone looking to recapture that feeling of reading Harry Potter for the first time will want to keep an eye out for an exciting new fantasy adventure from author Thomas Taylor.

Recent findings suggest that thomas Taylor's The Lantern and the Labyrinth is slated for release on April 8, 2027, through Penguin Random House's Puffin imprint in the UK. The 400-page novel marks the beginning of an entirely new fantasy series titled The Faerwell Lantern, with hardcover, ebook, and audiobook editions already listed for pre-order.

According to the official book description, Taylor's upcoming novel explores "magic, courage and the boundless possibilities of the imagination." The story follows a young protagonist named Tom who inadvertently discovers the boundary separating the ordinary world from Faery, a magical realm inhabited by supernatural creatures.

[The Lantern and the Labyrinth is] a dangerous magical world full of trolls, ghosts, dragons, and things from the darkest corners of the imagination.

Tom becomes an apprentice to a mysterious organization known as the Esotericals, whose intentions are called into question after he befriends a young witch named Persistence and her magical companion, Mortimer. Meanwhile, a terrifying entity known as the Smothering Dark threatens both worlds. The publisher describes the adventure as "rich, epic, immersive and exciting.".

Taylor has also confirmed that the novel is part of a three-book publishing deal with Puffin Press, revealing in his May 2026 newsletter that he was already editing the sequel, tentatively titled The Book of Everafter.

According to recent reports, taylor is perhaps best known for his award-winning Eerie-on-Sea series, which debuted in 2019 with Malamander and spanned five novels, concluding with Mermedusa in 2023. The series has enjoyed considerable international success, selling over half a million copies worldwide and being translated into 20 languages. Taylor has also written and illustrated numerous children's picture books and provided illustrations for Marcus Sedgwick's graphic novel Scarlett Hart: Monster Hunter.

In a recent development, interestingly, Taylor actually has a significant connection to the Harry Potter franchise itself. His very first professional illustration commission after graduating from art school was the now-iconic cover artwork for the original British edition of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone in 1997.

According to recent reports, nearly three decades later, the artist responsible for many readers' very first visual glimpse of Harry Potter is embarking on an ambitious upcoming magical adventure of his own. With HBO's adaptation set to introduce another generation to the Wizarding World, 2027 is shaping up to be an exciting year for fantasy fans, and The Lantern and the Labyrinth is certainly worth keeping on your radar.
