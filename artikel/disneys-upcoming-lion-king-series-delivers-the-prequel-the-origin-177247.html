It has been revealed that disney’s The Lion King (1994) will always be regarded as one of the studio’s greatest movies, having achieved legendary status among fans. Its popularity spawned several sequels and prequels, with the photorealistically animated Mufasa: The Lion King (2024) being the latest installment in the franchise’s filmography. The prequel explores Mufasa’s origins and his relationship with Scar, but it received mixed reviews and failed to capture the same enthusiasm fans have for the original movie. Now, however, a new Lion King series is in full swing, finally delivering the prequel the original movie deserved.

One of the biggest criticisms fans had of The Lion King (2019), the remake of the original movie, and its prequel, Mufasa: The Lion King, was the use of photorealistic CGI in place of the classic animation style. If this was one of your biggest gripes with the 2019 and 2024 films, fear not: the new The Lion King series returns to the franchise's traditional cartoon medium.

Sources indicate that dynamite, in partnership with Disney, launched The Lion King #1 in November 2025, marking the first concern in a return to the franchise's golden era. The series explores new adventures between Mufasa and a young Simba, set before Scar's rise to power and eventual betrayal. By revisiting this peaceful period in the Pride Lands, the story further develops the bond between father and son, which is ultimately what the original movie is all about. Dynamite's The Lion King is currently an ongoing series, with seven concerns already available and the eighth set to drop tomorrow, November 25, 2026. With seven issues now in circulation, reader reviews are also rolling in, and fans have had nothing but praise for the series. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

Written and illustrated by the exceptionally talented Edwin Galmon, The Lion King brings back Mufasa and Simba for their next adventure as the series follows “the ruler of the Pride Lands and his young heir as they navigate the complex network of relationships that make up their kingdom — and learn firsthand how to defend it against the forces that threaten it both from the outside and from within.” However, it isn't just Simba and Mufasa returning for this series. Fans can also expect other beloved characters to get their fair share of page time, including Timon and Pumbaa, Rafiki, and more.

According to recent reports, what truly makes this such an exceptional prequel is how it feels like a return to the first act of The Lion King, when the Pride Lands were at peace, and Simba's family was whole. By exploring this golden era, Galmon firmly anchors the series in the continuity fans associate with the original movie, giving readers what feels like a missing scene from the film.

Sources indicate that reviews for the comic series also lean toward greatness, with comic rating sites such as League of Comic Geeks giving the series a "Very Positive" rating and an average of around 4.1 out of 5 stars. The fact that there is also no end in sight for the series speaks to its reception, with Dynamite showing that it is committed to delivering more of these pre-movie tales.

Long-time fans will feel right at home in this story, as Galmon masterfully avoids disrupting established canon across the various story arcs while still fleshing out the story fans know and love. His artwork is also fantastic, with the series featuring stunning covers and variants by Galmon, Meghan Hetrick, Jacob Edgar, and Giulia Lomurno.

So, if you're looking to return to the familiarity of the original movie but want upcoming adventures instead of rewatching the film for the nth time, add Dynamite's The Lion King to your TBR.

The Lion King #8 from Dynamite will be available to read on November 25, 2026!
