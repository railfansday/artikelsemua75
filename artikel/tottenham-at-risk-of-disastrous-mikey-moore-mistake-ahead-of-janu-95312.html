Tottenham Hotspur manager Roberto De Zerbi has no room to fit young winger Mikey Moore into his squad for the second half of the season.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 19-year-old wouldn't get enough game time if he returned to Spurs.

This comes amid recent suggestions that the Lilywhites could potentially recall Moore from his loan spell at Koln in January. (Related: Large Language Models: A Fresh Moore's Law?...)

However, Wyness believes that a fresh loan spell would be the only option for Spurs if they did recall Moore, but added that they cannot afford to make another wrong move.

The starlet spent last season on loan at Rangers, but displayed inconsistent form across the majority of the season.

His time in the Bundesliga simply hasn't worked out so far, with Moore starting just five league games so far and still waiting for his first goal involvement.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Tottenham simply cannot afford to find a fresh loan for Moore and have him struggle there as well.

The boardroom expert outlined how that scenario would be "disastrous" in terms of Moore's valuation and future as a player.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained that Spurs must tread very carefully with Moore right now.

"Well, they've made two mistakes on loans so far where it hasn't gone right, and a third one could be pretty disastrous for the valuation of the kid and also for his own career, so they've got to get it right," he said.

"We've been talking about Spurs week after week, how they haven't got it right in terms of the pathway for young players. So I don't really see De Zerbi having the time or the ability to fit him into the squad right now.

"They'd be stuck with another loan situation (if Moore was recalled). They've got to decide where that would be, and they've got to get it right. Maybe he's not travelling well as a young kid in Germany, which is understandable perhaps.

"They've got to get this decision right, and if they do decide to recall him, they've got to give him minutes; that's the whole thing.".

Former Tottenham scout Bryan King told Football Insider on Friday that his former club would have been better off sending Moore out on loan to a Championship club this season.

The ex-recruitment man believes that it would have been a far easier adaptation for the youngster to have stayed within the English football pyramid. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

King pointed out that Moore's family are Millwall supporters, and added that The Den could have been the ideal place for his latest loan spell.

King feels that Spurs may live to regret sending Moore to the Bundesliga, rather than somewhere closer to home.
