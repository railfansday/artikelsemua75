Top of the Ticker: After a nearly 20-year run, Willie Geist will be departing MS NOW for an expanded role at NBC News. Geist will continue to focus his efforts on Sunday Morning and broaden his presence across Today and other NBC News platforms.

In announcing his departure on Monday, during Morning Joe, Geist remarked, “I am so grateful to both NBC and MSNOW for the unique opportunity to do what I love at both networks. He added, “This is a bittersweet goodbye to Joe, Mika, and the place that made possible everything else in my career over the last two decades, but I am thrilled to settle in with my family at Today and dedicate all of my time to that special place.”.
