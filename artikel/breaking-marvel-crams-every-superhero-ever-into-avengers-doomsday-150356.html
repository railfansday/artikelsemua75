Sources indicate that rumors have been swirling for days that we would be getting a final trailer for Avengers: Doomsday, the penultimate film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe’s Phase Six, heralding the end of the so-called “Multiverse Saga.” The rumors were true: Marvel just released the trailer, featuring pretty much every superhero ever represented in the MCU. And it looks like the Avengers will face consequences for messing with the timeline to reverse the blip in Endgame.

As previously reported, the film takes place 14 months after the events of Thunderbolts*. Per the official logline: “Beloved heroes from three distinct universes will be set on a deadly collision course and face an existential threat unlike anything they’ve ever encountered.” Those universes are Earth-616 (the Avengers, Wakandans, and New Avengers), Earth-828 (Fantastic Four), and the “original” X-Men.

Cast-wise, we have Avengers favorites Thor (Chris Hemsworth), the upcoming Captain America (Anthony Mackie), Bucky Barnes (Sebastian Stan), Ant-Man (Paul Rudd), Falcon (Danny Ramirez), and Loki (Tom Hiddleston). Then there’s the Wakandan contingent: Shuri as the new Black Panther (Letitia Wright), M’Baku (Winston Duke), and Namor (Tenoch Huerta Mejia), as well as Alex Livinalli as Namor’s ally, Attuma. And, of course, Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom.

We also have the Thunderbolts* (aka New Avengers): John Walker/US Agent (Wyatt Russell), Yelena Belova (Florence Pugh), Bob/Sentry (Lewis Pullman), Red Guardian (David Harbour), and Ghost (Hannah John-Kamen). And the Fantastic Four: Reed Richards (Pedro Pascal), Sue Storm (Vanessa Kirby), Ben Grimm (Ebon Moss-Bachrach), and Johnny Storm (Joseph Quinn). We’ll also see Charles Xavier (Patrick Stewart), Beast (Kelsey Grammer), Magneto (Ian McKellen), Mystique (Rebecca Romijn), Nightcrawler (Alan Cumming), and Cyclops (James Marsden). For good measure, Marvel threw in Gambit (Channing Tatum) and Xu Shang-Chi (Simu Liu). There will be plenty of cameos, like Steve Rogers (Chris Evans), revealed in one of four sneak teasers last year. We can also expect to see (at least briefly) Peggy Carter (Hayley Atwell), Spider-Man (Tom Holland), Hawkeye (Jeremy Renner), Doctor Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch), Thor’s adopted daughter, Love (India Rose Hemsworth), and Scott Lang’s daughter, Cassie (Kathryn Newton).

The first trailer dropped in July, just before San Diego Comic-Con, and it just consisted of a heck of a lot of cameos, as the superheroes from the three universes met each other over an ominous voiceover by Thor, warning of an unprecedented threat. It ended with the return of Steve Rogers—and guess what, he’s still worthy of Thor’s hammer, Mjölnir. The following month, Marvel dropped another trailer during its D23 fan event, hinting at Doctor Doom’s tragic backstory. (Related: Taco Bell is bringing back the Steakhouse Boss Burrito ...)

Sources indicate that rogers features prominently in the new trailer, which opens with him and Peggy discussing how they made the right choice, but Steve still feels bad about hiding this truth from their son, who is now a young teen. We knew he had opted to stay in the past and build a life with Peggy at the end of Endgame; what we didn’t know is that they did this in the same alternate universe as the Fantastic Four, who show up to recruit him to the latest cause.

“Years ago, we did what was needed to make the world right,” Rogers continues in a voiceover. “But we didn’t know those choices would have consequences.” Whatever the roots of Doom’s grudge against the Avengers, he believes “all of you have lived stolen lives,” adding an ominous: “But now you must give them back.”.

According to recent reports, the rest is largely footage we’ve seen before, with Thor admitting, “I don’t even know who half of you are” to the assembled superheroes, before making his call for them to “stand together” against Doom. Among the upcoming footage is Marsden’s Cyclops facing off against Doom and several Sentinels: He calls, “To me, my X-Men,” except it’s everyone who responds. We also see Steve and Thor bantering about how Steve wielding Mjölnir bothers the Norse god more than he cares to admit. There’s no sign of Wanda Maximoff/The Scarlet Witch, whom Doom revived in a post-credits scene at the end of VisionQuest. She might be among the trio of Latverian witches that briefly appear in the trailer.

Verdict: We don’t know if Doomsday will measure up to Infinity War and End Game, but it’s bound to provide a spectacle.

It has been revealed that avengers: Doomsday hits theaters on December 18, 2026. It will battle it out against Dune: Part III, which also releases that same day. Avengers: Secret Wars is currently slated for release on December 17, 2027, and will mark the conclusion of the MCU’s Phase Six.
