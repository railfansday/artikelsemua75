The 18-year-old wonderkid Luca Williams-Barnett inspired Tottenham to a fine 2-0 defeat of Ipswich on Saturday, but the occasion was marred by a fresh injury to Savio. (Related: How to train a fresh language model from scratch using ...)

The Brazilian winger was returning from a month-long lay-off as he came off the bench in the second half, but he lasted just 25 minutes before being forced off again, in a moment Roberto De Zerbi called “the worst thing from today.”.

His injury came hot on the heels of Sandro Tonali’s surprise absence from the matchday squad.

De Zerbi, though, will hope to welcome a number of fresh faces back into the squad before Sunday’s trip to Wearside, with a full week off between matches.

With that in mind, here is all the latest Tottenham injury news and potential return dates...

Making his first Tottenham appearance in over a month against Ipswich, Savio was unfortunate to suffer yet another injury.

He came off the bench late on, but pulled up after just 25 minutes and had to hobble off.

In a recent development, speaking at full-time, De Zerbi stated: "It is the same injury. And it is the worst thing from today.".

It remains to be seen how long he will be sidelined.

Sandro Tonali was a surprise absentee from the team to face Ipswich, with De Zerbi confirming ahead of kick-off that he is ill.

Sources indicate that the Italian confirmed that his countryman “came back (from international duty) not in the right condition. (Sandro) Tonali was sick for a couple of days.”.

Tonali was back in training on Tuesday having seemingly recovered from his illness.

Potential return date: Sunday, November 29 vs Sunderland (A).

Mathys Tel is facing up to three weeks on the sidelines with an Achilles injury.

He was on the receiving end of a crunching tackle from Jayden Bogle before the international break and left Elland Road in a protective boot before withdrawing from international duty.

Speaking before the Ipswich match, De Zerbi said of Tel: “Mathys Tel we need another two, three weeks.”.

He will be pushing to make a comeback in the north London derby.

Potential return date: Sunday, December 6 vs Arsenal (H).

Speaking ahead of the match, he explained the ex-Chelsea centre-back needs more time to build his fitness back up before he will be ready to return to the pitch.

“Tosin is already okay, but it is not the right moment to take any risks,” he stated.

He was absent from full first-team training on Tuesday, though his involvement this weekend has not yet been ruled out.

Potential return date: Sunday, November 29 vs Sunderland (A).

Mykhailo Mudryk has been all over the back pages during the international break after he revealed a surprise affection for Arsenal in an ill-advised interview.

But his wait to return to the pitch and win over the hearts of Spurs fans goes on.

His loan spell has so far yielded just one appearance, after which he suffered an ankle injury in training. He was thought to be closing in on a return, but De Zerbi confirmed on Friday he is still a fortnight away from returning.

“[The] plan was to play Ipswich or Sunderland, and then he felt no pain, but not comfortable last week. They take two weeks more,” he mentioned. “Step by step.”.

That could see him passed fit in time to face his beloved Arsenal in the north London derby.

Potential return date: Sunday, December 6 vs Arsenal (H).

Industry observers note that xavi Simons is still rehabilitating after rupturing his ACL in April. He is mentioned to be progressing well in his recovery and has shared footage of himself working on the grass, but a return to team training does not appear to be imminent.

Potential return date: December.

As with Simons, Wilson Odobert is at the moment working to recover from a torn ACL, sustained in February.

It is hoped he can return in the coming weeks.

Potential return date: December.
