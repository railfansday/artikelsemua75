A modder using AI to create The Last of Us Part 2 multiplayer that Naughty Dog never gave us notes Sony can't do much even if it wanted to shut it down.

According to recent reports, earlier this year, Sony put the brakes on one such mod. YouTuber Speclizer unveiled their mod at the start of this year, but two months ago, they announced it was canceled after they received a cease and desist from Sony Interactive Entertainment. However, another has emerged in the form of Twelve Weeks (via IGN), with the developer releasing a mod earlier this week featuring 4v4 team battles.

The creator, Kellie, admitted on Twitter that AI was used in its development, saying, "Claude code was used for most of the project." However, they note that no AI artwork has been used, with the dev explaining she made the artwork herself.

Over on a YouTube video for the mod, the developer's account claims that Speclizer's mod was subject to a cease and desist because it was paywalled, but "mine is released already for free and in open source." However, even if the devs do receive a letter from Sony, it's claimed that "since people have the source code to the full mod already, there ain't much they can do even if they do shut me down.".
