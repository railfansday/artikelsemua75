Ultras of Lazio’s Curva Nord have unveiled that they will temporarily lift their Stadio Olimpico boycott for their Serie A clash at home against Atalanta on December 6, but not all supporters agree with the decision.

It has been revealed that lazio supporters have been boycotting their home matches since the beginning of the calendar year in protest of owner Claudio Lotito. Fans are demanding that Lotito sell the club and had promised that they would not return to the stadium, aside from derby fixtures, until he had done so. Despite home season tickets plummeting to around 3,000 season and away supporters regularly outnumbering home fans in the Stadio Olimpico in 2026-27, Lazio have surged to the top of the Serie A table and remain unbeaten after the first 12 league matches of the season.

Now, the Curva Nord has declared that they intend to return to the Stadio Olimpico as a one-off for Lazio’s home match against Atalanta on December 6. They are also set to be in the stands for their home match against rivals Roma the following weekend, on December 13, as the boycott did not originally apply to derby matches.

Lazio fans are also expected to attend the Supercoppa Italiana final between Inter and Lazio in San Siro on December 22. (Related: Hawaiian Interisland Awards — Hop Between Islands...)

“For the month of December, we have decided to treat ourselves to a full month of Lazio,” read a statement from the Curva Nord on Tuesday.

“A year ago, this would have been the norm for the Biancocelesti supporters, but unfortunately, a tyrannical President and his host of Giallorossi fans have forced us into this act of love, which has taken us away from our home.”.

The statement continued: “The leadership of this club has done absolutely nothing to bring the fans closer again, continuing to go in the complete opposite direction and the people of Lazio have continued to choose which side to take.

“A few exceptions have stood out, and among them there is definitely the coach, Gattuso, who has repeatedly reiterated that he understands and respects our decision, but expresses his ‘dream’ of seeing the Olimpico full of his people.

“At every away game he and the team have come and stood under the away section to receive the well-deserved applause, and we know that they are with us.

“For him, for the team, but most importantly for the youngsters and fans who have joined the protest but couldn’t get tickets for the derby, and for all the Lazio fans who suffer while being away from Lazio, we’ve decided to enter the stadium for the Lazio-Atalanta match on December 6.

“This way, we will also fire up the lads ahead of the city derby, giving them a taste of the atmosphere they will find on  December 13, showing them that we are always by their side, because they deserve it.”.

Not all Lazio supporters agree with this decision, though.

A number of comments have been left in response to the statement, which was posted on Instagram. One fan wrote: “He [Lotito] has won, as usual. As far as I’m concerned, I will only be back when I hear an apology, or when the significant news is announced.”.

Another added: “I’m sorry, but this time I don’t agree and I won’t come. Only for the derby as was agreed.”.

Sources indicate that another agreed: “I’ll stay consistent, I won’t go.” Another fan commented: “History has been saying the same thing for 20 years. Intermittent protests depending on results. Once again, he has won.”.
