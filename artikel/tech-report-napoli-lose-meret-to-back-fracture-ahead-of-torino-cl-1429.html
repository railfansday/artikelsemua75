Napoli’s injury crisis has worsened, with goalkeeper Alex Meret suffering a fracture to his fifth lumbar vertebra in training.

According to an official club statement, via TMW, Meret was hurt during a training session and underwent tests at Pineta Grande Hospital, which confirmed the fracture. In a recent development, it’s another setback for Massimiliano Allegri, who already has a long list of absentees. Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Leonardo Spinazzola, Stanislav Lobotka, Benoit Badiashile, David Neres and Alessandro Buongiorno were all missing for the 1-1 draw with Lazio before the international break.

Sources indicate that vanja Milinković-Savić started in goal that day and impressed, with several vital saves to keep Napoli in the game. The Serbian now looks set to stay in the side.

Recent findings suggest that there was some better news for the Partenopei, as all their internationals have returned to Castel Volturno. Allegri’s squad trained on Thursday afternoon ahead of Saturday’s game against Torino at the Stadio Diego Armando Maradona, which kicks off at 20:45 CET [19:45 GMT].
