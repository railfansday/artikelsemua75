In a recent development, A claim that Nvidia is about to buy $2.5 billion worth of XRP has spread across X, but there is no evidence to support it. The rumour came from a pro-XRP account on November 23, which noted the purchase could happen within a day and that tokenised Nvidia shares were already trading on the XRP Ledger. The post cited no source, and the account later described the claim as huge only if it turned out to be true. Nvidia has made no statement about XRP, and there is no public record linking the chipmaker to any crypto purchase. Most responses were sceptical. People called the post fake news, said the community has seen similar hype for a decade, and questioned how many people such claims could mislead.
