There was every reason to expect a cracking game – the Barrowmen had won 4–1 at Hastings United the previous weekend in front of more than 1,000 fans, while visitors Punjab United were unbeaten in seven league games.

The floodlights came on just ten minutes into a cold Saturday afternoon, and a crowd of 125 saw Punjab race into a four-goal lead inside 34 minutes. Steyning pulled two back after the break, but the visitors held on for a 4–2 win.

A couple of minutes later, Edwards turned provider, picking out Roman Campbell, whose bullet header flew past Alfie Mansell to make it 2–0.

Things went from bad to worse for the Barrowmen before the half-hour. Campbell pounced on a mistake by Marcus Allen and squared for Jack Hopkins, who rolled the ball into an empty net for Punjab’s third.

In a recent development, steyning were forced into a change when Max Blencowe went off injured after a late studs-up tackle. The Punjab defender was shown a yellow card, while some thought the challenge worthy of a red. Bright Kuntu-Blankson came on to replace Blencowe.

Sources indicate that A couple of minutes later, Punjab had their fourth. Sam Wood swung in a corner in the 34th minute, and the ball somehow found its way into the net.

The Barrowmen had a couple of half-chances to pull one back before the break.

With his side four down, boss Brannon O’Neil had a big half-time team talk on his hands. And Town came out with more energy after the break and created several chances, but Punjab held firm.

Midway through the half, Jesse Sentamu came off the bench to score for the second week running, pulling one back with his third goal of the season.

Steyning kept up the pressure and Jared Rance added a second in the 90th minute. But The first-half damage proved too much to undo, and Punjab left the Shooting Field with all three points.

Man of the match: Roman Campbell.

Punjab’s win extended their unbeaten league run to eight matches and lifted them to ninth in the table while the Barrowmen dropped to 15th.

According to recent reports, steyning return to cup action in midweek when they travel to Faversham Town in the third round of the Velocity Cup. Kick-off is at 7.45pm. They visit Margate on Saturday.
