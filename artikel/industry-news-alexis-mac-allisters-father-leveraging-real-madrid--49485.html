Liverpool midfielder Alexis Mac Allister is using the reported interest from Real Madrid as leverage to negotiate a latest contract.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 27-year-old is trying to get the best deal possible.

This comes after Mac Allister's father told ESPN in South America that his son would leave Anfield if nothing was done about his deal.

Wyness believes that Liverpool are concerned about losing another player to Real Madrid on a free transfer following Trent Alexander-Arnold's exit.

Mac Allister's current deal doesn't expire until 2028, and the club have opened talks over an extension.

The Argentine star's father is also his agent, and Wyness has no doubt that he is taking a clever stance amid the negotiations.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Mac Allister and his father will be pulling every trick in the book to try and get the best deal possible at Liverpool.

He suggested that the pair will be pointing towards the newly introduced deals for Ryan Gravenberch and Dominik Szoboszlai earlier this year to suggest that Mac Allister is deserving of a new contract.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how the father's comments are perfectly normal for contract negotiations.

"It's just normal contract negotiations. His father is speaking like an agent, and they will mention Real Madrid," he noted.

"They will also know that some of the other players at Liverpool have had contract renewals, and they'll want to make sure that he gets the same sort of deal.
