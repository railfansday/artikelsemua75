Boxer Tyson Fury is getting candid about his mental health.

In a new feature-length documentary, Tyson Fury, the multi-time champion opened up about being diagnosed with schizophrenia and bipolar disorder in 2015, shortly after his world heavyweight championship victory over Wladimir Klitschko.

According to Deadline, which previewed the documentary ahead of its Friday, November 27, release via Netflix, Fury, 38, said, “Words can’t explain the darkness I was in.”. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

According to recent reports, “I came from the living version of the pits of hellfire. And I 100% thought at times I’d end up in a padded room with the door locked, and that scared me so much,” he went on. (Related: Breaking: Living Trust vs Will: Complete Comparison Gui...)

Industry observers note that “I was waking up in the night like this … the anxiety was crippling me. I just could not overcome it,” Fury added. “Completely gone, and that’s when I went and saw the psychiatrist down in London. She asked me like a gazillion questions, and I ticked all the boxes, whatever was going on. Depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder.”.

The documentary also features interviews with Fury’s father, John Fury, and his wife, Paris Fury.

Sources indicate that “I can tell you this, when I look back, it’s always been there,” John said in the doc. “Me being who I am and no understanding of anything, I didn’t want to see a depressed kid — and if I did see it, I paid no mind to it. But I understand now. It’s an illness, a terrible illness. I suffer with it myself.”.

In the doc, Fury recalled how he decided to get help and turn his life around after a sobering moment over Halloween 2017.

According to recent reports, “I went out and I was embarrassed of how I looked. I was a fat mess and I had this massive costume on, a glow-in-the-dark skeleton suit and I thought no one would recognize me in this,” he stated. “And I looked around me and I could see all like young people, and I thought I’ve had the world in my hands and I threw it all away. And now, I’m just a fat mess, addicted to alcohol and ‘go home.’ I just said, ‘I can’t do this on my own anymore. I need your help.’ And that was the part, the comeback.”.

Fury previously opened up about being diagnosed with bipolar disorder in a 2016 interview with Rolling Stone.
