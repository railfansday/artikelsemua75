Blackpool have sacked manager Ian Evatt on his birthday, bringing to an end his 12-month reign.

The Seasiders opted to part ways with Evatt just hours after sending him a birthday message on social media. (Related: Is Social Security Taxable? How to Calculate What You O...)

In a post published at 9.08am on X, Blackpool wrote: “Happy Birthday to our Head Coach, Ian Evatt. Have a great day, gaffer!”.

However, on Thursday afternoon, Blackpool confirmed Evatt’s departure from the club.

A 4pm X post read: “Blackpool Football Club can announce that Ian Evatt has departed his role as Head Coach after 12 months in charge.”.

Recent findings suggest that A statement on the club’s website added: “During a hugely successful playing career at Bloomfield Road, Ian made over 250 appearances for the Seasiders. In his six years at Blackpool, he played a vital role in the Club’s memorable rise from League One to the Premier League, which included two successful play-off campaigns.

“The Club thanks Ian for all of his efforts during his time at Bloomfield Road. Steve Thompson will take charge of the team for this weekend’s fixture, along with Stephen Crainey, Stephen Dobbie and Steve Banks.”.

Former Blackpool defender Evatt, who took over from Steve Bruce in October 2025, leaves following a run of seven defeats in the last 10 Sky Bet League One games with the team 20th in the table.

Blackpool have taken 12 points from their opening 13 matches, and are only above the relegation zone on goal difference. (Related: Primary vs Contingent Beneficiary: What's the Diff...)

It has been a difficult season for the club who famously played in the Premier League in the 2010-11 season under Ian Holloway.

Blackpool took the relegation battle to the final day of the season, but were beaten by champions Manchester United as they finished on 39 points, one point adrift of safety.

Blackpool are due to return to action on Saturday when they host Wigan Athletic.
