Live Nation is promoting all three, with the Byron Bay show presented alongside Beach Hotel and the Sydney date with Fuzzy.

The Melbourne set falls on the tournament’s final day, with the pair joined by Sonique, TV Rock, Zoë Badwi and Kailyn Crabbe.

Benassi helped define electro-house with “Satisfaction,” produced in 2002 with his cousin Alle Benassi as the Benassi Bros. The track’s distorted bassline and mechanical groove became a template for a decade of festival dance music. He has remained a fixture on the circuit since, with sets at Coachella, Tomorrowland, EDC and Ultra.

His influence has twice been recognized by the Recording Academy, in both directions. Benassi won best remixed recording at the 2008 Grammys for his Sfaction remix of Public Enemy‘s “Bring the Noise.” Four years later, the same award went to Skrillex for his remix of Benassi’s “Cinema” — a record widely credited with helping break dubstep in North America.

Benassi’s U.S. chart high came with “Beautiful People,” his 2011 collaboration with Chris Brown, which topped Billboard’s Dance Club Songs chart and came close to the Hot 100’s top 40. Earlier, “Illusion” reached No. 4 on Hot Dance Club Play. He has also worked with John Legend and System of a Down’s Serj Tankian. It has been revealed that le Grand broke through with “Put Your Hands Up for Detroit,” which topped Billboard’s Global Dance Tracks chart in 2006 and spent 49 weeks on it. The Dutch producer, who arrived at a moment when house was widely seen as stagnating, followed with “Let Me Think About It,” his crossover with Ida Corr, along with “The Creeps,” “So Much Love” and “Sparks (Turn Off Your Mind).” He has played Tomorrowland, Ultra and Coachella.

Sonique, who joins them in Melbourne, reached No. 8 on the Billboard Hot 100 and No. 1 on Dance Club Songs in 2000 with “It Feels So Good.”.

AO Live runs across the tournament, which takes place Jan. 10-31 at Melbourne Park. RuPaul headlines the smaller TOPCOURT stage on Jan. 29, the Open’s Pride Day, in a DJ set, with the program split between Live Nation at John Cain Arena and Untitled Group at TOPCOURT.

In a recent development, A Live Nation presale for Byron Bay and Sydney runs from Thursday (Nov. 26) at 10 a.m. local time until Friday at 10 a.m., with general sale Friday at 11 a.m. local. Tickets for the Melbourne show are on sale now and include an Australian Open ground pass.
