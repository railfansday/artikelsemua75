Love Blooms Behind an Open Prison’s Missing Bars in Venice and Busan-Selected Director Nidhi Saxena’s ‘Azaad Nagar’ at WAVES Film Bazaar

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Nidhi Saxena, whose films have premiered at Busan and Venice, is presenting “Azaad Nagar” (Redemption Road), a Hindi-language love story set inside an Indian open prison, at the Co-Production Market at WAVES Film Bazaar.

The film centers on Hina, a Kashmiri refugee who is convicted of murdering her husband. At the open prison, where inmates are permitted to leave during the day to work in the city but must return by 6 p.m. for roll call, she finds support and companionship in fellow prisoner Dharam. The two fall in love, but Dharam is consumed by guilt over a crime he committed 15 years earlier. His attempts at redemption include obsessively donating blood. When Hina discovers his past, she struggles to forgive him, even as she hopes the world will forgive her. “In most prison films, we see stories of escape and punishment. But in this prison story, the driving force is love,” says Saxena, who co-wrote the screenplay with Ram Kumar Singh, a fellow Jaipur native familiar with the prison and its inmates.

Sources indicate that the idea has its roots in Saxena’s childhood. An open prison in the middle of her city first introduced her to the concepts of rehabilitation and reintegration. Later, reading Dostoevsky’s “Crime and Punishment” and Victor Hugo’s “Les Misérables” deepened her interest in the relationship between crime, punishment and human change. “I hope audiences leave asking what they would do in Hina’s place, and whether a sentence ever truly ends for the person who served it,” she says. (Related: Sentence Transformers in the Hugging Face Hub...)

Saxena, an alumna of the Film and Television Institute of India, made her feature debut with “Sad Letters of an Imaginary Woman,” which premiered at the Busan International Film Festival in 2024 and received the festival’s Asian Cinema Fund post-production support. Her second feature, “Secret of a Mountain Serpent,” premiered at the Venice Film Festival in 2025.

Industry observers note that “I didn’t even know about the concept of an open prison before. And on top of that, it was a love story between two prisoners,” indicates producer Naren Kumar of Mumbai-based Kyra Kumar Kreations, whose credits include co-producing the “Jolly LLB” films and producing the series “Maharani” (SonyLIV). “I was drawn to a love story that refuses easy forgiveness, to two characters asking what a person owes the world after paying for what they’ve done.”.

It has been revealed that the project is in development, with a budget of approximately INR45 million ($473,684). Around 25% of the producer’s equity is committed, with the balance being sought from co-producers and investors. The team is targeting a winter 2027 shoot and is also looking for post-production, sales and distribution partners, as well as international labs and funds.

WAVES Film Bazaar runs as the market component of the International Film Festival of India (IFFI) in Goa.
