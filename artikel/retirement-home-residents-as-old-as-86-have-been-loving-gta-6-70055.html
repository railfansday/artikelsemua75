A group of elderly residents at a retirement home in France have been introduced GTA 6, and some are already hoping to get their hands on the game for Christmas.

Rockstar Games’ highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto 6 finally introduced on November 19, 2026, more than 13 years after the release of GTA 5. (Related: AAWireless releases newly introduced, smaller Nexus and...)

The open-world crime game has attracted enormous attention worldwide, with fans taking time off work to play it. According to French broadcaster TF1, requests for annual leave in France increased by 8% around the game’s launch. Now, the game has been introduced to the older generations. (Related: Umamusume horse girls are taking over a Denver Broncos ...)

On November 20, residents at the Domitys senior living facility in Serris, Seine-et-Marne, were introduced to GTA 6 as part of an organized gaming session.

As reported by ICI Paris Île-de-France, the residents swapped their usual activities for an opportunity to explore the game’s virtual world, with many particularly enjoying its boat-driving sequences.

Industry observers note that 82-year-old Jacqueline was among the most enthusiastic participants, successfully collecting two packages of cocaine during a mission. “I had never touched a controller before 9am this morning. And by 2:30pm, I was fishing out two packages of drugs,” she joked.

Another resident, 86-year-old Lucienne, was equally impressed by the game’s visuals, describing them as incredible and comparing the experience to watching a real film.

Meanwhile, 75-year-old Yvan admitted that the controls were difficult to master, joking that he was no longer 20 and struggled to keep up with the action.

Staff explained that the residents had found driving and shooting particularly challenging, but enjoyed exploring the environment by boat.

The experience proved popular enough that Lucienne remarked she intended to ask her children for GTA 6 for Christmas, while Jacqueline suggested installing a console in the communal lounge.
