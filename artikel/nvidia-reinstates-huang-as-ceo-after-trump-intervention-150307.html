Nvidia reinstates Huang as CEO after Trump intervention

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (84 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, nvidia on Monday reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive in a stunning reversal that President Donald Trump stated followed his own direct contacts with the tech giant's board. Huang resigned two weeks ago over an affair with a woman whom US intelligence agencies suspect of spying for China, a departure that sent Wall Street tumbling and stoked fears for a US economy reliant on the AI investment boom. FRANCE 24's Carys Garland and Wassim Cornet report.
