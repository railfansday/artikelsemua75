The January transfer window could be the first stop for Pochettino for some shopping but the budget given to him as per a previous report by readmanutd isn’t much to make a difference.

Paul Merson believes the club have several areas to improve rather than focusing only on their defence.

Speaking on Sky Sports, the former Arsenal midfielder made it clear that United need to bring in defensive reinforcements.

“Manchester United will have to get centre-halves in, defenders in and full-backs in,” Merson noted.

According to recent reports, however, Merson does not believe defensive recruitment alone will solve United’s problems. He also questioned the team’s ability to control games in midfield and expressed concerns about the quality of their attacking options.

“I wouldn’t say that’s just been the problem. I don’t think they’ve got legs in midfield, I don’t think they’re dominating games in midfield,” he explained.

He was also critical of United’s forward line, suggesting that the current attacking options are not consistently performing.

“I’ve never really been a massive fan of their front three. I think they’re not Manchester United standard, in my opinion.”.

According to recent reports, merson also compared the current generation with some of the club’s former greats, including Wayne Rooney, Ruud van Nistelrooy, Roy Keane, Paul Scholes, David Beckham and Cristiano Ronaldo. He compared players’ consistency with those earlier.

“Back in the day when it’s Rooney and them kind of players and Van Nistelrooy and Keane and Scholes and Beckham and Ronaldo, they were seven, eight out of ten every week. There wasn’t a four out of ten and a nine out of ten.”.

“And if they have a four the week after, it doesn’t really matter. So for me, it hasn’t just been the defence.”.

United could face difficult decisions when the transfer window opens, particularly if they want to strengthen multiple areas of the squad and with the budget available to Pochettino, that doesn’t seem to be the case.
