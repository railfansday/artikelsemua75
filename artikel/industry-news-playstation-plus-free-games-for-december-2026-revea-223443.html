In a recent development, playStation has revealed this month's batch of free games for PlayStation Plus subscribers. All PS Plus members will be able to claim a trio of freebies starting December 1--Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4, Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, and Amnesia: The Bunker. If you haven't claimed November's PlayStation Plus free games yet, make sure to do so before next Tuesday. Like the previous compilation of classic skateboarding games, Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 remakes the classic third and fourth games in the series. With enhanced visuals, familiar gameplay, and a killer soundtrack, it also adds extra content to the mix--and yes, the soundtrack still slaps. While Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3 won't be out until late next year, don't sleep on the first sequel. It's still a solid action-RPG where you craft your own custom fighter to join Trunks and the Time Squad, and it received a ton of post-launch support that stretched for years. The game also received a free current-gen enhancement in 2024, sharpening its visuals for modern systems. Finally, Amnesia: The Bunker is a pretty great horror game to play in case you need to escape all the festive season cheer. As French soldier Henri Clément, trench warfare takes on a terrifying latest form as you're hunted by sinister forces in the shadows, and the game keeps you on your toes as you try to escape the battlefields of World War 1 in one piece.
