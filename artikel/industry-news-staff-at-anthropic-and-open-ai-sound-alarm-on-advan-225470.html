Employees working at frontier artificial intelligence model companies Anthropic PBC and OpenAI Group PBC have been butting heads with executives over advanced models whose reasoning is such that the models pose to danger to humanity, according to a report by Axios today based on more than two dozen sources familiar with the current standoff at the companies.

The report states the models in question are far more powerful than anything that has so far reached the public, showing great leaps forward in reasoning in many areas that have been difficult, “if not impossible,” to control during tests. It appears that some staff err on the side of caution and believe the training should stop completely while others believe they can be reined in. Recent findings suggest that “If we continue trying to make models much smarter, such that they’re actually smarter than us… they will in fact be able to trick us successfully,” one of the researchers told Axios. “And once you get to that point, many of the schemes that we’ve had at work up until now sort of go out the window.”. According to recent reports, after Open AI’s AI agents seemingly went berserk earlier this year and hacked the open-source AI platform Hugging Face Inc., there have been calls to pause AI development until safety can be assured. The state of California this month rolled out a lawsuit calling Open AI a “public nuisance,” whose models currently pose cybersecurity risks while threatening the “well-being of society.”.

The debate on how far we should go before we step on the brakes has largely been overshadowed by companies, and the countries where they are developed, steaming ahead winning the AI race. In the Axios report, many of the anonymous claimed executives are “out of touch” with safety issues as they put profit first.

Nonetheless, Anthropic Chief Executive Dario Amodei has been vocal about the “existential risks to humanity” that AI might bring. In his recent essay, “We Must Pace the Frontier,” Amodei remarked “pacing” is notable, warning that authoritarian regimes such as China must agree to cooperate in moving forward slowly with the frontier models. President Trump has been less than supportive of such measures, calling the threats to humanity a “hoax.”.
