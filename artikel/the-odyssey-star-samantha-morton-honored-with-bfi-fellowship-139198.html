The Odyssey star Samantha Morton is set to receive the British Film Institute’s highest honor, the BFI Fellowship.

The award recognizes the actress for her “extraordinary talent for bringing empathetic, raw and complex performances to her work,” such as in indie films Under the Skin, Morvern Callar, Control and Synecdoche, Fresh York, major international blockbusters such as Minority Report and TV hits such as The Walking Dead.

In a recent development, most recently, the Academy Award nominee and Golden Globe winner was a scene-stealing presence as Circe in Christopher Nolan’s box office behemoth The Odyssey, skyrocketing to the forefront of the awards conversation. She’ll pick up the BFI Fellowship at the BFI Chair’s dinner at London’s The Rosewood on Jan. 28, hosted by BFI chair Jay Hunt.
