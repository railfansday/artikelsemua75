Fernando Mendoza's first career NFL touchdown pass is now in the books.

The Las Vegas Raiders quarterback connected with running back Ashton Jeanty for a deep touchdown in the second quarter of their matchup with the Denver Broncos on Sunday afternoon. Mendoza found Jeanty open up the right side of the field for what ended up being an easy 68-yard score at Empower Field.

The touchdown brought the Raiders within one touchdown of the Broncos just ahead of halftime.

Naturally, Mendoza was extremely excited.

In a recent development, while it was his first passing touchdown, it was not the first time that Mendoza has found the end zone in his NFL career. The reigning Heisman Trophy winner made his first start in the league last week following the Raiders' deal to trade Kirk Cousins to the Minnesota Vikings. Early in that contest, Mendoza capped his opening drive with a 10-yard touchdown run.

In a recent development, the Raiders entered Sunday with just a 4-6 record having lost six of their last seven games. The franchise selected Mendoza with the No. 1 overall pick in the draft earlier this year after a national championship run with Indiana, and the expectation was always that he'd start for them eventually.

Despite his touchdown throw to Jeanty, Mendoza and the Raiders entered halftime down 17-10 in Denver. Mendoza went 6-of-13 for 127 yards in the first 30 minutes.

The Broncos entered Sunday's contest with a 7-2 record and on a five-game win streak.
