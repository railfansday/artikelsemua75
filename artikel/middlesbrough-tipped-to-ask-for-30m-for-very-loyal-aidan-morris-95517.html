Middlesbrough have a number of key players, with Aidan Morris being one already linked with a January transfer window move away from The Riverside to join Bundesliga side VfB Stuttgart.

Will Lankshear's goals, Amario Cozier-Duberry's dribbling and creativity, and Adilson Malanda's composure may all catch the eye; but Aidan Morris has arguably been the heartbeat of this Middlesbrough side.

Kim Hellberg won't want to lose any of his best players in January, but Morris being linked with a move to Stuttgart from Middlesbrough could be a concern, per a recent report from German outlet BILD.

Sources indicate that FLW's Middlesbrough fan pundit Liam Day was asked about the fact Stuttgart are reportedly eyeing a move for Morris. We asked if this is hardly a surprise, is it? But also what price-tag would he place on the midfielder’s head right now and if he should be retained at all costs.

He replied: "I think simply because January, you know, you're in the midst of a promotion battle, you don't sell your stars.

"And I don't think there is any way he leaves; he seems very loyal to Boro, which is very nice.

"So, I think you've got to do all you can to try and retain him.

"But say Boro were to sell him, I think an offer of £30m-35m probably will do it.

"He is a great player, no doubt about it, but that sort of money in the Championship is hard to say no to.

"Because you can then improve your overall squad, but I don't think you can get another Aidan Morris.

"So, you do have to try and retain him at all costs, but an offer of £30-35 million will probably do it.".

With Hayden Hackney joining Everton for an initial £16.5 million, rising to £24 million, as reported by Sky Sports, Morris’ superior off-ball quality, defensive control, and relentless pressing, justify a £25 million valuation at minimum.

Unlike Hackney’s box-to-box roaming to use his passing, Morris dictates tempo while shielding the backline, making him the more complete midfielder overall at Championship level.

Born in Florida, Morris developed through the Columbus Crew academy and Indiana University before signing professionally in 2020. After winning two MLS Cups and earning USMNT caps, Middlesbrough signed him for around $4 million (£3.1 million) in June 2024 (The Athletic).

According to recent reports, under Michael Carrick, Morris rapidly became Boro's midfield engine. Now under Hellberg, his bravery on the ball and intelligence off it has elevated him into the club's undisputed focal point and standout player following Hackney’s departure.
