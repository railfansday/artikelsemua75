A Kvaratskhelia is about to be the centre of a massive trasnfer battle in England, but it isn't Khvicha.

Tornike Kvaratskhelia, the 16-year-old brother of PSG's superstar, has been making his own headlines this season.

Breaking through into the Dinamo Tbilisi first-team, the young Georgian winger has become a regular substitute for Temur Ketsbaia's side.

Now, Liverpool and Arsenal are among the teams chasing the 16-year-old, and things are heating up ahead of the winter window.

That's according to a report from TEAMtalk, which states that the two Premier League giants alongside Chelsea, Tottenham, Manchester City, Manchester United, Brighton and Aston Villa are in the race for the wonderkid.

With so many sides interested it is impossible to tell where Kvaratskhelia will end up, but Liverpool have a track record of adding players at academy level and presenting a clear pathway to first-team football.

Due to his age, Tornike reportedly wouldn't be eligible for a move to England until January 2028 at the earliest under current regulations, but that hasn't stopped a long list of clubs putting in the groundwork to secure the player.

There is also plenty of interest from teams on the continent, and no indication of how much Kvaratskhelia could cost, so the teenager's future could lay anywhere.

His surname will get supporters excited, but his potential is the more crucial factor, and Tornike looks set to be a superstar in his own right.
