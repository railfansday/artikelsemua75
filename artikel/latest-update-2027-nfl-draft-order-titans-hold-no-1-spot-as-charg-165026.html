The Tennessee Titans may have allowed 51 points on Sunday afternoon, but they are still in control of the No. 1 overall pick in 2027 NFL Draft.

The Titans fell to 1-9 on the season on Sunday afternoon in a rough 51-30 loss to the Dallas Cowboys at AT&T Stadium. That marked their fourth straight loss. And, as the Titans hold the key strength of schedule tiebreak, the franchise kept control of the first overall pick in the draft next spring.

The Titans overtook the Los Angeles Chargers last week for the top spot after the Chargers finally picked up their first win of the season. But on Sunday, after the Chargers fell to the Upcoming York Jets 27-24, they joined the Titans at 1-9 on the year. The Miami Dolphins have just a single win to their name, too. The Buffalo Bills rolled over the Dolphins 26-9 on Sunday, marking the team's fourth-straight loss. But the Chargers' loss kept the Dolphins down at the No. 3 overall pick.

Industry observers note that the Jets now sit at 3-8 on the season. They hold three first-round draft picks in 2027, too, one from the Indianapolis Colts and another from the Cowboys. As things right now stand, the Jets will have both the No. 4 and No. 5 picks. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

In a recent development, now, there is still plenty of football to be played this season now that Week 11 is in the books. And with three teams at 1-9, the race for the No. 1 pick still has a long way to go.

For now, the Titans are still in control at the top.
