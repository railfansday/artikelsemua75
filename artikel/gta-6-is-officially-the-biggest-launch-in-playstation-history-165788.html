📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that it's not exactly a secret that Grand Theft Auto 6 is the most anticipated game of the past decade. Thanks to the long wait and Rockstar Games' excellent hype campaign, it seems everyone with a PS5 (and a few Xbox owners, too) is playing GTA 6 now. But what does that translate to in terms of numbers? That's what the PlayStation Blog just revealed.

GTA 6 is officially the biggest launch in PlayStation history, period. The game shattered a list of records for Sony's console. While technically GTA 6 is also available on Xbox Series X, developer Rockstar Games is clearly playing favorites. The game's advertising campaign declared that it “plays best on PS,” and GTA 6 even got a DualSense controller. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

In a recent development, according to the data shared by Sony in the PlayStation Blog post, relative to the game's launch window between Nov. 19 and Nov. 22, more than 39 million unique players have experienced GTA 6 on PlayStation 5. GTA 6 also "set a new record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation, surpassing previous records across multiplayer, free-to-play, and single-player titles.".

The data become even more impressive when looking at the number of hours PlayStation players have poured into the game already. Across PS5, players logged 683 million hours of gameplay, with an average of 17.4 hours per player. It's true that Leonida is supposed to be the biggest map in the history of the GTA series, but those are still impressive numbers​​​​​​​. It surely helps that GTA 6 has a mind-boggling​​​​​​​ amount of side quests, Easter eggs, random events, and an apparently bottomless treasure chest of features for players to discover.

Sources indicate that predictably, GTA 6 also significantly contributed to the growth of the PlayStation community​​​​​​​. The game's launch saw "the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console’s launch, outside of end-of-year holidays. Since its launch, GTAVI was the first game played on 70% of newly introduced PS5 consoles.". (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

What does that all mean in terms of business? According to estimates provided by Alinea Analytics​​​​​​​, GTA 6 sold 33.7 million copies on PS5 (75.1%) and 11.2 million on Xbox (24.9%) by the end of the game's launch weekend. (Publisher Take-Two Interactive initially reported 32.5 million copies sold for $3.79 billion in revenue.) This amounted to $4 billion in gross revenue via sold-through copies in four days, with over $3 billion came from PS5.

As Rockstar said, this was truly the biggest launch in entertainment history. And as players keep exploring Leonida and engaging with the game, there is no telling just how big GTA 6 will get.
