📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

On my last siding project, I decided to make my own electrical mounting boxes out of PVC trim. At the time, I only knew about the thin, hollow vinyl boxes, and I wasn’t too keen on hanging heavy lights from them. Cutting the PVC to size was easy enough, but by the time I finished cutting out the hole for the electrical box in the second one (with 18 more to go), I knew I’d made a mistake.

I saved a few bucks, but it took a ton of time, especially since I had to custom-bend and install head flashing and fuss around with flashing tape on the sides of the blocks. Had I known about SturdiMounts from Westlake Royal Building Products, I wouldn’t have considered making my own. SturdiMounts are solid PVC mounting blocks with an integrated nailing fin and head flashing. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Sources indicate that they are available in square, rectangular, and oversized shapes. There is a split option and even one with an outlet box. SturdiMount blocks are paintable and come in either a smooth or textured finish. They cost between $20 and $35 each, depending on the configuration and whether they’re bought in bulk or individually. Look for them at siding suppliers and lumberyards.
