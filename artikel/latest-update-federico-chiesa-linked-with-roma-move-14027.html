Federico Chiesa could leave Liverpool as early as January, with Roma remaining among the clubs interested in bringing him back to Italy.

According to Calciomercato.com, the striker is considering a change of team to regain consistency, after another first half of the season spent almost entirely on the sidelines for the English club. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

The 1997-born striker played just two minutes in the Premier League, coming on as a late substitute against Manchester City on October 11th.

Having arrived from Juventus in the summer of 2024, Chiesa has made 51 appearances and scored five goals for Liverpool, failing to secure a regular starting spot even after Andoni Iraola took over as manager.

Liverpool have so far asked for around €15 million for the player, ruling out the possibility of a loan, although the main obstacle for the Italian clubs is his €7.5 million per season salary. However, this could change in the coming months, given his contract expiring in June 2028 and the possibility that Chiesa agrees to spread his salary over a longer agreement. Among the options reported is a three-year deal worth around €4 million annually. In addition to Roma, Inter Milan, Milan, and Napoli are also monitoring the former Juventus player, while possible destinations include Como and Lazio, and especially Saudi Arabia, where he would have a better chance of maintaining his current salary.
