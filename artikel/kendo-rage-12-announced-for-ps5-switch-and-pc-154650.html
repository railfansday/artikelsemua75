It has been revealed that edia has declared Kendo Rage 1&2, a two-game collection featuring side-scrolling action game Kendo Rage and fighting game sequel Kendo Rage 2. It will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch, and PC via Steam on March 18, 2023.

Kendo Rage first launched for Suepr Famicom in January 1993 in Japan, followed by Super Nintendo Entertainment System in October 1993 in North America. The sequel, which never saw a localized release, launched for Super Famicom in March 1995 in Japan, followed by PlayStation in November 1995. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

In Japan, Kendo Rage 1&2 will be priced at 7,800 yen for the standard physical or digital edition (8,580 yen with tax), and 14,800 yen for the physical Special Edition (16,280 yen with tax). The latter includes a copy of the game, a replica poster-style large tapestry, a soundtrack CD, and a replica flyer set. (Related: Breaking: Director of Machine Learning Insights [Part 2...)
