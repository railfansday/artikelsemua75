Perhaps the only thing more English in terms of culture than football and the EFL is the pub – and the two things go hand in hand.

The erosion of working-class culture when it comes to English football is something that perhaps requires more discussion and that, again, goes hand in hand with the almost relentless closing down of pubs up and down the country. Despite that, plenty remain standing, albeit perhaps less than there once was when clubs were a bit more ramshackle in the heart of a community, rather than often moved out to industrial estates. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

That stated, we have taken a look at the Championship and ranked the clubs with the most pubs within two miles of the ground.

Having moved away from the town centre in the late-1990s, there are still a few pubs inside a two-mile radius of Bolton Wanderers’ home, albeit most of the locals tend to go further afield into Horwich or even via the train from town. The Beehive is the designated away fans’ pub.

The Railway Inn is just 0.2 miles away from Swansea City’s home ground of the Swansea.com Stadium but there aren’t really an abundance of other options with just 15 pubs within a two-mile radius of The Jacks’ home ground in Wales’ second city.

In a recent development, with some nearby pubs such as The Royal Consort and The White Hart, there are 16 pubs within a two-mile radius of Deepdale. Preston North End supporters are blessed with quite a few more options in what is often a lively and entertaining city centre, though.

It has been revealed that not too far from the city centre, it is somewhat surprising that there is a slight shortage of pubs within two miles of Lincoln City’s home ground of Sincil Bank. There are around 18 with the closest being Lincoln’s home pub, The Imp and Angel, just 0.2 miles from the ground.

There are a few extremely busy pubs nearby to the Riverside Stadium, perhaps most prominently The Navigation, but there aren’t an abundance of options with around 20 pubs inside a two-mile limit of Middlesbrough’s home ground.

Carrow Road has 20 pubs within a two-mile radius of the ground with Norwich City supporters having plenty of options within half a mile, too, such as the Coach and Horses, The Fat Cat and Canary as well as The Jubilee.

With The Turf more or less attached to the Racecourse Ground, that tends to be the haunt for Wrexham supporters but The Red Dragons do have 20 pubs within the two mile cut off in North Wales.

Recent findings suggest that up in the heart of Lancashire, there are still plenty of pubs knocking about and there are 21 pubs within a two-mile radius of Ewood Park. The Fox and Hounds and The Fernhurst are the closest on a Blackburn Rovers match day.

Slightly out of the centre, there are still around 22 pubs in close proximity of Pride Park for Derby County supporters with the closest and most popular being The Merlin, 0.3 miles away from the ground.

Sources indicate that down in Bermondsey, there are around 25 pubs within two miles of Millwall’s famous home ground of The Den. It is often the case that away supporters head into Central London and around the London Bridge area before the short tube down to The Lions.
