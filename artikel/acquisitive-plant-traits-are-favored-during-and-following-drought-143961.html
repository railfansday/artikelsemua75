Acquisitive plant traits are favored during and following drought

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (56 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Researchers from the Institute of Applied Ecology (IAE) of the Chinese Academy of Sciences and their international collaborators found that prolonged extreme drought favors plant traits associated with acquisition in semiarid grasslands. These changes persist during post-drought recovery. Communities with more acquisitive strategies and greater functional dispersion showed lower sensitivity to drought.
