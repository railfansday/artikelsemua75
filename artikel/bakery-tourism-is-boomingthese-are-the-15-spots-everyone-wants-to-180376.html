Industry observers note that bakeries are no longer just a stop along the way—they’re the final destination. According to Yelp’s 2027 Food and Drink Trend report, bakeries are becoming such a hot commodity that people are planning entire trips around viral treats. Yelp searches for “bakeries near me” are up 561% from last year, while searches for a “sweet treat” are up 303%. Treat yo’ self culture is officially here.

Bakery tourism is on the rise, and honestly, I'm not surprised. I, myself, am prone to walking unfamiliar city streets just to get my hands on the town's most beloved pastry. And people are getting pretty specific about what they want. Searches for cardamom buns are up 24%, kouign-amann 67%, and salt bread a whopping 616%.

Industry observers note that it’s not just individual pastries getting all the attention, either. Yelp found that searches for “hot and upcoming bakeries” jumped 359%, while “best gluten-free bakery” rose 113%. Bakeries are also the most-saved type of specialty food shop on Yelp’s My Lists feature. Even New York City's Radio Bakery, which you’ll find below, saw searches increase by 176%. In a recent development, what started out as a pandemic-era creation has now morphed into a line-worthy brick-and-mortar. Bad Butter regularly draws block-long lines, with its breads, croissants, and pastries often selling out.
