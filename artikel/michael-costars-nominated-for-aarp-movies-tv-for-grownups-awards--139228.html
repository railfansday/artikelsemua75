Sources indicate that colman Domingo and Nia Long, who played Michael Jackson’s parents Joseph and Katherine Jackson in the hit film Michael, were on Monday (Nov. 23) both nominated for AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards, the annual celebration honoring outstanding films and television programs that spotlight the voices, stories and talent of people age 50 and older.

Sources indicate that jaafar Jackson, who played his uncle, the global pop icon, in the film, was not eligible in the best actor category. He’s only 30. Director Antoine Fuqua’s film set newly introduced box-office records as the top-grossing musician biopic (topping the Freddie Mercury biopic Bohemian Rhapsody) and the top-grossing biopic of any subject (topping Oppenheimer, about theoretical physicist J. Robert Oppenheimer). (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Two music documentaries were nominated for best documentary: EPiC: Elvis Presley in Concert, director Baz Luhrmann’s second look at rock legend Elvis Presley, following his 2022 feature film Elvis; and Darlene Love: I Know Where I’ve Been, director Barry Avrich’s  portrait of former backup singer Darlene Love (she sang the Crystals‘ 1962 smash “He’s a Rebel”) who has gone on to a long career.

The other documentaries nominated in the category are Give Me the Ball!, a portrait of tennis legend Billie Jean King; Marty, Life Is Short, about comedian Martin Short; and You Can See Everything, about Elizabeth Holmes, founder of the defunct health technology manufacturer Theranos.

Being Heumann, The Invite, The Odyssey, Tender Loving Care and Wild Horse Nine lead the race for best movie for grownups.

Rashida Jones, accomplished daughter of the late music legend Quincy Jones, is nominated for best screenwriter for The Invite, which she cowrote with Will McCormack.

It has been revealed that “This year’s nominees are proof that experience is one of the most powerful creative forces in film and television,” Dr. Myechia Minter-Jordan, CEO of AARP, said in a statement. “From legends like Meryl Streep and Helen Mirren to acclaimed creators like Christopher Nolan and Chris Rock, these artists are doing some of the best work of their careers and reminding audiences and the industry alike that great stories have no age limit.”. The AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards will take place on Saturday, Jan. 9, at the Beverly Wilshire Hotel in Beverly Hills, Calif. The show will be hosted by five-time Primetime Emmy winner Jane Lynch, who revealed the nominees on Monday during a live announcement on AARP’s YouTube, Instagram and Facebook channels.

According to recent reports, the ceremony will be broadcast by Great Performances on PBS. Great Performances: AARP Movies & TV for Grownups Awards premieres Friday, Feb. 26 at 9/8c on PBS, PBS’ website and the PBS app. It will stream on the Great Performances YouTube channel the following day.
