Prince Harry is sharing details about his return to the U.K. alongside wife Meghan Markle and their two children.

Industry observers note that “It’s good being back on home soil. I’m throwing myself into the winter weather. … I have missed the winters,” Harry, 42, said on the Friday, November 20, episode of ITV’s This Morning in his first live televised interview since he and Meghan moved from California to Britain in August. “Look at us Brits, talking about the weather!” he quipped to cohosts Alison Hammond and Dermot O’Leary.

In a recent development, harry, 42, and Meghan, 45, returned to the U.K. in August after six years in California. The couple’s children — Prince Archie, 7, and Princess Lilibet, 5 — began school in the U.K. for the first time the following month. During his This Morning appearance, Harry gave an update on how the kids are settling in to life in the U.K.

Industry observers note that “They love being back,” he mentioned. “It’s a big culture shift. I think that the kids in school uniforms is a big change for them, but it’s certainly helped. The confidence in both of them has gone through the roof since they’ve been in U.K. school, which is really, really nice.”.

Hammond, 51, then asked Harry whether Archie and Lilibet’s accents have changed since moving.

Sources indicate that “I’m squeezing the accents out of them, and their friends at school are doing the same,” Harry joked. “Lili does a good impression of ‘Mummy.’”.

As for his plans for his Christmas back in the U.K., Harry shared that Meghan and his mother-in-law, Doria Ragland, will be in charge of cooking.

Recent findings suggest that “My wife’s very good in the kitchen and so is my mother-in-law, so I’ll leave it to them. There’ll be a gumbo,” he remarked, adding that there will also be “every range of turkey you can imagine — full, roasted turkey, pulled turkey [and] smoked turkey.”.

Sources indicate that elsewhere, Harry shared his support for Robert Pattinson as the newly introduced James Bond. “I can’t wait to see it,” he enthused.

He also subtly shouted out Meghan, sharing that he prefers her former hit show Suits to the British soap opera EastEnders.

Harry previously addressed his return to the U.K. in an interview with GQ in October.

“I think that certainly, our attitude has changed,” he told the magazine. “I think once you’ve been through it, once you’ve been able to talk about it and raise awareness to it, then people see it for what it is. It makes the environment slightly easier. It’s not going to necessarily stop the behavior, but I think the behavior might — my hope [is] that it will — change.”.

It has been revealed that “Being able to put the kids in a British school has meant a lot to me,” Harry added. “I didn’t know how key it was to me. I always thought it was gonna be something that I would try and do or that we would try and do, but now they’ve been in school for two weeks.”.
