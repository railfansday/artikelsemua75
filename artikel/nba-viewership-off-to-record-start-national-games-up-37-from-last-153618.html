NBA viewership is off to a record start this season.

The league revealed Monday that viewership over the first month of the 2026-27 campaign is up 20% from last year on nationally broadcast games on NBC, ESPN and Amazon. National games are averaging a record 2.62 million viewers, too, which is up about 37% from last season. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

Industry observers note that the numbers are a solid increase for the league as it enters the second year of a new massive 11-year media rights deal, which will run through the 2035-36 season. The new deal extended games with ABC and ESPN, brought national games back to NBC and Amazon Prime Video entered the fold, too. Roughly 75 regular-season games are set to be broadcast on national TV each season, up from just the minimum 15 under the previous agreement. (Related: Industry News: ‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok f...)

Industry observers note that the league also declared that viewership of live games on NBA League Pass and through the NBA app is up 50%. NBA Cup games on Prime Video are breaking records, too. Those tournament games are averaging about 2 million viewers each so far this season, which is up 90% from last season. The NBA Cup is in its fourth season of existence.

The viewership spike comes after a massive showing during the NBA Finals last season. The series between the Newly introduced York Knicks and San Antonio Spurs averaged roughly 20.6 million views on ABC and ESPN, which made it the most-watched NBA Finals in nearly three decades.

Viewership numbers are calculated through Nielsen, which shifted to a new methodology in recent years that pairs both major data from smart TV and traditional panel boxes.

While the season still has a long way to go to live up to the NBA Finals numbers that the Knicks and Spurs drew last spring, it's clear that the league's new media deal is off to a very impressive start.
