Healthcare marketing teams can build dashboards around the metrics that matter most to them, then present or export them with their own branding.

HOUSTON, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ours Privacy, the HIPAA-compliant marketing and analytics platform built by healthcare marketers for healthcare marketers, today rolled out custom dashboards to its Advanced Analytics Suite. Alongside a series of expert-built dashboards covering the metrics teams rely on most, healthcare marketers now have full flexibility to build their own dashboards and combine the exact reports they need in one customizable view. Each dashboard runs on Ours Privacy's own secure infrastructure, covered by a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), so teams can quickly and easily build the exact views they need without relying on a technical data team or separate analytics tool. (Related: Deploy Hugging Face models easily with Amazon SageMaker...)

Build the exact dashboard your team needs.

Recent findings suggest that dashboards can be built around any visitor or event a team tracks across the Ours Privacy platform, from web events like page views or form starts through to backend events like tracked calls or attended appointments. Teams can query by any property, break results down by source, device, or campaign, and combine Insights, Funnels, Journeys, and Session Replays in a single view. Teams can even build their own attribution reports, spanning every channel and both frontend and backend events, and set their own conversion windows rather than relying on hard-coded definitions. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

It has been revealed that every healthcare marketing team is different, and Ours Privacy is purposefully designed to meet each one where it is. Teams can jump directly into pre-built dashboards, chosen by Ours Privacy's healthcare marketing experts and ready to use as soon as the team goes live on the platform. Teams that want to explore custom options can start with seven Analytics Recipes, step-by-step intends for dashboards such as bookings by service line, newly introduced-patient demand by channel, patient access, and new-patient growth.

Build dashboards with AI and connect them to existing tools.

Teams that know exactly what they need can build every dashboard from scratch to their exact specifications, and teams that work with AI don't have to build anything by hand. Through the Ours Privacy MCP server, REST API, and other connectors, teams can build dashboards with AI and connect to the business intelligence tools they already use, on their terms and with the permissions they set.

It has been revealed that "Nothing about healthcare marketing is one-size-fits-all," said Tyler Zey, co-founder and chief technology officer of Ours Privacy. "Reporting shouldn't be, either. It's key that teams are able to own, query, and shape their own data without having to send it through a patchwork of other tools.".

Share results with leadership in one click.

With a single click, any dashboard can be converted into a full-screen presentation or branded PDF, so healthcare marketers can share the data trends their executive and legal teams want to see without having to manually configure spreadsheets.

Trust your data's accuracy and privacy.

According to recent reports, "Healthcare marketers shouldn't have to trade away the reporting flexibility they know from tools like GA4 to protect patient privacy," stated Adam Putterman, co-founder and chief revenue officer of Ours Privacy. "With custom dashboards in Ours Privacy's Advanced Analytics Suite, they get both plus capabilities GA4 was never built to offer, like Session Replays and Heatmaps, all wrapped into the same platform.".

Custom dashboards join other recent additions to the Ours Privacy platform, including the AI Assistant, which answers questions about performance and setup in plain language and is covered by the customer's BAA, and Mobile Analytics for iOS, Android, and React Native apps.

Ours Privacy is a HIPAA-compliant customer data platform (CDP) and marketing platform built by healthcare marketers for healthcare marketers. The platform is trusted by leading health systems, digital health companies, and national medical brands to modernize their marketing while navigating a rapidly evolving privacy environment. Ours Privacy integrates with major channels including Google Ads, Meta Ads, DSPs, EHRs, scheduling platforms, and data warehouses. Learn more at oursprivacy.com.
