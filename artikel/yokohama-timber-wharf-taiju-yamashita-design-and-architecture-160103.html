Text description provided by the architects. Set in Rinko Park, the largest green space in Yokohama's Minato Mirai 21 district, Yokohama Timber Wharf is a multipurpose facility designed to connect architecture with the life of the waterfront park. Facing Yokohama Bay, the park's seaside promenade and broad lawns attract families, runners, and visitors throughout the year. The project brings dining and outdoor recreation support into this setting, creating a fresh gathering place and encouraging movement through Yokohama's coastal city center. DRAFT leads the development, while Taiju Yamashita Design and Architecture is responsible for the architectural design.
