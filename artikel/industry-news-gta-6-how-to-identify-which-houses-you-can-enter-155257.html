Recent findings suggest that theft is in the name, so what better way to plan a good heist than to have a good understanding of how the map system works in Grand Theft Auto 6? Well, thankfully, we have the solution for you. If you are wondering if you’re able to enter a specific property, there is an easy and handy way to identify it on the map. This guide will go over just how to do so.

There’s a surprisingly well-designed feature in the map that no one really ever tells you about. Rockstar has created both a 2D and 3D virtual map for you to set waypoints, filter certain facilities and see where you haven’t been yet. One aspect of it is color. The coloring scheme is very handy not only in the open world (especially during your focus), but also in the map. By opening up a map, you will find essentially two different colors for houses and structures.

If a structure is light blue, that means you won’t be able to get inside. If that structure is dark blue, that means you’ll be able to enter it. This can be done either through a doorway (where you can use a Snap Gun to avoid alarms or breaking in through brute force), or through a garage.

Recent findings suggest that with that said, not every dark blue structure can be accessed only because there are some that are tied specifically to the story and side missions. You will be able to move around in these houses during these quests but not in the open world.

There are two aspects to why you’d go through these houses. The first is obviously to rob them or find collectibles. This is very much encouraged for more bigger locations such as banks, stores and so forth.

Industry observers note that for standard houses, it’s all about environmental storytelling. Whether it’s coming across a house with what looks like a person who was recently tortured, a house with a disturbing number of female mannequins in it or a football player with a ton of trophies and footballs on display, Rockstar has created a fantastic world to just poke your nose in.
