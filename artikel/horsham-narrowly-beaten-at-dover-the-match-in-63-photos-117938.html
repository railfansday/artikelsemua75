Charlie Hester-Cook gave the Hornets an early lead but Euan Williams levelled quickly – and Ruben Soares-Junior’s second half goal proved the hosts’ winner.

Horsham ended with ten men after Lee Harding was sent off for two bookable offences.

The defeat leaves Horsham 14th in the table ahead of their next game, at home to Truro on Tuesday.
