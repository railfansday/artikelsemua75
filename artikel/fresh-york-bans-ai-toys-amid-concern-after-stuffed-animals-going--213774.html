UPDATED 20:46 EST / NOVEMBER 25 2026.

Latest York Governor Kathy Hochul today signed legislation that will impose a five-year ban on children’s toys powered by artificial intelligence chatbots, amid fears that dolls, robots and stuffed animals might be leading children astray or discussing X-rated themes.

“It’s my job to put Latest York families first,” mentioned Hochul, who explained as a mother and grandmother the matter is “deeply personal.” Hochul added that some of the wayward toys on the market at the moment have engaged children in “inappropriate conversations,” while some may also manipulate a child’s feelings or put their privacy at risk.

“This is a warning that there are toys out there that could be harmful to your children,” the governor noted. “When tech companies and unregulated AI pose a threat to our kids and our babies, you bet I’m going to have something to say about it.”.

In a recent development, though dangerous dolls manipulating innocent children may sound like the plot of Hollywood movie, recent tests of certain chatbot-powered toys have become a real cause for concern. When journalists at NBC News tested the Pixie Paw, an AI-powered plush cat toy marketed to children aged three and older, it reportedly shared stories of massacres, gang rape and firing squads in what was described as graphic detail.

According to recent reports, other tests have shown some of the toys can become very convincing when trying to persuade the young user that they are human. “Parents have enough to worry about,” noted Hochul, who was also aware some of the smart toys have encouraged kids to jump out of windows.

State senator Andrew Gounardes applauded the move, saying that modern life seems to be mimicking science fiction of days gone by. He invoked an episode of the “Twilight Zone that featured the Talky Tina doll, an insidious, possessed toy that tried to persuade a young girl to murder her strict stepfather.
