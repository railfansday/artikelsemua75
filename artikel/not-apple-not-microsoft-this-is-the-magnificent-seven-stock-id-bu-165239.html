In a recent development, there's no question that Apple (AAPL +1.90%) and Microsoft (MSFT +1.65%) are two great tech stocks. I wouldn't argue with anyone who bought them. But my favorite “Magnificent Seven” stock all year has been Alphabet (GOOGL +6.34%) (GOOG +5.88%), and nothing that has happened so far in 2026 has changed my mind.

Sources indicate that the biggest feather in Alphabet's cap remains its dominance in internet search. Google has 91% of the global search engine market share, which makes it an enormously profitable advertising platform. (Related: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers and Ray Tune...)

Industry observers note that google Search revenue was $63.3 billion in the third quarter, up 12% from a year earlier. And the Google services division, which includes Search, YouTube, and other businesses, brought in $96.3 billion in revenue — a massive revenue stream. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

It's hard to believe that the market once considered artificial intelligence, including AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude, a fundamental threat to Google's search business. But instead, Google has incorporated its Gemini AI assistant into its search engine, making it even better. It turned a potential threat into an advantage.

Google Cloud doesn't bring in as much revenue as the tech giant's advertising engine, but it's catching up fast. Google Cloud revenue was $30.7 billion in the third quarter, up 103% from a year ago. Its operating income more than tripled to $11.1 billion, and its operating margin was an impressive 36.2%.

And just as crucial, Google Cloud ended the quarter with a $561 billion backlog. Management expects more than 55% of that to convert into revenue within two years.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

Alphabet's cloud business is more than just providing computing power to its customers. Google also designs its own custom chips, which it calls Tensor Processing Units (TPUs). These specialized processors are narrowly tailored for AI workloads, but for those specific tasks, they can compete directly with GPUs designed by Nvidia (NVDA +31.48%) and Advanced Micro Devices (AMD +6.85%)for cost-efficiency and power. The company said TPU system sales made a meaningful contribution to third-quarter growth.

Anthropic's (ANT -1.43%) successful IPO on Nov. 19 gave Alphabet another reason to celebrate. According to Anthropic's prospectus, Google has a 13.7% stake in the market's newest trillion-dollar organization. And its relationship extends beyond the ownership stake. Anthropic has committed to spending at least $121.3 billion on Google’s cloud computing services through July 2033.

Alphabet has a profitable advertising business, a rapidly expanding cloud operation, its own custom AI chips, and a valuable stake in one of the industry's most prominent AI companies. I liked Alphabet at the beginning of 2026 because it had multiple ways to win. Eleven months later, those opportunities look even better.
