📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

To make money teaching English online, apply where your background fits. Without a teaching certificate, start on a conversation app, which pays a flat rate. With one, use a lesson marketplace, where you set your own price and the certificate justifies charging more. Then make yourself available when students book most.

Sources indicate that neither kind requires a degree or a teaching certificate. Credentials change how much you can earn.

In a recent development, without a certificate, start with Cambly, a conversation app that pays $0.17 for each minute you’re in a lesson, or $10.20 for a full hour. An hour with gaps between calls pays less than $10.20, and Cambly is temporarily limiting how many fresh tutors it takes. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

In a recent development, with a certificate, set your own price on Preply, a lesson marketplace where English tutors charge $15 to $25 an hour on average. Preply keeps the whole fee for each latest student’s trial lesson and, while you’re new, 33% of every lesson after that, a cut that falls to 18% as your teaching hours add up. At $15 an hour, you’d keep $10.05, a bit less than Cambly’s $10.20 for the same hour. A TEFL (Teaching English as a Foreign Language) certificate can let you charge more, Preply’s own guide to becoming a tutor says, and at $25 you’d keep $16.75 after the same cut. Sources indicate that count the costs, too. You need a quiet room, a headset with a microphone, and a reliable internet connection. Both platforms treat you as an independent contractor, so once your net earnings reach $400 in a year, you owe the IRS a 15.3% self-employment tax for Social Security and Medicare, on top of income tax. Set aside part of every payout for both in a separate savings account.

Recent findings suggest that set your hours around demand, not just your free time. Preply’s calendar shades the times students in your subject are most likely to book, and Cambly’s Priority Hours send you calls from latest students during hours you claim. A parent who teaches from 6 to 8 a.m. Eastern can finish before the kids wake up, while it’s evening for learners in Japan and South Korea.

According to recent reports, keep those hours the same every week. Cambly claims a consistent schedule helps attract regular students, and Preply says students are more likely to stay with a tutor who’s always free at the same time.
