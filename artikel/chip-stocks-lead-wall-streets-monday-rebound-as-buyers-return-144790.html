It has been revealed that SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Chip Stocks News Commentary - Semiconductor stocks were out in front on Monday morning as buyers came back to the chip group after a week of heavy selling. The biggest spark came before the opening bell, when NVIDIA declared that its board had reinstated co-founder Jensen Huang as President and Chief Executive Officer, and shares across the semiconductor supply chain moved higher with it, from AI processors and memory to the equipment that builds the chips. Active Companies in the semiconductor space with current developments include: NVIDIA Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA), Micron Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MU), Intel Corporation (NASDAQ: INTC), Arm Holdings plc (NASDAQ: ARM), Applied Materials, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMAT).

The Founder Is Back at the Helm.

Sources indicate that NVIDIA Corporation (NASDAQ: NVDA) announced on Monday that, effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board. The firm said the Board voted unanimously following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, and that it received input from senior levels of the United States government, on which basis the Board is confident that his reinstatement poses no national security risk. Huang had resigned on Nov. 12. NVIDIA also said its Board has formed a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors, that it is recruiting a Chief Compliance Officer, and that Colette Kress, who served as interim CEO, continues as Chief Financial Officer.

The business Huang returns to is growing fast. For its fiscal third quarter ended Oct. 25, 2026, NVIDIA reported revenue of $115.8 billion, up 20% from the previous quarter and up 103% from a year ago, including Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year ago. The tech giant mentioned its Vera Rubin NVL72 platform has shipped, and it expects fourth-quarter revenue of $130.0 billion, plus or minus 3%, for a quarter that includes an additional week. NVIDIA noted that its outlook does not assume any Data Center compute revenue from China.

Memory Makers Ride the AI Build-Out.

In a recent development, micron Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MU) reported record fiscal fourth-quarter and full-year 2026 results, with fourth-quarter revenue of $54.23 billion, compared with $41.46 billion in the prior quarter and $11.32 billion in the same period a year earlier. Revenue for the full fiscal year, which ended Sept. 3, 2026, reached $133.19 billion, up from $37.38 billion in fiscal 2025.

Industry observers note that micron guided fiscal first-quarter 2027 revenue to $61.5 billion, plus or minus $1.5 billion, and CEO Sanjay Mehrotra stated the company expects an even stronger fiscal 2027. Investors will hear from the company again soon: Micron said its executives will take part in a fireside chat at the 30th Annual UBS Global Technology and AI Conference in Scottsdale, Arizona, on Tuesday, Dec. 1. Processors From the Data Center to the Device.

Industry observers note that intel Corporation (NASDAQ: INTC) reported third-quarter 2026 results with revenue of $16.5 billion, up 21% year over year. Its Data Center and AI group posted revenue of $7.3 billion, up 78%, which Chief Financial Officer David Zinsner described as the manufacturer's strongest data center revenue growth in 15 years. (Related: Industry News: Faster TensorFlow models in Hugging Face...)

During the quarter, Intel unveiled prepares for a multiyear collaboration with Oracle intended to expand Intel Xeon-based computing on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure. CEO Lip-Bu Tan noted the organization's immediate priority is to unlock additional supply to meet demand for its Xeon family, and Intel is forecasting fourth-quarter 2026 revenue of $15.0 billion to $16.0 billion.

Arm Holdings plc (NASDAQ: ARM) delivered a record second quarter for its fiscal year 2027, which ended Sept. 30, 2026, with total revenue of $1.44 billion, up 27% year over year and its fifth consecutive quarter of total revenue growth above 20%. Licensing and other revenue reached $773 million, up 50%, while royalty revenue was $670 million.

The reach of the Arm platform keeps widening. For the first time, Arm's partners reported more than 10 billion Arm-based chips shipped in a single quarter, an increase of 1.5 billion chips from a year earlier, on a platform used by more than 30 million developers. Arm also noted it is confident of achieving up to $2 billion in Arm AGI CPU revenue across fiscal years 2027 and 2028.

Recent findings suggest that applied Materials, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMAT) reported record quarterly revenue for its fiscal fourth quarter of $10.41 billion, up 53% year over year, and record non-GAAP earnings per share of $4.16, up 92%. Annual revenue reached a record $34.45 billion, up 21%, and the manufacturer generated record quarterly cash from operations of $3.30 billion.

Industry observers note that applied Materials expects first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $11.1 billion, plus or minus $500 million, and non-GAAP earnings per share of $4.35, plus or minus $0.20. President and CEO Gary Dickerson said the company is on track to grow its Semiconductor Systems business more than 40% in calendar 2026.
