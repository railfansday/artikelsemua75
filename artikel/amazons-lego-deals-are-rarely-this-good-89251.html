As a collector myself, I like to gift Lego sets because a) there’s legitimately a set for everyone, and b) I want everyone to love Lego as much as I do. I will not rest until they do.

This year, I’m not waiting for the significant Black Friday sales, because to my surprise as a full-time deals tracker, I’m seeing a lot of great sets for up to 43 percent off on Amazon already.
