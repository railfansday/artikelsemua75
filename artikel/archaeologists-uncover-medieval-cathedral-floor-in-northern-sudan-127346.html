Archaeologists uncover medieval cathedral floor in northern Sudan

🌐 Source: Flipso • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (54 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that A Polish-Sudanese team uncovered the original floor of the Great Church of Jesus in Old Dongola, a monumental medieval cathedral that may have been larger than the White House. The find sheds new light on the scale and significance of one of Nubia’s most crucial Christian buildings.
