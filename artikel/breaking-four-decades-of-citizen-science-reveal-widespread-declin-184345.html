Breaking: Four decades of citizen science reveal widespread declines in native plant distributions in Japan

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that A latest study led by researchers at Kyushu University has found that nearly 45% of 1,864 native plant species surveyed across Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan, were recorded in fewer locations over approximately four decades. The findings, published in Communications Biology on Sept. 15, 2026, show how long-running citizen science surveys can reveal biodiversity changes that conventional assessments focused on threatened species may miss.
