TORRANCE, Calif., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Northwood Space Corp. ("Northwood") today unveiled completion of the key technical milestone of a prototype contract with the U.S. Space Force's (USSF) Space Systems Command (SSC), through the Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Battle Management Command, Control, Communications and Space Intelligence (BMC3I).

The key milestone involved a series of full-duplex TT&C contacts with a U.S. Government satellite in geosynchronous orbit (GEO), using Northwood's Portal phased array antenna. Northwood's network met the program's technical objectives within a year of award, with receive and transmit performance sufficient to command and control the spacecraft. Previous milestones included live-sky, full-duplex communications with a satellite in low Earth orbit (LEO). (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

Under the award with BMC3I, Northwood delivered advanced, assured connectivity services on its network, supporting the Space Force's augmentation from the Satellite Control Network (SCN) toward next-generation ground architecture. The operations for the key milestone ran through BMC3I's Joint Antenna Marketplace (JAM), a cloud-based system that improves satellite scheduling, resiliency and scalability through competitive prototyping. The combined commercial and government team onboarded the newly introduced site and the mission partner's configuration into the system in a matter of hours. "GEO satellites carry some of the most critical government and commercial missions, and they're some of the hardest links to close," stated Bridgit Mendler, co-founder and CEO of Northwood. "Our Portal system has now closed those links, building on earlier demonstrated capability in LEO. We engineered it for these hard, high-stakes missions, where government and commercial operators need assured TT&C across orbits, and we're proud to support Space Force PAE BMC3I and SSC's System Delta 85 in delivering this capability.".

Northwood's global ground station network, consisting of multi-beam phased array and high-throughput parabolic antennas, is purpose-built for diverse missions in contested operating environments, enabling simultaneous connections across LEO, medium Earth orbit (MEO), and GEO while delivering scalable capacity through commercial service agreements. This lets government and commercial operators add ground capacity rapidly, as missions need it. With the prototype complete, Northwood is eligible for a transition from prototype to production.

Northwood is building the network connecting spacecraft to Earth, starting with satellite ground stations. The software-defined, shared network lets customers connect with their entire fleet in hours instead of the years it would take to build their own ground network. Trusted by organizations and programs across commercial and U.S. and allied government space operators, Northwood delivers reliable, resilient, high-throughput links so operators are free to execute any mission and go further and faster.
