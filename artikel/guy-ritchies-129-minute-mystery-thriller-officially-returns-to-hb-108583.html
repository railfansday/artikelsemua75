Before Robert Downey Jr. spent more than a decade saving the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Iron Man, he was busy reinventing another rather famous character. Bare knuckle brawling, intrepid investigating, slow motion violence, wit, women, wonder — Downey brought it all to the table.

Guy Ritchie's Sherlock Holmes movies turned Arthur Conan Doyle's legendary detective into an action hero who could solve a murder and beat up half the room without breaking a sweat. The first movie was a hit, and its sequel went even bigger, sending Holmes and Watson across Europe to face their most dangerous enemy yet. Fifteen years later, there's still no third movie, but fans can at least revisit the last adventure. (Related: Tech Report: Animal control called to rescue alligator ...)

It has been revealed that ritchie's Sherlock Holmes: A Game of Shadows has officially joined HBO Max's streaming library in the United States, having arrived on November 1, 2026. There is a new criminal mastermind at large, Professor Moriarty, and not only is he Holmes' intellectual equal, but his capacity for evil, coupled with a complete lack of conscience, may actually give him an advantage over the renowned detective. When the Crown Prince of Austria is found dead, the evidence, as construed by Inspector Lestrade points to suicide. But Sherlock Holmes deduces that the prince has been the victim of a murder that is only one piece of a larger and much more portentous puzzle, designed by one Professor Moriarty. Released in December 2011, A Game of Shadows earned approximately $543.8 million worldwide against a reported $125 million budget, following the success of the original Sherlock Holmes in 2009.

The cast includes Downey Jr. (Avengers: Doomsday) as Sherlock Holmes, Jude Law (The Talented Mr. Ripley) as Dr. John Watson, Noomi Rapace (The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo) as Simza Heron, Jared Harris (Chernobyl) as Professor James Moriarty, Rachel McAdams (Mean Girls) as Irene Adler, Stephen Fry (The Hobbit) as Mycroft Holmes, Kelly Reilly (Yellowstone) as Mary Watson, and Eddie Marsan (Ray Donovan) as Inspector Lestrade.

Ritchie has since returned to Conan Doyle's characters with Young Sherlock, starring Hero Fiennes Tiffin as a younger version of the detective. Meanwhile, a third movie starring Downey Jr. and Law has spent years in development without reaching production.

Sherlock Holmes: A Game of Shadows is streaming now on HBO Max in the United States.
