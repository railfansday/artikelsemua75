Blizzard is making a lot of the Diablo 4's event-specific Diablo 2 anniversary content permanent because everyone loved it in Season 15

Blizzard has officially unveiled Diablo 4's next live-service season: Season of Tyrael's Gambit. This is the ARPG's 16th season, and it follows on from the popular and well-received Season of Hell's Legacy.

It has been revealed that in an unexpected move, Blizzard revealed it's keeping some of the nostalgia-laiden content from the previous season, Season of Hell's Legacy, as a permanent addition. This is unusual, as the ephemeral nature of live seasons means fresh content goes away at the end of each season. (Related: Find Orphan Pages and Buried Content: Build an Internal...)

Season of Hell's Legacy paid tribute to Diablo's legacy and its 30th anniversary by introducing a lot of content inspired by previous Diablo games, chiefly Diablo 2. The list includes the return of iconic character Deckard Cain, a recreation of Old Tristram, and a big fight against the three Prime Evils - Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto - in their classic forms. "We heard overwhelming requests for features from Season of Hell's Legacy to carry forward as permanent game features," Blizzard wrote in Season 16's announcement post. "While the entire season couldn't come along for the ride, we are implementing the following features with the launch of Season of Tyrael's Gambit that will carry forward in both Eternal and Seasonal realms.".

In a recent development, season of Tyrael's Gambit kicks off 1st December, so you still have time to access all of that in Diablo 4 until Season 15 ends. Certain things, such as craftable items, won't return, so you may as well try them while you can. That mentioned, Blizzard is aware of the love some of those items have, and the developer said it may bring them back in the future.
