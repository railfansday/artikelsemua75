Candace Cameron Bure is counting down the days until she can enjoy her newly expanded family’s merry holiday season with her two infant grandchildren in her arms.

“I know everyone’s grandchild is the cutest baby in the whole world, and mine are the cutest in the whole world because they’re mine,” Cameron Bure, 50, told People in an interview published Saturday, November 21, also revealing her “whole family” will be together on Thanksgiving and Christmas this year. Industry observers note that “Some family is flying in,” the Full House alum teased. “It’s going to be a significant family Thanksgiving and Christmas, and I couldn’t be more thrilled.”. (Related: Ukraine deploys homemade 'flying modems' in daring dron...)

Candace and her husband, Valeri Bure, are parents of daughter Natasha, 28, and sons Lev, 26, and Maksim, 24. The married couple recently became grandparents in September when Lev’s wife, Elliott Bure, gave birth to son Bond 10 weeks early. Natasha revealed the next month that she welcomed her first baby, daughter Winnie, with husband Bradley Steven Perry.

After Bond and Winnie’s arrivals, Candace has fully embraced her grandmother era — or “CandyGram” as she prefers to be called.

“It’s been incredible. There’s nothing like watching your own child become a parent,” Candace exclusively told Us earlier this month. “We just love the time and soaking it all up, and it’s been amazing.”.

According to Valeri, 52, he can barely find time to pry his wife away from the babies.

It has been revealed that “Candace is a baby hogger. She is the worst,” he jokingly told Us. “I would hold my grandkid and she would run up and just start taking it away and I’m like, ‘Stop, stop!’ I’m like, ‘This is my turn to hold the baby.’ So, I learned that she is a baby hogger. She’s taking it away from me.”.

Candace, who openly admitted to hogging Bond and Winnie’s snuggles, is also in awe that their arrivals made the former hockey star a “softy” in their presence.

“Listen, this is my time,” he quipped. “[Our kids are] like, ‘This is not our papa that we grew up with. Who is this person?’ I’m like, ‘Why is your baby crying? Give it to me. Like, baby should not be crying.’ Our kids was like, ‘Nope, they gotta cry it out.’ No crying for the grandbabies.”.

Candace and Valeri, who have been married for 30 years, have also been doling out helpful parenting advice to their kids.
