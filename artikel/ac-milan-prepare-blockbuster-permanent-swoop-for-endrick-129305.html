AC Milan are preparing a blockbuster swoop for Real Madrid starlet Endrick, according to a report from ESPN.

While the Los Blancos hierarchy might have made it publicly clear over the summer that they would not allow the Brazilian international to leave the club, Endrick has drastically failed to find any kind of regular first-team opportunities at the Bernabeu. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Along with registering just five La Liga appearances for the Spanish heavyweights this season, Endrick has also managed just 30 minutes of action from any of Madrid’s last three domestic showdowns on either side of November’s international break.

Enjoying what was a breakout spell after completing a January loan switch to French outfit Lyon last season, the 20-year-old registered 12 direct goal contributions in his 16 Ligue 1 appearances (five goals, seven assists).

With it speculated last month that the former Palmeiras sensation is once again keen on completing a January switch away from the Bernabeu, ESPN are reporting that AC Milan are preparing a blockbuster swoop.

ESPN suggested it over the weekend that Ruben Amorim’s side are actively working on a €70million offer and hope to complete a permanent deal when the window reopens.

However, while it might have been reported that Real Madrid could be easing to the idea of cashing in on the 26-cap international, ESPN are claiming that Los Blancos have not ruled out the prospect of including a buy-back clause in any potential deal.

El Milan prepara una oferta de compra por Endrick en enero. La información fue difundida por TNT Sports y confirmada por ESPN.

Sources indicate that según pudo saber ESPN, el Real Madrid está considerando pedir entre 60 y 70 millones de euros (casi 77 millones de dólares) por el joven delantero. Además, el conjunto blanco no descarta incluir una cláusula de recompra en el contrato en caso de que el brasileño destaque con la camiseta del Milan.
