In a recent development, prince Harry has surprised This Morning viewers with an extra-long appearance on the most famous sofa in Britain. The Duke of Sussex appeared alongside regular Friday hosts Dermot O’Leary and Alison Hammond and joined them for a few segments throughout the morning show.

Over the course of his appearance on the ITV chat show, Harry—who was there to discuss his work with the Invictus Games—revealed more insight into his children Lilibet and Archie’s new life in the UK and gave a hint at his upcoming Christmas intends with Meghan and their family. Here’s everything Prince Harry said throughout his appearance on This Morning.

Following the family’s move back to the UK in September earlier this year, Harry shared that the children, Lilibet and Archie, are finally getting British accents having spent most of their childhood so far in California, adding he was “squeezing the accents out of them.”.

He also revealed there was a bit of a “culture shift” for the children when it came to their fresh schooling as they now have to wear school uniform, something they didn’t have to do while in school in the U.S.

Recent findings suggest that “I think the kids being in school uniforms is a big change for them,” he explained. “But it has certainly helped. The confidence in both of them has gone through the roof since being in a UK school, which is really, really nice to see.”.

During his chat with Alison and Dermot, the hosts quizzed him on all things UK vs. USA, and Alison asked him for his thoughts on Robert Pattinson being cast as James Bond, to which Prince Harry responded, “I think it’s in great hands.” He also added he couldn’t wait to see the film.

He was also asked whether he preferred EastEnders or Suits (which is the show his wife Meghan Markle is most known for acting in) and he ultimately picked Suits.

Every Friday on This Morning, hosts Dermot and Alison pick a variety of music to play throughout the show, and Harry was also given the chance to pick a song for Friday.

He revealed his choice was “Runnin’ (Lose It All)” by Naughty Boy feat. Beyoncé and Arrow Benjamin, and added it’s the song he listens to while doing a cold plunge.

The Duke of Sussex joined Dermot and Alison for the cooking segment of the series where they were learning how to create a Christmas cake with Phil Vickery. (Related: Few-shot learning in practice: GPT-Neo and the 🤗 Accele...)

During the segment, the hosts asked him about whether he liked to bake and he revealed that children Lilibet and Archie liked to bake, but that the whole family was a fan of The Great British Bake Off, which is of course presented by Alison.

Harry added that the series was good this year and he was a big fan of Tom to win the series.

As the cooking segment wound down, Alison asked him who would be doing the cooking at Christmas in his home this year.

In a recent development, he went on to say his wife and mother-in-law were the better cooks in his household and so they would be doing the cooking for the festive season. The Duke also revealed they may do things differently this year and have a Christmas gumbo, too!
