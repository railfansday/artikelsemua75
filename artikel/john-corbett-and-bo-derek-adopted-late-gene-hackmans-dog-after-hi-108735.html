John Corbett and Bo Derek took in the late Gene Hackman and Betsy Arakawa’s dog Bear following their tragic deaths.

Derek, 70, revealed in a Friday, November 20, The Hollywood Reporter profile that she and Corbett, 65, initially needed convincing to adopt another pup in addition to their two German shepherds. After looking at a photo of Bear, Derek immediately changed her mind. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

“I was a goner,” the actress quipped, calling the pup a “perfect gentleman.”.

Us confirmed in February 2025 that Hackman and his wife had been found dead inside their New Mexico house. They were 95 and 64, respectively.

Industry observers note that “It is with great sadness that we announce the passing of our father, Gene Hackman and his wife, Betsy. He was loved and admired by millions around the world for his brilliant acting career, but to us he was always just Dad and Grandpa,” the Oscar winner’s daughters and granddaughter stated in a statement. “We will miss him sorely and are devastated by the loss.”. Hackman’s cause of death was ultimately ruled as hypertensive atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease with Alzheimer’s disease as a significant contributing factor. He died nearly one week after Arakawa, who died from hantavirus pulmonary syndrome.

In a recent development, hackman and Arakawa owned three dogs: Bear, Zinna and Nikita. Zinna died in February 2025 and was buried with Arakawa, while the other canines were taken into custody by a local Santa Fe animal shelter. The surviving pets also allegedly assisted members of the sheriff’s department in finding Hackman and Arakawa’s bodies.

“They realized [the dog] was trying to say, ‘Hey, come over here! Come over here!’” police chief Brian Moya recalled to USA Today, revealing the pet led the officers to a mudroom where Hackman was lying on the floor.

After rescuing the dogs, animal control called Derek and Corbett about a potential adoption. Once they agreed to take in the large dog, he was transported nearly 17 hours from Newly introduced Mexico.

It has been revealed that “Oh no,” Corbett recalled to THR of his first reaction to the dog’s large size. “What have we gotten ourselves into?”.
