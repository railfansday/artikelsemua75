Sources indicate that update [Sat 21st Nov 2026, 3:30am]: A fresh release date for the Switch version of The Wolf Among Us Remastered has appeared on the eShop this week. It's now "estimated" to launch on 29th November 2026, so we'll have to wait and see if the date actually sticks this time.

Original Story: [Sat 17th Oct, 2026 02:55 BST]: Telltale Games has announced the Nintendo Switch version of The Wolf Among Us Remastered "needs a little bit more time".

In an official statement shared on social media, it mentions how this remaster starring Sheriff Bigby Wolf will "launch as planned on October 29 for PlayStation, Xbox, and PC", and the "Nintendo Switch version will follow at a later date".
