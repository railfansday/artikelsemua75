📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Practical Claude, our guide series about Claude, reached KDP's series form on 17.11.2026 with no series description to paste. It was the fourth series in two months to stop there, after Exam Ready Study Guides (27.09), NeuraGrowth Little Coloring (04.10) and NeuraGrowth Little Dot Markers (25.10).

Sources indicate that the KDP package carried the series title and the book's number, never the description. The description lived in a vault note or in a file beside the package, so the person filling in the form had to go and find it.

Recent findings suggest that the change that shipped on 17.11.2026 in the new-Orqestra repository adds engine/kdp_series, a registry of series descriptions. It is stored as an AppSetting named kdp_series_registry, mapping series title to description, origin, date and first product. All four package builds now read it. It changed 8 files, with 662 lines added and 2 removed.

metadata.json now carries a series_kdp block with the title, Ordered or Unordered, the number, the description and the origin. When a series the registry does not know turns up as a first book, a model drafts the description. The record does not say more about how that draft is reviewed. Recent findings suggest that the same day, rebuilding the package for book #225, a Claude book, raised a warning. Seven of its "Check it yourself" boxes cite Claude support pages whose last-changed date is 2026-11-17, the same day we read them. The alert states to check whether each change came before the read or after it, and the record here does not settle that.
