Tech Report: Remedy CEO praises Rockstar's support for the Max Payne 1 & 2 remake and respecting Remedy's creative vision

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Alan Wake and Control studio Remedy Entertainment is presently developing a highly-anticipated Max Payne 1 & 2 remake. The Finnish studio is doing so in partnership with Grand Theft Auto 6 studio Rockstar, which owns the IP and is funding the remake's development.

So how has that relationship been? According to Remedy CEO Jean-Charles Gaudechon, working with Rockstar has been "amazing". "Honestly, it's been great," Gaudechon told Game File. "You know why? Because I think they're super respectful of Remedy.". (Related: Latest Update: Dividends Aren't What You Think The...)

Gaudechon is so positive on Remedy's working relationship with Rockstar that he emphasised his praise for Rockstar further. "I'll say it out loud: They've honestly been an awesome partner. And it's no bullshit," he added. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

The original Max Payne was released in 2001, and its sequel in 2003 - both developed by Remedy. Rockstar published the second game and worked on the original's console ports. Rockstar developed a third entry in the series, Max Payne 3, which Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser recently called something of a failure.

It has been revealed that "Some people liked it; lots didn't. So we got it wrong, or I got it wrong. I failed. My bit was not a success," said Houser.

The Max Payne 1 & 2 remake has been in the works since 2022, but only entered full production in 2024. The game is understood to be Remedy's current project, though the studio also has another unannounced project in the works.

In other Remedy news, the studio recently posted one of its best financial quarters, off the back of the release of Control Resonant, the studio's fastest-selling game ever.
