Pokémon Winds & Waves is not that far from release, with the game still slated to launch in 2027. However, before The Pokémon Tech giant has the opportunity to drop another trailer showcasing everything upcoming coming to the first exclusively next-gen entry, leaks and even official releases have set about spoiling much of what it has to offer. From Winds & Waves' battle system leaking early to art of the new Pokémon, we've already seen quite a lot, and more is undoubtedly on its way.

The latest reveal is of Pokémon Winds & Waves' 'mons. Previously, we've seen what Winds & Waves' legendaries look like, and now an official new release has given us a closer look at a handful of the game's other Pokémon. While this reveal may not be as exciting as some of the other bigger leaks, the fact that it's an official look at least makes it a more reliable insight into the Pokémon fans can expect to see in the upcoming Gen 10 games.

In a recent development, winds and Waves' Pokémon have been given the spotlight in official upcoming art from The Pokémon Firm as part of the series' 30th anniversary celebrations. The stunning new illustration showcases the starter Pokémon from across all generations, with the most recent, Gen 10's Browt, Pombon, and Gecqua placed front and center in the foreground. This is some of the cleanest art we've seen of the aforementioned starters outside the game, and a good look at them in more dynamic poses.

The Pokémon Firm has been fairly coy about showcasing any upcoming Pokémon beyond Browt, Pombon, and Gecqua. While we've officially seen 100 Pokémon in Winds and Waves thus far, when it comes to exclusive Gen 10 Pokémon, these starters are the only ones getting the spotlight. It's a shame, especially as fans may be sick of seeing them appear so much (they were even plastered on the sides of real-life planes), but understandable given that The Pokémon Company likely wants to keep some surprises for future trailers and the full game itself.

Sources indicate that it isn't clear when fans will start to get more official information regarding Winds and Waves. However, it is likely trailers and new promotional images will likely start rolling out around mid 2027 if Winds & Wave's rumored release date of November turns out to be true. We may even get one as early as Pokémon Day. Fans will undoubtedly be treated to deep dives on the Gen 10 Pokémon outside the three starters when these trailers begin to be released. With potentially a year left to wait until Pokémon Winds & Waves actually releases, we'll likely get to see some leaked new Pokémon very soon.
