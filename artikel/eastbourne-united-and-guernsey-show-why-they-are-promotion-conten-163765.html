A 4-1 defeat left Guernsey smarting and United in a very good position in second place in the SCFL Premier.

In a recent development, since then, the Green Lions have been on an unbeaten run, making themselves genuine play-off contenders along with United. It was obvious from the moment they arrived at the Oval that they would only be happy with all three points, preferably by at least the same scoreline. The early encounters went to United, and with just seven minutes on the clock James Jull shot home, only for the linesman to raise his flag for offside to howls of derision from the home support.

Guernsey started to make their mark with a couple of runs from Charlton Gauvain, but the visitors also left their mark on United’s players, collecting two yellow cards.

Hull went very close with an acrobatic scissor kick, and Joe Overy continued to needle away down the left wing, Charlie Ball forcing the keeper into a good save from one of Overy’s low crosses.

In first half added time, Guernsey took the lead with a fine shot from range beating Alieu Secka. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

United were unperturbed and Rhyle Ovenden was unfortunate not to even it up straight after the break, his free kick just scraping the top of the crossbar.

On the hour Hull was replaced by Callum Barlow, before the increasingly confident Ovenden had a shot well saved by the Lions keeper.

Five minutes later, Barlow’s lightning-fast cross found Overy, who turned and slotted home well for 1-1. Overy rarely misses an attempt in the area, and Guernsey looked crestfallen.

There was a third and a fourth yellow card for Guernsey, who were pondering returning with only a point, when Ball broke past the defences and slotted home with an inch perfect side foot to make it 2-1 to United to the joy of the crowd, and despair of the islanders.

In a recent development, rather than crumble, it galvanised Guernsey, and the United lead only lasted four minutes as Gauvain made it 2-2. Now it was a real blood and thunder contest, both teams showing exactly why they are in the top four, Secka made a wonderful save to keep it equal, Ball and Barlow both trying out the away keeper, but it remained 2-2.

After months out injured, the home crowd were delighted to see – on 85 minutes – Mason Creese come on, replacing Ball, and Freddie Jones took up position on the other flank replacing Overy.

According to recent reports, both added even more pace, and despite the cold, the crowd were heating up – while Guernsey overheated as they collected a fifth yellow in what was never a nasty encounter. As the final whistle went both teams were treated to an ovation as they left the field. This is the sort of fare non-league crowds absolutely love - hard tackling, fast attacking, stout defending and not a player from either side faking injury to get an opponent booked.

Both teams should be in the play-offs - one of them could be champions, time will tell.

Sources indicate that next up for United, this Saturday, is a home game with an improving Forest Row, who will be desperate for the points to help them out of the drop zone. It’s a 3pm kick-off at the Oval.
