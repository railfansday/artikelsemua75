Kevin Hart is as omnipresent as ever as 2026 winds down. The comedian, known for everything from the rom-com About Last Night to the buddy action flick Central Intelligence with Dwayne Johnson, has done it all this year, from being roasted by a series of celebrities hosted by Shane Gillis to helming his own stand-up competition in Funny AF with Kevin Hart, and, of course, starring in movies like 72 Hours. This Christmas, he's also getting back in the game with Jumanji: Open World, wrapping up the trilogy of live-action Jumanji reboot movies with a chaotic final act that brings the jungle to the real world. Before that, though, he's heading back to the 1990s once more to explore more stories based on his childhood.

December 9 marks the launch of Season 2 of Lil Kev, Hart's animated sitcom following his 12-year-old self in 1993 North Philadelphia. The series debuted last year as a chaotic portrayal of the comedian's childhood, shaped by the many schemes he partook in and the people around him, from his overprotective mother to his recovering father, troublemaker older brother, and ex-con uncle. After skating his way through an eventful Season 1, the show's second outing sees his actions catch up to him in the worst way. One moment of lashing out at the system forces him to grow up faster than expected when he lands in the juvenile justice system and becomes public enemy #1. Ahead of its premiere, Collider can exclusively share the official trailer and unveil the star-studded roster of guests entering this chaotic newly introduced chapter of Kev's life. The footage opens with Lil Kev in the middle of about the most trouble he could land himself in — behind the wheel of a stolen cop car in a slow-speed chase that brought out the entire police force. His family has surprisingly different reactions to his bout of grand theft auto, from cheering him on to threatening to punish him so harshly that even murderers are cowering in fear. For the rest of the neighborhood, it puts the Harts on the map, and not in the way they ever hoped. Kevin still gets himself in all kinds of wild trouble throughout the trailer, but he's also on a path to redemption for his mistakes. He's just not very good at it, as evidenced by the juvie riot he's behind, his failed attempt at early freedom by winning a spelling bee, and everyone trying to beat him up when he tries to make it up to them.

According to recent reports, A few of the massive names joining Hart's unfiltered animated hit are briefly shown off in the footage as well, including George Lopez, Ms. Pat, and Chris Rock, the latter of whom plays a stylish bicycle cop rolling with Kevin through oncoming traffic. They're just part of a massive collective of comedic minds joining Season 2, including Maria Bamford, Godfrey, Karlous Miller, Asif Ali, Wallace Shawn, Jess Hilarious, Rob Corddry, Phil Hendrie, Gabrielle Navaeh, Chris “Comedian CP” Powell, Donnie Simpson, and Rachel Stuart. Alongside Hart, the main stars also return, including Wanda Sykes as his mother Nancy, Deon Cole as Uncle Richard Jr., Jamar Malachi Neighbors as his brother Robert, and Gerald “Slink” Johnson as his dad Henry, with Anjelah Johnson-Reyes, Nicole Aimee Schreiber, Cree Summer, Mo Collins, Loretta Devine, Affion Crockett, Clayton Thomas, Justin Hires, Wé Ani, and Rick Ingraham rounding out the bunch.

Recent findings suggest that creators Matthew Claybrooks and Michael Price remain at the helm of Lil Kev Season 2 as showrunners and executive producers. This season is a bit different, though, with the series releasing under Paramount+ instead of BET+. In a statement to Collider, they expressed their excitement for the latest chapter with the Hart family. "We are thrilled to be part of the Paramount+ family and extremely proud of our work on season two," the duo stated. "We can’t wait to share the further animated adventures of Kev and HIS family with the world on December 9th.” For the animation, they once again worked with the prolific ShadowMachine, which has brought its touch to everything from Robot Chicken to Guillermo del Toro's Pinocchio, BoJack Horseman, and more recently, Long Story Short at Netflix.

Lil Kev Season 2 premieres all ten episodes on Paramount+ on December 9. Check out the trailer in the player above.
