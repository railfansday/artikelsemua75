📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In spite of the fact that Dell Technologies Inc. (NYSE: DELL) smashed third quarter (Q3) 2027 earnings, Amit Daryanani, a Wall Street analyst at Evercore ISI, has signaled a cautious bullish outlook.

In a recent development, on November 24, 2026, Daryanani reiterated a ‘Buy’ rating for Dell stock. He, however, lowered the firm’s 12-month price target for DELL to $400 from $650, representing a 38.46% decline.

With Dell stock trading at $295.28 at the time of reporting, Daryanani signals a potential 35.46% upside over the next 12 months.

Industry observers note that in September, the firm’s $650 target and $1,000 bull-case scenario for DELL relied on aggressive valuation expansion, projecting a 25-times forward price-to-earnings multiple. This outlook was paired with an upside fiscal year 2027 earnings per share scenario approaching $30, sitting well above the Wall Street consensus estimate of $25.88 and Evercore’s base estimate of $25.54. However, recent stock selloffs across artificial intelligence (AI) hardware equities forced a sharp downward reset to align near-term expectations for Dell with prevailing market pricing.

Following the cautious bullish outlook from Evercore, 21 Wall Street analysts surveyed by TipRanks over the last 3 months had set an average 12-month price target of $569.84.

As a result, the average DELL stock price target indicates fewer chances of a bull rally to a new all-time high (ATH) by November 24, 2027.

Wall Street analysts are moderately bullish on Dell stock over the next 12 months, despite beating Q3 revenue and EPS expectations. Specifically, Dell’s EPS and revenue beat analysts’ forecasts by 120.1% and 5.56%, respectively.

As Evercore signaled less bullish sentiment for Dell over the next four quarters despite record quarterly earnings, increased selling pressure had pushed the stock 11.9% lower on the day.
