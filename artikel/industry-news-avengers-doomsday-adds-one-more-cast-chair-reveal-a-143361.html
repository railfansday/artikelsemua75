The Avengers: Doomsday cast chair reveals show no signs of stopping as a possible Young Avenger joins the fray.

"So, I'm here with my movie Hexed, but it looks like they delivered a chair from a different movie?" Hailee Steinfeld, who plays Kate Bishop in the MCU, said in a new video posted by Marvel Studios on social media. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

A post shared by Marvel Studios (@marvelstudios).

Steinfeld first played Kate Bishop in Disney Plus series Hawkeye, before making a post-credits appearance two years later as Kamala Khan began recruiting for a fresh, younger team in The Marvels.

Recent findings suggest that since then, there have been plenty of rumblings about Young Avengers turning up in full force around Avengers: Doomsday. Kathryn Newton's Cassie Lang is part of the cast, but no other prospective or rumored members are currently confirmed for the crossover movie. The likes of Wiccan, Speed, and Ms. Marvel have been shown in concept art that did the rounds last year. And, hey, we need to see what happened to Hulk's son too, right?

The trend of Avengers: Doomsday's cast being declared via chairs began in March 2025, with a multi-hour stream that unveiled a who's who of Marvel actors – including OG Avengers, the Fantastic Four and Thunderbolts cast, and a sizable portion of Fox's X-Men roster.

There's no sign of Kate Bishop's mentor Hawkeye stringing up his bow anytime soon, however. Hawkeye actor Jeremy Renner posted a cryptic tease on Instagram back in August – so it seems there are plenty more secrets left for Doomsday to tell.

Avengers: Doomsday hits cinemas on December 16 in the UK and December 18 in the US.
