Nvidia's RTX Spark chip burst onto the laptop market just a month ago, and at least one model carrying Nvidia's first dedicated mobile SoC is already discounted for Black Friday. Both the 24GB and 32GB configurations of the Asus ProArt P14 are $200 off at Best Buy, which is a surprising discount to see so soon after the device released.

The discount is partially explained by memory capacity, as we haven't seen any discounts on devices with 128GB of unified memory that fully unlock the capabilities of the GB10 chip. At either 32GB or 24GB of memory, you won't be able to run most of the larger AI models available, at least at any reasonable speed. You'll need to resort to smaller, heavily quantized models. The Asus ProArt P14 with RTX Spark is a 14-inch laptop with 512GB of storage. This model includes 32GB of unified memory, a 6,144-core Blackwell GPU, and an 20-core Grace CPU.
