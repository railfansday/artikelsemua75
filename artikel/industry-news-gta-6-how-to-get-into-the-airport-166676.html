In a recent development, whether it’s driving around the vast city skyline of Vice City, boating in the Pacific Ocean or taking to the skies, there’s no shortage of ways to see Grand Theft Auto 6 in all of its beauty. While you won’t be taking planes to Liberty City or Los Santos, you can break into the airport and steal a plane.

Sources indicate that there are a couple of ways to get into the airport, not one being through the arrivals or departure terminals. To start things off, you will need to know where it’s located. The VCIA (Vice City International Airport) is located near the middle of the map, just west of the Salton district.

According to recent reports, probably the easiest way to get in is flying in from a helicopter or from a plane you obtained from the early portions of the game. You will be picked up pretty promptly as the threshold for the radar is low, but it’s definitely the simplest method in finding your way in. Recent findings suggest that outside these options, there are some really easy entrances to the airport if you don’t have a plane or helicopter at your disposal. While driving is probably the way to go, there are fences on the east and west portions of the map that can get in from.

Most of it is barbed, but you will find some that is a simple fence to hop over (as shown in the photo). Simply jump over and sneak around. Unfortunately, when you steal a commercial vehicle such as a Dreamliner in Grand Theft Auto 6, things are not going to go well for you. People will immediately notice when an aircraft isn’t supposed to leave and takes off.

In a recent development, if you completed most of chapter 4 of the story, you’ll know that the airport has numerous safety measures, including snipers perched on every corner. On top of that, air traffic control will immediately catch wind of what you’re doing.

It has been revealed that sadly, when you get off the ground, you will be met with multiple stars, and they will immediately call-in fighter jets. If you don’t put the plane down, they will do so forcefully. You will have a limited amount of time to finish your joyride before it’s removed from the air permanently.
