Ken Urker’s mom, Raina Urker, gave a candid insight into her son’s final days.

Speaking to TMZ in an interview on Thursday, November 19, Raina mentioned Ken was struggling with his split from ex-fiancée Gypsy Rose Blanchard as well as “cruel” cyberbullying before his death.

“He would internalize what people noted about him on the internet, and it bothered him when it was just so cruel,” she told the outlet.

Raina remarked Ken was particularly concerned about how the negative online commentary would affect his 22-month-old daughter, Aurora, whom he shared with Blanchard, 35.

“It was such cruel things being said about him, and that would bother him because of Aurora,” Raina continued. “He didn’t want Aurora to grow up and see these things, and, of course, phone calls were coming out from prison, and that bothered him.”. Confirming that Ken and Blanchard were not in a relationship at the time of his death, Raina added that the couple’s breakup was also weighing heavily on his mind.

“Gypsy and him were going through ins and outs, and that bothered him because he very much did not want Aurora to be raised with split parents,” Raina noted, adding that a united family unit was something Ken “wanted his whole life.”. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

“He wanted the parents to be together,” she explained.

Raina also insisted that Ken never would have died by suicide despite the speculation surrounding his death, stating, “He loved Aurora way too much to have done anything like that.”.

News broke in October that Ken had been found dead inside his Louisiana home on his 34th birthday.

The investigation into Ken’s death “remains ongoing” after a coroner confirmed he died from an accidental overdose.

Recent findings suggest that “The Lafourche Parish Sheriff’s Office continues to investigate the circumstances surrounding the death of Kenan Urker,” the sheriff’s office told Us in a Monday, November 15, statement. “While we understand the Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office has released updated information regarding the cause of death, our investigation is separate and remains ongoing.”.

Recent findings suggest that authorities added, “At this time, we have no additional information to release. We will provide an update when we reach a point in the investigation where additional information can be shared.”.

Earlier on Monday, Lafourche Parish Coroner’s Office confirmed that Ken’s cause of death was an accidental overdose.

“The final cause of death has been determined to be accidental poly substance drug toxicity,” a statement read. “The case is now complete, and no further testing is pending.”.

Sources indicate that ken had a complicated relationship with Blanchard. The pair were first connected when Ken wrote to her in prison in 2017, and they subsequently became pen pals.

At the time, she was behind bars for her role in the 2015 murder of her mother, Clauddine “Dee Dee” Blanchard.
