Three Kyiv bridges hit during major Russian missile attack

Russia launched a major missile and drone attack on several Ukrainian cities overnight.

Russia has targeted bridges in Kyiv and killed six during one of the largest air attacks of the war so far, days after Ukraine inflicted heavy damage on the strategically crucial Crimean Bridge.

Two people were also killed in Russian strikes on a railway station in the western city of Lviv. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Ballistic missiles hit three bridges over the Dnipro River, vital routes which people in Kyiv rely on to move around the city.

Russian President Vladimir Putin had threatened to retaliate for Ukraine's strike on the Crimean Bridge, which the Kremlin built to link Russia and the occupied peninsula.

The three Kyiv bridges targeted overnight - the Podilsko-Voskresensky Bridge, the Darnytskyi Bridge and the Southern Bridge - have been closed to traffic, adding to disruption created by retaliatory strikes on bridges earlier this week.

Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko urged people to "avoid travelling into the city by private car unless absolutely necessary".

He added that an unspecified critical infrastructure facility was also damaged and seven medical personnel were injured during the night's assault.

Ukraine's major operation to take out the road section of the Crimean Bridge earlier this week was a tactical and symbolic blow for Moscow.

Since illegally annexing the Crimea in 2014, Moscow has housed troops and military equipment there, with occupying forces travelling through the peninsula towards the front line.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky stated an operation to weaken the bridge's defences and ultimately cause part of it to collapse had been weeks in the making.

Russian President Vladimir Putin - who personally opened the bridge in 2019 to international condemnation - remarked the Kremlin's retaliation for the strike would be "far more painful".

Air raid sirens lasted non-stop throughout the night and were ongoing into the morning.

Ukraine's air force says almost 100 missiles were debuted by Russia in its overnight attack - one of the largest attacks since the invasion began in 2022. It also involved around 400 drones.

The attack was "one of the most powerful" yet, according to Yuriy Ignat, head of communications for the Ukrainian air force.

Prime Minister Serhiy Koretsky mentioned 28 people were injured, with an ambulance hospital in Kharkiv among the targets of an attack which hit nine regions.

Two people were killed in a hit on the Sknyliv railway station in the south-west of Lviv, the city's Mayor Andriy Sadovyi said.

He shared a picture of the ruined remains of what he said was the station's ticket terminal.

At least two people were killed in a strike on a railway station in western Ukraine.

Ten people were injured and a rescue operation at the site is ongoing.

A Russian projectile reportedly landed inside Poland, with the armed forces searching a site 45km (28 miles) from its border with Ukraine.

Polish jets were scrambled on Nato's eastern flank as Russia's attack on western Ukraine continued.
