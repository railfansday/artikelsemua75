Latest Update: Bare fist, megastructure climbing roguelike White Knuckle gets its penultimate region in The Core, a sterile, hostile reactor sector that you'll probably die in

Sources indicate that the beauty of stepping into a digital world is that it allows me to accomplish things I couldn't possibly dream of doing in the real one, like, say, climbing anything that's higher than your average step ladder. White Knuckle is one such game that's all about playing around with uncomfortable heights, and a newly introduced enhancement for the early access roguelike went live this week introducing a new, hostile environment: The Core.

Recent findings suggest that the sixth region to be added to the game, and the "penultimate challenge," implying that there's just one more area to come after this one, The Core takes you to Habitation Blue's reactor sector. "Use everything you have available to fight through the now Mass-controlled Superstructure Automated Processing System (SAPS) and her array of mechanized defenses," explains a Steam news post. "The Core is the sterile power station of the Superstructure, a massive network of coolant lines, offices, catwalks, and more, all of which is highly secured by an army of turrets, drones, and androids. Even without the Mass hot on your tail it would not be easy to escape.". (Related: Tiny Agents: an MCP-powered agent in 50 lines of code...)

There are a few newly introduced subregions to be found too, of course, but more importantly a new major artifact. It's called The Scissors, and with it you cut off your hands ("yes, really") to "gain a spectral form that increases your reach, hand stamina, and damage," while using the artifact. You can also throw the scissors like a boomerang to hurt enemies from afar, and "killing enemies also counts as eating if using Survival Mode binding, or the perks Conditioned Polyphagia & Unstoppable Conviction.".

Beyond this there's a selection of fresh weapons, like the piton crossbow which shoots "pitons at range, to set up routes and to deal extreme damage." The pulse pump is a new trinket that introduces a chargeable mid-air jump. There's new perks like one called nest egg, where you gain a "second major artifact at the start of the Nest, at the cost of halving all roach deposits in your run." Previous regions have also received some new room tiles, and there's a few fresh cosmetics to pick up too. There's some secret thing to find in this upgrade as well, "but you'll need to play to find out…" Hop to it, I guess! (Related: Introducing RTEB: A Fresh Standard for Retrieval Evalua...)
