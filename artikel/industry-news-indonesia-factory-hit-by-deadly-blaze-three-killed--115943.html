Industry News: Indonesia factory hit by deadly blaze: Three killed and 36 missing

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

At least three people have been killed and dozens are missing after a fire broke out at a factory in Indonesia producing sweets and snack products. Fifteen people were rescued alive from the plant, while firefighters were still battling flames in several parts of the facility more than 12 hours after the blaze began. The cause of the fire remains unknown, but survivors mentioned it started in a cooling unit on the third floor.
