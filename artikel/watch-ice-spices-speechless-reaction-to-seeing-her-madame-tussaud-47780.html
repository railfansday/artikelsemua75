Ice Spice got the wax-figure treatment at Madame Tussauds Newly introduced York on Thursday (Nov. 19), and the Bronx rapper was gagged at seeing her waxy twin for the first time — to the point where she was a little spooked at how the artists nailed her look, leaving her briefly speechless.

The look sported by Ice’s figurine takes fans back to 2022 and 2023, when she was rising star working her way up the rap ranks with her signature copper curls.

She’s rocking a red tiger catsuit, which was donated from the “Deli” rapper’s wardrobe, along with Black Air Jordan 4s, a replica of the diamond-encrused bracelet gifted to her by Doja Cat and her Icy Princess pendant chain.

Recent findings suggest that “It’s so accurate. I have a really unique face and I’m impressed they got it so right,” she mentioned before later joking, “This is so accurate, like oh my God, my big-ass forehead, you literally got it.”.

Sources indicate that the 26-year-old posted a series of photos alongside her wax sister to social media on Friday (Nov. 20). “Put it on wax ;),” she wrote in her caption.

Ice Spice (@icespice) reacts to seeing her wax figure for the first time @TussaudsUSA pic.twitter.com/4xtw5WHdUj.

The Ice Spice wax figure will be on display at the Times Square location of Madame Tussauds Fresh York in the heart of NYC into 2027.

“Ice Spice is a defining part of Fresh York’s music and culture scene, so bringing her into Madame Tussauds New York feels especially meaningful,” said Tiago Mogadouro, who serves as the general manager for Madame Tussauds New York. “She represents a new generation of artists who are shaping the city’s identity in real time, and we’re excited to give fans a way to celebrate that right here in Times Square.”.
