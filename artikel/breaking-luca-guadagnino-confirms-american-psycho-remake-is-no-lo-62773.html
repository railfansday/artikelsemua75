A remake of the classic horror film, American Psycho, receives an unfortunate upgrade.

It has been revealed that during an appearance on Deadline's Crew Call podcast with Anthony D'Alessandro, Luca Guadagnino addressed the status of his American Psycho remake. Although he praised the screenplay written by Scott Z. Burns, the director revealed that the project is no longer happening. However, he did not explain why the film has been canceled or whether there are any intends to revive it in the future.
