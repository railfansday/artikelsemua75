When CNN’s Kaitlan Collins pointed out that Russia is one of Iran’s biggest allies and asked Trump if Russia is a “friend to America,” the president of the United States of America said he ”can’t speak to America.”.

Recent findings suggest that so who can he speak for? Himself, of course, and the autocratic rulers like Putin whom he considered his close personal friends.

In a recent development, “I can’t speak to America,” Trump said, speaking from his position as the chief representative of the American people, government, and territory. “I’ve been dealing with Putin during the fake Russia-Russia-Russia hoax, and to be honest, he was very nice about it … and I consider him to be somebody that I have a very good relationship [with].”.
