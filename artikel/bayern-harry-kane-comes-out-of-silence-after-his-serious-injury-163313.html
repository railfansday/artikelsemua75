Harry Kane spoke after his injury announcement. Hit in the left ankle and forced to stay away from the field for several months, the Bayern Munich striker did not hide his disappointment. But the England international already prefers to look to the future and intends to make this difficult period an opportunity to come back even stronger. “The ups and downs of life! Obviously, I am very disappointed with this injury, but as always, it is an opportunity to show resilience and progress towards 2027.he wrote on Instagram.

A post shared by Harry Kane (@harrykane).

It has been revealed that “I will come back fresh and ready to attack the home stretch of the season! “Difficult times don’t last, strong people do”, it was my friend Julian Edelman who taught me this phrase! Thank you all for your many messages! Everything is fine !”he added. The Bavarian striker nevertheless wants to be optimistic and hopes to return to the pitch in top form for the last part of the season.
