Mariah Carey has a message to her supportive fans as she continues her annual holiday tour.

“Oh, and by the way, don’t believe everything you read on the internet,” Carey, 57, shared during her Saturday, November 21, performance at the Farmasi Arena in Rio de Janeiro. “Don’t be making me mad now.”. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

The Grammy winner proceeded to perform her 2010 song “Oh Santa!”.

Carey’s comments come after the singer kicked off her annual Christmas Time concert series in Brazil. Some concert footage went viral, with fans claiming Carey appeared “stiff” onstage when she performed “Fantasy” in São Paulo. (Related: Industry News: ‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok f...)

The “Obsessed” singer’s concert series is scheduled to last through December 12 with shows in England, Malta and the United Arab Emirates.

For many music fans, the holiday season isn’t complete without some Carey Christmas songs.

When asked to share her holiday traditions, Carey admitted that she goes major for her 15-year-old twins, Moroccan and Monroe, who she shares with ex-husband Nick Cannon.

“I know a lot of the time people are like, ‘Oh, yay! Look at her! She’s, like, so festive and such a Christmas girl,’ or whatever. But, really, Christmas makes me happy,” she told W magazine in November 2022. “People think I had this princess-style life or whatever, a kind of fairy-tale existence where I just emerged, like, ‘Here I am!’ And that is not what it is.”.

Sources indicate that she continued: “But when you grow up with a messed-up life and then you’re able to have this transformation where you can make your life what you want it to be? That is joy for me. That’s why I want my kids to have everything they can have. I want them to be able to understand that they can be anything they want to be.”.

For fans unable to see Carey live before the holidays, they can experience her Christmas magic at Mariah Carey’s Holiday Bar.
