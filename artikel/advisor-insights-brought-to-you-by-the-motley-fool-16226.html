What it means for your practice:.

Sources indicate that tech's slide has put losses on the books in some taxable accounts, and there are six weeks left to use them. Realized losses offset realized gains dollar for dollar, then up to $3,000 of ordinary income, with the rest carried forward. The catch is the wash-sale rule: buying the same or a substantially identical security within 30 days before or after the sale disallows the loss, and that includes purchases in a spouse's account or an IRA. Pair this with last week's year-end distribution estimates, since a harvested loss can absorb a fund's capital gain payout. Try: "The recent drop gives us a chance to turn paper losses into a lower tax bill, and I'll make sure the replacement holding keeps you invested.".

Nvidia answered last week's question and walked straight into a fresh one. In its first report since Jensen Huang's resignation, the company beat on revenue, earnings and guidance, then watched its shares jump after hours and flip to a loss. The stock closed down about 3.3% Thursday and is down more than a quarter this month, about $2.2 trillion below its October peak in market value.

Recent findings suggest that our read: the numbers back up last week's argument that the business is intact. Kress also remarked Nvidia's additional share repurchases now top $150 billion, and told CNBC the board has no plans to bring Huang back. But the stock was never trading on this quarter. It is trading on three open questions: who runs Nvidia permanently, how much margin gives way as volume climbs, and how long customers keep spending on AI at this pace. One analyst told Reuters the quarter was the kind Nvidia has delivered for years, which is exactly the point: a beat is the price of admission, not a catalyst. What to do with Nvidia shares still depends on position size and why a client owns them: a large position bought on conviction in one leader deserves a risk check, while a smaller position held for the products and AI demand has a thesis that this quarter supported.

What it means for your practice:.

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share on Nov. 18, above its $70-$78 target range, and began trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. The tech giant and selling stockholders sold 980 million shares for $81.3 billion, topping SpaceX's $75 billion offering from June as the largest on record, at a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion. The stock opened at $111.50, 34% above the offer, and touched $115 before fading to close up roughly 15%-17% from the offer, around $97, even as the broader market fell.
