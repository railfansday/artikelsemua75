Taylor Swift is never on her own, kid.

Best believe the singer was bejeweled when she walked in the room to perform her song "I Knew It, I Knew You" at the 2026 CMA Awards. And though Travis Kelce wasn't at Bridgestone Arena to cheer on his wife, he did share his support online.

It has been revealed that after all, the Kansas City Chiefs tight end hit the like button on the Nov. 18 Instagram video of her performance—her first at the annual country music awards show since 2013—posted by Swift's official Taylor Nation account.

Recent findings suggest that swift proved there was magic in the air as she swapped her bold yellow Oscar de la Renta gown for the perfect homage to Toy Story's Jessie: a frock and cowgirl boots. And she didn't shake off her country roots, mashing up her new track with 2007's "Our Song" from her debut album.

While Swift attended the show without her husband—whose team beat the Atlanta Falcons 35-13 on Nov. 15—host Lainey Wilson and longtime friend Brad Paisley teased the newlyweds for hosting their nuptials at the home of the Latest York Knicks and the New York Rangers in July. "I'll never forget what my husband remarked to me when we went looking for a wedding venue," Wilson—who married Devlin "Duck" Hodges in May—said before breaking out into song with Paisley: "'I beg your pardon, but I never promised you Madison Square Garden.'".

The "Patient Zero" singer was hanging tight, honey, as she laughed into pal Brittany Mahomes' shoulder after the joke—a reference to Lynn Anderson's 1999 hit "I Beg Your Pardon (I Never Promised You A Rose Garden).".

Swift mouthed to Wilson and Paisley—both of whom attended her fairy-tale wedding—after the quip, "That was good.".

As for why Swift and Kelce decided MSG was the perfect location for their white-veil occasion?

In a recent development, "We wanted to do it somewhere where we could have a very intimate and genuine setting without a lot of distractions," the NFL player explained on his Upcoming Heights podcast in September. "We needed it to be a private feel. MSG went above and beyond to give us that.”.

But it wasn't just the venue itself that helped sparks fly that evening in July.
