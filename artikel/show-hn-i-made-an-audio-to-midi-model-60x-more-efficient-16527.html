I began working on dettato about 10 months ago, but scrapped what I had in about April. The reason why was that I treated AMT as a data problem - but in reality, the bottleneck is architecture. There are a lot of parellels that you can draw for OMR (first model i made) and AMT. both traditionally were tackled early on using algorithms. later, in both fields they plugged in giant LLM components. These models would naturally become overfit to synthetic data, since globally the problems of AMT and OMR become different than if you treat it locally.
