Kaiser Exhibits Strong Prospects Despite Persisting Headwinds

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (253 words, 📖 Standard)

Kaiser Aluminum Corporation KALU is benefiting from rising commercial aerospace production, the completion of customer inventory destocking and sustained demand across defense, space and business jet applications. The company's Phase VII expansion at its Trentwood facility has increased plate production capacity, enabling it to capitalize on growing demand and strengthen its position in the aerospace market. In the third quarter of 2026, Aerospace/ High-Strength product’s conversion revenues increased 31% year over year to $131 million, supported by a 42% rise in shipments. The increase was mainly due to higher plate production after the planned shutdown at Trentwood in the same quarter last year.Kaiser is gaining from rising demand for higher-value coated aluminum products in the food and beverage packaging markets. The company's strategic investments at its Warrick facility, particularly the fourth coating line, are supporting a favorable product mix, higher conversion revenues and improved profitability. In the third quarter, Packaging conversion revenues increased 31% year over year to $180 million, supported by a 6% rise in shipments. The growth was primarily driven by the continued shift toward higher-margin coated products, additional coating qualifications and favorable pricing.Rising demand for specialized aluminum products, supported by semiconductor industry expansion, domestic manufacturing activity and inventory restocking at service centers, is aiding KALU. The company's KaiserSelect product offerings, technical expertise and reliable customer service are supporting higher shipments and favorable pricing. In the third quarter, General Engineering conversion revenues increased 21% year over year to $99 million, driven by a 12% rise in shipments.
