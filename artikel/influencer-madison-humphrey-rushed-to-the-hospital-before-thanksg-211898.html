Madison Humphrey appears to have suffered a serious health scare during Thanksgiving week.

“One minute you’re watching the Dancing With the Stars finale and the next, being rushed to the ER,” Humphrey, 27, wrote via her Instagram Story on Wednesday, November 25. “Thank you to the incredible medical staff who work holidays, nights, days and long hours to keep us all safe and healthy.”.

In her social media post, the influencer was seen resting on a hospital bed after being cared for by doctors and professionals.

Us has reached out to Humphrey’s rep for additional comment.

The content creator is well known for her comedic sketches and parody videos on TikTok. She has earned more than five million followers thanks to her various characters, including “Divorced Mom” and “Leasing Manager” personas.

Recently, the TikTok star was able to attend the 2026 CMA Awards in Nashville and walk the red carpet.

“Always a good time with @cma 🤠,” she wrote via Instagram on November 19 after attending the annual show at Music City Center.

Humphrey later poked fun at an article that highlighted the guests who may not have had the best looks of the night.

“POV You made the worst dressed at the CMAs list,” she wrote via TikTok. “No but they kinda ate up that description ngl #FYP.”.

Humphrey was previously named one of 10 creators in the Icon category of TikTok’s 2026 The Discover List.
