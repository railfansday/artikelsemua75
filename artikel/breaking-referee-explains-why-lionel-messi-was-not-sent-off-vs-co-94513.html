An explanation has emerged after Lionel Messi controversially avoided a red card during Inter Miami’s 3-0 defeat against Columbus Crew on Saturday evening.

Messi, widely considered the greatest footballer of all time, threw the ball in the face of Gonzalo Tapia after being blocked from taking a quick throw-in.

Sources indicate that however, Major League Soccer (MLS) referee Allen Chapman surprised many by only showing Messi a yellow card. Chile International Tapia was also booked for his part in the incident, which occurred on the stroke of half-time.

Inter Miami were already 1-0 down and one man down by this point.

Tapia opened the scoring with an early penalty before Gonzalo Lujan was sent off in the 36th minute for two bookable offences.

But Messi was allowed to stay on the pitch after Chapman decided the eight-time Ballon d’Or winner’s actions didn’t warrant a straight red card.

Despite the fact Messi was allowed to continue, Inter Miami slumped to a 3-0 defeat as second-half goals from Taha Habroune and Tapia sealed an impressive victory for Columbus Crew in the Round One best-of-three play-off clash.

According to recent reports, “Everybody knows that he deserved more than a yellow card,” Courtois stated in his post-match press conference, per The Guardian. “It’s just really sad.”.
