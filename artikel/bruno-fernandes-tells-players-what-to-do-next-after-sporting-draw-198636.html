Bruno Fernandes has urged Manchester United to stick together and fight for their season after they were held to a 2-2 draw by Sporting Lisbon in the Champions League.

In a recent development, the United captain scored twice on his return to the stadium, putting Manchester United ahead after Luis Suarez had given Sporting the lead. The home striker also scored twice, including a penalty, to ensure the points were shared. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

According to recent reports, senne Lammens had earlier saved a Suarez spot-kick, but the United goalkeeper was involved in another penalty incident that allowed the Sporting forward to find the net. United also had two goals ruled out after some controversial decisions, while Youri Tielemans was sent off late in the contest.

In a recent development, it was another frustrating result for United, who are still searching for their first win under Mauricio Pochettino. Fernandes believes there were positives in the performance, but insists the team must now focus on getting results. (Related: Breaking: 3D Asset Generation: AI for Game Development ...)

Speaking to TNT Sports after the match, Fernandes revealed that Pochettino wanted his players to show more aggression after the break.

In a recent development, “[At half-time the manager] wanted more aggression. more bodies. The first goal comes from that.”.

According to recent reports, the Portuguese midfielder felt United had controlled much of the first half despite conceding. He admitted they needed more presence in the final third.

It has been revealed that “Obviously, I think we started the game well. We gave a penalty away where Senne [Lammens] saved it. We gave one or two counters, but we were very good in recovery positions.

According to recent reports, “They got the goal, but we were still in control of the game. We didn’t have much people in the last third. Second half we got the two goals. That was the moment we should push a bit more.”.

United’s inability to hold on to their lead was another setback, but Fernandes refused to single out individuals for the mistakes.

In a recent development, “Mistakes are part of football and from that you get goals. Everyone has to take the blame for everything we do good and we do bad as well. We need to be together in the good and in the bad moments.”.

It has been revealed that “We need points to get through. and we need to focus on the next one. Now the moment is to get back to the Premier League. We need to stick together and fight for our lives.”.
