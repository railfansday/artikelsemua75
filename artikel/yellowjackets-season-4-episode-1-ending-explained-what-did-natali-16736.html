📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

One of the greatest things about showrunners Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson’s psychological horror show Yellowjackets is that you never know what bananas shenanigans will happen next. We’re only one episode into Yellowjackets season 4, and we’re happy to report that this still holds true, with a cliffhanger ending that will leave you wondering what the hell just happened.

It has been revealed that at the end of season 3, teen Natalie (Sophie Thatcher) had just managed to contact someone outside the remote wilderness using the satellite phone they’d recovered from the scientific expedition that had recently discovered them. Back at the camp, teen Shauna (Sophie Nélisse), now in charge, is furious at Natalie for calling for help behind her back.

This article contains spoilers for Yellowjackets season 4 episode 1, “Hivemind.”.

It was established in the first season of Yellowjackets that the girls (and Travis) would eventually be rescued, as the time jumps between 2021 and 1996 make clear. However, the mystery was more about how that would happen and what the consequences of the Yellowjackets' rescue would be. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

In “Hivemind,” the outcome of that phone call is made pretty clear. The girls and Travis are rescued and brought to a hospital in Canada, though several are missing, including poor Mari Ibarra (Alexa Barajas), who ended up being skewered in the same pit she was previously rescued from during one of the girls’ cannibalistic hunts in season 3. This leads to a few emotional scenes of the parents of the dead breaking down and the survivors exchanging agonizingly guilty looks. Industry observers note that at first, it looks like we’ll skip the immediate fallout as the rest of the Yellowjackets learn about Natalie’s call for help. Most of “Hivemind” in the teen timeline focuses on the reunion between the parents and survivors, and the teens’ efforts to cover up the deplorable things they did in the wilderness by lying to the ranger and therapist who question them.

In a recent development, that is, until the end of the episode, when the story jumps back to the wilderness, before the rescue. We see Natalie stumble back into camp, wide-eyed and terrified. She then asks a question that I too say at the end of every Yellowjackets episode: “What the fuck?”.

Yellowjackets’ first episode may have ended on a truly diabolical cliffhanger, but that doesn’t mean we don’t have clues about what Natalie saw to make her react with so much shock.

Sources indicate that for one thing, the opening credits and teaser trailers for the fourth season have shown clips of teen Shauna looking battered and beaten. We know, based on the snow surrounding Shauna in this scene, that unless there is a time skip, her looking this way is likely to take place during the Yellowjackets' time in the wilderness.

Recent findings suggest that what reason would the girls have to attack Shauna? A lot. Unlike Tai (Jasmin Savoy Brown), who wants to conceal the cannibalism and murders the girls committed during their time there, and Lottie (Courtney Eaton), who genuinely believes she has a spiritual connection to the wilderness’s supernatural goings-on, Shauna’s reason for staying in the wilderness is pretty damn selfish: she enjoys the power and hedonism that wilderness life encourages.

Her desire to be the alpha of this pack of wolves in girls’ clothing has, unfortunately, made her an easy target for the others’ hatred. While most of the girls want to go home, Shauna refuses to let them and has even trained a gun on some of her fellow Yellowjackets, including her situationship, Melissa (Jenna Burgess).

Sources indicate that if all the girls have turned on Shauna and left her in a bad way, Natalie could be reacting to that. After all, the first episode does show that teen Shauna has several scars and bruises on her face in the hospital, that weren’t there when we saw Shauna at the end of season 3.

That being said, we do know a handful of the girls don’t make it back to civilization — including the lone science researcher Hannah (Ashley Sutton), who murdered one of her own last season in a bid to be accepted by the teens. That appealed to Shauna for a little while, but given how volatile Shauna is about being rescued, and how little they know about Hannah, it's also likely that Natalie has seen Shauna and the others turn on Hannah as a consequence of Natalie’s call for help.

What Natalie has seen likely won’t be confirmed until next week. Even so, Yellowjackets likes to surprise its audience, and we wouldn’t be shocked if something we haven’t speculated about has turned up at camp, making the arrival of people searching for the Yellowjackets even more precarious.

Yellowjackets streams exclusively on Paramount Plus. Seasons 1-3 are available now, and season 4 premieres on Nov. 20, with episodes airing through Jan. 22, 2027.
