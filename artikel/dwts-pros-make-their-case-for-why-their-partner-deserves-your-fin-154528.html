As the countdown continues for the Dancing With the Stars season 35 finale, this year’s pros are making a strong case as to why their partner deserves America’s vote.

In a recent development, “[Amber Glenn is] the most deserving. She put herself out there – fully, 100 percent, vulnerable — every week, diving into every single style, trying to understand its essence through thick and thin,” Pasha Pashkov exclusively told Us before the Tuesday, November 24, finale. “[She was] fighting through any pain, any doubts and leaving her heart on the floor every single week.”. Moments after advancing, Arnold, 21, couldn’t help but gush over her partner and friend, vowing to keep working toward a standing ovation and the coveted mirrorball trophy.

“He has brought so much light into my life, and I definitely know he’s brought a lot of light into everyone else’s lives,” she told Us. “He is the most dedicated, hardworking person I know, and the most ‘I can do this’ person.”.

According to recent reports, while looking at her partner, she continued, “You always do it. You’re always positive. You’re always there. You’re always working so hard, and you deserve this. You are an example of coming from no dance experience to evolving into this beautiful butterfly.”.

Throughout the competition, Mark Ballas and Maura Higgins have been able to showcase their playful banter as they worked to earn their first 10s from the judges in week 10 of the competition.

While speaking to Us at the semifinals, Ballas, 40, had the opportunity to ask for America’s vote.

“I’m not good at this. I’m just grateful to be here,” he said. “If you feel compelled to vote for us and you enjoy our dancing, we will be very grateful.”.

It has been revealed that higgins, 35, added, “Vote or else. Shout at them like you shout at me in rehearsal.”.

Tatyana Ali and Jan Ravnik, along with Ezra Frech and Daniella Karagach, will also be competing for America’s vote on finale night.

During the Thursday, November 19, episode of the “Dancing With the Stars Official Podcast,” host Andy Richter unveiled that each finalist will perform a judge’s choice and freestyle.
