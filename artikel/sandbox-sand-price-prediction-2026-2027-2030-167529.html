It has been revealed that the Sandbox is one of the most recognized names in the blockchain gaming and metaverse space. Originally introduced as a mobile game in 2011 by Pixowl, the platform was acquired by Animoca Brands in 2018 and relaunched as a blockchain-powered virtual world in 2021, right at the peak of the metaverse frenzy.

Built on Ethereum, The Sandbox allows consumers to create, own, and monetize gaming experiences using NFTs and its native token, SAND. The Sandbox is betting its future on The Sandbox 3.0, a strategic pivot positioning the platform as a global distribution hub for digital culture, powered by web3 and AI.
