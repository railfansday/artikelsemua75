Bowen Yang has issued an apology for calling Regina Spektor an antisemitic slur on his “Las Culturistas” podcast, saying he was unaware of the word’s origins.

Industry observers note that “I want to genuinely apologize for using a word on the podcast without knowing its antisemitic history, especially since we’ve repudiated antisemitism on the show as recently as the last two weeks,” Yang wrote in an Instagram statement Friday. “Particularly upsetting to realize that I adopted language popularized by far-right movements whose rhetoric we should be wary of and act against.”. Modica remarked that Spektor had been “just, like, a little Israel hardcore,” referring to Spektor’s vocal pro-Israel advocacy. Yang added, “yeah, she’s Zio.”.

Short for Zionist, the derogatory term was popularized by Ku Klux Klan leader David Duke in the early 2000s and has been picked up by white supremacist groups.

It has been revealed that the Anti-Defamation League recognizes the word as a hostile phrase when referring to Jewish people or Israelis.
