KuCoin will delist Monero (XMR) on Nov. 30, the exchange revealed on Thursday. XMR dropped as low as $436 after the announcement and lost almost 15% within four hours, according to Kraken data on TradingView.

In the notice, KuCoin said that XMR would be delisted "in accordance with the Special Treatment Rules of KuCoin." The exchange closed XMR deposits on Thursday and will remove the XMR/USDT, XMR/USDC, XMR/ETH and XMR/BTC pairs at 08:00 UTC on Nov. 30. Industry observers note that kuCoin will also shut down XMR trading bots, Convert swaps and Earn products before the delisting. Owners can withdraw XMR until Dec. 31, and KuCoin said that it could close withdrawals earlier if the Monero network stops producing blocks or processing transfers.

On Thursday, KuCoin's XMR/USDT pair had $73.8 million in 24-hour volume, according to CoinMarketCap. It was the largest Monero spot market and accounted for 47% of XMR spot trading. HTX's XMR/USDT pair was second with $29.5 million.

According to recent reports, kuCoin did not mention its XMRUSDT perpetual contract in the notice. The contract had $33 million in 24-hour volume on Thursday, according to CoinMarketCap. Binance had the largest XMR perpetual market with $89.3 million.

It has been revealed that perpetualCow, who promotes the Wagyu trading platform on X, also responded to the delisting. "Over 50% of people sourced their XMR indirectly from KuCoin; it fuelled ALL instant swap exchanges," he wrote on Thursday. According to PerpetualCow, instant swap services "don't have their own liquidity" and "simply wrapped around KuCoin and sold/bought into their order books.". In the same post, he wrote that Wagyu had done more than $1.5 billion in volume in less than a year. He also shared a screenshot of Wagyu's XMR-USDC market together with the post, and it showed $63.5 million in 24-hour volume.

Recent findings suggest that earlier in November, the MEXC exchange also delisted XMR. The exchange removed XMR spot and perpetual markets on Nov. 13.

Recent findings suggest that OKX delisted XMR in January 2024, and Binance removed the coin on Feb. 20, 2024. Binance remarked at the time that it would conduct a deeper review "when a coin or token no longer meets this standard, or the industry changes." XMR fell more than 15% after the Binance announcement.

Sources indicate that kraken stopped XMR trading for clients in the European Economic Area at the end of October 2024. In August 2025, Kraken revealed an XMR delisting for Canadian clients "due to recent compliance requirements in Canada" and disabled trading for them on Sept. 2, 2025.
