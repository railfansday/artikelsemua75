DOWNERS GROVE, Ill., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Dover (NYSE: DOV) confirmed today that it will release fourth quarter 2026 earnings at approximately 6:00 a.m. Central time (7:00 a.m. Eastern time) on Thursday, January 28, 2027. Later that morning, Dover will host a conference call at 8:30 a.m. Central time (9:30 a.m. Eastern time) to discuss these results.

To participate in the conference call, please dial 1 (800) 225-9448 (domestic) or 1 (203) 518-9708 (international), conference ID DOVQ426. Due to the expected number of callers, please dial in at least 15 minutes before the conference is to begin and ask to be connected to the Dover teleconference.

A link to the live audio webcast will also be available on the organization website at dovercorporation.com. An audio replay of the conference call will be available from 12:00 p.m. Central time, January 28, until 10:59 p.m. Central time, February 18, by dialing 1 (800) 753-9134 (domestic) or 1 (402) 220-2678 (international). We deliver innovative equipment and components, consumable supplies, aftermarket parts, software and digital solutions, and support services through five operating segments: Engineered Products, Clean Energy & Fueling, Imaging & Identification, Pumps & Process Solutions and Climate & Sustainability Technologies. Dover combines global scale with operational agility to lead the markets we serve. Recognized for our entrepreneurial approach for over 70 years, our team of approximately 24,000 employees takes an ownership mindset, collaborating with customers to redefine what's possible. Headquartered in Downers Grove, Illinois, Dover trades on the Newly introduced York Stock Exchange under "DOV." Additional information is available at dovercorporation.com.
