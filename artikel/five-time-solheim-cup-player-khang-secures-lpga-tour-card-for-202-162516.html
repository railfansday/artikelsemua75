Five-time Solheim Cup player Khang secures LPGA Tour card for 2027

🌐 Source: Skysports • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The top-25 finishers and ties at the 2026 LPGA Q-Series final qualifying stage have been awarded LPGA Tour status for 2027; Megha Ganne won the event outright, while Megan Khang - part of Team USA for the past five Solheim Cups - is one of 27 players to claim a tour card for next season.
