According to recent reports, openStreetMap is different. It is volunteer-mapped, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL), and you can store and reuse the results commercially if you keep the attribution and share-alike terms. Coverage is not exhaustive, and a missing website on the map does not prove the business has no site in the real world. It only means none is recorded in OSM. I built an Apify Actor that queries that open data for a suburb, city or radius, and returns flat rows you can export to CSV, Excel, JSON, Google Sheets or a CRM.

This is the listing example: cafes and plumbers in the City of Newcastle, NSW, with no website recorded, and at least one mapped contact detail.

On 24 November 2026 (10:36 PM Australia/Sydney) I ran that example input. The run succeeded in about 20 seconds on build 1.4.9. It returned 9 leads, all cafes, all with a phone, 1 with an email, and 0 with a website. The map data came from the public Overpass servers, so the rows include a lastEditedAt date.
