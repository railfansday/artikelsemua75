Aaron Pierre starred as John Stewart in the HBO & DC Studios series Lanterns and was set to play him across the DC Universe.

However, with Peter Safran and James Gunn no longer co-heads of DC Studios, the future of the world they were building seems uncertain. In a new interview, Pierre reacted to Safran and Gunn’s exits and shared his thoughts on what happens next. (Related: Architectural Choices in China's Open-Source AI Ecosyst...)

“I haven’t had very much time at all to process the enormity of that news,” Pierre stated on the November 19 episode of the IndieWire: Screen Talk podcast about finding out that day that Safran and Gunn were leaving DC Studios. “But what I can say is that my relationship with James and my relationship with Peter is a beautiful relationship.”.

He continued, “I’ve really loved every moment, collaborating with them and working with them and getting to know them. And I’m excited to, beyond this point, in a different context, collaborate with them again. And I’m also excited to see what happens next for DC.” Watch on Deadline. According to recent reports, pierre was to reprise his Lanterns role in Gunn’s Man of Tomorrow, which releases in theaters July 9, 2027. With the DC Studios head changing, it’s unknown what future they are planning and could eventually make edits to the Superman sequel to keep it contained.

In a recent development, the actor was asked if his character could possibly get edited out of the film, to which he said, “I think for me, we did the film, and we really loved every moment of doing the film. And what happens beyond this point is not my decision. So I’m very at peace.”.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m very at peace, and what will be will be,” he continued. “But what I do know is that those friendships will last beyond this point. And also those artistic, creative, working collaboration relationships will live beyond this point as well. And as I say, I’m excited to see where DC goes from here.”.
