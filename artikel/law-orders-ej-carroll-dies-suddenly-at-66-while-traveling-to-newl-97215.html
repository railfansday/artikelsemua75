It has been revealed that law & Order and Boardwalk Empire actor E.J. Carroll died unexpectedly while en route to a voiceover job. He was 66.

Carroll’s manager, Jon Mason, confirmed on Saturday, November 21, that the veteran character actor collapsed on the street in Newly introduced York City on Thursday, November 19, while on his way to record lines for an upcoming commercial, per Entertainment Weekly.

A cause of death has not been determined. Mason noted that Mason had recently undergone a medical procedure to correct a hernia.

A memorial service will be held in Newly introduced York City at a later date.

“When E.J. Carroll performed, it was impossible to take your eyes off him,” Mason said on Saturday. “But his lasting legacy will be the kindness and generosity he indicated his friends, fellow performers and even strangers.”.

Mason added that Carroll was “as good a human being as it gets.”.

Us has reached out to Carroll’s representative for further information but did not hear back at the time of publication.

Carroll made his TV acting debut in 1998 via a guest spot in the original Law & Order. This brief appearance began a longstanding relationship that saw Carroll play at least seven characters across Law & Order and its spinoffs, Criminal Intent and Special Victims Unit.

He appeared in two episodes of acclaimed HBO crime drama Boardwalk Empire as the crooked Sheriff Victor Sickles, in addition to starring in six episodes of porn industry drama The Deuce as shop owner Fat Mooney. (Related: Industry News: Designing Azure silicon on Azure silicon...)

It has been revealed that throughout his career, Carroll made guest appearances in hit shows such as Pose, Blue Bloods, The Blacklist, Damages and The Punisher. He appeared on the substantial screen in Golden Globe-winning sports drama The Wrestler and holiday ensemble Everybody’s Fine.

“I think what makes an actor good is doing your homework, first of all, and you really have to love it,” Carroll noted on a July 2024 episode of the “On Acting” podcast. “I still love it more than anything. … Even if I’m doing a little commercial or something, I still [do research and make up a backstory].”.

His manager revealed that Carroll completed work on Delicate Arrangement, a film about the true story of Gone With the Wind actress Vivien Leigh‘s unlikely friendship with a blue-collar florist. The movie will feature Spy Kids alum Carla Gugino as Leigh and Stranger Things star Matthew Modine as the florist.

In a recent development, “I had the great fortune of working with E.J. last winter in Philadelphia,” Gugino, 55, told Entertainment Weekly in a statement. “He was a source of talent and light. My deepest condolences to his family and loved ones. RIP E.J.”.
