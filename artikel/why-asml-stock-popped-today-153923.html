It's a cyclical business downstream of broader chip demand, including more advanced chips used for AI. Therefore, the strengthening of the AI ecosystem is good news for ASML, and investors believe AI demand and its potential growth are stronger with Jensen Huang back running Nvidia.

Recent findings suggest that because of the nature of its business, ASML's results can swing quarter to quarter, so the long-term outlook is crucial for the stock. It already stands to benefit from the shortage in memory as orders from memory chipmakers look to expand capacity, and it should benefit from the continued growth of AI demand, which is supported by having Huang back as the Nvidia CEO.

It has been revealed that ASML is targeting revenue of 44 billion-60 billion euros for 2030. While the tech giant won't benefit directly from the return of Huang, investors should look for that figure to move higher as a sign that ASML is benefiting from tailwinds from AI growth.
