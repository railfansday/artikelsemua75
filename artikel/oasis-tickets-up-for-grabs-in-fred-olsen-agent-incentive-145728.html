Fred. Olsen is offering travel agents a chance to win one of ten tickets to see Oasis in Manchester next year.

Ten agents will get a full hospitality experience to see the band at Manchester’s Etihad Stadium on June 8, 2027, including food and drink in Zadies Bar, an overnight hotel stay and transfers between the hotel and the venue as part of the prize.

It has been revealed that the winners will be hosted by Fred. Olsen Cruise Lines account managers, Dan Scott and Pam White.

According to recent reports, “We’ve had an incredible year working with our trade partners in 2026, and we wanted to do something extra exciting over the Christmas and Latest Year period to say thank you for their continued support. And what better way than with the chance to be among the crowd at one of the most in-demand gigs of the year?” Stated Geoff Ridgeon, head of sales at Fred. Olsen Cruise Lines.

To qualify, agents must make a new booking for a Fred. Olsen cruise that sets sail between November 23, 2026, and March 31, 2027, or between October 1, 2027, and March 31, 2028.

In a recent development, all bookings must be made between November 23, 2026, and January 31, 2027, inclusive, and then registered via the agent’s own account with Fred. Olsen’s Travel Agent Centre online by February 5, 2027.

It has been revealed that for every eligible booking, the agent will receive one entry into the draw, plus one additional entry for bookings made via the cruise line’s F.OCAL system, and one additional entry for every “Upcoming to Fred. Olsen” guest booking. (Related: Tech Report: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system t...)

Qualifying agents must also have completed the first two training modules within the Fred. Olsen Academy. (Related: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for Summarization u...)

Eligible cruises include a mini cruise to Belgium, festive Christmas market sailings, a Mystery Cruise and chances to seek out the Northern Lights, as well as Mediterranean sailings as part of the Balmoral’s fly-cruise program.

“We’re always looking for fresh ways to reward agents while giving them even more reasons to talk to their customers about Fred. Olsen. We want to showcase our smaller ships to more guests who haven’t sailed with us before, so the additional entries for ‘New to Fred. Olsen’ guests make this a really exciting incentive,” added Ridgeon.

“Thank you to all our trade partners for a brilliant year, and here’s to another exciting year working together in 2027.”.
