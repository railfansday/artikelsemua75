Hila Klein officially exited the “H3 Podcast” a month after she revealed that Ethan Klein, her co-host and husband, had been having an affair.

In a post on Instagram, Hila apologized to fans for her departure. “I truly tried with every ounce of strength I had to make this work,” she wrote. “But after everything that has happened, I’ve come to realize that I can’t continue down this path while also giving myself the space I need to heal and protect my mental health.”.

She explained that her priority is to “focus on myself, my healing, and what’s best for me and my kids.”.

“I’m devastated that things have come to this,” she added. “I love this community we’ve built together so much, and I love the crew like my own family. Walking away from something that has been such a huge part of my life is incredibly painful. I will miss you all more than I can put into words.”. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Sources indicate that last month, Hila accused Ethan of having a more than six-month-long affair with an unnamed person in a lengthy statement on Instagram. In late October, Ethan confirmed he is working to cut ties financially and ownership-wise from his H3 Podcast following allegations that he cheated on his wife.

“I am working with Hila to urgently remove my ownership and financial stakes in any podcasts, Teddy Fresh, Teddy Glow or any joint venture that I would profit from,” he wrote on social media, referring to his wife’s fashion and beauty brands. “All of your support will go directly to Hila and the crew. I do this in the hope that people can still support Hila and the crew without the concern of enriching me.”. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Earlier this month, Hila addressed Ethan’s affair in an emotional video on YouTube. “When I found out, it was like my whole entire life just shattered in front of me,” she said. “And it was just like the floor just disappeared. All of a sudden, I realized that this person has been lying to me every single day.” She said that his actions went against “everything we’ve ever talked about” and “any morals that I thought he stood for,” adding that she in now divorcing him.
