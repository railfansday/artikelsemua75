Dual Orb Collection announced for Switch

🌐 Source: Gematsu • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • According to recent reports, mebius has announced Dual Orb Collection for Switch.
  • Dual Orb Collection will include the Prism Kikaku-developed RPG Dual Orb: Seireiju Densetsu and sequel Dual Orb II, which originally launched for Super Famicom in 1993 and 1994, respectively.
  • The 8,800 yen limited physical edition will include a two-disc original soundtrack featuring background music from the series.

According to recent reports, mebius has announced Dual Orb Collection for Switch. It will launch on March 18, 2027 in Japan.

Dual Orb Collection will include the Prism Kikaku-developed RPG Dual Orb: Seireiju Densetsu and sequel Dual Orb II, which originally launched for Super Famicom in 1993 and 1994, respectively.

The 8,800 yen limited physical edition will include a two-disc original soundtrack featuring background music from the series.
