It has been revealed that portsmouth and Swansea City will already have eyes on the January transfer window and potential targets. The Swans’ preparations are disrupted by their ongoing search for a new head coach, but the Championship pair could go toe-to-toe having identified Aston Villa’s TJ Carroll as an option.

According to Daily Mail reporter Tom Collomosse, Pompey and the Swans are alongside League One outfit Barnsley in showing interest in 19-year-old Carroll. The strategy is that the centre-back leaves Villa Park on loan in January, and potential destinations are already arising.

Carroll is a product of the Villa academy and he has been a mainstay in their U18s and U21s over recent seasons. He is yet to make his senior debut, so an EFL loan would mark his first foray into first-team football.

Carroll is yet to play competitive first-team football, but he has rich academy experience behind him.

Standing at 6ft 6in, the 19-year-old primarily plays as a centre-back but has been used as a left-back at times. Given his height, Carroll is unsurprisingly a physically dominant defender. He ranked in the top 7% for aerial duels won in the Premier League 2 last season, and his commitment is displayed by 22 blocked shots (top 3%).

As most modern centre-backs are required to be, Carroll is assured in possession as well. He made 77 accurate long balls and was regularly trusted in possession with 1.378 touches of the ball (all stats via FotMob). (Related: Scaling up BERT-like model Inference on modern CPU - P...)

Recent findings suggest that the Portsmouth, Swansea City and Barnsley target has shown he can contribute in the attacking third as well. Earlier this month, he scored twice in a 3-3 draw with FC Barcelona’s youngsters in the UEFA Youth League.

Portsmouth and Swansea City are two intriguing Championship options for Carroll.

Pompey have a great track record of developing young loan talents, with Chelsea’s Ryan Kavuma-McQueen currently impressing in his first season of senior football. However, opportunities may be limited for Carroll with all of Benjamin Arthur, Regan Poole, Conor Shaughnessy and Madiodio Dia on the books at centre-back.

Swansea City have fewer options, and Cameron Burgess is out long-term. Stephen Welsh has picked up a blow, but it is not expected to be serious, so Ben Cabango and Jenson Seelt are the only ones available at present. However, when Welsh returns, Carroll would likely be left as the fourth choice, limiting his chances.

Sources indicate that A move to Barnsley would be a challenge – they’re 23rd in League One with 24 goals conceded in 13 games. But, their defensive struggles would pave the way for Carroll to come straight into the team.

If game time is the priority, an Oakwell switch could be best for the Villa prospect.
