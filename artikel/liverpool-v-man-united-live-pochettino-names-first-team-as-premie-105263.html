Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of his first Manchester United game and is thrown straight into the deep end as he faces fierce Premier League rivals Liverpool at Anfield.

It’s been a whirlwind week for Pochettino since arriving back in England but the former Tottenham and Chelsea manager has outlined his plan to bring success back to Old Trafford after a turbulent spell and alarming dip of results under Michael Carrick.

In a recent development, united enter the weekend in the relegation zone, with Liverpool having the opportunity to pile more misery on their rivals and continue their progress under Andoni Iraola. One major thing in Pochettino’s in-tray will be the future of his captain, Bruno Fernandes, who has had an indifferent start to this season - much like his whole team - but has been instrumental in the past.

Fernandes has revealed that discussions over a new contract have stalled because it isn’t “the right moment” to be negotiating.

The Portuguese said an offer had been made in the summer, with his contract due to expire at the end of this season, but negotiations were not ongoing as he insisted on prioritising the club’s return to form.

The good news for Pochettino is that although Liverpool are fifth in the top flight and enjoying a decent spell under Andoni Iraola, they have won just two of their last nine games against United.

In a recent development, and they are winless in their last three clashes between these two rivals at home, while United won both fixtures last term. Liverpool only have one win from five at Anfield this season.

Liverpool host Man United at Anfield today with kick-off at 4.30pm GMT.

Viewers in the UK can watch the game on Sky Sports Main Event and Premier League, with coverage from 4pm.

After a run of eight points from a possible 30 and just 10 games of this campaign, United’s hierarchy opted to part ways with Carrick.

Pochettino is the next man in the hot seat - the eighth permanent manager at Old Trafford in 13 years - and he arrives with United having slipped from 18th to 19th in the Premier League table after yesterday’s results.

A win over arch-rivals Liverpool, at Anfield, on his first day in charge, would be quite the statement...
