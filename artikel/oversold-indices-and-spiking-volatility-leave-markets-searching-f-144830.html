VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Every major index closed Friday, November 20 deep in oversold territory after a brutal week down the board. The Nasdaq Composite led losses, down 10.02% on the week, while the S&P 500 fell 8.61% and the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 8.02%. The Russell 2000 declined 7.48% on the week. The CBOE Volatility Index (VIX) surged 85.96% on the week, and the PHLX Semiconductor Index tumbled 17.13% on the week, underscoring the scale of the selloff. (Nasdaq: PYPL), and SAP SE (NYSE: SAP). (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

The Dow Jones Industrial Average closed at 48,147.33 on Friday, down 1.93% on the day and down 8.02% on the week from 52,347.55 on November 13, with a relative strength index of 26.60 signaling oversold conditions. The S&P 500 ended at 6,910.39, down 3.70% on the day and down 8.61% on the week, with an RSI of 24.70. The Nasdaq Composite closed at 22,617.73, down 5.28% on the day and down 10.02% on the week, carrying an RSI of 25.60. The Russell 2000 finished at 2,581.79, down 2.76% on the day and down 7.48% on the week, with an RSI of 30.20.

Volatility spiked sharply alongside the equity declines. The CBOE Volatility Index (VIX) closed at 35.89, up 26.20% on the day and up 85.96% on the week from 19.30 on November 13. The CBOE Nasdaq-100 Volatility Index (VXN) ended at 45.25, up 24.04% on the day and up 54.02% on the week from 29.38 on November 13.

In commodities, gold fell to $3,515.40 per ounce, down 4.62% on the day and down 9.54% on the week from $3,886.30 per ounce. Silver dropped to $41.03 per ounce, down 7.41% on the day and down 16.31% on the week. WTI crude oil rose to $83.98 per barrel, up 0.93% on the day but down 5.17% on the week. Natural gas ended at $3.411 per MMBtu, up 4.38% on the day and up 5.60% on the week. The US 10-year Treasury yield fell 14 basis points on the day and 24 basis points on the week to 4.912%.
