U.S. insurer Allstate has been experiencing a tech outage since Monday morning, leaving customers nationwide unable to access their accounts, review their policy information, file claims, pay their bills, and more, just ahead of the U.S. Thanksgiving holidays.

The outage has been impacting both the firm’s website and mobile app, TechCrunch confirmed.

Industry observers note that the breakdown follows a report from earlier this month that Allstate was planning to lay off up to 10% of its staff as part of an AI-driven efficiency push. (The company had not confirmed the report, which was attributed to unnamed sources.). (Related: Industry News: huggingface_hub v1.0: Five Years of Buil...)

Allstate’s outage is the latest in a series of disruptions to services such as Microsoft Outlook and Teams, the airlines, Apple Pay, and several banks. Trump administration officials have said the outages are not being caused by cyberattacks, but are rather being caused by a recent set of upgrades to systems, many of which run on older technology. Those systems are now in need of more frequent security and bug fixes as AI makes it easier and faster to discover vulnerabilities. (Related: Hugging Face and VirusTotal collaborate to strengthen A...)

It has been revealed that downdetector, a crowdsourced outage-reporting website, demonstrates owners began reporting Allstate’s outage from Monday morning. However, the organization hasn’t yet issued a formal statement about the disruption, what’s causing it, or when it may be resolved. In the meantime, local insurance agents have been fielding calls and emails from customers who aren’t able to use the company’s online services.

Industry observers note that the timing of the outage is particularly bad given that Thanksgiving is a massive travel holiday in the U.S., which means there will be car accidents. Customers will be left without a way to get in touch with their auto insurer to file claims and get roadside assistance through Allstate’s app.

Recent findings suggest that allstate did not immediately return a request for comment. On Wednesday evening, after publication, the firm sent the following statement (below). It did not answer questions about the outage’s cause or planned resolution.

It has been revealed that allstate customers can reach us in many ways, including online, by phone and through local offices. Some access has been restricted over the last 48 hours, but we continue to be available for customers and respond to and settle claims. To the extent service interruptions impact a customer’s ability to make a payment, they won’t lose coverage or be charged late fees.
