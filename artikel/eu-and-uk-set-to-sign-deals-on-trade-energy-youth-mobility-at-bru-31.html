EU and UK set to sign deals on trade, energy, youth mobility at Brussels summit

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (73 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

The UK and the EU are poised to agree newly introduced deals on trade, energy and youth mobility at a summit in Brussels on Friday, as part of UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham's bid to reset ties with the bloc. Burnham – who has said he hopes to see Britain rejoin the EU in his lifetime – argues the deals will help fix some of the damage Brexit inflicted.
