David Raya has been apologised to by a Sky Sports commentator, who has accused the Arsenal goalkeeper of feigning his injury.

The Spaniard was forced off during the second half of Arsenal’s 2-2 draw with Newcastle on Saturday night following a collision with Newcastle forward William Osula.

According to recent reports, at first it appeared quite an innocuous incident, with Raya seemingly instigating the contact before falling to the grass. Commentator Bill Leslie suggested that the goalkeeper was exaggerating the incident.

Such a ploy has previously been seen from Arsenal and many other clubs to to give team-mates time to regroup.

But once it had become evident that Raya was in fact hurt, Leslie backtracked.

Industry observers note that he remarked on Sky Sports: "David Raya does indeed look in some discomfort. Apologies if I did him a disservice there because it really didn’t look like significant contact with Osula.". (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

Recent findings suggest that co-commentator Chris Sutton added: "Raya is holding his arm. It looks like there’s some sort of sprain.

In a recent development, “He is in a lot of pain. It really did look innocuous, didn’t it, the coming together.".

Arsenal's medical staff treated the goalkeeper on the pitch before he was helped off, with Kepa Arrizabalaga coming on as his replacement. The incident initially raised questions over whether Raya was attempting to waste time, but it became clear he was struggling with an injury.

Similar confusion was also broadcast on BBC Radio 5 Live, with former Chelsea midfielder Pat Nevin reporting: "He is coming off. I have no idea what happened.

Industry observers note that "It looked innocuous. It looked like David Raya knocked into the forward.".

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta later confirmed Raya would undergo an MRI scan on Sunday to establish the extent of the problem.

Industry observers note that "Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news. He will have an MRI assessment tomorrow, but at the moment I don't know," Arteta stated.
