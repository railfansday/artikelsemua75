Mikey Moore would be enjoying a more beneficial season if Tottenham Hotspur had sent him on loan to Millwall.

That is according to former senior Tottenham scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 19-year-old was loaned out to FC Koln instead, and despite enjoying regular minutes, is struggling to make an impact.

Moore has failed to score or assist in his opening nine games of the season, despite having found a goal and an assist against Championship side Charlton Athletic in the Carabao Cup before he left. (Related: Latest Update: Large Language Models: A New Moore's Law...)

Tottenham hopes remain high for the winger, who will likely remain in Germany all the while he is a consistent starter for Koln this season.

The teenager scored his first goal for England's Under-21s on Monday and will hope to carry the confidence back to his club form.

Harry Kane went on loan to Milwall for the second half of the 2011-12 season, featuring under the late Kenny Jackett.

The young striker scored nine goals in 27 games for the Lions, who were desperate to get him back in the following season.

Though the loan did not directly precede his breakthrough at Tottenham, it was the first taste of what he could become.

"For me, it was the wrong club. Okay, he might not have been playing in the first team [if he had stayed], but then you don't know," King told Football Insider.

"But I would have been happier for him to be working and training with the first team at Tottenham, and maybe looking for a Championship club.

According to recent reports, "I think I've noted before, his family are Millwall supporters. Millwall would have been a good little club for him on loan.

Sources indicate that "Millwall didn't do Harry Kane any harm. It's that kind of football he needs.".

FIFA rules prevent a player from appearing for more than three clubs in a single season, with Moore already having played for both Koln and Tottenham.

The Lilywhites likely have the option to terminate the deal in January, but it remains to be seen if they will.

Despite his struggles, the winger is playing regularly and gaining good experience in another league.

Given the competition at Tottenham, it is unlikely that Moore would see his return as a positive move.
