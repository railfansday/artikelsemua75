Cesc Fabregas reveals what ‘means more’ than winning trophies, and assures Como are on the verge of ‘a dream’ in the Champions League if they beat AEK Athens this evening.

It kicks off at the Stadio Sinigaglia at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

You can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and Manchester City vs. Napoli on the Champions League Liveblog.

The Lariani go into Matchday Five in fourth place for their first ever Champions League campaign, having won three of the opening four fixtures, losing only away to Feyenoord.

They are in a very strong position to be one of the eight teams who go through directly to the Round of 16, an extraordinary achievement considering their lack of experience.

“I am happy, because coaches can talk about winning trophies and tournaments, but it means more to me that I’m seeing my players get their international debuts,” Fabregas told Sky Sport Italia.

“Many started with me as teenagers, others like Lucas Da Cunha was my teammate in Serie B, so this makes me very proud. It’s a pleasure for a coach to see players grow.”.

Como made history in their last Champions League fixture with a 9-1 victory away to RC Lens.

“We are half-way through the competition, and it is very notable today, if we win today, then we are practically already in the 24 for the play-offs, which is a dream for Como,” added Fabregas.

“We must fight as if it is a Final, we are against a very strong opponent with a lot of quality and experience in the squad, so I am convinced we need a high level performance to win tonight.”.

Fabregas is always in favour of attacking football, and remarked Frosinone coach Massimiliano Alvini was right to take his approach against Milan, even though it ended in a 5-1 defeat.

It has been revealed that “When Frosinone had so many great performances, and beat us, Alvini was considered a phenomenon. He lost a couple of games and all of a sudden people have stopped, but I want them to keep saying Alvini is phenomenal, because you cannot judge on one victory or defeat. According to recent reports, “I saw the coach the other day, he won the Serie B Golden Bench award, discussed his style of football, and has very clear ideas. That’s what I meant, and don’t forget Frosinone amassed 20 points this season playing that football.

“It’s also true that everyone studies you and tries to find counter-measures, but we prefer to face everyone with our mentality, our approach, and Frosinone are doing the same with great results in Serie A after the step up from Serie B,” assured Fabregas.

Trevoh Chalobah makes his comeback in defence after sustaining a muscular injury on October 18.
