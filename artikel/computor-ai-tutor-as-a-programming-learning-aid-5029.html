computor: AI tutor as a programming learning aid

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (109 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that artificial intelligence is now capable of writing entire computer programs. This poses a challenge for university education. How can students still learn to program for themselves if an AI can do the work for them? Students should be able to use AI tools effectively, but they must also understand how programs work and how to troubleshoot errors. At the Institute of Theoretical and Computational Physics at Graz University of Technology (TU Graz), a team led by Christopher Albert has developed the "computor" learning platform for this purpose. The system is already being used in teaching and is available to the public.
