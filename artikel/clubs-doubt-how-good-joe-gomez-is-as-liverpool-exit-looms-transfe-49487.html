Joe Gomez could find himself in a tricky position next summer as he edges closer to a move away from Liverpool.

The 29-year-old is out of contract at the end of the season, and there is a growing expectation that his terms will not be renewed, and he could leave the club for free.

So far this season, the defender has started just three games, and is unlikely to play a massive role in Andoni Iraola's looks despite his continued involvement in squads.

However, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown warned Football Insider that there may be a lack of suitors for Gomez next summer, even on a free transfer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

The defender is likely to command significant wages to move on a free, while there are lingering concerns about his fitness and the fact he has never established himself at Liverpool.

After what will be 12 years at Liverpool come the end of the season, Gomez could struggle to find a newly introduced club, as they share the Reds' doubts over his reliability.

Brown told Football Insider on 18 November that Gomez is edging closer to a move away from Liverpool with no plans in place to agree a contract extension as things stand.

The situation is still up in the air, and there have been suggestions that the versatile defender would be willing to stay if he was offered the opportunity to pen a new deal.

However, with the growing expectation that he will move on a free transfer, questions have been asked about which clubs will be prepared to take the gamble.

"The problem for Joe Gomez is, there isn't a long line of clubs queuing up to sign him," Brown told Football Insider.

"So he could be left in no-man's-land if he leaves Liverpool on a free transfer, because then it's about whether any clubs are prepared to take a gamble on him.

"His fitness and injury record is going to be a major concern, while there are doubts about the fact he has never managed to establish himself at Liverpool.

"He's been there forever, it feels like, but can you name one time when he's had a real consistent run in the team and looked like he would be an vital player?

"He certainly hasn't done that in recent memory, so obviously that leads to doubts about how good he really is at this stage.

"I'm sure clubs will be keeping an eye on it, they always are when players like that are available for free, but I have my doubts about how many will be willing to make a move.

"I can't imagine he wages will be cheap either, especially on a free transfer, so all of these things have to be taken into consideration.".

Despite doubts about which clubs could be tempted to make a move for Gomez in the summer, he has had a number of long-term suitors keen to lure him away before.

In 2025, the likes of AC Milan, Crystal Palace and Brighton were all linked with the versatile defender, but he ultimately remained at Liverpool despite that interest.

Meanwhile, Football Insider reported during the summer that West Ham have shown an interest in signing Gomez and could be tempted to revisit the deal.

The decision was ultimately made to keep him at Anfield, but that is unlikely to have deterred the Hammers' long-term interest should he leave Liverpool for free.

His availability as a free agent is likely to spark further interest, despite the doubts over his fitness and his level, and the Championship outfit could make a move.
