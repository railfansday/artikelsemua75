Intuit to sell Mailchimp to Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, tax software maker Intuit mentioned Thursday that it has agreed to sell marketing subsidiary Mailchimp to Italian technology consolidator Bending Spoons for $3.68 billion in cash and stock. The deal is set to close in early 2027, according to a statement.

The sale is a costly retreat for Intuit, which paid $12 billion for Mailchimp in 2021 in a push beyond its core tax and accounting businesses. Five years later, the software maker is unloading the email-marketing manufacturer for less than one-third of that price after Mailchimp became a drag on its overall revenue growth. According to recent reports, the deal is the latest instance of Bending Spoons picking up a once promising technology asset. The company bought productivity software startup Airtable in September for 10% of what it was once worth. (Related: Industry News: What Is Considered Upper Middle Class? I...)

The Bending Spoons portfolio also contains AOL, Brightcove, Eventbrite, Evernote, Meetup, Vimeo, and WeTransfer.

In August Intuit started running Mailchimp as a distinct operating segment. The move was meant to give investors more transparency as the organization tried to manage the unit for profitability, Sandeep Aujla, Intuit's finance chief, mentioned at a September company briefing.

Recent findings suggest that also on Thursday, Intuit announced stronger-than-expected earnings and revenue for its fiscal first quarter, which ended on Oct. 31. The company reported $2.91 in adjusted earnings per share on $4.35 billion in revenue. Analysts polled by LSEG were looking for $2.70 per share and $4.31 billion in revenue.

While Intuit's revenue increased 12% year over year, Mailchimp revenue declined 1% to $313 million. Analysts polled by StreetAccount had expected $314.4 million.

In a recent development, management called for $22.88 to $23.12 in adjusted earnings per share for the 2027 fiscal year, with $23.081 billion to $23.365 billion in revenue. Analysts polled by LSEG had predicted $23.62 in adjusted earnings per share on $23.43 billion in revenue. Intuit shares tumbled 15% in extended trading.

In a recent development, the company said Mailchimp should produce $1.256 billion to $1.266 billion in fiscal 2027 revenue, which would be down 1% to flat year over year. In August Aujla said Intuit anticipated Mailchimp's higher effective prices offsetting increased client churn. Mailchimp was founded in 2001. Rivals include Klaviyo, which went public in 2023.

Berkshire Hathaway invested $600 million in Bending Spoons when the acquirer went public in July, and in October Bending Spoons said Berkshire invested another $330 million.
