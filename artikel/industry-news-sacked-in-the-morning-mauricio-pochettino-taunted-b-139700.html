The job of Manchester United head coach has become something of a poisoned chalice for a succession of managers but even latest boss Mauricio Pochettino could not have expected how promptly he would be taunted with jokes about him being sacked.

His reign began in humiliating fashion after an early red card and two goals led to the Kop chanting “Sacked in the morning” just 13 minutes into the Argentinian’s first game.

Chants of “Going down with the City” when the third goal went in – followed by “You’re not famous any more” and “Poch is at the wheel” – will only have made the trip back along the M62 more uncomfortable.

The match finished 4-0 when Victor Munoz slotted home in the 82nd minute, putting the seal on a disastrous bow in the United dugout for Pochettino.

In a recent development, "So painful, so disappointed,” Pochettino told Sky Sports. “It's not ok to start a game in the Premier League with this situation. The game changed completely after six minutes. Now we can explain everything but the damage is done, it's so painful.

"We also need to be critical on ourselves but some situations happened in the game that we are not happy.".

United’s all-time great boss Sir Alex Ferguson had a seat in the directors’ box alongside the actor and fan James Nesbitt – in Liverpool recording a latest Poirot television show – and it was more a case of boiling blood than ‘Cold Feet’ at Anfield.

Ferguson had shared a pre-match joke with fellow Scot and long-time Liverpool rival Sir Kenny Dalglish about their choice of chocolate, but that was about as funny as it got for the 84-year-old.

There were also some stony faces from chief executive officer Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox, sat immediately in front of Ferguson, after a shambolic start for the new head coach.
