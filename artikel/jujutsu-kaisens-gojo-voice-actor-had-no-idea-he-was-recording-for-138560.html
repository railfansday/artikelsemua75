Kaiji Tang, the English voice of Satoru Gojo in Jujutsu Kaisen, states he recorded dialogue for GTA 6 without realizing he was working on Rockstar’s game.

It has been revealed that tang also voices Sanemi Shinazugawa in the English dub of Demon Slayer. He’s played plenty of video game characters, too, including Ichiban Kasuga, the lead in Yakuza: Like a Dragon. (Related: Industry News: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hu...)

Tang announced his involvement on November 19, the same day GTA 6 debuted on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. He followed up by explaining that he had no idea which game he’d recorded for, adding, “imagine my shock lol.”.

i had no idea what i recorded was for this gameimagine my shock lol https://t.co/Lc1j0WuS0i.

Sources indicate that one fan brought up the line “sit on daddy’s lap” and asked what Tang thought he was recording. Tang joked, “i dunno demon slayer?”.

It has been revealed that when Tang first revealed he was in the game, he gave fans a clue about where to find him. “if you want to find me, watch cartoons in game,” he wrote in a reply.

Tang has three roles in The Johnsons, one of the cartoons players can watch on TV in GTA 6. He voices Jax, a therapist, and a character listed as “Guy in Parking Lot” in Rockstar’s official credits. The episode and character behind the line quoted in the X thread are still unconfirmed.

It has been revealed that the Johnsons is one of several shows made for GTA 6’s in-game TVs. There’s also Macca & Friends, a Saturday morning-style cartoon on Weazel starring the pink alligator featured in the game’s Collector’s Box.
