After 45 years, John Carpenter is still finding the right director to break an old cult classic out of lockup.

Having recently tapped Zack Snyder to write and direct a reimagining of Escape from Newly introduced York, Carpenter noted he’s been considering another candidate, despite being told Synder is “on fire” during a recent podcast appearance.

“He’s not anymore, but he’s trying to get back,” Carpenter mentioned on the Dropping Names podcast. “We’ll see. He’s a nice man. I liked him.”.

It has been revealed that the comment came after Snyder’s The Last Photograph premiered at TIFF in September to poor reviews, mostly over the film’s excessive violence and offensive content.
