Manchester United chiefs have agreed that they must back newly introduced manager Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window, according to the Times, even though they don't normally like doing business in the winter market.

Man United prefer to do their business in the summer, like in the previous two when they have rebuilt their midfield and attack, but the club has accepted they must sign players in the New Year to help their Argentine head coach revolutionise the club following a dreadful start to the season. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Pochettino saw first-hand the huge task facing him when the Red Devils found themselves 2-0 down, and a man down, after 13 minutes of his first match in charge against Liverpool at Anfield on Sunday.

United ended up losing 4-0 and, as well as lying 19th in the Premier League table as we approach December, the team has also conceded more goals than any other team in the top five European leagues, in all competitions.

Their defence in particular is a major concern if they are to avoid genuinely being involved in a relegation battle this term, while their lack of a recgonised striker in the absence of the injured Benjamin Sesko has hurt them too. (Related: Racehorse named IShowSpeed wins major in Australia...)

Even though Michael Carrick took charge towards the beginning of January this year and United wanted to push up the standings, they didn't sign a player in that month.

Things will be different this January, and they could look to add a new left-back despite Pochettino being a big fan of Luke Shaw, having worked with him at Southampton previously.

The problem with Shaw is, that even though he's capable of being a great left-back on his day and played all 38 Premier League matches last season, he has had big injury complications at Old Trafford and is right now on a run of eight games missed.

According to the Times, United looked at signing Barcelona left-back Alejandro Balde in the summer, and now the 23-year-old wants to leave the Camp Nou in January after failing to start any games for them this season.

The report adds that United could land him for £25m in the New Year, ensuring he is a lower-cost option than someone like Newcastle United's Lewis Hall.

United hold an interest in Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson, who Pochettino knows well from the USA setup, according to GIVEMESPORT sources.

The 29-year-old does have extensive Premier League experience, but there is something very tempting about signing Balde, especially for the reported £25m fee.

The Spain international has played seven times for the world champions, and he has racked up 173 appearances for Barcelona, the current LaLiga champions, although he has struggled for game time during the 2026-27 campaign.

Last term was his third league title won while playing for Barca, while he has also won four more trophies since coming through the ranks, which is impressive at the age of 23.

Balde has also played more than 30 times in the Champions League, so he brings experience from a competition the Red Devils are looking to progress in over the coming months.

The Barcelona-born full-back has all the attributes to be the perfect defender in a Pochettino system, as his strengths lie in getting forward to support the attack, his dribbling and his pace.

According to the Athletic, the 23-year-old has the physical qualities to perform at an 'elite' level, although he has frustrated some at Barcelona, including manager Hansi Flick, with the quality of his end product in the final third.

With 18 months left on his deal, January feels like a big opportunity for all parties, and while United could also move to sign a £75m star for Pochettino, they must decide if Balde is their number one left-back target.
