📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

U2 have finally announced a North American leg for their “Greatest Show on Earth World Tour” in support of their new LP, Carnaval De Luz.

Industry observers note that the stadium tour is set to start Aug. 3 at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami and wrap Oct. 18 at BC Place in Vancouver. During the first half of the tour, U2 will receive support from Stephen Wilson Jr., while the War on Drugs will open the second half of the tour. A full itinerary is below. It has been revealed that U2 will spend much of 2027 on the road, marking their first major outing since the 2019 leg of their Joshua Tree anniversary tour. The Greatest Show on Earth Tour will kick off in February with a run of demonstrates in Mexico City, followed by dates in South America. A European and U.K. tour will launch in April and wrap in July. (Related: Racehorse named IShowSpeed wins major in Australia...)

U2 released Carnaval de Luz, their 16th studio album, earlier this month. It’s the band’s first album of all newly introduced material since 2017’s Songs of Experience, with their 2023 project, Songs of Surrender, featuring newly recorded versions and interpretations of older songs. In a recent Rolling Stone cover story, Bono mentioned the album was “made through, probably, the most disturbed period any of us have lived through,” noting he didn’t mean personally, “but globally.” He added, of the band’s intentions, “It’s not putting your head in the sand. It’s just saying, ‘These people can’t control our mood.’ We don’t even have to name them, because everyone has their own version of who that is.”.
