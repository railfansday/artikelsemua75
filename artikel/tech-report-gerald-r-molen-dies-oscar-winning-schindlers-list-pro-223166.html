Sources indicate that born Jan. 6, 1935 in Great Falls, Montana, Gerald Robert Molen grew up in North Hollywood after his family relocated there.

In 1985, Molen began working with Spielberg as a unit production manager on The Color Purple, before going on to produce his movies Hook (1991), Jurassic Park (1993) and Schindler’s List (1993), which earned them the Best Picture Oscar. They also worked together on The Lost World: Jurassic Park (1997), Amistad (1997), Minority Report (2002) and Catch Me If You Can (2002).
