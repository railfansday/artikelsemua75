Sources indicate that meanwhile, I put a 550 block in place on all Microsoft email servers from India, since they were hitting me with business spam _constantly_. These were validish looking O365/M365 tenants that Microsoft was apparently fine w/ sending outbound spam.

I concluded pretty swiftly that I will never want to receive an email from an Indian server purporting to be an American business.

It's crucial that people not use Microsoft or Google email hosting just as a matter of principle, at this point. Email is supposed to be distributed, not concentrated in the hands of the worlds worst actors.

The protocols powering email were written in a time of complete trust, and are horribly "broken" today (they technically transport mail, but that's only 5% of the modern email situation). There are bandaids on top of bandaids on top of bandaids which all make it a black box for any given provider. If they told you their magic sauce then others can work around it, and people running their own mail servers are such a tiny part of the internet now that it's not worth working directly with you.

Actual bounces are annoying, but aren't the worst-case scenario. With a bounce, at least the sender (eventually) learns the message wasn't received.
