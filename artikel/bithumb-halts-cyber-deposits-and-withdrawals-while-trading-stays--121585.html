Bithumb halts CYBER deposits and withdrawals while trading stays open

🌐 Source: Cryptoslate • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (462 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Bithumb suspended deposits and withdrawals of the CYBER token after flagging signs of a possible security matter.
  • Bithumb set the cutoff for 2:18 a.m.
  • 21–22 exchange notices affected three different ways customers could use CYBER.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bithumb suspended deposits and withdrawals of the CYBER token after flagging signs of a possible security matter. The exchange said customers could still trade CYBER already held in their accounts. Kraken separately warned of possible delays for CYBER deposits sent over the Optimism network.

Bithumb set the cutoff for 2:18 a.m. Korea time on Nov. 22, or 17:18 UTC on Nov. 21. Seven minutes after the scheduled cutoff, the exchange confirmed that the pause was in effect. It also urged customers to take care when trading CYBER, saying the security concern could lead to sharper price swings. (Related: Industry News: How we sped up transformer inference 100...)

The Nov. 21–22 exchange notices affected three different ways customers could use CYBER. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Bithumb said CYBER deposits received during the pause would be credited in sequence after service resumed.

Industry observers note that for users who had already sent CYBER to Bithumb, the cutoff mattered because sending a deposit and seeing it credited to an account can happen at different times. Bithumb remarked network congestion or slow confirmations could hold up credit even for transfers sent before the cutoff. It said it would credit deposits received during the suspension in sequence once service resumed.

Industry observers note that customers who had requested a withdrawal before the cutoff and were still waiting for it to complete faced a separate process. Bithumb noted it would run system checks and process those requests in sequence, which could delay the account revision. The exchange’s processing note applied to requests already in its system when the suspension took effect. Newly introduced CYBER withdrawals remained subject to the pause.

In a recent development, bithumb said it would reopen transfers once the network was stable and would post the reopening in the same notice. It gave no reopening time.

Recent findings suggest that kraken flagged possible CYBER deposit delays on Optimism at 19:59 UTC on Nov. 21 and said its other funding methods were operating normally.

The incident page still listed the glitch as identified at 18:12 UTC on Nov. 22, with no resolution posted. Kraken’s upgrade left the cause and timing of a fix open.
