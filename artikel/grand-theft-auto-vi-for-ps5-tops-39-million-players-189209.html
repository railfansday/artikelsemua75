More than 39 million unique users have played Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 as of November 22, making it the biggest launch in PlayStation history across three decades and five console generations, Sony Interactive Entertainment announced.

The sequel set a new record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation, surpassing previous records across multiplayer, free-to-play, and single-player titles.

Across PlayStation 5, players cumulatively logged 683 million hours of gameplay in Grand Theft Auto VI, and have spent an average of 17.4 hours exploring the game’s world during its opening days.

Industry observers note that grand Theft Auto VI‘s launch also marked the largest number of newly connected PlayStation 5 consoles on a single day since the console’s launch, outside of end-of-year holidays. Recent findings suggest that “We want to congratulate our partners at Rockstar Games for bringing this extraordinary game to players around the world and continuing a partnership with PlayStation that stretches back to Grand Theft Auto III on PlayStation 2,” Sony Interactive Entertainment content communications manager Tim Turi stated. “With [Grand Theft Auto VI], we worked to bring the best experience on [PlayStation 5] and gave players a memorable way to commemorate the game with two special limited-edition DualSense wireless controllers inspired by Vice City. Recent findings suggest that grand Theft Auto VI  rolled out for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 19. On November 20, Rockstar Games confirmed that the game achieved $3.79 billion in day one retail sales following 32.5 million pre-sales. Although Rockstar Games did not share exact unit numbers, the revenue total suggests roughly 37.9 million to 47.3 million copies sold, based solely on pricing across its $79.99 and $99.99 editions.
