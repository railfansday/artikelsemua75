In a recent development, disney has created some of the biggest pop culture phenomena of all time. Just this year, a fifth installment in the Toy Story franchise smashed expectations by scoring near-perfect reviews and more than $1 billion at the box office. The company now also owns some of the biggest cinematic properties, including Star Wars and the MCU, with the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday from the directing duo Anthony and Joe Russo set to take Disney's 2026 revenue to stratospheric heights.

Recent findings suggest that one of the biggest pop culture discoveries for Disney came back in 2013 with the release of Frozen. Olaf (Josh Gad), Elsa (Idina Menzel), Anna (Kristen Bell), and Kristoff (Jonathan Groff) were instant favorites of children all over the world, with their popularity reflected at the box office. Against a reported production budget of $150 million, the film made more than $1.2 billion worldwide, earning more than Iron Man 3 and becoming 2013's highest-grossing film worldwide.

Sources indicate that the franchise is set to continue next November, as the long-awaited royal wedding between Queen Anna and Kristoff takes place. These first plot details were revealed at D23 in August, with excitement for the threequel beginning to take off. We also know that a fourth Frozen movie is in development, although the third and fourth films are no longer planned as back-to-back releases. With Frozen 3 just a year away, fans are heading back to the snowy backdrop of the original on streaming, clearly unable to Let It Go. Bell, Menzel, Groff, and Gad are all expected to reprise their roles for the latest natural history project, which reimagines the characters of Frozen "as if they were inhabiting the real worlds depicted throughout the series."
