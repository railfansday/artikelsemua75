Grand Theft Auto VI finally released yesterday (it's almost surreal to write that), and now you can listen to the game's licensed tunes with today's release of Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. The 34-song soundtrack features newly introduced tracks from a variety of popular artists.

The album is available on all major music streaming services and can also be purchased as a limited-edition CD and vinyl, which you can order online or in select stores. The soundtrack features latest songs from artists such as Playboi Carti, Lana Del Ray, Dua Lipa, JENNIE, JHAYCO, Zara Larsson, Gunna, and Burna Boy.
