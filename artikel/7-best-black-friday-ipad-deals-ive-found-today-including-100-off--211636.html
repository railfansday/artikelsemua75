Recent findings suggest that on sale for Black Friday, the iPad mini 7 is a powerhouse in a small package. Our review praised its fast performance, solid battery life, and crisp, portable display, qualities that easily outweigh minor compromises like a portrait-oriented front camera and no Apple Pencil 2 compatibility.

Recent findings suggest that the iPad mini 8 is the biggest leap forward yet for Apple’s smallest tablet. Keeping the same sleek, lightweight build and gorgeous OLED display as its predecessor, the iPad Pro M5 plays it safe on the outside. According to recent reports, if you want a larger screen, you can get the 13-inch iPad Pro model.
