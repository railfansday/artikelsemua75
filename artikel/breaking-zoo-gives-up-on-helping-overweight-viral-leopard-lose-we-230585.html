A zoo in China has ‘given up’ on helping an overweight and viral leopard finally drop some pounds, admitting he is “too old” to help.

Industry observers note that you might think that just because an animal is in a zoo, it’s being fed perfectly and to a specific diet strategy. Over in China, this has been the case for a few years with a leopard known as Pan Baobao. The 19-year-old leopard has gone viral a few times now, especially for his resemblance to Officer Clawhauser from the Zootopia franchise. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Keepers at the Panzhihua Zoo have, over the years, attempted to help the chunky predator lose a few pounds by changing his diet and getting him moving more. However, with the latest year approaching, they’ve decided to put an end to those attempts.

As per CNN Travel, a keeper told them that Pan Baobao has become too old to help him stick to a diet.

“He’s getting old, so weight loss isn’t advisable. The priority is to maintain his current condition and keep him healthy,” they said.

According to recent reports, an official at the zoo, Zhang Songwen, echoed this while speaking to First Post. “It’s not that we’re not controlling its diet, but it’s getting old and shouldn’t be losing weight,” Zhang added.

Keepers have noted that the significant cat is in “good condition,” and they just want to provide a comfortable life for Pan Baobao, who is still in “healthy spirits” too.
