Sneakernet is a system for securely1 sending digital messages to other people without using the Internet. Instead, messages are automatically passed on to other Sneakernet users on your local network – at your hackerspace, an event or conference, your local cafe, etc — and then physically carried to their destination on your “sneakers”, where they will be automatically delivered to any recipients on the same network (or handed over to be carried further on by someone else). (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Sneakernet encourages slow, thoughtful participation in community and face-to-face meetings with the consumers who will carry your messages onwards to their destination, and entrust their own messages to you. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)
