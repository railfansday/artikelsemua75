After working 9 to 5 on the Friday of Stagecoach weekend, country music fans will be able to enjoy a whole day devoted to Dolly Parton at the country music festival next year.

It has been revealed that as revealed Monday (Nov. 23) on The Voice, the first day of the three-day festival will officially be known as Dolly Day. Host Carson Daly explained on the singing competition show that Stagecoach will encourage guests to wear their Parton “gear” before throwing it over to coach Riley Green, who showed off his tee emblazoned with the late country icon’s image. As a few ladies in the crowd squealed loudly, Green tastefully lifted his hoodie slightly to show that his Parton shirt had nothing by way of sleeves, giving the crowd a narrow glimpse of his biceps.

Recent findings suggest that stagecoach will take place April 23-25 in Indio, Calif. Garth Brooks and Zach Top are also among the headliners, leading a bill that features HARDY, Turnpike Troubadours, Charley Crockett, Jo Dee Messina, Kaitlin Butts, Willie Nelson & Family, Kelly Clarkson, Brothers Osborne, Shaboozey, Dasha, Stella Lefty and more.

Industry observers note that it’ll mark the first year of the country fest since Parton died at the age of 80 in August following a cancer battle. About a month afterward the icon’s passing, Clarkson opened up about a meaningful moment she shared with the Queen of Country on the day of the 2023 ACM Awards, where the two women performed a duet of “I Will Always Love You” at the ACM Awards. Just 30 minutes before they went on stage, Clarkson learned that her divorce from ex-husband Brandon Blackstock — who died three years later — had been finalized.

Sources indicate that “I found out right before that performance,” Clarkson stated on Las Culturistas in September. “She, kind of, caught me. I was like, ‘I’m sorry. It’s just kind of a wild moment for me.’ We just had this moment … What she said backstage to me, it was just a really beautiful thing.”.
