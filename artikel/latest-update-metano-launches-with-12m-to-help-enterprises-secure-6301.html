UPDATED 08:00 EST / NOVEMBER 19 2026.

Artificial intelligence agent security startup Metano Inc. today launched with $12 million in funding to help enterprises secure AI agents across endpoints, cloud infrastructure and software-as-a-service applications. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Metano’s platform is aimed at giving enterprises consistent visibility and control over AI agents, bringing together agent discovery, context-aware threat detection and real-time enforcement in a single layer.

It’s seeking to address the glitch wherein security teams face a growing and fragmented attack surface as organizations deploy AI agents across devices, coding environments, cloud infrastructure and SaaS applications. Metano argues that existing security products protect individual infrastructure layers but lack the visibility and behavioral context needed to secure autonomous agents operating across them. (Related: Deploy Hugging Face models easily with Amazon SageMaker...)

Sources indicate that making the problem harder is that AI agents, which often run with legitimate credentials and approved tools, can make decisions and take actions dynamically. Instructions, external content and operating conditions can all change how an agent behaves, according to Metano, introducing risks that may not be apparent during initial testing.

“Traditional security tools weren’t designed for AI agents that can make decisions and change their behavior as they operate,” Chief Executive Neelima Rustagi explained.

The first part of Metano’s offering is discovery, with the platform identifying approved and unapproved AI agents, Model Context Protocol servers, skills and software packages, along with their access privileges and potential security exposure.

The second part is Hive, Metano’s real-time context graph, which connects agent tasks, identities, tools, data access and actions. By analyzing activity across an entire session, Hive identifies suspicious behavior and determines whether an agent’s actions align with its intended purpose, allowing security teams to understand why an agent’s behavior may pose a risk.

Metano also offers enforcement as the third part of its platform, placing security controls wherever agents operate. Security teams are given centrally managed policies and guardrails to stop dangerous actions as they occur, even when an agent’s activity may bypass traditional network gateways.

Sources indicate that the platform’s detections draw on research from MARS Labs, Metano’s own AI security research team. Malicious skills, compromised software dependencies and vulnerabilities in MCP connections are among the threats the group investigates. The company states its agent detections are mapped to MITRE ATLAS and the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications.

Rustagi co-founded the organization with Chief Technology Officer Ratnesh Pandey. The Metano team also brings security experience from ServiceNow Inc., Zscaler Inc., Palo Alto Networks Inc., FireEye Inc. and Microsoft Corp.

Recent findings suggest that among the companies already using the platform is data engineering firm Streamvector Inc., which does business as Sigmoid. Srivatsa Ramarao, Sigmoid’s chief information security officer, noted Metano has helped Sigmoid adopt AI securely while minimizing shadow risks, allowing it to “say yes to AI across the business.”.

Sierra Ventures led the seed round, with Correlation Ventures, High Alpha and 360 ONE also participating. According to Mark Fernandes, managing partner at Sierra Ventures, every platform shift in security produces a few companies that “own the latest control point,” with agents representing one of the biggest disruptions he has seen.

Metano’s platform is generally available now, and the company claims security teams can begin using it in under 30 minutes. Also on offer is a free Eval Edition, which provides 30 days of real-time agent scanning so security teams can identify agent activity, risky configurations and potential exposure in their own environments. Metano has completed a SOC 2 Type 2 examination and holds ISO 27001:2022 certification, along with a listing on Okta Inc.’s Okta Integration Network.
