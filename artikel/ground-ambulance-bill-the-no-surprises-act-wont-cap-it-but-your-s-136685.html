The No Surprises Act protects you from surprise air ambulance bills but not ground ambulance bills. Before you pay a ground ambulance bill, check whether your state caps it, make sure your insurer processed the ride as an emergency, and ask the ambulance service for a discount or payment blueprint.

Recent findings suggest that with private insurance, the No Surprises Act keeps an air ambulance from charging you more than an in-network one would, as long as your plan covers emergency care. What you owe a ground ambulance depends on your state’s law, which may cap the bill at what an in-network ambulance would charge you.
