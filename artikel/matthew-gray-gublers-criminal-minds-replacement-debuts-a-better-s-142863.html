There's no doubt that while Reid was a genius and helped to solve cases because of his encyclopedic knowledge, he warmed hearts with his heartfelt and earnest nature. It was disappointing to see him not return to Criminal Minds: Evolution, but he'll soon be back on screens in another fun role.

It has been revealed that out of all the characters on the 15-year run of Criminal Minds, Reid proved that it took more than brawn to get a job done, even when in the field. He didn't even necessarily like to carry or use a gun, instead using his brains to catch killers and lure them into plots. There was even a chance to see the weight of being a genius, as it came with awkward social skills and the inability to sometimes get a joke. He was certainly missed when Criminal Minds returned with its Paramount+ revival, Evolution, with the show missing his quick ability to correct someone's facts and find a flaw in a pattern.
