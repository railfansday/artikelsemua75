Former President Joe Biden has raised a shockingly small amount of funds for his presidential library compared to former President Barack Obama.

The figures are fueling skepticism about how history will remember Biden's single term in the White House.

In 2017, Obama’s first year out of office, the two-term president managed to bring in $232.6 million in donations and contributions to fund the foundation behind his presidential library. By comparison, Biden secured just $1.5 million in 2025 for his presidential library foundation, per tax filings.

This is less than 1% of what his former boss raised in the same amount of time after leaving office.

Even before leaving office, President Donald Trump’s library foundation has already dwarfed that of his predecessor by raising tens of millions of dollars from a variety of sources, including companies that settled lawsuits with him.

Industry observers note that though the Joe and Jill Biden Presidential Library Foundation has yet to decide on a construction site, it’s already running a deficit, spending $600,000 more than it raised in 2025. A Biden library official claimed that it wasn’t actively fundraising in 2025 in a statement shared with CBS News.

The lack of enthusiasm for Biden’s legacy has led some to claim that Americans may simply forget about his presidency as the years go on.

In a recent development, "Presidencies usually fade," Axios’ Zachary Basu wrote of his legacy. "But in just one year, Biden’s has been reduced to a footnote by a successor committed to dismantling every pillar of Washington’s old liberal order.".

Heritage Action press manager Doug Blair told Fox News Digital, "Joe Biden spent half a century in Washington and did nothing to benefit the American people.".

According to recent reports, "The anemic fundraising for his presidential library is a fitting bookend for a failed presidency Americans are eager to forget," he added. "Joe Biden left Americans worse off by every conceivable measure and his legacy will be a southern border blown wide open, the disgraceful image of him checking his watch as fallen American troops returned home from Afghanistan, and endless scandals and investigations surrounding him and his corrupt family.'.

It has been revealed that A January 2025 Gallup poll found that 54% of U.S. adults believed history would remember Biden as a "below-average" or "poor" president.

Further complicating Biden’s fundraising efforts, and his ability to nurture his public image, is his advanced age and Stage 4 prostate cancer, which has metastasized to his bones.
