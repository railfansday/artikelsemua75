📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that open a my529 account if your state has no 529 tax break or gives one for any state’s plan. Utah’s plan charges about 0.1% a year on most of its ready-made portfolios and lets you build your own mix. If your state’s break requires its own plan, use that plan.

In a recent development, at a 5% yearly return, a family saving $200 a month for 18 years pays under $600 in my529’s quoted fees on a portfolio tied to the child’s college start year, by our math. The same family would pay about $1,600 at Morningstar’s 0.30% average for direct-sold plans, the ones you buy without an advisor.

Industry observers note that savers whose state has no 529 tax break, or one covering any state’s strategy, get target enrollment portfolios at about a third of the average direct-sold fee and more say over their mix than most intends give.
