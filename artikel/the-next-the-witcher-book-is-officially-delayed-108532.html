When Hachette confirmed the English release of Crossroads of Ravens, the prequel about Geralt’s early days as a witcher, the deal came with something for fans who already owned the rest of the series. David French, who translated the prequel and six of the eight books that preceded it, would go back to the two Stok had translated. Hachette originally put both on its 2026 calendar. By August, their U.S. release date had moved to April 27, 2027.

Orbit now lists July 27, 2027, as the U.S. release date for French’s upcoming English translations of The Last Wish and Blood of Elves, putting both books another three months behind the April date. The publisher has updated the paperback and ebook listings for both titles. Crossroads of Ravens, Sapkowski’s most recent original Geralt adventure, is already available in English.
