These will not be predictable choices like a Jahmyr Gibbs or Bijan Robinson score that offer zero value, but they won't be wild long shots such as a team defense. Industry observers note that mark Andrews was Jacoby Brissett's go-to target in his first start for the Baltimore Ravens. The tight end caught all eight of his targets for 82 yards against the Los Angeles Chargers. Those eight targets marked a season-high for Andrews, as did his 82 yards -- which led the team. This Carolina Panthers defense the Ravens are up against on Sunday allows 26.2 points per game, which is tied for 25th in the NFL. Carolina actually allows 38.5 points per game at home. According to recent reports, it was fun to see the Kansas City Chiefs passing attack take off against the Atlanta Falcons last week. Patrick Mahomes threw for 348 yards and a whopping five touchdowns. We can't credit trade-deadline addition Josh Downs alone for this resurgence, but the former Colt did catch four passes for 53 yards and a touchdown.

This week, the Chiefs host the 3-7 Arizona Cardinals -- who are 16.5-point underdogs! The Cardinals are tied in having allowed the most passing touchdowns this season with 22. I'll take Downs to find the end zone in his first home game as a Chief.

It has been revealed that again, I hate paying a -130 price straight up, but this could be a parlay leg. Parlay it with the Over in this game! The Tennessee Titans and Dallas Cowboys statistically have the two worst defenses in the NFL.

In a recent development, tony Pollard has rushed for 279 yards with 112 receiving yards and scored six total touchdowns over the past three games. He's been great, and now faces off against his former team, who allows more rushing yards per game (154.1) than any other club. Spotrac mentioned that Pollard is one rushing touchdown away from a $200K bonus. The former Cowboy scores on Sunday.

According to recent reports, how about Chargers running back Omarion Hampton last week? He rushed 22 times for 105 yards and a touchdown in L.A.'s first win of the season in Baltimore. Good things happened when he got the ball, which is something I think offensive coordinator Mike McDaniel realized. This week, the Chargers host the flailing Upcoming York Jets, who allow 135.3 rushing yards per game. That ranks third-worst in the NFL.

Justin Jefferson is surely excited that Kirk Cousins is back. He caught nine balls on 13 targets for 74 yards in the Week 10 win over the Green Bay Packers. Those nine receptions marked a season-high, and the 13 targets were the most he's seen in a game in two years.

According to recent reports, the 49ers will surely be a tough test for this newly introduced-look Vikings offense, but Jefferson remains Cousins' go-to weapon. I'll take him to score and break out the "Griddy" in Mexico.
