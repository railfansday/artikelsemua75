Twelve South, the company behind a range of premium Apple accessories, has launched a notable expansion of its Airfly brand. The company today unveiled Airfly Drive, a dongle that turns wired CarPlay into wireless CarPlay. It’s available to order starting now. Here’s how it works. Airfly Drive works with most cars that have factory-wired CarPlay, according to Twelve South. This covers more than 75 makes and model years back to 2014. Airfly Drive itself has a USB-A connector, but Twelve South includes a USB-C adapter in the box. It has a compact, aluminum design and is meant to stay plugged in. (Related: Training Design for Text-to-Image Models: Lessons from ...)

In a recent development, twelve South’s Airfly Drive doesn’t require an app or account. You simply plug it into your car’s USB port, pair it to your phone once, and you’re set. Going forward, the Airfly Drive will automatically reconnect to your phone to enable the wireless CarPlay experience. It also supports two paired phones, controlled by a single button that switches between them.

Twelve South outlines some specs and features:.

Wireless CarPlay is significantly more seamless than wired CarPlay, especially for those shorter trips around town. It’s a great quality-of-life enhancement for your CarPlay experience.

It has been revealed that you can order Airfly Drive from Twelve South today for $39.99. It’s available in black and white. It also works with Android Auto.
