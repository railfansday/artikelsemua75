The scale of this phenomenon is documented by the Protest Monitor, a Columbia University initiative dedicated to tracking demonstrations in defense of democracy and freedom of expression worldwide. In October 2026 alone, the platform recorded mobilizations involving more than 3.1 million people in Brazil, between 410,000 and 800,000 demonstrators in a single day in France, and approximately 150,000 in Serbia. Protests continued into November, with around 400,000 people taking to the streets in Spain and tens of thousands mobilizing in India.
