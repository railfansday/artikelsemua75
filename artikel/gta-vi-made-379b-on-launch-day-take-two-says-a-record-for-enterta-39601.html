In a recent development, grand Theft Auto VI made $3.79B in sales worldwide by the end of its launch day on Thursday, publisher Take-Two mentioned on Friday. It called the game the biggest entertainment launch in history, across any medium. (Related: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming Game in 5 D...)

In a recent development, the game also sold 32.5 million units before release, a record, according to Take-Two. The sales figure is a manufacturer estimate, and Take-Two did not say how it was calculated.

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a upcoming chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” noted Sam Houser, Rockstar’s founder and president.

In a recent development, for comparison, GTA V made $800M in its first 24 hours in 2013, and $1B in three days, Bloomberg reported. The biggest film opening, Avengers: Endgame, took about $1.2B worldwide over a weekend in 2019.

Take-Two had noted pre-orders beat expectations, and the game tied Metacritic’s record with a score of 99.

It has been revealed that analysts estimated it cost up to $1.5B to make, which Take-Two has not confirmed. Take-Two’s CEO has said Rockstar built it without generative AI.

In a recent development, the game costs $80, or $100 for an edition with extra in-game items. A PC version and an online mode will follow later. Take-Two shares rose about 2.7% on Friday.
