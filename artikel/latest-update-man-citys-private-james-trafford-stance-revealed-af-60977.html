Trafford has since gone from strength to strength at Leeds, while Donnarumma has received criticism for regularly lacklustre performances this season.

Despite their differing fortunes, Man City chiefs do not regret letting Trafford leave, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast.

Trafford has kept four clean sheets in 10 Premier League appearances and is the leading goalkeeper for save percentage (82.2) in the top flight so far this season.

Donnarumma is 13th in the same list, with 65.8 per cent, while he has kept three clean sheets in 10 league games and conceded five more goals than the Englishman.
