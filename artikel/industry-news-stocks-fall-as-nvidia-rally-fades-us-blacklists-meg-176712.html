Industry News: Stocks Fall as Nvidia Rally Fades; US Blacklists Megaspeed, Alibaba Unit

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (100 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that US equity futures decline as the rally following Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia eases. The Trump administration has put Megaspeed and a Singaporean subsidiary of Alibaba on a US trade blacklist that bars it from buying advanced AI chips. China will continue exporting critical minerals to the US, maintaining the trade truce with Washington. Patrick Armstrong of Plurimi Wealth joins "Bloomberg Brief" to discuss the implications for the AI trade with Huang back as Nvidia CEO. Amy Wu Silverman of RBC Capital Markets examines the outlook for the volatility market. (Source: Bloomberg).
