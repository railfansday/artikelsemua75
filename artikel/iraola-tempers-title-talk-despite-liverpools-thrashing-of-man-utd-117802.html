Andoni Iraola believes it is too soon to consider Liverpool as potential Premier League title contenders after they thumped 10-man Manchester United 4-0 at Anfield on Sunday.

Liverpool were helped by Leny Yoro's eighth-minute sending-off for a last-man foul on Alexander Isak, with the Reds using their numerical advantage to ruthless effect. (Related: How to train a new language model from scratch using Tr...)

Dominik Szoboszlai flashed home the free-kick from Yoro's challenge in the 10th minute before Florian Wirtz superbly turned Rio Ngumoha's cross into the net three minutes later.

Mauricio Pochettino, taking charge of United for the first time, introduced Harry Maguire for Bryan Mbeumo in an enforced change to try and bring some more defensive stability, but Liverpool eventually breached the Red Devils' back line in the second half. Bradley Barcola's blistering run ended with a pinpoint cross for Ngumoha to tuck home, with Victor Munoz coming off the bench to seal an emphatic win eight minutes from time.

Since the start of the 2021-22 season, Liverpool's four wins by 4+ goals against United are the most any Premier League team has achieved against another in all competitions.

It was a result that moved Liverpool back into the top four and within one point of Manchester City and Brighton, who dropped points to Fulham and Hull City, respectively.

Arsenal are seven points clear of Liverpool after drawing 2-2 with Newcastle United, but Iraola was keen not to get carried away with the euphoria of beating the Reds' rivals.

According to recent reports, "It's still early days. I think we are missing the two massive games we lost here at home between Arsenal and [Man City]. They are making the difference in the standings," he noted.

"This is still so early. This doesn't stop because we have a very difficult game against Club Brugge, and then we have the big derby.".

Pochettino became the first manager in Premier League history to see his team concede twice and receive a red card in the first half of his first match in charge of a club.

Indeed, it was also the quickest Liverpool had gone 2-0 up in a Premier League match against United, going two ahead after just 12 minutes and 40 seconds.

Iraola is now unbeaten in his seven Premier League matches against United (W3 D4), and even quipped that his players could celebrate at half-time after going two goals in front.

In a recent development, "I think it's a big win; always against United it is a big game," Iraola added. "I think also you can enjoy it; you can see it coming.

"It's difficult to enjoy in the Premier League. Normally you suffer even if you win, but I think we could celebrate at half-time and enjoy it a little bit and have lovely moments.

Recent findings suggest that "We have missed some clear chances. In other games you will remember those chances; we've controlled the game, and everything was happening around the box.

"It's a 4-0 that was good for us in the standings but also confidence-wise, trying to get better results here at home.".

There was also another standout display from Wirtz, who created 10 chances in open play for Liverpool, the most by a player in a Premier League match since Man City's David Silva against West Brom in March 2015 (11).

Wirtz now has eight goal involvements (three goals, five assists) in 16 appearances across all competitions in 2026-27, with the Liverpool attacker also starring in the international break under former Reds boss Jurgen Klopp with three goals in two games for Germany in the Nations League.

"He's in a lovely moment, and we want to keep him there as much as we can," Iraola noted of Wirtz.

"He's in a situation against 10 players where you need players like him to control the situation and see the passes in behind.

Recent findings suggest that "He's also arriving more and not just scoring but being a threat. He's becoming another option, and I'm happy with him.".
