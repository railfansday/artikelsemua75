📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Anthropic stock touched an intraday low of $80.01 on Tuesday, an 8.7% decline, which put it under its $83 IPO price in the fourth trading session since its Nasdaq $NDAQ debut. The stock was down 7% on Tuesday.

The stock had already traded under the IPO price before the opening bell, reaching a low of $81.28 in premarket trading. Nasdaq futures were down roughly 2% ahead of the open.

Anthropic closed Monday at $87.64, down 1.43%, as other AI stocks rallied after Nvidia $NVDA reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. Nvidia stock jumped on the news. Huang had resigned from the chipmaker on Nov. 12.

Anthropic's underwriting banks have exercised the over-allotment option, according to Bloomberg. That means the banks have no way to prop up the stock should it slip beneath its offering price. (Related: Should I Quit My Job? A Decision Framework...)

Anthropic opened trading on the Nasdaq at $111.50 on Thursday under the ticker ANT, 34% above its IPO price, in the largest IPO on record. The stock closed that day at $97.17 and then fell 8.5% by Friday's close. (Related: House Democrats will start next Congress with largest m...)

On Monday, the stock moved between gains and losses in early trading while the broader market climbed. Alphabet $GOOGL stock rose 6%, Meta $META stock gained more than 5% and Microsoft $MSFT stock advanced 4%. The Nasdaq opened more than 6% higher after a 10% slide the previous week, its worst week since 2020, according to Bloomberg.

It has been revealed that concern about how rapidly AI spending is growing had pressured stocks last week. Anthropic's filings show it has pledged to spend nearly $901 billion through 2045.
