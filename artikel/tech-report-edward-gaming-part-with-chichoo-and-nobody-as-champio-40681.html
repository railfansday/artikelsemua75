EDward Gaming have lost captain nobody and CHICHOO, two days after releasing Smoggy to Bilibili Gaming. The VCT China partner team must now rebuild before the China Kickoff in Hangzhou in January.

EDward Gaming have parted ways with CHICHOO and captain nobody, two pillars of the side that won VALORANT Champions 2024. Both players left the VCT China partner team on the same day. EDG confirmed both exits on Weibo. Their exits end a five-year spell at the organisation. (Related: Architectural Choices in China's Open-Source AI Ecosyst...)

Recent findings suggest that the departures follow swiftly on from Smoggy’s move to Bilibili Gaming. Within a week, three players had gone from the EDG roster. No replacements have been named.

It has been revealed that wang ‘nobody’ Senxu joined EDG on 7 September 2021. He served as the team’s captain and in-game leader. He had started out as the side’s Initiator before taking on those leadership duties. CHICHOO, whose full name is Wan Shunzhi, played the Controller role. Alongside CHICHOO, he formed the core of the only Chinese team to win Champions. That title, sealed on 25 August 2024, made EDG the first organisation from China, and from Asia, to lift the trophy. The team’s total winnings stand at roughly $1,592,287. (Related: Umamusume horse girls are taking over a Denver Broncos ...)

Sources indicate that the pair were still central to the side in 2026. At Masters London, CHICHOO and nobody each played 12 maps. Their average combat scores were 179 and 184 respectively. EDG finished third in London and earned $125,000. Their run ended with a 0-3 defeat to Leviatán in the lower bracket final on 20 June 2026.

Recent findings suggest that where nobody goes next is not yet confirmed. Sheep Esports reported, citing sources, that he is expected to join Trace Esports for 2027. Trace’s Kai and deLb are noted to be under contract until the end of 2027. If that move happens, nobody and Smoggy would both start the latest season with different organisations.

The timing is awkward for EDG. The organisation was named a VCT partner team on 21 October, on a two-year contract. That deal now begins with a heavily changed roster.

The China Kickoff is an S-Tier LAN event. It is organised by TJ Sports, Hero Esports and Riot Games. The league’s partner sides make up eight places in the field, with the remaining four split evenly between visiting teams and open qualifier entrants. EDG will need a settled line-up in place to compete there.
