Norwich City have won five games on the spin following their 2-1 home victory over Cardiff City at the weekend, and one player's upturn in form has coincided with this incredible streak.

Norwich's campaign thus far has been something of a rollercoaster, as, after 16 games, the Canaries are still yet to draw a game in the Championship this season, winning eight and losing eight.

According to recent reports, now on a five-game unbeaten run courtesy of victories over Charlton Athletic, Watford, Derby County, Bristol City, and Cardiff, Norwich have flown up the table. However, before this winning run came to fruition, a six-game losing streak across all competitions preceded it.

Belgian head coach Philippe Clement will certainly be hoping for more consistency moving forward, and with the January window approaching, may well also be looking to replicate some past transfers.

With Clement arriving at Carrow Road in November 2025, the former Monaco and Rangers boss has officially ticked over a year in charge in Norfolk, and it would be fair to suggest there have been more highs than lows during his reign.

The January 2026 window proved to be especially fruitful for the 52-year-old, who oversaw the additions of the likes of Sam Field, Ali Ahmed, Mohamed Toure, and Paris Maghoma at Norwich.

Maghoma, 25, joined from Premier League outfit Brentford in a deal worth around £800k, having worked his way up the EFL with loans at AFC Wimbledon, Milton Keynes Dons, and Bolton Wanderers, but failing to nail down a place in the Bees' side.

The former Tottenham Hotspur youth prospect scored once and grabbed three assists in 14 Championship games during his first six months at the club, but now has seemingly found his groove.

At the beginning of this campaign, Maghoma initially struggled to nail down a place in the Norwich starting XI, as after playing 71 minutes in the 2-1 home defeat to West Bromwich Albion in the season opener, the Englishman would have to wait nearly another month for his next league appearance.

The attacking midfielder has found his stride in the Championship, and if he can continue this level of form, Norwich will certainly feel as though they have struck the transfer jackpot.

Having signed Maghoma for just £800k, Norwich will already be rubbing their hands at the prospect of a potential lucrative sale in the near future, especially if he can continue this rich vein of form.
