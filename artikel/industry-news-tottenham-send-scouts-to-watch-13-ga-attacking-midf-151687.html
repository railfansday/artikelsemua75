Tottenham Hotspur are keeping a close eye on Stade de Reims midfielder Yaya Fofana, with the highly-rated Ivorian reportedly keen on the prospect of playing in the Premier League.

Roberto De Zerbi’s side are considering midfield reinforcements as they look to improve their attacking options, and the 22-year-old has emerged as an interesting candidate after his impressive performances in France.

Sources indicate that according to a Footmercato report, Tottenham are keen to secure Fofana’s signature and have been closely monitoring his progress. The north London outfit reportedly sent representatives to watch him in action against Saint-Étienne as they continue assessing his potential.

According to recent reports, the youngster has attracted considerable attention with his performances for Reims, registering 10 goals and three assists in 13 appearances across competitions this season. His attacking contributions have made him one of Ligue 2’s standout performers.
