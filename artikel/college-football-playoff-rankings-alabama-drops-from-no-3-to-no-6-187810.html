Is Indiana in need of a miracle to get into the College Football Playoff?

The Hoosiers fell six spots from No. 8 to No. 14 in Tuesday night's CFP rankings. Indiana (9-2), needs help to make it to the Substantial Ten championship game even with a win over big underdog Purdue on Saturday night.

The Hoosiers are the fourth-highest ranked team from the Big Ten behind No. 9 UCLA, No. 12 Oregon and No. 13 Iowa. If the Bruins lose to No. 15 USC on Saturday and Iowa wins Friday and Oregon wins Saturday, there will be a four-way tie atop the Big Ten. In that case, both Oregon and Iowa make the conference title game.

UCLA fell five spots after its undefeated season ended with an 18-16 triple-overtime loss to Michigan.

"For the last six weeks, Indiana has had six one-score games, three of the last four have been in overtime," CFP selection committee chair and Arkansas athletic director Hunter Yurachek said. "And until Saturday, they had found a way to win those games. But Saturday they did not, they fell behind a team like Texas Tech with one loss and they were in direct comparison with their counterparts in the Massive Ten in Oregon and Iowa.".

Much to the relief of Tennessee fans, the Crimson Tide are finally behind Tennessee in the rankings.

Industry observers note that alabama dropped from No. 3 to No. 6 and is behind both Texas and Tennessee now. The Crimson Tide are still safely in the playoff if they beat Auburn on Saturday. Alabama needs some help to get to Atlanta and get another shot at the Bulldogs.
