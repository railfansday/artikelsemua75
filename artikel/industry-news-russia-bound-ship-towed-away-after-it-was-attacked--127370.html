Finland says work is continuing on the Sparta II ship on Sunday.

A Russian cargo vessel that was hit by a drone attack in the eastern Gulf of Finland is being towed away, authorities have said.

The Sparta II had been bound for Russia's Baltic exclave Kaliningrad when it was hit on Saturday morning, sparking a fire aboard.

Industry observers note that it then drifted into Finnish waters and its crew were evacuated to Russia. Finnish defence forces mentioned Ukraine was likely behind the attack. Kyiv has yet to comment. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Finland's Border Guard said on Sunday afternoon that fire was no longer visible from the outside but thermal cameras revealed that some containers on the ship were still warm.

The Sparta II was "slightly listing", Finnish authorities added, but the "situation is stable" and no fuel or other "hazardous substance leaks" had been detected. (Related: Industry News: Diffusers welcomes Stable Diffusion 3...)

Kaliningrad Governor Alexei Besprozvannykh stated two drones appeared to have been used in the strike and that there was "no threat" to his territory's supplies.

The Sparta II was sanctioned by the US in 2022 and Ukraine earlier this year, and is run by a Russian company, according to the Ukrainian and US governments.

The organization says it was established within the structure of Russia's defence ministry in 2011 and since 2015 has been operating within the military construction complex.

Finland's coast guard remarked it had been alerted to the attack at around 08:50 local time (06:50 GMT) on Saturday and that the cargo vessel had been located approximately 50km west of the island of Suursaari in the Gulf of Finland.

This prompted "temporary aviation and maritime restrictions" around the area, Finnish border officials noted.

"War is spilling over into our immediate vicinity," Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo noted. "Ukraine has the right to fight back against the aggressor.".

Shortly after the incident, Finland's defence ministry noted a Russian helicopter was suspected of violating the country's airspace, potentially linked to "rescue or tracking operations".

While Besprozvannykh initially remarked the crew of the Sparta II intended to repair the ship and carry on their journey, the Finnish Border Guard later said they had been evacuated to another Russian vessel that was en route to Russia.

Jukka-Pekka Lumilahti, director of maritime operations at the Finnish Border Guard, stated during a news conference on Sunday that one Sparta II crew member had suffered minor injuries.

The Russian rescue vessel Murman is leading towing operations with the support of a Finnish guard ship and a Estonian naval patrol vessel.

Kaliningrad is a Russian exclave that sits between Poland and Lithuania.
