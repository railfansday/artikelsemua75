Gemini 4 Hydrogen: a faster model for general purpose robotics

Over the past month, Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 4 Carbon brought frontier physical reasoning to robotics: understanding 3D scenes, reconstructing real environments in simulation, practicing tasks in sim before running them on hardware, and directly controlling robot arms from camera images. Last week, we introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, which brings strong spatial and embodied reasoning to a much faster model built for speed and efficiency.

Sources indicate that to help developers put Hydrogen to work, today we're launching the Robotics plugin on Google Antigravity: a set of skills for hardware onboarding, simulation and robot control turning Antigravity into a one-stop robotics workbench. Together, they enable a developer to go from a robot in a box to one that can perform a working application in an afternoon. To install the plugin, open Antigravity, go to Customizations and search for Robotics on the Build With Google shelf. (Related: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers and Ray Tune...)

Sources indicate that gemini 4 Argon and Carbon highlighted that a general model can drive a robot arm directly, and Gemini 4 Hydrogen preserves that ability. Give it a goal in plain language and a camera view, and it intends the task, writes and runs the control code, and keeps adjusting until the job is done. On the Artificial Analysis Robotics 0.1 benchmark, Gemini 4 Hydrogen controls a real 6-axis arm through eight everyday tabletop tasks, from pouring a drink to packing blocks into a box. Hydrogen scores 58, similar to Argon at a lower cost per token. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

It has been revealed that hydrogen brings the specialized skills of our Gemini Robotics ER 2 (Embodied Reasoning) models into the core Gemini 4 model series, and it beats them on nearly every axis. On the public ERQA benchmark, Hydrogen outperforms Gemini Robotics ER 2 on spatial reasoning tasks such as multi-view correspondence and 3D trajectory reasoning. For developers using Gemini Robotics ER 2 today, we recommend trying out Gemini 4 Hydrogen for these workloads.
