Netflix’s animated Cinderella retelling Steps focuses on the perspective of the princesses supposedly “wicked” and “evil” stepsisters, Lilith (voiced by Ali Wong) and Margot (Stephanie Hsu).

Recent findings suggest that amanda Seyfried voices Cinderella in the film, and she gets the chance to show off her vocals and sing some songs alongside needle drops from Olivia Rodrigo, Benson Boone and Jess Glynne to name a few. BlackPink member JENNIE also performed a song penned by Justin Tranter, Julia Michaels and Jason Gill for the film. Tranter is behind several of the original songs for the movie.
