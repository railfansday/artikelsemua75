Riyadh’s most key hotel season is turning into one of its weakest.

Industry observers note that the year’s final quarter is normally the capital’s strongest, built on corporate travel, conferences, exhibitions and entertainment. This year, Iran-backed Houthi strikes on King Khalid International Airport in October cut off inbound flights just as peak season began. A run of attacks since then, the latest on Nov. 24 in King Abdullah Financial District, has kept foreign airlines, event organizers and international visitors away.
