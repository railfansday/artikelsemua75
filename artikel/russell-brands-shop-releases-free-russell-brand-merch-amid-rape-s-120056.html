Sources indicate that A “Stand with Him” button takes site visitors to the merch shop, now selling two “Free Russel Brand” T-shirt designs and a baseball cap bearing the same phrase. All items are sold for $34.99.

It has been revealed that brand, 51, was accused of misconduct and sexual assault in 2023, vehemently denying the accusations and pleading not guilty to three counts of rape, three counts of sexual assault and one count of attempted indecent assault. The disgraced comedian, who has maintained his innocence, was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault in an October trial. (Brand was found not guilty of the remaining charges.).

Following his rape conviction, Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison.

Sources indicate that “Beloved brothers and sisters in Christ, God bless you for your support,” he wrote in a statement read aloud by his wife, Laura Gallacher, in a November 15 YouTube video. “As you know, I am not guilty for the offenses I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening.”.

He continued at the time, “The good news is that Christ is with us when we choose him, and I do. I am incarcerated in HMP Littlehey. Little as in small, and hey as in, ‘Hey, I’m in the biggest sex offenders prison in Europe.’ The good news is that I have already seen that the staff here are strong, they are kind and they are dedicated. And as for my fellow prisoners, I now know that I am in no position to judge anyone.”.

It has been revealed that brand and Gallacher, 39, have been married since 2017. She has remained steadfast by the Get Him to the Greek actor’s side throughout the misconduct scandal and trial.
