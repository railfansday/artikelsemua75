Breaking: Norwegian Luna cruise ship review: What to expect on board

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (187 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Norwegian Luna stands out among Miami-based cruise ships for its refined design and more intimate, upscale atmosphere, despite being smaller than some competitors.
  • The ship offers spacious and modern cabins, including exclusive solo and suite options, and features a wide range of dining venues—though many come with extra charges.
  • Entertainment includes tribute shows and unique experiences, but venues can be crowded and the ship has fewer large attractions compared to larger ships in the market.
  • Top-deck space is divided between family-friendly attractions and exclusive, extra-fee areas, resulting in a bifurcated experience depending on willingness to pay for added privacy.
  • Aggressive upselling and numerous extra charges for specialty dining, entertainment and exclusive areas may impact the overall experience.
  • Main pool areas and some included dining venues can feel undersized and crowded, especially on sea days.
  • Booking early is advised for solo cabins and show seating, as both are in high demand and fill quickly.
