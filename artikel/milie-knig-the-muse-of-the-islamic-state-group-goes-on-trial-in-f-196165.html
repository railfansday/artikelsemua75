Émilie König, 'the muse of the Islamic State group' goes on trial in France

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (85 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that french anti-terrorism prosecutors once dubbed her "the muse of the Islamic State group". On Thursday, Émilie König appears before a special criminal court in Paris, seeking to convince judges that she has been deradicalised. Originally from western France, König spent a decade in Syria and is accused of being one of the Islamic State group's most active French propagandists. Charged with criminal association with a terrorist organisation, she maintains that she has no blood on her hands.
