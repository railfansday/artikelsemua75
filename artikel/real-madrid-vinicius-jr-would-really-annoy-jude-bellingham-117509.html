Has Vinicius Jr lost his place in Real Madrid’s starting eleven? It’s the trend. Suspended in La Liga, the Brazilian saw Yan Diomandé perform very well in his place, and the Ivorian clearly put Madrid in his pocket. For Merengue public opinion, the former RB Leipzig must be ahead of Vinicius Jr. On the Cadena COPEthe former goalkeeper of the club and the Spanish selection Santi Cañizares also revealed that Bellingham is not particularly a fan of the Brazilian….

“Bellingham, when he’s on the field, he’s a very generous guy. If he sees that a teammate is not giving himself, it upsets him. There are others like Bellingham on the team, but Bellingham is the one for whom it is most visible. (…) The solution is difficult. If Mourinho is able to make Vinicius run and integrate him into the team dynamic, we will have to take our hats off to him”indicated the former goalkeeper. It’s pretty clear.
