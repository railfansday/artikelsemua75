Sources indicate that olivia Rodrigo performed her somber yuletide ballad, “Miss This Christmas,” for the first time live at her concert in Nashville Monday night, Nov. 23.

“Miss This Christmas” was included on the B-side of the vinyl version of Rodrigo’s first holiday single, “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),”  which was released earlier this month. But the song still hasn’t gotten a wide digital release, and as Rodrigo noted in her introduction, “no one’s ever really heard it before.” (Though some unauthorized rips have popped up online.).

It has been revealed that the performance began with Rodrigo singing over a simple piano accompaniment, the arrangement later blooming with choral vocals and a tender guitar line. Lyrically, “Miss This Christmas” is the obverse of “Take Me Home (4 Christmas),” with Rodrigo tending a broken heart and passing along a message to an ex: “Next year’s gonna look real different/Tell your folks I’ll miss this Christmas.”. Prior to her two-night stand at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena, Rodrigo stopped by Jack White’s Third Man Records to cut a live-to-acetate performance in the label’s famous Blue Room venue. The performance consisted of two 20-minute and included a fitting cover of the White Stripes classic, “Dead Leaves and the Dirty Ground.”.

The current leg of Rodrigo’s Unraveled Tour in support of her Grammy-nominated album, You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl In Love, will continue through next month, wrapping Dec. 20 in Las Vegas. She’ll return to the stage in 2027 two lengthy residency in Los Angeles (Jan. 12 through 29) and Brooklyn (Feb. 11 through 28).
