Liverpool are ready to sign the next Jude Bellingham, and he dreams of joining the Reds.

Sources indicate that it was Julian Ward who ultimately pulled out of the move to pursue the England international back in 2023. Still working as the club's sporting director, Ward though the deal would be too costly for the Reds - especially as they needed a complete midfield revamp.

It was Ward who then set in motion the policy of Liverpool buying not one but two and three for the price of Bellingham that summer with Alexis Mac Allister, Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch arriving at Anfield. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Merely two years later, all three would become Premier League winners while starting in the same midfield under Arne Slot.

So, it's fair to say Ward probably doesn't have an regrets about that decision.
