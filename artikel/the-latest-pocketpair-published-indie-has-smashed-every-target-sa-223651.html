May I remind you, GTA 6 wasn't the only game to come out in the last two weeks. Clicky Islands, a fun throwback island builder published by Palworld company Pocketpair, arrived earlier in the month, and according to the publisher's estimates, it's doing very well indeed.

On Twitter, John 'Bucky' Buckley, the head of publishing and communications at Pocketpair, takes a second to highlight just how successful the game's been. "Clicky Islands introduced ten days ago, and it's smashed every target!" he writes. "Really proud to be supporting this game and the two Polish wizards who cooked it up!".

It has been revealed that it is a delightful little gem that merges citybuilding and idle game ideas. Essentially, you land on a remote island, and start making it more habitable for your people, but how you do that is distinctly streamlined – you mostly just click around and let things be built, gradually accruing more resources.
