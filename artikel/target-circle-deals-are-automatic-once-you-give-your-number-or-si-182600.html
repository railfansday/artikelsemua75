In a recent development, join Target Circle free, then enter your phone number or scan the Target app’s Wallet barcode at every checkout, since its deals come off automatically only for members who identify themselves. Before each trip, open the app to activate your bonuses and add manufacturer coupons, which never apply on their own.

Those steps matter more since April 2024, when Target relaunched Target Circle and dropped its 1% back on purchases. Now a member who doesn’t identify at checkout gets nothing from the program.

Sources indicate that the relaunch also ended the old routine of tapping to save each offer, though bonuses and the manufacturer coupons in the app still need that tap. Bonuses are personalized offers that vary from member to member. Some take money off. Others pay Target Circle Rewards, credit you can spend on a later trip, once you reach a spending total or use a service like Order Pickup. Rewards expire if you go a year without earning or redeeming any. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Recent findings suggest that the coupons are worth adding because they come off on top of the automatic deals. Target’s coupon policy allows one manufacturer coupon per item alongside its own offers, so a bottle of detergent with a 20%-off Circle deal and a $1 manufacturer coupon you added gets both discounts at checkout. Two other products carry the Circle name. The Target Circle Credit Card takes another 5% off most Target purchases at checkout. Target Circle 360 is a paid tier at $99 a year or $10.99 a month. Its main perk is free same-day delivery on orders over $35, which costs other shoppers $9.99 an order, so 10 deliveries a year cover the $99 fee.

Wait to make substantial purchases until Target Circle Deal Days, the members-only sales Target held in March, June, and October in 2026, the October one with up to 40% off thousands of items.
