Industry observers note that author of 14 goals in 14 matches this season with Manchester City, Erling Haaland (26 years old) is effective as usual. Under contract until June 2034 with Manchester City, he is fully involved in the project linking him to the Mancunian club. However, his future could also be linked to the sanctions hovering over the Sky Blues for non-compliance with the Premier League’s financial fair play rules.

Sources indicate that in the podcast The Good, The Bad & The FootballPaul Scholes spoke on the subject. The former glory of Manchester United imagines him leaving for FC Barcelona in the event of Manchester City being relegated: “Barcelona. Even if he has the physique of a Real Madrid player and I can imagine him perfectly in the Real Madrid jersey, he will go to Barça.».
