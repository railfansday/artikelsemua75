Industry observers note that early Black Friday deals are popping up everywhere from Amazon to Costco. For those looking to upgrade an old but smaller TV to a newer model without splurging on an OLED, the 65-inch Samsung M80H Mini-LED TV is 29% off and down to a record-low $497.99 (originally $697.99). If you want to downsize (or go massive), the 55-inch, 75-inch, and 85-inch models are also discounted ahead of the holiday.

Industry observers note that while it’s considered an entry-level 4K smart TV, the M80H is packed with Samsung Vision AI features, and like the rest of the brand’s TVs, it runs on the Tizen interface. A high 144Hz refresh rate, as well as VRR and  AMD FreeSync Premium, makes it a decent choice for gamers compared to the standard 60Hz of cheaper models.
