On Tuesday, Zcash price showed a massive rally, rising more than 10%.

In a recent development, at press time, ZEC is reading near $961. The recovery follows a sharp a correction that pushed ZEC toward $860 over the weekend.

Despite the rebound, the ZEC remains under pressure from institutional fund withdrawals.

One of the most important developments is a Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) vote running from November 16 to November 30.

Community members are considering proposals to change how the grants treasury is managed, including a proposal to transfer 50,000 ZEC into the Network Sustainability Mechanism.

Industry observers note that zcash gained 7.69% on November 4, rising on high expectations surrounding NU7. The upgrade introduces several changes to the network's transaction processing and reward structure. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

Sources indicate that as the upgrade went live on November 5, the target block interval went from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. According to the project’s statement, the change makes private payments confirm three times faster. (Related: Future Value Calculator: How to Project Your Investment...)

Recent findings suggest that ZEC recently tested the $860 area before recovering. This level is notable because derivatives data previously identified a concentration of leveraged long positions around it.

Sources indicate that if Zcash returns to that zone, forced liquidations could amplify volatility. $950 is an initial recovery level, followed by the psychological resistance around $1,000.

However, break below $860 could expose ZEC to selling pressure and further decline.
