📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Lili Reinhart is in discussions to star in and executive produce Amazon MGM and Mattel Studios’ “Polly Pocket.” Reinhart joins the film after Lilly Collins exited due to scheduling issues. Collins remains on as an executive producer.

Sources indicate that clea DuVall will direct “Polly Pocket” with a screenplay from Dan Brier and Jordan Weiss. Producers include Robbie Brenner for Mattel Studios and Reese Witherspoon and Lauren Neustadter for Hello Sunshine. Mattel Studios, Hello Sunshine’s Emily Ferenbach, Jeff Waxman and Jennifer Madeloff serve as executive producers. “Polly Pocket” is set for release on Prime Video. Ever since Mattel hit big with 2023’s “Barbie,” the toy maker has pushed hard into the film market. 2026 saw the release of “Masters of the Universe,” which hit theaters in June, and “Matchbox,” which debuted on Apple TV in October. Other upcoming projects based on Mattel properties include “Barney” with Ayo Edebiri, “Hot Wheels” from producer J.J. Abrams and director Jon M. Chu and “Rock ‘Em Sock ‘Em Robots” written by and starring Vin Diesel.

The Polly Pocket and her miniature play sets first hit shelves in the summer of 1989 through Bluebird Toys. Mattel acquired the brand in 1998. Mattel reintroduced the toy in 2018 with a more modern design to appeal to a newly introduced generation of kids.
