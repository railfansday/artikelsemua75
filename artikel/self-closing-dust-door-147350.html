Recent findings suggest that dust containment is critical on remodeling jobs. It only takes a little dust on a dining room table to sour a client’s impression of even the highest level of craftsmanship. The zipper door has long been the go-to option for getting in and out of temporary dust-containment setups, but torn zippers and snagged plastic can make them frustrating to use.

Recently I decided to try the Re-U-Zip magnetic door. Instead of a zipper, the system uses a magnetic entry strip that attaches to peel-and-stick hook-and-loop backers adhered to the plastic barrier. When the job is done, the magnetic strip peels off for use on the next project. (Related: Tech Report: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system t...)

I’ve found that the magnets have just the right amount of draw, making them easy to separate when walking through with an armful of tools or supplies but strong enough to close behind you. One thing to note is that these doors don’t seal to the floor, so you’ll want to keep the work area under slightly negative pressure if possible.

A kit with a 6-mil vinyl panel, one reusable magnetic entry strip, and four mounting strips cost about $85 at reuzip.com.
