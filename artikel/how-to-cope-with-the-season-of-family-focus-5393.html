Many people save surprise pregnancy announcements for gatherings, which can be agonizing for anyone who wants nothing more than to make such an announcement themselves. Family and friend parties bring forced encounters. Intrusive questions are common: So when are you going to start a family? I hear you’ve been struggling. I think you should see my cousin’s doctor. You need to gain weight, exercise less, try my herbalist, or just adopt.
