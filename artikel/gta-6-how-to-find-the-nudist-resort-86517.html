Sources indicate that if you want to see some of Leonida’s freest citizens, look no further. We’ve covered exactly where to find the spot, plus everything you can do in this little chunk of paradise.

Industry observers note that finding the nudist resort will require you to swing a bit out of the way if you want to find it early, before the main story leads you further out than Hamlet.
