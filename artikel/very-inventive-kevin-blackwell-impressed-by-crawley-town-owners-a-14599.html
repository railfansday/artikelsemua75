It has been revealed that since Blackwell has been in charge, the club have brought in three new, experienced players – Connor Wickham, Ben Heneghan and Ayman Benarous – and now the new boss has a huge squad to work with. “When I was taking all of them, all 28 on my own, that was tough,” he mentioned.

And are the likes of Wickham and Heneghan fit too start? “Well, we've had to gradually get Conor and Ben a bit fitter and that's why maybe the international break has helped us a little bit.
