Sources indicate that katy Perry and Justin Trudeau had a rare red carpet date in West Hollywood last weekend, but now they’re far from California. The couple has spent the week in Europe, visiting three different countries.

It has been revealed that yesterday, they were photographed outside of the Ritz Paris dressed for late fall. Perry wore a tan suede jacket with black pants, while Trudeau opted for a navy and gray ensemble. (Related: The Positive Personality Trait Linked To Having A Crush...)

They were also seen earlier that day in Italy, leaving the Four Seasons hotel in Milan.

Two days before that, the former Canadian prime minister and the singer were in Spain, where they attended Atresmedia’s Metafuturo conference on November 17. Trudeau was one of the speakers.

Perry has posted her own glimpses of their relationship on Instagram here and there, though she hasn’t posted any photos from their Europe trip yet. (Related: Why Some Parents Struggle To Let Their Children Grow Up...)

According to recent reports, earlier this month, she revealed they spent her 42nd birthday together on a different vacation. “Climbed to level 42 this week!” she wrote. “Was feeling indifferent about the inbetweeness of age 42… Then it was explained to me that 42 is actually the answer to everything. Though I still don’t understand, I’m ok with turning 42 as long as I can turn 42 with you ♥️ back to living…”.

The singer described Trudeau as the love of her life in a September interview with People. She reflected on the past year, saying, “I learned how to step back into my purpose, and I got the greatest gift from this [Lifetimes] Tour: I got to meet the love of my life.”.

In a recent development, before dating Trudeau, Perry was manifesting their love story. “I always wanted this epic legendary love, and I was talking to my therapist about it: Everything in my life is epic, but this is kind of the missing puzzle piece—then it highlighted up,” she stated.

Industry observers note that she praised her boyfriend’s support of her music career, saying, “He knows the words, he’s supportive, and he’s in awe; it’s super healing. He is really heart-centered, and I think that we align in that way. I’m so grateful.”.
