How this cult-favorite cleaner burnished its branding to connect with its DIY-inclined audience

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, when Bar Keeper’s Friend needed to update its branding, it did exactly what it does best: gave it a little polish. The 144-year-old manufacturer’s April 2026 refresh of its signature powder cleanser and soft cleanser burnished the gold of its packaging, brightened the blue of its logo, and added a glimmering apostrophe, finally, to appease the grammar sticklers among its fans. “This product can make things you own look like they’re new again,” claims Felicia Zhang, Bar Keeper’s Friend’s head of marketing. “So we wanted to have it really stand out.” The company also added visual representations of the various surfaces (including a ceramic Dutch oven and an aluminum sheet pan) that the oxalic acid–based powder cleanser can clean and made the instructional text on the back bigger and easier to read. Bar Keeper’s Friend also upped its presence on social media, capitalizing on growing organic interest. From 2025 to 2026, user-made content featuring Bar Keeper’s Friend on Instagram and TikTok (from creator partners and organic consumers) more than doubled, and earned media value nearly tripled. View this post on Instagram The brand’s fans (which it calls “Keepers”) are eagerly consuming—and creating—this content. The company has found that though they skew somewhat older, Keepers are united by what Zhang calls “this shared belief that if a job’s worth doing, it’s worth doing right.” It has channeled that ethos into content about DIY projects, vintage finds, and cookware across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, aimed at people who love thrifting and restoring secondhand products. Zhang sees the growing interest in Bar Keeper’s Friend as a counterbalance to the current cultural focus on AI and tech. “We’re all inundated with technology,” she says. “There’s something really nice about a physical product that helps you care for other physical products.”.
