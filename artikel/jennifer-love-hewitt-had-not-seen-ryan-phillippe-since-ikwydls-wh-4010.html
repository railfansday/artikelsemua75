Nearly 30 summers later, Jennifer Love Hewitt recently had an onscreen reunion with Ryan Phillippe, courtesy of an upcoming 9-1-1 crossover.

The actress, who plays 9-1-1 dispatcher Maddie Kendall in the ABC flagship series, noted that before reuniting in the crossover with 9-1-1: Nashville, she and Phillippe had “not seen each other” since starring together in the 1997 teen slasher I Know What You Did Last Summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

“We’ve been having the best time,” Hewitt told People after they teased their reunion in an Instagram video. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Noting that it was “so great” getting to reunite with Freddie Prinze Jr. as their IKWYDLS characters Julie James and Ray Bronson in last year’s legacy sequel, Hewitt explained that working with Phillippe on 9-1-1 “was very different” as he is “newly introduced in that universe,” recently joining 9-1-1: Nashville in Season 2 as New York detective Rogue Kelly. Watch on Deadline.

Sources indicate that “I’ve been in this universe for a while,” said Hewitt, who has played Maddie since Season 2 of 9-1-1 in 2018. “It’s such a safe, lovely place to be with the best people. And he and I have not seen each other since I was 18 years old.”.

Having “never seen each other again” after wrapping the film, Hewitt noted that working together again “was a dream” and Phillippe “hadn’t — for me — changed at all,” adding that “he was the same in the most beautiful way.”.

“And it was great. When we did the first movie together, his character was so terrible to me in that movie and hated me, but he was so lovely. … all I wanted to do was drink Joy Cola and like hang out with him because he had to be so mean to me [in the movie] that I just like wanted to be his friend. And he did it,” Hewitt added. “He held that space for me still, which was really beautiful.”.

The upcoming 9-1-1 crossover with 9-1-1: Nashville comes amid the parent series’ 10th season and the spinoff’s sophomore season, airing back-to-back on Thursdays at 8/7c and 9/8c on ABC.
