Recent findings suggest that FARMINGTON HILLS, Mich., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The holiday season is upon us, which means it is time to start planning and preparing for those elaborate gatherings with family, friends, and co-workers. The International Hearing Society is sharing tips on how you can create hearing friendly environments for everyone to hear more cheer at every celebration. While many people can relate to the stress of planning and/or attending these events, fewer understand the added challenges of attending a party while also experiencing hearing loss. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), "Nearly 20% of people experience some degree of hearing loss and that percentage is increasing."1 In fact, "By 2050, nearly 2.5 billion people are projected to have some degree of hearing loss,"2 making accessible holiday hearing experiences that much more crucial.

Members of the World Hearing Forum, a World Health Organization (WHO) project, recently met in Geneva, Switzerland, to prioritize hearing loss prevention, identification, and treatment efforts, emphasizing that hearing care is primary care and that gatherings must remain accessible to people with hearing loss. About the International Hearing SocietyThe International Hearing Society (IHS) is a membership association that represents hearing healthcare professionals worldwide, including Hearing Aid Specialists. IHS members are engaged in the practice of performing hearing assessments, ordering the use of, selecting, fitting, and dispensing hearing instruments, counseling patients, and providing aftercare services. Founded in 1951, IHS protects, represents, and promotes the interests of hearing healthcare and hearing healthcare professionals. For more information visit www.ihsinfo.org.
