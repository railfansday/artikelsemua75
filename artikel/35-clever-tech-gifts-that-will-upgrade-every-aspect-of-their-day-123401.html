As much as we love a upcoming throw pillow or vase under the Christmas tree, we know that some of the most coveted presents are electronic. Cool tech gifts can genuinely change your giftee’s life for the better. They can help them travel better, upgrade their home, get them through the workday faster, or just make them smile. So whoever you’re shopping for, from the chronically online teen to the discerning coworker, will appreciate these genius gifts.

Recent findings suggest that just don’t forget to personalize the item to your recipient. If they have interests such as photography and videography, consider getting them a tripod or GoPro camera so they can document all their action-packed adventures. The nature lover in your life will appreciate a smart bird feeder, and the chef will use the smart stand mixer all the time.

Below, we rounded up dozens of the coolest tech gifts on the market. Of course, we included tested and approved favorites from our very own staff. The technology that changed our lives will make for some super thoughtful gifts. (Related: Tech Report: Course Launch Community Event...)

Sources indicate that the Brick duo is kind of the anti-tech tech gift. If your loved one has been wanting to cut down their screen time, this is the smartest tool to help them do that. It works as a separation between you and your most distracting apps. One that can’t be dismissed without physical interaction. “I keep a Brick at my desk and in my kitchen as barriers to my doomscrolling habit,” Associate Shopping Editor Janae McKenzie says. “But the Brick Night is a real game-changer—it’s Bluetooth location-based, so the moment I cross the threshold into my bedroom, all my social apps are locked, so I actually have to go to sleep at a decent hour.”. Poplights are playful, renter-friendly wall sconces that can fit in any space; no electrician required. Instead of hardwiring, they use a rechargeable battery pack, so you can display the warm light and vibrant color with no ugly wires in sight. Installation is as easy as attaching a few Command strips.

Industry observers note that the steamer is the upcoming wrinkle-releasing mainstay, leaving the iron in the past. But instead of merely spraying steam, the Nori Cloud Max comes with vacuum technology that pulls the clothes toward the steamer. This smooths out your clothes with way fewer passes.

Recent findings suggest that this is the ultimate travel tech accessory for frequent fliers. “Any overpacker knows how valuable suitcase space is,” McKenzie says. “I bought two of these travel bags because of the genius mini-vacuum that comes with them. They work like space-saving bags, but small enough to fit in a carry-on and take on the go. No more calls to the hotel front desk for a vacuum.”.

In a recent development, the easiest way to make your bathroom feel like a spa? Add a towel warmer. Stepping out of the shower, you can retrieve a fluffy towel from this heater to wrap up in, easing the harsh cold of tile floors. It comes with a timer, so you won’t have to worry about leaving it on overnight.

It has been revealed that yes, landlines are cool again. This retro receiver attachment plugs into your phone’s USB-C port, so when you pick up a call, you can have a more tactile experience. It comes with a mic and speaker, so it’s primed for everything from your next Zoom meeting to long-distance catch-up chats.

In a recent development, this bouncy LED lamp comes in three colors and two sizes. The spring-loaded body adds some whimsy to your home office. It lights up with a soft touch and provides up to eight hours of warm glow.

Canopy is known for its powerful water filtration, air purification, and humidifiers, but its latest launch is the most versatile. The inline shower filter is designed to work with any showerhead, from handheld extensions to rain showers. It’s compact and removes contaminants for cleaner skin and air.

Not everyone has the square footage for a full-scale smart fridge, so Rocco just launched a mini solution to that problem. The Mini Mini is sized for your desktop, countertop, or nightstand. Perfect for bedtime skin care essentials or a midday Diet Coke break.

We forget how stale the air quality gets. Birdie can help you remember. The mechanical indicator droops when you need to crack a window and let in some fresh air. It doesn’t hurt that the simple Danish design is so chic.

According to recent reports, this speaker is a built-in party starter. It’s waterproof and drop-proof, so it’s perfect to bring to the beach, pool, or anywhere you need some powerful tunes. The steady bass and 28 hours of playtime keep up with any gathering. It’s even a built-in power bank.

Industry observers note that no one knows they need an electric candle lighter until they have one. Take it camping, or just have a safer way to enjoy your candle collection. The slim, rose-gold profile is cute enough to leave on display.

We're not the first to call a desk pad "sexy," but something about this sleek Grove Made piece gives off that exact vibe. It's made from natural materials like pine rosin and wood flour, making it durable, antistatic, and fingerprint-resistant. The pad comes in a variety of eye-pleasing colors and five different sizes to elevate any office space.

Sources indicate that you don't have to be an avid bird watcher to enjoy this smart feeder. Armed with a smart camera, the feeder can take photos of birds as they come to feast on the seeds, alert you with bird arrival notifications, and identify up to 1,000 species so you can learn about the types that drop by for a snack. You can attach Bird Buddy to a tree, pole, wall, or fence.

"Fellow's sleek Stagg EKG electric kettle is now undoubtedly the coolest thing in my kitchen," says tester Katelyn Lunders. "I used to put my stovetop kettle on while I was getting ready in the morning and then sprint to turn it off when it started screaming at me, but Fellow's kettle is free of an annoying whistle (and requires no cardio). Instead, it has a touch screen that allows you to pick your perfect temperature and then swiftly brings the water to that point and holds it there until you're ready to pour it over your coffee or tea. A true revelation for hot beverage lovers everywhere.".

In a recent development, "When I first met my husband's parents, I gifted his dad a digital picture frame so that he can keep up with our lives in NYC and travels (they live in Virginia)," claims Senior Commerce Editor Marina Liao. "These types of frames are so convenient because you can upload photos directly from your phone, from anywhere in the world, and they will populate into the digital frame." The best part about Skylight is that should the Wi-Fi go down, this frame will still display the photos (you just can't receive anything until the connection is restored).

Some features to note about this waterproof model: the hindsight captures 30 seconds before you hit record so you don't miss a moment, and it has a new and improved slow-mo capability, plus TimeWrap 3.0 and night lapse.
