According to recent reports, but a major criticism of the league is the lack of relegation. There are no repercussions for teams that finish a season at the bottom of the table due to the system. Industry observers note that "So the playing style changes when you play a bottom of the league team in Europe. They fight for their lives. Muller's comments have gone viral, with many other big names sharing their reaction.

Son Heung-min, considered as one of the greatest Asian players in football history, moved to MLS in August 2025 when he signed for Los Angeles FC in a record-breaking $26.5 million deal.

Recent findings suggest that he was asked about Muller's comments ahead of LAFC's MLS Cup Western Conference first-round clash against St Louis City SC. Son admitted that he agreed with the German's assessment and urged the league to rethink the system. He said:.

In a recent development, "I can't control that, but I'm enjoying this league... But I think Thomas was spot on. I thoroughly agree. He had an amazing career in the Bundesliga and he feels he's right to say it.

According to recent reports, "I was in the Premier League - every week, it's the biggest pressure. If you're not performing, if you're not playing well, if your team starts struggling or is in the bottom half of the table then you're having a difficult time.

Industry observers note that "I think it makes it more fun. I want the best for the MLS, so maybe they can look into it and do something.".

According to recent reports, son has played 56 matches for LAFC since joining in August 2025. He has scored 21 times and recorded 25 assists.
