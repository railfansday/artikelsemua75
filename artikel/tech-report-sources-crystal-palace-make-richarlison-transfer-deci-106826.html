Crystal Palace are holding back on a move for Richarlison ahead of the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The 29-year-old has been cast out of Robert De Zerbi's plans in North London, and is widely expected to move on in January, leading to questions about his next destination. (Related: Industry News: Take-Two CEO is shutting down questions ...)

Following Thomas Frank's appointment, there have been suggestions that the Brazil international could follow his former manager to Selhurst Park in the coming months.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider Podcast, Crystal Palace are not right now considering a move to sign Richarlison despite the speculation. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

The addition of a fresh striker is unlikely to be a priority for the Eagles in January, given the options already at their disposal, as they strategy to address other positions first.

Tottenham, meanwhile, are unlikely to entertain a move for Richarlison to join a Premier League rival and the finances involved could put him beyond Crystal Palace's reach.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 18 November that Crystal Palace are planning to back Frank when the January transfer window opens.

That could include moves for his former players, should the new boss wish to target players he already knows well and could fit into his system better than his current options.

However, any move for Richarlison is nothing more than speculation at this stage, with Crystal Palace highly unlikely to make a move for the Tottenham star in January.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Probable from the Palace point of view on signing Richarlison, there's so many things sort of in the way there.

"I mean, Spurs aren't going to be willing to sell him within the Premier League at the moment, so that's already an issue.

"And Crystal Palace, I can't imagine them paying the sort of fee that Tottenham are going to want in January or all the wages that Richarlison is going to be on.

"It's not going to be a cheap deal by any means, and Palace are in a good position with the strikers they've already got, so they're not going to be looking at Richarlison as a priority.".

Suggestions that Crystal Palace could make a move for Richarlison has emerged as he is widely expected to leave Tottenham when the January transfer window opens.

Having been cast out under De Zerbi, a number of clubs across world football have been made aware of the forward's situation and could make a move in January.

However, Football Insider revealed on 20 November that Tottenham will block Richarlison from joining a rival Premier League club ahead of the mid-season window.

That will come as a blow to potential suitors including Crystal Palace and Everton, with the latter having also missed out on a move during the summer.

Instead, Tottenham would prefer to sell Richarlison abroad, where he has attracted interest from clubs in Turkey and Brazil in recent months.
