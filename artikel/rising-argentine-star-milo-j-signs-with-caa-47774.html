It has been revealed that argentinian rapper, singer, producer and songwriter Milo J signed with CAA, the agency unveiled Friday (Nov. 20).

Industry observers note that the 20-year-old artist released his career in 2021 as part of the Buenos Aires-based indie artist collective BajoWest and gained attention via YouTube and TikTok. In 2023, he received his first Latin Grammy nomination in the best rap/hip-hop category. That same year, he released his debut studio album 111, which featured Peso Pluma, Yahritza y Su Esencia, Nicki Nicole and Yami Safdie. He later signed with Sony Music Latin. Sources indicate that in September, Milo released his third studio album, La Vide Era Más Corta. In the wake of that release, which blended traditional Argentine folkloric influences into his sound, he began playing arenas and stadiums in Spanish-speaking markets. Earlier this year, he took home 13 trophies at Premios Gardel — Argentina’s equivalent of the Grammys — including artist of the year. He won four Latin Grammys at this year’s ceremony, including record of the year, and has been nominated for two 2027 Grammys, including best Latin song (“Niño”) and best Latin rock/alternative album (La Vida Era Más Corta).

It has been revealed that among other recent milestones, Milo recently performed on The Tonight Show With Jimmy Fallon. He’s slated to perform at a crop of upcoming events including Vive Latino, Coachella, Isle of Light, Panama Crossroads and Estéreo Picnic. He’ll also soon headline his first reveals in Brazil, including at the Rock in Rio festival, as well as shows across Central America at 7,000-17,000-capacity venues and at a stadium in Córdoba, Argentina.

Milo is managed by his mother, attorney Aldana Ríos.
