According to recent reports, in a Nov. 19 preprint, Atrey Desai and colleagues analyzed reports written between 2015 and 2026 for 26 double-blind conferences and workshops in machine learning and natural language processing. The researchers knew who wrote each report, but the reviewers’ names had never been publicly linked to them. In 19 cases, the text itself was already publicly available without a name.
