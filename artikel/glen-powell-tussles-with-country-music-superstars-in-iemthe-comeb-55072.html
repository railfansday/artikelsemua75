📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that rodney Dangerfield set the bar for return-to-school comedies in 1986’s aptly titled Back to School. He decried that “If a man has no education, he's got nothing” before proceeding to throw money at all of his problems and hilariously ignore his own advice for the next hour and a half. Then Adam Sandler put his own, unique spin on the film when he sent Billy Madison back to grade school a decade later. And when The Comeback King hits theaters on February 5, 2027, Glen Powell will walk into homeroom as a country singer looking to save his career by finishing his degree.

Sources indicate that in the first trailer, which debuted on Wednesday, November 18, Powell plays Aubrey King, a country music superstar dating the biggest pop star in the world (played by Madelyn Cline). His life is turned upside down when she cheats on him and then writes a song blaming him for the deed instead, which sends his career down the toilet. So, his manager convinces him to head back to school and rediscover what he wants to say with his music. Plus, fall in love with a teacher (Cristin Milioti) and joke around with The Comeback King’s insane list of guest stars—including Miranda Lambert, Jelly Roll, Brad Paisley, Chris Stapleton, Shaboozey, and Reba McEntire.

In a recent development, powell and director Judd Apatow mostly wrote The Comeback King as they filmed it—which is normal for Apatow, but not so much for Powell. “For a guy like me, who likes to be prepared, it’s a nightmare,” Powell told Esquire in our cover story last month. The script came together from the best lines of their improvisational takes. Spitfires of one joke after another until Apatow laughs, or says “That’s funny,” Powell says. But for now, there’s little of the actual schooling revealed in the trailer.

You can catch a lot of the jokes from our celebrity cameos, however, like when Jelly Roll tells a disheveled Powell that he “smells like how people think I smell.” There’s also a great line when one of Powell’s managers, played by Kumail Nanjiani, says that fans answered a poll about him and noted that his eyes were too close together, which wasn’t even one of the options. “They wrote it in like you were a Libertarian candidate,” he says. The Comeback King begins a busy 2027 for Powell, who also has the sci-fi film The Great Beyond coming out in October by J.J. Abrams. Also in the works? A Ron Howard firefighter movie that is reportedly not connected to Backdraft; the third season of his Hulu football comedy series, Chad Powers; a Lou Gehrig film with Richard Linklater; and likely the start of Top Gun 3—whenever Tom Cruise is ready. As Powell joked to Esquire, “Hopefully I’m not the age of Maverick in Maverick by the time I make it.”.
