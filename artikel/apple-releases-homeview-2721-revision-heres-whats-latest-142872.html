HomeView software 27.2.1 is now available for all consumers.

You can install the enhancement by opening the Home app on your iPhone then tapping the three dots in the top-right corner, opening Home Settings, and going to the ‘Software Update’ section.

Apple has not yet published release notes for the revision, so it’s unclear what changes it introduces.

Industry observers note that it’s possible HomeView 27.2.1 will address some of the Siri AI issues that users have been encountering and sharing online, or other bugs. It likely also includes security updates that first shipped in iOS 27.2.1 and related builds.
