Aberdeen chief executive Adrian Bevington is confident the club are set for a period of stability following the appointment of Mario Frick as manager on a two-and-a-half-year contract.

Recent findings suggest that the Pittodrie club have had three managers this year alone. Four of the last five appointments failed to reach the 50-match mark. Stephen Robinson, Frick’s predecessor, lasted just 28 games.

“That (stability) is the objective here," mentioned Bevington, who himself only joined Aberdeen less than three months ago. "What I would say is we have gone through a really robust process. It wasn't just looking at one or two names. The wider search to begin with was extensive then we narrowed it down.”. (Related: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers and Ray Tune...)

The former Club England managing director admitted the process of identifying a fresh manager began before Robinson was relieved of his duties after the recent 4-0 home defeat to Hibs.

In a recent development, “Any club nowadays, or most clubs, would always have some prep work going on, just as a matter of course,” he said. I think it's very difficult for clubs to be able to react to a situation if they've not already had some prep work going on. That's not any criticism of any individual manager.”. Bevington stressed that Frick had not been appointed on the advice of players. Goalkeeper Marius Muller has been reported as claiming he first suggested the former Luzern manager’s name to sporting director Lutz Pfannenstiel. “We had some player conversations,” noted Bevington. “But they weren’t the decision makers.”.

Stuart Duff, the Youth Phase manager, has been promoted to Frick’s first-team coaching staff, while Craig Hinchcliffe remains goalkeeper coach. Bevington stressed it was notable for Frick to be supported by those with knowledge of the club’s youth players.

Industry observers note that aberdeen right now has 24 players out on loan with various clubs, including striker Alfie Bavidge and full-back Dylan Lobban at Inverness Caledonian Thistle and Dunfermline respectively. There has been a clamour for Bavidge and Lobban to return.

“There's conversations going on at the moment as to what we're going to do moving forward,” mentioned Bevington. I believe that we have capability to recall certainly a number of the players. I'd need to look at every individual contract.”.
