It has been revealed that it's been 13 years since Anne Hathaway was last in the Oscars race, and that particular journey ended in success for the Hollywood favorite. Hathaway won Best Supporting Actress for 2013's Les Misérables, five years after her first nomination in the Best Actress category for the underrated 2008 film Rachel Getting Married. Now, she looks set to not just be nominated, but win again, as the favorite to pick up the Best Supporting Actress prize for her scene-stealing performance as Penelope in Christopher Nolan's historical epic The Odyssey.

Sources indicate that the latest ambitious feast of filmmaking from Nolan scored an incredible $1.8 billion at the box office, becoming his highest-grossing film of all time and one of the biggest non-franchise films ever. This was thanks in no small part to the film's incredible cast, which included Lupita Nyong’o, Charlize Theron, Matt Damon, fresh James Bond Robert Pattinson, and Hathaway's most likely contender for Best Supporting Actress, Samantha Morton.

As Hathaway's Academy Awards campaign gets underway, one of her other 2026 movies is proving a major hit on streaming. At the time of writing, the sci-fi monster movie The End of Oak Street is the most-watched movie on HBO Max in the world, outperforming the likes of Matilda, The Legend of Tarzan, and the Colleen Hoover adaptation Reminders of Him. Also starring Star Wars alum Ewan McGregor, the film follows the pair's quiet suburban home as it is ripped from its place and time and relocated into prehistoric danger. The End of Oak Street earned just $120 million against a reported budget of $85 million and received positive reviews from critics.
