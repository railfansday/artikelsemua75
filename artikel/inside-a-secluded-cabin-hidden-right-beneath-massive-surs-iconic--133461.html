A secluded retreat tucked beneath one of California’s most photographed landmarks in Significant Sur has come on the market for $3.65 million.

The home at 39320 Coast Road sits in Bixby Canyon and is one of only seven residences behind a private gate.

“Millions recognize Bixby Bridge, yet very few will ever experience the landscape beneath it,” says listing agent Michael Hornig of the Strock Team at eXp Realty. (Related: Tiny Agents: an MCP-powered agent in 50 lines of code...)

Industry observers note that the canyon also comes with a literary history: In 1960, Jack Kerouac retreated to a cabin here owned by Lawrence Ferlinghetti, the poet and co-founder of City Lights Bookstore. His time in the canyon formed the basis for his 1962 novel, “Big Sur.” While this is not the original Kerouac cabin, it shares the same setting. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

According to Hornig, the canyon has also been home to the film director Sam Peckinpah and the Disney illustrator Ron Dias.

Sources indicate that “There’s an extraordinary feeling about this canyon,” Hornig indicates. “You’re surrounded by a landscape that inspired some of America’s most influential writers, and remarkably, much of that atmosphere of solitude and wilderness remains intact today.”.

Industry observers note that built in 1984, the single-level home has three bedrooms, two full bathrooms, and 1,489 square feet of living space. Vaulted, open-beam ceilings, hardwood floors, and three wood-burning stoves characterize the interiors, as do large windows that frame the surrounding redwoods. A wraparound porch overlooks the forest.

Sources indicate that “Luxury doesn’t always have to be expressed through scale or elaborate architecture,” Hornig says. “Here, the luxury is the relationship between the home and its surroundings.”.

Recent findings suggest that the property covers two parcels: a 0.79-acre residential lot and an adjacent 0.33-acre creekside lot. It is move-in ready, with a private well, septic system, and electricity. There is also a fenced backyard, storage shed, pasture space and parking for 10 vehicles, including EV charging.

Owners share access to a privately held canyon enclave of more than 140 acres. There, a creekside trail winds through redwoods and ferns to a secluded beach beneath the bridge.

“You can walk from your front door through a redwood forest, follow the creek toward the Pacific, and emerge beneath one of the most recognizable bridges in the world,” Hornig says.

Next to the property, Brazil Ranch adds about 1,200 acres of U.S. Forest Service land for hiking, hunting, and recreation. The historic Old Coast Road offers miles of biking, horseback riding, and backcountry exploration. Highway 1 is about five minutes away. Carmel, Monterey, and Big Sur’s restaurants and state parks are about 20 minutes away.

“To me, the greatest luxury here is the privilege of belonging to a landscape that most people will only ever admire from a distance,” Hornig states. "It's this contrast between one of California’s most recognizable landmarks and the hidden residential community beneath it.".
