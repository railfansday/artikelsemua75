Disney is folding Hulu into Disney+ in December 2026. Four reasons explain the move, and the standalone Hulu app's fate is unconfirmed.

It has been revealed that anyone who pays for more than one streaming service knows the routine of opening one app, scrolling its menus and then opening another to find a show. The companies behind those apps are now combining them. Disney $DIS wants viewers to browse Hulu and Disney+ programming without switching apps, and its rivals are doing the same: HBO Max and Paramount $PARA+ strategy a joint discounted bundle for early 2027, according to LEVEL UP. Media companies once competed by launching more services, and they are now competing to keep viewers inside fewer of them. That change raises a practical question for anyone with a Hulu login, who wants to know what it means for the service they already pay for.

Recent findings suggest that hulu's indicates are not going away, only the separate place to watch them. So far the merger changes the screen, not the bill. The premium bundle of Disney+ and Hulu currently costs $21.99 a month, according to LEVEL UP, and Disney has announced no price increases tied to the integration.

A single search is only as useful as the catalog it covers, and the Disney+ catalog does not yet include all of Hulu. As long as those gaps remain, Hulu's separate app still has a use, and finding everything in one place is a goal rather than a description of the product Disney sells today. Recent findings suggest that disney presents that result as a gradual expectation, not as something it has already achieved. "We believe our continued focus on product and technology will yield further benefits in engagement and churn over time," Disney noted in its Nov. 17 earnings revision. Disney has attached no target to that gain, so retention, the reason it most needs to justify the merger, is also the one it cannot yet demonstrate.

Their answer is what Disney is betting on, and the company has not seen it yet.

It has been revealed that disney $DIS describes its absorption of Hulu as almost finished, and the last features to move are live TV and premium add-ons, the services that most set Hulu apart. "Our app unification of Disney+ and Hulu is nearly complete," Disney mentioned in its Nov. 17 earnings update. Live TV and add-ons join a fully unified Disney+ experience in December 2026, the final stage of a rollout Disney confirmed in 2023, according to Parade. The change has come in steps over several years rather than all at once. What Disney has announced is a unified experience inside Disney+, not the end of Hulu's own app, so whether the standalone Hulu app survives is still an open question.
