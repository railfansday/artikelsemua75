Wash it in the shade with car soap and two buckets, then vacuum and treat cloth seats and carpet with baking soda and diluted vinegar. Save a pro detail for paint scratches, stubborn stains or smells, and a car you’re about to sell.

It has been revealed that consumer Reports states to expect to pay more than $200 to have a car deep-cleaned inside and out. Neither the wash nor the wipe-down needs pro equipment.

According to recent reports, fill one bucket with water and car wash soap, not dish soap, which Consumer Reports warns may strip off the protective wax. Rinse your wash mitt in a second bucket of plain water after each section, so the grit doesn’t scratch the paint.
