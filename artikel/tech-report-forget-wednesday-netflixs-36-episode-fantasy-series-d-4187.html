Long before the arrival of Netflix’s Jenna Ortega vehicle Wednesday, the streaming service offered an earlier reboot of a classic ‘90s fantasy franchise, and its comparatively lengthy run highlighted the biggest problem with modern streaming highlights. As the wait for Stranger Things season 4 crept closer to a three-year gap back in 2022, Netflix prepared for the show’s eventual ending with a new, equally massive, nostalgic, and family-friendly replacement franchise.

It has been revealed that while the Stranger Things franchise started out dark and mature and gradually grew more PG-rated as the show’s audience got bigger, Netflix’s replacement series was far more family-friendly from the start. The Addams Family re-imagining Wednesday saw director Tim Burton offer his spin on the famous franchise, borrowing liberally from the live-action Addams Family movies of the ‘90s but shifting the show’s focus to Jenna Ortega’s titular deadpan heroine.

Thanks to a superb performance from Ortega, Wednesday became the colossal success that Netflix hoped for. However, as the wait for Wednesday season 3 continues, it is clear that there are cracks in the show’s formula. Outside a strong cast, the Addams Family reboot isn’t entirely sure of its target audience, has a wonky, uneven tone, and drags out its central mysteries. Moreover, Netflix’s earlier hit Chilling Adventures of Sabrina was a strikingly similar alternative that already addressed these complications in its longer run.

It has been revealed that originally developed by Riverdale creator Roberto Aguirre-Sacasa for the CW, but moved to Netflix midway through the show’s pre-production, Chilling Adventures of Sabrina was conceived as a spinoff of Aguirre-Sacasa's earlier teen show. While Chilling Adventures of Sabrina shares its main character and basic premise with the classic ‘90s children’s show Sabrina the Teenage Witch, the darker, more mature Netflix series was adapted from Aguirre-Sacasa’s subversive Archie Comics series of the same name.

Sources indicate that chilling Adventures of Sabrina began as a magic high school show akin to The Vampire Diaries spinoff Legacies, Wednesday, or Disney’s recent copycat show Coven Academy. However, as the series continued, Chilling Adventures of Sabrina offered viewers some daring experimental storylines that saw the supernatural fantasy series extend its tendrils beyond the world of witches and demons by introducing Lovecraftian Elder Gods and existential stakes. Chilling Adventures of Sabrina was essentially the same show as Wednesday, except the earlier Netflix series released 36 episodes in the span of two years, and this allowed the show to explore more exciting, unpredictable plots than the later, markedly safer Addams Family reboot. It has since become increasingly common for major streaming shows to take long hiatuses between seasons, meaning viewers have received only 16 episodes of Wednesday in the last four years.

The fact that Chilling Adventures of Sabrina’s two-season, four-part run released more than twice as many episodes as Wednesday in half as much time is not merely a question of quantity over quality. Like Wednesday, Chilling Adventures of Sabrina was a glossy, critically acclaimed fantasy series with expensive effects, a celebrated ensemble cast, ambitious serialized plots, and high production values.

No show that attempts to adapt the works of HP Lovecraft, no matter how tongue-in-cheek it might be, does so without putting a serious budget behind the endeavor. Thus, Chilling Adventures of Sabrina’s 36-episode run instead serves to highlight just how much streaming has changed in the years since the COVID-19 pandemic ended the show’s production in 2020.

It has been revealed that after Stranger Things introduced three-year waits between seasons of streaming indicates, this was adopted as a new normal, and expensive productions like The Rings of Power regularly featured waits of two or more years between seasons. While there is an increasing backlash against this practice, which might explain the humdrum reception of Wednesday season 2 part 2, it will be a long time before viewers can expect to see streaming shows return to Chilling Adventures of Sabrina’s prolific output rate.
