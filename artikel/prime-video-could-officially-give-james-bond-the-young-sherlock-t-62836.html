James Bond is back at the forefront of pop culture, thanks to the announcement that Robert Pattinson will portray the super-spy in Denis Villeneuve's upcoming Bond movie. While opinions on Pattinson's casting are mixed, it signals that Amazon MGM Studios is swinging for the fences with Bond. Now, a new report hints that Amazon is planning another project that could radically reshape how people see James Bond. The Sun recently ran a piece claiming Prime Video is looking to develop a television series focused on Bond's early years before he joins MI6.

Industry observers note that the reasoning for such a project is twofold. First isthe success of Young Sherlock, another prequel seriescentered on a literary icon. Young Sherlock quickly rocketed up Prime Video's streaming charts, thanks to Guy Ritchie's involvement and its exploration of a young Sherlock Holmes' evolution into a master detective. Second is the Young James Bond novels by Charlie Higson, which chronicle Bond's education at Eton College. The Sun also reports that Amazon has been considering some form of a Bond spin-off for quite some time:. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

"There was no way that Amazon were going to pass up on the opportunity to make more spin-off versions of the Bond franchise, and some sort of prequel is a natural choice.".

According to recent reports, while a series focused on Bond's younger years sounds appealing, it could be a step too far for the franchise. Going through with the project could also cement a fear most movie fans have held since Amazon took the reins of the Bond franchise. (Related: Tech Report: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons i...)

The dangers of a James Bond prequel run parallel to those of any prequel: approaching questions fans have wondered about could lead to disappointing answers. Take the Star Wars prequels. The idea that the Force is fueled by microscopic bacteria or that Emperor Palpatine's first name is "Sheev" stripped away much of the original trilogy's mystique. A young James Bond prequel could do the same thing, especially since most of the character's backstory was already explored during Daniel Craig's tenure. Learning that Bond was an orphan explains his life choices and why he joins MI6, and you don't really need to go deeper than that.

If Prime Video eventually goes through with a young Bond series, it raises a question: Will more Bond spin-offs follow? One could imagine a series being centered on Bond's CIA ally and close friend Felix Leiter, or the formation of the terrorist organization SPECTRE. But that approach could dilute the Bond franchise. It could also reduce the impact of the James Bond movies themselves, which is something Amazon doesn't want. James Bond is a character built for the big screen, and trying to spread out his adventures is a death sentence for the franchise.

While Prime Video might be eyeing a young James Bond series, a great story focusing on the super-spy's younger days already exists, and it was released earlier this year. That story is 007: First Light, the video game in which a 26-year-old Bond works to achieve his "00" status. First Light quickly gained critical acclaim due to its story, the gameplay that features Bond doing what he does best (aka killing bad guys and romancing beautiful women), and the game design feeling cinematic enough to be its own Bond movie. Trying to top First Light would be foolish.

According to recent reports, another elephant in the room is Casino Royale, the first movie in Daniel Craig's Bond era and a "de facto" secret origin in the vein of Batman Begins. In its opening sequence, the audience will see Bond earn his "00" status in a brutal fight, the origins of his playboy persona as he loses Vesper Lynd (Eva Green), the one person he truly cares for, and his full slide into the more polished secret agent fans know and love. Again, a bar has been set, and rather than trying to clear it, Amazon should focus on ensuring Robert Pattinson has a strong first outing as Bond.
