One fan posted a screenshot over on Twitter of the part of the trailer where the Fantastic Four visit Steve Rogers at his house and ask for his help. You may be thinking, 'Wait, isn't Doom the baddie?' Why would the Fantastic Four bring him to Steve Rogers' house to ask for help defeating himself? You're right, as far as we know, Doom is the villain of the story. But many fans think he may have pulled the wool over a few heroes' eyes and is masking as a friend in order to infiltrate and destroy them. Sources indicate that we know that Doom has been collecting Marvel heroes ahead of Doomsday. Thanks to the VisionQuest finale, we know that Doom has already resurrected Scarlett Witch, and we saw the baddie kidnap Hulk during the Endgame Encore added footage. So, could this be Doom trying to enlist the Fantastic Four and Steve Rogers?

As the villain states in the bulk of Doomsday trailers, he has a personal vendetta against the Avengers due to the multiversal damage they caused, ultimately resulting in the loss of his family. And who contributed to that damage? Steve, when he flip-flopped between timelines, returning the Infinity Stones, before finally going back to the past to be with Peggy.

Industry observers note that however, other fans think the person who has been edited out is actually Loki. This would make sense as we have already seen the God of Mischief visit Steve and Peggy at the end of Endgame Encore, setting up Doomsday. However, in the added footage, the duo are younger with a little baby, whereas in the trailer, the couple is found in a more modern setting with a grown child. Perhaps he has returned with the Fantastic Four to collect the second Steve – the one with long hair.
