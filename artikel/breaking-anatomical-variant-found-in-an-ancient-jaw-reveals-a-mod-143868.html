Breaking: Anatomical variant found in an ancient jaw reveals a modern-day mystery

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A jaw feature commonly seen by dentists but rarely reported in the human fossil record has been found in an ancient skull, revealing a modern-day medical mystery that dates back at least 2 million years. La Trobe University researchers have identified a pronounced mandibular torus—a harmless bony ridge that forms on the inside of the lower jaw—in a fossil of Australopithecus africanus from Sterkfontein in South Africa.
