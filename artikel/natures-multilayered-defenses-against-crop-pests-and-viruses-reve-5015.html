Nature's multilayered defenses against crop pests and viruses revealed

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, researchers from the University of Nottingham, in collaboration with Syngenta and Keele University, have discovered how a commercial barley hybrid uses three complementary natural defenses to protect itself against aphids and the damaging viruses they spread. The findings could help breeders develop crops with stronger natural protection against pests and diseases.
