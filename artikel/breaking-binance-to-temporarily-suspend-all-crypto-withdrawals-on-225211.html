In a recent development, in a latest announcement, Binance crypto exchange said it will perform wallet maintenance on November 25, 2026 at 06:00 (UTC). To support the wallet maintenance, Binance stated that withdrawals for all networks will be suspended beginning on November 25, 2026 at 06:00 (UTC). Recent findings suggest that the trading of tokens on Binance will not be impacted during the wallet maintenance. The exchange did not announce a corresponding suspension of deposits in the notice. Thus, the temporary suspension specifically concerns withdrawals across all networks. (Related: Scientists find “field” of washing machines in ocean du...)

In its notice, Binance remarked that withdrawal services will be resumed when the network is deemed to be stable and that no further announcement may be made.

Those looking to transfer funds should make their withdrawals before the wallet maintenance is set to begin to avoid delays and possible loss of funds.

Wallet maintenance refers to a standard operational process that supports blockchain updates, system improvements, and fund security.

This comes weeks after Binance suffered an historic hack with $6.3 billion in assets affected. Binance has since completed coverage of assets impacted in the security incident and published a new Proof of Reserves snapshot.

In a recent development, Binance stated it has engaged leading security firms Mandiant, SlowMist and Verichains for independent reviews of its security procedures.

These reviews, the exchange said, will validate its current security infrastructure, assess its security posture going forward, and help strengthen the protections expected from the crypto platform.

Binance also stated that it was coordinating closely with the U.S. Department of Justice, as well as with industry partners who have assisted in freezing assets impacted by the security incident in late October 2026. The collaboration is necessary throughout the asset forfeiture process, with the goal of supporting the return of affected funds as efficiently as possible.

Sources indicate that in separate news, Binance said it has integrated the Cboe Global Indices Feed into its platform, app and Binance AI, giving users access to real-time traditional market data. The initial rollout covers three of the world's most widely followed U.S. equity benchmarks, with more benchmark data to follow: S&P 500 Index, Dow Jones Industrial Average, Nasdaq-100.
