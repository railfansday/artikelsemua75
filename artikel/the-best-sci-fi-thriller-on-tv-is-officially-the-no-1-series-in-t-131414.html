For the past several months, the streamer has been adding shows and movies from different streaming services and studios, and it has seen some success with this acquired media. Less than a month ago, a cult-favorite horror series landed on Netflix for the first time, and it has seen major success in a short period. This horror-thriller comes from a smaller streaming service that remains relatively unknown to the public despite being owned by Amazon. Part of the reason is that it has undergone branding changes, but it is currently known as MGM+. The streaming service has been put on the map by its most popular show, From, which has built a cult following and has now racked up more viewership than anyone could have imagined.

Streaming data from Nielsen highlights that From has been the most-watched show, not just on Netflix but across all of streaming. In the week of November 2 to November 8, the series racked up 1.69 million views, making it the top scripted show for the week. Despite having 30 episodes on Netflix, it has beaten shows like Grey's Anatomy and The Massive Bang Theory, both of which have hundreds of episodes. The newly introduced figures push From past the 5 billion minutes watched mark on Netflix only.

It has been revealed that created by John Griffin, From is a horror thriller set in a nightmarish town that has trapped its residents and tortures them in every way imaginable. The series has been compared to Lost due to its unrivaled knack for creating mysteries that have kept viewers guessing for years. From a cold, chilling atmosphere to bloodcurdling monsters and arresting characters, From has mastered the art of eliciting all kinds of emotions while incentivizing viewers to click next.
