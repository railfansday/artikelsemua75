Ian Maatsen is looking to make more good memories with Aston Villa in Istanbul when they face Galatasaray in the Champions League.

Last season, Villa lifted the Europa League title at Besiktas Park, having beaten Freiburg 3-0 in the final, and they return to the Turkish capital for the first time since that triumph.

Villa are in a strong position in the Champions League table at the halfway stage, sitting sixth with three wins from four so far (L1), though that loss came last time out against Barcelona.

But Maatsen is hoping to draw from the positive memories of their last trip to Istanbul when they face Galatasaray for the first time in the club's history.

However, English sides have only won on one of their last 10 trips to face Galatasaray in major European competition (D3 L6), with Arsenal winning there in December 2014 (4-1 in the Champions League).

And though Villa were beaten by Barcelona last time out, they have not lost consecutive European matches since the semi-finals of the 2023-24 Conference League (both legs v Olympiacos).

Under Unai Emery, they have never lost consecutive group-stage matches in a European competition (26 played), and the Spaniard believes his team still have a good chance of causing an upset by finishing in the top eight.

"Each match is giving us a lot of information, and it's giving us how many points we have to continue focusing on this competition with different targets," Emery said.

"So, after we played four matches and we have nine points, we're still being with the possibility to be in the top eight.

"But we are not favourites, because with this different way in the Champions League, starting two years ago, the favourite teams usually they are learning.

It has been revealed that "Normally, if you are making some mistakes, you can lose [and still] be in the top eight and for the favourite teams, now you can analyse how it's going, the competition. They are trying to get points, and even they are trying to score a lot of goals.

"So, progressively, it's going to be more difficult to be [in] the top eight, because to surprise some favourite teams is going to be more difficult, but we have to do our work thinking each match.

"Of course, it is going to be very, very different [against Galatasaray], our target after the match, if we are winning, if we are losing, if we are drawing. But we are still being in the right way to try to dream to be in the top eight.".

Emery has only lost one of his last 18 Champions League games against sides from outside of Europe's big five leagues, winning 16 of those (D1).

That lone defeat was away to Club Brugge in November 2024 (1-0), with his Villa side winning each of the six since.
