Implementing sex into GTA 6 in a believable way was quite tricky to pull off, according to developer Rockstar Games.

This being both a Grand Theft Auto game and a love story, it's not a shocker that sex and intimacy (and the most nudity I've ever seen in a video game) are so prominent in GTA 6. Getting the balance just right was pretty tough, however.

"Exploring the physical relationship between Jason and Lucia required a lot of thought and iteration," Rockstar's vice president of animation, Mark Tennant, explains in quotes given to GamesRadar ahead of our GTA 6 review.

It has been revealed that "We needed to allow players to interact with the partner in a natural, loving way, with a believable progression based on their overall investment in the relationship," he adds. GTA 6's romance isn't a totally linear line. Jason and Lucia can be hotly passionate about each other or just be cordial partners in crime, depending on your choices, so how they interact physically needed to match their relationship status.
