Prolific game license revivers SNEG have released a newly introduced version of Soldiers at War, an ancient turn-based tactics game that will remind you strongly of the X-COM/UFO Series, except that it's set during World War 2. I don't believe they had Chryssalids back then. Pretty sure grandpa never mentioned any Chryssalids.

It has been revealed that originally developed by Random Games and published by Strategic Simulations, Inc, the game consists of 18 main scenarios spanning D-Day, Monte Cassino and Cherbourg. There's a mission editor, too, and some standalone extra skirmishes. In each scrap, you pick eight men to undertake such feats as raiding V2 sites and hijacking disabled tanks, drawing on a pretty dizzying range of individual soldier actions. Your squaddies level up with experience, and you can equip them with flamethrowers and bazookas. Among the key differences from X-COM is that there's no morale system - your men will never defy orders. Nor is there an overarching base/Geoscape layer. It has been revealed that "This edition updates Soldiers at War to run smoothly on modern Windows systems, delivering improved compatibility and performance," promises the GOG page (it's also on Steam). "The game now supports 1280x720 widescreen resolution, with rendering improvements for smoother drawing." There are cloud saves and Achievements, too, for today's soldier is not content with merely laying down their life for their country.
