Recent findings suggest that much like the Suzuki Jimny before it, the Toyota Land Cruiser FJ is a lovable little off-roader that combines cartoonishly tiny proportions with an actual body-on-frame design. Unfortunately for us American enthusiasts, the Land Cruiser FJ also follows the Jimny in not actually being available Stateside. That makes it all the more frustrating to learn that Japanese buyers can get their hands on an FJ with an aftermarket lift for under $32,000, as brought to broader attention by Motor1.

Recent findings suggest that that price comes from Kuhl Racing, a provider of body kits and other customization elements for a wide variety of cars. The company is imagining all kinds of ways to transform the Land Cruiser FJ, including lift kits, superchargers, and clearance-focused bumpers that should increase approach and departure angles.

It has been revealed that the most interesting offering is a complete package combining the lift kit with 18-inch wheels and all-terrain tires for just under 5 million yen, or about $31,500 USD. That comes out to more than $10,000 below the American starting price of the most basic possible Toyota 4Runner. The intricacies of the global auto market mean that prices cannot really be compared apples-to-apples across two different markets, but the lifted FJ would still likely come in somewhere below Toyota’s current range of mid-sized off-roaders in America.
