📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that it’s getting increasingly difficult to keep up with all the limited-edition tumblers, advent calendars, and seasonal releases that disappear off shelves before you’ve finished your morning coffee. And now IHOP is giving breakfast lovers another reason to make room in their kitchen cabinets.

Delish can exclusively reveal that IHOP is launching its first-ever Collector's Holiday Mug on Monday, November 30, with the mugs being sold at participating restaurants nationwide. It’s also the very first time the pancake chain has ventured into selling merchandise directly to customers—and, as it turns out, the idea has been brewing for quite some time. Sources indicate that according to Samantha Strong, IHOP’s executive director of brand calendar strategy, the manufacturer has heard from enough coffee-loving customers over the years to realize there was an actual appetite for owning an IHOP mug. “What started as a frequent question has grown into a genuine fan obsession,” she stated, and this holiday release is now the company's first attempt to turn that enthusiasm into something people can actually buy. It has been revealed that IHOP notes coffee is its second best-selling menu item, which means an awful lot of people have spent their mornings clutching one of those mugs while waiting for breakfast. But before you start planning an entire IHOP-themed kitchen makeover, know that each participating location will have just 24 (yes, only 24!) mugs available. Once they're gone, they’re gone, at least as far as this limited-time release is concerned.

There's certainly no shortage of things to collect this holiday season, but at least this one’s something you can actually put to use while doing some damage to a stack of pancakes.
