There was space in the starting XI for both Dominic Johnson-Fisher and Queens Park Rangers loanee Emmerson Sutton, with Sam Clucas on the bench on his first Woodside matchday.

One clearance forward was pounced upon by the former Truro City man, whose mishit effort still forced keeper Luke Hutchinson to push behind for a corner.

In a recent development, however, disaster struck midway through the first period as a rash defensive decision proved costly. Following Hutchinson’s clever distribution with a swiftly-taken throw, Joe Cook galloped across on a quest to intercept, only to be beaten to it by Ciaran McGuckin.

The Reds looked vulnerable. Sensing this frailty, Simon Weaver’s side probed and captain Lewis Cass’ effort was repelled by Harrison Foulkes into McGowan’s path, with the scorer connecting wildly on the rebound on the half-volley.

According to recent reports, just shy of the half-hour, the upright came to Worthing’s rescue. A heavy touch from Cook drew in the attention of Shawn McCoulsky and McGuckin latched on to the loose ball with his teammate brought down by the recovering defender. Opting to strike early, the side-footed attempt from Harrogate’s No35 caught out Foulkes before bouncing off the post – and Cook made amends on the rebound.

In a recent development, the Reds reclaimed some control and searched for parity. Razzaq Coleman De-Graft fashioned an opportunity to shoot with persistent footwork, but Hutchinson gathered the attempt prior to Sutton’s shot from close quarters being smothered.

Recent findings suggest that but perseverance paid off just before the interval as Young found a way through a retreating Harrogate defence. Digging out a cross into a congested area, neither Ricky Aguiar nor Johnson-Fisher could apply a touch, which deceived Hutchinson and allowed the ball to travel all the way through and into the back of the net – and that was Young slightly fortuitous fifth goal of the season.

That offered the hosts a blank slate heading into the second period and they built on that foundation in the opening exchanges with Sutton providing enthusiasm down the left flank. An inventive free-kick from Young scooped possession in Coleman De-Graft’s direction, but his subsequent volley was blazed over.

The encounter devolved into a tense and fragmented one but as the match opened up, Ellis Taylor’s drive was thwarted by Foulkes before the goalkeeper had to backtrack at breakneck speed to prevent Jayden Moore’s backpass from going in.

With the clock ticking towards 90, the Reds were in the ascendancy as they seemed more likely to find a winner. And found it ewas in minute 87 after a flowing team move.

Johnson-Fisher’s slide-rule pass slipped in Adjetey-Brew, who did superbly on the stretch to fire into the middle. Falling into Young’s path, the man of the moment coolly slotted into the bottom corner to send Woodside into raptures.

The closing stages were met with maturity and composure from Adam Hinshelwood’s team as they sealed their first league win of the month over a play-off rival.
