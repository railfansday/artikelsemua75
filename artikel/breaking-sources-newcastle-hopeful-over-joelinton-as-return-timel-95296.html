Newcastle insiders believe that Joelinton will be back fit in the coming weeks after he suffered another injury blow, sources have told Football Insider.

Joelinton had just been returning to the Magpies side before he suffered another setback before the international break.

He was linked away from the club in the summer before picking up a thigh injury on the eve of the season that ruled out a move. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

The 30-year-old missed the first six Premier League games of the campaign before making a return last month, but is now facing more time on the sidelines.

Matthias Jaissle has stayed coy about his current problem, but as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, he is likely to be back fit by at least mid-December.

Joelinton has impressed in his few appearances this season and Jaissle is keen to build his midfield around the Brazilian when he is fully fit.

Joelinton impressed last season, with six goals and two assists in 42 appearances, but has only been limited to five outings so far this term.

According to recent reports, jaissle updated fans on his status ahead of the Magpies' clash against Arsenal, saying: "I don't want to be too precise with the medical patch. We will get feedback from the specialists and we will find out how long Joe will be out. We will get an update, and hope he is back soon.".

Beyond his comments, those inside the club are confident that the 30-year-old will be back fit and ready to play before long.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "They are hopeful that this latest injury isn’t too serious and he could be back within a couple of weeks.

"He plays an crucial role at the club on and off the pitch, with his leadership qualities as well, so his absence is a blow for Newcastle, and obviously they’ll be hoping that he can return to fitness as soon as possible and help Newcastle get back to a decent run of form.

"Obviously he's been a big miss in that middle of the park, and his experience, having seen so many other experienced players leave in the summer, has been hugely missed as well.".

Joelinton's strong showing away at Crystal Palace last month coincided with Newcastle cruising to victory, as he made 10 defensive contributions, completed 100 per cent of his dribbles (2/2) and won the most aerial duels (7) of any player.

He is among the best Premier League midfielders so far this season for duels won per 90 (8.36, 96th percentile), aerial duels won per 90 (3.86, 100th percentile) and successful dribbles per 90 (1.93, 92nd percentile).

The Brazilian remains key to Jaissle's plans and the club are aiming to have him back fit as soon as possible.
