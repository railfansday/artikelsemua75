📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

"Electric cars lose value like a rock" is one of those things everyone repeats. I wanted to check it on real numbers, so I took two of the most common used cars in Germany, the Tesla Model 3 on the electric side and the VW Golf on the petrol side, and compared their asking prices by age.

It has been revealed that disclosure: I built the scraper used below (AutoScout24 Car Listings Scraper on Apify). The analysis works on any list of car listings with a price and a registration date.

Two searches on autoscout24.de (23 Nov 2026): tesla model 3 returned 343 Model 3 listings (the whole search, it stopped before my limit of 400), and volkswagen golf gave the first 400 Golf-family listings, of which 302 are the plain Golf (the rest are Variant, Plus and so on). And the field I did not expect to care about: AutoScout24 rates 48% of the Golf listings as top-price (its best rating), against 16% of the Model 3 listings. Golf sellers in this sample were all dealers pricing aggressively; more than a quarter of the Teslas (97 of 343) were private sellers, who tend to ask more.

These are asking prices on one day, not sale prices. The Golf sample is the first 400 results of the site's standard sort, not every Golf in Germany. Model 3 versions (Standard, Long Range, Performance) are mixed together; split by powerHp if you want to compare like with like.

Run the same input on a schedule and keep the rows: the median per year becomes a monthly depreciation curve, and priceRating plus mileageKm give you a shortlist of the cheap, low-mileage cars as they appear. The scraper also reads AutoScout24 Austria, Italy, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg ("country": "IT" and so on).

Both searches together (743 listings) ran in 15 seconds and cost about $0.34 on the free proposal ($0.45 per 1,000 listings). Apify's free $5 monthly credit covers roughly 11,000 listings, so a weekly run of these two searches costs nothing.

If you have the numbers for another pair (Model Y vs Tiguan, Zoe vs Clio), I would like to see them.
