Industry observers note that hyperliquid [HYPE] fell by 7.39% over the last 24 hours, a significant drop compared to the rest of the crypto market. The timing here is interesting since the slump followed Abraxas Capital reportedly exiting its Hyperliquid positions, selling 219,081 HYPE tokens estimated to be worth $13.25 million.

The wider market’s weakness has added to the uncertainty and pressure, with Bitcoin ETF outflows and extreme fear weighing on sentiment and keeping HYPE’s near-term outlook bearish.

HYPE was trading at $57.85 on the daily chart, within the 0%-23.6% Fibonacci zone. The decline resulted in the token being pushed below a price level it had managed to defend since mid-August, marking a potential shift in its overall price trend.

Recent findings suggest that furthermore, the RSI fell to 28.43, putting HYPE firmly in oversold territory as momentum remained bearish and sellers retained their control of the market. Also, the falling OBV seemed to suggest that buying volume has been unable to overcome the sustained selling pressure.

In a recent development, the 4-hour chart hinted at a similar story, with HYPE also trading within the 0%-23.6% Fibonacci zone and below the $59.25-level. Failure to reclaim this threshold has been keeping the immediate price structure bearish, leaving the $55.85 swing low completely exposed. The 4-hour chart’s weakness seemed to be reinforcing the bearish signals on the daily chart. This leaves HYPE vulnerable to another leg lower.
