In the morning glow, a pretty woman walks down the sidewalk with a carton of a dozen eggs. A faded black-and-white Marilyn Monroe poster greets customers at the Organic Deli. A photo in the paper demonstrates the presidents of the United States, China, and Russia against a giant painting of the Great Wall. A Madonna song whispers, “Express yourself, express yourself.”. I can’t really tell. In Chelsea, I follow a group of people into an art gallery just to see what they’re into. It turns out to be a massive mistake.
