According to recent reports, one last month of 2026 brings two more waves of Xbox Game Pass titles to close out the year. Subscribers can once again look forward to some newly introduced arrivals in the Xbox Game Pass library, ranging from gruesome co-op action to wholesome adventures featuring helpful mice on a mission. These announcements typically come in two waves, with a sneak peek split between them, so while this list isn’t complete yet, we do know what you can expect in the first half of December. As usual, we’ll enhancement this page once Xbox reveals the full lineup for this first batch. For now, you can look forward to an eclectic mix of games, starting with The Outlast Trials. Released in 2024, it’s a co-op survival-horror game that throws a team of up to four players into a gauntlet of deadly trials, where evasion is key to victory. Set in a magical forest inspired by Scandinavian lands, the game follows a group of mice on a journey to help people, at the behest of an old witch who can’t make the trip herself. This is also a day-one release for Xbox Game Pass, and it’ll be joined by World of Tanks Blitz on the same day. On December 3, Freestyle Football 2 debuts, bringing fast-paced, 5v5 multiplayer street soccer to Xbox and PC, and on December 14, you can grab Nubby’s Number Factory, a roguelite game all about making major numbers.
