Earlier this week, in response to the Waymo vehicles driving into active construction zones, California required that Waymo stop operating its robotaxis on highways for the time being. In addition to the above issues, Waymo robotaxis have not operated appropriately at times when manual traffic control has replaced traffic lights. Waymo issued an update recently following a crash that occurred as a result of this problem. “The ‌manual ⁠traffic recall is to ensure self-driving vehicles better responds to manual commands at intersections from law enforcement or other emergency personnel and follows a collision in which a Waymo followed directions from an officer, but the officer was then temporarily obstructed. That update was completed on November 6.”.

So, yes, Waymo robotaxis still aren’t perfect. However, Waymo continues to point out that its vehicles are much safer statistically than human-driven vehicles, as these vehicles are traveling far more miles between accidents than average human American drivers.
