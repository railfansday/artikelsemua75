While retailers have been developing agents that can shop on behalf of customers, Target’s fresh shopping assistant is not that—yet.

It has been revealed that in October, Target rolled out a beta version of its AI assistant, Ask Target, within its website and mobile app. The assistant finds items based on shopper preferences, shares product facts, compares items, summarizes reviews, and checks inventory availability.
