John Wick Franchise Officially Returning In New Prequel Set Years Before The Impossible Task

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

John Wick: Chapter 4 concluded with the death of the titular character, thus appearing to put an end to its epic saga. However, as phenomenal an ending as it was, there's clearly a lot more juice left in the proverbial engine, as future John Wick installments have been greenlit and confirmed to be in production. Keanu Reeves is also set to play John Wick three more times across different entries, so even the iconic actor is still very much attached to the series.

Among all of the upcoming John Wick projects is a brand-upcoming prequel release that is set to recount the events leading up to both the Impossible Task and the first film. This is a completely different release from the upcoming John Wick prequel movie, which was recently confirmed to still be in production with Keanu Reeves. Rather, this John Wick prequel will allow fans to get right into the action themselves and experience John Wick's penchant for gun fu up close and personal. The Untitled John Wick Game is an upcoming video game that is set to tell the story of John Wick prior to the Impossible Task. Developed by Saber Interactive (the same folks behind the incredible Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2) in collaboration with Lionsgate and John Wick director Chad Stahelski, the Untitled John Wick Game is going to be the most ambitious adaptation of the series yet, following in the footsteps of the far humbler, yet nevertheless excellent indie, John Wick: Hex.

Untitled John Wick Game aims to faithfully recreate the experience of watching the film, albeit in video game form, offering a robust gun-fu combat system that is paired with stylized camera angles, explosive driving set pieces, and cinematic storytelling. While the reveal trailer tells us little of the full experience, it does give fans a firm idea of the tone and type of gameplay style Saber Interactive is aiming for, as well as the familiar, and not so familiar, faces we can expect to see pop up during the story. It has been revealed that on the game's official website, Saber Interactive describes it as the "ultimate John Wick gaming experience that fans have been waiting for." It isn't hard to see how that could be true, especially with the stunning, near-photorealistic visuals (that still maintain the neon-soaked style of the original films) and intense combat animations. It's likely that the Untitled John Wick Game will be akin to the recent James Bond game, 007: First Light, or perhaps even Sifu, as a linear, action-focused title interspersed with cinematic cutscenes or set pieces.

So far, we know very little about the Untitled John Wick Game, something that should be evident from the lack of a confirmed title. It was confirmed back in February 2026, so it has been quite a while since Saber Interactive showed us anything. This could mean that more will be shown in the coming months, perhaps even during the Game Awards, although that is pure speculation. Regardless, it is exciting that fans are finally getting a big-budget John Wick video game, and one from a studio with a proven track record of working with existing IP.
