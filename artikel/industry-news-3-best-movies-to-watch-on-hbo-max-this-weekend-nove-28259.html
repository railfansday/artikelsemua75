According to recent reports, the best movies on HBO Max this weekend include huge genre fare, and smaller projects that will ensure every type of viewer will have a good time on the streaming service. On last weekend's HBO Max movie recommendations, I explored titles like The First Purge, Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes, and Sydney Sweeney's Madame Web, all franchise movies.

Now, in terms of newly introduced releases, I went with only one major action movie, and it still comes with a fresh perspective, as this is a dinosaur flick that can compete with the long-running Jurassic Park franchise, which is easily one of the most iconic movie franchises of all time. In addition to said release, I felt that a newly introduced biopic about a celebrity chef and a black comedy with a star-studded cast would prove enticing to viewers. These are the best movies to watch on HBO Max this weekend. (Related: Smart People Can Still Be Bad At Maths — These 3 Skills...)

In a recent development, anthony Bourdain was one of the most popular celebrity chefs for years, and after his tragic passing, A24's Tony comes as a gripping coming-of-age drama chronicling how a 19-year-old Bourdain took his first steps towards the career that would make him a household name. Tony pulls a lot from Bourdain's memoir, Kitchen Confidential.

One of the drama's biggest strengths relies on the mix of veterans and newcomers in its cast. Antonio Banderas grounds Tony as José Vatel, bringing gravitas and energy. The film's young cast includes Dominic Sessa as Anthony Bourdain, delivering one of the year's best and most exciting performances, fleshing out the man behind the legend without ever sounding like a caricature or an impression of the real man. Rising stars Leo Woodall and Emilia Jones add flair to the narrative.

Just like Tony arrived on HBO Max this Friday, November 20, so did the black comedy The Roses, which packs an all-star cast. Some of the best names in the comedy genre, such as Andy Samberg, Allison Janney, Kate McKinnon, and more help a star duo tell the story of a seemingly perfect marriage that is actually crumbling into pieces. Playing the unhappy couple are Benedict Cumberbatch and Olivia Colman.

It has been revealed that the Roses is an adaptation of the 1981 novel The War of the Roses, following Cumberbatch's Theo Rose and Colman's Ivy Rose as they are unable to keep up the perfect marriage when his career hits a slump and hers takes off. Theo resents his wife's success with her new restaurant, leading to a war as they try to settle on divorce terms. The Roses is a sharp comedy written by Tony McNamara, whose hit series, The Great, makes him perfect to tackle such a text with a bite. This is a fun one for the weekend.

It has been revealed that finally, The End of Oak Street is the current most popular movie on HBO Max in the United States, and it is easy to see why. While it was not a box office hit, grossing $121.6 million against a reported budget of $85 million, I maintain that it deserved so much more. The End of Oak Street feels tonally similar to Steven Spielberg's early Jurassic Park movies, with the same kind of tension and horror elements, such as using shadows to build up suspense for major dinosaur reveals.

It also packs a lot of heart and style, doing so through the fresh lens of a time travel story, as an entire neighborhood gets transported right into the middle of a wild jungle at the time dinosaurs ruled the Earth. Led by charismatic performances from Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor, whose characters, the Platts, are fighting not only to stay alive but also keep their family together amid relationship tensions, The End of Oak Street is one of the best dinosaur movies ever, making it a must-watch on HBO Max.
