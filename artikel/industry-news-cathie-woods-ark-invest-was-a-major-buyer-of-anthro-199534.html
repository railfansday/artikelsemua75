Cathie Wood was a major buyer of Anthropic following its debut Thursday on the Nasdaq, adding shares in all her major funds.

Wood's firm, which already owned private shares of the artificial intelligence tech giant in its Ark Venture Fund leading up to the initial public offering, bought $90.8 million worth of Anthropic in six funds, according to a trade notification from the firm.

In a recent development, "We've owned Anthropic since early '23," Wood told CNBC's "Squawk Box" on Friday. "It's our best performing equity by far in ARKVX, our venture fund. ... Our confidence has grown with time as it really took over the enterprise" AI business. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

Anthropic shares jumped 17% in their first day, but closed well off the highs of the session and were headed for sizable losses Friday. The Nasdaq, which was expected to get a boost from a major IPO reviving enthusiasm about the technology trade, fell by 2% on Thursday.

"We're climbing a wall of worry," noted Wood. "Everybody is worried about AI hype and the political situation after the election, but I think we'll look back and say, OK this is more like the '80s and '90s, climbing a wall of worry you know with bricks much like what we have today: inflation, interest rates, wars, new technologies and so forth so quite optimistic here.".

Sources indicate that the Anthropic stakes are relatively small across the funds, but Wood typically will buy shares in companies she likes steadily during long stretches. Wood noted she is "averaging down" with her purchases of Anthropic. (Related: Granite 4.0 Nano: Just how small can you go?...)

It has been revealed that in her $6.1 billion Ark Innovation ETF, Anthropic is a 0.3% stake. The biggest holdings in that fund are Tesla and SpaceX, representing more than 21% of the fund together.

Ark Innovation, which retail investors piled into during the pandemic as it gained about 150% in 2020, tumbled the subsequent next two years, losing 67% in 2022 alone. Wood's following was dented after the substantial losses.

The fund had a comeback stretch as the bull market revved back up from 2023 to 2025, but has fallen on hard times again this year, down more than 10% in 2026. The S&P 500 is up 4.8% this year.

Wood dismissed comparisons of today's environment to the dot-com bubble.

According to recent reports, "Back then if an Anthropic had gone public, it probably would have gone up threefold the first day," she mentioned. "That's how crazy things were.".
