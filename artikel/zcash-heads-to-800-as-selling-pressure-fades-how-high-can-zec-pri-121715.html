The Zcash price has rebounded from $760 toward $800, but reclaiming the $900 resistance zone remains crucial to strengthening its short-term recovery.

The $710 to $720 trendline support remains important, while a sustained breakout above $930 could open the way toward $1,042.

It has been revealed that the Zcash (ZEC) price is attempting to bounce back after a sharp sell-off pushed the price toward $760. Buyers have since stepped in, lifting ZEC back toward $800, but the recovery is still in its early stages. At the time of writing, ZEC is trading near $796, up 2.12% on the day.

The rebound comes after a steep decline from recent highs, with traders now watching whether the buying interest is strong enough to push the price higher. Recent derivatives data also points to short liquidations, although that alone does not confirm a trend reversal.

With ZEC approaching key levels on the daily chart, the question is whether this rebound can gather momentum before the month ends or whether sellers could regain control.

ZEC’s latest daily candle suggests buyers are attempting to regain ground. The price dipped to $760.82 before climbing to an intraday high of $809.51, bringing it back close to the $800 mark. However, the rebound hasn’t yet changed the broader chart structure, which remains under pressure following the decline from the $1,600 to $1,700 region. The $1,042 level, which previously acted as an key price area, has now become a resistance level to watch. Below it, ZEC is also facing resistance around the highlighted $890 to $930 zone.Â Â.

On the downside, the ascending trendline drawn from earlier lows remains relevant, at the moment lining up around $710 to $720. ZEC has bounced before reaching this trendline, but it could come back into focus if the current recovery loses momentum.

The derivatives indicators offer a mixed picture. Open Interest stands at around 164K, showing some recovery from recent lows, although it remains below several earlier peaks on the chart. Funding is slightly positive at 0.0025%, suggesting a modest preference for long positioning. Neither signal is strong enough on its own to establish that the rebound is sustainable.

Key ZEC price levels to monitor.

The Zcash price is showing some recovery after dropping toward $760, but it still has several resistance levels to clear before the chart starts looking stronger. The $890 to $930 zone is the first major hurdle, while $1,042 remains a more significant level on the upside.

For now, the $710 to $720 trendline area remains an notable support reference if the rebound fades. ZEC may be recovering, but whether it can turn this bounce into a sustained move higher will depend on how buyers respond at the resistance levels ahead.
