Among the most significant updates are the installation of water dispensers and the newly introduced restaurant, Best Pizza@Sea.

AIDA remarked in a press release that during her drydock stay, 53 water dispensers were installed in the cabin corridors. A reusable glass carafe is provided in every cabin and can be refilled as needed during the voyage. (Related: Tech Report: One Piece Chapter 1200: Release date & maj...)

Next in line for company drydocks are the AIDAmar, starting December 20, 2026, and the AIDAblu in the spring of 2027.

For the AIDAcosma, additional work included The Yacht Club Restaurant, which has also been given a visual refresh: the area now features a modern carpet design and newly designed seating.

AIDA stated that, as part of the maintenance, guest areas have been renovated, and furniture in the cabins and public areas has been reupholstered. Mattresses, flooring and tiles have also been upgraded.

Technical systems were modernized and included work on the electric Azipod propulsion system, the bow and side thrusters, as well as the stabilizers.

The AIDAcosma also received a new biocide-free underwater coating. The coating reduces fouling and drag, lowering fuel consumption.

The onboard IT infrastructure was also upgraded, resulting in the rebuilding of IT data centers and the updating of the network architecture. Restaurants, bars and shops were also equipped with new point-of-sale systems.

After its drydock, the AIDAcosma will first set sail on the “Pearls of the Mediterranean” cruise.

The “Andalusia & Canary Islands” itinerary will then reposition the ship to the Canaries for the winter.
