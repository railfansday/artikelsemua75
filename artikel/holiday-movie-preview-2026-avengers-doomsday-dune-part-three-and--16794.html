Industry observers note that the holiday season is officially here, and between festive parties and last-minute shopping, there’s plenty of room for one more tradition — a trip to the movie theater! Hollywood is closing out 2026 with a lineup more stuffed than a Thanksgiving turkey, including superhero showdowns and sci-fi adventures to inspiring true stories and comedies that’ll have audiences laughing their way into the fresh year.

Topping moviegoers’ holiday wish lists is Robert Downey Jr.’s highly anticipated return to the Marvel Cinematic Universe in Avengers: Doomsday — but don’t expect to find Iron Man waiting under the tree. Meanwhile, Timothée Chalamet and Zendaya swap cozy Christmas sweaters for the scorching sands of Arrakis in Dune: Part Three, while Dwayne Johnson, Kevin Hart, Karen Gillan, and Jack Black take on the jungle in Jumanji: Open World. It has been revealed that for those seeking a little motivation heading into 2027, true stories of unlikely heroes who beat the odds are hitting the significant screen. David Corenswet slips out of his Superman cape and into shoulder pads to play the New York Giants legend, John Tuggle, while Anthony Ippolito steps into the ring as a young Sylvester Stallone in I Play Rocky. Taking center stage for true story of actor Ian Charleson is Andrew Scott, for a testament to perseverance in the film Elsinore. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

As for anyone looking to leave their own drama-filled family gatherings, there’s no shortage of other people’s problems to get caught up in this season. Natalie Portman plays an ambitious gallery owner whose attempts to get ahead come with some rather messy consequences in The Gallerist, as Julianne Moore and Paul Giamatti bring their own dose of backstage theatrics in the musical comedy, The Debut. Pedro Pascal has a few complications of his own after years away from home in Behemoth!, and Andrew Garfield plays OpenAI CEO Sam Altman in the tech-world film, Artificial.

Keep scrolling to see which movies are worth adding to your holiday watchlist:.

Jumanji: Open World The third film in the latest Jumanji installment (Dec. 25) brings back franchise regulars Kevin Hart, Karen Gillan, Dwayne Johnson and Jack Black. This time, the video game breaks free of its console and unleashes jungle chaos into the real world. That’s fine, right?

Mr. Irrelevant This feel-good sports drama (December 25) starring David Corenswet follows the true story of John Tuggle, the last player selected in the 1983 NFL draft (a.k.a. Mr. Irrelevant). Michael Shannon plays legendary Newly introduced York Giants head coach Bill Parcells.

Sources indicate that avengers: Doomsday Robert Downey Jr. is back in the MCU (December 18), but not as Tony Stark. This time he’s baddie Dr.Doom, and multiple Marvel faves — including Chris Evans and Florence Pugh — must team up to stop him.

Elsinore Andrew Scott is undoubtedly awards-season-bound in this moving true story (November 20). He plays actor Ian Charleson, who, in the early days of the AIDS epidemic, was diagnosed while playing Hamlet at London’s Royal National Theatre. Olivia Colman and Luke Thompson costar.

Behemoth! Pedro Pascal is a session musician going through a midlife crisis when he returns home after 20 years on the road. Olivia Wilde and Will Arnett also star in this Tony Gilroy drama (December 4).

According to recent reports, dune: Part Three The sci-fi trilogy concludes with this epic (December 18), which begins two decades after the events of 2024’s Dune: Part Two. Timothée Chalamet, Zendaya, Florence Pugh, Jason Momoa, Javier Bardem and Anya Taylor-Joy are all back.

The Gallerist Natalie Portman is a gallery boss eager to prove herself —then one lie spins things out of control. Jenna Ortega and Sterling K. Brown are along for the playful ride (December 4), which is part satire, part thriller.

The Debut Is there any drama more high-stakes than that of community theater? Julianne Moore stars as a first-time stage actress, with Paul Giamatti as her demanding director in this musical comedy (December 11) written and helmed by Jesse Eisenberg.

Recent findings suggest that I Play Rocky This Peter Farrelly–directed film (November 6) tells the behind-the-scenes tale of 1977 Best Picture winner Rocky and its star and screenwriter, Sylvester Stallone. Anthony Ippolito is a young Sly, and Matt Dillon is his dad, Frank.

According to recent reports, artificial Andrew Garfield is likely headed toward his third Oscar nom, for his portrayal of OpenAI CEO Sam Altman in this Luca Guadagnino–directed dramatization (December 25). A tech satire and cautionary tale, the sharp film also stars Monica Barbaro and Mark Rylance, and it may be the scariest thing you’ll see all year — but in a fun way.
