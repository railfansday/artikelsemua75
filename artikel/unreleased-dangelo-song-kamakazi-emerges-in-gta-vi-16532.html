Over a year after D’Angelo’s death, the singer’s legacy lives on in Vice City thanks to an unreleased song that has appeared within the world of Grand Theft Auto 6.

GTA VI players uncovered the track — titled “Kamakazi,” as confirmed by D’Angelo collaborator DJ Dojah — on Worldwide FM, one of the many radio stations featured in the game; D’Angelo’s “Lady” also plays on the in-game Honey FM channel. D’Angelo’s music being posthumously immortalized in the most anticipated video game in a decade is a fitting tribute to the singer, given his previous collaborations with Rockstar Games, the firm behind GTA VI. Back in 2018, the usually reclusive D’Angelo contributed a fresh song titled “Unshaken” to Rockstar’s Red Dead Redemption 2. (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

According to recent reports, “[D’Angelo] would come in here [Rockstar’s Upcoming York city office] and just play the game. We weren’t even talking about doing music,” Ivan Pavlovich, Director of Music and Audio at Rockstar Games, told Rolling Stone at the time. “When D’Angelo comes through, he shows up at midnight, and he’s playing the game until four in the morning. Each time he was just like, ‘it’s incredible.’ It just blows his mind. He’s such a fan. It’s unclear whether “Kamakazi” was written and recorded specifically for GTA VI or if it was among the batch of unreleased music D’Angelo left behind following his death from pancreatic cancer in October 2025. Videos of “Kamakazi” uploaded onto YouTube have been hit with takedown notices from Sony Music Entertainment, which had D’Angelo signed to their RCA label.

In a recent development, in the year since his death, D’Angelo’s estate and collaborators have talked about the impending arrival of more unreleased music from the singer, including an entire LP’s worth of unheard music, a covers album, and a collection of B-sides. However, “Kamakazi” is the first taste of what remains in D’Angelo’s vaults.
