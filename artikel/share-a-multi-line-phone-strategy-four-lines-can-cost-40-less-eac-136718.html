Most phone aims charge less for each line as more lines join one account, so relatives or friends paying for separate plans can each pay less by sharing one. Check the proposal’s price at your exact number of lines, and share only with people you trust, since the account owner owes the whole bill.

AT&T’s Value 2.0 unlimited plan costs $50 a month for one line but $30 a line for four, with AutoPay from a bank account and paperless billing.

Two lines cost $45 each and three cost $35 each, all before taxes and fees. So four relatives each paying $50 for a line of their own on that plan spend $200 a month, while the same four on one account pay $120. Together they save $960 a year.
