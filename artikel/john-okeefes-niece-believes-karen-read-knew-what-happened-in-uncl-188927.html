Nearly five years after Boston police officer John O’Keefe died, his teenage niece revealed how she believes his then-girlfriend, Karen Read, was involved in his death.

While appearing in Netflix’s November docuseries The Trials of Karen Read, O’Keefe’s niece Kayley Furbush explained that she and her younger brother spent several years living with O’Keefe, 46, after their parents died within months of each other.

It has been revealed that “I think Karen knew what had happened, that maybe she had done something to JJ and she didn’t want [it] to almost, like, be a reality,” Furbush said of her uncle’s death. “Or after that, not wanting to believe what she, like, knows is true in her head.”.

After O’Keefe died in January 2022, Read, 46, was arrested for his murder when she was accused of hitting him with her car.

During the high-profile trials, prosecutors argued that Read backed her SUV into O’Keefe and left him in the snow while she was under the influence of alcohol. Meanwhile, Read’s defense claimed he was killed inside of his friend’s house and that she was framed for his murder.

Read’s first trial ended in a mistrial in 2024. She returned to court in 2025 and was eventually acquitted of second-degree murder, manslaughter while operating under the influence and leaving the scene of a collision resulting in death in June of that year.

Despite the major legal victories, Read was found guilty of operating a motor vehicle under the influence of liquor.

Furbush also reflected on Read’s celebrity-like status in the docuseries, admitting that she found her behavior upsetting.

“The fact that Karen’s trying to be in the public eye and take pictures with people and, like, smile walking into a murder trial, I’m just disgusted or, like, disturbed by her,” Furbush said while looking back on the trials. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Furbush said Read’s behavior in the spotlight was upsetting because she “treated me like a friend” during her relationship with O’Keefe.

It has been revealed that “She’s funny. She was always laughing or making us laugh,” Furbush mentioned of her past relationship with Read. “She meshed really well with me and my brother.”.

Despite the media interest in the case, Furbush mentioned she believes people have forgotten about her uncle due to the interest in Read.

“I think people have forgotten about JJ, but I still have hope that something will come from this. For people to understand this wasn’t just some crazy story,” she said. “This was about actual human beings with real emotions. Their jobs, community, close friends — ripped apart because of lies that have been spread.”.
