Star Clippers has revealed a Black Friday and Travel Tuesday promotion featuring prepaid gratuities and air credits on 48 select sailings departing December 2026 through December 2027.

The offer includes voyages aboard the Royal Clipper, Star Clipper and Star Flyer.

The offer rewards the first two guests booked in cabin categories 2-6 with prepaid gratuities and an air credit (or cruise fare discount) ranging from $300 to $500, depending on the sailing length. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

Bookings for new reservations must be made by December 10, 2026, and are available only to North American residents, the company said in a press release. (Related: Tech Report: One Piece Chapter 1200: Release date & maj...)

Industry observers note that to receive the promotion, guests or travel advisors need to use booking code “BLACK” when making a new reservation on an eligible sailing. The air credit can be applied toward airfare purchased through Star Clippers or used as a discount against the cruise fare, the tech giant added.

Among the featured departures is a five-night “Corsica & Italy” sailing aboard Star Clipper, departing May 31, 2027, with fares starting from $1,790 per person, double.

Also featured is a seven-night Antigua roundtrip aboard the Star Clipper, departing March 13, 2027, with fares starting from $2,010.

The Black Friday and Travel Tuesday promotion is live and available on select sailings only; it applies to upcoming bookings and cannot be combined with another offer or discount.

According to the press release, all reservations must be made by December 10, 2026. Fares, availability and full terms and conditions should be confirmed at the time of booking.
