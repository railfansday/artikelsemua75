ALMA resolves the gravitational reach of a black hole in the early universe

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that astronomers using the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA) measured the mass of a supermassive black hole in WISE J224607.56−052634.9, also known as W2246-0526. Its light has been stretched by the expansion of the universe to about 5.6 times its original wavelength. This shift, called redshift, tells astronomers that we see the galaxy as it was when the universe was about 1.3 billion years old.
