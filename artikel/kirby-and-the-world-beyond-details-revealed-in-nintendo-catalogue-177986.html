It has been revealed that new details regarding Kirby and the World Beyond have been revealed via Nintendo's winter catalogue in Japan (via Genki_JPN on X). While hardly comprehensive, it provides a little more insight into the game's story along with the abilities and environments available upon its release.

We also get a touch more context behind King Dedede's role, with the article stating that "a mysterious girl" has been kidnapped by the antagonist.
