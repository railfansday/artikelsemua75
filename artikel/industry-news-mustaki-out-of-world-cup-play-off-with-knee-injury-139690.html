Chloe Mustaki is a key figure for Carla Ward's Republic of Ireland side.

Republic of Ireland defender Chloe Mustaki has been forced to withdraw from her side's 2027 Women's World Cup play-off final against Poland next month after rupturing her anterior cruciate ligament (ACL).

The 31-year-old sustained the injury in action for Nottingham Forest against Liverpool in the League Cup last week.

Her club have confirmed the centre-back will miss the rest of the season and that their medical team will "determine the best course of action for her rehabilitation and recovery". (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Republic of Ireland manager Carla Ward remarked she was "absolutely devastated" for the player who will be a huge loss in the side's efforts to make it the tournament in Brazil next summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

She added: "It is a huge blow for the entire squad because of how important she is to this group, both on the pitch with her consistency and leadership, and off the pitch with the incredible character and positivity she brings to the dressing room.

"This is a really difficult setback, but Chloe is loved by us all. Everyone in the squad, the staff and the association is fully behind her, and we will give her all the love and support she needs to make a full recovery.".

Ward will not call up a replacement to her squad with the 24-player panel set to gather in Dublin on Tuesday.

They will play the first leg of the play-off in Gdansk on 1 December with the return fixture at the Aviva Stadium four days later. Both games kick off at 17:00 GMT.
