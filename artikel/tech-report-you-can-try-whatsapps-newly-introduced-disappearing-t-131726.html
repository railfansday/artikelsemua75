📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that like Snapchat messages, WhatsApp's "View Once" feature is a great way to send things you don't want living on your friends' devices. You can send voice messages, photos, and videos that will "self-destruct" after they're opened, limiting (if not eliminating) the risk of your friends keeping them forever. However, View Once has a major limitation: While WhatsApp will let you send voice memos and images that way, you can't use it to send texts, meaning every one you send will live on (unless you unsend it yourself).

It has been revealed that that's now changing. As reported by WABetaInfo, WhatsApp is now beta testing View Once for text messages, too. Soon enough, you'll be able to send voice memos, photos, videos, and texts as one-time-only messages. That way, you can send sensitive information via text—say, a password or an important account number—without worrying about it persisting. Here's how it works: You craft your text message as you usually do, but rather than tap the send button, you hold it. You'll see the usual "Schedule message" option, but once the feature rolls out, you'll also find a new "View Once" option as well.

As WABetaInfo highlights, the first time you use this feature, WhatsApp will present a pop-up explaining the feature, how "View Once text gives you more privacy," and that when you send the message, it'll disappear from the chat after the recipient opens it. WhatsApp also explains that recipients cannot share, forward, save, or take screenshots of View Once text messages. Recipients won't see the text appear in the usual chat interface, either; instead, your recipient will need to open the message in a separate window. Once they close this page, the message disappears.

As mentioned above, this feature is currently in testing, so it's only available to WhatsApp beta testers. What's more, only the Android version of the beta has access at the moment, so iOS beta testers are out of luck. (That mentioned, the WhatsApp iOS beta is perpetually full, so there's not much you could do even if WhatsApp made this test available on iPhone.). According to WABetaInfo, the feature is live as of WhatsApp version 2.26.45.6. To enroll, open the WhatsApp beta page on the Google Play store and enroll your account in the beta.
