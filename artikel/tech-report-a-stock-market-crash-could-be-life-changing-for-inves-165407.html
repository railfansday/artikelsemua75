Market volatility has ticked higher, with artificial intelligence (AI) stocks getting particularly hard hit of late. For example, Aehr Test Systems (AEHR +21.76%) and MongoDB (MDB +9.37%) are down 60% and 30%, respectively, over the past three months, as of this writing. Cisco Systems (CSCO +6.80%), a tech industry giant, is down about 18%. What's going on? There are two things happening here, and both could leave investors with long-term scars. Here's why these three AI stocks could be life-changing investments, but perhaps not in a good way. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Artificial intelligence as a technology is very likely to change the world. However, that doesn't mean every AI stock will be a long-term winner. You only have to look back at past technological revolutions to see the trend. Companies pile into a newly introduced technology, jockeying for a leadership position. Eventually, a small number of industry leaders emerge, and the other companies fall to the wayside. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

Image source: OpenAI (AI-generated illustration).

Sources indicate that along the way, there are usually substantial swings on Wall Street. Every stock associated with the fresh technology gets investor attention early on, with many rising to shockingly high valuations. When it is clear that companies can't live up to investors' expectations, the entire group usually falls in unison. If the technology is big enough and Wall Street's enthusiasm great enough, the industry downturn can lead to a broader bear market.

It has been revealed that cisco is actually a poster child for this very dynamic. During the dot-com bubble, the stock rose dramatically. When that bubble burst, its stock fell dramatically. It took around 20 years for Cisco's shares to recover from that decline. If you had owned Cisco at the peak of the dot-com bubble, your entire thought process around investing probably changed. Some investors who got burned during that period may have even decided to stop investing altogether.

As it turned out, the internet did change the world. However, the key story is that the technology didn't make every dot-com stock a good investment. While Cisco survived, many internet stocks did not. And this is where the risk of an AI bubble comes in, since AI is the technology dejur. Cisco is again in play, having leaned into the sector by building data centers. Its stock rallied sharply because of this involvement, but it has also pulled back sharply. And the 18% decline over the last three months is modest compared to the drops in Aehr Test Systems and MongoDB. Aehr Test Systems provides testing equipment for computer chips. MongoDB makes database software. Like Cisco, they are both involved in AI.

It has been revealed that however, the bigger glitch is that even after the huge price declines, Aehr Test Systems has a price-to-earnings ratio of 890x, while MongoDB's P/E is 400x. Cisco Systems' P/E actually looks fairly attractive at 24x, but that's above its five-year average of 20x, and its price-to-sales and price-to-book ratios are also above their five-year averages, as well. In other words, even after the declines experienced so far, all three of these stocks could still have more room to fall.

Sources indicate that it is entirely possible that AI stocks pull back and then head higher again, without the often disastrous industry shakeouts that have happened to fresh technologies in the past. History suggests that won't be the case, and eventually many investors will lose money by following the crowd, like lemmings, into expensive AI-related technology stocks.

Recent findings suggest that for newer investors who haven't experienced a deep, prolonged bear market, it could be a life-changing event that leads them to abandon investing. Do not underestimate how emotionally devastating it can be to own stocks when an investment bubble bursts; consider diversifying now if you are heavily overweighted in AI stocks. Investing, despite bear markets, is still one of the most powerful wealth-building tools you have if you stick to it for the long term.
