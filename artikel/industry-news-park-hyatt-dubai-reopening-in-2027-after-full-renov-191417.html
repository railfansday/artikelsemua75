Industry observers note that as it right now stands, the hotel is accepting reservations for stays as of April 1, 2027. Now, the hotel is accepting villa reservations as of January 1, 2027, but that’s separate from the main part of the hotel. We’ve known that everything about the hotel would change, but not many renderings had been shared up until now.

One thing is for sure — there’s no denying that the rooms were in desperate need of a renovation, given that they hadn’t undergone any meaningful updates since the hotel first opened.

The hotel has now updated its website to show pictures of the new room product, and you can find those renderings below. I’m conflicted here. I wish we’d see a little more sense of place.

On the plus side, the hotel’s grounds and general bones otherwise have a decent sense of place, so I hope that’s at least maintained with the updates.

Recent findings suggest that leadership at the Park Hyatt Dubai had been vocal about how they hope to charge much higher rates after the renovation.
