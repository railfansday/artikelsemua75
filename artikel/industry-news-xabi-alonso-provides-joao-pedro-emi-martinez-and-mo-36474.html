There was initial optimism that Joao Pedro would return in time for this weekend’s meeting with Leeds but Alonso has confirmed he is still some time away from full fitness.

“He is not fit yet to play tomorrow, so we need to wait a little longer,” Alonso said.

“He's not gonna be available for Leeds, and we need to see day by day how he's feeling.”.

It acts as a blow for Chelsea who have struggled to find a solution up front in Joao Pedro’s absence, with Chelsea opting against starting a natural striker in the past three league games.

Alonso also confirmed that Maxence Lacroix is similarly unavailable for the weekend as he continues to nurse a hamstring problem.

The Frenchman is joined by Jorrel Hato, Reece James and Jamie Gittens on the sidelines, though Alonso has revealed that Gittens has returned to training having not played a Premier League minute this term.

The Spaniard was able to allay fears about Moises Caicedo’s fitness after he was withdrawn while playing for Ecuador in midweek because he felt unwell.

Sources indicate that “Yes, it was discussed and he was sensitive after playing the first whole game that he could save some minutes,” he added. “That was good for him and he's fine for tomorrow.”.

Meanwhile, Emi Martinez is set to return in between the sticks after making his first appearance since recovering from a broken jaw for Argentina in a 7-0 win over Costa Rica.

“He's in the squad and was selectable against Sunderland,” Alonso mentioned. “It was two weeks ago, probably there was risk, but the plan was for him to train more with Argentina, to play one game. He played well, so he's available for tomorrow.”.
