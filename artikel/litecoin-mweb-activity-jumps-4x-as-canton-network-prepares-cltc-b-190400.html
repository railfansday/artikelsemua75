On Monday, the 23rd of November, the privacy-enabled network built for institutional finance, Canton Network [CC], announced that “Litecoin will be coming to Canton”.

Greywick Digital is an asset-agnostic tokenization infrastructure organization focused on bringing established assets onto the Canton Network. The company was developing cLTC, the reserve-backed Litecoin [LTC] token representation, for the Canton Network, with a late-2026 mainnet target.

Greywick said that it would require multiple signatures would be needed to move funds and independent checks would use published Litecoin addresses.

In other news, over the past ten days, Litecoin MWEB transactions have consistently numbered around four times the previous daily average.

The 4-hour chart revealed that Monday’s market bounce was retraced, but the altcoin has mostly held on to its weekend price bounce.

Even so, its overall H4 trend was bearish. To flip the structure bullishly, a trading session close above $44.79, the latest lower high in the downtrend, is needed.

While the H4 was bearish, the weekly chart indicated bullish potential. The downward trend of 2025 and 2026 saw a structural shift during the Litecoin rally to $75.63 in September.

The subsequent correction dipped below the $39.28 swing low, but has not closed a weekly trading session below it. Hence, this could be a liquidity sweep before a bullish reversal.
