Latest Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino has given his first interview since taking the reins at Old Trafford. And the Argentine has made a lot of things clear as he aims to bring the club back to where they are used to being. United sit 18th in the Premier League table after 10 matches, with the slow start prompting the decision-makers to sack Michael Carrick and pay compensation to the United States Soccer Federation for Pochettino. The former Southampton, Tottenham and Chelsea manager will be in the dugout on Sunday for an away game at Liverpool and gave a wide-ranging interview to mark his return to the Premier League.

In a recent development, "It's a mistake to see the [current] situation. We need to see the potential of this club," Pochettino remarked. "Of course, the circumstances are the circumstances, and we need to be responsible for the circumstances. But at the same time, we need to understand that it's a game and, to connect with the game, we need to feel free.". (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

After Sunday's trip to Anfield, there's another away game, against Sporting CP in the Champions League, while his first game at Old Trafford will be against Brentford on November 28.

Recent findings suggest that united have 10 games before the January transfer window, when the latest boss will be able to add to his squad. However, he has already stated plenty in his first interview and here are Express Sport's main takeaways...

There's no denying United's players have felt the pressure this season, both with their on-field performances and, in some cases, their comments away from the pitch. He made a point of pledging to relieve some of that pressure and indicated that, if United are to turn things around, freedom for players to express themselves is key.

"We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform. That is the most key thing, and that is our priority now: to work with them and try to take the pressure away.

"Pressure needs to bring energy; responsibility needs to bring energy, because if not, it’s too heavy to put all that pressure and responsibility on your back. I think when you connect with the game, you connect through your talent, and when you feel freedom, when you feel happy, when you want to play for your team-mate, when you want to play for your family, when you want to play for your club, your fans - that is what we need to create all together.".

In a recent development, 2. Most important thing is culture.

Last season, Carrick helped get the team back to winning ways after replacing Ruben Amorim. It was only a quick fix, though, and Pochettino believes a broader cultural shift is necessary if there is to be any permanent improvement.

According to recent reports, "To create the culture is the most vital," he stated. "Create the habits, the principles, and I think that is what we want to create. We need to [be] looking forward and to find a way to create the best habits and the culture that makes us to be close to winning every single game. At the same time, though, he recognised he is coming into a upcoming environment and needs to be respectful of United's history.

"When you arrive at a new club as a head coach and coaching staff, we need to adapt to the culture of the club, we need to know the identity of the club, the culture, the philosophy, and then with this sense of knowing the history of the club, try to create all together - because we are all different - a culture, a philosophy that can respect that identity of the history of the club, while at the same time performing," he added.

"If we don’t perform, the responsibility is mine, but that is why we need to communicate well, we need to create a good way to communicate with all the areas, and I think of course being involved in all the situations that affect the team.

In a recent development, 3. What he thinks about the squad.

It has been revealed that pochettino knows he can't make any changes to his squad until January - even then, United haven't always been to keen on mid-season arrivals. With that in mind, plenty of fans will have been hanging on what the new boss mentioned about the players at his disposal.

It has been revealed that "The best squad that we can have is the players that we have, and we need to love the players that we have," he said. "We need to support the players; we need to provide all the tools for them to perform. It’s only an act of responsibility now to start to work and provide them the facility to translate all the talent onto the field.".

Industry observers note that pochettino also acknowledged the difficulty of his first game in charge. "I know very well what that game means and how important it is. I think we know very well that it’s going to be a tough game, but at the same time we believe in our way, and for sure we are going to compete and we are going to go there to Anfield with the idea to win and being brave and being Manchester United," he said.

"You know, the history talks for us and [tells us] what we need to expect, and we are going to expect in Manchester United that we need to make sure that we are brave and we are protagonists and go there with no fears and challenge a very good team like Liverpool.".

4. Owners and directors need to be on the same page.

In a recent development, previous United manager Amorim hit out at the club hierarchy before his departure, while Lisandro Martinez was similarly pointed in his remarks about the higher-ups. Pochettino was asked about the owners and directors in his first interview and his response gives reason for cautious optimism.

"I feel all the support," he said. "We felt all the support from the directors and the owners, and of course now it’s up to us to provide what they expect from us. We have a clear idea of what the club expects from us, and we want to be at the level of the demand of this football club.".

He also had direct words of thanks for club directors as he prepares for his first game in charge. The Glazer family and Sir Jim Ratcliffe have drawn the ire of some fans but others may give them leeway to see if the change in the dugout has also prompted a genuine change upstairs.

Recent findings suggest that "Now I am focusing on adapting here as soon as possible - very rapidly," Pochettino added. "Thank you to all the staff and the people and the directors that made that adaptation easier in those first few hours.".

5. He was in Barcelona for United's Champions League win in 1999.

Pochettino isn't the first recent United boss to have been in Barcelona when the club clinched a Treble in dramatic fashion in 1999, having been a player with Catalan club Espanyol at the time. However, while he took a watching brief, as opposed to the starring role player by Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, he can appreciate the significance of the moment.

Industry observers note that "In 1999 I was in Barcelona in that final, that was an iconic final, and [upcoming goalkeeping coach] Toni Jimenez was too," Pochettino mentioned. "We were shouting for Manchester United from there.

Industry observers note that "I was so excited to see Manchester United, and more with admiration of Sir Alex Ferguson, for me the best coach in the history of the sport. And I know we admired him, and then to have the opportunity to meet when we went to Southampton, and then we played at Old Trafford in 2013—yes, that dream came true, and then the possibility to meet him in a restaurant in London.

"Yes, those are all the memories that I have about that club, and now being a head coach is an honor and a privilege that is a responsibility that brings full energy in my body, because the excitement to be here is massive.

It has been revealed that "I remember that game, that type of game where you stay sitting in your place and you’re so excited because you are watching the game," he added. "That was an amazing game, and then when what happened at the end happened, it’s like it’s impossible not to empathize with that team. And that was an amazing memory that made me pay more attention to United, to Ferguson, and the players that were there, who are all legends.".
