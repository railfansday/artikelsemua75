Leeds United are aware of the growing interest in Tarik Muharemovic but have no plans to entertain offers in the near future.

The 23-year-old has been outstanding since his £34million move to Elland Road during the summer, after Leeds moved promptly to secure his signature without alerting their rivals. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Multiple elite clubs from across Europe have since been keeping tabs on the centre-back's progress, and Daniel Farke's side are well aware they will likely receive offers.

However, former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Leeds will be doing everything in their power to ensure Muharemovic remains at Elland Road. (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

The Bosnia and Herzegovina international is under contract until 2031, placing the club in a strong position to dictate his future despite the growing level of interest.

Muharemovic will continue to be a crucial player for Leeds this season, with a January move completely ruled out, and their success could ensure he sticks around.

Former Everton CEO Keith Wyness told Football Insider on 1 November that Leeds are concerned about losing Muharemovic given the club who have been watching him.

The Whites have been urged to rebuff any moves for the centre-back, and they are in a strong position to do so, especially if they can build on their strong start to the season.

However, they are bracing to receive offers for Muharemovic given the level of interest in his signature, and are ready to demand a significant fee to even entertain talks.

"Of course Leeds will be aware of the interest in Tarik Muharemovic," Brown told Football Insider.

"It's always difficult for a club in Leeds' position, because they did really well to bring him in, they moved promptly so there was no noise that could have made it complicated.

"They got their man, he's come into the side, done really well, and already all of those top clubs have started keeping an eye on his progress.

"Now, Leeds will be doing everything in their power to keep hold of him, they don't want to lose him, and he's going to be a substantial part of any success they have this season.

"The problem is, they now have to be prepared to turn down any offers that come forward, and they are in a good position to do that because he's on a long contract.

"But when significant clubs come calling, it can make things difficult, so it'll be interesting to see how they handle the situation.".

There have been whispers surrounding Muharemovic's future at Leeds throughout this season, with a number of top clubs having been linked with a potential move.

Football Insider revealed last month that all of the Premier League's top clubs have placed the Leeds centre-back high on their respective shortlists after his impressive start.

Liverpool and Chelsea are believed to be two of the leading contenders in England, with both clubs keen to bolster their defences, while Muharemovic has caught the eye.

In Europe, meanwhile, Bayern Munich have been heavily linked with the Leeds star and could make a move to rival Premier League clubs when next summer's window opens.
