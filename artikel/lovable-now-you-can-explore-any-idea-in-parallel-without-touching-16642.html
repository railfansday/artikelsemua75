The best builders experiment, tinker, and tweak. Now you and your team can try different ideas in parallel and see how they'd really look in the wild before choosing your favorite, while your live app stays untouched.

Industry observers note that with drafts, you can copy your whole app, from design to database, as often as you like. Each draft has its own chat and preview, so you and your team can work on separate versions and compare them side by side. Nothing goes live until you accept a draft and hit publish. Now that each draft is a full copy of your app, backend included when needed, you can easily explore any new idea for your app, for example:.

In a recent development, open a project, click its name at the top of the editor, and hit "New draft." Lovable creates a complete copy of your app, from design to database. To edit the draft, just start prompting. You can create as many drafts as you like.

When you're happy with a draft, hit accept to add its changes to your project, then hit publish to make them live. Any data you add while testing stays in the draft and is cleared when you accept or delete it, so it never lands next to real customer data.

Industry observers note that changed your mind? Before you accept, just delete the draft. After you accept, you can still roll it back before you publish: open History, pick the version from before you accepted, and hit revert.
