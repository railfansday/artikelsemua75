How’d they get home? Is there really a supernatural force controlling them, in the wilderness and in New Jersey? Will anyone survive? What happened to Melissa’s hat? Follow this Yellowjackets season 4 schedule so you don’t miss a single second.

Recent findings suggest that we’re going back to the wilderness. We’re going back to the equally chaotic present-day timeline. And since we now get to see the immediate aftermath of the young Yellowjackets’ return to civilization and processed food... we’re also going back to high school.
