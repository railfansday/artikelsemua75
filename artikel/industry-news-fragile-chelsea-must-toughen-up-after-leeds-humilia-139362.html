Xabi Alonso is straying towards crisis territory. Here’s a mind-bending statistic: Chelsea are eighth but have now lost more league games than Manchester United. They were bullied by a rampant Leeds United, who fully merited their first win at Stamford Bridge since going top with a 2-0 victory in December 1999, and can look forward to another season with no European football if they do not sharpen up soon.

It is not really worth dwelling on the fact that the second Leeds goal, a Dominic Calvert-Lewin penalty converted early in an astonishingly one-sided second half, came after Morgan Rogers was harshly penalised for handball. That was a minor detail given the wider picture. Leeds, superbly coached by Daniel Farke and up to sixth after two clinical strikes from Noah Okafor, Calvert-Lewin’s sixth goal of the season and a late effort from Brenden Aaronson, were so dominant that they had no real need for a helping hand from the officials.
