Tesla’s FSD v15 adds Reality View, a live AI-generated view of the road

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Tesla Reality View in FSD v15, crossing the Golden Gate Bridge.

Recent findings suggest that tesla has started rolling out version 15 of its Full Self-Driving (Supervised) software, which it indicates upgrades every part of its end-to-end driving network. The company unveiled the update in a post on X on Saturday. It says v15 handles safety-critical cases 25 times better.

FSD v15 now starting to roll out – upgrading every part of the end-to-end driving network.

Safety 25x better handling of safety critical cases.

Tesla Reality View Adds a live visualization of the drive that lets you see & control what FSD is planning to do.

Navigation for start & end of the trip now learns end-to-end from fleet route preferences. Later v15 releases will expand this to the entire route.

You also should notice better detection of signs, animals, debris, potholes and standing water, plus more natural speed handling in school zones and inclement weather.

Lastly, FSD can now slow for speed cameras & Bike Rack Mode lets you enable FSD w/ bike racks or similar hitch accessories.

— Tesla (@Tesla) November 21, 2026.

The full release notes, posted by Tesla’s Super Intelligence account, tie the 25x figure to the critical intervention rate. They credit an upgraded vision encoder that FSD shares with Tesla’s Robotaxi and Optimus robot. Tesla trained it on millions of internet videos. (Related: Latest Update: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Industry observers note that the headline feature is Tesla Reality View. It streams a real-time generated view of the drive from a virtual camera above the car. Drivers can see and control what FSD plans to do, and upcoming turns appear in the view, the notes say. It replaces all the separate model signals with one visualisation that uses less total compute. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

“This is real-time AI video generation (no gaming engine!) using just half of one AI4 TRIP accelerator,” Elon Musk wrote on X.

Industry observers note that the view runs about a second behind the road, TechRadar reported. Musk noted it combines a shrunken version of one of Tesla’s world models with a diffusion model, and that Tesla will soon cut the lag by 35%, according to TechRadar. Only cars with Tesla’s latest hardware get it.

Drivers have compared the view to GTA VI, which launched days earlier, TechRadar reported.

Sources indicate that navigation now learns from fleet route preferences at the start and end of a trip, and later v15 releases will extend that to the whole route. Parking draws on each driver’s history, and the map can show several spots. Tesla also lists better detection of road signs, small animals, debris, chains, potholes and deep standing water.

Recent findings suggest that FSD can now slow down for speed cameras. If a driver cancels twice at the same camera, the car saves it and stops slowing there. Bike Rack Mode lets drivers use FSD with a hitch accessory, with reversing, Autopark and Summon switched off. Attention monitoring works more often with hats and sunglasses, and is less strict in faster driver profiles.

Recent findings suggest that the software is still Full Self-Driving (Supervised), so drivers must keep watching the road. Tesla has also started carrying riders in Cybercabs and plans to build them in Berlin.
