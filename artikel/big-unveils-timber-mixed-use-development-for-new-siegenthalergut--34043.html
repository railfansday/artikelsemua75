Sources indicate that SUBSTANTIAL has been selected to design a 13,000-square-meter mixed-use development within the latest Siegenthalergut urban district in Thun, Switzerland. Commissioned by Frutiger AG, the project, titled "Nachwachsende Stadt" (Growing City), brings together four timber buildings accommodating two hotels, residential units, and commercial spaces. Designed in collaboration with August + Margrith Künzel, the proposal was selected through a design competition organized by Frutiger AG in coordination with the city of Thun. The development is among the first two plots scheduled for construction within the larger Siegenthalergut district, and is expected to begin no later than 2028.

The building at the southwestern corner of the site, facing the fresh sports centre and the future Swiss Football Association headquarters, accommodates two hotels under one roof. The three- and four-star hotels share amenity spaces, which are arranged around a diagonal setback that shapes the building's profile. These stepped volumes create terraces overlooking the sports centre and the Bernese Alps. At ground level, a cantilevered corner marks the main entrance and opens onto a drop-off plaza, while commercial spaces address the boulevard. A restaurant is also planned on the rooftop.
