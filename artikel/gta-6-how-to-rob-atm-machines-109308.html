GTA 6: How to Rob ATM Machines

In a recent development, one of GTA 6's main objectives is to make as much money as possible. There are many ways to do this, such as stealing and selling cars, robbing stores, completing missions, etc.

However, there’s another way to get a good sum of cash that most players may not know about, and that’s robbing ATM machines. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

If you are looking for an additional way to earn money in the game, this guide has you covered. Here’s how to rob ATM machines in GTA VI.

Before you can rob an ATM machine in Grand Theft Auto 6, you must steal it first. To do this, you need two things: a vehicle with a flatbed and the Winch mod attached to it. (Related: Man charged after allegedly posing as Walmart delivery ...)

Trucks with flatbeds are generally easy to find and acquire by simply stealing one, while the Winch can be added to the vehicle by bringing it to a Mod Shop.

Keep in mind that Mod Shops each specialize in certain services, so not all of them will have a Winch available.

It has been revealed that if you are having trouble finding a Mod Shop that works, make sure to read the shop’s description in the map menu. The description will explain if it specializes in your vehicle.

Recent findings suggest that after attaching a Winch, all you have to do is back it up into the ATM machine to automatically latch it to your vehicle. Next, drive away with the machine attached to rip it out of the building.

In a recent development, with the ATM machine ripped out of the building and attached to your truck, it’s time to get it open so you can claim the money. There are two ways to do this.

It has been revealed that first, you can use Sticky Bombs to blow it up, which is the faster way. You can purchase Sticky Bombs from most Ammu-Nation shops across Leonida and they don’t cost much, only costing around 185 dollars.

According to recent reports, the other way to open an ATM machine is to continue driving with it attached to the Winch of your vehicle until it breaks and busts open. However, this method isn’t recommended since you need to do a lot of damage to it to open it.

Either way you decide, you will know when an ATM breaks open when dollar signs and blocks of money appear.

Simply put, not all ATM machines hold the same amount of money in GTA VI. From personal experience, each ATM has, on average, rewarded me with 4k to 6k. There have been some that rewarded me with more than that, though, so it really just depends.

Once you claim the cash from the ATM machine, there’s a good chance the money will spray pink paint on Lucia and James. Whether it does or not depends on how far you are in the story.

This is because all money from banks and ATM machines contain Marked Bills, and the paint helps cops track down the thieves who committed the robbery.

In order to deal with the paint and officially steal Marked Bills, you need to complete the mission “Credit Check” during Chapter 3 of the main story. Completing this mission will reward you with a Dye Pack Jammer that makes it possible to claim and spend Marked Bills.
