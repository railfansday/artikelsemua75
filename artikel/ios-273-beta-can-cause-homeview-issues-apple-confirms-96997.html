According to recent reports, apple’s upcoming HomeView just launched last Friday, but some early adopters reported problems with Siri AI in their first days of use. Apple shipped fixes in HomeView 27.2.1 yesterday, but it also confirmed the iOS 27.3 beta is responsible for some of the problems.

Yesterday Apple released its first post-launch software patch for HomeView, less than a week after the product debuted.

Per the firm’s official release notes, HomeView 27.2.1 focuses on bug fixes, including a fix for the following issue:.

Pairing with an iPhone or iPad running the iOS or iPadOS 27.3 beta may cause Siri requests to fail. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

In a recent development, it’s unknown how many reports of Siri issues on HomeView can be attributed to the iOS 27.3 beta. However, it wouldn’t surprise me if many of the reports were from beta users. That’s in part because early HomeView reviewers didn’t seem to have the same problems.

In a recent development, with HomeView 27.2.1 now available, those problems should hopefully be taken care of. However, if you’re on the iOS 27.3 beta, there’s a new matter you’ll need to deal with temporarily.

As discovered by pdfu, if you try to make a Personal Content request on an iPhone running iOS 27.3, Siri in HomeView 27.2.1 now notes: “I can’t help with that because your personal device is on newer software.”.

According to recent reports, if you’re on iOS 27.3, Siri on HomeView can’t use your iPhone for personal context, likely until December.HomeView won’t send Siri requests to an iPhone on newer software. Without a HomeView update, your only option is iOS 27.2.x.On-device requests like timers still work. https://t.co/8UbRs7z496.

It’s understandable that problems might arise from having iOS and HomeView software that’s out of sync. Unfortunately though, there’s no beta software available for HomeView at the moment.

That means unless Apple releases a beta soon, users might have to wait until the public launch of HomeView 27.3 in December to get Personal Content features back.
