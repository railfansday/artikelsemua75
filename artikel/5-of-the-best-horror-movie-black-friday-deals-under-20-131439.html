"Evil Dead Burn" is the latest entry in the long-running franchise, and it did something no previous "Evil Dead" film did before: it made some fans think the movie was just a little too nasty. It has been revealed that at this point, "Obsession" needs no further introduction. Curry Barker's mega-hit horror flick has a knockout performance from Inde Navarrette as Nikki, the object of affection for her longtime friend Bear (Michael Johnston).

Equal parts funny and scary, "Obsession" got a lot of hype, and I genuinely believe it was worth it: this movie works exceptionally well, and I wouldn't be surprised if Navarrette scores herself an Oscar nom for her work here.
