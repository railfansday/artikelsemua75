In a recent development, england’s route to the Nations League final will be confirmed this morning as the draw for the knockout rounds is made in Nyon, Switzerland. The Three Lions topped their group on the final matchday of the group phase with a thrilling 3-2 victory over world champions Spain in Madrid. It was a dramatic outing at the Metropolitano, as England were made to defend their three-goal advantage with ten men after Elliot Anderson was dismissed late on.

But they got the job done - with Thomas Tuchel later hailing the result as the finest of his time with the Three Lions - and will now push into the knockout stages as members of Pot One, alongside France, Germany and Portugal, who are pushing into a brave upcoming world without the aid of their usual talisman Cristiano Ronaldo after his retirement from international duty. Nations League draw latest updates.

The Nations League quarter-finals, as well as the League A/B and League B/C promotion/relegation play-offs, will be played in March 2027.

The first legs are scheduled for March 25-27, with the return legs scheduled for March 28-30.

The finals tournament is scheduled for June 2027.

The four group winners will face the four runners-up in a two-legged Nations League quarter-final.

The group winners will all be seeded and will have home advantage in their respective second leg ties. Teams from the same group cannot be paired together in the knockout stage, therefore England and Spain - for example - will be kept apart.

As a result of finishing top in Group A3, England’s confirmed potential Nations League quarter-final opponent are Belgium and the Netherlands after they were pipped to top spot in Group A1 and A2 by France and Germany respectively.

England’s remaining possible quarter-final opponents was revealed on Tuesday night as Norway produced a stunning comeback from 2-0 down pip Denmark to the runners-up spot behind Portugal in Group A4 by virtue of a superior head-to-head record.

The four group winners will be seeded in Pot 1, with the four runners-up will be unseeded in Pot 2.

Pot 1: France, England, Germany, Portugal.

Pot 2: Belgium, Spain, Netherlands, Norway.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live UEFA TV.

Live stream: You can also stream the draw for free online via the UEFA YouTube page.
