In a recent development, waiting more than two hours for your checked bag is frustrating. Watching it emerge covered in a brown substance that looks like human waste is considerably worse. That is what American Airlines passengers reported in Atlanta this week, though the airline notes the mess had a much less revolting explanation: peanut butter.

Passengers aboard American Airlines flight 2609 from Miami (MIA) to Atlanta (ATL) on November 16, 2026, reported an unpleasant surprise at baggage claim. Travelers waited more than two hours before their luggage appeared on carousel eight at Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport. When the bags arrived, passengers saw a brown substance smeared across them and naturally feared the worst. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

Passenger Derick McCallum recorded the scene and later questioned the airline’s explanation:.

“Even if somebody did pack peanut butter? How much peanut butter does somebody pack to cover that many bags?”.

Sources indicate that american later mentioned that liquefied peanut butter had leaked from a passenger’s checked bag, affecting nearby luggage. The airline said an environmental inspection confirmed its explanation.

“Unfortunately, a food item from a customer’s checked bag leaked during the flight, affecting several bags nearby.”.
