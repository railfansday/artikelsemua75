A new scheduling gap in the Pokémon anime might give us a clue to the Winds and Waves release date.

Serebii founder Joe Merrick pointed out on Bluesky that for the entirety of his time running the fan site, "there has been a fresh anime episode each week." However, it was declared today that there will be a nine-month gap between Winter Voyage in December and the new Liko and Roy series in 2027. (Related: Christmas market forced to cancel after backlash over A...)

Recent findings suggest that "Winds and Waves' obvious schedule changes really messed with things huh," Merrick noted. A new series of the show typically launch alongside a new mainline game, so delaying the series to October could be taken as evidence that Winds and Waves will launch later in the year.

Sources indicate that of course, it's also possible the anime's hiatus has nothing to do with Winds and Waves, and that the late schedule is intentional. But it lines up with the Pokémon TCG debacle.

Last month, McDonald's accidentally revealed an upcoming collab for its Happy Meals, with 15 latest cards arriving in November 2027 — interestingly, there are no new Pokémon included whatsoever. Not even the Winds and Waves starters. (Related: Tech Report: Throw a Friend for Monsters codes (Novembe...)

The theory goes that the newly introduced set will arrive too soon after Winds and Waves to include any new 'mons, which would line up with the anime releasing in October.

Put it all together, and it looks like Winds and Waves will launch in late 2027, narrowing down the release window.

Until we hear from The Pokémon Company, of course, this is all speculation, so it's worth taking with a grain of salt. However, considering that mainline games traditionally launch in November (or, at the earliest, September), it lines up.

In a recent development, if Winds and Waves has been delayed, explaining the wider-than-usual gap in the anime, then maybe it's for the best. Game Freak could certainly use a little more runway with the mainline games.
