Jodeci pulled the plug on their Forever My Lady 35th anniversary tour at the Louisville Palace on Sunday after fans had already gathered, calling off a show that’s part of the celebration of the trio’s 1991 debut album of the same name. Hailey, along with members JoJo, DeVante Swing and Mr. Dalvin, is slated to take the stage again on Tuesday night (Nov. 24) at the Orpheum Theatre in Memphis.

Recent findings suggest that “I just want you to know that I am truly, truly sorry to everyone that came out expecting to see us. Believe me, there’s nothing I love more than getting on that stage singing for you guys and feeling all the love … [that you] give us in return, so your support means so much to me than words can express,” Hailey mentioned in the video statement. “I never, ever take a single fan, a single ticket, or a single opportunity to perform for granted. So I just want to thank you for the medical staff last night — you guys were amazing.”.

According to recent reports, hailey seemed to provide proof that he’ll be back on stage tonight, informing fans that he was already in town. “Memphis, I’m here, baby,” he said. I see y’all tomorrow night. God bless you.”.

Forever My Lady hit No. 1 on the Billboard R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart in May 1991, spinning off the No. 25 Billboard Hot 100 hit “Forever My Lady” and the No. 11 hit “Come and Talk to Me.”.
