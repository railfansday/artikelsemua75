Admittedly there was an expletive in that chant to strengthen the sense of enthusiasm, as well as the belief Leeds are ascending to new levels.

Leeds boss Farke has made a conscious effort to dampen talk of planes and passports, not to spoil the mood but to manage the expectations of an intense fanbase.

By leading his side to a such a convincing victory over a team of expensive global stars, he did not help his campaign of pressure control. (Related: Animal control called to rescue alligator that turned o...)

“If you face a side who has world-class potential and world-class potential players, if you dominate the side, then you could label yourself to be more or less from the first to the last second of the game, the better team, it gives lots of belief and lots of confidence,” said Farke. “Yes, of course, it's always good for the mood.

“But it is even more key also for the table to add three more important points, so it's important during this period of the season to collect points in order to make sure that you finish in the right position.

“It will definitely give us more confidence, but to be honest, we also travelled here with confidence and belief, otherwise it wouldn't be possible to show such a dominant performance.”.

Chelsea drew first blood on the 25-minute mark, when the imposing Emmanuel Emegha rifled a shot past James Trafford at the goalkeeper’s near post.

Sources indicate that noah Okafor cancelled out the opener three minutes later - and from there Leeds only grew in confidence. The Swiss winger was to strike again, adding a third after Dominic Calvert-Lewin’s penalty had put the Whites ahead.

By the time Brenden Aaronson weighed in with a fourth from the bench, the party in the away end was well underway.

There is a common narrative when Leeds - or others who threaten the established elites in the Premier League’s upper echelons - gatecrash the top eight.

Pundits and fans have a tendency to focus upon physicality and intensity, sometimes discarding technical and tactical qualities to paint a picture of athletic brutes.

Leeds’ players undoubtedly have robust engines - and they certainly have aggression - but Chelsea were outwitted just as much as they were outfought.

“We looked very, very dominant today in our pressing and with our intensity,” stated Farke. “We always had pressure on the ball, but it has more to do with a tactical blueprint and a tactical solution and how you want to be good against the ball, how you structure your team and also how you want to bypass the opponent than really just fitness level, because on this level all the boys are fit and outstanding.

“If it would be just about physical strength, I would go into the gym and get some bodybuilders and set them on the pitch.”.

There is a widespread acceptance of Farke’s reluctance to discuss Leeds’ potential ceiling - and he is of course right not to get carried away. Leeds are just 11 games in and there will almost certainly be weeks in which they face criticism before the curtain comes down on the 2026/27 campaign.

The music that boomed out of the Leeds dressing room was not quite to Farke’s taste, but he knew better than to pretend it was not fun to bask in the glow of a remarkable afternoon.

“At my age right now, you're looking forward to heading home as soon as possible and I can enjoy a little bit of time on the sofa with coffee and cake.”.
