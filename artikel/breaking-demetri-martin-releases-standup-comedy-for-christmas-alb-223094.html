Demetri Martin is getting into the holiday spirit with a newly released album, “Standup Comedy for Christmas.”.

The album, out now, consists of 13 stand-up comedy tracks and one original, acoustic song that closes the set, titled “It’s Christmas FYI.” The latest material is available for purchase on limited-edition vinyl, CD and as a digital download on BandCamp — with three of the tracks available to stream for free. Starting Nov. 28, Martin will also embark on his first-ever Christmas comedy tour, bringing the holiday-themed live show to San Diego, Chicago, Brooklyn, Washington D.C., Santa Barbara, Sacramento, Anaheim and Los Angeles, where he will headline two demonstrates at the Comedy Store. Martin is an L.A.-based stand-up comedian and artist who is best known for his Netflix specials and his Comedy Central series “Notable Things With Demetri Martin,” which debuted in 2009 and ran for two seasons. The show, a cult alt-comedy favorite, blended Martin’s stand-up, comedy sketches and drawings.

On the film side, Martin appeared in Ang Lee’s “Taking Woodstock” and directed, wrote and starred in “Dean,” which won the Founder’s Prize at the Tribeca Festival.

According to recent reports, in 2027, Martin will release his next stand-up comedy special, the second part in a narrative trilogy for Netflix. His last Netflix special, “Demetri Martin: Demetri Deconstructed,” debuted on the streamer in 2024. He also put out Netflix hours in 2015 and 2018.

Martin is represented by UTA and Schreck Rose Dapello Adams Berlin & Dunham.
