Sources indicate that DUBLIN, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Aryza, a global provider of end-to-end AI-enabled solutions across the credit and debt lifecycle, has announced a long-term partnership with the Joe Duffy Group (JDG), providing the technology platform underpinning the entire lending and collections journey, which will help JDG's ambitious blueprint to transform motor vehicle finance in the Irish market.

JDG has launched its motor finance operation, and Aryza's platform will use data intelligently across the entire journey, from front-end decisioning through to collections at the back end.

Under the agreement, Aryza will deploy five of its core solutions across loan origination, anti-money laundering (AML)/ know your customer (KYC) onboarding, loan decisioning, loan management, and conversational and voice AI. The platform will cover every stage of JDG's lending operation, from initial application and credit decisioning through to agreement management and intelligent, AI-powered customer communications. Aryza is a global provider of end-to-end AI-enabled credit and debt lifecycle management solutions, specialising in innovative tools that streamline complex processes for financial services.

Aryza's solutions combine AI-enabled tools with integrated data sources to help businesses operate more efficiently and make better decisions in areas such as Lending, Collections, Recoveries & Governance Risk & Compliance (GRC).

With a focus on delivering value through technology, Aryza empowers organisations to improve efficiency, compliance, and customer outcomes.

Recent findings suggest that established in 1972, Joe Duffy Group is Ireland's largest motor dealer group and one of the country's leading automotive retail and services businesses.
