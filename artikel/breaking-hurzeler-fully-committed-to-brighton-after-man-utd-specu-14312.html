Fabian Hurzeler insisted he remains "fully committed" to Brighton after interest from Manchester United in their search for a upcoming head coach over the international break. (Related: Compound Interest Calculator: See Your Money Grow...)

It has been revealed that hurzeler has been a revelation since joining Brighton from St. Pauli in 2024, with the Seagulls flying high in the Premier League, sitting second after 10 matches in 2026-27.

Brighton have won six of their last seven Premier League games (L1), keeping a clean sheet in all six victories, with Hurzeler attracting interest from the Old Trafford hierarchy.

It was confirmed last week that Michael Carrick had been sacked by United following a dismal start to the season that left them in the relegation zone. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Hurzeler, along with Antonio Conte, Eddie Howe and Julian Nagelsmann, were some of the names being linked with the Old Trafford job, but the Red Devils instead chose to appoint former Tottenham, Paris Saint-Germain and Chelsea boss Mauricio Pochettino.

The 33-year-old revealed his decision to stay at Brighton came after strong conversations with Seagulls chief Tony Bloom, who stated he would back him in the transfer window.

Bloom also noted this week he would fight "ridiculously hard" to keep hold of Hurzeler amid interest from some of the bigger teams in the Premier League in recent months.

Hurzeler told a press conference: "I've spoken a lot about his [Bloom's] support, trust and belief.

"It's a privilege to work here and since my time here there's been a lot of rumours about other clubs, but they are just rumours, and I am fully committed to this club.".

But away from the talk off the pitch, Hurzeler will be hoping his team can continue to do so on it this Sunday when they make the trip to the MKM Stadium to face Hull City.

Hull won their first two Premier League games this season but are winless in all eight since then (D5 L3).

It's the fourth time a side has won their first two games then none of their next eight in the competition, along with Wolves in 2011-12, Hull in 2016-17, and West Brom in 2017-18, all of whom were relegated.

Sources indicate that however, Brighton are winless in all six of their visits to the MKM Stadium in all competitions (D2 L4). Their last away win against Hull came at Boothferry Park in the fourth tier in October 2000, and Hurzeler is expecting another difficult test despite the Tigers' recent run of form.

"They are doing an impressive job. They are very structured. Arsenal suffered so we need to be patient," Hurzeler added on the challenge of facing Hull.

"We know they are good in transition and have individual qualities, as well as set-pieces. I don't think it's an easy place to go.".
