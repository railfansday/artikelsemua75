“Come on. First of all, I’m putting it out there, Manchester United have to go and get him. There’s no doubt. That guy is unbelievable.

“Against Spain the other night, I knew he was good, but I didn’t know he was that good.

Industry observers note that “My goodness. Go and get him. Whatever it takes. I don’t care. Go and get him. I can’t keep that image out of my head. I watched him the other night, I was almost crying watching him against Spain like, ‘Why can’t we just go and get this guy? Just go and get him.’.

“I would have done the deal after the game. I would have been in the changing room after the game like, ‘Just call him, please. Let’s do the deal right here.’ Oh goodness, what a player.”.

Fortune was particularly impressed by the Newcastle defender’s display against the European champions and believes he has the qualities needed to improve United’s options at left-back.

The former Chelsea academy graduate has developed into one of the most promising young defenders in English football. His technical ability, attacking instincts and defensive awareness could make him an excellent addition to the Manchester United squad.

Industry observers note that united have struggled considerably at the back this season, conceding 34 goals in 16 matches across all competitions. Strengthening the defensive department will therefore be an vital task for Pochettino as he attempts to turn the club’s fortunes around.
