As the Marketing Operations Manager, you will serve as a deeply embedded, data-oriented partner to the Marketing team and help the business understand and drive performance across the Marketing-to-Revenue funnel.

It has been revealed that you will analyze the funnel from marketing engagement through qualification, pipeline, and revenue. You’re focused on identifying growth opportunities, conversion gaps, leakage, handoff problems and translating those findings into clear recommendations for Marketing, SDR, Sales, and RevOps leaders.

This is not a traditional reporting or martech administration role. Your primary value will be your ability to frame ambiguous business questions, diagnose the underlying drivers of performance, distinguish signal from noise, and help cross-functional teams make better operating and investment decisions.
