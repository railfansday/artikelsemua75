Efficiency is the sign of a well-run company, especially during tough times. Simpson is navigating a struggling North American housing market efficiently. Income from operations grew 9.5% in Q3 even as sales grew just 3.8%.

It has been revealed that both metrics are signs of strength, though. The U.S. housing market is in a deep freeze. Existing-home sales fell 5.5% in October from September and 8.8% from a year earlier, to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 3.75 million. That is the lowest level since the Great Recession.

It has been revealed that yet through that turbulence, Simpson continues to thrive. Announcing a newly introduced $1 billion share repurchase plan reveals management remains confident in the future. That would represent about 12.5% of Simpson's recent market cap, and adds to the more than 4% reduction in share count over the last three years.

That's a shareholder-friendly approach. And the underlying business should only improve as the housing slump eases in the coming years. That's why I believe Simpson Manufacturing stock will continue to beat the market and deliver excellent returns to shareholders over the next five years.
