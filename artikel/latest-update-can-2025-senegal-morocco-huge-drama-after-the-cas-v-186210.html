The series of the CAN 2025 final is not yet over. A few hours after the decision of the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS), which confirmed Senegal’s victory against Morocco, the Royal Moroccan Football Federation (FRMF) officially reacted. In a particularly firm statement, the Moroccan body expressed its disagreement with this decision and announced its intention to initiate fresh legal procedures.

“The Royal Moroccan Football Federation expresses its protest against the fact that its request was not taken into consideration”indicates in particular the press release published this Tuesday. The FRMF considers that its arguments concerning the alleged irregularities of the procedure have not been sufficiently examined by the sports court. It considers in particular that certain elements call into question “the integrity and credibility of the decision. ».

To support its protest, the FRMF highlights a specific element: “It was confirmed that contact had taken place between the German referee, Ulrich Haas, and the president of the Senegalese Football Federation, 48 hours before the decision was given. » The Moroccan Federation believes that this situation should have been taken into consideration when examining the file.

The Moroccan body also contests the interpretation adopted by the CAS concerning the temporary departure of the Senegalese players from the field during the final. She believes that the decision “still does not take into account the need to comply with the direction enshrined in recent decisions of the International Football Association Board (IFAB) and the Fédération Internationale de Football Association (FIFA). ».

The FRMF particularly emphasizes the regulatory consequences of the behavior of players who leave the field without the authorization of the referee. She thus evokes “the resulting responsibilities and disciplinary sanctions”considering that this aspect was not correctly taken into account in the decision rendered in Lausanne.

According to recent reports, despite the CAS verdict, the Moroccan Federation does not intend to turn the page. She clearly announced her desire to continue the process: “As a result, the Royal Moroccan Football Federation has decided to initiate all necessary legal procedures to challenge the CAS decision. ».

Industry observers note that as a reminder, the Court of Arbitration for Sport annulled the decision of the CAF Appeal Jury, which had awarded a victory on the green carpet to Morocco (3-0). He reinstated the decision of the Disciplinary Jury and confirmed the result obtained on the field, a victory for Senegal (1-0).
