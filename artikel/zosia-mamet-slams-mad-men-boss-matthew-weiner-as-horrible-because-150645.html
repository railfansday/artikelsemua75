Zosia Mamet recently revealed that her “absolutely horrible” experience working with showrunner and producer Matthew Weiner on the set of “Mad Men” caused her to quit the series after just five episodes.

Industry observers note that “All the other actors were wonderful, but he really was like the Catholic Church back in the day, like that bro rules with fear,” she noted while appearing on Dax Shepard’s “Armchair Expert” podcast on Monday. “He would dart every table read with this little speech… and he would talk about how he didn’t want any of us to take our scripts home… And he would be like, ‘If I see that, I’m going to find you, and I’m going to hunt you down. I’m going to kill you. Just kidding. Actually not. He would do this at every table read. And I was like, this not only isn’t funny, but it’s yucky. Industry observers note that mamet, who appeared on Season 4 and 5 of the hit drama as Joyce Ramsay, then recounted the moment that pushed her to quit the show, explaining that “he came in and was having a bad day and decided to set his sights on me.” “During a block rehearsal, [he] just fucking went to town on me in front of the whole cast and crew… I called my agents when I left, and I was like, ‘I can’t go back to that set,’” she told Shepard. “He just systematically dressed me down for like 45 minutes. And I was like, ‘I want to kill myself.’”. (Related: Industry News: ‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok f...)

She continued: “My level of insecurity was so great that I knew that I wouldn’t be able [to]. I was like, I will shut down,” Mamet said. “I won’t be able to function in this environment, and the fear of that happening again was just too great.”.

Sources indicate that the “Girls” actress had previously written about the showrunner in her 2025 essay collection, “Does This Make Me Funny?,” though she didn’t name him at the time. “I wrote an essay about it for my book where I don’t mention names,” Mamet remarked. “But I really wanted Matt Weiner to sue me because then he would be admitting to the fact that he knew I was writing about him.”.

Reps for Weiner did not immediately respond to Variety’s request for comment.
