Turkish outlet Takvim reports that Liverpool are in the running for Fenerbahçe left-back Archie Brown. The 24-year-old is wanted by several Premier League clubs and for good reason - he's homegrown.

Brown made a significant impact against Liverpool this season, of course, as he both scored and assisted in his side's 3-2 win over the Reds. It's something that's catapulted Brown into the spotlight as one to watch, particularly for Premier League sides.

In a recent development, the interest is significant enough that we can't imagine the player moves anywhere where he isn't first choice. He's set to have his pick of clubs, too, as a release clause in his contract guarantees that the fee won't get out of hand.

Sources indicate that that price is €23m, which is arguably a complete steal for a UEFA Champions League-level left-back right now. Especially one who's English.

We expect Brown to be one of Liverpool's most-wanted in 2027 and there's half a chance that they get the deal done in January. After all, that clause won't be around forever - someone is going to trigger it sooner or later. Another club, then, could force the Reds' hand by making a winter offer.

Absolutely, then, this is one to watch.
