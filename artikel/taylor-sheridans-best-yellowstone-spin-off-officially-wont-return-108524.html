Industry observers note that best known for his work writing shows like Yellowstone and Landman, Taylor Sheridan also carved out a name for himself over 10 years ago writing epic feature films like Sicario and Hell or High Water. Just a few weeks ago, news broke that the original Sicario trio — Josh Brolin, Emily Blunt, and Benicio del Toro — were reuniting for a third Sicario film, but there’s a catch.

Steven Knight (Peaky Blinders and the next James Bond film) is writing the new Sicario movie with Padraic McKinley (The Weight) directing. Sheridan is reportedly not involved in the third installment, not even as a producer. He does have a new Sicario-style action movie coming to theaters in 2027, though — we’re talking about F.A.S.T., the new crime thriller starring his 1923 leading man, Brandon Sklenar.
