It has been revealed that easily the most incredible aspect of Grand Theft Auto 6 is that you can pretty much do anything you want inside Leonida's massive open world. This is true from the very start of the game, whether that means just sitting around and having a few drinks watching the sunset on Jason's porch, or going full Top Gun by hijacking a multimillion-dollar military fighter jet from a high-security government base with hundreds of heavily armed guards firing machine guns at your head.

It's really not all that surprising that you can actually hijack your own military jet very early on in the game, and if you're a true Maverick looking to take to the skies, you just need to be fast and know where to go. (Related: Tech Report: Developer builds a ‘Discord kitten’ that’s...)

In a recent development, you can gain access to a military jet as early as Chapter 1 by heading southwest to the farthest Key location on the map, where there's a military base called Mile Zero Air Station. It's highly recommended to take a fast motorcycle down there, though even Jason's Dinka Enduro starting bike will suffice. Follow the main highway as far west as you can until you see the base on your left, where there will be an entrance with several Jersey barriers in the way. (Related: Breaking: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming G...)

Recent findings suggest that you'll want to use your focus ability to "slow down time" as you approach the entrance to the facility, where there will be dozens of guards and armored vehicles, Humvees, etc. As soon as you cross the gate threshold, they will start firing on you, so you'll want to be sure you don't crash or slow down.

It has been revealed that zoom past all of these guards and do your best not to get shot in the head, then veer slightly right once you reach the tarmac. You'll see a row of numerous jets lined up on the runway. Ride up to one, dismount your motorcycle, and hit triangle by the cockpit of any jet to get in the pilot's seat. You'll be able to fly this just like any other plane, which if you haven't done so yet, that means accelerating as normal and holding back on the left joystick.

In a recent development, as you might expect, stealing a jet is a crime in GTA 6, so you're going to alert the authorities almost instantly. They will come after you in armed helicopters and even other jets if you joyride too long, but fortunately you have a jet with missiles and a machine gun, so if you're skilled enough you may actually be able to fight them off. This is also one of the easiest ways to get up to six stars if that's something you're interested in achieving.
