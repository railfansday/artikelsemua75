Cristian Chivu was left to lament several errors that saw them squander two comfortable leads during their entertaining 4-4 draw with Atalanta at Newly introduced Balance Arena on Sunday. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Inter dropped further adrift of Lazio and Roma in Serie A despite twice leading by two goals against an Atalanta side that had won just one of their last five league games.

Francesco Pio Esposito netted twice in the first half, but Atalanta hit back through Eljif Elmas, only for Inter's young Italian striker to complete his hat-trick shortly after the restart.

Charles De Ketelaere netted either side of Petar Sucic's 76th-minute penalty, but Atalanta ensured a share of the spoils with Franck Kessie's stoppage-time spot-kick.

Inter failed to win an away Serie A match despite scoring four goals for only the third time in their history, while it was the first time they conceded four goals in a top-flight match since September 2025 against Juventus.

"We were leading 2-0, 3-1 and 4-2, so this is the regret," Chivu told DAZN Italia, as quoted by Football Italia.

Industry observers note that "We had an idea of what we should've done and did not do; we should've controlled the advantage, then when we felt them snapping at our heels, we immediately restored the two-goal cushion. I think we failed the exam in the final minutes. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

"It's hard to say why we were unable to control the lead. We have to congratulate Atalanta, as they sensed that we were a bit shallow, and pumped crosses into the box, put bodies in there, while we were a little soft compared to our usual approach.

"I'm sorry, I see this as two gifted points, but we will learn from it and know now to let the intensity and mentality drop in the next games.".

The last time Inter scored at least four goals and failed to win was in a 4-4 draw against Juventus in October 2024, but it was the Nerazzurri defence that had Chivu concerned.

Inter have now conceded more than 16 goals in their first 12 Serie A matches of a season for the fifth time in the three-points-per-win era, after 1998-99 (19), 2004-05 (20), 2011-12 (18) and 2022-23 (17).

Kessie's penalty (90:56) is the latest penalty goal Inter have conceded in Serie A since 24 February 2019, when Jordan Veretout scored for Fiorentina (100:07).

"We are conceding too many goals for a club that has high ambitions," Chivu added when asked whether he was more frustrated by dropping points or conceding goals.

"But we must do like we did in Rotterdam and, with Como, respond to a setback, not look over our shoulders to the past.

"We must learn that we cannot just control a situation; we've got to keep giving 100% until the final whistle.".

Inter will have to put the draw quickly behind them as they return to Champions League action on Wednesday, with Stuttgart the visitors to San Siro in a must-win encounter.

The Serie A giants sit 15th in the 36-team standings after beating Feyenoord 3-0 in their last match in the competition.
