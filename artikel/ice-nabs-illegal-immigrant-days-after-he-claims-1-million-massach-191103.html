According to recent reports, federal immigration officers arrested Ariel Antonio Velasquez-Morales in East Boston after a brief foot chase. The arrest came days after he claimed a $1 million Massachusetts State Lottery prize.

A man who smiled for a Massachusetts State Lottery photo with a $1 million check was arrested by federal immigration officers days later after a brief foot chase in East Boston.

Three ICE sources told Fox News that Ariel Antonio Velasquez-Morales, an illegal immigrant from El Salvador, ran when officers moved in to arrest him early Tuesday but was swiftly caught.

Multiple officers’ body cameras captured the encounter, the sources said.
