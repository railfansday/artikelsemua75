Niko Kovac believes Borussia Dortmund deserved their 1-0 victory over Borussia Monchengladbach, though he conceded that Felix Nmecha's 80th-minute winner was fortunate.

Following Bayern Munich's 2-2 draw with Koln on Friday, Kovac's side capitalised to move top of the Bundesliga and open up a two-point lead over their rivals.

Dortmund dominated the early exchanges at Borussia-Park, with Nmecha, Nico Schlotterbeck and Jobe Bellingham going close to breaking the deadlock.

Robin Hack and Joe Scally threatened for Monchengladbach at the other end, but it was Nmecha who ultimately settled the contest in the 80th minute, with his powerful, low 20-yard strike ricocheting off a helpless Daniel Batz and into the roof of the net.

Kovac's side are now unbeaten across their opening 10 Bundesliga matches for just the third time in club history, after 2002-03 and 2018-19.

Following Saturday's win, they have recorded 24 points – just one short of their record haul at this stage, set in 2010-11 and matched again in 2013-14.

Nmecha, meanwhile, has emerged as a pivotal key to Dortmund's success, leading the Black-Yellow for goals this season (five), having now scored in consecutive Bundesliga appearances for the first time.

"We weren't sharp enough in the first half. We gave the ball away too easily," Nmecha conceded.

Industry observers note that "We were better in the second half, and we're happy to have got three points. It's still a very long season.

"We don't want to look at others but simply have to continue the way we're doing now: focusing on ourselves. Hopefully, we'll get as many points as possible.".
