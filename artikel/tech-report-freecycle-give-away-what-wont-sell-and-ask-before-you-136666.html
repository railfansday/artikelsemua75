Before you pay to haul away something that won’t sell, offer it in your town’s Freecycle group, where neighbors pick up usable things for free. Joining costs nothing. Post a wanted request there before you buy, since a neighbor may already have one to give.

According to recent reports, A working treadmill that nobody will buy still costs money to get rid of. LoadUp, a national junk-removal service, lists treadmill pickup from $150.

According to recent reports, on Freecycle, a neighbor who wants it can haul it away for nothing. The Freecycle Network is a nonprofit that counts over 12 million members, and volunteer moderators run its more than 5,000 local town groups. Members post offers of things they’re done with and wanted requests for things they need.

Freecycle’s guidelines require every post to be free and rule out swaps and trading, so something too dated or worn to sell can still go to someone who needs it.

Industry observers note that to give, join your town’s group and post an offer with up to three photos and a description of the item’s condition and how pickup works. Keep your address and phone number out of the post. Freecycle suggests waiting for a handful of replies, or overnight, then picking one person rather than leaving the item out for whoever arrives first. Agree on a time with that person and leave the item on your porch or in the garage, which keeps strangers out of your house. If you’d rather drop things off than arrange a pickup, look for a town swap shop or free store. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

To get things, post a wanted request for the specific item you’d otherwise buy, such as a bookcase for a 30-inch wall. Freecycle’s guidelines also ask members to use wanted posts sparingly, and the limit on them varies by group. A neighborhood Buy Nothing group, another kind of local giveaway group, takes the same request. Answer an offer you want right away with a pickup time, then arrive when you noted you would. Collect the item from a porch or a public place, and bring someone along for any pickup inside a home.
