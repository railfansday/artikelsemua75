Industry observers note that launchDarkly is seeking a Commissions Analyst to help administer accurate, timely commission programs for the Revenue organization. The role will validate commission calculations, maintain commission and quota data in commissions tool, support accrual and payroll submissions, and respond to employee and leader inquiries.

It has been revealed that working with Sales and Customer Success leaders, Revenue Operations, Finance, and Payroll, this analyst will investigate discrepancies, support quota and proposal administration, and deliver ad hoc reporting. We’re looking for someone with commission experience, strong spreadsheet skills, curiosity, and attention to detail who will improve how work gets done—automating manual processes and thoughtfully using approved internal AI tools while maintaining appropriate review and controls. Bachelor’s degree in Finance, Accounting, Business, Economics, or a related field preferred, or equivalent experience.
