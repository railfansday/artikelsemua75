We've been talking GTA 6 all week here at Pure Xbox, but now it's time for the community to take centre stage! We want to know what you think about the newly introduced Grand Theft Auto based on what you've played so far.

Recent findings suggest that it's no secret that GTA 6 has been reviewing better than pretty much any game in existence since its release a few days ago. As things stand, it sits alongside the original Ocarina of Time as the best game ever made on Metacritic!

We helped contribute to that with our "Outstanding" 10/10 review score here at Pure Xbox:.

It has been revealed that I have no doubt that it's a 10/10 in my mind, but is it the joint-best video game of all time? That's a much harder statement to make, and I think it's going to need months and years before we can really evaluate it in that regard.

It should also be pointed out that despite the reviewers heaping praise on it, and the Xbox and PlayStation store ratings being extremely high for GTA 6 right now, the game's user score on Metacritic is surprisingly low - so clearly there are plenty of people who aren't so hot on what they've played and/or seen so far. We don't want you to give a definitive verdict on the game down below, but just let us know your initial thoughts and whether you're satisfied or not with your purchase.

Also tell us what you think of the vibe compared to GTA 4 and GTA 5 - we're very curious about that!
