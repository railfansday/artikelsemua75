2026 hasn't exactly been a banner year for Ridley Scott, who finally returned to the sci-fi genre after almost 10 years away to direct his newly introduced movie, The Dog Stars. Based on the critically acclaimed science fiction novel of the same name by Peter Heller, Scott even tapped Josh Brolin and Jacob Elordi to lead The Dog Stars, but that didn't translate to success at the box office.

In a recent development, the movie has since found success on VOD platforms like Prime Video and Apple TV after leaving theaters with one of the lowest-grossing box office totals of Scott's career. It won't take Scott long to seek redemption at the box office, either; he's returning to the screen in less than one year for a Treasure Island remake starring Hugh Jackman and Owen Cooper from the 2025 Netflix sensation, Adolescence. Ridley Scott is on something of a cold streak right now after his last few movies, The Dog Stars, Gladiator II, and Napoleon all failed to dazzle at the box office, but there was a time when seeing his name on a poster meant a movie was a guaranteed hit. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

Sources indicate that back in 2015, Scott teamed up with Matt Damon for The Martian, which is an adaptation of the best-selling sci-fi book of the same name from author Andy Weir, who also wrote the book that inspired Ryan Gosling's Project Hail Mary. The Martian is currently without a streaming home in America right now, but the demand to watch the movie at home is higher than ever. So many fans are renting or purchasing The Martian on Apple TV that it has now been in the top 10 for over 100 days now.

Industry observers note that the Martian and Project Hail Mary are quite similar, which makes sense considering both books were written by the same author, but there are some important differences. Both tales center around an astronaut who is lost millions of miles from home, but while The Martian follows Mark Watney's journey to make it back to Earth with his life, Project Hail Mary follows Ryland Grace as he attempts to save humanity from a dangerous race of space algae known as Astrophage. Project Hail Mary also features an alien named Rocky, and the film is considered a much harder sci-fi than its 2015 predecessor.

Check out The Martian on VOD platforms like Prime Video and Apple TV, and stay tuned to Collider for more updates and coverage of Matt Damon and Ridley Scott's future projects.
