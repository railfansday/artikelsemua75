Recent findings suggest that grand Theft Auto 6 is full of things to see and questionable decisions to make, but you'll want to make sure you're properly armed before getting too carried away in Leonida. If you're looking to expand your weapon collection, getting your hands on a sniper rifle is a pretty good place to start.

It has been revealed that the Shrewsbury SM-69 is a handy addition to your arsenal, as it has impressive range and accuracy. You won't need to spend a fortune to add this weapon to your collection. I'll detail just exactly where to find it, below. (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

Sources indicate that to find the Shrewsbury SM-69, head to the tall concrete watchtower near the starting location of GTA 6. You can find it to the northwest of Nifty Threads Island Wear and just to the east of Pay 'n' Spray - Key Lento.

Recent findings suggest that once you reach the watchtower, you'll need to hop over the fence to access the area. I did not encounter any guards or enemies here, so you should be able to walk straight inside without any trouble. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Sources indicate that once inside the concrete building, climb the stairs and continue up the two sets of ladders. Here, you'll find the Shrewsbury SM-69 Sniper Rifle waiting for you to collect. The weapon has 6.8 damage, 8.6 accuracy, 2.0 handling, and 8.9 range, which makes it a useful addition to your weapon collection.

Make sure you add the Shrewsbury SM-69 to your car loadout (in the trunk) after picking it up so you can save the weapon and keep it available. You can also find some additional loot on the railing, including Cigarettes and Zombix.

If you continue climbing all the way to the top of the watchtower, you'll find a parachute waiting for you. It's worth making the extra climb if you want to grab another useful item before heading back down.
