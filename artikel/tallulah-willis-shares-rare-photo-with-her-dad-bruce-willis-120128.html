Tallulah Willis is sharing a special moment with her dad, Bruce Willis.

Industry observers note that on Saturday, November 21, Tallulah, 32, offered a rare glimpse at the Die Hard actor, 71, posting an Instagram photo of the two sitting together on a porch. Bruce, dressed in a gray cardigan and salmon-colored shirt, holds Tallulah’s hand close to his heart while she smiles and wraps her other arm around his shoulders. Tallulah captioned the photo with two emojis, writing, “🆒😎.”.

Bruce’s Gasoline Alley costar Devon Sawa sent his love to the actor in the Instagram comment section while many relished the revision on the legendary action star.

It has been revealed that “Give him a hug from us,” one fan wrote, while another added, “So good to see Bruce. Love you guys.”.

Bruce retreated from public life in 2022 when his wife, Emma Heming Willis, and his family confirmed he’d been diagnosed with aphasia. It was later reported that Bruce was also diagnosed with frontotemporary dementia.

Sources indicate that “To Bruce’s amazing supporters, as a family we wanted to share that our beloved Bruce has been experiencing some health issues and has recently been diagnosed with aphasia, which is impacting his cognitive abilities,” they announced in March 2022. “As a result of this and with much consideration Bruce is stepping away from the career that has meant so much to him.”.

Emma, 50, subsequently disclosed during the 2025 documentary Emma and Bruce Willis: The Unexpected Journey – A Diane Sawyer Special that Bruce had been moved into a secondary home where he can receive round-the-clock care. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

“It was one of the hardest decisions that I’ve had to make so far,” Heming Willis stated. “But I knew, first and foremost, Bruce would want that for our daughters. You know, he would want them to be in a home that was more tailored to their needs, not his needs.”.

Bruce shares daughters Rumer, 38, Scout, 35, and Tallulah with ex-wife Demi Moore as well as daughters Mabel, 14, and Evelyn, 12 with Emma.

Tallulah has occasionally posted photos of Bruce since he left the public eye, including revealing that they shared a private moment prior to her August wedding to husband Justin Acee.

In June 2025, the Whole Ten Yards actress addressed criticism from one Instagram follower about whether it was appropriate to share photos of Bruce given his health complications.

In a recent development, “I don’t think you should expose your dad to the public! He is vulnerable,” an Instagram user complained. “And some things should be kept private! You don’t have his permission to post it!”.

Tallulah offered some clarity, explaining, “Hi. I hear that. As a family we use our discretion when posting. Today was a great day filled with smiles. I made the judgment call to show that to the world, because I know what he means to everyone.”.
