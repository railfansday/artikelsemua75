A new idea has emerged for AC Milan to strengthen their attack, and it is a player that incoming Head of Football Markus Krösche knows well.

According to recent reports, this morning’s edition of Tuttosport writes that the two dreams for January are Nicolò Tresoldi and Endrick, but a third option is starting to emerge at Milan. The player is equally effective in terms of goals, but perhaps less media-burdened than Gonçalo Ramos.

Sources indicate that the Portuguese striker has just three goals so far for Milan since joining for a club-record €73m fee, and his current drought is now over one month. This is why finding a viable alternative in the winter is key, but perhaps without spending another €60-70m on another forward.

It will be very hard to snatch Tresoldi from Club Brugge during the winter transfer window, as Arsenal and Atletico Madrid are also strongly interested in him. Meanwhile, Endrick’s departure from Real Madrid is currently being vetoed by Jose Mourinho. So, who could the Rossoneri turn to?

Milan could look towards the Bundesliga, more specifically Stuttgart’s Ermedin Demirovic. He is a name highly regarded by Milan’s new Head of Football – Markus Krösche – who has watched him up close for several years.

It has been revealed that the Bosnian striker’s contract expires in 2028, and there’s currently no agreement to extend it. Therefore, his price tag – despite an  excellent start to the season – isn’t likely to skyrocket and could become affordable.

It has been revealed that he’s already scored nine goals across all competitions: five in nine appearances in the league, and four in four in the Champions League. That seems to provide enough evidence that the Bosnia international is ready for a top club.
