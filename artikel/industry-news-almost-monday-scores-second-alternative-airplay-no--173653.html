Almost Monday earns its second No. 1 hit on Billboard’s Alternative Airplay chart (dated Nov. 28) with “No More Regrets.”.

Recent findings suggest that the song, released May 1 via Hollywood Records, rises two places to the summit thanks to a 5% increase in spins at the format Nov. 13-19, according to Luminate. (The Alternative Airplay chart measures songs’ weekly plays, as tabulated by Mediabase and provided to Billboard by Luminate, on 60 U.S. alternative radio stations.). It has been revealed that “No More Regrets” is Almost Monday’s eighth career Alternative Airplay entry. The group first made the ranking in October 2020 with “Broken People,” which reached No. 26. Earlier this year, “Lost” hit No. 18.

Sources indicate that the coronation is also a milestone for Hollywood Records, which earns its third Alternative Airplay No. 1. The label first topped the chart in 1998 with Fastball’s “The Way” and returned with “Can’t Slow Down.” In between, it reached the top 10 on hits by Limp Bizkit (“Take a Look Around”); Breaking Benjamin (“So Cold,” “Sooner or Later,” “The Diary of Jane,” “Breath,” “I Will Not Bow,” “Give Me a Sign [Forever and Ever]”); Atreyu (“Falling Down”); and Little Image (“Out of My Mind”).

Meanwhile, of the 15 songs that have ruled Alternative Airplay in 2026, “No More Regrets” is the fifth released on an independent label, following The Paradox’s “Get the Message” (Hundred Days); Yellowcard’s “Bedroom Posters,” featuring Good Charlotte (Better Noise); Young the Giant’s “Different Kind of Love” (Concord); and Temper City’s “Self Aware” (Thirty Knots). There were eight indie No. 1s on the chart in 2025 and three in 2024.

“No More Regrets” is on Almost Monday’s second studio album, Thank God It’s Almost Monday, released Sept. 9.
