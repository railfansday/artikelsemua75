The launch added a listed instrument for hedging the AI company’s shares or taking speculative positions following its initial public offering.

The early activity placed Anthropic among the 25 most heavily traded names in the options market, according to Mercury Alerts, the options analytics service that supplied the figures. Volume was roughly evenly split between calls, which give holders the right to buy shares at a fixed price, and puts, which give holders the right to sell at a fixed price.
