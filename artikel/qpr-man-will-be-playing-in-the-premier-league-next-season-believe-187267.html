QPR swooped to sign the stopper on loan from Manchester City in the summer. He has since become the Hoops’ number one and has made a positive impression with the London outfit in the Championship.

Charles, 21, has made 17 appearances for the Hoops in all competitions to date this season, 16 of which have come in the league, and he has kept four clean sheets. The R’s are right now in the play-offs along with Wolves, Birmingham City, West Brom, Southampton and Swansea City, with the likes of West Ham and Sheffield United chasing them down.

In a recent development, maxwell has delivered his verdict on Charles on the Not The Top 20 Podcast on YouTube: “In terms of a goalkeeper’s passing range and ability, I’ve quite simply never seen anything like this with Charles. Recent findings suggest that he added: “We really need to enjoy him while we can, I don’t think he’ll be playing in the Championship after this season. Player like this are genuinely the reason why you fall in love with the game.”.

According to recent reports, QPR’s decision to bring in Charles in the last transfer window has worked out well for them. He has settled into life with the Hoops well and has become their first choice.

In a recent development, the Northern Ireland international, who has 18 caps to his name, was on the books at Sheffield Wednesday from 2021 to 2026. He went on to play 36 matches for the Owls, 21 of which came in the last campaign.

Charles was snapped up by Manchester City on a long-term deal in July. He was then given the green light to head out the exit door on a temporary basis to join the R’s.

City will be pleased with his development under Julien Stephan. He has enjoyed plenty of game time at QPR and is getting plenty of valuable experience under his belt.

Sources indicate that QPR should savour him while they can this term as they eye promotion to the top flight. Manchester City will have a substantial decision to make on what to do with him when he returns to the Etihad Stadium.

If the Hoops go up, they will fancy their chances of re-signing him again. If they stay in the EFL though, it seems unlikely he would come back for a second spell.

Stephan’s side won 1-0 away at Millwall last time out with Nicolas Madsen getting their winner against the Lions at The Den in the first-half. They are back in action on Tuesday with an away trip to Norwich City.
