AI agents are hunting down forgotten subscriptions — and saving people money

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (109 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that nashville resident Scott Burka had been paying for his kids' iPad subscriptions and chess accounts without noticing. Muse, an AI agent Meta $META released in September to handle digital chores, found the charges. Burka told the agent he wasn't using any of them, "and it actually helped me cancel approximately $100 worth of expenses a month that were completely just under the radar," he told NPR. That's about $1,200 a year Burka had been spending without realizing it, or nearly two-thirds of what an average American spent on their entire subscription bill last year, according to Mastercard $MA and FT Strategies.
