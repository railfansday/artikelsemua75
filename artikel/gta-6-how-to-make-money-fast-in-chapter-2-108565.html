📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Once you hit Chapter 2 in Grand Theft Auto 6, you'll be extremely low on funds thanks to the final events of Chapter 1. Jason is not feeling very well and will be stuck in bed at the motel for the majority of the chapter, so it's up to Lucia to scrounge around for some money and get them back on their feet.

To make the situation worse, Linda the motel manager will be breathing down her neck for rent payments, and by default, the couple only has a little over $100 cash on them when Chapter 2 starts.

If you've made it to Chapter 2 and are wondering where all your money went, it's by design that Lucia and Jason are more or less broke after Jason's place was destroyed in the fire. Unfortunately, there's no way of recovering the money you deposited in the floor throughout Chapter 1, but that doesn't mean you shouldn't go back and check. The "Rainy Day Funds" from Chapter 1 will still be available at Jason's house in the rubble, despite your other cash being destroyed. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

Industry observers note that that remarked, getting back to Jason's house is a little more complicated than it might seem, largely because it's on the complete other side of the map and will be swarming with police, so you can't really just waltz in through the front entrance like it's no massive deal. Fortunately, there's a pretty easy way to get there and to get in undetected to recover the Rainy Day Fund, and you should even be able to do so before Linda tries to shake down Lucia for her beloved necklace. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

To get back to Jason's house without being detected, it's actually easiest to just take a plane and fly all the way down and parachuting in from above. Before you do anything else in Chapter 1, you'll see a little flyer on the wall going into the office at the motel. Read this, and you'll get directions to McKittrick Farm, which will kick off a quest where you can crop-dust for your buddy Kyle.

Sources indicate that for now, you can ignore this quest and just instead take one of the planes at the farm other than Kyle's, but make sure you have a parachute first otherwise this isn't going to end well. Fly south until you're over Jason's house (it's a long trip) and bail on the plane, aiming your parachute for the water behind the house and to the left of the dock. Note that there are two policemen standing at the dock, so give yourself some room to swim in undetected.

It has been revealed that you can sneak in from the dock itself or via the ramp in Jason's backyard, just be careful for cops as you make your way up the stairs and back to Jason's room, which is inside the destroyed house. You have to be undetected to claim the money, so be careful not to alert any officers in the process. While the money you deposited there in Chapter 1 will be gone, you can now withdraw the $5000 rainy day fund and retrieve the priceless photos as well.

You can also check in on Jason's pet snake, Delores, who is under the stairs outside with a nice little surprise.

According to recent reports, all that's left to do from there is to escape the property undetected again, deposit the cash into an ATM, and you should be able to afford a RydeMe ride back to the motel now that you have some money in your pocket. While the flight itself takes a little while, this is a relatively quick and easy way to make $5000 before completing any main missions in GTA 6, and one of the few ways you'll be able to pay rent at $900 before Linda jacks up the price.
