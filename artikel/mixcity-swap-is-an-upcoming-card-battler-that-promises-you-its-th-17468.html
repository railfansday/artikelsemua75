Whenever I mention that I write about mobile gaming, the inevitable first response from laymen and industry people alike is, 'Well, that's all just gacha, right?' And on the one hand, they're not necessarily right, but not wrong either. The random draw system is so pervasive that even many developers pit themselves against it, like with the upcoming Mixcity Swap.

Positioning itself as the anti-gacha, Mixcity Swap is an upcoming sports-themed card battler by Atypique Studio, releasing in 2027 for iOS and Android, that sees you taking on fast-paced, 10-minute matches. With only nine cards on the roster, the gameplay revolves around swapping in and out your athletes across different modes, doing so at the right interval as they activate their individual effects.

Sources indicate that in that way, I think positing it as anti gacha is a bit of a misnomer. At its core, Mixcity Swap looks to be an exciting standalone card game, and one that doesn't really need to pit itself against such a widely reviled mechanic to be successful.
