News on Wednesday of a new £375million cash injection targeted to help fund new Newcastle United stadium.

This comes with NUFC fans still waiting to hear definitive official confirmation of what is going to happen regarding stadium prepares.

Earlier today The Athletic revealed that the Saudi Arabia PIF are willing to sell 25 per cent of the club, from their 84 percent majority ownership of Newcastle United.

Now The Mail report that their inside sources have told them that ‘This is not a case of Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund looking to sell the club, but rather by creating new shares…’.

They say that the club’s owners are now getting close to confirming their plans for a fresh Newcastle United stadium AND that today’s exclusive from The Athletic ties in with that.

In a recent development, the Mail stating that the sale of up to a quarter of the total shares in NUFC by Saudi Arabia PIF ‘Would be to inject new equity to help fund infrastructural developments, including a stadium. With Newcastle said to be valued at £1.5billion, this could equate to a £375million cash injection. PIF would retain their majority ownership…As one source said: ‘This is not about taking cash off the table, it is about funding growth.’.

In a recent development, JP Morgan have now been appointed as advisers in selling part of the Saudi Arabia PIF stake in the club, with The Mail saying that fellow Wall Street firm are also involved, with their role ‘…set to be to provide a loan or other investment to help finance the stadium. As we reported last week, the cost of a newly introduced home would not be exclusively funded by PIF and co-owner Jamie Reuben, with loans, share issues and investments all part of the package. The expectation is that a new stadium, rather than a St James’ renovation, will soon be recommended to the club’s owners by the executive team. It will then be up to PIF to sign off on the aims and for a public announcement to be prepared.’.
