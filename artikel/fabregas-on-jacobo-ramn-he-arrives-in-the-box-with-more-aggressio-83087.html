Cesc Fabregas praised Jacobo Ramón’s attacking threat after the defender scored in Como’s 2-0 win over Cagliari, although he insisted the performance was far from perfect.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, Ramón headed Como in front from Yan Couto’s assist in the 24th minute, and Lucas Da Cunha added a second, also set up by Couto, early in the second half. The win takes Fabregas’ side up to fourth for now, and Como remain unbeaten at the Sinigaglia in Serie A this season. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

“Perfect? No. We conceded four counter-attacks, three of them big chances where they could have scored,” Fabregas noted. “They did something different going forward and found space in behind us, but from around the 35th minute things changed, and in the second half there was only one team on the pitch.

“I’m very happy for the lads. There was so much energy, and Lahdo came on very well. We had to finish with two right-backs because we don’t have the players. Today my only attacking option on the bench was Nico Paz.”. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

Ramón has just been called up by Spain for the first time, and Fabregas believes his scoring has improved a lot.

“There are players who are stronger, but he’s a player I like. I won’t say too much about how I feel about him, because he might read it later,” Fabregas joked. “We worked a lot on his finishing. Last year he couldn’t score in front of goal, even easy chances. Now he scores them. It shows you they’re kids, but they improve every year.

Recent findings suggest that “He arrives in the box with more aggression than a striker, even when you don’t expect it, and he can play long and short. In the air, he’s first or second in Serie A for duels won. But if you want to reach the top, you have to handle Thuram and Esposito, so the lads understand they always have to do more to get to that level.

“In five or six years, I’d like to see all these lads playing at World Cups and winning the Champions League. They have the chance to grow here.”.

Da Cunha started despite his efforts with France this week, because Maxence Caqueret picked up a minor problem on Friday.

“Honestly, I wanted to rest him today, but we can’t take risks now, we’re down to 11, 12, 13 players. He’s a gem for us. He always raises the level. I’d like every player in the world to have Da Cunha’s mentality.”.

He also warned there’s no room to ease off.

“There are seven games left before Christmas, and everyone has to play their part.”.
