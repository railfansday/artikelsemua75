Miley Cyrus has married her partner of four years Maxx Morando during a ceremony in Palm Springs, California.

Her mother, Trisha Cyrus, and sister, Noah Cyrus, could be seen in the background of one image. Her father Billy Ray Cyrus was not in the picture but was at the ceremony with his partner, actress Liz Hurley. (Related: Training Design for Text-to-Image Models: Lessons from ...)

Grammy-winning Cyrus, 33, wore a white silk dress designed by Anthony Vaccarello for Saint Laurent, while 28-year-old drummer Morando stated "I do" in a black suit from the same fashion house.

In one of the photographs, which was the first in a series of images shared by the singer in an Instagram post, the newly weds posed next to a golden retriever.

The post was captioned with "M+M" followed by a white heart and the ceremony date, 21 November.

Miley's sister Brandi Cyrus, singer Doechii and fashion designer Donatella Versace were among those to congratulate the couple in the comments.

The ceremony reportedly took place at a resort formerly owned by Fly Me To The Moon singer Frank Sinatra.

Guests stuck to an all-black dress code, according to reports.

Miley Cyrus confirms engagement to musician Maxx Morando.

The American singer confirmed her engagement to the 28-year-old drummer during an interview with US TV show Good Morning America last year.

She noted Morando had got down on one knee for the proposal during a trip to Asia.

"I am not easy to surprise because I love to control every situation and I had completely surrendered and I'm telling you, I was so, so, so surprised," she mentioned.

During an interview with Wonderland, Morando said he "had to be really sneaky" to keep his proposal plans a secret.

According to recent reports, "We went to Tokyo first for a couple days, and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day to throw her off the scent as much as I could," he noted.

Cyrus, 33, had previously been married to Australian actor Liam Hemsworth but the couple separated in 2019.

The singer, whose hits include Party in the U.S.A and Wrecking Ball, married Hunger Games star Hemsworth in 2018 after the couple were in a relationship for a decade.

Speaking to British Vogue in 2023, Cyrus stated she and 28-year-old Morando met after being "put on a blind date".

Sources indicate that morando is a drummer with LA-based band Liily. The band participated in NBC's Miley's Newly introduced Year's Eve Party - hosted by Cyrus - in 2022, alongside musicians including Cyrus's late godmother Dolly Parton and David Byrne.
