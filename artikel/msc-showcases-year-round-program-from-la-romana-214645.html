MSC Cruises has introduced its newly introduced year-round program from La Romana, Dominican Republic.

Departing weekly from MSC Cruises’ upcoming year-round homeport in La Romana, the program features a variety of Southern Caribbean destinations on a seven-night sailing or an extended 14-night back-to-back itinerary.

In a recent development, the itineraries include Martinique, Antigua and Barbuda and the British Virgin Islands. Highlights include Fort-de-France, Martinique and Virgin Gorda’s Baths.

MSC added that each departure includes a call at Catalina Sugar Beach, where guests can enjoy the newly enhanced beach destination.

Highlights include private cabanas, beachfront dining and tropical refreshments, as well as exploring the surrounding waters on a snorkeling or catamaran excursion.

According to the press release, MSC Cruises’ Stay & Cruise options give guests more time in the Caribbean, combining their sailing with a hotel stay in the Dominican Republic before or after their cruise.
