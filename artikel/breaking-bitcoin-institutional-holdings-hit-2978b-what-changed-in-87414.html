Breaking: Bitcoin institutional holdings hit $29.78B – What changed in Q3?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Things have changed dramatically since the third quarter of 2026, when institutional interest in Bitcoin [BTC] increased. After three quarters of net selling, institutional investors resumed net purchases of spot Bitcoin exchange-traded products (ETPs).

According to Alex Thorn from Galaxy Research, institutions added approximately 49,229 BTC-equivalent during the three-month period. Notably, by the 30th of September, institutional investment managers held a record 356,491 BTC-equivalent, worth approximately $29.78 billion.

The growing role of financial advisors and wealth management platforms played a major role.

Together, they held 144,307 BTC-equivalent by the end of September, representing 40.5% of all Bitcoin ETP holdings reported by institutional managers. If looked back, their holdings have increased for nine consecutive quarters and are now 3.3 times higher than their March 2024 levels.

That being said, large banks played a major role in this accumulation, with Wells Fargo, Bank of America, and Morgan Stanley being among the biggest buyers during Q3.

In a recent development, wells Fargo alone added 14,636 BTC-equivalent, taking its total holdings to 30,553 BTC-equivalent, making it the largest institutional holder of U.S. spot Bitcoin ETPs, overtaking Jane Street. (Related: Latest Update: Umamusume horse girls are taking over a ...)

Recent findings suggest that institutional ownership also shifted, with hedge funds dropping from 50.6% in December 2024 to 19%, while financial advisors and wealth platforms increased their exposure. That said, Bitcoin ETP holdings fell by 38,531 BTC between the 30th of September and the 16th of November.

However, the tokenized ETP sector continued to struggle, with its market capitalization falling 6.2% to $595 million and trading volume declining to $160 million, as previously reported by AMBCrypto.

Recent findings suggest that this comes as Bitcoin spot ETFs recorded net inflows of $2.65 billion in September 2026 and $3.52 billion in August, while July saw smaller inflows of $172.43 million. However, the trend reversed in October, with $515.03 million in net outflows, followed by significantly larger outflows of $3.47 billion in November 2026.
