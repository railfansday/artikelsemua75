NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Amazon (AMZN -4.10%), a global e-commerce, cloud, and digital advertising platform, closed at $246.82, down 4.10%. Shares fell on Friday amid an AI and mega-cap tech sell-off, while investors are watching capital spending, margins, and AWS growth.Trading volume reached 72.1M shares, coming in about 79% above its three-month average of 40.2M shares. Amazon.com IPO'd in 1997 and has grown 251,971% since going public.

Sources indicate that S&P 500 (^GSPC -3.70%) closed at 6,910, down 3.7%, while the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -5.28%) finished at 22,617, down 5.28%. Among broadline retail and cloud computing peers, Walmart (WMT +1.51%) closed at $127.95, up 1.52%, while eBay (EBAY -1.39%) closed at $115.59, down 1.39%; Walmart's stronger holiday outlook highlighted steadier consumer demand than Amazon.com's pressure points.

Negative sentiment from both its e-commerce and AWS segments seemed to weigh on Amazon stock today.

Soft consumer spending and an increased need for promotional discounting led it to underperform retail counterparts like Walmart, which, as previously mentioned, rose in today's trading session. (Related: One Familiar Item Of Clothing Scored Lowest for Intelli...)

Moreover, capital expenditures (capex) continue to weigh on the stock. The company's initiative to spend $220 billion has led to free cash outflows, a concerning sign for a company that generated tens of billions in annual free cash flow until recently. Furthermore, despite a massive amount of liquidity, it has had to turn to the debt markets in a rising-rate environment.

Sources indicate that such concerns were not unique to Amazon, and every stock in the Magnificent Seven, which contend with many of the same challenges, dropped today. Additionally, the Anthropic (ANT -8.50%) IPO has put AI in the spotlight this week. That stock has trended down since opening at $111.50 per share yesterday, likely adding to the negative sentiment surrounding AI.
