A Florida judge has sentenced a prominent plastic surgeon to death for the murder of a “beloved” lawyer who went missing in 2023, saying the doctor committed the crime in a “cold, calculated and premeditated manner.”.

Sources indicate that pinellas County Circuit Judge Joseph A. Bulone handed down the death sentence Friday to Dr. Tomasz Kosowski of Florida, who was convicted in September of murdering attorney Steven Cozzi, 41, whom he faced in a contentious lawsuit, Law360 reports.
