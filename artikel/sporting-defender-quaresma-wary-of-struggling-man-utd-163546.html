Sporting CP defender Eduardo Quaresma insisted they will not underestimate Manchester United ahead of their Champions League meeting.

United travel to Portugal to face Sporting on Wednesday, but the Red Devils are in dire form ahead of their trip to Estadio Jose Alvalade.

Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge ended in a 4-0 defeat to rivals Liverpool on Sunday, with Leny Yoro sent off after just eight minutes.

Accounting for three points per win all-time, United's eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since 1972-73 (seven), with the Red Devils sat in 19th.

Meanwhile, in the Champions League, United are winless in their last six away games (D2 L4), losing both this season. They last had a longer winless run away from home in the competition between February 2004 and December 2005 (eight matches – D3 L5).

Sporting are three points ahead of United in the league phase, but Quaresma acknowledged the threat their counterparts still pose.

"I think it's going to be a very difficult game because we're playing against a great club, even though they're not at their best," Quaresma told UEFA, as quoted by A Bola.
