It has been revealed that A couple of years ago, reports emerged that Challengers and Call Me By Your Name director Luca Guadagnino was working on his own reimagining of American Psycho, the 2000 film adaptation of Bret Easton Ellis' novel. Unfortunately (or fortunately, depending on your point of view), it doesn't appear his fresh take on the cult-hit psychological thriller will see the light of day.

It has been revealed that luca Guadagnino was first linked to a newly introduced American Psycho adaptation written by Contagion's Scott Z. Burns in October 2024. We had not heard much more about the project since then, so Deadline asked Guadagnino about it during the director's recent appearance on its Crew Call podcast. Sadly, Guadagnino did not have a positive enhancement on the project. (Related: Large Language Models: A Newly introduced Moore's Law?...)

“Scott Z. Burns wrote a fantastic script. Unfortunately for me, as we speak, it’s not happening," Guadagnino succinctly explained.

It has been revealed that american Psycho is a horror novel originally released in 1991, but it's mostly remembered for Mary Harron's 2000 film adaptation starring Christian Bale. The movie, which explores themes of toxic masculinity and materialism, was a subversive horror comedy for its time. Despite its very grim subject matter, Bale's over-the-top performance and key scenes from the movie have nestled their way into pop culture and become memes in the 26 years since the original's release. Even official accounts for kid-friendly franchises like Pokémon have referenced it.

Recent findings suggest that any newly introduced adaptation will have lofty expectations to live up to and will likely have to face the reality that many of the things the book lampoons have only gotten worse in the decades since its original release.
