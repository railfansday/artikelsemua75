📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Josh Silverman, President of Global Products & Publishing for Skydance, has set the division’s senior leadership team and confirmed the departure of one high-level executive.

Since the close of the $110 billion merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery last month, which created the latest iteration of Skydance, senior execs across the company have declared executive teams and structures.

According to recent reports, in a memo to employees obtained by Deadline, Silverman noted Disney and Lucasfilm vet Paul Southern, EVP, Consumer Products, will oversee global consumer products business and retail operations. Amy Kaplan Jarashow, SVP, Global Publishing, will oversee our publishing business, including our publishing imprint Skydance Publishing. She had previously held posts at Penguin Random House and Lego. Jim Lee, President, Publisher and Chief Creative Officer, DC, and Anne DePies, EVP and General Manager, DC, will continue to co-lead DC Comics, Silverman remarked. Watch on Deadline.

It has been revealed that mark Kaufman, EVP & Chief Content Officer, Theatre Ventures, will oversee live stage productions across the Skydance portfolio. In 30 years split between Warner Bros. and New Line Cinema, Kaufman has helped develop and produce acclaimed stage productions like Hairspray and The Lost Boys.

Sources indicate that luis Fernández, EVP, Creative, will lead the global creative vision, strategy and product development. He has more than two decades of experience at Disney and spent five years as head of consumer products at Skydance Media.

Melissa Lasdon, SVP, Brand Management & Marketing, will oversee the development of global, long-range, insight-driven brand strategies. At Disney, Lasdon held a number of leadership posts. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

Kedar Nadkarni, EVP, CFO & Head of Strategy and Insights, will lead finance for Skydance’s global businesses while also overseeing strategy, operations and insights. The veteran of Disney Consumer Products, The Wall Street Journal, and Deloitte Consulting will report to Silverman and company CFO Dennis Cinelli.

It has been revealed that as part of the organizational moves, Warner Bros. and WBD vet Robert Oberschelp will be leaving the manufacturer, Silverman noted.
