Atletico Madrid boss Diego Simeone quipped, "You don't win 345 matches defending" after he broke the record for the most wins as a manager in LaLiga history.

Simeone levelled Luis Aragones' record on October 31, but after a loss to Barcelona before the international break, he had to wait for his record-breaking victory.

And he got it on Sunday, with Atletico brushing aside Getafe 3-0 with a confident performance.

Alex Baena and Johnny Cardoso put Atletico 2-0 up by the half-hour mark, and Alejandro Grimaldo added some late gloss from the spot with a stoppage-time penalty.

It means that Simeone has now won 345 matches in LaLiga, officially surpassing Aragones in the standings, and doing so in 189 fewer games.

Simeone reached that total in 567 matches (also D123 L99), while Aragones tallied 344 wins in 756 outings.
