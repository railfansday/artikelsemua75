Sources indicate that crypto’s relief rally is separating winners from laggards. Ethena’s ENA and Hyperliquid’s HYPE are two crypto tickers attracting stronger buying, while Zcash is struggling to keep pace. Money appears willing to take risks again, but they aren’t buying every dip randomly.

According to recent reports, coinGecko shows HYPE up 5.7% over 24 hours and ENA up 5.8%. ZEC gained just 1.1%, leaving it well behind the pair. ENA has the strongest one-day move, HYPE combines a strong bounce with a smaller weekly loss, and ZEC is struggling to attract the same buying pressure. It has been revealed that HYPE’s resilience stands out. The token has lost less ground over the past week than ENA and ZEC, while today’s rally puts it back above $63. The $60 area remains an important level to watch after traders recently looked toward it as potential support.

According to recent reports, crypto Twitter account Ansem has also flagged November 23–24 as a potential local-bottom window for HYPE and ZEC, expecting a rebound afterward. His call puts the next couple of sessions in focus, although HYPE’s price still needs to prove buyers can sustain the recovery.

ENA is making the stronger daily move, but its weekly damage remains heavier. The token is down 12.5% over seven days, despite climbing nearly 6% today. This leaves ENA vulnerable to profit-taking if buyers fail to push the rebound further.

Industry observers note that ethena has some underlying developments traders may revisit. The protocol recently expanded USDe and sUSDe onto Stellar, extending its stablecoin ecosystem to another network. Ethena has also approved a programmatic ENA buyback framework tied to USDe supply milestones. Neither development is a confirmed explanation for today’s price move, but they give the token a story beyond speculative trading.
