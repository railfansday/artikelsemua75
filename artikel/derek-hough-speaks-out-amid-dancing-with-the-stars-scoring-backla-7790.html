Derek Hough Speaks Out Amid ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Scoring Backlash and Pros Accusing Him of “Phoning It In”

Derek Hough has seen Ezra Sosa’s viral TikTok livestream and has something to say! You know, the one where he admits to being so tired he just gave everyone nines? As he put it: “Listen, I’ve had three hours of sleep. I came from Seattle this morning on tour. I’m going to Florida right after this Disney special, and then back on tour…That’s why I think I’m giving nines to everybody today.”.

Industry observers note that the former pro and current judge defended himself after the semifinals, telling Us Weekly, “This season, there’s a lot going on. There’s the whole thing about me [giving out] the nines. The reason why that doesn’t bother me at all is because I think anybody that knows me, if you’ve been to one of my indicates, if you see me do what I do, if you see me host, if you see anything you see me do, it’s one thing I’ve always been. I consistently show up.” He added that he brings “110 percent energy” to what he does, which, fair enough. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

However, a source tells The Sun that the pros are unhappy with Derek’s judging, saying, “There’s a lot of frustration from the pros towards the judges this season but surprisingly, it seems most of the animosity is aimed at Derek specifically. He keeps praising the contestants while giving lower scores. He offers little to no constructive feedback and the pros feel it’s impossible to please him since he isn’t offering any critique.”.

It has been revealed that they add that “he just seems like he’s in more of a bad mood on set this season. It’s rubbing the cast the wrong way and they feel he just doesn’t want to be on set this year. It’s a strange shift from past seasons where he’s usually the most energetic on set. At least with Carrie Ann and Bruno, they justify their lower scores with feedback. Derek is just phoning it in.”.
