Rockstar Games has been accused of tracing a furry artist’s illustration for a decal on a custom submachine gun players can earn in GTA 6.

The gun comes from a random encounter with Chase, a fox-suited character cornered by police. Players can break through the blockade, lose the cops, and help Chase and another furry escape to the Furverts’ compound. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

Chase offers the custom SMG as an alternative to cash for helping them escape. The encounter had already drawn comparisons to a real-life fursuit after players noticed shooting-star markings on Chase’s knees that resemble those on FoxiFelix’s suit. (Related: Developer vibe codes Windows 11 alternative operating s...)

It has been revealed that on November 21, X user Glacius shared what they described as artwork pulled from GTA 6’s game files, including a view of the graphics used on the custom SMG. In a follow-up linking to furry artist GabyT’s work, Glacius asked, “IT WAS TRACED????”.

Rockstar definitely had an insider for GTA 6. Datamined furry artwork! pic.twitter.com/MjXvUz1PCq.

In a recent development, gabyT posted the illustration on December 8, 2021, nearly five years before GTA 6’s release. The portrait and the weapon decal both show a character with its head turned to the side, a half-lidded eye, and white fur flaring out around a dark muzzle. The version on the gun uses muddier colors and highlights the character holding a bone in its mouth.

Sockz the Puppet posted a video overlaying the two images, then shared a revised comparison after lining them up on a computer. “I hope the og artist was aware and gave permission,” they wrote.

bro what is this :/ I hope rockstar paid for this? https://t.co/kh35xMfHU2 pic.twitter.com/fIfvVX9vSM.

The replies were split over how closely the drawings matched. X user Eli Bunny stated the decal looked traced and wanted to know whether Rockstar worked with GabyT. Another user, Stick, said the images “barely line up” and described the overlap as “minor similarities.”.

It has been revealed that in a reply discussing how the artwork could have made it into the game, Sockz argued that the publisher was responsible for what it shipped. “When you publish something, it becomes your responsibility,” they wrote.

It remains unclear whether Rockstar contacted GabyT or had permission to use the illustration. Rockstar has yet to publicly address the tracing allegation.
