Freddie Highmore's 12-part assassin thriller officially returns on AMC+ with six upcoming episodes, a deadly conspiracy, and an explosive mother-son reunion. After its season 1 premiere in late 2025, Highmore's first TV follow-up since The Good Doctor ended in 2024 offers an intriguing blend of action thriller and dark comedy, with critics and fans alike calling it pulpy, quirky, and loaded with on-screen chemistry. If you're a fan of Highmore's previous television projects, like Bates Motel, this is one of the best new series to press play on this weekend.

In a recent development, freddie Highmore's thriller series The Assassin just released its season 2 premiere in the United States on Thursday, November 19, exclusively on AMC+. The series, which premiered on Prime Video in the UK earlier this month, is created by two of the producers of Prime Video's Emmy-winning comedy series Fleabag, making The Assassin worth checking out this week. The series earned a strong 72% critic score on Rotten Tomatoes and is praised for its striong leading duo of Highmore and Keeley Hawes, best known for Orphan Black: Echoes and Bodyguard.

Sources indicate that without getting into spoiler territory, The Assassin season 2 picks up right where season 1 leaves off after a shocking twist ending that completely changes the framework of the series. In the first season of The Assassin, Highmore's protagonist Edward meets up with his estranged mother, Julie, finding her on a Greek island living the life of a retired assassin. Edward is an investigative journalist searching for answers from his mother, specifically about their family history and their distant relationship. However, things quickly go sideways when Julie's past life comes back to haunt her, presenting latest threats for both her and Edward.

These new dangers force Edward and Julie to escape from their island getaway, sending them deep within a global conspiracy that directly relates to Julie's profession as a contract killer. Edward and Julie must find a way to work together and survive despite their damaged mother-son relationship and conflicting personalities, driving mysteries and conflict from both external and internal sources.

The Assassin carries a similar dysfunctional parental dynamic to Dexter: Resurrection, in which a son must come to terms with the reality of their parent's crimes and must determine where they stand on this undeniable truth. It is also reminiscent of Netflix's hit spy thriller/dark comedy series Black Doves, which just returned for its 6-part sophomore season earlier this month as well.

In a recent development, the Assassin is far from your standard action thriller due to its complicated mother-son dynamic that is very much the emotional core of the story. That remarked, nothing is complicated about the on-screen chemistry between Highmore and Keeley, who are not only the backbone of the narrative, but the most entertaining characters throughout The Assassin.

Given Julie's history, she often comes off as a no-nonsense leader who takes swift, even deadly, action, while Edward's profession as a journalist makes him inquisitive, cautious, and a deep thinker. Because Edward and Julie are quite opposite in their personalities, their relationship defies typical familial standards in interesting ways. The situation between Julie and Edward only becomes more nuanced in The Assassin season 2, as new secrets are revealed that have the potential to ruin the efforts they made in season 1 to make amends.

One fascinating aspect between Julie and Edward is their role reversal, with Edward generally standing as a figure of morality while Julie is frequently more impulsive and combative. Edward attempts to convince Julie to change her ways, disapproving of her life choices, while Julie is reluctant to change and doesn't take Edward's criticisms. Ultimately, The Assassin is one of the most unique thriller series on streaming right now, combining action, emotion, and humor to create a can't-miss viewing experience on AMC+.
