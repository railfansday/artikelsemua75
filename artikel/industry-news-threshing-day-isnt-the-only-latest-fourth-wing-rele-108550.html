Sources indicate that each Empyrean series premium puzzle collection box includes one puzzle, an A3-size poster, and a collectible bookmark inside a full-color box with foiling and a magnetic closure. Based on the difficulty level, the puzzles are recommended for ages 12 and up, and they measure 49 centimeters by 36 centimeters, or approximately 19.3 inches by 14.2 inches, when assembled.
