It has been revealed that saka has been out since limping off against Bayern Munich at the end of last month, and it is understood he has yet to resume light training. Arteta revealed more tests during the international break confirmed the England forward did not need surgery and that he was expected to be out for another four weeks. Then there is the rehab initiative that we have now, what he needs to do in the gym, what he needs to do in terms of treatment and on the field.”.

Arsenal will also be without Martín Zubimendi for Saturday’s trip to Newcastle after the Spain midfielder picked up a hamstring injury against England last Sunday.
