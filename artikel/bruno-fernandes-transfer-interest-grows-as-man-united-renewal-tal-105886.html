According to Corriere dello Sport, Milan are monitoring Fernandes closely and could make their move if he becomes available in 2027.

In a recent development, fernandes has reportedly rejected United’s last two contract proposals, with negotiations currently stalled. His existing deal expires next summer, although United hold an option to extend it by another season.

According to recent reports, milan already asked for information about Fernandes during the previous summer window without making a formal bid. A return to Serie A could appeal to the Portugal international, particularly if Milan qualify for the Champions League.

There is also a connection with Milan boss Ruben Amorim, who previously worked with Fernandes at United and shares the same representation agency.

United should not rush into planning life without Fernandes.

A recent CaughtOffside report revealed that Fernandes wants to assess United’s progress under Pochettino before committing to another long-term deal.

Industry observers note that that stance is understandable. Fernandes has remained one of United’s most influential players through several managerial changes, and at 32, his next contract could be one of the final major decisions of his career.

Pochettino has already made it clear that Fernandes remains his captain, which suggests he still views him as central to the rebuild.

United should therefore activate their extension option rather than risk losing negotiating leverage. That would give Pochettino time to convince Fernandes that the club is finally moving in the right direction.

Milan represent a credible alternative, especially with Champions League football, but United still control the situation.

If results improve under Pochettino, keeping Fernandes may prove easier than the current contract stand-off suggests.
