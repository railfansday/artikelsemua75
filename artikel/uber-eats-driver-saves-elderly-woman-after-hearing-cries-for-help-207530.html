An Uber Eats driver in Massachusetts helped save an elderly woman after hearing her faint cries for help while attempting to complete a delivery.

Brandon McSorley had started delivering orders through Uber Eats to earn extra money ahead of the holiday season when he received an order from a customer in Carver on November 22.

McSorley told CBS News that the woman had ordered Tylenol and ginger ale. Because the delivery contained medication, he was required to verify the customer’s ID before handing over the order.

However, the house was dark when he arrived, and nobody answered the door. After waiting several minutes and attempting to contact the customer, he began hearing noises from inside the residence. (Related: Breaking: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arriv...)

“I could hear, like, a faint calling. I thought it was potentially someone telling me to go away or something like that,” he said.

In a recent development, mcSorley remained outside until the woman’s cries became clearer, allowing him to hear her asking for help after suffering a fall. He immediately called 911 and waited for emergency services to arrive.

Recent findings suggest that first responders transported the woman to a nearby hospital. According to Carver EMS, she had been lying on the floor for approximately 45 minutes without access to a phone or a medical alert device.

Carver EMS Chief Michael Ryan credited the delivery driver’s actions with preventing the woman from potentially spending much longer on the floor.
