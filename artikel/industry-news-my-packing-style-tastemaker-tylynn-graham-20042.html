The apple orchards are pouring the last of the hot cider, and leaf-peeping season is at its tail-end. For TyLynn Graham, tastemaker and curator of Vette storefront TyLynn.shop, this means one thing: It’s time to hit the road for a cozy weekend in Upstate New York.
