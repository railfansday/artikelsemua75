Pat Nevin sees a lot of Newcastle United.

The former Chelsea and Everton winger a regular visitor at St James’ Park.

Pat Nevin often on media duties at United matches.

As a result of that, he has far more of a clue than your average pundit about Newcastle United, the good an the bad.

The one-time player was once again on media duties at St James’ Park on Saturday night for the Newcastle United 2 Arsenal 2 match. (Related: GTA 6 player hit with 30-day PSN ban after sharing nude...)

As is usually a case, his reaction to what he witnessed was pretty spot on.

According to recent reports, pat Nevin on BBC Radio Five Live, commenting on the first half moment when Bruno Guimaraes almost headed Arsenal into the lead: “That would have broken a few hearts around here, a great run from Bruno Guimaraes. A very deep header, he’s a deep-lying midfielder. These Newcastle fans have watched him so many times in black and white and that was a decent half-chance.”.

Pat Nevin after Eze gave Arsenal a 33rd minute lead: “You felt as though it was coming. The Newcastle midfielders pushed up further and the second they did so, they were shredded. That pass from Kai Havertz is an absolute delight, he’s such a special player. Eberechi Eze lashed it past goalkeeper Lukas Hornicek.”. (Related: Second Circuit denies Kalshi and Coinbase interim relie...)

Pat Nevin commenting after Harvey Barnes equalised four minutes after the break: “Newcastle have been fabulous at the start of the second half. They’ve closed down higher up the field, not giving Arsenal any time on the ball. They’ve struggled to be able to zip the ball about like they did in the first. But can Newcastle keep this up?”.

In a recent development, malick Thiaw giving United a 2-1 lead, Pat Nevin: “Beyond amazing. There is not a Newcastle fan in this ground who could see this coming. They have come out with such a brilliant attitude in the second half. Dan Burn at the back post with nobody on him. Malick Thiaw has the easiest tap-in in the world.”.

Pat Nevin as the match moves into the nine minutes of added time and the score 2-2, Noni Madueke having equalised on 78 minutes: “This has got the feeling of a cup tie. I love that Newcastle don’t want to settle just for a point against the champions. But I think they’re running out of players, Lewis Hall has sat down.”.

Sources indicate that pat Nevin with a perfect summing up after the final whistle: “Isn’t it a joy when you think you know what’s going on in football but you know nothing at half-time. In the first half Arsenal were all over Newcastle, there was not one area where Arsenal were not better. Then three changes at half time and Newcastle were superb in the second half and the fans will be wondering where that has been all season? A great performance coming back after being a goal down. They showed the capabilities they’ve got.”.
