Manchester City’s long winning home run in the Women’s Super League came to an end as they were stunned by Tottenham.

Khadija Shaw put City ahead with only 10 minutes gone but Cathinka Tandberg levelled late in the first half and goals from Matilda Vinberg and Jess Naz secured a 3-1 victory for the visitors.

City had been on a 17-match winning run at home in the WSL, with their last defeat coming back in March 2025 against Chelsea.

The result means City stay two points behind leaders Chelsea, while Tottenham are now only five points further back in fifth. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Arsenal remain without a win at Emirates Stadium this season after they were held to a goalless draw by West Ham.

Days after a home loss to Lyon in the Champions League, Renee Slegers watched her side endure more struggles in front of their own fans.

Boos greeted the full-time whistle after Arsenal were held to a sixth consecutive home league draw to further pile the pressure on Slegers.

Spurs remain a point adrift of Manchester United, who left it very late to claim a 2-1 victory over Birmingham.

Lee Geum-min put struggling Birmingham in front in only the fourth minute and United struggled to create clear-cut chances, while Phallon Tullis-Joyce made a couple of crucial saves.

The visitors finally found the breakthrough 10 minutes from time through Elisabeth Terland, and Simi Awujo broke Birmingham hearts with a header four minutes into added time.

Bottom side Charlton claimed their first point of the season at the 11th time of asking with a 1-1 draw against Aston Villa.

Keira Flannery put Charlton ahead with a first-half penalty but Lucy Parker pulled Villa level 19 minutes from time.

An early own goal scored by Maz Pacheco saw struggling Everton fall to a 1-0 defeat at home to Brighton.
