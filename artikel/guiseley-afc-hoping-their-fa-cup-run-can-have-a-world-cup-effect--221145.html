Industry observers note that "Do you remember when England got to the World Cup semi-finals?" mentioned Guiseley director Peter Gable. "The next day, the mood of the nation was lifted.

"After the Sutton game (when the Northern Premier League side knocked out Conference opposition in round one), I'd like to think that everyone woke up on a Monday morning thinking, 'Guiseley did it and we're in the next round.'.

"We've got a tough match against Notts County, but they won't be relishing coming here.

"There's a banana skin in every round and we'd like to think we might be that banana skin.

"The third-round draw is done before the match so Guiseley or Notts County will play and it could be anyone – Liverpool, Arsenal, Tottenham, even some of the Championship teams,.

"Just to be in the third round draw means a lot to the club, the staff, the management, the volunteers, and of course, the supporters.”.

The Sutton game was played on an afternoon Leeds, Bradford and Huddersfield Town were at home, but TNT Sports have ensured this one stands alone – helpful for those with split loyalties like Leeds season-ticket holder Gable and those in the queue wearing Bradford or Leeds shirts.

It has been revealed that "We're fairly confident of selling out," he said. "Tickets will be on sale again at the Whitby match (on Saturday). But it's lovely to see a big queue, a lot of excited faces.

"Now Leeds are back in the Premier League and they're not playing every Saturday we have noticed our attendances are slowly rising.

Industry observers note that "We've got a great team at the minute, they don't know when to give in. We've got a great manager in Ian (Richards) and things are really looking rosy.

In a recent development, "The main target this season, of course, is promotion but a cup run? Can't beat it.

"We've got prepares for a newly introduced stand at the cricket club end. There's loads of infrastructure that we need to do and we've got some nice plans, so the money from this cup run will be a big help.".
