By RJ Fulton – Nov 22, 2026 at 5:20PM EST.

Sources indicate that cryptocurrency markets demonstrated signs of stabilization on Sunday, November 22, following a brutal sell-off that triggered approximately $1.8 billion in forced liquidations across digital asset markets. As of early evening, Bitcoin (CRYPTO: BTC) was up 1.15% to $60,785.36, while Ethereum (CRYPTO: ETH) gained 2.23% to $1,719.76. Solana (CRYPTO: SOL) lagged the broader market, edging 0.16% lower to $70.10. Despite the modest recovery, major cryptocurrencies remain under considerable pressure as investors assess whether the recent wave of forced selling has finally subsided.

Industry observers note that the cryptocurrency market is attempting to stabilize following a sharp deterioration in global risk appetite that triggered widespread selling across equities, commodities, and digital assets. Friday's sell-off pushed Bitcoin to a two-year low near $55,000, while approximately $1.8 billion in leveraged cryptocurrency positions were liquidated as prices fell, forcing traders to unwind their exposure. Recent selling pressure across cryptocurrencies coincided with a broader retreat from risk assets as concerns over elevated valuations, persistently high interest rates, and tightening financial conditions weighed on investor sentiment. The simultaneous weakness across markets suggests investors prioritized cash and liquidity over rotating capital into safer investments.

U.S.-listed spot cryptocurrency ETFs finished Friday's trading session with substantial losses. The iShares Bitcoin Trust ETF (NASDAQ: IBIT) declined 5.87%, while the Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (NYSEMKT: FBTC) fell 6.16%. Meanwhile, the iShares Ethereum Trust ETF (NASDAQ: ETHA) plunged 9.16%.

These figures reflect Friday's closing prices, as U.S. stock exchanges were closed over the weekend. With Bitcoin and Ethereum recovering from their recent lows, Monday's reopening will provide the first opportunity for these ETFs to reflect the latest weekend price movements during regular trading.

The cryptocurrency market's modest recovery over the weekend offers some relief following last week's sharp sell-off, but it remains unclear whether the rebound reflects a meaningful improvement in investor demand or simply the exhaustion of forced selling. With leveraged positions liquidated during the downturn, the removal of excessive leverage could help stabilize prices. However, a sustainable recovery will require renewed buying interest rather than an absence of sellers.

In a recent development, the reopening of U.S. equity markets on Monday will provide an key signal for investors. Cryptocurrencies have been particularly sensitive to weakness in technology stocks, and another round of selling across risk assets could quickly undermine the weekend's gains. Investors should monitor whether Bitcoin can sustain its recovery above $60,000, Ethereum can regain $1,800, and Solana can establish support above $70.
