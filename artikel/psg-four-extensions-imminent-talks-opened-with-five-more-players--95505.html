Paris Saint-Germain are stepping up their process of contract extensions. Four signings are imminent.

For several weeks, PSG have embarked on a new wave of contract extensions. As expected, these talks are bearing fruit. On Thursday evening, Canal+ gave a full update on this important glitch. On the Late Football Club show, Olivier Tallaron revealed that four key players were going to extend: Ousmane Dembele, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, Desire Doue and Matvey Safonov.

The Ballon d’Or winner has agreed with the two-time European cham.

Recent findings suggest that at the same time, the Parisian club have opened negotiations with five other key players in the squad: Marquinhos, Warren Zaire-Emery, Vitinha, Achraf Hakimi and Nuno Mendes. PSG are offering their captain a one-year extension, which could take his deal until June 2029.

Paris are aiming for long-term signings until June 2032 with the four others named, who are younger and represent the future of the club.

Sources indicate that it is, in any case, good news for PSG, who had already extended Willian Pacho and Joao Neves last year. The Parisian club intend, and are well placed, to keep the core of their two-time reigning European champions for the long term.
