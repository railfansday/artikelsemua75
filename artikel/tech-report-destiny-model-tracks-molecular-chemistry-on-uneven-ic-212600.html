Tech Report: DESTINY model tracks molecular chemistry on uneven, ice-covered grains in interstellar space

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that interstellar space is the scene of active chemical processes: molecules form there in the gas, but also on the surface of tiny dust grains covered in ice. To better represent what happens on these surfaces, astrophysicists at the University of Liège are proposing a deterministic approach based on Markov chains with absorbing states, a mathematical tool that had not yet been utilized in astrochemistry. The paper is published in the journal Astronomy & Astrophysics.
