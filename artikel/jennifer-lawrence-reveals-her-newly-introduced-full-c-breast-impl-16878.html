Jennifer Lawrence is getting candid about going under the knife.

The actress, 36, confirmed she had gotten a boob job while appearing on the Thursday, November 19, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen alongside her Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping costar Josh Hutcherson. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

The admission came as she was giving her “hot takes” on Bravo celebrities. At one point, host Andy Cohen asked Lawrence, “Is it a mystery to you how a boob could break [Real Housewives of Fresh York City star] Hailey Glassman’s nose?”. (Related: I Asked About Dirty Dishes. You Delivered....)

In response, the Oscar winner noted, “Yes, and I have implants.” Drawing attention to her bust, she added, “Yeah, they’re new.”.

In a recent development, cohen, 58, complimented Lawrence, calling the implants “beautiful,” and questioned her about the size she opted for. “What is that? Are those Bs?” he asked.

Sources indicate that “No, it’s a full C. I think?” Lawrence replied. “It’s the same size that they were.”.

The Don’t Look Up star went on to joke, “Anyway, I’ve broken two noses.”.

Lawrence has previously been an open book about her cosmetic surgery procedures.

Speaking to Vogue for an article published on September 3, Lawrence revealed all the cosmetic work she’s had done.

Industry observers note that “I need to have movement in my face — I get baby Botox, but I can’t do a lot,” she shared. “If my skin is going to be a little saggier than I would like, at least it looks dewy and pretty.”.

Lawrence noted she was also considering fat transfer, which can help restore volume and smooth wrinkles, but had yet to take the plunge.

“I’m really nervous about these fat transfers,” she admitted to the magazine. “I support other people doing them because I want to continue to watch and see what happens, but I don’t know about that.”.

It has been revealed that “I mean, I would love to have a buccal fat removal,” the No Hard Feelings star added of the procedure that takes out fat pads from the cheeks to create a more sculpted face. “I can’t because everybody would know, but just know that I want that.”.
