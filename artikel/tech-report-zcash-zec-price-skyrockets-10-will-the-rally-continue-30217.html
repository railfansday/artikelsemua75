On Tuesday, Zcash price showed a massive rally, rising more than 10%.

At press time, ZEC is reading near $961. The recovery follows a sharp a correction that pushed ZEC toward $860 over the weekend. (Related: Reading: 10+ Ways It Changes Your Brain And Mental Heal...)

Despite the rebound, the ZEC remains under pressure from institutional fund withdrawals.

One of the most vital developments is a Zcash Community Grants (ZCG) vote running from November 16 to November 30.

Community members are considering proposals to change how the grants treasury is managed, including a proposal to transfer 50,000 ZEC into the Network Sustainability Mechanism. (Related: The Pedalling Prescription: 12 Ways Cycling Can Change ...)

Industry observers note that zcash gained 7.69% on November 4, rising on high expectations surrounding NU7. The upgrade introduces several changes to the network's transaction processing and reward structure.

According to recent reports, as the upgrade went live on November 5, the target block interval went from 75 seconds to 25 seconds. According to the project’s statement, the change makes private payments confirm three times faster.

ZEC recently tested the $860 area before recovering. This level is key because derivatives data previously identified a concentration of leveraged long positions around it.

It has been revealed that if Zcash returns to that zone, forced liquidations could amplify volatility. $950 is an initial recovery level, followed by the psychological resistance around $1,000.

However, break below $860 could expose ZEC to selling pressure and further decline.
