The Verge reported on November 24, 2026 that The Latest York Times had directed freelance photographers not to use Adobe Photoshop 2027, release 28.0.0 or subsequent updates, on Times assignments because its AI features may not be apparent to users and can produce metadata identifying photographs as AI-generated.

The email from Times photography leaders Meaghan Looram and Josh Haner states the features use AI “in ways that may not be apparent to users.” Its requirement applies to all contributing photographers working on Times assignments; it does not extend to every Photoshop version or to other editing software. The purpose is to keep those tags from appearing inadvertently in Times photographs. Neither Adobe nor the Times immediately responded to a request for more information.
