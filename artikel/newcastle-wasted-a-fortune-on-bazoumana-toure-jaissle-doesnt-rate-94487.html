Newcastle United hosted Arsenal in the Premier League on Saturday evening, and fans were not happy about the state of the team even before a ball was kicked.

The Magpies started with a team that looked very weak on paper, including young midfielders Seun Steur and Lewis Miley playing, with Joe Willock in midfield, and Jacob Murphy in attack alongside William Osula and Harvey Barnes.

The substitutes' bench saw much stronger options available, including the likes of Matias Fernandez-Pardo, Anthony Elanga, Nico Gonzalez, Yoanne Wissa, Tino Livramento, Jacob Ramsey, and Sven Botman.

But perhaps more significantly was the fact that two summer signings in Aladji Bamba and Bazoumana Toure were not included in the matchday squad at all by Matthias Jaissle, despite the club spending £72m on the pair under Eddie Howe. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Neither player was expected to be ready to make an immediate impact on the starting lineup this season considering their age, profile, and experience at the top level. But to not be included in the squad at all, despite the clear complications that the squad is having this season, suggests that the manager has no faith in their qualities.

Industry observers note that it's also worth noting that Newcastle were without Joelinton for the game, with the Brazilian injured and unavailable for selection. That would imply that both players are even further down the pecking order than it may seem initially. (Related: Industry News: Designing Azure silicon on Azure silicon...)

Sources indicate that bamba's lack of involvement is potentially not as worrying, because of the options in midfield. Steur has been pushing for an opportunity, and with Nico Gonzalez on the bench, and Jaissle a huge fan of Willock, him being left out on this occasion can be excused.

But for Toure, the situation is hugely different. Newcastle have been struggling for goals all season, managing just 13 goals in their 10 games before the Arsenal fixture, and their top scorers Barnes, Willock, and Elanga all have just two goals each.

Recent findings suggest that other attacking options in the squad have struggled to make any sort of impact too, while Toure has two goals this season despite having started just one game in all competitions. His incredible pace, dribbling ability, and creativity should mean that he should at least be considered by a manager looking for ways to turn the team's fortunes around.

When Newcastle agreed a £42m deal to sign Toure from Hoffenheim, it was with the idea of him becoming an integral part of the future of the club under Eddie Howe.

But before the season even began Howe had left, and it now looks evident that Toure is not someone that Jaissle wants as part of his squad.

The Ivorian hasn't started a game since August, and despite the team winning just three of their opening ten games in the Premier League, he can't get a look in. Jaissle has no interest in giving Toure an opportunity at this point, and his exclusion from the squad against Arsenal proves it.

If Newcastle had known that Jaissle was going to come in and not be a fan of Toure, then there is no way they would have committed such a large fee to bringing him into the club just to be sidelined. It's a huge oversight on behalf of the board, and it's a massive waste of money too.
