As anyone who has ever shopped at The Home Depot (or any hardware store) knows, power tools are expensive. That’s why Black Friday at The Home Depot is so important: It’s the perfect opportunity to get the tools you’ve been wanting at some pretty steep discounts. And the best deals even give you a free tool if you purchase a starter kit from one of the major tool brands (Ryobi, DeWalt, Makita, or Milwaukee).

Recent findings suggest that here’s how this year's “buy one, get one” (BOGO) deal works: Pick out a starter kit from one of the brands, add it to your cart, and you’ll be presented with a list of available tools you can add (they’ll be marked “FREE,” so you can’t get confused). When you go to check out, the price of the tool will be listed as “SAVINGS.”.

Makita 18V LXT Starter Pack (includes two 5.0Ah batteries and a rapid charger). Cost: $199.

Industry observers note that milwaukee M18 FUEL Combo Kit (includes a brushless, cordless hammer drill and an impact driver). Cost: $399.

Industry observers note that note that the Ryobi, DeWalt, and Milwaukee offers include 14 free tools to choose from, while the Makita offer only includes 8. Also, note that while the Ryobi starter kit is your cheapest option for a free tool, the batteries included are weaker. The DeWalt or Makita batteries cost more, but offer more power and flexibility.

According to recent reports, some of the deals go beyond just getting a freebie. For example, if you choose the DeWalt Starter Kit for $199, you can get an Atomic 20V Max impact wrench that normally costs $239 for free, which is a significant savings even if you already have batteries and a charger and don’t really need those items (plus, you can never have too many batteries).

Sources indicate that the Home Depot is also offering deep discounts on just about everything in the store. Some of the most notable tool deals include:.

50% off this DeWalt Cordless Brushless Oscillating Multi Tool, including battery and charger.

33% off this Ryobi Combo Kit, an incredible deal for a circular saw, reciprocating saw, and a power drill/driver plus two batteries and a charger; the batteries are small, but this is a great deal on a starter kit.

42% off this Combo Kit from Milwaukee, including a Packout toolbox, a hammer drill, an impact driver, a grinder, and a reciprocating saw, plus two batteries and a charger—a massive deal for anyone who needs to tool up rapidly.

45% off this Brushless Cordless Impact Wrench Kit from Ryobi, which includes a battery and charger.

That only scratches the surface—if you’ve ever needed anything from Home Depot, Black Friday is the day to run out and get it.

Of course, Black Friday can be a little chaotic, so here are a few tips to maximize your savings and experience:.

Recent findings suggest that check online and in-store. Some deals are in-store only, and some items (especially BOGO tools) may not be available at your nearest location—make sure you enter your ZIP code when you’re checking out to ensure you won’t have any problems.

Consider whether you need more batteries and chargers. Depending on your existing collection of batteries and chargers, buying more to get the discount may or may not make sense.

In a recent development, there's no price matching or rain checks. Remember that the store won’t honor price-matching or offer rain checks on Black Friday items.

Cyber Monday will have deals, too. More deals will be rolled out on Cyber Monday (November 30), which may include items you want but don’t see on sale for Black Friday. But keep in mind that Black Friday prices probably won’t go any lower—so snap up what you need as soon as you can.

The best early deals on tools right now are at Home Depot, where you can get up to 65% off on select Milwaukee tools, 50% off on DeWalt tools, and 30% off on Ryobi tools. They also have several offers for over 50% off on hand tools..

Industry observers note that home Depot and Lowe’s often compete with each other for seasonal sales, so it’s always a good idea to check both sites before buying. Right now, Home Depot has the best deals on DeWalt and Milwaukee cordless tool sets, but Lowe’s has a better deal on Craftsman hand tool sets.

The best prices of the year on tools are during the holidays in November and December. This is because of the holiday shopping push, as well as making space for new tools that come out in the spring. The second-best time to buy tools is in May and June during Father’s Day sales.
