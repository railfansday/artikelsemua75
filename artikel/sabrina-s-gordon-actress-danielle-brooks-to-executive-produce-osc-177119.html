EXCLUSIVE: Sabrina S. Gordon and Oscar-nominated actress Danielle Brooks have joined the award-winning short documentary Free Joan Little as executive producers.

Sources indicate that the Oscar-contending film, directed by Yoruba Richen, “recounts the groundbreaking 1975 case of a young Black woman in eastern North Carolina who killed a white jailer after he attempted to rape her,” according to a synopsis. “Little’s flight from custody and the ensuing media frenzy galvanized a nationwide movement for her cause, with support from civil rights icons Angela Davis and Rosa Parks.”.

Free Joan Little has won numerous awards, including Best Documentary at the LA Shorts International Documentary Festival, and Best Short Documentary at the Hot Springs Documentary Film Festival in Arkansas, Morehouse College Human Rights Film Festival in Atlanta, the Teaneck International Film Festival in Fresh Jersey, and the Reel South Award from the RiverRun International Film Festival in North Carolina. In addition, the documentary has made the prestigious shortlists for the Cinema Eye Honors and the IDA Documentary Awards. (Related: British Airways Avios — Short Nonstop Domestic Flights...)

It has been revealed that sabrina S. Gordon produced the Oscar-shortlisted Seeds (2025), Black Girls (2024), To the End (2024), and Flint (2020). In a statement, she said, “When Yoruba first showed me Free Joan Little as a work in progress while we were teaching a filmmaking workshop together in The Gambia, I knew immediately I wanted to support it however I could. Its artistic craft is as breathtaking as Joan’s remarkable story of defiance. Her courageous, uncompromising stand for bodily autonomy and human dignity against racism and sexual violence makes this film as urgent and relevant today as it was 50 years ago.”. Danielle Brooks, who earned an Oscar nomination for 2024’s The Color Purple, serves as the voice of Joan Little in dramatizations within Free Joan Little.

“I am so moved by Joan’s story and am committed to making sure that her story is heard and seen by all,” commented Brooks. “I am proud to join the filmmaking team as an executive producer and to support the film in any way possible.”.

Sources indicate that “We were deeply honored when Danielle Brooks agreed to lend her voice to Free Joan Little, and welcoming her now as an Executive Producer—alongside Oscar-shortlisted filmmaker Sabrina Schmidt Gordon (Seeds)—is an extraordinary gift,” remarked Richen. “Sabrina is a longtime creative collaborator and dear friend whose vision as a producer, editor, and strategist has shaped so many acclaimed documentaries. Bringing Joan Little’s story back into the national consciousness is as urgent today as it was in 1975. Partnering with Danielle and Sabrina, along with Reel South, PBS North Carolina, and Black Public Media, allows us to ground this vital piece of Southern Black history in its local roots while delivering its powerful message of self-defense and justice to a nationwide audience.”.

Sources indicate that free Joan Little is directed by Yoruba Richen, featuring the voice of Danielle Brooks. It is produced by Christalyn Hampton and Bonnie Bertram for Promised Land Film, in association with Retro Report. Executive producers are Sabrina Schmidt Gordon (Vespertine Films), Danielle Brooks, and Kyra Darnton. Hannan Vanderlan edited the film; Keith Walker serves as director of photography. Reggie Dokes composed the score. The film also featured the 1975 song “Joan Little,” written by Bernice Johnson Reagon and performed by Sweet Honey in the Rock.

Sources indicate that the film makes its international premiere in London on Friday at the Women of the Lens Film Festival. It will be showcased in Cinema Eye Honors Short List screenings in Latest York and Los Angeles in December. Free Joan Little has been acquired by Reel South and PBS North Carolina for local-to-national public media distribution, including streaming on YouTube.
