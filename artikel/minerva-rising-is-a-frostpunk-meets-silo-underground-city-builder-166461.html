Industry observers note that minerva Rising is a post-apocalyptic city builder in which your city extends deep underground, divided into levels with their own resources, industries, and communities. You are an artificial intelligence with overall control, but watch out: if you alienate the population of any particular level by, say, forgetting to supply them with oxygen, subversives may rise up and cut your feed, blinding you to their activities.

I'm keen to know whether it's possible for every level to lose faith in you and hack your cameras, reducing the game to a pile of "SIGNAL NOT FOUND" messages. I'm also imagining turning the feeds back on and discovering that everybody is having a massive orgy. Stupid sexy vault dwellers! You'll go forth and multiply when Deep Thought tells you to, and not a second before.

Who needs the sun? Can't get sunburn from a glowing mushroom, can you? Look, citizens of level 13: you'd better overcome your distaste for giant toadstools or I'm turning the power off again.
