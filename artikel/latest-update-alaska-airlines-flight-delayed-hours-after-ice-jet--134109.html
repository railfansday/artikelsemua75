In the end, the flight was delayed by hours, as the crew was no longer fit to fly, and the Port of Seattle is outraged. This is quite the story, as flagged by PYOK.

This incident happened on the morning of Saturday, November 21, 2026, and involves Alaska Airlines flight AS1306, scheduled to operate from Seattle (SEA) to Los Angeles (LAX).

This was first reported by a passenger on the flight, who explained that “ICE agents just arrested multiple people at SeaTac Airport inside the jet bridge as we were all boarding,” and went on to explain that they were “deplaning because the crew is being replaced due to poor morale.”. In a recent development, we are outraged about this morning’s incident involving ICE and two passengers as travelers boarded AS 1306 to Los Angeles. Immigration enforcement actions at SEA are conducted without the Port’s participation or knowledge. The Port has continually asked, and local federal immigration authorities have previously agreed to protocols at SEA, and we are in conversation with DHS on whether any of these protocols were breached. This incident was not only distressing for the affected family but the passengers around them, and Port officials continue to engage federal officials to understand more about what occurred.
