Industry observers note that DENVER, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- The Denver Broncos Foundation and College Track today announced a four-year partnership to expand access to College Track Denver's 10-year program for first-generation scholars. The commitment is the Foundation's third and largest grant to College Track, bringing its total investment since 2023 to nearly $1 million. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

According to recent reports, college Track is a national college completion program that equips first-generation students from under-resourced communities to earn a bachelor's degree and pursue meaningful careers. Its 10-year program walks with scholars from ninth grade through college and into their careers. The Foundation's earlier grants funded College Thrive, which guides scholars through their college years. The current grant extends that investment to the full 10-year program including College Readiness (the high school years) and Alumni & Early Career (post-college). College Track Denver will focus expansion efforts through additional recruitment of scholars from the start of ninth grade. The partnership deepens a relationship that began in 2023 and reflects a shared belief that lasting change takes long-term commitment. According to recent reports, together, the Foundation and College Track share a priority: expanding access to opportunity for Denver students. The Foundation's investment is structured to grow over the four years, alongside the program, and is projected to more than double the amount of College Track Denver scholars by 2029.

"The Denver Broncos Foundation is committed to ensuring young people have the knowledge, skills, experiences and credentials needed to be prepared for their futures," said Vice President of Community Impact & Executive Director, Denver Broncos Foundation Allie Engelken. "College Track's comprehensive model supports students from high school through college completion, helping them navigate critical transitions and achieve meaningful postsecondary and career success. We are excited to support their continued growth and impact across Colorado.".

"Lasting change in every young person's life requires a deep commitment from the people, organizations, and institutions that surround them", said Shirley M. Collado, Ph.D., College Track President and CEO. "We're thrilled that the Denver Broncos are joining us to walk alongside our Denver College Track scholars as they move through high school and launch to college. The Broncos are playing the long game in Denver, investing in the sustained, structural transformation that puts the dream of earning a bachelor's degree within reach for the city's first-generation college students.".

College Track Denver opened in 2016 and moved in 2023 to Colorado State University's Spur campus, becoming the first College Track site co-located with a university system. Through partnerships with Denver Public Schools and four North Denver high schools, the site has grown scholar enrollment nearly tenfold, and 92% of its scholars come from under-resourced communities. With this partnership, College Track Denver aims to welcome larger ninth-grade classes each year and recruit from more Denver schools, expanding access to the 10-year program for a larger number of first-generation scholars.

"In just the first months of this partnership, that growth is already taking shape," noted Dan Sullivan, Executive Site Director, College Track Denver. "Because we could offer transportation, we welcomed Montbello High School as a partner school, and nearly 50 of its students applied to become College Track scholars. The Denver Broncos Foundation is also creating career exposure opportunities that give our scholars firsthand experience of the paths ahead of them.".
