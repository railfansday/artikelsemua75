In this latest upgrade regarding his condition, Challinor has noted, as per the official club website: “Tyler is a really positive character because he’s had real disappointment over the short career he’s had so far. He’s had to deal with significant injuries, and that’s probably made him better. I think, looking at him, you always get to the point where you think he might be due a couple of games before the end of the season. But I think you probably have to step back and accept that there might be some setbacks along the way that sort of halt that.

At the age of 23, he is still young and has potential to develop down the line. When fit again, he will battle it out with the likes of Callum Slattery, Billy Mitchell and Barry Bannan, among others, for a place in the Owls’ starting XI.

As well the Hatters, he was also loaned out to Burton Albion and Forest Green Rovers in the EFL to get some experience under his belt.
