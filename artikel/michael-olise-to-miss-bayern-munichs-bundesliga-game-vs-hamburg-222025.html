Bayern Munich head coach Vincent Kompany has confirmed that they will be without Michael Olise for their Bundesliga game against Hamburg on Saturday.

The Frenchman was injured in last weekend’s 1-1 draw against Cologne and subsequently missed Bayern’s UEFA Champions League defeat against Lille on Wednesday night. Olise, who left the Allianz Arena on crutches after a challenge from Cologne’s Tom Krauß at the edge of the box, has been wearing a VACOped brace to help with the recovery process after suffering bruising on his left foot.
