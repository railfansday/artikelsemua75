Credit Union ONE’s board unanimously elected President and Chief Operating Officer Tina Tracy to succeed Gary Moody as CEO, effective Jan. 1, 2027, following a succession planning process initiated by Moody.

Tracy, who has held several leadership roles at the credit union, including chief financial officer, currently oversees its retail, lending, technology, marketing and finance teams, while Moody is stepping down after 20 years as CEO. NEXA Lending added Michael Lindsay, a military veteran and mortgage originator who funded more than $100 million in loans in 2025, with approximately 98% to 99% of his production coming from VA lending.

Licensed in about 35 states, Lindsay joins NEXA with a national mortgage business built largely through social media and consumer education, citing the company’s broader lender network, loan product flexibility and collaborative environment as factors in his decision to join. The Mortgage Bankers Association promoted Justin Wiseman to senior vice president of housing finance policy and general counsel, expanding his responsibilities to include oversight of the trade group’s legal affairs while continuing to lead its housing finance policy work.

Wiseman, who joined MBA in 2012 and most recently served as vice president and managing regulatory counsel, will also join the association’s Senior Management Team and oversee legal risk management, compliance and board governance.

First Horizon Bank named Andrew Finkle market president for Palm Beach and Broward counties in Florida, where he will lead the local team and oversee client relationships.

Finkle joined the bank in 2016 and previously served as commercial banking team lead for the two counties; he has more than 25 years of commercial and investment banking experience and five years of experience as a middle-market manufacturer’s chief financial officer.

Better Home & Finance Holding Manufacturer added two mortgage advisors to NEO Home Loans, expanding its presence in Southern California and along the Central Coast.

Brandon Goldberg, who leads Your Home Loan Surgeons in the San Diego area, and Lompoc-based Ryan Davis brought a combined $50 million in loan volume in 2025, with both specializing in purchase lending and local relationships with real estate agents and homebuilders.
