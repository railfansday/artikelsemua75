MDR certification enables broader clinical use of AI-driven diagnostic support across European healthcare.

HELSINKI, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CGI (TSX: GIB.A) (NYSE: GIB), one of the largest independent IT and business consulting services firms in the world, has confirmed that an artificial intelligence (AI) solution, CGI AI Head Analysis, developed in partnership with Helsinki University Hospital (HUS) and Planmeca, has received official certification as a medical device under the European Union Medical Device Regulation (MDR class IIb). The AI solution supports physicians in detecting brain hemorrhages from computed tomography (CT) scans. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

CGI AI Head Analysis analyzes head CT images to detect non-traumatic, spontaneous intracranial  hemorrhages and flags suspected acute blood on the images. It is easy for physicians to quickly review suspicious findings marked by artificial intelligence. The solution is particularly well suited for situations where a doctor performing diagnostics on head CT images wants to rule out that no potentially life-threatening intracranial hemorrhages go unnoticed, for example, in busy emergency rooms.
