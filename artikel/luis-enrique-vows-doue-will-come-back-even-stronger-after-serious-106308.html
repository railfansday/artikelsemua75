Luis Enrique confirmed Desire Doue suffered a "serious injury" during Paris Saint-Germain's victory over Nice, but insisted the winger will "come back even stronger".

PSG extended their winning streak to 12 matches in all competitions after prevailing 3-2 at Allianz Riviera, now the longest run of Luis Enrique's managerial career.

Mohammed Amoura had given Nice an eighth-minute lead before two goals in the space of a minute turned the game on its head for the defending Ligue 1 champions. (Related: Industry News: Animal control called to rescue alligato...)

Senny Mayulu's 31st-minute penalty was followed up by Doue's sensational finish just 55 seconds later, the shortest gap between two PSG goals across Ligue 1 and the Champions League over the last 20 years.

However, PSG star Doue was then stretchered off the pitch just before the break after appearing to twist his right knee awkwardly in a challenge with Sofiane Diop. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

The France international has been an influential figure for PSG this season with 10 direct goal involvements (six goals, four assists), but there is now a fear he could be set to join long-term absentee Khvicha Kvaratskhelia in the treatment room.

"We have to wait for the final exams to know exactly what the injury is, but it's a serious injury," Luis Enrique remarked of Doue.

Sources indicate that "It's clear. We could see it on television. It's the life of high-level professional players, we can see situations like that on almost every matchday.

"You have to accept it. I'm sure he'll be able to recover very well and that he'll come back even stronger.".

Industry observers note that "This was a very important win," Ferran said. "It wasn't our best display, but ultimately, we were competitive and got the three points.
