Peak holiday season is here, and some of the busiest travel days are still ahead; we drew inspiration from a few of our favorite celebrities to bring you the best travel outfits for women, making your next excursion all the more stylish.

According to recent reports, comfort is often top of mind, but that doesn’t mean you have to settle for just leggings. Kendall Jenner went for something polished, wearing a silky cream matching set, while Naomi Watts sported a leather bomber jacket, T-shirt, wide-leg sweatpants, and bright-red sneakers, showing the many different directions you can take to fit your own personal style.
