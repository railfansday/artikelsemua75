Jack Grealish has been offered an olive branch by Everton boss David Moyes and the sanctioned star is available for selection this weekend.

The Manchester City loanee was punished by the club for failing to report to training sessions and missed several matches. He has not featured for four games: one because of illness, then a ‘breach of club discipline’ and a lack of fitness thereafter.

Industry observers note that but Grealish will be considered for the squad when the in-form Toffees travel to Brentford on Monday night. Moyes confirmed Grealish is fit and available, saying: 'He’s back in training and back in and around the team.'. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

The 31-year-old issued a public apology on Instagram, to which Moyes stated: 'I don’t do social media but I’ve heard about it.'. (Related: Is Social Security Taxable? How to Calculate What You O...)

On whether the pair have held talks, the Scot added: 'We’ve just been getting on with our work.

It has been revealed that 'We’ve got a lot of other players who I need to put a lot of focus on as well, I can’t just concentrate on one player. I’ve been trying to make sure that everybody’s getting the right preparation and getting ready for our next game against Brentford.'.

Jack Grealish is available for Everton again after serving his punishment and regaining fitness.

Grealish's social media apology came after he featured in a behind-closed-doors match during the international break.

In a recent development, 'Nice to be back out there with the boys today after a few good days training!' Grealish wrote. 'I want to apologise to the fans (and) everyone at the club for not being able to help the team the last few weeks.

'I love playing for this club (and) can't wait to be back playing in front of you guys in an Everton shirt.

'See you all soon (blue heart)'.

Everton have won their last three games and go into Monday’s match sixth in the table.

At the start of his press conference Moyes was presented with a framed memento of his time in charge by defender James Tarkowski and former player Leon Osman after he set a upcoming club record of 593 games in the hotseat.

In a recent development, moyes stated: 'It’s something that I’m really thrilled about but, if I’m being honest, it’s not something I really think about. I understand I’ve suddenly become the manager who’s had more games at Everton than anybody before and that’s a real honour and privilege to be put in that position.'.
