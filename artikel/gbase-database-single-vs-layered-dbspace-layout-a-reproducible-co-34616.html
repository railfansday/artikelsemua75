The purpose of dbspace layering in GBase Database is to establish clearer boundaries for capacity management, troubleshooting, and backup — not to maximize the number of dbspaces.

Storage layout in GBase Database(GBase 8s) deserves careful planning, but careful planning does not mean blindly copying a recommended architecture.

Industry observers note that A single-space layout may be sufficient for a smaller workload. A layered layout may provide better operational boundaries when business data, historical records, temporary operations, and large objects grow at different rates.

The right decision depends on actual workload behavior and operational requirements.

Build two configurations, control the variables, measure the differences, and document the results.

One reproducible comparison in your own environment is more valuable than memorizing someone else's "best layout.".
