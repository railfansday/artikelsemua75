Jimmy Kimmel zeroed in on Jensen Huang on last night’s episode of Jimmy Kimmel Live, taking aim at Donald Trump‘s unabashed support of the Nvidia CEO.

Sources indicate that “Who doesn’t love a good comeback story?” Kimmel quipped of Huang, who was reinstated as CEO of Nvidia despite an ongoing investigation into multiple alleged affairs — including one with an accused Chinese spy and two with Nvidia subordinates. Trump took credit for the decision, telling CNBC that he personally called into an Nvidia board meeting and “convinced” it to give Huang, one of the president’s closest allies in tech, his job back.

Recent findings suggest that “After 10 grueling days of forced unemployment, the board at Nvidia today voted to reinstate their former CEO, who, according to our president, did nothing wrong,” Kimmel recounted. “All this guy did was cheat on his wife with two employees and a Chinese spy. Who hasn’t done that?”.

Kimmel explained that the board issued a statement saying they have “every confidence” in Huang. “Which, of course, had nothing to do with the pressure from our Silicon Man in Chief, who has at least 62 million reasons why he wanted his pal in charge. After the board made it clear that they were unequivocally moving on, Trump called them personally to harangue them. And if you think that sounds fishy, it’s not. It’s called being a good friend.”.
