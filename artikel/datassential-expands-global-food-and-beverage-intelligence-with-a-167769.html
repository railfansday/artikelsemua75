CHICAGO, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Datassential, the leading food and beverage intelligence platform, today announced a major expansion of its global offering with two AI-driven releases: Global Menu Analysis, a fresh way to analyze worldwide operator menus, and the Datassential AI Agent, which connects its database of 13M+ foodservice operators directly to enterprise AI tools.

It has been revealed that "Menus are where trends become real, and where sales are won," remarked Pascal Hartwig, General Manager of EMEA. "Global Menu Analysis gives food and beverage teams a direct line from what's happening on the menu to the operator serving it, so they can stop guessing and start acting. Pairing AI with our global menu data and trend tracking expertise opens up a level of insight the industry has never seen before.". Global Menu Analysis: Upcoming Tool for Sales Intelligence and Innovation.

Global Menu Analysis lets users search any food, beverage, flavor, or ingredient and see how common it is, where it appears most, what it is paired with, and how it is priced, from a countrywide view down to a single city. For sales and innovation teams, that means faster answers and sharper decisions. (Related: Breaking: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Faster ...)

In the coming months, teams will also be able to leverage Datassential's flagship Menu Adoption Cycle (MAC), built and calibrated for each market.

"Our customers have relied on Datassential menu data for decades to decide what to sell, where, and to whom," said Jim Emling, CEO at Datassential. "Bringing that to fresh markets, with menus and AI built in from day one, is one of the biggest investments we've made.".

Datassential AI Agent: Trusted Industry Data, Inside Your AI.

The Datassential AI Agent brings verified data on 13M+ global operators directly into the AI tools teams already use, including Claude, ChatGPT, and Copilot. Built on Model Context Protocol (MCP), it lets users ask plain-language questions (such as "Find me high-volume independents serving birria tacos in Amsterdam") and get accurate answers in seconds.

Sales teams can pair Datassential intelligence with their own data and internal systems to build target lists, identify whitespace, prepare for meetings, and size markets, all through a secure, read-only connection.

Sources indicate that with Global Menu Analysis and the Datassential AI Agent, food and beverage companies get a consistent, operator-level view of the market wherever they do business, so every pitch and product decision starts with real data. For more information, visit datassential.com.

Datassential is the global authority for food and beverage intelligence, helping brands, operators, and retailers make confident decisions in a complex and fast-changing industry. Founded in 2001, the firm delivers trusted food intelligence through its all-in-one platform, Datassential One, which brings together menu, consumer, and sales intelligence alongside AI-powered analysis. More than 90% of the leading foodservice and consumer packaged goods companies rely on Datassential to understand market change and stay ahead of what's next.
