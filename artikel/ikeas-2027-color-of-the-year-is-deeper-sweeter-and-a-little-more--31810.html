As a long year of colorful inspiration winds down, so many design brands are coming out of the woodwork with their 2027 trend predictions. IKEA has just joined the cohort with its 2027 Color of the Year: Wild Fig. Yes, the fiber-rich ficus fruit is setting the standard for the Scandi design destination. (Related: Why Some Parents Struggle To Let Their Children Grow Up...)

IKEA’s interpretation of the jammy color is deep and dark, with red, lilac, and brown undertones. It’s more imaginative than your typical neutral, but still grounded in nature. “Wild fig weaves together the richness of a deep botanical shade with the personality of a color that isn’t afraid to stand out,” the brand wrote in its release. It fits right in with the earthy color trends we’re anticipating next year. (Related: 3 Dark Personality Traits Linked To Infidelity...)

We’ve been keeping track of all the Color of the Year rolls out, and there’s a clear theme. Every pick so far can be found in your backyard. Valspar opted for the mid-toned blue of a cloudy sky, while Dutch Boy focused on the reddish-brown clay you’d find in the earth. And there’s been no shortage of greens, from Benjamin Moore’s pine-reminiscent pick to Sherwin-Williams’s bright celery shade. The trends reflect all the peace that’s found in a garden escape, and IKEA’s Wild Fig adds some sweetness and play, daring a bit past the typical tree shades to the fruit that blooms from its leaves.

The key to mastering this color, not unlike the fig jam on your charcuterie board, is what you pair it with. IKEA recommends pairing it with its 2026 Color of the Year, Rebel Pink, or an electric blue for contrast that brings out the character of the shade. And no matter your budget, there are so many easy ways to incorporate the look, from a patterned tray or tablecloth to a low-pile, checked rug.
