Nelson Dida believes that Ruben Amorim is showing he is a ‘great’ coach, and he gave his take on the Endrick to AC Milan rumours.

If anyone knows about the pressure of playing for Milan, it is Dida. Having been at the club from 2000 to 2010, he made over 300 official appearances for the Rossoneri and was part of an era that brought in eight major trophies under Carlo Ancelotti. According to recent reports, he is widely regarded as one of the finest goalkeepers of his generation and was decisive in the 2003 Champions League final penalty shootout against Juventus. He also returned briefly as a goalkeeping coach, working for a year with Maignan.

Dida spoke to reporters who were present at the Galbiati Trophy and he answered a few questions regarding his former team, with his words relayed by MilanNews.

How do you think Amorim is doing?

“Amorim is proving to be a great coach. He took over Milan and is having a fantastic season. I think the attention is right because at Milan you can never make mistakes, but he has the ability to keep the team together to do well in the league.”.

If Maignan is hurt, would you come back and put the gloves on?

Sources indicate that “We already have a goalkeeper at AC Milan: Lorenzo Torriani, a guy who’s doing well. I like him. Last year, too, he did well when he had opportunities. I think he’s a focused guy, if he gets more opportunities, he can continue to grow.”.

Are Milan growing towards something?

“Milan are slowly growing. They’re doing well in the league, I think we can still do more, while in Europe we’re still missing something.”.

Would Endrick be a good signing?

According to recent reports, “Endrick is a great player for me. When I see him play, he looks a bit like Romario, who is a bit small, but when he’s in the penalty area, he’s decisive. When he’s had the opportunity to play, he’s also scored goals.

“I think he can really do well for Real Madrid and the national team, but I don’t know if he’s available to join Milan. I hope a Brazilian returns to play in the Italian league soon and wears the Milan shirt.”.
