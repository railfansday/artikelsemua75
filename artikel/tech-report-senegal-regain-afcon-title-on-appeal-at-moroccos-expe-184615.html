Tech Report: Senegal regain AFCON title on appeal at Morocco's expense

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (89 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, senegal on Tuesday regained their Africa Cup of Nations title after winning an appeal at the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) against the decision by the Confederation of African Football to declare Morocco the winners following a chaotic final in January. CAS said in a statement it had "upheld the appeal by the Senegalese Football Federation (FSF) against the Confederation of African Football (CAF) and the Royal Moroccan Football Federation (FRMF)" by a "unanimous decision". FRANCE 24's Emerald Maxwell reports.
