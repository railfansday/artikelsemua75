Tech Report: Record-setting X-ray laser reliably generates flashes with wavelengths shorter than distances between atoms

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (71 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Researchers from DESY and European XFEL have demonstrated reliable production and transport of X-ray laser flashes with a wavelength of about 0.04 nanometers (or a photon energy of 30,000 electronvolts) at European XFEL, raising their own world record. This means that the X-ray's wavelength is much shorter than typical distances between neighboring atoms in solids, which enables the study of materials with higher spatial resolution than before.
