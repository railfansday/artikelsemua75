Sierra confirmed on November 23, 2026 that it is raising $1.75 billion in Series F funding at a $30 billion valuation, in a round led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners. The organization builds AI agents that handle customer interactions and carry out business tasks for large enterprises.

Sources indicate that the terms follow Sierra’s May 4, 2026 announcement that it was raising $950 million, led by Tiger Global and GV, at a valuation above $15 billion. Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks, GV and Tiger Global are participating again after previously serving as lead investors.

Sierra reported on October 30, 2026 that annual recurring revenue (ARR) had passed $400 million, including more than $150 million added in the third quarter. That quarterly addition exceeded the ARR it added during the entire prior year.

The manufacturer stated ARR took six months to double from $100 million to $200 million, then five months to double from $200 million to $400 million.

Customer adoption has accompanied that growth. Sierra reported working with more than 40% of the Fortune 50 in May and almost 50% by the October enhancement. The latter disclosure also described a large US retailer with more than $40 billion in revenue going from its first meeting with Sierra to a live agent in 27 days. It works only when software is highly autonomous and the outcome can be cleanly attributed to it; Sierra retains consumption-style pricing where that better fits the problem.

Ghostwriter sits above that pricing model. Sierra states teams define goals and guardrails, and the system builds, tests and deploys agents. A September 28, 2026 update made Ghostwriter proactive inside Slack and Teams: it can review customer conversations, recommend changes and experiments, monitor results and identify when those results are statistically significant.

Sierra says its constellation-of-models architecture assembles agents from 15 or more frontier, open-weight and proprietary models, selecting different models for tasks such as low-latency tool calling, classification, long-context reasoning and tone. Its Agent OS orchestrates those tasks, applies supervisors for guardrails and quality checks, and tracks provider latency, errors and timeouts so traffic can fail over to healthier models when a provider degrades.

The financing ultimately ties Sierra’s growth story to execution: rapid deployments supply the customer base, while its pricing model requires agents to operate autonomously and produce clearly attributable results.
