According to recent reports, another day, another festive fur-clad Jennifer Lopez moment to get us through the chilly season. The singer has spent the month covered in cozy statement pieces, from a winter-white bow-belt Valentino number to a hooded black alternative to several shearling-trimmed robes (worn both on stage and off). But for her latest go, she leaned into a less expected color palette.

Stepping out in Upcoming York City on Wednesday, November 17 to film The Drew Barrymore Show, the “Let’s Get Loud” singer was photographed bundled up in a voluminous high-pile faux-fur coat in warm blonde. The double-breasted outerwear, courtesy of Auter, was crafted with an oversize cropped silhouette and fluffy lapels, and was cinched at the waist with a matching belt. The soft light-tan shade set the style apart from Lopez’s usual go-to jackets, and sat in contrast to the rest of her otherwise monochrome look.

Underneath the coat, the artist wore a black fur crewneck top and a simple long skirt. The maxi, cut with a knee-high slit, reached her ankles—where it was met by a pair of jet-black heels with a pointed toe and strap at the heel. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

For jewelry and accessories, Lopez grabbed some oversize black sunglasses and carried a croc-embossed leather clutch, also in black. Designed by Liffner, the bag featured a slim east-west silhouette and was secured with a magnetic closure. She even kept things in the color family when it came to her rings, turning to David Webb for a couple of chunky Art Deco-inspired pieces in black and gold, with glistening diamond accents.
