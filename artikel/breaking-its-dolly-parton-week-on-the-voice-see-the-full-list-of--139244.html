Sources indicate that on Monday’s (Nov. 23) live episode of The Voice, the top 10 singers will honor a late legend for the show’s first-ever Dolly Parton Week — and Billboard is exclusively unveiling the full song list below.

Parton – who died in August at age 80 – had a frequent presence on the NBC singing competition, serving as a guest advisor to all four coaches in 2015, performing with her goddaughter (and then-coach) Miley Cyrus in 2016, and singing “I Will Always Love You” remotely on the season 23 finale in 2023 in honor of Blake Shelton’s retirement from the show. Now, her presence will be felt again as the 10 remaining singers cover beloved songs from her deep catalog.

Sister country duo Ferryn & Brinkley — who are on brand-new coach Riley Green’s team — have an especially close connection to Parton, having grown up in her Tennessee hometown and also performing at her Dollywood park and resort before auditioning for the show.

Sources indicate that “There are so many reasons why she is such an influence on us,” the Wright sisters tell Billboard. “Many people don’t know the thousands of songs she has written, because only a handful are mainstream, but she was an incredible songwriter! She never forgot where she was from, and her songs tell that story. We love that Dolly was always true to herself and didn’t try to fit into the standards of country music during her career. You either liked her or you didn’t, but she was one of the most kindhearted, genuine human beings (we think) on this planet. She did so much for our community, and still to this day her legacy continues. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

“She is EVERYTHING in our town,” they continued of their Sevierville birth place, “and the fact that we are on this stage this week involved in a tribute for her fills our hearts. What an honor! The beautiful thing about music is that we can continue people’s memories, even when they no longer walk this Earth with us! We will always love you Dolly! This week is for you.”.

Below, find the full list of Dolly Parton tribute songs that will be performed on Monday night’s episode, as well as more Billboard-exclusive quotes from the top 10.

The Voice singers: BIGCOUNTRY (Team Queen) and Dakota Cohen (Team Adam).

It has been revealed that hot 100 peak: No. 16 in September 2026 following Parton’s death; the song had previously peaked at No. 60 in 1974.

Dakota Cohen: “Dolly would always tell the truth about life and family with a sense of humor and a whole lot of glamour. I’ve always admired that about her. She was so unapologetically herself, and that inspires me to write songs about my life with the same honesty and humor I’d have if I were telling a story to a friend.”.

The Voice singers: Joe Henley (Team Riley) and Koumba Diallo (Team Kelly).

Sources indicate that hot 100 peak: No. 39 in February 2006.

Koumba Diallo: “Honestly, I didn’t grow up knowing a lot of Dolly’s music, but the more I learned about her, the more I realized she was so much more than just a voice. She was one of the hardest-working people in the business, and she was smart about it, too. She wrote her own songs, ran her own business, and never let anyone underestimate her because of how she looked or where she came from. She was also so quick and funny. In every interview I watched, she had the whole room laughing within seconds. Most of all, she was kind, and she used everything she built to help others, like sending books to millions of kids. As a 16-year-old just starting out, she is the kind of woman I look up to, and getting to honor her on that stage means everything to me.”.

The Voice singers: Ferryn & Brinkley (Team Riley) and Kyle Davis (Team Kelly).

In a recent development, hot 100 peak: No. 1 (two weeks) starting in October 1983.

Kyle Davis: “I was always amazed at her sense of philanthropy and how easily she moved through social and political groups with so much ease.”. (Related: Deploy Hugging Face models easily with Amazon SageMaker...)

The Voice singers: Lexi Stephens (Team Adam) and Mia Knight (Team Queen).

Release year: 2023, on Parton’s Rockstar album.

According to recent reports, hot 100 peak: N/A for the Dolly & Miley version, but the 2013 Cyrus original spent three weeks at No. 1 starting in September 2013.

Lexi Stephens: “Miley and Dolly’s connection was just so special so I’m so grateful to be singing a song they worked on together.”.

In a recent development, mia Knight: “One of Dolly’s quotes that I really relate to is: ‘Don’t ever try to be anybody else. You’re the only you there is.’ It’s a constant reminder that being myself and staying true to who I am as an artist is important. I don’t want to try to be someone else or make art just to fit in. I want my work to show who I am and what makes me unique.”.

The Voice singers: Connor Closs (Team Adam) and Brianna Cameron (Team Kelly).

Release year: 1992 (Parton’s original recording was released in 1974, but Closs and Cameron are covering Houston’s blockbuster The Bodyguard version).

According to recent reports, hot 100 peak: No. 1 (for 14 weeks) starting in November 1992.

Recent findings suggest that connor Closs: “Even though I was never a huge fan of country music growing up, I couldn’t help but be amazed by the genius songwriting of Dolly Parton. Her story has always stuck with me, too. Even though she grew up in a poor family, she never let anything stop her from sharing her music with the world, and continued to live her life with kindness, love, and generosity. Dolly will always be an icon in the music world, and it is an honor to have the opportunity to pay tribute to her on The Voice.”.

Industry observers note that brianna Cameron: “Dolly Parton is the best blueprint for how to be a fearless storyteller and stay 100% true to yourself. As a young artist and songwriter myself, I’m so inspired by how she could turn real, honest emotion into timeless songs that connect with everyone. Getting to honor her legacy on The Voice stage means the world to me.”.
