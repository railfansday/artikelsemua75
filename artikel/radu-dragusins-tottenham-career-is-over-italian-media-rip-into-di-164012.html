Radu Dragusin is finally getting regular minutes on the pitch, but he was forced to leave Tottenham Hotspur in order to do so.

The 24-year-old joined Fiorentina on loan for the 2026-27 campaign, with the deal including an obligation to buy with the Serie A side.

It is understood, however, that certain criteria must be met for the clause to be triggered, including number of appearances throughout the season.

If there are any performance-related clauses included, Tottenham may struggle a little bit more to offload the centre-back next summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

His latest performance was in Fiorentina's damning 3-0 defeat to Monza on Monday, 23 November, a game which left the team 16th in Serie A.

Paolo Vanoli's side never got firing at the U-Power Stadium, but it was even more embarrassing for Spurs' on-loan defender at the back.

Italian media didn't hold back in their scathing reviews of Dragusin, either, and some of their words will have cut close to the bone.

Italian pundit Riccardo Trevisani labelled the Romanian international a "disaster," awarding him a 4.5 out of 10 for his performance [Labaro Viola].

Industry observers note that that wasn't just a one-off either. Monza News described his game in one word - "disorientated" - while Tutto Mercato Web stated he "lost his bearing" against the opposition attack. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Several websites and newspapers rated him below a 5 out of 10, and anything close to that was being kind to the player.

Fiorentina may be thinking twice about splashing the cash on Dragusin's signature, and that could force him back onto the bench in weeks to come.

It certainly didn't help that Dragusin was the only player in the entire game to pick up a yellow card, being punished for his second foul.

In 90 minutes, he had one off-target shot, made two tackles, seven clearances, and four recoveries, as well as entering into 11 duels [FotMob].

He did, however, only win 60 per cent of his ground duels and 67 per cent of his aerial duels, while also being dispossessed four times [Sofascore].

Fiorentina are now just one point above the relegation zone, and it's difficult to see the loan deal being made permanent if they're playing Serie B football next season.
