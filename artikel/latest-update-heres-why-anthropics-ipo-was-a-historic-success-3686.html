Recent findings suggest that shares of Anthropic (ANT +17.07%) jumped 17% from its initial public offering (IPO) price of $83 on Thursday. The gains capped off a blockbuster debut for the artificial intelligence (AI) pioneer on its first day as a public company.

According to recent reports, anthropic's stock price opened at $111.50 and rose as high as $115.10 following its listing on the Nasdaq stock exchange. The AI model developer's share price closed at $97.17, placing its fully diluted market capitalization at a staggering $1.8 trillion. The AI trailblazer intends to use the proceeds to pay for the enormous amount of data centers, semiconductor chips, and other infrastructure it needs to train its cutting-edge models. According to recent reports, the sizable sum places Anthropic ahead of Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX -3.53%) in the all-time largest IPO rankings. SpaceX raised $75 billion in initial base offering proceeds in June.

Sources indicate that trading volume was also off the charts. Roughly 840 million shares of Anthropic were traded on Thursday, compared to about 520 million for SpaceX on its IPO day.

Those are stunning growth rates of 841% in 2024 and 1,088% in 2025. And for the first nine months of 2026, Anthropic's revenue growth accelerated to 1,206%, surpassing $33 billion.
