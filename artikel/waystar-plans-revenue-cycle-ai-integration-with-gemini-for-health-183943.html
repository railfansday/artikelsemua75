In a recent development, waystar announced on November 24, 2026 that it will integrate its AI-powered revenue-cycle analytics and insurance-eligibility tools with Google Cloud’s Gemini for healthcare. The planned connection would let healthcare providers investigate payment performance and initiate eligibility checks through natural-language interactions rather than switch among separate systems.

The integration extends Waystar’s AltitudeAI portfolio and a multi-year collaboration with Google Cloud on generative and agentic AI for healthcare payment operations. Waystar CEO Matt Hawkins noted a more autonomous revenue cycle requires “trusted intelligence, deep connectivity, and the ability to take action,” rather than AI alone. The project’s stated goals are lower administrative burdens, faster and more accurate provider payments, and more resources for patient care. In a recent development, the analytics component comes from Waystar’s recently introduced conversational AI. Waystar reports that early adopters using it reduced the time spent analyzing complex claims and payer-payment datasets by up to 75%. That figure covers early use of the existing product, not the planned Gemini integration, but it shows the workflow Waystar wants to bring into Google’s environment: natural-language investigation of large payment datasets. According to recent reports, waystar’s Model Context Protocol (MCP) connector supplies the other part of the proposed workflow. It is intended to support natural-language eligibility verification, which the announcement describes as a critical task that is often manual. Together, the two capabilities address analysis and action: a provider could examine revenue-cycle performance, then begin a coverage-related follow-up without moving between disconnected tools.

In a recent development, google Cloud introduced Gemini for healthcare on November 10, 2026, initially in preview. The platform connects electronic health records, billing platforms, clinical guidelines and Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services datasets, then identifies next steps and executes multi-step workflows within defined guardrails and human oversight. Waystar was already listed among the launch integrations for eligibility verification and payment source-of-truth data; the new announcement adds conversational analytics and MCP-based interaction to that relationship.

Data controls are consequential because the proposed workflow joins payment operations with clinical and administrative context. Patient records, clinical data, prompts and outputs remain within an organization’s dedicated Google Cloud enterprise environment, and Google Cloud notes customer data is not used to train its foundation models. The platform also provides access controls, audit logging and verifiable citations, while agents inherit customers’ existing role-based permissions. It is intended for Gemini Enterprise Standard and Plus customers, with per-user seat subscriptions or pay-as-you-go pricing available.

The announcement outlines a latest distribution route for Waystar inside Google’s healthcare workspace and deeper revenue-cycle functionality for a platform still in preview. The practical test is whether conversational analysis and eligibility actions can work together reliably across connected systems. That will determine whether the integration can reduce administrative handoffs while preserving the data controls and human oversight required for healthcare operations.
