Andrea Cambiaso and Zeki Çelik are back in the Juventus squad for Sunday’s game against Venezia, but Pierre Kalulu and Nicolás González both miss out.

According to Calciomercato.it, the pair returned to full training in the final session at the Continassa. That’s a welcome boost for Luciano Spalletti, who is short of options on the flanks without Kalulu. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

Spalletti confirmed at his pre-match press conference that Kalulu and González won’t be involved, along with the players who are out long-term. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

In a recent development, gonzález has been out for a month with a soleus injury in his calf, which he picked up against Rennes. Juventus will decide in the coming days whether he can return against Omonia Nicosia in the Europa League next week or will have to wait for the trip to Como. Kalulu is set to be out until at least early December.

Kenan Yildiz, Manuel Locatelli, Khéphren Thuram and Kamil Grabara remain sidelined.

Nick Woltemade is included after recovering from the flu that kept him out before the international break.

Seventh-placed Juventus host second-from-bottom Venezia at the Allianz Stadium on Sunday, with kick-off at 12:30 CET [11:30 GMT].

Juventus squad: Pinsoglio, Vicario, Neto; Çelik, Bremer, Gatti, Kelly, Cambiaso, Rugani, Lucumí, Cabal; Koopmeiners, Douglas Luiz, McKennie, Sarr; Conceição, Kolo Muani, Zhegrova, Boga, Alajbegović, Ekhator, Woltemade.
