It has been revealed that QPR defeated Millwall on Saturday, winning 1-0 through a first-half goal from the returning Nicolas Madsen. However, one man who sat out was captain Jimmy Dunne, who picked up an ankle injury while away with the Republic of Ireland over the international break.

The Hoops make the quick turnaround to face Norwich City on Tuesday, and manager Julien Stephan has admitted skipper Dunne is unlikely to return for the Carrow Road trip. As quoted by West London Sport, he stated: “Not definitely, but probably not. It’s not a bad one. I don’t know exactly when he will be able to come back. He is still in a little bit of pain, so let’s see in the next days how the situation evolves.”.

In a recent development, in better news for QPR, full-back Tariq Lamptey has returned to training and is likely to be available despite also missing the Millwall win. The 26-year-old has been out with an ankle matter and last played in the 1-1 draw away at West Ham on October 9th. (Related: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for Summarization u...)

According to recent reports, stephan has not been afraid to make changes and he is expected to shuffle the ranks for the trip to Norwich City on Tuesday night. Despite Dunne’s absence, he does still have room to make a change at centre-back if he wishes.

Recent findings suggest that ronnie Edwards captained QPR in the Millwall triumph, and he was partnered by Jake Clarke-Salter at centre-back on their way to a welcome clean sheet. The former Chelsea prospect will be the favourite to partner the stand-in skipper as a result. (Related: Industry News: Welcome spaCy to the Hugging Face Hub...)

But, if he wishes to make a switch, Stephan will have Liam Morrison to call upon. The 23-year-old is yet to make a competitive appearance this season and has been an unused substitute for 10 of the last 11 Championship matches.

According to recent reports, if one of the centre-backs was to drop out, it would likely be Clarke-Salter in order to manage his minutes. The 29-year-old has had injury problems over recent seasons but he has only missed two league matchday squads this term.

QPR don’t have the biggest senior squad in the Championship with 26 first-team players. However, they are well stocked in all areas and have already dealt with injuries this term, mainly in attack.

According to recent reports, stephan has shown a willingness to dip into the academy when needed as well. Leon Scarlett has emerged in the senior group, and 20-year-old midfielder Isak Alemayehu has appeared in matchday squads fairly regularly as well.

The Hoops have built the squad well over the past 18 months or so. The summer window saw them boost revamp their options at full-back and in goal, and there’s a balanced and well-rounded squad at Stephan’s disposal as a result.

It means he has room to make changes and ample cover when injuries hit.
