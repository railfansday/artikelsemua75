Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler revealed that Charalampos Kostoulas picked up a knock while on international duty with Greece.

Kostoulas - who has five goals in all competitions - has been Brighton’s main striker this term but was on the bench at the MKM Stadium as Georginio led the line in the 2-0 loss at Hull.

“Because Kostoulas came back from the international break with a small issue,” noted Hurzeler when asked why Georginio started ahead of Kostoulas.

“Therefore we had to make this decision, it wasn't a tactical decision, we had to take care of the health of the player.

”It's just a small issue, he came back from being with Greece, so something was there, but he didn't feel like 100 per cent.”.

Hull pounced at the death as Oli McBurnie struck twice in added time to snatch a 2-0 victory against high-flying Brighton at the MKM Stadium.

Hurzeler’s side were dominant but could not find a way through, thwarted on several occasions by Hull goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis.

Their push for an eighth victory of the season was then halted by Joao Costinha’s dismissal a minute from time and, from there, the wheels came off.

McBurnie bundled home a fine Hull move two minutes into stoppage time and the Tigers were not done there as the striker buried his fourth goal in as many matches to lift his side up to 11th.

Sources indicate that hurzeler added: "For 65 minutes we indicated a good performance then we dropped our intensity, levels and standards. In the Premier League they can punish you especially after a red card. Overall a frustrating afternoon a frustrating game but we have to keep going.

"We will analyse it. That's the process we follow. I always emphasize keep staying humble. We have to outwork the opponent otherwise the opponents are too good in the Premier League and that's what we have to do better next time.".

Brighton, for the first time this season, seemed to run out of gas in the closing stages and the the Costinha sending off tipped the balance in Hull’s favour.

Hurzeler said: "Of course when you are one man down, the red card plays a role. We made a decision, at that moment, a wrong decision, but we will analyse it. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

“My team was desperate to win it, of course, in certain elements be more patient, be more disciplined, be more structured.

“That's what we missed, not only in the last minutes, also in the minutes before, and again, we will analyse it and make it better.”.
