Recent findings suggest that opening at #1 at the box office last year, Berk & Olsen’s Novocaine stars Jack Quaid as a bank employee who cannot feel physical pain. Berk & Olsen are also known for their Paramount+ genre-bender Significant Other and the SXSW horror comedy Villains, among other projects.
