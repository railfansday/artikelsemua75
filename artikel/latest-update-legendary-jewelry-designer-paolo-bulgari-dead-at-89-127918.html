The fashion world has lost a legend.

In a recent development, paolo Bulgari—famed jewelry designer and chairman of the Bvlgari Group—has died, the firm confirmed on social media Nov. 21. He was 89. (Related: Breaking: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming G...)

“It is with deep sadness that we learned of the passing of Paolo Bulgari, grandson of the company’s founder Sotirio Bulgari and Chairman of the Bvlgari Group since 1984,” the company stated in a statement shared to Instagram. “Our deepest condolences go to his family, friends and all those who had the honor of sharing this journey with him.”. (Related: Latest Update: One Year Since the “DeepSeek Moment”...)

Sources indicate that “Thank you, Mr. Paolo,” they added, “your legacy as a brilliant beacon of creative inspiration will continue to inspire generations to come.”.

Born in Rome, Italy, in 1937, Bulgari—who shared four kids with ex-wife Alice Valli as well as two kids with wife Maite Caprio—was destined to carry on the family business from a young age.

“Paolo Bulgari began collaborating with his father in 1960,” the statement explained, “specializing in the knowledge of precious stones, of which he was one of the world’s leading experts, and refining the sensibility and aesthetic sense that are at the root of the Bvlgari style.”.

And it wasn’t long until he started making his own mark on the brand.

“A true creative soul, he poured his heart into his craft and into nurturing the talents of young designers,” added the statement, “leaving an indelible mark on the world of jewelry.”.

In fact, Bulgari, alongside his brothers Gianni Bulgari and Nicola Bulgari, played an integral role in shaping the brand’s distinctive style, becoming known for their use of bold shapes and standout gems.

They also expanded the business from jewelry into watches, perfumes and even hotels.

And over the decades, the brand’s creations have adorned stars from Elizabeth Taylor and Princess Diana to Zendaya and Anne Hathaway.

Recent findings suggest that just last month, Kim Kardashian stated she might just “faint” in a video shared to Instagram Oct. 28 while getting a private tour of Taylor’s jewelry collection at the Bvlgari store in Italy, in which she was able to try on one of the late actress’ beloved pieces—an emerald and diamond necklace gifted to her by Richard Burton in 1962.

As Kardashian put it, “I am freaking out!”.

And she of course had to thank her stylist Law Roach for making the dream come true, calling him on the phone to thank him for the “best” birthday present ever.

And while it’s true that Bulgari’s influence on fashion has stood the test of time, that’s because he believed “everything can be a source of inspiration.”.

In a recent development, as he used to say, per Bvlgari’s statement, “There are no rules in creativity. Never.”.
