Freiburg striker Igor Matanovic believes his team’s newfound ability to grind out difficult victories represents a significant step forward under head coach Julian Schuster. Speaking to Get German Football News in the mixed zone after Saturday’s 1-0 Bundesliga victory over Elversberg, the Croatian international explained why Freiburg are now consistently collecting points in matches that might previously have ended in frustrating draws.

Matanovic’s 68th-minute winner against the promoted Saarland club extended Freiburg’s winning streak to five matches across all competitions. The Breisgauer have recovered impressively from their October struggles, defeating Wolfsburg in the DFB-Pokal before adding victories against Augsburg, Trabzonspor, Union Berlin, and Elversberg. The latest success also moved Freiburg level on points with Bayern Munich near the top of the Bundesliga table.

The 23-year-old believes Freiburg’s improvement reflects considerably more than a fortunate run of results. Matanovic emphasized the squad’s collective resilience, suggesting that the team’s current momentum has been built upon a healthy foundation and a willingness to discuss shortcomings openly.

The striker specifically recalled Freiburg’s response to three consecutive defeats earlier in the campaign, praising his teammates for refusing to allow the setbacks to undermine their confidence. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

Saturday’s hard-fought victory provided another illustration of that mentality. As documented in GGFN‘s match report and player ratings, Freiburg struggled to create clear opportunities against a spirited Elversberg side before finally breaking through in the second half.

Sources indicate that matanovic explained that maintaining concentration and relying on familiar tactical principles were particularly key in such circumstances. Aggressive pressing, collective defensive work, and persistence in winning possession eventually helped Freiburg create the decisive opening.

According to recent reports, “It’s tremendous. Obviously, it’s very nice to be collecting the points as well,” Matanovic said when asked about Freiburg’s five consecutive victories. “I think that’s the difference compared to last season. What we were missing were the points. We often played well, but then we’d come away with just one point. Now we’re winning the dirty games.”.

Sources indicate that “Even recently against Union Berlin, you know how unpleasant they can be to play against, and we still managed to win 2-0,” the Croatian continued. “There’s genuinely a very high level of quality in this team, and of course we’re enjoying a certain flow. But the flow we have comes from this healthy foundation that we can always rely upon. I often mention the three consecutive defeats we suffered. How many teams collapse after something like that? How many lose their confidence?”.

According to recent reports, “But we always get back to basics,” Matanovic went on. “We address things openly and honestly, and that’s how we find our way back into our flow. Right now, we’re simply very, very difficult opponents to face. You have to maintain your focus, especially when you’re not creating many chances or opportunities. Then you have these basics you can rely upon. Keep pushing forward, press high, make life difficult for the opposition, and block plenty of balls.”.

“I think we also won possession back many times in the first half,” Matanovic concluded. “That’s something we force through our own efforts, and that’s how we eventually create scoring chances again. There weren’t many chances, but I think we could even have rewarded ourselves with more goals in the end.”.
