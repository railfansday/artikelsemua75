The SIE Society (Social Impact Entertainment Society) has unveiled the lineup for Impact+Profit26, its flagship conference “showing that storytellers don’t have to choose between impact and profit.”.

Industry observers note that TV uber-creator Chuck Lorre, Grey’s Anatomy EP Zoanne Clack and A Different World showrunner Felicia Pride are among those scheduled to take part in the two-day event, which is set for Dec. 7-8. (Related: Industry News: ‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok f...)

In a recent development, in addition, entertainment-education pioneer Miguel Sabido has been tapped to receive the SIE Impact Entertainment Visionaries Award at the conference, which will be held for the first time at USC Annenberg, home of the Hollywood, Health & Society, a program of the USC Norman Lear Center. Industry observers note that organizers mentioned the new venue carries special meaning for the field. USC Annenberg’s connection to impact storytelling traces back to the pioneering Soap Summits of the 1990s and early 2000s, which helped spark television’s social impact movement and paved the way for Hollywood, Health & Society.

More than 250 producers, executives, financiers and changemakers are expected to attend the conference, which is presented for the fourth consecutive year in collaboration with the Johns Hopkins Center for Communication Programs.

Industry observers note that “Bringing Impact+Profit to USC Annenberg connects us to the history of entertainment education in Hollywood,” noted Will Nix, a founder and co-executive director of the SIE Society, whose creative work as a producer includes film, television, theater and digital media productions. “For four years, Johns Hopkins Center for Communication Programs has helped us bring evidence and storytelling into the same room. Impact+Profit is built on partnerships, and we’re grateful to every collaborator who makes it possible.”.

It has been revealed that sabido will be honored on Day 1 of the event. The presentation will take place during “The Man Who Proved Stories Can Change the World: A Conversation With Miguel Sabido,” where Sabido will be joined onstage by Robert Rippberger, a founder and co-executive director of the SIE Society, filmmaker and author of The Power of Storytelling: Social Impact Entertainment.

“Long before anyone had a name for this field, Miguel demonstrated the world that a story told with care and purpose could change how millions of people live their lives,” Rippberger said. “His work is the blueprint for what social impact entertainment can achieve. As we celebrate his legacy, we’re also challenging the next generation of storytellers to carry that vision forward with the same courage and rigor.”.

In a recent development, the conference will close with “A Conversation With Chuck Lorre,” featuring the Golden Globe-winning writer and producer behind such comedy hits as The Big Bang Theory, Two and a Half Men, Mom, Young Sheldon and Georgie & Mandy’s First Marriage. Lorre, who also founded the Chuck Lorre Family Foundation, will sit down with Jay Sures, vice chairman of United Talent Agency, for a closing-night discussion on his career and his philanthropic work.

Other highlights on the lineup:.

Additional sessions will focus on AI and social media for measuring impact, reality TV as a force for good, faith and media, the podcast-to-feature pipeline, grassroots impact for independent filmmakers, and a hands-on workshop on designing transitional characters with the Sabido Method.

The Hollywood Reporter is a media sponsor of Impact+Profit26. The event is also supported by First Entertainment Credit Union, NBCUniversal, World Bank Group; media sponsor KCRW; and partners Population Media Center and Schulz Entertainment Law and the UTA Foundation.

In a recent development, regular tickets are $375 and student tickets are $150 (valid enrollment required). Tickets include two days of panels and workshops, six networking sessions, and food and drink. Registered attendees can use the SIE Society conference app for the agenda, curated networking and badge scanning. Tickets and the full agenda can be found at conference.siesociety.org.

According to recent reports, the SIE Society is a global community of nearly 1,800 entertainment, corporateand nonprofit professionals, from all storytelling media platforms, who are working at the intersection of storytelling and social change. The SIE Society works to support collaboration and measurable impact across industries through curated programming, thought leadership and year-round engagement.
