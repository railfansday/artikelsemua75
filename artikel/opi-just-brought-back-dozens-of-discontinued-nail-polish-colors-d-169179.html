OPI has cracked open its polish vault, and some of the best, previously retired OPI nail polish colors have officially made their comeback. In honor of its 45th anniversary, the brand has brought back about a dozen iconic shades that mani lovers have been begging to see again, plus even more shades inspired by OGs for a grand total of 24 new(ish) polishes.

It has been revealed that meet OPI’s Most Requested Collection, which combines several previously-retired fan favorites with some fresh shades inspired by some of the brand’s most beloved colors. The collection includes everything from vampy Lincoln Park at Midnight and nostalgic pink Mod About You Pastel to Route Beer Float, a very ‘90s, shimmery mauve. This fall 2026 collection includes 24 permanent shades, meaning these colors aren’t just making a brief cameo before disappearing again—they’re back for good. Twelve of them are offered in the classic OPI Nail Lacquer formula, while the other 12 have come back in the longer-lasting Infinite Shine option.

Whether you’re excited to reunite with an old favorite, discover a cult classic you missed the first time around, or try a new shade inspired by some of these icons, I highly recommend giving the full collection a scroll. To get you started, here are 12 shades from this buzzy launch that I’m especially excited to try. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

She’s baaaaccckkkkk! The inky purple shade originally introduced exactly 20 years ago and is the shimmery sister to one of my all-time faves, Lincoln Park After Dark. You know those polishes that look black until the light hits them? That’s exactly what you’re getting with this ultra moody, ultra iconic shade. In a recent development, another shade straight from the vault, this 2012 rich, deep violet with red shimmer became an instant favorite when it originally debuted. If you love burgundy nails for the colder months, consider your next manicure handled.

It may be fall, but not every manicure needs to be dark and moody rn. Mod About You Pastel polish brings a soft, creamy pink into the mix, making it a pretty option for anyone who prefers a sweet, minimalist manicure over the season’s deeper shades. It’s a slightly warmer-toned sister to another one of my faves, Mod About You.

Originally released in 1997, Route Beer Float is the oldest shade OPI brought back for this collection. A mauve-meets-caramel shimmery shade makes it a particularly chic option for the chocolate brown and chai latte manicures that have been everywhere this season.

Quiet luxury nail lovers, this one’s for you. Berlin There, Doin’ That is a creamy, light taupe that puts a fresh spin on OPI’s beloved Berlin There, Done That shade. It’s a little lighter and softer than the original, with a greige-like finish that makes it the perfect understated neutral.

OPI Ink fans, rejoice! Your favorite deep violet is getting a second life with Don’t Over Ink It. The shimmery, deep, inky navy-purple has all the moody, mysterious appeal of the original, but this latest version is shimmery rather than sparkly and leans a little more navy. Overall, though, the two are pretty dang similar. Is this your sign to revisit your purple polish era? I think yes.

Say bonjour to your next favorite neutral. This chic, creamy mauve-ish nude pays homage to the beloved Tickle My France-y shade. It’s more of a neutral than its predecessor, which was a much rosier, dusty-rose-meets-mauve shade. It’s soft, sophisticated, and gives your nails that effortlessly polished, je ne sais quoi look. Oui oui!

Industry observers note that did ya miss her? Talk about a throwback... It’s All Greek to Me is back from the archives, bringing its vivid, shimmery fuchsia pink finish along for the ride. The bold, metallic shade makes a statement, so I can see why OPI fans begged for its return. I’m ready to paint a couple coats on my fingertips ahead of the holiday party season. Who indicates bright pink nails are just for summer?

Industry observers note that I do! I do say yes to Suzi Takes a Vow. It’s a soft, romantic peachy pink that’s giving major bridal mani energy. Wedding bells or not, consider this your sign to commit to this classic light neutral.

Industry observers note that you know when you just have to try something based on the name alone? That’s how I feel about this one. With a name like Vampire State Building, you already know this is going to be dramatic. The deep burgundy shade—originally released in 2000—delivers all the vampy energy you could want from a dark red manicure.
