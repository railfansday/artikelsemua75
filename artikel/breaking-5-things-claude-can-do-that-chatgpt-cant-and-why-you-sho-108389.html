One of the most underrated reasons to download Claude on your iPhone is how well it works with Apple's native apps.

Through its iOS integrations, Claude can add items to your existing Apple Reminders lists, draft calendar events and prepare text messages and emails.

According to recent reports, imagine planning Thanksgiving dinner with Claude. Once you've settled on a menu, you can ask the chatbot to add the ingredients to your grocery list in Reminders. There's no need to copy and paste everything into another app.

Although ChatGPT has its own scheduled reminders and integrates with Apple Intelligence, Claude offers more direct access to Apple Reminders through its mobile app.

Give it a try with a prompt like: "Plan five easy weeknight dinners for my family and add the ingredients to my grocery list in Apple Reminders.".

If you've been using ChatGPT for years, switching to another AI assistant can feel like starting a upcoming job where nobody knows you.

It has been revealed that anthropic has a clever solution. Claude offers a dedicated memory import feature that helps you transfer personal preferences, professional details and other information from competing chatbots.

Claude provides a prompt that you can paste into your existing AI assistant. Once it generates a summary of what it knows about you, simply copy the response into Claude's memory settings.

Recent findings suggest that you won't transfer your entire conversation history, but you can give Claude a substantial head start. And you don't need a paid subscription to try it.

Both ChatGPT and Claude now offer Skills, which are essentially reusable sets of instructions that teach AI how to perform specific tasks.

However, Claude has an advantage when it comes to bringing those instructions into the applications you already use for work.

The custom Skills you enable in Claude are also available through its Microsoft 365 add-ins for Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.

Sources indicate that you could teach Claude how you like your business proposals formatted, how you organize monthly expenses or how you respond to customer emails. And rather than explaining your preferences every time you open a upcoming document, Claude can follow the instructions you've already established.

ChatGPT also supports Skills in some Office experiences, but Claude's integration spans all four applications.

Perhaps the most fascinating development in AI right now is that you don't necessarily need to write a prompt to teach a chatbot something new.

With Claude's workflow recording feature, Mac customers can demonstrate a task while Claude captures their screen, mouse clicks, keyboard activity and optional voice narration.

Claude then turns the demonstration into a reusable Skill that you can review and save.

In a recent development, think about all those little tasks you do on your computer every week. Maybe you organize receipts, patch a spreadsheet or compile information from several websites.

Instead of writing detailed instructions for an AI assistant, you can show Claude how you normally do the work.

OpenAI offers similar functionality through Codex's Record & Replay feature, but Claude makes this approach available through its general-purpose desktop assistant.

ChatGPT is excellent at generating images, but Claude takes a different approach to creative work.

Through its design tools, you can give Claude your existing logos, fonts, color palettes and design guidelines to establish a reusable visual identity.

You don't need to be a professional designer to appreciate the possibilities.

If you're starting a small business, running an Etsy shop or launching a newsletter, Claude can help establish a consistent look across your marketing materials, presentations and websites.

Rather than uploading the same reference images and explaining your preferred aesthetic every time, you can work from an established design system that Claude applies across projects.

ChatGPT can follow brand guidelines and reference templates, but Claude's dedicated design system offers a more structured approach to maintaining visual consistency.
