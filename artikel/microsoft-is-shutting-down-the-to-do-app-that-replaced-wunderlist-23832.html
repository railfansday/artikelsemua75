Microsoft revealed this week that its To Do app is shutting down soon, taking the legacy of Wunderlist with it.

Industry observers note that microsoft is shutting down its To Do app at the end of June 2027, with focus moving to the newer Planner app. The firm explains:. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

According to recent reports, as we bring personal task management into Planner, we will phase out To Do. The To Do apps will retire on June 30, 2027. Connections that other apps use to access To Do tasks will continue beyond that date, and we’ll provide separate notice before those connections are retired. We’re also updating existing task integrations across Microsoft 365 to connect with Planner, so you’ll be able to create or manage tasks in the Microsoft experiences you already use. Although To Do tasks are already visible in Planner, they will not be migrated to the latest private plan. Customers using personal Microsoft accounts will need to choose another app. Our To Do transition guidance  will help you understand what’s changing and plan your next steps. Recent findings suggest that to Do was built on the foundation of Wunderlist, a wildly popular to do list app that Microsoft purchased in 2015. Wunderlist later shut down in 2020.

Microsoft’s shuffle, notably, leaves general consumers without a replacement app. To Do was available to anyone with a Microsoft account, where Planner is available only to enterprise/business owners. So, for long-time Wunderlist users who followed Microsoft migration, this is the end of the road and it’s officially time to move to another app.
