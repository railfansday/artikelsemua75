📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the Takata airbag recall scandal started over a decade ago. To date, some 67 million airbags have been included in that series of recalls across dozens of brands. That list is expanding again today, as Toyota is recalling another 314,000 cars in the USA. W.

According to recent reports, hile the automaker hasn't spelled out exactly which models are part of the recall, owners should consider getting the fix as one of their highest priorities. According to the automaker, the new recall of 314,000 cars affects both Toyota and Lexus products. The tech giant did not break down the make and year models impacted, but the brand says that in all affected vehicles, the matter is consistent with previous Takata recalls. The inflator in the airbag has a propellant inside that can degrade over time due to humidity and temperature fluctuations. (Related: One Familiar Item Of Clothing Scored Lowest for Intelli...)

Recent findings suggest that if that inflator ruptures due to that degradation, it can send metal fragments through the airbag and into the cabin. Takata airbags impacted by this matter have injured and killed people over the years. Perhaps the biggest concern is that there is no warning sign of when this might happen. Industry observers note that toyota says it'll replace the front passenger airbag inflator or the entire assembly depending on the model. While we do not have a make and model list, Toyota does say that owners can cross-check their VIN at Toyota.com/recall or at NHTSA.gov/recalls. In the coming days, we'll likely see the formal documents published by the NHTSA with specifics.

It is worth noting that recalls like this don't necessarily suggest that Toyota was installing faulty airbags recently. According to the NHTSA, 9% of the bad airbags that went out into the automotive population are still out there. That might not sound like much, but that small sliver still accounts for over six million cars. Based on the latest available data, the 2017 Lexus GX460 was the most recent Toyota product included in Takata recalls. Other notable models include the 2008-2014 Lexus IS F, the 2010-2016 Toyota 4Runner, and the 2012 Lexus LFA.
