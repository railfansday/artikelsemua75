US President Donald Trump said on Thursday that he had urged his Russian counterpart, Vladimir Putin, to cease "military aggression" in Europe during their talks in China.

"I spent a lot of time talking about the fact that I would like to see President Putin pull back all Military aggression against territories throughout Europe, in the hopes that a Deal can be made between Russia and Ukraine, and made swiftly," Trump wrote on his Truth Social network.

In a recent development, however, the US president did not specify which European territories he mentioned when communicating with Putin. This week, Poland — a NATO member — accused Russia of deliberately violating its airspace.

According to recent reports, trump added that he believes there is a "strong possibility" of such a deal between Russia and Ukraine. Trump's post on Truth Social came a day after he hosted NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte in Florida. They issued a joint statement afterward calling on Russia not to expand the war in Ukraine.

"We also talked about, at length, the importance of Russia and Ukraine avoiding any actions that intentionally, or unintentionally, expand the War — and the importance of obtaining a peaceful resolution to the War, as rapidly as possible," the statement remarked.

Industry observers note that trump's comments represent a shift in tone after he cordially called Putin a friend during their meeting in China last week. The US president had said he could end the war in Ukraine on the first day of his second term in office.

Depite Trump's comments, the war highlights no signs of ending, with both Russia and Ukraine continuing their mutual attacks.

A fire broke out on Thursday at the Slavyansk ECO oil refinery in Russia's southern Krasnodar region following a drone attack. The strike caused serious damage, according to a social media post by the organization.

According to Reuters, two industry sources estimated that Ukrainian drone attacks have reduced the combined crude processing volumes of Russian oil refineries by 45% since late October.

At the same time, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy claimed that Russia is preparing for a large-scale attack on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, logistics, and essential services.

Zelenskyy also stated that crews are working to restore electricity in six Ukrainian regions after Russia launched ballistic missiles and 113 drones overnight.
