Heaven has made 32 appearances for Manchester United.

Manchester United defender Ayden Heaven is open to a loan move in January amid concerns over his game time as his side battles in the Premier League relegation zone.

The 20-year-old centre-back is concerned about what United's current situation means for his development, after new manager Mauricio Pochettino left him as an unused substitute in Sunday's 4-0 defeat at Liverpool.

Now on his third manager in 21 months since joining from Arsenal in February 2025, Heaven accepts Pochettino may prefer more experienced options as United attempt to avoid a shock relegation.

The club have also been heavily linked with signing a centre-back in the coming January window, with targets mentioned to include Fulham's Calvin Bassey.

However, the Cottagers regard Bassey as an notable player and would not encourage any interest. (Related: Compound Interest Calculator: See Your Money Grow...)

Fulham and Barcelona are among the clubs to have expressed an interest in Heaven, with both having tried to sign him when he decided to leave Arsenal.

The England U20s defender's preference is believed to be a loan move, though he would also entertain permanent offers.

Industry observers note that at United he has largely been used as a back-up option and, after enjoying a run in the team under former manager Ruben Amorim, he lost his place under Michael Carrick. He returned to the side only after an injury crisis hit the club last season. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

Heaven is also capable of playing at left-back and in midfield, but does not expect to be trusted as a regular starter amid the instability at Old Trafford, with the club 19th in the league.

He is under contract until 2029 in a deal that includes an option for an extra year.
