In a recent development, arsenal transfer news: Arsenal like Kevin Schade, but he isn’t the winger they want most. Football.London’s Tom Canton reports that the Brentford forward is on the shortlist for the left flank, with RB Leipzig’s Antonio Nusa ranked above him. Canton notes the 24-year-old Germany international is certainly appreciated at the Emirates.

According to recent reports, the ranking reflects an awkward summer for Mikel Arteta. Arsenal sold Leandro Trossard and Gabriel Martinelli, missed out on Vinicius Junior and signed only Christos Tzolis for about £34m.

It has been revealed that tzolis started the first five league games, then suffered a hamstring injury on international duty. Eberechi Eze and Noni Madueke have covered since, and Madueke rescued a point in Saturday’s 2-2 draw at Newcastle, which left Arsenal six points clear for now. Recent findings suggest that nusa, the 21-year-old Norwegian winger, has six goals and eight assists in 15 matches for Leipzig. Sky Sports reports that the Bundesliga club won’t sell him in January, so a summer deal looks more realistic, with Leipzig reportedly valuing him between £60m and £77m. Schade is the quicker route, but Arsenal asked about him in the summer and got nowhere, according to TEAMtalk. His contract runs until 2028. Schade has scored three times in ten Premier League appearances from 2.44 expected goals, and his pace suits a side that already creates through Tzolis. He can play either flank or centrally. The weaknesses show elsewhere: no assists, three chances created in 781 minutes, seven completed dribbles at a 25% success rate and 43.8% of duels won. FotMob values him at about £47m.

It has been revealed that not in January, and certainly not as a consolation. Arsenal lead the league, Tzolis remains the first-choice left winger, and Madueke has just shown he can deliver there. Schade would add depth, but he wouldn’t add what Arteta lacks: a winger who beats his man and drags two defenders with him against low blocks.

Recent findings suggest that nusa builds his game on exactly that, and his output at Leipzig now justifies waiting until next summer. Schade’s returns suggest he would sit behind a fit Tzolis, which is hard to justify at a price Brentford would set after already refusing an approach.

Recent findings suggest that A bid becomes sensible only if Tzolis breaks down again or Leipzig block Nusa completely, because Schade needs no time to adapt to the Premier League. Even then, anything above £50m would pay for potential rather than production, and a 25% dribble success rate tells Arsenal he isn’t the specialist they need.
