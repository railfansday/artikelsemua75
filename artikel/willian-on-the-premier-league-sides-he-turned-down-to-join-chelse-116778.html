Brazilian winger Willian enjoyed a successful career in English football, turning out more than 400 times for Chelsea, Arsenal and Fulham,.

During this time, he established himself as one of the Premier League’s most exciting attackers, most notably at Stamford Bridge, where he won two league titles, an FA Cup, a League Cup and the Europa League.

But this move to the Blues in 2013 almost didn’t happen, as the Brazilian had a host of options when he chose to leave Russian side Anzhi Makhachkala.

After enjoying success at Shakhtar Donetsk, where he won five Ukrainian Premier League titles in a six-season spell, Willian was snapped up by the ambitious Anzhi.

“I wanted a new challenge [after Shakhtar] and Anzhi had very massive ambitions. As well as Eto’o, Lassana Diarra was also there,” the Brazilian tells FourFourTwo.

But when the club’s ailing owner withdrew his financial backing, the project faced an uncertain future.

“But I ended up staying for only six months as, unfortunately, the owner had some health problems and decided he wouldn't invest in the club any more. So he sold all the players he had invested in, and that's why I left.”.
