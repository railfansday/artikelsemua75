It has been revealed that frosinone coach Massimiliano Alvini explains his surprising transformation to take on Ruben Amorim’s Milan. ‘It is a different strategy, but the principles remain the same.’.

It kicks off at San Siro at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET).

Rather than using Daniel Birligea as the centre-forward, Frosinone appear to have shifted from a 4-2-3-1 formation to 3-4-3 with Romano Schmid as the False 9.

“The players at your disposal is everything, because they are the ones on the pitch,” Alvini told Sky Sport Italia and DAZN Italia.

“We decided to push Milan as far as possible, to give few reference points, to always be moving forward. It is a different strategy today, but the principles remain the same, to run forward with defenders rather than have the midfielders back-tracking. We are aware of what awaits us, we’ll try to have our game.”.

While some sources had Giorgio Kvernadze as the False 9, and others picked Fares Ghedjemis, Alvini confirmed it is Austrian midfielder Schmid who has that position.

“Schmid is the centre-forward, Monterisi is in central defence,” he confessed.

That suggests it is a three-man defence, the first time all season that Frosinone have taken this approach.

The Canarini go into this game in sixth place, just behind Milan in fifth, and have been a massive surprise since earning promotion from Serie B last season.

“Milan are one of the teams who pass out from the back the most, so we’ll try to keep our ideas and put them into action, that is all. We come here to play, not to look around and admire the atmosphere,” added Alvini.

“I’m pleased for the lads that they feel ambitious, we must have the right attitude and take the game to them.”.
