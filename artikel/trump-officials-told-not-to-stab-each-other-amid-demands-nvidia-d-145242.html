Tensions are reportedly running high among two of President Donald Trump's top Cabinet officials, so Kennedy says he hopes the two can not "stab each other" if they testify before the Senate Banking Committee.

Asked about Nvidia founder reportedly Jensen Huang agreeing to appear amid claims he placed U.S. national security at risk by having an affair with a suspected Chinese spy, Kennedy stated he is "more interested" in speaking with Lutnick and Bessent.

It has been revealed that "I’m less interested in talking to Mr. Huang," Kennedy said during NBC's "Meet the Press" on Sunday. "You know, he did what he did.".

"I’m more interested in talking to Howard Lutnick and to Scott Bessent. It appears to me — I emphasize, 'appears' — that the Department of Commerce has allowed the Communist Party of China to circumvent our export controls on advanced chips, either that or they didn’t know what they were doing.".

"They’re so incompetent, you wouldn’t put them in charge of a food truck.".

But, the sharp-tongued Republican senator argued that those in charge are not incompetent but rather "knowingly allowed this to happen.".

"Scott Bessent has been saying, 'Stop it.' So I want to get both of them in front of a committee, put them under oath, ask them not to stab each other because I realize they don’t like each other, and let’s get to — let’s answer some hard questions," Kennedy concluded.

He claimed that if Lutnick "is giving our chips away, he needs to stop it.".

Kennedy's comments came right before Nvidia's board reinstated Huang as CEO on Monday following his resignation.

The Commerce Department and Treasury Department did not immediately respond to Fox News Digital's request for comment.
