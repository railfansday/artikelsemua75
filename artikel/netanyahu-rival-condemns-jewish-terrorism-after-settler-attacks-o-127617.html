Dozens of militant Israeli settlers raided communities on Saturday night, setting fire to vehicles and several homes.

Arson attacks by militant Israeli settlers on Palestinian villages in the occupied West Bank have caused a political row in Israel, drawing condemnation from Gadi Eisenkot, the opposition leader, who described them as “Jewish terrorism”.

In a recent development, dozens of settlers raided Palestinian communities overnight on Saturday, setting fire to about a dozen vehicles and several homes and scrawling racist graffiti. At least four communities were attacked. Israeli police said they had launched an investigation with forensic teams. (Related: Breaking: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming G...)

Photographs from Kafr Malik, near Ramallah, showed one slogan written in Hebrew that read: “This is the first revenge.” Several vehicles and homes had been set alight in the village.

Images from nearby Deir al-Sudan showed vehicles on fire. Palestinian reports stated an agricultural building had been set ablaze in Deir Ballut and cars torched in Deir Abu Mashal. (Related: Architectural Choices in China's Open-Source AI Ecosyst...)

An ⁠inconclusive election last month stripped Benjamin Netanyahu, the prime minister, of his majority. Eisenkot has been asked to form a government, though his prospects seem little better.

“Last night’s arson attacks in Judea and Samaria were acts of Jewish terrorism, in every sense of the term,” Eisenkot mentioned, using the biblical name for the West Bank. “Extremist groups that set fire to vehicles and homes are undermining regional stability, putting lives at risk and doing grave damage to Israel’s national security and foreign relations.”.

Referring to the far-right ministers Itamar Ben-Gvir and Bezalel Smotrich, he added: “The actions led by Ben-Gvir and Smotrich in the West Bank have brought the area to the brink of unchecked anarchy.”.

Eisenkot, who has been given 28 days to form a government by the president, Isaac Herzog, with a possible 14-day extension, urged the government and security establishment to “use every tool at their disposal – including administrative detention and targeted operations by the Shin Bet – to stop the rioters and impose harsh sanctions on them”.

In an unprecedented rebuke on Sunday, several other members of the Israeli parliament criticised Netanyahu over escalating violence by militant settlers against Palestinians.

Recent findings suggest that yair Lapid, the leader of Yesh Atid, held Netanyahu’s government responsible for encouraging the attacks. “Jewish terrorism is the product of criminal negligence by the Israeli government,” he noted on X, adding that ministers were deliberately encouraging the militant settlers’ violence “through their silence”.

Yair Golan, the leader of the Democrats party, warned of the possibility of a third intifada and described the West Bank as “on the brink of an explosion”.

“Anyone who wants to prevent a third intifada must replace the current government, which provides cover and support for Jewish terrorism and abandons security,” Golan stated on X. “Jewish terrorism harms innocent Palestinians and Israeli army soldiers, and pushes Israel toward unprecedented international isolation.”.

Recent findings suggest that noam Tibon, a retired army major general and Knesset member, gave a similar warning. “If Israel does not eliminate Jewish terrorism in the West Bank, it will destroy Israel,” he wrote.

Netanyahu has not yet responded to the accusations.

Australia confirmed sanctions on Wednesday against three people and four Israeli entities, including a construction tech giant, over their role in the expansion of illegal settlements and human rights abuses against Palestinians in the occupied West Bank.

A day earlier the UK imposed sanctions on eight Israeli construction companies involved in building illegal settlements in the West Bank, which the UN regards as Palestinian territory under Israeli occupation since 1967.

Industry observers note that since the 7 October 2023 Hamas-led attacks on Israel, there has been a surge of Israeli settlement expansion and violence against Palestinians in the occupied West Bank. More than 1,100 Palestinians have been killed there by Israeli forces or settlers.

On Tuesday, a 72-year-old Palestinian woman was shot dead in an overnight attack by Israeli militant settlers in a village near Jericho, where the group has recently moved in after taking over several Palestinian homes.
