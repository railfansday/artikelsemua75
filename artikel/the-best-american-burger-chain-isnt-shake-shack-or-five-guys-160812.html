The Best American Burger Chain Isn't Shake Shack Or Five Guys

As one of America's favorite fast food items, burgers are something you've almost certainly ordered from a chain at least once in your life — but not all chains are created equal. Everyone has a favorite, so the question of which is best is bound to stir up some fiery debate. Even so, we didn't let that stop us from ranking nine of America's favorite burger chains, and coming out on top of our list was Culver's.

Founded in Sauk City, Wisconsin, Culver's now has more than 1,000 locations across more than two dozen U.S. states. Its flagship item, the ButterBurger, is served on a fluffy Kaiser bun, and customers have the option to customize it with a long list of toppings, including some world-class Wisconsin cheese slices. Living up to its founding state's nickname as America's Dairyland, Culver's treats dairy as a core part of the menu. Its famous Wisconsin Cheese Curds arrive breaded and gooey, while its fresh frozen custard is the perfect way to round off a meal. Culver's uses fresh, never-frozen beef, with its cooked-to-order patties made from a flavorful blend of sirloin, chuck, and plate. But what really sets it apart from chains like Shake Shack and Five Guys is the seasoning. Culver's uses salt and pepper, while Five Guys forgoes seasoning altogether. The chain argues its beef is so good that seasoning is unnecessary, but not all customers buy that reasoning.

Meanwhile, Shake Shack does season its patties, but Culver's has one not-so-secret ingredient that tips the scales in its favor: butter. It's spread on the crown of the bun, which is then toasted until golden, adding a rich flavor that makes the bun just as tasty as the patty.

While the taste and quality of food certainly play a major role in where we rank a chain, they aren't the only factors to consider. On Reddit, one customer shared why they love Culver's: "[It's] probably the best [all-around] fast food there is," they claimed. "[High-quality] ingredients, consistent, always hot[,] and the restaurants are always clean." Which brings us to one seriously underappreciated category: cleanliness.

Recent findings suggest that just think about it: A good meal can promptly be soured by a grimy dining area. Fast food often gets a bad rap for the quality of its food, but when it comes to hygiene, the best chains can easily hold their own against high-end sit-down restaurants. Culver's is no exception, and customers and media outlets have even credited it with having some of the cleanest bathrooms in the fast food game, which can only be a good thing.

Beyond cleanliness, Culver's is also one of the few major fast food chains left that's still family-owned. Rather than answering to shareholders, the firm still has co-founder Craig Culver as its chairman. This has seen Culver's put plenty back into its community, most notably through its Thank You Farmers Project; the chain's website states that it has helped raise more than $8 million for agricultural education since 2013. Much of that comes from Scoops of Thanks Day, when customers get a scoop of custard in exchange for a $1 donation to their local Future Farmers of America chapter.
