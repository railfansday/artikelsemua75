Detroit Lions star Aidan Hutchinson is speaking out after suffering a devastating injury.

The athlete, 26, took to Instagram on Thursday, November 19, to share his shock over the incident, which will likely end his season prematurely.

According to recent reports, “I honestly can’t believe it. I’ve jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me,” Hutchinson wrote as he addressed injuring himself during a sack celebration at a game in Germany on November 15. Hutchinson also made it clear that he intends to bounce back, declaring, “I always come back stronger.” Seeing the funny side, he quipped, “That’s the last time I go airborne 😁👍.”.

“Hold down the fort til I’m back, boys,” he added.

Hutchinson’s post comes after the Lions’ head coach, Dan Campbell, described the loss of the star player as a “gut punch” earlier this week.

In a recent development, “He’s such a phenomenal player, an impact player, but more importantly he’s an unbelievable teammate and he’s one of our captains,” Campbell stated during an appearance on Detroit’s 97.1 The Ticket on Tuesday, November 17. “It just stinks, man.
