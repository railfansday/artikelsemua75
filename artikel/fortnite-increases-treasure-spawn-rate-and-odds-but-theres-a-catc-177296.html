Fortnite Increases Treasure Spawn Rate and Odds, But There's a Catch

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

According to recent reports, fortnite Treasure Hunters ends on Dec. 5, which means all treasure, including the Pirate Shop Access token, which grants players up to four weeks of free Item Shop goodies, will be vaulted soon. With less than two weeks to go, a large portion of the community fears that they will not be able to get their hands on the Pirate Shop Access token at all.

In a recent development, after opening thousands of chests in hopes of finding the token this season, next week will be the final stretch. Epic Games is giving players a bit of hope by hosting a Golden Week with improved treasure spawns. Treasure Hour odds will also be especially boosted during Golden Week, but unfortunately, not for the Pirate Shop Access token.

With Chapter 8 fast approaching, Epic Games has added all previous treasure hunt rewards to the loot pool. This means that gamers will be able to find previous weekly rewards — Solid Gold skins, vehicles, pickaxes, and more — as treasure in Fortnite. To give more players a chance to claim these limited-time rewards, Epic is hosting a Golden Week event. (Related: Bilt Rewards → Hyatt — Earn Points Paying Rent...)

According to recent reports, golden Week begins on Nov. 28 at 9 a.m. ET and lasts until the end of the season. The odds of finding treasure will be set to 1 in 21, instead of the current 1 in 62. While Pirate Shop Access token odds won't go up per se, gamers will be much more likely to come across a chest that spawns treasures and could get them the token. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

It has been revealed that golden Week Treasure Hours will be the final Treasure Hours of the season, meaning the last time that the Pirate Shop Access token will have increased odds. Unfortunately for fans strictly trying to find the token, Epic Games has confirmed that Pirate Shop Access token odds will only be boosted to 1.5x on Nov. 28. The event focuses on Solid Gold skins, which is why they will be 15x more likely to drop rather than 7x.
