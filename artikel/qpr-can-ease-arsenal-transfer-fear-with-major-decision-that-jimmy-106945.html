Leon Scarlett is already attracting the attention of some of Europe's biggest clubs as a result of his appearances for the Wales under-21 team, and this puts pressure on his current club to tie him down to a professional contract.

One of the more notable downsides of the information age is that when a football club unearths a rare gem, it won't be very long before the game's apex predators find out about them as well. With every game now recorded and scrutinised, it's seldom long before news of an emerging talent will land with them, and they will be likely to take an interest in the player concerned before they become prohibitively expensive.

In the case of the 17-year-old winger Leon Scarlett, Queens Park Rangers might have a star of the future on their hands. Scarlett has only made six appearances for Rangers, of which five have been as a substitute, and he has yet to record his first goal or assist in a Rangers shirt, but such is his already growing reputation that some significant names, including Arsenal, AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt have already been connected with an interest in him, among a rapidly growing list of clubs. In a tight Championship table, Queens Park Rangers have acquitted themselves well in 2026-27 and look capable of battling for promotion this term.

But while Rangers fans can get excited about the possibility of a serious bid for a play-off place or beyond following years of stagnation in the table, it's the attention being lavished upon Leon Scarlett which gives them cause to be the most excited, but in all likelihood also a little nervous over the possibility of losing him. Sources indicate that scarlett, who can play on either the left or the right wing, originally joined QPR's academy following his free transfer arrival from Arsenal in April 2025, and made his debut for them from the bench in their 3-0 loss at Ipswich Town at the very end of the 2025-26 season. So far this season, he's made a further five appearances in a hooped shirt, including a full debut for them against Preston North End in September, although he was withdrawn at half-time in this match.

Recent findings suggest that but QPR aren't the only team for whom he's been playing. Although still only 17-years-old, and having already played for their under-16 and under-17 teams, he made two brief appearances for the Wales under-21 team against Belgium and Austria at the end of September, while he also completed three appearances for the under-19 team during the November international break which included providing an assist in their 2-2 draw with Iceland in a play-off match for the Under-19 Euros which they won on penalty kicks.

These tastes of international football have grown the young winger's profile. At the end of last week, Anthony Joseph of Sky Sports reported that both AC Milan and Eintracht Frankfurt are keeping tabs on him, while it's also been reported that Brighton, Rangers and even his former club Arsenal could be plotting a move for him. And the pressure on Rangers over Scarlett could be all the greater for knowing that the winger hasn't yet signed his first full professional contract with the Loftus Road club.

Recent findings suggest that both the Queens Park Rangers club captain Jimmy Dunne and manager Julien Stephan are clear admirers of Leon Scarlett. As reported by Loft For Words in September, Dunne spoke about the teenager at a fans forum in glowing terms, saying: "Just watching Leon Scarlett today he’s so impressive, so bright, and so positive. You want to see him do well and thrive. We’ve got loads of exciting players like that. I was here at the U21 game the other day and they were fabulous.".

Recent findings suggest that julien Stephan also praised him at this forum, saying: "Why in the last two games did I pick Leon Scarlett? Because he deserves it. He highlighted in all the training sessions it was the right moment for him. Never a coincidence. Always logical.".

In April, as reported by the Evening Standard, Stephan had previously remarked of Scarlett: "The last game (Scarlett) did for the U21s, he did very well. I saw him in a cup game with the U18s earlier in the season, and he did a good first half. So I just wanted to see him during the session with us (the first team). And during the session, he did well.” Scarlett earned his senior debut shortly after this.

There is one thing that Queens Park Rangers can do to try and secure their position regarding this young talent. Scarlett remains on a scholarship with the club and hasn't yet signed his first full professional contract with them. Rangers should surely try to tie him down to a senior contract, which would mean that, in the event that he continues to show the promise that he has so far and clubs remain interested in snatching him from them, they would be able to land a substantial transfer fee for him.

This could, of course, end in disappointment. Scarlett has the right to say no to any offer made to him, and any refusal to agree terms with Rangers would surely be a precursor to him leaving the club. But as things stand, the club would have next to no chance of keeping hold of him, and with the list of clubs who are tracking him continuing to grow, Queens Park Rangers might be best advised to do everything within their power to keep this exciting young prospect at their club for as long as possible. Judging by his comments in September, it would certainly make Jimmy Dunne happy.
