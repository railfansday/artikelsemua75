Harry Kane's Ballon d'Or triumph was richly deserved and indicates that "the number nine is back", according to Brazil icon Ronaldo Nazario.

Last month, Kane became the first English player to receive football's top individual prize since Michael Owen in 2001, having enjoyed a stunning 2025-26 campaign at both club and international level. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

Kane plundered a remarkable 73 goals combined for Bayern Munich and England in 2025-26 – the only other instance of a player from Europe's top five leagues scoring at least 70 goals in a campaign came in 2011-12, when Lionel Messi netted 82 and also won the Ballon d'Or. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

And for Ronaldo, the sight of Kane hoisting the prize was symbolic of the return to prominence of outright strikers.

With Ousmane Dembele, Rodri and Messi winning the previous three editions of the Ballon d'Or, the last striker to lift the prize, before Kane, was Karim Benzema in 2022.

Before Benzema, Cristiano Ronaldo's back-to-back Ballon d'Or triumphs in 2016 and 2017 represented the most recent example of a striker being voted the world's best player.

Speaking on the Stick to Football podcast, Ronaldo mentioned: "It was nice and well deserved for him, I think.

"And I was happy for him, and for me too, because the number nine is back!

"I'm so happy. It's been a long time without a number nine winning something important. And then he's also a very nice guy. So, it's well deserved. I was very happy for him.".

Ronaldo himself won the Ballon d'Or twice, in 1999 and 2002. That latter triumph came in a year when he helped Brazil win their fifth World Cup, scoring eight goals in seven games.

There are only six instances of a player scoring more goals at a single edition of the tournament – Kylian Mbappe netted 10 times in eight appearances this year, while the other five all came in 1970 or earlier.

And Ronaldo feels the evolution of the game has made things easier for forwards, who now get more protection against rough treatment from defenders.

"I wish I had protection in that time. We suffered so much in that time. In our generation it was killers, killers everywhere," he mentioned.

"Everywhere, I played so many killers and we are not protected. Now, thank God, they are very protected and it's fair. I just regret I didn't have that protection in my time.".

Kane will be sidelined until the latest year after suffering a syndesmosis ligament tear in his left ankle, with Bayern drawing 1-1 with Koln and losing 2-1 to Lille since he went down with that injury.
