A book, in PDF and EPUB, that writes the Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis in tamnd/riemann-lean as mathematics: definitions, theorems, lemmas and their proofs, in words.

The claim is not checked. Nobody has built the Lean project in full yet, and no person has checked the written statements against the Lean code. This book explains the Lean code. It is not a separate check of the mathematics. See What is checked. (Related: Redditor forced to bring desktop PC in checked luggage ...)

Each declaration of the proof is inside the dominated subtree of exactly one other declaration (its immediate dominator, in the graph of uses from riemannHypothesis). The ledger gives an entry to each declaration whose dominated subtree is large, in tiers: 1,000 or more declarations (591 entries), 300 or more (1,927), 100 or more (5,764). It assigns each entry to a volume by its branch of the proof or by its Lean folder, and to a chapter by its Lean folder. In a chapter, the entries come from the top of the proof down.

Sources indicate that ledger/coverage.md has the numbers. prompts/entry.md is the brief for the writers of entries.

You need Python 3.11 or later, pandoc 3.8 or later and Tectonic. Put tamnd/riemann and tamnd/riemann-lean next to this repository.

The books go to build/. The pipeline is that of The Handbook of Algorithms: Markdown, then pandoc with filters/rhb.lua, then LaTeX with tex/handbook.sty, then Tectonic. The EPUB is EPUB 3 with MathML. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)
