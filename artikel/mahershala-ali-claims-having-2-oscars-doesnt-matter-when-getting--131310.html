With several acclaimed roles under his belt, Mahershala Ali still finds himself on equal footing in many casting rooms.

The 2x Academy Award winner recently explained that having the Oscar statuettes “doesn’t matter” in casting conversations, explaining that the coveted acting honor is “kind of just talk” and “doesn’t translate to anything real.”. (Related: Japan debuts a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t connect to inte...)

Industry observers note that “It’s a natural way for people to think. But on some level, what does an Oscar mean? It’s an award, and it’s an award where you’re being recognized, hopefully for doing good work. But there’s a lot of great actors that don’t have Oscars, who are also looking for good material. So, it doesn’t really mean anything. And that’s no disrespect to The Academy or the award at all, but what I’m actually saying is that, Oscar or no Oscar, there’s a lot of good actors out there,” he explained on the 7pm in Brooklyn podcast. “There’s more good actors than there is good material.”. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Sources indicate that ali continued, “And it’s not even that all these people are competing for these same spots. It’s just that out of all the material made, there’s only a handful of them that check certain boxes. And then you got to be right for it. Just because a script is good doesn’t mean that I’m right for it or whoever wrote it is thinking about me, of all people. So, who is centered? The vast majority of projects that get greenlit, there are not roles for me in those. You gotta think that way, you know what I mean? So, it doesn’t matter if I got 17 Oscars. If someone is not writing something for you or greenlighting something with you in mind, you’re like everybody else waiting and looking and searching.” Watch on Deadline.

Noting that rollbacks of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) programs “on some level impact what gets greenlit,” Ali explained “that if certain companies are allowed to not feel a pressure to be reflective of the world we live in, they’ll just keep centering who they see in their offices, and it ain’t a bunch of us in those offices.”.

Sources indicate that at the same time, Ali doesn’t want to be typecast in another “diversity project,” adding, “You just want to do great material as a Black person. You’re not thinking of yourself as this exotic other, per se. You just are living your life. You wake up. You put your pants on like everybody else. You just want to be like, ‘Yo, this is my offering. This is my story. This is my work.’ Opposed to, ‘Man, who are the best Black actors?'”.

With Ali earning Oscar buzz for his latest starring role in Bassam Tariq’s Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother, the actor previously won Best Supporting Actor for Moonlight in 2017 and Green Book in 2019.
