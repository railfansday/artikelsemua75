Don Toliver‘s concert at Co-op Live in Manchester, England, was cut short and evacuated after an “unknown substance” was released into the crowd.

In the hours since, the singer has since postponed his London concert at the O2 Arena to insure concertgoers’ safety. The rescheduled show will occur on Dec. 15 per Live Nation.

“I’m very sad to sit here and say I have to reschedule the O2 London show to Dec. 15 due to [sic] unfortunate incident last night Manchester,” Toliver wrote on Instagram Stories. “I just wanna make sure everyone is safe and the next show goes on properly. My heart and prayers are with with everyone that was injured last night.”.

It has been revealed that “We are sorry to everyone affected by last night’s events in Manchester and full refunds for that show will be automatically processed to ticket holders,” Live Nation noted in a statement. Tickets from the original date are valid for the newly introduced show. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

The Greater Manchester Police declared a major incident after more than 12,000 people were safely removed from the venue. Per BBC News, Toliver was escorted off stage approximately 30 to 40 minutes the show’s start. A “horrible smell” filled the arena, and on-site witnesses claim scenes of attendees “choking,” “screaming,” and “coughing up blood.” An hour after the initial incident, the concert was evacuated, with approximately 80 people assessed for “eye and respiratory symptoms.”.
