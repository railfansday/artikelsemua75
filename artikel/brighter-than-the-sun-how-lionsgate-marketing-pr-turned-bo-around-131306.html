In the wake of Lionsgate‘s last Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes hitting a franchise low at the box office, the Santa Monica, CA based studio got very serious — serious about marketing and reaching the younger audience segemts that the conglomerate built their brand upon with franchises such as Twilight and, of course, Suzanne Collins’ Hunger Games.

In a recent development, three years ago, it looked like the Hunger Games series petered out with a $44.6M domestic, and $100.8M WW opening and final of $166.3M domestic and $363.9M WW. But this weekend in a death-defying act worthy of Katniss Everdeen herself, Lionsgate completely resuscitated the franchise from its rock bottom. Prequel Hunger Games: Sunrise of the Reaping rose like a phoenix, leaving behoind the previous installment’s box office ashes with a great $120M domestic, $225M global opening. Among prequels at the domestic B.O., Sunrise on the Reaping‘s opening was second only to Rogue One: A Star Wars Story‘s $155M start. Sources indicate that that worldwide B.O. on Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes was worse than any installment in another popular YA franchise; the Harry Potter prequel/spinoff series Fantastic Beasts hit a low wih the last film, 2022’s The Secrets of Dumbledore, at $407.1M. Songbirds & Snakes repped the first time that Collins had written an origins story about one of the core characters, specifically, its villain Coriolanus Snow in a mentorship love story with District 12 tribute Lucy Gray Baird. There was a big bluegrass music element in the film, with Olivia Rodrigo on the soundtrack. Lacking, however, was an OG cast member from the first four films, given that it took place 64 years before the events of the initial Hunger Games novel. Watch on Deadline.

In a recent development, the novel was flat out rejected by fans, some pointing to Covid as an easy reason, others to the fact that it was about the bad guy, with 500K first week copies sold and final reported figure of 2.5M. Complicating business further was the fact that Songbirds & Snakes opened at the box office shortly after the actors strike ended. Even though Lionsgate, as an indie studio, had a SAG-AFTRA waiver for the cast to promote, it was still a challenge to prop up a tentpole during such times. Industry observers note that collins marched on and figured out what her fanbase needed, crafting her next novel, Sunrise on the Reaping about the rise of Katniss and Peeta mentor Haymitch Abernathy (played by Woody Harrelson in the first four films). There was an epilogue in the book, not just with the older Haymitch, but Peeta and Katniss as well. It was the novel that fans wanted and they demonstrated their love by snapping up 4.4 million copies around the globe. It was also a good enough roux for filmmaker Francis Lawrence, director on films 2-5, to work from. In a complete one-up from Songbirds & Snakes, Lionsgate brought back the original 2012 pic’s screenwriter, Oscar-nominated Billy Ray, to adapt Collins’ latest.

In July 2025, Lionsgate saw a changeover in their marketing and publicity with then EVP Strategy and Chief of Staff Amanda Kozlowski, EVP Worldwide Publicity Nasim Cambron and EVP Global Creative Advertising Jack Teed stepping up. Such exec maneuvers ultimately left Lionsgate all the richer with a female tentpole in last Christmas’ Sydney Sweeney and Amanda Seyfriend thriller The Housemaid ($400.4M, first weekend turnout of 18-34 was 63%) fueled by a dish plate smashing social media meme, and of course, the studio’s first billion-dollar grossing movie last summer, the Michael Jackson biopic, Michael.

Kozlowski was promoted to Worldwide Marketing President in May shortly after Michael‘s $218.8M global opening, in addition to Cambron being elevated to EVP, Worldwide Marketing & Publicity and Teed to EVP, Worldwide Marketing & Creative Advertising. Chris Daniel, SVP, Global Creative Marketing and other members of the Lionsgate Marketing team also get shoutouts here for this weekend’s B.O. success.

“There is a new generation of marketing brains who understand how people are communicating with one another and how marketing messages can make an impact. The leaders of our marketing group, digital, media, publicity and creative teams are going 14 months strong with The Long Walk, Now You See Me, Now You Dont, Housemaid, Michael, and they’re not doing things in traditional ways. They’re creating moments that permeate popular culture and get audiences excited and want to share them with their friends,” stated Lionsgate Motion Picture Chairman Adam Fogelson today in celebration of Sunrise on the Reaping.

Previously at a Deadline Regal event over the summer, Fogelson told us that, “All of the things when I was growing up in marketing that were sort of held to be certain and true about how you reach an audience, those things, especially for the generations we’re talking about, have changed.” Case in point: Don’t overshare too much in a trailer; if you do, “you’re going to get flat-out rejected,” he added at the time.

The overarching strategy for Sunrise on the Reaping was to make the sixth installment of the 14-year old franchise feel like the biggest games yet, not only reigniting the passion of longtime fans with a fave character in Haymitch, but also introducing a latest generation to Panem.

Industry observers note that A longtime motion picture marketing exec going back to Universal, Fogelson’s campaigns were always known for being anchored in character. Sources say Kozlowski has a similar philosophy, but also that the exec knows how to sell story and how to sell to a fanbase. At the center of this Hunger Games promo was Joseph Zada nailing a portrayal of a younger Haymitch.

Sources indicate that kozlowski tells Deadline, “From the beginning, Sunrise on the Reaping had a lot to offer Hunger Games fans — a fan-favorite character whose backstory many had long been asking for, the scale and spectacle of the biggest Games ever and a throughline that connected Haymitch’s tragedy to the rise of the revolution that culminated with Katniss. The marketing team combined those elements with a fan-forward campaign rooted in Gen Z and Gen Alpha online culture, peak Millennial nostalgia, activations that brought the world of Panem into our own and a wealth of unforgettable cast and filmmaker moments. As fans ourselves, it’s been gratifying to see moviegoers responding to the campaign with as much enthusiasm as we have for the world that Suzanne, Francis and Nina (Jacobson) have so lovingly created.”.

Industry observers note that the drumroll for Sunrise on the Reaping began a year ago when Lionsgate dropped the first teaser to 109 million global views in 24 hours, which became the studio’s second-most-watched trailer ever after Michael, which they had just debuted a few days prior. At the time, that trailer traffic for Sunrise was +48% ahead of Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, an excellent sign.

From there, Lionsgate created a card stacking (to use a Sunrise word) of high-impact reveals and cultural moments all the way up to release. The studio turned the casting announcements into a pop culture even with 16 individual announcements that generated 541 million views and 35.2 million engagements.

It has been revealed that when it came to the late campaign unveiling of the 29-song soundtrack, the studio executed a staggered, artist driven rollout with such performers as Noah Kahan, The Smashing Pumpkins, Ella Langley and Gigi Perez. Altogether this part of the pic’s marketing, which sought to expand beyond the franchise’s core, yielded 40 million+ social views and 186 playlist placements.

Sources indicate that A glittery part of the pic’s push was bringing Panem into the real world with immersive activations, fashion and lifestyle partnerships, and cultural integrations. Lionsgate set up Sunrise Gardens installations across 30 countries and six continents including Peru, Sweden, Brazil, South Africa, Denmark, Belgium, Singapore, Finland, London and more, which was picked up across social media.

The marketing also leaned into the pic’s themes of Capitol Propaganda with a customized Election Day activation, spearheaded by Lionsgate EVP Global Paid Media & Analytics, Dani Otero, across CNN and Fox News generating 79M+ social impressions and repping 31% of the Sunrise social conversation the following day.

Timed to Paris Fashion Week, there was the Elegance Is Authority exhibition at the Musée Rodin attracted 10,000+ registrations in 24 hours.

According to recent reports, also, there were beauty and fashion partnerships with Stila Cosmetics and Depop. Stila’s collaboration reached 1,500+ Ulta stores and generating 50 million+ press and organic social impressions.

In appealing to Gen Z and Gen Alpha, Lionsgate embraced creators, platforms, humor and content creators, inviting them to participate in the franchise rather than simply consume its marketing. Specifically, the studio teamed with a 16-year-old viral editor on the franchise’s first official fan-edit trailer, which pulled in 60.3 million+ views on TikTok. A global fan casting contest inspired 7,500+ submissions and 195.6 million+ views on fan-created videos, with participation extending beyond the core fandom to KATSEYE, Zooey Deschanel and Disney’s Stitch.

In a recent development, toward the end, Sunrise on the Reaping‘s propaganda built to a crescendo of peak nostalgia by paying homage to the old in the new. Lionsgate made a point to rope in OG cast members Harrelson, Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson with bookings on The Tonight Show and Bravo’s Watch What Happens Live.

In a recent development, the result was a great report card of stats for Sunrise‘s push: 1 billion+ owned-social views, 691M TikTok views, and 74 billion+ views of fan-generated content across relevant Hunger Games TikTok hashtags; 1.5 million newly introduced followers across the pic’s official social channels with a massive turnout by the 18-34 at 72% in PostTrak exits. The movie was the No. 2 pic on TikTok’s Trending Chart, behind only Avengers: Doomsday. Conversation volume on X was more than 4x than Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes at the comparable point in its campaign. The digital campaign was overseen by Lionsgate EVP, Head of Worldwide Digital Marketing, Briana McElroy.

The campaign for Sunrise on the Reaping generated renewed enthusiasm among women 17-34 who gave it a 36% first choice versus 24% for Ballad of Songbirds and Snake. Women under 25 showed up at 38% for Sunrise on the Reaping per PostTrak exits.

Overall, the media and social reach for the Sunrise push clocked 2.7 billion+ paid media impressions with heavy play on the NFL, NBA, World Series, and highlights such as Saturday Night Live, The Voice, Abbott Elementary, The Traitors and the Real Housewives.

In the end, Sunrise on the Reaping pulled in 7.1 million admission this weekend stateside per EntTelligence, +122% from the 3.2M opening weekend admissions of Songbirds & Snakes three years ago.

You could say that the odds were greatly in the favor of Sunrise of the Reaping‘s marketing campaign.
