Set to launch on December 8, Nothing has confirmed its mysterious AI product will be called “Ear (note),” and it’s an AI recorder with earbuds inside.

Speaking to Bloomberg, Nothing has confirmed that its new AI product is “Ear (note).” This builds on a pair of earbuds by turning the charging case into an AI-powered recorder.

The case has a “Talk” button, like Ear (3), which can be used to record your own voice, where a record button enables the ability to “record and transcribe conversations.” Ear (3a)’s ability to record audio from the earbuds will also carry over to Ear (note), giving the product the ability to record both conversations in person and over the phone.

Nothing is entering a crowded market here with products from Plaud, Amazon, and countless others already offering AI recording/transcribing products, but building in earbuds is indeed a unique selling point. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

According to recent reports, nothing Ear (note) is launching on December 8, though pricing is unclear. Pei’s comments suggest a subscription will be used in some capacity.
