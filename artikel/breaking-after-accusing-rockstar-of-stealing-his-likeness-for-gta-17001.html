According to recent reports, lawrence Sullivan, better known online as the 'Florida Joker,' has confirmed that he voices his lookalike in GTA 6. His character appears in an in-game viral social media video that shows his purple-haired homage confronting another man for stealing his look, knocking him out, and later being arrested.

In a recent development, florida Joker, so called for his distinctive face tattoos and bright hair color, went viral after people spotted his mug shot online. His image apparently caught the eyes of people at Rockstar, as a similar-looking character appeared in the announcement trailer for GTA 6. Rockstar never confirmed that the purple-haired person was based on Sullivan, but the tattoos bore more than a few similarities to Sullivan's.

Recent findings suggest that rockstar actually hired Florida Joker to play his character in GTA 6, and he’s included in the credits.
