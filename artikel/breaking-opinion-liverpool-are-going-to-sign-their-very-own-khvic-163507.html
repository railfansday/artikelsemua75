Liverpool have been linked with a lot of players over the last year or so.

The Anfield outfit have spent well over £600 million trying to improve their ranks and, it's still unclear whether they're any better off than they were a couple of years ago.

Obviously, some players that they've brought in are clear improvements from the ones that they've replaced but, for others, the jury is still out.

Thanks to the lack of form that Liverpool have been in, many more players have now been linked with a Liverpool move.

Some of them would get straight into Andoni Iraola's starting XI but others are being eyed more for the future than anything else.

And now, one player that Liverpool have just been linked with could change everything.

Liverpool have entered the transfer race for Tornike Kvaratskhelia.

But, these improvements can't take place unless transfers are made to help improve Liverpool's squad.

So, that's why this latest update from TEAMtalk is particularly interesting.

Tornike Kvaratskhelia could become Liverpool's own version of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia.

Khvicha Kvaratskhelia is one of the best attackers in the world.

Obviously, at the moment, he's out for the vast majority of the 2026-27 season with an injury which is far from ideal.

However, when he's fit and playing for Paris Saint-Germain, it's hard to think of another winger as good as he is.

He's got a lot more experience under his belt than his brother but, since Tornike has already played eight games of football this termand has contributed to four goals, it's clear that he's an extremely talented player.

Indeed, he's only 16 so he still has a lot of developing left to do but, with how much Liverpool are bulking their academy ranks up, there's no harm in them taking a punt on Tornike.
