FIFA: Infantino, Tashima and Diallo enter presidential race

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that three candidates have officially submitted their bids for the FIFA presidency. Gianni Infantino, who has been in office for 11 years, is seeking a fourth consecutive term. Backed by UEFA, French Football Federation president Philippe Diallo wants to offer an alternative to Infantino’s leadership. He will face Japan’s Kohzo Tashima, honorary president of the Japan Football Association. The election will take place on March 18, 2027 in Rabat, Morocco.
