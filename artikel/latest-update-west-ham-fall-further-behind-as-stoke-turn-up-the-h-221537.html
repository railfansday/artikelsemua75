According to recent reports, if you thought things couldn’t get any worse for West Ham, they certainly did last night, with the club dropping down to 13th in the EFL Championship. Four games took place on Wednesday evening, and the results made pretty grim reading for Hammers fans.

Blackburn Rovers leapfrogged West Ham courtesy of a 1-0 win away at Watford, with Todd Cantwell scoring from the penalty spot. Meanwhile, Birmingham City won 2-0 at Burnley, and West Bromwich Albion secured a 3-1 victory away at Bolton Wanderers.

Sources indicate that perhaps most worryingly of all, given West Ham’s next opponents, Stoke City recorded their fifth successive Championship victory by beating Millwall 2-1.
