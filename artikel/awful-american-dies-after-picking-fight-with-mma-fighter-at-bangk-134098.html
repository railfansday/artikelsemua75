Media in Thailand is reporting on an incident that happened early this morning (Sunday, November 22, 2026), whereby a 46-year-old American tourist died at a hotel in Bangkok’s Phra Khanong area, after an altercation with a 39-year-old Italian man, who was reportedly an MMA fighter.

According to recent reports, according to the initial police report, the American had been arguing loudly with the hotel staff at the reception desk, for reasons that are unclear as of now. As an Italian hotel guest walked past him and attempted to enter an elevator, the American reportedly grabbed him and pulled him out.

In a recent development, the confrontation escalated, and turned into a physical struggle, during which the Italian man placed the American man in a chokehold, until he lost consciousness. Hotel staff called emergency services, and while first responders performed CPR, the American couldn’t be revived. Police also found an abrasion on the victim’s forehead.
