Axelera AI, the Dutch organization building chips that run AI models, is in talks to raise $400M, Bloomberg reported today, and the capital would go towards developing its chips further. (Related: Breaking: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Faster ...)

The Eindhoven firm is also seeking hundreds of millions of dollars in EU grants, Bloomberg wrote, citing people familiar with the private talks. That capital would come through the bloc’s IPCEI scheme for strategic technology projects. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Sources indicate that the amount would be Axelera’s biggest yet, as the company closed a $250M round led by Innovation Industries in February, with BlackRock and Samsung’s venture arm among the backers. That took its total since 2021 past $450mn in equity, debt, and grants.

At the time, Axelera called it the largest investment ever made in a European AI chip organization. Another $400M would push the total towards $850M.

Axelera first made its name with its Metis chip, built to run AI on devices such as cameras, robots, and drones rather than in distant data centres. Running models at the edge cuts the power and bandwidth that cloud AI burns through, which is the tech giant’s core pitch.

Industry observers note that its newer products aim higher, as the Europa processor, introduced in September, targets generative AI and agentic workloads. The Titania chiplet, backed by an EU grant of up to €61.6M, goes after data centres and supercomputers.

It has been revealed that the business has scale to show too. Axelera mentioned in February it had shipped to its 500th customer, across defence, manufacturing, robotics, and security.

Industry observers note that brussels has reason to listen when the organization comes asking. Axelera pitches itself as the maker of Europe’s first fully homegrown AI acceleration platform. EU programmes already fund its scale-up as part of the push to cut reliance on foreign chips.
