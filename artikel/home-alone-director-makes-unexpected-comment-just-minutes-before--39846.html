On the same day, Variety reported that mere minutes prior to the world learning about that project, Columbus told the outlet why he believed that a true Home Alone sequel became impossible following the January 2026 death of Catherine O'Hara. Check out Columbus' comments below:.

Recent findings suggest that catherine, as I’ve always said, is 50% of Home Alone. There’s the yin and yang of Home Alone: Catherine O’Hara and Macaulay Culkin. Without those two in the film, there is no film, because they create the emotional epicenter of that film.

Since Variety published its report about Columbus' comments after details about the potential sequel were revealed, the outlet reached out to the director for further comment. However, he declined to make any additional remarks on the subject.
