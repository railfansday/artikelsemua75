EXCLUSIVE: Darsheel Safary and Zain Khan Durrani are leading an Indian drama about cybercrime from director Alok Naik.

Cyber will follow two young unemployed computer engineering graduates whose lives take an unexpected turn when they get an opportunity to work with a cyber agency. What begins as a chance to prove their abilities soon draws them into the complex world of cybercrime and investigations.

Recent findings suggest that the film is being produced by Raj Zacharias under the banner of Celebs and Red Carpet, which is known for backing South Indian films such as Prithviraj-starrer Anwar, Lalbagh and Rahadari among others. Cyber marks the production house’s first Hindi cinema production. Watch on Deadline.

The film has an ensemble cast comprising Ajit Koshy, Kabir Duhan Singh, Prasad Oak, Alisha Khan, Ayushi Tyagi, Soumya Srivastav, Rahul Singh and Vitthal Kale, alongside its leads. Safary is know for his debut as a child actor in Aamir Khan’s Taare Zameen Par, while Durrani has appeared in Shikara and Mukhbir.

Award-winning composer Gopi Sundar CS, known for his work in Geetha Govindam, Bangalore Days and Charlie is creating the music for Cyber.

“What attracted me to this was it’s a story of two engineering graduates who are jobless and trying to do something in life,” mentioned Zacharias. “It’s a common man’s story. It’s a pure, raw and realistic storyline about how they get a chance to work with a cyber agency and how their life changes.”.
