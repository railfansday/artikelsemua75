📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Uber is piloting a new feature in the U.S. that offers rides in which drivers have enabled the company’s recording feature, Record My Ride.

The option, which uses the front camera of the Uber driver’s phone to record the entire drive, will show up as “Recorded Rides” in the app.

The ride-hailing company is starting the pilot in Baltimore, Raleigh, Indianapolis, and St. Louis and will expand to more locations later.

Uber remarked it is testing recorded rides specifically for rides booked at late night or those involving teens. Earlier this year, it introduced a live video streaming feature for rides booked for teenagers. In another test, the manufacturer had enabled audio recording for late night trips in some cities. The organization is also testing a feature that will turn on recording when the app detects the ride going off-course, or when the vehicle might have had an accident. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Industry observers note that the tech giant said drivers can opt out of recording through the Settings panel. Similarly, riders can also decline permission to record their rides via an in-app notification.
