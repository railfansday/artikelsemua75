📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Audiences are divided over Netflix's upcoming science-fiction movie.

Sources indicate that war Machine, The Adam Project, and They Cloned Tyrone are among the original sci-fi movies that Netflix has previously made. While reviews for Alan Ritchson's War Machine were mixed, it became the streaming platform's tenth most-watched film of all time with 139.9 million views. The Adam Project fared even better, as it is in sixth place on the all-time chart thanks to 157.6 million views. Meanwhile, They Cloned Tyrone was acclaimed by critics, leading to a "Certified Fresh" 95% Tomatometer score, along with an 83% Popcornmeter score on Rotten Tomatoes. It has been revealed that now, Netflix's new sci-fi movie, Allen, has debuted to a 50% Popcornmeter score on Rotten Tomatoes, with audiences split between positive and negative takes on the film. Since the average is based on fewer than 100 ratings as of this writing, it may fluctuate as more are added to the aggregation site. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Sources indicate that allen is about an alien of the same name, who returns to Earth after he mysteriously disappeared 20 years ago. During his initial visit, he was best friends with Petey, and now reunites with the grown-up version of the character (T.J. Power). What Petey has dreamed about for years takes a dark turn when he learns about the real reason for Allen's initial visit, though, and what it now means for him in the present.

On Rotten Tomatoes, many of the more positive audience reviews praise the filmmaking by Australian production organization Ludo Studio, who makes all episodes of Bluey, and has now made their first live-action movie through Allen. Other positive takes highlight the story's humor, heart, delivering an original spin on the familiar premise of Steven Spielberg's E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial, and ultimately telling a hopeful and family-friendly story despite the dark plot twist.

It has been revealed that as for some of the more negative audience reviews, they include criticism of the movie's overall execution not living up to the creative premise, Petey being too unlikable and erratic to root for despite being the protagonist, uneven pacing that leads to the story feeling disjointed, and being too similar to E.T. and other alien movies in the end rather than feeling by inspired by them. A Tomatometer score has not been calculated yet, but the seven critics' reviews on the site are also divided, as four are "Fresh" and three are "Rotten.".

It has been revealed that despite the divisive reception, Allen became a Netflix streaming hit, which includes reaching sixth place on the streamer's U.S. chart and eighth place worldwide. In addition to the U.S., it has made it into the top 10 in Argentina, Brazil, Canada, the Dominican Republic, Egypt, Germany, Hungary, Jamaica, Kenya, Morocco, New Zealand, Nigeria, Switzerland, Thailand, Turkey, Ukraine, and the UK.

Allen was directed by Daley Pearson and Jeffrey Walker, based on a script written by Pearson and Sophie Miller. Along with Power in the role of the adult Petey, the cast features Charlotte Nicdao as Carly, Josh Helman as Jaden, Emily Barclay as Sarah, Benedict Hardie as Rory, Edward A. O'Sullivan as young Petey, Caitlin Niemotko as young Carly, and Tom Broadmore as young Jaden.

Allen is now streaming on Netflix.
