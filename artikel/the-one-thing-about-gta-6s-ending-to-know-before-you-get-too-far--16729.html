Industry observers note that while playing through Grand Theft Auto 6, you might notice that there are a lot of systems that can alter your experience. There’s the relationship system, your criminal profile, fitness routines that change your physique, and plenty more that gives you a lot of control over Jason and Lucia. In the moment-to-moment gameplay, it can be a little hard to figure out how much of that actually has an impact on the game.

In a recent development, be warned, though: every choice you make really, really matters. GTA 6 features multiple endings that will be shaped by how much you engage with its systems. And it never makes it clear that those choices matter. (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

In a recent development, while I won’t reveal how my playthrough ended, I will note that I was faced with a binary choice that could have pushed me toward two different endings. GTA 6 has a few branching narrative moments like this where players are presented with two choices, so that lined up with the rest of the game. In my mind, I assumed that the game only had two endings. That was very incorrect. (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

Recent findings suggest that that revelation came when Polygon spoke with Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson ahead of GTA 6’s release. I had just rolled credits on the game, and Nelson was eager to learn which ending I got. I told him, and made a comment about the binary choice. He revealed that there were actually several possible endings with varying degrees of difference. Nelson said that some of them are small, maybe changing a bit of dialogue in a scene.

Industry observers note that others are more drastic. The climactic moment of my ending took place on a specific bridge on the map. Nelson stated that some endings don’t go there at all. And while I’ve yet to see every ending play out (nor do I even know how many endings there are), I’ve already gotten the sense that the fates of certain characters can even change from ending to ending. If someone tries to spoil exactly what happens in GTA 6’s ending for you, know that you might end up getting surprised when something completely different occurs in your playthrough.

So, what determines which ending you get? Nelson told me that a few different factors go into it. The most obvious one is the choices that you make during branching story moments. You can also probably guess that the relationship system plays into it too. Your criminal profile is a factor as well, so even your choice of using lethal force in various situations can make a difference. On top of that, your ending can also change based on how much money you’ve stashed into your family savings account by the time you hit the credits. Needless to say, no decision goes without consequence in GTA 6.

If you’re stressing out about getting the “bad” ending by accident, don’t worry too much. Nelson noted that there aren’t exactly good or bad endings in GTA 6. Without spoiling where my story went, I can see where he’s coming from. The ending I got made perfect sense with the way I had been playing. And even within that moment, I still had a choice as to how it could play out. So long as you’re making deliberate choices in GTA 6 and not just ignoring systems for the heck of it, you should hopefully land on an ending that matches your playstyle. But keep all this in mind if you decide to ignore Jason and Lucia’s relationship. You’ll feel that eventually.
