According to recent reports, deadlock has already received numerous content updates and limited-time events to help spice up the pre-release gameplay experience, but several of the best creations for Valve's newest shooter are thanks to the community itself. On top of the countless skins, quality-of-life fixes, and trainers, some players have already created full-blown modes paying homage to Deadlock's predecessors.

Although it's not an official game mode like Street Brawl, the mod created and shared by Jetsworm on X fully recreates the classic experience of hunting down players hiding as objects with an impressive level of quality.

Allowing up to 32 players in a single match, the hide-and-seek style mode offers over 150 props to choose from when hiding, fully refined rotation, and several locked-off areas you'll need to either hide or hunt through as opposed to scouring the entire map for hours. Rather than having to host it yourself, the mod custom game mode also has official servers for you to play on either with your friends or random players online. On top of being completely free to play, the custom mode also offers points for each round you complete, which you'll then be able to spend on nameplate cosmetics for effects like changing its color, glow, background, and even animation style.

If you've never played Prop Hunt for yourself and are wondering what all the hype is about, the game mode that originates all the way back to Quake in the 90s before seeing its full success in Team Fortress 2 splits you up between a team that can take the image of objects to hide and hunters tasked with finding and killing them before time runs out.

Despite the simple premise, Prop Hunt has been ported to numerous games over the years, even beyond Valve's own Source Engine games, with franchises like Call of Duty recreating the mode with the release of Call of Duty: Black Ops 6, showing that the mode still offers timeless fun today.
