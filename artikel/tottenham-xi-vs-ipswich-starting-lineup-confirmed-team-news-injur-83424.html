Recent findings suggest that savio, meanwhile, is in line to return to action for the first time since October’s 1-1 draw with Manchester United. The Brazilian winger has missed Tottenham’s last four league games with a muscular issue and is on the bench.

According to recent reports, spurs sorely lacked a creative threat against Leeds. But despite naming Maddison on the bench at Elland Road, De Zerbi didn't bring him on in search of a goal, raising concerns about his fitness.
