Recent findings suggest that it seems that Naughty Dog can’t get enough of Tom Holland, after he starred in their blockbuster live-action Uncharted adaptation. Appearing as Nathan Drake, Holland was a hit with fans and casuals and the film raked in over $400 million at the global box office.

Now, the studio appears to have tapped Holland again to star in the next installment in their cinematic universe, with a fresh report placing the English actor as signed on for the much-rumored project. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

A new report through Production Weekly indicates that Holland is attached to a Jak & Daxter adaptation, a film which he has expressed a personal desire to appear in in the past. His name appears under the cast section, with the film being directed by Ruben Fleischer, who helmed Naughty Dog’s previous Uncharted adaptation.

The Production Weekly concern is from June 2025 but was recently unearthed by fans, and appears to be reputable, as it connects with a previous report from the same time that A Minecraft Movie writers Neil Widener and Gavin James had signed on to write the screenplay. Production Weekly is a long-running trade newsletter that tracks upcoming film and television productions, including details on projects in development, pre-production, and active filming. The project was set for a tentative 2026 shooting date, being filmed in London by Sony Pictures Entertainment. A synopsis of the movie reads: “After Daxter falls into a Dark Eco silo at the forbidden Misty Island, he is transformed from a human into an ottsel, a fictional hybrid of an otter and weasel. In a quest to return Daxter to human form, he and Jak set out to find the Dark Eco sage Gol Acheron. However, the duo find Acheron corrupted by Dark Eco, choosing to use the elusive Light Eco to defeat him and save the world at the cost of Daxter remaining in his ottsel form.”.

Holland himself has previously expressed that he’d love to play Jak in any planned adaptation, saying in 2022: "I would like to make a Jak and Daxter movie, and I would play Jak... But I would make it A24, so it was really weird and dark. Yeah, I would do a really weird live-action version of Jak and Daxter." No details about the film except the synopsis are available, or whether it'll be as dark as Holland hopes.

According to recent reports, A live-action Jak & Daxter adaptation would be the first time the franchise has made it to the silver screen, and would certainly breathe fresh life into Naughty Dog’s dormant IP, which hasn’t seen any new games since 2009’s Jak and Daxter: The Lost Frontier on PlayStation Portable. Despite the huge gap between releases, the franchise is still a cult classic and has a legion of dedicated fans—with Naughty Dog surely feeling another box office success starring Holland would be a great way to attract a new generation of players to their iconic duo.

Recent findings suggest that some players believe that the film may come alongside a major remake of Jak & Daxter’s original outing, which launched in 2001 on PlayStation 2. It would certainly follow the ongoing trend of studios remaking their classic titles for modern audiences, and would indicate a back to basics move for Naughty Dog after its major successes with Uncharted and The Last of Us franchises.

Whether Naughty Dog is willing to invest that much time and money in reviving a series still remains unknown, but it would certainly push the 3D platformer back into the public eye and hopefully mirror successes of other PlayStation classics that made the jump to modern consoles, like Spyro The Dragon and Ratchet and Clank.
