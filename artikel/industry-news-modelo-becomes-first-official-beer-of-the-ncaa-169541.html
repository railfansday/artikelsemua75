Constellation Brands’ Modelo has become the first-ever official beer of the NCAA, getting a leg up on rivals that historically have had a bigger presence in college sports.

The deal covers more than 90 men’s and women’s championships as part of a year-round platform. March Madness will be at the center of the program, but Modelo will also extend its focus to the College World Series and NCAA hockey’s Frozen Four, according to a statement from Constellation.

In a recent development, modelo was already involved with the NCAA. The brand is entering its sixth year as Official Beer Sponsor of the College Football Playoff, works with a 28-team roster of NCAA partner schools, and has increased its college football media investment 18% over last year.
