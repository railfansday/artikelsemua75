I’ve picked out what I think are the nine best deals on Hoka sneakers I’ve tested below, with options for road and trail running. If you shop the Hoka sale itself, you can also get bigger discounts by becoming a Hoka member (it's free and easy to join).

Doing so will entitle you to an extra $40 off orders over $200, $50 off orders over $250, and $60 off orders over $300, thresholds you'll clear with a couple of shoe purchases.

There are some colors of the Hoka Clifton 11 reduced to around this price on Amazon, but this deal on the Hoka Clifton 10 is consistent across all colors in the Hoka sale, and I found the two shoes to be very similar when I tested them out. Amazon's running shoe deals vary hugely based on size and color, but it's worth checking to see if a model of the Mach 7 that suits you is on sale, because it's an excellent lightweight trainer and one of my favorite debuts of 2026.

The Hoka Arahi 8 is a supportive stability shoe with a smooth ride that's great for easy and steady-paced daily training runs. It's very similar to the Hoka Arahi 9 based on my testing, so there's no need to pay more for the newer model if you're looking for a lightweight stability shoe.

It has been revealed that the Skyward Laceless is not a running shoe, but it is the Hoka shoe I wear most often. It's a highly cushioned slip-on sneaker that delivers abundant comfort for long days on your feet, and I love wearing it when recovering from tough runs.

The Hoka Challenger 8 offers a similar ride and level of cushioning to the Clifton 10 and Clifton 11 while having a lugged outsole that grips on light trails as well as being comfortable on the road. It's a great all-rounder for runners who mix up the surfaces they run on, and several colors are available in the Hoka sale.

Sources indicate that amazon's deals on the Clifton 11 are random, with the price jumping around depending on the size and color you pick, but it's worth checking what's available in your size. This is the latest model of the Clifton, and some colors are around the same price as the Clifton 10 on sale.

In a recent development, pretty much every color of the Bondi 9 is reduced in the Hoka sale right now. It's a max-cushioned cruiser that offers a little more comfort than even the Clifton provides, and a favorite among people who spend all day on their feet at work.

Industry observers note that the Hoka Speedgoat 7 is the latest version of Hoka's popular trail-running shoe and offers a comfortable ride and secure grip across a wide variety of terrain. The colors and sizes still left in the sale are limited, so you'll need to move fast to grab this deal.

In a recent development, this springy, carbon plate trail shoe makes every off-road run more fun, and if you are tackling epic ultramarathons and want a fast and comfortable ride, the Hoka Tecton X3 is one of the best shoes available. I've never seen it this cheap before, and there are still plenty of sizes available.
