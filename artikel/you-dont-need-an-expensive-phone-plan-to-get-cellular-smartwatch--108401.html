It has been revealed that I love paying as little as possible for my cell phone plan, and while there are plenty of MVNO (Mobile Virtual Network Operator) options out there that can save you big on your monthly bill, relatively few offer smartwatch data plans. That’s why I’m sharing options from many of the best cheap cell phone plans I’ve tested to show you where you can get your Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch, and Pixel Watch connected.

Recent findings suggest that massive carriers like Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile love to lock LTE wearable plans behind pricey flagship tiers — making it seem like you have to overpay just to leave your phone at home. However, you don’t have to settle for that. Instead, these are the best budget MVNOs that actually keep many of the best smartwatches connected for less. What I love most about Google Fi is that smartwatch data is already included with its cell phone prepares.

While Google Fi’s Flexible plan also lumps in smartwatch data at no additional cost, you have to pay for every gigabyte you use — which comes out to $10 per GB. You’ll have your choice of connecting either a Pixel Watch or a Galaxy Watch, though there’s still no support for the Apple Watch. I’ve been on Visible for nearly six years now, and it’s without question one of the best unlimited cell phone plans out there.

Sources indicate that presently, the base service plan with this Verizon-backed MVNO comes out to $25/month, which includes unlimited talk, text, and data — with the option to add smartwatch data for $10/month. That puts your total cost at $35/month and gives you the option to connect most smartwatches, like the Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch, and Pixel Watch. The only downside to this base plan is that you don’t get Verizon’s faster 5G Ultra Wideband support, which is reserved for Visible+ and Visible+ Pro.

I’ve gone back to Mint Mobile several times already with 6-month stints each time. Why’s that? Because the T-Mobile-owned MVNO frequently runs generous promotions that beat Visible on price.

Recent findings suggest that mint Mobile’s cheapest cell phone plan is its 6GB tier, available in either 3- or 12-month terms. It costs $15/month ($45 for 3 months, or $180 for 12 months) for 6GB of premium data, while adding smartwatch connectivity requires a $10/month add-on fee (also billed upfront for the term). The only catch is that Mint’s wearable plan works exclusively with the Apple Watch, leaving Galaxy Watch and Pixel Watch owners out.

According to recent reports, now, if you’re ok with that, you can get even greater value out of Mint Mobile right now thanks to its $15/month promotion across all looks — including unlimited. For example, you can get a full year of unlimited service with a one-time $180 upfront payment, which comes out to $15/month. It has been revealed that even though it’s not as popular as some of the other MVNOs, U.S. Mobile has a compelling smartwatch blueprint that’s easy on most budgets. You can pair its $25/month Unlimited Starter plan with its $6.50/month smartwatch add-on for a total of $31.50/month — getting you a cell phone plan that packages unlimited high-speed data, 20GB of hotspot functionality, and international calling.

Like Visible, U.S. Mobile supports popular cellular-connected smartwatches on the market right now — including the Pixel Watch and Apple Watch (just keep in mind your line needs to be on U.S. Mobile's Warp network). All you need for unlimited access is to add that $6.50/month smartwatch companion plan.

If you’ve been reading some of my latest articles, then you know how impressed I am with RedPocket Mobile. Out of all these MVNOs, it offers the cheapest entry fee for a cell phone and smartwatch bundle.

Sources indicate that redPocket Mobile’s $10/month Essentials plan comes with unlimited talk and text, 3GB of premium data, unlimited calling to 20+ countries, and more. However, it at the moment only supports the Apple Watch — which requires a $5/month smartwatch add-on, bringing your combined bill to just $15/month.

Sources indicate that while that rate is impressive on its own, you can save even more by going with its annual plans. For example, the Essentials plan normally runs $120/year, but a promo slashes it to just $60 for the first year before renewing at $110. The cell phone plan alone comes out to a mind-blowing $5/month — and throwing in the $5/month smartwatch add-on brings your total to practically $10/month.
