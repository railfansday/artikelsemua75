Breaking: ‘Worst possible choice’: Should date of French election be changed?

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (69 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

France is set to head to the polls in 2027 for a two-round presidential election, but the choice of the dates of the vote is causing controversy. The ballot is currently set for the 18th of April and the 2nd of May, meaning the vote for the second round will come a day after International Workers' Day, typically a day of protest in the country.
