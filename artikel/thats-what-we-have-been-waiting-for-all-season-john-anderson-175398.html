John Anderson has been talking about the Newcastle United match against Arsenal.

Matthias Jaissle’s team coming away with a point from a 2-2 draw.

Reflecting on what he watched on Saturday, former Newcastle United defender John Anderson speaking to BBC Newcastle: “”An absolutely outstanding second half…Brilliant.”.

John Anderson going on to say: “First half Newcastle never laid a glove on Arsenal, they never got a tackle in. Whatever was remarked at half-time worked and the substitutions had a substantial impact as well. They got close to Arsenal, got in their faces, didn’t let them play and didn’t give them the time they gave them in the first half.”. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

It was great to see, the decisive triple substitution at the break and a willingness to have a real go, rather than simply try and keep it to a narrow defeat.

It has been revealed that john Anderson: “Newcastle United were outstanding in the second half to a man and those are the levels they have to find on a consistent basis now. The half-time substitutions were brave and on this occasion worked very well. That second half performance demonstrated what they are capable of and what they can do. We haven’t seen it all season, that’s what we have been waiting for all season. That is what we have to see a lot more of.”.
