Industry observers note that alibaba Cloud confirmed on November 19, 2026, that its third data center in Malaysia’s Johor public cloud region is available, bringing its footprint in the country to six. The expansion adds local capacity for cloud and AI workloads, including for organizations that need to keep their data within Malaysia.

Johor became Alibaba Cloud’s second Malaysian cloud region in June 2026, complementing three data centers in Kuala Lumpur. Choong Hon Keat, general manager of Malaysia at Alibaba Cloud Intelligence, linked the additional capacity to AI adoption among partners in banking, telecommunications, digital payments, energy and creative industries.

Alibaba Cloud remarked the expansion brings its global infrastructure to 109 availability zones across 32 regions and forms part of the $53 billion AI and cloud infrastructure investment Alibaba revealed in 2025.

AFED Digital, based in Cyberjaya, already builds and runs its software on Alibaba Cloud infrastructure in Malaysia. Its EDAFY suite applies AI to complex subsurface and field data for upstream oil-and-gas exploration and production teams, while Dynectus is its enterprise resource planning product. It has been revealed that dayDreamer Group uses Alibaba’s Qwen and Wan models through Model Studio, Alibaba Cloud’s AI development platform, to power Theater, its live AI video-creation platform. Theater combines scriptwriting and translation through Qwen with visual and video generation through Wan, covering projects ranging from advertisements to short dramas. The companies have also jointly hosted workshops for Malaysian creators and businesses. (Related: British Airways Avios — Short Nonstop Domestic Flights...)

Other agreements are at an earlier stage. CelcomDigi, described in the release as Malaysia’s largest mobile network operator with more than 20 million customers, signed a memorandum of understanding to explore API-driven digital solutions, joint staff training and outreach. AIGC Lab Malaysia, a Mandarin-speaking AI-generated content community and learning platform, signed a separate MoU covering possible educational content, community showcases, workshops and industry events.

It has been revealed that alibaba Cloud and MyDIGITAL Corporation also jointly rolled out Kita Bersama AI under the AI untuk Rakyat initiative. Running through January 18, 2027, the contest aims to reach 100,000 Malaysians aged 18 to 30. It invites them to apply AI to everyday problems in studies, workplaces, small businesses and communities.

The contest is built around Smart Learning, Smart Working and Smart Creating, with tutorials and guided practice. In the proposed productivity track, participants will use Alibaba’s MuleRun agent platform to build AI-assisted workflows. In the creative track, they will use Alibaba Cloud’s WonderClip video-creation platform to develop original visual stories, storyboards or short videos.

Digital Minister Gobind Singh Deo connected the initiatives to Malaysia’s digital-economy ambitions: “Investment in local AI infrastructure and talent by partners such as Alibaba Cloud supports our ambition to make Malaysia a regional leader in the digital economy, and I look forward to seeing what our young people create through Kita Bersama AI.”.

Recent findings suggest that the new capacity is available, while several accompanying demand-side initiatives remain at the MoU or outreach stage. Turning those projects and AI skills programs into sustained local workloads is the central business question raised by the expansion.
