Poseidon Expeditions has unveiled seasonal savings on its 2027 Arctic voyages aboard the Sea Spirit, available until November 30, 2026.

The manufacturer remarked the Sea Spirit’s 2027 Arctic season offers the opportunity to explore remote regions on expeditions designed for immersive, off-the-beaten-path exploration.

Poseidon noted in a press release that every voyage is led by its team of polar guides, naturalists, historians and photographers.

Until November 30, 2026, travelers can enjoy 20 percent off all cabins except triples on the following departures:. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

“British Isles, Faroe Islands and Iceland” (May 20 to June 2, 2027) traces a route from the shores of the British Isles to the Faroe Islands and onward to Iceland, with landings beyond the reach of conventional cruise ship routes.

“In the Realm of the Polar Bear” (June 13 to 22, 2027) is a West Svalbard and Polar Ice Edge voyage designed to maximize time in prime polar bear habitat, with the expedition planning to spend several days exploring along the edge of the pack ice for opportunities to observe polar bears in their natural environment.

“West Svalbard and Polar Ice Edge,” a departure with Spanish-language support (June 29 to July 8, 2027), offers guests the opportunity to experience the Arctic in Spanish as well as English aboard the Sea Spirit.

The voyage explores Svalbard’s fjords, glaciers, walrus haul-outs and seabird cliffs, with opportunities to discover the shifting pack ice and look for polar bears, subject to weather and ice conditions.

“The Best of Svalbard in One Week” (July 25 to August 1, 2027) brings together the highlights of Svalbard, from glacier-filled fjords and Arctic tundra to historic sites, wildlife and changing ice formations.
