It was this time last year that silver was on the rise and beginning to take off, eventually hitting a peak of more than $121 per ounce in January 2026. What's happened since then, however, has been a rapid reversal.

In a recent development, today, silver trades at around $40. Along with its free fall, the iShares Silver Trust (SLV -5.05%) , which tracks the price of silver, has also tanked, losing more than 40% of its value this year, and it's down more than 65% from its 52-week high of $109.83. (Related: Breaking: Future Value Calculator: How to Project Your ...)

Given the massive sell-off and reduced valuation, could this silver-focused fund be a good investment right now?

Sources indicate that the substantial question for investors considering this fund is likely what will happen to the price of silver. Will it soar again, or is it destined to go even lower? If it takes off in value again, the iShares Silver Trust will quickly become a hot buy.

The problem is, there's no guarantee that will happen. What took place late last year was unexpected, not unlike what has happened to speculative investments such as meme stocks in the past. Silver caught the attention of retail investors and reached levels it's never reached before. There's unfortunately no reason to expect that might happen again, simply because it is a largely speculative asset.

Sources indicate that meanwhile, as interest rates have increased, there's even been more of a reason to move away from precious metals and into income-generating investments, especially as yields are higher. Thus, it's hard to make a bull case for silver these days.

NYSEMKT · As of 11:34 AM ET 11/24/26.

The main appeal of the iShares Silver Trust today is that it can help diversify a portfolio by giving investors access to investments outside of the broader markets. But beyond that, I don't see a reason as to why silver might suddenly soar once again or why to invest in the fund. A resurgence may not happen until there's reason to believe that interest rates might be coming down again, which doesn't appear likely anytime soon.

Sources indicate that since the performance of the iShares Silver Trust depends heavily on the price of silver, investors should treat it as a speculative, potentially highly volatile investment. While it is down major from its high, there's no guarantee that it'll get back to those levels anytime soon.
