Liverpool have opened preliminary talks over a fresh contract with Alexis Mac Allister following his strong start to the season, sources have told Football Insider.

Mac Allister's future on Merseyside has been uncertain over the last few months after he revealed his disappointment at not being offered a latest deal.

His current contract is set to expire in 2028, with an extension previously not believed to be in Liverpool's plans.

The 27-year-old has proven his quality with consistently impressive performances so far this season under Andoni Iraola.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, those strong showings have pushed the Reds' chiefs into opening talks over a upcoming deal with Mac Allister and his representatives.

The Argentine international is keen to extend his stay at Anfield beyond 2028 and looks set to continue being key to Iraola throughout this season.

Dynamic midfielder Mac Allister has already matched his Liverpool goal tally from last season in 39 fewer appearances, scoring his fifth of the campaign in their last outing against Crystal Palace.

He has returned to top form after a disappointing 2025-26, and a bright future is now believed to await for him under Iraola at Anfield.

In a recent development, speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "He’s playing his way to a new contract, really. He's arguably been Liverpool’s best player this season after a disappointing last campaign. He's popped up with some key goals and has been influential, nailing down a regular starting spot in Iraola's starting eleven.

"Early talks have taken place now, I think they have reached out to his father and his agent to try and discuss a new deal. Obviously I think it’s very early stages in these contract talks.".

The 27-year-old is among the best-performing Premier League midfielders this season for accurate long balls (3.68), line-breaking passes (14.4), fewest times dispossessed (0.36) and possession won in the final third (0.83) per 90 minutes.

He has been a breath of fresh air compared to what fans saw him become under Slot, and is clearly devoted to helping Liverpool return to the top in the years to come.

Sources indicate that mac Allister's latest quotes around his contract situation prove that, as he told the press in early November: "Last season I was told that the club's idea was not to renew it, at least for now. And obviously, that was a painful, unpleasant situation. I still had, and still have, time left at this club, which is what I want most – to stay here.".

"The reality is that the manager has now changed, the sporting director has changed, and new investors are coming into the club. So I think I'm now in a position where anything can happen. I've always noted that I'm very happy here, that I really enjoy it, and we'll see.".
