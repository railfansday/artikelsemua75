Fed’s Inflation Fight May Require More Hikes

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (92 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Neil Dutta, head of US economic research at Renaissance Macro Research, tells Bloomberg This Weekend that the Federal Reserve made a mistake by holding rates steady in October and may need multiple hikes as inflation remains stubbornly above its 2% target. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Dutta indicates the recent market selloff does not yet signal broader economic trouble, arguing that AI investment and a resilient labor market continue to support growth even as the Fed has less flexibility to cushion falling markets.
