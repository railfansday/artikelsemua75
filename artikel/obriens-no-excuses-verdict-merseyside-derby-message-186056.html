Jake O’Brien notes there are “no excuses” for Everton’s 4-0 defeat to Brentford on Monday, but believes Sunday’s Merseyside derby could provide the perfect motivation to bounce back.

A below-par display saw the Blues – reduced to 10 men after James Tarkowski’s first-half red card – defeated at the Gtech Community Stadium. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Speaking after the game, defender O’Brien stated: “It was really bad from start to finish.

“It was a knock-on effect of decisions and mistakes, and it carried on from there, really.

According to recent reports, “There’s no excuses. We were really bad, but I think we can put this behind us.

“We’re much better than this and we know we’ve got major games coming up, especially [against Liverpool].

“It was a really bad game and we apologise to all the fans who travelled.

“But I think we can put this behind us, and there’s no better game to come in firing and change things around.”.
