As one of the leaders of modern-day minimalism, it makes sense that Hailey Bieber would wink to the woman who arguably started it all: Carolyn Bessette Kennedy.

According to recent reports, this afternoon, Mrs. Bieber attended the 2026 DealBook Summit in Lincoln Center—a conference that unites some of the biggest leaders in tech, business, and politics—to discuss her mega-successful beauty brand, Rhode.

At the summit, she pulled out a supreme fashion reference, with an archival set from the Yohji Yamamoto Spring/Summer 1998 collection, once worn by none other than serious Yamamoto enthusiast CBK.

The finely tailored look consisted of a black Mandarin-collar jacket with a pinched waist and a split hem, plus a row of matching buttons dotting the front.

It was paired with a cream linen midi skirt that hugged Bieber’s legs, and for accessories, she matched the jacket with inky patent-leather pumps and one of CBK’s favorite finishing touches: an acetate headband.

It has been revealed that bessette Kennedy famously wore this look back in June 1998, when she attended the Robin Hood Foundation benefit with her husband, John F. Kennedy Jr., at the 26th Street Armory in Newly introduced York City. For the occasion, she tied her hair back into a ponytail and went sans jewelry.

Recent findings suggest that we were all reminded of CBK’s chic style earlier this year, when FX released the smash Love Story: John F. Kennedy Jr. and Carolyn Bessette miniseries. Stars like Bella Hadid and Sarah Pidgeon (the latter of whom portrayed Bessette Kennedy in the show) all seemed to take a page out of the former Calvin Klein publicist’s styling book for their own outfits. And Bieber also joined in on the fun.
