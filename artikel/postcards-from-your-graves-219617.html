2) Delivered via Western Union, water-stained and faded, with instructions to hold for delivery until the present day:.

In a recent development, aloha, B. You’ll never guess where I ended up! Hawaii! A bit of a detour from “two weeks ago” (I swear, I entered it correctly! The machine must have some bugs to work out!) I’ll write again with the exact date as soon as I can find a newspaper, but it must be the early ’40s. Lots of war talk among the men stationed here. Love, T.

I plot them all out like a madman, connecting dates and places and all the catastrophes that conspire to keep us apart. I haunt disaster sites, archives and museums, searching for clues for my collection, as if completing the puzzle might bring you back.

3) Scratched onto a lifeboat oar at the Titanic museum:.

Industry observers note that happened again, B. Not much time before the you-know-what. Wanted you to know I was here. T.

4) Sharpied discreetly on The Sphere by Fritz Koenig, displayed at Liberty Park, New York:.

Recent findings suggest that I hate knowing what’s coming, B. I want to warn these people. Would they even believe me if I did? T.

5) Printed in the editorial section of a 1900 newspaper:.

This cryptic telegram, addressed to our editor on Sept. 8th, was the last to arrive from Galveston before the recent storm.

To: Editor of the Houston Post.

Please print the following in your newspaper, word for word.

Sources indicate that “B. Trip to Hawaii was a disaster. Infamously. Zeroes got me. Somehow ended up here. Trying to figure out how to get back to you before the storm hits. Love, T.”.

I am the one who’s filled our home with history, while you’ve lived your life in dogged pursuit of the future. Is it ironic, then, that you’re there, in those places I know so well, while I’m stuck here, trying to sort out what happens next?

6) From a note scribbled in the communion ledger of St Michael’s Church, Old Town, Chicago:.

Recent findings suggest that B — I think it’s the large language model. Like fire, it follows the path of least resistance, the most well-worn ruts in the road. What rut is more worn in the road of time-travel stories than experiencing, preventing, observing disasters? And what disasters are more well-worn than these? T.
