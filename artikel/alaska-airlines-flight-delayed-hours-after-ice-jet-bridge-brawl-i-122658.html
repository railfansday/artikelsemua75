Industry observers note that an Alaska Airlines flight was delayed by hours after masked ICE agents stormed the jet bridge of a departing flight to arrest a Mexican national who allegedly had a work permit had overstayed his visa (you know, “the worst of the worst”). This ended up turning into a huge jet bridge melee, leading to additional arrests, as ICE claims that someone hurled hot coffee at the officers.

It has been revealed that in the end, the flight was delayed by hours, as the crew was no longer fit to fly, and the Port of Seattle is outraged. This is quite the story, as flagged by PYOK.

This incident happened on the morning of Saturday, November 21, 2026, and involves Alaska Airlines flight AS1306, scheduled to operate from Seattle (SEA) to Los Angeles (LAX).

This was first reported by a passenger on the flight, who explained that “ICE agents just arrested multiple people at SeaTac Airport inside the jet bridge as we were all boarding,” and went on to explain that they were “deplaning because the crew is being replaced due to poor morale.”. The Port of Seattle expressed its outrage about the incident, releasing the following statement:.

We are outraged about this morning’s incident involving ICE and two passengers as travelers boarded AS 1306 to Los Angeles. Immigration enforcement actions at SEA are conducted without the Port’s participation or knowledge. The Port has continually asked, and local federal immigration authorities have previously agreed to protocols at SEA, and we are in conversation with DHS on whether any of these protocols were breached. However, the Port remains committed to the safety of all of our travelers and staff and airline team. This incident was not only distressing for the affected family but the passengers around them, and Port officials continue to engage federal officials to understand more about what occurred.

Washington Governor Bob Ferguson was also angry, as he held a press conference about what happened, and released the following statement:.

ICE is completely and totally out of control. Like many Americans, I’m outraged by masked agents, who do not identify themselves, dragging people off jetways as they board a flight, creating chaos in our communities. Today’s actions at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport are just the latest example of ICE’s deeply un-American tactics. I’ll have more to say this afternoon.

Meanwhile Alaska Airlines took a more toned down approach, and just expressed “concern,” releasing the following statement:.

In a recent development, we are concerned by this morning’s law enforcement action during the boarding of Alaska Flight 1306. We recognize how distressing this incident has been for employees and guests. Alaska Airlines is required to comply with applicable laws and regulations when interacting with law enforcement officials. We do not direct or coordinate immigration enforcement activity, and we remain committed to protecting the privacy and safety of our guests and employees wherever possible.
