Christine Brown is shedding light on a dark chapter in her family history.

The Sister Wives star—who announced she was leaving her plural marriage to Kody Brown in 2021—detailed how the life and tragic death of her grandfather Rulon Clark Allred impacted her views around polygamy.

“The biggest reason I wanted to live a plural marriage was because of my grandmas,” Christine told husband David Woolley during the Nov. 22 episode of Sister Wives. “My grandpa was killed when I was 5 and my grandmas were still best friends after that.”.

“And they stayed together and they hung out together all the time,” she added. “So I thought that would be an ideal situation.”.

Before his death in 1977, Rulon had an integral role in his community as the leader of the Apostolic United Brethren fundamentalist group in Utah.

And throughout his time as a religious leader, the TLC star noted that her grandfather “believed in being public” and thought it was “really vital to open up and let people know” about his family life.

But not everyone was pleased with his teachings.

Industry observers note that “He had this article where he talked about how he believed he was a prophet, he was the mouthpiece for God on this planet. And there was someone else from another church and he felt like he was actually the mouthpiece for God,” she remarked. “He got his wives to kill my grandpa.
