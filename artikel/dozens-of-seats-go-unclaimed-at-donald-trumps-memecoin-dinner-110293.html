Presently, only 130 crypto whale wallets qualified for Trump’s fancy memecoin dinner at the golf club.

In a recent development, just 130 crypto wallets secured seats for the November 22 TRUMP token holder dinner at Donald Trump’s Virginia golf club, leaving dozens of the 185 available places unclaimed. The Official Trump token was trading near record lows at the time of the close. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

The event is designed to reward the largest holders, with the top 29 wallets also eligible for a VIP reception. Contest participants held roughly $8.6 million in tokens combined when the qualification period ended, a sharp drop in engagement from the project’s first holder dinner. Ranking was based on time-weighted holdings, which encouraged accumulation and retention through the window.

Sources indicate that more than 5,000 eligible holders reportedly did not register. The leaderboard’s combined value represented only a small fraction of TRUMP’s roughly $268 million market capitalization.

According to recent reports, TRUMP was trading around $0.95 in the latest reporting, down roughly 46% over the prior month after setting a record low near $0.896 on November 14. The token remains well below its peak, with one account describing it as down almost 99% from its high.

Recent findings suggest that large holders added to positions during the dip. Nansen data revealed the top 100 wallets increased their combined holdings by 1.27% over 30 days, while a separate whale cohort expanded holdings by 26.63%. Exchange balances fell slightly over the same period.

Holding incentives are temporary.

According to recent reports, top-ranked winners risk losing VIP benefits if balances drop below specified levels before the event, and lower-ranked winners can forfeit their seat by selling more than half their qualifying balance. Those restrictions expire once the dinner is over.

Ahead of the 2025 dinner, 92 of 220 invited holders reportedly sold their entire positions before the event.

A Super Bowl-themed leaderboard planned for February 2027 may give some buyers a reason to stay involved, but it does not remove the immediate question of whether winners will take liquidity afterward.
