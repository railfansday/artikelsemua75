Manchester United fans are becoming increasingly annoyed with the way the club is being run by the current decision-makers.

Chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox have come under fire already this season, but the club's continued struggles have ramped up the pressure.

Berrada in particular has been slammed after his recent interview where he insisted the Red Devils are heading in the right direction and the squad is strong enough to compete.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that the fans are not stupid and will see through the blatant attempts by the board to cover up their mistakes.

The decision to sack Michael Carrick and bring in Mauricio Pochettino felt inevitable, but while the fans are ready to get behind their latest manager, they remain angry at the board.

Ongoing concerns about recruitment and the managerial situation have grown, while off-field problems including the treatment of fans will also not be ignored.

Football Insider revealed last month that Man United are fearing a potential dressing room mutiny after both Lisandro Martinez and Bruno Fernandes spoke out.

The fans at Old Trafford are not the only ones who are unhappy with the board's decisions, with the players also growing frustrated, in what is a bad sign for the hierarchy.

While they continue to insist that there are no major problems and things will sort themselves out, the fans can see differently, and are now turning on the decision-makers.

"I still can't think of a single decision this board has made that has had a positive impact," Brown told Football Insider.

"Every now and then, one of them comes out with another statement where they try to pretend everything in the garden is rosy and that there are no major problems.

"Now it's Berrada, trying to convince everybody that they're heading in the right direction and that the squad is good enough, but the Man United fans are not stupid.

"They're not just going to listen to every word he states and think 'well that must be true', because they can see what's happening on the pitch.

"If he genuinely thinks this squad, and the players they've brought in, are good enough to compete, then I have to question how he is still in that job.

"The Man United fans are not happy, the players are not happy, and before long those frustrations are going to be made clear, and it's the likes of Berrada and Wilcox who will face the brunt of it.

"They cannot continue to get away with decisions which are making Man United worse on and off the pitch, because people aren't going to sit by and let it happen. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

"The way things have been handled is a disgrace, everybody can see that, but they'll continue to turn a blind eye to it, pretend everything is okay, and hope people will believe them.".

Man United's decision to replace Carrick with Pochettino was made in an attempt to arrest their dismal start to the season and kickstart a turnaround in their form.

However, after a 4-0 defeat to Liverpool in the newly introduced boss' first game, it has become clear that it will take more than a change in manager to resolve the Red Devils' issues. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Brown told Football Insider on 16 November that Pochettino is destined to fail to Man United and has already been hamstrung by the "incompetent" board.

There is a feeling that, regardless of which manager is brought in to steer the ship, they will face an uphill struggle to turn things around with the way the club has been run.

This comes after Pochettino became the fourth permanent manager to take charge at Man United in just four years, with each having suffered from the same problems.
