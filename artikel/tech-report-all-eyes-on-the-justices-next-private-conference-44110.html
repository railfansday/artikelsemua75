📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Senate confirmed Justice Joseph Story to the Supreme Court on this day in 1811. When he joined three months later at age 32, he became the youngest justice in Supreme Court history, as we noted in a Closer Look in May.

Sources indicate that on Tuesday, the Supreme Court indicated that several notable petitions for review will be considered at the justices’ next private conference on Friday, Dec. 4. The disputes address such issues as the Trump administration’s ban on transgender servicemembers and its approach to appointing acting U.S. attorneys.
