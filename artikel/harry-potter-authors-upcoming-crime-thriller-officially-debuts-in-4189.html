When thinking of an upcoming J.K. Rowling adaptation, you'd be forgiven for first thinking of HBO's new Harry Potter series. On December 25, a brand-new series of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone debuts on HBO, with the world waiting with bated breath to see if showrunner Francesca Gardiner and the rest of the team can pull off the most ambitious small-screen adaptation yet. Despite the ongoing controversy surrounding the Wizarding World's creator, hype for the series is clearly high, with the first trailer earning over 277 million views across platforms in its first 48 hours, making it the most-watched trailer in HBO history.

It has been revealed that A star-studded line-up might've unsurprisingly been assembled — including Janet McTeer as Minerva McGonagall, Nick Frost as Rubeus Hagrid, and Paapa Essiedu as Severus Snape — and a budget of roughly $25 million per episode reportedly assigned, but, for some, this isn't the most exciting Rowling adaptation on the horizon. That would instead be the upcoming seventh season of Strike, subtitled The Running Grave, which will adapt Rowling's 2023 crime novel written under the pseudonym Robert Galbraith.

Recent findings suggest that tom Burke's private detective Cormoran Strike and Holliday Grainger's Robin Ellacott will return for five more episodes in the hit BBC drama, which scored huge numbers for its last season. The Ink Black Heart, the most recent installment, averaged 7.1 million viewers across its run in 2024, becoming one of the BBC's most-watched dramas in the process. The next season will hope for similar success, and the BBC have now finally revealed when it will get started. Strike: The Running Grave will officially premiere on Sunday, December 6, with the following episode airing just 24 hours later on BBC One.

Set to be the most intense season yet, certainly if the source material is anything to go by, you'll want to strap in for another batch of must-watch episodes when Strike: The Running Grave debuts. But what will the latest season be about? "When Sir Colin and Sally Edensor approach Cormoran Strike and Robin Ellacott, they are desperate to reconnect with their estranged son, Will," the official synopsis begins. It continues, "The Edensors enlist the detectives to gather evidence to discredit the Universal Humanitarian Church, a religious cult who have indoctrinated Will and siphoned off his trust fund.".

Strike: The Running Grave will officially premiere on Sunday, December 6. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
