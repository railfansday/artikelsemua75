📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Country Music Hall of Famer Dolly Parton‘s catalog keeps achieving latest milestones.

Spotify presented The Dolly Parton Estate with a plaque on Friday (Nov. 20) to commemorate the country icon’s Billboard Hot 100 No. 16 hit “Jolene” surpassing 1 billion streams on the music streaming platform. Additionally, the Recording Industry Association of America presented a double-Platinum plaque honoring “There Was Jesus,” Parton’s collaboration with Zach Williams the same day. Parton’s niece Danielle Parton accepted both accolades on behalf of The Dolly Parton Estate during a meeting at Dolly’s Life of Many Colors Museum, located in Dolly Parton’s Songteller Hotel in downtown Nashville. (Related: Tech Report: Estate Planning for Early Retirement: The ...)

“‘Jolene’ is officially part of @spotify Billions Club,” Danielle Parton captioned her Instagram post about the news Friday. “So honored to accept this on behalf of @dollyparton at @dollyslifeofmanycolors Museum. 🦋 Her music will live on forever.”.

According to recent reports, spotify’s chief public affairs officer Dustee Jenkins presented the “Jolene” billions plaque, alongside Spotify’s Nashville-based and Global Music team members, among them Ashley Graver, Jackie Augustus, Katie Studley, Monica Damashek and Tim Foisset. Jackie Jones, RIAA’s svp of of artist and industry relations, presented the organization’s plaque celebrating “There Was Jesus,” which officially reached double-Platinum status on Oct. 6.

Parton released “Jolene” in 1973, and the song has become one of her most enduring hits. It topped Billboard‘s Hot Country Singles (now called Hot Country Songs) chart in 1974, and in 2014, was entered into the Grammy Hall of Fame. Several artists have covered “Jolene” over the years, including The White Stripes, Olivia-Newton John and Miley Cyrus, while Beyoncé included a lyrically revamped version of the song on her Grammy-winning album Cowboy Carter. (Parton herself was also featured on the album.).

There are fewer than 25 country songs that have reached the Billions Club on Spotify.

According to recent reports, as for “There Was Jesus,” Parton and Williams teamed up to release the track in 2019. The song reached No. 1 on the Christian Airplay chart, and earned a Grammy win for best contemporary Christian music performance/song. In 2024, Williams and Parton collaborated again, on the song “Lookin’ for You.”.
