Industry observers note that after launch, every client of mine gets a Sanity Studio to manage their site. This is the short version of the walkthrough I give them.

In a recent development, open yoursite.com/studio and sign in with the Google or email account you were invited with. You’ll see your content types on the left: pages, blog posts, settings and anything specific to your site. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Nothing goes live until you click Publish. On most sites I build, the page updates within seconds of publishing. You can also restore any older version from the history panel if something goes wrong.

That’s really it. If your current CMS feels harder than this, it might be time to move.
