Industry observers note that XPENG just reported 3rd quarter financial details today, while also forecasting what will happen in the 4th quarter. Everything is looking quite rosy at the moment.

As far as 3rd quarter financials go, on the back of strong sales, the firm logged 24.28 billion yuan ($3.643 billion) of revenue. That’s a upcoming quarterly record for the company. It’s 19% more than in Q3 2025 and 23% more than Q2 2026.

It has been revealed that that said, despite strong revenue, the organization still scored a net loss. It had a significant 870 million yuan ($130.5 million) net loss, in fact, which was up 128% from 380 million yuan in Q3 2025 and down 35% from 1.34 billion from Q2 2026. XPENG made a small profit in Q4 2025, and since then has again been trying to get its finances into the green again instead of in the red. One thing making that unlikely, though, is that XPENG’s R&D costs were up significantly, 17.5%. Also, general and administrative expenses were up 11.4% year over year. We’ll see what the 4th quarter brings.
