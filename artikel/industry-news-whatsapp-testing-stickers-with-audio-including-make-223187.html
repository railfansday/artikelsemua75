According to recent reports, whatsApp is presently testing a new sticker format that plays a sound when the recipient taps on it. You can choose from a range of standard stickers as well as create your own by converting a video ….

The feature was spotted by WABetaInfo in the beta version of the Android app.

WhatsApp is developing a fresh format to add an audio track to a sticker. This would allow people to send a sticker that plays a sound when the recipient taps it.

You can create your own audio stickers by selecting one of your videos.

Once people choose a video from their gallery, WhatsApp converts it into an animated sticker automatically. At the top of the editor, users can find a latest audio button that lets them add a sound to the sticker. If users tap this button, WhatsApp will display a new “Add sound” screen. This section lets users refine the new sound sticker by choosing which audio track to attach to it.

Sounds don’t play automatically, but recipients will see a small audio icon attached to the sticker, and tapping it will play the sound.
