West Bank attacks: Opposition leader Eisenkot decries "Jewish terrorism"

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (70 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Israeli authorities have opened an investigation after Palestinian vehicles and properties were set on fire in three villages in the occupied West Bank. Israeli soldiers were deployed to the area following the attacks, which local residents said were carried out by Israeli settlers. Gadi Eisenkot, a rival of outgoing Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, condemned the acts on X, describing them as “Jewish terrorism”. Antonia Kerrigan reports.
