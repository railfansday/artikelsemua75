Breaking: AEREDIUM bets parallel execution can raise blockchain speed limits

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (85 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Blockchain developers have long struggled to process high transaction volumes without slowing down network agreement. As digital payment systems expand, developers are exploring parallel processing to increase capacity across separate locations. According to researchers at the Bank for International Settlements, the trade-off among a blockchain's transaction capacity, the concentration of its stakeholders, and its security is known as Buterin's scalability trilemma. Because traditional blockchains process transactions sequentially, high demand often triggers severe congestion and rising user fees during peak market activity.
