According to recent reports, hedera [HBAR] has shed 3.79% in the past 24 hours, continuing its downtrend. In mid-October, the former support at $0.088 had been breached, setting off a steady wave of selling.

Sources indicate that the past week’s market-wide volatility did not help HBAR bulls. At the time of writing, they were defending the $0.055 support zone.

Industry observers note that in other news, it was reported that Canary Capital’s HBAR spot ETF, the only U.S. spot ETF offering exposure to Hedera’s native token, now holds around 549 million HBAR. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

That was around 1.3% of the 43 billion circulating supply. It is long-term bullish for tokens to be accumulated in regulated infrastructure. But is it enough to halt the dominant downtrend?

Recent findings suggest that hedera’s weekly price chart indicated that the bulls tried to push prices past the $0.107 swing level from February. They tried twice and failed twice, with the latest attempt coming in September.

It has been revealed that since this rejection, the price has closed a weekly session below the $0.0645 swing low. Moreover, the price action showed a bearish long-term trend continuation.

Besides, the $0.0575 level, which had been an vital support from November 2024, has also been breached.

Long-term HBAR investors might have to rethink their bullish bias following the steady losses and the altcoin’s inability to defend key demand zones.

The 4-hour chart demonstrated that the $0.059 and $0.054 were the key horizontal levels to watch. The RSI was still below neutral 50, showing bears continue to have the upper hand.

Recent findings suggest that the Stochastic RSI was about to form a bullish crossover, which could be followed by a price bounce. Swing traders can look to sell this bounce, if one appears and gets close to $0.058-$0.059.

It has been revealed that A breakdown below $0.0548 will confirm further bearishness. Meanwhile, a breakout past $0.0594 will signal some respite to the downtrend and possibly a bigger bounce.
