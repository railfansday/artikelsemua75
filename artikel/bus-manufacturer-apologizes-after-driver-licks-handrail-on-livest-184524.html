A bus driver in Japan has been removed from driving duties after licking the hand straps and handrails inside his bus during a livestream, apparently at the request of a female streamer.

The driver works for Keikyu Bus, which runs services across Tokyo and Kanagawa, including routes to Haneda Airport, and the incident took place on November 16 after the bus had finished service for the day. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

“I want you to lick the part you hold onto, a bit further up,” she remarked, according to Japanese outlet coki, to which he replied, “The part you hold?”.

The streamer has been identified on social media as Riakyun, who reportedly streams on the Japanese livestreaming app Fuwatch, though Keikyu Bus did not mention her in its statement.

The driver reported himself to the firm the same day, and on November 18, Keikyu Bus posted an official apology on its website.

“We sincerely apologize for the great discomfort and concern this has caused,” the manufacturer said, pledging to “thoroughly educate all employees” to prevent it from happening again.

Keikyu Bus told J-CAST News that the driver has been taken off driving duties, and that it intends to take disciplinary action against him in line with its internal rules.

The company also confirmed the bus involved has been identified and fully disinfected and cleaned.
