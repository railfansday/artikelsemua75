Breaking: Robot submarine exploring world's deepest ocean trench finds unprecedented volcanic activity

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (89 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that fifty-two million years ago, the Pacific tectonic plate began to slide under the Philippine Sea plate, forming the Mariana Trench, the deepest known valley on the Earth's ocean floor. The deepest part of this abyss, the Challenger Deep, is almost 11,000 meters (36,000 feet) below sea level. But why is it so deep? Scientists trying to answer this question sent a remotely operated vehicle to collect samples around the Deep, where they found evidence of the world's deepest underwater explosive volcanic eruptions.
