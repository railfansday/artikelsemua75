Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds decided to mark the 40th anniversary of their fourth album, Your Funeral … My Trial, by releasing bootleg video taken of a raucous concert they played in Manchester, England, on Oct. 15, 1986, a few weeks before it came out. You practically choke on the cigarette smoke and scratch away the itchy sweat as it coats your body and everyone else’s in the club as you watch the singer, just three years removed from the breakup of the Birthday Party, writhe about the stage.

Th band posted several gems from what appears to be a bootleg video, complete with tracking issues. One of the highlights is the band’s rendition of “Wanted Man,” a song Bob Dylan wrote and Johnny Cash recorded at San Quentin State Prison in 1969. Guitarist Blixa Bargeld (who didn’t join a rock band to play rock & roll) plays snarling slide licks as Cave falls about the stage listing every city where he’s a wanted man. He works himself into practically a religious fervor, even needing to sit down, as he gets lost in the music. It’s a brilliant performance, and Cave’s cover of “Wanted Man” is notable in music history for inspiring U2 to work with Flood.
