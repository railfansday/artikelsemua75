AWAY FAN GUIDE: BRENTFORD v EVERTON.

Stadium: Gtech Community Stadium. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Address: Lionel Road South, Brentford, TW8 0RU.

Matchday Updates: Brentford recommend their social accounts, as follows:.

Visiting fans are seated in the East Stand, using turnstiles H, G (Accessible entrance) and F. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Turnstiles will open 90 minutes prior to kick off and fans are advised to arrive early.

Please note that fans visiting the Gtech should expect a robust searching process as part of efforts to ensure a safe environment for all supporters. This may include the searches of bags and other personal items carried by supporters. Privacy can be requested when searching personal items, please speak to a member of staff who can help.

Fans without tickets for this fixture are urged not to travel to the stadium.

Everton Travel Stewards will be on hand to help supporters with any complications. For updates from Merseyside Police, follow @MerPolEFC on X.

Pubs around the stadium generally welcome both home and away fans.

The Pilot or The Gunnersbury are recommended for fans travelling from Gunnersbury.

The Express Tavern, One Over the Ait, The Bell & Crown, The Steam Packet and City Barge are the closest pubs to the stadium.

The Rose and Crown, The Greyhound, The Coach & Horses and The Botanist are the closest to Kew Green and typically all welcome away fans.

A map of pub locations can be found HERE.

This fixture is sold out and supporters should not travel unless they have a valid match ticket.

Supporters in the visitors’ section will have received a paper ticket for this game by post.

If any supporter has not received their ticket, please contact Everton's fan services at 0151 556 1878 or direct message the team on X @efc_fanservices.

If you need any further matchday assistance, you can visit the stadium box office, or speak to a matchday steward located near to turnstile entrances.

Any persons under the age of 14 must be accompanied by a responsible adult.

A summary of travel options is set out below.

There are eight stations located within a 20-minute walking radius of the stadium, so if you are planning to travel either by train or tube to the stadium, please consider the following:.

The nearest station, Kew Bridge, has one South Western Railway train every 15 minutes on matchday Saturdays (and up to four trains per hour on Sundays), with trains every 15 minutes on weekdays (up to 10pm, then every 30 minutes until the last train).

It has been revealed that trains to the stadium from central London are from Waterloo station. Gunnersbury station is the nearest station offering both the District Line underground and London Overground connections.

Fans should note that during busy times, there may be a long wait to access the station. For any supporters that require step-free public transport options, please contact accessibility@brentfordfc.com so that we can discuss and agree the best option for you.

Brentford station, the nearest step-free station, is a 24-minute walk.

Gunnersbury (nearest) 16 min walk.

The following routes serve the stadium: 237, 267, 65, H91 & 391.

Fans are advised to check for the latest information about bus routes at tfl.gov.uk or via other travel management apps.

It has been revealed that all London Style ‘Black Cabs’ are wheelchair accessible and can provide a suitable alternative to the bus or car. There is no formal drop-off point on site at the stadium. If you have difficulty walking from the surrounding area, please contact up at accessibility@brentfordfc.com and we will try to assist you.

Industry observers note that fans can make use of matchday car parking via JustPark HERE. Spaces must be pre-booked.

Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) has expanded across all London boroughs. You can check the roads that are inside the ULEZ and whether your vehicle meets the emission standards HERE. (Vehicles that don’t meet emission standards will be charged £12.50 per day to drive inside the expanded ULEZ area and fines are charged for those that do not pay this tariff).

Controlled Parking Zones will be in operation in residential streets near the stadium so you should not plan to park on nearby streets.

It has been revealed that four disabled parking spaces are reserved for away supporters. These are located very near to the away entrance on Capital Interchange Way and available on a first come, first served basis. Contact accessibility@brentfordfc.com for details.

Sources indicate that blue badge parking spaces are also available at Our Lady & St John’s RC Primary School where a mini-bus shuttle is available for those that need it. These spaces must be pre-booked. The shuttle will run every 30 minutes up until kick-off. If you require a shuttle timetable or more information, please contact accessbility@brentfordfc.com.

Industry observers note that the mini-bus shuttle will drop blue badge holders from Our Lady & St John’s RC Primary School to the Bees Superstore. If you require a wheelchair pick up when departing from the shuttle bus at the Bees Superstore, please email accessibility@brentfordfc.com detailing your expected arrival time.

Please note: the mini-bus shuttle can only transport foldable wheelchairs and there are a few steps to negotiate to access the shuttle bus.

All official away coaches must park on Capital Interchange Way, TW8 0EX which is adjacent to the away end turnstiles. If you have an accessible coach travelling or require any additional information, all contact should be directed to the Supporter Liaison Officer at supporterservices@brentfordfc.com.

Away fans will be discouraged from using Kew Bridge station post-match (see travel options, below) but any that do choose to use this station will have to walk round from Capital Interchange Way and up Lionel Road South to join the same queuing system as for home supporters.

There are two food and drink kiosks available in the away end, with a range of options on offer (including vegetarian and vegan options) as well as both alcoholic and soft drinks. All food counters have been designed at a height to make them fully accessible for fans in wheelchairs.

Industry observers note that payments: The stadium is fully cashless. All major contactless debit and credit cards will be accepted. Mobile and wearable payment options including Apple Pay and Google Pay are also supported.

Please note that fans visiting the Gtech should expect a robust searching process as part of efforts to ensure a safe environment for all supporters. This will include searches of bags and other personal items carried by supporters. Privacy can be requested when searching personal items - please speak to a member of staff who can help.

Brentford strongly recommends that fans do not bring bags with them to the stadium.

Bags that exceed the permitted dimensions of 34 cm (H) x 24 cm (W) x 11 cm (D) will not be permitted into the stadium, including suitcases. These dimensions are slightly larger than the height and width of an A4 sheet of paper.

Please note that bags cannot be folded to meet the size limit. Stewards will have apparatus to measure bag sizes. Exemptions to the bag policy are available to fans with medical and accessibility requirements. We can also allow exemptions for parents of small children who need provisions such as nappies and bottles with them inside the stadium. If you need an exemption, you should request one as soon as possible by contacting accessibility@brentfordfc.com and providing evidence and information as to why you need a larger bag.

Fans with oversized bags that are not permitted into the stadium will be directed to the bag storage facility at the Bees Superstore, located next to the A4/M4.

Prams and buggies can also be stored here.

Bag storage will cost £5 per item for most bags and £10 per item for the largest bags and suitcases. Any profits from the bag storage will be donated to Brentford FC Community Sports Trust.

A comprehensive Access Guide is available HERE.

All queries should be directed to Disability Access Officer, Connor Bagenal via the dedicated mailbox at accessibility@brentfordfc.com.

Wheelchair viewing: There are 14 wheelchair spaces available for visiting supporters.

In a recent development, on arrival, you will find a passenger lift that provides access from Thomas Layton Way down to ground level, where the turnstiles are located. Turnstile G is the designated accessible entrance and on the concourse you will have access to a large passenger lift serving the elevated accessible seating areas.

Audio Descriptive commentary: Headsets and transmitters are available where required and should be booked in advance by emailing us at accessibility@brentfordfc.com.

Away supporters will be able to collect their booked headset from the away end turnstile once into the ground. Please speak to the stewards, who will have the supporters' name and you will be issued with the headset. Please return the headset to a steward when exiting the stadium.

It has been revealed that accessible toilets: There are plenty of high-quality toilet facilities located throughout the stadium, including an ambulant accessible toilet with additional space in each toilet block, as well as separate accessible toilets, all of which are clearly signposted. Within the away end, there are also three accessible toilets available, with one located on the lower concourse and two on the upper concourse. Each accessible toilet is clearly signposted and fitted with a RADAR lock. Supervising stewards carry RADAR keys in case a supporter with access requirements does not have their own.

Recent findings suggest that changing Places: There are two Changing Places Toilets within the stadium, one on the second floor of the South Stand and one in the main concourse of the North Stand within the Family Area. Supporters in the West or East Stands (including the away end) requiring access to these Changing Places Toilets should ask a steward who will escort them. The Changing Place Toilets include a large space for both the user and carer to move freely within the room, accessible toilet, hoist, movable changing bed, privacy screen along with seating for other owners.

Assistance Animals: Brentford welcome assistance dogs at the stadium. Please inform them in advance at accessiblity@brentfordfc.com.

Smoking, or the use of e-cigarettes is not permitted anywhere across the stadium site.

Everton has a zero-tolerance approach to discriminatory behaviour or language at matches and while travelling to and from fixtures.

The Club’s All Together Now campaign promotes equality, diversity and a football family for everyone.

If you witness or experience any behaviour in the stadium which you believe  falls below the standard of behaviour that you would expect, we encourage you to report it as soon as possible. Please report what you see or experience to any matchday steward, or on WhatsApp* text +442038330327 with details of the incident and our Operations team will be notified. Alternatively, you can email report@brentfordfc.com at any time.

Everton supporters can also report discrimination confidentially at: reportit@evertonfc.com.

Please consider fellow Evertonians, particularly on concourses.

Behaviour such as throwing beer may result in ejection. More serious incidents may result in arrest and stadium bans.

Recent findings suggest that everton works with the relevant authorities to identify those responsible for unacceptable behaviour. CCTV can be used as evidence after an incident, even where an individual was not detained on matchday.

Everton does not condone pyrotechnics under any circumstances.

Possession or use may result in Club sanctions, arrest, criminal conviction and a football banning order.

Entering the playing surface without permission is a criminal offence.

Those responsible risk Club sanctions, football bans and criminal consequences which can affect employment, education and international travel.

Please enjoy your matchday and stay safe.

Thank you for your continued support and for helping protect Everton Football Club’s reputation while making the matchday experience enjoyable for everyone.
