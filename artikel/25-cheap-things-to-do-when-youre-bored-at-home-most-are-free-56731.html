25 Cheap Things to Do When You’re Bored at Home – Most Are Free

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

A bored night at home doesn’t have to cost anything. Play games and puzzles you already own, start a project that saves money, take a free online class, or borrow a movie from your library. Keep a short list of these ideas, and check it before boredom turns into a takeout order.

Sources indicate that takeout is only part of what a slow evening at home can cost. Boredom can also end in an online cart of impulse buys or one more streaming subscription.

Recent findings suggest that the cheapest fun starts with what you already own and what your library lends for free. Check what your library card covers, since many libraries lend e-books, audiobooks, and streaming movies along with the books on their shelves.

According to recent reports, projects that replace something you’d buy save money too. Say pizza delivery for your household runs about $45 with fees and tip, and the groceries for the same two pizzas made at home cost about $15. Swap two delivery nights a month for homemade pizza and a game night, and you keep about $60 a month. (Related: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using Gradio...)

The 25 ideas below include things to do alone, with roommates, or with kids, and most cost nothing.

Recent findings suggest that pick the five ideas from this list you’d enjoy most, and write them on a note for the fridge or in your phone. Check that note first whenever you reach for a delivery app.
