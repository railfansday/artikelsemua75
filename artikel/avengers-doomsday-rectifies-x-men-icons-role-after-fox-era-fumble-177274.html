Cyclops isn't just window dressing in Avengers: Doomsday.

Recent findings suggest that marking the first Avengers film since Endgame in 2019, the upcoming MCU epic hails once more from directors Joe and Anthony Russo, with some familiar faces returning to face off against Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. A handful of original Fox X-Men are confirmed to feature as part of Avengers: Doomsday's sprawling cast, with James Marsden set to reprise his role as Cyclops for the first time since a brief cameo in 2014's X-Men: Days of Future Past. (Related: Breaking: Future Value Calculator: How to Project Your ...)

It has been revealed that in a recent interview with Sharp Magazine, Marsden addresses Cyclops' return in Avengers: Doomsday, teasing that he gets far more than just a cameo this time around. The actor stresses that he was only interested in returning if Cyclops had a "genuine purpose," and he believes Doomsday really delivers in this regard. Check out Marsden's full comment below:.

First, I’d tell them that I love and appreciate them. The passion of Marvel and X-Men fans is extraordinary, and none of us would be where we are without that support. As for Cyclops, I think fans are going to be very pleased. One of the things people have remarked for years is that Cyclops is a leader, yet in many of the previous films he often took a back seat to Wolverine and some of the other characters. There are only so many minutes available in a film this large, but it was vital to me that my return felt meaningful. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

I didn’t want Cyclops to simply appear for a nostalgic cameo and then disappear. I wanted there to be a genuine purpose behind his presence. Without giving anything away, I feel very good about what we were able to do with the character. I haven’t seen the finished film yet, but I think fans will be excited not only to see familiar faces return but also to see that their appearances genuinely matter to the story.

There’s real purpose behind Cyclops being there, and that’s what excites me most.

According to recent reports, marsden is one of a number of original X-Men actors confirmed to return in Doomsday, alongside Ian McKellen as Magneto, Rebecca Romjin as Mystique, Kelsey Grammer as Beast, Alan Cumming as Nightcrawler, and the late Patrick Stewart as Professor X. Though Marsden had a cameo as Cyclops in Days of Future Past, his last substantial outing as the character was twenty years ago in 2006's X-Men: The Last Stand.

The Last Stand served as the divisive conclusion to the Fox X-Men trilogy, which started with X-Men in 2000 and includes 2003's X2: X-Men United. Cyclops plays an notable role in all three films, serving as the field leader of the X-Men team, but Hugh Jackman's Wolverine is really treated as the central character in those films. What's more, Cyclops is killed off relatively early on in The Last Stand.

X-Men: The Last Stand earned mixed reviews from critics and has a 56% on Rotten Tomatoes.

Doomsday, then, is seemingly making amends in that regard, with the most recent trailer for the film featuring Cyclops saying: "To me, my X-Men." This line further speaks to Cyclops' leadership role this time around and sets him up for an interesting dynamic with other MCU characters, most notably Chris Evans' Steve Rogers, the original leader of the Avengers.

In a recent development, other notable stars returning for Doomsday include Chris Hemsworth as Thor, Florence Pugh as Yelena, Pedro Pascal as Reed Richards, Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm, Anthony Mackie as Captain America, Channing Tatum as Gambit, Hayley Atwell as Peggy Carter, Tom Hiddleston as Loki, Sebastian Stan as Winter Soldier, Simu Liu as Shang-Chi, and Letitia Wright as Black Panther, among other actors. Clearly, the upcoming film will be a star-studded affair, and the sheer size of the cast does raise questions about just how central Marsden and Cyclops will be.

According to recent reports, still, Marsden's comments are a reassuring sign that Doomsday will make up for shortcomings in the original X-Men trilogy. Exactly how the film sets the X-Men up for the MCU's future remains to be seen, but audiences can evidently expect Cyclops to play a meaningful role in the face off against Doctor Doom.

Avengers: Doomsday releases on December 18.
