The Mauricio Pochettino era is up and running at Manchester United. The fresh head coach has arrived in England after taking charge of his final United States match and is preparing for a baptism of fire away at Liverpool. Pochettino takes over a team sat in the Premier League's relegation zone and facing an uphill battle to kick-start a challenging season. Things don't even get that much easier after the trip to Anfield, with a tricky Champions League trip to Lisbon to face Sporting CP on the horizon.

Pochettino's first home match comes against Brentford at the end of the month and the new boss will hope he can get some points on the board before the turn of the year. The January transfer window will offer an opportunity to freshen up the squad but it's notable for United to strengthen their position before dipping into the market. We'll have all the news for you today as the first match of the new era draws ever closer.

Benjamin Sesko won’t be leaving Old Trafford in January despite Juventus links.

That’s according to Fabrizio Romano, who told Affidabile that Sesko wants to stay put.

However, it could be a different story for the Slovenian’s team-mate Joshua Zirkzee, who is being eyed by both Juve and Napoli. (Related: Breaking: How to generate text: using different decodin...)

Former United midfielder Juan Mata retired from football this week and has reflected on the club’s movements when he was at Old Trafford.

“We had Van Gaal and Mourinho. It was just trying to look for success, I believe, in a short term view,” Mata told The Rest is Football.

“And we kind of achieved that sometimes. Like we won the Europa League and FA Cup. And of course we won trophies.

“But I believe that for a club like Man United, you know, you have to have a very strong culture of what you want to be, what you are, and walk towards that with the decisions of coaches and players and go to a certain direction.

“Because otherwise other clubs are signing right, are having the right coaches, and then you get distance from them.”.

United have some strikers on their radar as well as options to strengthen at the back, according to The i.

One of those forwards is Folarin Balogun, who thrived for Pochettino at the World Cup, while Freiburg star Ivan Matanovic is also an option after scoring 15 goals in 39 Bundesliga games.

At the back, we already knew about United’s interest in Calvin Bassey but the centre-back’s Fulham team-mate Antonee Robinson is thought to be a left-back option.

Manchester United’s financial results landed on Thursday and saw rises both in total borrowing and long-term debt.

However, revenue increased, thanks in part to the club returning to the Champions League under former manager Michael Carrick.

United’s cash reserves have fallen despite that revenue increase and it remains to be seen what this means for the winter transfer window.

But the most eye-catching figure is the club's debt, which remains in excess of £1.1billion.

Manchester United have received permission from the Premier League for Mauricio Pochettino to break a rule regarding press conferences.

Normally, 1.30pm on a Friday is the cut-off but an extension has been granted.

This means Pochettino will speak to the media at 2pm.
