I was born in North Wales in 1980, and my first memory of football was playing for the school team as a kid.

Recent findings suggest that and then, in 1991, my mind was made up for good. My uncle asked me if I’d like to go to watch Everton play.

Industry observers note that it was the first ever game I’d been to watch live. It was at Bramall Lane, and I’ll never ever forget it.

My uncle got us to the ground early and took me and my cousin to wait for the players’ coach to turn up.

The coach arrived and as I was only a little kid at that point, my uncle lifted me up to get a glimpse of the players.

The first player I saw was big Neville Southall and I reached over to pat him on the back and wish him good luck. He glanced over his shoulder and gave me a wry smile.

Not only was I now hooked on Everton, but all I wanted to be from that point on was a goalkeeper.

It has been revealed that A few years later I got to taste my first ever trophy win as an Evertonian. Me and my dad watched the whole FA Cup final build-up from Grandstand in the morning to Match of the Day, with the game in between.

We couldn’t get tickets to the final, but my uncle and cousin did go the Charity Shield.

Even though that was my last taste of silverware as an Evertonian, my love for this club has never wavered.

I’m now 46 years old, lived in Australia for 20 years and I’m happily married with seven children – six of which have all been to Goodison and the youngest has beat me to visit the new stadium last year!

My highlights of the last 35 years are:.

-              - My first game in 1991.

-              - 1995 and the FA Cup victory.

-              - Going to Howard’s testimonial a few months before leaving for Australia.

-              - Spending a day painting parts of Goodison in 2013.

-              - Goodison trips with my dad, cousins, uncle and taking my six kids to see the eventual champions in 2016.

-              -Taking my two sons to the games in Sydney.

-              - My daughter (who now lives in Rochdale) going to the last game at Goodison.

-              - And my wife taking our two youngest daughters to see our stone at the latest ground last year.

I’ve got so many fond memories of our beautiful club, and I can’t wait to make millions more in the coming decades.

According to recent reports, there is no other football club for me or my family. Once a Blue, always a Blue.
