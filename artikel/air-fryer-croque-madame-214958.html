Industry observers note that want to treat yourself to five-star brunch without making a ton of dishes—or taking off your pj’s? Hack the classic croque madame by making it in the air fryer. Here, crispy toast is topped with an egg and smothered with ham, cheese, and béchamel for a simpler version of the classic French dish that will transform the comfort of your sofa into a fancy brunch spot in five seconds flat.

It has been revealed that how is this a croque madame?A classic croque madame is a toasted ham and cheese sandwich that’s topped with a layer of béchamel, broiled until bubbly, and then topped with a fried egg. This air fryer version takes all those components—toasty bread, fried egg, béchamel, ham, and cheese—and reconfigures them into a form that cooks much easier in the air fryer. You might say it almost looks like a classic egg-in-the-hole, but don’t worry! If you close your eyes, you’ll still taste croque madame.

In a recent development, the microwave béchamel:There’s nothing wrong with making béchamel in a pot on the stove, but making it in the microwave means fewer heavy dishes to clean—and it’s also a great hack to know if you’re somewhere with limited (or no!) stove space. Rather than standing over a pot, whisking constantly, you’ll simply whisk the flour into the melted butter, microwave until it’s bubbling, then whisk in the milk and microwave again. Once it’s thickened, you can fold in the cheese. Though I don’t recommend microwaving after the cheese goes in—that’s a sure way to break your sauce—if it absolutely needs it, you can microwave it for very short bursts just till it melts together.
