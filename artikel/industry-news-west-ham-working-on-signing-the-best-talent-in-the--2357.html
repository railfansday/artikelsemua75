West Ham United have set their sights on Sheffield United star Sydie Peck as they aim to bolster Scott Parker's squad in January.

The 22-year-old is firmly on their radar, alongside a number of other clubs who have been keeping tabs on his performances at Bramall Lane over the last 12 months.

After a strong start to the season with Sheffield United, despite the Blades' struggles, the midfielder has been tipped as one of the best players in the second tier.

Former West Ham scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that the Hammers have plans to sign top Championship players in January to aid their chances of promotion.

Peck is one of a number of players who have impressed West Ham scouts, while they have already identified a number of key areas to improve when the transfer window opens.

Sheffield United's slow start to the season has led to suggestions that their top players, including Peck, could be tempted away with West Ham poised to take advantage.

Football Insider revealed on 17 November that Sheffield United are extremely reluctant to lose Peck mid-way through the season as he will play a crucial part in their looks.

The Blades are aware of the growing interest in the 22-year-old, with Premier League clubs also keeping a close eye on his situation.

However, West Ham could make a move to test the Blades' resolve, with Parker aiming to strengthen his squad with proven Championship quality in January.

"West Ham have got to be looking to strengthen their squad in January," Brown told Football Insider.

"There are areas where they need to improve if they want to get promoted, and one good way to do that is by looking at which players are impressing at that level.

"They will be looking at the best talent in the Championship, players who are thriving in the second tier and will be capable of making that step up to the Premier League.

"Sydie Peck is the latest player who falls into that bracket, he's been impressing for Sheffield United, who haven't had the best season by any means.

"So if there's a chance he might be available because his side has struggled, I expect West Ham will be all over it, because he would tick a few boxes for them.

"He's still only young, he's got experience in the Championship, but he's got plenty of room to develop and step up to a higher level.

"They'll be keeping a close eye on his progress and his performances, and if Scott Parker feels he needs to strengthen in midfield, he's certainly one they could look at.".

As previously mentioned, West Ham are far from the only club keeping tabs on Peck's progress, with a number of clubs across England tracking him closely.

The midfielder's performances have placed him firmly on the radar of Premier League clubs, who have been impressed by his displays, and could be tempted to make a move.

Football Insider revealed on 18 November that Newcastle and Sunderland are among the Premier League clubs looking to lure Peck away from Sheffield United.

Both clubs view Peck as a player with a massive ceiling and feel that he could become a top-level talent in future, particularly if he makes the jump up to the Premier League.

Peck has already chipped in with four goals and two assists this season, as his reputation continues to grow in the second tier, and there is no shortage of interest.
