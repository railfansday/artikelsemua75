Fighting in eastern Congo forces Ebola workers to evacuate, disrupts outbreak response

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that escalating fighting in eastern Congo has forced Ebola response workers to leave affected areas and disrupted efforts to contain the deadliest outbreak in the country, health officials and humanitarian workers mentioned Wednesday. Congo’s Ebola outbreak has infected more than 11,700 people and killed more than 5,700 since it was declared in May, according to the latest government data. FRANCE 24's Emmet Livingstone reports from Kinshasa, DR Congo.
