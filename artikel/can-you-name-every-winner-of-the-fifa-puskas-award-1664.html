Lionel Messi has been nominated for FIFA’s prestigious Puskas Award a record seven times, but it’s one individual accolade that evades him. Can you name all the players who have picked up the award?

The fella pictured above won the latest edition of the Puskas Award for his outrageous, acrobatic overhead kick against Everton.

He’s not the only famous footballer to have won the award, which is handed out to the player judged to have scored the most aesthetically significant, or “most beautiful”, goal of the calendar year.

However, there are plenty more players you’ve almost certainly never heard of playing for obscure clubs in far-flung corners of the globe. That’s part of the beauty of the award, but it means that you’re not getting full marks here. In fact, we’ll be very impressed if you can name even 10.

Alongside the year, we’ve given you the club that each player scored the Puskas-winning goal for. But that’s all you’re getting in terms of clues.
