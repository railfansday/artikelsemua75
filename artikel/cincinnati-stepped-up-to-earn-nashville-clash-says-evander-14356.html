Evander noted he made it his mission to ensure FC Cincinnati's season did not finish in the Wild Card match after starring in their 3-1 victory over Orlando City SC.

Cincinnati booked their place in the next round of the MLS Cup playoffs at Inter.co Stadium on Thursday, with Evander grabbing two goals and an assist in the comeback win.

Martin Ojeda had given Orlando the lead in the 26th minute, only for Evander to convert a 43rd-minute penalty to ensure Pat Noonan's side went in level at the break.

Evander was it again within four minutes of the second half to hand Cincinnati the lead, before he teed up Samuel Gidi late on to seal a date with Nashville SC this weekend.

"We didn't want to finish the season here," Evander mentioned. "We stepped up and give the fans this win, because there have been ups and downs during the season.

"We want to finish the season in a better way. So, of course, we are happy with a win, but we have to enjoy tonight because we go again on Sunday.".

"We'll see," Noonan remarked with a smile and a quiet laugh to himself when asked how they proposal to prepare for the game.

"We got a lot of guys banged up, and as you saw, to end the game, it was not an eleven that many would have imagined were seeing out that victory in the position they were in.

"So, hopefully, we get some good news on some of the players, and if not, guys are going to do their best to turn it around and be ready to go in Nashville.".

Evander now has 16 goals for the season with Cincinnati, only one fewer than Kevin Denkey for Noonan's side.

He also finished with team-high totals for shots on target (three), chances created (three), touches (67) and passes in the final third (29) against Orlando, and he will be key again.

MATCH PREDICTION: NASHVILLE WIN.

Nashville have won the last two MLS meetings with Cincinnati, with the latest triumph coming earlier this month when Surridge struck twice in the second half.

And BJ Callaghan's side will be looking to ensure they make the advantage of playing at GEODIS Park count. Nashville are unbeaten in home MLS matches this season with 15 wins and two draws from their 17 contests at home.

The Coyotes also have an 18-match MLS home unbeaten streak dating to November 2025 against Miami, and Cincinnati have not been at their best on their travels.

In league matches away from home this season, Cincinnati have four wins, seven draws, and seven losses, though they are unbeaten in their last four matches on the road.
