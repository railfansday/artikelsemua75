Sources indicate that her ranking? Hutcherson was the best, and then Hemsworth, Cooper, Chalamet and Pratt at the bottom.

Elsewhere in the episode, she was candid about her breast implants. During a segment where she was discussing hot topics in the “Real Housewives” universe, Lawrence was asked about a storyline in which “Real Housewives of New York” star Hailey Glassman stated her nose was broken by a woman’s breast.
