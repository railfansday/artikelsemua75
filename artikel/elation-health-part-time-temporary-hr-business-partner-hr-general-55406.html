Our team is committed to enhancing physician and patient quality of life through Elation, a SaaS cloud-based clinical platform. Since inception, we’ve been focused on building a world-class technology solution that creates an experience of delight and ease for physicians, and that our owners love.

We are seeking a people-focused HR Business Partner / Generalist to provide interim support while a member of our People team is out on leave. You’ll partner with the Chief People Officer and leaders across the business. The primary focus will be on employee relations, performance management, manager coaching, leave management, and ensuring consistent and thoughtful people practices. The ideal candidate brings strong business acumen, sound judgment, and deep knowledge of employment laws, particularly in California. This is a remote, part-time position (10 - 15 hours/week), anticipated to run from mid December through late April.This is an opportunity for a seasoned HR professional who enjoys operating as a trusted advisor, building strong relationships, and helping a growing organization maintain a healthy, high-performing culture.
