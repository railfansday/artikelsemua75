Nicolo Barella insists that it is not all doom and gloom for Inter after the disappointing end to their Serie A match away against Atalanta over the weekend, and while they will have an opportunity to redeem themselves in the Champions League on Wednesday night, the Italy international says that his biggest goal is to cement back-to-back Serie A titles.

Barella spoke to the media alongside Cristian Chivu at a press conference on Tuesday, ahead of Inter’s Champions League league phase clash against VfB Stuttgart on Wednesday evening. (Related: Latest Update: Clash Royale recruits creator of AI chea...)

Inter have won two and lost two of their first four games in the competition this season and will face a Stuttgart side that start the week one point and one position better off than the Nerazzurri in the table. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

It has been revealed that barella was asked several questions about Inter’s 4-4 draw against Atalanta on Sunday night. The Nerazzurri had led 3-1 for well over an hour, but a particularly poor final 10 minutes saw them come away with just one point.

Barella was asked by reporters on Tuesday whether he thinks Inter need to make a leap forward in quality as a team.

It has been revealed that “Yes,” he agreed. “I’ve always noted that the team makes the difference. I’ve always been around strong players ever since I joined Inter. I think it’s a great dressing room. Sometimes we could handle certain situations better. It’s a responsibility that is on us, we have discussed it among ourselves.

Recent findings suggest that “If I had the solution in front of me, it would all be very easy, but we have to continue working to improve the situation. It’s part of football.”.

Barella shrugged off suggestions that Inter do not have the right mentality.

In a recent development, “There are great players in the dressing room. We play with 11 and the subs aren’t a problem either. Everyone who comes on always adds something.

“We took the lead in Bergamo, and once they’d caught up with us, it was everybody’s responsibility. Maybe our attention dropped. We couldn’t afford to come home from Bergamo with just a point. It’s easy to say it now, but it’s clear that there will be slip-ups over the course of a season. I see the glass as being half full.”.

Asked how Inter will cope with the absences of Hakan Calhanoglu and Piotr Zielinski against Stuttgart, Barella said: “All absences weigh heavily because we have so many matches. Having lots of different solutions is an advantage. We will miss them.

According to recent reports, “We have to play as the best version of ourselves. We’ve seen Stuttgart’s characteristics. We will give everything we have and we want to do so.”.

It has been revealed that asked for a word on Pio Esposito, whose hat-trick against Atalanta was not enough to secure the three points, Barella said: “Pio’s maturity was shown in his post-match interview as well. He said he didn’t want to score three goals if they weren’t going to count for anything.

“It’s true that when you hit rock bottom, you try to give yourself a bit of a push, but over the last few years, Inter’s first thought has always been to go out and fight on all fronts. Some years we did better than others. I don’t think it’s a matter of failure, but that there will be ups and downs. It’s a team game and it is possible that sometimes Inter drops off.”.

Barella insisted that Inter can’t afford to give preference to one competition over another, but admitted that he does want to go back-to-back as Serie A champions by the end of the season.

Industry observers note that “We can’t think like that. I think the last team to win two consecutive league titles was Juve, and our goal is to confirm ourselves as champions. That’s our stimulus, and obviously we also have the Champions League and other competitions to respect.”.
