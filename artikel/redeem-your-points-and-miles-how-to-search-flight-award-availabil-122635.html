Redeem your points and miles: How to search flight award availability for the major airlines

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (146 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
  • Finding award flight availability can be challenging due to varying airline and search engine quirks.
  • Third-party tools like Seats.aero and Point.me streamline the process by searching multiple airlines and loyalty programs at once.
  • Comparing award prices across different programs can help maximize value, as partner programs sometimes offer better deals.
  • General tips include searching segment by segment, understanding married segments, and being aware of phantom availability.
  • Neither third-party tool can book tickets directly, so always confirm availability with the airline before transferring points.
  • Dynamic pricing and variable partner rates mean award costs can fluctuate significantly depending on the program and date.
  • Some premium cabin awards have eligibility restrictions, such as status requirements for booking certain first-class seats.
