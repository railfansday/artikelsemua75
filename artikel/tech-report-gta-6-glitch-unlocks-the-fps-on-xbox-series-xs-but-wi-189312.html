It has been revealed that original Story: If there's anything we could add to GTA 6 as things stand, it'd be an official 60FPS mode. That's obviously not possible right now due to how demanding the game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S hardware, even in the case of the PS5 Pro.

Interestingly though, over the weekend, players have discovered the ability to activate what some are calling a "60FPS Glitch" or "60FPS Bug" on Xbox consoles, forcing the game to unlock its FPS and run at a higher frame rate than it's actually supposed to.

Sources indicate that and finally, it should be noted that GTA 6 was never intended to be run this way on Xbox Series X|S, and therefore you could run into all sorts of issues - crashes, bugs, maybe even your console overheating. That last one is unlikely, but the game has been specifically built to run at 30FPS on these machines, so it's not worth pushing it in our view.
