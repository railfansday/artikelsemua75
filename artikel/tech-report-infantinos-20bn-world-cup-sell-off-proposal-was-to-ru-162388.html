Gianni Infantino’s failed Fifa Forward Enterprise proposal could have locked football in for up to 96 years while paying its management up to 5% of the scheme’s multibillion-pound value, according to documents seen by the Guardian.

According to recent reports, previously undisclosed communications by Fifa and Thrive Capital, the lead investor for the proposal, cast fresh light on the deal to sell off football’s crown jewels. They outline a plan, produced days before July’s final of the 2026 World Cup, to profit from the sport’s burgeoning commercial revenues for almost a century.
