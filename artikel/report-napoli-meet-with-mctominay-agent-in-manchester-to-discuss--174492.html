📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Tuesday’s reports claim that Napoli director Giovanni Manna has recently met with Scott McTominay’s agent, Colin Murdock, in Manchester to discuss the Scotland international’s contract situation.

Discussions over a latest contract for McTominay have been ongoing for several months, but an agreement has not yet been reached. The former Manchester United midfielder’s current deal with Napoli runs until the summer of 2028, which means that without a renewal, he will be heading into the final year of his contract during the summer transfer window. According to Tuesday’s reports from Sky and TMW, Napoli’s sporting director Manna has recently met with McTominay’s agent while in the UK for this evening’s Champions League match between the Partenopei and Manchester City at The Etihad. An agreement has still not been reached, and the latest updates suggest that there is still considerable distance between McTominay’s requests and the figures that Napoli are willing to provide.

Still, the parties are expected to remain in contact and continue discussions over a new contract in the coming weeks.

According to recent reports, according to TMW, McTominay at the moment earns a net salary of around €4m per season with Napoli and is requesting a raise to around €7m per season. Napoli, meanwhile, are hoping to rejuvenate their squad and lower their wage bill over the next few transfer windows.
