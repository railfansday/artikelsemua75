Nvidia’s board has voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO, and board director, effective immediately, the tech giant said on Monday. Huang resigned less than two weeks ago after reports emerged about a series of affairs, one with a woman suspected by U.S. intelligence of being a Chinese asset.

“The board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners, and customers,” board chair Harvey C. Jones said in the statement. Following Nvidia’s earnings call last week, the board said that it did not intend to bring him back. It has been revealed that “In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk,” the company said in a statement. (Related: Latest Update: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

President Donald Trump welcomed Huang’s return, and took credit for it, calling it the “correct and proper decision” in a post on Truth Social.

It has been revealed that “The country needs Jensen’s leadership at Nvidia in order to ensure the success and prosperity of not only the tech giant, but the American economy,” Trump wrote. He called Huang a “great patriot and national treasure” and said the U.S. government has “no reason to believe” Huang’s return poses “any risk” to national security.

Nvidia stock rose 29% immediately prior to the opening bell in Latest York, and was up roughly 34% in midday trading, sparking a market-wide rally that lifted the S&P 500 by 5%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average by 6.8%, and the Nasdaq by 3.2%.

In a recent development, the move marks a stunning U-turn by Nvidia’s board and follows a sharp sell-off in Nvidia stock last week that also hit the broader AI trade. Huang has also been one of the AI industry’s most influential boosters, and some investors had worried that without Huang, some of Nvidia’s investments and support of the wider ecosystem could weaken or collapse. Many in the tech world, including Tesla CEO Elon Musk, had lobbied for Huang’s return.

“I’ve never known the tech world to be more unanimous about anything than they are about Jensen Huang,” Paul Graham noted in a post on Friday, when many tech founders were vociferously calling for Huang to be reinstated. “Everyone in tech thinks the board made a huge mistake. Except the board. But they are not in tech.”.

Recent findings suggest that many are already celebrating the CEO’s return. “At the end of the day despite this Jensen saga the Board knew it had no choice but to bring him back and for the US technology sector this was the move it had to make,” Dan Ives, partner and senior managing director, at Yorkville Ives & Co.

Still, the investigations against Huang are ongoing. Nvidia says its internal investigation is continuing and that Huang has committed to cooperating with it. The SEC has also opened a civil probe into whether the organization’s disclosures misled investors, according to The Wall Street Journal.

“I am honoured to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a fresh industrial revolution,” Huang stated in a statement. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.”.

Here is the full text of the board’s statement:.

NVIDIA Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as President, CEO and Board DirectorNovember 23, 2026.

According to recent reports, NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) today announced that effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board. The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead NVIDIA. In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.

According to recent reports, “The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead NVIDIA, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers,” said Harvey C. Jones, Chair of the Board. “We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Tech giant. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as NVIDIA’s Chief Financial Officer.” Harvey Jones will remain as Board chair.

Sources indicate that “I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” said Jensen Huang. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process. I am especially grateful to Harvey Jones for his leadership as Chair, and to Colette Kress and our executive staff for leading NVIDIA during this period. I am also deeply thankful to our employees, shareholders, leaders across the industry and partners around the world for their extraordinary support. Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I can’t wait to get back to work.”.

In a recent development, as previously unveiled, in light of recent reporting that Mr. Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort, the Board formed a committee comprised solely of independent directors to investigate, and the committee retained independent counsel to assist it in that investigation. The Board reiterated that the committee’s investigation will proceed to conclusion. Mr. Huang fully supports the investigation and is committed to cooperating with it.

In a recent development, the Board also recently formed a National Security and Export Control Committee comprised solely of independent directors to oversee trade compliance and national security risks. The committee is engaging counsel with experience in national security matters, and such counsel will be retaining advisors with expertise in national security matters. NVIDIA is commencing a search to fill a newly introduced position, Chief Compliance Officer, to oversee compliance across NVIDIA and report to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee. NVIDIA will continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries.
