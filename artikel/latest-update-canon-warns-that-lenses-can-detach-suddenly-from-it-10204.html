In a recent development, regional Canon offices worldwide have issued an urgent service notice regarding the company’s RF1.4x and RF2x extenders, commonly known as teleconverters. Canon warns that if customers suspect anything unusual about how their extender feels in use, they should request an inspection and repair through Canon.

It has been revealed that canon Rumors claims this is a known concern with Canon RF-mount extenders, where a lens can feel like it is locked onto the extender even when it isn’t. In some cases, a lens could even detach from the extender and fall to the ground. Given that extenders are commonly used with expensive telephoto lenses, this could be an extremely costly problem.

It has been revealed that canon Malaysia’s notice on the matter is particularly interesting, offering slightly more information than the service notice from Canon U.S.A. and others.

“We regret to inform that a potential matter has been confirmed for Extender RF1.4x/RF2x,” Canon Malaysia says, which goes further than what Canon U.S.A. writes.

According to recent reports, canon U.S.A. stops short of mentioning any issue, only issuing a “service notice” and noting that Canon RF extender users should always check the following:.

Canon U.S.A. adds that if photographers notice “anything unusual,” which could include the lens release lever feeling “stiff” or like it is “catching on something and cannot be operated smoothly,” there is a chance the RF lens is not locked in place.

“If used in this state, the RF lens may come off,” Canon continues.

Any affected users, or those who have any concerns about the operation of their RF extenders, should contact the Canon Customer Support Center in their region for an inspection and, if necessary, a free repair.

According to recent reports, in any case, it is not immediately clear what the exact issue is, what causes it, or how it might be fixed. Given that Canon is offering free repairs, though, it is reasonable to speculate that there is a physical component that needs to be fixed to ensure the consistently safe operation of Canon RF extenders.

Sources indicate that any photographers with Canon RF extenders should carefully inspect them before use and, if worried at all, contact their local Canon customer support team. A lens falling off an extender is a significant risk.

PetaPixel has reached out to Canon for comment and will update this story if it hears back.
