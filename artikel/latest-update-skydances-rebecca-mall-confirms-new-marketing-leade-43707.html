Latest Update: Skydance’s Rebecca Mall Confirms New Marketing Leadership Team

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that skydance’s marketing division is the latest unit to reveal its latest leadership structure. Following its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery in October, Skydance has been busy working to mesh the operations of the two media companies.

Recent findings suggest that chief marketing officer Rebecca Mall, in a memo to her team, announced the newly introduced vision for the marketing team. “We have an incredible opportunity to build a more unified, innovative and effective marketing organization – one that breaks down silos, understands our audiences better than anyone and harnesses the power of creativity, data and technology to set a new standard for our industry,” according to the memo. She added that the team’s job is not just to market what they create, but also to help drive growth and success for the entire Skydance manufacturer.

According to recent reports, mall revealed that cross-tech giant marketing and corporate brand, data & analytics, media, partnerships, and franchise and kids & family will now be housed under one roof. The upcoming leadership team includes Annah Zafrani, who will be tasked with leading Skydance’s cross-company marketing and brand unit. Zafrani, who comes from Paramount Skydance, will coordinate priority campaigns across the media company’s businesses and lead corporate brand marketing. Reporting to her will be, also from the Paramount Skydance side, Katie Hutchings, svp, brand marketing, creative and strategy, and her team. Paramount Skydance’s Angie Sharma will lead franchise and kids & family, building one strategy per franchise across content, consumer products, games, and experiences, with kids & family viewed as a key growth priority.

Jamal Salmon, who comes from Paramount Skydance, will lead data & analytics for marketing, and will also report to Josh Goldstine, president of worldwide marketing & distribution, who will also be responsible for film marketing. Salmon will also have a dotted line to Hugh Williams, chief engineer and evp for consumer and monetization engineering; and Josh Walker, chief financial and strategy officer for direct to consumer. The DTC unit oversees streaming and video-on-demand.

Industry observers note that paramount Skydance’s Michelle Hagen will lead brand partnerships, with Julie Moore, evp of brand partnerships, reporting to her, and Dana Nussbaum, co-head of global motion pictures marketing, Warner Bros., focusing on theatrical. Malene Sam, svp, originals, who was part of Paramount Skydance, report both to Hagen and Shauna Spenley, who leads DTC media. Under Spenley, Sam will be focused on TV and DTC.

Finally, Seth Goren, who comes from WBD, will lead media, bringing together media strategy, media buying, and agency management across the company. He will report to Mall. But for theatrical media work, he also will report to Nussbaum. For his DTC media work, he will report to Spenley, and for his TV work, he will have a reporting line to Mike Benson, who leads TV Marketing.

The realignment of the marketing team has led to the departure of Meg Maro, the global head of franchise. Mall thanked her for her contributions during her time at the organization.

Maro concluded saying: “We have an extraordinary opportunity to redefine what marketing can achieve in our industry, and I can’t wait to see what we accomplish together.”.
