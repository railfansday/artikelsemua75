Fashion can’t get enough of freaky hats lately. Following the main character hat trend from a few seasons past, this time headwear has gone totally theatrical and over-the-top. Between Chanel’s fin-embellished Nemo-esque swim caps and Junya Watanabe’s super camp, oversize ’70s baker-boy-style caps, headpieces are seemingly obsessed with transforming their wearers into outlandish personas. And at a time when clothing is centered on escapism and storytelling, an eccentric chapeau might just be the most perfect finishing touch.

Simone Rocha’s sequin-embellished bonnets with chin straps might turn you into a living doll. Celebs, too, have already gotten the memo: Elle Fanning recently wore a Stephen Jones-designed makeup compact headpiece to match her Givenchy dress inspired by an archival piece he created over three decades ago, while doing press for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. Elsewhere, Cardi B showed up in a white embellished gown from Tamara Ralph’s spring 2026 couture collection, styled with a huge, showgirl-style feather hat. Ciara walked the Junya Watanabe spring/summer 2027 runway while wearing a bedazzled helmet dripping with chains. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

At Vaquera’s spring/summer 2027 show titled “I’m a put-on-a-show kind of girl,” the neon high-octane gowns were counterbalanced with huge, satirical boater hats, followed by a weird and whimsical chef hat and maid headpiece paired with shrunken, mini bloomer uniform onesies. Adding more humor to the collection, the designers ended the show by performing a live karaoke version of Sonny & Cher’s “I Got You Babe.” The brand is the king of the theatrical character hat. For fall/winter 2026, it showcased pillbox-style hats that eclipsed the face with cutouts for the eyes, fencing-mask-inspired headwear, and an elongated train conductor hat that resembles a cross between The Rockettes Toy Soldier costumes and English bearskin caps. Industry observers note that transforming is exactly the point: “Vaquera has always celebrated fashion’s transformative power,” read Vaquera’s show notes. “The collection confronts the tension between perfection and chaos. Can you be unhinged and complete at once? Its cast of characters—distorted drab mods, twisted ladies who lunch, abstracted leather daddies—all seem to suggest that the most covetable clothes for your future are those that mirror our fractured present.”.

Sources indicate that while years of the past were defined by singular aesthetics, 2026 lacks one solo homogeneous style. Formality, too, has been traded for casual looks. Hats used to be ubiquitous; now they’re sort of a rare dying breed, save for a baseball cap.

Perhaps that’s one of the reasons why these character-driven hats are so prevalent now. Many of them are tied to uniforms, occupations, or the everyday made extreme. It’s beautiful absurdism, verging on delightfully unhinged. Consider an over-the-top hat pure whimsy.
