Retraction to: Nature https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10702-5 Published online 24 June 2026.

According to recent reports, the authors retract this article, following a suggestion from the editors. Shortly after publication, a preprint (subsequently published (see ref. 1 below)) by Till Sawala identified an inconsistency in our analysis. Our calculations were erroneously comparing the comoving distance between the observed galaxies to the luminosity distance between galaxies in the simulated mocks. Correcting these complications reduces the discrepancy between the observed and simulated samples to 1.48–2.75 sigma, depending on the sample construction. This falls below the standard significance threshold of 3 sigma in the cosmology literature.

Recent findings suggest that despite the revised uncertainties, our method remains valid and provides tentative evidence for anisotropy in the large-scale distribution of galaxies in the Universe. The revised paper, with corrected uncertainties, will be submitted to another journal. This retraction will contain a link to the fresh publication, if and when it is published.

Both authors agree with this retraction.
