Ripple’s RLUSD Supply Hits Record $2.7 Billion, With Ethereum Holding Majority Over XRP Ledger

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that ripple’s RLUSD stablecoin has achieved another milestone. Its total market supply has reached $2.7 billion, making a newly introduced high level in circulation, according to data reported on the X platform. RLUSD supply climbed to the $2.7 billion record on Sunday, Nov. 15, a 57% rise from the $1.72 billion mark seen on Sept. 14, data revealed.

Sources indicate that the market supply of the Ripple-backed stablecoin has surged to a upcoming ATH, exceeding the previous $1.72 billion milestone set in September. According to the data, RLUSD’s circulating supply at the moment stands at $2.7 billion, up from the $1.2 billion level reached in January. This rise signals an expansion of RLUSD’s liquidity across the wider blockchain ecosystem.

The supply expansion indicates rising demand for RLUSD as global customers gradually embrace the stablecoin for payments and DeFi applications. This surge positions RLUSD as a ‘digital dollar’ that allows consumers to execute cross-border payments and on-chain settlements. Sources indicate that the growth is pushing RLUSD higher into the stablecoin market. Now, the asset ranks as the 7th-largest stablecoin by market supply. The more supply means deeper liquidity across the public blockchain ecosystem, supporting cross-border payments and on-chain utility. In a recent development, the Ripple-focused institutional stablecoin has reached another milestone, increasing its market supply to $2.7 billion. Interestingly, a large amount of this freshly issued liquidity operates on external public blockchains, not within the XRP Ledger network.

Metrics show that a large share of the fresh RLUSD supply sits on Ethereum, with Ethereum holding more than those in the XRP Ledger. This indicates rising use of Ethereum as a liquidity and settlement network for the stablecoin, while RLUSD continues to expand across protocols.

According to recent reports, on-chain data indicates that out of the $2.7 billion in RLUSD circulation, about $1.73 billion runs on Ethereum. The XRP Ledger holds around $855 million, representing about 30% of the circulation.

In a recent development, over time, Ethereum’s share of the supply has risen to about 60%. This shift is because Ethereum offers a deeper DeFi market, including lending and more. XRP Ledger is closer to activating its native lending infrastructure.
