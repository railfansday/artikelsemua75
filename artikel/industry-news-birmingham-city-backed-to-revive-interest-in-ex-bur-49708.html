It has been revealed that birmingham City are flying high in the Championship right now, and as things stand, sit pretty at the top of the Championship table. However, there could still be room for improvement at St Andrews looking ahead to the January window.

Indeed, Birmingham are soaring at the top of the Championship right now, level on points with Middlesbrough and Stoke City but with a superior goal difference.

Head coach Chris Davies has changed the way that the Blues set up and play, becoming a lot more rigid and difficult to beat, reflected in their eight wins, five draws, and just two defeats after 15 league games so far.

It wasn't an especially busy summer in the Second City, with the likes of Jhon Solis, Luis Vazquez, and Dael Fry among those to have joined the club permanently, as there were rumours of a former Championship star heading back to England in the form of Birmingham. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

According to TEAMtalk, Birmingham, along with West Ham United and Hull City, were interested in signing Brownhill in the summer, but no deal could be agreed.

Brownhill, 30, has made over 400 appearances in England's top three flights for the likes of Preston North End, Barnsley, Bristol City, and Burnley, netting 18 goals and grabbing six assists from midfield in 42 Championship games for the latter during the 2024/25 campaign.

The Englishman would head to Saudi Arabian outfit Al-Shabab in the summer of 2025 and is nearing a half-century of appearances for the club, but with a contract running until the summer of 2027, could return to England sooner rather than later, as Birmingham could renew their interest in the midfielder this winter.

Football League World's Birmingham fan pundit, Jason Moore, has shared their thoughts on whether they would like to see Brownhill at St Andrews this January if Blues revive their interest in the midfielder. (Related: Large Language Models: A New Moore's Law?...)

According to recent reports, jason stated: "You know what? I'm probably going to say no, not because there's no quality there; I'd just be curious about what kind of wage he'd want.

"And to be fair, we've got a pretty settled midfield. Max Bird can't even get a look in at the moment as the six or eight; he's playing at the ten. Paik's not getting a look-in because Solis and Iwata are absolutely solid.

"Don't get me wrong, if the Brownhill deal was there to be done, and it weren't silly wages and something the club wanted to explore, I'd one-hundred per cent back it because, I mean, look at how good he's been in the league.

"So it all depends. The age factor is a big thing for me as well. So it depends on what wages he wants, and if he's hungry to come and battle it out in the Championship again, because, to be fair, out in that part of the world he's probably earning an absolute wedge that Blues, well, let's be honest, no one in the Championship and part of the Premier League can even pay.

"So yeah, it depends if he's hungry, if he wants to come and has to drop a bit in his money, then yeah, bring him in and see if the club backs it.".

In a recent development, do you agree with Jason that Brownhill should only be signed if he is hungry for the move, or should the Blues go all out for his signature regardless? Share your thoughts below!

If Birmingham are indeed in a promotion battle by the time the January window rolls around, you'd be brave to back against the Blues going all out in the window to secure a return to the Premier League.

Brownhill would not cost a hefty fee, though whether he is prepared to take a massive cut in his wages at this stage of his career remains to be seen.

The Englishman would be an incredible addition in the middle of the park at St Andrews, and could well be the missing piece of the puzzle Davies is looking for.
