Sources indicate that netflix has been the beneficiary of some surprisingly massive titles from other streaming services throughout 2026. A deal with Disney this fall landed them a small treasure trove of content to introduce to a newly introduced audience, including the first two seasons of Disney+'s hit Percy Jackson and the Olympians series, all four seasons of ABC's crime procedural Will Trent, and the entirety of the mainline Ice Age movies, to name a few. Each has since exploded on the platform to varying degrees during their limited stay, with the latter becoming perhaps the biggest winner of the bunch and completely taking over the top ten after their debut. However, their latest third-party acquisition from MGM+ is reaching some truly rare territory.

The first three seasons of the sci-fi horror mystery series From made its Netflix debut on October 19 after years of being one of the smaller streamer's hidden gems, and it immediately took off. Season 1 reached #1 on the platform's English television top ten for that week, marking the beginning of a dominant run that has yet to end. In every week since, all three seasons have charted somewhere in the top ten, proof of just how many viewers are getting lost in the Harold Perrineau-led series about a town that won't let anyone who enters its borders leave. Nielsen is now putting some numbers to the performance, with some shocking results. (Related: Grandma who accidentally invited a stranger to Thanksgi...)

In a recent development, for the week of November 2 through 8, From scored a staggering 1.685 billion minutes viewed, nearly doubling the next highest performer — Grey's Anatomy at 857 million — among all acquired series on streaming. No other series even got half as much viewership as the horror hit created by John Griffin and showrun by Jeff Pinkner. That's particularly notable considering there are only 30 episodes available to watch, whereas the next lowest on Nielsen's top ten, Bluey in fourth place, has 154 episodes available. It's extremely unique territory for a streaming acquisition of that kind, one that has now totaled over 5 billion minutes viewed since its arrival on Netflix. The newfound Netflix interest in the residents of Fromville and the creatures that haunt them couldn't come at a better time, with MGM+ looking to bring the saga of survival horror to a close. Production began on the fifth and final season over the summer following a massive Season 4 cliffhanger that left the remaining survivors in a dire and deeply uncertain situation going forward. It'll mark the end of an era for the streamer, given that From was one of its biggest breakout darlings, earning rave reviews and comparisons to Perrineau's prior series, Lost, since its debut back in 2022. A premiere date hasn't been set for Season 5 just yet, but it's bound to bring back the main ensemble for one last scare, including Catalina Sandino Moreno, David Alpay, Elizabeth Saunders, Scott McCord, Ricky He, Hannah Cheramy, and Chloe Van Landschoot, among others.
