According to recent reports, max Allegri warns Napoli ‘must be realistic’ about their Champions League chances against Manchester City with a decimated squad. ‘We’ve got to be on our toes and play behind them.’.

It kicks off at the Etihad in Manchester at 20.00 GMT (21.00 CET).

In a recent development, you can follow all the build-up and action as it happens from this game and Como vs. AEK Athens on the Champions League Liveblog.

The Partenopei have had a dismal run in Europe so far, scraping the 4-3 victory over Bodo/Glimt, but losing the other three to Arsenal, Villarreal and FC Porto.

This is an incredibly tough trip to Manchester, but will be hoping for better than last season, when Giovanni Di Lorenzo was sent off just 21 minutes into a 2-0 defeat.

“This is a wonderful evening against one of the best teams in Europe, they have very technical, physical players,” Allegri told Sky Sport Italia.

“It is going to be very complicated, we must try to be precise, because they will pounce on any mistake and keep pressure on, and when we do make an error, we’ve got to be on our toes.”.

The injury crisis keeps getting worse for Napoli, who make the trip without Antonio Vergara, Benoit Badiashile, Alex Meret, Stanislav Lobotka, Giovanni Di Lorenzo, Alisson Santos, Alessandro Buongiorno and suspended Rafa Marin.

This might be a 3-4-2-1 or a 3-5-1-1, depending on Noa Lang, Kevin De Bruyne and Scott McTominay’s positions, but the coach seemed to hint there will be a lot of long balls and crosses.

According to recent reports, “When City press us, we must do well to play behind them, then develop it well. If we don’t, then they will intercept loose passes and go on the counter-attack,” replied Allegri when asked about the system. (Related: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to transformer...)

Napoli have a famously terrible record on English soil in Europe, never winning here, with just four draws from 12 attempts, and the coach seemed almost resigned to his fate before kick-off. (Related: Latest Update: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hu...)

“We are trying to get some players back to fitness, but these injuries and playing every three days, it’s hard to create any momentum, added Allegri.

According to recent reports, “As soon as the fixture list came out, I stated we had to be realistic. Only an idiot wouldn’t realise Napoli would gamble for qualification in the last three games. That doesn’t mean we came here expecting to lose, but we also need to prepare for another big game this weekend in Serie A.

Industry observers note that “I know you like to talk about identity and all these nice words, but we also have to be realistic. We’re against one of the strongest teams in Europe, who dominated for 10 years and then bought even more strong players.

“Football is at times very simple, if you have the best players, you win games. We will try our best, but we’re down to the bare bones, the same players have to keep going because we don’t have the options to rotate, and barely have time to train.

“We are still in the Champions League, regardless what happens tonight, and this weekend will be a very important match for the future of Napoli.”.

Napoli will visit relegation strugglers Sassuolo on Saturday.
