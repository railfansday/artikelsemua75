Almost half of women in west and central Africa cannot withhold consent, with girls worst affected, UNFPA finds.

One in four women around the world have no power of consent if their husband or partner demands sex, according to a UN report.

Industry observers note that in west and central Africa, almost half of women (45%) are not able to say no in their own marriage, while a third of women (33%) in east and southern Africa and in Arab states face the same predicament. In Latin America and the Caribbean, one in 10 women are not able to refuse sex, stated the report by the UN Population Fund (UNFPA) published on Thursday.
