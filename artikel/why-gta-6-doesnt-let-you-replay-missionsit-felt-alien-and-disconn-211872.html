One of the first things people noticed with Grand Theft Auto 6 is that you cannot replay missions, at least not in a simple, straightforward way. GTA 5 had a mission replay option, as did Red Dead Redemption II, so why doesn't GTA 6? Senior design director Scott Butchard told Kotaku that Rockstar "certainly" considered a mission replay feature "early on in development." However, Butchard remarked, "As the game came together, it felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell and felt a bit alien and disconnected."
