A latest idea has emerged for AC Milan to strengthen their attack, and it is a player that incoming Head of Football Markus Krösche knows well.

According to recent reports, this morning’s edition of Tuttosport writes that the two dreams for January are Nicolò Tresoldi and Endrick, but a third option is starting to emerge at Milan. The player is equally effective in terms of goals, but perhaps less media-burdened than Gonçalo Ramos.

It has been revealed that the Portuguese striker has just three goals so far for Milan since joining for a club-record €73m fee, and his current drought is now over one month. This is why finding a viable alternative in the winter is key, but perhaps without spending another €60-70m on another forward. (Related: Breaking: Elon Musk states he regrets midterm spending...)

In a recent development, it will be very hard to snatch Tresoldi from Club Brugge during the winter transfer window, as Arsenal and Atletico Madrid are also strongly interested in him. Meanwhile, Endrick’s departure from Real Madrid is presently being vetoed by Jose Mourinho. So, who could the Rossoneri turn to? (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

It has been revealed that milan could look towards the Bundesliga, more specifically Stuttgart’s Ermedin Demirovic. He is a name highly regarded by Milan’s latest Head of Football – Markus Krösche – who has watched him up close for several years.

Sources indicate that the Bosnian striker’s contract expires in 2028, and there’s currently no agreement to extend it. Therefore, his price tag – despite an  excellent start to the season – isn’t likely to skyrocket and could become affordable.

According to recent reports, he’s already scored nine goals across all competitions: five in nine appearances in the league, and four in four in the Champions League. That seems to provide enough evidence that the Bosnia international is ready for a top club.

Industry observers note that demirovic is raring to go and is eyeing potential suitors (including several English clubs). A couple of years ago, Juventus sought him as a partner to Dusan Vlahovic , when Cristiano Giuntoli was still in charge. Now, that move to Italy might finally come about.

Industry observers note that in recent weeks, the presence of Milan scouts at Stuttgart’s matches has not gone unnoticed. Demirovic has responded in his own way: with goals. Dzeko’s heir in the Bosnian national team can also play as a second striker and therefore, if necessary, partner Ramos.

In the past he has also been used as a winger in a three-man attack. This versatility is invaluable for a chameleon-like coach like Ruben Amorim, and it would allow Ermedin to be used in any type of situation.

It would also facilitate Francesco Camarda’s possible loan exit, so this is a situation well worth watching as we reach the weeks leading up to the winter window.
