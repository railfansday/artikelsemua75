Sources indicate that co-op is such a natural fit for The Binding of Isaac that it's a little hard to look back and imagine the game without it. But it's never been especially stable.

Sources indicate that launching into beta in 2024, the free Repentance+ expansion that added co-op had unending net code issues and constant de-syncs. For the life of me, I could never get Bethany to work in co-op without being booted to the lobby, and The Lost became a disconnect-magnet on The Lamb reruns. So, I've been eagerly awaiting its full launch, even if it was buried under the crushing weight of GTA 6 on November 19. (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

It has been revealed that the bugs, surely, were a hang-up of it being in beta, and would be remedied in the 1.0 release. Unfortunately, that's not how it worked out. Co-op is still a brilliant way to experience The Binding of Isaac, and I already have so many treasured memories of 2am runs with my girlfriend—accidentally killing her with Ipecac or ruining my mix-and-match Brimstone builds with Haemolacria while she made Hush look as easy as Larry Jr. However, the full launch is still plagued with net code issues and constant desyncs. In fact, the launch day patch notes don't even mention desyncs in its list of bug fixes. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Expectedly, the expansion was immediately hit with a wave of negative reviews, dragging its recent score down to Overwhelmingly Negative.

"A whole year with not one meaningful Rep+ update, complete radio silence the entire time and zero promotion, and then right before the update you spent a week awkardly leeching off the GTA 6 hype on X with worn-out memes — and what you finally served up is an absolute pile of dog crap," says one disgruntled reviewer.

According to recent reports, "OVERHAUL THE NETCODE," demands another. "The whole reason of this patch was playing online. I was playing on my Switch 2, it runs like a** (5g or Ethernet, normal input delay), desyncs, disconnects, and then one time I got into a lobby it froze.".

It has been revealed that "I don't get it. This 'release' is essentially the exact same version as the beta that came out a year ago but with some GTA things and a bunch of added bugs," one review mentioned. "What was the point of waiting a whole year with no updates if the game is in a worse state than it was previously? I get they were working on the Mewgenics port but c'mon.".

The full launch of Repentance+ came with a list of bug fixes that are at least worth reading over, but the PC launch wasn't the headline here. The headline is that the expansion is finally available on consoles, but bizarrely, while it's free for Steam consumers, those on PlayStation, Switch, and Xbox have to pay.

According to recent reports, after two years of waiting, it's disappointing, to say the least. The Binding of Isaac co-op is a truly novel way to re-experience a timeless roguelike classic and justify another 500 hours, but when the multiplayer is so inconsistent that absurdly overpowered, hours-long runs are at risk not because of RNG or skill, but because of desyncs and net code complications, it's hard to recommend Repentance+. Let alone for $10.
