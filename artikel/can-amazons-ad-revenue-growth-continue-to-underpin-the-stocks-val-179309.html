Can Amazon's Ad Revenue Growth Continue to Underpin the Stock's Value?

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (276 words, 📖 Standard)

Amazon $AMZN.com's AMZN advertising business remains one of the main supports for its earnings, and fourth-quarter 2026 guidance points to a rebound after a third quarter distorted by event timing. Amazon expects year-over-year advertising revenues to grow in the high teens in the fourth quarter of 2026. That would build on $21.3 billion of ad revenues in the year-ago quarter. The ad outlook sits within total fourth-quarter net sales guidance of $240.0-$247.0 billion, up 12-16%. Operating income is guided at $28.5-$33.5 billion compared with $25 billion a year ago.The third-quarter results show why the outlook matters. Advertising services revenues rose 11% (12% excluding foreign exchange) to $19.6 billion from $17.7 billion a year ago. That was a sharp slowdown from the second quarter, when ad revenues reached $19.8 billion on 26% growth. Amazon attributed the slowdown to timing anomalies, primarily related to Prime Day, plus a couple of other timing factors. Prime Day timing shifted this year, with the event occurring in the second quarter for most large countries. In 2025, Prime Day occurred entirely in the third quarter.Several recent developments support the high-teens target. Amazon Ads Agent now serves as a single AI-first platform for advertisers. Full-Funnel Campaigns introduced for all U.S. advertisers, and DVA+ began rolling out in late October. Live sports inventory is also deepening. Thursday Night Football's fifth season averaged 17.7 million viewers over six games, and NBA on Prime launched its second season in more than 200 countries.
