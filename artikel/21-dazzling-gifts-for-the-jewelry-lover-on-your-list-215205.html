21 Dazzling Gifts for the Jewelry Lover on Your List

As someone who loves jewelry and has covered it extensively over the years, I’m often asked for gift ideas for friends and loved ones. I always come back to the idea of collecting pieces over time and building a jewelry language of your own. Rather than abide by the usual conventions of gift-giving, however, think outside the box for pieces that surprise and might even become upcoming favorites for that special someone. A preference for yellow gold doesn’t rule out white gold, just as a love of diamonds leaves plenty of room for colored stones.

To build—and gift—that collection, start with pieces that feel personal and leave room for the unexpected. The mix here includes everything from heritage brands to independent jewelers that are destined to become heirlooms. To wit: Susanna Vega’s wood earrings and a lapis lazuli pendant by Haute Victoire that depicts the sun and eight planets in orbit. Personalization adds another layer: Renato Cipullo’s onyx initial pendant can be worn on a classic gold chain, as can Monica Rich Kosann’s colorful zodiac collection. The wrist offers room to play, too, with bracelets to stack and timepieces that make for memorable gifts. Below, find 21 ideas sure to find a home in their collection.
