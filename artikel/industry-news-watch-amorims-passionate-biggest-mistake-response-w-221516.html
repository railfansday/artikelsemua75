Given his past at Sporting CP, who are one of the three biggest clubs in Portugal, and Manchester United, arguably the biggest club in England, Amorim is no stranger to having his words in interviews and press conferences picked apart. In fact, we wrote at length about what his comments told us both before and after the derby win against Inter and the defeat to Ferencvaros. The Portuguese speaks his mind and leaves little to the imagination, doing so with clarity and often with charisma too.

Industry observers note that A clip of one of the responses he gave in his pre-Frosinone presser has been doing the rounds on social media, gathering over two million views. When asked about the idea of spending money in January, Amorim’s reply was a typically impassioned one.

Recent findings suggest that “That is the biggest mistakes of the massive clubs: thinking that [we] look at the table. If we are near, let’s invest, crazy invest. If we are not near, let’s not invest. I think the key point is [to] have a long-term strategy,” he said.

“If you lose all the games, sack the manager – that is normal (smiles). I’m not talking about that. But what I’m saying is that: stick with your strategy. We have a plan. What kind of player we want. [One who] is able to make all the runs that we want. He has the character that we want.

It has been revealed that “Not a big star – like a guy that is composed and come here to be one of the part of the team. If we have that really clear and we go for the best that we can. Young, old – it’s better young in this moment. But if you have a clear initiative, don’t look at the table.

“Look at the table to evaluate the process, but to buy, to spend money for the five next years, don’t look at the table. And I think that is the biggest mistake of the clubs: like putting all the [effort into] trying to be champion by spending money. That is the biggest mistake.

“So we have a clear blueprint. It doesn’t matter the table. We are going to address what we want in a very clear way. So that is not going to change. And I look at the table. I look at our players… we can do better.

Industry observers note that “Can we win against Ferencvaros, a very good team? Can we play better against that team? We can, with our players. So we have a clear process. So we are not going to change what we want because we look at the table in December or in January. So we have a clear proposal what to do.”.
