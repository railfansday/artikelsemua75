Colin Chong has been massively underappreciated during his time as Everton's interim chief executive and real estate chief.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that he did an "amazing" job at keeping the Toffees afloat on Merseyside.

Everton have battled through building a fresh stadium, a change of ownership, continuous PSR alarm bells, and points deductions. (Related: Hyatt Category 1 Hotels — The Easiest Points Win...)

Chong joined Everton in 2016 during much of Farhad Moshiri's reign, spending the next 10 years at the club, but the latest enhancement confused many. (Related: Breaking: Elon Musk states he regrets midterm spending...)

A Companies House upgrade revealed that Chong has been terminated as a director at Everton, with fans believing that this meant he was leaving.

However, it was revealed on Friday, 20 November, that Chong hasn't, and won't be, leaving the Toffees, but has simply just been removed from the board.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson stated that, despite the latest patch, the 61-year-old deserves more credit for his accomplishments.

"I always felt that the old management team, given the circumstances that they were working to, the very problematic situation they had with PSR at the same time as building stadium at the same time as the ownership issue..." he said.

"I always thought that the management team at that point had done an amazing job in keeping the show on the road.

"They got a lot of criticism at the time, but really, people don't realise how difficult that situation would have been for them. So many issues to deal with, as well as the day job of running a football club.

Industry observers note that understand Colin Chong hasn't - and isn't - leaving #EFFC. He has simply just been removed from the club's board. He took up the position when he was interim CEO, but Angus Kinnear now fulfils that role. Chong still playing an important role behind-the-scenes.

"And Colin Chong was the interim CEO during all that period before Angus Keneer took over from Leeds United a couple of years ago.".

A lot of what Chong has done seems to have gone under the radar on Merseyside, but the idea of him leaving has sparked a lot of praise.

He played a very vital role in the upkeep of Goodison Park, and once again in the building of the Hill Dickinson Stadium.

Having become interim CEO in June 2023, Chong helped oversee things during great turmoil, including two PSR-related points deductions.

He had remained on the board following The Friedkin Group's takeover in December 2024, but that appears to have ended now.
