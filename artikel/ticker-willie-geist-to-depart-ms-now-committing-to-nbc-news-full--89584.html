📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Top of the Ticker: After a nearly 20-year run, Willie Geist will be departing MS NOW for an expanded role at NBC News. Geist will continue to focus his efforts on Sunday Morning and broaden his presence across Today and other NBC News platforms.

In announcing his departure on Monday, during Morning Joe, Geist mentioned, “I am so grateful to both NBC and MSNOW for the unique opportunity to do what I love at both networks. He added, “This is a bittersweet goodbye to Joe, Mika, and the place that made possible everything else in my career over the last two decades, but I am thrilled to settle in with my family at Today and dedicate all of my time to that special place.”. Revolving Door: CNN has announced that Shery Ahn will join CNN en Español as an anchor and correspondent beginning Jan. 4, 2027. In a recent development, ali Bauman is joining CNBC as a general assignment reporter beginning November 30. Bauman was previously at CBS News, where she was a New York-based reporter and served as a Northeast correspondent for CBS Saturday Morning and the Saturday edition of the CBS Evening News. Meanwhile, Kylie Cohu joins CNBC as a senior equity analyst and correspondent on Dec. 7, reporting to Christina Cheddar-Berk, senior editor of stock analysis, and Katie Tarasov has been promoted to technology reporter, reporting to technology editor Megan Morrone.

Recent findings suggest that in addition, CNBC unveiled a slew of appointments to its editorial team, including Francesca Levy who joins as director of podcasts and digital shows on Dec. 7, reporting to Matt Quayle, senior executive producer of program development. Gary Cotton joins as a homepage editor today, and Penelope Patsuris joins as a weekend editor starting on Nov. 30, both were previously with CNN, and will report to deputy managing editor, Elisabeth Cordova. The network also announced that in September it hired Marshall Ritzel as a senior data visualization editor reporting to Kamelia Angelova, executive producer of digital video and audience development.

Court TV revealed that Ted Rowlands has been promoted to executive editor, and will continue his role as an on-air host. He joined Court TV in 2019.
