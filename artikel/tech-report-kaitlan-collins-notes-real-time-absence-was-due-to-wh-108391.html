The Season 24 finale of Real Time with Bill Maher featured a last-minute guest change as Kaitlan Collins was held up in Washington DC.

Although she was scheduled to appear on the HBO talk show alongside newly elected Texas Democratic congressman Bobby Pulido, the CNN anchor explained she was covering a White House press conference with Donald Trump amid a report that she was “quietly dropped” from the show’s lineup.
