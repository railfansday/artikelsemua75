No one can accuse Bryce Alakai of not being dedicated to craft. The 30-year-old, who broke out as the winner of Love Island alongside his now-girlfriend Trinity Tatum, has been steadily growing his profile as a music producer and DJ in the wake of the show. While it’s common for reality stars to pivot in another field after their breakthrough moment, Alakai has been hustling in the space long before the dating competition series.

When the FAA issued a ground stop on Nov. 13, halting all flights around the country, Alakai found himself in a bit of a pickle. He had four indicates back-to-back that weekend. Thankfully, he’d made it from Toronto to Atlanta, but that’s where things got tricky. The flights from Atlanta to Florida were canceled, so instead of canceling the shows, like others might’ve, he got in a car and drove about eight hours to his Florida shows. Alakai and Tatum were fan favorites, eventually winning the show’s eighth season earlier this year. Tatum remains his biggest cheerleader, and the feeling is mutual. “It’s just cool because we’re doing different lanes. She’s so deep in fashion. She’s enveloped in that world. I’m obviously maxed out in music right now,” he tells The Hollywood Reporter on a Zoom. “We’re supporting each other’s dreams.”. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

In a recent development, with a upcoming single “Conscience,” released on Nov. 20 through Ultra Records, Alakai is going all-in on his career as a musician. “I wanted my first release with Ultra to be just a very happy song, very upbeat,” he says.

“I like the lyrics because it always reminds me of Trinity — fall in love with your best friend,” he indicates. “The whole point of [the song] is just got to live your life, make your mistakes, do what you got to do — but also just enjoy the ride.”.

Below, Alakai speaks with THR about producing, balancing his relationship with post-Love Island fame and his newly introduced single.

Have the last few months been just nonstop for you?

It’s been literally the definition of zero to 100.

Were you anticipating that or hoping for that?

Sources indicate that no. I was 29, turning 30. I was in that place in my life, where it’d be nice to have a person. … I always thought if something comes from it outside of that, that’d be great. But I didn’t think it would be to the extent that it is. I thought it’d be pretty minor. Oh, you got a deal here or there and then you move on with your life. This has been crazy. But I have such a good team that just took the little fire and fanned the shit out of it.

It has been revealed that are you hearing from people after your sets? Are they excited?

Recent findings suggest that it’s insane to be honest. I was in Toronto for that show [I’d mentioned]. Even the venue told me it was the fastest-selling show they’d ever had there. I was so excited to be there because it was such a massive crowd. It was like 2,500 people. They’re all hard tickets. Dude, when I walked out, everyone was just screaming. It was crazy. I get the adrenaline. I probably shouldn’t do this, but I check a lot of what people have said after the show because I’m just curious. I’m always trying to see how people are receiving what we’re doing, and 99 percent is all positive. That makes me feel good. I had a girl send me a message the other night [that] said it was the best night she’s ever had in her life. I was reading it, I was like, “That’s insane.” Obviously those things are pretty validating, but I feel like the overall response has been really positive.

This is obviously something you were doing before Love Island, but have you felt people question if you’re taking it seriously in the wake of the show?

It has been revealed that oh, 100 percent. Thankfully I had all my stuff on TikTok already posted up from the past years, so there’s obviously videos of me DJing. Three weeks before I went to Fiji, I opened for Carlita and John Summit, which was my biggest show to date. I was already getting a little traction with it. We were starting to make some moves. When I got off [of Love Island] I saw that everyone kind of recognized [I’d been doing it], so it created a different … I think that’s why maybe the demonstrates have been doing so well, there was already that layer before. At first, the first show, I think people were questioning if I could do it. “Just because he did it before doesn’t mean he is actually good.” After each one of these shows, the credibility has slowly gone up. Now, we’re releasing this new song on Nov. 20 with Ultra. I just feel like I’ve gotten a lot of industry backing that is pretty rare from something like this. The collab interests that I’ve been getting, and the sessions I’ve been doing have been in — it’s been insane. I always feel like I’m in a dream because I feel like with any other career, you see the progression in front of you. With this, it was literally just zero to full on go mode, which I’m really grateful for. It is surreal just because it’s like you’re just jumping straight in.

There’s not much downtime these days, right? You’re also starting this new life with another person, so that’s an added element as well.

That definitely adds a layer to it. I’ve been with her today. We had just flew back last night, I’m with her today and then I have to fly to Vegas tomorrow. Thankfully, she’s coming to Vegas too a couple days later, but it really makes it so I have to check in with her and balance the time. It’s just hard because with these dates, I want to be my best at these performances, but I also don’t want to neglect her and make her ever feel like she’s not my priority because she is. It’s just a balancing game I’ve been learning that I haven’t had to do before and to make sure I water both aspects of my life equally.

I imagine she’s also quite busy.

Sources indicate that today, I have two sessions after this call and then she has an interview and then another thing and then shooting a video. We come back at night. All this shit just happened. It’s just a balancing thing on both of us, and we’re figuring it out. But with how busy we both are, it definitely doesn’t make it easier. I think our communication is so good that we figure it out.
