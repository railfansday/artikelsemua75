A breakfast strata is one of our go-tos when we want a hearty, shareable brunch dish, but it’s hard to put into words how good this French onion croissant strata is. It takes a few extra minutes to deeply caramelize the onions, but the extra flavor and richness make a HUGE difference.

Recent findings suggest that making it French Onion:Caramelizing the onions over medium heat helps them brown evenly and consistently. If the onions begin to stick or burn, add a splash of water to bring them back! Garlic, thyme, and black pepper are the aromatic unsung heroes in the onions, so you don’t want to skip them. Deglazing with white wine is essential in any French onion recipe, so use the one you’d sip! Once the wine has reduced, add some beef stock to impart meaty French onion soup flavor. The caramelized onions then get layered with torn croissants and Gruyère cheese, and topped with dollops of cream cheese.
