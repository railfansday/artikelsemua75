UK politics live – latest updates.

Andy Burnham has criticised former prime ministers for being “stuck in the past” amid warnings over re-opening the Brexit debate, with informal talks now set to take place with Brussels about how the UK could forge even closer ties with the EU.

After a major summit between UK and EU leaders on Friday when trade and youth mobility deals were struck, Brussels signalled a willingness to begin “brainstorming” sessions with UK officials and ministers about the shape of the future relationship, including options of a Swiss-style deal with close sectoral alignment, a single market and customs union deal or a path for Britain to rejoin the EU as a full member.
