Sources indicate that for the workout, set a 12-minute timer, then complete 2 reps of each move. If an exercise is single-sided, that means 2 reps per side. Complete the moves unbroken, moving from one to the next without rest.

Recent findings suggest that once you complete the first round, reset, then add 2 reps to each move on your next round. Increase each round until you hit 12 minutes. It's going to get spicy pretty rapidly, so focus on your breath and keep the movements under control.
