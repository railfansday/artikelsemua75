Why David Fincher Is The Perfect Director For Brad Pitt's Cliff Booth Sequel

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that one of the best movies of the 2010s was Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. The film, which was written and directed by Quentin Tarantino, perfectly recaptured the late 1960s aesthetic of Hollywood and, more broadly, the entertainment industry that plays such a huge role in the area. Not only that, but the two leading characters, Rick Dalton and Cliff Booth, were some of the best that Tarantino has ever created.

Tarantino isn’t particularly well known for making sequels to his work, which made the announcement that Brad Pitt’s character would be getting a solo film all the more surprising. The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, which Tarantino wrote and was directed by David Fincher, highlights the title character eight years after his last adventure, and, this time, gives him the spotlight as the film’s main character. In a recent development, with Booth firmly the main character of his own adventure(s), it’s likely that audiences will get to see a more complete look at him this time around. In Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, Booth’s backstory was teased a little bit, such as his service during the Second World War and the suspicious death of his wife (which he was, more likely than not, responsible for). Sources indicate that tarantino and Fincher may be an odd combination for screenwriter and director, but it may actually work in favor of the new movie. After all, Fincher is known for telling dark, brooding, and moody stories, so he might just be the perfect filmmaker to explore the darker sides of Cliff Booth in Netflix’s new Oscar contender.

It has been revealed that once Upon a Time in Hollywood was firmly a double act. On one hand, there was Rick Dalton, an often misguided but well-meaning actor obsessed with getting his career back on track. Then there’s Cliff Booth, his trusty sidekick, someone who has a mysterious past before he joined the entertainment industry. Brad Pitt arguably had the meatier of the two roles in the film, even though Once Upon a Time in Hollywood only teased the full extent of his character.

Industry observers note that for example, Booth is portrayed as something of a killing machine, taking down several bad guys throughout the course of the film. Even when he isn’t killing people, Booth still demonstrates his fighting expertise. For example, he beat none other than Bruce Lee in hand-to-hand combat, and did so with relative ease, something that is mentioned repeatedly in The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth.

Sources indicate that booth also defeated the Manson family murderers at the end of Once Upon A Time in Hollywood, and did so with such calmness that he actually seemed he was having fun. All of this teased the character, but it didn’t show how he became this cool, calm, and collected killing machine. Now, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth looks set to explore that side of its title character.

Who better to bring out the dark side of Brad Pitt as an actor than David Fincher? After all, the two have worked together before, and done so with great success. Se7en, for example, has an incredibly dark and twisted story with Pitt’s character at the center of it. Then there’s Fight Club, which is an even bleaker tale, and also features Brad Pitt as one of the darkest characters he’s ever played.

Sources indicate that with Tarantino writing the script, plus David Fincher directing the movie, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth may be the perfect vessel to explore the true nature of this character, including his darker side. Plus, Brad Pitt has remarkable range as an actor, and Once Upon a Time in Hollywood featured one of his career-best performances.

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth releases in theaters on November 25.
