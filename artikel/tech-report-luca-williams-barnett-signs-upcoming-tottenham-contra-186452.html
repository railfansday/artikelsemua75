Tottenham wonderkid Luca Williams-Barnett has signed a latest contract, tying his long-term future to his boyhood club.

The 18-year-old was in the final two years of his existing contract, but has now agreed fresh terms.

Much has been expected of Williams-Barnett for quite some time, after impressing at both domestic and international youth levels with Spurs and England, and he has had to be patient after being given his senior debut last season in the Carabao Cup aged just 16 years old.

Two short cameos in the Premier League earlier this season led to a first senior start on Saturday for the visit of Ipswich, in which the teenager produced a star performance with a goal and assist in a 2-0 win.

Williams-Barnett became Tottenham’s youngest scorer in the Premier League since Gareth Bale in 2007 when putting Spurs ahead after just 15 minutes, before turning provider to set up Dominic Solanke shortly after half-time.

Reacting to the man-of-the-match performance, which earned the youngster a standing ovation from the home crowd, Roberto De Zerbi compared the 18-year-old to Barcelona star Lamine Yamal.

"I’m positive about that because he has great confidence. He knows all his qualities, but I think he has a very good family and it’s crucial for this type of player," De Zerbi said.

It has been revealed that "I think he’s already in a top club. He has nothing to change because he’s already at the top of the level of the club he wants to arrive. He understands because he’s smart. He’s smart to understand the behaviour.

"I think I’m positive about his behaviour, his character, because I’ve been working with him for three or four months and I know him enough to say this.".

De Zerbi mentioned: "When I said the first 11 to the players, I said, ‘remember the first game, the debut of you, and help the kid tomorrow, because he’s one of us, and if he plays well, we have more chances to win the game.’”.
