Each account trading on the XRP Ledger's decentralized exchange moved an average of 4,328 XRP a day in the third quarter of 2026, up 437% from 805 XRP in the same quarter a year earlier, according to a report that XRP treasury company Evernorth released on Tuesday.

Sources indicate that according to Evernorth, the average swap on the XRP Ledger reached 35 XRP in the third quarter, up from 19 XRP in the second quarter and under 6 XRP a year earlier. "The growth came from bigger trades, not more of them," the manufacturer wrote in the report.

Industry observers note that at the same time, the number of accounts trading on the XRP Ledger's order book fell to 1,042 a day in the third quarter of 2026 from 2,027 a day in the third quarter of 2025. The number stood at 1,203 in the fourth quarter of 2025, 1,324 in the first quarter of 2026 and 1,125 in the second quarter, according to the report. Order-book volume on the XRP Ledger rose to 4.51 million XRP a day in the third quarter from 1.63 million XRP a day a year earlier, Evernorth data shows.

In total, 5.16 million XRP a day was traded on the XRP Ledger's exchange in the third quarter, and the figure was up 96% year over year, according to Evernorth.

Measured in U.S. dollars, exchange volume on the XRP Ledger fell 15% year over year in the third quarter of 2026, according to the report.

Sources indicate that evernorth also compared the XRP Ledger with Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Avalanche and Polygon. Across these seven networks, exchange volume in dollars fell 42% over the same period, the report shows.

XRP was trading at around $0.93 on Tuesday morning after reaching as high as $1.026 on Monday, according to CoinMarketCap.

It has been revealed that the report also indicates that the average balance of Ripple's RLUSD stablecoin on the XRP Ledger reached $937 million in the third quarter of 2026, up from $539 million in the second quarter. By the end of the quarter, the RLUSD balance on the XRP Ledger stood at $1.15 billion, compared with $670 million at the end of the second quarter, and the number of RLUSD holders on the network rose to 67,449 from 45,620.

Sources indicate that according to Evernorth, the XRP Ledger's average share of RLUSD reached 48.5% in the third quarter, up from 34% in the second quarter. Still, Ethereum finished the quarter with more RLUSD than the XRP Ledger. Evernorth noted that on Ethereum, RLUSD "can already be lent," and that on the XRP Ledger, "native lending is in validator voting.". As of Nov. 20, the Lending Protocol amendment had 24 of 35 validator votes, according to XRPScan. The amendment needs 29 votes, held for 14 days, to activate.

Sources indicate that evernorth completed its business combination with Armada Acquisition Corp. II on Oct. 9, and its shares began trading on Nasdaq under the ticker XRPN on Oct. 12. The organization entered the public markets holding approximately 473 million XRP, according to its press release.

Evernorth revealed the deal in October 2025, with investments from SBI, Ripple, Pantera Capital, Kraken and GSR.
