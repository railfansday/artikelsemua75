Latest Update: Anthropic IPO Fails to Calm AI Jitters

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (80 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Seaport Research Partners Chief Equity Strategist Jonathan Golub tells Bloomberg This Weekend the sharp AI-driven market selloff looks overdone, arguing that demand for computing power remains strong and semiconductor earnings expectations continue to rise. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Golub states a bigger challenge is whether markets can keep financing the enormous capital needs of AI companies as investors are asked to fund hundreds of billions of dollars in new infrastructure.
