In a recent development, topper Guild is getting into the animation game. Content creator Topper Guild is teaming up with animation manufacturer Toonstar to create an original series, which is set to premiere on YouTube. “Animation is a medium I’ve always wanted to work in, but I didn’t think it was possible to maintain that same pace my fans expect, or retain the creative control I have with my live-action content,” Guild said. “But Toonstar changed that. They’re unlocking animation for the creator economy.”.

It has been revealed that kane Parsons is going from Backrooms to BAFTA. YouTuber-turned-filmmaker Kane Parsons has been named as one of 14 creatives participating in BAFTA North America’s 2026 Breakthrough US cohort. The Backrooms director will join the program alongside rising entertainment icons like Heated Rivalry’s Connor Storrie and The ‘Burbs creator Celeste Hughey.

Entrepreneurs On Fire has been acquired. John Lee Dumas has reportedly agreed to sell the tech giant behind his business podcast, Entrepreneurs On Fire, to PR firm Pathos Communications for $3 million in cash, plus up to $6 million in “performance payments.” Dumas will continue to host the show as part of a three-year deal.

Recent findings suggest that youTube could pay billions over child safety concerns. Sources familiar with the matter reportedly told Bloomberg that YouTube is preparing to settle a wave of lawsuits regarding its allegedly “addictive” design by paying roughly $3 billion to thousands of school districts.

In a recent development, tikTok dropped some major stats in its 2026 Music Impact Report. According to a study commissioned by TikTok from Luminate, 94% of songs that entered the Billboard Global 200 last year went viral on TikTok first—a ten-percentage-point jump from 2024. The report also noted that songs “that go viral on TikTok spend four times longer on the Billboard Global 200 than those without a viral moment.”.

Recent findings suggest that A Meta vet is coming to OnlyFans. Whitney Rosenthal is coming aboard as OnlyFans’ first global head of creator partnerships. The exec previously worked at Meta and YouTube; most recently, she served as vice president of global creator podcast platforms at Podimo.

Recent findings suggest that streaming platforms are going all in on creator content. New data from Ampere Analysis highlights that U.S. streaming services debuted over 3,700 hours of “creator-led programming” in Q3 2026, up from 1,934 in the previous quarter. Distribution of new creator-led content on SVOD services also rose 75% over the last quarter, with Amazon Prime Video alone contributing over 230 hours (per Net Influencer). Sources indicate that GTA VI made billions on day one. Rockstar Games announced Friday that Grand Theft Auto VI hit $3.79 billion during its first day of retail sales, “setting a latest record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.”.

A microdrama studio will produce content for Black audiences. NBCUniversal and HarbourView Equity Partners are throwing their financial weight behind June, a microdrama studio introduced by Will and Dom Packer. The studio will focus on the development, production, and acquisition of original vertical dramas aimed at Black viewers.

It has been revealed that freeman & Forrest has a new majority owner. Falfurrias Management Partners has made a majority investment in B2B influencer marketing firm Freeman & Forrest. Co-founder Emily Freeman will stay on board as CEO, while co-founder Forrest Brazeal will continue in his role as president.

e.l.f. set a world record at TwitchCon. e.l.f. Cosmetics made history at TwitchCon 2026 by breaking the Guinness World Record for most people applying make-up simultaneously. The beauty brand’s massive GRWM included 733 in-person participants—and that was only the start of e.l.f.’s onsite activations at the con.

In a recent development, lifetime is turning a viral friendship into a film. In 2016, Wanda Dench and Jamal Hinton spread holiday cheer across the internet by sharing the heartwarming story of how an accidental text turned into an invitation to Thanksgiving dinner. Ten years later, Dench and Hinton have turned their shared Thanksgivings into an annual tradition—and now, Lifetime is turning it into a movie.

According to recent reports, the Denver Broncos are tapping into the power of anime. The anime horse girls of Umamusume: Pretty Derby have become a familiar sight for sports fans in recent months, with appearances at events ranging from Grand Prix de Paris to Japan’s Super GT championship. Now—as part of an official collaboration with the Denver Broncos—two of the anime’s most recognizable characters will be on hand to support the team at Empower Field when they face the Miami Dolphins next month.
