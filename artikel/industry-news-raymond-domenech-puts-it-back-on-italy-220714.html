In a recent development, last Thursday, during France’s 3-1 defeat against Italy, Raymond Domenech criticized the Nazionale at half-time when the score was 1-1. He had highlighted a “weakness in all sectors” and a technical level “disconcerting”. He was then criticized by the Italian press, who accused him of not having turned the page on the 2006 World Cup where the Italians beat France in the final while he was the coach.

According to recent reports, this Sunday, France beat Turkey (2-1) while Italy fell against Belgium (4-1). The Nazionale did not qualify for the final 8 of the League of Nations to the delight of Raymond Domenech. “France logically qualifies.
