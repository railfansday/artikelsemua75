Industry observers note that in Hollywood, having a lawyer on speed dial can mean only one of two things. A deal is imminent, or a jury is deliberating. If it’s the latter, chances are the attorney on the other end of the line is on this list.

The 25 Legal Troubleshooters on these pages handle the problems agents can’t negotiate away: criminal charges, custody fights, contested fortunes and who gets to sell the Lakers. Their firms represent stars, moguls and heirs, although lately the L.A. Public Defender’s Office has been giving them unexpected competition. The lawyers themselves are attracting attention, too. Brian Steel has a Drake song named after him; Laura Wasser has a fashion campaign. Others spend their days explaining why a chatbot shouldn’t rewrite your trust. Sources indicate that this firmspent the year representing famous clients facing charges of murder, rape and sex trafficking. Agnifilo and Geragos followed their defense of Sean “Diddy” Combs with Harvey Weinstein’s third trial, which ended in a hung jury, while another client, Oren Alexander, Kim Kardashian’s former real estate broker, was convicted of sex trafficking alongside brothers Tal and Alon. (Tal was represented by Deanna Paul, now a partner at Geragos & Geragos.) Friedman Agnifilo, meanwhile, helped take the death penalty off the table for Luigi Mangione, then pressed to dismiss his state murder case on double jeopardy grounds following his federal plea. But the firm’s most incendiary client this year has nothing to do with Hollywood: Winston Lee is one of seven former Cornell fraternity members accused in a civil suit of drugging and gang-raping a fellow student.

Sources indicate that actor Olivier Martinez had Baghdaserians in his corner during his headline-grabbing custody battle with ex-wife Halle Berry. In October, the lawyer secured a temporary restraining order and primary physical custody of their 13-year-old son. The judge later dissolved the restraining order but kept Martinez the primary custodial parent. Martinez is now seeking increased child support and $240,000 in legal fees from Berry. Another custody client: Basketball Wives’ Clayanna Warthen, whose dispute involved former NBA player Andre Iguodala.

Berk’s clients sit on both sides of criminal cases: She has represented stalking victim Jennifer Aniston and defended Harvey Weinstein. She claims three-quarters of her practice involves preventing charges from being filed. But singer D4vd’s murder case was impossible to keep quiet. Berk defended him against charges of killing 14-year-old Celeste Rivas Hernandez before withdrawing Aug. 31, when he qualified for a public defender — a growing trend in Hollywood (see Alan Jackson).

According to recent reports, two music cases, two different codas. Bloom’s firm settled Lauren Pisciotta’s harassment and assault suit against Kanye West in July, ending a two-year fight. But former Danity Kane singer Dawn Richard’s suit accusing Sean “Diddy” Combs of sexual assault and physical abuse was dismissed because most claims were filed too late. Bloom’s targets also include the FBI: Eight Jeffrey Epstein survivors she represents sued the bureau for allegedly failing to investigate reports of his abuse.

In a recent development, after winning “Blurred Lines” for Marvin Gaye’s family, Busch is taking on artificial intelligence. In October, he sued Anthropic on behalf of Elvis Presley’s rights holders, including Authentic Brands Group, alleging its Claude models trained on 274 Presley songs without permission. The demand: up to $41.1 million — or about $150,000 per song. Anthropic disputes the claims. Similar suits for music publishers Reservoir Media and Round Hill have made Busch a recurring adversary for the AI company.

Mitchell Silberberg & Knupp LLP.

In a recent development, more than 10 years after his death, Muhammad Ali is still one of Hollywood’s most active clients, and Eisen is the lawyer guarding his name. In January, the U.S. Postal Service issued an Ali Forever stamp, a milestone for an estate that the firm’s trusts and estates co-chair has helped steer through years of name-and-likeness deals. His firm’s other clients: Marilyn Monroe, Elvis Presley and Albert Einstein.

Findling and co-counsel Brian Steel dismantled the government’s murder-for-hire case against rapper Lil Durk, winning acquittals on all five counts. Findling argued that Durk’s former assistant, testifying for the prosecution, was himself the “mastermind,” urging jurors to see a “tsunami of reasonable doubt.” Afterward, 50 Cent dubbed him the “Billion Dollar Lawyer.” His client list also includes Cardi B and President Donald Trump.

Liner Freedman; Meister Seelig & Schuster PLLC.

Sources indicate that freedman wielded the machete and Schuster the scalpel for actor-director Justin Baldoni in his battle with It Ends With Us co-star Blake Lively. His $400 million countersuit failed, but her harassment claims were dismissed, and the two sides settled without a payout. Lively later won $407,000 for defending against Baldoni’s defamation claim — just 5 percent of her $8 million legal-fee request, which Freedman called a “significant victory.”.

As the case exploded online, his client faced threats, doxing and swatting, while supporters rallied under #IAmJaneDoe to protect her identity. New York Attorney General Letitia James is overseeing a criminal investigation.

Simpson “Dream Team” alum, Holley represented pitcher Tyler Skaggs’ family in its wrongful death case against the Angels, arguing the team failed to stop an employee supplying players with drugs. The club settled during jury deliberations. Alongside Mangels, she also helped record label Del Records cut a proposed federal fine — for doing business with a sanctioned, cartel-linked concert promoter — from $6.7 million to $1.8 million. Restraining orders are also a specialty; the two have secured them for Cindy Crawford, Kaia Gerber and Lana Del Rey.

Recent findings suggest that when gamers take their grievances to court, Kumar represents the companies behind the screens. She reps Microsoft and Mojang, creator of Minecraft, in suits accusing game companies of hooking children. Her clients also include Tinder and Hinge, for which she secured dismissals in two cases alleging the apps failed to remove user profiles and messages. She also helped technology trade group NetChoice keep key provisions of California’s age-appropriate online design law blocked on appeal.

Jackson, who has defended Harvey Weinstein and Kevin Spacey, exited as Nick Reiner’s attorney in January. That notorious patricide-and-matricide case is now being handled by what’s rapidly turning into Hollywood’s hottest latest legal shop — the L.A. County Public Defender’s Office. Not only is public defender Kimberly Greene now representing Reiner, but her colleagues Walid Kandeel and Lisa Roth took over singer D4vd’s murder defense from Blair Berk in August. Jackson, meanwhile, is teaming with former client Karen Read for a true-crime podcast, Read Between the Lines, debuting in early 2027.

Hudson and Moses didn’t just get Justin Baldoni’s $400 million countersuit against Blake Lively and Ryan Reynolds thrown out; they helped stick him with $407,000 of Lively’s legal bill, securing the first federal court award under California’s law protecting sexual harassment accusers from retaliatory defamation suits. Moses helped draft the motion; Hudson led Lively’s team. Lively’s own claims ended in a settlement without a payout.

In a recent development, nikas won $102.2 million for the Morgan Art Foundation against publisher Michael McKenzie over unauthorized and altered works based on Robert Indiana’s LOVE — the familiar sculpture with the tilted O. He calls it the largest art-litigation verdict on record. His partner Spiro had more mixed results with client Elon Musk: Delaware’s Supreme Court reinstated his roughly $56 billion Tesla pay package, but a San Francisco jury found he misled Twitter investors.

Industry observers note that some brothers fared better than others. Paul defended celebrity realtor Tal Alexander against charges that he and his siblings drugged and sexually trafficked women. The jury convicted all three, and each received a 30-year sentence. Geragos had more luck with the Lyle and Erik Menendez, helping them secure a latest parole hearing roughly 17 months ahead of schedule after the brothers were denied release last year. His TMZ appearances stirred up some controversy, though, with Geragos’ comments on the Cornell fraternity rape case prompted the accuser’s lawyer to allege that he was effectively assisting the defense team, which includes his daughter, Teny, at Agnifilo Intrater. Geragos denies it.

Media & Entertainment Litigation.

After helping Britney Spears escape her conservatorship, Rosengart fought for FKA twigs to reclaim her voice, winning an agreement lifting restrictions on discussing her sexual abuse allegations against ex-boyfriend Shia LaBeouf. He’s also defending Selena Gomez against investors who accuse her of failing to deliver on commitments to build and promote her mental health startup, Wondermind.

Entertainment & Business Litigation.

A letter from Marty Singer has ruined many a Hollywood morning. In November, he accused filmmaker Yi Zhou of harassing Jeremy Renner after she alleged the actor sent explicit messages and threatened to call ICE. He also represented Priscilla Presley against former business partners who accused her of concealing a $25 million jewelry collection; they dropped that suit in February, retaining the right to refile. In March, Lavely & Singer became Singer Weinsten Wolf & Jonelis, adding three longtime partners to the firm’s name.

According to recent reports, most lawyers hope for a referral. Steel got a Drake song named after him. After representing Young Thug and joining Sean “Diddy” Combs’ defense, the Atlanta attorney teamed with Drew Findling to win Lil Durk acquittals on all five counts in his federal murder-for-hire trial. Fans swarmed him outside court after the verdict. Durk remains jailed ahead of a separate racketeering trial in August 2027.

In a recent development, strauss and Forster help Hollywood’s wealthy pass fortunes to the next generation, minimizing estate taxes and planning for the transfer of family businesses. Lately, some clients have acquired another adviser: a chatbot. Strauss told CNBC that clients are uploading trust documents to AI, then returning with suggested revisions that can take hours to review. “I have not actually received a single workable suggestion from that process,” he states. The firm is updating its contracts to warn that sharing confidential documents with AI “lawyers” could jeopardize attorney-client privilege.

After helping secure Lachlan Murdoch’s control of Fox and News Corp, Streisand moved to another family power struggle: Jeanie Buss versus her five siblings over whether to sell the family’s remaining 17.8 percent Lakers stake. Her brothers and sister want to cash out alongside billionaire Mark Walter, who bought control of the team in 2025 and has agreed to sell to Bob Iger and Joshua Kushner at a $12.5 billion valuation. Buss wants to retain the shares. A hearing is set for Dec. 8.

It has been revealed that wasser doesn’t just handle divorces; she models for them, fronting fashion label Reformation’s “Divorce Collection” campaign this year. For Sia, she filed an October settlement keeping the singer the primary custodial parent of her toddler with Dan Bernad, who receives $42,500 a month in child support. Her newest client is Hila Klein, who filed to divorce husband and H3 Podcast co-host Ethan Klein on Nov. 2 after publicly accusing him of an affair.

Sources indicate that wigdor is leaving no stone unturned in Julia Ormond’s ongoing sexual assault suit against Harvey Weinstein — pursuing Disney, Miramax and CAA for allegedly failing to protect her. His firm even won an order requiring former CAA chairman Michael Ovitz to answer questions under oath about the agency’s representation of the actress. Other clients include Cassie Ventura, whose rape and sex trafficking suit against Sean “Diddy” Combs settled a day after filing. The firm is also suing Leon Black, alleging the financier filed retaliatory lawsuits to intimidate lawyers representing his sexual assault accusers.

It has been revealed that for Zwicker’s wealthy clients, moving away can be as complicated as passing a fortune down. Alongside estate planning for entertainers and multinational families, she advises on the tax consequences of changing countries. California, for one, doesn’t surrender its taxpayers easily. She told The San Francisco Standard that auditors examine ATM withdrawals and Apple Pay transactions to determine whether residents have really left — or just their tax bills. “They want to see all of the data on all of your devices,” she claims.
