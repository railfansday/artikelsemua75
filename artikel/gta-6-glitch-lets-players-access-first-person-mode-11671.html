A GTA 6 player has discovered a glitch that lets fans experience Rockstar’s new open-world game in first-person, despite the feature being unavailable at launch.

Rockstar previously explained that development was focused on third-person animations, although players can still use a first-person perspective in certain situations. (Related: Breaking: Democrat Hakeem Jeffries nominated to be firs...)

However, just hours after release, a player discovered a workaround that allows them to explore Vice City through the eyes of Jason and Lucia, complete with weapon animations.

The discovery was shared to YouTube by ‘oneboyler,’ detailing the find.

Recent findings suggest that on PS5, players need to change their touchpad settings from opening the map to switching cameras. They must then enter a vehicle, open their in-game phone, and rapidly press Circle and the touchpad to trigger the glitch.

If successful, the camera becomes stuck in first-person, allowing players to leave their vehicle and explore the world without returning to the traditional third-person view.

The footage shows the player walking around and using weapons, with animations already appearing to support the perspective.

“As you can see, the weapon animations for first person mode are already in the game,” the uploader explained.

“This is the only way to experience GTA VI in first person mode, since the game for whatever reason didn’t release with it enabled.”.

There is one major drawback, however. Once the glitch is activated, players cannot simply switch back to third-person. Instead, they must reload their save.

The latest discovery suggests that some first-person functionality remains accessible in the finished game, even if Rockstar hasn’t officially enabled it.
