This Thursday, we are playing the fifth day of the 2026/2027 Nations League with a clash between Italy and France. At home, the Squadra Azzurra are organized in a 4-3-3 with Gianluigi Donnarumma who officiates as the last defense. In front of him, we find Michael Kayode, Giorgi Scalvini, Alessandro Bastoni and Riccardo Calafiori in defense. Davide Frattesi and Nicolo Barella make up the midfield with Sandro Tonali. In front, Francesco Pio Esposito is in attack with Sebastiano Esposito and Nicolò Cambiaghi at his side.

Recent findings suggest that for their part, the Tricolores are structured in a 4-2-3-1 with Mike Maignan in the cages behind Malo Gusto, Jérémy Jacquet, Dayot Upamecano and Adrien Truffert. The midfield is provided by Rayan Cherki and Adrien Rabiot with Lucas Da Cunha. In front, Désiré Doué is supported by Michael Olise and Bradley Barcola.
