Set to arrive some time in 2027, Pokémon Winds and Pokèmon Waves is expected to be the most ambitious Pokémon entry of all time, as it takes players to a tropical archipelago region inspired by Southeast Asia, and it is here that players will be able to explore various underwater areas to find enough 'mon to fill up their 300 strong Pokédex. If the rumor of 300+ brand-newly introduced Pokémon is to be believed, at least.

Industry observers note that not only that, but Pokémon Winds and Pokèmon Waves will kickstart a new Pokémon era as these games will be the first entries in its tenth generation. As such, fans are expecting something major, and whilst details surrounding the games are being kept close to the developer's chests right now, one leak has hinted at the potential box legendaries. Recent findings suggest that for those who do not know, or are new to the Pokémon franchise, box legendaries are the Pokémon you will see displayed on the covers for the games. The Pokémon shown in the key art are very rare and powerful, and play an important role in the game's overall lore. For example, in Generation 2, Ho-Oh and Lugia were the flagship 'mon. (Related: OpenAI's AI models boost AMD's upcoming flagship GPU de...)

Whilst the key art for Pokémon Winds and Pokèmon Waves has not yet been revealed, and so neither have the box legendaries, one leak suggests that some interesting designs may be on the cards.

Industry observers note that according to Light over on Twitter/X, Pokémon Winds' box legendary will be a Garuda-inspired Legendary, whilst Pokémon Waves' will be a Naga-like mermaid Legendary. On top of this, the image provided highlights potential silhouettes of these Pokémon. As you can see, the Garuda-inspired Legendary features a large bird-like design, whilst the second is very clearly a mermaid with flowing hair and a crown of some sort.

Whilst these designs have not been officially released nor confirmed, it did not stop fans from taking to the comments to share their thoughts on the potential box legendaries for Pokémon Winds and Pokèmon Waves.

For one user, the mythology of such figures would definitely make sense. "Wait, Garuda and Naga are Indian words," they shared. "OMG, there is a legendary story of a rivalry between Garuda and Naga where Garuda is the hunter of Naga. They are part of Mahabharata and Ramayana also. The point is that Naga is weak to Garuda, and both have a Humanoid and God-like form.".

However, another user had different ideas, sharing, "Since it’s a leak as of yet, I was thinking for Winds, the mascot would be a pterosaur (Quetzalcoatlus) titan (Flying-Dragon), and Waves was a Mosasaurus (Water-Dragon) giant.".

In a recent development, with many of the comments consisting of fans approving of these leaks, it is safe to say that the person who made these designs is certainly onto something, especially if it plays into the myths famous in Southeast Asian regions. For now, however, we can only wait to see which box legendaries will grace the covers of Pokémon Winds and Pokèmon Waves when they launch on the Nintendo Switch 2 some time in 2027.
