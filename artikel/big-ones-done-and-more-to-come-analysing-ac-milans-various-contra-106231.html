AC Milan’s negotiating table has remained busy between the summer and winter windows, with a clear emphasis on contracts.

According to recent reports, in the past month alone the club have revealed new long-term deals for Strahinja Pavlovic and Christian Pulisic, and it believed that a couple more are not far from being announced. Extending the discussion to the youth teams, a lot of young talents have signed their first pro deals this year too.

Having been stung in the past by not preserving leverage when it comes to contracts, it marks a clear change in gear from the management to ensure that the prized assets for the present and future remain under control and do not slip away for nothing.
