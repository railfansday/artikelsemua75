For Justine Skye, the art of gift-giving comes down to how well you know someone. Whether it’s a nod to an inside joke, a reminder of a shared memory, or something they’d never think to buy for themselves, the singer gravitates toward presents with a personal touch. “I’m thoughtful and a little sentimental,” Skye tells ELLE. Naturally, jewelry is high on her list.

It makes sense, then, that this holiday season Skye is partnering with Kendra Scott, starring in the brand’s “Long Live Joy” campaign, directed by Andrew Thomas Huang. Set across three imaginative worlds—Celestial, Radiance, and Botanical—the campaign celebrates individuality, self-expression, and finding joy on your own terms.

Sources indicate that it’s a sentiment that resonates with Skye, whose own approach to accessorizing is equally thoughtful. “Sometimes I’ll build an entire look around one piece and let everything else fall into place,” she claims. Among her favorites from the holiday collections is the Fate Star Gold Short Pendant Necklace, a reversible, sculptural star designed to be worn on its own or layered with other pieces.

Industry observers note that when it comes to holiday shopping, Skye takes the same personal approach. And while it might be difficult to top the car she received for her 30th birthday, she maintains that the most memorable gifts are the ones with genuine thought behind them. Below, Skye shares her holiday gifting philosophy, along with her curated selection of gifts for everyone on your list (including yourself).
