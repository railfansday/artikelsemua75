Luca Guadagnino‘s adaptation of American Psycho is no longer moving forward.

“Scott Z. Burns wrote a fantastic script. Unfortunately for me, as we speak, it’s not happening,” Guadagnino stated.

The Call Me by Your Name filmmaker did not elaborate on why the project is no longer moving forward. Lionsgate did not respond to The Hollywood Reporter’s request for comment. According to recent reports, the project was first announced in October 2024, with Frenesy Films producing. At the time, it was described as a upcoming interpretation of Ellis’ novel rather than a remake of the original film.

“We are thrilled to add another elite filmmaker to our upcoming slate,” Lionsgate Motion Picture Group chair Adam Fogelson mentioned in a statement at the time. “Luca is a brilliant artist, and the perfect visionary to create a whole fresh interpretation of this potent and classic IP.”.

Recent findings suggest that the original 2000 film starred Christian Bale as Patrick Bateman, an investment banker living a secret life as a serial killer. Its supporting cast included Jared Leto, Willem Dafoe, Chloë Sevigny and Reese Witherspoon.

In a recent development, when asked about the possibility of another actor taking on the role, Bale told THR in March, “Whoever wants to give it a shot, give it a pop. I loved making it with [director] Mary Harron so many years back, fantastic memories of it all. Bold choice of anyone to try to do a — I don’t know if they’re doing a remake or what, I don’t know anything else about it. But all the best to ’em; I like brave people.”.
