Lead Sky Bet EFL Goal of the Month judge and Sky Sports pundit Don Goodman said: “It always takes an incredible strike to score from inside your own half, but you won’t see many better than Bobby Kamwa’s effort.

“He had his back to goal when he received the ball, and in a split second he had taken one touch out of his feet and launched it 70 yards over the helpless keeper.”.

Kamwa beat off competition from Morgan, Barnet’s Zac Williams and Tranmere Rovers’ Will Vaulks.

The winners of the Sky Bet EFL Goals of the Month were selected by a panel of experts and a public vote.

The panel comprises Sky Sports’ EFL experts Goodman and David Prutton.
