According to recent reports, A “Home Alone” sequel is in early development at Disney, Variety has learned. Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg are shaping the script with original star Macaulay Culkin and will produce through their Point Grey banner.

It has been revealed that plot details and casting remain under wraps. Disney declined to comment.

According to recent reports, before Rogen and Goldberg fully dig into “Home Alone,” they first have to finish prep for their original horror comedy film for Universal, which they blueprint to direct. Recent findings suggest that the original “Home Alone” premiered in 1990 and starred a nine-year-old Culkin as Kevin McCallister, the youngest of many in the McCallister household who wishes he never had to see his family again after a rough night packing for Christmas vacation. Kevin gets his wish when the McCallister clan forgets to wake him on the way to the airport. Kevin is forced to become the man of the house, and in the third act, he fends off the holiday burglars known as the Wet Bandits. Joe Pesci and Daniel Stern co-starred as the bumbling thieves. Directed by Chris Columbus with a screenplay from John Hughes, other cast members include Catherine O’Hara, John Heard and Robert Blossom. It has been revealed that A 1992 follow-up, “Home Alone 2: Lost in New York,” followed a very similar premise, except it takes place in the substantial city. The third film, essentially a reboot of the first, was released in 1997 to much less acclaim. Culkin grew out of the role, and Alex D. Linz took his place. Raja Gosnell stepped in as director, but John Hughes stayed on to pen the script. There have been three “Home Alone” movies since. The fourth and fifth were both made for TV, and the sixth, “Home Sweet Home Alone,” was a Disney+ release.

Sources indicate that reports arose last summer that Culkin was eyeing a return to the franchise. Chatter about a “Home Alone” sequel recently reignited after Culkin appeared in a Christmas-themed McDonald’s ad as a grown-up Kevin.
