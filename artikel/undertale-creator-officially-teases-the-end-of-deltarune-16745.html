📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It's been almost a decade since Toby Fox released the first chapter of Deltarune, the celebrated indie developer's follow-up to Undertale. Now the end of the story is in sight. In a recent newsletter enhancement, Fox revealed that his team is making good progress on the game's final two chapters.

Fox opened the Fall 2026 Deltarune newsletter by sharing that Chapter 6 is now "structurally complete" and can be played from beginning to end. He added that "some key scenes and details are still undercooked in their presentation" but that preliminary testing and localization would begin soon. Fox also revealed that Chapter 6 will be shorter than past chapters, but that he's still taking his time with development to make it as good as possible. Sources indicate that chapter 5 was released in June, and Fox shared that Chapter 6 is expected to release in 2027.​​​​​​​ The newsletter included a short scene for the upcoming chapter featuring the character Queen​​​​​​​. Even though Chapter 6 isn't out yet, some members of the Deltarune team are shifting their work to the game's ending​​​​​​​.​​​​​​​. (Related: British Airways Avios — Short Nonstop Domestic Flights...)

In a recent development, work on Chapter 7 has just begun​​​​​​​, and Fox is teasing his team with the plot of the finale​​​​​​​. "I read out the events and dialogue of Chapter 7 to the team," he wrote in the newsletter. "However, just when the story reached a critical moment, I stopped reading… I told my team they need to complete more of the game before I tell them what happens.".

Fox said he's learned a lot from the game's long development, admitting that "unlike every other chapter​​​​​​​, I actually feel like I know what I'm doing this time." Fox has also stated that Deltarune, unlike Undertale, will not have multiple endings.
