It has been revealed that anthony Mackie has spent decades building an impressive Hollywood career, moving between blockbuster franchises, action movies, and acclaimed television projects. From playing Sam Wilson in the Marvel Cinematic Universe to starring in The Hurt Locker and 8 Mile, Mackie has become a familiar face to audiences across several genres.

It has been revealed that now, the actor is adding another streaming success to his filmography. His latest project, a latest thriller that takes a high-stakes premise and puts Mackie front and center, has wasted little time attracting attention on Apple TV. Sources indicate that the eight-episode series 12 12 12 premiered on Apple TV on Nov. 13, and it took just three days to reach No. 1 on the platform's worldwide streaming chart, according to FlixPatrol. The crime thriller has rapidly established itself as one of the service's most-watched newly introduced releases, beating out several established Apple TV favorites in the process.

12 12 12 follows a disgraced FBI agent named Jack Strickland (Mackie) and an American career criminal named Paul Galvin (Jamie Dornan), who become entangled in a dangerous game of cat and mouse across Europe.

Recent findings suggest that at the center of the story is an ambitious attempt to rob a bank vault deep beneath the streets of Zurich. However, the series takes an unusual approach to telling its heist story, with each episode unfolding across three different timelines: the 12 months of planning, the 12 hours of the robbery itself, and the 12 days that follow. Recent findings suggest that despite its strong streaming debut, 12 12 12 has received a mixed response from critics and audiences so far. The series currently holds a 67% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, alongside a 47% audience score. Critics have praised the chemistry between Anthony Mackie and Jamie Dornan, as well as the show's ambitious three-timeline storytelling, but some have criticized its convoluted structure and familiar heist-thriller tropes.
