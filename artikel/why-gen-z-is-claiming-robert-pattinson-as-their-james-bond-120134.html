According to recent reports, for the first time in over 20 years, we have a latest James Bond, and it’s Robert Pattinson! The announcement was made after a long casting process involving Bond 26's director, Denis Villeneuve, the franchise’s new producers and Amazon MGM Studios, and all the reactions to Pattinson earning the role have been so entertaining to see. It’s ranged from the Royal Family releasing a statement to tons of jokes about him being in everything. But have you seen all the people who are calling him Gen Z’s James Bond?

Now, Robert Pattinson would have to be between 14 and 29 years old to be considered part of Gen Z. The guy looks great, but he’s 40 years old, making him very much a Millennial. So, is Gen Z claiming him as their own with comments like the one below?

Recent findings suggest that this is very much an “if you know, you know” situation. If you’ve been online for all the James Bond reactions after he was reportedly paid to audition for the role, you might have seen one of these Threads about Pattinson:.

So, why is Gen Z claiming Robert Pattinson as Bond? Let’s get to the bottom of this.

Recent findings suggest that this actually all stems from a single viral moment that happened between Robert Pattinson and Jennifer Lawrence when they were promoting their film Die My Love about a year ago. While doing a lie detector test with Vanity Fair, Lawrence called Pattinson out for cold-calling her once. When she noted odd behavior, he said it’s “Gen Z”. He then doubled down by declaring he’s Gen Z and he would not let go of the title. Check it out below:.
