Recent findings suggest that we have six apps of our own on the Solana dApp Store. Most of them started as web apps. Wrapping a web app into an Android app is now the easy part: Solana Mobile ships a free tool that does it in one command. What takes the time is everything that behaves differently once your site runs inside an app on a real Seeker. This post is the list we wish we'd had.

Solana Mobile's CLI builds a WebView shell around your site and signs an APK:.

init reads your web manifest for the name and icons and creates a signing keystore if you don't have one. build produces app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk. Solana Mobile now recommends this WebView shell over a Trusted Web Activity (the older Bubblewrap route): the shell needs no Digital Asset Links setup, and it handles Solana wallet requests natively, while browser network restrictions can break wallet connections inside a TWA. If your app uses Mobile Wallet Adapter through @solana-mobile/wallet-standard-mobile, update it to 0.5.1 or later so it detects the shell.

So far, so quick. We tried it on a live dApp that offers Google sign-in, and on a Seeker it loaded perfectly. Then we tapped "Continue with Google". (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

If your web app only offers Google and e-mail, test this first. It's the difference between a working app and one that loses every Google user at the door.

The same rule applies to window.open and target="_blank". Pay-with-card widgets, on-ramps, "share on X" buttons and help-desk chats often open that way. Some of that is fine (a share link should open X). Some isn't: a payment window that completes in Chrome leaves your app not knowing it happened. Go through every flow that opens something and decide: in the app, or out with a way back.
