Ian Wright has urged Arsenal to sign RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa in the January transfer window.

The Gunners legend believes his former team must strengthen their attacking options despite remaining six points clear at the top of the Premier League. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

In a recent development, arsenal were held to a 2-2 draw by Newcastle United on Saturday, with their attacking depth exposed during the second half. Eberechi Eze scored, while Noni Madueke impressed in the absence of injured Bukayo Saka, but the visitors struggled to make an impact from the bench. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Christos Tzolis was introduced in the second half but failed to impress, while Viktor Gyokeres again struggled after coming on.

Arsenal tried and failed to sign Julian Alvarez and Vinicius Jr during the summer, leaving Tzolis as their only attacking addition, with Gabriel Martinelli and Leonardo Trossard departing.

Sources indicate that the Gunners are understood to be interested in Nusa, who has enjoyed an impressive campaign with Leipzig, registering seven goals and eight assists in 16 appearances. The Norway international operates primarily on the left wing, with the German club reportedly demanding €80million (£67m) for his services.

Wright believes Arsenal need to strengthen on the left and in attack, while also suggesting increased competition could help Kai Havertz improve.

‘I’d want to go [and strengthen] on the left side and up front,” he said on Sky Sports.

“At some stage we have got to say ‘we have got to go for it’.

“We have been linked with Nusa at RB Leipzig. If think we should just go and get him.

“We saw today with Tzolis, I didn’t see enough.

“I think with [Kai] Havertz, if he’s got someone at the same level or better and push him I think we get more out of him which he needs, and I don’t think that’s happening through Gyokeres.”.
