According to recent reports, A post claiming XRP is already valued at $1,000 outside public markets has drawn widespread mockery from the crypto community on X. The post, shared by a popular XRP account on Friday and viewed nearly 76,000 times, offered no evidence and asked Grok, the AI chatbot built into X, to verify it. There is no data supporting the claim. XRP trades openly on exchanges at about $0.90, and large over-the-counter deals typically follow market prices. Replies were largely critical. An XRP Ledger validator called the post bad for the community, while other users accused the account of being a paid promoter and of deleting Grok’s reply. Some responded with humour, with one user saying a relative claimed bottled water also sells for $1,000 in private. Another joked that the secret market forgot to send him his share. The episode comes as XRP holders grow weary of extreme price claims after the token fell below $1.
