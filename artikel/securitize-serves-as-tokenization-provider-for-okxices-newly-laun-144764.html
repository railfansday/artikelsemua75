Regulated tokenization infrastructure supports newly introduced venue for trading securities onchain, with 113 assets confirmed for phased rollout.

Industry observers note that MIAMI, Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Securitize Corp. (NYSE: SECZ), the leading platform for tokenized assets, today announced that it is serving as the tokenization provider for the newly released OKXICE Tokenized Securities Venue (TSV), bringing its regulated securities infrastructure to a newly introduced marketplace for 24/7 trading of tokenized U.S. securities. OKXICE, a joint venture between OKX and Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), parent company of the Latest York Stock Exchange, launched its TSV today with 113 assets confirmed for a phased rollout, including stocks, exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and exchange-traded products (ETPs). Confirmed issuers and asset managers include Better.com, BitGo, Circle, Coinbase, Direxion, eToro, Fidelity Investments, Robinhood, Securitize, Strategy, Strive and Vida Global. Additional issuers and assets will be revealed in the future.

Sources indicate that as the venue's tokenization provider, Securitize brings its regulated securities infrastructure to support onchain trading. Each token represents a security entitlement backed one-to-one by an underlying share, with applicable shareholder rights and economic benefits, including dividends, voting rights and corporate actions.

Industry observers note that "Tokenization is moving beyond the issuance of securities onchain and into the infrastructure of how capital markets operate," noted Carlos Domingo, Chairman and CEO of Securitize. "The launch of OKXICE TSV is another vital step toward markets that can operate around the clock while preserving the rights and protections investors expect. Securitize is helping build the infrastructure that connects traditional securities with this next generation of capital markets.". Trading on OKXICE TSV occurs through automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools on Uniswap v4, built on X Layer, OKX's Ethereum Layer-2 network. Smart contracts limit trading and liquidity provision to eligible wallets. OKXICE TSV does not hold customer assets or private keys.

OKXICE TSV operates pursuant to the SEC's Innovation Exemption. Access is subject to eligibility, onboarding and geographic requirements. Participants must complete KYC, activate a CeDeFi Wallet and complete OKXICE onboarding. The venue is right now available to eligible U.S.-based OKX Inc. customers only.
