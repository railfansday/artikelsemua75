The Social Security Administration follows a strict payment schedule in which most people receive their checks on the second, third, or fourth Wednesday of the month, depending on their birthday. The later your birthday falls in the month, the later your checks arrive.

According to recent reports, but there are a couple of groups who don't follow this rule. Seniors who began claiming Social Security prior to May 1997 and those on Supplemental Security Income (SSI) usually receive their Social Security checks on the third of the month, regardless of their birthday.

Industry observers note that in most months, this isn't an issue, but sometimes the third falls on a weekend. In that case, payments go out on the last business day before the third. And that's what's happening with the first checks that include the 2027 Social Security COLA.

Recent findings suggest that jan. 3, 2027, is a Sunday, so it's not an eligible payment day. The weekday before that is Jan. 1, 2027, which is a federal holiday, so payments won't go out then either. Instead, older beneficiaries and those on SSI will get their first Social Security checks with the COLA on Dec. 31, 2026.

Recent findings suggest that it's not much earlier, but it could be a big help to some seniors, especially those struggling with holiday costs. Just bear in mind that, if you fall into this group, you won't get another Social Security check until Feb. 3, 2027, so make sure you're budgeting carefully to stretch your check until then.
