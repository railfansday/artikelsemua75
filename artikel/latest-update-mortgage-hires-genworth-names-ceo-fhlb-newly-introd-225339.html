Thomas J. McInerney, president and CEO of Genworth Financial, has stepped down from his roles at the company and its board of directors to focus on his health, the company confirmed. The board appointed Jerome T. Upton, its current chief financial officer, as president, CEO and director. Upton will remain CFO through Dec. 31, with Angela Simmons expected to assume the role on Jan. 1, 2027.

Fannie Mae appointed David Shafer to its board of directors effective Nov. 23. His term will end at the next annual board election, or if he resigns or is removed by the Federal Housing Finance Agency. His committee assignments have not yet been determined.

It has been revealed that the manufacturer also entered into retention agreements with four executives totaling $4.75 million. Chief financial officer Chryssa Halley and chief investment officer Erik Bisso will each receive $1.5 million, multifamily executive vice president Kelly Follain will receive $1 million, and chief risk officer Anthony Moon will receive $750,000.

The awards will be paid in two equal installments, in December 2026 and December 2027, provided the executives remain employed and comply with the agreements, according to filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission.

According to recent reports, the Federal Home Loan Bank of New York elected Charles E. Kilbourne, managing director of the Financial Services Volunteer Corps and an independent director since 2019, as board chair for a two-year term beginning Jan. 1, 2027. Kilbourne, who chairs the board’s housing committee, will succeed Chris Martin, who is retiring at the end of this year after more than a decade of service.
