In a recent development, hugo Ekitiké sees the end of the tunnel. After a visit to Clairefontaine in September to continue his treatment and a trip to the United States to consult a specialist, the French international, victim of a terrible rupture of the Achilles tendon last April, is preparing his return to training at Liverpool. The 24-year-old striker will not return to competition in the coming weeks. The Reds medical staff will give him time to play again at the start of 2027.

Recent findings suggest that “The strategy has always been January and that hasn’t changed. If everything goes incredibly well, he’ll be able to plan earlier, which would be much better for everyone. But for now, we’re staying focused on January and that’s the plan.”explained Andoni Iraola, as reported on the club website. The Basque coach hopes to count on Ekitiké for the last two Champions League matches against Inter and RC Lens, on January 19 and 27.

Industry observers note that the former PSG player will try to catch fire again on the Liverpool side. Recruited for 91 million euros (bonus included) last year, he had a first season in England with 17 goals and 6 assists in 45 matches in all competitions. He would have easily had his place in the France team squad for the 2026 World Cup without his serious injury. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

The French international will also challenge Leny Yoro, aligned on the right side with the Red Devils for the first of Mauricio Pochettino.
