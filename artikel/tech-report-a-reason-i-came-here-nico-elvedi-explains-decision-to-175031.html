Following persistent speculation, the pair were reunited at Elland Road in the most recent summer transfer window.

Elvedi has slotted seamlessly into the Leeds defence, most recently pairing up with Tarik Muharemović and James Justin.

Industry observers note that opening up on his decision to link up with the Whites on The Official Leeds United Podcast, Elvedi mentioned: “Years ago, Farke was my old coach in Gladbach. It was also a reason I came here.

“I talked to him before the transfer. I like him as a coach and as person. He’s a substantial reason I came here. I just love the club. It’s a lot of tradition and they are starting to build something and I’m just happy to be here.”.

When asked about Farke’s style and whether it had changed, Elvedi said: “I think it’s similar to Gladbach. Now you have better players and everything, it’s more successful, his style of play, in the Premier League.

“In Germany, it was a little bit difficult for him in Gladbach, just one year there. But here, he did a really good job.”.

An experienced centre-back, Elvedi boasts 79 Switzerland caps as well as a wealth of Bundesliga experience and believes his time spent on the international scene has given him the confidence to perform in the Premier League.

In a recent development, he stated: “It helps me a lot. Also, the World Cup that I played, I’ve seen that I can play on this level and also when I move to Leeds, I know I can play in a Premier League club and can play against the biggest clubs and biggest players in the world. It’s always fun to play and I love it here in the Premier League.”.
