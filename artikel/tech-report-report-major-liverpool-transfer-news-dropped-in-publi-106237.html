Recent findings suggest that at the very least, any newly introduced left-back would need to rival Kerkez for a place. Balde is someone who could do that and he's thought to be available.

But it might not be the case. If Barcelona's Football Coordinator Bojan is to be believed, it's definitely not the case.

“We are not planning for Balde to leave in January," he told TotCosta, as relayed by Fabrizio Romano.

In a recent development, “He’s not playing, that’s not easy and we understand that. But in football there are different moments. He’s always training well.".

Sources indicate that that would essentially end the chances of Liverpool landing their presumed no.1 target. We're not even sure what the plan would be from there but we'd guess that the Reds simply wouldn't sign a left-back this winter and then revisit the situation next summer.
