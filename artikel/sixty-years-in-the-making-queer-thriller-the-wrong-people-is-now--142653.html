EXCLUSIVE: Sixty-years in the making, post-production is nearing completion on feature thriller The Wrong People, an adaptation of the 1967 novel of the same name by Robin Maugham, a dark thriller exposing trafficking in North Africa in the sixties.

In a recent development, first announced as a feature film in 1972, the project was shot in-and-around London and Essaouira, Morocco, in early 2026, with cast including Luke Newberry (Anna Karenina), Caroline Goodall (Schindler’s List), Alec Newman (The Weight), Calum Ross (Wednesday), Branka Katic (The King’s Man) and Joe Anderson (Across the Universe). According to recent reports, steven Fierberg, the veteran cinematographer of movies including Secretary and Love & Other Drugs and hit series including The Affair and Emily In Paris, has directed the film from a script written by David Griffin. Pic is being produced by Stephen Israel (Swimming with Sharks) via his production firm School Pictures, alongside Ioanna Karavela (Bad Day at the Office). Executive producers are Brett Husbands and Tom Abell of LGBTQ specialist Peccadillo, which is handling UK & Irish distribution rights. Watch on Deadline.

In a recent development, the Wrong People “follows Arnold Turner (Newberry), a gay boarding-school teacher in England who travels to Tangier in search of respite from repression in the UK, where homosexuality remains illegal. There he befriends expat Lavinia Morris (Goodall) and falls in with the wealthy Clarence Baird (Newman), only to find himself blackmailed in the city’s darker corners into trafficking one of his students, Dan (Ross), to Morocco, a plan that has devastating consequences.”. Maugham’s first explicitly gay-themed novel garnered controversy at the time of its publication given that homosexuality was still illegal in Britain for most of the 1960s.

Originally published under a pseudonym after Somerset Maugham advised his nephew Robin Maugham “they will kill you”, the well-received novel was championed by Oscar nominee Sal Mineo (Rebel Without a Cause), who spent many years trying to get the project financed before his untimely death in 1976. More recently, we wrote about the project six years ago as having a script by veteran UK screenwriter David McGillivray. The producers of this version of the movie acquired the rights from McGillivray.

War veteran and politician Maugham (the nephew of popular British playwright and novelist Somerset Maugham) is probably best known in film and literary circles for his novella The Servant which was the basis for the 1963 Jospeh Losey film of the same name with Dirk Bogarde, James Fox and Sarah Miles. He died in 1981.

“The team has worked for many years to make this project a reality,” noted Israel who added, “we are excited to have finally achieved what Sal had hoped for. We dedicate this film to him.”.

Sources indicate that “Steven and I have long wanted to work together,” he noted. “I’m grateful we get to work on such an important addition to the canon of queer cinema.”.

“I feel incredibly fortunate to have Alec Newman, Luke Newberry, Caroline Goodall and the wonderfully gifted Calum Ross joining us,” said Fierberg. “It’s truly a dream cast. The story examines the depravity of human nature which some will exploit, and the corrosive effects of criminalizing sexual orientation.”.

Cinematographer is Alexandre Jamin (La Plain Verte), casting director Anna Kennedy (Nuremberg), production designer Sabrina Linder (I Swear) and costume designer Lily Faith Knight (The Gatehouse).

Recent findings suggest that abell added: “Robin Maugham used his place in the House of Lords to expose human trafficking when few wanted to listen, and Sal Mineo set out to film this story decades ago. We at Peccadillo admire the filmmakers’ bravery in at last bringing it to the screen, and are delighted to be distributing The Wrong People.”.
