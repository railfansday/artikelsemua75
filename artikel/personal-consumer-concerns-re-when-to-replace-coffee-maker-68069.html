The replaceable parts are also nice, so if something gets gross and caked in old coffee oils you can just replace it. OP said the coffee was cold, which might be a problem at the brewing stage, such as a filter clogged with fines.It doesn't feel as hot when you pour it out - at least compared to getting drip coffee from a coffee shop or using one of the commercial level machines.
