12th day of Serie A and Lazio still does not know defeat. Gennaro Gattuso’s men even crushed Lecce (3-0) and put pressure on Inter and Roma, who play later in the weekend. Thanks to Noslin’s double and a goal from Frattesi, the Romans took a four-point lead over the Nerazzurri and five over La Louve.

It has been revealed that como is just behind, in 4th place, after its home victory over Cagliari this Saturday afternoon. Cesc Fabregas’ club did not tremble with goals from Jacobo Ramon and captain Lucas Da Cunha. He is still a little point behind on the podium.

Lazio 3 – 0 Lecce: Noslin (25th, 68th), Frattesi (90th).

Como 2 – 0 Cagliari: Ramon (24th), Da Cunha (56th).
