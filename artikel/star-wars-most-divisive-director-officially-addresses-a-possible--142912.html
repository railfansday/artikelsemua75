📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that there's no question that 2017's Star Wars: The Last Jedi is the most controversial of the franchise's many films. Depending on who you ask, it is either the very best or very worst Star Wars has to offer. That has made director Rian Johnson (also known for the Knives Out movies) something of a hot topic in the fandom. Nearly a decade after The Last Jedi, Johnson has now spoken out about how he feels about Star Wars and whether he — like Palpatine — would ever return to the series.

During a talk at the Marrakech Film Festival (as reported by Variety), Johnson told the audience that, despite all the controversy surrounding The Last Jedi, he loves the series "more now than I ever have." Given his love for the franchise​​​​​​​, the director said​​​​​​​ that he would be happy to return "if the opportunity presented itself." For now, though, he's hard at work on his next project.​​​​​​​ He teased that the mystery project is not Star Wars nor is it a Knives Out film.

Recent findings suggest that johnson also pointed out that there isn't really a need for him to return to the series at the moment, given how many other projects are in the works. "It's exciting to me that there are a lot of different voices now, taking cracks at it." ​​​​​​​Johnson seems to have hoped that filmmakers would be free to interpret the universe and tell unique stories after The Last Jedi​​​​​​​, a film very much about moving on from the established types of stories Star Wars traditionally tells. While J.J. Abrams' Rise of Skywalker walked a lot of that back, the future of Star Wars may actually live up to what Johnson started with The Last Jedi, after all.​​​​​​​. In a recent development, on the film side of things, Star Wars fans will be able to experience a upcoming cinematic adventure in theaters next year, when Starfighter is released​​​​​​​. The film stars Ryan Gosling alongside Matt Smith (Doctor Who), Mia Goth, and Aaron Pierre (Lanterns), among others. Shawn Levy​​​​​​​ is directing. Ms. Marvel director Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy is also slated to direct a Star Wars film that takes place 15 years after Rise of Skywalker and is rumored to begin a new era following the New Jedi Order. Other untitled movies in the works are being developed by​​​​​​​ Dave Filoni, James Mangold, Simon Kinberg, and Taika Waititi.

On the small screen, Ahsoka season 2​​​​​​​ will be released on Jan. 20, 2027. Maul: Shadow Lord is set to get a second season sometime next year. Donald Glover's​​​​​​​ Lando project (which could be a movie or a TV series) is also allegedly still alive but has been perpetually in limbo. As next year is the 50th anniversary of the first Star Wars film, Disney is likely to celebrate with plenty of new announcements that could make the slate of upcoming movies and series clearer​​​​​​​.
