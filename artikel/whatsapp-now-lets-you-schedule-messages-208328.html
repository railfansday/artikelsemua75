Ever had something to say, but later? WhatsApp now lets you schedule messages that will be automatically sent at a designated time.

Industry observers note that you can line up messages to be delivered anytime from 10 minutes to 14 days later, either to your one-on-one or group chats. Scheduled messages can have text, photos, and videos. (Related: Industry News: Train a Sentence Embedding Model with 1B...)

The feature has been one of the most requested features on WhatsApp, the organization stated in a blog post.

To schedule a message in WhatsApp, press and hold the send button, then pick the time and date for the message to arrive. Key: The receiving party won't know that it's a scheduled message, and they'll be delighted that you remembered their birthday even though you merely used an AI agent to schedule everyone's birthday messages automatically (we kid, we would never).

Just like normal messages, the scheduled messages will be end-to-end encrypted. Also, WhatsApp points out that they'll be sent even if your phone is off or out of battery.

According to WhatsApp, scheduled messages are rolling out globally "over the coming weeks.".
