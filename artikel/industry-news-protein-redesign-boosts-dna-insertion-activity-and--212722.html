Industry News: Protein redesign boosts DNA insertion activity and reveals how molecular machinery selects targets

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (95 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that genome engineering is exciting, given its vast potential to treat and even cure diseases, but it is still a largely uncharted biomedical innovation. Scientists at St. Jude Children's Research Hospital recently uncovered the mechanisms involved in precisely integrating DNA into a target genomic location using CRISPR-associated transposases (CASTs). By studying type I-F3 CAST, the researchers unveiled structural findings that explain how this CAST family achieves successful DNA integration at precise target sites and offer insights for future genomic engineering approaches. The results were published in Nature.
