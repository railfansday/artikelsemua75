A rare cabinet photograph of the Newly introduced York Giants, one of early professional baseball’s most significant teams, taken by renowned baseball photographer Joseph Hall in 1889, is up for auction.

One of Major League Baseball’s oldest franchises, the Newly introduced York Giants rose to prominence in the late 19th century, winning back-to-back championships in 1888 and 1889, before the modern World Series was established in 1903. The team boasted several future Baseball Hall of Famers during this era. Its legacy continues today as the San Francisco Giants, following the franchise’s relocation from New York to California in 1958. (Related: Scaling up BERT-like model Inference on modern CPU - P...)

The photograph, which is being offered by auction house Huggins & Scott as part of a sale which runs through December 3, features the Latest York Giants team that won the 1888 World Championship. The auction house claims it has never handled another example of this particular photograph in its imperial cabinet format, making it one of the rarest of Joseph Hall’s baseball team photographs.

According to recent reports, hall photographed many of the Major League baseball teams in 1888 and was widely regarded as the leading baseball photographer of the late 1880s. Hall’s cabinet photographs of professional baseball teams and players are among the most sought-after photographic collectibles of the late 19th century, with his images of teams posing in uniform at ballparks commanding the highest prices. In a recent development, while a smaller cabinet card featuring the same image of the Giants appeared at auction several years ago, Huggins & Scott says it cannot recall seeing another example of this photograph in this larger format. According to the auction house, the size and quality of the print are particularly noteworthy given the limitations of photographic production in the late 19th century. Producing photographs of this scale and quality required a considerable investment of time and resources, and the auction house suggests that only a small number of prints may have been made.

Unlike other photographs Hall took of the Giants that feature an outdoor stadium background, the image highlights 19 members of the team posing together in a studio. Among those pictured are six future Baseball Hall of Famers: Tim Keefe, Roger Connor, Buck Ewing, Jim O’Rourke, John Montgomery Ward, and Mickey Welch.

The photograph is up for sale in the Fall Auction at Huggins & Scott Auctions, which runs through December 3.
