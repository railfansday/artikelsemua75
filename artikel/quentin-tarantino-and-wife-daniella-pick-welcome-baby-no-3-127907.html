Once upon a time… Quentin Tarantino became a father of three.

Recent findings suggest that four years after the birth of their second child, the Oscar winner and his wife Daniella Pick Tarantino welcomed their third baby, Quentin’s rep confirmed to People Nov. 22.

“Quentin Tarantino and his wife, Daniella Pick Tarantino, are overjoyed to announce the birth of their third child,” the rep said in a statement to the outlet, “a baby boy.”.

The couple—who tied the knot in 2018—also share son Leo, 6, and daughter Ariana, 4.

And while Quentin, 63, has kept his family life rather private, he has described fatherhood as an emotional experience. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Industry observers note that “I can’t even see his name written on a piece of paper without crying,” the Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood filmmaker told CBS This Morning in July 2021, referring to Leo, whom he praised as the “most charming human being I’ve ever met in my life.”. (Related: Industry News: Diffusers welcomes Stable Diffusion 3...)

Sources indicate that “Half the time, I look at him and I’m just laughing because he’s so funny,” he continued. “And the other time, I’m just bursting into tears.”.

And though Quentin’s first born shares a first name with one of his frequent collaborators Leonardo DiCaprio, he and his wife didn’t choose the moniker because of the Titanic star.

“We almost didn't name him [Leo] because people would assume that I named him after Leonardo DiCaprio,” Quentin shared during a June 2021 appearance on Jimmy Kimmel Live. “There's nothing wrong with that but I didn't.”.

As for how the couple picked the name?

“He's named after my wife's grandfather,” Quentin remarked, “but also because, just in our hearts, he was our little lion.”.

And for the Pulp Fiction director—who splits his time between Los Angeles and his wife’s native Tel Avi, Israel—fatherhood has been his top priority.

After all, Quentin revealed last year that he was delaying his tenth and final film to focus on his family.

It has been revealed that “The idea of jumping on a voyage when they’re too young to understand it is not enticing to me," the Hateful Eight filmmaker noted of his kids at the 2025 Sundance Film Festival, per People. “I want to not do whatever movie I end up doing until my son is at least 6. That way he’ll know what’s going on, he’ll be there, and it will be a memory for the rest of his life.”.
