Lincoln City consider move for member of James Morrison’s West Brom staff in new boss search

Lincoln City are on the lookout for a new head coach after they made the decision to part ways with Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw earlier this week. The Imps were thrashed 4-0 by Charlton Athletic on Tuesday night, and have now lost five Championship games on the bounce.

The search for a new leader is underway, and according to Alan Nixon, one man in the frame is Gavin Strachan. He at the moment serves as a first-team coach at West Brom, linking up with James Morrison’s staff in the summer following six years under various bosses at Scottish giants Celtic. Industry observers note that nixon states Strachan was first eyed by Lincoln City a couple of years ago but decided to stay with Celtic. He is happy in his current post at The Hawthorns, but he could be more tempted to take on a big job at Sincil Bank. (Related: Industry News: Apriel-H1: The Surprising Key to Distill...)

Strachan, son of Scotland and Celtic legend Gordon Strachan, has built a reputation in the coaching world. After retiring in 2011, he took up a role in the academy of former club Peterborough United, first serving as boss of their U18s.

He would then step into Darren Ferguson’s first-team staff as assistant manager, and he ended up following him to Doncaster Rovers and back to Peterborough United again in January 2019. When the opportunity to join the Celtic staff arose though, Strachan couldn’t say no.

It is in Glasgow that Strachan will have learned from some really established coaches. He was part of the backroom teams of Brendan Rodgers, Ange Postecoglou, Neil Lennon, Martin O’Neill and Wilfried Nancy, and he’s now at a West Brom side who are lauded for just how well-drilled they are under Morrison and co.

In a recent development, strachan’s sole first-team managerial role came at Ilkeston Town in 2015. That lack of experience may worry some Lincoln City fans, but his coaching experience could make him an exciting option.

It has been revealed that strachan’s lack of experience as a head coach or manager will concern some of the Sincil Bank faithful. Lincoln City are in a Championship relegation fight, and an experienced leader could be best for their current predicament.

However, the Imps have not been afraid to roll the dice with appointments in recent times.

Mark Kennedy had mainly worked as an assistant manager or in academy football when he was given the reins, and they gave Michael Skubala his first full-time senior head coach role as well. Then, Cohen and Shaw were promoted in a rare move to name joint bosses.

Appointing someone with strong coaching pedigree but limited experience of being the lead man is a theme at Lincoln City, and swooping for West Brom man Strachan would continue it.
