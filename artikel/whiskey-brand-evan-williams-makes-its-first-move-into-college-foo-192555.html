Kentucky-based whiskey brand Evan Williams Bourbon has become the first sponsor in the distilled spirits category to secure a sponsorship position across Disney-owned ACC Network’s college football live games or studio broadcasts.

This comes as Evan Williams and its ad agency, Method1, have partnered with the ACC and Major Ten as part of a broader national college football media plan.

According to recent reports, in a statement, a Disney spokesperson said Evan Williams is at the moment the only advertiser in this category with a presence on the ACC Network. The spokesperson added that while this could change as the season progresses, the deal does not include an official ACC Network designation, but “it further reinforces Evan Williams’ leadership position within the category and effectively cements the brand’s presence within the ACC Network college football ecosystem.”.
