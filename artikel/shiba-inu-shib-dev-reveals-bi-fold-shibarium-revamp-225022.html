Shiba Inu’s chain developer unravels a multi-layered patch: privacy-enabled transactions arrive with a new explorer.

Shiba Inu’s (SHIB) developer team has prepared upcoming perks for the vibrant Shiba Inu holder community, otherwise known as SHIB Army. Mazrael said the team had completed the necessary groundwork for the implementation on chain 109.

Shiba Inu’s dev revealed the Layer-2 chain’s compatibility with Oxbow’s Privacy Pools design coming up with a upcoming security patch. Privacy Pools, co-authored by Ethereum’s Founder Vitalik Buterin, let Shibarium customers transact privately while screening out stolen or illicit funds from the ecosystem.

It has been revealed that the upgrade includes Cancun opcodes with BN254 pairing for the Zero-Knowledge (ZK) proofs. Alongside, the developer’s X post offers collaboration on gas, testing & integration so ShibWallet can support it from launch, with a linked GitHub problem detailing the proposal.

Sources indicate that shibWallet plans to integrate this feature from day one. “Shibarium is ready either way, and ShibWallet already runs it on testnet”, Mazrael declared on X. The privacy upgrade boost is set to roll out with Bone ShibaSwap (BONE) first, adding SHIB along the way the possibility of other tokens, a mechanism powered by the Kohaku SDK.
