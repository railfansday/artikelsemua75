Brandon Armstrong is doing just fine after being accidentally hit by Ezra Frech’s airborne prosthetic leg.

In a Wednesday, November 25, TikTok video posted by his wife, Brylee Armstrong, the Dancing With the Stars pro gave fans a glimpse of a cut on his calf where Frech’s prosthetic injured him during the Paralympian’s freestyle on the season 35 finale the day before. Brandon, 32, was one of the backing dancers during Frech’s freestyle performance with professional partner Daniella Karagach. The routine got off to a rocky start when Frech’s prosthetic leg came off as he jumped onto the stage, but he managed to perform the whole dance and received 10s from all the judges. Recent findings suggest that “First of all, fine. Just happy that he’s fine,” Brandon said of Frech, who won the latest season with Karagach.

He continued, “When he jumped off, his leg was not on properly or all the way, so I took a freaking shot.”.

“It’s actually not too bad. It split me, but it’s not too bad,” Brandon said, showing his injury.

According to recent reports, the DWTS pro added, “I’m just happy that he is fine. That dance was unbelievable. Leg didn’t get on right, but it got on right the second time, and he killed it, so everything’s good.”.

Judge Derek Hough praised Frech’s expert recovery following the mishap during his freestyle on the Tuesday, November 24, season finale.

In a recent development, “Ezra, you are a hero. This performance will have a ripple effect far beyond this show. What you did tonight … for forever, kids will look up to you and will see what’s possible. The fact that the worst case scenario might have happened and you wouldn’t even have known, the way you rose in that moment,” Hough, 41, noted. “Ezra, you are incredible, man!”.

In a recent development, “What we witnessed tonight goes beyond dancing. This is a powerful, beautiful, life-affirming statement that will inspire the nation and the world, my darling,” fellow judge Bruno Tonioli added.

Carrie Ann Inaba said, “You have been the most amazing ambassador for people with disabilities, but also, now you’re an ambassador for dance.”.

The following day on Good Morning America, Frech said he “blacked out” during his freestyle routine.

“Last night was crazy. … I can’t believe this happened,” Frech, 21, shared. “I can’t believe we got that opportunity to represent [the disabled community].”.

“We started our freestyle without the prosthetic, that was a creative choice, and then things went a little off script,” Frech said on GMA. “I wasn’t able to suction my leg on and I knew it wasn’t on. Sometimes when that happens, it’ll re-suction, but it didn’t. It came off. She told me to put it on mid-dance, I kinda blacked out.”.
