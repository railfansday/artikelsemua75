Former Manchester City defender and assistant coach Kolo Toure indicates that Kevin De Bruyne never wanted to leave the Premier League side and says that he will want to show the fans that he is still at the top of his game when he returns to the Etihad Stadium on Tuesday night, and admits that Scott McTominay’s form in Serie A has been a surprise.

The Ivorian, who served as an assistant to Pep Guardiola at City last season and was also part of Roberto Mancini’s Premier League winning squad in 2011-12, spoke to La Gazzetta dello Sport ahead of the meeting between Napoli and Manchester City in the Champions League on Tuesday night. Napoli head to the Etihad under pressure in the Champions League with just three points to their name from their first four matches of the competition in 2026-27, leaving them at risk of missing out on qualification for the knock-out rounds. There will be returns to Manchester for a number of Napoli players, including Man City legend De Bruyne, as well as former Manchester United stars McTominay and Rasmus Hojlund, all expected to start on the night.

It has been revealed that asked how he sees Tuesday night’s game going, Toure told La Gazzetta dello Sport: “City will try to control the game and crush Napoli, and I think Allegri will set up with a compact team, careful in defence but ready to explode on the counter-attack, which could be dangerous. City will have to avoid those.”.

It has been revealed that asked what City can do to prevent being hit on the counter, Toure said: “Two ways. The first is to start by pressing opponents immediately after losing the ball. The second is to have players fast enough on the pitch to get back into defensive positions once the counter-attack starts.”.

Industry observers note that toure was asked for his thoughts on De Bruyne. The Belgian superstar is into the final year of his contract with Napoli and there have been reports that he could look to leave as early as January.

It has been revealed that “Kevin was one of the best players in the history of the Premier League,” noted Toure. “He is an incredible player, but more than anything, a great person. For me, what counts in football is what you do on the pitch, but what you do off it is also extremely notable. Kevin is a champion in that respect.

According to recent reports, “I know he didn’t want to leave City. I think he will want to show that he should have stated. I’m sure he wants to make a difference for Napoli, but also to show the fans who still love him that he’s still the top player he was at City.”.

Toure admitted that it has been a ‘surprise’ to see quite how much McTominay has kicked on since moving to Serie A.

“Honestly, yes [it has been a surprise]. But I listened to a podcast that he was on not too long ago and it became clear to me that he is a fighter, and that he’s very strong and intelligent.

Industry observers note that “He’s always been good physically, but he didn’t show enough of his technical qualities in the Premier League. He’s pulled them out in Italy and has also added the finishing touches in the final third. For me, he will be Napoli’s key player.”.

Industry observers note that asked for his predictions for Man City’s player of the match, Toure replied: “Enzo Fernandez. He’s in great form and is making the difference in midfield. Just like Haaland in attack.”.

Toure was then asked where he thinks the differences lie between Man City under Pep Guardiola last season and City under Maresca in 2026-26.

He replied: “When you change coach, especially one of Guardiola’s calibre, for me, the best there has ever been, it’s always a challenge. City have also lost key players like Bernardo Silva, like Rodri, the best midfielder in the role. And leaders like Stones and Ake.

“I think City did a great job in bringing in strong players like Fernandez and Bouaddi. Maresca needs time, but I’m sure they’ll find a way.”.

Toure is not concerned by the recent form of Italy international goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma.

It has been revealed that “Gigio is a fantastic person,” he noted. Maybe he’s going through a tough period, but he’s an exemplary professional and knows exactly what he needs to do to return to his best level. And I’m sure he will because he’s one of the best goalkeepers in the world.”.
