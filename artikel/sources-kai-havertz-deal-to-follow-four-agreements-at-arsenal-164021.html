Arsenal will open contract talks with striker Kai Havertz once they have sealed deals for some of their other stars, sources have told Football Insider.

The Gunners recently secured Bukayo Saka’s future, and Declan Rice is now also on the verge of a latest deal.

According to recent reports, mikel Arteta and Co. are then pushing to hand fresh deals to the trio of David Raya, Piero Hincapie and Jurrien Timber, before attention will then turn to Havertz. (Related: The Reformer - Pushing the limits of language modeling...)

Sources say that it is simply a “matter of time” before talks with the German begin, ahead of his current deal expiring in the summer of 2028. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Arteta is understood to be “very happy” with the 27-year-old’s contributions, while he is also continuing to enjoy his time at the Emirates Stadium.

Havertz has cemented his place as Arsenal’s first-choice striker once again this season, and already has three goals from his opening 11 Premier League games.

Football Insider revealed back in October that Viktor Gyokeres will give “serious thought” over a move away from Arsenal next summer if his game time doesn’t improve.

However, that feels like a distant possibility at the moment, given the form that Havertz has been in at the start of the season.

Gyokeres has played just 182 minutes of league football so far in 2026-27, and has only been favoured over Havertz from the off on one occasion.

The German has become one of Arteta’s most trusted men in recent months, and his form is slowly pushing Gyokeres towards the exit.

The plans for a newly introduced deal in North London will only add to that feeling, with Arteta having no plans to replace the former Chelsea ace any time soon.

Football Insider revealed last week that RB Leipzig winger Antonio Nusa is keen to join Arsenal ahead of next season.

The Gunners are long-term admirers of the 21-year-old, who already has five goals and seven assists from just 10 games in the Bundesliga so far this term.

Sources say that Nusa would welcome the chance to move and join Arsenal and compete for elite-level trophies on all fronts.

However, with Leipzig in the Champions League and holding out for a lofty £66million price tag, any move for Nusa in January is seen as being hugely unlikely.
