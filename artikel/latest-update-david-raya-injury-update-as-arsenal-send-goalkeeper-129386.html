David Raya will today undergo scans on a potential shoulder injury as Arsenal face a nervous wait over their goalkeeper.

Raya was forced off during Saturday’s 2-2 draw at Newcastle after colliding with William Osula following a corner.

The keeper required lengthy treatment on the pitch from Arsenal’s medical team, before the decision was made for him to come off.

Kepa Arrizabalaga played the final 20 minutes as Arsenal fought back to earn a point.

But the result was marred by an injury to who many consider to be Arsenal’s only irreplaceable player.
