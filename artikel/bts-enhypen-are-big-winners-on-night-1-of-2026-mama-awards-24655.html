BTS & ENHYPEN Are Big Winners on Night 1 of 2026 MAMA Awards

🌐 Source: Billboard • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (368 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways

  • BTS won artist of the year and best male group and ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male.
  • ENHYPEN won fans’ choice of the year, favorite MCD Stage, and also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

BTS and ENHYPEN were the massive winners on night 1 of the 2026 MAMA Awards, which were held Friday (Nov. 20) at the Kyocera Dome Osaka, in Japan. Night 2 of the awards is set for Saturday (Nov. 21). The awards are also streaming live worldwide on CJ ENM’s Mnet Plus platform.

BTS won artist of the year and best male group and ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male.

ENHYPEN won fans’ choice of the year, favorite MCD Stage, and also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male.

Recent findings suggest that both groups have topped the all-genre Billboard 200 album chart this year. BTS led for three weeks in April with Arirang. ENHYPEN was on top for one week in September with The Sin: Bliss.

Yeonjun of TOMORROW X TOGETHER won best male artist. The group also made the top 10 for Fans’ Choice Male.

In a recent development, soyeon of i-dle won best female artist. That group also made the top 10 for Fans’ Choice Female.

ALPHA DRIVE ONE and TUIDE were co-winners for best upcoming artist.

In a recent development, music released between Oct. 1, 2025, and Sept. 30, 2026 was eligible for this year’s awards. (Related: 3 Ways To Make Music More Powerful Against Pain (M)...)

Performers on night 1 included Street World Fighter: Directors’ War, ILLIT, Panic, ALLDAY PROJECT, ALPHA DRIVE ONE, BABYMONSTER, CL, ENHYPEN, Hearts2Hearts, INI, LNGSHOT, MODYSSEY, NCT WISH, RESCENE, TREASURE and TUIDE.

The following performers are set to take the stage on night 2: &TEAM, NAYEON (TWICE), HWASA, BIGBANG, CORTIS, IVE, izna, KiiiKiii, KO1KEYZ, LE SSERAFIM, WOODZ and ZEROBASEONE.

Key winners from night 1 include:.

Fans’ Choice of the Year with Visa: ENHYPEN.

Worldwide KConers’ Choice: ZeroBaseOne.

Best Breakthrough Artist: NCT Wish.

Favorite MCD Stage with Mnet Plus: ENYPEN.

Visa Super Stage Artist: Treasure.

Q100 Favorite Mega Artist: NCT Wish.
