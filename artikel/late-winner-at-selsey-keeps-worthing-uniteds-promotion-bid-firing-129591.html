Under joint managers Glenn Souter and Mark Curram, United have now collected 17 points from their last possible 21, recording five victories and two draws.

That sequence has included victories over Guildford City, Shoreham, Arundel, Saltdean and Selsey, alongside hard-fought draws against league leaders Infinity and promotion rivals Jarvis Brook.

It has been a run that has tested the Mavericks in different ways. From the dramatic late equaliser against Infinity to recovering from two goals down at Jarvis Brook, the side have shown a growing belief that they can get a result regardless of the circumstances.

Souter commented: “Saturday probably summed up where we are as a group at the moment. We weren’t necessarily at our best, but the players kept believing right until the final whistle, and to get a winner so late on was a fantastic feeling.
