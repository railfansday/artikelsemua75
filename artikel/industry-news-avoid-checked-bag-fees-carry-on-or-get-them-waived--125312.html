Recent findings suggest that carry on whenever your fare includes a carry-on bag, since a checked bag now costs $45 or more each way on Delta, American, and Southwest. If you check bags on most trips, get an airline credit card or elite status that waives the first bag for you and the companions on your reservation.

For a family of four checking one bag each, that adds up to at least $360 per round trip on Delta, American, or Southwest.

Carrying on costs nothing on those three, which let each passenger bring a carry-on and a personal item free.

It has been revealed that when you do need to check bags, a waiver can cover everyone on your reservation, not just you. The Delta SkyMiles Gold American Express Card waives the first bag for you and up to eight companions on Delta flights booked with the card, for a $150 annual fee after a $0 first year. That’s less than half the $360 the family of four pays on one Delta round trip.
