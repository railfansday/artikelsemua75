Taylor Swift and her nieces have already perfected their touchdown celebration whenever the pop star’s husband, Travis Kelce, scores for the Kansas City Chiefs.

Swift, 36, and her brother-in-law Jason Kelce’s 7-year-old daughter, Wyatt, were spotted adorably hugging in a private suite on Sunday, November 22, when Travis, 37, ran into the end zone during the Chiefs’ matchup against the Arizona Cardinals. In addition to Wyatt, Jason and his wife, Kylie Kelce, are parents of Ellie, 5, Bennett 3, and Finnley, 20 months. The whole crew all joined Swift and her parents, Scott and Andrea, in the box for the pre-Thanksgiving game. While watching the Sunday clash, Swift perched her nieces on her lap. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Swift started dating Travis in 2023, soon bonding with Wyatt and her sisters over baking, crafts and cats.

In a recent development, “As soon as I got the kids around the cats, it was my goal to prove to them that they weren’t poisonous,” Swift said on an August 2025 episode of the Kelce brothers’ “New Heights” podcast, debunking Jason’s claims to his kids meant to assuage them from wanting a new pet. “There’s no better way to prove that than to just hand them Benjamin, my ragdoll cat, who lets humans hold him like he’s a baby.”.

The pop star added, “My favorite thing ever is when Bennie will come up to me and just go, ‘I find Benjamin, I find him.’ And she’s just over there laying with him and petting him and holding him.”.

Swift has three cats: Meredith Grey, Olivia Benson and Benjamin Button. (She and Travis have since adopted a dog, a Samoyed named Wendy, together.).

Sources indicate that “The cats are so good with kids,” the Grammy winner gushed at the time. “They’re just like, ‘Meredith is here!’ I’m like, ‘And you know what? She didn’t bite you at all, did she? And if she did bite you, she wouldn’t be poisonous.’ They’re like, ‘That’s not what our dad noted.’”.

Jason, 38, and Kylie’s kids were major fans of Swift and admittedly starstruck to meet her for the first time.

Industry observers note that “We had to break that habit with your daughters. When they got to meet Aunt Tay Tay, they were calling her ‘Taylor Swift,’” Travis told his brother on a September podcast episode. “[It was] adorable.”.

The four Kelce girls later served as the flower girls in Swift and Travis’ star-studded July wedding at the famed Madison Square Garden.

In a recent development, “It was a special event for our entire family, and obviously my daughters are incredible,” Jason told reporters at the American Century Championship later that month. “They’re beautiful, they’re incredible personalities. And Travis and Taylor love them. So, very happy we were able to be a part of it. And more importantly, very happy for those two. It was an amazing day.”.
