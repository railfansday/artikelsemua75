It has been revealed that it took less than a week for shares of Anthropic PBC (Nasdaq: ANT) to dip below their IPO price. The AI manufacturer’s stock fell as low as $81.40 in premarket trading on Tuesday, November 24. The offering price in its high-profile stock listing last week was $83, with shares reaching a high of $115.10 on opening day. The drop is relatively quick in comparison with this year’s other major tech IPO: SpaceX. The Elon Musk-run company debuted (Nasdaq: SPCX) on June 12 with an IPO price of $135. Its shares first fell below that line over a month later, on July 15. As of publication, shares are hovering just below it at about $131—though it has spent most of its time on the market above its IPO price. A tale of two trades SpaceX held the title for largest ever IPO with $75 billion raised until Anthropic beat it with an $81.3 billion IPO. But the excitement around Anthropic’s IPO was short lived. The Claude creator’s shares fell sharply on Friday, just one day after their debut, and have since remained almost consistently below $90. The stock rallied a bit on Monday, likely helped by the surprise news of Jensen Huang’s return to Nvidia. However, stock futures fell again Tuesday, with Nvidia’s shares (Nasdaq: NVDA) also falling more than 5%. Tuesday’s premarket drop came as investors continue to worry about overvaluing AI companies, vast spending into the technology, and a bubble potentially bursting. Patch: As of this writing, Anthropic’s share have dropped to roughly $81.6 a share, down about 6.72% from Monday’s close.
