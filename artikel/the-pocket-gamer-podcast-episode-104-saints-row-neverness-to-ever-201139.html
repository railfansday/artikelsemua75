On open-world outlaws and realistic rides.

According to recent reports, perhaps the biggest question everyone's been asking everyone this week is: what do you think of GTA VI? Inevitably, we discuss the pros and cons of Rockstar Games' latest endeavour in this week's (New and Improved!) Pocket Gamer Podcast, plus a few mobile alternatives you can dive into if Grand Theft Auto isn't exactly your thing.

Iwan starts the bit - er, discussion - with the mobile release of Postal Classic & Uncut, in which he talks about why it was and still is such a controversial shooter. It brings us to the topic of violence in video games, which inevitably leads to GTA VI. (Related: Google Cloud C4 Brings a 70% TCO improvement on GPT OSS...)

Industry observers note that now, with plenty of other mobile games like Grand Theft Auto VI out there, you at least have choices to pick from if, like Will, you're not exactly as enamoured with Rockstar Games' most recent release. He gives us the lowdown on why GTA VI just isn't for him, and what the rest of the internet is currently saying. (Related: Industry News: huggingface_hub v1.0: Five Years of Buil...)

I offer Neverness to Everness as a tribute for anyone who's looking for a GTA-like in the anime space, as it's also got a Wanted system where you can commit crimes and then promptly pay for stated crimes. There's also Ananta launching on January 15th next year, which, given it's already got more than 27 million pre-registrations, is probably going to be a massive, massive thing.

Will brings up an interesting point, by the way, and that's people are apparently having fun doing non-crime things in GTA VI - this brings to mind OneState and its more roleplay-centric gameplay.

We also talk about touchscreen controls on open-world adventures like this, which is usually a challenge. But Feral Interactive has been bringing ports onto mobile - the most recent one being Saints Row: The Third Remastered - so here's hoping touchscreen controls will only get better from here.
