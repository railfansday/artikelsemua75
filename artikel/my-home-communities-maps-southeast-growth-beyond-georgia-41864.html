Georgia-based homebuilder My Home Communities is expanding beyond its home state with upcoming-home communities planned in South Carolina, Alabama and Northwest Florida, the organization declared earlier this week.

The privately held builder, founded in 2018, will launch sales this month at Stonebrook in Gaffney, South Carolina, is planning an 89-homesite community in Summerdale, Alabama, and is preparing an initial presence along Hamilton Bridge Road in Milton, Florida. “Georgia has provided an incredible foundation for our organization, and we’re excited to take what we’ve built here into latest markets,” said Jacob Prather, president of My Home Communities. “Expanding into South Carolina, Alabama and Florida is about building relationships, creating new opportunities for homebuyers and establishing My Home Communities as a recognized homebuilding brand throughout the Southeast.”. My Home Communities focused its first eight years on establishing a footprint across metro Atlanta and other Georgia markets, offering single-family homes and communities targeted to a wide range of buyers.

The tech giant stated continued investment in Georgia remains a core part of its strategy even as it exports its building processes and customer service model to other Southeast states.

According to recent reports, the expansion is being supported by the Tamra Wade Team, backed by RE/MAX TRU, which is leading sales and marketing efforts for My Home Communities in the latest markets. The team has worked with the builder for several years in Georgia, providing continuity as the company introduces its brand to buyers outside the state.

In South Carolina, Stonebrook in Gaffney is preparing to launch sales in November 2026, with prices expected to start in the high $200,000s, according to the organization. The community is My Home Communities’ first project in South Carolina.

In Alabama, The Retreat in Summerdale is planned as an 89-homesite community. Sales timing has not yet been revealed. The organization remarked the project will expand its reach into the Gulf Coast corridor, where newly introduced-home demand has been supported by employment growth and relative affordability compared to coastal metros.

In a recent development, in Northwest Florida, My Home Communities intends to start with two homesites along Hamilton Bridge Road in Milton. Additional community details and sales timelines will be released later.

Industry observers note that “Our growth is intentional, and we’re focused on finding the right opportunities in markets where we believe our homes and communities will resonate with buyers,” Prather mentioned. “With Stonebrook launching sales this month and additional communities planned in Alabama and Florida, we’re excited to introduce our brand to new audiences.”.
