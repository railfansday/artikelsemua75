Dezeen Events Guide has introduced its guide to the 2026 edition of Miami art week, which is sponsored by colour specialist Pantone.

The festival, which runs from 30 November to 6 December 2026, takes place across Miami, in neighbourhoods such as the Miami Design District and Miami Beach. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

The event includes fairs, exhibitions, installations, open showrooms, talks, rolls out, pop-up shops and parties throughout the week.

Highlights include the Design Miami and Art Basel Miami Beach fairs, alongside satellite fairs NADA Miami, Art Miami and Fridge Art Fair.

Alongside the guide, Dezeen has created an interactive map for the art week, helping attendees strategy their routes around the city. (Related: Estate Planning for Early Retirement: The FIRE Guide...)

There’s still time to feature in the Miami art week 2026 guide.

Dezeen Events Guide offers a range of listings. Get in touch with the team at eventsguide@dezeen.com to book your listing or to discuss a wider partnership with Dezeen.

Dezeen Events Guide is our guide to the best architecture and design events taking place across the world each year.

The guide is updated weekly and includes virtual events, conferences, trade fairs, major exhibitions and design weeks. For more details on inclusion in the Dezeen Events Guide, including in our guide to Miami art week, email eventsguide@dezeen.com.
