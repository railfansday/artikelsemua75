Grey’s Anatomy‘s fall finale came to a shocking end when a main character tried to perform an operation while under the influence — so what does this mean for their future on the show?

Sources indicate that during the Thursday, November 19, episode of the hit ABC series, Amelia (Caterina Scorsone) digs into her suspicion that Link (Chris Carmack) has an addiction to painkillers. Her concerns are confirmed when she notices that his pupils are dilated and his hands are shaking during an operation — and she corners him about it with help from Link’s wife, Jo (Camilla Luddington), who doesn’t know what is going on. “At the end of the midseason finale, Link is really he’s in the crosshairs and he is at a crossroad because Jo obviously is going to find out that something’s going on — unless he can joyfully lie his way out of it, which maybe he can,” Carmack, 45, exclusively told Us.

Carmack pointed out that Amelia “is onto” Link.

“She threw him out of surgery, so he’s going to have to answer on a professional level. There’s going to be repercussions on the personal level. There’s going to be repercussions with Jo,” he continued. “Not to mention, he’s coparenting a child with Amelia. So he’s at the point where there is no way out. He’s going to have to answer for something.”. (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

The actor expressed hope that the revelation doesn’t “tear their family apart,” adding, “It’s going to get rocky, but I would say being thrown out of a surgery because he’s incapable of holding his tools properly is definitely a wake-up call.”.

Carmack acknowledged the slow buildup to Link’s substance abuse struggles.

In a recent development, “There are some episodes where it doesn’t really rear its head in terms of telling the story about him getting pills. But I’ve approached the story line from an angle of really denial,” he noted. “He does not believe he has a problem or every step along the way where he kind of recognizes that it might be a problem and it’s getting a little worse. He really thinks he can take care of it himself.”.

Recent findings suggest that carmack continued: “He just sees himself as the guy who has to fix everything, help everyone and be everything to everyone. He’s exhausted, and he has his own trauma from a terrible injury and from the birth of his twins as well, but he’s not facing it. So these drugs, they’ve been numbing not just his physical pain but his mental turmoil.”.

Looking ahead, Carmack hinted at how dire the consequences would be for Link — and what it means for his time on the show.

Recent findings suggest that “A question that I was asking our medical supervisors and writers and producers all along has been about the standing of a doctor who’s using pain medications while he’s performing surgeries. The answer that they came up with based on research was that it was a very gray area,” he revealed. “You are allowed to medicate for pain, but of course they can’t be at a place of disability or inability when they’re performing their duties. That’s where I was living with this story line, not thinking he’s going to lose his job because he thinks he has everything under control.”.

In a recent development, link’s job may be in danger, but Carmack hinted that Amelia’s role will create complications, adding, “Amelia going through his medical records is a violation of his personal rights and a violation of HIPAA. Maybe not in a malicious way, but he knows the consequences that she might face.”.

Carmack remained largely tight-lipped about the second half of the season but did tease that “there are a lot of secrets being held” by various characters on the show.
