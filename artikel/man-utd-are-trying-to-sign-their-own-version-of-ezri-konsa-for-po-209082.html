Manchester United are looking to sign a newly introduced defender in the January transfer window, as they look to boost a backline that has endured a largely miserable season.

Ahead of tonight's Europa League fixtures, Man United had conceded more goals than any other club in the top five European leagues, and that is one of the main reasons why they presently lie 19th in the Premier League table.

While one report suggests that signing a newly introduced striker in the New Year is the priority, it's no secret that the Red Devils have massive issues at the back too.

United have carried out extensive rebuilds of their midfield and attack over the previous two summer transfer windows, and it's likely that the 2027 one would have seen them focus on the defence. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Those plans now need to be brought forward, given how poor the team have been defensively this term, with the likes of Lisandro Martinez, Diogo Dalot and Matthijs de Ligt struggling when they have been on the pitch.

The absence of Luke Shaw for the previous nine matches hasn't been helpful, especially after United put their trust in his availability this term following his 38-game Premier League campaign in 2025-26.

United ideally need a player for each of the centre-back and left-back positions, but with the January window being notoriously difficult for buyers, they could settle on an individual who is able to be used in both.

Speaking on the latest episode of GIVEMESPORT's Market Madness podcast, GMS senior correspondent Ben Jacobs said:.

“They will be looking at a striker and then either some form of hybrid defender who can play full-back and centre-back, or a full-back and a centre-back.

Recent findings suggest that “There is some appreciation of Nathan Collins [of Brentford], while Alejandro Balde [of Barcelona] could be available in the winter market. One dream target is [Adrien] Truffert, but Bournemouth are not going to want to let many of their stars go.".

While those three players are specialists in one position, Jacobs did suggest they've already found a player who can cover both roles for them in Fulham's Calvin Bassey, who they will try to sign.

The direction of travel at the moment seems to be Calvin Bassey if they can get him, but that’s not going to be easy with Fulham hoping to tie him down to a latest contract.

Some United supporters may turn their nose up at the prospect of signing Bassey from Fulham, but there are many examples of where the same type of transfer move has worked out.

Manchester City, Arsenal, Tottenham and Liverpool are just some of the clubs who have picked up centre-backs from Premier League clubs outside the top six in recent years, and it has proven to be good business when you look at how the likes of Virgil van Dijk and Marc Guehi have performed.

Van Dijk established himself as one of the best centre-backs in Premier League history at Anfield after joining from Southampton, but there is a centre-back in his prime who has been outstanding at Arsenal in Ezri Konsa.

While Bassey may not be considered at the same level as the England international, there are similarities in the fact that they can both play as a centre-back or a full-back, while both have shown their leadership skills.

Konsa was vice-captain at Villa Park prior to his switch to the Emirates, while Bassey has captained the Cottagers, to show that he is the right type of character for a United team in desperate need of leadership.

Coincidentally, both players have won 60 duels in the Premier League in the 2026-27 season to date, which is one of their biggest strengths.

Interestingly, the Nigeria international's contract at Craven Cottage is set to expire at the end of the season, and while there hasn't been a breakthrough yet on negotiations, it is suggested that both parties are keen to extend terms.

Whether Bassey's mind could be changed if Pochettino makes a serious move for him remains to be seen, but history suggests he could be a good signing for United.
