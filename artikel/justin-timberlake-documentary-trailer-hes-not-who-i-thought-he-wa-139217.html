Justin Timberlake is the subject of a latest four-part documentary series that promises to explore his personal and professional life.

Apple TV just unveiled and released a trailer (below) for Timberlake, which is described as a “raw, reflective and revealing portrait of the life and career of multi-hyphenate artist and icon.”.

The series is directed by Academy Award winner Fisher Stevens (The Cove) and spans three decades of music and popular culture, and features never-before-seen archival footage and exclusive interviews with Timberlake and his family, friends and collaborators.

The footage teases Timberlake’s wife, Jessica Biel, telling the director, “When I met him, he’s just not who I thought — at all” and “I think we gotta address some things.” While Timberlake tells the audience, “People aren’t going to understand who I am” and “You want to ask me about specific things, let’s do it.” Timberlake’s relationship with Britney Spears (who is not shown on camera in the trailer) is also discussed, along with the “level of insanity” in their relationship.
