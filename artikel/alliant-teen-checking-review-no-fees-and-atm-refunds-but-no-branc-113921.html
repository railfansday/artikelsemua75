According to recent reports, open Alliant Teen Checking for a teen of 13 to 17 if your family is comfortable banking by app, since it charges no monthly or overdraft fees and becomes a regular checking account at 18. Choose a bank with a nearby branch instead if your teen deposits cash often, because Alliant has none.

It has been revealed that next to a free teen account at a bank with branches, Alliant Teen Checking costs nothing more, and what it gives up is a branch where a teen can deposit cash. What it adds is refunds of up to $20 a month in other ATMs’ fees and an Alliant High-Rate Savings account, which pays 3.01% annual percentage yield (APY) in months its average daily balance is $100 or more.
