Ina Garten Says She Turned Down ‘Dancing With the Stars’ Because the Thought of Competing Gave Her ‘Nightmares’

Celebrity chef Ina Garten will not be cooking up a freestyle for “Dancing with the Stars” in the near future.

Sources indicate that garten told Rose Byrne on a recent episode of her “Happy Hour” podcast that she would be too nervous to compete. She opened the floor by asking Byrne if she had ever “not done something because it was too scary,” before answering her own question.

According to recent reports, “Somebody asked me to do ‘Dancing with the Stars,’” she mentioned. “I thought, ‘Nobody needs to see that. That’s not happening.’” “The closer I got to the day when I had to do it, the more I’d have nightmares,” Garten added.
