Haiti’s Sunrise Airways this week released a upcoming route to the U.S. Northeast.

It has been revealed that the carrier on Tuesday operated its inaugural flight between Cap-Haïtien and New York-JFK. Flights will operate twice per week going forward.

Sources indicate that sunrise is one of only two airlines right now connecting the U.S. and Haiti. It also serves Boston, Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and Miami from Cap-Haïtien.

In a recent development, american Airlines resumed service to the country earlier this month after a nearly two-year hiatus brought on by rising gang violence in and around Port-au-Prince. It is now operating daily nonstop flights between Miami and Cap-Haïtien.
