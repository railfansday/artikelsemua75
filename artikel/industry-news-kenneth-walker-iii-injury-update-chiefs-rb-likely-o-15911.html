The Kansas City Chiefs will likely be without Kenneth Walker III again for their Week 11 game against the Arizona Cardinals, head coach Andy Reid said Friday.

Walker reported groin tightness last week, and the team decided to give him a rest and sit out the 35-13 win over the Atlanta Falcons. Reid said on Wednesday that the running back was "feeling better," but they did not "want to do something stupid.".

On Friday, Reid remarked Walker, as well as offensive lineman Jaylon Moore and guard Trey Smith, are "improving" as they deal with injuries, but will "most likely" be ruled out.

Rookie running back Emmett Johnson took advantage of the first-team opportunity and rushed for 101 yards on 22 carries, while adding six catches for 70 yards and two touchdowns in Week 10. According to recent reports, the Chiefs are understandably playing it safe with Walker, allowing the star to rest his groin injury after Johnson more than held his own in last week's blowout win.
