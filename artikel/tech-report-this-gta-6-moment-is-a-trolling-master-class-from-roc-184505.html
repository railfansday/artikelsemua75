Industry observers note that have you ever seen those classic Looney Tunes cartoons where the murderous Wile E. Coyote dashes after the infuriating Road Runner? Usually, a high-speed chase ends with an unexpected comic twist, such as a bundle of dynamite exploding at the wrong time. Perhaps the most famous gag from these iconic animated shorts​​​​​​​ is the one where the plotting Coyote is tricked into running headfirst into a canyon wall painted to look like the entry to a tunnel.

Some people are at the moment learning that the silly gag can be more convincing than they thought, especially in a video game as hyperrealistic as Grand Theft Auto 6.

It has been revealed that since the release of Rockstar's open-world game, social media has filled up with clips​​​​​​​ of players running down a street at high speed. Maybe they're in the middle of a police chase, or maybe they're just not paying much attention. The high-speed drive ends in misfortune when the player realizes that the area in front of them isn't a street at all — it's a mural​​​​​​​ that looks like the natural continuation of the street.
