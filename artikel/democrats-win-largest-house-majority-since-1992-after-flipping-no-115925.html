Chuck Hubbard is a former journalist whose first political campaign was in 2022.

The Democratic Party has won the largest majority in the US House of Representatives since 1992, after Democrat Chuck Hubbard unseated Republican Representative Virginia Foxx in North Carolina.

Industry observers note that the race was called on Sunday, with Hubbard beating Foxx by 330 votes with 99% counted. It was one of the closest contests of the recent midterm elections, in which Democrats also secured the Senate. (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

The party has now won at least 258 House seats in the midterms compared to Republicans, who hold at least 173 seats, with four races still yet to be called.

Neither party had won as many House races since 1992, when Bill Clinton won the presidency and Democrats won a total of 258 seats.

View the 2026 midterm election results.

Recent findings suggest that sunday's result came after Foxx, who has represented North Carolina's 5th Congressional District since 2005, requested a recount. This is allowed under state law when the margin of victory is under 1%.

She conceded on Sunday, saying in a statement: "After running a campaign focused on my track record of commonsense solutions and conservative values, I'm humbled and grateful that so many North Carolina voters responded by supporting my candidacy, even if we came up just a few votes short.".

Virginia Foxx spent a decade in the North Carolina Senate before representing the state in the House of Representatives.

Hubbard, meanwhile, wrote on X that he had spoken to Foxx, "who graciously conceded following the results of the recount process".

He added: "I deeply appreciate her commitment to a smooth transition of the office and constituent casework. We may not always agree, but we both love North Carolina and the people of the Fifth District.". (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Hubbard's win, which comes after he lost to Foxx in the 2024 midterms by nine percentage points, followed a number of Democratic victories in deeply Republican districts including South Carolina, Texas, Alaska and Iowa.

Hubbard, like many Democrats running in the midterms, focused on the rising cost of living in the US during the war with Iran - a top issue for voters.

President Donald Trump himself - as well as Vice President JD Vance - have suggested inflation caused by the war in Iran was probably to blame for the Republican losses.

Hubbard has also remarked he will also prioritise working families and small businesses, supporting public education and expanding access to affordable healthcare, according to his website.

Sources indicate that the remaining House races yet to be called are in Tennessee and Minnesota, while two elections in Louisiana will go to a runoff in December. In Tennessee's fourth district, Democrat Victoria Broderick leads by fewer than 300 votes, and in Minnesota's first district, Democrat Jake Johnson is winning by fewer than 500 votes.

Sources indicate that democrats may use their upcoming majority in the House and control of the Senate to investigate conflicts of interest and other actions by the president and those close to him, while the Senate also has the power to confirm or block the president's cabinet and judicial nominations. They will also be able to pass legislation through Congress easily, though Trump still has to give final sign off for it to become law.

However, even with control of both chambers of Congress, they will still have limits on their power as Trump can veto legislation.
