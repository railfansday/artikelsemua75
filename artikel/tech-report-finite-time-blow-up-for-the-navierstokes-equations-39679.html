Industry observers note that this repository holds the paper Finite-time blow-up for the Navier–Stokes equations by L. Alpöge and T. Buckmaster and the Lean 4 formalization that accompanies it.

According to recent reports, the statement files at the top of lean/ give the definitions and state the theorems with sorry in place of their proofs; Solution.lean proves them from the library lean/Breakdown/. Each comparator*.json pairs a statement file with Solution.lean, names the theorems whose statements must agree, and permits only the axioms propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice.

The library is large (about 2,270 files and 600,000 lines), and a full build needs a great deal of memory and time. (Related: Tiny Agents: an MCP-powered agent in 50 lines of code...)

The formalization contains no data. The certificate whose hash the paper gives is not in this repository; the paper notes that it will be released. That the certificate satisfies the conditions of Appendix B is verified by a rigorous computation, involving interval arithmetic (128 bits of precision, with the Arb library through FLINT), which is not part of the formalization and is not in this repository either.

The source of the paper is not in this repository. (Related: Architectural Choices in China's Open-Source AI Ecosyst...)

According to recent reports, the package is the one of the paper's working repository, with one patch of 20 November 2026 applied to the cell-layer statements and to the library proofs that depend on them: the weight of one bound becomes $(|m|+\tfrac12)^2$ in place of $m^2+\tfrac14$, one constant of a cell may be zero, and the set of the harmonics of the model in a cell is restricted to those that do not vanish there. Appendix B of the paper states the conditions in this patched form. The patched package was not rebuilt when this repository was assembled.
