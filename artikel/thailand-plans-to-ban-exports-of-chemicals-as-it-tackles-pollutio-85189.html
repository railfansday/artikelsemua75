Thailand Plans to Ban Exports of Chemicals as It Tackles Pollution

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (45 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Thailand aims to ban exports and transit shipments of chemicals used in rare-earth mining following the detection of elevated arsenic levels in rivers in the northern part of the country and will work with China and Myanmar in addressing the pollution.
