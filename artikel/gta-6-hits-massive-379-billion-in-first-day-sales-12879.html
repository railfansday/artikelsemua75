Crime pays: Rockstar Games’ “Grand Theft Auto 6″ first-day sales reached $3.79 billion.

Per, Rockstar Games parent companyTake-Two Interactive, that total sets “a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history.”.

Industry observers note that for the sake of comparison, Sony Pictures’ “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” reaped $2.52 billion at the global box office in 2026, the No. 1 movie of the year to date. Christopher Nolan’s blockbuster movie “The Odyssey” has taken in just over $1.8 billion to date. On its first day of release, “GTA 6” made roughly as much as James Cameron’s last two “Avatar” movies combined. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a fresh chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” mentioned Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”.
