Recent findings suggest that leicester City’s rapid decline from the Premier League to League One means there has been a lot of change over the past two years. LCFC’s place as European football contenders is a thing of the past, and so are many of the players.

It has been revealed that among the mainstays in the Foxes’ Premier League days was right-back Ricardo Pereira, who joined from FC Porto in 2018 and grew into a starring role at the King Power Stadium. He was one of the league’s best in his position, but injuries hit and limited his involvement.

Industry observers note that pereira stayed throughout Leicester City’s yo-yo years, but when his contract came to an end earlier this year, it was decided that he would move on. And, over four months on from his release, he remains out of the game. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Pereira managed 39 appearances in Leicester City’s relegation season, proving he can still manage a demanding season despite previous injury challenges.

His pedigree at the very top of the game with the Foxes and Porto has seen him grow into a leadership figure over the years. The experience he has to his name is of huge value to younger players and his character would swiftly see him become an influential figure in the dressing room. Granted, the speed and physical traits Pereira indicated at his best aren’t as strong anymore. However, he has always been a technically gifted player in possession, and his pedigree helps him read the game well.

Pereira has shared images and videos of himself training during his time without a team. He would need time to get up to speed to play full games at a competitive level, but he has kept himself ticking over as he awaits his next opportunity after leaving LCFC.

It has been revealed that it remains to be seen just what awaits Pereira after a long spell out of the game. Opportunities do dry up as the season moves on from the summer transfer window, so there might not be many intriguing options out there.

The MLS regular season has just ended, so the Leicester City man could join a team for their 2027 preparations to be ready to go for the latest campaign. Alternatively though, Pereira may wish to see out the latter years of his career back in his home country of Portugal.

Industry observers note that teams in Liga Portugal would certainly benefit from his leadership and experience. Having broken through in his home country before leaving for bigger things, it could be a fitting end to his career to return and become an influential player for a top flight side.
