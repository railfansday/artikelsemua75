Tested on mushrooms, an AI model reveals potential for detecting crop stress with far fewer computations

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (73 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that A new artificial intelligence (AI) model can detect stress in agricultural crops using a nondestructive imaging technique, allowing changes in crop health to be monitored over time. Developed by a team of researchers at Penn State, the new model shows promise for accurately detecting crop stress caused by drought, disease, nutrient deficiency and other causes while using less computing power than some existing AI methods.
