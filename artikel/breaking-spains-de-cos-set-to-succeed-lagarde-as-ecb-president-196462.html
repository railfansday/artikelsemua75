Breaking: Spain's De Cos Set to Succeed Lagarde as ECB President

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (51 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Pablo Hernandez de Cos is set to become European Central Bank president, directing the region’s fight against inflation and possible bond turmoil. The 55-year-old Spaniard will take over from Christine Lagarde, who ends her term early on Feb. 7. Bloomberg's Zoe Schneeweiss reports from Frankfurt. (Source: Bloomberg).
