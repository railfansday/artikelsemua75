A nearly 100-million-year-old hairy snail pays tribute to the Prodigy's lead singer

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Myanmar amber (more commonly known as "burmite") is one of the most important sources of information about biodiversity during the Mesozoic Era. This amber dates back to the age of the dinosaurs and is approximately 99 million years old. Burmite preserves numerous now-extinct organisms (animals, plants, fungi, etc.) as inclusions, often in astonishing detail. This is because amber can preserve even soft tissues, which would otherwise decompose rapidly, and do so in three dimensions.
