It has been revealed that rogen recalled Pitt, Michael Fassbender, Chiwetel Ejiofor and other cast members from “12 Years a Slave” spending time on the set of his comedy “This Is the End” while the two films were both shooting in Latest Orleans. “Actually, what’s so weird is I had met him — we made ‘This Is the End’ at the same time they made ’12 Years a Slave’ in Upcoming Orleans together,” Rogen told podcast host Josh Horowitz. “Understandably, the cast and crew of ’12 Years a Slave’ were in a bummed-out headspace a lot of the time.”. Industry observers note that “12 Years a Slave” starred Ejiofor as a free Black man named Solomon Northup who was kidnapped in 1841 and sold into slavery in Louisiana. Fassbender played plantation owner Edwin Epps and Pitt played an abolitionist named Samuel Bass who helped Northup regain his freedom.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, Rogen was shooting “This Is the End” with Jay Baruchel, James Franco, Jonah Hill, Craig Robinson, Danny McBride and a host of other celebrities. The actors played dramatized versions of themselves in a bizarre apocalyptic scene at a Hollywood house party.

Sources indicate that “So they would come and hang out on our set, and we would go to bars and stuff, and they would come hang out with us all the time, I think just because we were in a great mood all the time,” Rogen mentioned. “We were having the time of our lives, and then you’d see Steve McQueen and Brad Pitt and Chiwetel would sulk in, and then we’d give them a few drinks and they’d kinda perk up.”.

“12 Years a Slave” went on to win Best Picture and Best Adapted Screenplay (from Northup’s 1853 memoir “Twelve Years a Slave”) at the 2014 Academy Awards. Among its seven other nominations were acting nods for Ejiofor, Fassbender and Lupita Nyong’o.
