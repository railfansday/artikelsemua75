Sources indicate that the first monograph devoted to the built work of Alejandro Muñoz Miranda (Granada, 1974), architect and professor of Architectural Design at the School of Architecture of Granada. The book brings together twelve works completed between 2004 and 2026 which, across very different programmes, keep returning to the same concerns: geometry, light and tectonics, the line of thought the author developed in his doctoral thesis with Kenneth Frampton.

The first monograph devoted to the built work of Alejandro Muñoz Miranda (Granada, 1974), architect and professor of Architectural Design at the School of Architecture of Granada. The book brings together twelve works completed between 2004 and 2026 which, across very different programmes, keep returning to the same concerns: geometry, light and tectonics, the line of thought the author developed in his doctoral thesis with Kenneth Frampton.
