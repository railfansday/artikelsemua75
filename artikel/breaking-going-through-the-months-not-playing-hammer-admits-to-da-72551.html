All credit to this young man, who has opened up on the reality of being excluded from Nuno’s West Ham squad. Particularly so at a time when criticism of another Nuno cast-off, Alphonse Areola, is flying around as Areola is apparently needed but not ready for Scott Parkers’ West Ham relaunch.

Ezra Mayers, invisible for months and on the fringes of the Hammers squad, seemingly unable to influence his manager, is suddenly thrust back into the spotlight after replacing Nuno’s choice Morato at left back during newly introduced manager Scott Parker’s first game in the dugout.

“It’s been really, really tough, going through the months not playing, not being in the squad. It was a case of making sure I stick at it and having the mentality to come in every day and still improve and perform when I couldn’t really see any opportunities coming my way.

That is definitely something that I’ve had to learn.

Nuno Espírito Santo apparently left out many from his ‘match day squad’ with whom he concentrated – Mayers was one who found it tough going.

And on the call from newly introduced manager Scott Parker which has thrust him back into first team contention:.

“I’ve been given an opportunity now and it’s been really good. The world works for you sometimes and I’m definitely going to give it my all and stake my claim to become a part of the team.”.

One of several young, talented players who’d become invisible under Nuno – Scarles and Ajala included – Ezra Mayers serves as a brilliant example of one who didn’t let his standards slip even whilst out of the team and the reckoning.
