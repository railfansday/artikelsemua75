E.J. Carroll, who appeared in projects including “The Deuce,” “Law & Order” and “Grand Theft Auto V,” died on Thursday. He was 66.

Carroll died suddenly Thursday morning, his rep confirmed to Variety. No cause of death has been provided.

Throughout his nearly 30-year career, Carroll’s credits included Darren Aronfsky’s “The Wrestler” and Robert De Niro and Kate Beckinsale’s “Everybody’s Fine,” as well as “The Cake Eaters,” “The Capers,” “The Winning Season,” “The Hungry Ghosts” and “A Fish Story.” One of the actor’s most recognizable roles was as Fat Mooney in HBO’s “The Deuce,” where his character ran a sex shop owned by Frankie Martino (James Franco). Born on May 21, 1960, Carroll made his on-screen debut in 1998 as a police officer in “Law & Order,” later returning for two episodes of “Law & Order: SVU” and an additional two in “Law & Order: Criminal Intent.” As a guest star, Carroll appeared in projects such as “The Blacklist,” “Blue Bloods,” “High Maintenance,” “Pose” and “The Punisher.” He also starred as Sheriff Victor Sickles in two episodes of Terence Winter and Martin Scorsese’s “Boardwalk Empire” in 2012 before going on to appear in an episode of “Damages,” which starred Glenn Close and Rose Byrne, that same year.

He also voiced a character in “Grand Theft Auto V” in 2013 and played Dr. Scott in the concert version of “The Rocky Horror Show,” titled “The Rocky Horror Tribute Show.” The concert ran for one night in 2007 to raise money for cancer charities Acting for a Cure and Komen for the Cure.

Sources indicate that prior to his death, Carroll most recently starred in “A Delicate Arrangement,” the upcoming Vivian Leigh biopic. Starring Carla Gugino as the “Gone With the Wind” actress, the film is written and produced by Jayce Bartok and directed by Nick Sandow.
