Oliver Glasner is not yet out of the woods despite relieving pressure on his position at Nottingham Forest, sources have told Football Insider.

The boss has endured a slow start to his tenure at the City Ground after he picked up just one win in his first nine Premier League game, leading to growing doubts. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

A 3-1 win against Man City before the international break could kickstart a positive run for Forest, with the former Crystal Palace manager looking to reach his ambitious goals.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Glasner could come under pressure again if Forest's form does not continue.

Prior to the win against Man City, he had publicly called out his players and demanded they step up, and while that had an immediate impact, it must now continue. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Glasner is presently comfortable in his position at Nottingham Forest, with the club backing him as part of a long-term project, but a downturn in form could raise alarm bells.

Former Man City financial adviser Stefan Borson warned Football Insider that Glasner could get himself sacked after his public criticism of his Nottingham Forest players.

His comments ultimately proved valuable as his side bounced back with a win against Enzo Maresca's side, but they must now prove they can continue this strong form.

Should Forest fall back into their negative spell from the start of the season, pressure will mount on Glasner once again, and he could be the next manager sacked.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Now that there's been two managers lost their jobs in the Premier League, it normally means that it does open the doors for more to follow.

"Oliver Glasner, there was bit of speculation about him as well before the international break, especially after that defeat to Brentford when he called out the players as well.

"But again, they bounced back and got a huge win against Manchester City as well, so he's another manager who was under pressure but picked up a much-needed win.

"You never know, for any manager in the Premier League, you're always under pressure. You’re only a few bad results away from potentially getting the sack.

"So if any team suffers a dip in form, there'll obviously be question marks asked about that manager.".

Speculation about Glasner's future comes amid questions about who could be the next Premier League manager to be sacked, with two dismissals already having taken place.

Michael Carrick and Pierre Sage were sacked by Man United and Crystal Palace respectively during the international break, and the floodgates may now have opened.

Football Insider revealed on 20 November that Alvaro Arbeloa is still under pressure at Fulham, and could be dismissed if their results take another downward turn.

Similar to Glasner, the Spaniard endured a slow start to life at his latest club, leading to speculatin he could be let go before a recent run of results bought him some time.

Should Fulham's results not improve following the international break when they face a tricky run of fixtures, Arbeloa will find himself in the firing line once again.
