Let me explain laser hair removal first. In a laser hair removal session, your provider will use a handheld laser device that uses intense pulsed light (IPL) all across your skin where you want less hair to grow. The laser uses concentrated beams of light to target the melanin in your hair follicle and destroy it using heat. This slows down hair growth and can even prevent future growth for a limited time. You won’t have to worry about not having enough to get the desired effect you want, because remember, laser hair removal isn’t truly permanent and will require touch-ups. You also need quite a few appointments depending on your hair type. Some with thick, coarse hair could need up to 16 appointments, while those with fine, thin hair might only require eight. It’s really personal, which is where an unlimited package can take away some of the stress.
