Latest Update: All About Maxx Morando, Miley Cyrus’s Husband

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Miley Cyrus and Liily drummer Maxx Morando are officially married. The couple tied the knot in Palm Springs, California, on November 21, nearly five years after they were first romantically linked. The ceremony took place poolside beneath an arch of white flowers, with Cyrus’s mother, Tish Cyrus, and sister Noah Cyrus among those in attendance.

Dating rumors linking the two musicians first bubbled up at the end of 2021, after they were seen together in Miami while Cyrus was in town for her Upcoming Year’s Eve special, Miley’s New Year’s Eve Party for NBC.

Recent findings suggest that there were also glimpses of them dancing backstage. Months later, in March 2022, Entertainment Tonight confirmed the two were together, with a source calling their relationship “low-key.”.

In a recent development, the couple enjoyed their love story in private and ultimately made their awards show debut at the Oscars in March 2025. That December, they unveiled their engagement.

Here’s what we know about Maxx Morando and their romance’s history.

Before joining the four-person Los Angeles rock band Liily, Morando played drums for the punk rock band The Regrettes from 2015 to 2018, and he even performed on Conan in 2017. He and Cyrus have many mutual friends and have known one another as friends for a while.

Cyrus and Morando were together on the red carpet at the Gucci Love Parade fashion show in Los Angeles in November 2021. But a source confirmed their relationship to E! in January 2022. “She’s happily dating Maxx,” said the insider. “It’s official between them.”.

In a recent development, the source added, “They are both artistic and creative, so they bond over being musicians,” and confirmed Morando joined her at the NYE party. “He was backstage with Miley and Pete [Davidson] at the New Year’s Eve rehearsals and taping having a good time with them. They were are all super friendly with one another and seemed to have a great night.”.

Entertainment Tonight added in a report published on March 1, 2022, that the two have been together privately for some time.

“Miley and Maxx have been having a good time together,” the source said. “They’ve been together for a little while and are just enjoying their relationship. Miley likes the fact that they are low-key as a couple and is just focused on doing her thing and being around people that make her happy, and Maxx does that.”. Then, on April 14, 2022, the couple was photographed kissing in West Hollywood, further confirming the relationship. They then walked down the street shoulder-to-shoulder.

Sources indicate that nearly a year later, in March 2023, Cyrus and Morando confirmed they were still happily together. They made a joint appearance, holding hands, at the Versace show in Los Angeles hours before she dropped her album Endless Summer Vacation. Later that night, they celebrated the new release together at a Gucci party. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

In September 2021, Cyrus mentioned Morando while talking to Vogue about her performance looks, attributing a print of cartoon monsters to the drummer, which he collaborated on with designer Shane Kastl.

Industry observers note that “This look is a one-of-a-kind collaboration between two of my favorite emerging artists, Maxx Morando and Shane Kastl,” she said. “Their collaboration is the perfect example of sustainability becoming the next fashion phase, and it proves that anything can be reinvented, which resonates with me deeply.”.

Cyrus also debuted a new song on NYE 2021 called “You,” which she said was so new she didn’t have a chance to record it yet. It later ended up as a track on her 2023 album Endless Summer Vacation. It’s been speculated that it’s about her new boyfriend. The lyrics go:.

“I got some baggage / Let’s do some damage / I am not made for no horsey and carriage / You know I’m savage / You’re looking past it / I want that late night sweet magic / That forever lasting love / But only if it’s with you.”.

In October 2022, a source told E! News that “Miley and Maxx are still going strong and their relationship is very serious...They have been together for about a year now, and Miley is really happy.”.

They added, “Everyone in her family loves them together and thinks they mesh really well.”.

Sources indicate that cyrus had apparently sold her home in Franklin, Tennessee, and moved to Los Angeles, the source remarked, explaining, “She wanted to take a step back from a crazy work schedule recently. She’s in a good place.”.

Years later, on May 9, 2025, Billy Ray Cyrus shared a selfie featuring his daughter and his son Braison to celebrate the latter’s 31st birthday. Morando also made an appearance in the photo, being hugged by Miley.

A Cyrus source told People in March 2023 that Morando is a “cool guy,” and the lack of drama attached to him has been really nice for Cyrus.

Recent findings suggest that “He has no interest in being a huge celebrity; he’s very low-key. Miley loves these qualities,” the source remarked. “Miley can be herself. They are supportive of each other’s careers. Miley is enjoying life and things are great.”.

According to recent reports, believe it or not, it’s true. Cyrus told British Vogue, “Well, it was blind for me and not really for him,” she clarified. “I thought, ‘The worst that can happen is I leave.’” Luckily, it turned out much better than that.

Another fun tidbit: His ringtone on her phone is “Tyrone” by Erykah Badu. “I call that ‘boyfriend sexy ringtone’,” she told the mag. Usually, though, Morando is the one with the phone. When Endless Summer Vacation was released, Cyrus didn’t have hers on her. “My boyfriend brings his phone, and I don’t bring one,” she said.

Cyrus was spotted out at a concert that Morando’s band Liily played on Oct. 11, 2023, in Los Angeles. Photos of her with brown hair alongside Morando surfaced on X.

On Feb. 4, 2024, the couple attended the Grammys together.

Industry observers note that the couple was caught on camera having a very intimate moment at Cyrus’s Grammys table. While they didn’t walk the red carpet together, Morando joined her inside the ceremony. Cyrus won her first two Grammys that night and performed onstage.

She seems more calm and harmonious. Everyone loves Maxx. He’s a great guy. She is very happy with him. He’s private and doesn’t like being in the press. It’s been good for her.”.

Sources indicate that on February 16, Cyrus and Morando walked the red carpet at SNL50, a celebration of Saturday Night Live’s 50th season. Cyrus performed at the live-streamed event and Morando was by her side.

The couple posed together on the red carpet.

As Cyrus and her friends and family were posing for photographs, Tish reportedly referred to Morando by saying, “We’re gonna bring the husband.”.

In an interview with Wonderland, Morando reflected on proposing to Cyrus during a trip to Japan, explaining that he deliberately waited until the end of their vacation to pop the question.

“We were in Kyoto, Japan,” he said. “For some reason—you’ll find this as crazy as I did—she’d never been to Japan before. Neither had I, and we’d both wanted to go for a really long time. There were talks before we went about me popping the question, but I was trying to keep it secret.”.

Industry observers note that he continued, “I had to be really sneaky about it. We went to Tokyo first for a couple days and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming.”.

Morando recalled proposing in a garden, saying, “It was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation. She was taking videos and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”.

It has been revealed that he admitted he was nervous. “I was sweating,” he noted. “I could feel my heart pounding. You grow up as a kid watching the movies where the guy gets on one knee and does the whole thing, and you know that’s what you’re supposed to do. But then you realize, ‘Okay, I literally have to get on one knee.’”.

On September 1, Cyrus and Morando were photographed together during a casual outing in Los Angeles. Cyrus wore a dark brown sweatshirt with loose black pants and black flip-flops, carrying a black leather tote. Morando coordinated with her laid-back look in a fitted white tank top, dark green drawstring pants, and a brown baseball cap.

During a September 17 interview with Zane Lowe for Apple Music, Cyrus revealed that she suspected Morando might propose during their trip to Japan—and even searched him for a ring while they were out walking.

“Me and Maxx were actually going on a walk…and because I won’t allow anything to happen organically for this man, I was like, ‘Oh, I’m gonna pat him down. I bet he has an engagement ring. This would be the best time,’” she recalled. “I didn’t find it!”.

Morando ultimately managed to surprise her. Cyrus recalled, “I turned around ‘cause I gasped and he was on one knee. He totally got away with it. He’s on the ground like, so sweet, and has a thing and claims, ‘You’re the love of my life. Will you marry me?’ I was like, ‘Oh my God, I almost botched it.’ He was like, ‘So bad. You really almost truly ruined it.’”.

Cyrus also reflected on what their relationship means to her now. “This part of my life is something I’ve always wanted, and this is what I hope other people get to have, what I have,” she stated. “I don’t know if everybody gets to find their Maxx.”.

On November 19, reports surfaced that Cyrus and Morando are planning to get married in Southern California over the weekend of November 21 to 22. The couple has not confirmed the news.

Cyrus and Morando got married in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, November 21. They had a poolside ceremony that was officiated by the Flaming Lips frontman Wayne Coyne, a longtime friend of Cyrus’s.
