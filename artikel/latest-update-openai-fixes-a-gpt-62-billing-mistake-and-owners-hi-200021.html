Latest Update: OpenAI fixes a GPT-6.2 billing mistake, and owners hit limits 40% faster

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that openAI says it made a billing mistake when it debuted its newly introduced models, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. Its fix means paying ChatGPT consumers will hit their usage limits faster. Compared with last week, the same work will reach the limit 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol. OpenAI executive Thibault Sottiaux announced the fix on X on Thursday, as Business Insider reported.

“We made a billing mistake at launch of GPT-6.2 that incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage for most people. We’ve fixed this now, so the same work will use more of your strategy’s usage allowance,” Sottiaux wrote.

According to recent reports, openAI will not charge customers for the difference on past usage, Sottiaux said. Both models introduced on 17 November. (Related: Supercharge your OCR Pipelines with Open Models...)

“As a small token to apologize for the mistake, we’ll be adding one banked reset for everyone on Go, Plus, Pro, and Business,” he wrote. (Related: Granite 4.0 Nano: Just how small can you go?...)

Other models are not affected, Sottiaux said, according to Neowin. Users should still get more out of GPT-6.2 than out of GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol, he added. OpenAI’s pricing pages did not change, the site added.

Reddit owners reacted with scepticism, TechRadar reported. One user called a single banked reset “pretty thin” as an apology. At launch, OpenAI had remarked both models would let users get more done within their usage limits.

It has been revealed that usage costs are a problem across the industry. Musk has said Grok Bot’s costs were unsustainably high relative to revenue, Business Insider noted.

According to recent reports, the fix lands a day after OpenAI began merging ChatGPT’s Chat and Work tabs for some Pro users. Under the new system, both will draw on a single usage allowance, an OpenAI spokesperson told TechCrunch.

Industry observers note that most users should not hit their limits more often, the spokesperson remarked. A small share of the heaviest users may. Chats with the Instant model stay unlimited and do not count towards the shared allowance. OpenAI plans to roll out shared limits more broadly in December, according to its support page.

In a recent development, openAI also gained a fresh hire from Anthropic on Wednesday. Thariq Shihipar, who left Anthropic in October, said he has joined OpenAI. He named Sottiaux and Cat Wu, Claude Code’s former product lead, among his new colleagues.
