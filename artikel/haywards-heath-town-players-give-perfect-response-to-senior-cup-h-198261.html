The Blues were thumped in their home cup clash with higher-division Broadbridge Heath nine days ago but responded with a 2-0 SCFL Premier win at Roffey thanks to Josh Wright’s double.

It has been revealed that boss Green mentioned: “It was a very vital win. They’re up there and competing so it was similar to the East Grinstead game – it’s nice to get the wins against the teams up there with you.
