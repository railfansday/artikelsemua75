Sources indicate that disney's 8-part supernatural fantasy epic returns this Friday with a highly anticipated new chapter featuring Greek gods, monsters, and an epic quest. With its latest 8-part installment, this Disney+ original series will increase its total episode count to 24 across three seasons, making it a perfect choice this weekend for a long-term binge. With its near-perfect 97% Rotten Tomatoes score, which includes a perfect 100% rating for its newly released third season, this is not only the biggest release on Disney+ this weekend, but on all streaming services.

According to recent reports, percy Jackson and the Olympians returns with its 2-part season 3 premiere this Friday, November 20, on Disney+ and Hulu. Based on the massively successful book and movie franchise, season 1 earned 13.3 million views in its first six days, placing it as one of the most anticipated fresh arrivals on any streaming platform this weekend. The new installment adapts Rick Riordan's The Titan's Curse, marking the first-ever on-screen adaptation of the source material, following season 1's The Lightning Thief and season 2's The Sea of Monsters. According to recent reports, there is an extra emphasis placed on the newest season of Percy Jackson and the Olympians because of the novel on which it is based. Before Percy Jackson became one of the most popular series in Disney+'s streaming library, it was previously adapted into two feature films, which adapted the first two books of Riordan's beloved series. The first two seasons of Percy Jackson and the Olympians rebooted the franchise for television, beginning with the two previously adapted books.

It has been revealed that now, The Titan's Curse has gotten its long-overdue rendition for the screen after the movie franchise essentially collapsed following negative reception from both critics and audiences, as well as underwhelming performances at the box office. Even Riordan himself made note of the two movies' failures, stating publicly that he was disappointed by some of the creative deviations made from his novels. Riordan, who was not involved in the production of the two films, serves as an executive producer of the Disney+ streaming adaptation.

According to recent reports, while there is a strong slate of upcoming releases across multiple platforms this weekend, including Netflix's The Altruists, Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3 is the biggest streaming event right now. Critics have already shown unanimous support for the new season, as evidenced by its 100% Rotten Tomatoes score. ScreenRant gave the series 8/10 stars, noting, "Everything about Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3 is more ambitious, from the duels to the visuals, characters, prophecies, and plotlines.". The Titan's Curse is among the top-rated books in Riordan's epic book series and paves the way for even more popular novels to be adapted through the Disney+ platform, including The Battle of the Labyrinth and The Last Olympian. Much like the Harry Potter franchise, the Percy Jackson novels become darker and more mature with each new installment, which marks the start of a new era for Disney’s Percy Jackson series.

According to recent reports, the Titan's Curse offers a significant tonal twist from previous novels, sending its titular protagonist on his most dangerous quest yet that results in serious loss and consequences. This paves the way for the full-scale war that's to come later in the book series, offering Disney's adaptation a concrete evolution of characters, story, and franchise as a whole. While Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 4 has yet to be announced by Disney+, it would be an outrage at this point if the Disney+ series did not make it to the finish line.

If you love the Percy Jackson books or simply love major franchises based on bestselling books, the 2-part premiere of Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3 has to be at the top of your Disney+ watchlist this weekend as it aims to land the streamer's #1 worldwide viewership spot from VisionQuest.
