The biggest game of the year and the biggest superhero team-up of the year collide as Marvel's Shang-Chi star Simu Liu uses GTA 6 to promote Avengers: Doomsday.

Over on Twitter, Liu shared a mock screengrab of the fresh game on Twitter, which reads, "Lucia will appreciate this: Buy tickets for Avengers: Doomsday." According to recent reports, there is some confusion as to whether this is a real screenshot. However, it looks like the graphic was made by artist Rafael Caban, who is taking a break from Doomsday art to play GTA 6. The mock-up replicates optional tasks that players can choose to complete when they play as Jason. Completed tasks boost your relationship rating with Lucia. The way your relationship pans out affects the overall story.

Sources indicate that it wouldn't be that out of the ordinary to see a Doomsday Easter egg in the newly introduced game, as fans have already found a bunch of nods to a number of TV demonstrates and movies in GTA 6. This includes Dexter's Miami apartment from the hit Showtime crime series, as well as references to Game of Thrones author George R.R. Martin. As for Marvel, the game includes an X-Men comic and a figure who looks a lot like the Hulk. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Recent findings suggest that however, we don't think Marvel fans need a reminder from Lucia to buy tickets for the upcoming Marvel Phase 6 movie. Presales crossed $50 million at the start of September, 100 days before release. Some reports now suggest that the movie has crossed $120 million, but Marvel Studios has yet to confirm.

Doomsday is set to follow "courageous teams from three universes" who must band together to face Robert Downey Jr.'s Doctor Doom. The synopsis continues, "Their choices and actions in this crucial moment will lay the foundation for the future of the Multiverse." Alongside Liu, the movie also stars Chris Hemsworth's Thor, Chris Evans' Steve Rogers, the Thunderbolts, a bunch of the original Fox-era X-Men, the Wakandans, and more,.
