Huang’s affair with a woman suspected to be a Chinese spy became the main headline news in the tech press after coming to light at the end of last month. The Newly introduced York Times broke the story on October 29, citing it then as “a close relationship.” Nvidia initially denied that the relationship was anything out of the ordinary.

Sources indicate that that changed on November 8 when Huang publicly admitted to the affair. Nvidia’s shares dropped 5%, and the firm’s board of directors opened an independent investigation. Further details emerged over the following days. Things got even worse when the same paper reported that Huang had also had an affair with an Nvidia executive who reports to him.

That led to Huang being fired resigning on November 12.

That changed today, with the firm making the announcement in a press release.

Nvidia today declared that effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board. The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang following a series of discussions and meetings with Mr. Huang, after which the Board was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead NVIDIA. In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.
