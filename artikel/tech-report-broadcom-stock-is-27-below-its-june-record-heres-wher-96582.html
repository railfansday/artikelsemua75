It has been revealed that broadcom (AVGO -12.19%) shares trade around $353 as I write this, about 27% below the record $481.57 they closed at on June 2. But in the quarter Broadcom reported in September, the chip designer's non-GAAP (adjusted) earnings per share surged 96% from a year earlier.

Where could Broadcom stock be by the end of 2030?

It has been revealed that I think it could hit around $600. That'd be a rise of about 70% from today's price, or about 14% a year.

Recent findings suggest that this estimate hinges on one earnings path, based mainly on Broadcom's artificial intelligence (AI) chips, and one ending valuation. It also comes before the company's fiscal fourth-quarter report on Dec. 9, which might change both. (Related: Latest Update: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

And it relies most on one assumption: that a really short list of customers keeps buying. (Related: How we sped up transformer inference 100x for 🤗 API cus...)

Sources indicate that showing how fast the AI business is scaling, Broadcom's AI semiconductor revenue climbed 221% year over year to $16.7 billion in the fiscal third quarter of 2026 (the period ended Aug. 2, 2026). Management sees fiscal Q4 AI revenue of $21.7 billion, an acceleration to 236% growth, which would take the fiscal 2026 total to about $58 billion. Custom AI chips, which Broadcom designs for specific customers, made up 73% of the third-quarter total. And AI networking revenue, from the chips that tie those processors together, climbed over 2.5-fold from a year earlier.

In a recent development, management also said on its September earnings call that it's locked in the supply to double AI revenue again in fiscal 2027, to about $115 billion. It sees a path to $230 billion in fiscal 2028, and it expects networking to grow just as fast as the custom chips.

"As a result, I got to say we are very much on target to exceed $30 in earnings per share in fiscal 2028," CEO Hock Tan noted on the call.

I use that $30 as my base for fiscal 2028. Then, I assume earnings growth slows to around 10% a year as the largest data center build-outs mature. This would put earnings per share at about $40 in fiscal 2031.

It has been revealed that today, the stock price is around 18 times the fiscal 2027 earnings per share analysts expect on average. The S&P 500 (^GSPC -3.70%) traded at about 18 times forward earnings in mid-November, according to FactSet.

Sure, Broadcom is growing far faster than the index now. By the end of 2030, though, investors will probably be pricing the stock on fiscal 2031 earnings, and growth should be much slower. So I assume a lower price-to-earnings ratio of around 15.

At 15 times forward earnings of about $40, the stock would be worth about $600.

That means all the gain in my estimate comes from earnings growth, with investors paying less for every dollar of Broadcom's profit than they do now.

Sources indicate that profitability looks like a smaller risk. Management expects adjusted gross margin to ease to around 73% in fiscal Q4, from 78% a year earlier, as memory-heavy custom chips grow. But it sees the adjusted operating margin staying at about 66%, flat year over year.

Sources indicate that the bigger risk is who buys those chips. Broadcom estimates that its top five end customers made up around 55% of its fiscal third-quarter revenue, versus about 40% a year earlier. In the first three quarters of fiscal 2026, that share was about 50%.

Recent findings suggest that broadcom makes custom AI chips for six customers. Management expects Anthropic to become the largest of them in 2027, and OpenAI the second largest in 2028.

The company's latest quarterly filing warns that losing any top-five customer, or a big fall in demand from one, could have a material adverse effect on its business.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Industry observers note that what if the significant deployments are around two years late? Then earnings per share might hit $30 in fiscal 2030 and about $33 in fiscal 2031. At the same 15 times forward earnings, the stock would land near $500 instead, for yearly gains of about 9%.

That's still a decent return. But a customer that stops buying entirely could do much more harm.

All in all, I think around $600 is a fair estimate for Broadcom stock at the end of 2030. If those customers keep building, today's price looks reasonable to me for a firm with that path ahead of it. But that's just a guess, and much of it rests on spending decisions Broadcom doesn't control.
