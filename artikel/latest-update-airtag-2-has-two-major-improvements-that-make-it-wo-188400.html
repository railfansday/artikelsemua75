AirTag 2 (now only $18, down from $29) launched earlier this year after a five-year wait, and in my experience there are two massive improvements that make it worth the upgrade.

Recent findings suggest that precision Finding is Apple’s best AirTag feature, letting you get step-by-step, highly precise directions to your lost item. But with the original AirTag, you had to be in fairly close proximity before you could benefit from Precision Finding.

With AirTag 2, Apple added a second-generation Ultra Wideband chip that makes Precision Finding now available from up to 1.5x farther away than before. It’s a huge increase that I’ve definitely noticed. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

According to recent reports, this expansion of Precision Finding to greater distances means the feature is now available a lot more often than with the old AirTag. For this reason alone, I highly recommend AirTag 2.

Sources indicate that when you’re trying to find a lost item, Precision Finding isn’t the only tool AirTag offers. There’s also a built-in speaker that can play a sound, triggered by your iPhone, to help you track it down.

In a recent development, with AirTag 2, Apple has made the built-in speaker 50% louder than before. The manufacturer says this enables hearing it from up to twice as far away.

According to recent reports, I’ve been impressed by how loud the AirTag speaker is able to get now. Between the fresh speaker and upgraded Precision Finding, Apple has made AirTag 2 better in the ways that people are likely to care about most.
