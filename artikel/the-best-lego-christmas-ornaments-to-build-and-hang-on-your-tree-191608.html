And considering how often LEGO retires its seasonal sets, some of the older designs have become fun little collectibles in their own right.

According to recent reports, this duo snowmen set is particularly cool because of how much personality LEGO managed to pack into such a small set. The larger snowman sports a top hat and scarf, and carries a candy cane and gift, while the smaller one has glasses, a winter hat, and a tiny lantern. Both come with hanging loops, though they look just as cute sitting together on a windowsill. It's also a great little project for a parent and child to work on together.

It has been revealed that if you’re hoping to decorate your tree with more than just one or two LEGO ornaments, this is the set I’d go with. It comes with four builds: a wrapped Christmas present, Santa Claus, a snowman, and a particularly expressive reindeer. They're all relatively flat, colorful designs, so they should fit nicely among your existing ornaments without taking up an entire branch.

Newly introduced to LEGO’s holiday lineup for 2026, this ornament collection picks up where the previous selection left off. According to recent reports, not every Christmas ornament needs to be red and green. This retired seasonal LEGO set features a little penguin bundled up in red earmuffs and a geometric snowflake, both of which come with strings for hanging.
