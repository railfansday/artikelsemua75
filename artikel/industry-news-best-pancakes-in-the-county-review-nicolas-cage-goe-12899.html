You have to admire Nicolas Cage’s penchants for loyalty and consistency. His latest effort reunites him with director-screenwriter Ken Sanzel, with whom he previously collaborated on 2019’s Kill Chain, a film that few saw and even fewer liked. And it features the sort of gonzo trademark Cage performance for which he’s become both admired and ridiculed, in this case featuring a degree of misplaced commitment to the bit that definitely doesn’t pay off. The only saving grace of Best Pancakes in the County is the excellent recipe advice about the titular food that will definitely improve your breakfast gatherings.
