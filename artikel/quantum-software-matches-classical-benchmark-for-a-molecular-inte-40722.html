Quantum software matches classical benchmark for a molecular interaction

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 21 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (66 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that predicting how molecules interact is central to drug discovery: it offers vital information about how a latest drug might work in the human body, the side effects it can cause and more. Computationally modeling these interactions can reduce the need for lengthy and expensive lab work, but some interactions still remain beyond the reach of classical computers.
