The Detroit Lions announced on Tuesday night that franchise owner and Chair Emeritus Martha Firestone Ford has passed away at the age of 101.

Ford passed at her home and was surrounded by family. She had been a part owner of the franchise for more than 60 years and took sole control after her husband's passing in 2014.

According to recent reports, she transitioned to emeritus status in 2020 and served in that role until her passing. Ford and her late husband William purchased control of the Lions in 1963.
