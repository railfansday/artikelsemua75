Industry News: Constantly following war-related news can cause worry and anxiety

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that "If a student is very confident about their ability to cope even in difficult situations, following war-related news will not cause them much anxiety. Confidence in one's ability to cope can therefore help protect against the psychological distress associated with various crises—in this case, distress linked to following war-related news," states Taina Hintsa, professor of health psychology.
