Sources indicate that I thought we'd already gotten our fill of prehistoric creatures on the 2026 movie schedule, thanks to The End of Oak Street. The film sees Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor spending a summer trying to survive a neighborhood full of creatures that should have been extinct for millions of years. Then, as soon as the movie hit streaming, the internet started arguing over footage supposedly showing a pterodactyl flying over Mexico. The timing feels a little too perfect.

Industry observers note that the latest monster-of-the-week discussion took off on November 13, when someone posted images to Reddit's r/Cryptozoology forum. The poster claimed the winged creature had been caught on airport security footage in Ciudad Juárez and that additional witnesses had photographs. None of that was verified, but you can probably guess how the comments went. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

I'll give the image this much: It's pretty cool. The creature has an enormous wingspan, a long, pointed head, and a silhouette you'd expect to see flying over the Platt family's house in The End of Oak Street. Of course, it also looks suspiciously like something you'd find in Jurassic Park. Several Reddit users noticed that resemblance, too, and weren't particularly convinced. There's apparently more to the sighting. According to Mexican outlet El Financier, an earlier video, reportedly recorded October 29 in the Anapra area of Ciudad Juárez, highlights a mysterious winged figure flying across the night sky. Jaime Maussan, a Mexican broadcaster known for his interest in UFOs and other unexplained phenomena, picked up the footage and helped spread the story. Before long, additional alleged sightings circulated online, and naturally, the internet had a field day.

It has been revealed that no cryptic sighting is worth its weight in dino dung unless the internet loses its collective mind, and the comments weren't disappointing. One commenter with the username r/alfred0t0rnad0 pretty much summed up my feelings:.

I WANT TO BELIEVE, but I just can't.

According to recent reports, somebody else was considerably more enthusiastic about the possibility of prehistoric creatures making a comeback. R/AsadAliShadowhunter wrote:.

At this point, I hope the Dinos are back.

Look, I'm not opposed to a little prehistoric excitement. I'd just prefer anything with a 20-foot wingspan to stay away from my backyard.

There's also the small matter of what local authorities have stated about the footage. EFE's fact-checking team reported Wednesday that Ciudad Juárez's civil protection director, Sergio Rodríguez, dismissed the recordings as digital entertainment content. One widely shared version also appears to show signs of AI manipulation, although the origins of the various clips haven't all been established.

According to recent reports, unfortunately for those of us hoping The End of Oak Street had somehow become a documentary, there's no credible evidence that a pterodactyl is flying around Mexico. Still, I have to admit I've enjoyed watching the internet speculate over the possibility.
