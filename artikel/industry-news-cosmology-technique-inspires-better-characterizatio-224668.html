Industry News: Cosmology technique inspires better characterization of ocean swirls and waves

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (67 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, A upcoming study from Caltech uses a mathematical framework borrowed from cosmology to analyze ocean data and characterize individual flow features in the churning chaos. With the technique, the researchers are able to measure and characterize features in maps of the ocean surface, allowing them to differentiate ocean flows that otherwise look identical when evaluated with standard analysis tools.
