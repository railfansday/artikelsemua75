There aren't many network TV franchises that have been around as long as NCIS, and it certainly has the fan base to show for it. The NCIS franchise has been around for well over 20 years, and while it may have begun with just one, it has turned into a web of the most talented operatives around the world working together to stop crime. One creator who has been involved with the franchise for years now is R. Scott Gemmill, who first worked with LL COOL J and Chris O’Donnell on the hit series, NCIS: Los Angeles. He reunited with LL COOL J all these years later for the new NCIS series, NCIS: New York. If Gemmill's name sounds familiar, it's because he's also the creator of The Pitt, the fan-favorite hospital show on HBO Max starring Noah Wyle.

According to recent reports, new episodes of both the original NCIS series and the aforementioned LL COOL J-led spin-off, NCIS: New York, can be seen on CBS every week. As for the prequel show, NCIS: Origins, new installments also drop every Tuesday, and all three shows are set to come to a head next year. Each series is going on a mid-season break soon, but, CBS still has plenty to give NCIS viewers in the next few months. Last week, Paramount announced a handful of new release dates for its mid-season primetime schedule for the 2026-2027 network TV season. This confirmed that the fan-favorite spin-off NCIS: Sydney, will premiere its fourth season on Tuesday, January 26 at 10 pm, with NCIS: New York airing a special original episode after the AFC Championship game on CBS on Sunday, January 31. NCIS, NCIS: Origins, and NCIS: New York will also return from their mid-season breaks on Tuesday, January 12, with NCIS: Origins airing its Season 3 finale one week later, on Tuesday, January 19.

Recent findings suggest that NCIS fans who are looking for another series to scratch the signature crime thriller itch should look no further than Tracker, which stars Justin Hartley and Jensen Ackles. Lost veteran Josh Holloway also recently debuted in the latest episode of Tracker, which already has fans asking more questions about potential spin-offs. More watchable CBS indicates include Cupertino, the newly introduced legal thriller starring Mike Colter from Marvel's Luke Cage franchise, Matlock, the legal drama starring Kathy Bates, as well as other series such as Ghosts, Einstein, and even Survivor.
