Mykhailo Mudryk has revealed that he wanted to join Arsenal.

The Tottenham winger, on loan from Chelsea, admitted that before he signed for the Blues in January 2023, he had wanted to move to north London.

He made the switch from Shakhtar Dontesk in Ukraine for an initial £61million fee, that could rise to as high as £89m.

Before he made the move to Stamford Bridge, he had been heavily linked with a move to Mikel Arteta’s side, but ultimately, he decided to join Graham Potter’s Blues instead.

Now, he has declared that his real desire was a move to Arsenal, which never materialised.

“Before going to Chelsea, I wanted to join Arsenal. It’s no secret,” he told the Ukrainian podcast ‘Guest of Gordon’. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

“Arsenal were the club I wanted to play for, and moving to the Emirates Stadium was my dream.

“There weren’t even any attempts to make my dream come true and move to Arsenal.

“The attempts from my previous agent were only to earn more money.

According to recent reports, “But at the time, I didn’t know about it. I only understood about the commission and all the rest later on.”.

Mudryk reunited with his former Shakhtar boss Roberto De Zerbi at Spurs on Deadline Day in the summer transfer window, having been cleared to play after a doping ban for meldonium which began in October 2024.

The winger made headlines on Wednesday when he blamed his country’s football association for his positive drugs test, and ex-Chelsea striker and now-president of the Ukraine FA, Andriy Shevchenko, has demanded an apology from the 25-year-old.

Mudryk played just one match for the north Londoners - away at Nottingham Forest - before suffering a “stupid” ankle injury in training, according to his manager.

He is expected to return to action this weekend, however, when Spurs take on Ipswich Town at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, looking to get back to winning ways after a 1-0 defeat at Leeds before the international break.
