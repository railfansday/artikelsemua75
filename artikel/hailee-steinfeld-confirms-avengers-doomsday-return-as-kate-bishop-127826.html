📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hailee Steinfeld will return to the Marvel Cinematic Universe as Kate Bishop in the forthcoming “Avengers: Doomsday.”.

Sources indicate that marvel Studios and Disney Animation revealed the news Sunday with an Instagram post featuring Steinfeld. “Hey, so I’m here with my movie ‘Hexed,’ but it looks like they delivered a chair from a different movie,” remarked the actress before panning to a black director’s chair with her name on it. “Catch Hailee Steinfeld in Disney’s ‘Hexed’ before she returns in ‘Avengers: Doomsday,'” read the caption. (Related: School sends out minibus to catch absent pupils playing...)

Steinfeld previously appeared as Bishop in the 2021 Disney+ series “Hawkeye” and in 2023’s “The Marvels.” In “Hawkeye,” the “Sinners” actress shared the screen with Jeremy Renner’s superhero, while she appeared briefly in a post-credits scene alongside Brie Larson, Teyonah Parris, Iman Vellani and more for “The Marvels.”.

“It’s really a privilege to be able to play a character that could in any way possibly inspire anyone,” Steinfeld previously told Variety about embodying the beloved comic character. “Kate Bishop is a character that I would have loved to have had growing up — somebody who is completely ambitious, driven and disciplined, and takes it upon herself to do whatever it is she has to do to achieve her goals.”.

And while the full list of MCU characters who will appear in “Avengers: Doomsday” may still be a mystery, her former costar Renner has made it clear he didn’t return as Hawkeye. “No, no, no, no. Not in that one,” said Renner when asked if he was returning for “Doomsday” in an Oct. interview. “’Doomsday’ I’m not in, so the Avengers will probably lose, just so you know… they didn’t call me, so it’s their fault. They’re going to lose.”.

In a recent development, prior to Steinfeld’s announcement, Elizabeth Olsen’s Scarlet Witch was most recently revealed to be joining the film via the season finale of “VisionQuest.” A post-credit scene replayed the ending of 2022’s feature film “Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness,” where Wanda Maximoff eradicated the Darkhold from all of the multiverse before (seemingly) being crushed from within a mountain. But this time, the scene continues with Robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom appearing, lifting the rubble off Wanda and addressing her, confirming fan speculation that Wanda would return.

“Avengers: Doomsday” hits theaters on Dec. 18.
