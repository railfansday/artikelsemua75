Recent findings suggest that finding Port Gelhorn's missing tourists and the serial killer in Grand Theft Auto 6 takes you on a twisted journey around the town, one with few guideposts and a lot of chances to get stuck. It's a macabre scavenger hunt with only a few pictures and knowledge of the surrounding area to go by, though the reward at the end is comparatively small. This is a quest where the storytelling, more than anything else, is the reward.

Below, we explain where to find Gelhorn's missing tourists in GTA 6 and complete the serial killer side mission.

In a recent development, you'll first come across this exploration mission in the Starlet Motel office after Chapter 2 starts. Lucia can examine a set of missing persons posters on the wall next to Linda's desk. There's a tip line with a number to call, but it just takes you to a voice mailbox.

And that's the only hint to go off of, as the poster info gives you no clues and nowhere to look. There might be other clues hidden somewhere around town, but we didn't find them. Instead, we stumbled across the quest's starting location by accident. It's an old bunker-like warehouse across the street from Moses' garage, next to the scooter store. You can approach the front for a prompt to open the door, but it won't work. Walk down the right side of the warehouse, and look for a hole at the base. Crawl inside.

Recent findings suggest that despite how creepy the location is, there's nothing inside to harm you, though there is a jump scare midway through. Enter the red room and the trophy room beyond it, and interact with the photos there. You'll see locations showing each of the missing tourists, and these photos are stored in your phone gallery if you want to view them later. The quest prompt changes in the map menu, too, with a latest prompt for each photo.

In a recent development, you can track them in any order. We're just listing them in the order we found them. We recommend tackling this part of the quest during the day, just so it's easier to make out landmarks.

In a recent development, the junkyard is where you'll find Susanne Keeble, and it's next to the Pay 'n' Spray shop in central Gelhorn. You're looking for a pink car, and if you're having trouble finding it, activate focus mode. The car will stand out as the only interactable object in the area. Report the body to the tip line after you find it.

In a recent development, donald Miller's body is out at Wild Hog Point, the marshes outside Port Gelhorn. Head toward the tree with no leaves near the center of the marsh, and look in the water nearby to find a car. Interact with it, report the body, and move on. Someone picks up the line this time.

In a recent development, patrick Murray is the final tourist, and his body is in a trailer at the far north end of Port Gelhorn in an abandoned trailer park near a water tower. The trailer in question is the blue one. Head inside, and pull up the floorboards near the door to discover Murray's body. The clipping in the trophy room mentioned a reward for information about him, though, for reasons you'll soon discover, the tip line operator isn't ready to pay out.

The tip line man asks you to return to the warehouse after finding the third body, and the quest can change depending on whether you go alone or with your partner. We played as Jason and went with Lucia, though who takes the lead doesn't matter. Your partner stays outside, and tense events unfold inside, after which the culprit dies and the quest ends.
