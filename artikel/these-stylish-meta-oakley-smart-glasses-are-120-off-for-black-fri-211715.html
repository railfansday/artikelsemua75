According to recent reports, there's is no shortage of smart glasses on the market right now, but many of them have an awkward, clunky aesthetic that doesn’t exactly blend into your everyday style. If you’re looking for an alternative that won’t stick out like a sore thumb, the Oakley Meta HSTN smart glasses are a fun, sporty option, and during Amazon’s Black Friday sale, they're discounted to $359 (originally $479).

While they have similar core features to the Meta Ray-Ban Gen 2 (also 25% off for the holiday), these frames are sportier, and unlike the Ray-Bans, they shoot video in 3K rather than 1080p. They also have a bigger battery designed to last all day (up to eight hours on a charge, and five additional charges from the case, for a total of 48 hours). In addition, the two open-ear speakers have been repositioned, allowing for audio to be directed more effectively towards the ears with less sound leakage.

Recent findings suggest that available in a range of stylish frame and lens colors, PCMag dubs these the best “smart glasses for Instagram addicts” given their social-focused design; consumers can use the embedded cameras to snap POV videos or photos and upload them seamlessly to Facebook or Instagram.
