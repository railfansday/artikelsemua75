In a recent development, NASA’s James Webb Space Telescope reveals the hot dust that makes up Cassiopeia A (Cas A) in this high-resolution, mid-infrared image released on Nov. 23, 2026. Remnants like these are the clouds of gas and dust that remain following the explosion of a massive star and will eventually seed a upcoming generation of stars and planets. The Webb data supports a scenario in which the interior of the star underwent a dramatic change, possibly just hours before its demise.
