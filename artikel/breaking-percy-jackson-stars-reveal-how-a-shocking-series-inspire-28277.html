The cast of Percy Jackson and the Olympians reveals that an iconic anime series was the inspiration behind their most emotional performances.

Percy Jackson and the Olympians is a fantasy adventure series streaming on Disney+. Based on the novel series of the same name by Rick Riordan, the show follows a group of young demigods as they face mythological creatures and powerful gods as they go on a dangerous journey. Sources indicate that percy Jackson and the Olympians cast members Walker Scobell and Aryan Simhadri have revealed a surprising connection between the Disney+ show and the hit anime series Attack on Titan. The two actors recently discussed how they used fan-made videos featuring characters from Attack on Titan to make themselves emotional before filming some of season 3's most heart-wrenching scenes. During an interview on Josh Horowitz's Happy Sad Confused podcast at 92NY, Scobell shared an unusual acting technique that involved watching emotional compilations about Eren Yeager and Mikasa Ackerman from Attack on Titan. Simhadri admitted that she did the exact same. However, the two both used this method before ever finding out that the other did it too.

Sources indicate that scobell, who portrays the titular demigod Percy, explained that he had been looking for something that could make him cry to prepare for an emotional scene. He explained that he watched fan edits of Eren and Mikasa while listening to "Red Swan," the opening theme of Attack on Titan season 3. He had hoped that the combination of the characters' emotional storylines and the music would be enough to bring him to tears, as it had worked in the past.

Recent findings suggest that however, the technique unfortunately did not produce the same results as it did previously once the actor arrived on the set of Percy Jackson and the Olympians. Despite successfully making himself cry before, Scobell admitted that he wasn't able to recreate the moment and cry before filming began.

Simhadri, who plays Percy's best friend Grover Underwood, revealed that he had also turned to Attack on Titan edits to get ready for emotional scenes. The actors only discovered their shared method shortly before discussing it publicly, making the whole situation a hilarious coincidence for the two actors.

First released in 2013, Attack on Titan became an instant hit and critically acclaimed for its complex characters, action sequences, and emotional storylines. Adapted from Hajime Isayama's manga, the series follows the fight against giant, terrifying creatures known as Titans, which eventually turns into a much bigger problem.
