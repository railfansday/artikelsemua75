Liverpool will not prioritise a move to sign Monza striker Gustavo Varela in January, sources have told Football Insider.

The 21-year-old has emerged as a target for the Reds, having struck 11 goals from his opening 11 games in Serie A so far this term.

The Reds are "keeping tabs" on the Portuguese youngster, with several top Italian clubs also having their "eyes fixed on him" at the moment.

However, sources say that Varela is viewed as more of a long-term option at Anfield, and the club are not likely to make a move for him this winter.

The Monza ace is currently the second-highest goalscorer in Serie A, having only made the switch to Italy from Gil Vicente in the summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Monza are relaxed over their frontman's future for now, but they know that further interest from top clubs is inevitable if he continues to perform.

Liverpool are well-stocked for striker options at the moment, and that is set to put off any chance of a move for Varela in January.

Alexander Isak is the clear starter for Andoni Iraola at the moment, who also has the option of playing Cody Gakpo in a central role.

Hugo Ekitike is also edging towards a return to the fold, which will provide another quality option to lead the line.

However, Liverpool are in no doubt that they will continue to keep a close eye on Varela's progress, given his eye-catching start in Italy.

The starlet is currently outperforming his xG by over four goals, and his 11 goals have come from just 32 shots, giving him an impressive conversion rate of over 34 per cent so far this term.

Liverpool are hugely impressed by what they have seen from Varela in recent months, but they need him to prove his quality over a longer period if they are to commit to a move.

Former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness recently told Football Insider that Liverpool's move to tie Alexis Mac Allister down to a latest contract "will get done" sooner rather than later.

The player's father has been trying to leverage interest from Real Madrid in order to push for the best deal possible at Anfield.

Wyness has no doubt that Mac Allister will pen a upcoming deal, with talks over the planned extension now underway.

However, he also suggested that it may not keep him at the club long-term, with Liverpool potentially planning to sell the 27-year-old after another two seasons.
