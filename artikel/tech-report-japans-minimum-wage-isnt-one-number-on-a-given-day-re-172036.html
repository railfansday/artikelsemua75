📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Japan sets its minimum wage per prefecture (47 of them), and each prefecture's upcoming rate starts on its own effective date. For FY2026 most prefectures switched in October/November, but as of 24 Nov 2026 three had not switched yet: Iwate and Kumamoto (1 Dec) and Okinawa (2 Dec).

Sources indicate that so "what is the minimum wage in Okinawa?" has two right answers depending on the date. Payroll checks, job boards, and AI agents that answer "is this job offer legal?" get it wrong when they cache one "current" value.

The official source is MHLW. The yearly list is a PDF; the history (H14 to R8) is an XLSX that also carries the previous rate and its effective date. I parse both and abort the build on any mismatch (all 47 matched).

ISO dates compare correctly as strings. With this, the staggered 1 Dec / 2 Dec switch is right on every date with no rebuild. After the end of the fiscal year, add a note that a newer rate may apply instead of silently serving a stale one.

In a recent development, users and LLMs send Tokyo, Toukyou, Hokkaidō, Kochi/Kouchi, JP-13, 13, 東京都, 東京. What worked:.

Recent findings suggest that my endpoint is pay-per-call (x402, 0.01 USDC on Base). Unknown prefecture, a bad date or a bad section returns 400 before the 402, so nobody pays for a typo.

It has been revealed that if you also return overtime rules: the 25% overtime / 35% holiday premiums are set by a cabinet order (政令), not the Act itself; the Act only holds the 60h/50% and 25% night rules.
