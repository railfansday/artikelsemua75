Meghan and Harry are preparing to spend Christmas in the U.K. with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, and Meghan’s mother, Doria Ragland. The holiday will mark the first time Meghan and Harry have celebrated Christmas in Britain with their children, following the family’s move back across the pond from California earlier this year.

In a recent development, according to an exclusive report from People, the couple are hoping to keep the holidays relatively low-key. “She wants the holidays to be as quiet and enjoyable as possible for the family,” a source noted. It has been revealed that harry also offered a glimpse into their Christmas looks during a surprise appearance on the British morning show This Morning. He joined hosts Alison Hammond and Dermot O’Leary to discuss the upcoming 2027 Invictus Games in Birmingham, the international sporting event for wounded, injured, and sick service members and veterans. He also shared insight into what it’s been like to be back in the U.K. and their plans for the holiday.

Industry observers note that harry shared that Meghan and her mother will likely be taking charge of the holiday cooking. “You know what, it will probably be my wife,” he stated. “My wife is very good in the kitchen and so is my mother-in-law, so I’ll leave that to them.” As for the menu, Harry suggested that gumbo could make an appearance alongside a more traditional holiday meal. “Maybe gumbo, yeah, maybe turkey, maybe gumbo,” he said.

The couple’s children are also looking forward to the festivities and seem to be settling in well.

Sources indicate that “It’s a big culture shift,” he mentioned, but “their confidence has gone through the roof since being in U.K. school, which is really, really nice.”.

He went on to jokingly say that they and their friends are trying to get them to have accents.

When asked what Archie, 7, and Lilibet, 5, are most excited about, Harry stated, “I think they’re looking forward to snow,” though he acknowledged that a white Christmas isn’t guaranteed.

“It’s always interesting when you’re living overseas and looking back in, you can see all sorts of stuff happening, and I think now that I’m back everything feels quite familiar and similar,” he said before adding that “obviously there’s lots of change, but as far as London, as far as the U.K., it’s home.”.

The family’s holiday prepares will look different from the traditional royal Christmas at Sandringham, which Harry and Meghan are not expected to attend. The couple last joined the royal family’s Christmas celebrations there in 2018, the year they married. Archie and Lilibet have never attended.
