If there’s one Apple product line where the recent price hikes hit the hardest, it’s the MacBook. Rising RAM and storage costs have pushed laptop prices up across the board, which makes scoring a upcoming MacBook on sale all the more mission critical.

Recent findings suggest that as luck would have it, Black Friday deals on Apple tech are already ramping up on Amazon, including discounts on MacBooks. While there’s always the chance these deals might continue to deepen in the next week, the current discounts are nothing to sneeze at if you can’t wait that long to shop. Sources indicate that of course, not all MacBooks were created equal. While the standard MacBook Air is great for most, those who are planning on running hardcore software might be benefit from a beefed-up MacBook Pro. And if budget is your primary concern, we’re of the opinion that the MacBook Neo is a worthy buy, even if it’s only $50 off right now. The best MacBook Air deal you’ll find right now is this $250 discount on the 15-inch model. With this laptop, you’ll get Apple’s M5 chip in a fanless, 3.3-pound frame that handles heavy multitasking and light video editing without breaking a sweat. Base storage doubled to 512 GB this year—a great upgrade for storing more apps, photos, and files—and the 15.3-inch Liquid Retina display is sharp and vivid. You also get a 12 MP Center Stage camera that’s great on video calls, WiFi 7 for fast downloads, and up to 18 hours of battery, enough for a busy workday and then some. It won’t replace a MacBook Pro for serious creative work or gaming, but for school, business, and general use, it’s the one to get.

Industry observers note that deals on 13-inch models aren’t as steep—yet. But we expect that to change in a week’s time.

It has been revealed that given that Apple just dropped a latest slate of M6 MacBook Pros, in addition to the manufacturer’s first-ever touchscreen laptop, we were anticipating discounts on the M5 range to clear out inventory. In the lead-up to Black Friday, you can shave $200 off of the 14-inch model, complete with a Liquid Retina XDR display with 120Hz ProMotion, up to 24 hours of battery, three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, an SD card slot, and up to 2 TB of storage.

Why go Pro over the Air? The GPU is fast, which makes a difference when editing video, working with high-res photos, or gaming, and the XDR screen is significantly brighter. If your daily workload has started becoming too much for the Air to handle, this is where you should look next. If you want the biggest, most powerful model possible, souped-up MacBook Pros are up to $500 off.

The MacBook Neo is Apple’s most affordable laptop, even with the recent price hike from $599 to $699. Right now, the Neo is modestly discounted, although we’re holding out hope that it might drop down to its inaugural price—or even lower—once Black Friday proper arrives. The Neo is an easy recommendation for anyone looking for their first Mac. It runs on Apple’s A18 Pro chip and is surprisingly quick for browsing, sending email, and streaming video. The 13-inch Liquid Retina display looks great for the price, and the battery lasts a solid 16 hours. It also comes in four fun colors, with the yellow-green citrus being a fan favorite.

There are some trade-offs with going with Apple’s budget model, including storage that starts at 256 GB, just 8 GB of RAM, and no keyboard backlight. You also get only two USB-C ports, so getting a USB-C hub is a must if you initiative to connect a lot of accessories. Spending an extra $100 gets you a Neo with 512 GB of storage and a Touch ID sensor for quick unlocking. For students or anyone who mostly sends emails and browses the Web, the Neo is hard to beat.
