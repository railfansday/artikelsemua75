A man was found guilty in a deadly attack against his girlfriend, who he stabbed and beat with an icepick and a sledgehammer before forcing his housemate to help him burn her body, federal prosecutors said.

Thomas Fields, Jr., of Washington, D.C, was convicted on Thursday, November 19, in the murder of 24-year-old Marquita “Keke” Lucas and the kidnapping of his housemate, the father of his nephew, in August 2019, as detailed in a news release from the U.S. Attorney’s Office for District of Columbia.

According to recent reports, fields, 42, was found guilty in D.C. Superior Court on several charges, including first-degree murder while armed, kidnapping while armed, assault with a dangerous weapon, possession of a firearm during a crime of violence, unlawful possession of a firearm, and tampering with physical evidence. According to prosecutors, Fields murdered Lucas inside his home on August 17, 2019, after he had been sending Instagram messages blaming her for causing the fatal overdose of his child’s mother, who died earlier that month, per DC Witness.

In court, prosecutors said Fields “stabbed her in the head with an ice pick over and over,” and “left Keke lying in a pool of blood,” the outlet reported.

He also allegedly beat her with a sledgehammer.

Following the attack in which he inflicted fatal injuries, Fields woke one of his housemates and highlighted him Lucas “lying in a pool of blood,” prosecutors said in the release. (Related: One Familiar Item Of Clothing Scored Lowest for Intelli...)

Fields used a gun to threaten the man into helping clean the aftermath of the attack and to help get rid of Lucas’ body, according to prosecutors. Fields also allegedly threatened to kill the housemate and the man’s children, which includes Fields’ nephew.

Afterward, they both drove to Maryland with Lucas’ body, then doused her in gasoline along a road in Prince George’s County, prosecutors said.

They set Lucas on fire, according to prosecutors.

After burning her body, Fields had his housemate dump four trash bags containing evidence at two different Maryland apartment complexes, prosecutors said.

The men bought some bleach before returning home.

Fields allegedly admitted to two family members that he killed Lucas.

During the trial, prosecutors mentioned Fields was “proud” of the murder, telling jurors he said, “I told you fool…they’re gonna write a story about this,” according to DC Witness.

Fields’ defense attorney, Shawn Sukumar, however, asserted his client was innocent and that the allegations against him were made by the housemate, who the attorney argued can’t “be trusted,” the outlet reported.
