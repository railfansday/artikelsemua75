Good morning. Ladies and gentlemen, I've been informed that there is a quorum present. And as it is now 10:00 a.m., I declare this scheme meeting of Titomic Limited open and welcome you to this first virtual meeting. My name is Dag Stromme, Executive Chairman of Titomic. Firstly, I would like to thank you for your interest in and support of the company and for your interest in our U.S. redomiciliation by the way of a scheme of arrangement. The proposed redomiciliation represents an vital step in Titomic's evolution into a global advanced manufacturing and defense technology company. Establishing our parent company in the United States is intended to strengthen our alignment with our largest growth opportunities, improve access to U.S. capital and strategic partnerships, support our expanding manufacturing and operational presence and provide enhanced oversight of U.S. defense operations.
