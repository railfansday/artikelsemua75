Swansea City identify manager with 2.32 points per game as leading contender to replace Vitor Matos

According to recent reports, swansea City are pursuing a newly introduced head coach after Vitor Matos left his role at the Swansea.com Stadium to take charge of RC Strasbourg. Ashley Williams, Leon Britton, Joe Allen and Kris O’Leary are leading the Swans on an interim basis, but ground is swiftly being made in the search for a new boss.

It has been revealed that according to a report from The Telegraph, the club have identified Icelandic manager Freyr Alexandersson as the leading candidate for their vacant head coach position. Talks have been held between the 44-year-old and the Championship club, and he appears to be their prime target at present. (Related: Hawaiian Interisland Awards — Hop Between Islands...)

In a recent development, alexandersson is presently the manager of Norwegian side Rosenborg BK, who appointed him on a three-year contract in June. He has overseen 14 wins in his 19 games in charge of the club, recording a points-per-game rate of 2.32. (Related: Industry News: Hyatt Category 1 Hotels — The Easiest Po...)

Alexandersson is a 44-year-old coach who has held a variety of roles in the dugout since beginning his coaching career back in 2008. He spent seven years at Leiknir Reykjavik as a player before moving into management.

According to recent reports, the Swansea City target started out in women’s football, managing club team Valur before a short spell in charge of former club Leiknir Reykjavik. Alongside this role, Alexandersson spent five years as manager of the Iceland women’s national side.

He then stepped into the men’s setup as an assistant to Erik Hamren and Arnar Vidarsson before a short spell as a No. 2 at Al-Arabi. From 2021 though, Alexandersson has worked strictly as a first-team boss in men’s football.

Sources indicate that he spent nearly three years in charge of Danish side Lyngby BK and 11 months at KV Kortrijk in Belgium before moving to SK Brann in Norway. Impressive work there saw him poached for the Rosenborg BK job, where he has continued to impress.

Sources indicate that swansea City head coach contender Alexandersson prioritises man management, working closely with his players to build strong relationships and placing importance on leadership. He has a strong reputation for developing players, which will appeal to the Swans too.

Tactically, his Rosenborg side set up in a 4-3-3 primarily, but he has used 5-3-2 or 3-5-2 formations in the past. Much like Matos, his teams press aggressively in the final third, and he demands frequent chance creation with players directed to suffocate opponents on and off the ball.

In terms of tactical similarities, Alexandersson feels like a great fit for Swansea City. The fact he wants his players to press high and play forwards much like Matos means that there can be a smooth transition into the newly introduced management.

Sources indicate that bringing in a boss with completely different ideas could bring teething problems. This squad has been built to play on the front foot with and without the ball, and Alexandersson will ensure they do so.

It has been revealed that the Jamestown Analytics influence feels clear with this pursuit. They pride themselves on finding gems in lesser-used markets, and Alexandersson could be the latest top boss uncovered.

After initial talks, time will tell whether the Swans can strike a deal to bring Alexandersson to Wales.
