The defensive depth is rather limited amid Matteo Gabbia’s lengthy injury absence, while Fikayo Tomori also sustained an issue, meaning a reinforcement in that area of ​​the pitch will be needed in January. This morning’s edition of Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews) confirms the No.1 centre-back target: Goncalo Inacio. Milan tried to snatch him from Sporting CP in August, but the Portuguese club repeatedly turned them down, having already sold Ousmane Diomande to Nottingham Forest.

The Rossoneri haven’t abandoned the idea of ​​reuniting him with Amorim, and so in January they will make another attempt for the 25-year-old, who is eager to join a top club. However, a significant offer of at least €40m will be needed to convince Sporting.

Industry observers note that the management believe that another central defender is needed too, so they’re starting to monitor some prospects for next summer. Among the players being closely monitored is Gabriele Calvani, who is on loan at Frosinone from Genoa.

It has been revealed that he has impressed so far and has a current valuation of €10m that could rise. For now, signing a young prospect isn’t a priority for the Rossoneri, as they are primarily looking for someone who can slot immediately into the starting XI.
