In a recent development, pokémon's next trading card expansion isn't launching until Feb. 6, 2027, but collectors can already secure preorders for Flowing Fists — including everything from single booster packs to 36-pack Display Boxes.

Preorders for the Pokemon TCG’s Flowing First expansion are already live on TCGplayer — which features over 110 cards for the Mega Evolution set, including Mega Lucario Z ex, Mega Garchomp Z ex, Mega Absol Z ex, and Mega Glimmora ex. In a recent development, flowing Fists Single Pack Blister (Tynamo) — $19.98 for one booster, a Tynamo promotional card, and a collectible coin. We expect this one to sell out quickly. It’s available a cent less than the standard booster pack, with the Tynamo promo essentially free.

Flowing Fists Booster Pack — $19.99 for one pack containing 10 random cards.

Flowing Fists Sleeved Booster Pack — $28.88 for a single 10-card booster in protective cardboard packaging.

Flowing Fists 3-Pack Blister (Wimpod) — $38.87 for three booster packs and an additional Wimpod promotional card.

Flowing Fists Build & Battle Box — $59.96 for four boosters, a 40-card playable deck with one of four foil promos, and a TCG Live code.

Recent findings suggest that flowing Fists Booster Bundle — $69.96 for six booster packs containing 10 cards each. That's approximately $11.66 per pack.

Flowing Fists Elite Trainer Box — $179.94, including nine boosters, a full-art foil Riolu promo card, 65 sleeves, 40 Energy cards, a player's guide, damage-counter dice, a coin-flip die, a plastic coin, a collector's box with six dividers, and a TCG Live code.

Sources indicate that flowing Fists Booster Display Box — $250 plus $14.99 delivery, for 36 boosters. At around $7.36 per pack, this at the moment offers the lowest per-pack cost.

According to recent reports, all prices include shipping unless otherwise stated, with TCGplayer sellers estimating dispatch on Feb. 6 (the expansion’s release date).
