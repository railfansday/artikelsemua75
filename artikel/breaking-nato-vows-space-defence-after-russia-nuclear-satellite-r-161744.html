Breaking: NATO vows space defence after Russia nuclear satellite report

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (77 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, NATO chief Mark Rutte said Monday the alliance would defend against threats in space when asked about German media reports that Russia may be preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite. The media reports, citing unnamed German military and BND spy agency sources, said Moscow was thought to be planning such a launch in the coming weeks, giving it the capability to destroy other satellites. FRANCE 24's Laurent Berstecher reports.
