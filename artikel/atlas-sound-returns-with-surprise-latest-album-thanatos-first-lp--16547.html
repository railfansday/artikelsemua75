Atlas Sound, the solo project of Deerhunter singer Bradford Cox, has emerged from a decade-and-a-half hiatus with the surprise fresh album Thanatos, out now digitally.

Arriving 15 years after 2011’s Parallax, Thanatos marks the third and final chapter in an album trilogy that began with 2009’s Logos; Cox himself had been out of the spotlight for the better part of seven years, as Deerhunter also hadn’t released an LP since 2019’s Why Hasn’t Everything Already Disappeared?

However, after a long musical dormancy, Atlas Sound reemerged this year with a couple of demonstrates, followed by the Halloween release of a new song titled “Octoberland”; that track now serves as the opener on Thanatos. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

Cate Le Bon, who produced Deerhunter’s Why Hasn’t Everything Already Disappeared?, praised Cox in a statement that accompanied Atlas Sound’s new album.

“Bradford Cox has always existed in extremes. Embracing and transcending the contradictions inherent in an artist reaching for all the corners. Impossible to capture or imitate,” Le Bon said. “Each vocal take is a performance. Each instrument played like it’s his last night on earth. That could always be true. Without this awareness of mortality; love, passion and creation lose substance.”. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

In a recent development, cox performed and recorded the entire 10-song album by himself “on a Tascam DP-008 in his attic in Grant Park, Atlanta.” “The Tascam, a tool to distil the process down to the fundamentals of subtraction and addition. The former a by-product of the latter. Nothing deleted but instead painted over with new emotion or the same gesture repeated with the urgency of the moment,” Le Bon added.
