Apple Working on Own AI Agent to Rival OpenAI and Anthropic

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (116 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that anurag Rana, Senior Tech Analyst for Bloomberg Intelligence, discussed the latest from Apple with Bloomberg's Paul Sweeney. Apple Inc. is developing its own autonomous artificial intelligence agent that can get things done on behalf of users, looking to enter a fast-growing market already being pursued by OpenAI, Anthropic PBC, Meta Platforms Inc. and others. The tech giant outlined the plans in job listings added to its website this week. In one, Apple noted it’s seeking a senior engineering manager for a proactive, agentic AI assistant. The system will write code, browse the web, control apps and navigate devices on a user’s behalf, according to the post. (Source: Bloomberg).
