In a recent development, every upcoming game has its… quirks. This is especially true in the modern era of gaming, as many games now release with game breaking glitches and bugs that have an effect on how people enjoy it.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is still fresh, and people are jumping into it as I write these words. While on the surface the game looks like magic, many people have been finding numerous bugs that have destroyed their experience. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Planes fly through buildings, cars teleport onto the highway, and it doesn’t seem to be bound to one console, as Xbox and PlayStation consumers have both been reporting these issues. (Related: Japan debuts a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t connect to inte...)

Textures and environments can be tricky, especially when you have a map that is as massive as the one in GTA 6. There are bound to be some barriers that end up becoming a bit glitchy, and players have found plenty of them.

There have been a number of reports that players have been glitching into walls inside buildings, and it seems to mostly happen when fighting.

Recent findings suggest that not all walls are affected, as it seems to only affect interior, lower cut walls, and not full size interior walls or exterior boundaries. Still it is pretty awkward when you want to fight someone and you both end up teleporting into a wall.

In a recent development, okay, so not taking damage isn’t necessarily a bad thing, but in the world of GTA, where things are supposed to be realistic, it is kind of strange. It would be nice to not take damage at times, but it enhances the realism of the experience.

Sources indicate that cars have always been a bit of a tricky thing, and they are still a bit tricky in GTA 6. Players have reported being able to teleport out of their car while flying down a street without taking damage.

Recent findings suggest that normally, you are supposed to dive from the car, but instead, players have said the character model just teleports out of the vehicle and starts running on the road until they come to a stop. It’s a bit strange.

Sources indicate that some players have noticed a peculiar thing while flying planes in GTA 6, and that is that they sometimes don’t respect the laws of physics, allowing them to phase through objects. It doesn’t happen a lot, but it is weird when it does happen.

It could be intentional, because it seems to only happen to large buildings, but it wasn’t like that in GTA 5, so it probably wasn’t an intentional choice.

Players have noticed that when they jump out of a plane, it seems to phase through all the buildings around it, allowing it to just crash into the ground instead of hitting the building and falling like it used to.

Cops can now tase you in GTA 6, and it is pretty amusing to watch yourself get tased. Just like in real life, it locks up your character’s muscles, and it makes them fall to the ground helpless. Shortly after, they are free to stand back up.

In a recent development, unfortunately, not every player has been able to stand back up, as some have noted that they get stuck in the ground after getting tased. Their character model clips into the pavement, causing them to get stuck in place for a while before their character gets released.

This can be a bit jarring, but it isn’t something that completely breaks the game. It just makes things a little easier for the police to catch you.

According to recent reports, early in the game, Donnie is taken hostage. The game uses this as an opportunity to teach you about stealth and combat. It’s a nice little tutorial that is very engaging. Unfortunately, some players have reported that they need to restart this level because the game breaks for them.

It has been revealed that when sneaking around the enemies, you will spot a cage. This cage has a tendency to be a bit buggy, and sometimes you will get pulled into it if you decide to take cover against it.

This traps you inside the cage, unable to get out, and therefore, unable to complete the mission. The only thing that is worse is that if the enemies know you are in there, you basically become an easy target.

People have been waiting 13 years to get their hands on GTA 6. It has been one of the most talked about games of the last decade, and some people can’t even get into the game. Others who can, have it crash on them shortly after launch.

According to recent reports, this isn’t a console specific glitch, though I have seen more people say they have a PS5 when it comes to the crashing issue. The issue is that the game automatically crashes shortly after launch.

According to recent reports, people have mentioned that they have tried to rebuild the database, and have even redownloaded the game, and nothing has worked. It is okay for some bugs to occur, but the game crashing every time you try to play it is entirely different.

Getting wanted by the police is a common occurrence in any GTA game, but things are a bit different in GTA 6. The cops are more lively, and they notice more things. There are a lot of systems in place to make the entire experience more thrilling.

Industry observers note that well, these same systems tend to bug out a bit right now, and they bug out by giving you an infinite wanted level. Well, almost infinite, as there is a way to remove the wanted level.

In a recent development, there have been reports of players being stuck with a wanted level for hours despite clearing all of the complications with it. The only way to get it to end seems to be to go to sleep. Getting stuck being wanted in an area for hours sounds like a huge inconvenience.

Sources indicate that this is a bug that is affecting both consoles, but seems to be affecting Xbox more than PlayStation. Players have reported objects spawning as they drive at high speeds. By this, I mean cars will magically appear, so will NPCs, and other items.

GTA 6 is a massive game, and it could be that the loading has not been completely optimized, and when driving at very high speeds, the game may not be able to keep up with the load times.

I am not a tech wizard, so I don’t know exactly what causes it, but I do know that it has been bothering a lot of players, and they want it fixed ASAP.

This is an glitch that appears to mostly be affecting those who are playing GTA 6 on the Xbox Series S, but there have been reports of players being unable to load textures and models, resulting in these low-poly models that are reminiscent of graphics you’d see on PS1 era games.

Everything turns blocky, the faces lose all texture, and all that can be made out is the basic shape of the body, some scraps for clothes, and the hair.

It’s a shame that this is happening to people, and it reminds many of the crazy problems that were happening when Cyberpunk 2077 first rolled out. Odds are, it can be fixed with an revision, but for the time being, it sets a bad example.

A major part of any GTA game is committing crimes, and one of the many crimes that you will commit frequently is murder. This is mostly against gang members and other criminals, but it still counts, and you need these people to be able to take damage.

Sources indicate that you also sometimes need to stop an NPC by hurting them and not killing them. Unfortunately, there is a bug that makes NPCs bulletproof, and some players have been saying they are emptying entire clips into an NPC for not a single bullet to affect them.

This breaks the game because it makes it so you can’t complete some missions. It is an urgent issue that Rockstar should fix so people can beat the game.
