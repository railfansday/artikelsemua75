Sources indicate that ubisoft just laid off another 166 developers, the latest wave in a months-long tsunami of job cuts that also includes some of the devs behind Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced. Yet the manufacturer's latest financial report brags of impressive financial success, driven by the popularity of the remake.

"Net bookings stood at €389m this quarter, above guidance of around €370m," the company indicates in its fresh earnings release, which marks a "50% year-on-year" increase. "The outperformance was mainly driven by Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced," Ubisoft says, also acknowledging "strong performances" from catalog sales of Assassin's Creed and Ghost Recon games, as well as its Invincible mobile game.

In a recent development, ubisoft's superlatives don't stop there, either. "Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced outperformed expectations in its launch quarter," the release continues, "supported by solid in-game spending and a higher-than-expected share of premium editions. The game’s performance also benefited from a more targeted marketing approach.". (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Black Flag Resynced was one of the first games to be launched after the Ubisoft's "major reset," which saw the organization cancel and delay a multitude of games as it restructured its studios under five "creative houses.".

Ubisoft states, "Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced illustrates its ability to deliver games that players love and that also perform financially. The newly formed Assassin’s Creed leadership made clear, decisive choices in the final stretch of development. This enabled the game to achieve an elevated level of quality and a commercial performance that positions Black Flag Resynced among the industry’s most successful Action-Adventure remakes.". (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

According to recent reports, the firm doesn't specify which action-adventure remakes it's comparing against here, but nowadays the statement certainly invites a thought toward Nintendo's wildly successful remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Black Flag Resynced's 3.5 million sales in two weeks is no match for Zelda's 5.2 million sales in four days, but who knows how the post-launch sales measure against each other.

Recent findings suggest that still, all that financial success isn't enough to slow down Ubisoft's "rightsizing" operation, which has seen another 1,100 jobs disappear from the firm in the past six months alone. Ubisoft has been warning shareholders that losing "key talents and skills" is a big danger for the company, but that hasn't stopped it from cutting many of the developers responsible for its most successful games.
