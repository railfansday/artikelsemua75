COBRA is the federal law that generally lets workers temporarily keep health insurance after losing benefits by paying the full premium out of pocket.

The memo blamed Rippling for the disruption and cited Foundation’s last pay run through the administrator in October as the reason benefits ended retroactively. Rippling remarked Foundation had struggled to meet payroll and benefits obligations for months. Under its Professional Employer Organization policy, clients that cannot meet those obligations for multiple months first receive a warning and are terminated if the obligations remain unmet.
