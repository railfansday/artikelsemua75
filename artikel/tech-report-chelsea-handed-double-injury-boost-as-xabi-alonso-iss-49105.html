Xabi Alonso has provided Chelsea with positive news about Moises Caicedo.

The Blues manager mentioned on Friday afternoon that the Ecuador captain is fit and available for Saturday’s Premier League clash with Leeds at Stamford Bridge.

Caicedo asked to be substituted at half time in his country’s 2-0 win over Bolivia on Tuesday and later said it was because he did not feel well and had “other commitments with my club”.

That had prompted some Chelsea fans to fear the midfielder might be an injury doubt against Leeds.

But Alonso has allayed fears by revealing the 25-year-old is available to face Daniel Farke’s side.

“Yes, it [Caicedo’s early withdrawal for Ecuador] was discussed and he was sensitive after playing the first whole game that he could save some minutes.

“That was good for him and he’s fine for tomorrow.”.

Alonso ruled out Joao Pedro, Maxence Lacroix or Reece James featuring in the game but he can choose between Mike Penders and Emi Martinez in goal.

“He is not fit yet to play for tomorrow and it’s a similar situation with Joao,” Alonso said in answer to a question about Lacroix.

According to recent reports, when asked whether Josh Acheampong or Jorrel Hato are fit, Alonso replied: “Josh, yes, Jorrel, no. Jorrel has a back problem and we need to be patient because it’s going to take a while.

In a recent development, “Jamie Gittens, he’s better. He’s already training with the team but we want to give him more time to come back in the squad. Reece, he’s getting there.
