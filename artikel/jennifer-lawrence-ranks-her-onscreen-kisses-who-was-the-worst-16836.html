Jennifer Lawrence just ranked her on-camera kisses from best to worst — and it’s bad news for Chris Pratt.

The Oscar winner, 36, was asked to evaluate her screen smooches over the years during an appearance on the Thursday, November 19, episode of Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen. “They were all so amazing,” Lawrence playfully said before naming Hutcherson, 34, as her top onscreen kiss.

Next came Hunger Games costar Liam Hemsworth.

In third place was Silver Linings Playbook costar Bradley Cooper and in fourth was Timothée Chalamet, with whom she shared a steamy scene in Don’t Look Up.

Lawrence’s Passengers costar Pratt, 47, ranked last among the five.

In 2015, the actress talked about sharing intimate scenes with Pratt — who was married to Anna Faris at the time — in a roundtable interview with The Hollywood Reporter.

It has been revealed that “I got really, really drunk,” she continued. “But then that led to more anxiety when I got home because I was like, ‘What have I done? I don’t know.’ And he was married. And it was going to be my first time kissing a married man, and guilt is the worst feeling in your stomach. And I knew it was my job, but I couldn’t tell my stomach that. So I called my mom, and I was like, ‘Will you just tell me it’s OK?’ It was just very vulnerable. And you don’t know what’s too much. You want to do it real, you want everything to be real, but then … That was the most vulnerable I’ve ever been.”.

As for Hemsworth, 36, Lawrence previously admitted to kissing her hunky Australian costar offscreen during a 2015 appearance on WWHL.

In a recent development, “Liam and I grew up together,” she told host Andy Cohen. “Liam’s real hot. … What would you have done?”.
