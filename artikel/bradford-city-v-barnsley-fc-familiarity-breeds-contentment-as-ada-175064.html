The attacking midfielder had four years at Barnsley, but arguably none better than their first when, under manager Michael Duff, they reached the League One play-off final.

Having come close a number of times, Phillips scored his first Bantams goal at home to Peterborough United at the end of October, and added two more and an assist against Marine in the FA Cup.

Being reunited with Michael Duff is bringing more out of the 28-year-old, hampered by injury in the early part of the season. He is determined to repay the manager’s faith.

"Working with a manager before always helps, especially for me because I probably had one of my best seasons there," says Phillips, part of the Barnsley team which took Sheffield Wednesday to extra-time in the 2023 League One play-off final.

“He puts an arm around me and gives me that confidence to go out and create chances and score goals.

“But he's also on me for getting fit and doing lots of running. So I need to keep working hard. He puts trust in me, so I need to pay him back.”.

It was not damaged by the fact in that 2023 Wembley final Phillips’s red card left Barnsley playing the majority of Duff’s final game in charge with ten men.

“There was just a little bit of banter (when Duff spoke to him for the first time since, having just taken over at Valley Parade) because I got sent off the last time I played under him," states Phillips. "But he got his move to Swansea anyway, so it was all good.”.

Duff was brought in as a "continuity" candidate because of his perceived similarities to Graham Alexander when his old Burnley team-mate left to manage Preston North End in September.

“He's not changed a lot, really," says Phillips. "He's implemented some of his idea but we're still getting results and doing a lot of the right things.

“He’s not just going to come in and rip it all up.

“But I think slowly he might add a few little things that he wants to do, which is fine, and the lads will get time to work on it on the training pitch.

“The lads are buying into it and you can see it on the pitch.”.

After a good start to the season, Phillips believes Duff can go one better than he did with Barnsley in League One. Following four league games without a win, the pressure will be on to take one at home to the bottom-of-the-table Reds.

“What the club achieved last year was really special, having just come up from League Two," states Phillips.

Sources indicate that “But the ambition this year is to be near the top and be fighting for promotion. We've got a great squad with a lot of talented players and we've started off really well.

“For the lads, it's just keeping the feet on the ground and taking each game as it comes.

“The fans always get excited and they can have their opinion but as a team and as a squad, we just keep ticking away and getting results and putting points on the board.”.
