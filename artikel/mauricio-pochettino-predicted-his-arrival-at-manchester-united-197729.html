It has been revealed that as the Argentine coach approaches the Red Devils bench, a statement made a few months earlier resurfaces in England. According to The Standardthe current coach of the United States would have reached a verbal agreement with the Mancunian leaders to succeed Michael Carrick. A situation which today gives particular resonance to an exchange that occurred during the 2026 World Cup. Questioned by journalists, Pochettino had joked with his faithful deputy Jesús Pérez about a possible arrival at Old Trafford. “ One day, your dream team will come true! You will go to Manchester United », he told her. His colleague immediately replied: “ United should have signed you! » Amused by this response, the former PSG coach then concluded by repeating: “ in the future, in the future! » A joke which should therefore become reality a few months later, while Manchester United now seem decided to entrust him with control of their first team.

This discussion notably referred to previous missed opportunities between Mauricio Pochettino and Manchester United. The Argentine technician had already been a candidate for the position in 2018 then in 2022, without managing to convince Mancunian leaders to entrust him with the project. Since then, the former Tottenham and Chelsea manager has never completely closed the door on a return to the Premier League. Earlier this year, he told The Team that he remained open to this possibility, despite his responsibilities at the head of the American selection. Now, the negotiations appear to have progressed considerably, although several administrative obstacles remain. Manchester United must still reach an agreement with the American Federation to release Pochettino from his contract, the departure clause of which would be estimated at 9 million euros, not including possible compensation for his assistants. Furthermore, the English club formalized the departure of Michael Carrick this Thursday. With 294 games managed in the Premier League, Pochettino will return to the English championship for the first time since his departure from Chelsea in the summer of 2024.
