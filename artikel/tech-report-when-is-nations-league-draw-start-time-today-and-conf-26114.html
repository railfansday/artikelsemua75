England will face Norway in the Nations League quarter-finals, and they could lock horns with a familiar foe in the last four should they make it.

It will be the first time that Thomas Tuchel’s side have played the Scandinavians since the World Cup quarter-finals, where England won out 2-1 in Miami, Florida thanks to a brace from Jude Bellingham after Andreas Schjelderup’s stunning opener.

Tuchel’s men finished top of Group A after a stunning 3-2 victory over world champions Spain in Madrid last weekend.

Luis de la Fuente’s European champions, meanwhile, will take on France in their last-eight clash, with the European giants set to face off for the first time since the World Cup semi-final, where La Roja emerged 2-0 winners thanks to Mikel Oyarzabal and Pedro Porro’s goals. The remaining two fixtures are the Netherlands against Portugal, and Belgium facing Germany.

The four quarter-finals will take place in March next year, with the winners going through to the finals in June next year.

The semi-finals were also revealed, with England on a collision course with historic rivals Germany, should the Three Lions get through Norway and Jurgen Klopp’s men beat Belgium.

The victors of Spain vs France will take on the winners between the Netherlands and Portugal.
