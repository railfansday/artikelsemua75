Industry observers note that rian Johnson successfully brought the star-studded murder mystery back to the significant screen with Knives Out, which has continued on with two Netflix sequels. He also turned to the small screen for a similar project that earned rave reviews for its two seasons, but now, unfortunately, the mystery is gone. Johnson recently revealed that, despite an attempt to revive it in a very unconventional fashion, the series has taken on its last case.

Poker Face debuted in 2023 on Peacock. The series starred Natasha Lyonne as Charle Cale, a woman with the uncanny talent to detect lies. Pursued by criminals after solving a murder, she took to the road in her 1969 Plymouth Barracuda; invariably, wherever she found herself, she'd be called upon to use her skills to sniff out a criminal. The series was cancelled by Peacock after its second season, but Johnson pitched the series to other outlets with a twist: Lyonne would be replaced by Peter Dinklage, who would be playing the same character.

Unfortunately, now we'll never find out how Johnson would have pulled off that strange metamorphosis. In a newly introduced interview with Variety, Johnson revealed that the series is dead and buried. Johnson ultimately wasn't able to make the re-cast make sense: "I got very excited about the notion of going out there with Dinklage, but then the more we kind of explored it, the more it kind of didn’t really make sense." However, he is hoping to collaborate with the Wicker star in the future: "...hopefully I can do something else with him someday. He’s the best." Johnson is staying busy: he's producing Joseph Gordon-Levitt's new thriller 2034, and is gearing up to begin production on a new science fiction conspiracy thriller of his own next year. In the second season of Poker Face, Cale crosses paths with a mysterious assassin known only as the Iguana, even as she found a upcoming friend in the form of Alex (Patti Harrison, I Think You Should Leave). However, after stumbling upon the Iguana's latest victims, Charlie realizes Alex is the Iguana; bored by her life as an assassin, Alex had trailed herself to fool Charlie's senses. The two of them end up in the Barracuda as it speeds towards a cliff. Charlie escapes, but Alex seemingly perishes as it crashes to the ground. Charlie, now wanted by the FBI as the Iguana's accomplice, hitchhikes away with a trucker as the wrecked Barracuda is recovered with no sign of Alex's body.

In a recent development, both seasons of Poker Face are now streaming on Peacock. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.
