Most creators write the title first, then ask for a thumbnail that "pops". The result is often the same sentence twice: the title claims "How to edit on your phone" and the thumbnail says "Edit on phone". Viewers get one idea for the price of two.

The youtube-title-thumbnail-brief skill for Hermes starts from your idea and what the video really reveals. It proposes five pairs, each with a title, a short thumbnail text, and a one-sentence visual description. The angles differ: a test, a mistake to avoid, a before/after, a question.
