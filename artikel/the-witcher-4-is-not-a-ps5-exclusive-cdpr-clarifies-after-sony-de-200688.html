Did anyone really think The Witcher 4 was going to be a PS5 exclusive?

For those who missed it, CDPR confirmed yesterday that it has signed a marketing deal with Sony on its upcoming RPG.

While the details are light, it’s expected this will take the same shape as PlayStation’s arrangement with GTA 6, where the title’s promoted “like” an exclusive – even though it’s technically not.

But it seems there was some confusion online about the nature of Sony’s deal, which CEO Michal Nowakowski has now clarified on social media.

Sources indicate that “We’re looking forward to our partnership with PlayStation! And just to be clear: The Witcher 4 and our other upcoming titles will remain multiplatform.”. (Related: Building for an Open Future - our new partnership with ...)

It’s as expected really, but Nowakowski still faced follow-up questions about whether Ciri’s story may launch first on PS5, before other platforms. (Related: Breaking: Introducing AnyLanguageModel: One API for Loc...)

“All the platforms release at the same time: PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. No one gets left behind.”.

Of course, this is all entirely as expected – the original comments mentioned a marketing deal only, and I don’t think Sony could afford to keep a game as major as The Witcher 4 off other platforms, to be brutally honest.

It does hopefully mean the game will be optimised for the PS5, though, taking advantage of unique features like the DualSense et al.

I’m sure we’ll learn a lot more about how Sony and CDPR are collaborating once we get closer to the game’s release.
