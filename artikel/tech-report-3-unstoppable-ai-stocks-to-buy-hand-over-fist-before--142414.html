According to recent reports, consistently investing in businesses that provide an essential service to the economy is a simple way to select winning investments that can power your retirement. This is what makes a stock truly unstoppable for a patient investor. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

It has been revealed that substantial tech's artificial intelligence (AI) build-out depends on three components: software to design chips, machines to make them, and cloud platforms to put them to work. Here's why ASML Holding (ASML +8.68%), Microsoft (MSFT +2.67%), and Cadence Design Systems (CDNS +7.41%) are unstoppable stocks that can help you profit from this opportunity. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

ASML makes the machines that chipmakers use to print circuits onto silicon, and it's the only organization that builds the extreme ultraviolet (EUV) systems needed for the most advanced chips. Without these tools, chipmakers couldn't expand production of the chips AI runs on.

Recent findings suggest that demand for the company's machines continues to exceed supply, but ASML also benefits from servicing these machines over their life. Revenue from servicing and upgrading them jumped 68% year over year in the third quarter. The current high demand for latest machines could extend ASML's revenue opportunity for years to come.

The next generation, High-NA EUV, is catching on: Intel has run more than 1 million wafers on it, and Samsung and Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing strategy to use it in volume production in 2028 and 2030, respectively.

Recent findings suggest that down payments for future orders have grown this year. This is reflected in the growth of contract liabilities, which jumped from about 3.4 billion euros to about 9.3 billion euros since the end of 2025.

NASDAQ · As of 11:46 AM ET 11/23/26.

It has been revealed that the biggest risk for ASML is customer concentration. ASML's largest customer accounted for about 24% of 2025 sales, and China accounted for about 29%. If a major customer paused orders or export rules tightened, sales could come under pressure. But this would be a temporary hurdle, as ASML remains the only provider of the machines required to make chips that power the global economy.

The stock trades at about 22 times 2027 earnings estimates, with earnings expected to grow about 33% next year. That's reasonable for an essential equipment supplier to the semiconductor industry.

Recent findings suggest that microsoft is the cloud provider behind Azure, where much of the world's AI is rented and run. Azure revenue grew 48% year over year in the September quarter, and management expects 49%-50% year-over-year growth in the December quarter.

While Wall Street is being more critical of companies spending aggressively on AI, Microsoft is squeezing more money out of every chip. Management credits efficiency gains across its GPU fleet for "higher revenue per token," and it found ways to rent out underused processor capacity.

Moreover, customer demand is there: Commercial remaining performance obligations hit $677 billion, up 73%.

NASDAQ · As of 11:49 AM ET 11/23/26.

In a recent development, the stock has pulled back over spending. Microsoft expects roughly $170 billion of capital spending in calendar 2026, according to The Motley Fool's research, while trailing free cash flow has fallen to about $60 billion. Most of that spending goes to short-lived chips, so if AI demand slows, they could lose value and squeeze margins.

It has been revealed that still, Microsoft is a highly profitable software giant. Enterprises and consumers rely on its services every day to get real work done, which makes it unstoppable for patient investors. At about 25 times this year's earnings estimates, Microsoft trades at a modest premium to the S&P 500 average multiple of about 19x -- a reasonable premium for a manufacturer expected to grow earnings about 16% a year.

Sources indicate that cadence sells the software that engineers use to design and verify chips, plus hardware that tests designs before they're manufactured. All the top AI companies are working with Cadence, and without them, chip designers couldn't catch costly flaws before production.

Recurring revenue accounted for 79% of third-quarter sales, and the backlog reached $8.2 billion. It expects more than half of that backlog to be converted to revenue in the next 12 months. Its emulation hardware posted another record quarter, and its AI design agents have more than 100 active customer engagements.

Revenue rose 22% year over year to about $1.6 billion in the third quarter, but that includes the Hexagon design and engineering business it acquired. Excluding it, management says growth was still in the mid-teens.

NASDAQ · As of 11:46 AM ET 11/23/26.

In a recent development, integrating its acquisition is one headwind. GAAP operating margin fell to 26% from the low 30s over the last year. If integration costs linger, earnings growth could trail revenue growth, potentially weighing on the stock price.

According to recent reports, cadence is the priciest of the three stocks at about 36 times this year's earnings estimates, with expected earnings growth of about 17% a year. But it's worth paying up for the recurring revenue stream, which makes revenue more predictable and typically earns a higher premium from investors.

I believe all three are worth buying now and worth adding to on dips into 2027.
