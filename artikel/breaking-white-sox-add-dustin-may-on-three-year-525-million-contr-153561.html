Industry observers note that the upstart Chicago White Sox have added one of the game's hardest-throwing pitchers to their rotation. Chicago and righty Dustin May have agreed to a three-year contract worth $52.5 million, reports FanSided.

May, 29, split this past season between the St. Louis Cardinals and World Series champion Milwaukee Brewers. He threw a career-high 145 ⅔ innings with a 4.70 ERA, though the underlying numbers (3.63 FIP and 4.50 expected ERA) were more respectable. May's 96.9 mph average fastball velocity was 15th highest among the 141 pitchers with at least 100 innings in 2026.

According to recent reports, rotation help was a priority for a White Sox team that reached the postseason for the first time since 2021, but used openers and bullpen games regularly throughout the season.
