Mikel Arteta has imposed rules on his Arsenal players and their Fantasy Premier League teams.

The Spaniard is known to be particularly guarded when it comes to his team news and measures have now been taken to remove one avenue for leaks.

There are accounts on social media that track the FPL teams of players and monitor their transfers for hints on team news.

A team that seemingly belongs to Tzolis sparked interest ahead of Arsenal’s trip to face Liverpool earlier this month.

The winger had not played since picking up a hamstring injury at the start of October but he brought himself back into his FPL team before that match at Anfield.

Tzolis also captained himself and when his team then became public once the FPL deadline passed on the Saturday morning, it gave an obvious clue that he would start the game more than 24 hours before kick-off.

He did indeed then start at Anfield, in what was a surprise decision considering he had been out for a month.

It has been revealed that at that stage, Tzolis had himself, Riccardo Calafiori and Ezri Konsa in his team. Before the next match, at home to Hull, Tzolis used his wildcard and removed all Arsenal players.
