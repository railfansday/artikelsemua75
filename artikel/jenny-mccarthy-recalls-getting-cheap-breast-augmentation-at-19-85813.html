Jenny McCarthy is getting candid about her previously botched breast augmentation procedure.

“This was back when I was living on the south side of Chicago and all of my girlfriends were going to Arizona to get this boob job that was on sale for $1,500,” McCarthy, 54, recalled on the Thursday, November 19, episode of her eponymous podcast. “They would come back with the most gorgeous freaking cleavage you’d ever seen. Growing up as an athlete and not having a lot of meat on my bones, I craved boobs.”. (Related: Breaking: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

Following in her friends’ footsteps, a then-19-year-old McCarthy “saved up” her money to get her own procedure.

“I go to Arizona, I do the appointment. He’s like, ‘OK.’ I tell him, ‘I just want small C’s just so they’re not that noticeable.’ I go in for the surgery. I’m very, very nervous,” she said of the plastic surgeon. “Halfway through the surgery, I wake up on the table and I literally say to the doctor, ‘Um, I’m awake.’ And he goes, ‘Oh, yeah. Well, we’ve got just one more boob to go.’”.

McCarthy was admittedly “freaking out” that the anesthesia allegedly wore off before the second implant was even surgically placed in her chest. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

“He’s like, ‘Just hang out, just chill. You’ll be fine.’ I remember him saying, “You won’t remember a thing.’ I remember everything, buddy,” she quipped. “Now I know why it was so cheap ‘cause they didn’t put us to sleep!”.

McCarthy continued, “During a boob job surgery, I had no idea they did this, but once they put the implants in, they press a button … and the table rises you up to a standing position, and all of the doctors and nurses go to the other side of the room and they go and they’re literally examining you from across the room to see if you’re even.”.

Since McCarthy was awake, the examination was especially terrifying.

In a recent development, “Now, this would be fine if you’re asleep right now. Imagine you were me, OK? I’m standing there strapped to the table like this having these people stare at me to see if it’s even,” she mentioned. “I wanted to go home so bad. I’m like, ‘This was the biggest mistake of my life.’”.

Once the surgery was over, the Masked Singer panelist also had a difficult recovery.

According to recent reports, “I am throwing up everywhere,” McCarthy mentioned. “I was never in so much pain in my entire life because I felt the pain during the surgery. It made it a hundred times worse afterwards. … I should have known something was different when I was driven through a back alley for the entrance.”.
