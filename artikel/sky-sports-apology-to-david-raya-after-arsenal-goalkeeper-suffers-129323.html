Sky Sports commentator Bill Leslie was forced to offer a swift apology in the wake of the injury David Raya sustained during Saturday’s 2-2 draw against Newcastle.

The Gunners dropped two points after letting a commanding position slip at St James’ Park, but the damage done to their No.1 goalkeeper may end up being more significant than their failure to extend an impressive winning run.

Mikel Arteta confirmed in his post-match press conference that he would not learn the extent of the injury until the Spain international undergoes a scan.

Raya was hurt shortly after Newcastle had overturned a first-half deficit and taken the lead through Malick Thiaw.

The former Brentford star collapsed under seemingly little pressure from William Osula as he collected a corner in the 70th minute, but Leslie’s initial reaction implied the 31-year-old was trying to initiate a tactical timeout.

Instead, it quickly became apparent that Raya was in serious pain and would be unable to take any further part in a match that Arsenal ultimately earned a point from as a result of Noni Madueke’s excellent equaliser.

‘David Raya does indeed look in some discomfort,’ said Leslie as Raya made his way off the pitch.
