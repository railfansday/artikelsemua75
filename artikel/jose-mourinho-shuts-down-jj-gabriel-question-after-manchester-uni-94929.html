Jose Mourinho has distanced himself from Real Madrid’s move to sign JJ Gabriel from Manchester United.

Earlier this week, Gabriel confirmed his departure from United by posting a farewell message to the club on social media.

Gabriel requested the cancellation of his registration with United in September as his entourage were unhappy with the lack of first-team opportunities at Old Trafford.

‘You gave me moments I will always treasure. To every coach and every member of staff, to my teammates and everyone who supported me, thank you for believing in me and helping me grow,’ Gabriel mentioned.

‘As I begin my next chapter, Manchester United will always be a part of my story. Thank you for everything, JJ.’.

Gabriel was not short of interest from several clubs in the Premier League and in Europe, but he has now agreed to join Real Madrid, with the Spanish club now due to pay a compensation fee to United.

However, when asked about Madrid’s push to sign Gabriel during his press conference on Friday, Mourinho shook his head and noted: ‘I have nothing to say.’.
