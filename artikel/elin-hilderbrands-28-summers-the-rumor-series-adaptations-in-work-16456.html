Elin Hilderbrand’s ’28 Summers’ & ‘The Rumor’ Series Adaptations In Works At 20th Television With ‘Five Star Weekend’ EPs & ‘Perfect Couple’ Creator

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that EXCLUSIVE: Elin Hilderbrand‘s Nantucket TV universe is expanding to a newly introduced studio. 20th Television has optioned two popular novels by the bestselling author, 28 Summers and The Rumor, for Sue Naegle and Ali Krug‘s studio-based Dinner Party, which is behind Peacock’s popular adaptation of Hilderbrand’s The Five Star Weekend starring Jennifer Garner.

Industry observers note that jenna Lamia, creator of another hit series based on a Hilderbrand novel set in Nantucket, Netflix’s The Perfect Couple, is set to write and executive produce The Rumor. The 28 Summers and The Rumor series, both in early development, are executive produced by Hilderbrand, Naegle and Krug. They fall under the overall deal Naegle and Krug signed with 20th Television in September, moving their Dinner Party company to Disney from NBCUniversal. Inspired by 1978 film Same Time Next Year, 28 Summers explores the agony and romance of a one-weekend-per-year affair and the dramatic ways this relationship complicates and enriches their lives, and the lives of the people they love.

The novel, originally published in 2020, was previously optioned for development as a movie by MRC.

Written by Lamia based on Hilderbrand’s 2015 novel, The Rumor unfolds over a Nantucket summer in which island gossip turns dangerous, and the only person with the story straight is a local novelist who must decide whether to publish it.

The Bold Type co-executive producer Holly Whidden, a Nantucket insider who was a consulting producer on The Perfect Couple, also serves as executive producer.

According to recent reports, “I’m thrilled to have two of my most beloved novels adapted by Dinner Party, the production team who turned The Five Star Weekend into an elevated, spellbinding, and hugely successful series,” Hilderbrand stated. “I’m grateful to Karey Burke at 20th TV for championing my work and assembling such a quality team, including Jenna Lamia, who created The Perfect Couple. I could not be more optimistic about the future of the ‘Hilder-verse’ in Hollywood.”.

Sources indicate that some of the optimism likely stems from the performance of Netflix’s The Perfect Couple and Peacock’s The Five Star Weekend. Both were developed and produced as limited series, and both became franchises after the initial installment’s ratings success.

The Five Star Weekend, created by Bekah Brunstetter, has been renewed for a second season featuring the same core characters, with Naegle and Krug executive producing.

The Perfect Couple is taking an anthology approach with a potential second season, now in development, based on another Hilderbrand novel, Swan Song.

Naegle also executive produces Peacock’s thriller drama The Day of the Jackal, which is returning for a second season. It is produced by Universal Global Television, the studio behind The Perfect Couple.

Industry observers note that prior to launching Dinner Party Prods., Naegle served as Chief Content Officer at Annapurna Pictures, overseeing TV, Film, and Theatre, with Krug as her Co-Head of Television. The two executive produced Hulu’s Pam & Tommy and Monsterland as well as HBO Max’s The Staircase, starring Colin Firth and Toni Collette; Prime Video’s Dead Ringers, starring Rachel Weisz; Apple TV’s The Changeling, starring LaKeith Stanfield; and Netflix’s Soundtrack. Naegle began her career at UTA, rising to partner and co-head of the TV Lit Department before being appointed President of HBO Entertainment in 2008. She and Krug are repped by UTA.

Industry observers note that in addition to serving as creator, executive producer and showrunner on The Perfect Couple, starring Nicole Kidman and Liev Schreiber, Lamia co-executive produced NBC’s Good Girls and co-executive produced MTV’s Awkward, appearing in seasons 4 and 5. She also was a writer and recurring cast member on Syfy’s Resident Alien. In film, Lamia wrote and produced the supernatural comedy horror My Best Friend’s Exorcism for Amazon based on the novel by Grady Hendrix. She is repped by UTA and Ben Rubinfeld at Ziffren.

Hilderbrand is repped by UTA, InkWell Management and Kleinberg, Lange, Cuddy & Carlo. Whidden, who also was a consulting producer on HBO’s The Undoing, is repped by Karen Gottlieb at Grubman Shire Meiselas & Sacks.
