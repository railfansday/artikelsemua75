📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Thibaut Courtois has signed a newly introduced Real Madrid contract through June 30, 2028. Madrid confirmed the extension on Friday, a day after announcing a new deal for Aurélien Tchouaméni.

In a recent development, courtois joined from Chelsea in 2018, so the extra year would take him to a decade at the Bernabéu. He has made 348 appearances for Madrid and won 14 trophies, including two Champions League titles. He was named player of the match in the 2022 final against Liverpool.

In a recent development, madrid gave Tchouaméni a contract through June 30, 2032, matching the deal Arda Güler signed on Wednesday. The French midfielder has played 205 games and won eight trophies since joining from Monaco in 2022.

Recent findings suggest that captain Federico Valverde could follow. AS reports that he has agreed to extend his contract by two years, from 2029 to 2031. The newly introduced terms would make the Uruguayan one of Madrid’s highest earners.
