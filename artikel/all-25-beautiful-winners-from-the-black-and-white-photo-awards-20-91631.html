It has been revealed that the Black and White Photo Awards 2026 winners have been unveiled. Photographers competed across six categories, each vying to be named Black and White Photographer of the Year.

Speaking of which, this year’s substantial winner is Shengwei Dai, who took the top spot for a spectacular shot of a man jumping between two moving trains, with dozens of passengers looking on.

Dai earned a €1,500 cash prize for the win, the largest single award in the competition’s five-year history.

Alongside Dai’s overall win, competition judges selected winners across six categories: Architecture, Fauna and Flora, Landscape, Portrait, Street, and the newly introduced Open Theme.

“The upcoming category aims to welcome photographs that, through their artistic, narrative, or conceptual approach, do not fit neatly into any of the traditional disciplines,” the Black and White Photo Awards explains.

Sources indicate that each category also includes Golden, Silver, and Bronze mentions, which serve as runner-up and honorable mention distinctions. Category winners each receive a €200 cash prize.

In a recent development, mohammad Mirza’s photo, Silent Majesty, earned the top spot in the Fauna and Flora category. The image captures a white rhinoceros emerging through the mist and trees in Kenya’s Solio Reserve, a private wildlife sanctuary.

Jie Fischer’s Flamingo in Flight 2 bested the competition in the Landscape category. The nearly abstract aerial shot captures a flock of flying flamingos from a top-down perspective against a high-contrast, pattern-filled terrain. Happy Mukherjee’s portrait, Behind the Silence, depicts a young girl and invites viewers to wonder about her expression and gaze.

In a recent development, cow Watching by Chongfeng Wu won the Street category. Unlike most street photos captured in urban environments, Wu’s pastoral shot is much quieter.

The inaugural Open Theme winner is Lloyd Lane, whose image, Convergence, captures a flock of starlings over Brighton Palace Pier in England.

The judges also selected finalists across all six categories, and their work can be viewed on the Black and White Photo Awards website.

The Black and White Photo Awards is already looking forward to its sixth annual competition in 2027, with entries opening on January 1.
