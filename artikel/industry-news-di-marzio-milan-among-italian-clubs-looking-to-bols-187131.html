AC Milan are thinking about bolstering their academy with the Malmö FF talent Leon Da Silva, according to a report.

Amidst all of the first-team rumours, however, Milan are also working on the academy. Jovan Kirovski is evaluating players for the Futuro side with his team, while Vincenzo Vergine (pictured) and his team are doing so for the Primavera and below.

Recent findings suggest that according to Gianluca Di Marzio, one of the players on Milan’s wish list is Leon Da Silva, who currently plays in the academy of Malmö FF. At the age of 16, he’s yet to make his debut with the first team, but he did feature in a friendly game last month.

Industry observers note that da Silva plays as a centre-back and as the report states, he has even been compared to Virgil Van Dijk for how he plays. Since he’s still very young, if Milan were to secure his services, he would start with the Primavera team (or even in the younger sides).
