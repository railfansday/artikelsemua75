Industry observers note that SLEEPY SALE. Christie’s topped the tail end of a week of auctions on Thursday evening in Upcoming York, bringing in $145.9 million worth of 21st-century art, reports ARTnews. But with the hammer total of $117.7 million, the two-hour sale fell considerably short of its estimate (including withdrawals), which had been set for between $126 million and $192 million. SOFT LANDING. The Landing, a beloved Los Angeles gallery whose program was built around imaginative pairings of historical and contemporary artists, announced it had closed, reports ARTnews. “It has been a privilege to champion extraordinary artists, present ambitious exhibitions, and create a space for meaningful exchange and discovery,” wrote gallery founder and owner Gerard O’Brien on the gallery website. “End of an era,” he added in an Instagram post. O’Brien founded the Landing in 2012 as an offshoot of his design-focused Reform Gallery. “I never really thought there was a big difference,” he said of design and art. The Landing’s roster included four deceased artists (Jeremy Anderson, Richard Bowman, Leslie Kerr, and Fred Reichman) and five living artists (Yevgeniya Baras, Ryan Fenchel, Matt Phillips, Jonathan Ryan, and Amanda Valdez). Several LA galleries have shuttered of late, including Murmurs, the Box, LA Louver, Blum, plus the outposts of Marian Goodman Gallery and Tanya Bonakdar. “While our gallery doors have closed, our commitment to art and artists continues.
