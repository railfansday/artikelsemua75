Manchester United's chief executive Omar Berrada and sporting director Jason Wilcox are both under the threat of being replaced at Old Trafford.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the duo are part of a group of "three or four" boardroom figures facing uncertain futures.

However, Wyness believes that the interview hasn't had the desired effect for Berrada and has instead only placed further scrutiny on his position. (Related: GTA 6 made real gyms empty out as players chose in-game...)

The 48-year-old suggested that the club hierarchy feel that United's squad was fundamentally improved ahead of this season, despite recent results suggesting otherwise.

Football Insider also recently revealed that Pochettino is keen to see Wilcox replaced as sporting director, as he is not convinced by his work for the club so far.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Berrada and Wilcox are both "in flux" at United at the moment.

The pair are both occupying roles that have seen changes in personnel regularly at Old Trafford in recent years, and also have their own futures under scrutiny right now.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness was keen to point out that the squad has never been the problem for United.

"Look, the Man United story is incredible in terms of the scale and the attention it's getting as well," he stated.

"But I've got a funny idea about this in the sense that it's not to me that the squad is the problem. To me, the squad is the symptom of the bigger problem that is above the football management side.

According to recent reports, "Obviously, recruitment's not been great. That's proven with the results. But it's a symptom.

It has been revealed that "So there are three or four probably top positions, including those of Berrada and Wilcox that are in flux right now. I mean, there's intense scrutiny on them.

"And for Berrada himself, I don't know if that Sky interview helped that much.".

Wyness told Football Insider earlier this week that United have a hugely limited chance of signing Newcastle left-back Lewis Hall any time soon.

The boardroom expert believes that the Red Devils have no chance of "unsettling" the England international and convincing him to leave Tyneside.

Wyness outlined that players are now aware that United are not what they once were as a club, and are thinking twice before joining them.

He believes that Newcastle is the best place for Hall right now, and does not feel that a move to Old Trafford would provide any benefit for him.
