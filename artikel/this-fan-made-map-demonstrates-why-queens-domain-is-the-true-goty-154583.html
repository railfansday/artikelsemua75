In a recent development, the world wants me to write more about Grand Theft Auto 6 and I bravely answer "No". Last week, I obtained 50% of the endings available in Queen's Domain, the best videogame to release in the year 2026. The next 50% require, well, a lot of nook-scouring and cranny-uncovering—many more hours of gameplay which I am preserving for myself as a Christmas holiday treat.

And when I do, I suspect I might make a little (just a little) use of this rad Queen's Domain map by a creator called Leny15. Barely a week ago I wrote that "I suspect a cross-section of [Queen's Domain's] goliath, entwined map would make for a killer bit of wall art". Well, I was right. Here it is and it looks rad as hell. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

Sources indicate that no, really. Even if you're not a Queen's Domain player—or maybe especially if you're not—take a look at this thing. It's one thing to wander these maps as a player, it's another to get a totally flexible birdseye view of how dense, twisty and interconnected they are. I will never stop being blown away by how knotty and elaborate this game is; I love it. (Related: GTA 6 player hit with 30-day PSN ban after sharing nude...)

I will also never believe I have actually explored all of it. I'll be honest, gang, I really feel like I've plumbed the depths of this game, but this map has entries for areas I ain't even heard of, and even those areas that I have heard of seem to contain secret sub-areas I've barely touched.

It has been revealed that my favourite feature of this thing has got to be the ability to upload your save to it, at which point it will give you a completion percentage and tick off the collectibles and secrets you've already attained (reminder that you can download your Steam cloud saves right in your browser). I was heartened to read that I have, it seems, done in about 70% of this game already. I was even more heartened to learn that the remaining 30% seems even stranger, more complicated, and more baffling than all the rest. I can't wait to check it out.
