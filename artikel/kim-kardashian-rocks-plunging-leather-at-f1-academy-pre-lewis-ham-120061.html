Kim Kardashian’s paddock style is already unmatched.

Kardashian, 46, rocked a plunging, fitted black leather long-sleeved blouse with coordinating skintight pants to the F1 Academy finale on Saturday, November 21.

F1 Academy is Formula One’s all-women racing series, introduced in 2023 to help prepare aspiring female drivers to move up the ranks in the high-speed sport. Kardashian attended alongside managing director Susie Wolff and special guest Venus Williams, who had the honor of waving the checkered flag at the race’s starting line. Kardashian has been a fixture in the Ferrari garage since she started dating longtime friend Hamilton, 41, earlier this year.

Sources indicate that “I was really single for five years, and that felt great,” she told The Times of London in an October profile, making a rare comment about her dating status before finding love with Hamilton. “I could focus on my kids and projects that I really wanted to be involved with. (Following her divorce, the Skims mogul was briefly romantically linked to Pete Davidson and Odell Beckham Jr., respectively.).

According to recent reports, “I felt like I needed my time,” she noted to The Times. “I wasn’t ready to open up. I really wasn’t even thinking about it. The universe works in your favor when you just allow it to.”.

Kardashian, however, has declined to speak about her romance with the British racer at length.

“In the most respectful way, I’d probably rather not,” she quipped to the outlet when pressed for further details about her blossoming romance.
