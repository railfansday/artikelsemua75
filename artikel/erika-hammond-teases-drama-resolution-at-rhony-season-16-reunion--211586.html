Erika Hammond is teasing The Real Housewives of Newly introduced York City Season 16 reunion, revealing there will be “drama” and “resolution.”.

In a upcoming interview, Hammond hinted that although some problems among the group will be resolved, she doesn’t see a path forward with the new “friend of” Devyn Simone.

According to recent reports, “I will say it is a must-watch. There’s a lot going on,” Hammond told Extra. “There’s a lot more drama, but there is a lot of resolution. I think most of us got to resolve things that need to be resolved, and we walked away.
