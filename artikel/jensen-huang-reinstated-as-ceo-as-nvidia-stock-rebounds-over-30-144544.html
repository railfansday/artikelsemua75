Nvidia's board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, president and board director.

Shares reportedly rebounded from around $125 to an intraday high of $166.50 on Monday.

Meanwhile, the $150 and $130 levels could return as support if the stock loses momentum.

According to recent reports, nvidia’s leadership drama has taken another sharp turn. Jensen Huang reinstated as CEO is the headline after the board unanimously approved his return, sending Nvidia shares sharply higher following Friday’s sell-off.

The board mentioned it received input from senior US government officials and believes his reinstatement poses no national security risk. However, an independent investigation will continue.

The board stated its decision followed discussions and meetings with Huang. It also confirmed that the investigation into recent foreign-intelligence-related reports would proceed, with Huang committed to cooperating.

Sources indicate that huang noted he was honored to return and lead the firm. Board chair Harvey Jones described the decision as being in the best interests of shareholders, employees and partners.

Sources indicate that the market reaction was dramatic. Nvidia shares reportedly fell 14% on Friday, closing at $125.53 after breaking below $150 support on November 19 and $130 the following day.
