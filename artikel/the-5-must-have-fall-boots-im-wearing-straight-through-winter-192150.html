Growing up in Southern California, I was a flip-flop girl through and through. But since moving to New York, I've come to appreciate the importance of a cold-weather shoe collection. In fact, boots now outnumber sandals in my closet.

According to recent reports, whether I'm wearing a plaid mini skirt and sheer tights or straight-leg denim and a knitted sweater, boots complete the look. They're what ballet flats are to the warmer seasons, providing a tried-and-true footwear staple to rely on for the rest of fall and all winter long.

While I certainly own a few striking pairs of boots (like bubblegum-pink iterations and cow-print block heels), curating a timeless collection that effortlessly pairs with your wardrobe is essential. This way, you can build upon the curated collection season after season, rather than splurging on trend-specific footwear every fall.
