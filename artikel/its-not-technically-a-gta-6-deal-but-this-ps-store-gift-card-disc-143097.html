📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that you're probably going to have to wait quite some time for GTA 6 to get any kind of reduction in price. That doesn't mean there's no way to save money on the fresh AAA title, though, as a new Black Friday deal in the UK proves.

Industry observers note that shopTo is currently discounting PS Store gift cards for between 9% and 15% off, which in the case of the £70 card, means you can pick it up for £60.85. A digital download of GTA 6 costs £70. See where I'm going with this? That's right: you buy this card for £60, redeem it on the new game, and you've basically saved money on a game that won't see a real discount for ages. Of course, you don't have to buy GTA 6, but ShopTo clearly thinks you will: the background of the gift card listing page is artwork from the game. Industry observers note that shopTo has discounted PS Store gift cards for £10 off right now. Simply head through checkout and then check the digital section of your account, where you'll receive a code ready to redeem against your account on PS5. (Related: Industry News: Faster TensorFlow models in Hugging Face...)

Industry observers note that if you live abroad and begun concocting a plan, let me warn you: this PS Store gift card can only be redeemed in the UK, by UK PlayStation accounts. There isn't a way to use a VPN or geolocation tricks to finagle this discount from elsewhere.

ShopTo is a UK retailer, so even though buyers abroad can see the gift card in their home currency, it's still a UK gift card so all of those rules apply.

Sources indicate that this gift card isn't tied to GTA 6. You can use it on anything on the PlayStation Store, including DLC and subscriptions as well as games themselves. This includes a PlayStation Plus membership.

Sources indicate that given that it's an incredibly recent game, the odds of there being a discount on copies of GTA 6 are almost zero. I technically can't rule it out completely, but I'd say the odds of it happening, are roughly the same as the chances of a PC port shadow-drop over the Black Friday weekend. Pretty low.

Recent findings suggest that GTA 6-adjacent deals like this one are more common, but I think it's unlikely that a better discount than this one will crop up. If you're worried about that happening, you could just buy this gift card now and wait to redeem it until the sales have fully begun — you can always redeem it on another game (or save it for the potential online version, if you're feeling optimistic).

Fans of GTA will be better suited embracing early Black Friday deals to upgrade your GTA 6 set-up, with items like headsets, controllers and displays seeing some solid reductions.
