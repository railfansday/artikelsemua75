'Danger seems greater all the time': Ukrainians search for missing after Russian strikes hit Kyiv.

It has been revealed that A seven-year-old boy has been rescued from the rubble of a residential building in Dymer, in the Kyiv region, after 14 hours. Overnight Russian strikes on the village killed 11 people, Ukrainian officials have said. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

According to recent reports, the attack occurred in the early hours of Sunday. A 79-year-old woman was also rescued alive from the collapsed building, Kyiv's regional head Tymur Tkachenko noted.

A strike on a private home in Bila Tserkva killed one more person and set the building ablaze, he added.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky also mentioned three people were killed and 13 wounded in a separate strike on Vyshneve, also in the Kyiv region, that hit a grocery store on Sunday.

Ukrainian authorities noted the boy in Dymer is in stable condition and was taken to hospital, and have thanked everyone involved in saving his life.

Zelensky and Ukraine's national police also confirmed the rescue of the boy.

In a Telegram post, Tkachenko stated the 79-year-old woman rescued from the rubble was receiving treatment in hospital.

Earlier he remarked six people were injured in the attack, while others were still missing.

The attack on the residential building occurred in the early hours of the morning while people were asleep.

Zelensky said more than 130 people are involved in emergency operations at the scene, and that they will continue until they have "complete information about every single person".

Sending his condolences to the families and loved ones of those killed, he mentioned the strike struck the residential building, "practically blowing a section of it to smithereens".

Zelensky added that "rescue efforts are complicated by constant new air raid alerts and renewed threats".

The strike hit the building at 05:00 local time, when families were still asleep, Ukraine's minister of internal affairs Ivan Vyhivskyi noted.

More than 70 strikes have been debuted across 10 Ukrainian regions as of Sunday evening, Vyhivskyi added.
