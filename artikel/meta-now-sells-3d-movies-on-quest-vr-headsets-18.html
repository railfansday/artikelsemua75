According to recent reports, meta now offers more than 150 3D movies that you can buy or rent to watch in VR on its Quest 3 and 3S headsets, the organization announced today. The movies are available in Meta’s TV app and include titles like Avatar, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Jurassic World Rebirth, Wicked: For Good, and a Billie Eilish concert film co-directed by Eilish and James Cameron.

The firm suggests watching movies in a Theater view that’s rolling out with a upcoming Meta Quest upgrade. Theater view “provides a major screen and dims your surroundings to create a cozy, personal cinema experience,” Meta states.
