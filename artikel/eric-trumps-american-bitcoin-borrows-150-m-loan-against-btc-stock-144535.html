Eric Trump’s American Bitcoin Borrows $150 M Loan Against BTC, Stock Jumps 20%

American Bitcoin secured a $150 million revolving credit facility from FalconX, backed by bitcoin.

American Bitcoin shares jumped about 17% in early trading after announcement.

However, The manufacturer reported a $126 million third-quarter profit, helped by Bitcoin gains.

Eric Trump-backed American Bitcoin has secured a upcoming $150 million funding option without having to sell its Bitcoin. The company signed a two-year revolving credit facility with FalconX at a 5.95% annual loan fee, using part of its growing Bitcoin reserve as collateral. Following this news, American Bitcoin shares jumped about 20% to trade at $4.19, showing that the new access to capital was welcomed by the market.

American Bitcoin, co-founded by Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr., signed the agreement through a subsidiary on November 18. The facility gives the organization access to as much as $150 million, which it can borrow, repay, and borrow again during the two-year term.

Importantly, the company has not drawn the money yet. As of November 23, the credit line remained unused.

It has been revealed that that gives American Bitcoin access to cash when needed while allowing it to keep its Bitcoin holdings. The firm can use the funds for operations, growth, and other corporate needs without selling BTC or issuing latest shares.

“This $150 million facility provides American Bitcoin with non-dilutive capital to support our continued operations and growth initiatives.”.

The deal comes after American Bitcoin’s Bitcoin reserve crossed 8,500 BTC, giving the company a larger pool of assets it can use to secure financing.

The credit line is backed by some of that Bitcoin, which means the value of the manufacturer’s BTC holdings will directly affect the financing.

Recent findings suggest that american Bitcoin must maintain an initial collateral ratio of 143% when it borrows. If the value of the pledged Bitcoin falls below 130% of the outstanding loan, FalconX can glitch a margin call.

If the ratio drops below 105%, FalconX can declare a default and sell the pledged Bitcoin under the agreement’s terms.

The structure gives American Bitcoin flexibility, but it also means a sharp Bitcoin price fall could put pressure on the manufacturer’s ability to maintain the facility.

For now, the company is keeping its Bitcoin accumulation strategy intact while gaining another source of liquidity.

FalconX Director of Sales Oliver Sitt said the facility allows public companies to raise working capital without selling their digital assets.

“Collateralized lending gives American Bitcoin a way to access liquidity while it continues to grow its Bitcoin reserve and scale its business.”. (Related: Introducing Optimum: The Optimization Toolkit for Trans...)

It has been revealed that the financing also follows a strong third quarter for American Bitcoin. The company reported a $126 million profit, largely helped by gains in the value of its Bitcoin holdings, while revenue reached $63.4 million.
