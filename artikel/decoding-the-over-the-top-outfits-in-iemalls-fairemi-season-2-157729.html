Then it was really just about combing through the season’s collections and seeing who did what, and which silhouettes go where, because at this point we’ve kind of developed our characters. The massive difference for season 2 was we really upped our jewelry game.

So, it started with Glenn. She had asked if she could have some real pearls that she just wore all the time. And so we wrote to Tiffany’s, and then we wrote to everybody, we found jewelers that we’d never heard of that did fantastical things. Tiffany loaned us three incredible brooches that were so ridiculously valuable, and some pearls and diamonds and earrings for Glenn. Boucheron did jewelry for everybody for the Scorpio Ball. We had to have newly introduced safes installed in the office and in the truck.

Yes. We bought a lot of vintage for Teyana Taylor. We had vintage Alaïa and Gaultier, and we bought an incredible vintage outfit that—I can’t say who wears it in the end—we bought this Galliano runway piece that I had them split into two invoices so that I could put it in the budget. But obviously the opening, and re-creating the scorpion cape (for Kim) was amazing, because there still are a few ladies who do that beading, who did it in the ’50s and ’60s for Judy Garland and Liza Minnelli, and worked with Bob Mackie. I think we all, even though it’s not good for our shoulders, it’s not good for our backs, most women I know, we all have a set of really great tote bags that we put our very heavy computers in and carry all our stuff in.
