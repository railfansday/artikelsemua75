If a character wears layered textures—such as a fuzzy pistachio mohair shrug over a sheer ivory silk-organza slip dress with pearl hair clips—text descriptions alone will drift across shots.

The Fix: Generate a neutral off-white cyclorama 3-view character turnaround sheet first (front, 90° profile, and close-up portrait) before introducing dramatic scenery:.

Instead of burning video credits to test composition, pass your approved character sheet into a still keyframe node to validate warm-vs-cool light interaction—like a coral-amber glass lantern illuminating twilight pond mist:.

Once your keyframe is locked, delete static wardrobe descriptions from your video prompt. Feed the approved frame into Seedance 2.5 or Kling 4.0 at 720p (ideal for fast, credit-smart iteration with synchronized ambient audio) and specify only physical camera kinematics and secondary environmental motion:.
