Manchester United are reportedly keeping a close eye on Atalanta defender Giorgio Scalvini, with Liverpool also interested in securing the highly-rated Italian international ahead of the 2027 transfer window.

The 22-year-old has attracted considerable attention across Europe, and his recent return to top form has strengthened his reputation as one of the most promising young defenders in Italian football.

According to recent reports, according to a TEAMtalk report, Manchester United and Liverpool are both assessing their central defensive options, with Scalvini emerging as a potential long-term addition. Chelsea are also monitoring his situation, while Newcastle United have maintained their interest in the defender for several years.
