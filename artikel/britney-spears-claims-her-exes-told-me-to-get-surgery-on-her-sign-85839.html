Pop star Britney Spears is opening up about her body image issues and how her exes allegedly talked about her forehead.

“I have a really massive forehead!!” Spears, 44, wrote via Instagram on Saturday, November 21, alongside footage of what appeared to be the pop star flying on a private jet. “Every guy I’ve ever dated has told me to get surgery and put hair implants so my forehead isn’t so big…Why are men such dick heads?…IM JUST SAYING.”. She added, “PS talking nonsense, we’ll all die in the end anyways!!!”.

In a recent development, spears has had a slew of high-profile relationships in the decades she has spent under the harsh Hollywood spotlight, including Justin Timberlake and her ex husbands Jason Alexander, Kevin Federline and Sam Asghari. (Spears and Federline welcomed two sons, Sean Preston and Jayden, before their 2007 split.). (Related: Democratic stars try to keep 2028 spotlight as newbies ...)

Later on Saturday, Spears again took to social media to apologize for what she claims was her past treatment of unnamed family members.

It has been revealed that “I’m so blessed to have such a beautiful family,” Spears wrote via Instagram. “I’m so very sorry for bringing up past problems, but I think I did because I realized I needed to protect my heart.”.

Sources indicate that she continued, “It’s the way I love and when I love you, I REALLY love you. My stubbornness is childish and embarrassing. Thoughts on the past, I’m obviously human and there actually might be something to those things I mentioned phycology [sic] wise, either way I’m sorry to my family bringing up those memories. I’ve never actually spoke to them face to face on it. It’s frankly [because] I just miss them like crazy!!! And I’m very sorry!!!”.

Sources indicate that spears claimed in a 2021 court hearing that not a single one of her family members had interfered with her father Jamie Spears alleged mismanagement of his 13-year conservatorship over the pop star. Both Jamie, 74, and her mother Lynne Spears have long denied Britney’s accusations, with the matriarch still hoping the fractured family can find common ground sometime in the future.

“[Britney] and her mom still have highs and lows. One day they’re working on their relationship, the next they won’t speak for weeks,” a source exclusively told Us in September 2025. “Lynne tries to treat the situation with as much care as possible, but she also doesn’t want to sit back and do nothing.”.

The insider added, “She’d love more than anything to be close with Britney again. They used to be best friends. Lynne understands Britney is still hurt and traumatized by the conservatorship, but she hopes one day they can focus on the future and not the past.”.

In July of this year, an insider also revealed that the pop star was making more of an effort to reconnect with her two previously estranged sons.

“Britney has been reaching out to her sons more and wants to start spending more time with them when they are ready,” the source told Us at the time.

In 2022, Jayden told Australia’s 60 Minutes in 2022 that they had “emotional trauma” stemming from their relationship with the pop star.

Sources indicate that “I think Mom has struggled giving us both attention and showing us equal love and I don’t think she revealed enough to Preston and I feel really bad for that,” Jayden told the outlet at the time. “We’ve both been through so much pressure in the past that this is our safe place now, to process all the emotional trauma we’ve been through to heal, heal our mental state.”.
