It has been revealed that “It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” Dangerfield wrote. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss. Industry observers note that mangubat and Dangerfield’s relationship was documented on Before the 90 Days season six in 2023. After meeting through a Facebook group for deaf and hearing-impaired singles in 2021, Dangerfield traveled from Nebraska to the Philippines to meet his then-girlfriend Mangubat in person for the first time. They eventually got engaged on the show but later broke up in October 2025 due to K-1 visa challenges, financial strain and distance.

Mangubat is survived by her son, Jhonreil, from a previous relationship.
