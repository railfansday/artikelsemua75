Deep Venture: A newly introduced class of ocean exploration vehicle developed

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (93 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, researchers have a new capability for exploring some of the least understood regions of the ocean. Developed by Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution (WHOI) in collaboration with NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) in Southern California, Deep Venture is a next-generation autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) for deep-ocean research, capable of supporting a wide range of scientific missions, from shallow coastal waters to the deepest places on Earth. It can withstand extreme pressure, rated for depths of up to 11,000 meters (about 6.83 mi), or full ocean depth.
