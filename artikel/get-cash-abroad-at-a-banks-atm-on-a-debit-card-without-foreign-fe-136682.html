In a recent development, before a trip abroad, get a debit card that charges no foreign transaction or ATM fees. When you arrive, use it to take out local cash at a bank’s ATM, and pick the local currency over dollars when the screen asks. That withdrawal costs less than changing money at a counter or taking a credit card cash advance.

Recent findings suggest that A card with no foreign transaction fee can pay for much of a trip abroad, but local transit, tips, and small purchases still call for cash in many countries. Where you get that cash decides what it costs you.

An exchange counter, whether at the airport, at your hotel, or at your own bank before you leave, sells currency for more than it pays to buy it back.
