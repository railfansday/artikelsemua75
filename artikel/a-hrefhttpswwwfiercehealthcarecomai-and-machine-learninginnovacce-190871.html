Autonomous artificial intelligence healthcare operations platform Innovaccer is expanding its Gravity Voice AI capabilities with a new integration of Grok Voice.

Through the integration, the platform’s healthcare intelligence data, workflow infrastructure and voice AI will be combined with Grok Voice at scale. Trained on real phone audio, Grok Voice is HIPAA-eligible and is available in more than 25 languages. (Related: Leveraging Pre-trained Language Model Checkpoints for E...)

CEO and co-founder Abhinav Shashank said in a statement that healthcare voice AI is moving from "answering questions to doing work.".

Recent findings suggest that "We scaled from hundreds of thousands of interactions to more than five million and demonstrated measurable results in appointments, referrals and engagement," Shashank said. "By leveraging Grok Voice, we are bringing frontier intelligence into that foundation and extending where these agents work across the healthcare journey.". (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Industry observers note that specifically, Innovaccer claims patient voice interactions have grown from roughly 250,000 to more than five million since its launch six months ago. And it has tripled referral intakes, helped organizations book "significantly more" appointments and reduce documentation time by 90% per case.

In a recent development, founded in 2014, the company builds software solutions to unify enterprise data and applies AI to automate manual tasks and streamline workflows for payers and providers. As of May, it says it serves more than 200 health systems and payers, 80 million patients across the U.S. and 95% of community pharmacies.

The Grok integration is now live for customers.

The platform aims to integrate other Grok models via API in the future, executives say, including Grok Build and Grok Bot.
