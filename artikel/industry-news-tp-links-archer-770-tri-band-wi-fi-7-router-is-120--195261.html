The TP-Link Archer BE770 is a tri-band Wi-Fi 7 router that offers incredibly fast speeds on the 5 GHz and 6 GHz wireless bands. It also offers a 10 Gbps WAN/LAN port and a 10 Gbps LAN port to handle your faster fiber internet connections and wired devices.

Sources indicate that the HomeShield tab provides a subset of its full feature suite for free. Network Check scans your network to identify security risks and areas to improve overall performance. There are limited parental controls you can implement per device, and QoS settings you can enable to limit download and upload bandwidth for individual devices. But for the full breadth of controls, TP-Link offers its Security+ for $35.99/year or its Total Security Package for $69.99/year (for the first year, $129.99 starting with the second year).

If you’re looking for a high-performance standalone Wi-Fi 7 router, the Archer BE770 definitely fits the bill. At $400, it’s definitely a hard sell, but for $280, it’s an easy recommendation.
