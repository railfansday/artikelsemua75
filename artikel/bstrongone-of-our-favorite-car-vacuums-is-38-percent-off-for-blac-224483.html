The Slim V10 Apex packs plenty of functionality into a compact design. Weighing just over a pound, it’s small enough to store in your trunk or glove compartment and easy to maneuver around seats, cup holders, and tight spaces. Its 120-watt brushless motor delivers up to 19,000Pa of suction, making it well suited for tackling crumbs, dirt, pet hair, and the everyday debris that accumulates inside a vehicle.

Beyond vacuuming, it doubles as a blower and inflator. The included attachments help reach narrow crevices, clean delicate surfaces, and blow dust out of areas a traditional vacuum can’t easily access. A digital display reveals battery status and suction settings, while USB-C charging makes it easy to keep it powered.

Battery life reaches up to 30 minutes on the lower setting or approximately 15 minutes at maximum power. Its small dustbin makes it better suited for regular touch-ups than major dirty jobs, but that’s also what makes it convenient to keep on hand.
