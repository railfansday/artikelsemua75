Base compensation range starts at $129,000 annually.

Industry observers note that the Manager, Structured Finance owns complex financing workstreams supporting Vesper’s solar and battery storage portfolio, including project finance, tax equity, credit facilities, financial modeling, transaction execution, and ongoing financing compliance. The role independently manages transactions and areas of financial expertise, develops recommendations for senior leaders, and partners with Development, Engineering, Legal, Accounting, Asset Management, and external financing parties. Education: Bachelor’s or advanced degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field. Relevant certifications (e.g., CFA) preferred.

Years of Experience: 4 to 7 years of relevant experience in financial modeling/analytics, renewable energy finance, project finance, investment banking, infrastructure investing, corporate finance, or a related field.

Strong understanding of solar and storage project economics and financial modeling. (Related: Latest Update: Understanding BigBird's Block Sparse Att...)

Proven experience with construction loans, term loans, and tax equity structuring/modeling.

Demonstrated experience with deal execution, including managing due diligence and closing transactions.

Demonstrated ability to model Vesper’s values, Accountability, Respect, Courage, Collaboration, and Continuous Improvement, through day-to-day behaviors (e.g., flags risks early, communicates directly, involves the right stakeholders early, and documents and shares learnings).

Working knowledge of construction debt, term debt, tax equity, letters of credit, revolving credit facilities, and the commercial and contractual considerations affecting renewable energy financing.

Ability to influence without formal authority by building credibility, communicating directly, and aligning internal teams, lenders, investors, counsel, consultants, and other transaction parties.

Ability to mentor colleagues informally, provide constructive review and feedback, share transaction knowledge, and help strengthen financial capability across the organization.

Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and proficiency in Microsoft Word and PowerPoint, including the ability to prepare clear financial analyses and decision materials for senior leaders and Vesper’s Board of Directors.

Using Vesper model template, independently build, review, and maintain financial models for utility-scale solar and battery storage projects supporting construction debt, term debt, tax equity, portfolio valuation, pricing, and other financing decisions, including model design, assumption management, scenario analysis, quality control (testing assumptions, reconciling outputs, and identifying errors before they affect decisions), and communication of key findings.

Lead assigned project-finance and tax-equity workstreams from financing strategy and initial lender or investor engagement through due diligence, documentation, funding, closing, coordinating internal teams, lenders, investors, legal counsel, independent engineers, tax and insurance advisors, and other third parties to resolve concerns and complete closing requirements.

Translate firm and functional priorities into clear financing looks, and maintain clear ownership of deliverables, decisions, risks, and timelines, with limited oversight and consistent attention to accuracy, documentation, and follow-through.

Manage deal execution processes, including coordinating bank/investor due diligence and working with external consultants to ensure successful closing of transactions.

Develops strong relationships with banks, investors, third-party legal, technical, and tax advisors to manage deliverable quality, cost, and schedule for financial closes.

Apply strong judgment to identify financial, commercial, execution, and compliance risks and deliver clear, data-supported recommendations on financing structures, transaction economics, risk allocation, and commercial tradeoffs by analyzing project data, market terms, tax considerations, interest rates, merchant forecasts, tariffs, and supply-chain developments. Develop portfolio valuation updates, financing-status reports, and Board materials that clearly communicate project economics, sensitivities, transaction progress, significant risks, and recommended actions to senior leaders.

Establish practical transaction controls, trackers, templates, review procedures, and documentation standards that improve accuracy, accountability, and execution across multiple concurrent financings.

Partner with Development, Engineering, Legal, Accounting, Asset Management, and other functions to evaluate project readiness, resolve financing dependencies, support M&A diligence as applicable, and incorporate financing requirements into project and portfolio decisions.
