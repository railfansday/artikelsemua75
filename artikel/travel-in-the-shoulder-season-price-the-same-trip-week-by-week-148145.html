Travel in the Shoulder Season – Price the Same Trip Week by Week

Sources indicate that move your trip into the destination’s shoulder season, the weeks just before or after its peak, and the same flights often cost less, with thinner crowds and weather that’s usually still good. The discount varies by place and year, so compare prices for several weeks around your dates before you book.

In a recent development, the biggest savings are usually in the airfare. Data from the travel app Hopper, reported by CNBC, demonstrates round-trip flights from the U.S. to Europe costing 22% less, on average, in fall 2026 than that summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Industry observers note that the discount isn’t automatic, though. The same data puts average U.S. domestic fares for fall 2026 just above summer’s. And Expedia found that lodging in the 10 U.S. destinations most searched on its site cost 20% more, on average, in fall 2026 than in summer.

Start by finding when your destination’s peak falls. In much of Europe it’s summer, so look at April or October. In the Caribbean, the peak runs from mid-December to mid-April. Try late April through June, before hurricane season’s most active stretch from mid-August to mid-October. Or go in early December, after the season ends Nov. 30.

Expect trade-offs. Some attractions shorten their hours or close for the season, and the weather is less predictable than at peak. For a trip during hurricane season, buy travel insurance before a storm is named, since a policy bought later likely won’t cover that storm.

In a recent development, then price the trip week by week. Google Flights’ price graph highlights a route’s fares by week, so compare your first-choice dates with two or three shoulder weeks, and check hotel rates for the same weeks. Say a couple’s round-trip tickets to Rome cost $1,000 each in July. At Hopper’s 22% average, mid-October tickets would cost about $780 each, $440 less for the pair than in July.

It has been revealed that book the shoulder week whenever its flights and room together cost no more than those for your first-choice dates. At the same price, you still get the thinner crowds.
