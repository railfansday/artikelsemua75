Fresh details about New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye have come to light.

Maye, 24, fractured his right fibula, according to a Friday, November 20, ESPN report. The outlet claimed the pro athlete’s injury likely won’t require surgery, though he is expected to be sidelined for a portion of the remaining NFL season.

Maye sustained an injury after he was sacked by Detroit Lions’ Aidan Hutchinson during their teams’ Sunday, November 15, clash in Munich. Both Maye and Hutchinson, 26, left the game after the play. (Hutchison suffered a season-ending knee injury from his sack celebration.).

While Drake has not publicly addressed his injury, wife Ann Michael Maye shared her support.

“ILY always,” she wrote via her Instagram Stories on Tuesday, November 17, alongside a heart emoji.
