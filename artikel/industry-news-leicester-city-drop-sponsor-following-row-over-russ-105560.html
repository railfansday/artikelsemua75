Leicester have confirmed that fans who bought a shirt within the last 28 days through official channels are eligible for a full refund or exchange, even if the shirt has been worn or printed with a name on the back.

It was recently claimed that the club would struggle to terminate their partnership with BC.GAME, from a legal standpoint – while there may be further financial problems from losing the sponsorship.

This mishap is the latest in a long line of events at the King Power Stadium that fans would rather forget, with the 2015/16 Premier League champions now languishing in League One and making the headlines for all the wrong reasons.

Following relegation from the top flight in 2024 – the first campaign that BC.GAME adorned Leicester's shirts for – the Midlands outfit suffered a shock back-to-back relegation last term to return to the third tier for the first time since 2009.

Life in League One has been a struggle under Russell Martin, too, with the club sitting 11th in the table, failing to win their first three fixtures and suffering a notable 4-0 home defeat to Aaron Ramsey's Oxford United.

Leicester head to Huddersfield Town this weekend, for their first match in a sponsorless shirt.
