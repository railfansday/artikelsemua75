Arsenal take on Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League this evening, but it will not be shown on TNT Sports, as is usually the case for European football in the UK.

The Gunners go into this game looking to get back to winning ways after a surprise draw with Newcastle at St James’ Park, where the north Londoners were given an almighty scare.

Noni Madueke netted a late equaliser for the Premier League champions, who had fallen behind after Malick Thiaw bundled home from a set piece after Harvey Barnes had cancelled out Eberechi Eze’s opener.

They take on a Dortmund side that are top of the Bundesliga after 10 matches, and they are two points ahead of Klassiker rivals Bayern Munich. (Related: Industry News: Hyatt Category 1 Hotels — The Easiest Po...)

Amongst Niko Kovac’s cohort is Ethan Nwaneri, who is on loan at the German side from Arsenal, and he is eligible to face his parent club tonight due to Champions League rules permitting loan players to be available to face the teams they are contracted to.

Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund will not be broadcast live in the UK on TNT Sports. That is because under the current Champions League rights agreement on these shores, Amazon Prime Video has the first pick of one Tuesday night fixture to show exclusively on their platform up until the semi-finals.

This week they have chosen the encounter at the Emirates Stadium, which kicks off at 8pm GMT at Old Trafford. Coverage on Amazon begins at 6:30pm, hosted by Gabby Logan.

Amazon’s current arrangement will continue from 2027-2031 as per the terms of the next Champions League TV rights deal, which will see the bulk of matches head to US streaming giants Paramount in an agreement struck in 2025 worth well over £1billion.

Highlights will remain on the BBC, with Sky Sports buying the rights to show all Europa League and Conference League games in the UK from next season.
