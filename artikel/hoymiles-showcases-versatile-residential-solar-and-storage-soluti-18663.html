Sources indicate that LAS VEGAS, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Hoymiles showcased its latest residential solar and energy storage solutions at RE+ 2026, featuring the HiFlow Pro plug-in microinverter, HiBattery 4020 series and HMS series microinverters. Designed for a range of household energy needs, the portfolio covers emerging plug-in PV applications, expandable home energy storage and rooftop solar, highlighting Hoymiles' continued commitment to the North American market.

HiFlow Pro brings flexibility to plug-in solar.

Recent findings suggest that hiFlow Pro microinverter, named to TIME's Best Inventions 2026 list, is the first plug-in microinverter in the U.S. market to meet the UL 3700 safety standard. The HiFlow Pro 1-in-1 model connects to a single solar module and weighs approximately 1.8 kg, making installation straightforward. With a startup voltage as low as 22V and peak MPPT efficiency of 99.8%, it is designed for efficient solar generation in a compact form. Built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity enable real-time system monitoring through the S-Miles Home app. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

HiBattery 4020 X combines solar generation and storage.

The HiBattery 4020 X combines solar generation and storage in one compact system, featuring a built-in 4.02 kWh battery, four MPPTs, up to 4 kW PV input and 1,200 W output. It supports Hoymiles and third-party smart meters, with MQTT connectivity for integration with external energy management platforms. The system features 48 BMS protection functions and multiple safety measures, encased in an IP66-rated enclosure. HMS microinverters offer flexible rooftop configurations.

Recent findings suggest that hoymiles also presented three HMS microinverter models: the HMS-500-1T-NA, HMS-1000-2T-NA and HMS-2000-4T-NA. With one, two and four PV module connections respectively, the microinverters provide flexible options for different rooftop layouts.
