Ripple's blueprint to bring bank-style lending to the XRP Ledger is about to materialize as the XRP lending protocol amendment has reached a majority, surpassing 80% validator support.

The lending protocol allows on-chain fixed-term, uncollateralized loans from pooled funds in a Single Asset Vault.

Industry observers note that lending would give stablecoin holders another way to earn a return on the network. The setup includes Vaults, which are pools where lenders park one asset, like XRP or a stablecoin and receive share tokens representing their share.

According to recent reports, the 'SingleAssetVault' amendment sets the stage for multiple depositors to pool assets. This amendment is intended to be used with the on-chain Lending Protocol. Also, the SingleAssetVault and LendingProtocolV1_1 amendments have received majority support and are entering the two-week window prior to mainnet activation.
