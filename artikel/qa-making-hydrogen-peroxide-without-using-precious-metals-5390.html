Q&A: Making hydrogen peroxide without using precious metals

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Hydrogen peroxide is used in many industrial processes, with several million tons of it being manufactured worldwide every year. Whereas the standard production method requires rare precious metals, chemists from Rutgers University–Newark in the US and the University of Bonn have now described an alternative technique that does away with these metals, making it potentially cheaper and more sustainable. Their findings have now been published in the journal Nature Communications. In the following interview, the authors describe the technology.
