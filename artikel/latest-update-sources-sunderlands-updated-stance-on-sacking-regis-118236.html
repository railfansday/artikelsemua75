Sunderland are not presently considering sacking Regis Le Bris despite their disappointing form, sources have told Football Insider.

The Black Cats have endured a slow start to their Premier League season so far, and remain inside the relegation zone, having taken just one win from their last nine games.

Following a 0-0 draw against Aston Villa on Saturday, doubts have lingered about their performances, and the manager is now facing questions about his future in the dugout.

However, as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Le Bris has plenty of credit in the bank after his success with Sunderland last season.

Having led them to Europa League qualification against the odds last time out, and after securing a 1-0 win against Chelsea before the international break, the boss is safe for now.

However, after both Pierre Sage and Michael Carrick lost their jobs with their clubs joining Sunderland in the bottom three, Le Bris is under pressure to turn things around.

Former Sunderland scout Mick Brown told Football Insider on 18 November that Le Bris is "in trouble" at Sunderland after a string of disappointing results.

On top of their struggles on the pitch, the Black Cats have also suffered significant injury blows, and will be without some key players in the coming weeks.

Those absences are set to make Le Bris' job even harder, and while the club are keen to stick by their manager, they could be forced into a difficult position before long.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "Now that there's been two managers who've lost their jobs in the Premier League, it normally does open the doors for more to follow.

"Regis Le Bris at Sunderland was another one who was under pressure before the international break, because it's been a very disappointing start to the season for them.

"He picked up a very impressive win against Chelsea last time out so I'm sure that will buy him more time, and he has got a lot of credit in the bank as well from what he did last season.

"But while they're in the bottom three in the Premier League table, there are always going to be doubts about his position.

In a recent development, "You never know, for any manager in the Premier League, you're always under pressure. You’re only a few bad results away from potentially getting the sack.".

Le Bris is not the only manager coming under fire in the Premier League, with the first two dismissals set to open the floodgates for other clubs to pull the trigger.

Sunderland remain in the bottom three as things stand, but after slow starts to the season, Alvaro Arbeloa and Oliver Glasner have also entered the firing line.

Football Insider revealed on 20 November that Arbeloa could qucikly find himself sacked by Fulham if their performances take a dive, despite their recent improvements.

A 1-1 draw against Man City last time out will have done the Spaniard a world of favours, but there are concerns about the lack of wins at Craven Cottage so far.

The Whites have now lost just one of their last nine Premier League games, however, in a remarkable run of form which could mean Le Bris is the most likely contender to be sacked next.
