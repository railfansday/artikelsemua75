Konami has provided zero indication that it looks to bring any Silent Hill games to the Switch 2, so while we wallow in despair, we can at least take comfort in the knowledge that something remarkably similar is on the way.

Industry observers note that revealed at The Horror Game Awards 2026, Ugly Within is published by Serafini Productions and confirmed for Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC. There's no release date as of yet, but judging from the quality of the trailer, it seems development is proceeding well. (Related: Industry News: ‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok f...)

Sources indicate that featuring a familiar over-the-shoulder viewpoint, Ugly Within sees you take on the roles of Shu Onodera and Reiko Miyama as they investigate the mysterious Kamata estate. The former utilises stealth abilities to slip by monsters unseen, while the latter confronts them head-on via exorcism.

In a recent development, the trailer is unsettling, to say the least, and the production value seems high. There's one sequence in which a woman's face just peels open to reveal a bulging eye surrounded by teeth, and it's proper gross. (Related: Woman evicted after apartment’s AI assistant mistakenly...)

Let's check out the key features:.

- See the Past: Use Shu's ability to read the echoes left in rooms and objects, uncovering clues from the past to solve present-day mysteries.

- Two Protagonists: Switch between stealth, quiet exploration and puzzle-solving as Shu and direct confrontation and exorcism as Reiko.

- Secluded Estate: Explore a traditional Japanese house filled with dim corridors, tatami rooms and hidden underground spaces, each holding a piece of the Kamata family's story.

- Survival Under Pressure: Follow the records and traces others left behind, manage limited resources and find a way to survive what the family has been hiding.

So yes, while we continue to lament the lack of Silent Hill on Switch 2, we'll keep a close eye on Ugly Within in the coming weeks and months. It looks neat, and hopefully it'll prove a worthy alternative upon release.
