Recent findings suggest that deribit’s November Bitcoin options are due to expire at 3 a.m. ET on Friday, Nov. 27, an hour before early trading begins on NYSE Arca after Thanksgiving. ByKaranteli’s Monday snapshot put open interest in the expiring coin-margined contracts at about 112,000 BTC-equivalent. Traders using those contracts to hedge through Arca’s early open would need replacement coverage in place before expiry.

ByKaranteli’s Deribit chain highlighted 111,900 BTC-equivalent of open interest in the Nov. 27 expiry in its 07:00 UTC capture on Nov. 23. Deribit reports public open interest with an eight-hour delay, so the 07:00 UTC capture reflects older positions. Traders can also close or roll contracts before Friday, changing the amount left to settle.

A separate Nov. 23 daily reading for the same expiry put calls at 58,200 BTC-equivalent and puts at 53,800 BTC-equivalent. In the hourly capture, the $68,000 call held open interest of roughly 2,400 BTC-equivalent. The $48,000 put indicated 1,100 BTC-equivalent, and the $54,000 put showed 883. Recent findings suggest that under Deribit’s inverse options rules, an option with intrinsic value settles into a matching futures position at the option’s strike. That future shares the option’s expiry and cash-settles immediately in BTC. Out-of-the-money options expire worthless. For a put holder, the payout reflects where the index settled relative to the strike. Once that amount is fixed, price moves during Arca trading leave the November payout unchanged.
