Sources indicate that ted Lasso is coming back for a fifth season, per the Hollywood Reporter. Final calls on the cast and a premiere date have yet to be announced, but the highly optimistic sports comedy will once again appear on Apple TV.

According to THR, season four of Ted Lasso attracted about 9.8 billion minutes of viewing time during its run this year. Just as popular as when it premiered at the beginning of this decade, the fourth season will likely be the most watched original streaming series of the year. While the first three seasons primarily focused on a men’s football club, the fourth season turned its attention to a women’s team, the Lady Greyhounds.
