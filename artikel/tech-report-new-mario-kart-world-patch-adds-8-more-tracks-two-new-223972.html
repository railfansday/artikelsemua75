Recent findings suggest that mario Kart World has a brand-fresh upgrade available to download right now on Nintendo Switch 2, bringing eight new courses to race on, two new Knockout Tour routes, and more. This new racing content arrives as part of update version 1.9.0 for Mario Kart World.

The big highlight of the update is the eight new courses, which are actually remixed spins on classic Mario Kart tracks from across the series' history. They are: Mario Bros.
