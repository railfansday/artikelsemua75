With the 24-team College Football Playoff officially set for next year, it's not too early to start thinking about how that expanded field would look.

Sources indicate that currently, the top four teams in the 12-team playoff get first-round byes while Nos. 5-12 play each other across four first-round games. In 2027, the top eight seeds will get a bye, while teams Nos. 9-24 will play each other over eight first-round games.

How about another iteration of Ohio State and Oregon, but this time at Autzen Stadium? The Ducks put themselves back in the conversation for the actual 12-team playoff with a 41-28 win in Columbus on Nov. 7. That was Ohio State's second consecutive loss after a Halloween defeat at USC.

It has been revealed that we're going with another Texas Tech game again this week. A trip to Lubbock by Ole Miss could result in both teams scoring 40 or more points. Oklahoma and Arizona would also pique our interest, especially now that Jason Witten appears to have the OU offense performing at a functional level.
