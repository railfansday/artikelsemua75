Grey’s Anatomy ended its season 23 fall finale on a cliffhanger after a main character suffered a health scare when their legs stopped working.

During the Thursday, November 19, episode of the hit ABC show, Wes (Trevor Jackson) surprised Simone (Alexis Floyd) by breaking up with her. They didn’t have much time to talk until the end of their shifts when he came to apologize. Things took a turn, however, when Wes collapsed because his legs stopped working and he said he could no longer feel them. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Sources indicate that this surprise story line comes after Wes revealed he had CVID (common variable immunodeficiency) and the condition prevented him from being able to get vaccinated. He was placed on leave after coming in contact with a patient with measles and wasn’t able to return to the hospital for 28 days while they waited to see if he would develop symptoms. Simeone, meanwhile, had a difficult season as well after thinking she was pregnant. She chose to terminate her pregnancy, but there were complications, so Simone had to go through a dilation and curettage (D&C), a procedure where the cervix is dilated in order to remove tissue from inside the uterus. (The procedure is commonly used for abortions and to treat missed or incomplete miscarriages.).

Industry observers note that it remains to be seen how Wes’ emergency will affect his future on the show. Grey’s Anatomy has had its fair share of shakeups this season with an even more limited number of episodes. The medical drama previously had as many as 27 episodes per season, but this year, there are only 13.

“But even in the last two seasons with what you saw this year, we had to reduce the cast a little bit [because of industry-wide budget cuts], and everybody has very passionate feelings about their favorite characters,” executive producer Meg Marinis told The Hollywood Reporter in May 2025. “But if we can get the writers upset then we know we’re doing it right. It’s not as satisfying a story if people aren’t upset when someone leaves. It’s supposed to make people upset and sad, so I’m sure there’ll be some arguments. But I’ve been persuaded before to change my mind on things.”.

Despite the major changes, Marinis is holding out hope that some former cast members might find a way to return this season.

“I hope so! We love bringing former cast [members] back when they’re available and it makes sense story-wise,” she told The Hollywood Reporter in October. “We have some ideas percolating, so I’m hopeful we’ll have some fun nostalgic surprises for fans this season.”.

Grey’s Anatomy is streaming on Hulu and returns Thursday, January 7.
