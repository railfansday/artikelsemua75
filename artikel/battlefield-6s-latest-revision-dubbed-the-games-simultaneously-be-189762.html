Battlefield 6's latest revision dubbed the game's simultaneously best and worst patch as it runs into all kinds of complications

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that there’s been a lot of anticipation for Battlefield 6’s version 1.1.3.0 patch. The patch kicks off the third and final phase of the game’s first season, adding some new holiday-themed content. More the new content itself, the enhancement’s biggest additions are almost entirely gameplay-related.

Unfortunately, for as much as the changes in 1.1.3.0 have been well received, the launch has been marred by a series of technical issues, some of which are a little unusual. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Recent findings suggest that immediately after patch 1.1.3.0 went live, players on PC started reporting massive stutters that would occur every few seconds. The issue is especially pronounced in Redsec Battle Royale, but much deadlier in traditional multiplayer.

According to recent reports, battlefield Studios quickly acknowledged it, pointing out a workaround that appears to be working for players on Steam. According to those players, going offline on Steam’s friends menu has fixed stuttering for them.

The issue remains for other players on PC, however, and the developer stated it’s working on a proper fix.

Perhaps the most disrupting problem the patch has caused is one that prevents players from being able to interact with the game’s menu. Tiles become unresponsive, or outright unselectable when you try to hover near them.

Ironically, the top set of tiles designed to make it easy for players to swiftly jump into the new content has been the most affected. Restarting the game sometimes fixes the issue, and some players report that it only happens upon first launch after installing the revision.

It has been revealed that but Battlefield Studios later clarified that, unsurprisingly, there’s an actual reason behind the bug. Once again, a fix is being investigated, and the developer will offer an update once some progress has been made.

Recent findings suggest that A little further down the list of importance is an problem that stops players from being able to skip tiers within the upcoming Winter Offensive bonus path in the game’s battle pass. BF Studios took out the feature entirely while it works on a fix, so while you can continue making progress in the bonus path, you won’t be able to skip tiers.

According to recent reports, this is all a shame to see. Outside of all the problems, the meat of the new patch has been almost universally praised. The visibility improvements, and netcode updates have been celebrated. Many players were also happy to see meaningful updates to footstep audio and overall clarity.

Recent findings suggest that it’s also unusual for a Battlefield 6 patch to run into so many issues at launch. The sentiment around 1.1.3.0 so far is that it’s the game’s best and worst upgrade yet. Hopefully those issues will be fixed before the development team breaks for the holidays.
