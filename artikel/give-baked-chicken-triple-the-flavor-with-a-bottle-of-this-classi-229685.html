Sources indicate that perfectly baked chicken is one of those meals that offers a wonderful balance of texture and flavor, with salty, crispy skin and a tender, juicy interior. It only takes a few minutes of prep, and, sure, you may need to keep an eye on the oven, but it's relatively hands-off, and you can be working on flawlessly fluffy mashed potatoes or other amazing sides, like baked candied yams, as it cooks. And to give it a huge hit of flavor right out of the bottle, you should be baking your chicken with Heinz 57 Sauce, available on Amazon. Sources indicate that this trick will work with a whole chicken, but you may want to use skin-on breasts or skin-on, bone-in thighs or drumsticks for maximum juiciness and a smaller surface area that will allow the sauce to coat the meat more thoroughly. Baking your chicken with Heinz 57 will give it some nice, tangy vinegar high notes with deep, semisweet, rich, and unctuous undertones. According to Heinz, it's made with 57 ingredients (some of them secret for over a century), so you'll save time not having to make your own concoction of sauces, sweeteners, herbs, and spices. The base is tomato puree, which brings the umami. Add to that some malt and white vinegar, garlic and onion powder, apple puree, turmeric, and dozens of other components, and you have a delicious amalgam of flavors that is cohesive and distinctive: zesty, smoky, lightly sweet and sour, and bold without being overbearing. You could pour Heinz 57 Sauce directly over your chicken pieces and toss them in the oven, but with another ingredient or two, you'll get a thicker coating. The very simplest option uses equal parts honey and Heinz 57. You simply mix them together, brush the viscous liquid all around the chicken, then baste occasionally as it cooks, taking care not to let the sauce burn. The honey brings some extra sweetness, but the fusion of the two will caramelize beautifully as the chicken cooks.

You can also roast your chicken over a bed of vegetables or surround the pieces with halved bell peppers to make it a whole meal in a pan — and some lemon slices for brightness won't hurt. If you want more smokiness or heat, add a dash of paprika or cayenne to your marinade, and for more savory seasoning, try dried thyme or oregano.

Whether you're cooking chicken thighs, drumsticks, or breasts, an oven temperature between 400 and 425 degrees Fahrenheit typically works well. An internal temperature of 165 degrees is the minimum your chicken should reach for food safety, but you can cook it to a higher temperature for a fall-off-the-bone texture, particularly with thighs or drumsticks. Just be careful not to dry it out. To properly use a meat thermometer, you'll want to insert it into the thickest part of the meat and be careful not to hit the bone, or the reading may be inaccurate. And aside from the incredible flavor, the beauty of using Heinz 57 Sauce on your baked chicken is that you can prep the marinade in the time it takes to heat up the oven.
