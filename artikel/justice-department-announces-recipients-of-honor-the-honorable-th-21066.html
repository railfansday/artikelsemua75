Sources indicate that attorney General Todd Blanche today unveiled the recipients of Honor the Honorable: The Attorney General’s Award for Distinguished Service in Policing. This award represents the Department of Justice’s reaffirmed commitment to uplifting the valor and exceptional contributions to policing of our great nation’s law enforcement personnel.

According to recent reports, the Attorney General’s Honor the Honorable Award recognizes individual or teams of rank-and-file officers from state, local, Tribal or territorial law enforcement agencies for exceptional efforts in policing. The awarded officers, deputies and troopers have demonstrated exceptional service in one of three areas: criminal investigations, field operations or exemplary community involvement. Within each category, awards were given to law enforcement officers and deputies who serve small, medium and large jurisdictions.
