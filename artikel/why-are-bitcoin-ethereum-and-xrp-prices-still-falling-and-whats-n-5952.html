Why Are Bitcoin, Ethereum and XRP Prices Still Falling and What’s Next?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that bitcoin fell to $61,496.58, down 0.7% in 24 hours and 11.5% over the past week. Ether dropped 1.3% on the day to $1,724.13 and is down 12.3% for the week. XRP held up better than both, edging 0.3% higher to $0.9593. It has lost 5.5% over seven days.

A short rebound earlier in the week ran out of steam at resistance, and sellers took control again.

It has been revealed that bitcoin climbed off a low near $62,000 last week but stalled in the $65,000 to $67,000 zone. That band had worked as support before the crash, and once broken it turned into resistance. Rejections there were expected, and the price has since slid back toward its lows.

According to analyst Josh of Crypto World, several factors are still weighing on the market:.

Bitcoin Back at Its Key Support.

Sources indicate that at $61,496, Bitcoin is back inside the $61,000 to $62,100 support zone, where it bounced last week. This is the most key level on the chart in the near term.

The base case calls for choppy sideways trading, with Bitcoin holding above its recent low for a week or two rather than staging a sharp recovery. Even a move above $67,000 would not guarantee a quick run to $74,000 while momentum stays weak.

Ethereum Slips Under Its Support Band.

Ether has fallen to $1,724, just below the bottom of its $1,730 to $1,800 support zone. Several tests of this area in recent days had failed to produce a daily close below it. A daily close under $1,730 would be the first confirmed break.

Industry observers note that the daily RSI had started to recover from oversold levels, pointing to a short pause in the crash. That recovery is now being tested. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

If the break below $1,730 holds, Ether could slide toward $1,650 sooner than expected.

XRP Holds Up Better but Faces a Wall at $1.

XRP is the strongest of the three this week. The token climbed toward the $0.99 to $1.02 resistance zone but struggled there, as expected, and has eased back to about $0.96.

The daily RSI is close to leaving oversold territory, which leaves a little room for a further mild bounce.
