Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +6.58%) stock flew 9.2% higher in the first 10 minutes of trading Monday morning -- and that's not surprising. Just about everything tech is going up today, as investors rejoice at the return of Jensen Huang to CEO Nvidia (NVDA +30.77%).

But that's not the only reason SpaceX stock is soaring -- or even the main reason.

Investment bank Jefferies initiated coverage of SpaceX stock this morning with a "buy" rating and a $210 price target, implying the space stock could gain another 50% over the next 12 months.

As StreetInsider.com explains, "SpaceX dominates mass-to-orbit of Earth," and its huge lead in space rockets makes it the low-cost provider of access to orbit -- making it also the low-cost provider of space-based communication and artificial intelligence. Jefferies analyst Sheila Kahyaoglu predicts that SpaceX's new Starship megarocket, which executed a picture-perfect tower capture after returning from orbit earlier this month, will launch 315 flights in 2029, increasing the firm's ability to put payloads in orbit by more than 10x from where it is today.

Recent findings suggest that that means 10x more revenue-generating Starlink satellites. 10x more high-tech military Starshield satellites. It means -- in the not-too-distant future -- 10x more solar-powered artificial intelligence data centers in space, where they won't have to worry about data center regulation on Earth, pay electricity bills, or manage water use.

Simply put, says Kahyaoglu, "SPCX's space launch franchise has become its core competitive advantage, enabling Connectivity today and Orbital SI in the future." It will enable SpaceX to grow from $32 billion in revenue last year to nearly $500 billion by 2029.

No other space manufacturer can do this yet -- but soon, SpaceX can.
