'Licence to kill': French parliament adopts police shootings law slammed by activists

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (62 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

French senators gave final approval on Friday to controversial legislation granting police officers a presumption of legitimate self-defence when using firearms, fulfilling a long-standing demand of the far right even as weeks of student-led protests have thrust the concern of police brutality back into the spotlight. Left-wing lawmakers have vowed to challenge the bill before France's Constitutional Council.
