📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that now that Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially been released to the public, the full scope of its unprecedented popularity has been felt worldwide. While the fact that Rockstar's long-awaited sequel to Grand Theft Auto 5 is the best-selling PlayStation game globally isn't all that surprising, the exact sales figures for GTA 6 put it above not just any game before it, but the industry as a whole.

Initially shared by Jason Schreirer on X, a recent Rockstar report has declared GTA 6 has made over $3.7 billion in revenue in the first 24 hours of its release, which is not only an astronomical achievement for video games on their own, but marks GTA 6 as "the biggest entertainment launch of all time.". A large portion of GTA 6's success also came from pre-orders as well, with Schreier noting that over 32.5 million units of the game were sold before it was even officially released, which has now skyrocketed on the charts for the best-selling video games of all time despite only being out for a day.

Putting the sales into perspective with a follow-up post, Schreier even noted that the highest-grossing film of all time, Avengers: Endgame, only made $2.94 billion by comparison "across its entire box office run," which is hundreds of millions less than GTA 6 has in a single day.

While the staggering sales figures are hard to fully comprehend, it's not hard to believe considering you can't go anywhere on the internet without seeing countless clips, reactions, and discussions over GTA 6's massive scale and release, with search trends skyrocketing for the game even before it went live.

While there have been some concerns that GTA 6's high price tag could cause an industry shift towards more inflated triple-A game prices in addition to leaving behind physical games entirely to save money on production, Rockstar's latest release is likely going to bring an entire industry's worth of eyes onto the gaming market with its success story.
