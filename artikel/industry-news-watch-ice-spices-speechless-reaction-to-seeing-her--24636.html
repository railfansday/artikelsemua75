According to recent reports, “It’s so accurate. I have a really unique face and I’m impressed they got it so right,” she remarked before later joking, “This is so accurate, like oh my God, my major-ass forehead, you literally got it.”.

Recent findings suggest that the 26-year-old posted a series of photos alongside her wax sister to social media on Friday (Nov. 20). “Put it on wax ;),” she wrote in her caption.

The Ice Spice wax figure will be on display at the Times Square location of Madame Tussauds Latest York in the heart of NYC into 2027.

“Ice Spice is a defining part of New York’s music and culture scene, so bringing her into Madame Tussauds New York feels especially meaningful,” mentioned Tiago Mogadouro, who serves as the general manager for Madame Tussauds New York. “She represents a new generation of artists who are shaping the city’s identity in real time, and we’re excited to give fans a way to celebrate that right here in Times Square.”.
