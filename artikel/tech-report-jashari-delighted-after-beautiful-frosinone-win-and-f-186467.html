Ardon Jashari claims he promised the AC Milan fans he would score, so he was happy to deliver on his promise.

Milan put on a counter-attacking clinic at San Siro on Sunday night against Frosinone, winning 5-1. An own-goal from Gabriele Calvani kicked things off, with Goncalo Ramos, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic also netting.

There was a special moment for Jashari just after coming on at half-time as he scored the third goal of the night and his first for the club. It came in transition, with the Swiss midfielder running in behind and thumping a powerful shot in off the near post, leaving the goalkeeper stunned.

Jashari spoke to SportMediaset after the final whistle at San Siro to reflect on what was a great night both for the team and for him on a personal note, with his words via MilanPress.

How do you feel to have scored and won?

Industry observers note that “Finally, I’m very happy with my first goal for Milan. It was a great feeling, and I’m happy for our team. We played a very intense, beautiful game against a tough and united team, both on and off the ball. We won, and we have to continue like this on Thursday, and for the rest of the season.”. Can we expect the same in Europe now?

“I hope so. I’m happy with today and I’ll try to continue like this for the team and the fans. It’s very crucial to have won.”.

What can you tell us about your position today?

Recent findings suggest that “I played next to Luka today. Normally, strikers score goals, but today it was my turn. When I score, I score beautiful goals (smiles). It seemed like the goal against Venezia, but today it was even better because it was mine. The celebration was the same. I promised the fans I would score, and that’s what happened.”.

“In my opinion, it’s very key to continue like this. The season is too long until May. Now it’s November. We’ve had a good season so far, but there are five months to go until May. We need these results against all our opponents. Today it was Frosinone, there’s still a lot to do.

In a recent development, “When you play for Milan, you have to win games and trophies, but it’s November. Everyone knows what it means to wear the Milan shirt, it’s crucial to continue like this. When you win, you’re good, when you lose, you’re not. It doesn’t matter who scores.”.
