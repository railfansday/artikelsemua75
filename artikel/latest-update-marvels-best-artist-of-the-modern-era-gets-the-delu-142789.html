Industry observers note that one of Marvel’s best artists of the modern era is getting an awesome retrospective, and ScreenRant is pleased to bring fans a tantalizing first look. Recently, Marvel partnered with Clover Press to release a series of art books devoted to some of the publisher’s best artists. So far, 11 volumes have been released, and now one of the most exciting artists to hit the House of Ideas in recent memory, Peach Momoko, is getting her own spotlight.

In a recent development, debuting onto the American comics scene in 2019, after years of working in other fields, Peach Momoko would swiftly become one of the most sought after cover artists on the scene today. Momoko’s art is regarded for its amazing use of watercolors, which has given her a style all her own. In addition to a long list of variant covers, Momoko, who was also one of Marvel’s “Stormbreakers,” has also written and illustrated both ongoing and miniseries as well, most notably Ultimate X-Men and Demon Days, which placed Marvel heroes in feudal Japan.

Industry observers note that peach Momoko continues to be a popular and in-demand artist, as well as an award winner. She has received two Eisner awards for her cover work in addition to numerous accolades from critics, fans and peers alike. Momoko’s art is a joy to behold, and now Marvel and Clover Press are giving the amazing artist her due in a upcoming art book celebrating her best covers. Sources indicate that today, Marvel and Clover Press are announcing a Kickstarter campaign for The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko. Clocking in at over 200 pages, the coffee-table sized book shines a light on not only Momoko’s art from Demon Days and Ultimate X-Men, but also covers for a variety of Marvel icons, such as Jeff the Land Shark, Elektra, Spider-Man and many others. Clover and Marvel are going all out for The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko. In addition to the book itself, fans can pick up an impressive bounty of goodies, such as posters, prints and even a jigsaw puzzle.

It has been revealed that the Marvel Art of Peach Momoko is the latest volume in The Marvel Art Of…series. Previous volumes have featured Alex Maleev, Mike Zeck and David Mack, among others. These gorgeous books give the artists who made Marvel great the respect they deserve, and Peach Momoko, who has made a splash like few others, certainly warrants a volume as well. Hank Kanalz, publisher at Clover Press, had this to say about Momoko joining the series:.

Stunning watercolors and gorgeous compositions set Momoko’s work apart from anything else in comics...We’re excited to add her collection of work to our line of books and prints.

Recent findings suggest that peach Momoko, in just a few short years, has become renowned for her unique style, which pushes the boundaries of what mainstream comics can achieve. When Momoko debuted, her art was unlike anything else at the time, and fans responded enthusiastically. Her covers alone would guarantee a spot in comic book history, but Demon Days and especially the transcendent Ultimate X-Men make her a modern master. Marvel and Clover Press have recognized Peach Momoko’s genius, awarding her a Marvel Art book not even a decade after her arrival in American comics.
