📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hailey Bieber turned 30 on November 22, but the birthday girl has been celebrating for weeks. From a latest navy-blue Rhode drop to a pre-birthday birthday party (for which she wore a vintage python bustier top from Dolce & Gabbana’s Spring 2005 collection), she’s been ushering in the new decade with work milestones and time with friends and family. And before the end of the month, she finished off the festivities with a trip to the Bahamas.

Joined by her husband, Justin, along with a handful of girlfriends—Kendall and Kylie Jenner, Camila Morrone, Justine Skye, and more—the beauty founder spent her birthday weekend on island time. And, of course, with an island style to match. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

In photos shared to her Instagram, Bieber is seen in everything from a gold metallic bikini to a raffia cowboy hat and some beachy party fits.

It has been revealed that over the itty-bitty two-piece, the star layered a sheer cover-up from the Dolce & Gabbana Spring 1997 runway. A warm-weather take on the fur-trimmed silk-brocade robe favored this fall, the long leopard-print piece featured deep-brown fur lapels, long sleeves, and a breezy, billowing body. She paired the look with sunglasses and a flap bag from Matthieu Blazy’s Resort 2026 collection for Chanel. The on-theme accessory was crafted with a braided raffia body and “fluffy” yellow straw trim.

Later, Bieber changed into straight-off-the-runway pieces from the Roberto Cavalli Spring 2027 show. On top, she wore a forest-green top with a lace-up front and flared trumpet sleeves, and for bottoms, she went with low-rise zebra-print trousers.

As for her friends, the Jenners were photographed in beachy ensembles of their own: Kendall in a bikini and mustard-yellow sarong, and Kylie in a brown crochet cover-up dress.
