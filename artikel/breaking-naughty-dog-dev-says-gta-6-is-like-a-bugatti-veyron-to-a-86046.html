Naughty Dog’s Michael Barclay – whose credits include Uncharted: The Lost Legacy and The Last of Us 2 – states he’s “made video games for 19 years and GTA 6 appears to me as a Bugatti Veyron would to a nervous Victorian child”.

And it’s being treated like witchcraft by devs, who naturally have a greater appreciation for the feats on display.

Barclay, who’s currently working on next year’s Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet, continued:.

“I'm always astonished that [Rockstar’s] environmental lighting is mostly dynamic and looks as good if not better than most baked lighting.”. (Related: Tech Report: Japan releases a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t ...)

In really, really simple terms, baked lighting is a game development technique used to save computational cost by calculating light and shadow data in advance.

The downside to this is that it’s fixed, but obviously given the ever-changing nature of GTA 6’s sandbox, it needs to have a dynamic solution.

Barclay is saying that GTA 6’s dynamic lighting matches and even surpasses those pre-determined solutions in many scenarios, making it a real accomplishment.

These comments triggered discussion from various other industry professionals, including Tim Soret from Odd Tales, the makers of The Last Night, who outlined some of his thoughts on the title:.

GTA 6 sets a standard so unfathomably high I do wonder at what point it’ll ever be topped.

I suppose raising the bar like this will motivate the many other talented devs around the world, but not every studio will have access to the time and the estimated $1 billion budget that allowed Rockstar to deliver this.

Either way, it’s clear industry professionals are utterly blown away by what the studio has delivered here.
