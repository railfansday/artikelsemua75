In a recent development, discount sites, shoe chains’ online clearance sections, and brands’ own sale pages sell brand-name shoes for less, but the return terms decide whether a bad fit costs you. Before you order a size you haven’t worn, check the return window and the cost of a mailed return, since several sellers charge $8.50 to $9.99.

Shoes in the same size fit differently from one brand to the next, and even between models from one brand, so a pair ordered online is a guess until the box arrives.

Return terms split the eight sellers below into three groups. Three pay for a mailed return, though one does so only for members and another only in the lower 48 states. Three more take returns free in their stores but deduct $8.50 or $9.95 from a refund by mail, and the last two make you pay for every return you mail.
