Recent findings suggest that season 35 of “Dancing With the Stars” is officially coming to an end. On Tuesday, Nov. 24, the five remaining celebrities — Tatyana Ali, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins and Connor Wood — compete one last time for the mirrorball trophy. Each finalist will perform three routines: the instant dance, a freestyle, and judges’ choice.

Variety will be updating this story live, so keep refreshing through the night for updates. Tatyana Ali and Jan RavnikFor judges’ choice, the duo kicked off the night with a sexy salsa to “Jamaican (Bam Bam),” directed by Carrie Ann Inaba. The duo scored a 27/30. When it came time for the instant dance, the duo drew a jive, even though she preferred the foxtrot.

Amber Glenn and Pasha PashkovThe pair’s judges’ choice dance, with direction from Derek Hough, was a quickstep to “Sing, Sing, Sing,” and they scored the first perfect score of 30 of the night.

Connor Wood and Rylee ArnoldCarrie Ann suggested the Viennese waltz for the judges’ choice dance, and they danced to Noah Kahan’s “Willing and Able.” The emotional number earned the pair a 28/30.

Maura Higgins and Mark BallasAssigned a tango by Bruno Tonioli, Team Tink danced to “Gallo Ciego” by Orquesta Color Tango, scoring the second perfect score of 30.

Ezra Frech and Daniella KaragachGoing last in the judges’ choice dances, Bruno assigned Ezra and Daniella a steamy salsa to “Danza Kuduro” by Bruno; their routine earned a perfect score.
