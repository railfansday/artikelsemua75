When it comes to video games, some of the most popular have always been the likes of action/adventure titles. Video games provide a great escape for the player. Giving them the chance to be an action hero, swinging around Upcoming York City, stealing cars, shooting up bad guys, or becoming the samurai of their dreams makes them as popular as they've become.

Like any genre of any medium, though, there are some games that most certainly stand out among the others. This isn't to say that they're better than all the rest—there are too many games to make such an objective statement—but there are some certified bangers that come to mind when the discussion of action video games begins.

It has been revealed that this wonderful series by Rocksteady Studios is known as one of the best superhero video game franchises of all time, and for very good reason. A lot of those reasons were either expanded upon from the first game, or established in the second title, Batman: Arkham City. This is known as one of the best superhero games out there, and has gone down in history for it.

Batman: Arkham City is well-known for the common notion that it makes you truly feel like Batman (Kevin Conroy). The gameplay mechanics are very well-executed, and the story is thrilling from start to finish. Not to mention, the return of Kevin Conroy as Batman and Mark Hamill as Joker is a huge win and makes this truly feel like some of the many definitive versions of the characters.

If there's one thing PlayStation is excellent at, it's having some amazing console-exclusive titles. One of the best of recent years is none other than Horizon Zero Dawn. Following the hunter known as Aloy (Ashly Burch), Horizon Zero Dawn takes players on a journey of trying to discover the truth about the hunter's origins, and, in turn, the world around her itself. This project takes place in a post-apocalyptic setting, but not in the manner of another PlayStation exclusive, The Last of Us. The landscape of Horizon Zero Dawn is wide, vast, and filled with high-technology—machine creatures that are now the apex predators, to be exact. The combat—using Aloy's hunting skills and weapons—is wonderful, and such a blast to play. Not to mention, this newly introduced world is such a joy to explore and keeps the players actively wanting to discover more about it via the story and the open-world nature of the title. It has been revealed that when it comes to action, there are few that bring the amount that Hades does. That's because the entire point of the game is, well, action. While there is a story to Hades, the game, at its heart, is a rougelike dungeon crawler title that puts the player against hoards and rooms upon rooms of foes to face as the son of Hades (Logan Cunningham), Zagreus (Darren Korb), attempts to escape the Underworld.

Players will die but, every time they begin anew, they can spend their hard-earned resources to level up and go at it again with new powers. The method to the madness of Hades is failing, trying again, failing, and going until the player finally makes it through. It is an absolute thrill ride the entire way through.

According to recent reports, while Sega is most known for the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise, they have another video game series that is widely beloved, Yakuza. The best game in said series is none other than Yazuka 0. The Yakuza series is a great time, and the way it brings together gritty crime drama with some hilarious comedy that keeps the players on their toes.

As stated, the best of the best, Yakuza 0, brings all of these things to the table and enhances them. It follows two stories simultaneously, and that really helps in keeping the players invested. The combat mechanics are phenomenal and the true, main highlight of the game. Yakuza 0 is truly the perfect example of how to make an incredible Yazkua game.

While the recent God of War games are easily some of the best PlayStation titles in recent years, if fans of action want something in the series to seriously deliver on that, they need to look no further than God of War II. This sequel truly delivers on action, spectacle, and fun gameplay. It has a blockbuster-level story that is engaging for the entire runtime.

Sources indicate that not to forget, the players get to take on the gods of Olympus. Kratos (Terrence "T.C." Carson) is out for blood, and he is going to stop at nothing to get it. This allows players to experience some serious spectacle as they fight their way through Olympus and take down the gods. It's a ride filled with intensity till the very end and worth playing every second.

Industry observers note that much like Batman: Arkham City, Insomniac Games' hit title, Marvel's Spider-Man, is well-regarded because of how much it makes the player truly feel like Spider-Man (Yuri Lowenthal). The swinging physics/mechanics are stellar, and easily some of the best in any Spider-Man game ever. Swinging mechanics are so much more vital to a Spider-Man game than one would realize on paper. For quite some time, Spider-Man 2's video game was known to have the best.

Sources indicate that many consider it one of the best modern superhero video games of all time. The execution of Marvel's Spider-Man was done with grace and prowess. This reimagining of the Spider-Man world—where Peter Parker is older, experienced, and facing off with enemies he's never faced before—is so well-done, and it's a fan-favorite Spider-Man universe. Playing Marvel's Spider-Man truly does make the player feel like Spider-Man, and, alongside that, has an astounding story.

It has been revealed that there are a few things in this life that will always be cool: cowboys, superheroes, spacemen, and . . . samurais. A modern game that might genuinely capture the samurai vibe the best is none other than 2020's Ghost of Tsushima. If there's one thing that can describe this game, it's that it is vastly cinematic. It feels like players are playing through a classic samurai film.

Playing the ever-gorgeous Ghost of Tsushima feels like playing through a movie in all the best ways. The camera, shot framing, battles, and more all make this story amazing to experience. The melee combat is borderline perfect. Sword duels and other action feel so natural and exhilarating to play—it's genuinely difficult to put this amazing game down.

It has been revealed that 2016's Doom is about as action-packed as it gets. It is a literal non-stop space adventure that has players firing bullets of all kinds into horrifying monsters. Doom rewards players for being aggressive and jumping straight into action, rather than strategy. There is always a battle or chase going down, and it never stops being abundantly exciting.

But players aren't just firing the same kind of gun the entire time—no, there is a gigantic arsenal of weapons players will have the choice to utilize throughout the entire game. Just as importantly, the score behind the action is one of the best action game scores of all time. Composed by the legendary Mick Gordon, everything that is great about Doom is enhanced by Gordon's excellent work.

Industry observers note that another sword-wielding adventure—reminiscent of Ghost of Tsushima—from recent years that is phenomenal is the ever-thrilling Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice. Taking place in the late-1500s, this 2019 adventure follows a shinobi named Sekiro (Daisuke Namikawa and Noshir Dalal), players get to step into the shoes of an incredible swordsman as he goes on a journey to rescue his captured lord, Kuro (Miyuki Sato and Amber Hood).

In a recent development, sekiro: Shadows Die Twice is everything a typical FromSoftware game—Dark Souls, Bloodborne, and Elden Ring—is, in all the best ways. It truly challenges the player in ways other FromSoftware titles don't. It also has a setting unlike the others, too, being set in 1500s Japan. It is so distinct, vast, and exciting that it feels like a must-play for those who love some good sword combat . . . even if it has the classic tough FromSoftware difficuly.

In a recent development, there is absolutely no question that Grand Theft Auto VI is the biggest game of the decade—maybe even the last two decades. People have been waiting for this game for so long that the cultural impact has been increasing little by little every year, exploding in one great release this year. Ask anyone who's played it, and they'll most likely say, "It was worth the wait.".

Recent findings suggest that this game is truly something special. It has attention to detail unlike any other, gorgeous visuals, skilled performances, groundbreaking mechanics across the board, and a wonderful story at the core of it all. There are dozens of reasons to love this game, but one of the main highlights are the action sequences—of which there are plenty. Robberies, assassinations, gunfights, etc. bring Grand Theft Auto VI to the top of the "best of all time" list.
