Italy international Gianluigi Donnarumma received criticism lately, but Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca and former goalkeeper Joe Hart rushed to his defence.

The shot-stopper was guilty of a few howlers for the Azzurri in the Nations League, most notably in the 3-1 victory over France, but also made a series of big saves over the two matches.

“He has been good,” assured Manchester City coach Maresca with regards to Donnarumma.

“I think with the international team, he made a mistake in the build-up. But in Italy he was the man of the match in the game, he made many good saves. (Related: How to Build a Healthcare Robot from Simulation to Depl...)

In a recent development, “We made some mistakes in the last games. Gigi was part of that. He is aware. Hopefully he’s not going to make any more mistakes. He is going to start against Fulham.”.

Former Manchester City, Torino and England goalkeeper Hart has also used his column on the official City website to heap praise upon the Italian, who only recently won the Champions League at Paris Saint-Germain.

Donnarumma also won the Yashin Trophy, the goalkeeper’s equivalent of the Ballon d’Or, twice.

In a recent development, “I think some of Gianluigi’s performances were breathtaking, absolutely astonishing. He’s the captain of Italy. Simply put, he’s at the top table of goalkeepers,” wrote Hart.

Industry observers note that “I think he’s proven his quality and his ability on numerous occasions since joining City. Yes he’s not had the best couple of months but that’s the life of a goalkeeper. He’ll have experienced it before. He’ll experience it again. We shouldn’t forget all the brilliant stuff he does for the team.”.

Hart praised Donnarumma’s role in the locker room too, noting he is “a really beautiful human being” and “renowned for his hugs.”.
