Rangers are not likely to pursue another move for former loanee Vaclav Cerny in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The 29-year-old winger is at the moment plying his trade in Turkey with Besiktas after joining from Wolfsburg in 2025.

He was linked with a return to Rangers over the summer following a disappointing debut campaign in the Super Lig. (Related: Latest Update: Taco Bell is bringing back the Steakhous...)

Derek McInnes pushed for other targets to be added to his squad, and the Czech international has started this season strongly at Besiktas.

They are still open to selling him in January, but as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the Gers will not be one of the clubs targeting Cerny again. (Related: Redditor forced to bring desktop PC in checked luggage ...)

McInnes is content with his attacking options as it stands, and the club's chiefs are set to focus on bolstering other areas of his squad.

Cerny registered 18 goals and nine assists in 52 appearances across all competitions during the 2024-25 campaign at Ibrox, while on loan from Wolfsburg.

His strong showings and rapport with supporters fuelled talk of a summer return, but both parties have now moved on, and he will not be back at the club in the latest year even if he does depart Besiktas.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don’t think bringing in another wide player is the top priority probably for Rangers right now. They’ve got so many quality players out there who can play out wide right now as well.

"I think the price tag involved for Cerny, and obviously Rangers being well stocked in that position, probably means that they don’t really need to pursue a move for the Czech Republic international.".

Gassama has nailed down a spot as a regular starter on the wing since joining Rangers in 2025, effectively succeeding Cerny in the starting eleven.

He has registered five goal contributions in 18 appearances so far this season and remains a key player under McInnes.

With interest in his services prevalent in the summer, and remaining ahead of January, sources have exclusively revealed to Football Insider that Rangers have no plans to sell Gassama midway through the campaign.

They will only change their minds if a sizeable offer for his services arrives, but that seems unlikely, given his lack of experience in a top five European league.
