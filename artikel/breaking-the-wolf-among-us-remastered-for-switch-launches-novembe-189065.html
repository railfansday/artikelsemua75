The Switch version of The Wolf Among Us Remastered will launch via Nintendo eShop on November 29 for $24.99, Telltale Games announced.

While originally scheduled to launch on October 29 alongside the PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Xbox One, and PC (Steam, Epic Games Store) versions, the Switch version was delayed for further quality improvements. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Here is an overview of the game, via Telltale Games:.

It has been revealed that fairy tales are real. Murder is just the beginning.

Step back into Fabletown in The Wolf Among Us Remastered—the definitive edition of Telltale’s acclaimed noir narrative thriller inspired by DC’s award-winning Fables comic series.

Critically acclaimed, The Wolf Among Us became one of the most celebrated story-driven adventures of its generation, praised for its unforgettable characters, sharp writing, and meaningful player choice. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Fully remastered with enhanced visuals and modern presentation, this definitive edition brings Bigby Wolf’s first investigation to life with greater atmosphere, immersion, and detail than ever before – honoring the original experience while introducing Fabletown to a newly introduced generation of players.

Investigate brutal murders, interrogate suspects, explore crime scenes, and uncover a conspiracy hiding beneath the streets of Fabletown, where myths and legends live secretly among us.

Every choice carries consequences. In Fabletown, justice isn’t clean, and sometimes the Big Bad Wolf is the only one willing to do what must be done.
