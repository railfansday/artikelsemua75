'We are ready to facilitate the removal of Netanyahu' from power: Head of Israel's Joint List

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (72 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, in an interview with FRANCE 24, the head of the Arab-majority Joint List mentioned his political alliance is prepared to play a decisive role in bringing down Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's government following the recent election.
