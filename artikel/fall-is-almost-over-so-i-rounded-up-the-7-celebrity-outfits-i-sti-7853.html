📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Like much of the TikTok generation, I spend way too much time looking for style inspo on social media. While that sometimes means letting the dishes pile up in favor of a few extra minutes of scroll time, I rarely miss a street-style moment. Whether it’s Hailey Bieber in a date-night outfit or Taylor Swift in this season’s hottest color combination, I’ve been stowing away fall outfit inspiration for the final stretch of the season.

Hollywood’s cool girls are squeezing every last wear out of fall, wearing silk midi skirts, sheer tops, and cape-like scarves. Beyond fall essentials like trench coats and dark-wash denim, they’re also reaching for this year’s breakout trends, including maraschino-cherry-colored accessories and striped pullover sweaters, keeping their seasonal rotation fresh before winter takes over.

While I can’t recreate every ensemble on the internet, I can round up the seven autumn outfits most on my mind, ranked by how eager I am to make them my own before the temps drop for good. According to recent reports, on the set of Die Alive, Zoë Kravitz made a case for laid-back elegance and an unexpectedly bright fall color choice. The slinky midi skirt is polished, while the basic gray tank is casual. The trench coat makes the outfit perfect for chilly weather, with a pair of chocolate pumps elevating the style. Consider this your go-to look for weekend brunch prepares and cocktails with the girls alike.

Selena Gomez put a fresh spin on the capsule wardrobe, creating a monochromatic outfit using closet staples, including sleek capris, mesh pointed-toe heels, and a quilted cinched bag. However, instead of a basic black tee, she reached for a sheer iteration, turning the simple ensemble into a fresh and fun look. To pull it off for a chilly night, I’d add a leather bomber jacket. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

Kristen Bell managed to wear just about every style trend at once—without it looking like a slop wardrobe. Her striped pullover served as the base, with coordinated fall-coded colors sprinkled throughout the rest of her outfit. Her dark-wash, wide-leg jeans reminded me why denim is an autumn classic, and I officially want her glove flats. However, it was Bell’s cherry-red beanie and merlot Chanel handbag that injected personality into her look.

Sources indicate that I’m all about a cozy fall wardrobe, but when date night rolls around, I want something a bit more elevated and sleek. Ashley Graham understood the assignment, wearing a silky plunge halterneck top and draped, gray trousers. While she opted for open-toe sandals, pointed, closed-toe pumps can beat the chill.

Karlie Kloss’ chic look is a nod to office wear. Her polished autumnal look features closet basics, including a white button-up shirt, above-the-knee skirt, and ballet flats. Kloss added an extra dose of flair by intentionally draping a black shawl across her shoulders, making it the perfect look for strolling Fifth Avenue and museum hopping alike.

Sometimes the best looks are the simplest. Daisy Edgar-Jones layered a classic white T-shirt underneath a sapphire-blue trench coat, taking a closet basic and elevating it with an unexpected twist. Light-washed, straight-leg jeans elongated her frame, and timeless black pumps turned a casual look into a polished ensemble.

Industry observers note that elizabeth Debicki played with fall textures, reaching for a bold leopard-print turtleneck sweater and slick leather pants. Leopard print is both timeless and trendy, which makes it especially easy to style. It’s the ideal outfit for a night on the town, perfectly cozy yet fashion-forward.
