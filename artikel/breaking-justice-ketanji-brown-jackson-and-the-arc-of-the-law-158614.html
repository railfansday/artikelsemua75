It has been revealed that in September, Justice Ketanji Brown Jackson received an award named after the late Justice William Brennan, a liberal lion. In her acceptance speech, Jackson discussed Brennan’s role as the Supreme Court became more conservative, noting that he “believed that even his dissents could make a difference and remind the nation that the pursuit of equal justice under law is an ongoing effort.” Jackson indicated that she agreed with that idea, telling the audience that she saw “dissent[s] as more than just a disagreement with the outcome of a case, I see them as an expression of faith in the future and a demonstration of hope.” As she put it, “However desolate the moment feels, we who write for a future age have hope that the noble and collective work of bending the long arc of the law toward justice will continue.”.

Industry observers note that since joining the court in the summer of 2022, Jackson has sought to promote her understanding of that view. Although she has been consistently part of the court’s liberal bloc, even among the three Democratic appointees she has forged a unique role. As her invocation of Brennan suggests, it often appears that her goal is not simply to express disagreement with the majority, but to offer an alternative vision and to speak to the public directly – even if she does so without the support of her fellow liberals.
