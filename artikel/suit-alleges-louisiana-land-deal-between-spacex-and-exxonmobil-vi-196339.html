A new lawsuit now alleges they did so in violation of the state’s open meetings law.The lawsuit, filed by three south-central Louisiana residents, follows state and Vermilion parish officials’ approval of what is estimated to be a record $27.5bn tax incentive package for SpaceX’s Starbase Louisiana project, revealed over the summer. The officials violated the state’s open meetings law eight times while facilitating the agreement in attempts to “keep the public in the dark about what they are doing for SpaceX”, the lawsuit alleges. Suit claims parish board was manipulated to allow deal to go through and settlement agreement hidden from public.

Recent findings suggest that after ending a lawsuit over ExxonMobil’s damage to wetlands, Louisiana officials moved rapidly to approve a deal giving SpaceX access to 125,000 acres (50,600 hectares) of marsh formerly owned by the oil company. A new lawsuit now alleges they did so in violation of the state’s open meetings law.

In a recent development, the lawsuit, filed by three south-central Louisiana residents, follows state and Vermilion parish officials’ approval of what is estimated to be a record $27.5bn tax incentive package for SpaceX’s Starbase Louisiana project, announced over the summer. The officials violated the state’s open meetings law eight times while facilitating the agreement in attempts to “keep the public in the dark about what they are doing for SpaceX”, the lawsuit alleges.
