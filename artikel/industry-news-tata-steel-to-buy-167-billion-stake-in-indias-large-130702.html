Industry News: Tata Steel to buy $1.67 billion stake in India’s largest steel pipe maker; shares plunge nearly 5%

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Tata Steel, one of the world's leading steel producers, on Monday mentioned it will buy a 26.4% stake in India's largest structural steel pipe and tube maker, APL Apollo, for 155.1 billion rupees ($1.67 billion).

The acquisition will boost Tata Steel's downstream integration to around 53% from 35%, the organization said in a stock exchange filing. That will lead to more value addition, improved product mix, and greater exposure to higher-margin downstream segments, it said.

Shares of Tata Steel were nearly 5% lower on Monday, while APL Apollo shares tanked over 10% in early trade. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

APL Apollo stated after the deal it looks to more than double the downstream capacity to 15.5 million tons from the current 7.5 million tons, making it a leading global downstream steel firm. (Related: Japan debuts a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t connect to inte...)

This capacity addition will be completed by 2035 and would include around 9 million tons per annum of steel tubes and around 6.5 million tons per annum of color-coated products, as per Tata Steel.

Tata Steel will acquire a stake from existing shareholders of APL Apollo for 2,100 rupees per share and will ​also make an open offer to acquire an additional 26% ‌stake for 2,301.40 rupees a piece.

If the open offer is successful, Tata Steel would get a controlling stake of 52.38% in APL Apollo. World's second-largest steel maker ArcelorMittal was also reportedly in the fray to acquire APL Apollo.

The acquisition of APL Apollo would "create a downstream powerhouse for the future" that will serve the growing Indian market, T V Narendran, chief executive and managing director of Tata Steel, said in the release.

Tata Steel, which has an annual crude steel capacity of over 35 million tons per annum, is one of the world's most geographically diversified steel producers with presence across India, Europe and Thailand.
