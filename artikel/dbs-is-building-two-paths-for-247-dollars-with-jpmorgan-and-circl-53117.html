In a recent development, DBS and Circle announced a partnership on Nov. 18 to let eligible institutions in Asia turn USDC back into US dollars directly through DBS.

For treasury teams that hold the stablecoin, the bank route would offer an alternative to selling it on an exchange or elsewhere in the secondary market.

Recent findings suggest that the partners described near-instant, round-the-clock settlement when clients mint or redeem USDC through DBS. The announcements describe the conversion itself and give no timetable for when clients could use the resulting dollars for other payments.

Under the arrangement unveiled by DBS, an eligible institution could request dollars for its USDC through the bank. A treasury team could choose between the two routes based on the transaction it needs to make and the terms available. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

The arrangement would also let eligible clients use dollars to mint, or obtain newly issued, USDC through DBS.

Industry observers note that DBS had announced a separate service with Kinexys by JPMorgan on Nov. 16, connecting tokenized dollar deposit accounts in Singapore and the US so institutions can move dollars between them.

The Circle partnership adds a way for eligible clients to convert between dollars and a stablecoin.

It has been revealed that DBS and Circle mentioned the partnership builds on their participation in Project BLOOM, an initiative led by the Monetary Authority of Singapore. Its work includes helping different forms of tokenized money operate across financial networks.

The companies gave no market-by-market rollout or detailed eligibility rules, and they also left the onboarding process, supported blockchains, fees, and transaction limits unspecified.

The round-the-clock settlement the companies described would address one part of an after-hours conversion.

To decide whether to use the route for a payment or other treasury need, an institution would still need to know whether its account qualifies, what size redemption DBS will handle, and what the transaction will cost.
