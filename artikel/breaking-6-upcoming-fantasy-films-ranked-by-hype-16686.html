From another Shrek movie to a fresh video game adaptation in the form of The Legend of Zelda, there's a lot to like for fantasy lovers who enjoy going to the theater. If fantasy is one of your favorite genres, then there are certain movies you absolutely need to know that are coming out soon, so you can order your tickets ahead of time. Recent findings suggest that the newer Jumanji movies are a guilty pleasure franchise for many. So, while it's highly likely the movie will pack theaters upon release, there aren't as many people posting about it on social media. The brave fans who are willing to publicly admit they watch the movies are all thrilled to be getting the third installment of the new Jumanji movies. And with good reason, it's certain to be a fun movie packed with action and humor. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Jumanji: Open World will be the fourth movie in the series, including the one from 1995. The upcoming movie will hit theaters on December 25, 2026, making it a great family outing during the week between Christmas and Newly introduced Years. The movie will feature the same star-studded cast we're accustomed to, including Dwayne Johnson, Jack Black, Karen Gillian, and Kevin Hart. Open World will take place in the "real world." Instead of characters being sucked into a video game, the game will be exploding into the real world — just like the original movie. This means the teens who have been getting dragged into the video game will be contending with the characters they play rather than becoming them. This will be the final movie in the new trilogy of Jumanji movies.

Video game to movie adaptations have a rough reputation for disappointing fans of the original source content. That doesn't stop Hollywood from continuing to try and lure gamers into flocking to their local theater, though. The latest attempt is The Legend of Zelda and no, it will not be animated. The production will be a live action starring Benjamin Evan Ainsworth as Link and Bo Bragason as Zelda.

Life-long fans of the Zelda games are understandably wary of the movie and whether the film's interpretation of the games will match their expectations. Anyone curious will have to wait a few more months though, because The Legend of Zelda movie is expected to release in theaters on April 30, 2027. So far, social media has mostly been hesitant about the movie, but if the past is any indication, that won't stop fans from lining up to see it.

Children of Blood and Bone is a high fantasy movie based on a book with the same name. The book was written by Tomi Adeyemi and Children of Blood and Bone is the first of a trilogy. The story takes place in a fictional country called Orïsha, which many people presume to be a magical version of pre-colonial Nigeria. This isn't the Euro-centric style of fantasy Hollywood usually produces, so it's not surprising that many people are thrilled that this movie with a fresh perspective will be releasing soon.

According to recent reports, there was a social media scandal during the summer of 2026 when the author of the original story, Adeyemi, blocked some of the people working on the movie adaptation and even posted that she was distancing herself from the film. However, the author also made it clear that she did not want anyone to boycott the movie. Confusion further erupted when Adeyemi enthusiastically posted the movie trailer for Children of Blood and Bone when it was released.

Readers are still hyped to see Children of Blood and Bone when it enters theaters on February 5, 2027. The movie is directed by Gina Prince-Bythewood. The cast is composed of powerhouse stars including Idris Elba, Chiwetel Ejiofor, Cynthia Erivo, Regina King, Viola Davis, and more. If you're always on the lookout for a movie that isn't a reboot, remake, or sequel, this needs to go on your must-watch list immediately.

In a recent development, this one is a contentious one online, but it has certainly received a lot of discussion, both good and bad. Narnia: The Magician's Nephew is based on the sixth Narnia book published by C.S. Lewis, but chronologically in the Narnia timeline, it's the first story. The Magician's Nephew hasn't ever been turned into a movie, despite the existence of multiple versions of The Lion, the Witch, and the Wardrobe.

Industry observers note that this new Narnia movie is directed by Greta Gerwig, the same director as The Barbie movie from 2023. While Gerwig has a solid fanbase who will watch anything she makes, those who adore the source material for Narnia are less excited about this movie. Gerwig's trailer features an Aslan voiced by Meryl Streep, a new setting in the 1950s instead of the early 1900s, and a rock-opera theme. While some have found their curiosity piqued by the trailer, others are already disappointed that the source material was so readily ignored.

Recent findings suggest that the movie will be in theaters on February 12, 2027. But Narnia: The Magician's Nephew is already set to enter Netflix's catalog on April 2, 2027, for anyone who would rather stream it. Other notable actors in the movie include Carrie Mulligan and Daniel Craig.

You belong somewhere old enough that the mountains have names and somebody can still tell you who gave them. Middle-earth is for people who think the right thing is worth doing even when it will not be finished in their lifetime.

You want the myths to be real and loose in the actual world, and you want to find out at about thirteen that you were never anywhere near as ordinary as everybody kept telling you.

Sources indicate that godzilla x Kong: Supernova is the sixth Godzilla movie to come from Hollywood production companies and is directed by Grant Sputore. The movie will reach theaters on March 26, 2027. The movie, written by Dave Callaham, will star Sam Neill, Kaitlyn Dever, Jack O'Connell, and Dan Stevens. A teaser trailer was released on May 29, 2025, letting fans know that production had begun.

This movie will bring in a new threat in the form of SpaceGodzilla, though not much more is known about the storyline at this time, aside from the phrase "cataclysmic threat" being tossed around. Despite having few details, that hasn't been stopping Monsterverse fans from being very excited and chattering online with enthusiasm. At this time, there is no full-length trailer for Godzilla x Kong: Supernova.

Far and away, the fantasy movie coming to theaters in the near future with the most hype is Shrek 5. Theories, hopes, and dreams regarding a fifth installment of the animated franchise were shared long before it was ever confirmed. And there was plenty of celebration when the announcement of Shrek 5 finally came. The movie has been a long time coming and fans are clamoring for what comes next in the series of movies.

Recent findings suggest that the animated movie will be released to theaters on June 30, 2027. However, it had originally been slated for a December 23, 2026 release. This new date will likely give it the space to dominate the box office without competition from the likes of Jumanji: Open World and other Christmas-time releases such as Dune 3. The cast of the other four films have been brought back for the latest movie, including Eddie Murphy, Mike Myers, and Cameron Diaz. The cast will also feature Zendaya, Marcello Hernández, and Skyler Gisonodo as the voices of Shrek's three children.
