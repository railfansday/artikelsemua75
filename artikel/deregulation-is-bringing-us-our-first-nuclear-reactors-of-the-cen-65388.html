Federal regulators have approved construction of four advanced nuclear reactors at a Dow chemical plant in Texas, the first approval of its type in 60 years and the next step in America’s red tape–free nuclear renaissance.

The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) issued a construction permit for the proposed plant featuring four Xe-100 small modular reactors (SMRs) at Dow’s Seadrift site in Texas — the first construction permit for a high-temperature gas-cooled reactor issued in the U.S. in more than half a century.
