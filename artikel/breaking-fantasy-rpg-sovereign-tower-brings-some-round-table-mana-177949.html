Sources indicate that sovereign Tower launched on Steam earlier this year and promptly picked up its fair share of fans for its wacky round table management. Today, developer Wild Wits and publisher Curve Games have revealed that we Switch fans will be able to get in on the fun in the new year.

That's right, this one is coming to Switch on 14th January 2027. In it, you play as the sovereign of the titular tower, thrust into power after the untimely demise of your predecessor and tasked with keeping the kingdom up and running. To do so, you'll have to recruit knights and match their unique skills to tasks across the land — nabbing stolen manuscripts, taking down evil beings, negotiating with vampires, you know the drill.
