Sources indicate that in most cases, that's ads and data collection. The cheaper sets are sometimes subsidized by the brand because they know they can show you ads on the TV or collect your usage data and sell that to advertisers instead.

Sources indicate that this is not the case with every TV, and even on those it does apply to, you can generally turn off some of the data collection settings. The best way I've found around this is to plug in a separate streaming device and use that instead of the brand's software.
