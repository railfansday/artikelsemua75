In a recent development, on the surface, Courney Clenney and Christian Obumseli seemed like they had a great relationship. She was a successful influencer and OnlyFans model, and he was her handsome, crypto-trading boyfriend, and together they’d just moved into a luxury apartment in Austin, Texas. But their neighbor, Aidan Nesvisky, had a different experience with them.

Sources indicate that “At first they seemed like a normal couple, but soon it was obvious they weren’t,” Nesvisky says in a new episode of Dateline, which premieres tonight at 10 p.m. ET. “We could hear them all the time, and it was not pleasant. It was a lot of fighting, a lot of breaking glass, a lot of shouting.” He remembers a painting being thrown off their balcony and landing in his. “It was this crazy tiger painting.
