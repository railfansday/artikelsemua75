Fishlock ended her playing career earlier this month after her Seattle Reign side lost to Gotham FC in the National Women's Soccer League (NWSL) play-offs. (Related: School sends out minibus to catch absent pupils playing...)

The 39-year-old played on for one final season at Reign having retired from international football in October 2025.

Fishlock will be part of Wales' coaching staff for for next month's two-legged tie against the Norwegians, who visit the Cardiff City Stadium on Tuesday, 1 December, before the return leg in Oslo four days later.

Despite some speculation over whether Fishlock could play some part on the pitch, Wilkinson said: "I think you get retirement right when you know there's still something in you to give.".

The Wales boss added: "It takes years to recognise that you got it right, because you're sort of [thinking] 'oh should I have kept playing', but when you get it wrong injuries and someone else makes the decision that you're finished, that's a tough way to to end your career.

"I think Jess has qualified [for Euro 2025], made history with Wales, done such a great job as well as building the game over in the US and equally so much in Wales.

"[But] she's retired. Jess is here to further her technical and tactical awareness, but also for her experiences and that kind of confidence that she brings to the environment.".

While there will be no Fishlock, Wales will welcome back a number of experienced players from injury for the Norway games.

Wales are hoping to cause an upset as they bid to reach the Women's World Cup for the first time, though beating former champions Norway will likely not be enough to guarantee a place in Brazil next summer.

The complex nature of the qualification process means that Wales may have to negotiate inter-confederation play-offs next February to secure a World Cup place, even if they triumph over Norway.
