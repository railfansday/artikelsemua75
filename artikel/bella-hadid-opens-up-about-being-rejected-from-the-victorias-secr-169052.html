Bella Hadid Opens Up About Being Rejected From the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bella Hadid has become one of the biggest supermodels in the world, and this year, she even opened the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show—a runway on which she has become a regular. However, her success is the result of a lot of work, and many rejections.

In a recent development, in her December 2026/January 2027 cover story for Harper’s Bazaar, Hadid opens up about a time, over a decade ago, when she was rejected after auditioning for the VS show. In a conversation with Bazaar’s editor in chief, Samira Nasr, Hadid is asked how it felt to come full circle. Nasr recalls: “On one of our first shoots together, you were really upset because you didn’t get the Victoria’s Secret show. And I remarked to you, ‘One day, they will be begging you to be in their show.’ And I could tell you didn’t believe me. Your whole body shut down. And three days ago, you opened Victoria’s Secret. Holy shit.”.

In a recent development, hadid admits that she often feels like she’s in a daydream. “I still don’t believe you, to be honest,” the Orebella founder says to Nasr. “I feel like people are just AI-ing me onto the runway. I think that it’s so crazy how time passes and you still have some kind of impostor syndrome.”. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Sources indicate that she goes on to add: “No matter how hard you work, there’s always another girl who is more deserving, more beautiful, and has worked so hard to be able to get that spot. Ten years ago, I remember that moment too, where I never thought I would get the show, but I think that God always has a proposal, and I truly believe at that time I wasn’t ready. I don’t think I’ve been ready until this year, to be honest. In past years, I was going to my horse-show finals in the morning, flying straight to the Victoria’s Secret show that day, doing rehearsal right before, and then pulling what we call a Bella, and Belinda comes out, and we just act. But this year, I felt really strong.”.

According to recent reports, over the years, Hadid has opened up about turning to an alter ego, named Belinda, to handle the pressures of her career. However, when Nasr asks if she feels like it was her or Belinda walking the Victoria’s Secret runway this time, Hadid responds: “You know what? Fortunately and unfortunately, I think it was Bella this year.”.
