Mexican soldiers have detained eight alleged members of a drug cartel whose leader was arrested on Friday, sparking a wave of violence in northern Sinaloa state.

The Mexican defence ministry remarked the soldiers were shot by the suspects on Sunday while on patrol in the town of Sinaloa de Leyva, trying to re-establish order in the wake of the arrest of Fausto Isidro Meza Flores.

Meza Flores, also known as "Chapo Isidro", was on the FBI's 10 most wanted list and had managed to evade capture for years.

The ministry stated the eight had also been involved in the retaliatory violence unleashed by Meza Flores's cartel in the hours after his arrest on Friday. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

In a statement published on social media, external, the ministry of defence stated that one of the detainees was under age.

It added that its special forces had seized six vehicles as part of their operation, two of which had been fitted with bullet-proof armour and gun turrets.

The US Department of Justice described Friday's arrest of Meza Flores as "a spectacular military operation" and congratulated Mexico's military and special forces on his capture.

It remarked that the US Homeland Security Task Force had supported the Mexican military and praised the "bilateral effort".
