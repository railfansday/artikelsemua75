In a statement, a BBC spokesperson added: “We apologise to any viewers offended during the live broadcast of Strictly this evening. We are investigating how this happened.” The noise was edited out of the version of the show available on iPlayer. Watch on Deadline.

Industry observers note that the police were called to the incident. A Lancashire Police spokesperson told The Guardian: “We are aware of an incident that took place during the filming of Strictly Come Dancing in Blackpool.

“We have been in contact with the event organisers, and a formal complaint has been made to us. A mobile phone has been seized and we are now looking into the matter to see if any criminal offences have taken place.”.

Recent findings suggest that it is the second time a BBC live show has been interrupted by sex noises. Coverage of an FA Cup tie between Wolves and Liverpool was plagued by an apparent pornography prank in 2023.

Gary Lineker was anchoring live build-up to the replay match at the Molineux Stadium when he was interrupted by what sounded like sex noises coming from the studio.

The former England player looked perplexed by the noise in the studio, joking to fellow presenter Alan Shearer: “I don’t know who’s making that noise … It’s toasty in this studio, it’s a bit noisy as well. I don’t know if somebody is sending something on someone’s phone, if it’s a joke. I don’t know whether you heard it at home.”.

Lineker later tweeted a picture of a phone, which he stated had been taped to the back of the set in an apparent act of sabotage.
