Sparkling in Como, Nico Paz chose to permanently join the Italian club, to which he was loaned last season, instead of staying at Real Madrid. A profitable choice since the 22-year-old attacking midfielder shines under the orders of Cesc Fàbregas and has even become international with Argentina who see him as the successor to Lionel Messi. His performances with Como have not gone unnoticed by Madrid, since Real Madrid seem inclined to activate his buyout clause estimated at 80 million euros, confirms COPE.

According to recent reports, according to the Iberian media, the club chaired by Florentino Pérez wishes to repatriate its former player, trained at La Fábrica. As a reminder, this clause is only valid for next summer. Last summer, Como paid 60 million euros to buy him from Madrid. Since then, he has played 14 matches for a total of eight goals and six assists, in all competitions, and has confirmed his new acquired status.
