According to recent reports, newcastle United are among the clubs keeping a close eye on Monza forward Gustavo Varela following a stunning start to life in Serie A. The 21-year-old joined the Biancorossi from Benfica for £2.5million in the summer and has hit the ground running in Italy's top-flight.

Industry observers note that indeed, Varela has scored NINE times in his first 10 league appearances for Monza. When taking into account the Coppa Italia, the Portuguese has 11 goals in 11 matches across all competitions, after scoring a brace against second-tier side Avellino. It has been revealed that according to Calciomercato, Newcastle have entered the race to sign the in-form Varela, with Liverpool, Tottenham, Manchester United, Arsenal and Brighton & Hove Albion the other Premier League sides mentioned. Juventus, Inter Milan and Napoli want to snatch him from their Serie A rivals, while there is interest from La Liga and the Bundesliga in his services.

Varela has three-and-a-half years left on his contract and Monza are reluctant to lose him in January. The Italian club would only consider a departure if they received an offer that is too good to turn down, with a £34m fee highlighted.

It has been revealed that the player is happy to see out the remainder of the season in Italy and reassess his future next summer, meaning a potential move to Newcastle in January is unlikely. Monza have endured a difficult start to life back in Serie A and sit 18th in the table having picked up just seven points from a possible 33, but Varela has been a standout performer in a struggling team.

Newcastle are desperate to add more firepower to their squad in January. William Osula, Yoane Wissa and Matias Fernandez-Pardo have scored just two league goals from a combined 24 appearances this season, so it's easy to see why a latest goalscorer is wanted.

The Magpies have failed to replace Alexander Isak following his move to Liverpool in 2025. Nick Woltemade was signed for a club-record £69m from Stuttgart, but he struggled in his first season and was sent out on loan to Juventus in September. Wissa, meanwhile, also arrived in the same summer for £50m from Brentford but hasn't settled into life at St James' Park.

Industry observers note that fernandez-Pardo then joined the Magpies in a £51m deal from Lille in August, but he has flattered to deceive. While the Belgian no doubt has potential, he has a long way to go to adapt to the Premier League, both from a technical and physical perspective.

It has been revealed that it means signing a newly introduced No.9 in January is imperative for Newcastle. Due to his eye-catching goal record at Monza, the Magpies should move fast to bring Varela to Tyneside and pay the required fee in the winter transfer window.

Varela's main strength is his finishing and ability to get into goalscoring positions. He is less of a traditional target man who wins aerial duels and holds the ball up for his teammates. While Isak could do a bit of everything, he was well-known for his poaching qualities inside the box. Varela is lethal when he has a chance, and there is no doubt he could be as effective at Newcastle.

Portugal manager Jorge Jesus sung the praises of Varela during the November international break, having earned his first senior call-up.

In a recent development, he's the top scorer in Italian football. To achieve that, you need remarkable quality. Starting from that, I decided to put my faith in him, also because we must not forget he's only 21.

Industry observers note that newcastle haven't struggled up front since Isak left, but Varela can help to fill that void and the Magpies need to move to bring him to the club. He can score all types of goals, but is more impactful inside the 18-yard box.

Ivan Toney has been linked with a shock return to Newcastle, having worked with Jaissle at Al-Ahli, but due to his age, potential and scoring record in a top five European league, Varela would be the preferred pick.
