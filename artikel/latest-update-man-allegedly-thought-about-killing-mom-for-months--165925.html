A New Jersey man allegedly confessed to police he stabbed his mother to death after he had been thinking about murdering her for roughly six months.

Marcus Toth, of Clark, was arrested on charges of first-degree murder, third-degree possession of a weapon for unlawful purposes and fourth-degree possession of a weapon in relation to his mother Ingrid Toth’s death on Thursday, November 19, the Union County Prosecutor’s Office mentioned in a news release that same day.

Police discovered Ingrid dead at her Clark home when officers responded to a report of a medical emergency at the residence the afternoon of November 19, according to prosecutors.

Officers found Ingrid in the backyard after she had been fatally stabbed, per WABC-TV.

Marcus, 26, arrived at the Clark Police Department that afternoon and requested to speak with an officer privately, then allegedly “spontaneously” confessed, “I murdered my mother,” according to a probable cause affidavit obtained by Patch.com. While speaking with police, Marcus stated was “thinking about killing his mother for approximately six months” before November 19, according to court documents viewed by WABC-TV.

Marcus made a virtual appearance in court on Friday, November 20, when his public defender Todd Hess pleaded not guilty on Marcus’ behalf, per the TV station.

Neighbors who spoke with WABC-TV noted Marcus had lived with his mother and father for more than 10 years.

Marcus is in custody in Union County.

In a statement on the case on Friday, Clark Mayor Angel Albanese said, “I extend my appreciation to everyone involved in this investigation.”.
