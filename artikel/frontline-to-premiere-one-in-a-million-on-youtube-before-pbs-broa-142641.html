EXCLUSIVE: Frontline Features will debut the Oscar-contending documentary One in a Million for free on its YouTube channel ahead of the film’s PBS broadcast, in an unprecedented move.

Raney Aronson-Rath, Frontline editor-in-chief and executive producer, commented, “We’ve been publishing all our full documentaries on YouTube for nearly a decade, building a highly engaged, consistent, and growing audience hungry for the stories we tell. One in a Million marks an exciting first for FRONTLINE: releasing a feature documentary on YouTube ahead of its broadcast premiere. It feels like exactly the right film to break new ground, bringing Itab and Jack’s extraordinary documentary directly to our digital audience after the incredible response it has received from festival and theatrical audiences.” Watch on Deadline. In a recent development, one in a Million premiered at Sundance, where it won the Audience Award for World Cinema Documentary and the directing award in that category, recognizing Azzam and MacInnes. It has been honored at Sheffield DocFest in the UK, the Montclair Film Festival in Latest Jersey, and the Dallas International Film Festival; both DOC NYC and the IDA Documentary Awards have named One in a Million to their shortlists of the year’s best documentaries.

The film, shot over a 10-year period, follows protagonist Israa from childhood to adulthood “on an extraordinary journey from Syria to Germany — and back again,” notes a synopsis. “We first meet her at age 11 as she and her family are preparing to cross the Aegean Sea, risking everything in search of safety and a better life in Europe. What unfolds is a deeply personal chronicle of war, exile and heartbreak, as Israa grows up navigating adolescence, identity and belonging far from the home she was forced to leave. As Israa settles into life in Germany, new freedoms collide with the stabilizing pull of Syrian traditions, testing bonds and reshaping her sense of self.”. According to recent reports, frontline, American television’s longest running investigative documentary series, is produced at GBH in Boston and is broadcast nationwide on PBS. Its 2023 film 20 Days in Mariupol, directed by Mstyslav Chernov, won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature. The series has won 113 Emmys and 35 Peabody Awards.

Frontline’s Aronson-Rath will be a guest on Deadline’s Doc Talk podcast on Tuesday, December 1, discussing the success of the series and the worldwide acclaim for One in a Million.
