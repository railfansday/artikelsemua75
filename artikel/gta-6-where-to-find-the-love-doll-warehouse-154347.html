Grand Theft Auto 6 is absolutely packed with Easter Eggs, and seeing as the game has only been out for a short amount of time as of this writing, we've likely only seen the tip of the iceberg so far. However, by far one of the weirdest and wildest random encounter in the game that I've come across is located in a random warehouse in Vice City.

In a recent development, if you're a diligent player, you likely noticed the shipping container full of sex dolls when searching for a car during the "Meet The Heders" mission in Chapter 3. Lucia opens up several containers during this quest, and one of them is packed with sex dolls, which is funny by itself, but many players likely aren't expecting this to go anywhere beyond this brief scene. However, players can actually find an entire backstory to these seemingly random sex dolls if they know where to look, and it's absolutely terrifying if you're thinking of checking it out. For starters, players will only be able to enter the sex doll warehouse if they're playing as Jason at night, though you can have Lucia with you when walking by the location. The best time to check out the warehouse is actually after completing the first main mentor mission with Yago, as it's located just across the parking lot once the mission ends. However, you can find the warehouse again by looking for the giant pink pistol statue nearby. See the map locations below for exact coordinates:. According to recent reports, the warehouse itself is underneath a sign for Vice Noveltease, a firm that has been foreclosed due to a product recall, as you'll learn when you go inside. You'll see a large pane of glowing pink windows and a half open garage door that Jason can duck under, though it will close behind you automatically when you enter and Lucia will not be able to come in.

Once inside, prepare for a real horror show as the sex dolls are more than malfunctioning and borderline possessed. You can interact with each of the sex dolls as you enter, either taunting or defusing the situation, but either way they will bug out eventually and collapse. As you progress through the warehouse, you'll be attacked by several more sex dolls, both male and female, and there's even one with penis hands that will pin you down, so be ready for that.

It has been revealed that aside from demented sex dolls, there's also a rare collectible card on the workbench in there, Chastity from the America's Next Top Hooker set. There's also a Zombix in the First Aid Kit on the wall you can grab, but otherwise you can head out the door in the back. You'll be attacked on your way out, and if Lucia is with you, she won't be able to shoot the sex dolls because you're in the way, and there will be some hilarious banter between the couple after Jason finally escapes.
