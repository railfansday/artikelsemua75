Sources indicate that the openSUSE Project has been working on the Agama installer for four years; it was the default installer for openSUSE Leap 16.0 when it was released in 2025, and has been adopted by other openSUSE flavors as well. Now the project is gearing up to go all-in on Agama for Tumbleweed, the rolling-release edition that is meant to be where updates and latest features land first. Even so, the community has concerns about losing some of YaST's legacy functionality and the impact on installing Tumbleweed on systems that have less RAM.
