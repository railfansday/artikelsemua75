📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Tom Hanks is no stranger to a family-favorite movie. Earlier this year, he starred again in one of the best family franchises of all timeas Toy Story 5 and a fight against modern technology was unleashed on global audiences. Woody, Buzz (Tim Allen), Jessie (Joan Cusack), and the rest of the toys returned to huge success, scoring more than $1 billion at the global box office, which currently ranks as the year's third-highest-grossing film.

Unlike Toy Story 4, this fifth installment was also a significant hit with critics. "The impressive fifth installment not only justifies its existence but feels like a necessary response to how much the world has changed in the past three decades," wrote Collider's Taylor Gates, who gave the movie an 8/10 rating in her review. Hanks is a go-to voice for a family animated movie to succeed, something that happened when he joined the cast of his only feature-length Christmas film to date, voicing an impressive five characters. Sources indicate that the Polar Express, directed by Hanks' long-time collaborator Robert Zemeckis, was released in 2004 and has since become a festive favorite in households across the globe. However, reviewers were mixed in their initial response, criticizing the film's supposed uncanny animation, which has now been reframed as part of the film's quirky charm. The Polar Express earned $320 million at the global box office, split between a domestic haul of $196 million and a further $124 million from overseas markets, and against a reported $170 million. 22 years later, the film is a hit again as the festive season begins. At the time of writing, The Polar Express is one of the ten most-watched movies on HBO Max and has surpassed the 100-day mark on the streamer. (Related: Twitch streamer’s PS5 catches fire after they pause GTA...)

It has been revealed that as The Polar Express scores big on streaming, Hanks fans can look forward to one of his most beloved '90s movies officially getting the reboot treatment. Earlier this month, Warner Bros. confirmed it was bringing back Hanks' 1998 rom-com favorite, You've Got Mail, with Jason Orley attached to direct. Orley is working on a script that is based on a previous take by Deborah Kaplan and Harry Elfont, with most other details being kept tightly under wraps.
