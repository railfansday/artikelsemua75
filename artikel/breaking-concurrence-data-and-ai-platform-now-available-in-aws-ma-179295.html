Health systems, payers, biopharma companies, and public health organizations can now deploy clinician-trained AI agents on AWS and turn every patient interaction into evidence for better care.

NEW YORK, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Concurrence (formerly Amigo), the healthcare data and AI tech giant, today unveiled that the Concurrence Data and AI Platform, which deploys AI care delivery agents on a unified data foundation, is now available in AWS Marketplace. AWS Marketplace helps organizations easily discover, try, test, buy, deploy, and manage thousands of software solutions, including pre-built AI agents and ready-to-integrate tools, all in one convenient destination.

Concurrence deploys purpose-built AI across the care journey, tailored to each organization's clinical protocols, data, and systems. Each interaction adds new clinically relevant data to the organization's Health World Model, which constructs rich longitudinal records of each patient and traces every intervention to the outcomes that follow. Over time, this allows organizations to learn from the care they deliver and see which interventions drive clinical and operational outcomes. (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

It has been revealed that the Concurrence Data and AI Platform is built and operated on Amazon Web Services (AWS), with data-resident deployments worldwide. A forward-deployed team of Applied AI engineers and deployment strategists builds each organization's workflows on the Concurrence platform, and every agent is validated against millions of simulated patient encounters before it goes live.

It has been revealed that organizations can now procure and deploy the Concurrence Data and AI Platform directly through AWS Marketplace, simplifying procurement with consolidated billing and enabling faster time-to-value for healthcare data and AI initiatives across their AWS environments. Concurrence is Deployed on AWS, meaning eligible purchases through AWS Marketplace can count toward an organization's existing AWS commitment, subject to its agreement's AWS Marketplace allowance. Healthcare institutions can engage Concurrence through their AWS account team, in AWS Marketplace, or through the Concurrence website.
