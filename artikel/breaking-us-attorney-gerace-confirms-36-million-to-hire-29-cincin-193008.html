Breaking: U.S. Attorney Gerace confirms $3.6 million to hire 29 Cincinnati police officers

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (59 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that CINCINNATI – U.S. Attorney Dominick S. Gerace II announced the Department of Justice has awarded nearly $3.6 million under the 2026 Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS) Hiring Program to the Cincinnati Police Department. Cincinnati is one of six agencies receiving the funding in the Southern District of Ohio.
