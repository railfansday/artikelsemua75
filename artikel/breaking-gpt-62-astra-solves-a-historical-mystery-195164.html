GPT 6.2 Astra Solves a Historical Mystery. Here's an interesting case-study in using GPT-6.2 Astra for historical research, from history professor Mark Humphries:.

Sources indicate that with only minimal prompting, GPT-6.2 Astra was able to autonomously identify the creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook held at Yale’s Beinecke Library. It did so extremely quickly (in just 22 minutes), using publicly available primary and secondary sources. To do so, it discovered a little known source in a paper archive, predicted that it would prove its hypothesis if examined and, in the end, it did. Not only that, but this newly introduced source turns out to be a major find on its own that will be of significant interest to both Canadian and fur trade historians.
