Leicester City are eyeing an immediate promotion back from League One to the Championship this season under the guidance of head coach Russell Martin. LCFC slipped out of the second tier earlier this year after they finished in the bottom three along with Sheffield Wednesday and Oxford United.

In a recent development, the Foxes should target a move for Hull City attacking midfielder Kieran Dowell this winter to boost their chances of going up. According to recent reports, dowell, 29, has played only once for the East Yorkshire outfit in the Carabao Cup so far in this campaign and a change of scene will do him good. He is out of contract at the MKM Stadium in June next year after they handed him an 18-month deal last January when he signed.

Dowell would give Leicester City more competition and depth in their attacking department. He could also inject more quality into LCFC’s squad.

According to recent reports, he played with Foxes’ midfielder John Lundstram at Hull City last term. The pair helped the Tigers win the play-offs and reach the top flight against the odds.

According to recent reports, dowell’s game time has dried up under Sergej Jakirovic since then though and he is now surplus to requirements. He was offered to Sheffield United in the summer but nothing materialised on that front in the end and he stayed put beyond the last deadline. Industry observers note that dowell had a spell in League One with Birmingham City in 2025. He helped the Blues win the title under Chris Davies.

Therefore, he knows what it takes to get out of Leicester City’s league. His experience would come in very handy for LCFC and he would be a useful character to have in and around the dressing room.

Dowell rose up through Everton’s academy as a youngster. He has also had stints at Sheffield United, Derby County, Norwich City, Wigan Athletic, Norwich City and Rangers.

A deal wouldn’t break the bank, and it could give Russell Martin a valuable extra creative spark.
