Unity Software (U +6.10%) might surprise a lot of investors in 2027. A multi-year pivot to adtech is finally bearing fruit. Although the stock has lost more than 70% of its value over the past five years, investors are betting on a rebound as fundamentals finally catch up to the transformation.

Unity's Vector infrastructure uses AI to display ads to people who are more likely to click them. It's a similar model to the one that AppLovin (APP +7.36%) uses, which resulted in that growth stock roughly quadrupling over the past five years. This part of the business is already experiencing substantial growth. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

According to recent reports, vector revenue surged by 126% year over year, and that included a 40% sequential jump. Unity's leadership has talked about this day for a while, but it's finally here, and Vector even powered Unity to its first GAAP profitable quarter since its IPO.

Unity CEO Matt Bromberg highlighted the flywheel effect that's taking place. More developers create games on Unity's platforms, and those extra games lead to more online ads.

According to recent reports, although Unity reported 126% year-over-year growth from its Vector segment, its overall revenue was only up by 32% year over year. Looking into this further paints a much brighter outlook as legacy businesses fade away.

Industry observers note that for instance, Unity's non-strategic revenue dropped by 73% year over year to $25.5 million. This part of the business primarily consists of a former advertising network that was sunset in the second quarter, along with its Supersonic business, which was sold off on Aug. 4, 2026. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Non-strategic revenue is only projected to reach $5 million in the fourth quarter and will soon vanish from the income statement. Strategic Revenue, which represents Unity's future, was up by 59% year over year.

It actually gets even better when you look deeper into Strategic Revenue, since it consists of two parts. The Strategic Create Revenue is the smaller segment, and it was up by 11% year over year. Unity projects a 4% to 6% growth rate for this segment in Q4.

Strategic Grow Revenue, which includes Vector, was up by 90% year over year. It's projected to reach $520 million at the high end of Q4 guidance, implying a 20.5% sequential boost and 106% year-over-year growth.

Recent findings suggest that as non-strategic revenue vanishes and growth segments continue to ramp up and make up a larger percentage of total revenue, Unity should continue to post elevated growth rates in the upcoming quarters. Unity also pivoted from a -27% net profit margin in third-quarter 2025 to a 20% positive net profit margin in Q3 2026.

The significant switch to profitability and soaring margins suggest that Unity's net income will grow promptly alongside its Vector advertising infrastructure.
