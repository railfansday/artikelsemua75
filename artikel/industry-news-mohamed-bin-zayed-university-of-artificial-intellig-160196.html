During Frieze Abu Dhabi week, the Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) confirmed that it will launch a commission with a budget of up to $250,000. The task is to create a major sculpture or installation for the university’s Abu Dhabi campus.

The winning commission will be displayed in the university’s new cultural center that’s slated to open in 2027, and the artwork will be acquired for MBZUAI’s permanent collection. Artists applying for the commission can choose from one of three themes: the landscape, heritage, and communities of the United Arab Emirates; artificial intelligence and scientific inquiry; or the history between science and computation in dialogue with art, mathematics, and machines. “We shaped this open call as an extension of the collection we have built, which holds works spanning a century by artists who engage science through research, method and technique, treating knowledge as both an intellectual tradition and a material practice,” said Jasmine Soliman, head of art, archives, and special collections at MBZUAI, in a statement. MBZUAI is building its art and special collections. The works in its inventory span from the 13th century for special collections and art collections dating to 1925. The university collects works based on the arts and sciences, as well as materials that document “the human pursuit of knowledge, creativity and research.”.

Industry observers note that dekyi (Hong) Liang, vice president and chief of staff at MBZUAI, stated in a statement that it’s the university’s responsibility to “invest in cultivating a culture that embraces creativity and innovation. Art has always been part of that story of human endeavor,” adding that the upcoming commission for the campus reflects a space “where science and culture converge.”.

The deadline for the commission is December 18. The university will choose a shortlist of finalists, who will be invited to create detailed proposals, and each applicant will receive a $5,000 honorarium.
