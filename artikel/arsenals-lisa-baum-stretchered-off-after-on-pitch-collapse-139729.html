Arsenal winger Lisa Baum was stretchered off while receiving oxygen after collapsing off the ball in her side’s WSL match with West Ham.

The 19-year-old hit the deck while the ball was out of play close to the half-hour mark, with Arsenal’s medical team rushing onto the pitch in her aid.

She required lengthy treatment with oxygen being used, before she was carried off on a stretcher in the 34th minute. She was replaced by Caitlin Foord.

Arsenal have since issued an patch Baum’s wellbeing, stating that she was suffering from breathlessness but is now recovering.

Industry observers note that this was reiterated by Gunners manager Renee Slegers, who remarked: "She was short of breath on the pitch. She has two goals and one assist to her name across all competitions this season.

Arsenal have struggled for form this season and are still yet to win at home in the league.

They were again frustrated in a goalless draw with West Ham and Mariona Caldentey recognised the team’s difficulties in front of their home support in a half-time interview.

"We are conscious about that and feel sad about it, the fans are always on our side and we want to give back what they give to us,” she mentioned. “Everyone cares so much, and we are trying. We are trying to change the dynamic quickly.

According to recent reports, "Individually and collectively, we are a really good team, we need to have confidence and belief in ourselves. When you are in a hard moment it's hard to change the dynamic and you get frustrated; you put in a lot of hard work and effort but it's not working.”.
