Mikel Arteta has effectively confirmed that Kepa Arrizabalaga will start in goal for Arsenal in the absence of David Raya.

The Gunners will be without first-choice stopper Raya on Tuesday night for the Champions League match at home to Borussia Dortmund.

Sources indicate that the Spaniard was forced off late on against Newcastle at the weekend with a shoulder injury. While that has not proved to be a serious issue and Arsenal have been encouraged by the results of early scans, the Dortmund match will come too soon.

Arteta has split minutes in the Carabao Cup this season between back-up goalkeepers Kepa and Illan Meslier, but it is Kepa who has been on the bench throughout the Premier League season and he came on for Raya against Newcastle.

Discussing his options to replace Raya on Monday, Arteta indicated that Kepa is expected to serve as deputy.

“I think it’s clear for us who is going to play tomorrow,” the Arsenal boss said at his pre-Dortmund press conference.

Kepa started the Carabao Cup third round win over Ipswich earlier this season, but summer signing Meslier then got the nod in the shock last-16 defeat at fourth-tier Fleetwood. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

That was despite Kepa being fit and in the matchday squad. However, that was due to a desire to reward Meslier with an opportunity, rather than the Frenchman taking the No2 spot from Kepa.

“Because I want everybody to be part of it,” Arteta noted, explaining the decision to start Meslier against Fleetwood.

“To feel part of it, you need to play. Illan has been really good with us. We believed that it was the right thing to share those minutes in the competition.

“To give a player that every single day is so consistent, so reliable, so supportive with the rest of the boys, to give the feeling that we trust him as well.”.

Arteta was coy on whether Raya will be available on Sunday when leaders Arsenal host Manchester City in the Premier League.

The Spaniard is set for further tests to determine an exact timeframe for his return but any absence would be a huge blow for the Gunners.

Raya kept seven clean sheets in the first 11 league matches of the season and is firm favourite to win a fourth consecutive Golden Glove award, assuming he does not face an extended spell out.

“We have seen what David brings to the team but as well that is why we are very protected with Kepa and Illan,” Arteta noted.

“Everybody needs to be ready because these things unfortunately happen and now we need to show everyone how well we are protected and the good keepers that we have with Kepa and Illan.”.

Raya is key to Arsenal not just as a shot-stopper but in terms of the team’s build-up play too, with his confidence on the ball both playing short and over the press.

Asked whether Arsenal would have to play differently without him, Arteta mentioned: “We adapt to players’ qualities, they all have different ones.

“There are certain things in our game or in the way that we understand and want to play the game, everybody has to be 100 per cent committed to do.

“But then we make adjustments in relation to the qualities of the players.”.
