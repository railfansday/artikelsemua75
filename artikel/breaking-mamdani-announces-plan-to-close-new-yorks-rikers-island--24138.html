Mamdani on Friday unveiled a blueprint to close the Rikers Island complex by 2032, to build four new jails and drastically reduce the city's detainee population.

In a recent development, looks to shutter the troubled facility date back to 2019, when the city council first voted to close it by this year. But plans have been delayed over the years in part due to the logistical difficulties over where to place inmates. (Related: Tech Report: What Couples Married For 40 Years Do When ...)

Dana Kaplan - who Mamdani appointed as the city's "Close Rikers Czar" told the BBC News channel on Friday that his latest proposal is not just about getting rid of a decrepit facility.

It is also a "generational opportunity" to "build a different type of detention system" and figure out how to detain people more appropriately, she mentioned.

Sources indicate that the Rikers Island jail complex is perched on a 413 acre (167 hectare) island between Queens and the Bronx, and has earned a variety of nicknames over the years because of its dilapidated conditions, including Gladiator School and Torture Island. The jail has housed a number of high-profile inmates awaiting trial, including Tupac Shakur, DMX and Harvey Weinstein.

It houses more than 6,500 inmates at a given time. Mamdani hopes to reduce that number to 4,100 - a goal that has been met with skepticism by the Newly introduced York City correction officers' union.

According to recent reports, "[B]uilding a new system that puts an arbitrary cap on the City's available jail beds is ill-conceived, irresponsible and jeopardizes the safety of all New Yorkers, including our Officers," NYC Correction Officers' Benevolent Association said in a statement. "The City cannot keep the jail population at around 4,100 without irresponsibly releasing individuals who committed violent felony charges.".

"It's actually possible to have fewer people in jail in Fresh York City - and that is what we should be planning for," she noted.

Industry observers note that the jail complex has long faced backlash over staffing shortages, crumbling conditions and inmate deaths in the facility, including nine this year. Legislation required the jail to be shuttered by 2027, but Mamdani blamed lags under former Mayor Eric Adams' administration as well as the Covid pandemic for the delays.

Last year, under Adams, a federal judge removed Rikers from the city's control, appointing an independent outside official to make decisions concerning the facility.

In an 88-page report released on Friday, Mamdani remarked Rikers was a product of "deteriorating infrastructure" and that officials had a "moral imperative" to close it.

Upcoming York City Council must approve the updated timeline, with members, including the city's speaker, indicating they support the proposal.

Mamdani's office said the city would open up four new jails - one in Brooklyn by 2029, two in the Bronx and Queens by 2031 and one in Manhattan by 2032.

Recent findings suggest that the mayor's office argues the upcoming, smaller facilities will provide inmates "access to daylight, fresh air and clean space" leading to better outcomes for detainees. The facilities will be easier for families to access as well, as travel to the remote Rikers can take hours.

Mamdani noted his administration would work to reduce the number of people being held in jail across Fresh York City, including by offering alternative programmes to detention for some awaiting trial, by making it easier for families to post bail online and by trying to speed up the pretrial legal process.

He also wants to open up hundreds of beds in therapeutic housing units for prisoners with serious mental health concerns.

Kaplan said Mamdani's administration had already opened 100 therapeutic beds at Bellevue Hospital in Manhattan, moving 100 people off of Rikers Island.
