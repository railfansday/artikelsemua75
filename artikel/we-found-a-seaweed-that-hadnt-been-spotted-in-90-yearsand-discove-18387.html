We found a seaweed that hadn't been spotted in 90 years—and discovered vast underwater forests

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (50 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A tangle of long, caramel-colored seaweed. That's what botanist A.H.S. Lucas found in 1935 while conducting research near Lord Howe Island, a World Heritage–listed site known for its unique biodiversity. After collecting some large adult specimens of this previously unidentified seaweed, Lucas named it Sargassum howeanum.
