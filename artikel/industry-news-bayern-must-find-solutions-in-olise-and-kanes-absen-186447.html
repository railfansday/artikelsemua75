Vincent Kompany quipped Bayern Munich are missing "130 goals from last season" ahead of their Champions League trip to Lille, with Harry Kane and Michael Olise unavailable.

Bayern arrive at Stade Pierre-Mauroy just days after it was confirmed that Ballon d'Or winner Kane is set to miss the next few months with an ankle ligament injury sustained against Koln.

Kane has either scored or assisted in each of his last 11 games in the Champions League, which is presently the joint-longest run, along with Cristiano Ronaldo (between 2017 and 2018) and Ruud van Nistelrooy (between 2002 and 2003).

The England captain has 10 goals and 11 assists in all competitions this season, with Kompany also without Olise, who has failed to recover from an glitch with his foot.

Olise has 18 goal involvements (12 goals, six assists) across all competitions this season, the sixth-highest tally across Europe's top five leagues in 2026-27.

Kane and Olise join an extensive list of injuries for Kompany to contend with. Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari, Alphonso Davies and Manuel Neuer are also sidelined.

Bayern have scored in each of their last 25 Champions League matches, dating back to January 2025, but he mentioned they must find different ways to attack against Lille. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

"It's about always being realistic and finding solutions – then getting the results," Kompany told reporters.

Sources indicate that "130 goals from last season aren't there, but we have guys who can run and fight. Then we have our fans too. We're going into the match with hunger and desire.

"We want to show that we're Bayern Munich on the pitch.".

Kompany did, however, have some good news on the injury front, with Jamal Musiala and Luis Diaz both expected to play at least some part in Wednesday's clash.

The only previous meetings between Lille and Bayern came in the group stage of the 2012-13 Champions League, with the Germans winning home (6-1) and away (1-0).

Since the start of last season, Bayern's only two defeats in 18 Champions League matches (W13 D3) have been against the 2026 finalists Paris Saint-Germain (April this year) and Arsenal (November 2025).

And they come up against a Lille side who are on a six-game winless run in Europe (D1 L5). Only once have they had a longer run without a victory in major European competition (13 games between December 2012 and December 2019).

Sources indicate that "Until last week, they were second in France. They always have a lot of talent; they're well known for their academy," Kompany noted of Lille.

"They have a lot of pace on the counter; they defend aggressively from the front. They've got a few guys with international experience as well.

"They'll want to put on a show at home. I expect high pressure and a good game for the spectators.".
