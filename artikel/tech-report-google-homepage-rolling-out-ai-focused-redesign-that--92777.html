The Google Search homepage is embracing Gemini in a big way, with a fresh sidebar redesign that puts your chat history just a click away.

Google’s AI ambitions have been anything but subtle in their integration with Search, but Gemini and Search have had some clear separations. But, with its latest update, Google is very much intertwining the two.

Confirmed by Google’s Robby Stein on Twitter/X, Google is now rolling out a desktop homepage revamp that gives Gemini near-top billing.

At first glance, you’ve got the typical blank canvas with a Google logo and Search bar at the center, but the new changes start with Gemini-powered shortcuts underneath for actions such as creating images, analyzing files, and “brainstorming” with Gemini. In a recent development, beyond that, a latest sidebar pulls your Gemini chat history and tasks into the Search homepage as well as including a “New chat” shortcut and everything else you’d find in the Gemini desktop app/site. The AI-heavy adjustment is very perhaps one of the biggest embraces of AI in Search since Google introduced AI Overviews and AI Mode.

Stein explains this change is because users are coming to Search with questions that aren’t “one and done.”.

People are coming to Search with more questions that aren’t just one and done – and in testing, this sidebar made a big difference for making progress & continuing to explore the web.

Industry observers note that the upcoming Google Search homepage is now rolling out globally, Stein says, though we’re not seeing it live just yet on our devices. Sound off in the comments if you’re seeing the new look and whether or not you like this functionality.
