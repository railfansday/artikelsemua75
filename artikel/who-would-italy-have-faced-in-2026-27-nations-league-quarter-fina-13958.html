Italy will not be competing in the Nations League quarter-finals, but they have recently learned which opponents they would have come up against had they managed to avoid defeat against Belgium in Brussels on Sunday.

Heading into the final round of group stage fixtures, Italy had sat at the top of their group thanks to a 3-1 victory over France in San Siro last week. Their hard work against Les Bleus was undone, however, by a 4-1 defeat away against Belgium on Friday, which saw the Azzurri drop from top spot down to third place by the end of the night.
