Allegiant and Sun Country are adding fresh destinations and a combined 16 routes ahead of the summer of 2027.

Recent findings suggest that allegiant said Tuesday that it will resume service to Minneapolis, while Sun Country will expand to Belleville, Illinois (via MidAmerica St. Louis Airport), Gulf Shores, Alabama, and Knoxville, Tennessee.

Allegiant acquired Sun Country in May, though for the time being they are continuing to operate as separate airlines.

Recent findings suggest that “Minnesota is an essential part of the future of our combined manufacturer, and today’s announcement is another step in delivering on that commitment,” Allegiant CEO Greg Anderson remarked in a news release. “MSP is our largest base of operations by a wide margin, and we see opportunities for continued growth there. Combining Sun Country’s strong foundation in the Twin Cities with Allegiant’s nationwide network of communities will provide Minnesota travelers with more convenient, affordable nonstop travel options.”.
