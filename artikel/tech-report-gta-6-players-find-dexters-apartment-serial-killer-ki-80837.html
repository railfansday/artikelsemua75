GTA 6 players have discovered Dexter Morgan’s apartment in Vice City, complete with a collection of his signature killing tools and even a boat resembling the one used by the TV serial killer.

Back in August, fans spotted what appeared to be Dexter’s apartment in GTA 6 after Rockstar released its 26-minute extended gameplay showcase. (Related: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using Gradio...)

The building closely resembled the real-life Miami apartment complex used throughout the original eight seasons of Dexter, but fans had no way of knowing whether Rockstar had included any additional references. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Now that GTA 6 has introduced, players have tracked down the waterfront property and uncovered a number of Easter eggs that make the connection even clearer.

As shown in footage shared by DETONATED, players can explore the complex, which Rockstar has named “Trinity Apartments.”.

That’s a reference to the infamous Trinity Killer, Arthur Mitchell, who was played by John Lithgow in Dexter Season 4 and remains one of the show’s most iconic villains.

Dexter Easter Eggs in Grand Theft Auto 6 🔥💥 Dexter's Apartment at 'Trinity Apartments'💥 'Slice of Life' Boat💥 Tools on Roof including SyringeLocated on Washington Island (Vice Beach) pic.twitter.com/q8lnAMR3rH.

Although players can walk right up to Dexter’s apartment door, they can’t actually enter the property. There is an air conditioner, though, an ideal place for hiding blood slides.

However, those who make their way onto the roof will discover something much more sinister.

Hidden on top of the building is what appears to be one of Dexter’s signature kill kits, containing a knife, tape, rope and a syringe. These are all tools the serial killer famously used to subdue and murder his victims throughout the series.

The references don’t end there, either. Nearby, players can find and steal a Lure Predator L35P, a boat resembling Dexter’s beloved “Slice of Life.”.

It has been revealed that in the show, Dexter regularly used the Slice of Life to transport and dispose of his victims’ remains in the ocean. While the GTA 6 vessel doesn’t carry the same name, players can take it out onto the water and recreate some of the Bay Harbor Butcher’s infamous trips.

It’s an impressive tribute to the series, especially given that Rockstar hasn’t explicitly identified the location as Dexter’s home.

With GTA 6 only just launching, players have already begun uncovering secrets across Leonida. Fans previously spotted a Vice City Easter egg hiding in the game’s trailer, and there are likely plenty more discoveries waiting to be made.
