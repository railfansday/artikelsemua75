📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

We are delighted to introduce Hugging Face’s fresh blog for Chinese speakers: hf.co/blog/zh! A committed group of volunteers has made this possible by translating our invaluable resources, including blog posts and comprehensive courses on transformers, diffusion, and reinforcement learning. This step aims to make our content accessible to the ever-growing Chinese AI community, fostering mutual learning and collaboration.

In a recent development, we want to highlight the remarkable achievements and contributions of the Chinese AI community, which has demonstrated exceptional talent and innovation. Groundbreaking advancements like HuggingGPT, ChatGLM, RWKV, ChatYuan, ModelScope text-to-video models as well as IDEA CCNL and BAAI’s contributions underscore the incredible potential within the community. Industry observers note that as we embark on this newly introduced chapter, our collaboration with the Chinese AI community will serve as a platform to bridge cultural and linguistic barriers, fostering innovation and cooperation in the AI domain.
