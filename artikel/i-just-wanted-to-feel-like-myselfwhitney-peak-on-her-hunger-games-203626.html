Where others zig, Whitney Peak zags.

The actor, who just wrapped the lengthy press tour for her upcoming film, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, easily could have indulged in a method dressing spree (and many of her castmates did!). But it was Peak’s restraint that earned her a spot on Vogue’s 2026 Best Dressed list. “It’s my first time doing a press tour of this scale, and with all nerves and excitement, I just wanted to feel like myself in whatever I was wearing,” the actor tells Vogue. (Related: Tech Report: Easily Build and Share ROCm Kernels with H...)

Recent findings suggest that “We talked about [method dressing], but ultimately decided not to,” she says of working with stylist Karla Welch. “I think the fun was in watching fans find their own connections between my character and what I was wearing.” Instead, Peak and Welch were guided by instinct. “I had a very general Pinterest board that I shared with Karla: images, textures, shapes I loved. But honestly, we worked very intuitively with each other and established a real flow,” she says. “The looks reflected that—they feel connected, have a rhythm, and they feel authentic.”. (Related: Unlock the power of images with AI Sheets...)

Though she wore Chanel aplenty (“I had too much fun in that dress,” she says of her black multi-patterned look in Berlin), brands of all size were on the table. Peak was particularly enamored with the quilted floral silk jacquard Dries Van Noten coat she paired with a suit and tie (“THAT COAT! Everything.”) and the cutout Torishéju she trotted out in Upcoming York (“I’d never worn anything like it.”).

It has been revealed that and, although she swore off method dressing, Peak did end with an overt nod to her character, Lenore Dove Baird, in a blue velvet Robert Wun, a white dove pulling at her suspended shoulder strap. “The Robert Wun was the perfect, magical ending,” she claims.
