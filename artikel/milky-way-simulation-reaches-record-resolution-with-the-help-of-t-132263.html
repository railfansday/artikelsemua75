Milky Way simulation reaches record resolution with the help of three supercomputers

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A team of researchers, including those from the University of Tokyo, have developed a latest way to model the Milky Way at a level of detail never achieved before. They simulated the entire galaxy down to the level of individual stars by combining the power of some of the world's most powerful supercomputers using an AI-accelerated model that works on a variety of hardware platforms. Their methods can improve our understanding of galactic evolution.
