Mahershala Ali is getting candid about why “there are not roles” for him in “the vast majority of projects,” despite having already won two Oscars.

During a recent interview on Carmelo Anthony and Kazeem “Kaz” Famuyide’s 7PM in Brooklyn podcast, the actor — who previously took home Academy Awards for best supporting actor for 2016’s Moonlight and 2018’s Green Book — opened up about how earning Hollywood’s highest recognition doesn’t guarantee access to great roles.

“There’s a lot of great actors that don’t have Oscars, who are also looking for good material,” he noted. “And that’s no disrespect to The Academy or the award at all, but what I’m actually saying is that Oscar or no Oscar, there’s a lot of good actors out there. There’s more good actors than there is good material.” Related Stories Movies Katelin Arizmendi on Bringing a Latest Perspective to Action With 'Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother' Movies Mahershala Ali Put His Two Oscars Into a Cabinet: "I Felt a Sense of Accomplishment, Not Fulfillment".

“It’s not even that all these people are competing for these same spots. It’s just that out of all the material made, there’s only a handful of them that check certain boxes. And then you got to be right for it,” Ali continued. “The vast majority of projects that get greenlit, there are not roles for me in those. You gotta think that way, you know what I mean? So, it doesn’t matter if I got 17 Oscars. If someone is not writing something for you or greenlighting something with you in mind, you’re like everybody else waiting and looking and searching.”. Given the current political climate, Ali later added that rollbacks of diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) funding and initiatives also “on some level impact what gets greenlit.”.

“I don’t know how much of an impact it has, but I just know that if certain companies are allowed to not feel a pressure to be reflective of the world we live in, they’ll just keep centering who they see in their offices, and it ain’t a bunch of us in those offices,” he stated.

The Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother star explained that the only other option for actors is to produce and create their own projects; however, “that’s a five-year journey itself.”. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Famuyide later asked Ali about the fan notion that Black actors are “validated” only after winning an Oscar. “It’s kind of just talk. It doesn’t translate to anything real,” the actor replied.

According to recent reports, “You don’t want to find yourself in a situation where, oh, that’s ‘the diversity project.’ You just want to do great material as a Black person. You’re not thinking of yourself as this exotic other, per se. You just are living your life,” Ali added. “You wake up. You put your pants on like everybody else. You just want to be like, ‘Yo, this is my offering. This is my story. This is my work.’ Opposed to, ‘Man, who are the best Black actors?'”.
