Brajan Gruda scored two goals and provided two assists in his first two appearances for the German national team against Serbia and the Netherlands over the recent international break and with four assists in seven Bundesliga games for RB Leipzig in 2026/27, the 22-year-old’s stock continues to rise.

On loan from Premier League side Brighton and Hove Albion, Gruda’s move to Leipzig will become permanent in the fresh year if he plays every match between now and Hoffenheim on 16 January. The fee: €25m. The newly capped German international will then pen a contract until July 2031, without a release clause.

That ultimately means that Leipzig can command any fee they wish for their rising star. With interest from Liverpool and Bayern Munich, and with no intention of selling, BILD estimate Leipzig’s valuation of Gruda to be around €100m.
