Bruno Mars knows his dad was amazing, just the way he was.

The “That’s What I Like” shared that his father Peter Hernandez died at the age of 74 and shared a heartfelt tribute to the impact he had on his life.

Recent findings suggest that “Last week I got to hug my father one last time,” Mars wrote on Instagram Nov. 19. “If you’ve ever seen me talk about him I’ve always said that he’s the man that taught me everything I know, especially when it comes to music.”. The 41-year-old—whose real name is also Peter Hernandez—added that his father helped raise six children, including Mars, his sisters Tiara, Tahiti, Presley and Jaime and his brother Eric, saying, “In my eyes he was the greatest dad in the world, and I will miss him everyday until we meet again.”. (Related: Breaking: Leaning To Savour Everyday Pleasures Rewires ...)

According to recent reports, mars—whose mother Bernadette San Pedro Bayot died from a brain aneurysm in 2013 at the age of 55—concluded his message by writing, “I Love you Pops. Kiss mom up there for me.”.

Recent findings suggest that alongside the emotional message, the “Just the Way You Are” singer shared a photo of himself and his father sharing an embrace in a hospital bed along with several pictures of his father from throughout the years. He did not confirm his father’s cause of death.

Industry observers note that mars and his dad had shared a truly special bond through the years as the “Uptown Funk” singer’s love of music was inspired by his father’s own history of performing. While Hernandez was born in Brooklyn, he later moved to Hawaii, where he founded a doo-wop revival group called The Love Notes.

And from a young age, Hernandez—who split from Mars’ mother when the Grammy winner was 12—knew that his son was destined for greatness.

“He was a ham,” Hernandez told MidWeek about Mars in 2014. “As soon as he could walk and express himself, he had to be in the middle of everything and wanted to be a part of whoever he’d seen on TV.”.

Of course, he was incredibly proud that his son was “the product of a lot of love from all his family.”.

“No woman could ever love Bruno as much as his late mother did,” he added, “and no man could ever love Bruno as much as I love him.”.

And Hernandez was there for his son right until the end, offering Mars advice on what to say when he was honored with a Las Vegas street named after him in April.

It has been revealed that “He states, ‘My loving son, I know you don't like advice, but getting a street named after you is a monumental moment,’” Mars recounted in a video from the event shared by AP News. “‘I just wanna say, I know you must be very proud, ‘cause I’m so proud for you. If you are gonna say anything on the mic to the crowd, both God and people love humility. So be humble and tell your fans it's their street, and you're so honored and grateful.’”.
