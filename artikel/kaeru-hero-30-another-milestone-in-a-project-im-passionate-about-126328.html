I've been dedicating a significant amount of my time to evolving Kaeru Hero, an indie platform game that has become one of my most exciting personal projects. Seeing it gradually transform from an early concept into a more polished and engaging adventure has been incredibly rewarding. Over the past few days, we've made substantial improvements to enemy behavior, gameplay balance, camera movement, checkpoints, and the death and recovery experience. We've also refined the progression system so players can preserve their achievements and unlocked abilities after respawning.

Recent findings suggest that version 3.0.0 has now been submitted to Google Play, and I'm already working on what's next: expanding the first level with latest areas, more challenging encounters, and our first miniboss. There's still a long road ahead, but that's part of what makes game development so fascinating to me — continuously learning, experimenting, solving problems, and watching an idea come to life.
