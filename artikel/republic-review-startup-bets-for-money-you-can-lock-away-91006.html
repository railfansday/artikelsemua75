📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Use Republic for small bets on startups only with money you can leave alone for years. Most of its startup deals pay off only if a company is sold or goes public, often four to six years later, and most investments carry a 2.5% fee. Put in only money you could lose entirely.

Republic lists funding rounds for startups, small businesses, and films, and most are open to anyone, not just the wealthy investors the SEC calls accredited. Each company sets its own terms, so the platform suits investors who read every offering’s documents before they commit.

According to recent reports, investors who can read a tech giant’s filings and afford to lose every dollar can spread $100 checks across many companies here, paying the usual $5 fee on each.
