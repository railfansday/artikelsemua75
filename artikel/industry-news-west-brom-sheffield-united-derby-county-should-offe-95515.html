Kasey McAteer has had a frustrating season so far at Ipswich Town, with the winger way down the pecking order under Gary O’Neil.

It was always going to be a busy summer for the Tractor Boys following their promotion back to the Premier League, and the club strengthened their options out wide with the additions of Daizen Maeda and Abdul Fatawu. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Therefore, McAteer, who was struggling to get in the XI anyway, has now failed to make the matchday squad most weeks, and his last first-team appearance came back in September in the EFL Cup defeat to Arsenal.

At 24, the Ireland international will know he needs to leave to get his career back on track, so a January move could be on the cards.

However, with Ipswich having spent £12m on the player, who has a contract until the summer of 2029, it won’t be easy for the Suffolk outfit to shift him permanently.

Nevertheless, you’d expect some agreement to be reached, and here we look at THREE Championship clubs that should be plotting a move to sign McAteer in January….

West Brom have had a fantastic season so far, and, barring an unlikely turn of events, they will be in the hunt for promotion going into the January window.

So, Albion will be an attractive option, and James Morrison will be seeking attacking reinforcements to help the team last the distance as they target a top eight finish as a minimum.

It’s clear that the Baggies need attackers with pace, so McAteer could fit the bill, and they are lacking depth out wide, with the ex-Leicester man capable of playing down both flanks.

Albion won’t be in a position to splash huge sums, but a loan move for McAteer, perhaps with an option depending on promotion, could be ideal for all parties.

Sheffield United had a very difficult summer window, with Callum O’Hare, Gus Hamer and Andre Brooks all departing Bramall Lane.

Despite losing the influential trio, Chris Wilder had to utilise the loan and free agent markets to strengthen his squad, and even though the Blades are doing reasonably well, it’s clear the squad is lacking in key areas.

The recruitment team will surely be seeking more creativity in the final third, so McAteer could be just what Sheffield United need, as his pace, energy and direct style could make a difference to the team.

Again, a loan is the only realistic way of making this happen, but Wilder’s man-management skills could be just what McAteer needs right now, and he could play a major role for a team that will hope to push for the play-offs.

McAteer hasn’t played football week in, week out for a long time now, and he may be in a position where he just wants to guarantee minutes, even if that means dropping to a club lower than he originally expected.

That could open the door to Derby, with the Rams currently struggling in the bottom three, and they’re going to be in a fight to stay up this season.

Sources indicate that A reason for their struggles has been a ridiculous injury list, with John Eustace robbed of several key players. They are specifically lacking individuals with pace, so McAteer could instantly become a crucial player at Pride Park.

So, this could be a move that suits all three parties, and McAteer would hope for a strong end to the season before assessing his long-term future in the summer of 2027, as it seemingly lies away from Ipswich.
