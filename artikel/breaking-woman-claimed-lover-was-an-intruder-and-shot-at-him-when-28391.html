A Michigan woman was arrested after she allegedly pretended her lover was an intruder and shot at him when her boyfriend unexpectedly returned home.

Lasacia Gore has been charged with discharging a firearm in a building, assault with a dangerous weapon, false report of a felony, lying to a police officer and two counts of felony firearm.

According to recent reports, the Macomb County Prosecutor’s Office announced in a Wednesday, November 18, press release that Gore, 34, called police in Eastpointe, Michigan, on Monday, November 16, to say an unknown man had broken into her home and got into a fight with her boyfriend. Gore also claimed she fired a shot at the man, but didn’t hit him.

After she told officers her story, officials rapidly realized there were inconsistencies.

According to recent reports, officers received text messages from her lover, which proved he was invited to the home and that Gore “left the door unlocked for him to enter,” according to prosecutors. Gore allegedly told her boyfriend that the other man was an intruder, and the boyfriend began wrestling with the man and as Gore allegedly shot at him.

Officers noted that the boyfriend believed Gore when she claimed that the other man was an intruder, and he never suspected that she was cheating on him.

“Firing a weapon during a dispute puts lives at risk and can have devastating consequences. Equally troubling are allegations that someone attempted to mislead law enforcement about what actually happened,” Macomb County Prosecutor Peter J. Lucido remarked in a statement. “Police officers must be able to rely on accurate information when responding to dangerous situations. We take both gun violence and the integrity of criminal investigations seriously, and those who engage in such conduct must be held accountable.”.
