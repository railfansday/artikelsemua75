Not to brag, but here at Cosmo, we’re pretty talented at gift-giving. One of our present-scouring pros is assistant beauty editor Jasmine Hyman. “My personal gifting philosophy is knowing my giftee’s interests and combining them with fresh and trending products,” she claims. “For nailing a style gift, I love getting someone a piece they’ll use or wear often, and something I think they would like but might not necessarily spring for on their own.”.

And because she knows that gifting isn’t one-size-fits-all, Jasmine relies on Target: “It’s my gift-shopping go-to because it truly has something for everyone—whether it’s my sports-enthusiast dad, fashion-loving mom, or fitness-obsessed boyfriend, there’s a variety of options with every aisle you stroll—or scroll—down.”.

According to recent reports, we challenged her to curate her top cozy-season style finds to gift this season. Keep reading for her top Target picks.

Sources indicate that “A set that can double as both loungewear and athleisure is always a win in my book, and a monochrome look can immediately make them feel more put together. The half-zip is perfect for throwing over a workout outfit on a cold morning, and this light blue hue is winter-ready.”.

Recent findings suggest that “This pair is the perfect winter white to go with any sweater shade they have in their closet. They’re soft, comfy, and ideal for lounging around during the cold winter months.”.

“Seasonal staples like gloves make perfect gifts because your loved ones can likely never have enough.”.

“A chic water bottle that will make you want to stay hydrated is a must-have, and Owala makes some of the best around. I always keep one in my gym bag and work tote. It never spills and always keeps my water at an optimal temperature, plus the colorways—especially the special-edition holiday options—are so cute. It’s perfect for the wellness or workout lover in your life.”.

“A classic winter print like Fair Isle is a crowd-pleaser. They can pair this goes-with-anything gray-and-white pattern with jeans and a tee, or a skirt and boots for a more dressed-up occasion.”.

“If you’re unfamiliar, this isn’t just any plain old ring—it tracks sleep, steps, and so much more—and can blend in with the rest of your jewelry. It comes in a bunch of different sleek colorways, so it will go with your someone special’s everyday pieces. It’s also a great gift to self—you deserve it!”.
