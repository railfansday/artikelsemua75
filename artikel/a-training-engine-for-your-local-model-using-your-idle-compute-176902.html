Recent findings suggest that use your local model as usual through opencode, pi or any OpenAI-compatible tool. While you work, Songe notices what happens after each of the model's actions: the tool result, the error, your next message. When your agent is quiet, it teaches the model to see those coming. Machines without a GPU still serve and record, but do not train. Tried Songe on another setup? Share the result on the testing board.

In a recent development, with auto-dream on, Dreams start when your agent has been quiet for a while. Or press Dream in the dashboard (songe open). A Dream never just disappears: it keeps going on the page, or says why it stopped.

It has been revealed that before the first training Songe measures how much your computer can learn at once, and trains on what fits. See DESIGN.md.

uv tool upgrade songe, pipx upgrade songe or brew upgrade songe: the service moves to the newly introduced version by itself in a quiet moment.

Sources indicate that songe uninstall stops the service and restores your agent settings; add --purge to also delete its data and learned versions. Run it before brew uninstall songe.
