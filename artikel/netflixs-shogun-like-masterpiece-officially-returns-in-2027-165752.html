Sources indicate that the best animated indicates in Netflix's history have been a surprisingly eclectic collection, but one has stood out for its visually stunning artwork and compelling story that has had viewers describing it as similar to both Shōgun and John Wick. This is an impressive comparison, with Shōgun revolutionizing the historical epic genre and John Wick becoming an iconic revenge story with a franchise that is still building on itself. Even so, Netflix's animated series is one of the greatest revenge sagas ever told, and fully deserves the comparison. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

During Japan's Edo period, when relations with other countries were strictly limited, four white men remained behind, and one of these fathered Mizu. Suffering at the hands of her community, Mizu trained as a warrior and set out on a journey of revenge. This is the premise for Blue Eye Samurai, the multi-award-winning series that builds a fully tangible world and characters you care about in just eight episodes, while many TV series fail to do this in multiple seasons. Blue Eye Samurai can be binged in one sitting, but ended on a cliffhanger which will be resolved next year. (Related: Leveraging Pre-trained Language Model Checkpoints for E...)

Blue Eye Samurai's ending saw Mizu boarding a ship and leaving Japan for London, seemingly a step closer to getting the revenge she is after. Of the four possible candidates for the identity of her father, Mizu killed one before the events of season 1, and has reluctantly spared the life of Fowler, the second possibility, as he knows where the other two men are hiding. With Taigen and Princess Akemi still in Edo, and Ringo believing that Mizu is dead, the series set itself up to continue the story in multiple locations if renewed for a second season.

According to recent reports, the series was renewed for its much-anticipated second season in 2023, and it has been confirmed that Blue Eye Samurai will officially return in January 2027, with six fresh episodes. The shorter season length has received a mixed reception from viewers and critics, but with episodes lasting almost an hour and with animation that takes a significant period of time, most are cautiously optimistic. Netflix has been criticized for canceling shows early, but the streaming platform has such faith in Blue Eye Samurai that the series has already been renewed for a third and final season.

Sources indicate that action shows tend to evoke a specific mental image, with plenty of grit and explosive live stunts. Unfortunately, this results in animated TV shows often being overlooked, but Blue Eye Samurai has quietly claimed a place among Netflix's best action series, with some even considering the show's eight episodes a worthy Game of Thrones replacement, which speaks to the quality of its worldbuilding, political intrigue, and character writing. The series has a well-deserved 97% and 93% Rotten Tomatoes rating from critics and audiences, respectively, and has attracted some of the world's best voice talent.

Blue Eye Samurai was nominated for seven Annie Awards, winning six of them.

It has been revealed that many characters in Blue Eye Samurai may sound familiar, with the voice cast including Maya Erskine (BoJack Horseman,) Masi Oka (Heroes,) George Takei (Star Trek,) and Kenneth Branagh (Oppenheimer,) and it is not surprising that the series has come from the writing team responsible for both Logan and Blade Runner 2029, as the show's emotional beats hit hard and the visuals feel grounded and cinematic rather than cartoonish. These are the aspects that give an action series the depth it needs to become more than an exciting spectacle, and Blue Eye Samurai surpasses even some of the greatest action reveals.

Rather than relying on fast editing and a punchy soundtrack, Blue Eye Samurai makes fighting look hard. Mizu is not a superhero, but a character who has been through immense emotional pain to get the expertise and skill she has. The show incorporates the blood and sweat of a battle while the tactics of winning do not go ignored. Putting this against a stunning backdrop and a mission that takes as much of Mizu's strength as the fight itself makes Blue Eye Samurai a success on multiple levels, and a legitimate successor to many better-known movie and TV franchises.
