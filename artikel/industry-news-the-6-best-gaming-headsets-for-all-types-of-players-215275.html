Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs).

Are wired or wireless headsets better for gaming?

Recent findings suggest that if you care about latency (aka lag), we are going to recommend wired over wireless. The added hassle of wireless charging and connecting doesn't necessarily make up for the hassle of having a wire. Plus, you don't have to worry about it not working on some devices—which you do with pretty much every wireless device you buy.

Do all gaming headsets work across all platforms?

I'm glad you asked. No! As you may have noticed, there tend to be two kinds of headsets. Those that work with Xbox.. and those that work with everything else. Since Xbox uses its own wireless standard instead of Bluetooth. In practice, this means Xbox gamers need to be extra careful when buying headsets to make sure the one they're getting is compatible. SteelSeries, for example, sells two versions of each headset, one marked for Xbox.

According to recent reports, it's actually more complicated than "all headsets that work with PC also work with PlayStation and you have to get ones especially for Xbox," but in many of these cases that is the gist. Although the PlayStation headsets work on PC and Mac but not Nintendo Switch. So, yeah, it's pretty inconsistent. Seriously, just read the list of compatible platforms before you buy a headset.
