Butterball CEO on Thanksgiving Dinner Outlook

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Jay Jandrain, Butterball CEO, indicates U.S. turkey production is slightly higher than last year and holiday turkey prices are broadly consistent with prior years. Some retailers are offering turkey deals below last year’s prices, and Thanksgiving meal baskets may cost the same or less. Jandrain says retailers decide whether to discount products to attract shoppers, while Butterball welcomes the promotions. He speaks with Romaine Bostick on "The Close." (Source: Bloomberg).
