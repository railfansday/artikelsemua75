Dots are also coming to all ChatGPT Business Standard consumers starting today.

OpenAI is expanding access to Dots, the proactive, always-on AI agents that can toil at tasks for you without any supervision or assistance. After launching the feature for higher-tier looks from ChatGP Pro and up, the organization announced that it's testing Dots with a small number of people on the cheaper $20 per month ChatGPT Plus proposal. Dots can interact with over 4,000 apps thanks to their access to OpenAI's plugin ecosystem. The agents can work autonomously on tasks and make progress even when you don't interact with them. They can even connect to your PC, allowing them to control it on your behalf and work alongside you. You can check up on their progress by opening a Dot's "computer" at any time.

Originally powered by GPT-6 Astra, Dots can now run on GPT-6.1 Terra as well. The organization just debuted several latest features for them as well, including iOS widgets and Shortcuts integration, along with iMessage and RCS support, plus iOS notification replies. If you're on a Plus initiative, you may gain access starting today, but OpenAI has yet to say whether the upgrade will be permanent.
