Sources indicate that yesterday was a bizarre and surreal experience at the London Stadium as we watched West Ham’s half-hearted attempts to beat a stubborn Preston North End side. Sure, the Hammers had 30 shots and an impressive 79% slice of the possession pie. But scratch below the surface and there are still major problems with Scott Parker’s team, some of which I suspect will not be sorted out until the January transfer window.

Industry observers note that despite West Ham’s impressive array of shots, passes and corners, I never truly felt that the team had the magic or the cutting edge to win the game. And as crazy as it sounds, considering we’d hit the post twice and had 14 corners, I don’t actually think we threatened enough. The Hammers lacked sparkle and magic, for sure. Sources indicate that edson Álvarez was an excellent addition to the team, but he is a defensive midfielder, and realistically, the defence was improved. Herrick made a bit of a blooper in goal for the opener, but I felt he redeemed himself with an excellent double save. By and large, West Ham restricted Preston’s chances, and so we should have done. Sources indicate that but our biggest problems came in attack. Edvin Austbø on the left wing flattered to deceive, cutting inside and shooting just off target at one point, but never really terrorising his marker, despite the fact that he had him on a yellow card. Joël Piroe did okay but fluffed his big chance when put through by Arne Engels.

According to recent reports, as for the supply line, Divine Mukasa flitted in and out of the game and did not create anywhere near enough, given his deployment in the number 10 position. Jarrod Bowen was wide. I mean, very wide for so much of the game that he was probably on first-name terms with the linesman. But realistically, he was just out of the game for large parts of it. We’ll get into more detail on that later.

Overall, it was nowhere near enough quality. Sure, we had momentum. Sure, we had possession. But this did not look like a group of Premier League-standard players against the worst team in the division.
