As part of its Black Friday discounts, Amazon has discounted the SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Omni down to $344.99. However the Omni is the model which we recommend above any other pair of cans for gaming, and best of all, this price is lower than it's ever sold for before. Industry observers note that the Omni is SteelSeries' most premium gaming headset, and the brand has other options reduced in the Black Friday sales. They're not all at latest low prices, but there are some solid discounts.

I'm a major audio guy: to me, sound is basically the most important thing in getting me immersed in a game (I'd rather take a low-res, low-refresh display than poor-quality audio).

If you have that in common with me, you'd be well suited to the Nova Pro Omni. It offers high-resolution sound, comfortable earcups, and efficient connection options. If loudspeakers aren't an option for you when gaming — or you need a microphone — it's basically the option to buy.

In a recent development, not everyone can justify a $350 headset, though, and that's understandable. If high-quality audio isn't your priority, the Nova Pro Omni will be excessive. There are other SteelSeries deals on Amazon which are more affordable.
