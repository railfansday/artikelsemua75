📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Shopify App Store reviews are one of the most honest sources of product feedback in e-commerce. Merchants say exactly what broke, what they love, and why they left, and developers answer in public. There is no official API for these reviews, but the review pages on apps.shopify.com are public and need no login. This post shows two ways to collect them: a short Python script, and a no-code option for when you need many apps, a schedule or a clean export.

It has been revealed that every app has a review page at https://apps.shopify.com//reviews. Add sort_by=newest and page=N and you get 10 reviews per page, newest first. Each review is an element with a data-review-content-id attribute, and the app's overall rating sits in the page's JSON-LD.
