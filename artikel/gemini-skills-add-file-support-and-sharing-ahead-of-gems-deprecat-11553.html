Ahead of the Gems shutdown that starts rolling out tomorrow, Google is adding two key features to Gemini skills.

These “reusable, custom instructions” now support files from your device, Google Drive, Gemini Notebook, and Google Photos that can be used as context. You can upload files, up to 100 MB, when you create a skill.

Gemini will “reference these files every time the skill runs in chats and tasks.” Files from Drive and Notebooks will “update in real time so your skill always works from the latest version.”. (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

Meanwhile, skills can now be shared. Go to the Gemini app’s Customize > Skills page on the web to grant View or Edit permissions. Recipients will receive an email and can review the skill before adding it.
