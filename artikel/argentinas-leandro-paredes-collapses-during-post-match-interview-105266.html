Argentina international Leandro Paredes collapsed mid-interview after his Boca Juniors side won the Copa Sudamericana in stifling heat and humidity in Barranquilla, Colombia.

The Boca captain, who came off 58 minutes in, became emotional as he celebrated his side ending an 18-year trophy drought with a 2-0 victory over Brazilian side Atletico Mineiro. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

After lifting the trophy he fainted partway through his post-match interview with Dsports and was pictured being helped by a reporter as medical staff rushed to his side.

The 32-year-old received medical treatment and was able to walk to the dressing room before re-joining the celebrations, and later posted to Instagram: “Thank for the messages, it was just dizziness,” accompanied by laughing emojis.

The conditions had been a key talking point before the match, with temperatures forecast to rise to 32 degrees Celsius and humidity reaching 80 per cent at the Estadio Metropolitano Roberto Melendez.

Boca Juniors manager Rodolfo Arruabarrena said he saw Paredes, who was part of Argentina’s World Cup-winning squad in 2022, appearing dizzy in the first half. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

He said: “He had already begun to feel dizzy and physically weak towards the end of the first half.

“At half-time he seemed to have recovered, but ten minutes into the second half he was struggling with lateral movements and was honest enough to ask to be substituted.”.

It has been revealed that paredes began his career with Boca Juniors before spells with Roma, Paris Saint-Germain and Juventus. He returned to Boca in 2025.

He was fined £66,000 and banned for 10 international games after sparking a brawl at the end of this summer’s World Cup final, when he was seen grabbing Spain’s Eric Garcia by the throat and throwing opponent Gavi to the ground.
