In a recent development, lisa Lennon was born on the Isle of Man, a place steeped in myth and folklore. That perhaps shows in her art style, which defines as magical realism with a focus on classical portraiture. She works with watercolours, building up many layers while adding details.

In a recent development, “This science fiction piece was dreamed up while walking by the sea. Her headpiece is inspired by the pebbles there. I love wide open spaces; they give me a lot of scope to play with atmospheric scenes.”. (Related: Tech Report: 3D Asset Generation: AI for Game Developme...)

Recent findings suggest that “This is where fantasy meets science fiction. I was working with local folklore and became obsessed with goblins and elves. I developed a collection called ‘Space Goblins’.”.

Industry observers note that “Another in the ‘Space Goblins’ series. How did Tudor clothes end up in outer space? We can only speculate, but I do love a good riff, and it’s always a large technical challenge to get it right.”.

“One of my classic goblins. I so enjoy painting these rather cantankerous, sassy personalities. My focus here – technique-wise – was to have a vintage look to the colour and quality, all while retaining a contemporary, whimsical feel. I love this one.”.
