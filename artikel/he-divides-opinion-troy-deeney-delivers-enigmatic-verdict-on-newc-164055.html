Troy Deeney has picked his best 12 performers from the top tier action.

The Premier League team of the weekend (and manager) that have most impressed him.

Included in his dozen choices is one from Newcastle United.

This NUFC player was a key player in the first half as United battled to stay in the game.

Then after the break, proving pivotal in the turnaround.

Troy Deeney explains why he has chosen Malick Thiaw for his Premier League team of the week and then the full eleven (twelve) are listed below:.

“Malick Thiaw (Newcastle) He divides opinion but he played really well and got his goal.”.

Well, the second part anyway of his very brief appraisal of Malick Thiaw.

What I we supposed to make though of the enigmatic “He divides opinions..”???

Recent findings suggest that malick Thiaw was thrown in the deep end last season, arriving in summer 2025 it ended up with far too much asked of the German defender due to injuries. Thiaw played in 54 of the 58 matches, starting 51 of them, as Newcastle United found themselves playing every midweek for much of the season. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

He was very good for most of the season but maybe understandably his form tailed off a little bit late on, with the team struggling.

Recent months have been chaotic at St James’ Park to say the least but with injuries beginning to clear, the returning Dan Burn playing alongside Malick Thiaw against Fulham and Arsenal, they look like a partnership that can do well for United this season. Especially if Tino Livramento and Lewis Hall can stay fit and complete what looks a promising back four, as we saw in the second half on Saturday. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

Having a settled back four is a huge help at club level and hopefully this is what can carry us forward this season.

Also, Jurgen Klopp has started Malick Thiaw in three of his last four team selections with Germany and the United defender has played well.

Troy Deeney Premier League team of the week:.

Konstantinos Tzolakis (Hull City) James Hill (Bournemouth) Malick Thiaw (Newcastle) Keane Lewis-Potter (Brentford) Noah Okafor (Leeds) Jack Rudoni (Coventry) Elliott Anderson (Manchester City) Luca Williams-Barnett (Tottenham) Florian Wirtz (Liverpool) Oli McBurnie (Hull City) Rio Ngumoha (Liverpool).
