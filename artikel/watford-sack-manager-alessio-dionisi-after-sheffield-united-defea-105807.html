Watford have sacked manager Alessio Dionisi following their heavy defeat away to Sheffield United at the weekend.

The Hornets have won just three times in their last 13 games, and despite two strong results ahead of their game against the Blades, time is expected to be up for Dionisi. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

Watford right now sit just outside the relegation zone on 15 points, having won just four times in the Championship all season. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

Their away form has been particularly concerning so far this campaign, picking up just one point from their eight games on the road.

Watford were 2-0 down after just eight minutes against Sheffield United, but they barely laid a glove on their opponents despite chasing the game for almost 90 minutes.

They produced just seven shots with a total of 0.32 xG, having just nine touches inside the opposition box.

GIVEMESPORT understands that Watford have sacked manager Dionisi following their 4-0 defeat away to Sheffield United.

The Hornets have won just three times in their last 13 games, and although Watford tried to give him the benefit of the doubt and support after losing 4-0 against Burnley and 5-1 against Cardiff City, his position has now become untenable.

Sources say that Dionisi was liked as a person internally at the club, but a lack of identity in their style of play, poor in-game management, and failing to know his best starting XI are all factors as to why he's been relieved of his duties.

GIVEMESPORT reported back in October that Watford are aware that they've been too hasty in the past when it comes to sacking managers, and were looking to give Dionisi a little more time.

Unfortunately, the defeat against Sheffield United was the final straw for Dionisi, with Watford still searching for their first win of the season away from home.

Speaking after the heavy defeat against Sheffield United, Dionisi apologised to the supporters.

Sources indicate that "I'm sorry for our supporters. Because when we play away, we play without courage and we weren't aggressive; it's impossible. When you play away against Sheffield United or any club, it's impossible when you play without courage and without personality.".

Dionisi must have known the writing was on the wall for him following the heavy defeat.

GIVEMESPORT understands that Dionisi has been hanging on to his job by a thread for several weeks now, and the result against Sheffield United was clearly the final straw.

It's unclear what direction Watford are going to turn in now, but with a five-point cushion above the drop zone, their season is far from over.
