Recent findings suggest that courtney Love has praised Tracy Marander – Kurt Cobain‘s one-time girlfriend and photographer behind Bleach‘s album cover – as the “unsung hero” of Nirvana following announcements she is entering hospice and suspending treatment for her cancer. Marander’s family launched a GoFundMe last week to finance her medical bills at her request. As of Monday afternoon, the GoFundMe has raised over $80,000.

Industry observers note that “In the early years, [Tracy] gave Kurt a home and all the space in the world to make music. She believed in him before he believed in himself,” Love wrote on Instagram. “The medical bills have piled up. The American medical system is broken. Give what you can.”.
