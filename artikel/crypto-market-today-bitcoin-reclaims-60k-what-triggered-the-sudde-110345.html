Crypto Market Today: Bitcoin Reclaims $60K, What Triggered the Sudden Crypto Rally?

It has been revealed that the crypto market today is staging a sharp recovery after Bitcoin plunged to a two-year low, triggering aggressive selling across digital assets. Bitcoin price rebounded more than 8% from its intraday low of $55,326 and briefly reclaimed $60,000, while major altcoins also recovered. The bounce followed a brutal sell-off that pushed Bitcoin below key support levels and intensified fears of a deeper market downturn. However, the recovery comes against a backdrop of weakening institutional demand, heavy ETF withdrawals and a broader retreat from risk assets, leaving investors questioning whether the latest rally marks a genuine reversal or simply a temporary rebound.

Industry observers note that bitcoin price recovered sharply after falling to $55,326, its lowest level since September 2024. The cryptocurrency subsequently bounced above $60,000, recovering a significant portion of its intraday losses as buyers stepped in following the steep decline.

It has been revealed that the sell-off had pushed BTC below its previous bear-market low near $57,742, breaking a level that had held since September 2024. Bitcoin has also lost more than half its value from its record high near $126,200 reached in October 2025, highlighting the scale of the correction.

The rebound has offered short-term relief, but Bitcoin remains vulnerable after a weekly decline and a broader deterioration in market sentiment.

The recovery extended across major cryptocurrencies, although the rebound followed substantial losses earlier in the session. The broad-based recovery suggests traders are attempting to buy into the sell-off after prices fell sharply.

However, the earlier decline remained significant, with Ethereum price still below $1800, and XRP below $1, while Solana is still below $100 and other major tokens still facing pressure from the broader market downturn.

The rebound appears to have been driven by dip-buying after an aggressive sell-off, with easing liquidation pressure allowing prices to recover. Bitcoin’s decline below key support levels triggered forced selling among leveraged traders. As prices approached the $55,000 region, the sharp decline created an opportunity for traders to buy at lower levels, helping BTC recover rapidly.

It has been revealed that but the broader market’s underlying conditions remain fragile. Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies have faced pressure from weakening technology stocks, concerns over AI valuations, higher borrowing costs and institutional withdrawals from spot Bitcoin ETFs.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded approximately $582 million in net outflows, while monthly withdrawals have approached $3.5 billion, weakening a major source of institutional demand. The combination of ETF redemptions and broader risk aversion has left the market susceptible to sharp price swings.

Bitcoin’s recovery above $60,000 is an notable short-term development, but it does not yet establish that the market has bottomed. The cryptocurrency needs to hold above reclaimed levels and regain additional resistance zones to strengthen the bullish case. A renewed breakdown could quickly erase the rebound, particularly if institutional outflows continue or broader markets remain under pressure.

The crypto market today is witnessing a sharp relief rally, but Bitcoin’s ability to sustain its recovery above $60,000 will determine whether buyers can turn the bounce into a broader reversal or whether the market faces another wave of selling.
