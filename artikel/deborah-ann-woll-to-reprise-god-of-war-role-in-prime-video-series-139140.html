Prime Video‘s God of War series has cast the role of Faye with an actress who’ll be familiar to people who have played the video game.

Industry observers note that deborah Ann Woll, who voiced the character in God of War: Ragnarok, will play Faye on screen in the series. Faye is the mysterious late wife of Kratos (Bautista) and mother of Atreus (Vinson). Her death puts the events of the series in motion, as father and son discover secrets about Faye that lead them to wonder how well they really knew her. Recent findings suggest that prime Video greenlit God of War back in 2022. After a change in showrunners in 2024, production on the series began earlier this year — only to stop after an on-set injury in July forced Ryan Hurst, who was initially cast as Kratos, to bow out. Bautista was cast in August, putting the show on track to restart production in Vancouver in the fall. The cast also features Max Parker, Ólafur Darri Ólafsson, Teresa Palmer, Alastair Duncan, Jeff Gulka, Danny Woodburn, Sonya Walger, Island Austin, Ben Chapple, Louis Cunningham and Evelyn Miller.

It has been revealed that sony Pictures Television and Amazon MGM Studios are producing God of War in association with PlayStation Productions and Tall Ship Productions. Ronald D. Moore (Outlander, Battlestar Galactica) is the showrunner and executive produces with Maril Davis, Cory Barlog, Naren Shankar, Matthew Graham, Asad Qizilbash, Jeff Ketcham, Hermen Hulst, Roy Lee, Brad Van Arragon and Lawrence Trilling. Joe Menosky, Marc Bernardin, Tania Lotia and Ben McGinnis are co-EPs. Frederick E.O. Toye (Shogun, Fallout) directs the first two episodes. Woll stars in Marvel’s Daredevil: Born Again as Karen Page; the show’s third and final season is set to premiere on Disney+ in 2027. She’ll reprise her role as Faye in God of War: Laufey, also due for release next year, and will appear on season two of The Testaments on Hulu. Her credits also include True Blood and horror film The Cycle. She is repped by Luber Roklin Entertainment and Goodman Genow.
