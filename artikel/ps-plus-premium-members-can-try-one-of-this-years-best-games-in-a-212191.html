Everyone is (understandably) going gaga over GTA 6 right now, but real ones will know that 2026 has also brought us one of the best racing games of the PS5 generation — Star Wars: Galactic Racer.

The arcade racing title is the debut effort from Fuse Games, and it's tremendous. If you're a PS Plus Premium member, you don't need to take my word for it.

The game's store page confirms you can play the full game on a trial basis for two hours, which is plenty of time to get a grasp of Galactic Racer's excellent roguelite campaign.

Official accounts made the announcement today, as well as highlighting a 20% discount on the game until the end of PS Store's Black Friday sale.

In other Galactic Racer news, it was recently confirmed the game will be getting some pretty cool free and premium content moving forward.

As well as a bunch of newly introduced vehicles, an enhancement will add lots of new features, like private lobbies for up to six players, more multiplayer ranks, and new customisation options.
