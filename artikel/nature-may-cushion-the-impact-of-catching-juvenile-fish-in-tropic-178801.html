Nature may cushion the impact of catching juvenile fish in tropical fisheries

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (88 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, much of the bycatch in tropical fisheries is kept as "trash fish," which is processed into fishmeal, mainly for aquaculture feed. But this bycatch contains many juvenile fish, and there is concern that catching so many juveniles could lead to a decrease in the adult population. A new study led by researchers at Thailand's Department of Fisheries and Hokkaido University suggests that in these fisheries, the loss of juveniles might be partly offset by better survival among the remaining juveniles.
