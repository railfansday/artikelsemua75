Princess Cruises is extending its Princess Plus and Princess Premier package benefits ashore, making them available at Celebration Key next year.

The packages bringing 50 to 70 percent savings per day and guest on things like drinks, dining and Wi-Fi will apply to participating locations throughout Celebration Key beginning February 1, 2027, according to a statement. (Related: Taco Bell is bringing back the Steakhouse Boss Burrito ...)

Prior to this, Princess package benefits will also become available at RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay, starting November 23, 2026. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

This follows the inaugural call of the Star Princess at Celebration Key last week, which marked the beginning of 69 scheduled calls during the 2026-27 season, followed by 32 planned calls in 2028, across six ships.

Yesterday, Carnival announced fresh additions to both destinations: two capacity expansions are planned for Celebration Key in 2028 and 2030, while RelaxAway, Half Moon Cay will get a complimentary retreat for guests 21 and older, Serenity Beach, in 2027.
