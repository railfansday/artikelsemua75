Medtronic stock slips as sales growth slows and misses expectations

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Medtronic $MDT reported second-quarter revenue of $9.364 billion on Tuesday, up 4.5% from a year earlier and 6.5% on an organic basis. Medtronic stock slid 2.5% to $98.20 ahead of the opening bell.

Revenue fell short of the $9.48 billion analysts expected, and the growth rate marked a slowdown from recent quarters, according to The Wall Street Journal. The company said revenue was about 50 basis points above the midpoint of its guidance. (Related: Industry News: Hyatt Category 1 Hotels — The Easiest Po...)

Recent findings suggest that cardiovascular revenue rose 10.2% to $3.785 billion, or 10.4% on an organic basis, led by a high-teens increase in Electrophysiology Therapies. Cardiac Ablation Solutions grew 58%. Neuroscience revenue rose 5.1% to $2.705 billion, or 3.2% organic, with a low-single-digit decrease in Neuromodulation. Medical Surgical revenue rose 3.9% to $2.245 billion, or 4.4% organic.

In a recent development, non-GAAP diluted earnings per share were $1.34, down 1.4% from a year earlier. Analysts had estimated $1.33, according to The Wall Street Journal.

GAAP net income was $2.802 billion, or $2.20 per diluted share, compared with $1.374 billion, or $1.07 per share, a year earlier. The GAAP results include a $2 billion gain on the separation of Medtronic's diabetes business, MiniMed Group, which the manufacturer completed through an exchange offer on Oct. 15, 2026. Medtronic reiterated its fiscal 2027 guidance of 7.25% to 7.75% organic revenue growth and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $5.94 to $6.00. The company stated it is maintaining those ranges while absorbing the impact of the diabetes separation.

"Our leading franchises are performing well, newer growth drivers are scaling, and we continue to invest organically and through disciplined M&A to add fresh technologies and expand our growth opportunities," Chairman and CEO Geoff Martha noted in a statement, according to The Wall Street Journal.

The quarter ended Oct. 30, 2026. Medtronic also stated it declared its intent to acquire Contego Medical in November and completed enrollment of the Sphere-360 U.S. pivotal trial.
