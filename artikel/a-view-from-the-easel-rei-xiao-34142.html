How long have you been working in this space?

Describe an average day in your studio.

In a recent development, I work from my apartment. The most predictable parts of my studio day start with putting on my sunscreen and painting clothes. It’s a very small space that I paint in, so I usually have to push stuff aside when I start, like my painting cart, stool, and trash bin. I, then, turn on my air purifier, open the lids of my palettes, scrape paints from the previous session, and play either TV shows I’m familiar with or podcasts hosted by comedians in the background. The day usually ends with my cleaning the palette, emptying and wiping my brush cleaning tank, and diligently washing my brushes. Then I wash my face and go to sleep.
