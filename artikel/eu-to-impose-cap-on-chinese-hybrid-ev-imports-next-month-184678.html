Temporary quotas on vehicles entering the bloc will also apply to cars from the UK, Japan and Turkey.

The EU will next month impose its threatened caps on the number of Chinese hybrid cars that can be imported into the bloc, the European Commission has confirmed.

The quotas will also apply to hybrids from Japan, Turkey, South Korea and the UK, but the principal purpose is to limit imports from China after an agreement with Beijing in October that has led to a rise in exports to the bloc.
