Windstar Cruises completed its third Mystery Cruise, an eight-night journey along Spain’s coast aboard the Star Legend.

Sailing from Barcelona to Málaga November 4–12, 2026, the voyage took travelers to a series of surprise destinations, giving them clues for each destination approximately 36 hours before arrival.

Beginning in Barcelona and concluding in Málaga, the eight-night sailing called at six ports: Mahón, Menorca; Castellón; Cartagena; Gibraltar; Motril; and Puerto Banús.

Windstar visited Castellón and Puerto Banús for the first time and returned to Motril after 18 years, having last called there in 2008.

“Our Mystery Cruises capture what Windstar is all about – a spirit of discovery, a sense of adventure, and the opportunity to experience the world in unexpected ways,” noted Chris Prelog, president of Windstar Cruises.

Recent findings suggest that “There’s something special about stepping aboard without knowing exactly where the journey will take you and trusting that incredible experiences await. The enthusiasm we’ve seen for these sailings has been remarkable, and we’re excited to continue bringing this unique concept to fresh destinations around the world.”. Throughout the voyage, guests had the opportunity to experience the destinations through shore excursions showcasing the region’s history, culture and gastronomy.

Experiences ranged from coastal hiking adventures and explorations of historic fortresses to visits to local wineries, villages and regional culinary experiences.
