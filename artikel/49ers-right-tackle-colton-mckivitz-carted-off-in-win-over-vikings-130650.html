San Francisco 49ers right tackle Colton McKivitz left Sunday's dominant win over the Minnesota Vikings due to what looked like a significant injury to his lower left leg.

According to recent reports, the injury took place during the second quarter of the 35-10 win that was held in Mexico City. McKivitz's left leg got pinned under multiple players at the end of a Christian McCaffrey run. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

When McKivitz pulled his leg from underneath the pile of players, his foot was twisted in the wrong direction.

McKivitz's teammates immediately knew he had sustained a significant injury. Multiple players took a knee around their injured teammate, and left tackle Trent Williams slammed his helmet into the turf, then held the back of his head in his hands.

Trainers rushed onto the field to tend to McKivitz. He was eventually taken off the field on a cart. He was upright and interacting with his teammates as they saw him off the field.

The nature of McKivitz's injury wasn't initially clear.

McKivitz is key to San Francisco's top-ranked offense. He's a strong run blocker who hasn't allowed a sack this season.

Recent findings suggest that the 49ers have thrived despite injuries to key players on both sides of the ball this season, and Sunday night was no exception. They entered halftime with a 28-10 lead and cruised the rest of the way to secure the 25-point win. Brock Purdy went 21-of-26 for 255 yards with three touchdowns, two of which went to George Kittle.

They now sit at 9-1 on the season after the win, which marked their third straight.
