IPVanish Recognized for Privacy Protection and Independent Verification.

Privacy transparency remains an crucial consideration when selecting a virtual private network. A VPN encrypts internet traffic between a connected device and a VPN server, helping protect information from exposure across local networks. The technology also masks the original IP address, providing another layer of privacy during everyday browsing.

IPVanish has strengthened its privacy commitments through recurring independent assessments of its no-logs policy. In November 2026, the organization declared the results of its third independent no-logs audit, following earlier assessments conducted in 2022 and 2025. The latest assessment was completed on September 25, 2026. (Schellman Compliance, September 2026)).

The audit found no evidence of traffic, metadata, or session logging that contradicted the manufacturer's stated no-logs policy. These findings provide additional support for IPVanish's position that customer internet activity is not recorded through its VPN infrastructure.

It has been revealed that independent verification is particularly relevant because privacy policies represent commitments that require meaningful evaluation. An external assessment provides an opportunity to examine whether actual operational practices align with published privacy statements. Although an audit cannot guarantee complete anonymity or eliminate every online security risk, recurring assessments contribute to greater transparency.

It has been revealed that IPVanish also continues to develop its server infrastructure, including a transition toward RAM-only servers. This infrastructure approach is designed to prevent persistent information storage on server hardware because data held in volatile memory is erased when a server restarts. The transition is expected to continue through 2027.

Security Features Support Safer Online Shopping.

Industry observers note that online shopping frequently involves accessing accounts, entering personal details, and completing transactions across different internet connections. A VPN can help protect connection-level information, particularly when browsing through public Wi-Fi networks in locations such as airports, hotels, cafes, and shopping centers.

It has been revealed that IPVanish supports modern VPN protocols, including WireGuard and OpenVPN. These protocols provide secure connection options while supporting everyday browsing and other internet activities. Encryption helps prevent unauthorized parties on local networks from easily reading transmitted information.

The service also includes a kill switch designed to block internet traffic if the VPN connection unexpectedly drops. This feature helps prevent an unprotected connection from continuing without the user's knowledge. DNS leak protection provides another safeguard by helping keep DNS requests within the protected VPN connection.

These capabilities can be useful during major shopping events, when consumers frequently visit retailer websites, compare product prices, and access payment accounts. However, VPN protection addresses only certain online risks. It does not independently prevent phishing attempts, fraudulent websites, malicious software, or retailer data breaches.

Industry observers note that IPVanish also supports unlimited simultaneous device connections. The feature allows a single subscription to cover multiple supported devices without requiring separate accounts for each connection. This arrangement can accommodate households using smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers for different online activities.

Recent findings suggest that device flexibility is particularly relevant as shopping increasingly takes place across mobile and desktop platforms. Consumers can maintain VPN protection while moving between devices, provided the appropriate applications are installed and configured.
