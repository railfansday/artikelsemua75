Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are married—and she opted for a very timeless wedding dress. The singer posted photos from her ceremony, unveiling the long-sleeve, backless gown she wore. She accessorized with earrings and slingback heels.

The couple revealed they were engaged in December 2025. For the most part, they have kept their wedding prepares under wraps.

Morando ultimately revealed how he proposed to Cyrus. In July, he told Wonderland that they had been in Kyoto, Japan. “For some reason—you’ll find this as crazy as I did—she’d never been to Japan before,” he mentioned. “Neither had I, and we’d both wanted to go for a really long time. There were talks before we went about me popping the question but I was trying to keep it secret. She was not trying to keep it a secret, if you know what I mean. So I had to be really sneaky about it. We went to Tokyo first for a couple days and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day to throw her off the scent as much as I could.”.

“We took a walk around the hotel grounds,” he continued. “There was this little outer garden that you had to cross a plank to get over. There was a sign that said you couldn’t cross; I was like, ‘That’s crazy, let’s go over there.’ She’s like, ‘No, it’s blocked.’ Eventually I persuaded her and we walked in and it was a little dead end, this beautiful Japanese forest-y situation. She was taking videos, and was turned away. That’s when I got on one knee and tapped her on the shoulder.”.
