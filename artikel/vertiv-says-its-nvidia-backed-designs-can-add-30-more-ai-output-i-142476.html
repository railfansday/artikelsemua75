In a recent development, vertiv (VRT +14.02%) isn't a designer of artificial intelligence hardware or software, but the company's status as a provider of power services, cooling technologies, and other infrastructure means it's positioned to play an crucial role in the AI buildout. The company reported its third-quarter results at the end of October, and strong data-center demand helped the company beat sales and earnings expectations and paved the way for management to raise its full-year guidance. (Related: Tech Report: Accelerating PyTorch distributed fine-tuni...)

In the company's quarterly conference call, management also highlighted the advantages of its Nvidia-supported time-to-token improvement strategies and noted they can cut deployment times in half and produce up to 30% more AI tokens within the first year from project kickoff. These advantages could help Vertiv continue scoring big wins in the artificial intelligence data center market, but there's a catch.

Vertiv and Nvidia unveiled their time-to-token partnership at Nvidia's GTC Berlin conference in October and laid out their vision for reducing the amount of time it takes data center customers to go from purchasing graphics processing units (GPUs) and other related hardware to actually producing and monetizing tokens. Vertiv's claim that it will enable the production of up to 30% more tokens through a project in the first year represents a dramatic improvement, but the catch is that the company still needs to demonstrate this in real-world scenarios. (Related: Tech Report: How we sped up transformer inference 100x ...)

Sources indicate that thus far, Vertiv's 30% time-to-token improvement is based on modeled scenarios rather than proven real-world results at scale. Through the use of digital twin configurations that allow for the creation of a simulated virtual data center facility that's used as a model, Vertiv's cooling assets, and prefabricated, modular construction resources the company sees a path to substantially reducing building times and accelerating operational launches.

NYSE · As of 11:50 AM ET 11/23/26.

On the other hand, Vertiv's path toward achieving those efficiency improvements don't look infeasible. Reducing design, validation, and deployment times could significantly improve time-to-token dynamics. Achieving uniform improvements on the targeted level probably won't be possible in many cases, but even more moderate levels of improvement would still be significant for data center operators.

Recent findings suggest that the use of pre-fabricated, modular construction paired with optimized design tools could give Vertiv a data center infrastructure platform that could prove very attractive for customers in the space. And while there's still proving to do on that front, the organization's partnership with Nvidia represents an key vote of confidence from the foundational player in the AI hardware space. The business is already seeing very strong momentum in the AI data center space, and its time-to-token project could help take growth to the next level.
