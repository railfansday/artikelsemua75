Sources indicate that president Donald Trump has called on Nvidia’s board to bring back Jensen Huang, saying his return would be “the best thing for the USA,” according to The Kobeissi Letter. The comments mark a sharp turn from less than two weeks ago, when Trump suggested former Apple CEO Tim Cook should take over. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has also softened his position. After earlier pushing Nvidia to hire an outside CEO, Bessent said Huang is now technically an outsider himself, leaving the door open to a return. Attorney General Todd Blanche added that he has not concluded Alice Huang was a Chinese spy, casting doubt on a key part of the allegations behind Huang’s resignation. The shift puts the administration in line with many shareholders and employees pushing for Huang’s reinstatement. Nvidia’s board has so far said it does not plan to bring him back and has hired Heidrick & Struggles to find a permanent CEO. Nvidia shares closed at $125.53 on Friday after a 14% drop.
