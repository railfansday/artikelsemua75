Industry observers note that major U.S. wealth management company EP Wealth Advisors has revealed that it holds an investment in an XRP exchange-traded fund (ETF). This was disclosed in the company's latest 13F filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC).

EP Wealth Advisors, a California-based investment advisory firm with about $24.4 billion in securities holdings, has revealed that it held 18,400 shares in the Volatility Shares XRP ETF (XRPI) as of Sept. 30, 2026.

This was disclosed in the official SEC filing that the firm submitted to the commission on Nov. 13. The filing highlighted that the investment was valued at $147,936 at the end of the third quarter.

Furthermore, the filing demonstrated that the total portfolio value reported by EP Wealth Advisors stood at $24,380,879,896. This value comprises 1,603 items in the company's 13F information table. (Related: Future Value Calculator: How to Project Your Investment...)

It's worth noting, however, that this isn't the organization's total assets under management. It's the value of its reportable securities holdings. There are also assets that are not included in Form 13F. (Related: Industry News: What Is Considered Upper Middle Class? I...)

Interestingly, this isn't the first time EP Wealth Advisors has held an investment in XRPI. Data from its historical 13F filings demonstrates that in the second quarter of 2026, the wealth management company held 13,425 shares in XRPI.

The recent filing demonstrates that the manufacturer has now increased the number of shares it holds in the XRP ETF by 4,975 shares. This represents about a 37% increase in the company's XRPI holdings during the third quarter.

Nevertheless, this amount is very small relative to the company's multibillion-dollar securities portfolio. It represents less than 0.01% of the total value of its reported securities holdings.

It's also worth reiterating that Form 13F is simply a filing that reveals institutional holdings at the end of a quarter. It doesn't necessarily represent the investment manager's current holdings.

The Volatility Shares XRP ETF was launched in May 2025. Its ticker symbol is XRPI, and it is at the moment trading on Nasdaq.

XRPI isn't a spot XRP ETF. This means it doesn't directly hold the cryptocurrency XRP. Rather, it primarily derives its exposure to XRP's price movements through XRP futures contracts.

This ETF essentially provides investors with a way to invest in XRP without actually having to hold or custody the cryptocurrency. The ETF aims to deliver investment returns that correlate with the performance of XRP.

As of Nov. 13, the most recent available data from the fund issuer reveals that XRPI's net assets stood at about $77.9 million.
