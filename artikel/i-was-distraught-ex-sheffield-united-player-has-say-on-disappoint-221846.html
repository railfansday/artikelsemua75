Industry observers note that sheffield United cut ties with the striker following their last promotion to the Premier League. SUFC decided not to hand him a contract extension at Bramall Lane and he subsequently became a free agent.

He has opened up about his departure from his boyhood club three-and-a-half years ago on Sky Sports’ Essential EFL Podcast: “I had a chance about three or four years before (to leave), Colorado Rapids were potentially going to take me but it wasn’t the right time. I didn’t leave Sheffield United. Sheffield United didn’t offer me a latest contract which was so disappointing for me because I got told by the owners and by the manager three times that I was going to get another year. I got told on the day of the parade that I wasn’t going to get it which absolutely ruined the day for me. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

“I was distraught. I was going to retire at 37, that was me thinking ‘do you know what, I’ve had such a good career and I can’t see myself playing for another team’. I wanted to end at Sheffield United but I was out of contract for the first time in my career and (then) LA Galaxy came calling.”.

In a recent development, sharp rose up through Sheffield United’s academy as a youngster and was a regular for them at various different youth levels. He went on to play twice for SUFC’s first-team before leaving for Scunthorpe United in 2005.

Sources indicate that the Blades re-signed him two years later and he scored 13 goals in 64 matches before embarking on spells at Doncaster Rovers, Southampton, Nottingham Forest, Reading and Leeds United. Chris Wilder lured him back to his hometown team for a third time when they were in League One in 2015.

Recent findings suggest that sharp helped Sheffield United rise from the third tier to the Premier League and found the net on 116 occasions in 311 outings. However, his former club didn’t offer him fresh terms under Paul Heckingbottom and he subsequently became available.

In a recent development, as he has now alluded to, he considered hanging up his boots before LA Galaxy came calling. His stint in America was brief though and he later returned with Hull City before Doncaster Rovers brought him back.

Recent findings suggest that sharp will go down as one of Sheffield United’s greatest ever strikers. He is a legend at SUFC and it wouldn’t be a surprise to see him go back there in some capacity down the line.

The Blades are currently looking to claw themselves out of the EFL and back into the top flight if possible. They drew 1-1 at home against Southampton on Tuesday night with midfielder Sydie Peck scoring a late equaliser.

Wilder‘s side are sat in 9th place in the table after 17 fixtures and they are only one place outside the play-offs. They are one point behind Swansea City in 8th and could rise into the top eight this weekend if results go their way.

Sheffield United face a tricky away trip down to QPR on Saturday. Their upcoming opponents are positioned in 7th.
