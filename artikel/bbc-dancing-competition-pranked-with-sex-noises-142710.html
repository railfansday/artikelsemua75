A recording of what definitely sounded like sex noises interrupted a recent episode of a British dancing competition called — and presented totally without comment — Strictly Come Dancing.

According to recent reports, the BBC series was broadcasting live from the Tower Ballroom in Blackpool on Saturday, Nov. 21. While judge Motsi Mabuse was delivering feedback to two contestants, the faint strains of some vigorous and ecstatic moaning could be heard emanating from somewhere behind her. While there appeared to be some awkward looks and throat-clearing from the other judges, Mabuse handled the moment like a pro, quipping, “It’s not me!”.

At the end of the episode, Strictly Come Dancing (again, presented without comment) host Emma Willis addressed the audio malfunction, saying, “Before we see the judges’ leaderboard, we’d like to apologize if you heard any audio disruptions earlier in tonight’s show and for any offense caused.”.

As detailed in The Standard, Jarvis was livestreaming Saturday night and not-so-subtly suggested he thought one of the other Strictly Come Dancing judges, Craig Revel Horwood, was about to be the target of a “naughty noises” prank.
