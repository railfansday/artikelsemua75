It has been revealed that fiona the hippo received a formal tribute from the U. S. Senate on Friday, one day after the beloved Cincinnati Zoo resident died following a medical emergency.

Sen. Bernie Moreno, R-Ohio, introduced the resolution honoring Fiona's life and legacy while expressing the chamber's gratitude to the zoo's staff for their care and dedication. The measure passed by unanimous consent.

Speaking from the Senate floor next to a photo of the hippopotamus, Moreno described Fiona's death as a "devastating loss" and a tragic accident.

The tribute came one day after the Cincinnati Zoo announced that Fiona died after ingesting a knit hat that accidentally landed in her enclosure last week. According to the zoo, a visitor tossed the hat into the air outside the exhibit, where it caught on a ceiling fan and was rolled out over the viewing windows into her habitat.

It has been revealed that "The vet staff tried everything they could to help her after she ingested a hat that ended up in her habitat by accident last week," the zoo said in a Thursday statement. "Despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome.".
