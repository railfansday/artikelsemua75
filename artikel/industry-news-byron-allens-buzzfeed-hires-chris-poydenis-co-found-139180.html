Industry News: Byron Allen’s BuzzFeed Hires Chris Poydenis, Co-Founder of Creator Marketing Agency Influential, as Chief Revenue Officer

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Byron Allen is hoping to turn things around at BuzzFeed, the money-losing digital publisher he envisions becoming a major free, ad-supported streaming property, with the hiring of Chris Poydenis as chief revenue officer.

It has been revealed that poydenis previously was chief revenue officer and co-founder of Influential, a leading creator-marketing organization. In joining BuzzFeed, Poydenis is bringing over Jesse Batres, formerly Influential’s EVP, head of national sales and partnerships, as president of sales and partnerships. In 2024, Poydenis and his partners sold Influential to the ad agency conglomerate Publicis for about $500 million. Related Stories Pink Announces 2027 Sphere Residency in Las Vegas.

Recent findings suggest that byron Allen, chairman and CEO of BuzzFeed, acquired a controlling 51% stake in the organization earlier this year. Under the deal, he paid $20 million in cash at closing and issued a $100 million promissory note due within five years (accruing interest at 5% annually). In July, BuzzFeed laid off 35% of its employees, cutting about 180 jobs across the company. The company’s three brands are BuzzFeed, HuffPost and the food-focused Tasty.

“Chris and Jesse are exactly the kind of proven revenue leaders we want driving BuzzFeed’s sales team as we build BuzzFeed into an outstanding free-streaming premium content platform,” Allen said in a statement. In addition to BuzzFeed, Allen serves as chairman and CEO of Allen Media Group, a collection of TV and streaming businesses.

Industry observers note that poydenis commented: “I am profoundly honored and humbled to join BuzzFeed at such a consequential moment in the evolution of free-streaming. My conviction in Byron’s vision is unequivocal, and I am resolute in my commitment to translating that vision into a tangible and enduring commercial reality – a reality that empowers brands to achieve measurable business outcomes through meaningful engagement across platforms, formats, and audiences.” Batres added, “Many of us have grown up with the beloved brand BuzzFeed. Under Byron Allen’s stewardship, we are confident we have the right elements in place to build BuzzFeed into a premier free-streaming service.”. It has been revealed that debuted in 2006, BuzzFeed had become a buzzy upstart and hit a peak valuation of $1.7 billion in 2016. Among its investors was NBCUniversal, which pumped $400 million into the manufacturer. But BuzzFeed has seen its revenue crumble dramatically in recent years and has endured multiple rounds of layoffs. Currently, BuzzFeed has a market capitalization of under $40 million.

In a recent development, for the third quarter of 2026, BuzzFeed reported total revenue of $26.0 million (down 44%) and net loss of $18.7 million (versus a net loss of $7.4 million the year-earlier period). Allen, in announcing the results, mentioned Q3 was “a transitional quarter.”.

“The restructuring we announced in July reduced our workforce and dedicated contractors by approximately 35% and we expect this to yield approximately $30 million in annualized savings,” Allen stated. In the fourth quarter, “our strategy is to bring compelling content to audiences wherever they are — across digital platforms and connected TV — as we work to position BuzzFeed to be a premier global free streaming platform.”.
