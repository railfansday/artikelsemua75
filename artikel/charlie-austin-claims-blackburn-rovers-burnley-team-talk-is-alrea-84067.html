Charlie Austin believes Blackburn Rovers will be determined to beat Burnley for Tony Mowbray when the two rivals meet on Sunday.

It has been a tough few days for Rovers, as the club announced that Mowbray had stepped back from his role as head coach due to health complications.

Many had tipped Blackburn for relegation before a ball was kicked this season, but they have enjoyed a very positive campaign so far under Mowbray, with the team going into the weekend fixtures just three points outside the play-off places. Therefore, news of Mowbray’s health issues is a real setback for the club, although it obviously puts football into perspective, with the football world coming together to wish the ex-Middlesbrough chief well.

And, speaking on his Chaz Kicks Off podcast, former Burnley striker Austin echoed those thoughts, and he also explained why the news will unite the Rovers group going into the game.

In a recent development, “When anybody gets ill, or has issues in their personal life, you wish them all the best. But you see from all the players and people that know him, that have made comments, it reveals what kind of bloke he is. (Related: Intel Diamond Rapids reveals signs of life in SC26 demo...)

“Blackburn’s teamtalk is done. It’s an easy one to say, let’s win this for the manager, let’s win it for Mowbray. He’s got more important things going on in his life, which puts football second, but let's go and give everything. Not 110%, but as a collective, let's give 150% and go and run over our neighbours Burnley and make a big statement.

“That’s how I’d see it if I was in the Blackburn dressing room, and it’d be the same if I was a coach or a fan - go and put on a performance for Mowbray.”.

This news came as a shock to the Rovers supporters, so it presumably was the same for the players, and, as Austin says, the initial reaction is to send your thoughts to Mowbray.

But, football will go on, and it’s inevitable that the Blackburn group will want to get results for the manager, as he was a hugely respected figure at the club, and he has personally helped so many of the current squad.

It’s not like they need extra motivation to beat Burnley when you consider the hatred that comes with the East Lancashire derby anyway, but, if they did, then this will provide it, and you can be sure that Mowbray’s name will be spoken about before the players go out on Sunday.

Of course, the coaching staff won’t want to add too much emotional pressure to the players, so they must channel it properly, but the Blackburn squad will head to Turf Moor with full belief that they can pick up three points.

This is a fixture that the Clarets have dominated recently, with Burnley having not lost to their rivals in the last 11 attempts, which is a run that stretches back to 2010.
