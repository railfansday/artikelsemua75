Sources indicate that world leaders including the U.S. President Donald Trump and Russia's Vladimir Putin gathered Thursday for the second day of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit, ahead of a historic trilateral meeting between the U.S., Russia and China later in the day.

It has been revealed that international Monetary Fund Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva kicked off Thursday's agenda after China's President Xi Jinping's welcome note. Georgieva mentioned that artificial intelligence and energy supply shocks had accelerated global inflation in many economies, calling for monetary policy tightening. According to recent reports, japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi was met with another cold reception. She spent several minutes trying to talk to Xi who did not appear to respond with no interpreter present.

It has been revealed that trump held separate bilateral meetings with Putin and Xi earlier in the day. The president praised Xi's leadership and remarked Washington was negotiating deals with several major APEC economies.

In their first in-person talks in over a year, Trump told Putin that "We've known each other for a long time, and I think a lot of wonderful things can come out of this — or at least start," referring to Putin as a "friend.".

The three leaders are due to hold their first-ever trilateral meeting later Thursday, with the Middle East conflict, Russia's war in Ukraine, trade and artificial intelligence likely on the agenda. Expectations for the historic three-way meeting to yield any concrete outcome remain low.

The summit will conclude Trump's whirlwind Asia trip, after which the American president will return to Washington, D.C.
