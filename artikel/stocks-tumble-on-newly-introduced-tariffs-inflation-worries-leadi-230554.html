Recent findings suggest that U.S. stocks tumbled on Wednesday as a new round of tariffs, and worries about inflation, triggered another AI-sell off leading into the Thanksgiving holiday. All three major markets fell Wednesday, but had made some corrections by Wednesday afternoon at the time of this writing, with some of those losses blunted as investors bought on the morning dip. Tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC) which had been down nearly 3% earlier in the day, was down less than 1%, while the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI) was down 0.5%, according Yahoo Finance. The the S&P 500 (^GSPC)—which sank more 1.3% Wednesday morning—was down .2% by the afternoon according to Yahoo Finance. The market dip was partially a response to the Trump Administration’s overnight announcement of new tariffs, on 14 economies, including China, the European Union, Taiwan, Japan, and India despite the Supreme Court’s March ruling striking down his previous tariffs. Also rattling Wall Street: The Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index, which showed prices increased at a higher rate in October than expected—up 3.3%, compared with 3.1% a month earlier—leading some analysts to predict a Fed interest rate hike in December. Leading the sell-off: Nvidia. “Last week, the hope was that Nvidia could find U.S. buyers for chips that might otherwise go to Southeast Asian data centers serving Chinese customers. Today’s tariff news complicates that trade,” Brian Stutland, portfolio manager at Equity Armor Investments, tells Fast Organization. “Chips designed in America could still cost more to bring in if they’re made overseas, just as U.S. data centers are taking longer to come online. That’s why chip stocks are under pressure.”   Just as the company was finally seeing a healthy rebound—following the reinstatement of ousted founder Jensen Huang—the stock (Nasdaq: NVDA) dipped down again about 5%, at the time of this writing on Wednesday afternoon, leading the decline among AI, chip and tech stocks, many of which made double-digit gains just two days earlier: Micron Technology, Inc. (Nasdaq: MU) down about 7% Sandisk Corporation (Nasdaq: SNDK) down over 7% Oracle (Nasdaq: ORCL) declined more than 4% Alphabet (Nasdaq: GOOG) shed nearly 3%. Western Digital Corporation (Nasdaq: WDC) down over 3% Seagate Technology Holdings (Nasdaq: STX) down over 4% Dell Technologies (Nasdaq: DELL) was also down 1% at the time of this writing Wednesday afternoon despite reporting strong third-quarter earnings, beating analyst expectations, and providing strong fourth-quarter guidance, as CNBC noted.
