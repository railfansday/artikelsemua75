In a recent development, get the Netspend Prepaid Card if you have no bank account and need to load cash at stores, and switch it from the default proposal, which charges $1.99 a purchase, to the $4.99 Monthly Plan. A month with $500 in paychecks or benefits by direct deposit then drops that plan to $0 automatically.

The Netspend Prepaid Card is a reloadable prepaid Visa for people without a bank account who get paid by direct deposit or load cash at stores. At 30 purchases a month, the default Pay-As-You-Go proposal costs $59.70 in purchase fees, while the Monthly Plan costs a flat $4.99 and the Reduced Monthly Plan costs nothing.
