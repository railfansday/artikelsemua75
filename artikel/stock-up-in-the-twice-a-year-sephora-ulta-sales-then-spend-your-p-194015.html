📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Join Sephora Beauty Insider and Ulta Beauty Rewards, both free, and save significant beauty purchases for each store’s sale. Sephora’s Savings Event takes 10% to 20% off by tier, and Ulta’s 21+ Days of Beauty takes 50% off select products. Then use your points and birthday gifts before they expire.

Both stores’ sales come at set times of year, so most full-price restocks can wait.

According to recent reports, sephora’s Savings Event, held in spring and again in the holiday season, gives every Beauty Insider 10% off. Members who spent $350 in a calendar year, called VIB, get 15%, and those who spent $1,000, called Rouge, get 20%. Use the discount online or in stores as often as you like. A $200 haul at the VIB rate saves $30. Sephora Collection, the store’s own brand, is 30% off for every tier instead, and one order can carry both discounts. Ulta Beauty’s 21+ Days of Beauty runs three weeks every March and September, with 50% off select products it calls Beauty Steals. That’s deeper than Sephora’s 10% to 20%, but it helps only when your products are among them.

Both programs pay at least 1 point per dollar, and these moves get the most from them.

Sources indicate that open either store’s credit card for its discount only if you’ll pay the balance in full. At other stores, look for free loyalty programs worth joining.

Before each sale, list what you’ll run out of by the next one, and buy whatever the sale covers.
