Selling the OC’s Kayla Cardona is asking for support after her 20-year-old son, Jordan, faced a life-threatening medical emergency.

Sources indicate that “What began as unexpected internal bleeding swiftly turned into a terrifying fight for survival,” Kayla, 37, wrote via a GoFundMe page. “As suspected, Meckel’s diverticulum did NOT appear on Jordan’s CT scans, colonoscopy or endoscopy. It was only during emergency intestinal surgery that doctors confirmed Meckel’s diverticulum, the very condition they had been urged to investigate.”.

According to recent reports, according to the Cleveland Clinic, Meckel’s diverticulum is a congenital defect in the small intestine. It occurs in 2 percent of the population and only 2 percent of people who have it develop complications or symptoms.

In a recent development, “Throughout his hospitalization, Jordan required nine blood transfusions, three units of plasma, and one platelet transfusion to survive,” Kayla wrote. “Although Jordan is finally home, his journey toward healing and recovery is just beginning.”.
