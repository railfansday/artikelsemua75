Former Bachelorette Kaitlyn Bristowe is sharing her new ritual with dog Pinot amid the pooch’s terrifying health battle.

Bristowe then panned the camera to show Pinot lying beside her, adding that the dog is a “gangster.”.

“You are so strong. Big dog,” Bristowe said. “You are literally the strongest dog I’ve ever met. You are strong. You are kind. You are going to fight this battle. It’s like we’re in the ring. Because you are a gangster. The one and only Pinot G, you know?”.

It has been revealed that bristowe, who is a dog mom to golden retrievers Pinot and Ramen, shared last month that she was “told to say goodbye” to her dog after he accidentally overdosed on 120 prescription pills for his preexisting heart condition. After five days in a critical care unit, Pinot returned home. Weeks later, Bristowe shared that Pinot “cannot catch a break” and is facing a upcoming health scare. Bristowe went on to thank her “partner,” seemingly referring to rumored boyfriend Zac Clark, for being by her side amid Pinot’s health battle.

According to recent reports, “Pinot and I would love to thank his doggy dad and my partner,” Bristowe added. “He has been a hero, the amc, and for your prayers to keep him going. I’ve never met a dog willing to fight so hard through all of this.”.

She continued, “We will keep everyone posted. Just know when you see me online, you might not know what’s going on behind the scenes.
