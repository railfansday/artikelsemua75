Former Celtic managerial target Robbie Keane has been tipped for a surprise return to the dugout after being named a major favourite for a top EFL Championship job, with ex-Rangers and Hibs bosses also in the running.

Keane, 46, was believed to have held talks with the Celtic hierarchy over becoming the club’s newly introduced manager in the summer. However, talks broke down over fan anger at his decision to remain in charge of Israeli Premier League outfit Maccabi Tel Aviv during the outbreak of the nation’s war against Gaza, before moving to Ferencvaros in January 2025. Out of work since quitting his role as head coach of the Hungarian side in May, the former Republic of Ireland international is now one of the leading favourites to take over at promotion-chasing side Swansea City following Vitor Matos’ decision to depart to become the new manager of Ligue 1 side Strasbourg on Tuesday.

According to recent reports, at the moment sixth in the EFL Championship table, Swansea signed former Scottish Premiership trio Stephen Welsh, Elijah Just and Ross Stewart in the summer, and had started strongly under Matos. However, despite being just three points off the automatic promotion places, the Portuguese manager opted to leave for Stade de la Meinau.

Industry observers note that while former Mainz manager Bo Henriksen is the current 5/1 favourite, Keane is joint-third on the list alongside recently sacked Manchester United boss, Michael Carrick, at 14/1. Former Rangers boss Russell Martin is also among the candidates, with the current Leicester City boss having an outside chance of a sensational return to the club that he left in the summer of 2023. Appointed as head coach of Swansea in August 2021, the 40-year-old won 39 of his 99 games in charge of the club before being poached by Southampton, where he led the club to promotion via the EFL Championship playoffs.

It has been revealed that even more interestingly, former Hibs head coach Paul Heckingbottom is named on the list of favourites following his sacking at Preston North End in September. The 49-year-old endured a tough eight-month spell in charge at Easter Road in 2019, but went on to achieve automatic promotion to the English Premier League in his next role with Sheffield United.

Recent findings suggest that after leaving the Blades, he spent two seasons at Deepdale, but was relieved of his duties earlier this season after losing six of his first seven games.
