Halide 3.4 is out today as the latest enhancement to the pro camera app. The update adds several new features, including a larger viewfinder for iPhone Duo and more.

The new Halide upgrade also lets you use the outer screen on iPhone Duo as a live preview or a fill flash. It brings back a few camera tools from Halide Mark II as well. (Related: NVIDIA brings agents to life with DGX Spark and Reachy ...)

Lux Optics first added iPhone Duo support in October with Halide 3.3. With the phone fully open, the initial iPhone Duo upgrade let you review your most recent photo alongside the live viewfinder.

Today’s upcoming XL mode gives you the option to swap the photo preview for a larger viewfinder. Controls on both sides enable two-handed shooting.

While the Duo is open, the outer screen can mirror Halide’s camera controls or show a full-screen viewfinder without controls. The second option lets the person you’re photographing see how you’ve framed the shot. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

In low light, Halide can use the outer screen as a fill flash. That gives you a softer source of light for portraits, and Halide’s developers recommend turning the screen brightness all the way up before taking the photo.

Halide also now places the histogram along the Duo’s crease. The revision revises the Photo Lab interface for editing with the Duo half open as well.

On other iPhones, Halide 3.4 restores the color and luminance histograms and the shutter timer from Mark II.

Recent findings suggest that halide Mark III introduced back in May with a redesigned camera and the fresh Photo Lab editor. Recent updates added RAW-only capture in 3.1 and Aperture Priority for iPhone 18 Pro in 3.2. Halide Mark III version 3.4 is available now on the App Store.
