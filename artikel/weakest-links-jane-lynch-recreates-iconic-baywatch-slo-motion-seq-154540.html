In honor of Celebrity Weakest Link‘s season finale, Jane Lynch recreated the iconic Baywatch intro — complete with a slo-mo run.

In Us‘ exclusive first look at the opening for the Monday, November 30, episode of the Fox series, Lynch, 66, is joined by several lifeguards wearing the squad’s signature color of red in a slow-motion sequence set to “I’m Always Here” by Jimi Jamison. Lynch, who hosts the show, even took one for the team by letting herself be drenched in water, saying, “That’s a lot of water.”.

The funny bit took inspiration from Baywatch, which ran from 1989 to 2001. Celebrity Weakest Link will celebrate the show by reuniting Kelly Packard, Nicole Eggert, Michael Bergin, Alexandra Paul, Traci Bingham, David Chokachi, Gregory Alan Williams and Gena Lee Nolin as they compete against each other to win money for their selected charities.

Recent findings suggest that baywatch centered around the lives of lifeguards who worked on California (and Hawaii) beaches while dealing with a variety of situations including earthquakes, shark attacks and serial killers. At one point during its run in the ’90s, Baywatch made history as the most-watched television series in the world.

Industry observers note that the series had dozens of cast shakeups over the years but ultimately turned into a cultural phenomenon. Some of the show’s memorable cast members include Eggert, 54, Carmen Electra, David Hasselhoff, Billy Warlock, Jeremy Jackson and Erika Eleniak. In September 2025, Fox confirmed that a “reimagined” version of the iconic series is coming to the network. The network recently released the official trailer where Hobie (Stephen Amell) met his daughter, Charlie (Jessica Belkin). Hobie will want to build a relationship with his daughter as she joins his workplace as a lifeguard.

Romance will be explored between Charlie and Luke (Noah Beck). Sparks also fly between Trina (Shay Mitchell) and Brad (Thaddeus LaGrone). Elsewhere in the promo, Hobie shared scenes with Nat (Hassie Harrison) while Selene (Brooks Nader) appeared on multiple occasions to save the day.

Sources indicate that there will also be cameos from OG cast member Chokachi, 58 — and rapper Meg the Stallion. Before the premiere, the cast spoke to Us about the hard work that took place behind the scenes.

“It’s physically one of the most demanding roles I’ve ever done,” Harrison, 36, told Us in May about preparing to suit up on the show. “[Then there’s] the mental aspect where I try to say nice things to myself some days but other days it’s harder.”.

Industry observers note that she continued: “But what an honor that we’ve got cast and chosen for this role. We just want to give it our best.”.
