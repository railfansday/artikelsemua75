Hila Klein has unveiled that she is stepping away from the H3 podcast amid her divorce from Ethan Klein, as he has been teasing a comeback.

Now, Hila has announced that she has decided to stop working on any upcoming show, saying she is “devastated” to be stepping away.

According to recent reports, “I’m so sorry to share that I’ve made the difficult decision to stop working on the fresh show. I truly tried with every ounce of strength I had to make this work, but after everything that has happened, I’ve come to realize that I can’t continue down this path while also giving myself the space I need to heal and protect my mental health,” she said on November 23.

Hila added that she needs to “focus on myself, my healing, and what’s best for me and my kids,” before noting that she is “devastated” about ending the show.

Recent findings suggest that “Walking away from something that has been such a huge part of my life is incredibly painful,” she continued. “I will miss you all more than I can put into words. Thank you for all the love and support over the years.”.
