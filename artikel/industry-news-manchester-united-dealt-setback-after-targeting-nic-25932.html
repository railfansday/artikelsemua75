Manchester United have been dealt a blow in their pursuit of Nico Paz as Real Madrid intend to activate their €80 million (£67m) buyback clause to sign the midfielder from Como, according to reports in Spain.

Paz joined Como from Madrid two years ago and has established himself as the talisman in Cesc Fabregas’ side. (Related: Tech Report: What Couples Married For 40 Years Do When ...)

So far this season, the 22-year-old has been one of the standout performers in the Champions League, scoring three goals and registering five assists in four games.

That included a man-of-the-match display in Como’s 4-1 win over United last month while piled more pressure on Michael Carrick.

Last week, reports claimed that United had identified Paz as a potential successor to Bruno Fernandes and were considering a move for the Argentina international next summer.

However, Cadena SER reports that Madrid are ‘determined’ to re-sign Paz at the end of the season and are planning to trigger the €80m (£67m) buyback clause which is part of their agreement with Como. (Related: One Familiar Item Of Clothing Scored Lowest for Intelli...)

Madrid had the option to re-sign Paz this year but the midfielder personally requested to stay at Como for another season.

It has been revealed that ‘I thought this was where I would grow the most. I think I made the right decision and I’m happy I did,’ Paz remarked in September.

Como spent €60m (£51.77m) on their deal to keep Paz at the club but Madrid, crucially, insisted that a buyout clause would remain part of their agreement.
