Industry observers note that ruben Amorim admits Serie A is educational for him, as he is stunned by Frosinone’s tactical tweaks against Milan today. ‘There’s a lot of knowledge in this league, we need to be ready for anything.’.

It kicks off at San Siro at 17.00 GMT (18.00 CET).

The biggest problem for the Rossoneri this season has been consistency, as they seem to follow one strong performance with a disastrous result in the next, which has left them struggling in the Europa League more than Serie A.

Frosinone are without Antonio Raimondo, Florian Grillitsch and suspended Gabriele Bracaglia, but surprisingly opt for a False 9 system rather than starting Daniel Birligea upfront. (Related: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to transformer...)

“It’s strange to see that, we tried to look right away for one time they played like this,” Amorim told DAZN Italia and Sky Sport Italia.

Sources indicate that “The managers are really smart and know how to adapt to the opponent, there’s a lot of knowledge in this league. It’s fun, but on the other side we need to be ready for anything.”.

Mike Maignan shook off a knock on international duty to start in goal, but Strahinja Pavlovic is still not at 100 per cent, with Matteo Gabbia, Samuel Chukwueze, Fikayo Tomori, Ruben Loftus-Cheek and Alexis Saelemaekers still injured.

It is a front three of Goncalo Ramos, Diego Moreira and Christian Pulisic, who were all in action for their countries last week, but are they suited together when Ramos works harder for the team than the team works for him?

“The most important thing if you see Roma, the principles and rotation of the team is more clear than for us. We need time to achieve that, and we had injuries that changed our way of playing, so we need time to work on every principle,” replied Amorim.

“The characteristics of the players are important, but Rabiot is a player who can give goals, Pulisic and Moreira can give goals, so it’s more the principles of the team and the rotation of the team than the characteristics of the players.
