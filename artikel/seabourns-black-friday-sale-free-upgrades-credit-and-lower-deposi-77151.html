Seabourn has announced its Black Friday Sale, combining free upgrades, shipboard credit and reduced deposits.

Valid for newly introduced bookings made from November 17 through December 8, 2026, the offer covers select ocean and expedition voyages departing in 2026 and beyond.

Guests can choose from nearly 780 itineraries operated by all five ships, sailing to hundreds of destinations across the globe.

“For those who love to travel, few gifts are more meaningful than the promise of a new journey,” remarked Laura Whittaker-Rawlinson, vice president of commercial and revenue management for Seabourn. (Related: Breaking: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipel...)

“Black Friday Sale is back with the same added value our guests have embraced year after year, now on almost every voyage we offer. With sailings open into mid-2029, further ahead than ever before, it’s a wonderful opportunity to proposal what comes next and make every journey even more personal.”.

Shipboard credit can be used for shore excursions, boutique shopping, spa treatments, and the enhanced Stream Wi-Fi Package powered by Starlink, as well as private guided tours, culinary experiences and reservations at The Retreat, Seabourn’s exclusive open-air cabana experience aboard the Seabourn Encore and Seabourn Ovation.

Full World and Grand Voyages are excluded from the offer.
