The Championship season is now well underway, meaning we have witnessed several inspiring team or individual performances.

The second tier of English football is at the moment home to some of the country's most high-profile clubs, players and upcoming talents who certainly have the potential to go on to bigger and better things. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

This is the case in various positions, but, unsurprisingly, Championship goalkeepers continue to make plenty of headlines for both good and bad reasons.

In recent times, we have seen England internationals, Jordan Pickford and James Trafford, excel in the division, and there are many looking to follow in their footsteps.

With that in mind, Football League World have looked at the 20 most-valuable goalkeepers in the second tier right now, owing to Transfermarkt figures.

All figures have been converted from Euro to GBP.

Getting us underway is Preston North End's Daniel Iversen, who is at the moment one of three names valued at £2m.

The Danish shot-stopper rejoined the Deepdale outfit last summer on a free transfer from Leicester City on a four-year contract, but after a mixed first season, this campaign could very much end in relegation for the Lilywhites. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Norwich City parted with £2m to sign Bosnian keeper, Vladan Kovacevic, from Liga Portugal giants, Sporting CP, last summer, and his valuation has since stayed the same.

During that timeframe, the Canaries have endured mixed fortunes on the pitch under Liam Manning and Philippe Clement, while the 28-year-old is yet to make it into a double-figure tally of clean sheets in over 50 appearances.

Jacob Widell Zetterstrom's £1.4m switch to Derby County two summers ago initially looked inspired, but it has been a difficult 2026 for the Swedish international goalkeeper, who is also valued at £2m.

After recovering from an illness earlier this year, the 28-year-old became the subject of interest from Premier League side, Leeds United, but remained at Pride Park, where he has returned to the lineup in a difficult period for John Eustace's side despite a four-game absence with a knee concern.

Alphonse Areola's valuation at West Ham United has taken a major hit following the club's relegation, with the five-time French international's cost now claimed to be in the region of just £2.4m.

This is also impacted by the fact the 33-year-old is out-of-contract at the London Stadium next June, whilst he is yet to make a competitive appearance in 2026/27.

Max O'Leary is also valued at £2.4m right now, but the Irishman has proven to be an inspired signing for West Bromwich Albion.

After being a key figure in the Baggies' survival mission under James Morrison last season, the 30-year-old has already come to his side's rescue on multiple occasions after signing a fresh three-year contract, preventing the most goals in the division whilst having an extremely strong save percentage.

Gregoire Coudert arrived at Burnley from Stade Brestois in July, with the Frenchman the third and final shot-stopper right now with a £2.4m value.

After finding himself on the bench in the Clarets' first eight games under former head coach, Nicky Hayen, Coudert has become a mainstay under Fabio Celestini as the club look to avoid the embarrassment of back-to-back relegations.

Jose Sa has made over 180 appearances for Wolves, having joined from Olympiacos in 2021.

The Portuguese international, despite being in and out of the side last season, is currently Cesar Peixoto's 'number one' at Molineux, although it remains to be seen for how much longer this will be the case.

The 33-year-old is out-of-contract next summer, meaning his current worth is £2.9m.

The only Championship goalkeeper with a £3.3m market value at present is Burnley's Max Weiß.

Weiß moved to Turf Moor last summer from FC Karlsruher in a reported £4.3m deal, but has only made 15 appearances for his current employers so far.

A contributing factor to the German's decrease in value is the fact he is yet to register a clean sheet during that timeframe.

Watford goalkeeper, Federico Ravaglia is up next, with the Hornets seeing the Italian pen a six-year contract at Vicarage Road in the summer.

As such, the 27-year-old is presently valued at £4.1m, having emerged as one of few bright sparks in Hertfordshire during a testing start to the season under Alessio Dionisi.

Arthur Okonkwo impressed during his first season at Championship level with Wrexham last term, but despite keeping 10 clean sheets, was loaned out to divisional rivals, Charlton Athletic, in August.

Despite having a mixed start to life in South London with his temporary employers, and the fact his Red Dragons contract expires next summer, the 25-year-old is also valued at £4.1m right now.
