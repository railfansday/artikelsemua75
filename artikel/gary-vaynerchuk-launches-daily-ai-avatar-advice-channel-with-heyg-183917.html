Sources indicate that the project turns audience questions into a recurring public video series rather than a private chatbot. Industry observers note that the avatar was made from footage Vaynerchuk recorded and is designed to reproduce his appearance, voice and delivery. The arrangement keeps scripting and approval with people while automating the presentation, with each chosen answer posted publicly on @Hey_GaryVee.
