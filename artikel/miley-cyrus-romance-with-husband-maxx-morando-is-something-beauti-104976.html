Sure, Miley Cyrus can buy her own flowers, write her name in the sand.

But it's hard to argue that anyone can love the three-time Grammy winner better than her newly minted husband Maxx Morando can.

In a recent development, just under a year after they got engaged, the pair officially said "I do" Nov. 21, according to Entertainment Tonight.

It has been revealed that and before the Hannah Montana alum and Liily drummer walked down the aisle, they made sure their guests had the best of both worlds. The couple kicked off the weekend with a rehearsal dinner that was certainly a party in the U.S.A. serving up filet mignon and Maine lobster ravioli.

While the menu was sweet, Cyrus' feelings for Morando are even sweeter. (Related: Breaking: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons in a...)

According to recent reports, "We've been together six years, and I've never gotten the ick," she told Zane Lowe in an interview shared by Apple Music Sept. 17. "All of a sudden I got in my head, I was like, ‘Oh my God, we’re about to get married. What if I get the ick?'".

It has been revealed that "I asked him, 'What happens if you get the ick once you’re married?'" she continued. "He’s like, 'You deal with it and you move on and you don’t be a baby. The ick is for when you’re in fifth grade.'".

It has been revealed that "I think that’s the thing that maybe generations prior didn’t teach us," Cyrus said, "that marriage doesn’t need to be a prison. It can actually be a playground.".

Recent findings suggest that having watched her union with Liam Hemsworth metaphorically burn after their Malibu home went up in flames in 2018, Cyrus was good with being alone. She was gold, actually.

But when a friend floated the idea of setting her up with 28-year-old Morando on a blind date, she reflected to British Vogue in 2023, "I thought, 'The worst that can happen is I leave.'".

Instead, she found the kinda dream that can't be sold.

Mostly because, this time around, she was looking for more than just a piercing stare and effortless curls.

In the past, she acknowledged in a joint sit down with Tish Cyrus and sisters Brandi Cyrus and Noah Cyrus, her mother had encouraged her to stay "with the wrong guy," she explained to New York magazine's The Cut, "'cause they're hot.'".

In a recent development, though, make no mistake, she insisted of Morando, "My man's hot as hell too. But my man also respects me.".

With the rocker, she continued, "I ended up with a person who means a lot to me and treats me really well and respects me.".

And that's just a few of the far more than seven things she likes about her spouse.

With his birthday mere days before hers, "he’s a Scorpio and so is his grandfather," Cyrus raved during a June 2025 appearance on the Sorry We're Cyrus podcast, "that’s what makes Maxx just so cool because he can sting.".

The "Can't Be Tamed" musician admittedly can get a bit crazy, so she "couldn't be with" someone that doesn't have that edge, she noted. "They would have been toast already.".

But not long after she and Morando turned up at Gucci's Love Parade fashion show together in November 2021, it became clear they had the right recipe.

According to recent reports, "We just don't take life too seriously," she told Harper's Bazaar in 2024, describing her frequent collaborator as "very similar" to herself. Decades into her career, she continued, she had a pattern of working "with the people that I love. And Maxx just inspires me so much.".

As the pair embark on something beautiful, take a look back at their journey so far.

Sources indicate that miley Cyrus and musician Maxx Morando began dating in 2021. "We got put on a blind date," she recalled to British Vogue in 2023. "Well, it was blind for me and not really for him. I thought, 'The worst that can happen is I leave.'".

Miley didn't leave and, as their romance blossomed, the pair went red carpet official at the Gucci Love Parade event on November 2, 2021.

Miley and Maxx, who is a drummer in the band Liily, enjoyed a night out seeing Chicago on Broadway.

The "Flowers" singer and her boyfriend stepped out together at a Versace runway show in West Hollywood on March 9, 2023.

Maxx was by Miley's side when she won her first-ever Grammy at the 2024 award ceremony. The couple even shared a sweet smooch at their table during the event, and Miley referred to Maxx in her acceptance speech as "my love.".

Sources indicate that they returned to the Grammy Awards in 2025 for another public date night. "He’s very similar to me," Miley told Harper's Bazaar in an interview published November 2024. "We just don’t take life too seriously.".

Maxx, who is six years younger than the Hannah Montana star, joined Miley on the red carpet at SNL50: The Anniversary Special, to celebrate 50 years of Saturday Night Live.

The musicians got glammed up for a big night out at the 2025 Oscars ceremony.

And later that night they stepped out in fresh looks for an appearance at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party.

It has been revealed that it was a party in the U.S.A. when the pair got married in Plam Springs.
