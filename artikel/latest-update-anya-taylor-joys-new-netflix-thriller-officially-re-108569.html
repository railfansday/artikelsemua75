Anya Taylor-Joy has rapidly become one of the world's biggest up and coming stars, and she's developed a liking for characters who, to be quite frank, don't particularly care for following the rules. A chess prodigy with a drug dependency in The Queen's Gambit, a lonely sniper in The Gorge and a young Furiosa have all been notched up to date. Her next Netflix series looks set to continue that tradition, although this time the trouble is going to be considerably more personal. Based on a bestselling novel, the fresh thriller follows a young woman who has some very serious challenges with her extremely wealthy family.

According to recent reports, newly introduced first-look photos have emerged of Taylor-Joy filming How to Kill Your Family in Barcelona, Spain, as production on the Netflix series continues. The images, published by What's on Netflix, show Taylor-Joy as Grace Bernard wearing a white minidress, patterned headscarf, and oversized sunglasses while filming along the waterfront. According to recent reports, the Barcelona scenes come approximately a month after production began in London in October. The latest pictures also show Taylor-Joy carrying a suitcase with a luggage tag marked NCE, the airport code for Nice, suggesting the Spanish city could be standing in for the French Riviera. Netflix hasn't confirmed the location being depicted.

Based on Bella Mackie's 2021 novel, the eight-episode series follows Grace, the illegitimate daughter of billionaire Simon Artemis. After her father rejects her and her mother dies, Grace decides to take revenge on the wealthy relatives who abandoned them, with a substantial inheritance potentially waiting at the end of her murderous plans. The cast includes Clive Owen (Children of Men) as Simon Artemis. Orlando Norman and David Shields (The Crown) have also joined the series, although their characters haven't been officially disclosed.

Emma Moran (Extraordinary) is writing the adaptation, with Josephine Bornebusch (Baby Reindeer) and Tom Kingsley (Ghosts) directing. Taylor-Joy is also an executive producer through her LadyKiller production organization. Filming is expected to continue until February 2027, although Netflix has yet to announce a premiere date.
