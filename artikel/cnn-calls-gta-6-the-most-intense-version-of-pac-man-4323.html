Wow! What a terrible way to describe GTA 6. Or really any popular video game made in the last 20 years. Look, I’m not suggesting these people need to be gaming experts. But video games aren’t a tiny niche only played by kids down at the arcade. They’re a massively popular, successful, and influential medium.
