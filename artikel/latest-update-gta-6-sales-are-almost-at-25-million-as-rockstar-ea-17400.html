Now that GTA 6 is actually out and people the world over can finally play it, we'll start seeing figures for just how much money Rockstar's latest epic is raking in. One set of estimates place current revenue at north of 2.3 billion US dollars, and over 24.5 million copies sold.

These numbers come from Sensor Tower, an analytics firm that keeps a live database to track the returns for releases across the industry. Naturally, GTA 6 is getting a healthy amount of attention, and the site is primed with up-to-date amounts. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

According to the tool, Grand Theft Auto 6 has brought in around $2.36 billion thus far. The bulk of that money will have come from pre-orders, and as of now, the amount of copies sold on all platforms is thought to sit at 24.58 million. I don't think anyone would complain about seeing that kind of consumer response to their game. Sensor Tower provides a platform breakdown too, for where GTA 6 has sold more, and to nobody's surprise PS5 is thought to be in the lead, taking 75% of the copies shifted, whereas the Xbox Series X/S only make up for 25%.

According to recent reports, that's quite a disparity, but it's one we saw coming well before the game's release date hit. Tracking consistently placed PlayStation 5 sales ahead of the Xbox Series X/S. There are simply more of them out in the world, creating a significantly bigger install base, and Sony went quite hard in terms of partnering with Rockstar and general advertising.
