Brandy may have just delivered the theme song for Netflix’s A Different World reboot, but she might return to the studio to cut a new version of her Moesha theme song for a possible reboot!

During an interview on The Jennifer Hudson Show that aired Friday (Nov. 20), the Grammy-winning R&B icon reflected on her recent surprise appearance at Usher and Chris Brown’s R&B Tour, as well as a potential reboot of her classic ’90s coming-of-age sitcom, Moesha.

“I know I’ve said this in so many different places and so many different times,” Brandy began. “But Moesha can really be something in today’s time, especially since we’ve seen the success of [Netflix’s] A Different World [reboot]. I just really think that she could come back.”. (Related: Dividends Aren't What You Think They Are...)

Brandy starred in titular role of Moesha, which aired on UPN from 1996 to 2001. The beloved sitcom also starred Emmy-winning Abbott Elementary star Sheryl Lee Ralph and featured cameos from ’90s-defining Black megastars such as Usher and the late Kobe Bryant. (Related: Breaking: Democrat Hakeem Jeffries nominated to be firs...)

During Usher and Chris Brown’s R&B Tour stop at California’s SoFi Stadium on Sept. 26, Brandy made a surprise appearance during her former Moesha costar’s set for lively renditions of “Top of the World” and “I Wanna Be Down.” “It was so magical! I love Usher, I’m his biggest fan,” she gushed, later reminiscing on the playful vocal battles they waged backstage while on set. “And we worked together back in the day on Moesha. He was actually Moesha’s boyfriend, so we connected and the chemistry was unbelievable. Unmatched.”.

Her pop-out previewed the Nov. 10 announcement of her and Monica’s The Boy Is Mine Las Vegas Residency. The run will consist of 10 shows between May 29 and July 17 at Resorts World Las Vegas, marking the first Sin City residency for both Grammy-winning R&B superstars.

The dynamic duo’s Vegas takeover follows their blockbuster arena-headlining Boy Is Mine Tour — both promoted by The Black Promoters Collective — which earned $44.7 million from 350,000 tickets sold across 32 indicates, according to Billboard Boxscore. Both the tour and residency are named for the stars’ classic 1998 duet, which topped the Billboard Hot 100 for 13 weeks.

In a recent development, on Nov. 28, Brandy will star alongside her daughter Sy’Rai Smith in Lifetime’s new original holiday movie, Christmas Everyday: Married and Bright. The new film caps off a busy year for the mother-daughter duo; Brandy received her star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame in March, while Sy’Rai dropped her debut studio album, Cold War, in August.
