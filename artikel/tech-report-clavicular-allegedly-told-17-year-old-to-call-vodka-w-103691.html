Streamer Clavicular is accused of instructing a 17-year-old girl to lie about her age and pretend she was drinking water instead of vodka to avoid getting his Twitch livestream banned before an alleged sexual assault.

The 20-year-old influencer, whose real name is Braden Peters, has been indicted on two counts of rape and one count of providing alcohol to a minor in Massachusetts.

According to a police report detailed by Fox News, the allegations stem from an encounter on May 23, 2025, at his family’s home in Chatham, Cape Cod.

The teenager told investigators that she had connected with Peters through Instagram before he invited her to the property to create looksmaxxing content.

Peters allegedly arranged an Uber to bring her to the home, where he was livestreaming on Twitch.

According to recent reports, according to investigators, Peters knew the girl was 17 but instructed her to tell viewers she was 18. He also allegedly told her to refer to the vodka she was drinking as “water” to prevent his Twitch account or livestream from being banned.

The teenager claimed Peters supplied her with vodka throughout the evening, leaving her increasingly intoxicated.

Police subsequently reviewed footage from the Twitch broadcast, including a moment when the girl noted, “Wait, I’m so drunk though,” before the two kissed.

It has been revealed that she alleged that Peters later had sex with her while she was heavily intoxicated. She also told investigators that she woke up the following morning to Peters having sex with her without her consent.

On November 18, Boston 25 News reported that Peters had been indicted on two rape counts and one count of providing alcohol to a minor.
