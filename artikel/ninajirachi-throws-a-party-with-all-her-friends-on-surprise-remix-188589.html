Industry observers note that ninajirachi adores her computer more than almost anything — she noted as much on her eclectic debut album, I Love My Computer. But the Australian electronic artist and producer wanted to turn the dial up further. How could she max out all the love she has in her heart? With the help of some friends.

On Tuesday, Nov. 23, Ninajirachi surprised fans with I Love My Computer, All My Friends Are Inside It, a remix album that reimagines the original through collaborations with Wet Leg, Underscores, Frost Children, Jonny Chopps, Dylan Brady, Horsegiiirl, Genesis Owusu, Madeon, and more.

“Most of my time making music is spent alone, so hearing these songs reimagined by an amazing lineup of artists has been really inspiring and emotional,” Ninajirachi remarked. “For an album that was written about experiences I’ve had all on my own, I Love My Computer has connected me to people all around the world more than anything else ever has. It is hard to be eloquent about the time I’ve had with it.”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

It has been revealed that ninajirachi wrote her message from Mexico City, where she’d connected with even more people who she first crossed paths with through music online. “There is no time I’d rather be alive than right now. I’m so grateful to the artists for bringing so much of themselves to every upcoming version,” she said. “Some are new friends, some I’ve known since I released my first song as Ninajirachi. We’ve played highlights together and been in the studio together, but we all found each other’s music on the computer first.”.

Later this week, Ninajirachi will host two b2b sets with Porter Robinson in San Francisco. Earlier this month, Robinson released his own remix album, Smile! : D Plus+, which featured Ninajirachi alongside Underscores on an amped up newly introduced version of “Perfect Pinterest Garden” from his 2024 album Smile! : D. “[Underscores] and Nina just brought all of the life back into it,” Robinson told Rolling Stone about the track. “The added verses, the re-recorded parts, the way it’s kind of turned into an electro dance remix — it’s just brimming with life now.” He added, “Ninajirachi, Underscores, Frost Children, they’re forging their own path. They have a machete and they’re cutting through the wilderness and discovering something new.”.

Recent findings suggest that ninajirachi’s I Love My Computer, All My Friends Are Inside It collaborators echoed the sentiment. “Nina is an icon,” Genesis Owusu shared in a statement. “We’ve been mutual admirers for a while, so being able to start getting together creatively has been great. She remixed my track, LIFE KEEPS GOING at Triple J’s One Night Stand, and now I get to remix her London Song. Remix city over here. Hopefully the first of many.”.
