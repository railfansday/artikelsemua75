Bloomberg reported on November 23, 2026, in an account carried by The Verge, that Nothing CEO Carl Pei had demonstrated Ear Note earbuds with a microphone-equipped charging case for recording and AI transcription ahead of a launch planned for December 8, 2026.

It has been revealed that the system can capture audio through either the earbuds or the case, while a connected app manages recordings, transcripts, and AI-generated summaries. Pei noted Nothing had not settled on a price before the planned launch. (Related: Breaking: Porting fairseq wmt19 translation system to t...)

The microphones make the charging case a recording device in its own right, while a Talk button provides a direct way for users to capture their own speech. The earbuds will also support interactions with various AI agents, including a more basic voice-control system developed by Nothing, allowing users to engage in back-and-forth conversations. Nothing’s own system is described as the more basic option among those agents, so the earbuds are not limited to a single assistant.

Pei described an unnamed product in a separate post on X published on November 23; he did not name Ear Note in that post. He called it “the first of many products building a hardware context layer for AI” and said Nothing had made something people already wear and trust intelligent. He said it works in the background so users can stay in the moment, with more details promised for the same date. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

That statement connects the recording hardware to Nothing’s AI ambitions. Captured conversations provide material for transcripts and summaries, while agent support gives users a conversational route through the same earbuds. The approach uses an accessory people already wear as both the input and the interface, rather than requiring a separate device dedicated to recording.

The Verge placed the preview against earlier and competing products. Nothing’s Ear 3 already included recording capabilities and a case button, while the Ear 3A introduced an Audio Snapshot recording feature earlier in 2026. Anker’s Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro Max can also record directly to its charging case and generate AI transcripts and summaries in an app. The comparisons extended to Apple’s upcoming Watch feature and Amazon’s Bee wearable.

Bloomberg’s account also mentioned Nothing’s rumored AI smartwatch and smart-glasses aims had been delayed, while the company would pursue a more aggressive smartphone release schedule in 2027 than it did in 2026. That places the earbuds within a broader reshaping of Nothing’s device roadmap rather than an isolated accessories announcement.

Ear Note’s distinction lies in combining background context, AI agents, and summaries rather than in recording alone, which Nothing and its competitors have already offered. With pricing unresolved, the value of that added AI layer remains the central open question around the launch.
