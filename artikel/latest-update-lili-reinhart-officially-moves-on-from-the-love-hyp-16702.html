Sources indicate that like many of her Riverdale co-stars, Lili Reinhart has been busy since the soapy Archie Comics adaptation took its final bow in 2023. Now, she's eyeing a new role in an Amazon MGM Studios movie that the studio is hoping might be their answer to 2023's blockbuster Barbie.

Reinhart, who starred as wholesome Betty Cooper for seven seasons of the CW series, followed it up by starring in the mystery thriller American Sweatshop and the Mubi miniseries Hal & Harper. This year, she starred in The Love Hypothesis, a well-regarded romantic comedy with Tom Bateman, and the edgy horror film Forbidden Fruits. She's also been active behind the scenes, serving as an executive producer on American Sweatshop, Hal & Harper, and The Love Hypothesis.

Industry observers note that now, Reinhart is making her bid to play another blonde icon of the 20th century. Deadline reports that Reinhart is now circling the lead role in Polly Pocket, after previous star Lily Collins (Emily in Paris) dropped out due to a scheduling conflict; Reinhart has a prior relationship woth Prime Video, which released The Love Hypothesis. Collins, however, will remain onboard as an executive producer. The project will adapt the popular Mattel toy line of the 1990s: it consisted of a series of makeup compacts and other household objects that opened up into miniature dollhouses for the inch-high Polly Pocket figurines. It has previously been adapted into animated films and TV series, but this will be the franchise's first foray into live-action. It will also be the continuation of a relationship between Amazon and Mattel, after last summer's Masters of the Universe; the toy-based actioner disappointed at the box office, but has been a streaming smash.
