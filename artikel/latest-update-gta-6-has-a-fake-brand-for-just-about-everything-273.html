In a recent development, there's plenty to do between missions in Grand Theft Auto VI. Players can grab a burger, call a ride-share, or pull out their phones and scroll through social media. Spend a little time doing just about anything in Leonida and you'll come across a brand that looks recognizable...but different.

Industry observers note that making up brands is a longtime GTA habit. GTA V had Lifeinvader, a Facebook parody that Rockstar also rolled out as a real website 13 years ago, along with Sprunk soda, Burger Shot, and the Los Santos Customs auto shops. There was even Snapmatic, a phone camera players could use to take pictures around Los Santos. But a lot has changed since GTA V came out in 2013. Snapmatic has become an Instagram-style feed, and there's a ride-hailing app called Ryde Me. That's not all, though. There are specific brands for about everything, from the Arby's-style chain with a roughly 30,000-calorie combo meal to a Telemundo-style network with its own telenovela.

Here are some of the names to look for around Leonida, and what real-life chain they are inspired by.

According to recent reports, chacey's — Rockstar opens its extended gameplay preview with an ad for a roughly 30,000-calorie meal, otherwise known as the Prairie Sandwich Box Meal from Chacey's, Leonida’s answer to Arby's, complete with curly fries called Lasso Fries. The chain has locations in Southside and Salton, according to PowerPyx.

It has been revealed that hank's Waffles — For a roadside diner fix, there's Hank's Waffles. It’s one of 21 fast-food spots mapped around Leonida, alongside places like Pollo Diablo, Taco Bomb, and Peewees.

It has been revealed that sprunk — Rockstar's take on Sprite is back. Kotaku spotted a can of the lemon-lime soda in Jason's fridge in the gameplay preview.

According to recent reports, eCola — Jason keeps Rockstar's version of Coca-Cola in the fridge, too. The soda even got its own ads around Miami, complete with the slogan "Spread Love, Spread eCola.".

Sources indicate that fizzAzz — Fanta gets the GTA treatment with FizzAzz. Its logo pops up in a convenience-store scene in Rockstar’s extended gameplay preview.

24/7 — Leonida's version of 7-Eleven has a location in Key Lento and a drink called the Significant Chug. The Big Chug even got its own ad in Miami.

Dix — Rockstar's take on Dior comes with its own lipstick campaign: a close-up of glossy red lips with the slogan "These are Dix lips.".

It has been revealed that video Forever — Vice City still has a DVD rental store, at least for a little while. The shop in Rockstar’s extended gameplay preview is already having a closing sale. Realistic!

Sources indicate that WAINK — There's an app for car theft now. WAINK can scan a parked car and tell Jason or Lucia how much it’s worth and whether it has an alarm or a tracker.

It has been revealed that marbles — Leonida has a burger-and-milkshake chain that looks like a riff on Checkers. A Marbles takeout bag and a shake cup appeared in a scooter basket in one of Rockstar's screenshots, and there's a dine-in location in Key Lento.

According to recent reports, angstipan — Even the prescription drug ads get the GTA treatment. An ad for the fictional antidepressant demonstrates up in Rockstar's extended gameplay preview, and a billboard for it appears in the first trailer.

It has been revealed that weazel News — Rockstar's riff on Fox News pops up in the first trailer. The network's slogan, "Never Miss Information," is worth the mention.

Megamundo — Of course Leonida's version of Telemundo has its own telenovela. It’s called El Destino Extiende Sus Alas, and it even got an ad spot in Miami. Apparently it's a parody of the real-life telenovelas Pasión de Gavilanes and Yo soy Betty. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Industry observers note that iFruit — Rockstar's iPhone parody has its own take on Apple's "Shot on iPhone" ads. In one Miami ad, a woman snaps a selfie in front of a burning car.

Snapmatic — Remember the camera app from GTA V? It's back with an Instagram-style feed, so now there’s something to scroll through on your phone in Vice City, too.

Ryde Me — Leonida has its own version of Uber and Lyft. Open Ryde Me on the in-game phone to call a car and fast-travel.

In a recent development, pißwasser — The beer from GTA IV and GTA V is back on store shelves in Rockstar's gameplay footage. A Florida brewery also filed labels for Pißwasser and another beer from the game, Patriot.
