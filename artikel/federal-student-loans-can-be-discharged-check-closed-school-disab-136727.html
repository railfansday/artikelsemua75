Industry observers note that your federal student loans may be canceled if your school closed while you were enrolled, misled you, or signed you up for a loan you weren’t eligible for, or if you have a total and permanent disability. Check which of these discharges fits you and apply free through StudentAid.gov before your next payment.

Sources indicate that years of payments aren’t the only way out of federal student loans. The law also cancels them through discharges, and most happen only after someone asks.

Every application is free, through StudentAid.gov or your loan servicer, so never pay a manufacturer to apply. Say your for-profit college closed two months before graduation and you never enrolled elsewhere. A closed school discharge would cancel your remaining $14,000 and refund what you’ve paid.
