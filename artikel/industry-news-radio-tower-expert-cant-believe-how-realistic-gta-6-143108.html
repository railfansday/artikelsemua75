Grand Theft Auto 6 is a massive game, yes, but if you dig into everything inside of it, you’ll swiftly discover that nearly every inch of this major world is filled with incredibly realistic details. Case in point: a content creator who has also worked on large communication towers was recently stunned by GTA 6′s attention to detail. Folks, we got Erathed tower legs. Content creator MrMcg92, who primarily plays first-person shooters and military sims, is also someone who has, in real life, worked on and climbed large communication and radio towers. So whenever he comes across a radio tower in a video game, he takes some time to do a “Tower Inspection.” He’s looked at towers in Battlefield, Days Gone, and Far Cry. And while all of these towers had some nice details, they often made mistakes or were missing notable bits MrMcg92 would expect to find on a real comms tower. That wasn’t the case when he did a tower inspection in GTA 6. Instead, he was flabbergasted and impressed by the tower’s hyper-realistic details. He then spotted a red power cable running up the middle of the tower and claimed that if there was an aircraft warning light at the top, he would get a “stiffy.”Â As he climbed the tower, he continued to be impressed by how Rockstar’s artists included every tiny detail that should be found on a real-life tower like this.

“Oh, boys, we got proper AA clamps on the mount points. Tails running out the bottom of the panels to the RRU,” pointed out the creator on his climb up the tower.
