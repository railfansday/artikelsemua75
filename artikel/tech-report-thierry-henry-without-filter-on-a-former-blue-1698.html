Whether at Arsenal or with the French team, Thierry Henry and Sylvain Wiltord shared great moments. But if the 1998 world champion has remained in the spotlight, notably as coach of the Espoirs tricolores at the Paris 2024 Olympic Games and as a consultant for CBS Sports, his former teammate has somewhat disappeared from the radar.

Industry observers note that however, the former striker trained at Stade Rennais was a player like few others, claims “Titi”. “People forget… Sylvain, he’s in a training team with you, he doesn’t lose. You are with him, you win”he recalls in the podcast Kampo. For him, the ex-Gunner may have suffered from an image that was completely out of step with what he really was. According to recent reports, “There were always lots of things that came out about Sylvain, and no one ever said… He was always with his little bottle of water, respectful of the collective, where he is, working, setting goals. Everywhere he went, he either won or was close to winning, or he was the most vital player.”recalls Thierry Henry at the microphone of journalist Smaïl Bouabdellah.

It has been revealed that “There are players like that who haven’t always had the recognition they deserve. He was decisive in everything. Always happy, funny. Afterwards, like everyone else, he didn’t really like talking to the press, but frankly… There are guys like that. It’s putting yourself at the service of the collective”concludes the former Arsenal idol, winner with Sylvain Wiltord of Euro 2000, two Premier League crowns and two FA Cup titles.
