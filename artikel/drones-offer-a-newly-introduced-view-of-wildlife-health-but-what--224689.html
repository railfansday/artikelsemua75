Drones offer a newly introduced view of wildlife health. But what are they missing?

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (60 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that drones are transforming how scientists study wildlife, allowing researchers to photograph and measure animals in their natural environments, monitor larger populations and collect observations with less disturbance. While a photograph can reveal changes in an animal's size and shape, it cannot confidently tell researchers what those changes mean for its health.
