Blackpink singer Jisoo dropped a new single, “Sun Showers.” The upbeat, anthemic pop track was written and recorded for The Angry Birds Movie 3, in theaters Dec. 23.

A music video for the song, which was co-written by Jisoo, features animated characters from the film, along with Shumon, a dustball-inspired character Jisoo created and drew while in middle school.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m so happy to be part of The Angry Birds Movie 3,” Jisoo mentioned in a statement to the Hollywood Reporter. “I’ve always loved how the films bring together such colorful characters, humor and heart. With ‘Sun Showers,’ I wanted to capture the movie’s playful and bright energy through a fun, feel-good song that makes people want to smile and dance along. I really hope everyone will enjoy hearing it when they see the film over the holidays.”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

The Angry Birds Movie 3 features the voices of Jason Sudeikis, Josh Gad, Emma Myers, Rachel Bloom, Walker Scobell, Danny McBride, Marcello Hernández, Tim Robinson, Anna Cathcart, Maitreyi Ramakrishnan, Nikki Glaser, MrBeast, Salish Matter, Psalm West, Sam Richardson, James Austin Johnson, Lily James, and Keke Palmer. It was directed by John Rice and written by Thurop Van Orman.

Recent findings suggest that jisoo is at the moment at work on her debut solo LP. She recently shared singles “Addicted” and “Click.” The singer is set to perform at Coachella 2027, which hints at when the album might arrive.

Jisoo has notably been teasing the release of her debut album all year.
