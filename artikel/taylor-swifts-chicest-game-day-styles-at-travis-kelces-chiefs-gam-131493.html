Taylor Swift’s game day outfits are the definition of trendy and festive.

Swift started attending Kansas City Chiefs games in September 2023 amid her then-budding romance with tight end Travis Kelce. From official Chiefs gear to miniskirts and leather jackets, Swift has been supporting her beau in style. (Swift and Kelce said “I do” in July 2026.).

At the Chiefs vs. Los Angeles Chargers game on October 22, Swift was a must-see in a red crewneck featuring the team’s logo and name. She paired the outerwear with a pleated black skirt, dainty jewelry, her classic red lip and straightened hair. Jason then asked if he meant his football jersey number or his phone number, to which Travis replied, “You know which one.”.
