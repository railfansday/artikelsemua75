Jason Segel (Shrinking, The Muppets, Forgetting Sarah Marshall) has joined the Oscar-qualified short film Meeting Your Maker as an executive producer.Written and directed by Gregory J.M. Kasunich, the heartfelt 18-minute dark comedy film follows Otis, a beloved children’s TV puppet who, following a suicide attempt, awakens in a workshop and must confront his creator. Otis must grapple with questions of identity, purpose, and what it means to be alive.The film stars Jonathan Kite (2 Broke Girls) alongside Thomas Lennon, Amanda Maddock, Jason and Randy Sklar, Lenny Jacobson, Anna Chazelle, Kayla Radomski, Dylan Playfair, Dylan Mooney, Victor Yerrid and Darcy Rose Byrnes. The 18-minute short was produced by Derek Tramont and Lee Sacks.Segel has explored similar emotional territory throughout his career, from his work writing and starring in The Muppets to his performance in the Apple TV+ hit series Shrinking. Blending practical puppetry, surreal production design, and an ensemble of comedic performers, Meeting Your Maker explores the complicated relationship between artists and their creations, asking what happens when the character you’ve spent your life bringing to life begins to question their own existence. In a recent development, “There’s something really special about Meeting Your Maker,” stated Segel. “It has this wonderful balance of comedy and heartbreak, this sort of magical melancholy that I’ve always been drawn to. It makes you laugh, and then, almost without realizing it, you find yourself thinking about some pretty big questions like who we are, what gives our lives meaning, and what happens when we lose sight of our purpose.” Added Segel: “I’ve always believed that puppetry has this incredible ability to make us feel things we might otherwise be afraid to confront. There’s something about that innocence and imagination that allows us to be a little more honest with ourselves. And I think that’s especially important right now, when so many people are struggling to feel seen or connected. What Gregory has created is funny, strange, and ultimately hopeful. It reminds us that even in our darkest moments, there’s still room for laughter, connection, and magic. I was really moved by it, and I’m proud to come aboard as an executive producer.”. It has been revealed that “Jason’s work has always resonated with me; he proves that comedy can be ridiculously absurd and profoundly moving all at once,” stated Kasunich. “This is a deeply personal film about identity, purpose, and finding a reason to keep going. Having someone whose work, from puppets to mental health, to dark comedy, has inspired so much of my own storytelling is incredibly meaningful.”.

The film qualified for the Oscars 2027 through its best narrative short film award win at the Dallas International Film Festival and has since garnered additional festival honors.

Meeting Your Maker takes audiences to the set of Yappy Hour, a scrappy children’s TV show approaching its 300th episode. Otis, one of its beloved puppet stars, is unraveling. When the show is renewed for three more years, he spirals into despair, wanders the streets of Los Angeles and throws himself off a bridge. That’s where the journey really begins, though.
