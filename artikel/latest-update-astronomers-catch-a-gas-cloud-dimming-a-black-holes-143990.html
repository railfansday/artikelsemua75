Latest Update: Astronomers catch a gas cloud dimming a black hole's glow for weeks

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (55 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that some active galactic nuclei (AGNs)—galaxies that are powered by actively feeding black holes—undergo rapid changes in their appearance, which manifest as a change in their spectral properties. These are called "changing-look" AGNs. Their appearance changes over time, getting brighter or dimmer, or losing or gaining certain spectral features.
