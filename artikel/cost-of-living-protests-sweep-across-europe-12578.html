Cost of living protests sweep across Europe

🌐 Source: Co • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (76 words, ⚡ Quick Read)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Protests took place across European cities on Friday as marches were held over the cost of living and other issues, including housing shortages and underfunding in education.
  • Demonstrations took place in Germany, France, Croatia, Italy, the United Kingdom and elsewhere, attracting a lot of young adults.
  • The BBC's Europe correspondent, Nick Beake, has been reporting from Rome, where thousands of people took part in a march organised by Italy's main trade union.
