League Two footballer Isiah Noel-Williams has been charged with rape.

Noel-Williams, who plays for League Two side Barnet, has been charged with three counts of rape and one count of sexual assault by penetration, Hertfordshire Police said.

The 23-year-old, of High Oaks, St Albans, was remanded in custody following a hearing at Hatfield Magistrates' Court on Saturday.

In a statement, the club remarked it was "co-operating fully with authorities" and would determine further action "in due course".
