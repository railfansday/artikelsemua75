David Storch had to make a significant decision at Sheffield Wednesday recently by replacing Henrik Pedersen with Dave Challinor, which is paying off as he has sent an update to the "unreal" Owls fans.

The goodwill towards David Storch and Arise Capital remains in place but they had to make the brave choice to sack Henrik Pedersen, with Dave Challinor coming in and winning his first two league games in charge. (Related: A lifelong brawler is coming to Congress. Does he have ...)

Sheffield Wednesday are firmly in the automatic promotion hunt, let alone the play-off picture, following a 2-1 win away to South Yorkshire rivals Barnsley at Oakwell on Friday night. With other teams in the top half dropping points, they are well-placed to kick on.

It has been revealed that speaking after the Barnsley game on Saturday, Storch took to X (formerly Twitter) to outline his thoughts. He stated: "love all things Wednesday! @swfc Fans last night were unreal… we have the best supporters anywhere.

"3 points away away away!! The energy, passion, devotion… You could feel it all the way to Chicago. Love you guys! Missing the live action so much. Hope to see you guys soon!

"And thanks so much for all the well wishes. Getting a little stronger everyday! #WAWAW". (Related: Grandma who accidentally invited a stranger to Thanksgi...)

Storch is at the moment recovering from back surgery, but he will be delighted that his first huge footballing decision has paid off already, with Storch reuniting Simon Wilson with Challinor in the process.

It could prove to be a masterstroke for Sheffield Wednesday, given the pair's previous working relationship and the manager's exceptional record in the EFL. Challinor has repeatedly demonstrated an ability to build winning teams, secure promotions, and get the best out of his players, making him an outstanding appointment for a club with Wednesday's stature and resources.

It has been revealed that the pair enjoyed considerable success at Stockport County, taking them from the National League to League One, winning the National League title in 2021/22 and the League Two title in 2023/24. Challinor also boasts seven promotions across his managerial career, having previously enjoyed success with AFC Fylde and Hartlepool United.

His Stockport side narrowly missed out on the Championship in consecutive League One play-off campaigns, losing to Bolton at Wembley in 2025/26 after finishing third. Wednesday should now have the ingredients to challenge for automatic promotion — and anything less than a serious push would be disappointing.

Many of the Sheffield Wednesday fans responded on social media to his upgrade, with one replying: "Delighted to hear you're recovering well after your surgery. You made a great decision bringing in Challinor. A proper football man that'll help build us a club with identity. Look after yourself ☺️".

Another Sheffield Wednesday supporter added: "I have no nails left watching that last night! 😂 Fans were amazing could hear them so much on the TV. Can't wait to see them play at Hillsborough on Saturday! Get well soon Mr Storch. Positive vibes for you and the owls 🦉 💙 🤍".

Industry observers note that A further reply from a Sheffield Wednesday fan praised another aspect which has improved at Hillsborough, with one Owl stating: "Fab night at Hirsty’s social last night at the watchalong . Brilliant idea from the club".
