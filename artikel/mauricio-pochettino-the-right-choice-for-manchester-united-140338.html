Recent findings suggest that manchester United have made their choice, it is Mauricio Pochettino they want to see on their sidelines to rectify the situation, which has gone from disappointing to catastrophic with an 18th place in the Premier League. However, there were many free coaches with excellent reputations, from Antonio Conte to Didier Deschamps via Eddie Howe, but the MU board finally decided to conclude with Pochettino, around whom it has rotated on numerous occasions over the last ten years. In May 2016 after the dismissal of Van Gaal, in December 2018 after that of Mourinho, in April 2022 before the appointment of Erik Ten Hag and in October 2024 after his dismissal.

After so many failed meetings, this seems to be the right one for Mauricio Pochettino, who has never hidden his admiration for Manchester United and its legendary coach Sir Alex Ferguson. But is this the right choice? “Appointing Pochettino would fall somewhere between the misguided sentimentality of turning to fan favorites with little or no track record of success, such as Carrick and Ole Gunnar Solskjaer, and the easy way out of bringing in coaches with a proven track record, such as Van Gaal and Mourinho.”attempts to decipher the BBC.

Sources indicate that for us, the French public, Mauricio Pochettino mainly boils down to two things: his long time on the Tottenham bench with a Champions League campaign ending in the final in 2019 and his shortest stay on the PSG bench, far from memorable, both in terms of trophies and play. But in England, the Argentine coach maintains a hell of a rating, thanks to his successful stints at Southampton and Tottenham, eclipsing his adventure of barely one year on the Chelsea bench in 2023-2024, most recent.

Sources indicate that to read the English press, Mauricio Pochettino is a builder, whose teams are distinguished by a great riot of energy in midfield and produces attacking football that is pleasant to watch play. He also knows how to help his players progress (Harry Kane and Son at Tottenham), exploiting young talents to highlight them (Cole Palmer at Chelsea). We can contrast this idyllic picture with some flaws which could be prohibitive in many clubs. Starting with the fact that he has not won a single trophy, except during his time at PSG for a year and a half (a Ligue 1, a Coupe de France and a Champions Trophy). PSG followers sadly remember its soothing press conferences, filled with ready-made phrases and a total lack of transparency, and the poor quality of the matches, in content. As if the Argentinian didn’t really want to be there, he who promptly dreamed of a return to the Premier League. He had it with Chelsea, but he stood out for some attacks on recruitment, admittedly eccentric, from the new management, and an average quality of play. He never knew how to get rid of his image as Tottenham coach among Blues supporters, who were not really convinced by his time there.

Finally, his exile at the head of the American selection from 2024 to November 2026 will have allowed him to be forgotten a little, to return with a form of freshness. He will need it to face the heated public at Old Trafford, burned by several years of questionable choices, in the recruitment of players and coaches. “Mauricio Pochettino and Manchester United: a poisoned chalice or a dream renovation job? », asks The Athletic in an article dedicated to Pochettino’s challenge. Perhaps marriage comes at the right time for both parties, but the challenge proves daunting.
