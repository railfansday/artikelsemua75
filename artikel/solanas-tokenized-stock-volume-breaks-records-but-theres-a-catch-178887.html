In a recent development, solana [SOL] just had a record week for tokenized stocks. And yet, Internet Computer [ICP] pulled ahead on speed.

Here’s a closer look at both platforms.

In a recent development, between 16th and 22nd November, spot trading volume for tokenized equities on Solana DEXs came in at about $1.84 billion. That beats… literally every week before this one. (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

This includes the $1.4 billion spike, back in late June.

Solana also grabbed a bigger slice of the pie. Its share of tokenized equity volume across all chains rose to 43%. That’s the highest its ever been since June.

Industry observers note that should this take you by surprise? No. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

For most of last year, its weekly volume barely registered on the chart. It started picking up early this year, before taking off from September. After that, many weeks passed the $1 billion-mark.

In a recent development, last week, Internet Computer averaged 5,053 transactions per second (TPS), according to Chainspect. Solana managed about 1,766 over the same timeframe.

Sources indicate that ICP is at about 2.9x Solana’s throughput. It’s not even a one-off flex. In fact, ICP is at the top of Chainspect’s rankings for average weekly throughput!

Sources indicate that there’s more going on too. Developer activity on ICP reportedly picked up this week, implying that builders have been watching. TPS tells you how many transactions a network is handling, not how much money is moving through them.

Simply put, while Solana’s $1.84 billion week is about value, and ICP’s has to do with volume of activity.

SOL and ICP are both down 40% since early October.

Recent findings suggest that since 5th October, SOL has dropped by about 37% on the charts while ICP has fallen by 40.8%. Both of these tokens have mostly moved in lockstep, falling lower throughout November.

There’s just the slightest flicker of life though. SOL hiked by 8.5% over the last 36 hours while ICP bounced off the low of the 21st.

Still, one green day does not make a recovery. Both tokens need buyers, more of them.
