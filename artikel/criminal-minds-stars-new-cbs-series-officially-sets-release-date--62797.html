It has been revealed that for 15 seasons, Matthew Gray Gubler captured viewers on CBS' hit crime procedural Criminal Minds, portraying Dr. Spencer Reid from 2005 to 2020. While the script laid out a genius with an IQ of 187 and multiple PhDs, Gubler transformed Reid into a beloved television icon by infusing the role with his own quirks and warmth, even directing twelve episodes of the series. Defying the typical tough-guy detective trope, the actor deliberately styled Reid with sweater vests, mismatched socks, and ever-changing hair, while adding improvised nervous habits like humming and rambling off-script statistical tangents.

Industry observers note that viewers also connected with the profound emotional vulnerability Gubler brought to Reid’s darkest traumas, from addiction to his mother's dementia, as well as his genuine off-screen camaraderie with the cast that made Reid the Behavioral Analysis Unit's endearing little brother. Criminal Minds would be revived in 2022 for an era subtitled Evolution, but Gubler did not return as a series regular, sitting out the first two seasons due to scheduling conflicts. The actor returned for a guest appearance in 2025, but with Evolution set to conclude soon, it's unclear whether viewers will see Dr. Reid again. One reason Gray Gubler did not return was that CBS was developing a new procedural, and the actor was being eyed to star. Titled Einstein, the drama was picked up as a series and now has a premiere date. The series has also cast another actor who played a fanfavorite quirky character, Tony Shalhoub. He starred on Monk for eight seasons as Adrian Monk, a brilliant detective with OCD. Monk was created by Andy Breckman and executive produced by Randy Zink, both of whom also serve as executive producers on Einstein. The series will air on Fridays at 8:00 p.m. ET/PT on CBS, beginning with its series premiere on February 12, 2027.
