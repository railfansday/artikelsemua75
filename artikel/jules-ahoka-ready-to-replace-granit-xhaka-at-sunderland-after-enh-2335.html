Jules Ahoka could end up ousting Granit Xhaka from the Sunderland starting eleven long-term as the captain faces a while longer on the sidelines.

Sunderland captain Xhaka was closely linked with a move away from the club in the summer window. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

He was ultimately forced to publicly commit his future to the Black Cats after Chelsea made a move for his services.

Xhaka has struggled to have a similar impact on Regis Le Bris' side in this campaign compared to what he did last term.

He is currently sidelined for the foreseeable future and could soon have his place in the team taken by budding youngster Ahoka.

The 20-year-old has been a revelation in his fleeting appearances this season and looks set to become a real star for the Wearside club.

Xhaka was forced off injured in Sunderland's final game before the international break against Chelsea.

Le Bris has confirmed this week that the 34-year-old remains sidelined for the next month as he continues to recover from his calf issue.

Ahoka came on for him in the fourth minute against the Blues and went on to make the most defensive contributions (14) and win the joint-most ground duels (6) of any player on the pitch.

The summer signing has already hugely impressed in the Europa League, with standout stats among midfielders for duels won percentage (59.1), aerial duels won per 90 (2), touches in the opposition box per 90 (3.2) and recoveries (6) per 90 in his three starts so far.

Xhaka's levels seem to have dropped off so far in this campaign, and he could well find it tougher than ever to return to the Sunderland team when he is back fit, with Ahoka clearly primed to become a key man under Le Bris in the coming weeks.

Ahoka was named Sunderland's official Player of the Match for his star role in their vital 1-0 win over Chelsea last time out.

Le Bris will need to rely on him for the next few league games, and clearly feels like he is ready to step up after praising him following the Blues clash.

He said: "[He was] impressive. Jules had some minutes in the Carabao Cup, in the Europa League as well, and needed experience in the few games he played to adapt, to connect with his teammates. He has a strong physicality, a good understanding of the game, and is calm under pressure. Really good performance. (Related: Understanding BigBird's Block Sparse Attention...)

According to recent reports, "He wants to learn, he wants to understand the mistakes. He’s got significant attributes, he’s really young. In Sunderland, we believe that we can have experienced players on one side, and on the other develop players.”.
