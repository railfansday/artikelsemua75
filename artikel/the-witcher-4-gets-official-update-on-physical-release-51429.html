The Witcher 4 Gets Official Update on Physical Release

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

CD Projekt has confirmed that a physical version of The Witcher 4 will be released, even though the RPG is slated to launch after Sony Interactive Entertainment intends to stops producing physical games for PlayStation 5. Whether The Witcher 4 will be released on a physical disc is another matter entirely.

Sony aims to end the production of game discs for PlayStation consoles in January 2028. The Witcher 4 is the highest-profile AAA game currently slated to launch that year, and it comes from a studio that enjoys releasing its games physically. In an interview with The Game Business from August, CD Projekt joint CEO Michał Nowakowski confirmed that CD Projekt would still like to release a physical version of The Witcher 4. (Related: Tech Report: Course Launch Community Event...)

"We’re massive fans of people having access to tangible collectible things. We’re nerds ourselves, and we like that. We always supported that," Nowakowski explained. "So, whatever is the case, we’re going to be for sure having a box. We’re going to have a box that’s going to have some feelies in it. Something you’re going to be wanting to open up and enjoy.". (Related: Latest Update: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

That stated, Nowakowski said he's unsure whether the game itself will be on a disc, as CD Projekt has no control over what Sony decides to manufacture. "We know what PlayStation has announced. And we have no influence on the policies of the first parties," he admitted. "The discs for the first parties, including PlayStation, have to be manufactured in their own factories. So, if they stop manufacturing indeed by 2028, then yes [we won’t have a disc].".

In a recent development, although Nowakowski went on to tell The Game Business that he has "no influence" over whether it'll even be possible to print discs for The Witcher 4, it's good that CD Projekt is committing to a physical release regardless. That said, it's clear that PlayStation needs to better explain its aims to developers, some of whom are clearly still expressing demand for physical discs.

Industry observers note that the Witcher 4 will be released on PS5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X sometime in 2028. Before then, fans of the series can look forward to a current-generation remaster of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt on Sept. 29 and the Songs of the Past expansion for that game sometime in 2027.
