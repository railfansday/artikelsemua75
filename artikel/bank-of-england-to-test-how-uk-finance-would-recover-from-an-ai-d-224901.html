According to recent reports, the Bank of England will use a frontier AI scenario in its 2027 operational stress test to see how a disruption involving advanced AI could affect UK finance. One question is whether firms’ recovery plans would work together if several institutions faced disruption at once. The voluntary, exploratory exercise will examine both individual firms and the wider sector.

Industry observers note that the Bank set out the proposal for its Cyber and Operational Resilience Stress Test in its Nov. 26 Financial Stability Report. It is discussing possible scenarios with firms, industry groups and technical experts. The Bank plans to invite suitable participants for testing in summer 2027 and publish findings on common themes in the fourth quarter. In its wider resilience work, the Bank is examining whether firms’ workarounds can collectively sustain a minimum level of activity in retail and wholesale payments and sterling interest-rate markets. In those markets, a priority is keeping settlement of UK government bond trades running. That work illustrates the stakes of joint recovery while the Bank weighs scenarios for the AI exercise.

Industry observers note that the Bank notes a prolonged interruption to retail payments could leave households and businesses unable to make or receive essential payments. In wholesale payments, disruption could tie up cash and collateral and leave firms unsure when incoming payments will arrive, putting pressure on other obligations.

Industry observers note that an October survey by the Bank and the Financial Conduct Authority indicates where firms already rely on the same providers. The three largest cloud and computing providers accounted for 79% of reported relationships in that category. The three largest providers of model access and hosting accounted for 54% of reported relationships in theirs.

Industry observers note that the survey also found that adoption of AI agents was widespread among respondents and broad autonomy was rare. Among firms that answered the respective questions in the full survey, 80% noted they had deployed AI agents and 2% said they had deployed agents with broad autonomy.

The Bank’s stress-test program includes a separate 2026 exercise on disruption involving a cloud provider and third parties, with findings planned for summer 2027.
