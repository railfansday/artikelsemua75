We get it, Dario - you don't want people calling out sick to play.

Well, it’s official: Anthropic will become the first publicly-traded frontier AI lab in history come the opening of markets on Thursday.

The firm kicked off its IPO roadshow last week, announcing plans to offer 825 million shares with an IPO price between $70 and $78 a share when sales begin Thursday.

The company's most recent SEC filing cites that price range but allows for the possibility of a higher price, which should be determined Wednesday night. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

In a recent development, late on Wednesday, Bloomberg reported that Anthropic would price at $83, citing people familiar. Whatever the case, Anthropic is likely to be valued at more than $1 trillion. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Anthropic’s debutante ball has been on the horizon for a while, and documents leaked in late September made it all the more inevitable as the firm’s finances revved up and it sought to capture the momentum of its best quarter to date before things start to tail off.

The organization revised its IPO documents on November 2, warning that the 12x growth it was touting was unlikely to continue in the long term due to compute infrastructure investments to the tune of nearly $900 billion it was on the hook for over the next few years.

It has been revealed that "In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, our revenue was $33.2 billion, representing a year-over-year growth rate of 1,206%," the revised prospectus reads. "We do not expect our revenue will continue to grow at comparable rates, and we cannot assure you that it will continue to grow at all.".

It has been revealed that it’s possible, in other words, that Anthropic may never be a money maker despite the hype and its position as the first mover. The IPO prospectus is short on answers to critical questions and leaves a lot unanswered - like, for example, whether it’s going to actually be able to pay its bills.

"Our ability to meet the substantial and growing financial obligations of these requirements to secure compute depends on our ability to generate significant revenue growth," the updated prospectus admits.

Investors don’t seem to care right now, though.

According to recent reports, anthropic already has compute commitments amounting to roughly $70.7 billion in the next 12 months. That’s just shy of its $81 billion run-rate as of October 31.

Expect more hot air to be injected tomorrow, adding additional surface tension and the possibility of even more losses if, and when, this monster pops. ®.
