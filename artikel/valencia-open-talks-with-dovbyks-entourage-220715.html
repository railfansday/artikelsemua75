In a recent development, now Valencia are serious and considering signing Dovbyk. One of the issues to be resolved is his €3 million net salary, of which €2.5 million is paid by the Rossoblu. The Spanish club is expected to pay half.

According to Il Resto del Carlino, recent news has revealed direct contact between the Spanish club and the Ukrainian player, who has reportedly expressed interest in a return to La Liga.

The 1997-born player has left a lasting impression, having scored 24 goals for Girona. Valencia has initiated initial discussions with Roma to understand potential terms and costs: approval will be needed from Bologna, which will pay Dovbyk €2.5 million of his €3 million net salary.
