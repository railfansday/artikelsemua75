Industry News: Texas’ population boom is opening doors for new real estate agents

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that few states are changing as quickly as Texas. It added more people than any other state between 2024 and 2025, and builders there got the green light for more fresh homes in 2025 than anywhere else in the country. More people and more houses mean more deals to be done, and more room for new agents to get in on them.

Texas’ population has passed 31 million, and the suburbs surrounding Dallas, Houston, Austin and San Antonio are stretching farther and farther out. Competition has cooled compared with the pandemic boom, yet the demand behind the market continues to climb.

Sources indicate that expensive markets with little buildable land work differently. In Texas, the business depends on sheer size, people relocating and a pipeline of newly built homes. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Recent findings suggest that over the 12 months ending July 2025, Texas grew by 391,243 people, a bigger gain than any other state posted. The population now sits near 31.7 million, 8.8% higher than in 2020.

It has been revealed that each newcomer is a future tenant, homebuyer or seller, and many will start households of their own. That means agents don’t have to count on climbing prices for opportunity. The bigger driver is people relocating and settling in for the long haul.

Raw population counts miss how often Texans relocate. In 2024, the state accounted for 7.8% of every move between states, a share topped only by Florida. Meanwhile, 3.4 million residents changed addresses without leaving Texas, the highest in-state figure in the nation.

It has been revealed that more newcomers still come from California than from anywhere else. Once all the arrivals and departures between states are netted out, Texas came out roughly 73,000 residents ahead.

All that activity is what keeps agents busy. A client arriving from out of state and one moving a few miles away both need help, which gives relocation-focused agents a reliable stream of business in and around the big metros.

It has been revealed that look at every U.S. city with 20,000 or more residents, and the five growing fastest are all in Texas. Fulshear, outside Houston, is the only one that isn’t in the Dallas-Fort Worth orbit.

Industry observers note that austin hit 1 million residents that same year, becoming the fifth Texas city of that size alongside Houston, San Antonio, Dallas and Fort Worth. For latest real estate agents, though, the bigger openings are in suburbs, master-planned developments and up-and-coming markets, not city centers. Recent findings suggest that no state approved permits for more new homes in 2025 than Texas, which authorized 210,217 units. Dallas and Houston ranked first and second among all U.S. metros, with Austin seventh.

All three metros issued fewer permits than in 2024, yet each is still well ahead of most of the nation. Whole subdivisions are under construction right now, and that work supports agents who sell new builds, partner with builders, focus on master-planned communities, help relocating families or guide people buying their first home.

Statewide active listings were up 2.8% in Q3 2026, and 18 of Texas’ 26 metros saw inventory increase. The median price across the state dipped 0.4% to $333,628, even as 15 metros posted price gains and 12 recorded more sales.

That extra selection lets buyers shop around. Sellers, meanwhile, are counting on their agents to set a smart price, present the home well, get it seen and negotiate effectively.

Among Texas REALTORS® surveyed in 2026, 69% said their buyers faced no competing offers, compared with 57% a year earlier. Seventy-one percent described buyers’ search timelines as ample or reasonable, and only 6% blamed a stalled search on thin inventory.

That changes what clients need from an agent. Outbidding the competition matters less than helping buyers choose well. The agents who stand out have deep neighborhood knowledge, can lay out what different homes will really cost each month and know how to get the most out of builder incentives.

Being a trusted advisor counts for more than processing forms.

International purchasers bought roughly 7,780 Texas homes from April 2025 through March 2026, spending a combined $4 billion. Texas captured 12% of the nation’s international homebuyers over that stretch.

According to recent reports, buyers from Mexico made up the biggest group, with India, China, Nigeria and the U.K. rounding out the top five. More than half, 58%, settled in the suburbs. Agents comfortable with overseas relocations and cross-border transactions have an expanding specialty to tap into, and much of it is in suburban markets.

Texas leaves plenty of room to shape your own business. One agent might become the go-to expert in a single booming suburb. Another might focus on fresh construction where builders are most active, or on families arriving from out of state or relocating within Texas.

Other specialties are open too, from international clients to first-time buyers, move-up buyers and rental investors. You also get to decide whether you’d rather work in a large metro or one of the state’s smaller fast-growing towns.

Whichever direction you choose, expect paychecks to be inconsistent at first. Every specialty depends on regular lead generation and on building relationships over time.

Sources indicate that texas requires aspiring agents to finish 180 hours of qualifying education before they can be licensed. Colibri Real Estate delivers those courses online, so you can fit your studies around the job you already have.

If you choose a package with exam prep, you’ll also get practice exams and live sessions where instructors answer your questions, all aimed at getting you ready to pass.

According to recent reports, building a real estate career in Texas takes effort, and your first years will be spent generating leads and growing your business. Still, if you’re ready to do that work, the conditions are strong: millions of residents relocating, suburbs springing up and homebuilding happening on a massive scale.

It has been revealed that latest households and new housing markets keep appearing across Texas. For agents committed to the work, there’s plenty of room to grow alongside them.
