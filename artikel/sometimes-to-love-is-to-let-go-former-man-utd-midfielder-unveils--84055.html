Former Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has confirmed his retirement from professional football at the age of 38, bringing an end to a beautiful career of around 20 years.

Sources indicate that the Spaniard went on to win one of the biggest trophies in professional football. The former Manchester United midfielder confirmed his decision on Wednesday after ending his playing career with Australian side Melbourne Victory. Industry observers note that mata enjoyed a memorable spell at Manchester United after arriving from Chelsea in January 2014. He spent more than eight years at Old Trafford, making 285 appearances, scoring 51 goals and winning four trophies. (Related: Industry News: Take-Two CEO is shutting down questions ...)

His first trophy with Manchester United came in the 2016 FA Cup final against Crystal Palace. He also helped the club win the Community Shield, League Cup and Europa League during the 2016/17 campaign.

Industry observers note that before moving to Manchester, Juan Mata had established himself as one of Chelsea’s most creative players. He won the club’s Player of the Year award in both of his first two full seasons at Stamford Bridge before eventually joining United as David Moyes’ marquee signing.

Sources indicate that mata probably had the biggest moments of his career in the Spain shirt. He earned 41 international caps and scored 10 goals, becoming part of the most talented Spanish team in history. He went on to win the 2010 FIFA World Cup and Euro 2012 with Spain while etching his name in Spanish football history with a goal in Spain’s 4-0 victory over Italy in the European Championship final.

After leaving Manchester United in 2022, the midfielder continued his career with Galatasaray, Vissel Kobe and Western Sydney Wanderers before joining Melbourne Victory in September 2025.

Recent findings suggest that his final season in Australia was an impressive one. Mata registered five goals and 13 assists, won the Johnny Warren Medal and earned a place in the A-League Team of the Season.

Reflecting on his decision, Mata spoke about the reason that kept him playing for so many years.

“I was able to keep going for so many years because of the sheer joy of playing,” he remarked.

He also made it clear that retirement would not mean walking away from football altogether.

Sources indicate that “Football will never leave my life. There are so many ways to continue loving the game.”.

Manchester United also paid tribute to their former midfielder, writing on social media: “Great player, better person. Thank you for representing our colours with class, Juan.”.
