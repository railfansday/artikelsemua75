So with that in mind, we expect iOS 27.3 to be released sometime during the first half of December.

The redesigned Health app is back in iOS 27.3. This was first available during testing of iOS 27.2, but did not ship as part of that final revision.

In a recent development, the redesigned Health app is split into three tabs: Insights, Longevity, and Browse. The app features an all-new design that dynamically adapts based on your data, including Apple Intelligence. The Longevity tab includes a new Health age feature that uses Apple Watch data to show how your metrics track relative to your actual age.

For Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 users, it also offers easy access to view your Readiness score every morning.

Recent findings suggest that for Apple Fitness+, iOS 27.3 adds a newly introduced Gym Strength feature. This creates progressive, four-week plans based on your strength goals, experience, schedule, and available equipment.

As you complete each week of workouts, the following week adapts based on the reps and weights you logged.

In a recent development, for iPhone Duo people, iOS 27.3 adds Reachability support. This setting shifts the interface on the outer display to the right or left, making it easier to reach the far corners. It’s designed to make iPhone Duo easier to use with one hand, despite the wider outer display. You activate it by swiping right on the right edge of the screen or left on the left edge.

Sources indicate that reachability for iPhone Duo is on by default. You can turn it off in Settings > Accessibility > Touch > Reachability.

According to recent reports, iOS 27.3 also brings a new face unlock feature to iPhone Duo. This is not full-fledged Face ID. Instead, Apple is using the front-facing camera on the iPhone Duo’s outer display to recognize you, then unlock the device. It doesn’t work for things like Apple Pay and passwords.

iOS 27.3 also tweaks the iPhone Duo’s software keyboard by making the keys slightly narrower and the font slightly smaller.

According to recent reports, finally, iOS 27.3 includes a new option in the Settings app to have your iPhone Duo lock when closed. Once enabled, your iPhone Duo will lock when you fold it, rather than transition content from the inner display to the outer display.

In a recent development, when it debuted, the iPhone Duo included a more robust version of StandBy with new clock faces and expanded customization options. iOS 27.3 brings the iPhone Duo’s StandBy experience to all iPhone models.

This includes the redesigned Analog and Digital Clock faces, as well as additional customization options for colors and complications. The revision also adds support for Shared Albums to the Photos face.

Recent findings suggest that iOS 27.3 brings several key Siri AI updates. First, the revision expands Siri AI with many new capabilities across Apple apps. Apple explains:.

We started rolling out these newly introduced capabilities across many Apple apps, including Calendar, Files, Finder, Journal, Mail, Maps, Messages, Music, Notes, Photos, Preview, Reminders, Safari, Shortcuts, and Weather, so customers can do things like find a coffee shop they visited last week or archive files that haven’t been opened in over a year.

It has been revealed that there are many more actions available now as examples for you to reference. To see what you can do in each app, just ask Siri AI.

Recent findings suggest that with iOS 27.3, Apple is also starting to test fresh ways for developers to create Siri AI integrations for the first time. This is described as an “early Developer Preview.” Apple explains:.

Today’s preview allows any app to bring its content and actions directly into Siri AI by using App Intents, making it easier for all apps to tap into Siri AI’s newest capabilities, including personal context understanding, onscreen awareness, natural language, and more. So people can just ask for help in their own words, and Siri AI carries out any custom action you’ve built, from checking a delivery to booking a reservation to placing an order.

While this is being tested as part of iOS 27.3, Apple claims it will start shipping to customers in a few months.

iOS 27.3 also expands Siri AI to new languages, including French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish.

Sources indicate that you can unlock higher usage limits with an eligible iCloud+ plan, starting at $2.99 per month. If you hit your usage limit, basic Siri requests will still work. That includes things like setting timers and alarms.

In a recent development, iOS 27.3 is a major update to Safari. First, the update adds support for a upcoming vertical tabs design layout on iPhone Duo’s inner display. If you choose this option, your Safari tabs will be arranged vertically along the left-hand side of Safari, rather than along the top. Vertical tabs are also available on Mac and iPad with macOS 27.3 and iPadOS 27.3.

In a recent development, iOS 27.3 also adds a new Adaptive Dark Mode feature. This feature can automatically apply Dark Mode to websites that don’t natively support it. You can choose to have Adaptive Dark Mode automatically kick in or use it on a site-by-site basis.

In a recent development, for Apple Music customers, iOS 27.3 adds a new “Selfie Sing” feature that lets users record a video of themselves while singing karaoke. To use this feature, start playing a song and go to the lyrics view. Then tap the new camera button that appears on the right-hand side of the lyrics.

The upgrade also refines the design of viewing lyrics while your iPhone is in landscape mode.

According to recent reports, there are two key changes to the Apple TV app in iOS 27.3. First, the update introduces video podcasts in the TV app. You can now watch your favorite video podcasts in the TV app, and sync your playback progress between the TV app and the Podcasts app.

Second, iOS 27.3 adds vertical video to the TV app. The app now includes a latest “Explore Clip Collections” section where you can browse clips from Apple TV content in a TikTok-style design.
