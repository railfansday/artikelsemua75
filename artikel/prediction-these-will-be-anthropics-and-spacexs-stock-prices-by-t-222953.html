According to recent reports, anthropic (ANT +8.67%) and Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +3.63%) were the two hottest initial public offerings (IPOs) of 2026. But where will they be in 2027?

When you factor in how swiftly they are growing and their current valuations, both of these companies look promising.

It has been revealed that at first glance, Anthropic and SpaceX don't seem like they compete. Anthropic is an artificial intelligence (AI) lab, and its flagship model, Claude, has emerged as one of the best available options. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

NASDAQ · As of 10:43 AM ET 11/27/26.

SpaceX is a space exploration company and releases satellites and other payloads into space with its reusable rocket system. It also operates a satellite internet business. However, before going public, SpaceX acquired xAI, giving it exposure to the same industry in which Anthropic competes. xAI operates the Grok family of models, a worthy competitor to Claude, although the two specialize in different areas. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Recent findings suggest that xAI has also become the largest part of SpaceX's business. In Q3, SpaceX's revenue totaled $14.6 billion, with $7.56 billion of that coming from its super intelligence (SI) division, which houses its AI operations. This segment also includes X, the social media platform formerly known as Twitter. When that platform is stripped out, SI generated $7 billion in revenue.

That's an impressive figure, but it still trails Anthropic.

During Q3, Anthropic's revenue rose 12-fold to $17 billion. That is a far larger segment that's growing promptly.

NASDAQ · As of 10:45 AM ET 11/27/26.

Both companies are growing incredibly fast, and seeing what's in store for 2027 will be a treat for investors. It's clear that AI usage is only going to increase, and many businesses have barely started using AI.

Sources indicate that wall Street analysts estimate Anthropic will grow at a 134% pace next year to $128 billion in revenue. SpaceX's revenue is expected to grow 272% to $182 billion. That's a huge outperformance, although I think the Anthropic growth projection is a bit light. Also remember that analyst projections are ultimately guesses and may prove inaccurate.

SPCX Revenue (TTM) data by YCharts.

Recent findings suggest that regardless, this is the best we have. So, where does that put these stocks by the end of next year?

Sources indicate that as of now, SpaceX has a $1.7 trillion market cap. Anthropic's is at $1.2 trillion. So, these two companies aren't that different in size. With SpaceX becoming more and more like an AI tech giant, I'd argue that these two should have the same valuation. What that level is, however, is up for debate. Historically, rapidly growing software companies’ shares can trade at a 20 times sales, so I'll use that as the baseline.

If the Wall Street estimates listed above pan out, that would price SpaceX stock at a $3.64 trillion market cap and Anthropic at a $2.56 trillion market cap. As for percentage gains, SpaceX would rise 109% to $267 per share and Anthropic would rise 115% to $191 per share.

Both of those are fantastic returns and would easily stomp the market. I think the market is underestimating Anthropic's return potential given the sheer speed at which it's growing now, and its returns could be even higher if it can maintain its impressive growth rate. SpaceX is a true wildcard, but with the progress it has made in AI, I think it's a solid stock, too. I think each of these stocks is worth buying right now, as 2027 looks bright for them.
