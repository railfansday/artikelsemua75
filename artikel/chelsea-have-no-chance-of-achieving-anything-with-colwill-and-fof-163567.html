Chelsea's poor form in 2026-27 continued after the international break following a 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United on Saturday.

The Blues lost by 3+ goals while conceding 4+ goals at home in the Premier League for just the fourth time, last doing so against Brentford in April 2022 (also 4-1).

It also marked Xabi Alonso's joint-biggest losing margin at home to any side as a manager (also 0-3 v Porto with Bayer Leverkusen in the 2022-23 Champions League), while it was the Blues' 100th home defeat in the Premier League (658th home match).

Chelsea are eighth in the English top-flight after 11 games, and only Crystal Palace (27), Manchester United (23), Ipswich Town (22) and Coventry City (21) have conceded more goals in the competition this term than Alonso's side (20). With summer signing Maxence Lacroix out injured, Colwill and Fofana started against Leeds, but Carragher insisted Chelsea must buy a new centre-back in January.

"Chelsea have got a massive problem at centre-back and if they don't change it in January, they're going to have a big problem in terms of where they feel they should be this season with the Champions League," Carragher mentioned on Sky Sports.
