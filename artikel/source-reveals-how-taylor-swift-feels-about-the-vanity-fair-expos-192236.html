It has been revealed that wondering how Taylor Swift feels about the Vanity Fair piece about her? She’s not sweating it. A source tells Page Six that the singer is unfazed by the article, explaining that “Taylor has never pretended to be perfect, but she doesn’t believe a collection of old grievances and unflattering anecdotes defines who she is or what she’s accomplished. To her, it feels like the conclusion was already written and they just went looking for people willing to support it.”.

They add that “At the end of the day, she’s proud of what she’s accomplished and isn’t going to lose sleep over any of this, especially given how the piece has been received by others in the media.”.

According to recent reports, as for Travis Kelce, Page Six’s insider notes that he “is always going to have Taylor’s back. He knows the person she is behind closed doors, and that’s what matters to him. He sees how hard she works, how much she cares about the people in her life and how much of herself she puts into everything she does. He’s incredibly proud of her and isn’t about to let someone else’s perception of who she is change the way he sees her. If anything, he thinks the whole thing says more about the people trying to tear her down than it does about Taylor.”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)
