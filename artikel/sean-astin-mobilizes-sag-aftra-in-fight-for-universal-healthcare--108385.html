On Friday, Screen Actors Guild President Sean Astin unveiled the President’s Healthcare Task Force to address the “broken healthcare system” while several actors have faced losing their insurance under the union’s minimum eligibility requirements. For our members who don’t qualify, there is very little that can be done to provide coverage.”. (Related: University launches $5m smart system that checks who is...)

Sources indicate that astin added, “SAG-AFTRA has supported efforts to expand healthcare for all through the AFL-CIO. This task force seeks a upcoming level of participation in the national movement to expand care for everyone. We will actively engage with policymakers, gather allies and provide a structured way for our members to participate formally in the process. The President’s Healthcare Task Force will be an instrument of support in the movement for universal healthcare.” Watch on Deadline.

The SAG-AFTRA National Board voted unanimously on Friday to form the Task Force, which seeks to mobilize SAG-AFTRA’s collective strength and public influence to fight for universal access to affordable, comprehensive and reliable health care.

SAG’s fresh initiative comes amid financial scrutiny for the union following National Executive Director Duncan Crabtree-Ireland’s recent contract renewal, after his salary surpassed $1M in 2023.
