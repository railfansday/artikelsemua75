Draper Associates, Planetary VC and Epoch VC also participated. The seed followed a pre-seed angel round of less than $1 million. Maple prepares to raise a Series A in the first half of 2027, according to the report, which attributed that timetable to investor interest.

Maple’s product pitch is to enforce privacy through system design rather than rely only on a provider’s data policy. On its official site, Maple notes open models operate inside encrypted enclaves and conversations have end-to-end encryption; it also says those conversations are not used to train AI or shared with advertisers by design. Industry observers note that CEO Mark Suman described a process intended to keep Maple from seeing raw user data in flight or at rest. Information is encrypted on the user’s device using a password known only to that user, decrypted for processing inside a Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) — a server with hardware encryption — and encrypted again before being returned. The models run in these environments in a U.S. data center, with European support provided through a partner operating a server in Europe. Suman mentioned customers can inspect the open-source code and verify what runs on the servers: “They can audit everything themselves and know how their data is being handled.”. Recent findings suggest that maple Chat combines long-form conversations, document analysis and web search across mobile and desktop apps. Users can select open models from providers including OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek and Kimi, or switch between a quicker option and a more powerful one.

Industry observers note that suman previously spent six years at Apple, where he worked on AI and machine learning with a focus on computer vision. Co-founder and CTO Anthony Ronning has a background in defense technology, security and networking. Ronning started the predecessor privacy-focused fintech in 2023; Suman joined him in August 2024, and Maple’s mobile product became public in January 2025.

Sources indicate that the new Maple Work desktop app moves beyond chat by operating the computer and completing tasks across local files, applications and a web browser. An integration store connects services such as Gmail and calendars. For supported tools such as Claude and Codex, Work can use the Agent Client Protocol, a connection for compatible AI tools, to create a subagent while limiting shared information to what the task requires.

Industry observers note that maple is targeting small businesses with Work. Its stated plan for 2027 is to begin enterprise pilots offering the company’s version of zero data retention: most customer data is not retained, while information that must be kept is encrypted. Maple is completing SOC 2 compliance work and security audits ahead of that expansion.

It has been revealed that the manufacturer earns revenue through subscriptions. Its pricing page lists a free tier, Pro at $20 per month, Max at $100, Max+ at $200 and Team at $30 per user per month. Pro includes $20 of monthly usage, while Max and Max+ include $200 and $400; Chat, Work and API usage share one balance, and additional usage is never charged automatically.

It has been revealed that maple declined to disclose its registered customer count but reported 60-fold growth over the three months preceding the report. The Austin- and San Francisco-based startup has four employees and is actively hiring.

For Maple, the central test is whether the same encrypted architecture can support persistent cloud agents and enterprise security requirements while preserving the privacy boundary on which its product pitch depends.
