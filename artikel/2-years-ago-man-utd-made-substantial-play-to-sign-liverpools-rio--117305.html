It was a mountain to climb from then on for the Red Devils, even if they had 11 men on the pitch.

Liverpool made it 3-0 after 66 minutes as Ngumoha finally got himself on the scoresheet.

It was then 4-0 in the final 10 minutes, which prompted many United fans to head for the exits.

It was a game that summed up many of United's problems, particularly defensively, and it certainly highlighted Mauricio Pochettino that he's got his work cut out.

Ngumoha in particular was incredibly impressive, and it sums up United's problems that he was a player the Red Devils were hoping to get.

According to journalist Laurie Whitwell, Manchester United made a substantial play to sign Ngumoha before he chose to move to Liverpool from Chelsea.

After that display, United must have been thinking, "What could have been," with the young winger tearing them apart despite his age.

Ngumoha gave Diogo Dalot a torrid afternoon, constantly looking to beat his marker and make things happen.

United could certainly do with a player of Ngumoha's ability and potential right now, and they must be living to regret not getting a deal over the line.

He certainly wouldn't solve all of United's problems, considering they desperately need defensive reinforcements, but it's a real kick in the teeth for the youngster to tear them apart at Anfield.

It has been revealed that united had an experienced right-back in Dalot. Going head-to-head with him, but the Portuguese defender certainly lost that battle.

This is likely to continue being a common theme for United going forward, considering their current position in the Premier League table.

The Red Devils sit in 19th position, having won just once this season, and are in real danger of getting relegated unless Pochettino can turn things around swiftly.

As a result, United are going to find it incredibly difficult to attract players, just like they did with Ngumoha.

In the past, United have always been able to attract players due to the stature of the club, but their league position is going to carry a lot of weight going forward.

The Red Devils are going to fall further and further behind as players such as Ngumoha choose other clubs.
