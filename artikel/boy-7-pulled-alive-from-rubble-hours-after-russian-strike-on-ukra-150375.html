A strike on a private home in Bila Tserkva killed one more person and set the building ablaze, he added.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky also remarked three people were killed and 13 wounded in a separate strike on Vyshneve, also in the Kyiv region, that hit a grocery store on Sunday.

Ukrainian authorities stated the boy in Dymer is in stable condition and was taken to hospital, and have thanked everyone involved in saving his life.

Zelensky and Ukraine's national police also confirmed the rescue of the boy.

In a Telegram post, Tkachenko mentioned the 79-year-old woman rescued from the rubble was receiving treatment in hospital.

Earlier he noted six people were injured in the attack, while others were still missing.

The attack on the residential building occurred in the early hours of the morning while people were asleep.

Zelensky said more than 130 people are involved in emergency operations at the scene, and that they will continue until they have "complete information about every single person".

Sending his condolences to the families and loved ones of those killed, he remarked the strike struck the residential building, "practically blowing a section of it to smithereens".

Zelensky added that "rescue efforts are complicated by constant new air raid alerts and renewed threats".

The strike hit the building at 05:00 local time, when families were still asleep, Ukraine's minister of internal affairs Ivan Vyhivskyi said.

More than 70 strikes have been rolled out across 10 Ukrainian regions as of Sunday evening, Vyhivskyi added.

Emergency workers continue to search for those missing after Russian drone and missile attacks in Dymer.

In his video address posted online, Zelensky also mentioned three people were killed and 13 injured in a strike on an "ordinary grocery store" in Vyshneve.

Mayor Illya Dikov wrote on social media that three children were among the injured.
