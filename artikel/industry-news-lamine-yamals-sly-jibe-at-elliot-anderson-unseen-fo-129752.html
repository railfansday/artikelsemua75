Lamine Yamal mercilessly taunted England rival Elliot Anderson as tensions flared during the Three Lions’ spectacular victory over Spain on Sunday night.

England held on for a memorable 3-2 victory in Madrid after Anderson was given his marching orders with just under 30 minutes remaining.

Thomas Tuchel’s side were three goals in front and cruising when Anderson - looking to atone for a sloppy error from Jarrel Quansah - cynically hauled down 19-year-old Yamal as he barrelled down on goal.

Spain were awarded a penalty for the infringement, and Anderson was sent off - much to the Manchester City star’s bemusement.

But Yamal, aggrieved by Anderson’s challenge, made a beeline for the midfielder following the challenge. According to Spanish reports, the attacker mouthed ‘tonto, tonto’ to his rival following his dismissal.

The colloquial term can mean a number of things, varying from idiot, fool or stupid, but in any case is far from a term of affection.

A fired-up Yamal galvanised the reigning world champions as they searched for an unlikely comeback.

Mikel Oyarzabal, the man who scored Spain’s winner against England two years ago in the 2024 Euros final, converted the spot kick before Nico Williams scored Spain’s second after latching on to an excellent Yamal cross.

However, Las Rojas fell short, with Sunday’s defeat their first in an imperious 43-game run.

A jubilant Thomas Tuchel hailed the match as his ‘best performance of my time as manager’.

‘What a performance we put out there for 60 minutes to be deservedly 3-0 up,’ Tuchel stated after the game. ’Thirty minutes against Spain with ten men feels like three hours.
