Industry observers note that presentation from Mitchells and Butlers for our financial year '25-'26. I'm pleased to be able to report another successful year where we outperformed the market once again. We delivered 2.1% like-for-like sales growth and adjusted operating profit growth despite the impact of employers' National Insurance, which hit the first half by an incremental GBP 12 million and despite the super inflation in the red meat category, which helped drive year-on-year cost headwinds of circa GBP 120 million.

Industry observers note that with the lowest team turnover the organization has ever seen, very high team engagement score, the highest guest review scores we've ever had and a strong safety record, it feels like the business is in good shape. We're confident that with a very strong balance sheet, we have a very bright future ahead of us. We accept that we potentially have another tough year ahead given the geopolitical complications that are driving high fuel costs. But in Ignite and our capital program, we're working hard to mitigate for this. In any case, this will be temporary. It should not take the shine off the underlying trajectory and health of the business. And as 2030/'31 gets ever closer when our debt service costs drop by GBP 130 million per annum, the business is very well placed to capitalize on any opportunities that arise. I'll now hand over to Emma to take you through last year's results before I return to talk in more detail about where we are as a business and where we're heading.
