Xabi Alonso’s side were thumped 4-1 at home by rivals Leeds on Saturday and have conceded 20 goals in 11 Premier League games this season.

With £52m summer signing Maxence Lacroix sidelined with a hamstring injury, Fofana and Colwill have been Alonso’s centre-back pairing for the Blues’ last three matches.

Former Liverpool defender Carragher delivered a scathing assessment as he analysed Leeds’ equaliser, scored by Noah Okafor. (Related: Industry News: I Asked About Dirty Dishes. You Delivere...)

“That is as bad as it gets in terms of conceding a goal in the Premier League,” Carragher said on Sky Sports’ Monday Night Football.

Recent findings suggest that “You feel like you’re seeing kids playing in a men’s game. That’s the best way to describe it.”.

Only the bottom two, Crystal Palace and Manchester United, and promoted sides Ipswich and Coventry have worse defensive records than Chelsea.

The Blues have lost three of their last four top-flight matches and are already 12 points adrift of leaders Arsenal and five behind fourth-placed Liverpool in the race for Champions League qualification.

“Chelsea have got a massive problem at centre-back and if they don’t change it in January, they’re going to have a big problem in terms of where they feel they should be this season with the Champions League,” mentioned Carragher.

“Lacroix is a decent player, that’s a sticking plaster until the next transfer window in January or the end of the season.

“Until they sign that dominant centre-back and leader, they’ve got absolutely no chance of achieving anything.”.

Chelsea were booed off by the home supporters who remained at Stamford Bridge after their thrashing by Leeds.

The heavy defeat followed a 2-0 home loss to London rivals Tottenham and a 1-0 reverse at Sunderland, either side of a 2-0 victory over Manchester United.

“Colwill at Sunderland against (Brian) Brobbey gave the penalty away, and the manager took him off at half-time,” said Carragher.

“Fofana’s performance against Manchester United, especially in the first half, was one of the worst you’ll see from a centre-back this season.

“As a partnership, this will never work because they’re not physical enough and there’s no real leader there.”.

Frenchman Fofana, 25, joined Chelsea in a £75m deal from Leicester in 2022, while 23-year-old Colwill came through the club’s academy and has been capped five times by England.

“It almost feels like you’ve got two novices playing, but one cost £75m, and Colwill has been talked up a lot not just at Chelsea but at international level as well,” added Carragher.

Eighth-placed Chelsea return to action away to Nottingham Forest on Friday evening.
