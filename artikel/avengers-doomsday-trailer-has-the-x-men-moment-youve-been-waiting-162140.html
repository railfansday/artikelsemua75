In a recent development, avengers: Doomsday will unite the superteams of the X-Men, the Fantastic Four, and of course, the Avengers (both new and classic). However, the film's trailers have rarely showcased what a true collaboration between these three squads will look like — until now.

According to recent reports, in Avengers: Doomsday's latest trailer, Marvel goes all in on crossovers, making the momentous meetings from Avengers: Infinity War look like run-of-the-mill morning office greetings. The Fantastic Four pull Steve Rogers (Chris Evans) out of his cozy life with Peggy Carter (Hayley Atwell) and their son. Scott Summers (James Marsden) battles Sam Wilson (Anthony Mackie). Faces who haven't been spotted in the MCU in years, like Shang-Chi (Simu Liu) and Kate Bishop (Hailee Steinfeld), return, all in the hopes of stopping the threat of Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.).

But of course, the most hype-building moment comes at the very end of the trailer, when Marsden's Cyclops utters the line fans have been waiting to hear in live action for 26 years: "To me, my X-Men.". (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

The line cues a circling group shot reminiscent of 2012's The Avengers team-up moment. X-Men Gambit (Channing Tatum), Nightcrawler (Alan Cumming), Mystique (Rebecca Romijn), and Beast (Kelsey Grammer) surge into action against Doctor Doom's army of gargantuan Sentinels. But they aren't alone. They're also joined by non-X-Men, like Sam, Yelena Belova (Florence Pugh), Shang-Chi, Reed Richards (Pedro Pascal), Thor, and, of course, Steve.
