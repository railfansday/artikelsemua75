Rockstar Games has released Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. Just 24 hours after the long-awaited video game arrived, the accompanying soundtrack has hit streaming services.

The original album is loaded with 34 tracks from varying genres, so there’s something for just about every fan of the iconic video game series.

While 12 of the 34 tracks were released early as singles, fans will hear latest music from Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Cardi B, Lana Del Rey, Gunna, Don Toliver, Yung Lean, Future, Metro Boomin, Travis Scott, Fred again.., PinkPantheress, Morgan Wallen, Tame Impala, Fuerza Regida and many more.

According to The Hollywood Reporter, which sourced marketing intelligence firm Sensor Tower, the anticipated video game already generated a staggering $1.84 billion in sales. For reference, that sales number is already higher than the box office for Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey film.

There are several radio stations gamers will listen to throughout the campaign, hosted and curated by artists including Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Trina and Trick Daddy, PinkPantheress, Burna Boy and Robyn.

In a recent development, grand Theft Auto VI will be celebrated with a music festival at Miami’s Mana Wynwood on Nov. 21. The festival is slated to feature performances from Cardi B, Travis Scott, Rauw Alejandro and more, including a Cactus Jack set featuring Scott, Don Toliver, SoFaygo and a guest appearance by Kodak Black.

In a recent development, the Miami Heat hosted Vice City Night on Wednesday (Nov. 18) in celebration of the video game, which saw appearances from Travis Scott, Sexyy Red, Offset and Quavo.
