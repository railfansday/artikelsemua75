There is increasing evidence of an evolution not only in approach but also mentality, highlighted by a return to the scene of Asian Cup heartbreak.

Industry observers note that last week’s 3-0 dismissal of Qatar at the Abdullah bin Khalifa Stadium marked the first time the Socceroos had returned to the Gulf state since their heartbreaking elimination from the Asian Cup just under three years ago. Back then, coach Graham Arnold called Doha a “home away from home” after they relocated there during the Covid pandemic, and went so far as to suggest that they would have some form of advantage at the 2022 World Cup as a result.
