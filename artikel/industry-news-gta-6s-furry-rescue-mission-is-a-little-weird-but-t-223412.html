According to recent reports, no matter what you do in GTA 6, it pales in comparison to being a Furry in that game. Anyone looking to externalize their inner animal spirit is in danger of being hunted, and you'll find out for yourself just how much the fictional state of Leonida hates Furries when you're given the chance to save one of them from being murdered by the cops. Should you help them? Absolutely, if you want an easy payday or a newly introduced weapon for your arsenal. How to find Chase the Fox Like several other missions in GTA 6, helping out the Furry resistance appears to trigger randomly. The mission takes place after Chapter 3, on Miramar Drive in Crosstown, Vice City, and you'll want to be cruising around that area at night, between midnight and 4 AM. Once it starts, you'll see cops pull up to hunt down a Furry by the name of Chase, and if you drive up to him, you can either give him a lift or leave him to his brutal fate. Outrun the cops If you do choose to help Chase, then get ready to be hunted down by cops. You'll pick up a three-star Wanted level, which means the cops will be in mildly hot pursuit of your vehicle. Reach the parking garage Chase will direct you to a nearby parking garage, where his Furry allies are waiting. After a short standoff, they'll welcome you into their ranks as an honorary Furry. Courier the group to safety To get the law off your polyester tail, one of your new allies will take your car and use it to distract the cops. Get inside the van, and you can bring the other Furries home to their swanky pad in Starfish Island. While you're driving, the Furries will interact with each other, and you can watch their hijinks. Choose your reward Once Chase and his pals are back home, you'll be offered one of two rewards: $15,000 in cash or the unique Furry DW-9 submachine gun. Personally, I'd go with the gun since it's easy enough to make cash in GTA 6, and having more weapons is never a bad idea. You'll also gain safehouse access to the Furry group's hideout, where you can stock up on weapons and body armor.
