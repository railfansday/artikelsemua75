GTA 6's T-shirts Are True Works of Art

We've known for some time that fashion would be a big deal in Grand Theft Auto 6. In Rockstar's official promotional materials for the game, both Jason and Lucia were prominently featured wearing stylish clothing, from Lucia's body-hugging gold dress to Jason's slick Miami Vice-inspired aqua sport coat. Even supporting characters like Brian wear Tommy Bahama-inspired aloha shirts yanked straight from the closet of a dapper middle-aged stoner. GTA 6 has drawn praise from all quarters for its scrupulous attention to detail in every aspect of the game, and there's one area the developer went far, far harder than it needed to: the vast and immaculate selection of T-shirts.

Having a diverse wardrobe isn't just a fun little customization touch in GTA 6. If you're reported for committing a crime in Leonida — and this will happen very often — police will be able to recognize you based on your car, your physical appearance, and what you're wearing. Keeping a couple spare outfits in your trunk or hastily buying a fresh ensemble on the run can be the difference between a successful job and ending up busted (or wasted). Recent findings suggest that I'm taking my time with GTA 6 and still kicking around the introductory area of Key Lento. I know I should be putting money in my family savings account. It would probably be in my best interest to learn how to drive without sideswiping semi-trucks or taking out stop signs. Perhaps I ought to be buying more guns. Instead, I'm trawling South Leonida for all the vintage and souvenir shops I can find. You can keep your fancy cars. I've got a T-shirt collection to maintain. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Much like in real life, you never know when you're going to find a gem in a vintage boutique or souvenir shop in GTA 6. My prized Rusty Anchor shirt came from an optional mission where you take Cal for drinks, he has a few too many, and you have to take him home. Apparently Cal won a wet T-shirt contest at the Anchor and this was his reward, but he's too hungover to claim the prize.

During my wanderings, I also discovered a riff on that three-wolves-howling-at-the-moon shirt that everyone had about a decade ago, including me. But this one has gators on it. I bought it from Sharleen's yard sale in Jason's very own neighborhood of Key Lento. She had a couple of inexpensive clothing items on offer, including some pretty slick teal sneakers that were clearly inspired by Air Force One low-tops. I was delighted to learn that there are multiple yard sales in the game, including one in Port Gellhorn where you can buy a dupe of Arthur Morgan's hat from Red Dead Redemption 2.

Industry observers note that even the cheesiest souvenir shops, like Leroy's in Key Lento, have the exact kind of "so bad it's good" T-shirts you'll see dozens of times over the course of a weekend in a tourist trap. Do I need to spend $38 of my ill-gotten dollars on a horrendous blue granny shirt with a dolphin on it, with "Vice Beach" written in collegiate font? Certainly not. But I am tempted.

Rockstar has a bunch of GTA 6 merch on its official website, including T-shirts, though much of it is currently sold out. Here's hoping they expand the offering with a couple of these in-game gems. If you're hard up for a three gator moon shirt though, you can already find them on Amazon and Etsy.
