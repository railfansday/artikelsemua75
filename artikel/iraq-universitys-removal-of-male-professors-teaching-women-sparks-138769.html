Al-Qadisiyah University’s move a sign of the country’s slide into a Taliban-like gender segregation, say activists.

The removal of male professors teaching female students at a university in southern Iraq has sparked protests and been criticised by activists as a Taliban-like gender segregation policy.

As many as 400 women are estimated to have joined protests over the past two weeks calling for the return of their professors at Al-Qadisiyah University in a move that appears to have surprised its administrators.
