Tech Report: A Major Marvel Villain Returns To Team Up With Wolverine In 2027

🌐 Source: Screenrant • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (681 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Marvel is bringing back Wolverine's biggest bad, but as an ally.
  • Spoilers ahead for Marvel's "Feral Wolverine" arc!
  • That storyline kicks off next week, with Wolverine #30, and Marvel is promising a bloodbath.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Marvel is bringing back Wolverine's biggest bad, but as an ally. It is as true in superhero comics as it is in professional wrestling: when a bitter blood feud has reached an epic, 5-star payoff, sometimes there is nowhere else for the two combatants to go as enemies, so instead the best creative choice is to pair them up as a tag team. That is exactly what is set to happen in January, as part of Marvel's "Feral Wolverine" arc.

That storyline kicks off next week, with Wolverine #30, and Marvel is promising a bloodbath. The problem is that it will be "innocent blood that stains [Wolverine's] hands and claws" this time, if he can't "rein in his bestial temperament," as the synopsis for the concern puts it. The synopsis for the following issue mentions a trail of "blood and death," but it also teases "the help of an unexpected ally." The thing is, Marvel has already spoiled who it is going to be. A villain who has been dead since 2024.

According to recent reports, and not just any villain. Wolverine's archnemesis, Sabretooth. Following his dramatic death at Logan's claws at the end of the spectacular "Sabretooth War" arc, Victor Creed is finally back in the mix in the main Marvel timeline. The "how" is TBD, but the "why" seems clear, as surprising as it might be: to help Wolverine when he's at his lowest point.

Recent findings suggest that the art for one of Wolverine #31's variant covers gives away Sabretooth's return, but artist Ian Churchill renders them in familiar form. That is, at each other's throats. Crashing out of what appears to be a hotel window, plummeting toward the swimming pool below. Cover art, especially variant cover art, doesn't always correspond to what is going to happen in the issue, but this image seems so specific that it might, in this case. Sources indicate that either way, with Wolverine in a state of "feral fury" in the issue, even helping him is going to involve fighting him. If nothing else, Sabretooth will give Logan a target to focus on that can fight back, keeping him from going on a rampage and hurting civilians, more than he already might have.

Beyond that, though, Marvel has already tipped its hand narratively, in terms of how this latest round in the renewed rivalry between Sabretooh and Wolverine will play out. The synopsis and cover for Wolverine #32 spoil the swerve in their historic feud, which will have them fighting side-by-side again for the first time in years.

"Logan reunites with...Victor Creed?!" That's how the synopsis for Wolverine #32 puts it. The cover shows the two running into battle together. There is good reason to be flummoxed by this. The last time the two met in the present-day Marvel Universe, it was after Sabretooth waged a campaign of terror against Wolverine, attempting to destroy everything and everyone Logan loved before killing him permanently. Sabretooth very nearly succeeded, but in the end, he was the one who was dispatched by Wolverine.

It was a fitting send-off for the villain, but this is Marvel Comics. Sabretooth's death was never going to last forever, or for very long. He has made subsequent appearances in flashbacks, and as an apparition, and in alternate continuities, but now he's confirmed to be resurrected in the mainstream Marvel canon.

Recent findings suggest that sabretooth has had tenures, throughout his X-Men history, as an anti-hero. Even a borderline hero at times. This might or might not be the start of that. Considering the fact that it will be hard for Sabretooth to top his "Sabretooth War" actions as a villain, a redemption arc might be the best option for the character right now. Marvel fans will find out over the course of 2027, as the year kicks off with the villain's return as the "unexpected ally" who might just save Wolverine from himself.

Wolverine #31 will be available January 6, 2027 from Marvel Comics.
