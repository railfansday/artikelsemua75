I keep it low key this Sunday to give myself a chance to mentally reset before a substantial final week on the Appalachian Trail.

It has been revealed that today was nothing but an attempt to keep myself relaxed while putting the finishing touches on my blogs for the week. I’ll wrap up Pennsylvania with a 2-day backpacking stint on Monday and Tuesday and have reserved an off day on Wednesday to make the final preparations for my version of the 4 state challenge. The preliminary plan is to hike about 20 miles south on Thursday, a further 12 miles on Friday, just 7 miles on Saturday including some time poking around Harper’s Ferry. I’ll camp up at the Virginia/West Virginia border Saturday evening and hopefully find the strength to hike 44.5 miles on Sunday in time to catch a sunset at Pen Mar Park. My parents are planning to meet me at the park which will make it even more special. I may even see Pebbles, Rebar, and Greasetrap for some portion of this section as they are now ahead of me on the trail, but of course I have already completed the southern section of the trail in Pennsylvania and will therefore be skipping ahead. I did pick up some secret weapons from the store today, granola bars and beef sticks. You know you have been lacking variety in your diet when granola and meat in the form of sticks are cause for excitement. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

Recent findings suggest that it was quite a smooth week all things considered. There was some adverse weather for the first time in a long time, but it didn’t manage to slow me down. It has gotten colder, but my shoulder-season hiking load-out is proving up to the task. The final milestones are falling as well. I added a picture of the 1,200 mile marker to my collection, completing a full set of northbound 100 mile markers. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

In a recent development, what is there to say other than this is it. I’ll be finishing the hike, the only question is whether I am able to have the strong finish I have when I rejoin the trail and attempt to complete the 4 state challenge. I’ll talk more about this in Wednesday’s post, for now I am planning a zero on that day to rest and triple check everything before I set off.
