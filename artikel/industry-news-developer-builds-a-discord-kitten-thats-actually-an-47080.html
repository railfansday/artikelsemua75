A developer has built an AI girlfriend that can hold a conversation on Discord, giving the years-old “Discord kitten” meme a new spin.

It has been revealed that the developer, who goes by @tgu_non on X, posted a video demo of the project on November 19. The project immediately drew comparisons to the popular “Discord kitten” meme, as the clip showed a snippet of a call between the creator and his AI girlfriend. In a recent development, “Discord kitten” is internet slang tied to flirting and role-play on the chat platform. The nickname became a running joke across Reddit, Twitter, and TikTok, referring to a romantic partner whom someone interacts with primarily through Discord. (Related: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming Game in 5 D...)

It has been revealed that in the Japanese-language post, tgu_non described the AI girlfriend as an early prototype. They stated they initiative to improve how it thinks and make its voice sound more human.

The 30-second clip shows tgu_non talking to the chatbot in a Discord call. He asks the bot a question, and after a brief pause, the bot responds by giggling, then answering in a voice similar to that of a Vocaloid singer.

The interaction left viewers divided, with some calling an AI Discord kitten “dystopian” while others seem genuinely excited by the idea, and have asked for a release date.

It has been revealed that other AI companions already run on Discord. Thea, for example, lets users create characters with their own avatars and personalities. Its listing advertises long-term memory and even an option to marry a character.

AI companions have also found audiences outside Discord. In 2023, Snapchat influencer Caryn Marjorie charged $1 per minute to talk to an AI version of herself. In 2025, Grok climbed to the top of Japan’s App Store charts after xAI released Ani, its anime-style AI companion.
