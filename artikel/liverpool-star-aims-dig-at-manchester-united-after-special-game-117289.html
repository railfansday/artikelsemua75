Liverpool winger Rio Ngumoha aimed a subtle dig at Manchester United by suggesting his side should have scored more than four goals in today’s game.

Ngumoha was in superb form for Liverpool as they beat Man Utd 4-0, and it’s fair to say it was a pretty easy game for the Reds, with plenty of chances for an even bigger score-line.

Speaking after the game, the 18-year-old sounded delighted to shine in this big game, admitting that it’s special to score and assist at such a young age against Liverpool’s rivals.

According to recent reports, “Games like Liverpool-United can’t really get much bigger in my eyes. To get the three points here at Anfield is special. It was a really crucial game today,” Ngumoha noted in his post-match interview, as quoted by BBC Sport.

It has been revealed that on possibly scoring more against the Red Devils, he added: “Sometimes when we are two goals up we can get a substantial complacent really. We kept our heads in the game and started controlling it and in the second half we took the game by the scruff of the neck and showed the mentality. We didn’t slack off.
