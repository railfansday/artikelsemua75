In a recent development, ever since appearing in the original Clerks in 1994, Jay and Silent Bob have become pop culture icons, popping up well beyond Kevin Smith's View Askewniverse. From an appearance in last year's wine comedy Napa Boys to a cameo in a Dunkin' commercial with Ben and Casey Affleck, showing up on the set of the latest Stab movie in Scream 3, and appearing in Archie comics, they continue to find themselves in strange new places three decades later, and they have a third solo movie on the way. So recognizable are the pair that they've become practically synonymous with Smith and Jason Mewes themselves whenever they appear on-screen. However, for once, the duo are playing roles together that aren't their instantly recognizable stoners or a nod to stated pair.

Sources indicate that this Wednesday, November 25, Smith and Mewes will be heading to Disney+ to join the world of young Cricket Green. Big City Greens Season 5, Episode 8, features two upcoming tales involving the titular family, starting with "Perked Up," which sees Doug Perkins regaling Gloria with his adventures alongside the Greens. However, the second half, "Chicken Coup," introduces the inseparable Jay and Silent Bob actors as a new dynamic duo — Ricky (Mewes) and Dave (Smith), two starving coyotes who encounter the Greens' power-hungry rooster, Cogburn (Alfred Molina), in the woods. Ahead of the episode's arrival on streaming, Collider can exclusively share a new sneak peek that sees them circling the fearsome fowl, but he may have a plan to avoid becoming chicken dinner.

According to recent reports, the footage opens with Cogburn's audience of forest creatures fleeing upon the arrival of Ricky and Dave, who look more fearsome than the average coyote. They each appear as if they've been in a scrap or two before, with the former missing part of his ear and the latter having an intimidating scar running down his left eye that matches Cogburn's. Like the predators, the Spider-Man villain-voiced rooster is no slouch in a fight, but his biggest asset is his brain and his charisma.

He catches them off guard by feigning sympathy for the coyotes' hunger, promising them an endless bounty of food from his farm if they follow him. To prove he's serious, he promises a way around the electric fence that keeps wreaking havoc on Ricky and Dave's hunt. With that, he has two new loyal soldiers for his army, which he'll presumably use to get revenge on the Greens and take what he desires.

Recent findings suggest that though they're not as raunchy as usual, Smith and Mewes still bring a bit of their chemistry and humor to Significant City Greens. They add to a lengthy Season 5 guest roster, headlined by the return of former The Suite Life of Zach & Cody star Dylan Sprouse to Disney. Stephen King, Luke Combs, Chad Michael Murray, Meghan Stalter, Cobie Smulders, Clark Gregg, Mike Colter, and Steve Scirripa are also among the big names appearing in this latest run. Speaking to Collider's Maggie Lovitt, Mewes was thrilled to join the adventures of the mischievous Cricket and his family in part because his kids loved the show. It was also a rare opportunity for him and Smith to do something outside their typical wheelhouse together that still played to their friendship.

"It was great because, again, it was — we're not, I know, as people who know Kevin well and myself, we're not very scary, so it was cool to be sort of a scary character in there and get to play sort of scary. All the other animals were like, 'Whoa,' but then, being part of it, and then all of a sudden, there's this twist at the end. But again, it was fun because coyotes. I mean, any animal would be great, but to get to play a coyote was fantastic. And, you know, they roll in packs, so again, Kevin and I together as a pack, beautiful.".
