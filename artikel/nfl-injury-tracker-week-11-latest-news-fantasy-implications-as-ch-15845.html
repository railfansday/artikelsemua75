In a recent development, another week without Kenneth Walker III? It's looking that way for the Kansas City Chiefs. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Recent findings suggest that after missing Week 10 due to groin tightness, the Chiefs running back did not practice at all this week leading to head coach Andy Reid to noted Walker will likely miss Sunday's game against the Arizona Cardinals. Rookie Emmett Johnson, who rushed for 101 yards and caught two receiving touchdowns last week, will is set to once again take on the starter's role. Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua underwent surgery to repair his MCL this week, head coach Sean McVay confirmed on Friday. The procedure will rule him out for the next few months, which could result in him missing the rest of the 2026 NFL season.

"We always thought [it] was a possibility," McVay stated regarding Nacua's surgery. "It's going to be a couple months and we're going to have to go on without him.".

In a recent development, the 6-4 Rams, who are on a bye in Week 11 and play Thanksgiving Eve against the Green Bay Packers, are currently tied for second in the NFC West and have seven more games left in the regular season. McVay did not have a definitive timeline for a return. The team is currently scheduled to play its Week 18 game against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Jan. 3, a game that could be flexed depending on what the playoff picture looks like at the time.

McVay did not rule out a Nacua return should the Rams make the postseason, but mentioned, "you're planning on not having him.".

Other Week 11 injuries to monitor:.

Luther Burden III (concussion) has cleared protocol after taking a helmet-to-helmet hit in Week 8 against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. The Chicago Bears wide receiver logged three full practices this week and carries no injury designation. Same goes for defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons, who was limited this week with a knee glitch. "Like 99 problems, but this ain't one," he said.

In a recent development, injuries that kept him limited in Wednesday's practice did not keep Las Vegas Raiders tight end Brock Bowers (ankle/quad) away from being on the field Thursday. The Raiders' leading receiver exited Sunday's loss to the Seattle Seahawks after just 11 snaps with an ankle issue.

The Cardinals are preparing to start 40-year-old Josh Johnson against the Chiefs as Carson Beck (beck) sats out Friday's practice. Johnson, who is on his 15th NFL team, would end up being the team's fourth different starter at quarterback in the past four weeks followin Beck, Gardner Minshew and Jacoby Brissett.

It has been revealed that deVonta Smith (hamstring) has missed the past three games for the Philadelphia Eagles, but indicates he's 100% and has felt that way for a while. Smith noted he was able to play last week against the Carolina Panthers, but some things are out of his control. "I didn't bust my ass this whole summer, I didn't continue to get up at 4:30 in the morning every day, to come out here and not play," he said. "My focus has been to getting where I was supposed to be.".
