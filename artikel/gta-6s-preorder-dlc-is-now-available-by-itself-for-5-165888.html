It has been revealed that rockstar Games is now selling GTA 6's preorder DLC as a standalone purchase. The Vintage Vice City Pack is now available to buy for $5. The DLC includes '80s-inspired content, including the '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, along with a garage for it on Ocean Beach. The DLC also comes with outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, along with a tropical pattern inspired by GTA: Vice City main character Tommy Vercetti's palm tree t-shirt. Some of the cosmetics for Jason and Lucia in the Vintage Vice City DLC. As with GTA 6's other DLC that's included in the Ultimate Edition, the Vintage Vice City content isn't unlocked all at once. Instead, players get access to it as they progress through the story. GTA 6's other extra content from the Ultimate Edition is not available separately, but people who bought the $80 edition can pay $20 to upgrade to get all of it. As declared previously, the Ultimate Edition includes tons of extra content, including a car, special guns and cosmetics for them, and access to an exclusive hair salon, tattoo parlor, and mod shop, just to name a few of the perks. GTA 6 launched on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it reached 32.5 million copies sold via pre-purchases alone. On day one, GTA 6 generated $3.79 billion in revenue. Over the first four days, GTA 6 has climbed to 44.9 million copies sold and more than $4 billion in revenue, according to a report. For more, check out GameSpot's GTA 6 review.
