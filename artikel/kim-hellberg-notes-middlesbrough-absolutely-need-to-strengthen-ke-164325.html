In a recent development, middlesbrough are flying high in the Championship, with Saturday’s victory over Bristol City moving them top of the table. Ahead of Tuesday’s clash with Swansea City though, potential January transfer window reinforcements were on Kim Hellberg’s mind.

In a recent development, and, the Boro boss said that the midfield is an area that needs to be bolstered. His statement comes after a long-term hamstring injury to Rokas Pukstas, while Connor Barron is out of favour and also dealing with an injury problem of his own.

Speaking to The Northern Echo, Hellberg stated: “So, if you look at all those things, of course we need to strengthen that in January, absolutely.”. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

It has been revealed that hellberg is right in saying that a newly introduced midfielder is an absolute must for Middlesbrough. The injury to Pukstas and Barron’s absence and lack of favour mean that the Boro boss’ options in the middle of the park have dwindled significantly. However, whoever joins has a tough battle on their hands to get into the team. American teammates Aidan Morris and Sebastian Berhalter have forged an excellent partnership in the first third of the campaign, and they are going to prove tough to dislodge.

Riley McGree can drop into a deeper role if needs be, but he is at his best in a more attacking role. The same goes for Myles Peart-Harris, so a defence-minded midfielder would bring more balance and depth to the department.

The player signed will have to be up for the fight to break into the team though. They may also have to accept that starting opportunities could be somewhat limited, with Morris and Berhalter among the Championship’s best performers of the season to date.

Sources indicate that middlesbrough spent big in the summer, with much of the funds going towards reinforcements in attack and defence. Will Lankshear and Ashley Phillips joined from Spurs for sizeable fees, and a package deal worth £14m was struck for Radek Vitek.

Berhalter, Peart-Harris and Pukstas joined as new midfielders. But of those, Boro’s new American star Berhalter cost less than £900,000 up front, and Peart-Harris was signed on a free transfer.

In a recent development, despite the busy summer transfer window, it is clear that a newly introduced midfielder is needed. Boro can not afford to rest on their laurels in the Championship promotion push, with a deep and talented squad needed to deal with the rigours of a 46-game league season.

At least one midfielder – preferably a defence-first one – could help push Hellberg and co over the line after last season’s Championship play-off heartbreak.
