Mikel Arteta remarked that goalkeeper David Raya will undergo an MRI scan on his injured shoulder as the Gunners boss lamented his side dropping two points following a 2-2 draw at Newcastle.

Eberechi Eze put Arsenal ahead just past the half hour and he saw a free-kick saved on the stroke of half-time after Noni Madueke spurned a couple of chances to put the visitors further in front.

But Newcastle, booed off at the break, rallied on the resumption and Martin Odegaard being dispossessed from a throw-in allowed Harvey Barnes to score while Dan Burn’s free header from a corner caused a goalmouth scramble and Malick Thiaw capitalised to poke home from a yard out. Arsenal then lost goalkeeper Raya to injury, although they claimed a share of the spoils thanks to Madueke 12 minutes from time as the Premier League leaders moved six points clear of Brighton, who could halve the deficit when they play at Hull on Sunday. Arteta said: “My feeling is we have lost two points. We were exceptional in the first half from the beginning to the end. We should have been two, three or four-nil up clearly.

In a recent development, “When you don’t do that then the opponent is going to try to react and right from the beginning (of the second half) we weren’t at the races. One thing is to give them belief and another thing is to give them a goal. We did both.

Sources indicate that “After that, we came back and we were exceptional. We scored the second goal and we had opportunities to go and kill the game. We lacked a moment of magic to be able to do that.

“It’s a pity because the margin should have been much bigger for the level we highlighted. I never remember that we played at that level here, never. We have won football matches playing much worse than we did today here.”.

Raya was forced off in the 70th minute, having received treatment when he slumped to the floor after coming out to collect a corner, and left the field clutching his arm with an apparent shoulder problem.

According to recent reports, arteta mentioned: “Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is never good news. He will get assessed now and he will probably have an MRI tomorrow to see what it is exactly because at the moment, I don’t know.”.

Bruno Guimaraes also made way when Raya was withdrawn, with the former Newcastle midfielder’s every touch heavily barracked on his first return to Tyneside since his £75m move in the summer.

It has been revealed that arteta, though, spoke favourably of the Brazilian’s performance, adding: “It’s never easy, emotionally, you have so much going on, especially the way he feels connected to this club. But I thought he had a very good performance.”.

Matthias Jaissle reflected on a performance of two halves – but Newcastle’s head coach hopes they can take their second 45-minute display into the rest of the season.

The German stated: “It was pretty clear in the first half we were not that front-foot or that intense, somehow hesitating to make the last step or last yard.

“This is something you can’t afford against a top team. But the second half was pure belief, pure conviction, no hesitation any more, pure trust between the team-mates that they are doing their jobs.

“This is the Newcastle DNA and I hope we can build on the second half.”.
