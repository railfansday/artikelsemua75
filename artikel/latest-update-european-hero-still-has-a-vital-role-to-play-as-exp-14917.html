Sources indicate that west Ham’s veteran midfielder has been the subject of an honest interview in his home country on Czech radio coinciding with the release of his autobiography. One of the more revealing comments Tomas Soucek made concerns the pressure and the expectation of playing in front of a substantial audience – such as West Ham will undoubtedly face tomorrow as the season re-commences with a home tie at London Stadium in front of 60,000 expectant supporters.

Sources indicate that unlike several others, from his words it is clear that Soucek seems to thrive on the pressure and the weight of expectation upon him. As one of only a handful of medal winners still at the club from the 2023 campaign, Soucek’s experience should become a steadying influence in the dressing room even though he mostly starts games on the bench these days.
