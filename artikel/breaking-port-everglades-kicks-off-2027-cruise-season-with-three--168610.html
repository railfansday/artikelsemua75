Port Everglades kicked off its 2027 cruise season with several events welcoming high-profile ships as their homeport.

“Port Everglades is honored to be selected as the homeport for such historic, innovative and beautifully designed ships,” said Port Director Joseph Morris.

“The County and port remain focused on cultivating key client relationships that facilitate a positive experience for cruise guests as part of the County’s ongoing efforts to ensure economic development.”.

According to recent reports, on November 3, Commissioner Alexandra P. Davis presented the ceremonial Key to the County while Morris exchanged ceremonial plaques with the Orient Express Corinthian Captain Eric Saint-Plancat.

On November 11, Davis presented the honorary Key to the County to Crystal Symphony Captain Tonci Hladilo and exchanged plaques with him.

That same day, Davis attended the Veterans Day Naming Ceremony for the 7,600-guest Legend of the Seas, which recently completed its first season in Europe.

On November 14 at the Plaque & Key Exchange, Broward County Vice-Mayor Michael Udine and Commissioner Steve Geller bestowed the Key to the County while Morris presented a commemorative plaque to Legend of the Seas’ Captain Sindre Borsheim.

All three captains also received the port’s Challenge Coins.

2026 was another record year for the Port, in which it welcomed 5.38 million guests.

This season, it will host 40 different cruise ships from nine lines. Apart from the three mentioned above, this includes Celebrity Cruises, Cunard Line, Disney Cruise Line, Holland America Line, Princess Cruises and Viking Ocean Cruises.
