A split-second, a zoomed in frame, an element you’ve never noticed: close-up photography puts the tiniest details into focus.

Sources indicate that the Close-up Photographer of the Year 8 (CUPOTY 8) awards honor the greatest achievements in close-up, macro, and micro photography. A record-setting 15,388 images were entered into this year’s competition before being whittled down to a shortlist of 59 images across 11 categories.

According to recent reports, “Close-up photography has a wonderful way of revealing details and perspectives that often go unnoticed,” competition co-founder Tracy Calder stated in a statement. “This year’s shortlist is a fabulous showcase of imagination, technical expertise and natural curiosity.”.

The overall winner of CUPOTY 8 will be unveiled in January 2027.
