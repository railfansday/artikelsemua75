It has been revealed that 2026 really has been one of the biggest years in the history of the Pokémon franchise so far, as it celebrated 30 years since its initial arrival on the scene in the mid-1990s. If you are a fan of the TCG, then the 30th anniversary set likely drew you in, if you managed to pick up some packs to rip yourself.

It has been revealed that at the same time, fans were also treated to a raft of announcements in other mediums, including confirmation of the first games of the tenth generation, Winds and Waves. There were also myriad physical releases for everyone's home collections, in what ended up being the perfect opportunity for the community to come together and mark a huge milestone for the franchise we all love. Now, those in charge have managed to sneak in one more announcement before the year draws to a close, and it's something the series hasn't really seen up to this point.

Sources indicate that over on Twitter/X, the official page for The Pokémon Organization in Japan confirmed the release of an upcoming music video entitled Evergreen. The post reads:. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

Sources indicate that to be continued with Pokémon. The Pokémon Special Music Video “Evergreen” premieres on Monday, November 30 at 11 p.m.!

Song: “Evergreen” by Official HIGE DANdism.

You can also “set a reminder” here!

The music video is set to premiere in six days on November 30, and they declared it with exactly 151 hours to go before its release, in a nice little nod to the set of Pokémon that started it all. The response to the announcement from fans was also overwhelmingly positive, as they flooded the comments section to share their anticipation and delight at those involved in the project.

"The ridiculously star-studded production team is insane, but the “151 hours until release” in the replies gave me goosebumps," one comment read.

While another added, "Yogee Upcoming Waves × Pokémon × MADHOUSE × director Naoko Yamada—what an incredible lineup. A new Pokémon story brought to life through music and visuals—I can’t wait! November 30 can’t come soon enough.".

"Director Naoko Yamada has worked on The Tale of the Heike and The Night Beyond the Tricornered Window, so I’m really looking forward to this. Can’t wait!" a third stated.

"Setting the countdown to “151 hours until release” is such a classy move, going straight for the fans who’ve been into Pokémon since the original," another said.

There's no real indication just yet about what the music video will contain or what the theme will be, but given that it's still a part of the 30th anniversary celebration, expect Pokémon from across the different eras to appear.

Outside of that, we're going to have to sit tight as Pokémon gaming fans to wait and see what Game Freak are cooking up with their next efforts. There's no doubt that fans are looking for something quite different to refresh the gameplay loop and experience, so let's hope they take a relatively massive swing to make that a reality.

Pokémon is a Japanese multimedia franchise created by Satoshi Tajiri and Ken Sugimori, rolled out by Nintendo, Game Freak, and Creatures in 1996. Centered on fictional creatures called Pokémon, the series spans video games, trading cards, animation, films, merchandise, and competitive events. Players assume the role of Trainers who capture, battle, and trade Pokémon. It is one of the highest-grossing entertainment franchises in history.
