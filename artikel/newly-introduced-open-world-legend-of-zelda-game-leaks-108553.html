When it comes to open-world settings in The Legend of Zelda series, only The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild and The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom fall under the bracket. Offering fully open-world maps, complete with various dungeons, side missions, and points of interest, 2017's Tears of the Kingdom was the first in the long-running Nintendo franchise to open up the fantasy world for players, and now, another open-world Zelda game has been leaked.

Up until 2017, The Legend of Zelda entries focused more on a semi-open and non-linear formula, but despite receiving some criticism for its artstyle, both Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom were praised for their stunning maps that were full to the brim with activities, especially the latter which introduced brand-latest mechanics such as construction. Recent findings suggest that if it's time to move on from the Tears of the Kingdom Hyrule that's now spanned two games, it hasn't overstayed its welcome. The memories this game is capable of creating just because of its ambitious systems mean that no two players will ever have the same experience - except that of joy, and the excitement that comes with unknown possibilities. Anyone worried that there would be some fatal flaw that came to ruin what seemed to be a can't-miss Switch launch can now rest easy. Tears of the Kingdom is a monumental achievement, and it's going to be talked about relentlessly for years to come.

With reports previously suggesting that the Wilds' subseries had come to an end after just two entries, it seems as though this may not have been the truth, as recent leaks suggest that a third entry may right now be in development at Nintendo.

The news comes as a shock, considering that Nintendo is not known for making direct sequels, let alone a three-part series, but it seems as though the success of Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom may just have made the decision for the company.

During episode 294 of the Xbox Era Podcast, Shpeshal Nick and Jeff Grubb discussed rumors over a potential third installment in the Wilds subseries, and according to them, it will include even more dimension hopping which will expand the open-world even further.

It has been revealed that according to the ongoing rumors, players will be able to travel through various dimensions through tears in reality and, as such, they will be able to solve puzzles by bringing various items through the different realms. One leak suggests that we will need to lure a charging bull towards a target in one dimension, in order to open a door in another, for example.

Of course, these rumors should be taken with a pinch of salt, as Nintendo has not hinted at potentially continuing the Wilds' subseries into the future. However, with previous entries being such a success, and the open-world formula having no restrictions, it would not be surprising to see Link return to a sprawling Hyrule in the future.
