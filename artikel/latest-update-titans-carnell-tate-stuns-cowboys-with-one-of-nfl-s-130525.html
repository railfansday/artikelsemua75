In a recent development, the Dallas Cowboys made a change on defense ahead of their matchup with the Tennessee Titans, stripping defensive coordinator Christian Parker of play-calling duties and handing them to Mike Pettine. The change didn't help Dallas prevent one of the wildest touchdowns you'll see this season, as Titans rookie Carnell Tate broke loose for a 59-yard score on a play where he wasn't touched down.

Sources indicate that in the third quarter, Cam Ward hit Tate out to the short left on a slant. The rookie wideout went down on his rear end, but got up and ran another 50 yards to the end zone!

Sources indicate that it looked like Tate was touched down by Cobie Durant, but as it turns out, he got up in time and needed to be tackled. Check out what happened here:.
