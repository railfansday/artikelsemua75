Fairly, a Portland, Ore.-based vacation rental startup led by Vacasa founder Eric Breon, raised a $22.4 million Series A round, the company announced Tuesday. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

Oregon Venture Fund led the round, with participation from Elevate Capital and Breon.

Fairly pairs vacation rental owners with local caretakers who look after the homes. Its software handles pricing, bookings and scheduling, and uses AI to draft routine guest messages and flag maintenance problems. (Related: Latest Update: Jimmy John’s reveals Christmas Vacation ...)

Guests booked more than $66 million in reservations through Fairly over the past year, up from about $5 million the year before, the manufacturer said. Nearly 900 caretakers now manage about 2,000 homes across 47 states, Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands.

Sources indicate that owners pay a single 20% fee, which Fairly splits with the caretaker, compared with the 25% to 40% the tech giant says other managers typically charge. Fairly also says it doesn’t tack extra fees onto guest bills, so guests pay less overall.

Recent findings suggest that “At Vacasa, I led a 7,000-person team, and delivering quality, individualized care at that scale is not an easy task,” Breon remarked in a statement. “At Fairly, we take a completely different approach: we empower dedicated, hyperlocal caretakers to care for homes, and our systems do the heavy lifting around them.”.

Breon founded Vacasa in Portland in 2009 and spent more than a decade there. The company went public via a SPAC deal in 2021 and was acquired in May 2025 by Phoenix-based Casago, a vacation rental manager.

In a recent development, fairly, founded in 2023, emerged from stealth in December 2024 with $10.1 million in pre-seed funding. The manufacturer has now raised $32.5 million in total.

Fairly now employs 106 people, up from 25 at its launch in 2024. Co-founder Subechya Person remains chief product officer; co-founder and former CTO Jeff Flitton left the organization in February.
