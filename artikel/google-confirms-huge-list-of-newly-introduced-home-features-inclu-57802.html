Google Home is constantly rolling out upcoming updates and, today, the tech giant is detailing some of its latest updates including a faster Gemini for Home experience and an upcoming Nest Thermostat upgrade.

In a post today, Google details several Google Home updates that are widely rolling out to users. (Related: Industry News: My Journey to a serverless transformers ...)

The first was announced last week – home keys in Google Wallet. This leverages compatible NFC and UWB (Aliro) locks on newer Pixel and select Samsung Galaxy smartphones, letting you unlock your door by simply tapping your phone.

Cameras are also getting an upgrade for Activity Zones:.

According to recent reports, …we’ve tightened Activity Zone boundary precision on our newest wired cameras to significantly reduce false alerts from passing cars, animals, and motion just outside your zone borders. We also redesigned the camera settings page to put Activity Zones front and center, and added a confirmation prompt on iOS when exiting an edited zone so you never accidentally lose your custom boundaries.

The Google Home website also gets faster scrubbing and upcoming keyboard shortcuts – Left or Right arrow keys skip back or forward in 5-second increments, rapidly to jump 30 seconds or more. (Related: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Faster Language M...)

Gemini for Home continues to get faster, with Google saying that the latest updates have resulted in an overall speed improvement of 20% on “everyday requests.” Gemini for Home has also expanded to six new countries including India, Taiwan, Singapore, Colombia, South Korea, and Brazil starting “next month.”.

Finally, Google is also preparing some useful upgrades for Nest Thermostat.

The first is a dashboard for Nest Thermostat owners in the US and Canada that indicates “estimated cumulative savings” spurred by their thermostat. You’ll be able to see these savings in “the Climate space, thermostat controller, and Favorites widget.” The second is the abiltiy to use third-party room temperature sensors with the 4th-Gen Nest Learning Thermostat. Google has its own sensor for this that sells for $40, but there are more affordable alternatives that often bring more functionality too, so this is a really nice addition. Compatible brands will include IKEA, Eve, and Aqara.

According to recent reports, starting in December, Nest thermostat owners in the U.S. and Canada can check the app to see estimated cumulative savings. And they can also balance hot and cold spots with compatible third-party room sensors with their 4th-gen Nest Learning Thermostat.

Recent findings suggest that A third patch is to the Dynamic Farsight faces on the latest Nest Learning Thermostat. “Newly introduced” faces are coming with “designs to match your aesthetics,” while a new Lunar face will track moon phases.

Dynamic Farsight already lets you see the time, temperature, weather, and air quality from across the room, and starting this December, we’re rolling out latest Dynamic Farsight faces with designs that match your aesthetics. Keep an eye out for seasonal animations on the Temperature face, or switch to our new Lunar face to track the phases of the moon right on your wall.
