The Partenopei have lost five of their last seven group stage/league phase matches in the Champions League, as many defeats as they suffered at this stage of the competition across their previous 23 combined.

Napoli have also been shown more cards than any other team in the Champions League this season (13 – 12 yellows, one red), including five in their defeat to Porto last time out.

With league phase games against Club Brugge, Sabah and Viking still to come, Allegri insisted there is no added pressure ahead of their trip to Etihad Stadium.

"I just said that it won’t be decisive, because mathematically, even if we win, six points won’t be enough to qualify," said Allegri.
