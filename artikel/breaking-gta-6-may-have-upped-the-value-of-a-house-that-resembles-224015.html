📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

GTA 6 is and will continue to have a huge cultural impact for years to come. What I wasn’t expecting was for the game to seemingly affect house prices. That appears to be what has happened to a home listed for sale in Key Largo, Florida, simply because it kind of looks like the house you start your journey in with Jason.

In a recent development, complex appears to be the cause of the listing going viral. The account shared side-by-side images of the real house in Key Largo and Jason’s in GTA 6, boldly confirming that the former was the inspiration for the latter. (Related: Man calls police on his ex for taking back her Xbox acc...)

According to recent reports, realtormoc, a real estate account on Instagram, has also shared information about the property, which is listed on Zillow, with GTA 6 fans having a little fun with it in the replies. Most of them are jokes about Brian, the character who owns the house in the game, trying to make some extra cash from it, while my personal favorite reply is the one that reads, “Hey, I forgot something under the floorboards, I’ll call by at the weekend .”.

In a recent development, the house is listed for $739,000, which appears to be quite a bit more than it was worth as recently as a month or two ago. Zillow’s “Zestimate” for the two-bedroom home is $675,000, so the asking price is considerably higher. Those who have played through the first chapter of GTA 6 know damn well that Jason and Lucia weren’t living in a house worth that much, and that’s because despite the viral hype surrounding this real-life house that kind of resembles the one from GTA 6, it isn’t the house that inspired Jason’s starter home at all. (Related: Tech Report: One Piece Chapter 1200: Release date & maj...)

According to recent reports, that house has already been discovered by people far more talented than those who saw a blue house in Florida on stilts and connected two very distant dots. Shared by GTABOOM, the house that almost certainly inspired Jason’s much more weathered waterfront home is hundreds of miles away from this one. That’s according to GTA mapping sites that used GTADB to reach that conclusion.

Sources indicate that the house that’s more likely to be the one Rockstar used to create Jason’s is far smaller and less looked after, as opposed to the shiny, polished house on stilts you can buy for an inflated $739,000. The potentially real Jason house isn’t for sale, but is estimated to be worth around $257,000, which seems far more fitting for the type of life Jason and Lucia have at the start of the game.

It has been revealed that if you were to walk around Key Largo, you would almost certainly see dozens of houses that look like they’ve been ripped right out of GTA 6 because Key Lento, the fictional starting point of the game, is based on that particular part of Florida. It’s only because the $739k house is blue and listed for sale that it has been arbitrarily and falsely dubbed the house that inspired Jason’s in GTA 6.
