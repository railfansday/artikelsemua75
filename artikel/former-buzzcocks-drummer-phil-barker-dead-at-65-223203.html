Phil Barker, drummer for British punk rock band the Buzzcocks from 1992 through 2006, died on Nov. 21 at age 65 on the Isle of Wight, England. His death following a battle with an unspecified form of cancer was unveiled by the band on Nov. 26.

According to recent reports, “We’re saddened by the news of the passing of our longtime drummer Phil Barker,” the band wrote on their website. “He died peacefully in his sleep on Saturday, November 21, 2026 on the Isle of Wight, after a five-year battle with cancer.
