Generative AI has been in the limelight for some time now, with many fearing what effect it could have on game development and employment – including Capcom veteran and Resident Evil creator Shinji Mikami, apparently.

According to recent reports, speaking with Inven Global in a new interview, he says he sometimes wishes that the highly controversial technology would altogether disappear – not just from the video game industry, but from the world in its entirety. This makes sense, seeing as gen AI is now just about everywhere, from creative spaces to more technical areas like those involving coding or other forms of math and sciences.
