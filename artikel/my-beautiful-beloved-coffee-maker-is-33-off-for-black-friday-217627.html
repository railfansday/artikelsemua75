Every year one of my favourite items in my house, the Moccamaster Select coffee maker, gets a nice discount for Black Friday, and I tell you all to buy it because it's a beautiful object and, almost as importantly, it makes delicious coffee. Welcome to the 2026 edition.

In a recent development, moccamaster has knocked 33% of its entire range of Select coffee makers at Amazon, bringing the price of the KBGV model down from $369 to $247.95. That's a massive saving of $122 – and one that I'm frankly furious to have missed out on myself. (Muggins over here paid full price for the thing in 2022.). Meanwhile, the KBT model, which features a manually adjustable drip-stop, is also 33% off, down from $349 to $233. This version offers a little more control over how fast coffee flows into the carafe.
