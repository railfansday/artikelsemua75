Donte DiVincenzo is one step closer to returning to play, seven months after rupturing his right Achilles tendon.

Recent findings suggest that the Minnesota Timberwolves unveiled on Tuesday that the 29-year-old guard has been cleared for team basketball activities. DiVincenzo suffered the injury during Game 4 of the opening round of the Western Conference playoffs in April.

Recent findings suggest that according to The Athletic's Jon Krawczynski, DiVincenzo is aiming to return to play in January. No official timeline has been confirmed.

Industry observers note that diVincenzo tore his Achilles on April 25 and underwent surgery one day later. A return nine months later would beat that of Jayson Tatum of the Boston Celtics (298 days). (Related: Industry News: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Hugg...)

Sources indicate that "I'm going to push, push, push," DiVincenzo mentioned in October. "That was the biggest thing for me. They've had to tell me let's wait another week or two versus, 'c'mon buddy, let's get going.'".

DiVincenzo played in all 82 regular-season games in 2025-26 and averaged 12.2 points, 4.1 rebounds, 3.8 assists and 1.3 steals per game.
