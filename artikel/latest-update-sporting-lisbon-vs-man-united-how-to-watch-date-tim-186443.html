Manchester United will be aiming to return to winning ways when they continue their Champions League campaign away to Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday night.

Mauricio Pochettino's side are 17th in the overall table, boasting six points from four matches, while Sporting sit 10th in the division, picking up nine points from four games.

Here, Sports Mole provides details of how to tune into the contest.

The Champions League match will kick off at 8pm UK time on Wednesday.

The Champions League match between Sporting and Man United will be played at the Portuguese club's home ground of Estadio Jose Alvalade.

Man United's only previous visit to Sporting in the Champions League came back in 2007, with Cristiano Ronaldo's goal handing the Red Devils a 1-0 victory in the group-stage fixture.

The Champions League match will be shown live on TNT Sports 1.

The 20-time English champions are presently involved in a relegation battle in the Premier League table, and they have not managed to win a domestic match since the end of August. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

That mentioned, Man United have picked up two Champions League wins this season against Sabah and Roma, and they are in a relatively solid spot in the table at this stage of proceedings, only three points behind eighth-placed Liverpool.

Sporting have three wins from their four Champions League matches this term, though, and are certain to provide tough opposition for a struggling Man United.

Bruno Fernandes is set to captain Man United against his former club, and the Red Devils desperately need some inspiration from the Portugal international.
