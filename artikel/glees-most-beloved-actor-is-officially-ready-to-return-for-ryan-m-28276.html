Recent findings suggest that ryan Murphy has been as busy as ever in 2026. Although The Shards and The Beauty weren't quite the hits he would've hoped for, the latest installment in his horror anthology series, Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story, was once again a smash-hit success on Netflix. Ella Beatty, the daughter of Annette Bening and Warren Beatty, stars as the first female serial killer in the series, with plenty of other famous faces taking part. Despite facing critical backlash, the series was a ratings triumph, earning 12.2 million views on Netflix in its first four days.

But the biggest story to come from the so-called 'Murphyverse' in 2026 isn't the success of The Lizzie Borden Story, it's that Murphy is returning to his most famous series. He is teaming back up with Brad Falchuk and Ian Brennan for a reboot of the show-choir comedy phenomenon Glee. During its incredible run, Glee picked up six Primetime Emmy Awards and four Golden Globes, and even once scored 26.8 million viewers for its post-Super Bowl special, "The Sue Sylvester Shuffle.". It's that very character name that many remember most fondly about the series, and fans would certainly argue that any reboot would be incomplete without the return of Jane Lynch's iconic red tracksuit-wearing, student-shoving cheerleading coach. Thus far, Lynch's return has not been confirmed, but the woman herself has now spoken out about her desire to be part of the reboot. Appearing on The Jennifer Hudson Show, Lynch mentioned, "If I am asked to partake, the answer will be yes," adding that she "cannot wait" for the reboot. She continued:.

Recent findings suggest that "Oh, I would love to do Sue Sylvester for the rest of my life. You know, I wonder though how far we can take her now. Because if you look out in the world, there are a lot of Sue Sylvesters out there.".

Several of the original cohort from William McKinley High School have already signed on to be part of the new New Directions. In the least surprising news, it was confirmed that Lea Michele would return as Rachel Berry, although whether flying high on Broadway or back in Lima, Ohio, is anyone's guess. It was also confirmed that Chord Overstreet as Sam and Heather Morris as Brittany S. Pierce would join Rachel in the reboot.
