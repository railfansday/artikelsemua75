It has been revealed that according to a Foundation for Individual Rights in Education survey, 9% of Americans have full confidence that the Democratic Party will protect their free speech rights, and a statistically indistinguishable 10% have full confidence that the Republican Party will do so. Assuming no overlap between the two groups, that means 80% of Americans do not have full confidence in either party on this, which of course is the right answer.
