Tech Report: UTMB Moves to Wednesday in Major 2027 Schedule Shakeup

According to recent reports, the 2027 UTMB week-long festival events will take place on Sunday, August 22 to August 28. The flagship Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc (UTMB) will start on Wednesday, August 25, Trail Runner Chamonix correspondent Doug Mayer confirmed with UTMB officials. Industry observers note that there are certain immutable facts in the world of trail running. Kilian and Courtney are the Greatest of All Time. A trailhead IPA will always be the best recovery drink. And UTMB week at the end of August in Chamonix, France, culminates with a 108-mile lap around Mont Blanc otherwise known as the eponymous UTMB. One of those things will change in 2027, as the world’s most famous trail running race series juggles its long-established order of events, Trail Runner learned from UTMB officials.

It has been revealed that for the past eight years, the alphabet stew of race acronyms comprising UTMB week started with the epic 300-kilometer PTL at the beginning of the week, followed by MCC (40K), TDS (148K), and YCC (a 5-15K youth event), before the three UTMB World Series Finals races: OCC (57K), CCC (108K) and finally UTMB (174K). A 15K event, ETC, was added in Courmayeur, Italy, in 2022. (Related: See where votes are being counted in Senate and House m...)

There are three notable changes for next year’s event:.

Race start times are expected to stay largely the same for the UTMB World Series Final races. Start times for other races will be forthcoming shortly.

It has been revealed that two blazing hot meteors of the global sports world are colliding head on. The International Cycling Union (UCI) Cycling World Championships are taking place in France’s Haute Savoie region—which includes Chamonix. The dates? August 24 to September 5.

And one date looms particularly large: Sunday, August 29, when the UCI elite men’s road race is scheduled to take place in the towns of Sallanches and Domancy, just a handful of kilometers from Chamonix.

After the UCI revealed the dates for the world championships, President of the French Cycling Federation Michel Callot suggested that UTMB shift its dates.

UTMB couldn’t move into September, as the Mont Blanc Tunnel that connects Chamonix with Courmayeur, Italy, is tentatively slated to be closed again for maintenance starting on Monday, August 30. The tunnel is critical to the infrastructure of the race series—in fact, it was the reopening of the tunnel in March, 2002, after a tragic fire that forced a nearly three-year closure, that allowed race organizers to host the first edition of UTMB a year later.

Earlier dates presented their own challenges, too. “We would have been right in the middle of the tourist season,” Isabelle Viseux-Poletti, director of UTMB Mont-Blanc, tells Trail Runner. “In terms of visitor numbers and the number of people using the trails, that simply wouldn’t have worked. Everyone around the table agreed on that point.”.

Beyond UTMB officials, stakeholders in this decision included the towns up and down the Chamonix Valley and the regional government. In the end, they went with the most reasonable option: juggling the flow of the events to better accommodate the regional impacts, and shifting UTMB week a day earlier to avoid a literal collision of events on Sunday, August 29.

It has been revealed that the 2027 schedule was developed in close coordination with the office of Chamonix Mayor François Xavier Laffin. Laffin, who himself raced UTMB in 2007, came into office this past spring promising to review UTMB’s relationship with the town, with an eye towards mitigating the growing impacts of the race on the local population and the valley’s fragile alpine ecosystem.

Those two sporting event meteors have been heading towards a collision for more than a year now.

This will be the first time the UCI is holding a world championship that brings together all of its disciplines in the same place. That includes 20 events ranging from road cycling to mountain biking, para-cycling, and BMX. The overlap with UTMB in a region about the size of Rhode Island triggered a range of concerns about transportation, safety, and accommodations.

Sources indicate that from the very beginning, the UCI worked jointly with UTMB. “We’ve been discussing this for quite a long time,” Viseux-Poletti claims. ”From the outset, we’ve been working collaboratively to find solutions.”.

But coordinating two significant sporting events is about much more than just aligning the dates, and UTMB officials had to wait until the UCI could share details about its needs, too. “In particular, we needed to understand their requirements regarding road access and law enforcement,” Viseux-Poletti notes. Predictably, some of those decisions took months. And so, it was only in the last several months that coordinating decisions between the entities began in earnest.

Could this schedule change-up, which further draws attention to the marquee events of the week, be a harbinger of things to come? UTMB officials are offering no clues.

It is clear, however, that juggling the schedule of UTMB’s many races opens a Pandora’s box of coordination headaches. There are three countries with whom to deal, plus regional authorities, nearly two dozen towns, and multiple public and private rescue services. Even for onlookers, it’s migraine-inducing.

UTMB officials declined to comment about whether this new schedule will remain the same in 2028. But they did say more changes are coming. Soon after the 2026 UTMB World Series Finals ended, UTMB officials and the Chamonix’s Mayor’s office began a series of meetings to address areas of concern raised by the mayor. Both parties have promised changes to address concerns. Viseux-Poletti expects the race series and the mayor’s office to jointly announce the news in about a month.
