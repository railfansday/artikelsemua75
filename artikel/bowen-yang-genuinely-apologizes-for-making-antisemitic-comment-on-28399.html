Bowen Yang has broken his silence after making an antisemitic comment on a recent episode of his “Las Culturistas” podcast.

“I want to genuinely apologize for using a word on the podcast without knowing its antisemitic history, especially since we’ve repudiated antisemitism on the show as recently as the last two weeks,” Yang, 36, wrote in a Friday, November 20, Instagram statement. “Particularly upsetting to realize that I adopted language popularized by the far-right movements whose rhetoric we should be wary of and act against,” Yang added in his statement.

After uploading the statement to his Instagram Stories, the comedian added a follow-up slide with the caption, “At the same time, Free Palestine.”.

During his podcast episode last month, guest Edy Modica  claimed Spektor, 46, was “a little Israel hardcore.” In response, Yang mentioned, “Yeah, she’s Zio.” (The term is widely considered to be a slur popularized by former Ku Klux Klan leader David Duke.).

The Grammy nominee, who has not publicly addressed Yang’s comment, has long been vocal about her Jewish faith, especially in the wake of the fatal October 7, 2023, terrorist attacks.

“The only place where I can rest from this fresh reality is sleep,” Spektor told Haaretz in a 2023 interview. “It’s hard for me to understand when I see with my own eyes so much hatred against Jews; it takes a toll on me, I feel it personally.”.

She continued at the time, “I think there are people who are emerging from the shock and trying to be active in any way they can. But above all, many Jews like me are still in shock and sorrow.”.

Spektor has continued to share her opinions about the Israeli-Palestinian conflict via social media, often condemning the deadly Hamas attacks.

“I didn’t think [about] whether or not to upload the posts. If I thought about it, maybe I’d be afraid, but I felt so sad at that moment that I just had to write,” she told the outlet. “If all I received was anger, and not messages of support from Israelis and people around the world who wrote me that it makes them feel less alone, it probably would be much harder. In those moments I stopped feeling so useless. It’s a privilege to help someone feel less alone.”.
