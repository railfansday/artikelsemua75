Cristian Chivu argues that Inter should not be judged off what he described as a poor 10 minutes at the end of their Serie A match against Atalanta over the weekend, but has warned that the Nerazzurri will have to be at their best to beat VfB Stuttgart in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

Chivu sat down for his customary pre-match press conference on Tuesday afternoon ahead of Wednesday’s visit from the Bundesliga side in the Champions League.

According to recent reports, inter began the week in 15th place in the Champions League table after two wins and two defeats in their first four league stage matches in 2026-27. Tuesday’s opponents are one point and one position better off in the table than the Nerazzurri ahead of kick-off. (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

“It’s another chance in the Champions League,” Chivu told reporters on Tuesday (via TMW). “It’s the fifth game, we know what the standings say. We know what our ambitions are and what sort of team we’re facing tomorrow.

In a recent development, “Stuttgart are strong and aggressive. They’ve shown a lot of good things. We have to be ready to do our best. All three strikers are available.”.

In a recent development, creating chances and scoring goals has not been an issue for Inter this season, but at times, their inability to keep them out has cost them points in Serie A and the Champions League. The Nerazzurri had led 3-1 away against Atalanta over the weekend, only to come away with a 4-4 draw by full-time. (Related: Industry News: Hyatt Category 1 Hotels — The Easiest Po...)

Industry observers note that “Inter can’t be judged based on a single part of the game. We know where we are, the numbers say it. We have to analyse clearly, without losing track of the things that we have done well. We will try to improve and smooth out the flaws,” noted Chivu.

Asked what he wants to see his team do differently, Chivu remarked: “We have the obligation to be Inter, there’s not much else to say. We have to be ourselves without making presumptions and while acting with humility.

“We’re aware of what works and what doesn’t work. The negative aspects make headlines and attract more media attention, but we need to be clear-headed and find continuity.”.

When one reporter suggested that Inter’s defence was struggling with ‘old vices’, Chivu responded: “Inter have not returned to its old ways. We can analyse what went wrong in the last 10 minutes against Atalanta, we’ve just talked about it.

“This team has shown character in recent years and it’s not fair to talk about just 10 minutes of one match. When we talk about defending, we’re talking about all 11 players who must have the same intensity. We played a good defensive game in Bergamo, but we needed to do even better.”.

Providing a quick injury patch ahead of the Stuttgart clash, Chivu said that Stones is “doing better, he’ll be called up tomorrow as he was in Bergamo.” Piotr Zielinski, however, “will not be available.”.

Industry observers note that lastly, when asked if he would like any additions to his squad inJanuary, Chivu replied: “We have two players in every position, we even have one extra in midfield. The squad is competitive.

Recent findings suggest that “I can’t make predictions about the market. For me, my players are the best in the world. Let’s not look for excuses. The team is used to working in a certain way and prefers to play rather than train.”.
