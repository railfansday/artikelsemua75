She’s helping build out Collider’s gaming coverage, with Pokémon as one of her specialties. If she isn’t writing about a monster, she’s probably watching one on a tape from her VHS collection.

Kevin McCallister got left behind in Illinois, then lost in Newly introduced York, and somehow Macaulay Culkin made both look like pretty appealing ways to spend Christmas. He could order his own pizza, charge an unreasonable amount of room service to his father’s credit card, and make two burglars regret every decision that brought them to his door. The obvious question for another Home Alone movie is what happens when Kevin becomes the adult somebody else wants out of the house. Culkin has recently been playing Kevin again for McDonald’s holiday campaign, and he told Collider's Hannah Hunt there’s one thing the character would never grow out of: “Defying authority," he remarked, pointing to the rebellious streak that makes Kevin so easy for kids to root for. Culkin figures adults still understand the appeal of getting one over on somebody bigger, too, because no matter how old you are, “You always have a boss.".

Recent findings suggest that A new Home Alone movie with Culkin is now in development at Disney, according to new reports. Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg have reportedly closed a deal to produce through Point Grey and will work with Culkin to develop the script. Disney declined to comment on the report, so as of now there’s no release date attached, but the reported deal gives Kevin’s potential movie comeback a strong producing team.

Culkin spoke with Collider earlier this month about returning as Kevin for McDonald’s, and he sounded happy to have another reason to revisit the character. He described Home Alone as a “wonderful sandbox to play around in,” particularly now that he gets to see people who grew up with the movie introduce it to another generation. “It’s taken on its own life,” he noted. That becomes especially obvious whenever November rolls around. Culkin estimates he gets recognized ten times more between November and January, joking that he “cornered the fourth quarter market.”. Sources indicate that at screenings now, he gets to watch an audience arrive with years of Christmas memories attached to the movie. “They bring their nostalgia with them,” he told Collider. For those viewers, seeing Kevin again comes with the memory of being a kid who thought having an entire house to themselves sounded like the best present imaginable. For now, Culkin’s latest return is the McDonald’s campaign, which he described as a natural fit for a movie families revisit every year. “The actual food and the movie are comfort food, really,” he remarked.

It has been revealed that A new movie would give those families something they haven’t had since Home Alone 2: Lost in New York in 1992: a Christmas movie with Culkin’s Kevin where they don’t already know what happens next. Stay tuned to Collider for updates on the next Home Alone movie.
