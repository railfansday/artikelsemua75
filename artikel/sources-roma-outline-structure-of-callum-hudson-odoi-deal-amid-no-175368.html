Roma are keen to sign Nottingham Forest winger Callum Hudson-Odoi on loan in the January transfer window, sources have told Football Insider.

The Italian giants are hoping to add another wide attacker to their group this winter, and view Hudson-Odoi as one of their leading targets. (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

However, it is understood that the Serie A outfit would prefer a loan move mid-season, rather than forking out for a permanent switch.

Sources say that Chelsea's Jamie Gittens is Roma's "number one" target, but Hudson-Odoi is following closely behind as a backup option.

The 26-year-old has played just 45 minutes of Premier League football at Forest this season, and remains firmly down the pecking order for Oliver Glasner.

An exit from the City Ground has become a genuine possibility for him this winter, with his situation at Forest unlikely to change any time soon.

Hudson-Odoi made 43 appearances for Forest in all competitions last season, and remained one of the first names on their team sheet throughout their managerial changes.

However, things have swiftly unravelled for him following Glasner's summer arrival, and he has been forced to the fringes of the squad.

Forest's new boss has always favoured a 3-4-2-1 formation, and crucially for Hudson-Odoi, that means an absence of wingers in his setup for most games.

At the moment, the Austrian doesn't trust the ex-Chelsea man's defensive skills enough to use him in one of the wing-back positions. (Related: Breaking: Living Trust vs Will: Complete Comparison Gui...)

However, he has also been unable to dislodge the duo of Morgan Gibbs-White and Dan Ndoye to force himself into an attacking midfield role.

Hudson-Odoi's lack of game time has caught the attention of Roma, who are now prepared to offer him greater opportunities in the second half of the season.

Football Insider recently revealed that Premier League clubs are likely to try and sign Forest winger Omari Hutchinson next summer.

The youngster is currently out on loan at AC Milan, but doubts have promptly emerged as to whether the Rossoneri will trigger their purchase option.

Sources say that Hutchinson still has plenty of admirers elsewhere in England, with clubs ready to pounce if he returns to the City Ground.

Hutchinson was in a similar situation to Hudson-Odoi in the summer, but was seen as more of a priority to offload by Forest.
