Laser light reveals where electrons accumulate in atomically thin semiconductor stacks

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 25 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

According to recent reports, stacking semiconductors—the chips that power phones, computers and so much more—enables quicker signal movement with less energy, but how does the process actually work? Turns out shining a literal light on the semiconducting materials can help elucidate how the electrons move in the stack, according to a multi-institutional team in Japan. The approach could help inform the development of more efficient, low-power electronics, the researchers mentioned.
