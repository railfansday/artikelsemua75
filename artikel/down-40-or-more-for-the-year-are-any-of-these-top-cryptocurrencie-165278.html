It's been a difficult year for the crypto market, to say the least. Market bellwether Bitcoin (BTC -1.54%) is down 30% for the year. And six of the top 20 cryptocurrencies, as ranked by market cap, are down 40% or more for the year.

Sources indicate that I wouldn't touch five of these beaten-down altcoins with a 10-foot pole right now. They are simply too risky. But there is one cryptocurrency that could be worth a closer look, as long as you're willing to accept a significant amount of volatility.

The six top cryptocurrencies down 40% or more for the year are: Ethereum (ETH -1.35%), XRP (XRP -0.79%), Solana (SOL -2.48%), Dogecoin (DOGE -0.14%), Cardano (ADA +0.48%), and Bitcoin Cash (BCH -0.31%).

It has been revealed that A few of these are an easy "no." Why would anyone want to invest in a speculative meme coin such as Dogecoin right now? Why invest in Bitcoin Cash if you can invest in Bitcoin instead?

Sources indicate that and why invest in Cardano, given that it's trading 95% below its all-time high? This crypto has essentially dropped to zero, and even its co-founder Charles Hoskinson has warned of "a wave of failures" within the Cardano blockchain ecosystem.

Industry observers note that that leaves XRP, Ethereum, and Solana. Stablecoins continue to erode the core use case for the XRP token, and this cryptocurrency, too, is best left on the sidelines. During the past 90 days, XRP has been one of the worst-performing major cryptocurrencies, and its price has now dropped below the psychologically key level of $1. So, if you're looking for a potential deep value play right now, that leaves Ethereum and Solana. My personal pick is Solana, given that it has emerged as Ethereum's top rival. In fact, it has often been characterized as a potential Ethereum killer due to its much faster speeds, lower costs, and higher throughput. Moreover, if you look at crypto price performance during the past 90 days, three of the top-performing cryptocurrencies in the world have actually been Solana-related. These three cryptocurrencies are Raydium (RAY -3.57%), Jupiter (JUP -4.35%), and Pyth Network (PYTH -2.89%).

Raydium and Jupiter are decentralized exchanges for Solana, while Pyth is a blockchain oracle, similar to Chainlink (LINK +0.50%), bringing real-world data to blockchains. Their ability to outperform the broader market during the past quarter could suggest that Solana blockchain activity is rebounding and that key pieces of the Solana blockchain ecosystem remain healthy.

Recent findings suggest that in part, this is due to Solana doubling down on decentralized finance (DeFi) as a potential growth avenue. DeFi is one of the most important niches of the blockchain world, and Solana has established itself as the No. 2 Layer-1 blockchain network in DeFi, trailing only Ethereum.

Sources indicate that based on the above, Solana could be undervalued at just $73 (as of Nov. 23). The crypto is trading more than 75% below its all-time high of $294 from January 2025. There's just too much potential upside to ignore, and I'm fully expecting a Solana price rally in 2027.
