Recent findings suggest that bradford City man Nick Powell has had an injury-plagued career and has opened up about how he considered retiring last January due to his constant setbacks. But the former Manchester United midfielder has stayed fit this season, and is reaping the rewards for his availability.

On Saturday, Powell secured his first league start of the season and repaid Michael Duff‘s faith by scoring the opening goal in a 2-2 draw with Yorkshire rivals Doncaster Rovers. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

The Bantams saw three points snatched from them after a 95th-minute equaliser by Sean Grehan. Despite the disappointing end to the clash, there were positives to take for the hosts, with Powell’s performance and start being one of them. Recent findings suggest that bradford City boss Duff spoke highly of Powell post-match, acknowledging that it was a risk to start him before highlighting his importance to the side. As quoted by the Yorkshire Post, he said:.

It has been revealed that “It was his first league start (this season), and he’s been here a long time. It was always a risk. There are lengthy conversations from a psychological and a physical point of view, which him, the physios, the sports scientists and myself are all involved in, because he can do things that other players at this level can’t.

Sources indicate that “Nick’s missed a lot of football in his career and he’s now in his later years and it looks like he’s enjoying being out on the pitch. The only reason he’s played at this level is because of his body, his injury history. He’s influential in the group. They look up to him because none of our other players and staff have ever been at Man United.”.

The midfielder-turned-striker has found his feet this season at Valley Parade and is growing in confidence, week by week. His injury record has hampered his chances of breaking into the City starting squad sooner, but right now, the No. 9 spot is his to lose.

The 32-year-old is in a rich vein of form right now, scoring four and assisting one in his last three appearances.

According to recent reports, nobody has ever doubted Powell’s ability on a football pitch, whether that was him playing in the Premier League or when he was playing in League Two. But, the one thing that has held him back in his career is injuries.

Right now though, the Crewe-born man has strung a healthy run of games together, and to go with that, plenty of positive performances too.

Sources indicate that powell’s manager and Bradford City teammates all speak so highly of him and how vital he is to the squad both on and off the pitch. His wealth of experience playing in higher divisions is crucial for the Bantams as they look to secure promotion to the Championship this season.

Recent findings suggest that if Powell can continue his great run of form and maintain his availability during the busiest period of the season, then he will only grow in confidence regarding his body. Even though we are only in November, he has already played in more games this season than in any of the previous two campaigns in the league, with 12 appearances.

According to recent reports, the Bantams are in a tricky spot right now when it comes to league form, having won just one of their last seven. But Powell has been a shining light for Bradford in what has been a difficult run of results.
