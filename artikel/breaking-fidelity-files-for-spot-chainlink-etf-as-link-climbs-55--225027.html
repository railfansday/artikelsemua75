📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent turn of events, Fidelity Investments filed a Form S-1 registration statement with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) to launch a spot Chainlink [LINK] exchange-traded product (ETP).

According to the 25th November filing, the proposed fund is expected to trade on Nasdaq under the provisional ticker FLNK. That noted, the fund would also hold LINK tokens directly. This allows investors to gain exposure to LINK through traditional stock market accounts without having to purchase or manage the tokens themselves. At the same time, the fund aims to track the Fidelity Chainlink Reference Rate, which calculates LINK’s price using a volume-weighted median methodology based on data from eligible spot markets.

Notably, its holdings would be managed by three proposed custodians, including Anchorage Digital Bank, BitGo Bank & Trust, and Coinbase Custody Trust Company.

Recent findings suggest that as expected, this news did have a positive impact on LINK’s price, which was trading at $9.16 at press time after a hike of 5.5% in the past 24 hours. With this, Fidelity becomes the third asset manager to launch a LINK ETF besides Grayscale’s GLNK and Bitwise’s CLNK. (Related: Latest Update: F1 star Max Verstappen played 40 hours o...)

In fact, according to SoSo Value data, GLNK and CLNK have already recorded cumulative net inflows of $128.12 million and $47.88 million, respectively. This in turn has brought their combined cumulative inflows to approximately $176 million. However, neither fund recorded any latest flows on the 25th of November, with both showing $0 in daily net inflows.

Sources indicate that this news comes as the broader cryptocurrency investment products recorded widespread outflows across major digital assets in the month of November. Starting with Bitcoin [BTC] ETFs, which witnessed the largest withdrawals, worth approximately $5.39 billion in net outflows, facing their worst month since launch.

Ethereum [ETH] ETFs too saw $1.30 billion worth of outflows. This was followed by XRP ETFs, which saw $29.18 million in outflows, while Solana [SOL] ETFs registered withdrawals of $57 million. Meanwhile, Dogecoin [DOGE] ETFs recorded relatively smaller outflows of $409,130.

Industry observers note that interestingly, not all products experienced negative flows, as HBAR ETFs attracted approximately $1.71 million in net inflows. This made it the only asset in the sea of ETFs to record positive flows on the day.
