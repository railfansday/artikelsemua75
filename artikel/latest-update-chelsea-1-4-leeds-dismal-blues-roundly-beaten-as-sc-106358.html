It has been revealed that moises Caicedo was forced off after 20 minutes after a landing awkwardly, arguably the point at which the hosts began to lose their way. Calvert-Lewin soon found room again amongst Chelsea’s defence for a header that was this time straight at Emiliano Martinez.
