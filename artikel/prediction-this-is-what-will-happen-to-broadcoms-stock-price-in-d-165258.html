Broadcom (AVGO +3.00%) stock has been crushed in recent months, falling 35% from the 52-week high it reached on June 3, amid dwindling confidence in the semiconductor sector.

It has been revealed that chip stocks, which have delivered incredible gains over the past four years amid the artificial intelligence (AI) boom, have taken a big beating lately. The hotter-than-expected inflation, a hawkish Federal Reserve, and the sustainability of the heavy spending on AI data center infrastructure have led investors to press the sell button and book profits. This explains why Broadcom has taken a big beating lately.

Investors, however, will be looking for a turnaround in the stock's fortunes when it releases its fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter results on Dec. 9. Let's see if the upcoming quarterly results could indeed give Broadcom stock a much-needed boost.

Recent findings suggest that broadcom has beaten Wall Street's earnings per share (EPS) expectations in each of the last four quarters. Broadcom's AI business has played a central role in its outperformance, which isn't surprising as it is the market share leader in custom AI processors, and is also benefiting from the booming demand for networking components.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

The company reportedly controls an estimated 60% of the fast-growing custom AI processor market, according to Counterpoint Research. Not surprisingly, Broadcom's shipments of custom AI processors increased by 3.5x year over year in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. Meanwhile, its revenue from sales of AI networking components is also increasing at a solid pace, jumping 2.5x year over year in fiscal Q3.

Broadcom expects $34.8 billion in fiscal Q4 revenue, an improvement of 93% from the year-ago period. That will be a significant jump over the 28% growth it delivered in the same period last year. The company's AI revenue is expected to have increased by 236% year over year in the previous quarter to $21.7 billion.

However, Broadcom's revenue could exceed expectations. That's because the demand for its AI chips is exceeding supply. It has been working to secure additional supply, which is why its financial performance may be better than expected. Moreover, Broadcom sees phenomenal growth in its AI revenue over the next two fiscal years.

It anticipates AI revenue will almost double in fiscal 2027 to $115 billion, compared with the fiscal 2026 estimate of $58 billion. What's more, it sees $230 billion in AI chip revenue in fiscal 2028. However, these estimates are based on the supply that it has already secured, which explains why Broadcom management noted on the September earnings call that it may increase guidance as it strengthens its supply chain. Sources indicate that on the other hand, the demand for optical networking components that Broadcom designs is "far surpassing supply out there in the industry." This supply shortage should ideally lead to higher prices for optical networking components, which is why Goldman Sachs estimates that optical networking revenue will increase from an estimated $15 billion this year to $154 billion in 2028. The investment bank also notes that an increase in average selling price (ASP) could boost the gross margins of optical component suppliers. All this suggests Broadcom's revenue and earnings could once again surpass Wall Street's expectations when it reports results next month. Additionally, the manufacturer has dropped enough hints that it could continue to upgrade its guidance as supply chain improvements materialize. As such, Broadcom's guidance may surpass expectations, setting the company up for a strong rally in December.

Sources indicate that analysts predict a 71% jump in Broadcom's fiscal 2026 EPS to $11.65, well above the 33% average earnings growth estimated for S&P 500 companies. The chip designer is expected to post another solid 66% increase in EPS next year to $19.33, again exceeding the 17% jump that S&P 500 companies are expected to deliver. The fiscal 2028 EPS estimate is also quite impressive.

It has been revealed that broadcom's forward earnings multiple of 16 is slightly lower than the S&P 500 index's forward earnings multiple of 18. This semiconductor stock should ideally be trading at a premium valuation due to its market-beating EPS growth. Assuming Broadcom trades at 25 times earnings after a year and its EPS reaches $19.33 in fiscal 2027 (based on the consensus estimate seen in the chart above), its stock price could reach $483.

That implies a potential 54% jump in a year. Analysts, however, are more upbeat about Broadcom's performance, as its 12-month median price target of $522 implies 66% gains. All this suggests that Broadcom could be a solid buy ahead of its quarterly report, as strong results could supercharge the stock in December and pave the way for a much-improved performance in 2027.
