Rangers will be holding talks behind the scenes over the possibility of a move to secure further investment in the coming years.

That is according to former Aberdeen chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the 49ers could soon make a move to mirror their latest plan at Leeds United.

The American owners are looking to sell a small percentage of the Premier League outfit, in order to bring in fresh money to help develop the club on and off the pitch.

Wyness believes that Rangers could explore a similar move within the next few years, but still have plenty of improvements to make before they reach that stage.

The disparity between the two clubs is significant at the moment, even though Wyness believes that the 49ers’ plans are beginning to work at Ibrox.

He feels that if the Gers can continue to progress on their current trajectory, then the prospect of similar investment is not out of the question in future.

Aberdeen’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Pittodrie between 2000 and 2004 and now runs a consultancy business advising elite clubs – believes that Rangers fans shouldn’t be concerned over the 49ers’ ability to fund both clubs long-term.

The Scottish businessman sees their push for a latest investment as a move to help realise their ambitions, rather than an indication that they are struggling to help the clubs progress.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider’s Inside Track podcast, he explained how “conversations” are likely already taking place for Rangers to get a similar financial boost.

In a recent development, “Look, the fact is Rangers are not as attractive as Leeds in terms of the revenue situation and the profit going forward. But Rangers are showing signs of turning things around a little bit as a financial model,” he stated. (Related: How to train a new language model from scratch using Tr...)

“Now, I think Rangers should be very happy having the 49ers still as owners there. And I think they're making progress.

“But, I wouldn't be concerned about Leeds getting extra investment because I think Rangers can get that extra investment if they put forward the right case. But they've got to show that they're disciplined, that things are happening in the right way.

Industry observers note that “I think they’ll be the conversations that are going on. They're starting to show signs of doing things correctly and getting the right structure together. So no, I wouldn't be concerned in the slightest if I was a Rangers fan. I think it's just good, prudent management from the 49ers.”. Football Insider recently revealed that Besiktas are ready to make winger Vaclav Cerny available for transfer this winter, amid sustained interest from Rangers.

The Czech ace enjoyed a hugely successful loan spell at Ibrox back in 2024-25, and the Glasgow giants have been trying to secure a reunion ever since.

However, sources say that a move back to Ibrox remains “unlikely” for Cerny in January, given that Rangers were priced out of the deal in the summer.

Besiktas are ready to accept an offer for the wideman, but Rangers may have to break the bank in order to get hold of him.
