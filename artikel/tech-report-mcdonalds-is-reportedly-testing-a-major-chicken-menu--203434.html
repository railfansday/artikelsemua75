Tech Report: McDonald’s Is Reportedly Testing A Major Chicken Menu Overhaul—Here’s What We Know

🌐 Source: Delish • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (404 words, 📖 Standard)

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that mcDonald’s has been paying close attention to its chicken menu lately. Between the return of the Snack Wrap, the introduction of McCrispy Strips, and several newly introduced dipping sauces, it’s clear the chain is looking to become a bigger player in the fast-food chicken game. If the latest reports are true, some of its biggest changes might still be ahead.

McDonald’s is reportedly testing five hand-breaded chicken menu items in select U.S. markets, including Baton Rouge, Louisiana, and the Tucson, Arizona, area. The information comes from McFranchisee, an account on X that regularly shares details about the chain’s operations and upcoming menu changes.

Industry observers note that the biggest difference with this potential menu overhaul is how the chicken would be prepared. Rather than simply introducing upcoming flavors or sauces, McDonald’s is reportedly experimenting with having employees bread the chicken by hand inside its restaurants. Among the alleged test items is a new McSpicy chicken sandwich. According to the report, all five items are also available as meals, with prices ranging from $8.19 to $9.99 at the location pictured in the social media post.

A similar test is also taking place in Ireland.

In a recent development, I’m particularly interested in how hand-breading could change the texture of McDonald’s chicken. Having taste-tested plenty of fast-food chicken sandwiches and tenders, I’ve found that the breading can make or break the entire experience. A crispier, more substantial coating could make McDonald’s a much stronger competitor to chains like Popeyes and Chick-fil-A. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

Rolling out hand-breaded chicken across thousands of restaurants, however, is easier said than done.

Industry observers note that the reported tests are designed to evaluate customer demand, preparation costs, and how the additional steps could affect kitchen operations. Some franchisees have reportedly expressed concerns about increased labor requirements and potential kitchen renovations.

While McDonald’s hasn’t officially confirmed the five-item lineup or specific U.S. test locations, the idea of hand-breaded chicken isn’t entirely out of left field.

Industry observers note that during its November earnings call, the organization confirmed that it had begun setting up operational pilots for hand-breaded chicken in the U.S. and Ireland. It also revealed looks for larger U.S. sales tests at roughly 100 restaurants in early 2027.

In a recent development, for now, there’s no confirmed nationwide U.S. release date or guarantee that all five reported items will make it onto the permanent menu.
