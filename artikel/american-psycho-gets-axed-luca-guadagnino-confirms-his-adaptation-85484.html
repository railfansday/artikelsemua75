Recent findings suggest that luca Guadagnino has to return some videotapes. The Oscar-nominated director is passing on Scott Z. Burns‘ American Psycho adaptation.

While appearing on Deadline’s Crew Call podcast, Guadagnino told Anthony D’Allesandro that he is no longer attached to the projects, two years after we exclusively revealed he and Burns were adapting Bret Easton Ellis’ 1991 novel. “Scott Z. Burns wrote a fantastic script,” stated Guadagnino. “Unfortunately for me, as we speak, it’s not happening.”.

Industry observers note that in October 2024, Deadline revealed that Guadagnino was in final talks with Lionsgate to direct Burns’ adaptation, which would be produced by Frenesy Films, and executive produced by Sam Pressman, the son of Edward R. Pressman, producer of 2000 original movie, through his firm Pressman Film. Watch on Deadline. (Related: Industry News: Take-Two CEO is shutting down questions ...)

Serving as a brief respite from the years-long casting hunt for the next James Bond, there was plenty of speculation about who would play Patrick Bateman. One actor to campaign for the role was Patrick Schwarzenegger, while Guadagnino’s Queer (2024) star Drew Starkey noted he read the “amazing” script.

But in February, Ellis claimed that multiple high-profile actors “have turned it down,” adding that Burns had since completed a upcoming draft after the unnamed talent passed on the previous version.

Ellis teased that the film would be “completely different” from Mary Harron’s 2000 adaptation of American Psycho, which starred Christian Bale as homicidal investment banker Patrick Bateman, the literary character that Ellis created as a satirical take on masculinity. The Harron-helmed adaptation also starred Justin Theroux, Josh Lucas, Chloë Sevigny, Reese Witherspoon, Jared Leto, Willem Dafoe and more.
