When two of Hollywood's biggest directors sit down together, unexpected ideas are bound to come up.

It has been revealed that christopher Nolan's adaptation of The Odyssey follows Odysseus' journey home after the Trojan War. The movie stars Matt Damon as Odysseus alongside a star-studded ensemble including Tom Holland, Anne Hathaway, Robert Pattinson, Zendaya, and Charlize Theron. However, Steven Spielberg believes another story from Greek mythology could be worth exploring. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

According to The Playlist, Spielberg playfully encouraged Nolan to adapt The Iliad, Homer's companion epic to The Odyssey. The suggestion came during a November 17 post-screening Q&A at the DGA Theater in Los Angeles, where Spielberg praised Nolan's filmmaking before proposing another adaptation:.

"The only other thing I want to say, not to challenge you or anything, but there's always 'The Iliad.'".

"No pressure, no pressure. But this could be the most successful movie in history that never had a sequel.".

Nolan laughed at the suggestion, replying, "I think now you're advocating for Universal." Spielberg responded that he was speaking on behalf of Universal Pictures executives Donna Langley and Peter Cramer, adding, "And me too.".

Interestingly, Nolan previously had an opportunity to adapt The Iliad, the companion epic to The Odyssey.The filmmaker was originally attached to direct Troy, the 2004 historical epic inspired by Homer's poem. However, Wolfgang Petersen ultimately directed the movie, which starred Brad Pitt as Achilles.

During the conversation, Nolan reflected on his previous attachment to direct Troy before The Odyssey, revealing that an idea he developed for the movie remained with him for more than two decades. The director had envisioned an image of the Trojan horse buried in sand, comparing it to the Statue of Liberty in Planet of the Apes.

Industry observers note that nolan's interest in Greek mythology also dates back to childhood. The director previously revealed that one of his earliest memories was watching a school production of Ulysses when he was just five or six years old. His brother, Jonathan Nolan, also praised The Odyssey as an incredible achievement, revealing that the two had discussed Christopher's approach to adapting Homer's epic. Recent findings suggest that although The Iliad and The Odyssey are closely connected, the former would not be a traditional sequel. The Iliad centers on events during the Trojan War, particularly the conflict involving Achilles and Agamemnon, while The Odyssey follows the aftermath through Odysseus' journey home.

Recent findings suggest that spielberg's suggestion follows the enormous box office success of The Odyssey, which has grossed over $1.8 billion worldwide. The film became both Nolan's and Universal Pictures' highest-grossing release of all time, while also breaking the record for the highest-grossing R-rated movie worldwide. With the film's historic performance, another Homer adaptation could be an appealing opportunity for Universal.

Sources indicate that coming from a filmmaker behind some of the best movies of all time, such as Jaws, E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial, Jurassic Park, and Schindler's List, Spielberg's suggestion carries weight.

In a recent development, however, Nolan has not declared plans to adapt The Iliad. His producing partner Emma Thomas recently revealed that the director is considering ideas for his next projects on what movies that should come after The Odyssey, although nothing has been decided. Whether Spielberg's encouragement will influence Nolan's next move remains to be seen.
