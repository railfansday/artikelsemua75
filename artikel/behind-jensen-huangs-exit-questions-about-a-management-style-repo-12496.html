Earlier this week at the DealBook conference in Latest York, Elon Musk, beamed in from a trip to China with President Donald Trump, was asked about the ouster of his longtime friend, former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Musk compared it to kicking Michael Jordan off the Chicago Bulls’ roster during his historic run. Of Nvidia’s employees, he stated, “They love [Huang]. They love him.”.

Industry observers note that well, maybe not all of them. Thousands of employees have signed a letter asking for Huang’s return, but others tell The Newly introduced York Times he ruled through praise and fear, as well as by humiliating people. It has been revealed that according to the Times, Huang told subordinates that Nvidia’s wealth meant it wasn’t possible to motivate employees with money, so humiliation was the only lever left. He reportedly told one executive his mother should have aborted him and told another employee he would rip his arms off and beat him to death with them. According to recent reports, naturally, over a 33-year career, regrettable things may have been mentioned, but the Times found a long paper trail that suggests the incidents weren’t isolated. In 2005, for example, Michael Winston, a former HR chief, threatened to sue and called Huang’s conduct the most abusive he’d seen. Nvidia paid him more than $1 million. Huang’s spokesman calls the allegations fabrications.
