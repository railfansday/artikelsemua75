Martin Short and Selena Gomez stopped by Jimmy Kimmel Live to promote the upcoming season of Only Murders in the Building, which is set in London. But before they could discuss the new episodes, Jimmy Kimmel asked Short to revisit a story he told on the late-night show in January, which involved the actor almost ruining Gomez’s wedding to Benny Blanco.

Kimmel urged Short to “summarize the story” before getting to Gomez’s perspective. “First of all, it’s insane because I am blameless in the story,” Short stated. “Selena has the most fabulous, beautiful wedding imaginable. And in this one major tent where the after-ceremony took place. [There were] 18, 20 musicians and a dance floor and a nightclub.”.

In a recent development, he recalled there being sections with sofas and chairs for groups to sit. “And there was this cake there,” he mentioned. “And I just assumed it was a cake for each individual [group]. It turned out to be the wedding cake.” Short, who admitted to “maybe” having had a drink, cut into the cake with a fork as Steve Martin was leaving the party. Kimmel then revealed a photo of the mutilated cake.

In a recent development, “I love the teeth marks of the fork where he tried to save it,” Gomez mentioned. “Really nicely done.”.

Gomez added that Short “apparently insisted” that no one tell her what had happened. “They managed to put it together,” she remarked. “And I didn’t care. I was there, but I didn’t know what happened. And then all of a sudden, somebody comes up and they’re like, ‘Marty cut your cake. And I think he was eating it.’ I was like, ‘Oh!’ So I run and he looks like a little dog with his tail between his legs. I go, ‘I heard you ate my cake.'”.

Recent findings suggest that gomez explained that the cake wasn’t intended for all of the guests, but instead as a moment for her and Blanco. It turned into a moment for her, Blanco, and Short. “We will always have that memory together and, honestly, I will never forget it,” she said.
