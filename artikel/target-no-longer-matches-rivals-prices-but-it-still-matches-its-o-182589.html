Target No Longer Matches Rivals’ Prices – but It Still Matches Its Own

🌐 Source: Moneycrashers • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (467 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Target no longer matches Amazon’s or Walmart’s prices, but it still pays you back when its own price drops.
  • Target’s price match is a price adjustment by another name, and it pays only when you spot the lower price and ask.
  • It has been revealed that for 14 days after a purchase, Target matches its own lower price on the identical item at Target.com, in your local store, or with a deal for Target Circle members.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Target no longer matches Amazon’s or Walmart’s prices, but it still pays you back when its own price drops. If something you bought there gets cheaper at Target.com or in your local store within 14 days, ask for the difference at Guest Services or in Target’s online chat.

Target’s price match is a price adjustment by another name, and it pays only when you spot the lower price and ask. The same item can cost less on Target.com than on your store’s shelf, and a sale can start days after you buy. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

It has been revealed that for 14 days after a purchase, Target matches its own lower price on the identical item at Target.com, in your local store, or with a deal for Target Circle members. That works both ways, so a store purchase can be matched to Target.com’s price and an online order to your local store’s. Under Target’s Holiday Price Match Guarantee, a holiday purchase can be matched to a lower Target price any time through Dec. 24, 2026.

It has been revealed that A store purchase can’t be matched to a different Target store’s price, though, and clearance, closeout, and refurbished items never qualify. Neither does a purchase made with a Target coupon or one that earned a gift card in a Target promotion.

Say you buy a coffee maker at your local Target on Monday, and that Friday Target.com lists the same model for less. Show Guest Services your paper or digital receipt and the listing on your phone, and it refunds the $15 difference between the $60 you paid and the $45 online price. Industry observers note that target won’t accept a screenshot, so show the live price on Target.com or in the Target app, set to the store you’re in. For an online order, chat with Target online or call Guest Services at 1-800-591-3869 with your order number ready.

Sources indicate that another retailer’s lower price hasn’t counted at Target since July 28, 2025. Buy from that store instead, or from one of the stores that still match rivals’ prices.

Keep the receipt from any big Target purchase until its window closes, since Guest Services needs it to refund the difference.
