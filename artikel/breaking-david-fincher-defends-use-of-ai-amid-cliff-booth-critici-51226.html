“I’m not a Luddite, so I don’t fear the unknown,” Fincher told the Los Angeles Times.

In a recent development, “Is everyone satisfied that we have figured this art form out enough that what we really need to do is go back to celluloid?” he continued. “How long have people painted? 40,000 years?
