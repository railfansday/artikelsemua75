How To Get The Legendary Crabshack VF5 SMG In GTA 6

Recent findings suggest that in Grand Theft Auto 6, you will spend quite a bit of time ascending the criminal ladder, maintaining Jason and Lucia's relationship, and exploring Leonida. Unsurprisingly, there are a lot of weird things and people you can come across while exploring in and around Vice City.

One of the strangest is a large mutated crab you can find in the swamps that feels like something ripped out of the Fallout games. If you're looking to find this mutated crustacean and wondering what you will get for your troubles, here is how to find the giant crab, get the legendary Crabshack SMG, and find all the codes for the research lab.

Recent findings suggest that to start, make your way south to Pelican Valley in the Gullet Marsh region. Located around the center of the swamp (circled above) is a lone research station that you can dock your boat next to. If you're struggling to find it, I recommend looking for the building with all the tall antennas sticking out of it, as this can act as a visual marker for where the station is. It has been revealed that additionally, you can visit this area as soon as you can freely roam around as Jason. It's relatively close to his home, making it a nice place to grab a great early-game legendary weapon; however, I recommend leaving the Stash Bag there since you won't be able to sell Marked Bills till later in the game. There's no combat or anything required to enter, as you won't actually be fighting the mutated crab. (Related: Latest Update: Two weeks later, Senate vote count is co...)

Recent findings suggest that once you arrive at the research station, don't bother heading inside just yet. Instead, go around the back of the large house and look for a breaker box on the wall. Approach and interact with the box to restore power to the building. Now go inside the research station and head down the short hallway into the scientist's bedroom.

On the floor near the door are some words carved into the wood. Hold down the Left Trigger when near it to examine the writing and make note of the word CLEAVES that is at the bottom. Once you've examined it, return to the living room and approach the computer in the corner.

It has been revealed that interact with the computer to be prompted to enter the password on the right side of your screen. Select the word "CLEAVES" to gain access to the scientist's notes. There will be a section that states "SAFE CODE = DOUBLE SUCCESS." Once you're done reading, exit the computer to trigger a cutscene where a monstrous crab will attack you.

Recent findings suggest that fight it off via mashing the Square (PlayStation) / X (Xbox) button, and then leave the main building. Go back to where the breaker box was and look for a shack behind the house. Inside, you will find a bunch of terrariums where the crabs were being studied.

Recent findings suggest that specifically, look for a room with a much larger terrarium and examine the clipboard on the table next to it. You'll see that crab 36 was the one that was a success, meaning the code to the safe inside of the research station is 0072.

In a recent development, we get this number because the note on the computer states that the code is DOUBLE SUCCESS, meaning you need to double the number of the crab that survived the experiment. Inside the safe, you will find another note about an emergency protocol and $1,100 to spend.

We're not done yet. After you open the safe, head outside of the main building and look for a metal gate you can walk through along the wall (shown below). Follow the path to another shack on the water and enter the code 0212 at the door to open it.

Next to the body of the scientist, you'll find a Stash Bag with $10,000 Marked Bills and the Crabshack VF5 legendary submachine gun. If you're visiting here early, leave the Stash Bag to grab once you can sell the Marked Bills. You can then open the garage door, allowing you to drive the jet ski out!

Recent findings suggest that remember, once you grab this legendary weapon, it's part of your arsenal forever. You can always pick it up again at any weapons locker throughout the game, so it's a great firearm to snag early to make combat a little easier.
