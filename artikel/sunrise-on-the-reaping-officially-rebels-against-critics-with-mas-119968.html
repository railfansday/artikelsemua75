It has been revealed that the latest installment of The Hunger Games has arrived, and audiences have spoken. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is the second prequel in Suzanne Collins' popular YA dystopian franchise, and it didn't sit well with critics upon release. But what about audiences? Now that the movie has entered theaters, the viewers have spoken about their return to Panem. (Related: The Age of Machine Learning As Code Has Arrived...)

Sunrise on the Reaping takes place 24 years before the events of the first Hunger Games story, during the 2nd Quarter Quell, a special Hunger Games with new rules added every 25 years to remind the districts of their defeat in the rebellion. This is the game Haymitch Abernathy participated in, now played by Joseph Zada, and the new rules for this Hunger Games were that each district would send double the number of tributes, increasing the total from 24 to 48 children in the arena.

It has been revealed that when the critics' score came out, Sunrise on the Reaping debuted with a 74% rating on Rotten Tomatoes, and as of writing, the number has dropped to 67%. However, now that the movie has entered theaters, audiences disagree, and it sits at a 95% Verified Hot Audience Score. Audience reception was also positive on Letterboxd, with the film earning a 4.1-star rating. Collider's Victoria Luxford gave Sunrise on the Reaping an 8/10 score in her review. She praised the movie's "dark and gritty" portrayal of Collins' world but criticized the runtime, especially for those "not heavily invested" in the series.

Sources indicate that ever since The Hunger Games film franchise began in 2012, six films have been released: four main movies and two prequels. According to Rotten Tomatoes critics' scores, the highest-rated Hunger Games movie is the sequel, The Hunger Games: Catching Fire, with a 90% score, followed by the first Hunger Games movie at 85%. And despite Sunrise on the Reaping's low critics' score, the lowest-rated Hunger Games movie from critics is the first prequel film, The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, with a 64% rating.

Audiences, however, viewed the franchise differently: Sunrise on the Reaping is the audience's highest-rated Hunger Games movie ever, followed by Catching Fire with an 89% rating, then the first Hunger Games movie with an 81% audience score. The audience's lowest-rated installment is The Hunger Games: Mockingjay, Part 2, with an audience score of 66% on Rotten Tomatoes.

According to recent reports, the Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is now showing in theaters. Follow Collider for more updates.

Taking place in Panem 24 years prior to the original series, this upcoming Hunger Games entry focuses on the reaping of the 50th Hunger Games.
