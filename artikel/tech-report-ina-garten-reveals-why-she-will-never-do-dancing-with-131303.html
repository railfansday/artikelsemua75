Celebrity Chef Ina Garten revealed this week on her Happy Hour podcast that she once turned down Dancing with the Stars.

Discussing fear, overcoming it and whether she had ever turned something down because it was “too scary,” Garten said, “I think I absolutely have…Somebody asked me to do Dancing with the Stars. I thought nobody needs to see that. That’s not happening.”. (Related: Animal control called to rescue alligator that turned o...)

Guest Rose Byrne agreed, ‘I would be terrified of that.” Watch on Deadline.

Recent findings suggest that “If you push yourself enough, then you have to figure out how to deal with fear. I mean, I feel like um it’s always with me. I never get rid of it. It’s like in the side car next to me while I’m going along.”.
