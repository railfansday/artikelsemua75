📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Macy's on Wednesday declared it's postponing its Thanksgiving Day Parade, originally scheduled for Thursday, due to severe weather.

The parade, held each year on Thanksgiving morning, will now take place on Saturday at 8:30 a.m. ET, according to the tech giant. The Latest York City area is expected to have heavy rain and thunderstorms on Thanksgiving.

"The safety of our participants and spectators is our top priority, and after close consultation with our city and agency partners, we have made the decision to move this year's celebration," Macy's stated in a statement.

It has been revealed that this year's event marks the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade. The annual event has never been canceled due to weather, though it was suspended between 1942 and 1944 for World War II.

In a recent development, the parade's viability this year had been in question after concerns arose over strong winds that could become dangerous for the massive balloons that the event is known for. The postponement also means thousands of volunteers and artists slated to perform will have to adapt to Saturday's schedule.
