It has been revealed that he had been accused of child sex abuse on the set of the former Fox/Warner Bros. Television series The Cleaning Lady, episodes of which he directed between 2022 and 2024.

Recent findings suggest that throughout the proceedings, Gilbert defended Busfield, who denied the allegations. She told ABC’s George Stephanopoulos in April 2026, “For Tim, it’s done. He’s canceled. And believe me, if I thought for a second that Tim Busfield hurt a child, he’d have a lot more to worry about than prison.”.
