Borussia Mönchengladbach star Wael Mohya is attracting interest from several big clubs, including Bayern Munich, and was subsequently named in Jürgen Klopp’s 24-man squad for the November international break, but the 17-year-old isn’t for sale.

In a recent development, speaking about the transfer links on Sky90, Gladbach sporting director Rouven Schröder stated: “He is definitely not for sale in January. And the rest will depend on how the market develops.”.

Mohya has played 10 Bundesliga games for Gladbach this season and has found the net two times while assisting once. He has already matched the tally from last season, in which he made 15 Bundesliga appearances in total.
