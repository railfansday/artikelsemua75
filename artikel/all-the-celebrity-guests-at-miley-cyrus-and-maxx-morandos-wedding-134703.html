It has been revealed that miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando are officially married! The singer and the drummer tied the knot during an intimate ceremony in Palm Springs, California, on Saturday, November 21. The couple exchanged vows surrounded by romantic white florals beside a swimming pool, according to photos published by People.

Several members of the Cyrus family were also in attendance, along with a few other familiar faces. Below, see who celebrated the couple’s significant day.

In a recent development, the bride’s mother, Tish Cyrus, attended alongside her husband, actor Dominic Purcell. The couple coordinated in black for the occasion, with Tish wearing an off-the-shoulder, fitted floor-length gown. Tish shares Miley, Noah, and Braison Cyrus with her ex-husband Billy Ray Cyrus. Miley’s father, country singer Billy Ray Cyrus, was also there to celebrate his daughter’s wedding. He attended alongside his girlfriend, actress Elizabeth Hurley, in coordinated all-black velvet looks, with Hurley’s gown featuring a prominent slit with detailed embellishments and a feathered jacket to match. The pair first met while filming the 2022 holiday movie Christmas in Paradise and confirmed their relationship in April 2025.

Miley’s younger sister, singer-songwriter Noah Cyrus, was among the family members present for the celebration. Noah has released several projects of her own and once described her sister as “a great role model for how it all works.”.

Recent findings suggest that hurley was joined by her son, model and filmmaker Damian Hurley. The 24-year-old has followed in his mother’s footsteps in the fashion and entertainment industries, and the two frequently attend events together. He was also photographed posing with the newlyweds and members of the Cyrus family at the after-party.

Sources indicate that dominic Purcell’s son, Joseph Purcell, was also photographed arriving at the wedding. The 27-year-old is Miley’s stepbrother following his father’s marriage to Tish in 2023.

Coyne was photographed officiating the wedding. The Flaming Lips singer and Miley have a longstanding creative relationship, having collaborated on her 2015 experimental album, Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz.
