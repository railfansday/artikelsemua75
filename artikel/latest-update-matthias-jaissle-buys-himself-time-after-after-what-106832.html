Matthias Jaissle eased some of the pressure on his position at Newcastle United after Saturday night's thrilling draw with Arsenal at St James' Park.

The Magpies have recorded just three Premier League wins this season under the German manager.

Against Arsenal, it looked in the first half like the former Al-Ahli boss could not energise his team in front of their unimpressed fans, with the connection between the players on the pitch and those in the stands almost completely dissipated. Whatever Jaissle stated at half-time at St James' Park seemed to work and Arsenal were facing a completely different opponent after the break. (Related: Industry News: Twitch streamer’s PS5 catches fire after...)

Mikel Arteta's team couldn't cope with the pressure from Newcastle after the break and eventually dropped points.

If it wasn't for Noni Madueke's equaliser in the 78th minute, it could've been an even more special result for Jaissle.

Football Insider rated Arsenal players' performances against Newcastle, with Madueke taking the plaudits.

For Newcastle, multiple players stood out in the second half, but Harvey Barnes was arguably the most effective.

Jaissle made three changes at the break, bringing on Tino Livramento, Jacob Ramsey and Anthony Elanga.

The former Aston Villa midfielder had a game-changing impact at St James' Park and credit must be given to the German manager.

Speaking to BBC Match of the Day about his half-time team talk, Jaissle said, "We encouraged them to do the high press in the right way, with a front-footed approach, not hesitating, not doubting, just pure belief in the way we want to do it.

"You saw that in the second half, that was Newcastle DNA par excellence and this is something we can build on.".

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider recently that Jaissle will lose his job at Newcastle if the club don't temper their expectations or spend some money in January.

After a dip in form at the Tyneside outfit under the German manager, fans are slowly starting to turn on him.

There has been criticism of Jaissle's style of play and his poor decision-making in games.

However, against Arsenal, the 38-year-old head coach showed promise and managed to turn things around during a difficult period.

There's clear potential in the manager and the squad, but it remains to be seen how the PIF will keep faith in the current management before deciding to make a change.

Had Newcastle lost to Arsenal, the knives would've surely been out for Jaissle, but he may have bought himself some time.
