Despite continued weakness across the broader crypto market, Ethereum [ETH] is showing an interesting development on the staking front.

According to Santiment’s data, Lido Staked Ethereum (stETH) recorded net outflows of 4,186 tokens from exchanges, marking its largest single-day outflow since the 30th of May.

As a result, there has been a decline in the amount of stETH immediately available on exchanges for potential selling.

This comes as Ethereum was trading near $1,753.91 at press time, after a drop of 2.9% in the past 24 hours.

According to recent reports, at the same time, around 836,798 ETH is at the moment waiting to enter Ethereum’s validator network. Of this, the estimated waiting period currently stands at 14 days and 13 hours, with a processing rate (churn) of 256 ETH per epoch.

Meanwhile, the exit queue presents a different picture wherein around 164,005 ETH is waiting to leave the validator network, with an estimated waiting period of 3 days and 20 hours. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Looking at the broader picture, Ethereum presently has approximately 773,650 active validators, with 44.6 million ETH staked. This represents around 36.5% of Ethereum’s total supply, while the network’s annual percentage rate (APR) stands at 2.42%.

So now, if exchange outflows continue, the entry queue remains elevated, and ETH prices begin stabilizing, these developments could strengthen the argument that investors are positioning themselves for the longer term rather than preparing to sell into market weakness.

Interestingly, all of this coincided with a recent report by AMBCrypto where Ethereum’s staking ratio has reportedly reached an all-time high of 36.3%, with approximately 44.5 million ETH locked in staking, up from 42.1 million in mid-August.

Meanwhile, institutional players such as BitMine and SharpLink continue expanding their staking activities, with BitMine holding 5.22 million staked ETH and earning $121.4 million from staking and validation.
