Does the stability of the pet category outweigh the high growth of Latin American e-commerce? Choosing between Chewy Inc. (CHWY -2.06%) and MercadoLibre Inc. (MELI +1.18%) requires balancing recurring revenue against regional market dominance. (Related: Hyatt Category 1 Hotels — The Easiest Points Win...)

It has been revealed that chewy focuses on the North American pet market through specialized fulfillment and a high-margin subscription model. MercadoLibre operates a massive integrated commerce and fintech ecosystem across 18 countries in Latin America. Investors often compare them to see if a niche leader or a geographic titan offers better long-term potential.

Recent findings suggest that chewy operates as a leading online retailer for pet owners, providing everything from food and toys to prescription medications. The company partners with approximately 4,000 brands and has developed its own private labels such as Frisco and American Journey. A significant portion of its business comes from the PracticeHub platform, which is presently used by roughly 50% of all veterinary clinics in the United States. This specialized focus among retail stocks allows the company to build deep loyalty through subscription-like programs. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

In the 12 months ended Aug. 2, 2026, revenue reached about $13.1 billion. This represented growth of roughly 5.9% compared to the prior twelve-month period. Net income for the period was about $273.7 million, as the organization continues to focus on improving profitability through its high-margin Autoship subscription service and pharmacy sales.

As of its February 2026 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio is approximately 1.0x, meaning total debt is equal to shareholder equity. The current ratio, which measures a company's ability to cover short-term debts with short-term assets, is roughly 0.9x. In the fiscal year ended Feb. 1, 2026, free cash flow was around $562.4 million. Note that for the fiscal year ended Feb. 1, 2026, stock-based compensation represented roughly 43.1% of operating cash flow, which inflates reported cash generation since stock-based compensation is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.

Industry observers note that mercadoLibre provides an integrated commerce and financial services ecosystem across 18 different countries in Latin America. Its Marketplace platform facilitates both direct sales and third-party transactions, supported by an extensive logistics network known as Mercado Envios. The manufacturer also operates Mercado Pago, a fintech powerhouse that handles online payments, credit, and asset management for millions of users. By combining these services, the company captures growth from both the rise of digital shopping and the increasing adoption of digital financial tools in emerging markets.

In the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached about $38.5 billion. This reflected significant growth of approximately 47.1% compared to the prior twelve-month period. Net income for those 12 months was around $1.9 billion, showcasing the company's ability to generate significant profits while expanding its footprint across the region.

According to recent reports, as of its December 2025 balance sheet, the debt-to-equity ratio is roughly 1.7x, indicating that total debt is 1.7 times the value of shareholder equity. The current ratio, which compares short-term assets to short-term liabilities, is approximately 1.2x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was about $10.8 billion, representing the cash remaining after the business pays for its operations and capital expenses.

Chewy faces intense competition from mass merchants and large online retailers like Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN -3.32%) that can use their scale to lower prices. The business relies heavily on third-party shipping and the performance of its own fulfillment centers to maintain customer satisfaction. Regulatory changes in pet pharmacy and telehealth, along with the risk of cybersecurity incidents compromising customer data, could also impact future operations.

MercadoLibre operates in several volatile economies, making it susceptible to high inflation and currency fluctuations in markets like Brazil and Argentina. It faces competition from both global e-commerce players and local financial institutions entering the fintech space. Extensive regional regulations covering banking and data protection, combined with the logistical challenges of operating across 18 countries, present ongoing risks for the business.

Chewy appears to be the more value-oriented choice based on its lower Forward P/E, which measures price against future earnings estimates, and its lower P/S ratio, which compares market value to sales over the past twelve months.

Valuation metrics include those sourced from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) and may differ from those of other data providers.

MercadoLibre is in another year of heavy investment, as the manufacturer continues to expand its Mercado Envios and Mercado Pago platforms. The business delivered another strong quarter in the third quarter of fiscal year 2026. Net revenue surpassed $10 billion for the second straight quarter, growing 46% year-on-year after 50% growth in the second quarter. Net income was $488 million, which was modest growth that fell below analysts' expectations. There is some margin compression going on, however, due to higher energy costs resulting from the Iran war.

Recent findings suggest that for the full year 2026, analyst consensus is that MercadoLibre will see revenue around $41 billion, a substantial $11 billion jump from 2025. That should come with net income of $1.8 billion, a figure that analysts have been revising downward largely due to energy price pressure.

According to recent reports, chewy is establishing a steady growth pattern. In a recently reported quarter, management noted that its Autoship program grew faster than overall sales, implying that many customers' standing orders will continue to provide a solid base for the company. The company also believes that in the long run, veterinarian and general pet health services are a huge untapped market for Chewy. The business recently closed on the acquisition of Modern Animal, which delivers high profitability per location and is a strong complement to Chewy's existing business.

In a recent development, for fiscal 2027, sales are seen rising 7% to $13.5 billion, with net income up more than 40% to around $317 million, according to consensus analyst estimates. American consumers have shown they will spend more to take care of their pets.

However, Chewy likely doesn't have a great competitive moat against brick-and-mortar competitors like PetSmart and Petco Health and Wellness Co.(WOOF -1.21%).

So which stock is the better addition to your 2027 portfolio? MercadoLibre comes at a premium valuation that may not be justified by Latin American consumer spending. This view is falling back on historical behavior, given the U.S. consumer has traditionally been the more reliable spender in good times and bad. Since Chewy comes at a cheaper valuation relative to MercadoLibre, it's the stock to buy.
