Uber is piloting a upcoming feature in the U.S. that offers rides in which drivers have enabled the organization’s recording feature, Record My Ride.

The option, which uses the front camera of the Uber driver’s phone to record the entire drive, will show up as “Recorded Rides” in the app. (Related: Tech Report: Taco Bell adds ‘Make It Flamin’ Hot’ optio...)

It has been revealed that the ride-hailing manufacturer is starting the pilot in Baltimore, Raleigh, Indianapolis, and St. Louis and will expand to more locations later.

Industry observers note that uber said it is testing recorded rides specifically for rides booked at late night or those involving teens. Earlier this year, it introduced a live video streaming feature for rides booked for teenagers. In another test, the company had enabled audio recording for late-night trips in some cities. The manufacturer is also testing a feature that will turn on recording when the app detects the ride going off-course, or when the vehicle might have had an accident.

According to recent reports, the manufacturer noted drivers can opt out of recording through the Settings panel. Similarly, riders can also decline permission to record their rides via an in-app notification.
