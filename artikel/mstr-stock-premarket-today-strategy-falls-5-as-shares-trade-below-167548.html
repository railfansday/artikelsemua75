📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Strategy shares slid 4.98% in premarket trading on Tuesday to $80.20, down $4.20 from Monday’s close of $84.40, giving back part of an 8.48% rally in the previous session. The drop leaves the manufacturer’s stock market value well below the worth of the Bitcoin it holds. Based on Monday evening prices, Strategy’s common equity was valued at about $36.4 billion, compared with roughly $52.1 billion for its Bitcoin stash, or about 0.70 times its holdings. The comparison used an after-hours MSTR price of $84.80 and a Bitcoin price of $61,151.67 on Coinbase. Strategy reported owning 851,305 Bitcoin as of November 22, according to a filing dated November 23. Its share count stood at 409.981 million Class A shares and 19.640 million Class B shares. The discount reflects investor warning toward Bitcoin treasury companies after the cryptocurrency’s sharp slide this month, even as it rebounded on Monday alongside tech stocks.
