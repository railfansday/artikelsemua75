Industry observers note that “I’ve never told this story before. He asked me on a date once,” Rannells mentioned while guesting on “The Downside” podcast hosted by Gianmarco Soresi. “He sent me a letter to ‘The Book of Mormon.’ Handwritten.” The actors went on to explain that the Oscar winner came to see the musical and “came backstage” to meet him after the show. There’s a knock on my door. It was Goldie Hawn, Kevin Spacey, and Joel Schumacher. They were not together, but they all walked up together. So then we were all crammed in my dressing room.”. C’mon, c’mon,” he recalled. “And then like a week later, I got a handwritten letter from him asking me on a date.” Rannells concluded by noting he “never responded” to the note.

It has been revealed that rannells originated the role of Elder Price in the long-running musical, performing from the show’s opening in 2011 through June 2012. The part earned him a Tony nomination for best leading actor in a musical.
