📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that join Zipcar if you live near its cars and need one a few times a month, since one price covers gas, insurance, and parking. Skip it for a daily commute or a long road trip, where it bills every hour the car sits parked and every mile past a daily allowance.

Zipcar rents cars by the hour or day from reserved spots in cities and on campuses to people who need one now and then. AAA puts the average cost of owning a fresh car at $1,072 a month, so at UNC Chapel Hill’s campus rate of $10.50 an hour, owning costs less only past about 100 hours of Zipcar a month, before taxes.
