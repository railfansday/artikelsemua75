According to reports, Tom Brady is in discussions to buy a small stake in a Premier League club. The news comes just weeks after the American football legend sold his minority share in Birmingham City.

Brady is one of the greatest athletes of all time, having become a Super Bowl champion on seven occasions. He is best remembered for his 20 seasons with the Newly introduced England Patriots, before finishing his career with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

Having retired in 2023, the former NFL ​quarterback joined Birmingham's ownership group in August 2023 through a partnership with Knighthead Capital Management. Following the Guardian's report, GIVEMESPORT understands it will be a small stake. The deal is not done yet, but all sides are optimistic. The investment comes down to the relationship between Brady and the managing partner of Clearlake Capital, Behdad Eghbali.

After Todd Boehly and Mark Walter sold their shares in September, Clearlake Capital now owns 86.5% of Chelsea.

Sources indicate that brady was notably a guest of the Premier League side when they won the 2025 Club World Cup in Latest Jersey. He was then seen celebrating with the players after their 3-0 final win over Paris Saint-Germain.

Brady also owns a minority stake in the NFL team Las Vegas Raiders.

If Brady does invest in Chelsea, it remains to be seen how hands-on he will be. At Birmingham, he was appointed chair of the club’s advisory board and made headlines when he questioned the “work ethic” of the then manager Wayne Rooney.

During the 2025 Amazon Prime documentary Built in Birmingham: Brady & The Blues, Brady stated: "I’m a little worried about our head coach’s work ethic.".

Business manager Ben Rawitz agreed that Rooney "comes across as lackadaisical." Brady added: “I don’t know, I don’t have great instincts on that.”.

According to recent reports, rooney later spoke out to express his disappointment at the exchange, claiming that it "felt unfair". He noted: "I think Tom came in once, which was the day before a game where the days are a little bit lighter anyway, and I don't think he really understood football that well. But what he does understand is, he's a hard worker, we know that.".

He then added: "Football is not NFL - NFL works for three months a year. Players do need rest as well, so I think he's very unfair, the way he's come out and portrayed that.".

Rooney lasted just 15 matches over 83 days at Birminghambefore being replaced, leaving the team 20th in the Championship.

A statement announcing Brady’s 2026 departure from the club read: “Birmingham City Football Club expresses its sincere gratitude to Tom Brady for his involvement with and unwavering support for the club over the last three years.”.
