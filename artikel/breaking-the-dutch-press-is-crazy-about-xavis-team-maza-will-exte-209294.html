The daily particularly emphasizes the complementarity between Frenkie de Jong and Joey Veerman, whose association completely stifled the Greeks. Same enthusiasm in De Telegraafwhich speaks of a real “ stroke of genius » to describe this midfield duo. Recent findings suggest that bayer Leverkusen does not want to lose its latest jewel! Ibrahim Maza, who already attracts the desire of several European substantial names such as FC Barcelona, ​​PSG and Manchester City, would be very close to extending his adventure in Germany. According to BILDthe club sent a written offer to the Algerian attacking midfielder to extend his contract until 2031. And the German leaders are ready to make a significant financial effort to convince their nugget. The player’s salary could simply double to around 5 million euros per season. The formalization of this extension is expected before Christmas. Suffice it to say that Bayer wants to take the lead before the contenders take action.
