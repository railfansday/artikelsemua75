It has been revealed that red jeans have a reputation for being risky, the kind of pants that live at the back of the closet until a bold mood strikes. But here’s the secret stylists have known for years: adding a pop of red to your wardrobe is one of the easiest ways to make it more versatile. Think of it as a neutral with attitude. It pairs effortlessly with white tops, gray and brown knits, and classic leather sneakers or loafers, adding instant energy to outfits that might otherwise fall flat.
