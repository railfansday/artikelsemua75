Alt Video is a digital, faux Blockbuster video store that you can fill with movies from your Letterboxd watchlist and Plex server

Since the beginning of the year, I have watched almost 150 films. I know this, because over the past few years, I've become one of those people… a Letterboxd user. Gasp! I know, I know, but I don't think I'm too insufferable about it. I'll only do a joke review in the hopes of like farming if I think it's genuinely funny! But many of these films were watched digitally, meaning I don't have a bunch of DVDs to admire on a shelf. So what am I to do? Well, I could check out Alt Video, a not quite job simulator where you can use your Letterboxd account or media server to fill up a digital, faux Blockbuster.

This is, obviously, not so dissimilar to Boxroom, a game where you can design your own bedroom that lets you use your Steam profile to fill it with games you actually own. With Alt Video, you do things slightly differently just because of the focus on a different format. So, say you have a Letterboxd account. After exporting some data from your account, you can bring that into Alt Video, and this fake video store will then get filled up with shelves of DVD cases of all the films you've watched. Alternatively, and perhaps more enticingly, if you run something like a Plex or Jellyfin server, you can hook that up to the game too, where again all of the films you own digitally show up in store. What's more interesting here is that there's a cinema room in the store that lets you watch films on your Plex server, and it's possible to invite your Steam friends to watch along with you. There's even a few other rooms, like a '90s bedroom or family room for more of a CRT kinda vibe. It's cute! If you like you can even manually add any DVDs or Blu-rays you own to appear on all the shelves too.

Recent findings suggest that there's a lot of other nice touches too. In the case of your Letterboxd data, your ratings even show up on the back of the boxes. It's not just relegated to films either, you can put in shows or even your games. The whole thing can be played in VR if you really want that immersive feel. There's a store clerk who can give you some recommendations if you're not sure what to watch. And there's even a day and night system, weather, and seasonal shopfront facelifts.

In a recent development, alt Video is currently slated for release next week on November 24th, and it'll be doing so in early access. Worth noting that for now the focus is on the browsing, but features involving serving customers and running the store are on the way. You can wishlist it on Steam right here.
