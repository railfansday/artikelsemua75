Barbara Eden does much more than think and blink, even as a nonagenarian.

Recent findings suggest that "I go for about, I don't know, 35 minutes on the bike," Eden told Fox News Digital of her main exercise these days, which occurs on a stationary bike. "It's good.".

And though she doesn’t go to the gym anymore, her previous routine involved “spinning class for an hour,” she explained, “and then I worked with a trainer for a half hour, lifting weights.".

But she hasn’t completely put down the weights, though she doesn’t lift quite as heavy as she used to.

For Eden, staying healthy through fitness has long been a personal wish she’s happy to grant.

It has been revealed that “You have to be disciplined about being active when you’re an actor," Eden—married to Jon Eicholtz since 1991—said. "My husband and I both have a trainer that comes in and helps us out.".

Despite her disciplined routine, however, the Flaming Star actress can’t pinpoint just one source of her longevity.

Sources indicate that nevertheless, “I'm glad I'm here,” she stated. “And I'm glad I'm still working.".

She does, of course, try to make healthy choices—within reason.

It has been revealed that "I'm careful about what I eat, but I'm not overly stupid about it, you know?" she noted. "I've never smoked. I don't know if that means anything.”.

For Eden, however, her philosophy about wellness goes beyond the personal and the physical, with external connection being a constant priority.

Sources indicate that “I think one of the things that's most important in your life is that you recognize other people,” she mused. “You realize that they're not so different than you are and that you have to get along. And when you do that, you find life is much more interesting, and people are much more interesting.".
