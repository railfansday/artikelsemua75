Steven Gerrard fears Mauricio Pochettino is already on collision course with one of his senior Manchester United players following Sunday’s damaging 4-0 defeat against Liverpool.

Any hope of a fresh manager bounce was extinguished during a frantic opening quarter of an hour at Anfield yesterday when the hosts roared into a two goal lead following Leny Yoro’s sending off.

Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz piled on the agony in the closing stages and ensured the scoreline gave a more accurate representation of Liverpool’s near total dominance.

While playing the entire match at a numerical disadvantage certainly played a role in the one-sided nature of such an eagerly anticipated contest, United were second best from the off and were made to pay for their new manager’s questionable team selection.

The decision to drop Harry Maguire at the expense of Matthijs de Ligt, who is still feeling his way back from a long-term injury, was questioned by the likes of Jamie Carragher and Gary Neville before kick-off and their skepticism was vindicated when the Dutch centre-half played a role in Yoro’s red card.

Gerrard, meanwhile, went a step further in his criticism by accusing Pochettino of showing a lack of respect to Maguire by relegating him to the bench in his first game at the helm.

‘I thought he got it wrong,’ Gerrard told The Overlap with Sky Bet. ‘I thought he got it wrong in the back line. I think to start someone in a game of that magnitude, it’s the biggest game in the country. All eyes are on it all over the world.

It has been revealed that ‘To play someone that hadn’t played since last November. You can understand the manager wanting to come in and put his stamp on it and change a few things that had been going on previously, but Harry Maguire’s their best defender at the moment.

‘Forget the youngsters that are maybe not ready at the moment. Matthijs de Ligt’s been out for a year. Harry Maguire’s their best defender at the moment. He should have started the game.
