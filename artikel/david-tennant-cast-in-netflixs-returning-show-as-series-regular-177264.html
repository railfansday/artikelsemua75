David Tennant is officially going to Netflix.

According to recent reports, the actor has been cast in Netflix's returning comedy drama show as a series regular, with new details revealed about his upcoming role. Tennant's most famous roles include having portrayed the Marvel Cinematic Universe villain Killgrave in Netflix's Jessica Jones series. But, beyond the streaming platform, he's also had a number of famous roles, from the Tenth Doctor in Doctor Who to the sympathetic demon Crowley in Prime Video's Good Omens. He's also set to star in Only Murders in the Building season 6, premiering in early December of this year. (Related: Estate Planning for Early Retirement: The FIRE Guide...)

Industry observers note that now, Deadline has confirmed Tennant will be reuniting with Netflix for a main cast role in The Four Seasons season 3. Tennant previously demonstrated up in a cameo appearance at the end of season 2, his arrival originally seeming like a callback to an imaginary boyfriend Anne (Kerri Kenney-Silver) had made up. Instead, he'll be featuring as a main cast member in the now-filming show, making him the first person to ever be added to the main cast of the series across its history. Tennant will be playing the same character he did at the end of The Four Seasons season 2, that being Gianpiero. He's described as "a Scottish-Italian ex-pat" who joins the main characters on their upcoming travels, becoming a mainstay of the group in the process. Although it's unclear what his connection to the rest of the group will be, it seems like he'll be tied to Annie based on the way in which season 2's ending introduced him.

Just like the previous seasons, The Four Seasons season 3 will remain focused on its core friend group, with Tennant and Kenney-Silver joined by Tina Fey, Will Forte, Marco Calvani, Erika Henningsen, and Colman Domingo. Just like previous seasons of the show, it'll have four, two-episode arcs, with each one centered around a different season as the friend group travels. Tennant's arrival means there will be plenty of unexpected surprises in store as the series continues to evolve.

Sources indicate that this also means Tennant is juggling two major streaming roles at the very same time. While his upcoming appearance in Only Murders in the Building might be a one-off, he's still a series regular on Rivals, which was renewed for a third season. This means Tennant will have two main roles on different TV demonstrates right around the same time, all while juggling other TV and movie projects at the same time. The actor's prolific, high-profile work speaks to his effort and how commanding a screen presence he always is.

While there aren't many more details about his character in The Four Seasons, the actor joining the show means there will be plenty of drama and funny moments featuring Gianpiero. His character seems like it'll be a welcome addition to the cast, while also providing plenty of new and exciting elements to the series in the process. As filming continues, the extent of Tennant's character and how he meshes with the rest of the friend group will likely be revealed in the months to come.
