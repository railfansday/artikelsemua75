Ecuadorian Leader of Los Choneros, a Violent Foreign Terrorist Organization, Indicted in Brooklyn Federal Court on International Drug, Terrorism, and Gun Charges as Part Of Homeland Security Task Force Initiative

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (137 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that A federal grand jury in Brooklyn yesterday returned a six-count indictment charging Ronald Javier Macías Villamar with international cocaine distribution conspiracy, international cocaine distribution, using firearms in furtherance of drug trafficking, and conspiring to and providing material support to a foreign terrorist organization. From at least July 2025, the defendant was one of the top leaders of Los Choneros, a designated Foreign Terrorist Organization (FTO) based in Ecuador that has been responsible for significant drug trafficking into the United States, firearms trafficking from the United States, and acts of extreme violence. The defendant became one of the top-ranking members of Los Choneros after his brother and former highest-ranking leader José Adolfo Macías Villamar, also known as “Fito,” was extradited to the Eastern District of New York in July 2025.
