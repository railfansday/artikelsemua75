One of the best features in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt Remastered is the ability to use mods across all available platforms. CD Projekt Red included a fair number of popular mods when the Remastered version launched at the end of September, but console players had far fewer options than PC players. CD Projekt Red is looking to bridge that gap with a fresh update.

In a recent development, CD Projekt Red released patch 5.02 on Nov. 26 across Nintendo Switch 2, PlayStation 5, Windows PC, and Xbox Series X. The revision includes a plethora of tweaks and bug fixes, such as fixing some recurring problems with Photo Mode, the main attraction is the expansion of the mod library. According to recent reports, some of the highlights include "Glowing Witcher Eyes," a more lore-accurate depiction of Geralt that leans into the creepy nature of the Witchers. You can freshen up Geralt with a upcoming hairstyle or try out a boat race. The most impressive addition is the expansion-sized "Tales of the Witcher: Cintra," which adds a dozen quests. They were responsible for the fan-favorite Dark Urge storyline in Baldur's Gate 3.
