Grand Theft Auto VI achieved $3.79 billion in day one retail sales after selling 32.5 million units pre-release, setting a new record for entertainment and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history, Rockstar Games confirmed.

The long-awaited latest entry in the open-world action adventure game series introduced for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series on November 19. It at the moment boasts a 99 out of 100 on review aggregators Metacritic and OpenCritic, making it the highest-rated game of all time on both platforms.
