Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta claims he is very happy with Kai Havertz and trusts club chief Andrea Berta to sort out a upcoming contract for him.

The Germany international has been a key player since joining the Gunners from Chelsea back in the summer of 2023, scoring 40 goals in 125 appearances for the club and helping them win the Premier League title. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Still, there has not yet been much of an revision on Havertz and a new contract with Arsenal, with the 27-year-old’s current deal set to expire in 2028.

That’s not exactly imminent, but it would still be ideal for Arsenal to get Havertz tied down to a longer contract so as to avoid any uncertainty by the time he has just a year left by next summer, which would lead to his market value tumbling.

“In the background, the club, ownership, Andrea in particular is very much into that and I leave it to him because I fully trust him.”.
