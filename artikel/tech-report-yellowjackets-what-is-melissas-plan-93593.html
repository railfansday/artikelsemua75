According to recent reports, if three seasons of Yellowjackets has taught us anything, it's that you don't mess with Shauna Shipman (aka Shauna Sadecki). Melissa learned that the hard way last season, when she sent a tape that put the Yellowjackets on her scent.

It has been revealed that shauna (Melanie Lynskey) didn't just find her, attack her, and bite off a bit of Melissa's arm. She also tried to feed it to her. Well, Season 4 of Yellowjackets came back with a vengeance, and so did Melissa (Hillary Swank).

Fresh from killing Van (Lauren Ambrose), Melissa begins episode 1 on the run with her wife, Alex (Jaylee Hamidi), and their child. In a grimy hotel room, she confesses to her partner the truth (or some of it). Her name is not Kelly, it's Melissa. She was a Yellowjacket. She was on the plane that crashed. In a recent development, melissa states she "rebooted her life" — which is a wild way to reframe faking your death. "I'm still the same person you married," she insists, "just with a different name and way, way shittier past than I told you about on our first date.". Then this conversation reveals something huge:.

Alex responds to Melissa's partial confession with: "My mom was out there. She's the one who called for help. She's the reason they got rescued. ".

So, there were signs last season about how Tai (Jasmin Savoy Brown), Van (Liv Hewson), Shauna (Sophie Nélisse), and Misty (Samantha Hanratty) talked about Hannah (Ashley Sutton) that suggested she didn't make it out of the Yellowjackets camp alive. Alex's revelation further cements that Hannah died out there. When Alex asks her wife if she met her mom out there, Melissa lies, "No. I didn't know her.".

Melissa goes on to say that it was because she wish she'd known Hannah that she moved to the late scientist's hometown to start over. "Maybe I could create a life in her honor," she says. That might be true. But even as Melissa comes clean, she's still lying to Alex. And by sharing any of this, Melissa could be putting Alex in danger from the others. At least she's honest about that.

The episode begins with the Yellowjackets in a Canadian hospital, seeing their injuries tended to. But there's no sign of Hannah.

As the Yellowjackets share their story of survival with authorities, they all recite the exact same story:.

"The plane crashed. Our friends died. And the rest of starved and scavenged and prayed until they finally found us.".

Sources indicate that they claim to have no knowledge of the missing frog scientists. To cover their tracks, they let the adults assume Hannah made the distress call. So, Natalie (Sophie Thatcher) never gets proper credit for her heroics there.

How Hannah died, however, is still a mystery.

In a recent development, after the scene of Melissa's confession to her wife, a title card says four weeks have passed. Shauna is acting out in her frustration, lashing out at a convenience store clerk and a stinky detective, the latter of whom has lost any trace of Melissa and her family.

Cut to: Melissa and Alex setting up in a newly leased home — which is right next door to the Sadecki household. Alex seems nervous about the initiative, as Melissa assures, "Hey, we can't run forever.".

Then, Melissa confronts Shauna by showing up at her door with a "Hi" and a bottle of wine. To this, Shauna indicates, "Well, this was very stupid... You moved in next door to me. Now I know where you are.".

Melissa responds, "Yeah, and everyone else will too," just as a news van pulls up behind her!

In the wilderness and in the present timeline, Melissa knows full well how Shauna's rage can result in violence, sometimes deadly violence.

Recent findings suggest that however, she also knows Shauna is strategic and a survivor. By staying in plain sight, Melissa thinks Shauna will be powerless to hurt her, because to do so would bring fresh attention to the Yellowjackets and their past.

Is she right? We'll can't wait to find out.
