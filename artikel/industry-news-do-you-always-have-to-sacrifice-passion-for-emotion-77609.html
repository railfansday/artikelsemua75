“Your pleasure is constantly evolving and if you want to continue to have a thriving and exciting sex life, a couple must be able to talk about most recent desires,” she says. “What turned you on at 25 may not still turn you on at 40.”.

This is why communicating your desires and feelings to your partner is so crucial. Sure, you and your partner may be pros at working through disagreements and emotionally supporting each other through life’s changes—but does that energy translate into your sex life? Do you share your curiosities? Do you hold space for them telling you they’d like to try something different? If you don’t, it might be time to make a change.

And just the feeling of being wanted can ignite some of that passion from the early days of your relationship.

Most importantly, you should date your partner every day. Dr. DeSeta recommends “Daily Dates” to couples she sees in therapy. This is when you set aside 20 minutes everyday to make your partner a priority.

“All too often long-term couples put their relationship second, so this tune up is always needed,” she notes. “During these 20 minutes the phones are silenced, logistical conversations are off the table, and the time is dedicated to emotionally connecting with their partner.”.

By doing this, she explains that you slow down the daily hustle and really learn how to reconnect with your partner. You might even learn a few new things about them in the process.

And no, you don’t have to be constantly ripping each other’s clothes off or feel nervous in order to have a passionate relationship. But you should aim to feel the same deep desire and attraction to your partner that you did when you first started off—if not more.
