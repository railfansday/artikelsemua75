Recent findings suggest that GARDEN GROVE, Calif., Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Harbinger, an American industrial technology platform company, today announced it has closed financing agreements totaling more than $100 million with Erebor Bank, co-founded by Palmer Luckey, and Pinegrove Venture Partners, which is sponsored by Brookfield Asset Management and HRTG Partners. The complementary financing structures include senior debt provided by Erebor and a separate convertible note from Pinegrove.

The transactions mark an vital step in Harbinger's evolution from an electric vehicle manufacturer to a broader industrial technology platform company reinventing the critical systems the world depends on across mobility, power, defense, autonomy and components. Harbinger develops advanced electrification technologies, including all-electric and hybrid medium-duty vehicle platforms, battery and drivetrain systems, energy storage and auxiliary power solutions, autonomous driving technology and defense applications. According to recent reports, the debt financing comes on the heels of Harbinger's recently unveiled Series D funding round, and represents an expansion beyond the equity-driven funding model Harbinger has historically utilized as an emerging technology company. By accessing financing structures more commonly associated with established industrial manufacturers, Harbinger is building a more diversified capital strategy to support long-term growth while demonstrating how a new generation of American manufacturers can finance production at scale.

In a recent development, erebor is a nationally chartered bank financing the companies rebuilding American industry. Erebor serves customers across 43 states, including businesses in manufacturing, defense, energy, AI, robotics, and aerospace, industries critical to America's national security and economic growth. Built on modern technology, regulated by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), and a member of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), Erebor has grown to $8 billion in assets by expanding access to capital for businesses historically underserved by legacy banks.

Pinegrove operates as an integrated investment platform with over $15B of assets under management across venture fund primaries, venture and growth secondaries, and private credit.
