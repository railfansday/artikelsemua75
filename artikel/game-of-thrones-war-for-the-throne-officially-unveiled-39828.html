Game of Thrones has transformed many times throughout the years. What originally started as a TV show back in the 2010s now encompasses so much more, as the greater fandom has become more away of the source material and all of author George R.R. Martin's side stories. Plus, we're all still waiting for the story to actually conclude in book form.

Industry observers note that we're knee-deep in Westeros history with shows like House of the Dragon, featuring many characters that are merely referenced in the greater mythos. Now, Game Of Thrones: War For The Throne has officially been unveiled, and you can already partake in its festivities. (Related: Hakeem Jeffries and his team will seek to deliver a new...)

Hotpie, the "Gravy Knight" is coming to Game of Thrones: Conquest as par of the War of the Throne event, as well as Mace Tyrell, "Lord of Highgarden." Fans will recall these characters being part of the show, and they're recreated with their likenesses in Conquest.

According to recent reports, A new gear set is also coming, which focuses on ranged attack and defense, as part of the Highgarden Harvest Festival event. A new Dragon Gear set is also included called Terror of the Tundra, and predictably focuses on buffing your dragon stats.
