No matter what you're protecting against, the strategy is the same: diversify. And one way to diversify a stock-heavy portfolio is to take a different approach with an investment like the iShares 0-3 Month Treasury Bond ETF (SGOV +0.00%).

Sources indicate that SGOV invests in very short-term U.S. Treasury securities, making its value less sensitive to interest rate changes than longer-term bond funds. While it doesn't guarantee against all losses and can't deliver the growth potential of stocks, it can provide a haven for cash you may need soon or to calm portfolio volatility.

The idea behind adding a short-term bond ETF to a portfolio that relies mainly on stocks is simple. Companies matter stocks to raise money, a deal that can work out well for all parties as long as the company performs well.

However, stock prices can fall sharply if investors fear weaker profits or a slowing economy. In fact, it doesn't take much more than a weak rumor or a poorly researched news story to scare some investors off.
