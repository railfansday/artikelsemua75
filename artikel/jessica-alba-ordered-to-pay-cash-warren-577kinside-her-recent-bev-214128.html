Jessica Alba’s divorce from Cash Warren is back in the headlines after a latest court order awarded Warren $577,481 from Alba’s retirement benefits.

The ruling comes after the former couple sold their longtime Beverly Hills–area home and Alba later purchased another property in the area.

According to recent reports, the retirement award comes from Alba’s account in the HonorBar Inc. Profit Sharing Blueprint, according to court documents reported by TMZ.

Any money left in the account after Warren receives his share is to go to Alba. The documents also allow Warren to take the award as a lump sum or move it into an individual retirement account or another retirement plan.

The court order also addresses account records: If Warren requests statements in writing within the next year, Alba must provide them within 10 days.

According to the outlet, transferring the money into a retirement account could avoid a 20% tax withholding.

The ruling is the latest financial development following the couple’s divorce, which was finalized in February 2026.

It was previously reported that Alba was ordered to pay Warren $3 million to balance the division of their assets.

Their settlement also divided interests in several companies, shares in The Honest Organization, and residuals from projects made during their marriage. (Related: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using Gradio...)

Meanwhile, Alba and Warren have already put their real estate ties behind them.

In a recent development, their former Beverly Hills–area home was sold in 2025. Alba and Warren purchased the seven-bedroom estate for $9.9 million in 2017, then put it on the market in April 2025 for $18.99 million. It was sold for $18.2 million, with the closing recorded on June 27, 2025.

The property sat on about 1.86 acres and included 8.5 bathrooms, a guesthouse, a home theater, and extensive grounds.

Alba and Warren undertook an 18-month renovation after buying it, with Alba involved in decisions about the home’s design.

The estate had been a family residence for years, and Alba spoke about the family’s looks for the house during a 2019 Architectural Digest tour.

After the sale, Alba picked up a splashy Beverly Hills estate in September 2025 for $4.75 million.

The roughly 5,000-square-foot property boasts four bedrooms, five bathrooms, tiled roofing, and modern amenities. (Related: Scaling up BERT-like model Inference on modern CPU - P...)

The two transactions mark a change in Alba’s recent housing history: the end of her and Warren’s ownership of a sprawling family estate, followed by her purchase of another Beverly Hills property.

Alba and Warren announced their separation in December 2024 after 16 years of marriage.

Alba filed for divorce citing irreconcilable differences, and the sale of their longtime home followed the announcement.

The latest retirement-benefit order concerns a separate part of their financial settlement, while the Beverly Hills transactions provide a look at the real estate changes that accompanied their split.
