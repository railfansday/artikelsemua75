Scott Wolf is paying tribute to his dad, Steven Wolf, after his death.

“My father, Steven Charles Wolf, passed away a few days ago,” Scott, 58, wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, alongside photos of his dad through the years. “His passing leaves a hole in the center of our hearts, and our lives. He was my hero, and I have lived my entire life trying to be like him. He was the most kind, honest, caring, and loving man. He didn’t just adore his family, he lived for us.”.

It has been revealed that he continued, “His warmth and authenticity made better the lives of everyone who was lucky enough to know him. He was brilliant, funny, and truly made every room, and this world, a better place. He leaves behind a legacy of kindness, hard work, fairness, service, compassion, and most of all, love.”.

Scott described his late father as “the best husband, father, grandfather and friend” who “lives on in the hearts of all of us who were blessed to know his love.”.

According to recent reports, “I miss you dad,” Scott concluded. “I hope your days are filled with golf, seafood, card games, cashews, and hugs from those you have rejoined. I love you so much. 💔❤️.”.

Steven’s death comes two months after Scott and Kelley Wolf confirmed they were moving forward with a divorce after facing a challenging year.

It has been revealed that “Since early spring, we have been home together as a family, focusing on healing, rebuilding, and finding stability after an extraordinarily difficult season,” the pair told Us in a September statement. “After much care, consideration, and reflection, we have decided to move forward with our divorce.”.
