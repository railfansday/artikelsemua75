Friday’s reports confirm that both Andrea Cambiaso and Zeki Celik have returned to training with Juventus and that they should both be available for selection for Sunday’s Serie A match against Venezia.

Cambiaso missed Juve’s final match before the international break, a 2-2 draw away against Fiorentina, with a muscular problem, which ruled him out of selection for Roberto Mancini’s latest Italy national team squad for the November break.

Juventus find themselves in seventh place in the Serie A table ahead of kick-off against Venezia. They have won five, drawn three and lost three for a total of 18 points in the league thus far, 11 less than the current table-toppers, Lazio.

A match at home against the 19th-placed Venezia, on six points after 11 matches, is an opportunity for the Old Lady to return to winning ways when league action resumes this weekend.
