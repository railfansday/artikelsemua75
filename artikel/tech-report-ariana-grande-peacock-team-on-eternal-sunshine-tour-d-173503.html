Ariana Grande is ready to give fans a behind-the-scenes look at her return to the stage in a documentary and concert film set to debut on Peacock next month.

Industry observers note that peacock announced Tuesday the upcoming doc, the eternal sunshine diaries: the making of the tour, which followed Grande during the 30 days leading up to the opening of her sold-out tour. “At the same time, Grande is putting the finishing touches on her eighth studio album, giving audiences an intimate window into her creative process during a pivotal moment,“ the streamer describes. The eternal sunshine diaries: the making of the tour will premiere on Friday, Dec. 11 followed by the concert film the eternal sunshine tour on Saturday, Dec. 12, exclusively on Peacock.

The three-month tour kicked off in June following the release of her seventh studio album, Eternal Sunshine, in 2024 and her eighth studio album, Petal, released at the end of July.

Peacock describes the tour as one that “journeys through the collapse of [Grande’s] reality, a cosmic rebirth, a reconnection with her inner child and the memories that shaped her. She transforms the Eternal Sunshine Tour into a cinematic celebration of healing, connection and the music that has defined her life.“.

The eternal sunshine diaries: the making of the tour documentary and the eternal sunshine tour concert film are both executive produced by Grande, Luga Podesta, Andrew Lerios and Christian Breslauer.

In a recent development, the release of the doc and concert film come amid Grande securing four Grammy nominations for song of the year, best music video, best traditional pop vocal performance and best pop vocal album. Grande will next be seen on the major screen in the fourth Meet the Parents movie, Focker-in-Law, alongside Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro, releasing on Wednesday.
