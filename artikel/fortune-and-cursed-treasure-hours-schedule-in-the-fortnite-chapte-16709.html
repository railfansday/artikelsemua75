Fortnite is continuing the tradition of special Saturday hourly events during the Chapter 7 mini season, though this time they're treasure hours, centered around the pirate-themed "Treasure Hunters" season. During these hours, certain sprites will get increased drop rates, and you'll drop into the game with special items.

According to recent reports, this week features Fortune and Cursed treasure hours, increasing the drop rate for both Fortune and Cursed variant sprites. You'll also start the game with The Leekcapper and a self-revive device. Fortune sprites reveal nearby chests, and Cursed sprites will increase your odds of finding rare Sprite variants, but you'll also take damage in water. Notably, Epic Games has confirmed via X that drop rates for cosmetic treasure (including the Pirate Shop Access Token) increase during these treasure hours. The drop rate for the token is usually 1 in 137,500, but during the treasure hours, the drop rate will be increased by 1.5x. The chances also may increase further, but Epic Games has not announced that, as of writing this.
