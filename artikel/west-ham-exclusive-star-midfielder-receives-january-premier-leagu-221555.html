It’s something that hasn’t gone unnoticed by Premier League clubs, who clearly see his goalscoring record and top-flight experience as attributes they’d like to add to their squads.

Soucek’s lack gametime for West Ham has attracted interest from Premier League clubs.

Sources indicate that it has been reiterated to Claret & Hugh by Souček’s representatives that he is not pushing for an exit from the London Stadium. That remains consistent with what we have previously been told by the midfielder’s camp.

However, it goes without saying that he would probably like to be involved in more games than he currently is.

Ultimately, it will be for manager Scott Parker and head of recruitment Nils Koppen to decide whether they wish to retain or cash in on the 31-year-old midfielder, who only signed a contract extension in October.
