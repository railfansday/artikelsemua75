Breaking: The first flash of a dying star can reveal its hidden surroundings

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (85 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, when a massive star dies, the universe does not always announce it with the familiar, slowly expanding glow of a supernova. Its very first signal can be a dazzling, short-lived flash of invisible light—a cosmic camera flash that lasts only hours. That fleeting moment offers an unusually direct glimpse of the star at the instant of destruction. But what if the light has to fight its way through a cloud of gas the star expelled before dying?
