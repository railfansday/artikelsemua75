West Brom beat competition from Premier League side Hull City to land Collyer in the last transfer window. WBA could offer him the prospect of more game time.

The Baggies brought in the Worthing-born man to add more competition and depth to their midfield department. At the age of 22, he is still young and has potential to grow and develop further down the line.

Collyer is a talented player and can have a bright future at West Brom. However, he needs to stay injury free at WBA.
