Archive of Our Own (AO3) is down again, just four days after the popular fanfiction platform came back online after a prolonged outage that left millions of readers scrambling for alternatives.

Sources indicate that on Thursday, Nov. 19, users began reporting problems accessing the site, with outage-tracking website Downdetector showing a spike in reports beginning around 6:08 a.m. ET.

Sources indicate that at 6:27 a.m. ET, AO3 confirmed the disruption, attributing it to preventative maintenance.

"AO3 is down for preventative maintenance," the platform wrote in an enhancement. "We apologize for the inconvenience, and will provide updates when we have them.".

The latest downtime comes after AO3 went offline on Oct. 29 for what became a 17-day maintenance period. At the time, the Organization for Transformative Works (OTW), the nonprofit behind AO3, warned that restoration could take two to four weeks, leaving fanfiction readers and writers without access to one of fandom's most important online archives.

AO3 finally returned on Nov. 15, though the site wasn't fully operational. User icons, imports from other sites, and downloads in formats other than HTML remained temporarily disabled as volunteers continued restoration efforts. The prolonged outage prompted fans to look for other places to congregate online. Tumblr reported a 49 percent increase in fresh sign-ups during the first several days of AO3's absence, compared with the preceding two weeks, underscoring just how central the fanfiction archive has become to fandom communities.

In a recent development, but the disruption exposed growing tensions between fanfiction writers and readers. As readers scrambled to find copies of their favorite stories, some began circulating shared Google Drives containing downloaded works, prompting backlash from authors who hadn't consented to their stories being redistributed. The controversy sparked a larger conversation about ownership, consent, and the expectations that have developed between writers and readers in online fandom communities. According to recent reports, it's unclear whether Thursday's preventative maintenance is directly connected to the earlier outage or how long the latest disruption will last. AO3 has not provided an estimated restoration time.

For fanfiction readers who only just got their favorite archive back, it's another unwelcome plot twist.
