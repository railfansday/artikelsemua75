Recent findings suggest that avengers: Doomsday was recently revealed to include another Marvel Cinematic Universe hero in addition to the many previously announced cast members, and this impending return is set to fix one of the biggest mistakes in the Multiverse Saga. Avengers: Doomsday's cast is one of, if not the most exciting Marvel has assembled to date, and that is saying something.

According to recent reports, after all, with movies like Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, the MCU's Avengers franchise seemed to have peaked, featuring characters from all over the shared universe and an intricate Infinity Stone heist/time travel plot. Well, with the whole live-action Marvel multiverse available for the franchise to play with, we are finally getting the Avengers, the X-Men, and the Fantastic Four in the same movie.

According to recent reports, with Fox's X-Men coming back in less than a month, it is easy to understand why the focus has turned to the multiverse heroes. Before Doomsday, MCU movies like Spider-Man: No Way Home, Deadpool & Wolverine, and more revealed how a lot of fun can be had by bringing back actors from non-MCU Marvel movies. That stated, the film will also see some much-needed franchise returns.

The fact that we are finally getting to see characters like Shang-Chi, Namor, and more after their brief presence in the franchise is incredibly exciting. That said, one of the most exciting character returns in Avengers: Doomsday is that of Hailee Steinfeld's Kate Bishop, who was a last-minute reveal for the film and appeared in the final trailer. Kate's return fixes a Multiverse Saga mistake.

Steinfeld made her MCU debut in one of the most entertaining Disney+ Marvel series to date, Hawkeye, which was released on the streaming service all the way back in 2021. While she has reprised the role in animation, the fact that Steinfeld's only live-action MCU appearances so far were as the main character of a six-episode show five years ago and as a cameo in The Marvels, the MCU's lowest-grossing movie of all time, is quite disappointing. Steinfeld's performance as Kate Bishop was electric, infusing the character with a charisma that only few possess. She deserved another season of her Marvel series. She delivered the comedy and the energy, while the older Hawkeye was the veteran and the voice of reason, helping the younger hero realize her potential. Renner has since revealed that Hawkeye season 2 has not happened yet — he left the door open — because Marvel lowballed him, telling High Performance in 2025 that he was offered "half the money" he got paid for season 1. Sources indicate that hawkeye stands at a near-perfect 92% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes and a stellar 88% audience score. As such, Hawkeye was not canceled because of negative reviews or poor viewership, as Marvel fully intended to make at least another season of the show. After her last-minute Avengers: Doomsday confirmation, Kate Bishop's return in the biggest event of the Multiverse Saga thus far serves as the perfect consolation prize following Hawkeye's disappointing fate.

According to recent reports, hawkeye came out in an age of MCU TV where the mandate was to have movie-like seasons cut down into six episodes, with most projects being limited series. That approach has since changed, with traditional showrunners hired and the focus turning to multi-season series, which is what Marvel expects its recently confirmed spy thriller Zodiac to be, and what Charlie Cox's Daredevil: Born Again has become. Given how Renner left the door open in that interview by saying, "I’d still love to do it," if Marvel makes the actor a better offer after the Multiverse Saga ends, Hawkeye season 2 might happen.

It has been revealed that with Steinfeld back in the live-action MCU in Avengers: Doomsday, she likely would also be game to sign on for another season of the Disney+ show. That said, the upcoming Avengers movie is expected to change Kate Bishop's standing in the MCU, as the Young Avengers should make their debut in the film, given how Kate and Kathryn Newton's Cassie Lang are confirmed for the new movie, both of which were recruited by Iman Vellani's Ms. Marvel. As such, Marvel's intends for that team after Avengers: Doomsday could impact Hawkeye season 2's chances.
