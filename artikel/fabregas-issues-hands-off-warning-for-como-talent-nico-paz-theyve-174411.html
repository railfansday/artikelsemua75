Cesc Fabregas has admitted that clubs have already tried to sign Nico Paz, but insists the Argentine is going nowhere for now.

With quotes via TuttoMercatoWeb, the Como coach was speaking to TNT Sports Mexico and was asked whether any of Europe’s giants had come knocking for the 22-year-old. (Related: Industry News: I Asked About Dirty Dishes. You Delivere...)

According to recent reports, “They’ve already come here to take him,” Fabregas said with a laugh. “For three years I’ve heard people around here saying he’s going to leave, but for now I still see him here. So don’t worry, he’s our player, and we’ll see what happens.”. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Sources indicate that paz’s future was the subject of a long saga in the summer. Real Madrid triggered their buy-back option on the player, then gave Como a preferential option to keep him. Como eventually paid around €60 million to make the move permanent, although Real Madrid kept a buy-back clause for 2027, so the question will come up again next summer.

Fabregas also talked about managing such a young dressing room, which is now competing in Serie A and the Champions League at the same time.

Recent findings suggest that “I’m not just a coach. I’m here coaching 12 lads aged between 19 and 22, so I’m an educator, a father,” he mentioned.

“I’m very clear about who we are at Como. We’re not Real Madrid, Bayern Munich or Manchester United, so we have to stay humble, respect everyone and grow at the right time.

Industry observers note that “If one day I saw that a lad thought he was superior, or above what he really is in this context, at this club, in this team, then we wouldn’t like that any more. Because no, we’re not like that. It’s exactly the opposite.”.

He also had more praise for Paz: “Nico is an exceptional boy and he has an incredible personality. I love his character and the way he sees things, and he’s a competitor. In the end, that’s what I like about him.”.

In a recent development, como host AEK Athens in the Champions League tonight at 21:00 CET [20:00 GMT]. A win would go a long way towards securing a place in the knockout phase play-offs.
