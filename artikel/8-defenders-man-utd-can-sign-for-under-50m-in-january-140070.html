In a recent development, mauricio Pochettino's first match in charge of Manchester United couldn't have gone much worse. After spending the past week being reminded by the media of just how poisoned the chalice he was taking from Michael Carrick had become, with warnings about the fate of the seven managers who had tried and failed to turn the club's fortunes around, Sunday afternoon's action was somehow even worse than anyone could have envisaged.

After just eight minutes, the Red Devils were down to 10 men, with Leny Yoro sent off for denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity. And by the 15th minute, Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz had taken full advantage of a worryingly fragile defence to put Liverpool two goals ahead. Second-half strikes from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz then twisted the knife, condemning United to a chastening 4-0 defeat.

The result leaves Pochettino with plenty of fires to put out at his adopted club. United sit 19th in the Premier League with just one win from 11 matches, have suffered the worst defeat by a new manager in 100 years and, alarmingly, have conceded more goals than any other side in European football, with 34.

It has been revealed that after spending much of last summer rebuilding their midfield and putting all their eggs in the attacking basket the year before, United are now desperately short of answers at the back. There is a clear need to strengthen their defensive ranks in January before their relegation battle spirals further out of control - and here are eight fresh faces they should consider, whose Transfermarkt market values have also been listed. With long-standing problems at full-back, Pochettino may want to keep the pressure on the club's top brass to finally get a deal over the line, particularly given that Diogo Dalot has increasingly become the fans' favourite scapegoat in that position.

A more versatile, cheaper alternative to the aforementioned Hall would be to raid Fulham for Antonee Robinson - a player that Pochettino knows well from his time as USMNT boss.

Recent findings suggest that he is out of contract in 2027, but with United needing reinforcements now, they could choose to bring the German in for a cut-price fee in the coming couple of months. He may not be as fast as other defenders on their radar, but he more than makes up for that in intelligience and experience.

Industry observers note that rumour has it that Calvin Bassey is nearing a contract extension at Fulham, and that instantly puts United on the back-foot if they were to negotiate a move for him. But a club of their stature shouldn't be completely put off by that.

A central defender known for his athleticism and ball-carrying abilities, he would fit Pochettino's system, which relies on a high-octane brand of football to succeed.
