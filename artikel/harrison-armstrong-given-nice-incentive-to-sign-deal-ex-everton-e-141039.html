Harrison Armstrong is keen to make history with Everton in the coming years and will agree a upcoming deal.

That is according to former senior Everton scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 19-year-old is thought to be in talks over a upcoming long-term deal with his current contract set to expire in 2028, although there is stated to be the option of an additional year.

Armstrong has been a constant for Everton in the Premier League this season, despite having gone incredibly close to leaving in the summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Nottingham Forest had agreed a deal in the region of £40million for the England youth international before the news broke, sparking huge protest within the fan base.

The Toffees eventually pulled out of the deal, and now look to have learned the error of their ways.

Armstrong has spent his entire career and youth career with Everton so far, and that does not look like it will change anytime soon.

The young talent has all of the tools to be a fantastic Premier League midfielder, and from the looks of it, is keen to take the Toffees with him.

"It was a shock that Nottingham Forest were the team that were going to buy him," King told Football Insider.

It has been revealed that "I don't want to insult Nottingham Forest but I'd have thought a bigger name would have been apparent there. Even so, they were prepared to buy him but then Everton decided to keep him and, by the look of it rightly so.

Industry observers note that "They're in the quarter-finals of the league cup, they've got a good position in the league. Sources indicate that "They've got a brand latest stadium, they've gone from 39,000 every week to 52,000 every week. I'd have thought that's a nice incentive for him to play at the Hill Dickinson Stadium now.

Recent findings suggest that "The atmosphere at the football club looks pretty good. Moysey [David Moyes] seems to be in a good place as well.

"Armstrong has turned out to be a player who they were thinking about selling and has now come back. He wants to prove people wrong. He wants to have his future at Everton football club.".

Armstrong has already been tipped for future senior England team involvement, such is the excitement surrounding him.

"It’s difficult to say it’s everything you think it’ll be because it’s a lot of hard work, and it’s a lot of maintaining everything you’d probably expect," he mentioned in November when asked about his season so far.

"You need to maintain your levels every day, when people watching and when they aren't watching, too.
