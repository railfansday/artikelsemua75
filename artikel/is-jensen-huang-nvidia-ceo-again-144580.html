📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Yes. Jensen Huang is back in charge of Nvidia after the chipmaker’s board restored him as president and chief executive on Monday, a sudden turnaround just days after directors had turned down his offer to come back. Board chair Harvey C. Jones stated the company is confident Huang is the best leader for Nvidia and that his return serves investors, staff, partners and customers. President Donald Trump added that Washington has seen nothing to suggest Huang’s comeback creates a national security threat, easing concerns tied to his relationship with a woman suspected of spying for China. In his own statement, Huang said he was humbled by the decision and recommitted to the role. He credited Jones, outgoing interim chief Colette Kress and Nvidia’s senior team for steering the company through a turbulent stretch, and thanked employees, shareholders, industry leaders and his family for standing by him. “I can’t wait to get back to work,” he said. Kress will go back to serving as chief financial officer.
