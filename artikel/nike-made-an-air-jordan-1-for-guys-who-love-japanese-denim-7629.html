Nike Made an Air Jordan 1 for Guys Who Love Japanese Denim

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that menswear guys love Japanese denim. Menswear guys also love sneakers. So why not make some kicks for the people who can tell you exactly which artisanal mill produced their selvedge? That seems to be the thinking behind the latest Social Status x Air Jordan 1 Low OG ‘Sashiko.’.

It has been revealed that social Status is a North Carolina-based sneaker and streetwear boutique owned by the Whitaker Group, the same firm behind A Ma Maniére. Over the years, it’s worked with Nike on everything from milk-inspired Dunks to Air Pennys. The shop’s last proper Jordan collab was an extremely hairy Air Jordan 6 from 2019, but now the pair are back with something a little easier to wear. Social Status x Air Jordan 1 Low OG ‘Sashiko’.

It has been revealed that social Status’s take on the AJ1 Low starts with the classic ‘Chicago’ color palette. The toebox is white, the overlays are red, and the Swoosh and collar are black. But rather than keeping everything box-fresh, Social Status has roughed up the leather so it arrives pre-scuffed, as though you’ve owned these shoes for years.

It has been revealed that the major switch happens across the sidewalls. The usual white leather panels have been replaced with mismatched patches of indigo fabric covered in white sashiko-style stitching. Sashiko is a traditional Japanese embroidery technique used to reinforce, repair, and extend the life of clothing. It’s particularly associated with vintage workwear and denim, which is why these Jordans look like they’ve been mended using a very expensive pair of jeans. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

There’s also an old-school nylon tongue, a slightly aged sole, and a custom shoebox wrapped in the same blue pattern.

Social Status x Air Jordan 1 Low OG ‘Sashiko’.

Industry observers note that while you might normally expect this sort of significant Jordan release to land on the High, it’s nice to see the Low silhouette get some love. Some dudes prefer it, after all—it’s less bulky around the ankle and works better under wider trousers. You still get all the bits that make an Air Jordan 1 instantly recognizable, only without half the collar.

Recent findings suggest that the Social Status x Air Jordan 1 Low OG ‘Sashiko’ is set to drop on December 18 for $155 through Social Status, with a wider release via Nike, the SNKRS app, and select stockists expected to arrive later on. Japanese denim guys, get your credit cards ready.
