After surviving the Upside Down, Noah Schnapp apparently volunteered as tribute for the latest Hunger Games film.

Marking the opening weekend of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, the Stranger Things alum revealed that he auditioned to play Haymitch with a selfie in a blonde wig, admitting that Joseph Zada was the right choice.

“POV: my audition for Haymitch,” wrote Schnapp on his Instagram Story with some laughing emojis. “@josephzada think they picked right for this one.”.

Now playing in theaters, the Hunger Games prequel from director Francis Lawrence and writers Billy Ray and Michael Lesslie is based on Suzanne Collins’ 2025 novel Sunrise on the Reaping, set during Haymitch’s game in the Second Quarter Quell. (Related: Director of Machine Learning Insights...)

Starring Zada as Haymitch, Sunrise on the Reaping is set 24 years before 2012’s The Hunger Games, in which Woody Harrelson originated the role. It follows 2023’s The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes in continuity, set 40 years later. Watch on Deadline.

The movie also stars Elle Fanning, McKenna Grace, Ben Wang, Jesse Plemons, Glenn Close, Billy Porter, Maya Hawke, Kieran Culkin, Kelvin Harrison Jr., Lili Taylor and Ralph Fiennes.
