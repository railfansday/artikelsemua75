According to recent reports, the next spicy meeting of The Hunting Wives is almost in session. After emerging last year as one of Netflix's latest addictive hits, racking up 2 billion minutes watched in just its second week in the U.S., the Texas-set thriller will bring Sophie (Brittany Snow) and Margo (Malin Åkerman) back together on Thanksgiving, November 26. This time, they'll be venturing into dangerous and uncharted new territory after Season 1 covered everything from author May Cobb's book of the same name, but confidence is so high that the series will continue to deliver on the steamy twists and turns that a third season has already been ordered. It's now at the center of the streamer's world of book adaptations, and the success has reflected on Cobb, with her original novel shooting back up the charts ever since.

Industry observers note that cobb is so much more than The Hunting Wives, though. Since publishing the novel in 2021, she's added five latest titles to her resume, including My Summer Darlings, A Likeable Woman, The Hollywood Assistant, and, earlier this year, All the Little Houses. Apple TV is already working on bringing the latter bestseller to life as a series, following a competitive bidding war for the rights last month. Cobb will even serve as co-creator alongside one half of Prime Video's The Girlfriend's creative duo, Naomi Sheldon. If it ultimately becomes a success, it's already set up well to run for multiple seasons, as the author just released the sequel novel this past week, on November 17. Recent findings suggest that all the Little Secrets is now available on store shelves, picking back up in 1980s Longview, Texas, in the aftermath of All the Little Houses' explosive ending. Billed as a standalone thriller sequel, it follows the town's residents as they grapple with the unexpected murder of the social queen bee and the shockwaves her fall causes. With the throne vacated, others are vying for status in the power vacuum, including a trad wife who's now established herself in the neighborhood and an ambitious daughter ready to take what's hers. The stakes are even higher for her than just pinning a murder on someone, though. Sources indicate that cobb's novels share a lot of similar themes and often embrace their Texas setting, but it's rare for her to make an actual sequel to her stories. All the Little Houses and Secrets are an exception because, as she shared with Us Magazine, when she was writing the first of the two books, "I realized I had way too much for one book — and way way too much drama — so I knew that I needed to make it a series." That being remarked, she doesn't plan on more Longview-based drama after Secrets at this time, but she's not going to rule out revisiting her little world of secrets and scheming at some point. "I would love to keep that open as a possibility. Right now, we don’t have prepares for a book 3 for this. It is a duology, but never say never.”.
