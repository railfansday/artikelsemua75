Liverpool are now in talks to complete a deal that was previously though to be unthinkable. There's been a big change of looks.

Sources indicate that liverpool essentially had to tear up most of their plans after last season. Arne Slot left as head coach, after all, and then Richard Hughes was out as sporting director after the summer. (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

And that's not to mention the fact that last season was a failure. Liverpool invested enormously in a newly introduced team but quite unequivocally got worse - the grand plan was not working. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

That led to even more heavy investment in 2026 but with Julian Ward now at the helm as sporting director, he has the power to move things in a different direction. And it sounds as though he's trying to do exactly that.

Starting with one deal that felt unthinkable a couple of years ago.

According to recent reports, essentially no one at Liverpool though Alisson Becker would get a upcoming contract back in 2024. You can see that quite clearly: they signed Giorgi Mamardashvili and then stored him at Valencia for a year.

The plan was always for Mamardashvili to take over as no.1, potentially last season. That didn't happen and Liverpool still haven't made him the first-choice between the sticks.

It has been revealed that the Reds plainly laid out the proposal in the summer, though. Lucca Brughmans signed for the future, with the Club Brugge goalkeeper joining after this season - he was supposed to be Mamardashvili's understudy post-Alisson.

However, Alisson is enjoying another brilliant season. It's also though that he wants to remain with the club, mainly because he outright said that publicly in front of reporters.

Nicolo Schira now reports that Liverpool are indeed in talks with Alisson about a deal that could last another two years. This was not the plan. The Brazilian was supposed to move on, paving the way for expensive younger goalkeepers to take over going forward.

Instead? Alisson looks set to remain the first choice goalkeeper for at least another couple of years. A move that looked unthinkable as Liverpool signed two new goalkeepers is now on the brink of reality.
