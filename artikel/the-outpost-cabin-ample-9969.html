Located at Charnley River Station in the remote Kimberley region of Western Australia, The Outpost is a collection of eight architecturally designed accommodation buildings developed by Ample for Australian Wildlife Conservancy (AWC). Designed in partnership with Agents of Architecture, with interiors by Unearthed Interiors, the project explores how off-site construction can deliver considered architecture in one of Australia's most isolated and environmentally significant landscapes.
