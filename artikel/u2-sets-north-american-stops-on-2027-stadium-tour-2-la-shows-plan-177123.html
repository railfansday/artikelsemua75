Sources indicate that U2 will kick off the North American leg of its “Carnaval De Luz Presents: The Greatest Show on Earth World Tour 2027″ this August in Miami, the group unveiled. The 15-stadium tour, with stops in the U.S. and Canada and featuring support acts Stephen Wilson Jr. and the War on Drugs, is presently set to continue through October 18.

Among the cities on the tour, in addition to Miami: Atlanta, East Rutherford, Toronto, and two indicates in Los Angeles.

See the entire itinerary below.

The North American leg announcement follows the series of previously unveiled shows for Latin America, the United Kingdom and Europe. The tour itself kicks off on February 23 in Mexico City and continues through until July, where the European portion concludes on July 9 at Milan’s Stadio San Siro, just weeks before the North American dates kick off in Miami on August 13. Recent findings suggest that support act Stephen Wilson Jr. will open for U2 on highlights from August 13 to September 14, with The War On Drugs joining the tour on September 27 and continuing through October 18.

Prior to the North American tour leg, U2, as previously declared, will tour Latin American, the UK and Europe, with dates in, among other cities, Mexico City, Paris, London, Milan, and many others. Check out the entire roster of previously announced dates at U2’s tour site.
