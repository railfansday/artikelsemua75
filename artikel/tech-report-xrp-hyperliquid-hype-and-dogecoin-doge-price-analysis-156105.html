Hyperliquid continues to face significant selling pressure, trading near $62.43 after a steep decline from September highs around $97. The daily chart reveals a deteriorating technical structure, with lower highs and lower lows suggesting bears remain firmly in control.

It has been revealed that HYPE has slipped below its major moving averages, including the 200-day moving average near $68, reinforcing the bearish outlook. Meanwhile, the Relative Strength Index (RSI) hovers around 35, indicating weakening momentum and approaching oversold conditions.

In a recent development, immediate support lies between $60 and $61. A decisive breakdown below this region could expose $57, followed by the crucial 52–55 demand zone. Conversely, buyers must reclaim 68–70 to establish a meaningful recovery, with stronger resistance around 76–78.
