The draw for the 2026-27 Nations League quarter-finals is upon us, with England joined by seven other nations in the pot.

Thomas Tuchel’s side enjoying a near perfect group phase, which culminated with a stunning 3-2 win in Spain to top Group A3 at their host’s expense, and that means they are likely to avoid another European heavyweight in the quarter-finals.

Elsewhere, France sealed top spot in Group A1 with a win over Turkey, while Belgium thumped Italy 4-1 to finish as runners-up.

In A2, Germany topped the table with a 5-0 thrashing of the Netherlands, who slipped to second on goal difference after such a swing.

Portugal’s position was Group A4 was confirmed after their win over Wales on Tuesday, and it is Norway joining them in the knockout phases following a remarkable comeback win against Denmark.

The Nations League draw for the 2026/27 quarter-finals will take place today, Friday, November 20.

As well as the quarter-final draw, the promotion/relegation play-offs draw will also be conducted.

Coverage of the draw is due to start at 11am GMT, with the draw itself expected to begin shortly afterwards.

The Nations League draw will take place in Nyon, Switzerland.

TV channel: In the UK, the draw will be televised live UEFA TV.

Live stream: You can also stream the draw for free online via the UEFA YouTube page.

Live blog: You can follow the draw as it happens on the day through Standard Sport’s live blog.

The four group winners will face the four runners-up in a two-legged Nations League quarter-final.

According to recent reports, the group winners will all be seeded and will have home advantage in their respective second leg ties. Teams from the same group cannot be paired together in the knockout stage, therefore England and Spain - for example - will be kept apart.

The four group winners will be seeded in Pot 1, with the four runners-up will be unseeded in Pot 2.

Pot 1: France, England, Germany, Portugal.

Pot 2: Belgium, Spain, Netherlands, Norway.

The Nations League quarter-finals, as well as the League A/B and League B/C promotion/relegation play-offs, will be played in March 2027.

The first legs are scheduled for March 25-27, with the return legs scheduled for March 28-30.

The finals tournament is scheduled for June 2027.
