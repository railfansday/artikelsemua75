Liverpool will be hoping to welcome Ryan Gravenberch back into the fold after the midfielder missed the 4-0 victory over Manchester United.

The Reds were rampant at Anfield last weekend, thrashing their fierce rivals to spoil Mauricio Pochettino’s bow as the Red Devils’ new boss. (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

Dutch midfielder Gravenberch was forced to sit out with a persistent heel fracture, but manager Andoni Iraola has mentioned that the injury is a “day by day” situatiuon.

Fellow Netherlands international Cody Gakpo was also sidelined for the visit of United with a calf problem that he picked up on international duty, and he is set to miss out again here for the trip to Belgium, when the Merseysiders take on Club Brugge in the Champions League.

Jeremie Frimpong and Virgil van Dijk were welcome returners from their respective injury scares, while Rio Ngumoha played a starring role in the demolition job on Sunday in place of Gakpo off the left flank. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

You would expect Iraola to plump for a similar side, with Ronald Araujo at right-back, Jeremy Jacquet partnering Van Dijk and Milos Kerkez completing the back four ahead of Alisson.

In the engine room, Alexis Mac Allister was joined by Dominik Szoboszlai, who himself had been an injury concern, and that should be the partnership once more unless Gravenberch returns or Trey Nyoni is given an opportunity.

Ahead of them, Florian Wirtz’s good form continued as he registered a goal in the thrashing, and he was flanked by the dangerous Ngumoha and summer signing Bradley Barcola.

Alexander Isak led the line by example on Sunday, and should get the nod once more, with Hugo Ekitike still out with his Achilles rupture.

There could be returns to the squad for defenders Giovanni Leoni and Conor Bradley, with the pair finally making their comebacks to training after their serious knee injuries.
