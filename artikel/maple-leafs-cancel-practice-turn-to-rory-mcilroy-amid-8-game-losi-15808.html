The Toronto Maple Leafs are switching things up: After suffering their eighth straight loss on Thursday, the Maple Leafs turned to golf star Rory McIlroy to inspire them as their season starts to tank.

It has been revealed that toronto's losing streak continued with a 4-3 shootout loss to the Los Angeles Kings at home on Thursday. As a result, the team cancelled practice on Friday morning and instead met with McIlroy and sports psychologist Dr. Bob Rotella for a team meeting, via multiple reporters.

Sources indicate that mcIlroy — who was also present at Thursday's game — and Rotella reportedly spoke to the Leafs for two hours as part of a discussion moderated by Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment (MLSE) president and CEO Keith Pelley. Toronto coach Jim Hiller called it a "reflective day" when speaking to reporters afterwards.

According to recent reports, "That was probably the most impactful thing we could get out of the day," Hiller stated, via TSN's Mark Masters. "Two of the best in the business spent that time with us, it was pretty awesome.". (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Not everyone decided to forgo practice completely: Right winger William Nylander, who is second on the team in points, was spotted doing a solo workout on the ice on Friday despite the schedule change.

It has been revealed that it's been a rough stretch for the Maple Leafs, who have yet to win in November. The team's last victory was on Oct. 31, a 4-2 win over the woeful Vancouver Canucks; since then, the eight-game losing streak has dropped Toronto to a 8-13-3 record so far this season.

In a recent development, despite the notable talent on the Leafs' roster, the skid has left the team at the bottom of the Atlanta Division table. It also raises questions about how much more captain Auston Matthews will be willing to take before he joins his Team USA teammates in demanding a trade.
