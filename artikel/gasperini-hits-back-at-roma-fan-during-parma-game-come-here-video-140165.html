A Roma fan sparked a hilarious exchange with Gian Piero Gasperini during the Giallorossi’s 5-1 win over Parma on Saturday, telling the coach to sub Nahuel Molina off. ‘Come over here,’ Gasperini replied.

Sources indicate that roma cruised to a 5-1 victory over struggling Parma on Saturday. Inter’s 4-4 draw with Atalanta the following day allowed the Giallorossi to move above the Nerazzurri into second place.

A video circulating widely on social media captures the exchange between Gasperini and a Roma supporter at the Stadio Olimpico during the match.

Speaking in a strong Roman accent, the fan urged the coach to “take Molina out.” Gasperini quickly retorted, “Come over here,” jokingly inviting the supporter to take his place on the bench and make the tactical decisions.

Gasperini’s response drew laughter from the other supporters, many of whom applauded the coach’s comeback.
