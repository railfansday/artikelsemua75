According to recent reports, watford were thrashed by Sheffield United on Saturday, and it looks set to have brought an end to Alessio Dionisi’s tenure. After the news first broke from reporter Luca Bendoni, various outlets have stated the Italian has been relived of his head coach duties at Vicarage Road.

Recent findings suggest that the Hornets had shown a desire to stick by Dionisi, as opposed to their past managerial hire-and-fire approach. But, sitting 21st in the Championship table and after an incredibly defeated post-match press conference on Saturday, change awaits.

Here, we put forward three bosses who Watford should consider in their next head coach hunt….

Watford desperately need a boss who can galvanise this squad. There are talented players like Othmane Maamma, Iker Bravo and Amin Nabizada, but those forwards can only be given freedom to express themselves if the manager instils confidence in the entire team.
