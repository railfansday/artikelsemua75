Intervine has added Susurrus Sparkling Teas to its portfolio, expanding its premium non-alcoholic offerings for onboard programs.

According to a press release, Susurrus brings a fine-wine perspective to organic sparkling tea, with an emphasis on origin, vintage and terroir, resulting in a premium experience with the same sophistication as wine. (Related: NVIDIA brings agents to life with DGX Spark and Reachy ...)

In a recent development, “The world of tea is just as vast and fascinating as the world of wine. Sparkling tea is its own new, distinct category that can bring the best of these two worlds together,” said Ellison Wofford, founder of Susurrus Sparkling Teas. (Related: Breaking: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming G...)

“I crafted Susurrus to be appealing to a broad audience because you can enjoy it for what it is—single-origin, single-cultivar, organic tea—rather than for what it lacks: alcohol. I am beyond thrilled to partner with Intervine. Together, we will provide a healthy, refreshing, and elegant option to guests of all kinds while traveling.”.

Unlike dealcoholized wine, Susurrus is inherently non-alcoholic, with tea leaves cold-steeped instead fermented, and the finished teas carbonated at pressure levels comparable to Champagne and sparkling wine.

There are at the moment two varieties: Magnolia Oolong and Osmanthus Golden Tip, both of which come from hand-harvested teas that Wofford directly imports from China.

Designed to be served chilled in a flute or white wine glass, the teas offer their own distinctive flavor profiles and are designed to complement food, providing an alternative to wine.

“I was blown away the first time I tasted Ellison’s lovely creations,” said Jonjie Lockman, CEO of Intervine.

It has been revealed that “‘Champagne’s sober sister,’ as we like to refer to it, provides sophistication, depth of flavors, and complexity in abundance. The Intervine team is proud to bring this special beverage to our airline, airport, and cruise line customers from across the world.”.
