90 Day Fiancé alum Sheila Mangubat has died. She was 35.

According to recent reports, “It breaks my heart to share the news from her family that Sheila passed away on Saturday in the Philippines,” Sheila’s former fiancé, David Dangerfield, wrote via Instagram, announcing his ex’s death. “I am devastated and still trying to process this loss.”.

It has been revealed that he continued, “Please keep her son, her family and me in your prayers. Thank you for all your love and support. ❤️🕊️.”.

A cause of death has not been publicly revealed at the time of publication.

Recent findings suggest that “Rest in peace, my beloved sister shiela mangubat😥,” her sister, Grace Mangubat, wrote via Facebook. “Sorry kaau layo kaau ko, dili ko kauli DHA😥😥😥 fly high my Sister we will miss you♥️.”.

David and Sheila’s fellow 90 Day alums have also offered their condolences.

In a recent development, “Waoo this so sad 😞,” Anny Francsico wrote via Instagram comment. “She was so young, sorry David😭😭😭.”.

In a recent development, sheila met David, 45, in a Facebook group for deaf singles in 2021, soon embarking on a long-distance relationship. Their engagement was featured on season 6 of TLC’s 90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days in 2023.

“I’ve lived in Omaha on my own for a long time now. I want to start a family and have a wife,” David, who was born deaf, said in a June 2023 episode. “I was open to dating a hearing, deaf or hard of hearing person. I was putting myself out there consistently and getting a lot of ‘no’s.’ It hurt my feelings because I wanted to be in a relationship.”.

Eventually, David connected with Sheila, who used a hearing aid to help her partial deafness.

“I’m happy I never gave up because I found my love,” he mentioned at the time. “I have a girlfriend who’s name is Sheila, and she’s beautiful. She is 31 and lives in Cebu, Philippines. When I first saw Sheila’s picture, it got my heart beating.”.
