Recent findings suggest that what’s inside the bag? For starters a bubblegum pink “gun,” which Knight teased is actually her fashion hack when she needs on-the-go alterations.

Recent findings suggest that “Say you need to be snatched like J.Lo style on the red carpet, you just [snap],” Knight told Us, demonstrating how the pink tag gun quickly attaches two pieces of fabric. “‘Woah, woah, I don’t have time to sew this, I gotta go.’ And now you’re snatched.”.

Inside her tote, Knight carries a clear “health bag,” complete with everything a boss babe might need, including a Neom Energy Pen.

“Put it on your wrist,” she told Us, noting the scented roller gives you a quick jolt of energy. “Woah, I am alive!”.

Knight pulled out a hormone replacement patch, joking that her hormones have no power over her day to day.

According to recent reports, “What’s up perimenopause?” she noted with a laugh, looking directly at the camera. “I’m ready. Oh, you can’t get me low estrogen.”.

It has been revealed that “I’ve got some stickers I made of Andy Cohen,” the E! News fashion correspondent revealed, showing off a sheet of stickers of Cohen, 58, rocking different looks.

Knight proceeded to peel one and place it on the side of a planter. “Little drops of Andy all over New York City,” she smirked.

It has been revealed that “What, is this a book that I wrote? With my own brain?” Knight teased as she pulled out a copy of her new book, The F*ck Them Theory: Self-Help for People Who Are Done Being the Bigger Person, on sale Tuesday, November 24.

Industry observers note that when asked what when was the “last time” that she was unable to apply her “f*** them theory” for professional purposes, Knight told Us, she sent a “really mean” email at 3 a.m. the night before.

According to recent reports, “I expect perfection from myself. I expect perfection for the people that I work for,” she confessed. “The thing is, I know not everything is going to go right. And that’s what this book is about.”.

Knight explained that her book is really about “taking the rejection and the heartbreak and your own sorta self-loating, and self stuff and turning it into rocket fuel.”.
