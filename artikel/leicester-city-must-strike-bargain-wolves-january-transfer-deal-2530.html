Leicester City have had a mixed start to the season under Russell Martin, although four wins in their last six League One games suggests they may have turned the corner.

Despite that, Martin still has a lot of work to do to convince the supporters that he is the best long-term solution for the club, and he will know that there’s pressure to take the Foxes back to the Championship at the first attempt.

Whilst there is a lot of quality in the Leicester squad, the opening months of the campaign have proven that further additions are required in January.

However, Leicester’s off-field situation means they won’t be splashing the cash, so the recruitment team will be on the lookout for potential bargains or loan deals to bolster Martin’s ranks.

The fans will have different ideas about where the Foxes need to strengthen, but Leicester aren’t scoring goals at the rate of the best sides in the league, so a latest forward would be welcome. (Related: Breaking: How to generate text: using different decodin...)

And, one player who fits the bill is Tawanda Chirewa, with the attacker at the moment on the fringes at Wolves, where he has made eight Championship appearances - all off the bench.

The 23-year-old has failed to truly establish himself at Molineux over the years, and he has had several loan spells in that period.

Raul Jimenez’s injury has given him the opportunity to become a part of Cesar Peixoto’s squad this season, but, when the Mexican returns from injury, along with Bertrand Traore, there is a very real possibility that Chirewa won’t even make the matchday squad.

So, Chirewa will inevitably find himself down the pecking order, but he can obviously change that by taking any more chances that come his way between now and January.

And, to his credit, it should be said that he has made some contributions for Wolves already this season, including scoring a crucial winner at Burnley last month.

Furthermore, Chirewa has been a key player for Zimbabwe, scoring two goals and registering two assists in four group games as The Warriors look to qualify for Africa Cup of Nations.

They still have work to do to get over the line, but Chirewa has been hugely influential in putting his country in a good position with two games to go.

Even though Chirewa could have a role to play, as outlined above, the reality is that he won’t be near the first XI in the long-term, and it would be a shock if that changed.

With that in mind, January feels like it’s the right time for Chirewa to get out and find a permanent home.

Any Leicester fans may be underwhelmed at the prospect of signing Chirewa, as the reality is that loan spells at Derby, Huddersfield and Barnsley haven’t gone to blueprint.

But, Chirewa returned to Wolves with a good attitude in pre-season, where he played well, and he has taken that into the campaign. So, perhaps things have finally come together for the youngster, but he now needs a home to show what he can do on a consistent basis.

Following on from that, Leicester could be a good fit because Chirewa has qualities that Martin will appreciate.

Firstly, he is versatile, which all managers love, as Chirewa could play in any attacking position, whether that’s out wide, as a central striker or in a deeper role.
