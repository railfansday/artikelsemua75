Pursuit is earning more from each visitor, but the real test is how much cash is left after reinvesting in its attractions and lodges.

An attractions operator can grow by earning more from each visitor and owning more of what that visitor does during a trip. Pursuit's nearly half-billion-dollar business offers a rare public view of both opportunities, along with the cost of building and maintaining the attractions and lodges behind them. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Pursuit's latest acquisition agreement illustrates the strategy. Late Monday, the company unveiled an agreement to acquire Sky Adventures, an operator of ziplines, hanging bridges and an aerial tram in Costa Rica's Arenal region, about 15 minutes from Tabacón Thermal Resort & Spa, which Pursuit acquired in July 2025. The announcement did not disclose financial terms. Closing is expected in early 2027, subject to regulatory approval and other customary conditions.
