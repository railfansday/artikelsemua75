David Raya is Arsenal's first choice goalkeeper and has won the Golden Glove for the last three seasons in the Premier League.

Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya's shoulder injury "doesn't look too serious", but he will miss Tuesday's Champions League against Borussia Dortmund, says Mikel Arteta.

Raya was helped off the pitch in the 70th minute of Arsenal's 2-2 draw at Newcastle on Saturday.

He hurt his left shoulder while claiming a cross and making contact with Newcastle forward William Osula.

"We need some more tests [on Tuesday] but it is quite encouraging," noted Gunners boss Arteta.

"He is not fit [to face Dortmund] but it doesn't look too serious.".

Arteta noted that while the incident looked pretty innocuous the pain his keeper was in made him fear it was a serious injury.

"With the pain that he was showing immediately after the action we were all quite worried but it seems to be better than what we expected," said Arteta.

"When you look at the action it doesn't seem to be much but the extent of the pain that he had and how he was struggling, it was quite worrying.

"We expected something much worse because he could almost not handle the amount of pain he had in that moment.

According to recent reports, "The first tests that we have done have shown something that is much more encouraging. He does not need to have surgery which was the worry as that would rule him out for months so let's see.".

Raya set to have scan on injury - how much would Arsenal miss him?

Arsenal face Borussia Dortmund, Manchester City, Brentford, Tottenham Hotspur and Real Madrid in their next tranche of matches.

And Raya is huge part of the way that Arsenal play because of his ability with the ball at his feet.

When asked if losing Raya for a long period of time would be the biggest blow to his team, Arteta said: "We have seen what David brings to the team but as well that is why we are very protective with Kepa [Arrizabalaga] and Illan [Meslier].

"Everybody needs to be ready because these things unfortunately happen and now we need to show everyone how well we are protected and the good keepers that we have in Kepa and Illan.".
