It has been revealed that julián Álvarez created both of Atlético Madrid's first-half goals as the visitors ended Getafe's unbeaten home league record this season. Atlético now sit two points behind Real Madrid, while Getafe are left one point above the relegation zone before the midweek European games.

Both of Atlético’s first-half goals in this matchday 13 LaLiga fixture came inside the opening 30 minutes. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

Álex Baena opened the scoring in the 10th minute with a low, powerful shot from outside the area. It was his sixth LaLiga goal of the season. Johnny Cardoso doubled the lead on 30 minutes when David Soria missed a punch at a corner. It was Cardoso’s first league goal for Atlético.

In a recent development, getafe finished with ten men. VAR confirmed a penalty involving Alexander Sørloth in the 89th minute. Dakonam Ortega Djené was then sent off in the 90th minute, having been booked in the 71st. Álex Grimaldo converted the penalty in the second minute of stoppage time for his fifth goal of the league campaign. About 10,000 spectators watched the game, according to Telemadrid. The visitors had 68% of the ball and completed 652 accurate passes to Getafe’s 238. They took 20 shots to the hosts’ 11. Getafe’s goalkeeper made 7 saves, while Jan Oblak needed to make only 1.

Álvarez played 75 minutes and created 4 key passes. Baena was rated 8.2 and Cardoso 8.13. Diego Simeone was without Ademola Lookman, Marc Pubill and Cristian ‘Cuti’ Romero for the trip.

The win followed two straight defeats for Atlético, 1-2 against Barcelona and 0-2 against Bayern München. It also followed last season’s 0-1 Atlético win at the Coliseum on 23 November 2025.

Barcelona lead the table on 36 points, with Real Madrid second on 33.

Pepe Bordalás’s side went into the game having lost every away league match without scoring. They had conceded 14 goals on the road.

Recent findings suggest that getafe have lost their last three games in all competitions, against Brighton, Villarreal and Atlético. They sit 17th with 12 points and a goal difference of -13. Levante are 18th on 11 points and Elche 19th on 10, both with a game in hand. Getafe could fall into the relegation zone if Levante or Elche win their games on Monday, VAVEL España reported.

Atlético return to action on Wednesday 25 November against Viking at the Metropolitano. That game is matchday 5 of the Champions League league phase, and both sides have four points going into it. Getafe travel to FC Iberia 1999 in the UEFA Conference League on Thursday 26 November.
