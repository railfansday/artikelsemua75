His forecast places early 2027 on investors’ radar, although the timing remains an individual market expectation rather than confirmation of an approaching price reversal.

XRP has experienced considerable selling pressure during November, with the token losing the $1 psychological level and struggling to attract sustained buying interest.

Broader crypto-market liquidity, Bitcoin’s direction, institutional demand and developments across the XRP ecosystem will remain crucial catalysts. A sustained recovery in these areas could improve XRP’s prospects heading into 2027. Conversely, continued market weakness could delay the anticipated bullish phase.

According to recent reports, XRP is trading near $0.95, with its daily chart showing early stabilization after a prolonged decline. The immediate demand zone sits around $0.90–$0.92, where buyers are attempting to establish support.

However, XRP remains below several important moving-average resistance levels, keeping the broader technical structure bearish. A sustained move above $1.00–$1.06 could provide the first meaningful indication of improving momentum. Further resistance appears around $1.13, $1.20 and $1.29.

According to recent reports, A sustained recovery above $1.06 could strengthen the bullish structure and bring $1.13 into focus. However, a daily breakdown below $0.90 could expose XRP to another decline toward $0.85.

Michel’s forecast has introduced a potential recovery timeline, but XRP still needs to establish stronger price support before investors can consider the correction over.

The first notable signal would be sustained buying around $0.90, followed by a recovery above $1.00. Improving institutional demand and a broader crypto-market rebound could provide additional support. For now, the technical picture remains cautious. XRP’s ability to defend $0.90 and reclaim $1 will be crucial in determining whether the token can build a stronger foundation ahead of the February–March 2027 rally anticipated by Michel.
