Sandra Bullock is soaking up some quality time with her children at the happiest place on Earth.

Bullock, 62, took to Instagram on Sunday, November 22, to share family vacation photos featuring kids Louis, 16, and Laila, 13, during a trip to Disneyland in Anaheim, California. In one image, Laila looked at Bullock as they rode a rollercoaster, while another picture revealed the actress taking a selfie as the group walked ahead.

“Sometimes you just need to stop worrying and remind yourself of what it feels like to be a kid,” she captioned the series of photos. “FYI There are no calories in ice cream if you ride the Guardians of the Galaxy ride and don’t cry 😉.”.

Bullock and her children were also joined by longtime friend Josh Holloway, wife Yessica Holloway and son Hunter, 12. While tagging them in the caption, Bullock added, “Family and friends ♥️.”.

In a recent development, bullock adopted her son in 2010 and added her daughter to their family in 2015. Five years later, Bullock’s partner, Bryan Randall, was diagnosed with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). Randall died in 2023 at age 57.
