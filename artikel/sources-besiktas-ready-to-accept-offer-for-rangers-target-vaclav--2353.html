Besiktas are open to offloading Vaclav Cerny in the latest year amid reports of Rangers' renewed interest in his services, sources have told Football Insider.

Cerny impressed during a season-long loan spell at Rangers in 2024-25 after he had fallen out of favour at Wolfsburg.

The 29-year-old winger registered 18 goals and nine assists in 52 appearances across all competitions in his sole campaign at Ibrox.

He was closely linked with a return to the club over the summer after a mixed season at Besiktas, as Derek McInnes looked to add to his wide options. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the Turkish giants may accept an offer for his services in January, but Rangers are unlikely to seal another move for Cerny.

McInnes is content with his attacking options as it stands, with the Gers currently in the midst of a 12-game unbeaten run.

Cerny registered seven goals and eight assists in 34 games for Besiktas last term, falling out of favour towards the end of the campaign.

He started this season strongly, with four goals and an assist in his first 12 appearances, but reports of an exit have increased in recent weeks, as he has only notched one assist in his previous seven outings.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "He [Cerny] could be made available by Besiktas in January. I would imagine a move back to Rangers is probably unlikely, despite the constant noise mainly coming out of Turkey, rather than Scotland, about Rangers potentially making a move to take him back to Ibrox.

"Obviously he had a great loan spell there before, and they were interested in potentially signing him in the summer, but were priced out of a move by Besiktas.".

Gers fans were keen to see Cerny re-sign in the summer, and the same is likely to apply now as they aim to finally topple Celtic in the Premiership once again.

Club hero and ex-boss Barry Ferguson insisted in August that the Czech international's signature should have been a priority ahead of the new season.

In a recent development, speaking via Go Radio, he stated: "Yeah, no-brainer, if a deal can be done. He's a difference-maker, he's a top professional.

Recent findings suggest that "He understands Glasgow, he understands what goes on here. He's been here before and he's a quality operator, so he ticks all the boxes for me.".
