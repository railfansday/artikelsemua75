Keeper close to tears at Newcastle after hurting shoulder.

Arsenal held 2-2, with Jaissle hailing ‘excellent’ display.

Arsenal were left sweating on the fitness of their goalkeeper David Raya after the Spaniard was forced off with a shoulder injury during the second half of the 2-2 draw at Newcastle on Saturday evening.

Sources indicate that it left Mikel Arteta’s side six points clear of Brighton and Manchester City at the top of the Premier League but Arsenal’s manager was more concerned about Raya following an accidental collision with Newcastle’s William Osula. The Spaniard was helped off in evident pain and looked close to tears.
