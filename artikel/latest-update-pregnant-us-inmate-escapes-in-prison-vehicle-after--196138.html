A pregnant inmate has escaped from custody in the US state of Newly introduced Mexico, after taking control of a police vehicle while returning to prison from a prenatal appointment, the state's corrections department has said.

Rebecca Taylor, 26, took control of the Dodge Journey and drove away when the team transporting her and another inmate stopped to pick up food in Albuquerque, local media reported. (Related: Breaking: Introducing AnyLanguageModel: One API for Loc...)

The Latest Mexico Corrections Department remarked that the vehicle and her prison jumpsuit were later recovered, but Taylor remains on the run.

She had been imprisoned for multiple theft and burglary charges, the department stated in a Facebook statement.

Local media reported that another inmate, also travelling back from a prenatal appointment, was able to get out of the car as Taylor took control on Wednesday morning.

The corrections department has remarked that a multi-agency search for Taylor is under way. The BBC has approached it for an update.

Sources indicate that the Albuquerque Journal reported that Taylor is presently in her second trimester of pregnancy. For this reason, she was not restrained during transportation, department spokesperson Brittany Roembach stated.

"It is the understanding right now that one officer was with the vehicle but not inside of the vehicle when this occurred," she was quoted as saying.

All prisons in New Mexico went into lockdown following Taylor's escape, but it was lifted at 06:00 on Thursday.

However the prison where she was being held, the Western Latest Mexico Corrections Facility, will remain on "modified movement", and no visitors are at the moment allowed.

The public has been told not to approach Rebecca Taylor if they see her, and call police straight away.
