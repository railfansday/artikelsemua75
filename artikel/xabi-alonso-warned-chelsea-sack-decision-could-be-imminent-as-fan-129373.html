Xabi Alonso has been told that Chelsea will sack him as manager unless both performances and results improve fast.

The Blues suffered a humiliating 4-1 defeat by Leeds at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, coming two weeks after a defeat at struggling Sunderland and a very rare home loss to rivals Tottenham.

Alonso’s substitutions have often come under question, and his introduction of Malo Gusto against Leeds was met by chants from a section of supporters of “you don’t know what you’re doing”, while there is said to be a general acceptance that his methods will take a while to implement. The defeat leaves Chelsea sitting in ninth place, 12 points off Premier League leaders Arsenal with just 11 games of the season played.

Former Chelsea player Jason Cundy described the defeat to Leeds as “disgusting”, and expects there to be a newly introduced manager in the Blues dugout if there is not an instant improvement.

Chelsea travel to strugglers Nottingham Forest next weekend before hosting relegation-threatened Crystal Palace the week after.

Cundy mentioned on talkSPORT: “We are rotten. We were so bad, it was so bad… I’m in disbelief at what I’ve just witnessed.”.

In a warning to Alonso, he added: “That was a worrying performance, worrying. If that carries on, Alonso won’t keep his job, I’m telling you that for nothing.

“You can’t do what you did against Sunderland (1-0 loss), you can’t do what you did against Leeds today and lose those two games.

“We were disgusting today, absolutely disgusting, I’m still trying to find the words… as every minute ticked by, we got worse, and Leeds got better.”.
