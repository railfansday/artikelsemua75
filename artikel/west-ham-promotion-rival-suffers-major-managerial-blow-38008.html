West Ham United’s Championship promotion hopes may have received an unexpected boost following confirmation that Swansea City manager Vítor Matos has left the Welsh club to take charge of French side Strasbourg.

Sources indicate that the Portuguese coach had established Swansea as genuine promotion contenders, with the Swans sitting sixth in the Championship. His departure comes at an awkward time for a club attempting to maintain its challenge near the top of the table.

Sources indicate that strasbourg officially appointed Matos on Wednesday evening, replacing Hugo Oliveira. And whilst there’s no guarantee Swansea will suffer without him, I certainly regard this as good news for West Ham.

Matos was an extremely capable manager who had Swansea punching well above their weight.

It has been revealed that when he arrived in November 2025, Swansea were languishing in 20th position. He eventually guided them to an 11th-place finish before establishing them amongst the promotion contenders this season.

I suspect they’ll drop off without him.

According to recent reports, that stated, with only one win in their last five matches, Swansea were struggling anyway. But I have little doubt this departure won’t help their cause.

Interestingly, Claret & Hugh reported last month that Matos could be targeted by Crystal Palace, highlighting how losing their manager could benefit West Ham’s promotion ambitions.

The destination has turned out to be Strasbourg rather than Selhurst Park, but the potential consequences for Swansea remain much the same.

From a West Ham perspective, I’ll certainly take it.
