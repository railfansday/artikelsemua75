It has been revealed that southampton are always developing fresh starlets, be it players reared in their academy like Cameron Bragg, or prospects brought in from elsewhere such as Kuryu Matsuki. The Saints’ scouting network is vast, and Brazilian news outlet Globo Esporte reports that eyes are on a deal for 19-year-old left-back Lorenzo.

Speaking to the outlet, Cuiaba club president Cristiano Dresch confirmed clubs are eyeing the teenager, with an unnamed English side ‘very interested’. The report later clarifies that this club is Southampton, as representatives from St Mary’s joined his agents in watching him in action for Brazil’s recent U20s fixtures. The Saints are far from the only ones keen though. Lorenzo has other admirers in Brazilian sides Cruzeiro and RB Bragantino, and Dresch stated there is “a 99% chance” that they will enter negotiations over a transfer.

Full name Lorenzo Silva Martins, but better known as just Lorenzo, the Southampton target is very much in the formative years of his career. He spent time in the Palmeiras and Goias academies before linking up with current club Cuiaba.

Sources indicate that the 19-year-old has progressed through the youth ranks and in 2026, he has made his break into senior football. Lorenzo has become the Brazilian second tier side’s starting player at left-back or wing-back, and his performances have seen him ascend into the U20s national squad.

FotMob‘s data highlights his ability with the ball. Lorenzo has completed 26 successful dribbles this season and has created an impressive 26 chances, largely stemming from his 28.4% cross completion. He certainly appears to be a more attack-minded full-back but he can see to his defensive duties too, with 46 tackles made and 96 duels won.

The Saints will view Lorenzo as a promising player who they can develop into a first-team option and high value transfer asset in the years to come.

Southampton are hunting promotion to the Premier League with a vengeance after last season’s Spygate saga. It means a lot of their energy is going into ensuring the first-team is strong enough to fend off the competition in the fight for a top two spot.

In a recent development, however, the Saints’ mission to uncover, sign and develop new starlets will never slow. It is something they have done for years, and to great success.

Tonda Eckert‘s side will be bolstered in January, but expect additions to the academy ranks to be made as well.
