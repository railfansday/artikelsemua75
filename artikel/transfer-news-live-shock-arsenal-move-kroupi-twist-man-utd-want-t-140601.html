Latest transfer news and rumours.

Sky Germany reports that Bayern Munich are weighing up a move for Schalke 04 goalkeeper Loris Karius.

However, the 33-year-old says he hasn’t spoken to anyone about a potential transfer.

Diario Sport claims that Barcelona now face competition from Chelsea, Manchester United and Manchester City for FC Twente defender Ruud Nijstad.

Barca have been leading the race for the 18-year-old, but Twente’s asking price of over €20million could complicate a January move.

Manchester United have hired former Arsenal coach Sam Wilson, who has already started work as part of Mauricio Pochettino’s backroom staff, writes Matt Verri.

Wilson left Arsenal in the summer, having been at the club for 12 years.

He was the lead physical performance coach under Mikel Arteta up until the end of last season, playing his part in Arsenal’s Premier League title-winning campaign.

Wilson left the Gunners as part of the club’s overhaul of their medical department, with significant changes made in an attempt to address the injury problems that have hindered the squad in recent seasons.

Chelsea could potentially sign both Alex Scott and Lamine Camara in January.

The Blues are on the hunt for midfield reinforcements after losing Enzo Fernandez to Manchester City on summer deadline day and seeing a move for Monaco’s Camara fall through at the 11th hour.

Bournemouth’s Scott has become their No1 target amid a fierce transfer battle with Liverpool, while both clubs are also remarked to retain an interest in Camara.

TEAMtalk report that Chelsea have not ruled signing both players in January.

Manchester United are stated to be pondering a January move for Ivan Toney.

The England striker is believed to be keen on returning to the Premier League in 2027 after a year-and-a-half in Saudi Arabia with Al-Ahli.

Several clubs are noted to be interested in Toney, with Football Insider stating that Manchester United are being kept closely informed of the situation.

TEAMtalk claim that Arsenal, Chelsea, Tottenham and Crystal Palace have also been made aware of the availability of the former Brentford frontman, who could be signed on an initial loan deal that includes an option to buy.

Arsenal have a long-term interest in Jamal Musiala, reports claim.

Musiala’s Bayern Munich future has been thrown into doubt of late as he receives ongoing treatment for a neurological dysfunction that saw him faint twice during pre-season friendlies over the summer.

He has only played once since September 18 due to a muscle injury, leading to suggestions that Bayern no longer view his substantial wages as feasible amid public criticism from global players’ union FIFPRO.

According to talkSPORT, Arsenal and Liverpool both hold a long-standing interest in Musiala, with Chelsea also linked with his services in the past.

Calvin Bassey has confirmed he is in talks over a new contract with Fulham, amid interest from Manchester United.

As reported by Standard Sport earlier this week, 26-year-old centre-back Bassey, was expected to be a transfer target for Mauricio Pochettino in the January transfer window.

Defence is a key area to strengthen for Man Utd, and Fulham captain Bassey has an admirer in Pochettino.

But Bassey, who is in the third year of a four-year contract at Fulham, is happy in west London and ready to commit his future to the club.

Fulham, who hold the option of extending Bassey's contract by a further 12 months, are keen to keep hold of Bassey, especially after Alvaro Arbeloa promoted him to captain in the summer.

Confirming Standard Sport's reporting, Bassey told TNT Sports: "We’re still negotiating, we’re still talking and obviously it’s an amazing place to be.

Sources indicate that "I’ve settled in nicely and the manager has put a lot of faith and trust in me. So, it’s nice to be here and hopefully we will see what happens with the negotiations.”.

Bruno Fernandes has revealed that talks over a latest Manchester United contract have ended.

The club captain is stated to have rejected two offers from Man Utd to extend his current deal, which is set to expire at the end of the season though the club have the option to extend by an extra 12 months.

But the decision has been made to put discussions on hold as he believes the club's priority should be returning to winning ways.

"Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, highlights that they want me to stay,” he told Sky Sports.

“And there are no negotiations going on.

I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations.

"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side.

“I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.".

AC Milan are reportedly considering a move for Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes, who has rejected another latest contract offer at Old Trafford.

According to Corriere dello Sport, Milan are keeping an eye on Fernandes’ situation at United, with the possibility of a move being explored should circumstances allow.

The midfielder’s contract status could make him an attractive target for the Rossoneri, although no formal negotiations or agreement have been reported at this stage.

Inter Milan are weighing up a summer move for Cristhian Mosquera.

Per reports in Italy, the Nerazzurri feel a move might be feasible, with Mosquera having been pushed down the Arsenal pecking order since Ezri Konsa’s arrival.

They think he could be available for around £25m.
