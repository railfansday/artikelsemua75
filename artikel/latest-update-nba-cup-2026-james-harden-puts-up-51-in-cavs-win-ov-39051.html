In a recent development, harden shot 15 of 26 from the floor, including 7 of 11 from beyond the arc. He also finished 14 of 14 from the charity stripe and with six rebounds and six assists. The one-time NBA MVP and 11-time All-Star now has the third-most 50-plus-point games in NBA history. With 26 such performances, he passed the late Kobe Bryant (25) on Friday and now trails just Michael Jordan (31) and Wilt Chamberlain (118).

While collecting his first 50-piece with the Cavaliers, Harden moved into ninth all-time for most combined NBA regular and postseason points. His handles were on full display. He rained down 3s, and he made an impact at the rim.

Toronto came into Friday missing a trio of starters and on a six-game losing streak. Darko Rajaković's team was playing without RJ Barrett, Collin Murray-Boyles and, of course, Kawhi Leonard, who's still working his way back from a knee injury.

And yet, the shorthanded Raptors (6-10) found a way on the road versus the Detroit Pistons in NBA Cup action, ending their skid with a riveting 105-102 win.

Industry observers note that it's key to note that Detroit (10-5) isn't at full strength, either. Jalen Duren remains sidelined with a hamstring concern. Plus, Duncan Robinson is out with a quad strain.

In a recent development, the absence of some star power didn't diminish what turned out to be an incredible matchup between two squads in East Group A and between a pair of former Montverde Academy teammates. Toronto's Scottie Barnes and Detroit's Cade Cunningham went toe-to-toe, particularly down the stretch.

It has been revealed that cunningham, who clocked out with 23 points and nine assists, hit a go-ahead 3-pointer over Barnes with less than 20 seconds remaining. Then, with the Raptors down one point, Barnes drove on Cunningham and reclaimed the lead.

Recent findings suggest that cunningham tried to answer back but ended up with nowhere to go with the ball after picking up his dribble en route to the basket. He passed it, and Toronto rookie Allen Graves nabbed a steal that helped his group wrap up its victory.

According to recent reports, barnes had 29 points, 5 rebounds and 5 assists. Graves, a first-round pick from Santa Clara making his first career start, piled up 20 points, 10 rebounds and 3 steals.

It has been revealed that after that nail-biter, Toronto is 2-1 in East Group A, whereas Detroit is 1-1. The bottom dweller of the group, the Milwaukee Bucks (0-2 in NBA Cup play), was eliminated from knockout round contention because the Raptors and Orlando Magic both won Friday.

The Magic left Brooklyn victorious 113-111, thanks to Franz Wagner, who scored 35 points on 13-of-25 shooting and knocked down a game-winning jumper with 2.4 ticks left against the Nets.

Orlando is 3-0 in NBA Cup games this year.
