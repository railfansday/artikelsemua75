Doubling Down With the Derricos alum Karen Derrico hit several legal setbacks at her latest court hearing months after being arrested for allegedly threatening to kill her ex-husband, Deon Derrico, and their 14 children.

Court records obtained exclusively by Us reveal that Karen, 46, and her legal team asked a Nevada judge to drop her charge of preventing a witness from testifying through threat of violence and aggravated stalking when they appeared in court on Thursday, November 19.

In a recent development, after her June arrest, Karen was ordered to wear an ankle monitor and to stay away from Deon, 56, when she was released on a $2,000 bond. On Thursday, the judge ordered that Karen’s no-contact order remain in place.

According to court records, Deon took the stand against his wife during the hearing, and Karen waived her right to testify or present evidence.

Her arraignment has been scheduled for November 30.

Us reached out to Karen’s lawyer for comment.

Recent findings suggest that earlier this year, Karen was arrested in Las Vegas and charged with harassment, violating a restraining order, preventing a witness from testifying through threat of violence and aggravated stalking. She was arraigned on June 23 and pleaded not guilty.

Karen was accused of threatening to kill Deon and their 14 children. Deon and Karen confirmed in June 2024 that they were getting divorced, but had still been living together. When Us spoke to him after her arrest, she had recently moved out.

“She had done threatening things to me and things that made me question my life,” Deon exclusively told Us when he broke his silence on June 26.

Deon claimed he told his mother that if anything happened to him, they should “look at Karen.”.

“That’s the level of anger and hatred I was feeling from her,” he explained.

In a recent development, deon told Us that he had filed a protective order against Karen earlier this year. He claimed Karen had allegedly sent him threatening emails asking him to drop the request.

Sources indicate that “I just ignored her emails, and then they kept getting more and more aggressive,” Deon alleged. “And she had already been threatening the children about killing them.”.

TMZ released a phone call between Karen and some of her kids in June. In the audio, Karen could be heard telling them, “Don’t ever do that again. Because you make me regret it. Don’t play with me.”.

Karen and Deon rose to fame on the TLC reality series Doubling Down With the Derricos, which documented the former couple’s life raising their 14 kids, including sets of twins, triplets and quintuplets. The show ran for five seasons from 2020 to 2024, when Karen and Deon announced their divorce.
