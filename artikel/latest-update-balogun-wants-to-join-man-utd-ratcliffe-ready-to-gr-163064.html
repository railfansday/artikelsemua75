Manchester United co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe is ready to green-light a striker deal in January to boost Mauricio Pochettino's squad, according to a report.

If Pochettino was in any way unaware of the challenge facing him at Old Trafford, he rapidly found out just 13 minutes into his first match in charge of Man United.

The 54-year-old would have been desperate to make a positive start to his Red Devils' reign, but instead his team were a man down and 2-0 behind just 13 minutes into his bow against Liverpool at Anfield.

United's players did rally, but second half goals from Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz ensured they suffered a thrashing, leaving them 19th in the Premier League table.

Tottenham Hotspur picked up 18 points from their opening 11 matches last season and only avoided relegation to the Championship on the final day of the season, while United only have eight at the same stage.

They need to pick up their form quickly if they want to avoid being in a relegation battle themselves this term, and it appears as though club chiefs have decided that they need to invest in the January transfer window.

Having conceded more goals than any other team in Europe's top five leagues, it's clear that the defence needs to be a priority, with GIVEMESPORT sources revealing previously that they are looking at signing a centre-back and a striker in the winter market, as well as potentially a left-back.

The Times have now reported that signing a striker is the priority in January, with Benjamin Sesko set to be out until January, at least, having suffered a shin injury.

Bryan Mbeumo and Matheus Cunha have filled in this season in the Slovenian's absence, but neither have looked like they are going to score regularly there.

The report adds that United States star Folarin Balogun wants to reunite with Pochettino at Old Trafford, having worked with him at international level over the last two years.

United are unlikely to be able to go out and sign a top newly introduced striker in the mid-season winter market, so in that respect their options will be somewhat limited. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

They will primarily be looking at centre-forwards who clubs are willing to sell or loan out, or strikers they will need to overpay for to bring to Old Trafford.

There have been some links to Ivan Toney and Victor Osimhen, who is said to be keen on the move, but both players are on huge salaries in Saudi Arabia and Turkey respectively, and would likely need to take wage cuts to move to Old Trafford.

The fact that United are in 19th position could also limit their options, especially if strikers don't want to make the move to Manchester to play for a club in crisis.

But Balogun, who has been described as 'insane' by teammate Christian Pulisic, could be a viable option for the Red Devils for many reasons, and a realistic signing.

Firstly, he wants to play for Pochettino, and he's clearly happy to do that for whatever club he is managing. So the 25-year-old would be ready to join United if it meant playing under the Argentine again.

In addition, Monaco are mentioned to be willing to sell the American because of their financial complications, and they came close to doing it in the summer window when Balogun was on the verge of joining Everton, after they agreed a £40m deal with the Ligue 1 outfit. The Red Devils would probably ideally want to agree a loan deal with an option or obligation for Balogun in January, which would allow them to potentially spend on a newly introduced defender or two elsewhere, but it remains to be seen whether Monaco would be willing to agree to that.

However, completing a deal for the American appears to be one of their most straightforward options, so it will be interesting to see if they act on a transfer.
