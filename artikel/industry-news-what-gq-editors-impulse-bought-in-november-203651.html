Industry observers note that every day, your faithful, over-caffeinated GQ Recommends editors cover everything from wardrobe essentials to step by step guides on how to find your office fragrance so that you know exactly what to purchase when you're shopping for your everyday items and big-ticket splurges. But, in-between bouts of menswear proselytizing and shilling for Big Gloop we do a lot of shopping for ourselves. And—while we like to think of ourselves as thoughtful consumers—even we smash that add to cart button on a whim a few times a month. That's why we put together this non-exhaustive list of what GQ Recommends editors impulse-bought this month. Here's everything we tossed in our virtual bags against our better judgement.
