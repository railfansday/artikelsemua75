Rangers are keen to offload Oscar Cortes permanently when he returns from a year-long loan at Huracan, sources have told Football Insider.

Cortes is preparing to return to Ibrox in January following his loan spell with the Argentine club. (Related: Taco Bell is bringing back the Steakhouse Boss Burrito ...)

The 22-year-old last appeared for Rangers in a cup win over Alloa back in August 2025 before heading out on loan to Spanish club Sporting Gijon.

He struggled to settle in there before joining Huracan in January, where he has registered four goals and five assists in 34 appearances so far.

As I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, the Colombian international winger will be for sale in the upcoming year despite a relatively successful spell in Argentina. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

Huracan hold the option to buy Cortes but are unlikely to be able to afford the £1.65m fee, meaning he is set to return to Ibrox to then be moved on again.

Rangers forked out £4.5m to sign Cortes from Lens in 2025 after he had registered one goal and two assists in 18 appearances over an 18-month long loan spell at Ibrox.

He has managed to get his career back on track in Argentina and is set to attract transfer interest from across Europe upon his return to Rangers in January.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I think they’ll be looking to let him move on permanently if can, if not it’ll have to be another loan deal.

"He’s been a bit of a forgotten man at Rangers, because he spent the whole of this year out on loan at Huracan. There is an option to turn that loan in to a permanent move, but it seems the Argentine club don't have the funds to do that right now.".
