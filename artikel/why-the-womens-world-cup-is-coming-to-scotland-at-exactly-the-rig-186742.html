Hot on the heels of the launch of the Scottish government’s ‘Project 2035’ drive to make Scotland a leading force in women’s football comes the news that Scotland will host Women’s World Cup games during that same year.

Sources indicate that the tournament, to be jointly staged with England, Northern Ireland and Wales, will see games played at Hampden Park and Easter Road in Edinburgh. So girls dreaming of footballing glory can now add the spine-tingling Hampden roar to imagined wondergoals.
