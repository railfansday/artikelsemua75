It has been revealed that initially, I was like, “Wow, I love this car, I have to write about it!” Then I realized there were only going to be 333 units of the model built, so it really isn’t going to make a difference to any significant degree at all…. But I love the car anyway, so here’s the story.

Industry observers note that NIO has released the limited edition Firefly Pianke Liujin, which includes a cool orange design with ginkgo leaves painted onto the side of the car. The model costs only 135,800 yuan ($20,336) despite the limited production total. Oh, you can also get one for as little as 95,800 yuan ($14,346) if you lease the battery, which has a starting cost of 399 yuan ($60) a month.

“Inspired by fall ginkgo leaves and sunset light, the latest edition features ginkgo-themed exterior decals crafted with a dual-layer metallic-base spray process, multi-spoke alloy wheels, and the brand’s first-ever sunroof decal design,” CnEVPost shares.

“Inside, the cabin features a golden-hour-themed interior, including double-stitched ginkgo embroidery, embossed headrests, an exclusive fragrance, a full-width front floor mat, and an exclusive ambient-ring theme wallpaper.”.
