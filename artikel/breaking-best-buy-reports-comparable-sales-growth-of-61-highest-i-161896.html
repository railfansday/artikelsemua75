Breaking: Best Buy reports comparable sales growth of 6.1%, highest in five years

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Best Buy on Tuesday beat Wall Street expectations on the top and bottom lines for its third fiscal quarter and raised its full-year guidance.

The consumer electronics retailer reported comparable sales growth of 6.1%, a significant beat over expectations of 2.3%, according to market intelligence platform StreetAccount. The firm said that growth was its highest in five years. It reported a domestic comparable sales increase of 6.2% and international comparable sales growth of 4.9%.

Shares of Best Buy rose 5% following the report.

Best Buy also raised its guidance for the rest of the year. It's now expecting full-year revenue of between $43 billion and $43.3 billion compared to a previous expectation of between $42.3 billion and $42.8 billion. It's expecting comparable sales to be up 3.4% to 4% compared to prior guidance of between 1.9% and 3%.

The earnings report is the company's first since Jason Bonfig took over as CEO from Corie Barry at the beginning of the month.

"Disciplined execution, a compelling product cycle and continued market share gains helped drive broad-based topline momentum across nearly all product categories, our services offerings, and both our digital and store channels," Bonfig said in a statement of the most recent quarter. Sources indicate that best Buy reported net income of $353 million, or $1.66 per share, compared to $140 million, or 66 cents per share, the year prior. Adjusting for one-time items, including restructuring charges, Best Buy posted adjusted earnings of $1.62 per share.

Revenue rose to $10.24 billion from $9.67 billion in the year-ago period. Best Buy reported domestic revenue of $9.42 billion and international revenue of $820 million.

Tuesday's earnings are the latest in a series of strong reports from the retailer, which has been instituting a turnaround strategy to return to growth after getting hit by declining foot traffic, lower consumer sentiment, tariffs and memory costs.

Shares of Best Buy are up nearly 45% this year.

Bonfig told CNBC last quarter that the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI in the current quarter is expected to drive sales in the gaming category, which has been seeing gains over the past year. Last month, Best Buy introduced a new gaming experience for customers featuring gaming content, technology and special offers, among other features.
