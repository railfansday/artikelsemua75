According to recent reports, at his DealBook interview with Andrew Ross Sorkin last week, Palmer Luckey, founder of AI weapon producer Anduril, made a clear case for the American defense sector, one based on recognizing that our society is protected by physical military power, that America is fundamentally good and deserving of this protection, and that the rest of the world benefits from a muscular (and not necessarily confrontational) America. He argued that we have to be realistic about the fact that “peace through strength” means being able to present a credible threat of retaliatory violence to would-be adversaries and that these actions support a constrained and functional government.

It’s not often that we hear contentious positions on American strength explained without cartoonish warrior-posturing. It is refreshing.

Skyler Safriet is a William F. Buckley Jr. Fellow in Political Journalism for National Review. She is a graduate of Boston College, where she studied philosophy and competed as a heptathlete in track and field.
