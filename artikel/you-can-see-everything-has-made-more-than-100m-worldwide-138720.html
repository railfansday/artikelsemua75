📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

“You Can See Everything,” Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim’s documentary about Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, reached an impressive box office milestone this weekend, becoming one of the few documentaries to make more than $100 million worldwide.

Sources indicate that variety reports that almost all of the film’s $102.4 million box office has come from U.S. audiences, from whom it has grossed $93.4 million so far.

Sources indicate that “You Can See Everything” will be opening in many more countries over the next few months — starting with Mexico, Brazil and Argentina on Nov. 25 and 26 — so its box office total could end up significantly higher.

And while the documentary was already an undeniable commercial success for indie studio A24 (especially with a reported budget of $7 million), it could now surpass the records set by the highest grossing documentaries of all time — “March of the Penguins” ($127 million globally) and “Fahrenheit 9/11” ($222 million globally).
