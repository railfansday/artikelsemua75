Wolves are starting to build momentum as they target an immediate return to the Premier League under Cesar Peixoto.

The Black Country outfit were always expected to be in the mix for promotion due to the quality of the squad they boast after eight years in the Premier League.

Whilst there was a feeling that Wolves could’ve spent more in the summer, they did manage to retain several key players, and the likes of Ladislav Krejčí and Andre are emerging as huge figures for Peixoto. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

The latter has started every Championship game this season, which speaks volumes to his importance when you consider how often Peixoto has rotated.

Yet, he recognises the qualities that Andre brings, and the reality is that even though Wolves have some good midfield options on the bench, the Brazilian is easily the strongest defensive midfielder in the squad. (Related: Deploy Hugging Face models easily with Amazon SageMaker...)

Therefore, it will come as no surprise that Peixoto has already warned Wolves against selling the former Fluminense man in the New Year, as he told the club’s media that a sale must not be on the agenda.

Industry observers note that “I don’t need to say how key he is for us. We have to keep him with us until the end of the season. He’s a very, very important player, a key player for us.

According to recent reports, “I hope, and I expect, that he stays with us. In my head, I don’t want to hear anything about Andre leaving. It is impossible to be clearer than that. For sure, there’ll be interest in him in January as he’s a great player, but he’s our player, and he’s going to stay with us.”.

Following on from that, you could make a strong case that Andre is the most notable player in the Wolves squad right now.

Such is the strength in depth at Molineux, Wolves have two players for every position, and they’ve coped reasonably well without Raul Jimenez for a chunk of the campaign already.

Boubacar Traore is a decent backup to Andre, but he doesn’t possess the same attributes as the 25-year-old, and his fitness is a concern as well.

Sources indicate that at his best, Andre is the man who makes things tick for Wolves. He plays with real intensity in shutting down the opposition, and his tactical understanding of the game ensures he is always in the right place. Then, on the ball, he is effective at moving it swiftly, and he is capable of switching play.

Andre also relishes the physical side of the Championship, and his presence in the middle of the park is a major factor in why Wolves have the best xG against in the division.

Peixoto’s comments about Andre were understandable, as there will be interest in the midfielder, but the pleasing thing for Wolves is that they hold all the cards when it comes to negotiations.

That’s because, to the shock of many, Andre signed a fresh long-term contract at Wolves shortly after the Premier League season finished.

Clearly, he has bought into what the club is about, and he was obviously sold on the long-term project at Molineux.

The new contract was an initial four-year agreement, with Wolves having an option to extend it by another 12 months.

Of course, that doesn’t mean Andre can’t be sold, but many would’ve had the 13-cap Brazil international to be one of the certainties to leave after relegation.

So, the fact he didn’t push for a move back then, and actually committed his future to the club, suggests he is happy to stick around for at least one year.

With Wolves in a position to push for the top two, it’s unlikely that Andre’s situation will have changed too much at the moment, and his focus will be on helping the club back to the Premier League.

Looking beyond that, it seems inevitable that Andre will leave Wolves if they don’t win promotion this season.

Obviously, he won’t want another year in the Championship, and the club would be in a different financial position if they don’t go up, and Andre is undoubtedly a prized asset.

But, that is a discussion for the summer, and all connected to Wolves will hope that Peixoto’s demands are met, which means Andre will see the season out.

If he does, and he maintains the levels he has set in the opening months, then there’s every chance Wolves will be celebrating promotion come May, and the midfielder will have made himself a hero at Molineux after showing remarkable loyalty following relegation.
