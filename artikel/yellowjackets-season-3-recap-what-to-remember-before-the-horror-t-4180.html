Recent findings suggest that few demonstrates have been as unhinged as Showtime's Yellowjackets. Ever since its premiere in 2021, the series has attracted a large following thanks to its thrilling premise, intriguing storylines set in two different timelines, and endlessly fascinating characters. There are shockingly gruesome scenes (yep, there's cannibalism) and themes like the brutality of girlhood and the long-lasting effects of trauma — but no matter where the story has ventured, it's consistently entertaining to watch. Yellowjackets is returning for its fourth and final season on November 20, so here's a refresher on what went down in Season 3.

Industry observers note that when Season 3 kicks off, the Yellowjackets girls' soccer team have actually found a surprising sense of contentment in the wilderness. They've set up new living conditions after the cabin burned down, and they've officially survived the harsh winter. But the cracks in the society the girls have built start to appear. While Natalie (Sophie Thatcher) has been in charge of the girls as Antler Queen, Shauna (Sophie Nélisse) still believes that she's the group's rightful leader. Ultimately, the group decides that Coach Ben (Steven Krueger) should be killed because of his potential involvement in the destruction of the cabin. They begin to torture Ben until Natalie puts him out of his misery, adding one more death to the team's tally. This is viewed as a traitorous act, and Natalie is dethroned as Antler Queen, with Shauna stepping in as her replacement. We also learn that the Pit Girl we first witnessed at the very beginning of the series is actually Mari (Alexa Barajas), who unluckily drew the Queen of Hearts card from the deck, and becomes a target for the group to hunt.

The Yellowjackets Season 3 finale features every storyline firing on all cylinders. In the present-day timeline, the mystery of Lottie's (Simone Kessell) death is solved, with the show confirming that Shauna's (Melanie Lynskey) daughter Callie (Sarah Desjardins) accidentally pushed her down the stairs during an argument. In another twist of fate, Van (Lauren Ambrose) doesn't die from cancer, as fans expected, but is stabbed to death by Melissa (Hilary Swank), who had tried to escape the group's collective past but finds herself drawn back in by her shared connection with her former teammates. Taissa (Tawny Cypress) is devastated by losing Van, and fully descends into madness — cutting out Van's heart, eating it, and burying her love in the wilderness. Shauna also seems to be embracing the unraveling of her life after her family deserts her, while Tai and Misty (Christina Ricci) team up to figure out what to do about Shauna next.

Back in the wilderness, the girls decide on a celebratory feast, intent on gobbling up poor Coach Ben. In the middle of the madness, some researchers stumble across the team, witnessing their cannibalism in the process. Lottie (Courtney Eaton), in the midst of a psychotic episode, murders one of them with an axe. Scientist Hannah (Ashley Sutton) sees an opportunity to survive by teaming up with the girls, and kills her wilderness guide, Kodiak (Joel McHale). Amidst the chaos, the group becomes divided on whether they actually want to be rescued. Van (Liv Hewson) wants to leave the wilderness behind and successfully repairs the scientists' satellite phone, which Natalie climbs to a mountaintop with to send out an SOS that will likely lead to the team's rescue in the final season. There are still so many questions that Season 4 of Yellowjackets needs to explore. We don't know exactly how the team will be rescued, or how they will be able to fold back into normal society. In the present day, will Shauna successfully win back her family's trust, and what will happen now that Tai and Misty have agreed to team up against her?
