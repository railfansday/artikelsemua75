When They See Us is not only one of the saddest miniseries of all time, it's also based on the true story of the 1989 Central Park jogger case. Sources indicate that every actor's facial expressions throughout the miniseries represented the pain the Exonerated Five and their families felt during the events leading up to their exoneration. Adolescence is a miniseries that's a 10/10 masterpiece that is both brilliant and terrifying. The four-episode show centers around Jamie Miller (Owen Cooper), a 13-year-old boy accused of brutally murdering a classmate. His arrest leads to shocking details about the events before and after the crime, as well as the pain his family endures from everyone who is furious at Jamie.

Miller's performance was unforgettably brilliant, and it's still hard to believe that this was his debut acting role, even a year after the miniseries premiered. It's usually difficult for an adult actor to give audiences chills for their antagonistic performance on a television show, but Miller had no problem doing so during the third and best Adolescence episode. Besides Miller, Stephen Graham and Erin Doherty were phenomenal in portraying the vulnerability and emotions behind each character, and their performances led to the trio each winning an Emmy and a Golden Globe Award.
