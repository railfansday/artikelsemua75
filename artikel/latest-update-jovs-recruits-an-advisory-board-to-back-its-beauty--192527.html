Recent findings suggest that on November 20, JOVS, a beauty tech brand, confirmed the creation of its Scientific Advisory Board (SAB). This board brings together medical professionals from dermatology, plastic surgery, and physical therapy; and is geared toward making beauty tech more accessible and trustworthy.

The formation of the Scientific Advisory Board also reflects a larger shift in the beauty industry, where many skincare brands have begun marketing products as healthcare as opposed to simply cosmetics. (Related: Industry News: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using G...)

Industry observers note that the tech giant described the board as a long-term collaborative project. It will involve the advisors contributing their expertise to JOVS’ product research and development to ensure that its tech aligns with the wants and needs of consumers.

In a recent development, “Our vision is to make professional beauty tech accessible to everyone, and that is a long-term commitment.” remarked James Zhang, director of e-commerce and marketing, JOVS. “Self-care is moving away from long, multi-step routines and toward simpler, more effective daily practice, and we want to lead that shift by simplifying complex science without diluting it. The Scientific Advisory Board helps us stay honest to that mission, informing and keeping us accountable to the standards that matter as the brand grows.”. With an increased availability of skincare products, more consumers are seeking out products with substantiated claims beyond heavy marketing. A 2026 survey from YouGov found that 81% of Gen Z shoppers expect ingredient transparency and skincare products with science-backed data.

Many Korean brands have already changed the language of skincare marketing, presenting skincare as a holistic experience with a focus on individual ingredients. Even some makeup products are marketed as having skin benefits, such as blush with “hydrating properties” or “serum” foundations. These products have become integrated into the US beauty scene.

Sources indicate that JOVS creates health tech for at-home laser therapy. In 2023, the tech giant released the first FDA-cleared red light therapy mask.

“JOVS has always been built on a simple belief that professional-grade beauty tech should not be confined to clinics.” said Junghong Lin, founder of JOVS. “Our growth has never been about adding more devices to a category. It has been about building the scientific foundation that lets an entire category move forward.”.

The brand shared that the Scientific Advisory Board will begin providing insights as the holiday season approaches.
