MIAMI, Nov. 20, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Sanctuary Wealth ("Sanctuary"), a leading platform that provides elite financial advisors the freedom, flexibility, control and choice to thrive in an evolving industry, today confirmed that existing investor Kennedy Lewis Investment Management ("Kennedy Lewis") has committed $250 million in latest growth capital to the firm. The investment will allow Sanctuary to broaden the expertise its Partner Firms can draw on to serve clients more comprehensively, attract new business and pursue acquisitions. It will also give Sanctuary greater flexibility to provide capital when Partner Firm owners want to finance growth, monetize a portion of their equity or prepare for succession. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Recent findings suggest that "Sanctuary's platform was built to help wirehouse advisors become independent, deepen relationships with their clients and build enterprise value in firms of their own. That heritage remains central to Sanctuary's identity and growth strategy," mentioned Adam Malamed, Chief Executive Officer of Sanctuary Wealth. "As our Partner Firms continue to evolve, this newly introduced capital enhances our ability to invest in what they need as established businesses, giving them more ways to grow and their owners more options for realizing the value they have created.".

Industry observers note that the depth and breadth of the platform Sanctuary has developed over the past eight years has attracted increasing interest from advisors and independent wealth management firms across the industry. In response, the firm has added affiliation options to give the industry's most sophisticated advisors more choice and flexibility to pursue their vision and objectives.

Industry observers note that "Our Partner Firms are our clients, and we have to earn their business every day," mentioned Vince Fertitta and Robert Walter, Co-Presidents of Sanctuary Wealth. "After years of helping advisors build enterprise value in their firms, we want them to have more ways to put that value to work. Founders should be able to monetize their businesses on their own timeline by selling a minority or majority stake and keep leading the business on the same independent terms that made ownership so attractive to them in the first place.".

Sanctuary's network now includes 125 Partner Firms, with $71 billion in assets on the platform or in transition. To date, Sanctuary has made 13 minority investments in its Partner Firms.

Industry observers note that building on a partnership established in 2022The $250 million commitment from Kennedy Lewis, a multi-strategy private credit manager with approximately $40 billion in assets under management, consists of $50 million in mandatory convertible debt and a $200 million delayed-draw term loan. Kennedy Lewis has also elected to convert its existing outstanding convertible debt investment in Sanctuary into equity.

Sources indicate that "Our partnership with Sanctuary demonstrates what can be accomplished when a talented leadership team and a committed capital partner share a common vision," said David Chene, Co-Founder of Kennedy Lewis. "We have tremendous confidence in Adam and his team, who have overseen growth that includes doubling the number of Partner Firms and almost tripling the assets on the platform since our initial investment in 2022. We believe Sanctuary is uniquely positioned to help independent advisors build and grow valuable businesses and are excited to deepen our partnership to support this next chapter.".

It has been revealed that "Sanctuary carefully considered the best path forward for this upcoming phase of our evolution and chose to expand our relationship with Kennedy Lewis," continued Malamed. "Kennedy Lewis understands our business and is completely aligned with our focus on helping our Partner Firms achieve their full potential. We look forward to continuing this constructive relationship and working together to capture Sanctuary's long-term opportunity.".

The transaction follows a thorough evaluation and negotiation of financing proposals by a dedicated committee of Sanctuary's Board. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart Scott Rodino Act and FINRA approval.

Goldman Sachs served as exclusive financial advisor to Sanctuary in connection with the transaction, and Willkie Farr & Gallagher LLP served as legal advisor. Akin Gump Strauss Hauer & Feld LLP served as legal advisor to Kennedy Lewis.

About Sanctuary WealthSanctuary is a leading hybrid RIA that provides comprehensive wealth management solutions and platform partnerships to sophisticated independent wealth management firms.

Through its Partnered Independence model, Sanctuary helps advisors seeking greater freedom, flexibility, control and choice transition from large financial institutions to build enduring businesses of their own.

Sanctuary Wealth was built to serve elite wirehouse breakaway advisors. Today, Sanctuary supports a growing community of Partner Firms across a range of business models with integrated technology, marketing, operations and a robust, open-architecture platform, enabling advisors to serve clients at the highest level while building long-term enterprise value.
