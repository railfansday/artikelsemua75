Anthropic PBC (ANT -10.03%) stock is falling on its second day of trading, down 8.7% as of 11:15 ET as the excitement around its market debut fades, and AI stocks sell off more broadly.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC -2.32%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC -3.72%) are down 1% and 2%, respectively.

NASDAQ · As of 2:10 PM ET 11/20/26.

The AI company priced its initial public offering (IPO) at $83 per share -- above the original top end of the target range of $78 -- but as investors scrambled to get in on the action, shares opened at $111.50 on Thursday, rapidly climbing as high as $115.10.

But since then, the stock has given most of that away, falling as low as $85.80 on Friday morning.

Even after the drop, Anthropic is valued at about $1.2 trillion, or roughly 18 times its annualized revenue -- last quarter's revenue times four.

According to recent reports, I won't deny that the growth is incredible. Last quarter, Anthropic's revenue rose 47% sequentially, but spending on compute -- the lifeblood of its business -- jumped about 1.7 times as fast, and the firm swung from its first and only operating profit to an operating loss.
