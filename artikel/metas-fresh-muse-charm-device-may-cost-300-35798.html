Meta aims to charge $300 for Muse Charm, its upcoming Tamagotchi-like AI device that was teased at the manufacturer’s Connect event earlier this year, The Information has reported.

So far, Meta has made a relatively successful push into consumer AI, launching Muse — its agentic helper — along with a variety of cute avatars that users can use to give the system more of a personality. (Related: Breaking: Unlocking Agentic RL Training for GPT-OSS: A ...)

Muse Charm, the tech giant’s push into AI hardware, will allow users to carry a physical personification of its software around in their pocket (or potentially wear it around their neck), talking to it, asking it questions, and giving it commands.

It remains to be seen whether customers will bite or not. AI wearables have had a rough go of it so far (here’s looking at you, Humane Ai pin), but companies continue to iterate on the concept in an attempt to break through to a broader audience.

Indeed, $300 is arguably a pretty steep price for a product the size of a keychain, although it’s also a price point that’s in line with what other companies have been charging. The wearable launched by Friend — the AI startup whose device lets you text and talk to an AI persona — is at the moment retailing for $250. On the other hand, Amazon’s all-newly introduced Bee Frontier wearable AI device — which transcribes conversations and tracks tasks — lists for $75 and is currently on a Black Friday, 30% off sale of $52.49. Even with its $99/year subscription to let it tie into a user’s apps (photos, emails, etc.), it’s still far less expensive than Muse Charm.
