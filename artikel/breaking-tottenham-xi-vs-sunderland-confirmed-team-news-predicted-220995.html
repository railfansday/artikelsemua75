Sandro Tonali has returned to training ahead of Tottenham’s visit to Sunderland this weekend, but Savio is expected to miss out.

Tonali missed Spurs’ 2-0 victory at home to Ipswich last Saturday due to illness, as Roberto De Zerbi’s side earned three points on their return from the international break, but is set to be available for selection when Spurs travel to the Stadium of Light on Sunday.

Savio, meanwhile, was named on the bench against Ipswich, having missed the last four games with a muscular injury. The 22-year-old was introduced in the second half, but lasted just 26 minutes before he had to come off injured. (Related: Steam is pitting 10 years of Game of the Year winners a...)

Savio was visibly upset as he headed down the tunnel, and De Zerbi confirmed he had suffered a recurrence of the same injury that had kept him out for the last six weeks.

"I'm sorry for Savio's injury, because it was the same injury as last time, and we need him. He's a crucial player for us,” De Zerbi said in his post-match press conference. (Related: Among Us player & his mom reveal how they helped save k...)

“He’s unlucky in this period. We have to be close with him to help him to become the top player we bought at the beginning of this season.".

Savio is expected to join fellow wingers Mathys Tel and Mykhailo Mudryk on the sidelines, meaning teenage sensation Luca Williams-Barnett, who scored and assisted on his full senior debut against Ipswich, could be in line for more minutes.

It has been revealed that fears over an injury to Omar Marmoush remain despite the forward appearing in training footage posted across the club’s social channels on Thursday. The Egyptian was this week subject of online speculation that he required assessment over a possible knee injury.

It has been revealed that centre-back Tosin Adarabioyo is expected to be available for selection again after returning from a calf injury. Tosin was fit to play against Ipswich, but De Zerbi chose not to take any unnecessary risks.

Rodrigo Bentancur could also return to the starting lineup, having been named on the bench against Ipswich after returning late from international duty.

Long-term absentees Xavi Simons and Wilson Odobert are both nearing a return to action early in the latest year, but neither player is expected to be challenging for minutes in the coming weeks.

Tottenham predicted XI: Kinsky, Porro, Van Hecke, Van de Ven, Robertson, Bentancur, Tonali, Fernandes, Kudus, Solanke, Williams-Barnett.

Injured: Simons, Odobert, Mudryk, Savio, Tel.

Time and date: 2:05pm GMT on Sunday, November 29.
