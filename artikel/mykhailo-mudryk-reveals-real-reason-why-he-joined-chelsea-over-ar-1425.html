Chelsea completed the signing of Mykhailo Mudryk in January 2023 as he completed a mega-money £89 million move from Shakhtar Dontesk.

The winger was widely sought after following a number of electric displays for the Ukrainian side. Regining Premier League champions Arsenal were one of the clubs that were believed to be interested in acquiring his services.

However, it was the Blues who won the race to sign him as he penned an eight-and-a-half-year deal with the club.

"I’m very excited to be here and be part of the Chelsea project. This is a substantial club and I’m happy to be here," he noted after his move was made official.

However, Mudryk himself has now revealed that he would never have made the move to Chelsea had he got his way.

Earlier this week, Mudryk appeared on the Ukrainian podcast 'Guest of Gordon', where he made a number of bombshell claims.

He pointed the finger at the Ukrainian National Team for his drugs ban, saying that their nutritionist gave him meldonium, which led to him being out of action for 20 months. He also slammed the Ukrainian FA (UAF) and its president, Andriy Shevchenko, for not giving him sufficient support.

Industry observers note that mudryk, now 25 years old, also revealed that it was his dream to join Arsenal from Shakhtar. "But the agent, as practice has shown, never defended my interests at all, but defended exclusively his own. I understood this later. And there were no attempts to fulfill my dream, to fulfill my task - to move to Arsenal. There were only attempts to earn more money, but I didn't know about it at that time.".
