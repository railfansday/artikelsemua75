Industry News: Cybersecurity AI startup Corma reaches $1B valuation in about 3 months

🌐 Source: TechCrunch • 📅 16 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (220 words, 📖 Standard)

Three months after introducing its product, AI cybersecurity startup Corma has raised a $125 million Series A round at a $1 billion valuation led by NEA, the tech giant noted. Corma offers AI models and agents trained for cybersecurity defense. It has now raised a total of $185 million from backers, including Sequoia Capital, Khosla Ventures, and Coatue.

In a recent development, the startup came out of stealth in August. Interest in its product skyrocketed after a series of security incidents sent chief information security officers scrambling on October 21 when a critical flaw was found in PHP, a popular web programming language. Another flaw was found in Node.js, the open source JavaScript runtime environment. Popular open source tool LiteLLM, a proxy for routing requests to multiple LLM providers, also disclosed a security incident. Other incidents were disclosed that day, too. Other examples include Kevin Mandia’s new startup Armadin, as well as Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, and other established players, and too many other startups to count.
