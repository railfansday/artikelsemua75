It has been revealed that if your aim is to be best dressed at Thanksgiving dinner, you might want to give one of these trends that got our street stylers snapped up at fashion month a try. Midi skirts give you a family-appropriate look, while a cozy cardigan will keep you bundled up no matter what coast you’re on. If you’re the one cooking, then why not try out a designer apron from the likes of Miu Miu, or if you only plan on contributing by helping to clear out the dining table then a food bag from Moschino might be the way to go. The likelihood of you having one or more of these pieces in your closet is probably high, which means you can spend what you don’t with cash through all the calories at Thanksgiving dinner.
