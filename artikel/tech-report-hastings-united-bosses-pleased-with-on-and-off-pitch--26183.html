There were big changes at the Pilot Field in the summer, with Wood taking on the role of head of football and Ronnie Dolan appointed first-team manager.

Dolan, his right-hand men Sam Adams and Trevor McCreadie and a reshaped squad got off to a flying start in the league but have hit a rough run of results – most recently last Saturday’s 4-1 home loss to Steyning, which came after they led 1-0 at the break. But Wood said everyone at the club was committed to making a success of the season – not just for the men’s team but for United’s women and the younger teams too.

Wood said of the Isthmian South East promotion bid: “We have to be challenging for the play-offs, as a minimum.

"The primary objective is to reach the last day of the season with the title to play for, or already won, or a play-off place to secure or in the bag. Mid-table would be a failure.”.

Wood noted those in charge at the Pilot Field were working to get the club’s local identity back after a couple of years when it had drifted alongside the team dropping from step three to step four. (Related: Tech Report: What Couples Married For 40 Years Do When ...)

In a recent development, he remarked these efforts were reflected in the type of players they had recruited and added: “We’ve wanted to bring in players whose character fits the club. We want people here long-term – we don’t want a revolving door.

"As for the season so far, we’re up there challenging. We’ve hit a sticky patch but every team does that. Everyone’s learning – it’s Ronnie’s first job as a manager and many of the players are very young.”.

Industry observers note that on that note, Wood stated the club’s ‘pathway’ was beginning to bear fruit, and he praised the younger teams’ coaches for developing some highly promising talent. Some are knocking on the first-team squad’s door – others are being loaned out to step five clubs.

The under-18s in particular are going well, Wood mentioned: “They include so many young players who will enrich local football in years to come.”.

United’s women’s team are having a tough run in the London and SE League Division 1. Wood said it was a transistional campaign and hard work was going into building the women’s set-up.

There is also work going on behind the scenes to progress the vital redevelopment of the ground, with a latest pitch and main stand among aims on the table. And Wood thanked fans for continuing to support the club in great numbers.
