Sources indicate that as you approach the final missions in the game, Jason and Lucia start working with Giorgio, a very rich shipping magnate who works with Eastern European criminals to smuggle drugs across the ocean on his major ships. And things aren’t going well, forcing Giorgio to work with Jason, Lucia, and others on a big, ambitious plan to steal back all the cocaine confiscated by the Coast Guard as part of a large heist. But before they can pull off the heist, a higher-up from the big Balkan cartel Giorgio has been working with arrives to kill him for screwing up, but gives him one more chance to get back the coke. That badass hitman? Niko Bellic, the playable protagonist of 2008’s Grand Theft Auto IV.

I asked Nelson if they’d planned to include Niko from the start, and he tells me that they hadn’t. Instead, he told me that the cameo came about “very organically.”.

“[Early in development], we had Giorgio and we had him working with a sort of Balkan cartel,” explained Nelson. The GTA 6 boss further explained that this whole storyline was based on research they’d done about actual Italian shipping companies that allegedly smuggled drugs for various gangs in South America. Apparently, a boat run by such a tech giant was carrying 20 tons of coke when it was raided by U.S. officials. That number should sound familiar to anyone who has played the later missions of GTA 6, as that’s the amount of coke seized by the Coast Guard in the game.Â.
