Liverpool are likely to sell midfielder Alexis Mac Allister after another two seasons in their colours.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who does not believe a new deal will truly secure the Argentine's long-term future at Anfield.

The 27-year-old opened talks with the Reds hierarchy over a newly introduced contract earlier this week, with his current deal due to expire in the summer of 2028. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Wyness believes that Mac Allister will ultimately pen a upcoming deal on Merseyside, but he believes that Liverpool will only do it to end the immediate speculation over his future.

The former Brighton ace has been heavily linked with a move to Real Madrid in recent months, and Liverpool are concerned about potentially losing another star to the Spanish giants.

Mac Allister's performances have been criticised at times this season, despite him already starting eight Premier League games under Andoni Iraola.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Liverpool are ultimately likely to give in to Mac Allister's demands and offer a new contract on his terms.

The boardroom expert previously told Football Insider that the player and his father, who also acts as his agent, have been trying to leverage the Real Madrid interest to secure better terms.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how Liverpool are likely to handle Mac Allister over the next few years.

"They'll probably give him the deal that he's looking for, and you know he may be sold in two seasons' time; I would be looking at something like that," he said.

"But I think this one will get done, and they'll do it in plenty of time to avoid any further speculation out in the marketplace.

"But all it is is just the normal gossip as you'd expect at this time in contract negotiations.".

Former Manchester United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider earlier this week that Liverpool defender Joe Gomez may be left in no man's land if he leaves on a free transfer next summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

The 29-year-old is out of contract at the end of the season, but Brown is adamant that clubs are not yet queuing up to sign him, even on a free transfer.

The ex-recruitment man outlined that Gomez's lofty wage demands are likely to be a concern for any interest parties, as well as his concerning injury record.

Recent reports have suggested that Liverpool are ready to let Gomez go once his deal expires, but he is unlikely to find it easy to get another club.
