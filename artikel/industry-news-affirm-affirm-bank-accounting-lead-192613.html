At Affirm, we exist for the moments that matter—giving people a clear, predictable way to pay over time, with no hidden fees, no surprises, and no tradeoffs on what matters most.

The Finance team ensures Affirm remains financially sound and strategically positioned for growth. Our team manages financial planning, accounting, pricing, vendor management, tax, investor relations, and corporate development. We deliver timely insights, accurate reporting, and careful analysis to support decision-making. From day-to-day financial operations to major investments, we enable sustainable, informed growth by maintaining strong fiscal discipline.

Recent findings suggest that we are looking for a self-motivated, hardworking, and analytically minded professional to join the Bank Record to Report (Controllership) team during a pivotal moment of growth! The Record to Report team is a growing function within the Bank CFO department, responsible for core day-to-day accounting. As Affirm Bank is currently in its formation phase, we are actively building out our foundational team. Reporting to the Bank Controller, the Accounting Lead will play a key role in the formation and operational build-out of the Bank, including the G/L and daily operational flows, ensuring successful integration of our accounting systems and processes into Affirm’s systems and processes, and developing the regulatory function. Working alongside the Affirm, Inc. Accounting function, we ensure all business transactions are recorded accurately and timely, for ultimate reporting to management, regulators, and the Bank board.
