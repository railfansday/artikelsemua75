📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Mark your anniversary with something that’s about the two of you, such as a return to your first-date spot, your wedding menu cooked at home, or the year’s traditional gift made by hand. Each costs far less than a hotel weekend, so keep that for a milestone year like your fifth or tenth.

At the $168.35 average nightly rate the data firm CoStar reported for the first week of November 2026, two hotel nights cost about $337 before taxes, meals, or gas. Every idea below costs less than one of those nights.

Recent findings suggest that the ideas that make an anniversary feel substantial do one of two things. They revisit your history as a couple, or they have you try something upcoming together.

Recent findings suggest that trying something new has research behind it. In a 2000 study led by psychologist Arthur Aron, couples who spent seven minutes on a new, exciting task together reported a bigger rise in relationship quality than couples given a routine one.
