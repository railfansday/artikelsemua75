If you’re a fan of discrete minimalism or stealth-wealth, this week’s red carpets were not for you. Instead, the best dressed stars of the week leaned into extra opulence—choosing voluminous silhouettes or slightly OTT details (think: Feathers! Crystals!) that instantly turned heads. And the timing makes total sense: Given we are now entering the season of glitzy holiday style, now is certainly not the time to hold back.

According to recent reports, A few of the week’s most noteworthy fashion moments? Jennifer Lawrence hit The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping premiere in New York City wearing in a full Dior look from the pre-fall 2026 collection, including an embroidered black robe coat with a sweeping train. Chic. In Nashville, Taylor Swift returned to her country roots at the 2026 CMAs, wearing a bright-yellow, strapless Oscar de la Renta gown. Talk about a color that will get you noticed.
