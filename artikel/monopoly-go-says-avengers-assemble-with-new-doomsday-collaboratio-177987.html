Industry observers note that monopoly Go has hosted some major names in pop culture for collabs over the years, ranging from K-pop Demon Hunters to The Simpsons. Now, however, they're teaming up with what could be their biggest partnership yet, as Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday arrives on the board starting November 25th!

The Avengers: Doomsday x Monopoly Go collab runs from November 25th all the way to January 27th, and sees you opposing the machinations of Doctor Doom. You'll traverse the masked tyrant's homeland of Latveria in the Doomstadt map, as well as take on the Doomsy in an all-upcoming hero battle. All in all, a great event for fans of Monopoly Go and the Avengers. I can't be the only one who feels like the marketing blitz for Avengers: Doomsday is trying a bit too hard, though. I remember back when Infinity War and Endgame released, and you didn't need a KFC collab or Monopoly Go even to make it clear what a momentous cinematic moment it was.

Still, even if the movie turns out to be a certified stinker, this event and its runtime from November 25th to January 27th will offer you plenty of time to grab all the rewards and experience all the content that this upcoming crossover event has to offer!
