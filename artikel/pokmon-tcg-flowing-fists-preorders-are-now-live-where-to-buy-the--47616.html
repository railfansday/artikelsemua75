TL;DR: Pokémon TCG Mega Evolution Flowing Fists preorders are already live at TCGplayer, with single pack blisters for $19.98, standard booster packs starting at $19.99 each, and Booster Bundles priced at $69.96.

It has been revealed that pokémon's next trading card expansion isn't launching until Feb. 6, 2027, but collectors can already secure preorders for Flowing Fists — including everything from single booster packs to 36-pack Display Boxes.

Preorders for the Pokemon TCG’s Flowing First expansion are already live on TCGplayer — which features over 110 cards for the Mega Evolution set, including Mega Lucario Z ex, Mega Garchomp Z ex, Mega Absol Z ex, and Mega Glimmora ex.

Listings at retailers like Amazon and Walmart are not yet live, but here are all the Pokemon packs and boxes you can preorder for Flowing Fists right now:. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

According to recent reports, flowing Fists Single Pack Blister (Tynamo) — $19.98 for one booster, a Tynamo promotional card, and a collectible coin. We expect this one to sell out promptly. It’s available a cent less than the standard booster pack, with the Tynamo promo essentially free.

Flowing Fists Booster Pack — $19.99 for one pack containing 10 random cards.

Flowing Fists Sleeved Booster Pack — $28.88 for a single 10-card booster in protective cardboard packaging.

Flowing Fists 3-Pack Blister (Wimpod) — $38.87 for three booster packs and an additional Wimpod promotional card.

Flowing Fists Build & Battle Box — $59.96 for four boosters, a 40-card playable deck with one of four foil promos, and a TCG Live code.

In a recent development, flowing Fists Booster Bundle — $69.96 for six booster packs containing 10 cards each. That's approximately $11.66 per pack.

Flowing Fists Elite Trainer Box — $179.94, including nine boosters, a full-art foil Riolu promo card, 65 sleeves, 40 Energy cards, a player's guide, damage-counter dice, a coin-flip die, a plastic coin, a collector's box with six dividers, and a TCG Live code.

Sources indicate that flowing Fists Booster Display Box — $250 plus $14.99 delivery, for 36 boosters. At around $7.36 per pack, this currently offers the lowest per-pack cost.

Sources indicate that all prices include shipping unless otherwise stated, with TCGplayer sellers estimating dispatch on Feb. 6 (the expansion’s release date).
