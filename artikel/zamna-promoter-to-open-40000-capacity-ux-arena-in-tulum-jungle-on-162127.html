Zamna Promoter to Open 40,000-Capacity UX Arena in Tulum Jungle on Latest Year’s Eve

Sources indicate that A new 40,000-capacity music venue is set to open in the jungles of Tulum, Mexico, on Dec. 31, 2026, Billboard can exclusively announce Tuesday (Nov. 24). Called UX Arena, the venue is being developed and operated by Universal Xperience (UX), the tech giant behind electronic music festival Zamna, as part of a broader effort to expand Tulum’s live entertainment offerings beyond its established festival circuit.

Located between Playa del Carmen and Tulum, UX Arena is built around a natural lake and will feature three stages connected by jungle pathways: the Arena Stage, designed for large-scale productions and headline performances; the Garden Stage, intended for daytime and sunset events; and the smaller Isla Stage, situated by the water for artist showcases and special performances. According to recent reports, “Zamna was born in this jungle. Ten years on, the music and the productions have grown, and the venue needed to grow with them,” Fabricio Ledesma Heinrich, CEO of Universal Xperience, said in a statement. “UX Arena keeps the same DNA and adds a new dimension.”.

In a recent development, the venue will debut during Zamna Tulum’s 10th anniversary season, running from Dec. 31, 2026, through Jan. 10, 2027. The six-event series kicks off with RENAISSANCE Tulum NYE, featuring Sasha, Maceo Plex, Sébastien Léger and Ida Engberg, followed by Amnesia’s 50th anniversary celebration (Jan. 2), Anyma’s ÆDEN (Jan. 4), Black Coffee (Jan. 6), Adriatique’s X (Jan. 8) and Adam Port (Jan. 10). “Zamna has become a world-class festival within electronic music, but UX Arena is designed for a much wider audience,” Heinrich added. “Our vision is to welcome artists from every genre and establish UX Arena as the leading live entertainment venue in southern Mexico, bringing destination concerts to the Tulum jungle and connecting with artist tours across Mexico and North America.”.

The venue will also feature 65,000 square meters of parking, designated transportation areas and expanded hospitality, medical and security facilities.

It has been revealed that the opening comes as Tulum’s tourism scene expands beyond its beaches and resorts, with luxury hospitality developments and large-scale electronic music events drawing visitors. UX Arena represents an effort to build on that demand with a permanent venue capable of accommodating major touring productions throughout the year.

UX estimates that Zamna attracts around 160,000 international visitors to Tulum, generating an estimated $320 million in destination spending and supporting 6,100 direct jobs. The organization says UX Arena was developed in collaboration with the government of Quintana Roo, Mexican Caribbean tourism authorities and the Municipality of Tulum.

Tickets for Zamna’s anniversary season are available at ZamnaFestival.com.
