Kerim Alajbegovic underwent medical examinations in Turin on Monday after coming off at half-time during Juventus’s 4-0 victory over Venezia on Sunday evening, and the initial reports indicate that there is no major injury concern for the young Bosnian talent.

The 19-year-old was replaced by goalscorer Nick Woltemade at half-time of Juventus’s 4-0 win over Venezia on Sunday, their first game back since the November international break; Alajbegovic playing the full 90 minutes in both of Bosnia and Herzegovina’s Nations League matches.

The youngster reported for tests at JMedical on Monday afternoon, and according to reports from Gianluca Di Marzio and various others, he is suffering from a slight muscular overload in his right leg.

His condition is set to be monitored day by day, but he is not expected to be ruled out for a significant period of time.

Gianluca Di Marzio suggests that Luciano Spalletti could even have Alajbegovic back in time for Juventus’s upcoming Europa League fixture against Omonoia at the Allianz Stadium on Thursday evening, although he could be minded to give the youngster a rest.
