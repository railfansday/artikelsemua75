Zendaya and Tom Holland may have tied the knot, but they’re still marking latest milestones together. The actors have reportedly purchased their first piece of real estate together. The Daily Mail broke the news, alleging that the Spider-Man co-stars paid about $10.8 million (£8.25 million) cash for a five-bedroom house in an undisclosed London suburb described as “exclusive” and “leafy.”.

It has been revealed that there are few publicly known facts about the property—which isn’t much of a surprise, considering it took months for the notably private couple to confirm that they had gotten married. While AD could not independently confirm the purchase, reports say that the stars scored a sizable discount on the property, which was listed with a price tag of $11.8 million (£9 million). Per the Daily Mail’s reporting, the 5,000-square-foot dwelling sits on a one-acre plot and features four bathrooms and four reception rooms. “My youngest is very excited that Spider-Man is going to be living across the street,” an inhabitant of a neighboring home told the Daily Mail. “I told him, ‘You will have to kick a ball a long way to reach their back garden.’ I doubt they will have a housewarming party.”. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Since 2018, Holland has owned a six-bedroom home in Kingston-upon-Thames in southwest London. He paid $3.35 million (£2.5 million) for the six-bedroom residence, which he extensively renovated from 2020 until 2023, when Zendaya reportedly moved in. The Dune star maintains several properties stateside: a $1.4 million five-bedroom, five-bathroom Mediterranean-style house near Los Angeles; a 5,000-square-foot, ranch-style home in Encino, California, for which she paid $3.97 million; and a $4.9 million three-bedroom condo in Brooklyn’s luxe Quay Tower.

Industry observers note that the Hollywood pair is likely to remain tight-lipped about their alleged fresh property, since privacy has been an problem for them in the past. Last year, it was reported that they were spending $1,300 a day on security for their Kingston-upon-Thames dwelling after fans started gathering at the front gates.
