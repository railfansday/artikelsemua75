Following last week‘s PlayStation 5 and PC pre-order launch, Switch 2 and Switch digital pre-orders for Professor Layton and the Latest World of Steam are now available for $64.99 / £53.99 / €64.99 / ¥7,920, LEVEL-5 announced. The official website has also been updated with story and gameplay information.

Sources indicate that as previously announced, pre-orders include the “Layton Series Soundtrack Selection,” a digital soundtrack featuring 11 pieces of music from the Professor Layton series. The tracks are accessible via the “Secret Content” menu in-game.

This upcoming adventure takes place one year after the events of Professor Layton and the Unwound Future.

The story unfolds in America, where the Wild West era has ended and the country is forging ahead into a new age of development.

A letter from Luke leads Layton to Steam Bison, a town renowned for its inventions….

Sources indicate that steam Bison has undergone rapid technological advances thanks to the steam engine. But mysterious events are unfolding one after another, from a phantom gunman who has kidnapped a wealthy heiress to a gang of zombies threatening the town. Layton and Luke join forces once again to investigate these strange occurrences and embark on a grand puzzle-solving adventure.

◆ A Story That Unfolds as You Solve Puzzles.

You’ll encounter all kinds of puzzles as you explore the streets of Steam Bison.

—Even to meet Eggmuffin, an inventor, you’ll need to solve a puzzle to find the entrance to his room….

—Solve the puzzle, and the story moves forward.

◆ The Most Puzzles in Series History.

Layton and Luke face more puzzles than ever before in the series. Solve a wide variety of puzzles as you progress through the story and get to the bottom of the mysterious events unfolding in Steam Bison. Along the way, you’ll even come across puzzles that play a part in Steam Bison’s development. Solve them to upgrade the town’s facilities and watch the town flourish.

—Solve puzzles to develop facilities!
