Leny Yoro’s one-match ban has been lifted by the FA after Manchester United successfully appealed against his sending-off at Liverpool on Sunday.

Yoro was shown a straight red card by referee Sam Barrott in the eighth minute for a challenge on Alexander Isak, and United’s 10 men went on to lose 4-0 in Mauricio Pochettino’s first game in charge.

The club launched an appeal over the decision and the FA have confirmed that Yoro will now be available for Saturday’s game against Brentford at Old Trafford. (Related: Should I Quit My Job? A Decision Framework...)

A statement read: ‘Leny Yoro’s one-match suspension has been removed following a successful claim for wrongful dismissal. The Manchester United player was sent off for denying an obvious goal scoring opportunity during their Premier League match against Liverpool on Sunday 22 November. The Independent Regulatory Commission’s written reasons for the decision will be published in due course.’.

Barrott sent off Yoro for pushing Isak in the back on the edge of the penalty box, and Dominik Szoboszlai scored Liverpool’s first goal from the free kick.

However, United insisted that the Frenchman did not deny Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity because goalkeeper Senne Lammens would have reached the ball first.

Leny Yoro has seen his red card suspension overturned following an appeal by Man United.

It’s the third time in the past 14 months that United have been on the wrong end of incorrect decisions by match officials.

In September, Pro Ref boss Howard Webb apologised to United when Erling Haaland’s winning goal for Manchester City in the Old Trafford derby was allowed to stand after VAR incorrectly ruled that teammate Enzo Fernandez was not interfering with play as he tried to head the ball in an offside position.

Last season, Webb acknowledged that the match officials got it wrong when they decided not to send off Brentford captain Nathan Collins for hauling back Bryan Mbeumo inside the box as United lost 3-1 at the Gtech Community Stadium.

Latest United head coach Pochettino blamed Yoro’s red card for the heavy defeat in his first game in charge, and was unhappy that Barrott took no action over a similar incident involving Jeremy Jacquet and Mbeumo in the Liverpool penalty area moments earlier.

‘It's not okay to start a game in the Premier League with this situation,’ stated Pochettino.

‘The game changed completely after eight minutes. Now we can explain everything, but the damage is done – it's so painful.

‘In Leny's case, it wasn't intentional. It was an unusual situation that is normal. Everyone who plays football can feel it.

‘If you review the VAR you can see for sure, he never had the intention to connect with Isak. The ball was going to the goalkeeper.

According to recent reports, ‘If you look before, in the sixth minute, Jacquet on Mbeumo. If you compare the actions, that was a penalty. We were punished badly and they were not punished, that is the truth.

Sources indicate that ‘It's clear that after eight minutes the proposal was in the bin. Do you think that is easy? Even the best team in the world playing in this situation concedes four goals or maybe more.

‘The players are feeling the pain of the result. I told them to the calm: it was an unfair situation that happened.’.
