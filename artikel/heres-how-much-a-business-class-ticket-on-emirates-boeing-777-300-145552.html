According to recent reports, the Boeing 777-300ER is by far the most numerous 777 variant in the Emirates fleet, with 116 aircraft of the type owned by the airline. As a result, the jet is used to fly to a vast number of destinations at an incredibly high frequency. It will achieve around 5.7 billion available seat miles in November 2026 alone. Emirates passengers have plenty of 777-300ER business class seats to choose from across much of the network.

Emirates has one of the world's best business class products, ranking 11th in the Skytrax World Airline Awards business class category. Tickets for this elite class come in at a little over $1,000 to over $3,000 for the most frequent routes operated by the airline using the 777-300ER. According to recent reports, emirates has eight 777-300ER routes that will be operated 90 times or more in November 2026. The most frequent route is to MLE. The airline will operate the DXB to MLE route 115 times across the month, totaling almost 40,000 seats and over 73 million available seat miles. Meanwhile, the least frequent route operated by the carrier's 777-300ER aircraft is to Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA). The carrier will operate this route two times during the month for 652 available seats.

There is no fixed price for an Emirates business class ticket. The carrier alters its specific pricing, as with any airline, based on the distance of the particular route involved and the level of demand. A route at the edge of a 777-300ER's range that has an almost sold-out business class cabin will demand the highest prices. Meanwhile, you'll be able to access discounts if you buy your ticket before most of them have been sold or are traveling on a short route.

A unique feature of Emirates' business class ticketing structure is that there are multiple tiers to choose from, although each gives you access to the same seat, dining, and lounge access experiences. Firstly, the cheapest tier is Saver. You will get an 88 lb (40 kg) check-in bag, as with the more expensive tiers, but you'll get the highest fees if you want to change or cancel your ticket. Saver passengers also earn fewer miles.

Emirates 777-300ER business class is a mixed experience. The aircraft has traditionally used a legacy and comparatively cramped 2-3-2 configuration. The layout, as pictured, means that passengers can spend thousands of dollars extra and still end up in the dreaded middle seat.

To amend the concern, Emirates is undertaking a substantial retrofit program. It will cost $5 billion and involve 110 A380s and 109 777s. The main change is that the cabin will switch to a modern 1-2-1 layout, giving each passenger more space and ensuring that they all have direct aisle access. The retrofits also ensure consistency between the Boeing 777X and older 777 variants.

Industry observers note that outside the business class cabin, the retrofitted aircraft has four cabins and has an updated color palette. Parallel to the retrofit program is the roll-out of ultra-fast Starlink on-board WiFi.

According to recent reports, simple Flying's Abid Habib reviewed the old 777 business class in 2024. The route was from London to Dubai. He wrote that the space available couldn't compete with Emirates' flagship jet: "However, because of the space available onboard the A380, the seats offer more space and amenities, such as extra storage spaces, direct aisle access, and the onboard lounge.".

Industry observers note that the Points Guy has reviewed the new 777 business class product on a trip from DXB to Brussels Airport (BRU). He enjoyed the fantastic lounge in Dubai, the lie-flat seats, and the higher level of luxury available. Yet, he was also disappointed by the lack of an onboard bar and privacy sliding doors. Amenity kits are also still not available on daytime flights.

It has been revealed that "The refurbished business-class seats on Emirates' Boeing 777 represent a huge improvement from the previous seats still flying on most Emirates 777 aircraft," reads The Points Guy's conclusion. However, he noted that he would still choose Emirates A380 business class.
