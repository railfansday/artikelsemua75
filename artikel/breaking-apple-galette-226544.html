Honestly, we can’t think of anything better than a galette. They've got all the delicious elements of pie—flaky, buttery crust and tender baked fruit—but none of the fussy technique. With galettes, you don’t have to worry about any crimping, latticework, or blind baking. Sometimes, it's okay to take the easy road. This classic apple galette is one of our favorite examples. Warm, comforting, and bursting with sweet, spiced flavor, it’s everything you love about apple pie with a fraction of the effort.

A galette is essentially an open-faced pie. Thinly sliced apples are tossed in cinnamon and sugar and layered on top of a single sheet of pie crust. The crust is folded over the apples in a rough circle and baked until the crust is flaky. Apple pie has a top and bottom crust and is usually formed in a pie dish. The extra crust and overall shape are the only real differences, and for the apple obsessed, galettes provide the perfect ratio of crust to filling.

Sources indicate that anything you'd serve with an apple pie would work here as well. That means your favorite ice cream, frozen yogurt, caramel sauce, or candied nuts would be great galette sidekicks.

Any leftover galette can be stored for up to 4 days in the refrigerator.
