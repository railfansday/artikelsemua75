Roberto De Zerbi has issued a worrying update on Savio after the Tottenham forward was forced off shorting after returning from injury. (Related: Redditor forced to bring desktop PC in checked luggage ...)

The Brazilian was making his first appearance in over a month, since suffering a muscular problem against Manchester United in October. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

Savio came off the bench to replace the dazzling debutant Luca Williams-Barnett, who scored one and assisted another on his first senior start for Spurs.

As the game approached the 90th minute, Savio pulled up after being dispossessed and immediately pulled his shirt over his head.

After being attended to by the club’s medical team, Savio was replaced by Archie Gray to see out the 2-0 win over Ipswich.

Speaking at full-time, De Zerbi admitted he had reaggravated the same injury which had kept him sidelined for the last few weeks.

"Yes, I'm very angry about that, because Savio is a crucial player for us,” mentioned the Italian.

“What he can make and he has to make the difference for us. It is the same injury. And it is the worst thing from today.”.

It is not absolutely clear how long Savio will now be sidelined, with Tottenham next in action away at Sunderland next Sunday.

Savio’s injury comes after Sandro Tonali missed out on a place in the matchday squad due to illness.
