Against that backdrop, newly released public records show SpaceX has retained five veteran lobbyists from Mehlman Consulting, a bipartisan D.C.-based government affairs firm, several of whom have deep ties to Democratic lawmakers and former administrations.

SpaceX, with a market capitalization of nearly $2 trillion, is home to Musk's holdings in aerospace engineering, artificial intelligence, telecommunications and space transportation. If Democrats successfully knock it down a peg, the resulting loss in value could send shock waves throughout the economy.

Musk has worked to publicly distance himself from the right wing since the GOP's poor midterm performance, recently stating that he’s "not MAGA" at a Latest York Times summit.

"I feel pretty foolish about having spent so much money and not seeing much. Or maybe it would have been much worse without that," he remarked at the event. "I’m not sure what MAGA means, but if that means sort of blind adherence to a set of policies by the Republican Party, then I certainly don’t have that.".

Industry observers note that musk isn’t the first tech leader to throw money to Democratic operatives, seemingly to prepare for the incoming congressional majority. Palantir, another frequent target of Democratic criticism, has also retained lobbyists with ties to the party.

"I think at the end of the day, these companies have an inordinate amount of money, and they will try and put it wherever they can in order to stave off any real accountability," Aya Ibrahim, a senior fellow at the AI Now Institute and a former Biden administration official, told NPR of Palantir’s hirings.

Technology stocks took a sharp hit in the days after Democrats won their stunning midterm victory, with AI infrastructure and data-center companies among the hardest hit as investors weighed the prospect of tighter regulation.

The throng of lobbyists adds to Musk’s already large arsenal of paid influencers, which includes Republicans and Democrats at the state and federal levels.

Musk’s new agents include the former deputy chief of staff to Sen. Maria Cantwell, who is slated to take over the powerful Commerce Committee in January, a former aide to Vice President Al Gore and a host of other one-time high-ranking Democratic staffers. The effective date of registration listed on the lobbying records was Oct. 1, around the time many were predicting Democrats would win control of Congress.
