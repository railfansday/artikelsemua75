Keeping up with all your favorite streaming services can get pretty expensive. Between Netflix, Prime Video and all the other subscriptions fighting for a spot in your monthly budget, it’s easy to feel like you’re paying more for streaming than actually watching it. That’s why I’m always a fan of a good Black Friday deal. It’s the perfect excuse to cut down those subscription costs, discover something latest or finally give a streaming service another shot without paying full price.

Recent findings suggest that and Peacock is giving you plenty of reasons to spread your wings this Black Friday. There are two discounted memberships up for grabs, with the Select tier dropping to just $2.99 a month for three months, or $29.99 for an entire year. Prefer a little more to watch? Peacock Premium is also getting a 30% discount, bringing it down to $8.99 a month for three months or $89.99 annually. The deals run from November 23 to 30, 2026, so you’ve got a week to bag yourself a bargain. Here’s everything you need to know.

If you’re mainly interested in catching up on NBC and Bravo favorites, Peacock Select is the cheapest way in. It includes shows like “The Office,” “Yellowstone” and “The Real Housewives,” alongside select TV channels. Just be aware that movies, live sports and Peacock Originals aren’t included, and you’ll have to put up with ads.Get Peacock Select tier for $2.99 a month for 3 months or or $29.99 for 1 year.

It has been revealed that for those who want the full Peacock experience, Premium is the tier I’d recommend. You’ll get access to hundreds of movies, hit Peacock Originals, NBC and Bravo, plus live sports and events, including Premier League and NFL coverage. However, you’ll still have to sit through ads, but there’s plenty here to keep you entertained.Get Peacock Premium tier for $8.99 a month for 3 months or $89.99 for 1 year.

There’s plenty coming to Peacock over the next few months, making this a pretty good time to grab a cheaper membership. Movie fans can already stream new additions like “Disclosure Day,” “Finding Emily” and “Alien: Romulus” (my favorite movie of 2024), while “One Night Only” arrives on December 4.

There’s also plenty for TV fans to look forward to, including “Ted: The Animated Series,” “The Traitors” season 5 and the “Friday the 13th” prequel series “Crystal Lake,” which is already streaming. That’s a lot of entertainment to get through, and paying less for your subscription makes the prospect even sweeter.

Sources indicate that one thing to keep in mind before signing up: these discounted prices won’t last forever. Once your three-month promotional period ends, your subscription will automatically renew at Peacock’s then-current regular price unless you cancel. So, if you only want to take advantage of the deal and aren’t planning to stick around, make sure you cancel before your next billing date to avoid any unexpected charges.
