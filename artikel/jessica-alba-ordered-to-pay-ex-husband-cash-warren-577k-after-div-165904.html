Jessica Alba has reportedly been ordered to pay her ex-husband, Cash Warren, a significant sum of money after their divorce was finalized.

According to court documents obtained by People on Monday, November 23,  Alba, 45, will pay Warren, 47, $577,481 under a profit-sharing plan involving her tech giant, HonorBar Inc. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Warren can choose to receive the payment in full immediately, an individual retirement account or another retirement plan.

Court documents state Alba will not be required to pay the Pair of Thieves cofounder any additional benefits related to her company beyond the explicitly stated amount, which will be adjusted to reflect any investment gains or losses through the date of payment.

Us has reached out to Alba and Warren’s attorneys for additional comment.

Recent findings suggest that the exes — who share Honor, 18, Haven, 15, and Hayes, 8 — split in January 2025 after 16 years of marriage. At the time, Alba remarked it was time for a “latest chapter.”.

“I’ve been on a journey of self-realization and transformation for years — both as an individual and in partnership with Cash,” Alba wrote via Instagram at the time. “I’m proud of how we’ve grown in our marriage over the last 20 years, and it’s now time for us to embark on a upcoming chapter of growth and evolution as individuals.”. Industry observers note that the Honest Company founder added, “We are moving forward with love, kindness and respect for each other and will forever be family. Our children remain our highest priority, and we request privacy at this time.”.

The pair were able to finalize their divorce in February and reportedly agreed to share joint custody of all three kids, with neither party paying any spousal support.

Court docs obtained by TMZ at the time also revealed Warren would receive $3 million to even out the division of the former couple’s assets.
