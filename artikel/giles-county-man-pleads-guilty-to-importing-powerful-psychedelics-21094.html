Giles County Man Pleads Guilty to Importing Powerful Psychedelics

🌐 Source: Justice • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (81 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that ROANOKE, Va. – A Ripplemead, Virginia man, accused of importing powerful psychedelic drugs from Brazil, pled guilty this week to distributing LSD. Charles Herman Aardema III, 29, was arrested in April 2025 after U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) officers at the Miami international mail facility examined a parcel due to suspicious anomalies revealed by X-ray. The parcel was addressed to Aardema’s Ripplemead home and shipped by a “Raul Wictor Silva Batista” of Brazil.
