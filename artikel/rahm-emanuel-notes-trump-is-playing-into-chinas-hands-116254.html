Rahm Emanuel Notes Trump Is Playing Into China’s Hands

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (88 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that former US Ambassador to Japan Rahm Emanuel tells Bloomberg This Weekend that he is seriously considering a 2028 presidential run as he lays out an economic vision focused on reforming capitalism, combating corruption and strengthening the middle class. Speaking with hosts David Gura and Christina Ruffini, Emanuel also criticizes President Donald Trump’s approach to China and Russia, arguing that the US should work more closely with allies to counter Beijing and protect American economic and national security interests.
