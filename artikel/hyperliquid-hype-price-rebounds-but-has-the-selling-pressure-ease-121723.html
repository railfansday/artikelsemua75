The Hyperliquid (HYPE) price is attempting to rebound after a prolonged decline, with the token trading around $62.54 on November 22, up 1.90% on the daily candle shown. The recovery comes after HYPE fell nearly 36% from its swing high of $97.82, bringing the price closer to a key support zone that could determine its next move. While the bounce offers some respite, the broader daily price structure remains under pressure.Â.

Declining open interest and moderating funding rates offer additional clues into derivatives positioning as traders assess the possibility of a recovery. With HYPE hovering near the $62 Fibonacci retracement level, the focus is shifting toward whether selling pressure is easing or the rebound is merely a temporary pause in the downtrend.

Recent findings suggest that HYPE is showing signs of a rebound around $62.54, but the broader daily trend hasn’t turned bullish yet. Since peaking near $97.82, the token has continued to form lower highs and lower lows, keeping sellers in control. The latest bounce offers some relief but does not confirm a trend reversal.Â.

Industry observers note that the $62.22 level is the first line to watch, as it aligns with the Fibonacci 0.236 retracement. If HYPE manages to hold here, buyers could attempt to push the price toward $69.02, the 0.382 retracement. A move beyond that could bring $74.52 and $80.02 into focus. This makes the region worth watching, as it could offer support or become an area where selling pressure resurfaces. A gradual rise in CVD while the price is holding above $62 would be the first bullish signal to watch.

HYPE’s price decline has been accompanied by a steady drop in Open Interest, suggesting traders have been pulling back from the derivatives market. Open Interest, which had climbed above $3.9 billion, has since fallen toward $2.5 billion.

When price and open interest fall together, it often suggests traders are closing positions or reducing leverage. Funding rates, on the other hand, remain largely positive, meaning long positions have generally been paying shorts.

Sources indicate that this suggests that while the market still leans toward long positioning, funding is not particularly elevated compared with the period shown. Together, the declining open interest and relatively moderate funding rates suggest that leverage has come down as HYPE’s price has fallen, while the funding bias remains tilted toward longs. This doesn’t necessarily signal a reversal, but it offers a useful look at how traders are positioning themselves as HYPE tests support.

Bullish scenario: HYPE price needs to stay above $62.22 to keep the rebound going. If buyers manage to hold this level and push the price past $69.02, the next hurdles could be $74.52 and the $77 to $80 region. A move higher would be more convincing if it comes with stronger buying activity, rather than just a short-lived bounce.

Bearish scenario: If the Hyperliquid price slips below $62.22 and struggles to reclaim it, the rebound could lose momentum. The price may then revisit $59.60 to $60, with a deeper decline potentially bringing $53.50 to $55 into play. If selling continues, $51.22 remains the lower Fibonacci level to watch.

Sources indicate that collectively, the decline in Open Interest and moderation in funding rates suggest derivatives exposure has cooled during the sell-off, but neither confirms that selling pressure has fully eased. For now, HYPE’s ability to hold support, reclaim resistance and attract stronger buying activity will determine whether the rebound develops into a more meaningful recovery or fades into another move lower.
