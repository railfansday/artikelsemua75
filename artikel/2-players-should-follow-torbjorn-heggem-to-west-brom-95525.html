While the January transfer window is still just under six weeks away, West Brom are already being touted to make an early addition.

The Baggies are presently in a great position to attract players who could have a major influence on the rest of their season, with Albion well-placed in the promotion mix under James Morrison.

This week, Albion have been linked with taking Bologna defender Torbjørn Heggem on loan in January for the remainder of the season, with Full Throstle writer Steve Tudgay reporting that Heggem is willing to consider a return to The Hawthorns on a six-month deal following a lack of playing time at his current club.

Heggem has fallen out of favour due to a managerial change at the Serie A outfit, who parted firm with Domenico Tedesco in September after just four league matches into the season.

They then appointed his successor in Raffaele Palladino, who has since changed the formation that the Bologna team plays, which has subsequently affected Heggem’s minutes.

Meanwhile, controlling shareholder Shilen Patel, who retains a minority stake in Bologna, could play a crucial part in making this deal happen at the turn of 2027.

If Heggem was to seal a dream return to West Brom in January, then it’s a signing that would certainly put Albion in a stronger position to achieve promotion, with their defence undoubtedly a lot stronger with him on the books, as seen from two years ago.

In what would be a statement signing in the backline, the Baggies will have to look at bolstering at the top end of the pitch too, with a couple of arrivals in that department also going a long way to helping them achieve their goals come the end of the campaign.

So, with this in mind, FLW has listed two other players that Albion should move for during the January transfer window.

During the January transfer window, a department West Brom must address is on the flanks.

Starting off on the right-hand side, Femi Azeez could be a signing of intent given his previous Championship exploits with Millwall.

His heroics at the Den last season earned him a move to Premier League outfit Brighton, signing on the dotted line for a reported fee of £12.5m.

However, a hamstring injury means he is yet to feature for Fabian Hurzeler’s side, although Azeez is believed to be closing in on a return to full fitness.

But with Brighton flying high in the Premier League, it’s hard to see where Azeez will fit in once he comes back, with Hurzeler not wanting to disrupt a team that is in full flow and playing so well.

Meanwhile, Yankuba Minteh is now back involved after his own injury problems, and, given his quality, he will presumably stay ahead of Azeez in the pecking order. Not to mention Kaoru Mitoma is scheduled to return this month as well, so it’s difficult to see the 25-year-old getting much, if any, game time.

Therefore, a loan move may well be on the cards in January, and a return to the Championship could be exactly what Azeez needs to get back playing regularly and rediscover his old form.

After scoring 11 goals and registering eight assists from 35 Championship games last season, Azeez has proven himself to be such a livewire in the second tier.

And with the Baggies needing players to hit the ground running, Azeez is more than capable of doing that given his previous pedigree. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

While there will be stiff competition to overcome if he is available in January, Azeez is a player who could add an extra gear to Albion’s attacking line, while the Black Country outfit are in need of reinforcements there given Kareem Tunde is the only recognised right-winger on the books at this moment in time.

As well as bolstering out wide, the arrival of another striker could go a long way to helping West Brom achieve their promotion ambitions.

The Baggies were busy during the summer window revamping some of the forward line, replacing the outgoings of Josh Maja and Daryl Dike with Barney Stewart and Jimmy-Jay Morgan. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Morgan in particular has been a roaring success so far, grabbing eight goal contributions in his opening 15 league matches for the club, but is not a conventional centre-forward, drifting over to the left-hand side while a proper number nine stays central.

In contrast, the more traditional centre-forwards, like Stewart, have found opportunities hard to come by, netting just once for his fresh club so far, while Aune Heggebo in his second season at the club has also found himself in and out of the side, with most of his success coming after being introduced from the bench.

Thankfully, much of Albion’s early success has been down to players in other positions chipping in with goals, but over time, that’s at risk of drying up, so Albion must do all they can at the turn of the fresh year to bring in a forward who has an eye for goal and can make a real difference when leading the line.

And a name that could well be on West Brom’s shortlist is Ellis Simms, who himself has struggled to gain regular minutes for Coventry City in the Premier League.

It won’t be a name that generates a lot of excitement among the Albion faithful, but his return of 13 goals in the second tier last season despite playing just over 1,600 minutes meant he recorded the second-best goals-per-90 ratio in the division.

Simms has proven that, even when not having the best minutes, he’s a player who can contribute goals in the Championship at a steady rate, and that could well help this Albion team tick over with enough strikes to keep them in the promotion conversation.

At the same time, it’s a realistic deal for the Baggies to achieve. Simms is local to the West Midlands, and with him set to enter the final six months of his contract in January, it gives Albion the option to either pursue a cut-price permanent deal or instead take him on a six-month loan to help provide further depth for Heggebo, Stewart and Morgan.

After featuring just four times in the top-flight for the Sky Blues so far, it seems a deal that could work out well for all parties involved.

Proven at Championship level and physically suited to the division, Simms is a player Albion could depend on for the second half of the season, with the team needing that extra bit more at the top end of the pitch to keep up their high-flying start.
