Rio Ngumoha played a starring role as Liverpool thrashed Manchester United 4-0 on Sunday to hand Mauricio Pochettino a disastrous start to his reign at Old Trafford.

The Reds were on fire at Anfield, with Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Ngumoha and Victor Munoz all on target for Andoni Iraola’s men, who secured a second straight win.

It followed Leny Yoro’s eighth-minute red card after denying Alexander Isak a clear goalscoring opportunity, as Pochettino watched on in horror from the sidelines.

The Argentine made unwanted history on his debut, becoming the first Premier League manager to suffer conceding two goals and receiving a red card in the first half of his first game in charge.

There was more positive news for the hosts, however, and more specifically, their teenage sensation on the left wing.

Ngumoha, who has caught the eye of England manager Thomas Tuchel with his performances, became the youngest goalscorer in the historic fixture, overtaking Michael Owen as he slotted home the third goal, and he also became the youngest Liverpool player since Owen in 1997 ever to score and assist in a Premier League match.

There is another historic figure whose company he has joined in the history books too.

Now, at just 18 years and 85 days, Ngumoha is in the tech giant of United legend Cristiano Ronaldo, after he became just the second teenager to create three or more chances in a Liverpool vs Man United fixture since records began back in the 2003-04 season.

Before he was substituted late on by Iraola, replaced by fellow goalscorer Munoz, he had registered more touches than any other player on the pitch with 48, and he had twice as many successful dribbles than anyone else (four), terrorising Diogo Dalot down the Liverpool left.
