An Ohio mother has been accused of knowingly lying about her daughter’s alleged leukemia diagnosis, forcing the child to undergo unnecessary and painful treatments.

Danielle Rowan was arrested on November 13 and charged with endangering the welfare of children.

Authorities became aware of the allegations when officers with the Powell Police Department responded to a home on November 10 after they received reports of a domestic dispute, according to a probable cause arrest affidavit viewed by Law & Crime.

Rowan and her husband got into an argument when the husband demanded to see their daughter’s medical records. The husband then reportedly took the girl and her sister to Rowan’s parents’ house, where the defendant later highlighted up and allegedly demanded she give her daughter medication.

Rowan, 32, also allegedly claimed she was the only person who could administer the medication.

Rowan’s husband and her parents explained to authorities in a second call to police that Rowan had claimed her daughter had been diagnosed with leukemia in May.

After Rowan announced the diagnosis, the girl allegedly received several steroid injections that caused her to gain weight and become irritable.

Sources indicate that the family later grew suspicious of the girl’s diagnosis when further treatment was continuously delayed, including having a semi-permanent IV line installed. After Rowan allegedly indicated her family the cancer diagnosis from the Cincinnati Children’s Hospital’s oncology department, her family members showed the document to a doctor who reportedly confirmed the so-called report was fake.

The family then had a doctor at a children’s hospital examine the girl, who determined she did not have cancer.

Rowan has also been accused of falsely telling a pediatrician that her daughter could not eat solid food, which prompted the doctor to prescribe the child high-calorie PediaSure. The defendant allegedly gave her daughter three to four bottles of the PediaSure each day, which caused her to suffer pain and regular constipation.

Recent findings suggest that rowan eventually spoke to investigators, at which point she allegedly denied telling anyone her daughter had cancer. However, the detective presented her with her social media posts in which she publicly talked about her daughter’s faux diagnosis. Rowan then claimed to authorities that she “did not remember making the posts.”.

It has been revealed that rowan was taken into custody at Delaware County Jail following her arrest. She’s since been released after posting bond.

“That’s not the Danielle that I knew, but at this rate, what did I know? What didn’t I know? What is true? What’s not true?” the friend remarked.
