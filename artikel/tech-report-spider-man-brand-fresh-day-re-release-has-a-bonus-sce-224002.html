Spider-Man: Brand Fresh Day is swinging back into theatres this December and, now, we know it's coming bundled with an Avengers: Doomsday bonus.

Already, Marvel has deployed Avengers: Doomsday teases in various movies, indicates, and marketing spots.

The Avengers: Endgame re-release featured Hulk on Doctor Doom's radar, while the VisionQuest post-credits scene saw Latveria's ruler resurrect Scarlet Witch from underneath the Darkhold Castle.

Now, it seems the most successful non-Avengers superhero movie in history is going to do the same trick – and it could potentially link into a Brand Newly introduced Day post-credits scene that spotlighted the webhead on the Spidey-Tracker, before seemingly showing him being teleported to outer space, complete with some now-trademark Doom static effects. Spider-Man: Brand New Day's second run in theatres – arriving for a limited time from December 4 – will also include more scenes and "surprises." One of the confirmed additions is a deleted scene featuring Tom Holland's Peter Parker and Rosario Dawson's Claire Temple, reprising her role from Netflix's street-level Defenders shows.

No word yet on if Tom Holland will appear in Avengers: Doomsday, though new actors are being included all the time – such as Hailee Steinfeld, who is back as Kate Bishop and even demonstrated up in the final Doomsday trailer released earlier this week.
