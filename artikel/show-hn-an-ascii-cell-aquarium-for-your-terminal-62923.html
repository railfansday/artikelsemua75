The package is terminal-cells; the command is cells. From a source checkout, run ./cells. See Ubuntu packages for .deb downloads and builds, or terminal troubleshooting if colors, glyphs, or curses fail. (Related: Tech Report: Developer builds a ‘Discord kitten’ that’s...)

Use a terminal at least 80×24; 120×40 or larger gives organisms more room. Press q to quit, Space to pause, + / - to change speed, r for a new slide, and ? for help and the species legend.

Macrophages, neutrophils, red blood cells, nine microbial forms, fungi, and phages share the slide. Watch engulfment, mitosis, budding, viral replication, and rare visitors. Periodic arrivals keep populations varied during long runs.

Choose 1 Inspect, 2 Lyse, 3 Feed, or 4 Stir, then click to apply. Keyboard targeting also works: arrows or hjkl to aim, Tab to select, Enter to apply. Tools act while the slide keeps moving.

The GIF uses rendered simulation frames. The 12-second terminal recording captures the curses app; replay it with asciinema play docs/demo.cast after cloning.
