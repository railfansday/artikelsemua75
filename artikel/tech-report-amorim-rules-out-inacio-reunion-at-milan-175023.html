AC Milan boss Ruben Amorim has ruled out the potential signing of Sporting CP defender Goncalo Inacio in January.

Amorim coached Inacio during his tenure at Sporting from March 2020 to November 2024, with the defender progressing through the club's youth ranks. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

Inacio played 123 Primeira Liga matches under Amorim's tutelage, winning the league title in 2020-21 and in 2023-24, though the Portuguese coach left midway through the latter victory to join Manchester United.

Among Sporting defenders during Amorim's reign, Inacio ranked second for tackles won (94), interceptions (140), duels won (460) and aerial duels won (219) in the league, coming in behind Sebastian Coates in all four categories.

Sporting are third in the Primeira Liga this season, but have won three of their four Champions League games (L1). (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

Inacio has been linked with a move to Milan in January to reunite with Amorim, but the latter has denied the rumours.

"No, that is not true. Goncalo will not arrive," Amorim told Sport TV, as quoted by O Jogo.
