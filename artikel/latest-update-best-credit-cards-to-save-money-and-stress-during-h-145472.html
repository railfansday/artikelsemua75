Latest Update: Best credit cards to save money and stress during holiday travel

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (180 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Holiday travel often brings higher costs, crowded airports and unpredictable delays, but certain card benefits can help ease these challenges.
  • Key perks include free checked bags, expedited security screening, lounge access, ride-hailing and food-delivery credits, hotel timing benefits and complimentary breakfast.
  • Many travel cards offer statement credits for airport security programs, hotel bookings and ride services, as well as elite status with hotels and rental car companies.
  • Choosing the right card depends on your specific travel needs, such as saving on baggage fees or maximizing lounge access.
  • Some benefits, such as free checked bags or elite status, may require enrollment or payment with a specific card and can have restrictions on companions or eligible bookings.
  • Credits for security programs or ride services often have expiration dates or require activation before travel.
  • Hotel and lounge benefits are subject to availability and may require booking through designated programs or platforms.
