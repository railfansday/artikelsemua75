Recent findings suggest that while streaming platforms have become known for longer episodes, Netflix is experimenting with a shorter sitcom format. Now, one of the best newly introduced Netflix shows to binge is testing whether audiences are interested in episodes that run closer to 15 minutes. In a recent development, according to Deadline, Minimum Wage is expected to return with 14 additional episodes in spring 2027, following its October debut. The workplace comedy, produced by Murder Mystery 2 director Jeremy Garelick and Will Phelps through American High, released its first 14 episodes with runtimes ranging from 12 to 20 minutes. However, it remains unclear whether the upcoming episodes will be considered the second part of season 1 or an entirely new season. Phelps explained why he believes shorter episodes could become a larger part of television's future:.

Recent findings suggest that "The future is these 15-minute kind of SpongeBob-length episodes where it’s really fast. There’s not any fat.".

Garelick also revealed that Netflix specifically requested shorter episodes when the streaming service acquired the series:.

It has been revealed that "When we closed the deal, [Netflix] remarked that they wanted it to be a minimum of twelve and a half-minute episodes. It was an interesting challenge because most episodic TV shows are 22 to 30 pages. It was almost like we were inventing a new structure as we were going and it was hard.".

The production team ultimately capped the episodes at 20 minutes, with shooting scripts running approximately 15 pages. The cast was also encouraged to improvise, combining traditional television writing with the fast-paced content designed for today's shorter attention spans.

According to recent reports, minimum Wage follows a group of teenagers working at The Slice Club, a pizza restaurant where everyday problems promptly become overwhelming. The comedy stars Grace Reiter, Aidan Micho, Hyde Healy, Julia Dicesare, Luke Burke, Ryan Micho, Tommy Armstrong, and WandaVision star Randall Park.

In a recent development, the approach builds on American High Shorts' established success on social media. According to Netflix, the production organization's sketches have accumulated over 7 billion views across platforms including YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Snapchat since launching in October 2022.

Before arriving on Netflix, Minimum Wage began as an American High Shorts series that accumulated over 30 million views across YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram since launching in May 2024. Following its October debut, the Netflix adaptation reached the streamer's global Top 10 with 2.4 million views, marking an early streaming achievement for the short-form comedy.

Sources indicate that other streaming comedies have also embraced shorter episodes. Netflix's Tires typically features episodes lasting around 20 to 23 minutes, while HBO Max's Stuart Fails to Save the Universe includes episodes as short as 15 minutes. Both series show how streaming comedies can vary in length without following a fixed episode runtime.

In a recent development, netflix initially ordered 20 episodes, but American High ultimately produced 28 after developing additional scripts. According to Garelick, the production team already has approximately 50 more draft scripts, leaving room for the comedy to continue beyond its upcoming release.
