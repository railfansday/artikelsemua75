President Donald Trump publicly urged Nvidia to rehire Jensen Huang at 02:54 UTC on Nov. 23, according to TrumpAlert's archive.

Bubblemaps reported that 11 Polymarket accounts received their funding between 03:49 and 10:44 UTC that morning and placed their first bets within about six minutes of each deposit.

The earliest deposit trailed Trump's post by 55 minutes, and the last cleared about three hours before Nvidia announced Huang's reinstatement at 13:55 UTC.

Industry observers note that A presidential endorsement, Huang's stated willingness to return, and the campaign for his reinstatement gave any trader a public reason to buy cheap Yes shares. Polymarket priced a return low, and Bubblemaps cited purchases as cheap as 1.5 cents a share. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Public records traced wallet 0x307 receiving 99.49 pUSD at 10:44:40 UTC, and Polymarket's activity feed shows a $99.29 Yes purchase on a Dec. 31 return one second later, at roughly 10 cents a share. Nvidia declared about three hours and 10 minutes later.

On Nov. 17, Nvidia Chairman Harvey Jones publicly ruled out reinstating Huang, even as Huang mentioned he was willing to return.

Industry observers note that bubblemaps counted 16 accounts with five funded wallets on Nov. 16 and 11 funded on Nov. 23. Each account started trading within about six minutes of funding and traded exclusively in Nvidia-related markets. (Related: Industry News: Welcome spaCy to the Hugging Face Hub...)

Sources indicate that the group wagered roughly $20,000 and earned roughly $350,000, a return near 1,750%, and its four largest named winners hold $275,777 of that total. Bubblemaps attributed the $350,000 to all 16 accounts combined, which leaves the Nov. 23 group's share undetermined, and both totals carry its rounding.

The five Nov. 16 accounts resist the public-information explanation that fits the later group. Their funding ran from 15:39 to 21:12 UTC, and the first deposit arrived before Trump’s support was reported at 16:52 UTC.

One account highlights about $94,900 in realized profit on the November contract, according to Polymarket's closed-position data.

Bubblemaps reported the operators as unidentified, and the on-chain links among the accounts as unestablished, so shared ownership and access to confidential information would need separate proof.
