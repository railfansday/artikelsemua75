Alongside the huge list of upcoming updates Google detailed yesterday, the organization also announced that a “faster and more natural” version of Gemini Live is coming to speakers and smart displays “soon.”.

Google explains this is from Gemini Live moving to the “latest speech-to-speech architecture” and brings “significantly faster response times.” Gemini Live isn’t the default experience when you start a “Hey Google” command, rather hiding behind a “let’s chat” or other similar command to start a session.

Initially, Live sessions were purely for brainstorming or longer conversations and lacking support for commands and other functions mid-session, but Google now states that this upgrade will add support for actions such as smart home controls, setting timers, and more without leaving a Live session.
