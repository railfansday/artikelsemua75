On a Saturday laden with Scottish Premiership goals, Ryan Naderi's was far from the most impressive - but vital for Rangers all the same.

The Czech Republic forward's deflected shot from close range gave his side a 2-1 win at Motherwell and kept the gap between Scottish Premiership leaders Celtic and Rangers at four points.

That could change on Tuesday when resurgent Aberdeen travel to Ibrox for Rangers' game in hand.

But, until then, Rangers supporters will be celebrating the Naderi shot that found the net via a Martin Moormann deflection - a record-equalling 29th goal for a Premiership weekend.

Three goals in 11 appearances last season was hardly prolific as Naderi adjusted to the hustle and bustle of Scottish football. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

According to recent reports, fast forward a few months and the 23-year has 10 in 22 so far this term. Among his goals this season have been winners against Motherwell at Ibrox, Celtic at Celtic Park and Aberdeen in the League Cup semi-final at Hampden.

Such form has led to his first international caps, though goals at that level have so far eluded him in four appearances.

When asked by Sky Sports if he was claiming the winner at Fir Park, rather than it going down as a Moormann own goal, Naderi noted: "I'll take it.".

Recent findings suggest that "We had a few chances, we didn't score and those moments, you need a stable defence," he mentioned. "We had that lucky punch.". (Related: Score TP-Link Archer with full Wi-Fi 7 support and 10 G...)

Manager Derek McInnes was similarly delighted.

"When the game is still in the balance and you're putting a bit of pressure on, it's good to know that you've got a bit of evidence there that you can score late winners," he told BBC Scotland.

"I think there's a belief in the team that there's goals, certainly a belief from myself and the staff that we feel there's a goal coming.".

And, on Naderi, the manager commented: "I thought it was a proper number nine performance, I've mentioned that to him.

"He ran the channels when he needed to, he pinned defenders when he needed to and he linked the game brilliantly.

It has been revealed that "His overall performance was a boy who looks very confident at the minute. I'm so glad we've got him and he should be really proud of what he's producing at the minute.".
