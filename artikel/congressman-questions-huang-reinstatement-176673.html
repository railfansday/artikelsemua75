Congressman Questions Huang Reinstatement

🌐 Source: Bloomberg • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (50 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Democratic Congressman Raja Krishnamoorthi is raising national security concerns over Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s reinstatement, citing unanswered questions about alleged access to sensitive information. He also criticized US chip export controls and called for Congress to strengthen oversight of advanced semiconductor exports to China. (Source: Bloomberg).
