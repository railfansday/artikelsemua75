It has been revealed that arsenal, Liverpool and Chelsea have all contacted Bournemouth about England midfielder Alex Scott, according to CaughtOffside, which cites The i Paper. Liverpool and Chelsea want to move in January, while Arsenal would rather wait until next summer. Bournemouth’s stance on selling the 23-year-old has softened, but the reported asking price is above £100m, and Scott turned down two contract offers over the summer.

The interest has clear reasons behind it. Andoni Iraola coached Scott at Bournemouth before taking over at Anfield in June, and Liverpool want to reunite them. Chelsea lost Enzo Fernandez to Manchester City in a £125m deadline-day deal and had a reported £64m bid for Scott rejected earlier in the summer. Arsenal already have Declan Rice, Bruno Guimaraes, Martin Zubimendi and Myles Lewis-Skelly, so CaughtOffside sees Chelsea or Liverpool as the more logical destinations for his playing time. According to recent reports, scott wins the ball, presses and carries it through midfield, and he started Bournemouth’s first five league games this season before a quadriceps injury on England duty in October kept him out for weeks. Fitness is the first concern, because knee problems have interrupted much of his time on the south coast. End product is the second. He has scored five league goals in 85 appearances since joining in 2023, so he will not solve a shortage of chances alone.

In a recent development, not at £100m, and not as a January priority. Liverpool rank 18th for massive chances after ten league games, according to This Is Anfield, and Scott offers energy rather than creativity. The real gap sits beside Ryan Gravenberch, where Curtis Jones left in the summer, and nobody arrived to share the defensive work. Scott could be the aggressive partner in Iraola’s double pivot, freeing Dominik Szoboszlai and Florian Wirtz to play higher, yet that is a heavy fee for a player only just rebuilding match fitness, with Liverpool fourth and nine points behind Arsenal.

Sources indicate that chelsea’s case is simpler. The division’s biggest summer spenders lost their midfield engine and can justify the price more easily. Arsenal’s patience makes sense, since Rice, Guimaraes and Zubimendi leave no room for a £100m squad player. A Liverpool bid would only inflate the market Chelsea are already shaping. If Iraola wants a January signing, a specialist holding midfielder for far less money does more for the top-four push.
