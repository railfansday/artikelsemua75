Industry observers note that sentiment in the crypto market hasn't been this bad since February. That was when the Crypto Fear & Greed Index hit an all-time low of 5 out of 100. Right now, the index reading is 8 out of 100, suggesting that most crypto investors are scrambling for the exits. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

That might be the case, but several cryptocurrencies continue to soar in value, regardless of what's happening in the broader market. Here are six cryptocurrencies that are up 12% or more during the past 90 days, including one that has gone absolutely parabolic.

According to recent reports, no surprise here, but four of these six cryptocurrencies can be categorized as artificial intelligence (AI) cryptos. In other words, they are cryptocurrencies at the intersection of AI and blockchain technology, making them attractive to crypto investors looking for more exposure to the AI sector.

In a recent development, leading the way is Pieverse (PIEVERSE -10.11%), up a whopping 275% during the past 90 days. Also making massive gains are NEAR Protocol (NEAR -2.34%) and Venice Token (VVV -0.09%), both up 50% during that period. And finally, there's Kite (KITE +2.49%), up 22%.

The primary investment thesis for these cryptocurrencies is simple: They are helping to build out the blockchain infrastructure for the AI agent economy. Simply put, if millions of AI agents are going to coordinate and collaborate in real time, they will need a blockchain platform to process those transactions.

It has been revealed that and that's why NEAR Protocol could be the biggest winner of these four cryptocurrencies. It's a Layer-1 blockchain optimized for agentic AI payments. For good reason, it has zoomed up the ranks of the top cryptocurrencies, and now ranks 20th overall, with a market cap of nearly $4 billion. After a sizzling 90-day period for AI cryptos, it could be time for a pullback in all things AI-related.

If that's the case, there are still a handful of other names to watch in the crypto market. They have not skyrocketed in value as much as the AI names above, but have attracted attention from substantial-name investors.

Industry observers note that arbitrum (ARB -0.53%), for example, is up 16%. This is a Layer-2 scaling solution for Ethereum (ETH -1.35%), so it can piggyback on growth in this dynamic ecosystem. Standard Chartered (SCBFY +5.90%) predicts it is capable of 70-fold gains by 2030.

According to recent reports, another crypto to watch is Monero (XMR -0.88%), up 12%. It's a privacy coin, just like Zcash (ZEC -6.88%). For much of 2026, Monero has underperformed Zcash, but that may be about to change.

It has been revealed that with crypto sentiment in full-on panic mode, investors need to keep a careful eye on what's happening in the AI sector right now. The Anthropic IPO in November could turn out to be a pivotal moment for what happens next.

It has been revealed that the Anthropic IPO could mark the next leg up in the AI bull market, or it could lead to the long-awaited bursting of the AI bubble. So if you're thinking of buying AI cryptos now, be sure to do your due diligence.
