Individual meerkats are ace solo navigators

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (76 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

After meerkats emerge each morning from their burrow to warm in the early sun, there's one thing on their collective mind, and that's to find food. They navigate communally as they head off, working slowly along their route before returning home swiftly at the end of the day. But although the tight-knit band navigates as one, some members of the clan could be more experienced after roaming solo, shaping the community's maneuvers.
