The lawmakers' concerns come as the nation continues to experience tech outages, including at the National Security Agency, and grapples with whether the recent disruptions were isolated technical failures or signs of a broader cybersecurity threat.

In a recent development, president Donald Trump's administration says the incidents were unrelated and were not caused by malicious actors. Regardless of what caused the disruptions, lawmakers warned that the incidents underscore a broader concern: America's reliance on interconnected technology has created potential vulnerabilities across critical infrastructure, from local water systems and power grids to banking and aviation.

It has been revealed that "The localized municipal-based infrastructure in America is a soft target," Sen. Tim Sheehy, R-Mont., told Fox News Digital. "And that's been flagged for decades.
