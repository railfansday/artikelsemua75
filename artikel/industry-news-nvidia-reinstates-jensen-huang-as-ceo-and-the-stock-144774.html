Industry News: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO — and the stock soars 35%

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (70 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that nvidia $NVDA reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO on Monday, less than two weeks after he resigned following revelations of multiple affairs, including with a suspected Chinese spy. Shares of the leading AI chipmaker, which had been hammered in recent weeks, rose a whopping 35% in Monday morning trading after the announcement, adding more than $1 trillion in market capitalization compared to Friday's close.
