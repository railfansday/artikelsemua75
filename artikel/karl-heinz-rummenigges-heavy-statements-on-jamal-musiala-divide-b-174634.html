Karl-Heinz Rummenigge’s heavy statements on Jamal Musiala divide Bayern more than ever

  • Rummenigge thinks above all about the future of the club.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, “What we know suggests that it will be difficult to reach the level he has so often displayed here. We need to talk to the family, the player and especially his representatives. These sums are no longer possible – sorry, with all due respect.» Karl-Heinz Rummenigge opened Pandora’s box last night. After the draw against Cologne where the German champion lost Harry Kane for three months and Michael Olise for a few weeks, the member of the supervisory board, and former chairman of the club’s board of directors, hit hard on the young attacking midfielder.

It has been revealed that the latter suffers from a neurological dysfunction which prevents him from being able to take part in high-level meetings. Despite a treatment protocol and an illness that is not serious, the attacking midfielder is subject to dizziness on the field. He even passes out sometimes. The problem has been known to Bayern for a long time and until recent months seemed rather controllable. For some time now, Musiala has experienced several discomforts in matches and training. His level of performance is no longer the same and his case necessarily raises questions. He is one of Bayern’s highest paid players.

Rummenigge thinks above all about the future of the club. If he wants Musiala to return, the former striker, now 71 years old, does not believe it. The contract which locks him in until 2030 has even become a burden according to him. “I can’t imagine Jamal leaving the club or another club buying him in these circumstances. This is problematic.» This statement may be official, but it is not shared by everyone within the club. Since last night and this famous interview which caused a lot of noise, managers, coaches and players have taken a stand, and are not all going in the same direction. In a recent development, “He is someone who has to endure a lot at his young age. We support it. We believe he has the potential to return to formsays Max Eberl, the sports director, while tempering the situation. It’s legitimate to be worried, especially after what happened.» Before Rummenigge, Uli Hoeness, the honorary president of Bayern, and Jan-Christian Dreesen, the CEO, had expressed their concerns after Musiala’s first discomfort during two friendly matches this summer. According to Bildthere are now two opposing camps on the subject within the club. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

In a recent development, on one side we find Hoeness, Rummenigge and the majority of the supervisory board, as well as Dreesen. They would like Musiala to agree to lower his salary since he is no longer as efficient as before. Rummenigge’s statement last night is also seen as a first step of pressure by the clan opposite. This is embodied by the sports management, Max Eberl and Christoph Freund at the head. Vincent Kompany is also their ally in this affair. The technician refused “to enter into this debate» after the match this Friday evening, while affirming that “it is completely normal for people who have done a lot for the club to have their opinion.».

According to recent reports, the locker room is obviously on the side of the German international, like Konrad Laimer, also questioned in the mixed zone. “Jamal is a great guy who works incredibly hard every day. Given what I see every day, I am absolutely certain that he will return to his best level. I’m very happy, we’re all very happy to see him back on the pitch, and I’m sure he’ll show everyone what he’s made of.» Musiala made his return to competition yesterday after a muscle problem which kept him away from the field for a month and a half. He played the last 20 minutes against Cologne.
