Recent findings suggest that earlier this week, security researchers reported that certain inference providers had data compromised or otherwise shown evidence that their data policies were not being upheld. We have been collaborating closely with the researchers and with providers to understand the findings and encourage the providers to address them.

According to recent reports, these findings did not show any breach or unauthorized access of OpenRouter. The results of this testing revealed new findings about specific inference providers.
