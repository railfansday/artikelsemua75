It's almost that time of year again. The time when Spotify reminds you exactly how many hours you spent listening to the same five songs, exposes your most embarrassing musical obsessions, and gives you a colorful little slideshow to share with everyone you know.

Yes, Spotify Wrapped season is upon us.

Sources indicate that every December, Spotify turns a year's worth of listening data into a personalized recap of your musical habits, from your most-streamed artists and songs to the total number of minutes you spent listening. Over the years, Wrapped has become something of an annual internet holiday, flooding social media feeds with colorful graphics, questionable listening habits, and people desperately trying to explain why a certain artist made their top five. (Listen, BTS released a upcoming album this year. What did you expect me to do?). (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

And with November winding down, there's one question on everyone's mind: When does Spotify Wrapped 2026 come out? (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

Spotify has not officially announced a release date for Wrapped 2026. However, based on previous years, we can expect it to arrive in late November or early December.

For reference, here's when Spotify Wrapped dropped over the past five years:.

Industry observers note that notice a pattern? Spotify has released Wrapped on a Wednesday for five consecutive years. If that tradition continues, Wednesday, Dec. 2, 2026, seems like a reasonable prediction. But that's just an educated guess, not a confirmed date.

It has been revealed that spotify has already started teasing this year's rollout. On Nov. 5, the streaming platform published a blog post encouraging listeners to prepare for Wrapped by updating their apps and reviewing which music influences their listening profiles.

In other words, the countdown is officially on.

Once Wrapped goes live, you'll be able to access your personalized recap through the Spotify mobile app.

In a recent development, update your Spotify app. Make sure you're running the latest version on iOS or Android.

Sources indicate that open Spotify. When Wrapped releases, look for a dedicated Wrapped banner or section on the app's home screen.

In a recent development, explore your results. Tap through your personalized listening recap to see your top artists, songs, and other statistics.

According to recent reports, share your Wrapped. Spotify typically provides shareable graphics so you can post your results on Instagram, TikTok, or wherever else you feel like showing off your taste in music.

You can also check Spotify's official Wrapped page for updates.

Keep in mind that some Wrapped features have eligibility requirements, so not every listener will necessarily receive the same experience.

In a recent development, if you're hoping to squeeze a few more streams of your favorite artist into this year's results, you might be wondering whether Spotify is still counting. Despite a persistent internet rumor, Spotify doesn't automatically stop tracking your listening habits on Oct. 31.

For Wrapped 2025, Spotify confirmed that its listening-data window extended from January through mid-November, just a few weeks before the annual recap released.

It has been revealed that the organization hasn't confirmed the exact cutoff for Wrapped 2026, so it's unclear whether your late-November listening will be included in the final tally. Of course, that won't stop me from listening to the Grand Theft Auto VI soundtrack on repeat.

According to recent reports, spotify also lets consumers exclude individual tracks and playlists from their taste profiles, preventing them from influencing certain Wrapped results. So if your sleep playlist or that one song you played on a loop for a week doesn't exactly represent your musical identity, you have some control over what counts.

Spotify has yet to reveal this year's latest Wrapped features, but the platform has a history of finding increasingly creative ways to interpret our listening habits.

Previous editions have assigned people a Listening Personality, matched them with a Sound Town based on their musical preferences, and even estimated their Listening Age. In 2025, Spotify introduced additional personalized insights and interactive features that went beyond the usual top artists and songs.

Whatever Spotify has planned for 2026, you can probably count on plenty of statistics, a few surprises, and at least one result that makes you question everything you thought you knew about your music taste.

Until then, there's still time to revisit your favorite albums of the year, or make peace with whatever Spotify is about to reveal.
