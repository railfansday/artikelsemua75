In a recent development, A pro is worth paying where the right answer takes judgment, such as how much of a rental’s price was land or what an inherited house was worth when its owner died. Software accepts whatever you type.

In a recent development, A rental shows why a pro can be worth the fee. Say you’re in the 22% tax bracket, you start renting out a house in January, the building alone cost $220,000, and the rent nets $10,000 after other costs. Depreciation, the yearly deduction for a building’s wear, comes to about $7,700 that first year, which cuts your tax by about $1,700. Skip it, and you lose that $1,700 unless you fix it later, yet the IRS still adds the skipped depreciation to your taxable gain when you sell.

Industry observers note that for a middle path, H&R Block’s paid tiers, from $65 plus $49 a state, include a tax pro on call, and TurboTax charges $59 to $209 plus state for its version. Or pay an online tax preparation service to do the whole return.
