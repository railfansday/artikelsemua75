Director Bassam Tariq, who was previously attached to direct the Mahershala Ali Blade movie that never came to fruition, was candid that the lack of a strong, complete script caused the Marvel Cinematic Universe movie to fall apart before it even started shooting.

According to recent reports, the production timeline of the MCU Blade movie was brutal. It was first declared at San Diego Comic-Con in 2019 and was supposed to come out in 2023. Tariq signed on as director in 2021, and pre-production seemed in full swing by the summer of 2022, but Tariq stepped away from directing that September. While director Yenn Demange was then attached to the project, the 2023 actor's strike and ongoing production problems pushed the movie back even further. Industry observers note that demange left the project in June 2024, and by 2025, the film had completely fizzled out. Ali has shared that he has moved on from wanting to play Blade, and he and Tariq subsequently collaborated on the action movie Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother. Speaking to The New Arab about the movie, Tariq was pretty transparent about why he walked away from making it. (Related: Grandma who accidentally invited a stranger to Thanksgi...)

Industry observers note that "It was funny and strange to start casting and building without a proper script. You're sitting there and explaining to actors what their role will be, and they just have to believe you," he explained. "That's also very dangerous because if the text comes and it doesn't add up to what you were saying, your credibility gets completely shot. All I have is my integrity, and I've been thinking about that a lot. If you're the bearer of bad news to your actors, but you're not the one making any of these decisions — and it's actually coming from a corporate entity — that's not a great way to be a director.".

Industry observers note that ultimately, Tariq's significant takeaways from his work on Blade were that "life's too short to make shit films" and that he wants full control over the scripts for the movies he makes going forward. That was the case for Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother, which has received rave reviews.

It's well documented that Marvel movies don't often start production with locked scripts, and major changes to the story and certain action set pieces can happen mid-production. In Blade's case, that process didn't seem to work particularly well for Tariq as a director, and the movie fell apart as a result. While it's definitely a bummer that we'll never see Tariq and Ali's take on a Blade movie, we at least got Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother out of it.
