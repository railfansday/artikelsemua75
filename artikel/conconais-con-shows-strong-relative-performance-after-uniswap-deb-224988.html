ConConAI’s $CON Shows Strong Relative Performance After Uniswap Debut, Rising More Than 600% From Its Recorded Launch-Day Low

💡 Key Takeaways

  • In a recent development, the performance follows a sold-out presale on November 4 and a public market debut two days later.
  • A lot has changed since the token’s first days on the market.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that conConAI’s $CON token is attracting market attention following its November 6, 2026 launch on Uniswap v4. In a CoinMarketCap snapshot retrieved November 26, $CON traded at approximately $0.06582, up more than 619% from its recorded launch-day low of $0.009144. The token has also moved above its planned $0.01 launch reference price, while ConConAI continues developing its AI advisor network and partner listings system.

In a recent development, the performance follows a sold-out presale on November 4 and a public market debut two days later. According to ConConAI’s launch update, $CON reached the number-one top-gainer position on CoinMarketCap during its first 24 hours of trading. With the presale completed, the token has entered a newly introduced stage of market exposure, allowing a wider audience of traders, investors, and crypto publications to assess its performance and the project behind it. A lot has changed since the token’s first days on the market. ConConAI now reports more than 1,800 holders, a completed audit of the live $CON contract, a completed first monthly burn, and expanded development capacity. The project says its next phase will focus on building the listings system, advisors, and token-payment functionality in parallel. CoinMarketCap recorded $CON’s launch-period low at approximately $0.009144 on November 6. The token subsequently reached an all-time high of approximately $0.08434 on November 24. In the November 26 snapshot, it was trading at approximately $0.06582, representing an increase of about 619.9% from its recorded low.

According to recent reports, the movement has not been a continuous upward trend. After its initial market debut, $CON experienced price fluctuations before reaching a new all-time high later in November. Such volatility can occur during the early trading period of a newly rolled out cryptocurrency, as buyers and sellers establish a market price. The token’s current distance from its latest high is therefore worth monitoring alongside its longer-term performance.

The November 26 CoinMarketCap snapshot revealed a market capitalisation of approximately $3.01 million and 24-hour trading volume of about $155,650. It also reported approximately 2,180 holders. These figures provide a more recent view of market participation than the November 12 snapshot.

Sources indicate that the token’s performance occurred during a difficult month for the broader crypto market. ConConAI says $CON’s movement has been driven by its own fundamentals, including a live product, transparent token information, and continued development. That explanation reflects the project’s position; price movement alone cannot establish what caused buying interest, and the broader market can still affect $CON.

Several developments give $CON a potentially constructive outlook as it moves beyond its initial listing period.

It has been revealed that first, the token has established market visibility through its CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko listings, its reported first-day top-gainer position, and its early trading performance. Continued visibility could bring additional market participants to the project, although listings and past gains do not guarantee future demand.

Second, ConConAI has reported that 40.15 million tokens have been burned from the original 100 million supply, leaving an effective supply of 59.85 million $CON. The first two burns removed 20 million tokens each, followed by a monthly burn of 150,000 $CON on November 19. The project states further 150,000-token burns are scheduled monthly, with the next one planned for December 15. Future burns remain scheduled events until completed and verified on-chain.

It has been revealed that third, the token has a planned utility within ConConAI’s commercial network. If the platform attracts consumers and businesses, the intended use of $CON for partner listings, placements, and performance-based fees could create a more substantial demand story than trading interest alone. The development tracker at the moment places the first partner payments in $CON in February 2027.

From the November 26 reference price of approximately $0.06582, a return to the November 24 high of $0.08434 would represent potential appreciation of around 28.1%. The combination of early market strength, reduced token supply, and an evolving utility model gives market participants several reasons to consider buying $CON.

ConConAI’s broader proposition is built around bringing back the personal purchase advisor, powered by artificial intelligence. Instead of navigating endless search results and comparing countless products, people describe what they need. A specialist AI advisor asks relevant questions, interprets the requirements, and returns one recommendation with the reason behind it.

According to recent reports, the consumer remains in control. The AI does not make purchases on the user’s behalf, and the final decision always belongs to the user.

Seven specialist advisors are live, covering categories that include investment guidance, travel, cars, film, health information, music, and real estate. The project’s aim is to make AI useful in everyday purchasing decisions by helping people move from describing a need to identifying a relevant option.

Businesses can also submit products and services to the network. ConConAI’s stated model is that payment does not buy recommendation rank: a listing is intended to appear only when it honestly matches what the user asked for, with fit taking priority. This approach is designed to connect consumers with suitable offers while giving participating businesses another way to reach potential customers.

Recent findings suggest that conConAI claims development capacity has been scaled up, with the listings system, advisors, and $CON payments now being built in parallel. Its public development tracker is updated as progress happens and identifies current work on partner listings, advisor improvements, and payment functionality. The project plans to begin its first partner payments in $CON in February 2027.

The exchange roadmap has also changed. ConConAI now says four Tier 2 centralized exchange listings are planned for December 2026, one after another. The project says each listing will be announced once confirmed. These are prepares, not confirmed listing agreements, and should not be treated as guaranteed dates or outcomes.

It has been revealed that the MEXC listing is currently on hold because of the exchange’s regulatory situation. ConConAI says it will review the situation from December 10, after which the MEXC listing may proceed or be replaced by another Tier 1 exchange. Until an exchange makes its own announcement or an agreement is confirmed, the intends remain subject to change.

The $CON presale sold out on November 4, and the token has traded on Uniswap v4’s official CON/ETH pool since November 6, 2026. Its liquidity position is reported to be locked with UNCX until November 6, 2028. The project claims pool fees at the moment help cover development and marketing costs, with partner payments expected to contribute once the listings platform opens.

ConConAI reports that the live $CON contract audit has been completed. It also cites a TokenSniffer score of 100/100, contract verification on Etherscan, and listings on CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko, DEXTools, and DexScreener. These are useful verification and discovery signals, but they do not eliminate market, smart-contract or third-party platform risks.

Industry observers note that the project’s transparency page provides live wallet balances and burn records. Its published allocation includes 30 million $CON for the community, 15 million for liquidity, 10 million for centralized-exchange liquidity, and approximately 5 million for project purposes. The project notes its allocation wallets use Safe multisig arrangements. The official contract address is 0x7ad1A8Fc42Ac150B04dED5825D21a0C90b6d28D8.

Industry observers note that $CON’s early market performance has given ConConAI a strong starting point following its Uniswap debut. Its ability to maintain interest will increasingly depend on trading participation, liquidity, product delivery, and the development of real commercial activity around its AI advisors.

If the project converts its existing development progress into wider consumer adoption and partner participation, the token’s intended utility could become an increasingly notable part of its market proposition. Combined with the reported supply reduction and community-led roadmap, these factors provide a positive basis for watching $CON’s next phase, while recognising the volatility and risks associated with newly released crypto assets.

ConConAI is developing a network of AI-powered personal purchase advisors and commercial partner listings. Its $CON token is intended to support partner activity across the ecosystem, including listings, placements, and performance-based fees.
