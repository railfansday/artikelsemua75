📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In a recent development, the 60th annual CMA Awards, which aired Wednesday (Nov. 18), surged to 7.62 million total viewers and a 1.01 rating among adults 18-49. The three-hour show was live on ABC and Disney+ from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena.

Sources indicate that hosted by Lainey Wilson for a third consecutive year, the show rose by double digits over last year in both total viewers (+26%; vs. 6.03 million) and adults 18-49 (+55%; vs. 0.65 rating), drawing its largest overall audience in seven years and its best performance in the key adult demo in three years – since 2019 and 2023, respectively.

The CMA Awards ranked as the No. 1 program on Wednesday night in both total viewers and adults 18-49 across all broadcast and cable.

Several factors probably played a part in the ratings surge:.

It has been revealed that ABC got a second dose of strong ratings news courtesy of the CMAs. On Tuesday (Nov. 17), ABC News’ annual primetime special promoting the CMA Awards, Darius, Dolly and 60 Years of Celebration: Countdown to the CMAs with Robin Roberts, earned 2.99 million total viewers, a 0.29 rating among adults 18-49 and a 0.38 rating among adults 25-54.

It was the most-watched Countdown to the CMAs special in three years and best performance in both key adult demos in six years – since 2023 and 2020, respectively.

Recent findings suggest that this was Roberts’ 16th year leading ABC’s annual CMA countdown special. Roberts provided  an inside look at the documentary she executive produced, Darius Rucker: They Called Me Hootie (hence the ‘Darius’ in the walk-up show’s title).

The 60th Annual CMA Awards was a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton served as executive producer, Alan Carter as director, and Jon Macks as head writer.

For Darius, Dolly and 60 Years of Celebration: Countdown to the CMAs with Robin Roberts, Janice Johnston was senior executive producer. David Sloan served as senior executive producer for ABC News Studios. Katie Conway and Emily Whipp were executive producers. Monica Escobedo was senior entertainment producer.
