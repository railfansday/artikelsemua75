Recent findings suggest that if you're looking for a dividend stock, Realty Income (O +0.64%) is one of the best. It's a real estate investment trust (REIT), a business structure for real estate companies that pays out 90% of income as dividends, which is why REITs are often on best-dividend-stock lists.

It has been revealed that realty Income has an excellent track record, a high yield, and plenty of reasons to be confident about its future success. If you invest $10,000 right now, here's how much you can expect to get in dividends in 2027.

Realty Income has supported its top dividend by running a massive real estate business that leases properties to large essential retailers. These are chains you know and love and likely shop at, like Walmart (WMT +1.51%), Home Depot (HD +0.70%), 7-Eleven, and Dollar General (DG -2.23%). These are stores with well-established brands and strong cash flow, making them reliable renters. They have multi-year leases, some as long as 20 years, and Realty Income's occupancy rate is a high 98.8%. It has maintained a rate between 97% and 99% since 2000.

Industry observers note that grocery and convenience stores make up almost 20% of the portfolio, providing a resilient revenue stream, but the company has also branched out into other areas as it expands and diversifies. The U.K. is one of its largest markets today, accounting for more than 15% of total properties, and Wynn Resorts (WYNN -2.07%) has become one of its biggest customers. Industrials make up a healthy 17% of the portfolio, and Realty Income is also getting started in data centers, which make up 1%. (Related: Industry News: Designing Azure silicon on Azure silicon...)

It has been revealed that the manufacturer has more than 15,500 properties in 96 industries, and it's always buying newly introduced properties to keep growing. It has a $5.5 billion revolving credit facility, and it's raised more than $2 billion so far this year from various sources. It has a large pipeline of quality properties to choose from, and expanding beyond retail offers it more opportunities.

Sources indicate that realty Income has paid a dividend monthly for the past 677 months, or more than 56 years. It's one of the few dividend stocks that cuts you a check every month.

It also raises its dividend quarterly rather than annually, which is unusual among great dividend stocks. It has raised the dividend for the past 117 quarters, or more than 29 years.

At the current price, the dividend yields 5.6%, an excellent yield for a reliable dividend stock.

Realty Income is a dividend play, and the stock hasn't changed much over the past 10 years. However, now has been a particularly challenging time for the company as interest and mortgage rates remain high.

Since its business model involves borrowing lots of money, higher interest rates mean it has to pay more to service its debt.

While shares are down 7% over the last six months, it doesn’t appear to be in any danger, and it’s flourished across a range of environments for more than 50 years, paying its dividend without missing a monthly payment.

As of its latest raise last week, Realty Income pays $3.288 in annualized dividends, up from $3.258. That's a small change of only 0.9%. If it continues like that for the next four quarters, by the end of 2027, it should be $3.408 in annualized dividends. The average of the two is $3.348, and divided by 12, that's an average monthly payment of $0.279.

If you invest $10,000 today at a share price of $57.66 (at the time of writing), that gets you 173 shares, and next year, you'd get about $48.27 every month.

Industry observers note that that may not seem like much, but it's reliable and will continue to rise. It also highlights the need to save for retirement to ensure a livable monthly income.
