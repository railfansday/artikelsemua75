The best-selling author of Red, White and Royal Blue has revealed their next book, and it’s far from what we’ve come to expect.

Casey McQuiston announced Friday that their long-awaited next book will be released in October 2027, and we already have the title and a synopsis. However, this next work will mark a major genre departure for the romance author with a cult following.

McQuiston, best known for clever, queer new adult and YA rom-coms like Red, White and Royal Blue, One Last Stop, and I Kissed Sara Wheeler, is officially making their foray into another beloved genre: romantasy.

According to recent reports, mcQuiston’s next novel, titled The Knight and the Bookseller, is a classic portal fantasy with a queer, romantic twist. According to the author’s brief synopsis, it follows Molly Darling, a bookseller who “stumbles out of her quiet life into the world of her beloved childhood fantasy series—and the arms of her favorite character, the heroic (and haunted) paladin Bronwyn em’Cadogan.”.

In a short video shared on Instagram Friday morning, McQuiston can be seen in medieval cosplay, wandering Latest York with a poster bearing the book’s title and release date. (Related: British Airways Avios — Short Nonstop Domestic Flights...)

The official cover is still under wraps, but McQuiston did share one more clue for fans: a list of tropes found in the novel, including “stories about stories,” “slow burn + high heat,” and “sword lesbians,” among others.

Though McQuiston has experimented with magical realist elements in past novels like One Last Stop (2021), about a woman who falls for a time-traveling subway rider, The Knight and the Bookseller will be their first full entry into the romantasy space. Before sharing the title and synopsis on social media, McQuiston teased this genre switch-up to fans, posting a mood board to Instagram featuring images of Joan of Arc, medieval knights, and several 17th-century paintings.

Sources indicate that the clues didn’t stop there. After sharing the book’s title, McQuiston shared a set of clues about the book’s main character, Molly, including “fantasy novels,” “unused Earth Sciences degree,” “sourdough starter,” “mushroom foraging knife,” “childhood trauma,” “delayed lesbian awakening,” and “cat.”.

The clues also came with a segment of McQuiston’s character-inspired playlist, which included the songs “I Want It All” by Katie Gavin, “Ankles” by Lucy Dacus, and “Ready” by CMAT, among others.

The Knight and the Bookseller isn’t the only McQuiston release on the horizon. A companion novel to Red, White and Royal Blue, titled Red, White and Royal Blue: The Private Correspondence, releases December 1.

Described as an “epistolary novella,” The Private Correspondence is written in the form of love letters, postcards, and journal entries shared between the two main characters in Red, White and Royal Blue: first son Alex Claremont-Diaz and British Prince Henry Fox.

The novella is part companion, part sequel to the first book—the letters begin during the events of Red, White and Royal Blue and span the next 20 years of Alex and Henry’s relationship, revealing their future after the first book’s ending. Meanwhile, a sequel to the 2023 film adaptation of Red, White and Royal Blue has already wrapped filming. Titled Red, White and Royal Wedding, the sequel film is rumored to release on Prime Video before the end of the year.
