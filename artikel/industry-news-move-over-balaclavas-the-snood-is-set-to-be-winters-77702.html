It was just a few years ago that the balaclava was everywhere. Fashion people referred to it as the winter headpiece you absolutely needed. But now, I’ve noticed a latest star, and that’s the snood, alongside the hooded scarf. Most people over the years have blended these three headpieces, but I’m here to inform the masses: you’re probably wearing a snood or hooded scarf now, not a balaclava.

Balaclavas are a full-coverage piece of headgear that wraps around the entire head, neck, and face (leaving only the eyes, nose, or mouth exposed), whereas a snood is a continuous circular loop or tube of fabric designed primarily to insulate the neck and cover the top of the head. It has been revealed that balaclavas tend to be closer to the face, similar in fit to a ski mask, while snoods are loose-fitting and have a sort of cowl design to them. According to the winter sports community, balaclavas are better for skiing and extreme cold, but for most of us, we’re just looking to warm up a little with our winter accessories.
