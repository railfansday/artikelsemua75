In a recent development, star Trek has been through so many wonderful eras over the years, all of which bring something a little different to the proverbial party. Whether you're a fan of the darker, more grounded tone of Deep Space Nine, or the iconic crew of a show like Next Generation, there really is a little something out there for everyone, and that's arguably the franchise's greatest strength.

For me, Enterprise is arguably the most underrated of the bunch, capturing the spirit of the Original Series, while the prequel setting gave us a look at humanity's earliest forays into the stars. If you were also a fan at the time, one of Star Trek's best ongoing adaptations is set to bring in elements from the show, further immortalizing its legacy in the process. Recent findings suggest that if you're yet to play it, Star Trek Online really is a shining example of the franchise as a whole, giving fans of all types the opportunity to engage with the universe in an interactive way. A massive part of its success has been the game's willingness to draw on all the different iterations of the show we've had to date, catering to as broad an audience as possible in the process. According to recent reports, now, the team behind the game has confirmed their roadmap for the next few months, and that trend is showing absolutely no signs of slowing down. For Enterprise specifically, the Terran NX Starship is being added as a usable option for PC players in December, before it heads over to consoles for January.

In a recent development, fans of other highlights have plenty to come besides, including the Dominion War Starfleet Starship Bundle from Deep Space Nine, and the Vau N'akat Starship from Star Trek: Prodigy. Arguably the coolest addition of the bunch, though, is the Tarellian Plague Ship from The Next Generation, which is set to debut into the game in December on both PC and consoles.

Those are all set to join the Romulon Narada Starship from Star Trek (2009) and the Vidiian Starship from Voyager, both of which are now in the game after debuting over the last couple of months.

Recent findings suggest that heading into the newly introduced year, there is also set to be a lot going on in the game besides the addition of new ships to the roster. Season 38 begins in February, celebrating 17 years since the launch of the game with a bunch of new content, missions, encounters and events for players to get involved with.

Sources indicate that outside of that, things are starting to look up for Star Trek as a whole. Though the final two ongoing reveals tied to the IP will be drawing to a close next year, the upcoming film from John Francis Daley and Jonathan Goldstein is set to leave JJ Abrams' Kelvin Timeline behind with a whole cast of new characters. Though we don't know a lot about that just yet, it could be exactly what's needed to give the franchise a renewed impetus heading into the 2030s.

According to recent reports, star Trek is one of pop culture's biggest multimedia franchises, spanning multiple movies, TV shows, books, comics, video games, and various other media. The franchise was created by Gene Roddenberry and started with the 1960s TV series starring William Shatner and Leonard Nimoy. Over the decades, several equally popular series have come out since as Star Trek: The Next Generation and Star Trek: Discovery.
