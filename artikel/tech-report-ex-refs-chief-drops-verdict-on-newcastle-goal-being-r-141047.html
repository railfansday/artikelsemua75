Martin Odegaard may feel aggrieved, but giving Arsenal a foul to disallow Newcastle United's first goal would have been very harsh.

That is according to former PGMOL chief and ex-FIFA referee, Keith Hackett, who exclusively told Football Insider that Chris Kavanagh should have blown immediately if he was going to award the free-kick.

The Magpies claimed a point in a thrilling 2-2 draw against Mikel Arteta's side at St James' Park on Saturday, 21 November.

Eberechi Eze took the lead, but after efforts from Harvey Barnes and Malick Thiaw, Noni Madueke's goal was needed for the Gunners to avoid defeat.

There was a huge debate over whether Newcastle's equaliser should have stood to begin with, as Joe Willock left Odegaard in a heap on the floor.

Play was allowed to continue, with Barnes scoring as a direct result of the Arsenal being dispossessed, but it was never called back for a foul.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Hackett has disagreed with Arsenal fans asking for a foul, more than happy to go with the referee's decision.

"If you're going to give the free-kick, you're going to have to blow immediately before the ball's hit the back of the net," he mentioned.

"It's hit the back of the net too late. You're not going to give it. That refereeing. And therefore, in that situation, let's go with the referee's call again.

It has been revealed that "Support the referee. It's easy to give these free kicks. Odegaard could feel slightly aggrieved, but I've seen much worse not penalised either.
