Huang resigned Nov. 12 following an adultery scandal, triggering a sharp sell-off in the stock and stoking worries about the AI trade. There were also national security concerns related to advanced chips ending up in China, bypassing export controls.

This statement from Nvidia today appeared to suppress those concerns: "In the process of reaching its decision, the Board received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk.".
