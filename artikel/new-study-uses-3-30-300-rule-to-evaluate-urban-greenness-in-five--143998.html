New study uses 3-30-300 rule to evaluate urban greenness in five major Canadian cities, finds room for improvement

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (68 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that when you look out your window, can you see at least three large trees? Is your neighborhood well-shaded by a canopy of trees? And can you walk to a park or other high-quality green space in less than five minutes? These three questions lie at the heart of the 3-30-300 rule, a benchmark that's increasingly used to evaluate urban greenness.
