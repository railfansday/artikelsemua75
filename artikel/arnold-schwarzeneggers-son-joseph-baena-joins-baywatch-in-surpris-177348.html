Joseph Baena is joining Fox’s Baywatch revival in a surprise role.

It has been revealed that “I’m Livvy Dunne’s boyfriend in the show,” Baena, 29, exclusively told Us on the set of Celebrity Weakest Link. “And she was amazing — such a great talent and she was such a talented and special gymnast. To see her in Baywatch was great.”.

Industry observers note that “She killed it. As did the rest of the cast,” he added. “Thaddeus [LaGrone], Noah Beck and everyone else is doing a phenomenal job. It was amazing to spend time with them on set and work out with them on set. I’m just really excited for the episode to come out of the whole show.”. According to recent reports, while Arnold Schwarzenegger‘s son had a small role as Pete in season 1, he hinted he would love to see “a little more” of the character in a potential second season, adding, “We have to make the plot line [happen]. I get that — in the first season — they need to establish everyone. Maybe in the second season they can have a little fun and bring Pete back.”.

The original Baywatch ran from 1989 to 2001 and showcased the lives of lifeguards who dealt with unusual circumstances — from shark attacks to serial killers — on beaches in California and Hawaii.

According to recent reports, “I was too young to watch it live when it came out [on] TV, but I have such a connection to David Hasselhoff when he was in the SpongeBob movie. It’s one of my favorite movies of all time and such a big part of my childhood,” Baena recalled. “Especially when David Hasselhoff comes out in the show and is the speedboat to take SpongeBob back to Bikini Bottom. It’s awesome.”.

Recent findings suggest that he continued: “So the fact that they brought the show back and are reviving it, it was such a cool announcement. I got so excited. I wanted to audition. I unfortunately didn’t even get a chance to audition until they had already started the show. I’m so grateful that I was able to make an appearance in one of the episodes.”.

Recent findings suggest that the guest appearance made Baena want to do more scripted projects. For now, TV viewers can catch Baena on the Monday, November 23, episode of Fox’s Celebrity Weakness Link.

“Non-scripted reality TV has been super fun. I think I have a good personality for it,” he told Us. “Celeb Weakest Link is [an] amazing show. And I did Dancing With the Stars — that was phenomenal. It was such an amazing time.”.

Baena noted that the “main vision” for his career is to be a leading man.

“To be in scripted movies, film and TV. I’ve been doing theater for a little while. I’ve been doing acting classes for a long time and just working my way up,” said the actor, who has also been working on a cookbook inspired by what he learned from mom Mildred Baena. “I’m paying my dues and I know how it goes in the industry. It’s difficult, and you never know when your time is, but I think my time is coming soon.”.
