Mauricio Pochettino has suffered an absolute nightmare start in his first game as Manchester United manager against Liverpool.

In many ways, it’s a useful wake-up call for Pochettino in what is bound to be a hugely challenging job for him at a Man Utd side that has been going downhill rapidly in the last few years.
