Plenty of beauty lovers can appreciate an effective slick-back style—myself included—but supermodel Gigi Hadid is making the case for undone, playful hair that doesn’t take itself too seriously.

Sources indicate that in her latest “High School Curls” look, created by the star’s stylist Dimitris Giannetos for a recent photo shoot (perhaps an upcoming Guest in Residence drop?), Hadid’s hair is as lived-in as it is carefree, featuring loose, beachy waves and a relaxed center part with plenty of body and texture. The airy style lets the tonal dimensions of her blonde and bronde coloring shine through.

Sources indicate that “High school curls are the hair you don’t put too much thinking behind it,” Giannetos writes in an Instagram post about the style. “You know what you like, you know how to style your hair but not put too much effort to it! It’s the High School Curls!”.

The easy beauty look is touchable and voluminous, almost next of kin to the staple bombshell glamour styles seen on the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show catwalk, where Hadid is a longtime resident. Along with incorporating piecey, mid-back waves throughout her hair for a slightly grungier look, the stylist tousled Hadid’s roots at the crown to maximize overall fullness.
