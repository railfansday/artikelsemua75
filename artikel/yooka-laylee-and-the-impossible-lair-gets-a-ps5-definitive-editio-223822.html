Playtonic has announced Yooka-Laylee and the Impossible Lair is being treated to a upcoming Definitive Edition on PS5, and it's right around the corner.

The upcoming version releases on 15th December 2026, and anyone who owns a digital PS4 version of the game gets a free upgrade. It appears anyone with a physical copy won't get the upgrade.

Industry observers note that on base PS5 and PS5 Pro, the latest edition will support a 4K resolution at 120fps, better lighting and ambient occlusion effects, higher-fidelity shadows, and two orchestrated soundtracks. Also, if you don't already own it, the Trowzer's Top Tonic Pack is automatically bundled in with the PS5 version. (Related: Industry News: Uber Eats driver saves elderly woman aft...)

Sources indicate that one of the best platforming adventures of its generation is back, enhanced for PlayStation 5. Yooka & Laylee return for a full-scale 2.5D adventure, with a 3D Overworld that's an adventure in its own right, levels you can alter to create latest ways to play, and Capital B's Impossible Lair waiting to test your skills. (Related: 3D Asset Generation: AI for Game Development #3...)

Sources indicate that A 2D and top-down side game that accompanies the main Yooka-Laylee game, the Impossible Lair went down really well upon its original launch. It's got an 82 rating on Metacritic, and in our own review, we called it an "improvement" on the original adventure.
