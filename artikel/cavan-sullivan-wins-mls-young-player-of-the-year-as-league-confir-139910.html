In a recent development, with the MLS Cup playoffs now in full swing, it also means that we are beginning to see who is taking home honors at the end of the season. Nashville SC have already seen Maxwell Woledzi win Defender of the Year, and they have potential honorees up and down the awards docket alongside the youngest MVP finalist in league history, Cavan Sullivan, who has taken home young player of the year honors. Antoine Griezmann has also won Newcomer of the Year in only half of a season with Orlando City SC.

In a recent development, becoming the youngest MVP finalist in league history at only 17, it should come as no surprise that Cavan Sullivan has won Young Player of the Year, beating out the Red Bull New York duo of Julian Hall and Adri Mehmeti. During the World Cup break, Sullivan spent time training with the Union's MLS Next Pro side instead of taking the time off for himself, and the results showed in the second half of the season as he helped lead the Union into a playoff spot. With nine goals and 14 assists (including secondary assists), no teenager has ever been involved in more goals in a season than Sullivan, and it has also led to his first cap with the United States men's national team, where he was able to grab his first goal against Mexico. If this is the tip of the iceberg, we're in for something special, considering that this has been a banner year for young players in MLS, with more minutes being given to teenagers than ever before. With Guilherme receiving MVP votes to become a finalist for the award, it was going to take a special performance for someone else to win Newcomer of the Year, and joining Orlando City SC, Antoine Griezmann did just that. Prior to his arrival, this was a team floundering below the playoff line after moving on from manager Oscar Pareja. In only a half-season, he came in and scored 14 goals and assisted eight more as the first player ever to produce 20 goal contributions in only half a season, while turning this team into contenders. It's going to be tough in the playoffs for Griezmann to overcome the defense, but considering what he has already done with the squad, there's no reason why Orlando should be counted out with the Frenchman next to Justin Ellis.

It has been revealed that en route to setting a points record with 77 points to win the Supporters' Shield, Nashville SC were also led by a record-setting defense that only let the ball into the back of the net 25 times in an entire campaign. That success was rewarded with all three finalists for Defender of the Year coming from Nashville, but it's their newest signing that took home the award. Joining from Norwegian club Fredrikstad ahead of the season, Woledzi didn't need much time to adjust, appearing in every regular-season match and helping Nashville build from the back to maintain possession. It was a team effort to tie the MLS clean sheet record with 17 during the season, so it's only natural for one of their defenders to get the honors this season.
