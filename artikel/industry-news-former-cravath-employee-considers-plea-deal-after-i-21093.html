A former Cravath, Swaine & Moore employee who was arrested on charges of insider trading last week is reportedly considering a plea deal.

Federal prosecutors in Fresh York allege that Chetram Balli, who is also known as Tony Balli, 54, accessed nonpublic information through his role as a document processing specialist for Cravath and traded ahead of at least five of the law firm’s major deals.

According to Law360, a one-page form order signed Thursday by U.S. Magistrate Judge Marcia M. Henry of the Eastern District of New York reveals that federal prosecutors and Balli are engaged in plea discussions. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

Law360 requested but did not receive comment from either party.

In a recent development, balli began working for Cravath in 1989. According to prosecutors, he supported attorneys by producing legal documents and U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filings and at times had access to confidential information.

Balli was arrested Thursday after prosecutors filed a criminal complaint against him and alleged that he made more than $70,000 in illicit profits.
