The Challenge star Cory Wharton is offering a candid revision on his mental health amid a series of challenges in his personal life.

Industry observers note that “It’s okay to NOT BE OKAY,” Wharton, 35, wrote to fans via Instagram on Thursday, November 19. “These past couple weeks have been some of the hardest of my life. Just taking it one day at a time.”. (Related: Industry News: Politics Takes A Toll On Your Health — W...)

Industry observers note that in October, Wharton and his longtime girlfriend, Ex on the Beach alum Taylor Selfridge, officially went their separate ways after nine years together. Cory previously apologized after cameras caught him flirting with Izzy Fairthorne on The Challenge: Cutthroat. (Cory and Taylor, 32, share daughters Mila, 6, and Maya, 4, while he also welcomed 8-year-old daughter Ryder with ex Cheyenne Floyd.). (Related: Tech Report: What Couples Married For 40 Years Do When ...)

“A new chapter. Just me and my girls. ❤️ Taylor and I have decided to go our separate ways, as hard as that is to say publicly, I think it’s crucial to be honest about where my life is at this moment,” Wharton announced via Instagram on October 8.

In a recent development, he continued, “We shared a lot of years, memories, love and most importantly, we have two beautiful daughters ❤️. I’m sorry, Taylor, that things didn’t work out the way we both hoped they would. I know there were times I could’ve been better, and I’m sorry for the ways I let you down.”.

Recent findings suggest that two weeks later, Wharton confirmed that his and Selfridge’s daughter Maya recently underwent her second open-heart surgery to address tricuspid atresia, a rare congenital defect that blocks blood from flowing directly between the lungs and heart. (Maya had her first heart procedure done when she was only 7 months old in January 2023 and later had her first open-heart surgery in October 2025.).

“I’ve shared Maya’s journey with you all from the beginning. The good days, the scary days & everything in between,” he wrote in an October update for his followers. “Life is going to throw things at you that you can’t control. There will be battles you never asked for and days when you don’t know how you’re going to get through them. But you keep showing up. You keep believing. And you keep fighting.”.

Having dealt with so much upheaval in the last few months, Wharton shared that he’s been struggling with his mental health.

Recent findings suggest that “Change has been happening and I’ve just been really, really sad,” he noted in Thursday’s post. “Life has not been good … This next chapter of my life is all about change.”.

Wharton thanked his fans for their support, acknowledging that he’s “had a lot going on” in recent months.

“My little ones are all safe. They’re all OK,” he stressed. “Life has just been throwing a lot of curveballs at me and it’s hard to juggle it all. You don’t always juggle them all. It’s hard. This life thing is not easy and I’m just here to remind you that it’s OK to not have a good month or a couple of weeks. It’s OK.”.
