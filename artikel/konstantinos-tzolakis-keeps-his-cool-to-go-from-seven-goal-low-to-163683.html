Even the finest goalkeepers have bad days and sometimes they are not even their fault.

Despite being one of the best stoppers in this season's Premier League, the £20m signing from Olympiacos has been questioned over a couple of the goals he has conceded in the early stages of his Hull career, such is the life of a goalkeeper.

Apart from the largely irrelevant sixth, Tzolakis was fairly blameless as Greece lost 7-0 in the Netherlands during the last international break.

Even so, he was taken out of the firing line for the final Nations League group game, where Greece needed a goal difference miracle to qualify for the knockout stages.

But in his next game, for Hull at home to in-form Brighton and Hove Albion, he was outstanding, making a string of notable saves in the first half without which, two-goal Oli McBurnie would never have been able to secure their first win since August in stoppage-time.

It spoke wonders for the character of the 24-year-old, who has the highest save percentage of any goalkeeper in this season's top flight.

"If you don't keep content and forget it, it's more difficult to survive in this Premier League," reflected Tzolakis after the 2-0 win.

Recent findings suggest that "It was a bad day for our team (in Amsterdam). We didn't play well and it's easy after one, two, three goals to concede more goals. Three days later the Netherlands conceded five goals against Germany.

"But the most important thing is to forget this and to continue.".

Jakirovic has been there and got the T-shirt having lost his only Champions League game so far as a coach – and his last match for Dinamo Zagreb, as it quickly turned out. So he knows how to deal with disappointment.

"We spoke about it just through jokes, because I know how it is," said the 49-year-old. "I lost 9-2 one time against Bayern Munich, it was like we played on a PlayStation.

"You can't do anything (about it), like England-Croatia 7-0, you can't do anything, you can put on substitutions, fresh blood, newly introduced energy, no way (will it change things). You lost the game and that's it. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

"You just have to keep going, go forward and ciao. You cannot change anything, it's the past.".

And Hull's win was massive for their confidence.

They might have gone into the game as the highest-ranked of this season's newly-promoted sides, and they might have kept above the psychologically-crucial point-per-game mark, but their last win was starting to feel a long way away, at Coventry City on the second weekend of the season.

"The feeling is great for us," stated Tzolakis. "After Coventry, we also had some good games but we didn't take three points.

"It was a very good performance for our team. We suffered in the first half, but we achieved two goals in the last minutes, so we took the three points.

"I enjoyed it a lot because I helped my team to achieve the target for the day.

"tI's very good when you have a very good performance, to have someone to score a goal and to take the three points.

"It was a huge victory against a very good team like Brighton, but we have to continue because we have a lot of games and we have to survive in the league.

Sources indicate that "It's my job to keep my team in the game. We suffered a lot in the first half, but I think in the second half we managed the game better.

"We had some chances before the red card (for Brighton’s Joao Costinha), but after the red card it was easier for us to score.

"Every single point is too important for the Premier League, but if we score a goal and we take three points, it's much better than one point.".

And if the win was a boost to Tzolakis' confidence, the same was true of McBurnie, who scored a penalty and a free-kick to break his duck for the season at the end of October, before adding two six-yard box finishes against the Seagulls.

It has been revealed that "He's doing a lot," mentioned Jakirovic. "On Sunday he played against (Luka) Vuskovic, who is a Croatian star, excellent young player, (born in) 2007.

"Oli was good, the fight was there, excellent, and I'm very happy for him because finally he scored from open play.

Recent findings suggest that "I don't have any doubt about Oli McBurnie because he's a natural leader. He doesn't need (the armband), he's leading his team together with (Regan) Slater, with Coyley (Lewie Coyle), with Crooksy (Matt Crooks). They are very vital.

"John Egan is also very important for us.".
