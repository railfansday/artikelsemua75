📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

As stocks continue their tech-driven sell-off, prediction markets traders are seeing a bleaker outlook for the remainder of the year.

It has been revealed that on platform Kalshi, speculators place just 6% odds that the S&P 500 will cross 8,000 in 2026. On Oct. 13, those odds were as high as 72%.

According to recent reports, on Friday, the Nasdaq Composite turned negative for the year as investors continued to dump tech stocks, primarily those with exposure to the artificial intelligence build-out. The Dow Jones Industrial Average and the S&P 500 fell into correction territory, too.

The S&P 500 has slashed its year-to-date gain to just over 2%.

The Stock Trader's Almanac in a Thursday note broadly agreed that the hopes for a December rally are fading, with the caveat that a "Santa rally" could still appear at the end of the year.

"The market is under pressure and more downside can't be ruled out," the note said.

It has been revealed that the Stock Trader's Almanac sees 2026 following the year-end pattern of 2018, when the Federal Reserve was in a tightening cycle in a midterm year. The Fed is widely expected to hike rates at its December meeting.

In that scenario, the Stock Trader's Almanac thinks more churn is to come. "The low is more likely in mid- to late December than this week.".
