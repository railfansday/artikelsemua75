Jose Mourinho did not expect Celta Vigo to pose quite as much of a challenge to Real Madrid as they did, but was delighted with how his team pushed themselves to the maximum.

Madrid fell behind to Pablo Duran's sixth-minute goal, but second-half efforts from Jude Bellingham and Carlos Espi got Los Blancos over the line, while Kylian Mbappe missed a penalty. (Related: Breaking: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons in a...)

But he was delighted with how they kept pushing themselves to get the result.

Industry observers note that "I thought it was going to be a tough match, but not this tough. I didn't expect to win it the way we did, but we were up against a good team that played really well," he remarked.

Recent findings suggest that "Some of us arrived on Monday, others on Tuesday, others on Thursday. Some were on a plane for 12 hours. Normally, after international duty, the ideal is to play on Sunday, but we couldn't because we play [in the Champions League] next Tuesday.

It has been revealed that "I saw some players who were physically exhausted, but I also saw some who pushed themselves to the limit. That's the strength of the group, of a group of friends who want to win.".

Espi stepped off the bench to become Madrid's matchwinner.

He is yet to start a LaLiga match for Madrid, but he has scored three goals in 44 minutes played in LaLiga 2026-27, a ratio of one goal every 15 minutes, the best in the top five European leagues among players with at least three goals scored.

However, Mourinho says the 21-year-old is satisfied with a super-sub role for now.

"There are players with specific characteristics," he mentioned.

Industry observers note that "I don't want to say he can't be a starter, but Carlos is a penalty-box striker, not so much a link-up player. He's a guy who has quality in the box, who attacks the near post and the far post well, who plays with his back to goal.

"The history of football is full of decisive players like him who didn't play much. He's the kind of guy who doesn't get caught up in stories about needing to play more; he's happy to be at Real Madrid and has the respect of his coach, his team-mates, and the fans.

"He's happy, and I hope to be able to give him starting or regular starting positions.".

Vinicius Junior also had to settle for a cameo from the bench, but Mourinho headed off any questions over the Brazilian's status.

"So you don't ask me on Monday, I'll tell you now: he's going to start on Tuesday [against PSV]," Mourinho mentioned of Vinicius.
