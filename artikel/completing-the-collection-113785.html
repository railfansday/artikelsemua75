Completing my double hike of the AT isn’t the only commitment I am devoted to keeping. I check the box on another one despite a chilly morning.

Around midnight it hit me. I was camping practically at the northbound 1,200 mile marker. I hadn’t seen a marker yesterday, so it fell to me to whip one up on my way out of camp so I could my collection. This wasn’t ideal, it was barely above freezing. Every part of me wanted to stay in my tent as long as possible. When it did come time to hit the trail, I would want to get moving and stay moving, preferably all the way back to my van. My silly rituals will probably be my undoing some day.

I remainder tucked under my quilt, even going so far as to eat my breakfast inside its cozy confines. I am continually impressed how well the down insulates and warms me. I feel like I’d be warm in 20 degree weather, maybe even lower. I don’t intend to ever camp in such weather, but to know it’s possible is exactly what I had in mind when I purchased this particular quilt. It kept my warm until 7:45AM when I initiated my pack up procedure, completing the various tasks by 8:00AM, the cold having motivated me to move fast.

In a recent development, I checked in at the Showers Steps viewpoint since it was just a handful of steps away. It sure looked like I’d made the right choice to prioritize sunset the previous night, there wouldn’t have been much to see at sunrise given the heavy cloud cover to start the day. I located the 1,200 mile point on the trail to the tenth of a mile and rapidly pieced together a small marker. It wouldn’t last long, but I just needed a photo for my collection so I can sleep at night. I am planning to post them all in a blog post, along with other notable milestones like state lines after my hike. (Related: LA Noire 2 leak resurfaces after source gets GTA 6 endi...)

Sources indicate that my thin gloves were doing little to warm my fingers, but other than that I was comfortable even with the temperature sitting in the mid 30s. South of Showers Steps the trail briefly winds amongst some mountain laurels, which, unlike rocks, are in fact the state flower of Pennsylvania. A few miles later I crossed route 501 and approached the Kimmel Lookout. I spent a moment or two setting up a picture or two before whipping around and continuing down the trail. I didn’t realize it until it came time to put together today’s blog, but one of the pictures turned out kind of epic. The early morning sun radiates through clouds above speckling its light across the rocky ledge and trees. Colorful late fall trees poke up from the ground below the ledge with large meadows and farms visible further in the distance. (Related: Industry News: Animal control called to rescue alligato...)

It has been revealed that I followed the same ridge for the next 10 miles to Swatara Gap. Although it was a Saturday, it had been relatively quite on trail. I only passed a trio of hunters on the trail before encountering a lively group of women at a viewpoint along the trail. One of them had just had an interesting experience a short distance down the trail. She had stepped off the trail for a restroom break only to find out after the fact that a hunter had been just overhead on a tree stand the entire time. She didn’t seem nearly as put off by the experience as I would have been. The discussion moved to my double AT attempt. I wish I could have gotten a picture of their reaction upon hearing what I was up to. They were even more impressed that I was essentially in the final week of the task. I forgot to tell them that FarOut commenters frequently mentioned a family of 5 or so copperheads hanging out on the rocks we were all standing on, though I believe they aren’t keen on cold temperatures and were probably in hiding.

Recent findings suggest that soon I was making my way back down to Swatara Gap and under Interstate 81. I made it the final mile back to my van before 2:00PM. After cleaning up and trimming my increasingly beardy face, I headed to Lebanon, PA to complete yesterday and today’s blog posts as has become my usual routine. There is no Formula One tomorrow so I am not sure what to do with myself, I haven’t have a Sunday morning free from blog writing or sports watching in a while. I am sure I’ll think of something.
