Meta’s WhatsApp is making a huge change to chat backups, with a newly introduced built-in cloud backup option on Android that doesn’t rely on Google, and is free.

It has been revealed that spotted by WABetaInfo, WhatsApp for Android is adding its own cloud backup option. Prior to this, the app relied on Google Drive backup for transferring chats between devices or keeping a backup in case of accidental deletion. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Sources indicate that “WhatsApp cloud storage” is showing up in the WhatsApp for Android v2.26.46.4 beta patch, rolling out now, and includes 2GB of free storage for cloud backups. Everything is still end-to-end encrypted. Beyond the first 2GB, WhatsApp Plus ($2.99/month) adds 100GB, and Meta One ($7.99/month+) offers up to 4TB of cloud storage. WhatsApp will keep your backup for at least 4 months after the subscription ends, even if you are over the 2GB gap.

You can still choose to use Google Drive backups instead of the built-in option, but those using devices without a Google account or who are running low on Google storage may appreciate the option to switch. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

It’s unclear exactly when this will be available to all WhatsApp customers.
