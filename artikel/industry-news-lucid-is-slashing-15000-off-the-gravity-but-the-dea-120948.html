Industry News: Lucid Is Slashing $15,000 Off The Gravity, But The Deal Won’t Last Long

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Lucid Motors is holding its own Black Friday, and the American EV startup has some pretty sweet deals on the table. For a limited time, customers can purchase a 2027 Lucid Gravity SUV with a $15,000 discount, while buyers who prefer an electric sedan can get a 2027 Lucid Air for $10,000 less.

The incentives effectively lower the starting price of the Gravity family hauler to $66,750, down from the usual $81,750, while the Air sedan’s MSRP goes down to $66,400, making the two luxury EVs more appealing than usual.

Industry observers note that all this being said, there is some small print that needs to be considered, with the most vital limitation being time. The Black Friday Credit, as Lucid calls it, is only valid for cars ordered and delivered by November 30, which completely eliminates build-to-order cars from the equation, given how Lucid quotes at least eight weeks for a custom Air or Gravity build. (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

Recent findings suggest that in other words, the only EVs eligible for the hefty discounts are those in inventory. Thankfully, there are plenty of those, with several Air Touring and Gravity Touring models available now and ready for the company’s generous incentive.

Besides having to take delivery of a latest car by the end of the month, there are two other things to consider: the vehicles need to be bought with cash or through third-party financing, and the Black Friday Credit cannot be combined with other offers, incentives, or discounts.

That’s an key distinction, because Lucid has a few other offers on the table, including a $3,500 discount for people who want to lease a new Air or Gravity EV, a $3,000 loyalty offer for customers who already own a Lucid, and a $1,000 Demo Drive Credit for people who order a new Lucid after test driving one.

When it comes to leasing, the 2027 Gravity Touring can be leased for $749 per month for 36 months with $6,249 due at signing, while the 2027 Air Touring can be leased for $859 per month for 36 months with $7,359 due at signing.

In a recent development, the base Gravity Touring has an EPA-estimated range of 337 miles and is powered by two electric motors that make a combined 560 horsepower. The Air sedan’s entry-level trim is called Pure, but none of the available vehicles in Lucid’s inventory are spec’d like that. Instead, the firm has several Air Touring models in stock, which start at $81,400. Powered by two electric motors with a combined 620 hp, the Air Touring is good for 431 miles on a full charge and can go from zero to 60 miles per hour in 3.4 seconds.
