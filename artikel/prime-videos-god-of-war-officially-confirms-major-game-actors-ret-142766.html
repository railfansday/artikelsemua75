Dave Bautista will go face-to-face with an iconic game actor for God of War.

According to recent reports, as filming gears up to resume in the near future, Prime Video has confirmed that the God of War series has officially added Deborah Ann Woll to its cast. The star will be reprising her role of Faye from the recent games, making her the first major game actor to take part in the series after Ryan Hurst was injured and had to depart his role of Kratos.

Adapted from the PlayStation video game franchise, God of War is an action-adventure drama series in development under Amazon.
