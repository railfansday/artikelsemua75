96% pass completion, four chances created: Returning QPR man’s importance shown in 1-0 win over Millwall

QPR continued their strong campaign to date with a triumph over last year’s Championship play-off semi-finalists Millwall. Just one goal was enough to get the job done, as the Hoops claimed all three points.

Industry observers note that zak Sturge was sent off for the Lions early on, and the R’s made their man advantage count. There were solid performances across the board with Ilias Chair impressing in attack, and the displays of defensive trio Ronnie Edwards, Jake Clarke-Salter and Kealey Adamson gave Julien Stephan plenty to smile about. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

According to recent reports, however, on the day, it was midfielder Nicolas Madsen who stood out. On his return to the starting lineup after a spell out through injury, the Dane was almost faultless in possession and scored the decisive goal for QPR. (Related: Taco Bell is bringing back the Steakhouse Boss Burrito ...)

Madsen’s goal saw him pounce on a saved shot to side-foot goalwards, and after Mark Sykes’ attempt to clear, referee Josh Smith gave the signal that the ball had indeed crossed the line.

It has been revealed that the QPR star was central to Sturge’s red card too. Madsen took one touch before curling a fantastic pass in behind to Paul Smyth, who was bundled over by the Millwall defender.

The numbers behind Madsen’s display are just as eye-catching. He completed 88 of his 92 passes, equating to a success rate of 96%, with 13 of these balls being into the final third and four of them created chances for his Hoops teammates.

Recent findings suggest that no one saw more of the ball than Madsen, who had 102 touches across his 74-minute outing. Millwall’s red card meant they couldn’t threaten much and only recorded 31% possession, but Madsen was on hand to help out defensively when needed with one interception, one clearance, and a 66% duel win rate (all stats via FotMob).

Madsen had not played in over a month due to injury. However, having recorded two goals and an assist in his last two games before being cast to the sidelines, the QPR star picked up right where he left off with a fine performance against Millwall.

With his second-half substitution, Stephan clearly had one eye on his involvement against Norwich City on Saturday. Rightly so, as he will be key in the battle against their strong midfield containing Paris Maghoma, Jacob Wright, Kenny McLean and former Hoops man Sam Field.

According to recent reports, few QPR fans needed a reminder of just how influential Madsen is, but his display against Millwall emphasised his value. He brings goals from midfield, reliability and creativity in possession, and strength in duels when needed.

If the R’s are to maintain their push for the Championship play-offs, Madsen will have a key role to play.
