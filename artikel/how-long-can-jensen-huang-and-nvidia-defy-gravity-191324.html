It’s foolish to be astonished by anything anymore. Still, Nvidia’s reinstatement of Jensen Huang as president and chief executive officer, just eleven days after his resignation and six days after the board of directors noted it had no intention to reinstate him, is surprising, to say the least.

Recent findings suggest that the Wall Street Journal reports Nvidia’s statement that the board had “received input from senior levels of the United States government” and is “confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.”.

Andrew C. McCarthy is a senior fellow at National Review Institute, an NR contributing editor, and author of Ball of Collusion: The Plot to Rig an Election and Destroy a Presidency.
