Sources indicate that we know that Pokémon Winds and Waves will launch on Switch 2 at some point in 2027, but that's it. The Pokémon Company is still keeping quiet about the upcoming Gen 10 entry, but an revealed gap in the long-running anime has some people already narrowing the release window.

Earlier today, it was declared that the Pokémon anime would be taking a whopping nine-month gap between the finale of 'Winter Voyage' in December and a newly introduced series starring Liko and Roy, which kicks off in October 2027. Serebii's Joe Merrick was quick to point out that the anime typically doesn't take breaks like this — usually airing an episode a week, bar some minor gaps. Given that fresh series of the show typically launch alongside their mainline game, does this mean that there has been a scheduling change at The Pokémon Company, with Winds and Waves being pushed back until later in the year? According to recent reports, it certainly seems like a delay behind closed doors is possible — it is strange that TPC would schedule a gap this massive in the anime run intentionally. If the upcoming series is set to tie in with Gen 10, then that would put Winds and Waves launching sometime around October 2027.

Hell, any extra time that TPC can be developing the next mainline title gets a major thumbs up from us.

Of course, it could also just be a wild intended pause in the anime, which had no sway on the game at all. TPC always eyes up the big pre-holiday November window for its mainline releases, so who's to say Gen 10 would ever have been any different?

Or things could be really off the wall, and this scheduled anime series is actually nothing to do with Winds and Waves, which TPC could go crazy and release any other time in the year instead!

In short, we're still waiting on something concrete from The Pokémon Tech giant before we know the next release date for sure. But if this anime start date is anything to go by, we imagine we'll be seeing Gen 10 in the same November window that we're used to.
