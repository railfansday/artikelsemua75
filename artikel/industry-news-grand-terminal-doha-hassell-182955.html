The Grand Terminal provides berths for two megaships, which can enjoy a variety of leisure and cultural amenities offered by the impressive waterside complex. Hassell Principal and Head of Design Ashley Munday remarked, "We are really excited that our Grand Terminal is beginning its life as a festival venue for the World Cup, but what excites us even more is its future as a loved piece of the city, embedded in the cultural fabric of Doha. It is a building that doesn't just have a singular function as a cruise terminal, but has been designed to absorb other uses during the year, like markets, exhibitions, and festivals. For us, this is a civic project that has to respond to many complex needs and requirements at once, but still has an 'outward' role to face the world and welcome people into a place that is really just emerging as a destination.".
