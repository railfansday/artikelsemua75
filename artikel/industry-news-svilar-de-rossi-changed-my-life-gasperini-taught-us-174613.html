After more than a month’s absence, Mile Svilar returned to the pitch, defending Roma’s goal in the match against Parma at the Tardini.

The Giallorossi number 99 then gave an interview to Cronache di Spogliatoio, discussing, among other topics, his relationship with De Rossi and Gasperini.

Industry observers note that the night against Feyenoord in the Europa League in 2024: “That night was the real start of my professional career. From there, everything changed. In those three games—the two against Feyenoord and the one against Frosinone—I started to be a professional. I once told my wife that many goalkeepers start out as second or third-choice, then start playing well due to injuries and start like that. Up until that point, it had happened to me many times in my early years at Benfica, a little less so at Roma, but I never had the feeling inside that it was the right time. “. It has been revealed that “had the feeling that it could never happen to me. But that week, I had it, I felt that my moment had come. A feeling you can’t explain. I felt that something would happen like it does to the others. I knew it was the moment. I never mentioned it, but I knew it. I had a different adrenaline rush, the feeling of being crucial.”.

“In every job, you need someone who believes in you. And De Rossi was the person who believed in me, who gave me the push. He put me on the field, he had the power to change something, and Daniele changed my life, the life of my family.”. (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

“The week before his arrival, I played with AC Milan, but it ended a bit badly. Then we had Verona at home, and during the week I already knew I wouldn’t play. I wasn’t surprised. But he was the first person who called me into his office and remarked, ‘Look, Mile, I chose not to let you play and let Rui play. But in training, I saw something really interesting, so train because your time will come soon.’ For the first time, someone wanted to explain to me before the match and tell me I wouldn’t play. I immediately felt that he would be different for me.”.

“De Rossi’s dismissal hurt. I felt bad. He was someone who changed everything for me, in football and therefore in life. I will be grateful to him for the rest of my life.”.

“Last year was spectacular. But there were some low points, and many around us no longer believed in it. But when you keep doing, keep doing, like we’re doing with the coach, it’s something I’ve never seen.”.

“Gasperini’s biggest contribution? The mentality. The physical and tactical work the boys put in because it’s a different way of playing. There’s a winning mentality, which is what we needed because this club truly has everything it takes to do well.”.

Sources indicate that “Our objectives? It’s hard to say right now. As everyone can see, we’re doing really well. The tactical and physical work is putting a lot of us ahead. Maybe we were a little behind or something like that before, but now we’re moving up.”.
