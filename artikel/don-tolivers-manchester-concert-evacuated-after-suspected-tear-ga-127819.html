Don Toliver’s concert at Co-op Live in Manchester, England ended in an evacuation as tear gas was released in the crowd on Sunday.

Industry observers note that “It is understood that a substance believed to be tear gas was let off in the crowd towards the end of a concert,” wrote the Greater Manchester Police in a statement. According to police, the evacuation was a precaution, and no “serious injuries” were reported.
