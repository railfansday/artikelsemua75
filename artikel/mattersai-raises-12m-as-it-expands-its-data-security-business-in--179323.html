Enterprise data security startup Matters.AI today revealed that it has raised $12 million in upcoming funding as it expands its business in North America. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

It has been revealed that matters.AI offers a platform called the AI Security Engineer for Data that is designed to protect sensitive data used by employees, artificial intelligence systems and AI agents across the enterprise. Formerly known as OptiQ.AI, the company has teams in the San Francisco Bay Area and Bengaluru, India. (Related: Is Social Security Taxable? How to Calculate What You O...)

In a recent development, AI agents, the manufacturer argues, now have the same access to enterprise data as the employees who run them. Visibility into who or what is using that data is missing from most data security tools, according to Matters.AI, leaving security teams to connect alerts from separate tools by hand.

Security teams using Matters.AI get one place to see where sensitive data resides, where it has traveled and who or what has accessed it. Data is tracked across cloud environments, on-premises systems, software-as-a-service applications, endpoints and browsers. The firm’s AI analyzes the context of that data and the intent behind each use.

In a recent development, underneath that view, sensitive data across the data estate is discovered and classified, according to the manufacturer. Matters.AI also uses patented fingerprinting, built on large language models, to follow data as it moves between systems.

Insider risk and compliance risk are identified by correlating four signals, those being data sensitivity, misconfiguration, access and intent. Within policies set by the security team, the AI Security Engineer for Data is designed to gather the evidence on a flagged risk before remediating it on its own.

The platform is being pitched as a way to bring together work that enterprises typically buy as five separate products: data security posture management, data detection and response, database activity monitoring, data loss prevention and insider risk management.

Coming into its new funding round, Matters.AI has been deployed at more than 50 organizations. Customers include major financial institutions, brokerage and asset management firms, large retailers and digital media and entertainment platforms, the tech giant notes.

Sources indicate that “Enterprises want to move fast with AI, but no one can trust an agent with data they cannot follow,” Harsh Sahu, co-founder and chief technology officer of Matters.AI, said in the organization’s announcement. According to Sahu, the platform works across petabytes of data and billions of access events a day.

Co-founder and Chief Executive Keshava Murthy argued that security teams do not need “another tool telling them something might be wrong,” adding that they instead need a system that can investigate a problem and take action.

According to recent reports, tanglin Venture Partners Pte. Ltd. led the pre-Series A round, with IIFL Fintech Fund, Piper Serica’s Bharat Tech Fund, Foster Ventures and Tetration Ventures also participating. Existing investors Kalaari Capital and Endiya Partners also invested in the round. The pair co-led a $4.75 million seed round announced in October 2025, when Matters.AI also disclosed an earlier $1.5 million pre-seed round led by Better Capital and Carya Venture Partners.

Sankalp Gupta, managing partner at Tanglin Venture Partners, said that rather than “adding another layer of alerts, the team is rethinking how enterprise data security operates.”.

According to recent reports, research and development in data lineage, autonomous investigation, endpoint and browser intelligence and the prevention of sensitive data misuse by AI agents will be the focus of the money raised. Including the upcoming funding, Matters.AI has raised $18.25 million to date.
