Black Friday 2026 is here, and PlayStation is offering substantial discounts on games and gaming hardware for the big holiday shopping event. PlayStation's official Black Friday hardware deals also include PlayStation 5 hardware, including the DualSense controller, PlayStation VR2, and Pulse audio accessories.
