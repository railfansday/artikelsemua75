Niko Kovac insisted Ethan Nwaneri will "play many more games" for Borussia Dortmund, but faces "fierce competition" for minutes.

The 19-year-old has made 12 appearances for Dortmund this season, with four of those coming as a starter, but is yet to record a direct goal contribution.

However, the teenager impressed for England's Under-21s during the international break, assisting twice in a win over France's Under-21s.

Dortmund are second in the Bundesliga and are yet to lose in the competition this season.

They defeated Elversberg last time out, and Kovac wants to see a fast start against Gladbach following the international break.

"We can't afford to take any time to get going," added Kovac.

Industry observers note that "There are still eight games left this year. In these games, we want to cement our place at the top of the Bundesliga. The other teams are pushing from behind. We have to be ready right away.

It has been revealed that "The focus is on this game. It's plain and simple: we have to play our next opponent – Gladbach – in a way that ensures we win the game. Positive results build confidence and fuel the hunger for more.".

Borussia Monchengladbach – Joe Scally.

Amid a season of struggles for Gladbach, wing-back Joe Scally has stood out for the Bundesliga's bottom side.

Among defenders in the competition this season, only Anthony Caci (six) has directly contributed to more goals than Scally (five – three goals, two assists).

Borussia Dortmund – Felix Nmecha.

Felix Nmecha scored his second brace of the Bundesliga season before the international break and has already equalled his personal best of four goals from the 2024-25 campaign.

However, Nmecha has scored 13 of his 14 total Bundesliga goals in home matches, with his only away goal coming in Eintracht Frankfurt in January 2026.

MATCH PREDICTION: BORUSSIA DORTMUND WIN.

Dortmund have started a Bundesliga season unbeaten in nine games for only the fourth time, after 2002-03, 2018-19 and 2023-24 – in 2023-24, their first defeat came in their 10th match.

Across two seasons, Dortmund are unbeaten in 11 Bundesliga games, with their last defeat coming against their upcoming opponents Gladbach in early May (0-1 away).

Meanwhile, four points from nine matches is a new club-record low for Gladbach in the Bundesliga.

Of the 18 Bundesliga teams to have managed four points or fewer after nine games in the three-points-per-win era, only six avoided relegation (33%), most recently Mainz in the 2023-24 season.

However, Dortmund have won only one of their last six Bundesliga away games against Gladbach (D1 L4).

Since the start of the 2020-21 season, Dortmund have not lost more Bundesliga away games against any other side, while Gladbach have not celebrated more Bundesliga home wins against any other opponent in that time.
