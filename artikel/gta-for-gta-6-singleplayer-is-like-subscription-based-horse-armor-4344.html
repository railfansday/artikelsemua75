Yep, weird as it sounds, there's a subscription option for a singleplayer game.

Sources indicate that well, how does free rides on public transportation sound? Great, right? Can I sweeten the pot with 10% off haircuts? Huh? Huh? How about if your $10 scooter rental was comped. Are you in?

It has been revealed that OK, you do also get a nice exclusive car and an extra garage to store it in. But there are already more than 500 vehicles in the game, so unless you're a diehard completionist, I don't think one more coupe is going to be missed, especially if you have to pay $7.99 to claim it.

And it's not like you're gonna show it off to anyone. This is a singleplayer game. Remember Bethesda's infamous horse armor DLC? Imagine that, but you keep paying for it month after month.

I have no doubt that when (if) GTA 6 gets an online component, GTA+ will be more substantial, but right now it's not the meatiest offering I've ever seen for a monthly bill on a singleplayer game, unless you want those GTA Online perks, too.

Industry observers note that it's a cool looking car, and I can't dispute that those are definitely bonuses. But midway through the game you're not exactly going to be sweating rideshare costs or how much you're charged for new hairstyles.
