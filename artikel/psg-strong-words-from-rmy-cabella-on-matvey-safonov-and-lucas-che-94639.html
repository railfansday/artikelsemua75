Since the rise of Matvey Safonov at Paris Saint-Germain, Lucas Chevalier’s situation has become complicated. Recruited to become the Parisian number one, the former Lille player finally lost his starting place to the Russian goalkeeper last season. A situation that Rémy Cabella, who worked with Safonov in Krasnodar, observes with a particular eye. At the microphone of Canal+the former Montpellier resident wanted to salute the qualities of his former teammate, while having a thought for his friend Lucas Chevalier, now relegated to the bench.

“I always noted he was too strong. He had his own style, like handball goalkeepers in their outings. It’s weird because I also have my friend who is second goalkeeper at PSG, it’s Lucas (Chevalier). It makes me feel bad for Lucas, but I’m also happy for Mota. I call him Mota. When he signed for PSG, we spoke. I was happy that he came to France because it’s strange to see a Russian in France, and even abroad. We sent each other a few messages and played video games together too, because I’m a geek.”explained Cabella.
