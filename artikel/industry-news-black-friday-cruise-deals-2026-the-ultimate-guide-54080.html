Industry News: Black Friday cruise deals 2026: The ultimate guide

🌐 Source: Thepointsguy • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (186 words, 📖 Standard)
  • Cruise lines are offering extensive Black Friday deals for 2027 and later, including discounted fares, free upgrades, onboard credits and promotions like third and fourth guests sailing free.
  • Both ocean and river cruises are included, with offers from major brands, small-ship operators and expedition lines covering destinations worldwide.
  • Savings vary by cruise line and itinerary, with some deals requiring full payment upfront or offering additional perks for early booking or loyalty members.
  • Travelers can find promotions on a wide range of itineraries, from Caribbean beach escapes to Arctic expeditions and European river journeys.
  • Many deals are time-limited, with booking windows ending between late November and early December, and some require nonrefundable payments or deposits.
  • Availability may be limited on popular ships or itineraries, and the best prices are often for select sailings or cabin categories.
  • Always review the total fare, including all taxes and fees, and understand the value of included perks before booking.
