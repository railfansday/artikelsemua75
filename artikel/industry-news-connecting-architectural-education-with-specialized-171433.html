These shifts also raise questions about architectural education and how professional training can connect with specialized fields. SCI-Arc's new Accelerated Master of Science pathway offers one approach, allowing students to combine a professional architecture degree with a postgraduate degree in artificial intelligence, ecological design, media, or architectural pedagogy through one additional year of study.
