Whether it was roasting a chicken, pouring an outrageously large Cosmopolitan, or just organizing her pantry, I’d happily watch Ina Garten do just about anything on television. But there’s apparently one TV gig that even the Barefoot Contessa can't stomach, and I’m a little devastated we’ll never get to see it. (Related: Large Language Models: A Latest Moore's Law?...)

Recent findings suggest that during the November 18 episode of her Happy Hour with Ina Garten podcast, Garten got to talking with guest Rose Byrne about opportunities that felt too intimidating to pursue. Ina didn’t have to think very hard to come up with her own example.

It has been revealed that “Somebody asked me to do Dancing with the Stars,” she revealed. “I thought, ‘Nobody needs to see that. That’s not happening.’”.

In a recent development, with which I respectfully disagree. I think quite a few of us would happily clear our schedules to see Ina Garten attempt a salsa. Unfortunately, Garten wasn’t particularly eager to find out how she'd fare in the ballroom. “The closer I got to the day when I had to do it, the more I’d have nightmares,” she admitted.

Byrne was sympathetic, acknowledging that she’d be terrified of competing, too.

Industry observers note that still, this is a woman who’s made some pretty bold decisions throughout her career. Garten left her job working on nuclear energy policy in Washington, D.C., to buy a specialty food store in the Hamptons with virtually no experience running one. There were some early mishaps, including a batch of roast chickens nobody wanted until she changed how they were displayed, and those experiences eventually helped shape the approachable cooking philosophy behind her hugely successful cookbooks. Give Ina some good food and someone she loves spending time with, and she's in her element.
