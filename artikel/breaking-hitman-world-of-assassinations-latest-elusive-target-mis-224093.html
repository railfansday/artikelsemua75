Hitman World of Assassination is getting a brand-new elusive target. Available to play on iOS, Hitman World of Assassination offers plenty of opportunities for murderous mischief in exotic locations. And, starting today, the latest celebrity face to get embroiled in the action is none other than rock star Lenny Kravitz!

Lenny Kravitz stars as Bawma, the pirate king who serves as the central antagonist of 007 First Light, another IO Interactive game. Of course, given he has to survive to face down Bond, Bawma is not the actual target here. Instead, Agent 47 is tasked with eliminating three rogue lieutenants under Bawma's watchful gaze on Ambrose Island. The mission is available to play until December 24th.
