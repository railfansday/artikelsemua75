Edia has declared Kendo Rage 1&2, a two-game collection featuring side-scrolling action game Kendo Rage and fighting game sequel Kendo Rage 2. It will launch for PlayStation 5, Switch, and PC via Steam on March 18, 2023.

Sources indicate that kendo Rage first introduced for Suepr Famicom in January 1993 in Japan, followed by Super Nintendo Entertainment System in October 1993 in North America. The sequel, which never saw a localized release, launched for Super Famicom in March 1995 in Japan, followed by PlayStation in November 1995.
