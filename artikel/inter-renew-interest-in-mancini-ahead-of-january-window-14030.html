Inter have not forgotten Gianluca Mancini and could come knocking on Roma’s door again.

The Giallorossi defender was already in the Nerazzurri’s sights over the summer, but Gian Piero Gasperini categorically ruled out the possibility of his departure.

Industry observers note that despite some mistakes this early in the season, Mancini remains a key figure in Gasperini’s defense. The coach continues to focus on the center-back, whom he knows well from his time at Atalanta. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

According to Calciomercato.it, Mancini’s name is still on Inter’s list for the next transfer window.

Chivu needs defensive reinforcements, especially given the possible departures of Bisseck and Stones.

It has been revealed that the Nerazzurri are also pursuing Lazio’s Oliver Provstgaard, but Mancini remains a target. Inter reportedly intend to make another attempt in January, after a summer move that was stalled by Gasperini.
