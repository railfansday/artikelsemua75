📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that before you buy a bigger item new on Amazon, check Amazon Resale, formerly Amazon Warehouse. Amazon sells inspected returns and open-box goods there below the new price, with its usual return policy. Read the condition grade first, since a lower grade can mean missing accessories or parts, and buy only when the discount covers their cost.

One product can be listed on Resale in several grades at once, each at its own price.

Amazon inspects each item before giving it one of four grades, and it tests electronics and resets them to factory settings. A Like Newly introduced item works perfectly and has every essential accessory, though its packaging may be damaged. A Very Good one may show light use and minor cosmetic flaws, and it can lack non-essential accessories. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

In a recent development, A Good item can show wear from moderate use, and an Acceptable one can show clear signs of use, such as scratches and dents. Either may be missing accessories or parts you’d have to buy before you can use it, and the listing’s description names anything missing.

Industry observers note that to find offers, browse Amazon Resale by category, or look on a regular product page for the Save with Used or Buy Used section and open See details to compare grades. Resale purchases fall under Amazon’s return policy, which gives most items 30 days. Each item is one of a kind, though, so Amazon can’t swap a disappointing one for another in exactly the same condition. Test your item the day it arrives. In a recent development, check the maker’s warranty too. Under FTC rules, a written warranty on a product over $15 must say so if it covers only the original buyer, which may not be you. For a laptop or phone you’d want covered by a warranty, look at refurbished electronics sold with one instead.

Say a stand mixer is listed as Like Fresh at 20% below the new price because a buyer opened the box and sent it back. Every essential accessory is included, so you get a fully working mixer for 20% less, in an opened box. At a lower grade with the bowl missing, the discount has to cover a new bowl before it saves you anything.

Like New and Very Good are the grades to start with, since neither can be missing anything the item needs to work.
