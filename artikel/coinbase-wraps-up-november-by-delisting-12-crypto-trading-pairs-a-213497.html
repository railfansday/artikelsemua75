The delisting took place on November 25, as Coinbase revealed earlier. The affected pairs include APE-USDT, ATOM-USDT, ALGO-BTC, CRV-BTC, ETC-BTC, XLM-BTC, ICP-BTC, and ADA-ETH, among others.

The full list includes APE-USDT, ATOM-USDT, ALGO-BTC, CRV-BTC, ETC-BTC, ICP-BTC, XLM-BTC, APE-EUR, ADA-ETH, 1INCH-GBP, CHZ-GBP, and SNX-GBP, with trading suspended across Coinbase Exchange, Advanced Trade and Coinbase Prime.

Coinbase regularly monitors the markets on the exchange and, in an effort to improve overall market health and consolidate liquidity, has removed a number of non-USD trading pairs for supported assets.

In similar delisting news, Coinbase has disabled trading for Stargate Finance (STG), Vulcan Forged (PYR), Neon EVM (NEON), and Syndicate (SYND), in line with the shake-up on its platform in November. Sources indicate that based on recent reviews, Coinbase will suspend trading for Centrifuge (CFG) on December 18, 2026, at or around 2 PM ET. Centrifuge has separately announced it is ending token support for CFG and allowing holders to convert CFG tokens into equity in Centrifuge.

Also in December, Coinbase will be performing a system upgrade. In an X post, the exchange stated it has scheduled a system upgrade for Saturday, December 5, 2026, around 7 a.m. PT, estimated to last about 4 hours.

During this time, Coinbase stated that all trading and transfers will be unavailable, and the Coinbase Exchange will be temporarily offline. People are advised to take note and prepare accordingly so as to avoid any delays on that date.
