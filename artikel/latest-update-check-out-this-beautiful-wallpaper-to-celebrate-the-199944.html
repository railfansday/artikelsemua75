Sources indicate that apple has quietly announced the upcoming opening of its latest retail store in Hyderabad, India. Located at the Lakeshore Mall in Kukatpally, it opens at 5pm on Thursday, December 10.

As is now usual, the manufacturer has created some exclusive wallpaper to mark the event, and you can download it for iPhone, iPad, and Mac ….

Sources indicate that the store will be the seventh one to open in India. There’s no press release or photos as yet – the organization has simply quietly added the store to its list in the country. (Related: Nemotron-Personas-India: Synthesized Data for Sovereign...)

In a recent development, as we’ve previously explained, Apple had been trying to open retail stores in India since at least 2015, but had to undertake a lengthy battle with the government before finally being allowed to do so in 2023. Since then, the tech giant has averaged two newly introduced stores a year. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

According to recent reports, india has taken a clever approach to economic development, offering both incentives to establish local manufacturing plants, and barriers to imports of foreign-made products. That carrot-and-stick approach has proven successful, with companies like Apple jumping through the necessary hoops in order to sell their products to the second largest population in the world.

In a recent development, an India First initiative required companies to manufacture a significant proportion of their products within the country in order to avoid high import tariffs. This forced Apple, Samsung, and other companies to establish manufacturing plants in India, bringing jobs to the country.

Additionally, India does not allow company-branded retail stores unless the same conditions are met. That particular requirement was mostly intended to target Apple.

The proposal worked, with Apple asking its suppliers and iPhone assemblers to establish manufacturing plants within India. Initially, that was just for the iPhone SE, but more advanced phones have gradually been added, and today the country is the second largest manufacturing base after China. All iPhones sold in the US are made in India.

Apple has for some time now created unique wallpapers to mark the opening of each fresh store, and the latest example is particularly beautiful.

According to recent reports, to download it, you need to visit this page on the device for which you want to use it. For example, if you visit the page on a Mac, you will automatically be taken to the desktop version.
