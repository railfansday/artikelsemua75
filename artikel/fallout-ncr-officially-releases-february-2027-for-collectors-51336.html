There's little doubt at this point that the Prime Video adaptation of Fallout has done wonders for restoring some confidence in the franchise, and bringing in new fans from around the world. The unfortunate reality is that we still haven't had another single-player game in the series, and we're looking at quite a lengthy wait before one does debut.

What the show also did was bring back many memorable locations, factions and characters from the older games in the series, which really opened it up to nostalgia from long-time fans. Shady Sands is probably the most obvious example of this from the first season, with the arrival of Upcoming Vegas in the second continuing that trend to the benefit of the audience.

As we know with New Vegas, there are a ton of different factions who call the Mojave home, and one of the most iconic of those is about to be further immortalized thanks to a new physical release that would be a worthy centerpiece for any collection. According to recent reports, over on the Sideshow website, you should be able to find the product page for the NCR Power Armor statue by Dark Horse Comics. Cast in durable PVC, it's a budget-friendly option that still manages to include a ton of details and little references for fans of the show.

The power armor itself is modeled on the suit that Maximus wears in his epic fight with the Deathclaws at the entrance to the New Vegas strip, and it sits on a base that's designed to look like a vault door, and includes the title in its recognizable font.

In sharing a little about the inspirations behind the statue, the manufacturer included the following description:.

"Dark Horse, in partnership with Amazon MGM Studios, continues our line of figures from the acclaimed Prime Video series, Fallout, this time pulling characters straight from the scenes of Season 2. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

In a recent development, in the procession to rescue Lucy, The Ghoul takes Maximus and Thaddeus to an abandoned NCR safe house where they unearth the revered armor from the New California Republic. The NCR is a post-war federal republic-esque faction that values democracy and the rule of law. Maximus wears the armor to battle the Deathclaws as they make their way into New Vegas. Standing at 9.75” tall, this figure captures every detail of the NCR Power Armor as seen in Season 2.".

As mentioned at the end of the description there, it measures in at a little under ten inches, so it shouldn't be too difficult to store and display at home. Priced at $80, the item is at the moment available for pre-order, with an expected shipping date between February and April 2027.

The third season of Fallout has also wrapped filming, and though we don't have a release date for that just yet, it's expected to drop in May or June 2027. The Ghoul is likely going to give us our first proper look at Colorado in the universe we all know and love, maybe even giving us a preview of where the next game is going to be set in the process.

Recent findings suggest that based on the hit video game series and set 200 years after an apocalypse, Fallout follows residents of luxury shelters as they re-enter a post-nuclear world. Confronted with a bizarre and violent landscape, the series explores the stark contrasts between their sheltered existence and the harsh realities of the outside universe.
