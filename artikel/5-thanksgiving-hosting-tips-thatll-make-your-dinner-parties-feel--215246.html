For many households, Thanksgiving is the main hosting event they look forward to all year. But what is it about this holiday that makes it such an annual highlight, and how can you emulate that same essence more than just once a year?

In a recent development, we reached out to entertaining experts to learn which Thanksgiving hosting tips you can apply to year-round gatherings. These are the lessons that will make even your most laid-back get-togethers all the more memorable. (Related: Hosting your Models and Datasets on Hugging Face Spaces...)

According to Whitney Ray, owner of Wyeth Ray Interiors, one reason that hosting Thanksgiving feels so special is that it's the one time of year most hosts actually break out their nice tableware. However, she believes you should be using these beautiful and sentimental pieces more often. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

Sources indicate that "So many people wait to use their best serving and dinnerware pieces at holidays like Thanksgiving, but I say those beautiful dishes should be used all year long," she states. "A Tuesday night dinner with a few friends doesn’t need to be formal, but mixing your nice china with your everyday pieces elevates a normal night into something more.".

Hovik Harutyunyan is an event designer, etiquette specialist, and the founder of Harutyunyan Events. He believes that one key logistical trick a host can apply to any dinner or party they host is curating a menu full of familiar foods that are easy to prepare ahead.

"Thanksgiving menus work so well because the recipes are familiar as well as tried-and-true," he notes. "For example, let's talk about the pies. Thanksgiving dessert is so easy because most of us opt for pumpkin and pecan pie. We know the dessert, and it's extremely easy to make in advance.".

In a recent development, he also notes that it's best not to try out complicated, brand-new recipes for a major hosting event. You should opt for an elevated menu that is easy to scale and distribute across a large number of guests.

Recent findings suggest that thanksgiving is one meal where guests love contributing by bringing a side dish or dessert. While not every gathering needs to have a potluck element, there are other ways you can lighten your cooking load.

It has been revealed that christina Samatas and Renee DiSanto are principals of Park & Oak, and DiSanto is also the owner of Marcel’s Culinary Experience, a culinary community and cooking school. Both of them fully endorse taking a few shortcuts when it comes to hosting any major dinner.

In a recent development, "Not every detail needs to be homemade to feel special," DiSanto indicates. "There are many ways to save you a few steps, such as using pre-cut veggies or incorporating a few prepared appetizers or sides. Spend more time on the details that matter the most to you, rather than the little things.".

According to recent reports, for any event you hold, it's pretty much inevitable that guests always end up in the kitchen. "Whether you invite guests in or not, Thanksgiving demonstrates us the kitchen is where people naturally congregate before a meal, whether they are wanting to help or simply catch up before dinner is served," Ray indicates.

Industry observers note that for this reason, she notes you should always plan to entertain in the kitchen at any gathering. Consider adding small bites and a drinks station on your kitchen counter or island, and try to clean as you go while making the meal.

Sources indicate that thanksgiving is a celebration of gratitude, and Harutyunyan believes you should apply the same spirit to any party. Anytime you gather with loved ones is an opportunity to give them a special experience.

"Thanksgiving, and the holidays in general, is one of the most magical times of the year," he notes. "As stressful and chaotic as they may be, we love to gather with our loved ones to enjoy each other's company. We share stories, memories, and gratitude. The holiday spirit takes over all of us, which is what drives us to create such fabulous dinner spreads, decorate the home, and create an intentional experience for those on the guest list.".
