I think what you’ll find is that most (maybe all?) of the private schools that’d provide meaningful need-based aid to folks at your income level also have low acceptance rates. So in the case of a student who isn’t in the top several percent or so of their class (as I assume a 3.5-3.8 gpa student wouldn’t be), the probability of admission to one of the schools where this matters is exceedingly low. Sure, test scores and activities and essays and recs can move the needle a bit but the odds still aren’t great. There’d be nothing wrong with not opting for those schools or not being accepted, it just would mean that aid for your family most likely would come from merit at less selective privates and some publics (some of this could be based on academics, some could simply be differential pricing).I’m not sure I’d act on the basis of potential need-based aid in your situation if it didn’t make sense for other reasons.Statistics: Posted by Pdxnative — Tue Nov 24, 2026 7:24 pm.
