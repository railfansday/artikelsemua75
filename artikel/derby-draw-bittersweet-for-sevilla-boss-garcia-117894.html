Sevilla boss Luis Garcia feels his team were by far the better team against Real Betis, labelling their last-gasp draw as a "bittersweet" result.

Kike Salas netted late on in Saturday's derby to snatch a 1-1 draw for Sevilla, who fell behind to Antony's effort early in the second half.

Sevilla had 25 shots to Betis' six, and accumulated chances worth 2.3 expected goals to their opponent's 0.6.

They registered 47 touches in the Betis box and also hit the woodwork, though Garcia's team did fail to create a big chance.

And Garcia said a point was the least Sevilla deserved.

"We drew at the end, and it's a tremendous joy, but we all have a bittersweet feeling, because we were much better [than Betis]," he noted.

"We had 25 shots on goal compared to just six, playing in the opposition's half, creating more scoring opportunities.

"I think it's one of the matches we dominated the most.".

Garcia did concede, though, that Sevilla are lacking real quality up top.

"We're lacking talent up front, not from the forwards themselves, but moments of inspiration in the final third," he added.

"Football is about quality; it's about the difference in talent. But the team deserves respect. We keep adding points and fighting until the end.

"I respect [Betis coach] Manuel [Pellegrini] because I'm fond of him, and coaches come to press conferences wearing their team's shirt, but if anyone deserved to win, it was Sevilla.".

Betis defender Marc Bartra, however, felt Sevilla got lucky, as he claimed Salas should have been sent off for an earlier tackle on Isco.

"It's clear we deserved to take all three points, absolutely," Bartra stated.

"They equalised with a player who should have been sent off. I prefer to focus on how the team performed.
