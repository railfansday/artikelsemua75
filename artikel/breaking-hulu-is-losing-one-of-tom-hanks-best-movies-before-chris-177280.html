Over three decades, Hanks' rootin'-est, tootin'-est cowboy has brought joy to theaters and homes around the world, and this year, he, Buzz (Tim Allen), Jessie (Joan Cusack), and the rest of the gang returned for their latest adventure.

According to recent reports, toy Story 5 debuted in June this year, immediately becoming the must-see movie of early summer. The film earned more than $1.1 billion in an impressive box office run, against a reported production budget of $250 million, which is right now enough to rank it as the third-highest-grossing film of the year. It was also a massive hit with critics, shaking off the mixed response to the previous installment. "The impressive fifth installment not only justifies its existence but feels like a necessary response to how much the world has changed in the past three decades," Taylor Gates wrote, giving the movie an 8/10 rating in her review.
