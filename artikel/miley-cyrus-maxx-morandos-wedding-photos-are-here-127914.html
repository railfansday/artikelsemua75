Miley Cyrus & Maxx Morando’s Wedding Photos Are Here

Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando’s wedding pictures are here. The bride and groom shared the first official look at their marriage ceremony on Sunday, the day after their intimate Saturday (Nov. 21) event in Palm Springs, Calif.

Cyrus posted four wedding photos on Instagram along with the caption “M + M 11.21.26” surrounding a white heart emoji. Photographer Jose Villa was credited for the images.

The newlyweds’ portraits are in black and white, matching the timeless elegance of Cyrus’ long-sleeved, silk Saint Laurent gown with a train and Morando’s classic black tux — which he accessorized with his signature dark shades. The couple are seen embracing in the romantic shots, and in one photo they’re joined by a presumed member of the family: a sweet dog lying comfortably at their feet.

Paparazzi footage of Cyrus, 33, and Morando, 28, getting married poolside, at a former residence of Frank Sinatra — that captured an apparently emotional Cyrus wiping away tears as she and her soon-to-be husband stood in front of their loved ones to say “I do” — made it online Saturday night.

Other family members from the Cyrus family spotted at the wedding were Miley’s dad, Billy Ray with partner Elizabeth Hurley; her mom, Tish, with husband Dominic Purcell, and her sister Noah.

Flaming Lips frontman Wayne Coyne, a longtime collaborator and friend of Cyrus, reportedly served as officiant at the ceremony.

Cyrus’ wedding dress was designed by Anthony Vaccarello for Saint Laurent; Morando also wore Saint Laurent, according to People.

Industry observers note that cyrus was first linked to Morando, who’s the drummer for Los Angeles rock band Liily, in 2021. He co-starred in the lovey-dovey music video for Miley’s “Let’s Get Married,” a single from the star’s latest No. 1, her Billboard 200 chart-topping 2026 album Bass Persuades.
