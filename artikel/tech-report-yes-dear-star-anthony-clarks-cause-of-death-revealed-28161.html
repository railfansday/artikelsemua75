Anthony Clark’s cause of death has been revealed.

The Nineties stand-up comedian who starred in the long-running sitcom died on Nov. 10 at 63. His death certificate listed his cause of death as septic shock, with influenza A and chronic alcoholism noted as underlying conditions, People reported. Clark portrayed Gregory Thomas “Greg” Warner in Yes, Dear, which chronicled two married couples figuring out parenthood. Clark’s tightly wound Greg comically contrasted to his wilder brother-in-law played by O’Malley. The show became a hit with audiences, enjoying six years on CBS from 2000 to 2006 and an even longer run in syndication.

“I was a fan before I was a friend. We spoke on the phone three weeks ago. He had us laughing loudly once again,” remarked O’Malley in his tribute, alongside photos from their last episode of Yes Dear after 122 total episodes. “Rest in peace. Tony.”.
