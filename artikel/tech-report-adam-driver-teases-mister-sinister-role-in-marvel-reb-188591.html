Adam Driver is joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe in the upcoming X-Men reboot directed by Jake Schreier.

The Paper Tiger actor is set to play the villain Mister Sinister, and while appearing on Late Night with Seth Meyers, he teased his upcoming role.

It has been revealed that when the late-night show host asked Driver if he was aware of his character before joining the superhero movie, the Girls alum noted, “Yes. Not to the degree—I was more aware of Magneto. I was a substantial X-Men fan.”.

“My cousin Tony would come over, and he made these Sabretooth costume things with the yellow rubber gloves and put toothpicks out the side, and I would dress as Wolverine,” Driver added. The Girls alum noted that his “son is excited about X-Men,” adding, “He’s getting older, and he hasn’t seen anything that I’ve been in. He’s been to [film] sets … he wants to see me in something.” Watch on Deadline.

Sources indicate that marvel is expected to release the X-Men reboot on May 5, 2028. The cast also includes Christopher Abbott as Professor X, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Inde Navarrette as Rogue, Maya Boyd as Storm, Asa Germann as Angel, and Sadie Sink as Jean Grey.

Earlier this year, Driver talked to Jimmy Kimmel about joining the MCU after rejecting other offers in the past.

“I was a big X-Men fan when I was a kid,” said on ABC’s Jimmy Kimmel Live. “Me and my cousin, Tony, he would be Sabretooth and I would be Wolverine. It’s a big deal.”.

According to recent reports, he added, “I was a big fan. I mean, I was a fan of the other things too. But this one felt like a right time, and then it’s X-Men.”.
