Industry observers note that billy Ray Cyrus shared photos from his daughter Miley Cyrus‘s Palm Springs wedding after she married Maxx Morando on Saturday. Members of the Cyrus clan — including mom Tish and sister Noah — were spotted gathering for the couple’s rehearsal dinner in Southern California before the ceremony, and her father attended alongside girlfriend Elizabeth Hurley and her son Damian.

According to recent reports, “My little girl married the love of her life! I can’t describe how beautiful it felt to have all my family together in one room, united by love,” wrote Billy in his tribute posted to social media alongside snapshots from the celebration including a black-and-white photo of him dancing with Miley.

“[Miley] words can’t begin to express how proud you make me every day… I’m the proudest dad alive!! [Maxx] I’m so honored to welcome you to the family!” he continued. Billy also wished his daughter a happy birthday on Monday, two days after the wedding, and called the couple “a match made in heaven,” adding, “I can’t wait to keep celebrating you both today and forever!” The musician also posted smiling photos taken alongside Hurley, Damian, Noah, his son Christopher, and a childhood picture of Miley winking at the camera while sporting a “Rockstar” shirt.
