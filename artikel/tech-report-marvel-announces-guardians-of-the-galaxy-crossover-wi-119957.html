In a recent development, the Guardians of the Galaxy are crossing over with a familiar Marvel character who had never met them. Throughout the decades, the Guardians of the Galaxy have always been a gloriously mismatched ragtag crew of cosmic outcasts from radically different backgrounds. While no longer as famous, the original 31st-century Guardians squad that brought together Vance Astro, Martinex, Yondu, Charlie-27, and Starhawk thrived on clashing alien cultures long before the modern Guardians. When Dan Abnett and Andy Lanning assembled the current incarnation in 2008, the new Guardians doubled down on the eclectic formula with half-human outlaw Star-Lord, deadly Zen-Whoberi assassin Gamora, vengeful warrior Drax, cybernetic raccoon Rocket, and gentle tree-monarch Groot.

Industry observers note that an equally varied lineup of Earth-based and galactic icons has crossed paths with, or temporarily joined, the Guardians of the Galaxy roster over the years. Mutant favorites like Peter Quill's temporary Star-Lord replacement Kitty Pryde, Iron Man, the Thing, Captain Marvel, Angela, Ant-Man, Hercules, and Nova have each suited up as official or honorary members. Beyond their own ranks, the Guardians of the Galaxy have teamed up with Thor, Doctor Strange, Beta Ray Bill, Silver Surfer, Deadpool, the brutal Frank Castle variant known as Cosmic Ghost Rider, and most recently with Spider-Man himself. Years after welcoming a former villain into their ranks, the Guardians of the Galaxy reunite with a different member of his species. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Sources indicate that in Jordan Morris and Luke Ross’s The Amazing Venom #3, former Spider-Man villain Fred Myers a.k.a. Boomerang, now the red-and-blue symbiote hero Comeback, travels through deep space with his symbiote partner Passenger to reach Klyntar. Running low on fuel, Fred and Passenger make an emergency stop at Knowhere, where Fred compares the local bar to the headquarters he used to share with his fellow Spider-Man rogues, the Bar With No Name. To his understated surprise, he finds Rocket Raccoon fighting the bartender inside.

In a recent development, rocket’s solo presence on Knowhere is a direct result of the Guardians of the Galaxy's disbandment after Jonathan Hickman's Imperial event and Dan Abnett’s Imperial Guardians series. While Peter Quill rules Spartax as Emperor of the Galactic Union and Gamora works to rebuild a proper Guardians roster following Maximus's coup, Rocket works largely on his own. Meanwhile, members like Groot, Drax, and Mantis are still scattered after the original team broke apart. After his team-up with Spider-Man, Rocket Raccoon is acquainted with the wall-crawler's villains, which is why he isn't shocked to see the former Boomerang on Knowhere.

The Guardians of the Galaxy share a notable if surprisingly brief history with the symbiotes, highlighted most prominently during Flash Thompson’s celebrated stint aboard the team in the mid-2010s. Appointed as Earth’s official liaison to Star-Lord’s squad in Brian Michael Bendis’s Guardians of the Galaxy run and Robbie Thompson’s Venom: Space Knight, Agent Venom traveled to Klyntar, where his symbiote was temporarily cleansed of its rage. Besides Flash’s tenure as an "Agent of the Cosmos", the Guardians also crossed paths with symbiote chaos during the Venomized event and later fought off Knull’s symbiote dragons during Al Ewing’s Guardians run in the King in Black crossover.

In a recent development, still, considering how close the Guardians of the Galaxy are to the symbiotes' cosmic nature, it's odd that the Guardians haven't been far closer to symbiote lore. Klyntar is an ancient cosmic civilization tied to Celestials, Gorr the God Butcher, and the same areas of space that heroes like Star-Lord, Gamora, and Rocket patrol every day. Yet, symbiote sagas like Absolute Carnage, King in Black, and Queen in Black have largely unfolded on Earth rather than among the stars. Curiously enough, it takes characters like Fred Myers and Passenger a trip from Earth to Knowhere for a Guardian to meet another symbiote.
