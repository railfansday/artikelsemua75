GTA 6 can run at 60fps on Xbox Series X if you use a glitch, but there's a catch

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that grand Theft Auto 6 runs at 30 frames-per-second across all the consoles it's currently available on, including the more powerful PlayStation 5 Pro. The game doesn't offer a 60fps performance mode or an option to unlock the frame-rate, but one industrious player has managed to do it anyway on Xbox.

Reddit user Meowwie64 devised a way to essentially unlock GTA 6's frame-rate on Series X. Their hack relied on the Xbox's rarely-discussed Quick Resume feature, which lets you park multiple games in memory and switch between them without having to re-load each game.

It has been revealed that in a detailed post, Meowwie64 shared the steps they used to trigger their glitch. For starters, you're going to need a 120Hz monitor display or TV, as the glitch requires adjusting refresh rates.

Industry observers note that first, you need to limit Xbox to display at 60Hz under the console Settings menu. Then, you start GTA 6 (version 01.002.001), wait until the six-star logo appears and then go back to Xbox Settings to change the refresh rate to 120Hz. Before you return to GTA 6, the final step requires launching another game on your console. This has to be a cold boot, meaning the game shouldn't already be running parked in Quick Resume.

After the second game debuts, you now need to use Quick Resume to resume playing GTA 6, which is when you're going to notice the higher frame-rate. The Reddit user said the game could reach 90-plus FPS at certain moments.

Sources indicate that unfortunately, the user also discovered that game-speed appears to be tied to frame-rate in GTA 6, at least when it comes to certain gameplay animations and when performing certain actions. "I have a stable 90fps and everything works fine but Jason's animations run at 2x speed or more," Meowwie64 wrote. Incidentally, cutscenes do not appear to be affected by this matter, so they run at their normal speed.

As much as a 30fps frame-rate makes GTA 6 feel less smooth and potentially less enjoyable to play compared to 60fps or higher, none of the current-generation consoles are capable of delivering frame-rates beyond that. Tech experts Digital Foundry recently explained why even the PS5 Pro isn't powerful enough to run the game at 60fps or even 40fps.
