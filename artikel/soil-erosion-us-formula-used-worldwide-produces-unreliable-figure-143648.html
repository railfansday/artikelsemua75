Soil erosion: US formula used worldwide produces unreliable figures in the Global South

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (70 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Rain and wind erode fertile soil worldwide. The topsoil, where plants take root and where most of the nutrients are found, is washed away from fields by heavy rain, particularly on slopes and where the ground is not covered by vegetation. Once washed away, it takes a very long time to reform—spanning decades to centuries. To estimate how much soil is lost, researchers use computational models.
