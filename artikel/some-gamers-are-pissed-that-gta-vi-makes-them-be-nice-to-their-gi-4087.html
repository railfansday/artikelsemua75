Fellas, is it emasculating to love your virtual girlfriend? Does buying her flowers make you a cuck? Why can’t you hire a prostitute in the murder and grand larceny game?

All great questions. Let us explore.

According to recent reports, after 13 years of buildup, Grand Theft Auto VI is finally here. The latest installment of the GTA franchise is unique in that gamers are playing both sides of Bonnie and Clyde style romance in Vice City. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos serve as the game’s main protagonists, with Lucia being the first playable female protagonist in the history of the franchise. Historically, GTA has represented culturally reprehensible instances of violence with what they say is a satire-heavy approach intended to exaggerate the criminal world they are depicting. Some of it — like killing prostitutes in order to steal their money in GTA III — has not aged as well as the rest of the franchise.

This time things will feel a little different. Keeping up the romance between Jason and Lucia is a huge component of the gameplay. They are not only romantically involved but partners in a criminal enterprise; your decisions in relation to each other affect the gameplay. But for a certain selection of male gamers stuck in the GTA of their youth — in which beating strippers and hiring sex workers were some of the first viral video game content of the internet age — the notion that they might have to be nice to a collection of pixels arranged in a female form was a step too far. GTA VI is challenging the notion that a female protagonist in a video game — a cultural medium where women represent half of the American market — can only be compelling to the consumer as an avatar for their desires and escapism, and not as an equal partner in a story where character development is interactive and moldable. It is the most ambitious open world game of the modern era, and — overall – has been received with overwhelming acclaim by critics and owners alike.

Fortunately, for most of us, the notion that doing small things for your partner helps improve a critical relationship is not confined to fantasy spaces, or virtual escapism. You can still rob stores, steal an old woman’s moped, or even take a jet ski ride with a dog. You can shoot up a bank and careen through car chases with Vice City PD. And if you’re so inclined, you can take a stroll on the beach with your latina baddie to recharge the next day. It’s a game folks, don’t forget to play.
