Winter is slowly creeping up and something I’m always thinking about is good footwear. As a Floridian, now in New York City, I have been severely underprepared when it comes to figuring out my winter fashion. I can admit, I have slipped and slid across the icy, frozen over snow a few times. And so, it’s really important to me to have a few solid pairs of winter boots on hand.

Now while warmth and durability are top of mind, we will never let style fall by the wayside. Below, I’ve gathered some stylish and handy winter boots for all the trudging you’ll be doing the next few months.

It has been revealed that A fun silver pair of laced ankle boots is a chic way to weather the winter days. Not only are they built for the cold, but they also add a bit of fun with the mixture of brown suede and silver.

For my maximalist girls, getting a fun platform pair of fur snow boots is how you fight off the winter blues. They truly make any outfit you’re wearing that much more exciting and fashion-forward.

If fur isn’t your thing this winter, try out a good knee-high leather boot. They’re durable and go with just about anything in your closet.

According to recent reports, whether it’s a UGG boot or something a bit more fun, like the style above, you’re going to want to invest in a shearling boot. And why not make it uber fashionable while you’re at it? These work well for all the cold and snowy days we have ahead of us.

Recent findings suggest that now, I want to preface, if it’s snowing or raining, maybe don’t pull these out. But on the cold winter days where you want to get a little more sleek, try out a suede pointed-toe heel. It’s one of my personal favorite boot styles and elevates your winter fashion with ease.
