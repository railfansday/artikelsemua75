Speaking of the “Stevie Wonder can drive” theories, Lionel Richie told Kelly Clarkson back in 2019 that he spent most of his life thinking that Stevie could see and told a funny anecdote about them being in a car once.

Sources indicate that “I went to his house, he indicates, ‘Wanna hear a new song? I’ve got it in the car, come on, come with me.’ We got out to the car, he says, ‘You sit on the passenger side, I’ll sit on the driver’s side.’ He goes in, he cranks the car up, he puts the tape in, and then he does this [motions that Stevie turns around as if he’s backing the car up] and starts driving back down the driveway. And I scream, I say, ‘Stevie! What are you doing?’ And he says. ‘Got ya, didn’t I?'”.

2019 must’ve been the height of the theories because Shaquille O’Neal also told a hilarious story about the time he and Stevie lived in the same building when Shaq played for the Los Angeles Lakers. According to him, the legendary musician walked into their building’s elevator by himself, said “What’s up, Shaq,” pressed the button to his floor and walked to his apartment.
