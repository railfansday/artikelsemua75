Sheffield United host high-flying Southampton at Bramall Lane on Tuesday night, as both sides will be aiming to maintain their positive run of form in this thrilling clash.

Sheffield United, after a tricky summer transfer window and subsequent tricky run of results, have turned their form around to become outside shouts of a play-off spot this season, with four wins, one draw, and one defeat in their last six Championship games. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Head coach Chris Wilder will certainly have his focus set on a place in the top eight this campaign, but if the Blades are to have any realistic chance of doing so, claiming all three points when they host Southampton on Tuesday would go a long way.

The Saints are on an excellent run of form themselves, with five wins, two draws, and one defeat in their last eight games as well, climbing from -4 points up to 6th in the table after 16 games.

Under the lights in South Yorkshire, it's shaping up to be a tense affair, as Football League World takes a closer look at the players set to miss out on this clash.

Japhet Tanganga is the first of numerous absentees from Tuesday's fixture at Bramall Lane, with the former Tottenham Hotspur set to sit the game out through suspension after picking up five yellow cards.

13 players were booked in Sheffield United's 4-0 home win over Watford at the weekend, including Tanganga, as Mark McGuinness or Jamal Baptiste will likely step in during the 27-year-old's absence.

Not injured and not suspended - Ben Brereton Diaz will not be allowed to face Southampton on Tuesday night due to EFL rules dictating that he cannot play against his parent club, having returned to South Yorkshire for a third separate spell this summer from St Mary's.

Wilder will be forced to mix up his attack, with Tom Cannon, Tyrese Campbell, and Patrick Bamford vying for a place in the Blades' starting XI.

English right-back Femi Seriki is to face a year on the sidelines after suffering a serious knee injury six minutes after coming on as a substitute in the Blades' 2-1 away defeat to Charlton Athletic. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

Wilder described the 23-year-old's absence for the next 12 months or so as a 'hammer blow', and in his place, Hamza Choudhury will likely retain his place at right-back, with another option in that area also set to miss this fixture against Southampton.

Since joining Sheffield United on a free transfer in the summer, Irish defender Matt Doherty has managed just 41 minutes of league football for the Blades, having frequently appeared as an unused substitute this campaign.

Wilder has confirmed that he expects the Irishman to be available before the end of the year, but is set to miss this one, with the Blades gaffer saying: "Hopefully, he'll be available before January. We've got five weeks left of that, so maybe mid-December he'll be available for selection.".

Scottish striker Ryan One has found minutes in South Yorkshire difficult to come by following his positive loan spell in League One with Lincoln City last season, managing just 30 minutes of Championship football at this stage.

It has been revealed that on One's return to action, Wilder remarked: "He’s about a month or 6 weeks away. So, I think the start of 2027, he’ll be back. We're going to have to take a little bit more time with him. That's not that we overwhelmed him or pushed him too hard. We didn't, but we're just going to have to take that little bit more extra time on him now to make sure there's not a recurrence of the injury.".

Former Blackburn Rovers, Bournemouth, and Leeds United midfielder Joe Rothwell has found minutes at Bramall Lane difficult to come by since joining in January 2026, with just over 400 minutes of league football this season.

Industry observers note that wilder confirmed before the most recent international break that the Englishman was to undergo a minor operation on an injury, saying: "Joe's got to have a minor op that's going to keep him out until after the international break. So he might miss the Watford and Southampton games.".

Onto the Southampton players set to miss this one, and the list looks much more positive for the Saints than it does for the home side, though with German defender Keven Schlotterbeck to miss this one, it's not all rosy on the South Coast.

The 29-year-old has made 13 Championship appearances for the club since his summer move from Augsburg, replacing the outgoing Taylor Harwood-Bellis, but having dislocated his shoulder against Preston North End, he is not expected to be back in action until at least December, with Zach Abbott likely to retain his place in the heart of the Saints back line.

Schlotterbeck's compatriot and teammate Casper Jander has been blighted with injuries this campaign, making just three league appearances throughout October before missing the most recent chunk of games.

It has been revealed that when asked about Jander, Southampton boss Tonda Eckert mentioned: "He'll be back this week. I'm not 100 per cent sure if we have him on Tuesday. I'm hopeful that we have him on Tuesday, but he'll be back this week.".

There is a chance the former Nuremberg midfielder will make the cut for this away trip to Sheffield United, though it's more likely he will be back next weekend for Middlesbrough at home.
