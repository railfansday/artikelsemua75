Britney Spears can’t wait for the world to finally get a piece of her long-awaited jewelry line.

“Heading to London to work on my newly introduced jewelry line and thinking of changing the name,” Spears, 44, wrote via Instagram on Friday, November 20. “I don’t want to offend anyone by calling it ‘B Tiny’ because I know how mean people can be to people who are overweight or underweight. So many judgments by others, it made me sad … that was so last year.”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Spears has long been dreaming about diving into the jewelry space for some time and amid a slew of personal and legal battles.

“I do not have a clothing line, [but] I’m actually thinking about doing a jewelry line,” the pop star previously said during a 2003 radio interview. “It’s going to be called B Tiny, and it’s all, like, really small, intricate, little, tiny jewelry.”.

Decades later, Spears formally announced her B Tiny venture in 2024.

“My first jewelry line coming soon!!! Delicate extremely different and one of a kind pieces,” she wrote via Instagram, confirming the name of her brand. “I’m so excited !!! Realizing how cool it is to be an artist that can be passionate in so many different ways !!! B Tiny coming soon 🌷!!! Girls, you’re gonna f***ing love it !!!”. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Spears debuted the website for B Tiny earlier this month, which teased the range was “23 years in the making.”.

“The promotion looks like a Disney movie, and I quite frankly don’t mind, considering it’s Christmas and all,” the “Oops! I Did It Again” singer gushed in her Friday upload about the pink-colored design. “There’s a sword involved, and it’s a religious symbol for each and every one of us to live by. In each first purchase sold, a very tiny sword made in Japan with unbelievable detail will be in a very tiny red velvet bag.”.

Spears continued, “It’s justice for women all around the world who have ever felt scared, unworthy, less than, unbelievably hurt. The sword represents strength that can allow a woman to finally be vulnerable and in her skin. Because she feels protected!!!”.

The jewelry collection marks one of Spears’ first upcoming projects since her 13-year conservatorship was terminated in 2021. Us exclusively reported earlier in November that it’s also “less and less likely” that Spears’ future endeavors would include new music following her 2016 record.
