A live-action adaptation of the 1990s video game “Metal Slug” has been set up with “I Love My Dad” writer James Morosini.

From Erik Feig’s Saudi-backed indie production studio The Arena, the “Metal Slug” movie will follow Marco Rossi, Tarma Roving, Eri Kasamoto and Fio Germi, four misfits and inseparable friends who become the army’s most unlikely squad — until General Morden’s tyrannical uprising forces them to become the improbable heroes standing between the world and an even greater evil lurking behind him. The Arena’s Melissa Goodall and Sajan Algiri are overseeing the “Metal Slug” movie adaptation/.

Sources indicate that developed by Japanese video game company SNK, “Metal Slug” is a classic run-and-gun video game franchise that follows elite soldiers as they battle the ruthless Rebel Army and other formidable enemies like aliens, yetis and zombies in a fight to save the world. Known for its fast-paced action, distinctive hand-drawn animation and pixel art, over-the-top weaponry and explosive combat, the franchise blends intense military gameplay with irreverent humor and a colorful cast of characters.

Recent findings suggest that the “Metal Slug” movie is one of several SNK video game-inspired projects in the works at Feig’s The Arena, which was founded in October 2025 with backing from Saudi Arabia’s MBC Group and MiSK Group, the owner of SNK. The Arena is producing a “Fatal Fury” film adaptation from writer David S. Goyer and an “Art of Fighting” webtoon from online creator Brandon Chen. Additionally, The Arena is adapting Oliver Lowe’s upcoming novel “Kill Current: A Jon Hendricks Thriller,” starring Keanu Reeves, as well as a feature film adaptation of the video game “Samurai Spirits,” with Laeta Kalogridis set to write the screenplay.

Morosini is best known for his breakout role as the writer, director and star of “I Love My Dad,” which won both the Grand Jury Prize and Audience Award at its 2022 SXSW premiere before being distributed by Magnolia Pictures. Up next, Morosini is writing and producing Lionsgate’s “Mommy’s Home” with Dave Franco and Willa Fitzgerald and Chernin Entertainment’s erotic thriller “Palm Grove,” starring Kate Hudson and Ana de Armas. Morosini starred in Netflix’s “It’s What’s Inside,” which premiered at the 2024 Sundance Film Festival and made headlines with the fifth-largest sale in the festival’s history at $17 million.

Morosini is repped by CAA, and Matt Rosen and Eric Brooks at Goodman Genow Schenkman Smelkinson + Christopher, LLP.
