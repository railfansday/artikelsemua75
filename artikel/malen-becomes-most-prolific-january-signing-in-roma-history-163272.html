On Sunday  at the Tardini, for many, it was like reliving a scene they’d seen before.

Specifically, February 2001, when a center forward wearing the number 18 in the Giallorossi’s away section led his team to a magnificent brace, turning the match against Parma around and perhaps setting one of the key gems in the crown of Roma’s third Scudetto.

Recent findings suggest that as Corriere della Sera recalls, Malen’s performance yesterday reminded many of Gabriel Omar Batistuta. The difference? Bobby scored four yesterday against a combative and determined Parma, further increasing his goal-scoring average for the Giallorossi with truly extraordinary numbers.

A few numbers to get a clear idea of ​​what a remarkable intuition Malen had for Roma last January: in the last 40 years of Giallorossi matches, only Vincenzo Montella—on March 10, 2002, in a 5-1 derby win—had managed to score four goals in 90 minutes.

26 league goals in the calendar year 2026, no one in Europe like him; Ballon d’Or winner Harry Kane—for example—is on 21. And again: 12 goals in 11 league appearances and 16 overall this season. Malen is the dominator. Even his teammates know it, having in fact pushed him to the front row at the end of the match to receive the applause of his fans. The Dutchman had the ball under his arm.
