So what’s next for the duo, who both lost the $246,300 prize pot to Shane Beatty?

“That is a great question,” Victor told E! News in a an exclusive interview. “I mean, can a Traitor and a Faithful figure it out?”.

Chimed in Katie, “I guess it’s star-crossed lovers.".

And while they did go on a date after the show, they’re still, understandably, working out some trust challenges.

Recent findings suggest that “Funny enough, I always tell him, like, I feel like I can’t trust him,” Katie shared. "And then he’ll always be like, ‘You’re the Traitor. I don’t think you seem to remember that.’”.

Joked Victor, perhaps one of the show’s greatest Traitor hunters, “Outside of the castle, she has the trust issues.".

Of course, Katie wasn’t the only contestant to catch Victor's eye. Earlier in the season, he confessed he enjoyed “talking to pretty girls” and even sparked romance rumors with fellow Faithful Madeline Kostopulos.

According to recent reports, but their castle connection didn’t last. “He’s got some wandering eyes,” Madeline, who was banished at the first Round Table, told E!’s Hot Goss in October. “I don’t think he stayed loyal to me once I left.”.

In a recent development, the next season of The Traitors premieres Jan. 14, 2027 on Peacock and will return to an all-celebrity cast, including The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives’ Whitney Leavitt, Love Island narrator Iain Stirling, and Bachelorette runner-up Joey Graziadei.
