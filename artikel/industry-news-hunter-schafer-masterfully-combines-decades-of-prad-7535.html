In a recent development, the Blade Runner franchise imagines a bleak dystopian future, one where the world has been ravaged by nuclear war and humans are burdened with inequality and threatened by rogue robots. But if everyone dressed like Hunter Schafer, how bad could it really be?

While promoting her new miniseries, Blade Runner 2099—which serves as a sequel to the Blade Runner (1982) and Blade Runner 2049 (2017) movies—Schafer has been on a hot streak of futuristic, otherworldly looks.

Sources indicate that so far, she’s worn everything from a neon-green Nihl hoodie covered in beaded florals to a double-layer Lii minidress that appeared to peel open from the back. Now, her latest ensemble plays with time in a different way—by looking space-age, sure, but also by uniting decades of fashion history. At the Blade Runner 2099 photocall in London earlier today, Schafer stepped out in a pop-art inspired look that combined periods of Prada fashion.

Styled by Dara, Schafer embraced the exposed-underwear trend with a satin, cerulean bra, which was paired with a white wrap-around midi skirt with a patch of neon-green color. Both were pulled directly from the Prada Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear collection by Miuccia Prada and Raf Simons. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

While Schafer has long been a supporter of the storied Italian brand, here, she and Dara pulled out a reference for the true fans, layering on a transparent, PVC raincoat pulled from Prada’s Fall 2002 collection.

In a recent development, with its thick black trim, the vinyl outerwear could have been plucked straight from the pages of a comic book. And to match the edges, the coat was cinched with an inky leather belt, while Schafer wore a pair of pointy black heels down low. For a finishing touch, she rocked electric-blue eyeshadow that winked to the color of her bra.

Sometimes, it can be hard to feel optimistic about the future. But at least Schafer is here to imagine one that’s wonderfully colorful, fun, and stylish.
