Leighton Meester has fallen more in love with Adam Brody as the years have gone by.

Industry observers note that “It’s amazing that when you marry someone, you’re so, so, so in love and you think of the future and you fantasize and talk about it and proposal,” Meester, 40, told Elle in a profile published Wednesday, November 18. “Then you find yourself in the future, and somehow you’re even more in love.”. She continued, “The intimacy and the love and the depth of the relationship is that much more, and you’re like, ‘I never thought that I could love you more.’ Life is filled with ups and downs and going through everything together, you get closer and it’s kind of hard to believe that’s possible.”. (Related: The Positive Personality Trait Linked To Having A Crush...)

Meester and Brody, 46, have been married since February 2014, going on to welcome two children together in August 2015 and October 2020, respectively.

“I know that I felt this way [about love] when I met him, but even more so now, my life is better living it with him,” the Guarding Stars actress gushed to the outlet. “And to be loved and understood by somebody, especially when you think the world of them, it’s kind of surreal and it’s the best thing in the world.”.

Meester and the Nobody Wants This leading man especially clung to each other after losing their house in the devastating Los Angeles wildfires in January 2025.

“We are very, very lucky because we have each other and that’s the main thing,” Meester told Elle. “Actually, that’s the only thing, full stop, that we all have [is] each other. It’s just true that nothing else matters.”.

Meester and Brody’s marriage has also helped the actors navigate the ups and downs of Hollywood.

According to recent reports, “I think it does help, but I also feel like he’s the best person in the world, so no matter what job either of us did, I would think the world of him,” Meester mused. “ I do think that having that in common is really nice. It gives you a shorthand of being able to talk about it and support each other, for inspiration and input, and just an ongoing dialogue about what it all means.”.
