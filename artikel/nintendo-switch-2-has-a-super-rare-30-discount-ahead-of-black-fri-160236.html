We've been tracking Switch Black Friday since the OG console was released back in 2017. In the US, demand for Switch consoles has tended to remain relatively high compared with supply, and retailers haven't needed to cut prices.

Sources indicate that we're not expecting this year's event to be any better after Nintendo hiked the Switch 2's MSRP from $449 to $499 on 1 September amid the twin challenges of tariff concerns and the component crunch. We've only seen two real discounts since then, both of them to $469 at Woot (other than the tiny $5 discount at GameStop). The last time it came up at the start of November, it sold out promptly.

In the UK, it's more likely that the deals will stick around throughout the sale period. Several retailers now have £30 off the consoles, so there are options if one sells out (also see Argos and Currys).
