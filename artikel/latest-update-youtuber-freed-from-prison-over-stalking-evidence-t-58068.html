An Arizona judge ordered YouTuber Lane Myers released from prison after county prosecutors raised doubts about an email that helped send him there.

Myers, 43, pleaded guilty in July 2025 to felony stalking and harassment charges involving Shannon Walker, the wife of Tucson city prosecutor Matthew Walker. He was placed on probation under conditions that restricted what he could post about the couple. (Related: Could Meta fix its glasses privacy concerns with an LED...)

In October 2026, Pima County Superior Court Judge Douglas Metcalf sentenced Myers to three years in prison for violating that probation. Prosecutors remarked Myers had posted fresh YouTube videos about the Walkers and argued those videos prompted others to send Shannon Walker threatening and harassing emails. Investigators tied at least one of those emails to an IP address associated with the Walkers’ home, casting doubt on whether the message came from someone reacting to Myers’ videos. Who sent it remains under investigation.

Deputy County Attorney Rachel Stiles submitted the latest evidence under seal and discussed it only in broad terms at the November 17 hearing.

In a recent development, tucson police searched the Walkers’ home on November 2 and seized several devices, including Matthew Walker’s city-issued laptop. County prosecutors first asked for Myers’ release at an emergency hearing the next day. On November 5, the city of Tucson put Matthew Walker on paid administrative leave while police investigated possible misconduct connected to the probation case.

Industry observers note that metcalf returned Myers to probation on November 17, and his 2025 stalking and harassment convictions still stand. Stiles mentioned county prosecutors still believe Myers violated his probation and will review the evidence before deciding whether to ask the court to revoke it again. Metcalf scheduled a status hearing for December 8.
