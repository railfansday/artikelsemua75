Industry observers note that AC Milan would have found its priority to strengthen its attack during the next winter transfer window. According to information from La Gazzetta dello Sportthe Rossoneri leaders have made Nicolò Tresoldi their main target and are ready to negotiate a transfer estimated at 60 million euros with Club Bruges. At just 22 years old, the German striker has had an excellent start to the season in Belgium, with 14 goals scored for club, including eight in the league, five in the Champions League and one in the Belgian Super Cup. He also has four goals with the German Espoirs. Performances which convinced Milan to favor his profile over that of Endrick, also followed in recent months. The young center forward knows San Siro perfectly, where he scored a hat-trick against the Milanese in the Champions League last October. A supporter of the Italian club since childhood, Tresoldi would be particularly enthusiastic about joining the Rossoneri. His family and representatives would also welcome a transfer to Serie A, an element likely to facilitate discussions between the two teams.

Sources indicate that but to complete this operation, AC Milan will have to convince Club Bruges, who would not wish to part with their striker for less than 60 million euros. A substantial amount that Gerry Cardinale, owner of the Lombard club, would be willing to negotiate personally. The two clubs already have relations on the transfer market, notably after the operations concerning Charles De Ketelaere and Ardon Jashari. Milan wants to accelerate from January, even if an agreement could be postponed until next summer. The recruitment of a upcoming center forward has become a priority due to the offensive performance considered insufficient by the current squad. Gonçalo Ramos has only scored three goals in all competitions, while Francesco Camarda has yet to find the net. The latter could also be loaned in order to benefit from more playing time. At the same time, the Endrick track seems to be fading away, the Brazilian having recently reaffirmed his attachment to Real Madrid and his wish to continue his career there. The Italian club is now focusing its efforts on Tresoldi, with the ambition of swiftly strengthening its offensive sector to achieve its objectives in the league and return to the Champions League.
