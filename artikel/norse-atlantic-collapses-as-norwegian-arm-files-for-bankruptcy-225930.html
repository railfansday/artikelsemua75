Sources indicate that norse’s efforts to diversify beyond low-cost, long-haul flying ultimately proved insufficient to keep its planes in the air. The focus now shifts to what, if anything, can be salvaged from the business.

Norse Atlantic Airways has canceled all flights and discontinued ticket sales after its Norwegian operating subsidiary said it is filing for bankruptcy.

The collapse comes a day after the low-cost, long-haul carrier suspended flights and two days after it described its financial situation as “critical.”.

In a stock exchange filing on Friday, Norse confirmed that the board of its Norwegian operating subsidiary, Norse Atlantic Airways AS, had concluded there was no longer a basis for continued operations.
