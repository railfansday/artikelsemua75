Recent findings suggest that jamie Miller is No. 1 for a fifth week on the U.K.’s Official Singles Chart with breakthrough single “Babydoll” on the list published Nov. 20.

According to recent reports, the Cardiff-born singer first hit the top spot in October, becoming the first male Welsh solo act to hit the summit of the charts since Shakin’ Stevens in 1984. “Babydoll” is currently up to No. 37 on the Billboard Hot 100, a upcoming peak.
