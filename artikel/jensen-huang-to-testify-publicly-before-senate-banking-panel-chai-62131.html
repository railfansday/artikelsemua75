Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang will testify publicly when he appears before the Senate banking panel, a spokesperson for Committee Chair Tim Scott noted Friday.

Recent findings suggest that the South Carolina Republican confirmed earlier this week that Huang had agreed to appear, but the details of that appearance remained unclear. Huang, who resigned from the organization he co-founded earlier this month over a series of extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy, also reached an agreement with three House committees to appear for a closed-door, transcribed interview on Dec. 1.
