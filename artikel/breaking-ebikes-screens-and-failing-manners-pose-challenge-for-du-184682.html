Awareness campaign reminds public ‘traffic is a team sport’ amid less civility and more collisions on roads.

For decades the Netherlands has been famed for its proud cycling culture, with a network of 23,000 miles of bike paths used by everyone from children under eight to adults over 80.

In recent years, however, like so many countries around the world, it has seen a dramatic change in the type of vehicles using those lanes: no longer the preserve of pedal bikes, they are used by all manner of two-wheeled vehicles including electric cargo bikes, thick-tyred “fatbikes”, speed pedelecs, electric scooters and even some micro cars.
