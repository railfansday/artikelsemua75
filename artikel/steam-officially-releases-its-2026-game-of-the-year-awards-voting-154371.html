Steam Officially Releases Its 2026 Game of the Year Awards Voting

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Golden Joysticks has already weighed in on 2026's Game of the Year and The Game Awards has revealed its nominees for this year's best of the best. But one awards ceremony stands apart from the typical Game of the Year awards contest: the Steam Awards.

According to recent reports, on Monday, Valve opened up nominations for its fan-voted awards, which include Game of the Year and what promises to be a highly contested category: the Hall of Fame Award. That brand-fresh category will pit a list of winners of the Steam Awards’ Game of the Year category from the past 10 years against each other to celebrate a decade of Valve's awards.

From now until Monday, Nov. 30 at 10 a.m. PST, Steam people can vote on the following categories, in addition to the Hall of Fame Award.

The final nominees will be revealed, and voting for the 2026 Steam Awards will begin, on Thursday, Dec. 17. That same day, the Steam Winter Sale kicks off. That's one week to the day after The Game Awards 2026, meaning that if any PC game was slighted by that awards ceremony, Steam owners will have a chance to rebuke the TGAs voting body. (One thing that Steam Awards voters won't get to weigh in on is this year's GOTY frontrunner, Grand Theft Auto 6. Rockstar Games hasn't announced a PC version of the game yet.). It has been revealed that last year's Steam Awards Game of the Year was Hollow Knight: Silksong. Other big winners from last year's Steam Awards include Baldur’s Gate 3, Hades 2, Peak, Silent Hill f, Arc Raiders, and Clair Obscur: Expedition 33.
