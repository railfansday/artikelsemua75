EXCLUSIVE: Jon Kilik (The Hunger Games, Foxcatcher, Babel) has joined Jana Hojdová’s documentary Oscar contender Robert Richardson: The White Devil as an executive producer.

The intimate portrait of three-time Academy Award-winning cinematographer Robert Richardson, who collaborated with Oliver Stone, Quentin Tarantino and Martin Scorsese on many films, explores the artist behind some of the most iconic images in modern cinema.

Producer Kilik and Richardson share a connection dating back to their time at the University of Vermont, where both studied under influential film professor Frank Manchel in the 1970s. Sources indicate that their careers have also intersected through their separate collaborations with filmmaker Stone: Kilik produced Stone’s Alexander, while Richardson served as cinematographer on numerous Stone films, including Platoon, JFK, Born on the Fourth of July, Nixon. In a recent development, kilik’s other credits include Spike Lee’s Do the Right Thing, Malcolm X, 25th Hour, Da 5 Bloods; Julian Schnabel’s The Diving Bell and the Butterfly and At Eternity’s Gate; Alejandro González Iñárritu’s Babel and Biutiful; and Bennett Miller’s Foxcatcher. He also produced the first four films in The Hunger Games franchise.

According to recent reports, “It’s an honor to be part of Jana’s beautiful film that skillfully blends a lifetime of Bob’s personal archives with a front row look behind the scenes of some of the greatest films and greatest directors of the last 40 years. As Quentin T states in the film, ‘There is Robert Richardson and there is everyone else,’” noted Kilik.

Robert Richardson: The White Devil traces the unlikely creative partnership between Hojdová and the cinematographer, when she was a cinematography student at Prague’s FAMU film academy, and reached out to him for a university project.

It has been revealed that what began as a student thesis evolved into a years-long collaboration and personal friendship. Richardson granted Hojdová access to hundreds of hours of private video archives and welcomed her into his personal and professional world, resulting in an unusually candid portrait of the cinematographer.

Industry observers note that “I’m truly honored that Jon Kilik is joining our project. His work on films I deeply admire has been a great source of inspiration to me, and I feel incredibly grateful and excited to have him on board,” remarked Hojdová.

Featuring Richardson alongside filmmakers Stone, Scorsese and Tarantino, as well as Kate Hudson and Woody Harrelson, the documentary explores Richardson’s creative vision, uncompromising artistic temperament and extraordinary career.

It has been revealed that richardson is one of the most celebrated cinematographers in film history, having received three Academy Awards on JFK, The Aviator, Hugo and seven Academy nominations. His collaborations with Stone, Scorsese and Tarantino have helped shape the visual language of contemporary American cinema.

It has been revealed that the film premiered at the Karlovy Vary International Film Festival and had its U.S. premiere at DOC NYC in New York this month. It is represented by sales tech giant B-Rated International and seeking US distribution.
