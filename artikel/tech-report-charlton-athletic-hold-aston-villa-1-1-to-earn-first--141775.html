Charlton Athletic drew 1-1 with Aston Villa in the Women’s Super League on Sunday 22 November, ending a run of 10 league defeats at The Oakwood in Crayford. Flannery put the hosts ahead from the penalty spot in the 17th minute before Parker levelled in the 71st.

Sources indicate that the draw gives Karen Hills’ side their first point of the campaign after 11 matches. It came despite Charlton having only 13% of the ball.

In a recent development, lined up in a 5-4-1, Charlton struck first. Flannery converted from 12 yards to give them a 1-0 half-time lead, and the hosts were the more threatening side in front of goal across the afternoon. They had 12 shots to Villa’s 8 and built expected goals of 1.74 against 0.9.

Recent findings suggest that accuracy was the concern. Six of Charlton’s attempts went wide, compared with one for Villa, and each side managed 3 shots on target. Flannery, who was booked for a foul in the 56th minute, was replaced after 82 minutes.

Charlton are 14th with 1 point and a goal difference of -25. Their 1-0 win over Ipswich Town is the only victory in their last six matches in all competitions. Before Sunday they had lost 2-0 to West Ham, and earlier conceded six against Chelsea.

It has been revealed that aston Villa are 8th with 14 points and a goal difference of -5. They have now gone two games without defeat, following a 1-0 win over Birmingham City with this draw.
