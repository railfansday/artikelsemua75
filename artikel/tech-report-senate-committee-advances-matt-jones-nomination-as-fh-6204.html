Matt Jones’ nomination to serve as assistant secretary for housing at the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) and as Federal Housing Administration (FHA) commissioner advanced in the Senate on Thursday, moving through committee on a narrow vote.

The Senate Committee on Banking, Housing and Urban Affairs approved his nomination 13-11, sending it to the full Senate for consideration. Jones would permanently fill the vacancy left by Frank Cassidy in June and succeed Joseph Gormley, who has been serving in the role on an acting basis.

Committee chair Tim Scott (R-S.C.) noted Jones’ mix of public- and private-sector experience is central to his support. According to Scott, housing affordability remains one of the biggest challenges facing families across America, but if confirmed, Jones “will play an important role in implementing the 21st Century Road to Housing Act and helping more families achieve the American dream of home ownership.”.

Jones presently serves as HUD’s deputy assistant secretary for single-family housing. He previously was associate vice president of government housing finance at the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA), senior adviser for capital markets at Amerifirst Home Mortgage, and senior counsel for the Senate’s Finance and Banking, Housing and Urban Affairs committees.The 13-11 vote reflected partisan divisions over the direction of federal housing policy and FHA’s role in addressing affordability and ownership gaps.

Elizabeth Warren (D-Mass.), the committee’s ranking member, voted against the nomination, saying Jones’ office has “cut resources for first-time homebuyers and proposed weak rules for keeping foreclosed homes in the hands of families instead of corporations.”.

“We should vote to make life more affordable for American families. But these nominees will do the opposite, accelerating the Trump administration‘s disastrous economic policies,” Warren stated. If confirmed, Jones would oversee FHA’s single-family, multifamily and health care mortgage insurance portfolios. FHA at the moment insures roughly $2 trillion in mortgages. The FHA’s Mutual Mortgage Insurance Fund posted a strong financial performance in fiscal year 2026, even as its capital ratio declined from 2025 levels. The results have renewed industry pressure on FHA to cut annual mortgage insurance premiums to ease costs for first-time and lower-income borrowers. Jones would also inherit an agency that has seen a series of policy adjustments under HUD Secretary Scott Turner, including efforts to revise servicing standards and modernize technology infrastructure.

MBA president and CEO Bob Broeksmit noted the trade group supports the nomination due to Jones’ “extensive mortgage industry experience, deep understanding of government housing finance, and leadership of FHA’s single-family operations.”.

“We urge the full Senate to confirm Matt Jones before the 119th Congress adjourns and look forward to working with him and HUD to continue modernizing FHA programs and to improve affordability for owning and renting a home,” Broeksmit mentioned.
