In a nifty bit of cross promotion, Disney used its surefire blockbuster and a helpful coincidence to reveal an Avengers: Doomsday casting as well as promote a smaller animated title.

Earlier today, Marvel and Walt Disney Animation posted a video of Hailee Steinfeld, who’s in Europe promoting Disney Animation’s Hexed, ahead of its release\ next Friday. (Related: Breaking: Introducing Waypoint-1: Real-time interactive...)

In the clip, the actress says, “Hey, so I’m here with my movie Hexed, but it looks like they delivered a chair from a different movie.”. (Related: Japan debuts a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t connect to inte...)

The camera then pans over to show a director’s chair with her name and a green bow on it. Green is, of course, the key color in promotional materials for Doomsday. And last year, Marvel famously used director’s chairs with actors names on them to reveal the first swath castmembers for the film.

Lest those details be lost on fans, Marvel added the following caption: “Catch Hailee Steinfeld in Disney’s Hexed before she returns in Avengers: Doomsday.” Watch on Deadline.

Steinfeld appeared in Marvel’s Hawkeye limited series as Kate Bishop, the character who takes up the Hawkeye mantle from Jeremy Renner’s Clint. She also appeared as Bishop in The Marvels.

Now, it seems, she is a part of the Doomsday ensemble.

Disney and Marvel are releasing Avengers: Doomsday on December 18.
