Oddo: ‘Italy have to be patient with talent, Camarda couldn’t wait’

Massimo Oddo believes that Italy has plenty of young talent coming through, but has urged for players, clubs and supporters to be more patient and pointed to Francesco Camarda as an example: ‘He needed a steadier path, like Pio Esposito’.

Oddo, who worked with Camarda during his time as Milan Futuro head coach, spoke about the development of young Italian talents in an interview with La Repubblica.

Oddo pointed to teenage sensation Camarda as an example of how not to handle emerging young talents. The Milan striker is still only 18 years of age, but there is a growing feeling that his development has stalled somewhat after his first experiences in senior football.

By the age of 14, Camarda had scored a staggering 485 goals from just 89 matches for Milan’s youth teams with an average of 5.45 goals per game.

Industry observers note that he went on to become the youngest player ever to feature in Serie A at the age of 15 and 260 days old. At 16 years and 226 days old, Camarda also became the youngest ever Italian to feature in the Champions League. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

He was sent out on loan to Lecce last season with the aim of gaining regular opportunities at Serie A level, and though he did manage to score his first Serie A goal, becoming Lecce’s youngest ever league goalscorer in the process, he was only able to start on seven occasions in the Italian top flight.

In a recent development, since then, Camarda has returned to Milan, where opportunities are again limited behind club-record signing Goncalo Ramos. The emergence of Alphadjo Cisse has also meant fewer opportunities for Camarda with Milan this season.

Oddo, speaking to La Repubblica, claims that Camarda was too impatient to make the step up to Serie A and says that he would have been wise to copy somebody like Pio Esposito, who spent two years on loan in Serie B before bursting onto the scene with Inter and then Italy.

“The talent hasn’t just disappeared, but you have to be patient. I take Camarda as an example, one of the most promising young strikers I have ever seen,” said Oddo.

“He couldn’t wait and pair for his early jump up to Serie A. He needed a steadier path, like Pio Esposito.”.
