We interrupt your Grand Theft Auto 6 playtime to bring you the following announcement: there are more games launching today.

Yes, while 19th November 2026 will forever be known as 'GTA 6 Day ', a handful of other games are also braving the launch window and are releasing on Switch today. (Related: Tech Report: Course Launch Community Event...)

We know that your adventures in Leonida will take priority, and that's okay, but we thought it was only right to highlight the other experiences that didn't back down from the substantial boy's launch aims. And no, we're not highlighting everything launching on Switch today, since, well, the eShop is always bombarded with questionable titles that probably aren't worth checking out.

If, however, you're on the hunt for something with fewer Vice City vibes, here are some other options launching today.

Industry observers note that there's every chance that you have already played through Bedtime Digital's Figment and Figment 2: Creed Valley when they debuted on Switch 1 a few years back, but the Switch 2 versions are also launching today with some neat performance bumps. We're talking 60fps at 1080p and "enhanced graphics". Sounds ripe for a replay, if you ask us. (Related: Industry News: The Reformer - Pushing the limits of lan...)

Published and developed by room6, this is a roguelike all about gathering cute pixel art plushies to your aid and using their powers to survive a nighttime onslaught of monsters. Need we say more? It seems to strike that balance of cute/creepy pretty well, and we can see it being quite the time sink, with 40 recruitable plushies, 100 items and 20 costumes available to mix up every run.

Sources indicate that those who frequent Japanese merch stalls will no doubt recognise the Sumikkogurashi gang. This one sees the team of adorable little blobs tasked with helping out at onsen inns to ensure their smooth running. There's plenty of minigames and scope to expand the inns further, but come on, the cuteness is the significant draw here. Just look at those little guys!

It has been revealed that it's The Binding of Isaac again (again). This re-release features all of the game's DLC content and the chance to play it with up to four players online.

It has been revealed that desperate for more throwback shooting after breezing through Boltgun? Auroch Digital has got you covered. After launching on other consoles last month, Warhammer 40,000 Boltgun 2 finally arrives on Switch 2 today with even more strafing, shooting and scary monstrosities. The score features a surprise cameo track from composer Mick Gordon (DOOM, DOOM Eternal, Wolfenstein: The Fresh Order), which is an added bonus for music fans.

It has been revealed that naturally, the eShop will be home to many more debuts today, but the above selection gives a pretty good range of options (and a certain quality that a bunch of the other releases lack). Let us know in the comments if there's another newbie today that we've missed!
