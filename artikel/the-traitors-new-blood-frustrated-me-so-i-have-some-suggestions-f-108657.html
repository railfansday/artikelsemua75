According to recent reports, it’s not necessarily a bad show, but it doesn’t seem to understand how to present itself in a more appealing package. It has been revealed that for a long time, people used cliffhangers to entice readers or viewers to continuously return. It’s been part of the reality TV landscape for many decades. However, it’s not the only way to ensure viewership. The original The Traitors uses cliffhangers quite often, but it never felt as tedious as it does on The Traitors: New Blood. According to recent reports, this could be because many of the other versions aren’t using a one-episode-a-week format. They either drop multiple episodes weekly or many on one day. Usually, Peacock’s version, for at least a few weeks, releases more than one episode weekly.. The Traitors: New Blood starts this way, but eventually shifts to one episode a week.

The switch is ultimately when the cliffhangers start to feel excessive. The episodes feel like a lot of nothing happens just to rush a cliffhanger ending. The end doesn’t have to be stuffed with cliffhangers. Newly introduced Blood either needs longer episodes to flesh out the story better, or multiple episodes per week.

A major appeal of The Traitors US is that it’s full of familiar faces. We know these reality TV stars, so we can get excited to see what they bring to the show. The Traitors: Newly introduced Blood has some familiar influencers, but they are not as recognizable as reality TV stars and celebrities. Therefore, we want to get to know them.

Newly introduced Blood didn’t showcase these players well. Victor and the Traitors stood out the most, but I wanted to get to know everyone a little bit. I wanted more rooting interest and people I want to see again on other reality TV demonstrates. Season 2 needs a better balance of highlighting various personalities.

Fruitgate starts very interestingly and produced one of the season’s best episodes. However, the constant talk about it made me grow sour about the topic. It made me question if it’s fair that the Traitors have to make so many risky moves.

According to recent reports, the murders in plain sight have never bothered me before. They’re fun. However, New Blood’s version felt like it set the Traitors up for failure. I don’t want to lose that element of the show, but maybe it’s time to revamp it because having someone hand someone a fruit out of the blue feels too obvious.

According to recent reports, the Traitors: Canada Season 4 has a subtle version of this. The Traitors just have to bring a bowl of strawberries to breakfast. They don’t have to do anything but slightly encourage a few people to eat them.

I still have faith in The Traitors: Upcoming Blood; I just think the producers need to reevaluate the show and not misunderstand why it isn’t working for many dedicated fans.
