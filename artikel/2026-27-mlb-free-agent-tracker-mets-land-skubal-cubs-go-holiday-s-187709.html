According to recent reports, tarik Skubal is going to be a Upcoming York Met. The two-time Cy Young winner is joining the Mets on a nine-year, $450 million contract that will keep him in Flushing through 2035, according to multiple reports. That contract breaks the record for both total value and AAV for a pitcher (excluding Shohei Ohtani).

The Cubs, meanwhile, have kicked off their rotation rebuild by adding lefty Trevor Rogers on a five-year, $120 million contract, then added NPB slugger Teruaki Sato for six years and $120 million. ALCS MVP Freddy Peralta is staying in the AL East with a five-year, $125 million deal with the Orioles, while the Blue Jays have finally landed a free agent in Kaima Taira.

It has been revealed that now, the rest of the free agent class, including solid options like Jazz Chisholm Jr., Randy Arozarena and Freddy Peralta, are racing against the clock before the expected lockout after the CBA expires at 11:59 p.m. on Dec. 1.

Where will everyone land? CBS Sports will be keeping track of the top 50 free agents below.
