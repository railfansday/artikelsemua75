Industry observers note that luxury real estate developers are always looking for upcoming ways to appeal to potential buyers, with resident amenities—from wellness centers to bowling alleys to sports courts—becoming commonplace. The latest offering? A private glam room.

The Greenwich, a lavish 88-story tower in New York City’s Financial District, landed on the feature as a way to give residents the ultimate luxury—a little more wiggle room in their schedule.

“From the start, we’ve looked for ways to make life easier for our residents and set The Greenwich apart from other condominium buildings,” developer Gregory Davis, Director at Fortress Investment Group, tells Realtor.com®.

Industry observers note that “When we thought about who our residents would be and how they live, time stood out as the ultimate luxury. An in-building beauty room offering nail and hair services was a natural fit,” Davis continues. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

In a recent development, residents reportedly book the salon’s services weekly, with benefits ranging from quick touch-ups for meetings to full glam ahead of a gala or special occasion. The building leverages a concierge partner, LIVunLtd, to make sure scheduling is a breeze. (Related: Hugging Face and Graphcore partner for IPU-optimized Tr...)

“It’s a popular and convenient amenity, particularly for those who appreciate having access to beauty and wellness services without leaving the building,” notes Liz Wexler, Lifestyle Manager at LIVunLtd.

An extra perk? The salon sits on the 86th floor, 800 feet above the city, so residents can enjoy stellar views while they get primped.

According to recent reports, at The Greenwich, the salon is exclusively accessible to residents. The space also includes two private treatment rooms that can accommodate services such as esthetics and massage therapy.

The amenity doesn’t function exactly like a typical salon. Residents can reserve the space, or a treatment room, at no added cost and bring in their own preferred stylists. But for those who don’t have a makeup artist on speed dial, the aforementioned concierge partner can be used to book professionals through a “curated network of independent beauty and wellness providers with a range of specialities.”.

This approach seems to be preferred over having a dedicated glam squad on staff.

“Our residents are sophisticated Upcoming Yorkers who travel frequently and are often very familiar with the latest beauty and wellness trends,” Wexler adds. “Many have specific preferences, whether that means organic products, a stylist experienced with extensions, or a technician specializing in Russian manicures.”.

Sources indicate that “It’s not simply about booking a manicure,” she continues. “It’s about understanding what someone is looking for and finding the right professional to deliver it. When residents aren’t quite sure what they want, we’re happy to make recommendations and introduce them to providers they might not otherwise discover.”.

On that note, pricing is dependent on the provider and service.

According to recent reports, so far, manicures, pedicures, and blowouts have been the most popular uses of the salon. Residents have also booked the treatment rooms for lash technicians and estheticians who offer facials.

Soon, residents will be able to easily access cosmetic injectables.

“We’re at the moment developing a partnership with Bel Angé Medical Spa in Tribeca to bring a Botox pop-up experience directly to the building,” Wexler reveals.

The salon has become a useful spot for the building, which occasionally hosts resident events. In June, a “Sip and Style” party invited those who live in the building to head to the 86th floor for rosé, light bites, and professional dry hair styling.

“We were especially delighted by the lively mix of men and women who attended. It was a lovely opportunity for residents to connect while enjoying something a little different,” Wexler says. “We love being able to bring these kinds of experiences into the building and give residents access to wonderful local partners without ever having to leave home.”.

The Greenwich seems to be ahead of an emerging trend, with a number of upcoming luxury towers looking to offer residents similar amenities, too.

Three upcoming luxury developments in Miami will soon offer salon suites as part of the amenity package, including Bentley Residences, the Perigon Miami Beach, and Fouquet’s Hotel and Residences Miami.
