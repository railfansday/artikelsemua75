The San Antonio Spurs were without both Victor Wembanyama and De'Aaron Fox on Monday night.

Wembanyama was ruled out of the Spurs' matchup with the Sacramento Kings due to back tightness, the team announced shortly before the game was set to tip off at the Golden 1 Center. Fox is dealing with a shin contusion, too.

While losing two star players for a contest like that is undoubtedly concerning, the two injuries were described as simply "bumps and bruises" by San Antonio Express-News' Jeff McDonald. Both players are likely to return for their NBA Cup matchup against the Portland Trail Blazers on Wednesday, too. (Related: Taco Bell is bringing back the Steakhouse Boss Burrito ...)

It has been revealed that the game is the first that Wembanyama has missed all season. He's averaged 29.9 points, 12.3 rebounds and 23.9 blocks through the first 17 games of the season, all of which are career-highs for the 22-year-old. He's fresh off a wild 50-point outing on Saturday night, too. Wembanyama outscored the New Orleans Pelicans starters by himself in the first half before rolling to the dominant win. It was his second 50-point game already this season, following a 52-point performance against the Dallas Mavericks earlier this fall. Fox has averaged 12.4 points and 5.8 assists per game this season. The 28-year-old is in his second full season with the Spurs, who landed him in a deal with the Kings during the 2024-25 campaign.

Recent findings suggest that the Spurs hold a 14-3 record entering Monday's contest in Sacramento, which is tied for the best record in the league. The Kings, meanwhile, are just 2-14 and have only won one of their 10 home games so far this season.
