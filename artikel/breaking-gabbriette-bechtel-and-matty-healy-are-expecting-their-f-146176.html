Gabbriette Bechtel and Matty Healy have a fresh gig: parents! The 1975 frontman and model, musician, and Charli XCX muse are expecting their first child together, as per People’s report.

Recent findings suggest that the couple were photographed on a grocery run at Bristol Farms in West Hollywood, with Bechtel showing a visible baby bump. Healy carried several shopping bags, dressed in a navy jacket, gray T-shirt, distressed jeans, and boots, while Bechtel wore a black midi dress with a plunging neckline and simple black ballet flats. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Bechtel and Healy were first romantically linked in September 2023, when they were photographed kissing in Latest York City. In June 2024, Bechtel confirmed their engagement on Instagram with a photograph of her custom black diamond ring, featuring an elongated cushion-cut stone, halo setting, and triple-split shank. Her announcement, made following Charli XCX’s Brat show in Brooklyn, was fittingly irreverent: “MARRYING THE 1975 IS VERY BRAT.”.

The happy news comes just four months after the couple wed at Castillo del Lago, Madonna’s former Hollywood Hills estate, in July. For the occasion, Bechtel enlisted Matières Fécales to create three bridal looks: as shown in her bridal fitting with Vogue, these included a sculptural white silk taffeta wedding gown with a dramatic triple bustle and Stephen Jones veil, a reworked vintage Martin Margiela dress, and a leather after-party ensemble. “Bridal is whatever you want it to be,” she told Vogue at her final fitting in Paris.

Now, the couple are embarking on their next chapter together—and their biggest collaboration yet. Congratulations once more to Gabbriette and Matty.

Vogue has reached out to the couples’s reps for comment.
