It has been revealed that between Marcus Thuram and Inter Milan, the love story started with the Frenchman’s arrival in the summer of 2023, and his association with Lautaro Martinez has borne fruit. The former Guingamp player has started his 4th season with the Nerazzurri, and it is time to discuss a contract extension, with the current one ending in 2028.

However, according to La Gazzetta dello Sportthe discussions turned out to be more complicated than expected. Thuram hopes for a salary increase, which Inter is reluctant to give other than through individual and collective bonuses. Especially since in 2028, Thuram will be 31 years old, and the Italian club already has his successor on the squad, in the person of Italian international Pio Esposito. So, will the love story really continue?
