It has been revealed that the Small Magellanic Cloud (NGC 292), a dwarf satellite galaxy of the Milky Way about 200,000 light-years away, appears to the naked eye like a fuzzy white patch, comprising billions of stars. But narrowband filters reveal it is also studded with glowing clouds of hydrogen, oxygen, and sulfur gas, many of them stellar nurseries.
