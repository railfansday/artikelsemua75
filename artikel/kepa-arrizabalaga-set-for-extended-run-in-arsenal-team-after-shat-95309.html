Kepa Arrizabalaga was called into action as Arsenal dropped points against Newcastle United on Saturday.

David Raya was almost in tears as he walked off the pitch at St James' Park on 21 November.

Just before the Spain international's substitution in the 70th minute, he came off his line to get a corner and, in the process, seemed to hurt his left arm.

Raya clashed into Will Osula in the moment and that ended his afternoon in the 2-2 draw against Newcastle.

The 31-year-old went down with an issue earlier in the game, but continued after some treatment.

While the severity of the Spanish goalkeeper's injury is unknown, he is likely to be out for a considerable period, potentially months, judging by his reaction as he left the pitch.

Arsenal's biggest fear may be that Raya could be out for months if he has dislocated his elbow or shoulder.

The way the shot-stopper held his hand suggested it could keep him out for a significant period.

Recent findings suggest that speaking in his post-match press conference, Mikel Arteta said, "Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news. He will have an MRI assessment tomorrow, but at the moment I don't know.".

Raya has been the North London outfit's first-choice goalkeeper for several seasons now and he has been a major reason for their success.

Without the former Brentford ace, Arteta will definitely have to change the Gunners' build-up play from the back.

Kepa has been a liability at times for Arsenal since his move from Chelsea.

Arteta has chopped and changed several aspects of his starting lineup in recent seasons, but Raya has been an ever-present.

The former Brentford shot-stopper's possible absence will be a major blow for the Gunners as he seems irreplaceable.

When Kepa came on at St James' Park, he was called into action to make some routine saves.

However, the rest of the defence seemed reluctant to pass the ball back to the goalkeeper after Raya went off.

Illan Meslier is also at Arteta's disposal, but Kepa seems like the backup to his Spanish compatriot and may be set for an extended run of games during the festive period.
