Percy Jackson and the Olympians is officially back for its third season on Disney+. The new season adapts The Titan's Curse, the third book in Rick Riordan's original series and the first to have never been adapted before. With the first two episodes having just premiered, it's pretty cool to see all the great nods and references to the original material, as well as the key changes that have been made. (Related: The Positive Personality Trait Linked To Having A Crush...)

Kicking off with Percy, Annabeth, and the newly revived Thalia Grace answering Grover's call for help about a pair of fresh demigods in need of saving, the first two episodes of Percy Jackson season 3 waste no time getting to the action with a major monster fight, just like in the original novel, teeing up the book's major villain, and debuting a brand-new faction of heroes beyond the demigods of Camp Half-Blood.

Keeping that in mind, here are over 30 of the biggest and most notable Easter eggs, references, and key book changes in the first two episodes of Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3. (Related: Reading: 10+ Ways It Changes Your Brain And Mental Heal...)

"My Rescue Operation Goes Very Wrong" — The title of Percy Jackson season 3's first episode matches the first chapter title in The Titan's Curse.

Recent findings suggest that aegis — Shown fighting a Laestrygonian giant, Thalia Grace wields Aegis for the first time on-screen, an impressive shield from Greek mythology connected to Zeus and Athena, bearing the face of the gorgon Medusa, capable of instilling fear in even the most fearsome of monsters. When Thalia isn't using it, the shield rests on her arm as a bracelet, which she can activate by saying the shield's name.

Industry observers note that guests of Grover— While Percy, Annabeth, and Thalia are introduced to Dr. Thorn as guests of Grover's and potential recruits to Westover Hall, the book saw Thalia manipulating the Mist to convince Thorn and a fellow teacher that they were already students of the school.

Winter Dance Punch— While scouting the Westover Winter Dance, Percy and Annabeth stand near the punch bowl, which feels like a fun nod to Grover's improvisation in the opening of The Titan's Curse novel, backtracking after exclaiming that his friends "made it" by claiming they made the punch (not realizing Thalia had already used the Mist to mask their arrival).

It has been revealed that mrs. Gottschalk— While Mrs. Gottschalk isn't with Dr. Thorn to greet Percy, Annabeth, and Thalia as she does in the book, she's hilariously shown as the school dance's DJ instead.

Mythomagic — Just like in the books, Nico di Angelo is obsessed with the Greek mythology card game known as Mythomagic, which also features dice and minifigures. However, Nico reveals in the first episode of Percy Jackson season 3 that his dice and minis were confiscated by Dr. Thorn. Additionally, some of Camp Half-Blood defectors working for Luke in season 2 (The Sea of Monsters) were shown playing the game as well.

Annabeth Invited To Hunters— In the book, Annabeth was interrupted during the dance before she could tell Percy that she had been invited to join the Hunters and Artemis' Eternal Hunt. Percy doesn't find out till later, when Grover finds the brochure in her backpack. However, the show sees Annabeth telling Percy everything during the dance itself, confirming her intention to remove herself from the equation so she can't be used against Percy or Thalia and the Great Prophecy.

Italy, Washington D.C., Las Vegas—As Annabeth talks with Bianca di Angelo during the dance, it's confirmed that she and her brother Nico originally lived in Italy before they moved to Washington D.C. and then Las Vegas. However, Bianca's memories are fuzzy on the timeline. This is thanks to the manipulations of her and Nico's godly father and his efforts to protect them from Zeus, having had their memories wiped, with the duo then spending years in Las Vegas' Lotus Casino, which was featured in Percy Jackson's first season (The Lightning Thief).

"I Serve At The Pleasure Of The General" — Just like in the book, Dr. Thorn confirms to Percy at Westover that he works for "The General", the major villain of The Titan's Curse who's ultimately revealed to be Atlas, lead commander of Kronos' armies.

“We’re Not Here For Thee” — Despite her younger appearance, Zoë Nightshade has been a member of Artemis' Eternal Hunt and the goddess's top lieutenant for an incredibly long time, as evidenced by her use of older English vocabulary.

Thalia’s Spear — Just like Percy's sword and its more innocuous form as a ballpoint pen, Thalia's spear appears as a can of mace when not in use.

Industry observers note that artemis vs Helicopter— When Artemis and her immortal Hunters arrive to face the manticore that is Dr. Thorn, Artemis waves her hand to turn the incoming helicopter meant to pick Thorn up into a flock of birds, just like in The Titan's Curse.

"An Old Dead Friend Comes To Visit" — The title of Percy Jackson and the Olympians season 3, episode 2 is the same as the title of the sixth chapter in The Titan's Curse.

Industry observers note that wolves Standing Guard— When the Hunters make camp outside of Westover Hall, wolves are shown standing guard. This mirrors the book, which also had falcons in the trees acting as sentries for Artemis and her Hunters.

Recent findings suggest that A Quest Granted By Artemis— Unlike in the book, Percy Jackson convinces Artemis to grant him and Thalia a quest to save Annabeth after she was taken by Thorn via Titan magic. In the book, the Oracle grants the quest to Zoë Nightshade specifically.

Apollo’s Sun Chariot— Artemis summons her twin brother Apollo to transport the Campers and Hunters to Camp Half-Blood. Arriving in his sun chariot, Apollo's ride initially has the form of a Maserati before turning into a large bus, mirroring The Titan's Curse novel. However, both the car and bus are orange, while the car in the book was cherry red.

In a recent development, god of Poetry— Beyond the sun, Apollo is also the god of archery, prophecy, and poetry, hence his recitation of an original poem: "Forbidden children in the melting snow, cannot go where they do not know." However, his haiku in the book is much funnier: "Green grass breaks through snow. Artemis pleads for my help. I am so awesome.".

The Winter Solstice— Before leaving, Apollo tells Artemis that he'll see her at the Winter Solstice, an imminent gathering of the gods in just a few days where Artemis' presence will be crucial to motivate the other gods to take the threat of Kronos more seriously.

Thalia’s Dangerous Driving— In both the book and the new season of Percy Jackson, Apollo lets Thalia drive his sun chariot, which she does rather dangerously before crashing it into the lake near Camp Half-Blood. However, the show makes things more interesting with Apollo giving up the driver's seat midway to make a point about fate, the Great Prophecy, and Percy and Thalia's role within it.

Always Summer— Once they hit the borders of Camp Half-Blood, the snow is gone, and everything is nice and sunnier, with the comment being made that it's "always summer" at the camp for demigods. While it's true that the entire camp is magically climate-controlled, snow was allowed to fall during the winter months in The Titan's Curse novel.

It has been revealed that bianca’s Potential— Making their way to camp, Zoë notes that she sees Bianca's potential and the "strength of a lioness", teeing up Bianca eventually becoming one of the Hunters. This is a solid improvement from the book, as Bianca joined the Hunters while they were still camping at Westover, a decision many felt happened much too quickly with very little thought towards her brother. It seems the show plans to expand and present a more natural decision for Bianca going forward.

Travis & Connor Stoll— Travis and Connor Stoll make their official debut in Percy Jackson season 3. Both are sons of Hermes with the same mother. Travis is the older brother, while Connor is a couple of years younger (despite them looking like twins).

Clarisse’s Secret Mission— In Percy Jackson season 3, Clarisse meets up with Percy before she heads out on a secret mission for Chiron. In the book, Clarisse is already gone before Percy arrives at Camp Half-Blood. The mission itself involves setting up the events of the fourth book, Battle of the Labyrinth (fingers crossed for a fourth season!).

Annabeth Takes The Burden— In the show, Annabeth is far more willing and understanding of what it means to take on the Titan's Curse/Burden of Atlas, and she's not necessarily tricked or manipulated by Luke as it's depicted in the novel.

Quest for the Golden Apple— Luke mentions his past quest to retrieve a Golden Apple, the quest he received from his father Hermes, where he failed and received his scar from a dragon. This was the event that began his animosity toward his father and the gods.

Percy vs Thalia — While it doesn't happen during a game of Capture the Flag between the Hunters and Campers like in the book, Thalia and Percy still come to blows over losing Annabeth and Luke, with both featuring impressive displays of their power before Chiron intervenes.

Bessie— While searching for Annabeth's dagger in the lake, Percy finds and rescues a cute baby sea cow he ends up naming Bessie in The Titan's Curse, otherwise known as The Ophiotaurus, a creature that is far more crucial than Percy realizes.

It has been revealed that the Golden Fleece (No Dragon)— Thalia visits the tree she once inhabited before she was revived at the end of The Sea of Monsters. However, no baby dragon is protecting the Golden Fleece like there is in the book.

"Regulation Capture The Flag" — Seeing Thalia chained by the Hunters who'd been given orders from Artemis to keep the Forbidden Children at Camp, Mr. D notes, "That doesn't look like regulation capture the flag," a clear nod to the aforementioned game that happens in the book.

In a recent development, the Bane of Olympus — Just like on the page, Mr. D has no idea who or what "The Bane of Olympus" is, the creature Artemis went off to hunt alone.

Industry observers note that oracle Walking Around Camp Half-Blood— Another key parallel to the book, the Oracle of Delphi leaves the Substantial House's attic and walks around Camp, something it had never done before. Spewing green mist, the Oracle reveals Artemis being captured by the General/Atlas. Naturally, the Oracle walking around Camp explains the aforementioned chapter and episode title.

The Titan’s Curse Prophecy — The Oracle uses a vision of Annabeth to give this season's prophecy, and the wording is the same as it is in The Titan's Curse book:.

"Five Shall Go West To The Goddess In Chains, One Will Shall Be Lost In The Land Without Rain, The Bane of Olympus Highlights The Trail, Campers and Hunters Combined Prevail, The Titan’s Curse Must One Withstand, And One Shall Perish By A Parent’s Hand".

Industry observers note that the Oracle Chooses The Five — While a big meeting was held to decide the five quest-goers in the book, the Oracle chooses the five herself in Percy Jackson season 3. The first four chosen are Zoë, Phoebe, Bianca, and Grover, with tendrils of green mist wrapping around each of them. The fifth member will either be Thalia or Percy, with the episode cutting to the credits before the choice is revealed. In the book, Thalia is officially chosen, while Percy ignores the rules and joins the quest anyway after Phoebe's incapacitated via a prank from the Stoll brothers before the quest even begins. It will be interesting to see how this plays out on-screen.

End Credits Mural— Like the previous two seasons of Percy Jackson, the end credits feature a large frieze/mural depicting the events of The Titan's Curse in its entirety, featuring major moments like the fight at the Air and Space Museum, the giant bronze automaton Talos, San Francisco, and more.
