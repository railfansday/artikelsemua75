It has been revealed that and that's caught Liverpool's eye, it seems. Christian Falk claims they've now got a 'concrete' interest in Wael Mohya, who is lighting the Bundesliga up for Borussia Mönchengladbach.

Recent findings suggest that jürgen Klopp actually had a look at Mohya for Germany in the latest international break - a massive show of faith in the 17-year-old, even if he didn't make his debut. But it feels as though it's just a matter of time before he's in that senior team.

Recent findings suggest that and maybe in Liverpool's, too. They view Mohya as potential backup for Wirtz, as well as long-term competition for the player. They're not alone in their pursuit, mind, as Brighton would also like to make him their latest young gamble.

It has been revealed that we imagine Mohya will cost a fortune for an 18-year-old next year, though it won't be in January. Gladbach are trying to tie him down to a fresh deal and refuse to sell in the winter window - so it'll be the summer at the earliest if Liverpool are to get ahold of him.

In a recent development, bayer Leverkusen and Bayern Munich are both keen admirers as well, meaning the price here could actually get out of hand. But Liverpool are fans and certainly want a player in that position. They'll just be tested for how much they want that player to be Mohya.
