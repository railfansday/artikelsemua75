According to recent reports, carlos Augusto and his agent have arrived at Inter headquarters to sign the improved contract until June 2031. ‘The objective was to recognise his latest status.’.

The versatile defender has been one of the most reliable figures in the squad, especially under Cristian Chivu, playing as part of the back three or in a more advanced role on the left as a wingback in place of Federico Dimarco.

He even found a knack for scoring big goals, netting four with three assists in just 14 competitive appearances so far this season.

However, he was still one of the lowest-paid figures in the squad, pocketing €2.2m per season plus performance-related bonuses.

According to various reports, the focus of the talks were to reflect those performances with an improved salary, which is now expected to start at €3.5m plus add-ons.

“That was the objective of the negotiations in the end, to recognise his newly introduced status,” confirmed agent Beppe Riso as he walked into the club headquarters this afternoon for the signing. (Related: Tiny Agents: an MCP-powered agent in 50 lines of code...)

The 27-year-old joined Inter from Monza in the summer of 2023, initially on loan with obligation to buy for a total of €15.25m.

His current contract was due to expire in June 2028, but Carlos Augusto has now committed to the San Siro giants until 2031.
