Mauricio Pochettino lamented the "completely unfair" decision to send off Leny Yoro in Manchester United's 4-0 thumping at Liverpool on Sunday.

Pochettino made a nightmare start to life as United boss, with a drubbing at the hands of their old rivals, with Yoro's eighth-minute red card setting the tone.

After Matthijs de Ligt misjudged a bouncing ball, Alexander Isak raced in on goal and Yoro clumsily brought him down outside the box, resulting in a straight red card for denying a goal-scoring opportunity.

Dominik Szoboszlai netted from the resulting free-kick, with Florian Wirtz turning in an acrobatic volley to double Liverpool's lead three minutes later.

Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz added gloss to the scoreline in the second half of a dominant display from Liverpool as United struggled to truly test Alisson until first-half stoppage time.

Pochettino insisted Yoro should not have been sent off, believing Isak was not in control at the time of the ball when the challenge happened. (Related: Animal control called to rescue alligator that turned o...)

"So, so painful. So disappointed," he told Sky Sports. "It's not in your head to start here in the Premier League in this situation.

"The only problem was that the game changed completely after six minutes, and now we can talk and explain everything, but the damage is done.

Industry observers note that "It's so painful. The players are feeling the pain of this type of situation. I told them they need to be calm, and it was a completely unfair situation that happened. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

"And also we need to accept to increase our concentration and also make better decisions on the field, and be much better. We need to be critical of ourselves, but also at the same time, we need to put some situations that happened during the game that we are not happy.

"The ball was going to the keeper. I think in that situation, if you review the VAR, you can see, like we see on the cameras now, that he [Leny Yoro] never had the intention to connect with [Alexander] Isak. The striker saw that the ball was going to the keeper, and he delayed a little bit of his run and goes down.

"But if you go two minutes before. Sixth minute. Jacquet on Mbeumo, they are similar, no? When you compare the actions, that's a penalty, no?

"And if we score after six minutes, for sure we are talking in a different way. But that is sometimes one of the things where we need to take responsibility, and I am here to take responsibility and keep pushing.

"I'm not going to change what happened, and being respectful is always notable. At the same time, I need to defend my club and say what I feel. I feel like that was an action that everyone who plays football can see was clearly not a red card.".

Pochettino became the first manager in Premier League history to see his team concede twice and receive a red card in the first half of his first match in charge of a club.

He also suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a United manager in his first game, along with Lal Halditch, who suffered a 4-0 defeat in October 1926 against Bolton Wanderers in the top-flight.

Accounting for three points per win all-time, United’s eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since 1972-73 (seven), and that tally at the moment leaves them 19th in the table, only one point above bottom-place Crystal Palace.

Pochettino has a mammoth task on his hands to turn things around, but he is determined to get United improving soon.

"I told the players [it was a] completely unfair situation, but when a situation is like this, to turn a negative to positive is we need to make double effort," Pochettino told BBC Sport when asked how his side responded after going 2-0 down so early.

According to recent reports, "More concentration, more focus, care more about everything. We have players, and in a way, after the 12th minute, when we conceded and started to play with 10 men, the players were committed, and they tried everything.

It has been revealed that "We made effort, we ran, we tried. A very difficult team, a team that found the goal when there was the red card. Every day, we will find the possibility to improve.".
