According to recent reports, michael B. Jordan has an absolutely massive fantasy project on the way with Prime Video. It seems that, in recent years, this genre has completely overtaken the entertainment industry, but that only makes it that much harder to make an impact. The success of Game of Thrones inspired a massive wave of projects, some of which succeeded while many more have failed. With this oversaturation, it's difficult to predict what will land with viewers, and there's no longer a guarantee that TV highlights based on popular fantasy book series will succeed. Still, there are exceptions.

The coming years will bring with them the Prime Video TV show Fourth Wing, based on Rebecca Yarros' bestselling The Empyrean romantasy book series. When looking at the premise of these novels, it's easy to see how they became such hits. The story revolves around the protagonist, Violet Sorrengail, as she navigates a fantasy world that demands brutal effort to become a dragon rider. It's success or death, and this dynamic is further intensified by Fourth Wing's open-door, steamy romance. (Related: Latest Update: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Sources indicate that amazon MGM Studios officially ordered the Fourth Wing series in May 2026, so the project is still in the early stages of development. Still, we already know that there are some truly excellent names attached. Michael B. Jordan is executive producing through his organization, Outlier Society, while Meredith Averill (The Haunting of Hill House) will serve as showrunner. Yarros herself is closely involved with the Fourth Wing TV show, as she will executive-produce alongside Athena Wickham, Elizabeth Raposo, and Sherryl Clark. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

Recent findings suggest that as further evidence that Prime Video is taking its Fourth Wing TV series extremely seriously, the first small round of casting has already been completed. Jordan previously stated that he wanted relative newcomers to star in the series, thus avoiding cheesy pandering that might break the immersion. Prime followed through with the news that Esther McGregor will play Violet Sorrengail while Emilio Sakraya takes on the role of Xaden Riorson. Suffice it to say, Fourth Wing is gaining easy momentum, so it's clear this project is expected to be a pretty major deal.

There have been several hopeful fantasy projects to hit the scene in the 2020s, many of which were expected to be as major a deal as Game of Thrones. Prime Video's The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power is the most expensive fantasy TV show ever made—a significant indicator that MGM planned for it to be its flagship fantasy after previous failures. While Rings of Power has done well in its own way, it by no means struck the scene as the same sort of fantasy event as Game of Thrones. This comes down to a primary struggle that Fourth Wing shouldn't face.

It has been revealed that when it comes down to it, Rings of Power requires a lot from its viewers. This is a prequel in a larger franchise, which means audiences must have some foundational knowledge of Lord of the Rings. This makes it a niche project regardless of the budget. The same is true for other fantasy TV shows of the decade. HBO expanded Game of Thrones into House of the Dragon and A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, neither of which has been quite as substantial as their parent series. Even fantasy projects like The Witcher, which picks up midway within the lore, ask too much of mainstream viewers.

Industry observers note that fourth Wing is uniquely equipped to avoid this problem while delivering on all the key high points of the fantasy genre. As a dragon rider story full of adult themes, this show will check off some of the same boxes as Game of Thrones. As a brand-fresh TV show that picks up right at the beginning of a fairly recently established fantasy adventure, Fourth Wing will be highly accessible to any adult viewer who happens to tune in. This is, essentially, the birth of something new—a chance to experience the fantasy event of the decade from day one.
