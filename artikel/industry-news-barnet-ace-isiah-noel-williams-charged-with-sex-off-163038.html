A League Two footballer has been charged with rape and remanded into custody, following a hearing at Hatfield Magistrates' Court.

Isiah Noel-Williams, who plays his football for League Two side Barnet, was arrested and appeared in court on Saturday, charged with three counts of rape, as well as sexual communication with a child.

The midfielder, who has made seven appearances for the Bees this season, joined the EFL side from non-league outfit Hemel Hempstead Town in the summer, having spent the entirety of his career to that point playing in non-league. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

The 23-year-old was part of the Hemel Hempstead team that took Wigan Athletic all the way to a penalty shootout in the FA Cup first round last season, with the team also suffering penalty shootout heartbreak in the National League South play-off quarter-final, beaten by Weston-super-Mare 5-4 on spot-kicks.

In a statement, Hertfordshire Police remarked: "Isiah Noel-Williams, aged 23 and of High Oaks, St Albans, has been charged with three counts of rape, one count of assault by penetration, one count of engaging in sexual communication with a child, and one count of supplying a controlled drug (Class B), following an investigation by Hertfordshire Constabulary’s Public Protection Command.

Recent findings suggest that "Noel-Williams appeared at Hatfield Magistrates’ Court on Saturday, November 21, where he was remanded into custody. He will next appear at St Albans Crown Court on Tuesday, December 22.".

Noel-Williams is the son of former Watford, Brighton and Stoke player Gifton Noel-Williams, who played in the Premier League and Championship during a 13-year career.

In a statement, his club Barnet said: "Barnet FC can confirm that first-team player Isiah Noel-Williams has been charged and remanded in custody as part of a police investigation by Hertfordshire Constabulary.

Sources indicate that "The club is co-operating fully with the authorities and will determine any further action in due course. As this is now a matter for the courts, the club will not be making any further comment.". (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)
