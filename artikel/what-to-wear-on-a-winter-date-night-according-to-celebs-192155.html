Dressing for date night is pretty simple during the spring and summer: grab a slinky tank top, slip on a mini skirt, and throw on a pair of heeled flip-flops. But once the weather cools, things get a bit trickier. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Recent findings suggest that oversized fisherman sweaters, corduroy pants, and snuggly slipper-like boots are all great for cozy, laid-back days. But sometimes you want to look a bit more elevated, whether you're heading out on a hot date or meeting your best friends for dinner. You want an outfit that feels special and isn't going to leave you shivering in the frigid cold—and Hollywood's biggest stars have it figured out.

It has been revealed that lola Tung, Kristen Bell, and Olivia Munn are a few recent celebrities spotted in cold-weather pieces, including funnel-neck band jackets, slick pants, and turtleneck dresses. Each celebrity-approved ensemble additionally includes rich winter fabrics, like suede, velvet, and leather, creating both a bold and warm fashion option.

If your calendar is stacked with dinner reservations and holiday plans, you're going to want to keep reading to discover a slew of style-forward early winter outfits, straight from your favorite celebrities to your closet.

Lola Tung knows the power of an all-black ensemble. To keep the ensemble from looking flat, she slipped on pieces featuring different finishes, like shiny leather and sheer nylon, to add dimension. This combination made each item—from her knee-high boots to her edgy band jacket—stand out. For added visual interest, Tung threw on a patchwork Coach bag in warm fall hues.
