By Eric Volkman – Nov 19, 2026 at 8:07PM EST.

Despite the monster debut of an important latest stock on Thursday, the market as a whole slumped. Not every title was caught in the downdraft, though, and one of its winners was veteran cybersecurity company CrowdStrike (CRWD +2.71%). Thanks in no small measure to the solid quarterly results of a peer that lifted many sector stocks, CrowdStrike's equity gained almost 3%, on a day when the benchmark S&P 500 index tumbled by 2%.

That peer is Palo Alto Networks (PANW +6.42%), which reported its first-quarter fiscal 2027 numbers just after market close on Wednesday. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

The firm, like CrowdStrike a grizzled cybersecurity operator, posted revenue that not only notched a new record, but was up by a meaty 39% year over year to $3.44 billion. Per-share net income also climbed at a double-digit rate, improving by 13% to land at $1.05 per share. Both figures were comfortably above the consensus analyst estimates.

CrowdStrike isn't Palo Alto Networks, of course, and it's got its own approach and strategy to combating digital threats. But the market is fertile for all kinds of companies in the field, since the threat of malicious actors is constant. This is compounded by artificial intelligence (AI), which can be harnessed for aggressive and potentially damaging purposes. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

In a recent development, lately, this worry has been compounded by a series of high-profile and widespread outages. Although the factors behind these incidents have yet to be definitively identified, they are, for the most part, heightening the need for robust cybersecurity regimes across all types of enterprises and organizations.

Industry observers note that if anything, these threats will intensify, and I don't believe we've seen the last of the outages. The solutions developed by the CrowdStrikes and Palo Alto Networks of the world are looking increasingly like necessities, not options. And that alone bodes very well for their collective future as businesses.
