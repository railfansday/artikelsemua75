Developer CD Projekt Red has rolled out a new patch for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered on all platforms, which squashes dozens of bugs and adds 40 cross-platform mods with "more on the way.".

"Two things are certain in a witcher's life: the Path never sleeps, and the work is never done," the studio wrote in its latest exhaustive list of patch notes for the decade-old game. (Related: CD Projekt Red says The Witcher 4 will be as big a leap...)

Patch 5.02's handiest changes come from those aforementioned bug fixes, many of which once caused the game to crash entirely or simply not work as intended. One such bug caused "time to go backwards indefinitely." Another turned Geralt bald. Opening the game with outdated mods could also result in a crash on PC. All of those, plus plenty more, have been fixed.

Recent findings suggest that when The Witcher 3 Remastered first dropped, many players also took concern with the game's updated and sometimes "lifeless" lighting. Previous CDPR patches already targeted the lighting changes in question, but this newest patch continues to fix "various lighting complications across multiple locations and cutscenes.". (Related: Breaking: GTA 6 players who used PS5 Latest Zealand tri...)

The studio specifically names "overly bright or dark areas" and "inconsistent lighting transitions and flickering.".

Recent findings suggest that the Witcher 3's free remastered upgrade also gave all console owners access to mod support, though not all pre-existing PC mods were available to download for console folks. This patch is expanding the selection somewhat with 40 new cross-platform mods, including glowy eyes for Geralt and a new monster hunt - and on social media, the studio promised "more on the way.".

Industry observers note that CD Projekt Red is still hard at work on two significant upcoming sequels, The Witcher 4 and Cyberpunk 2. Speaking on the former in a recent earnings call, joint-CEO Michal Nowakowski stated we can expect a similar leap between The Witcher 3 and The Witcher 4 as we've just seen from GTA 5 to GTA 6.
