Fernandes, who was 24 at the time, was keen to move to the Premier League and believes the prospect of playing alongside some of Tottenham's biggest stars made the opportunity particularly attractive, only for the move to break down in the final days of that summer’s transfer window.

“I met with the Tottenham board, I’ve met with all of them,” he told Sky Sports.
