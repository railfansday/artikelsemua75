Wolves are mounting their challenge for an immediate return to Premier League football and sit 3rd in the Championship table. Their talented squad are in impressive form, but Cesar Peixoto and co will have eyes on potential reinforcements ahead of the January transfer window.

Industry observers note that according to Sky Sports, among the players on Wanderers’ radar is Liel Abada. The 25-year-old winger sees his contract at Charlotte FC expire in December, and his impending free agent status means he is being tracked by a number of Championship clubs. There is a catch, however. Abada may not reach the requirements for a work permit, so he could be signed on a free transfer before being loaned out to a top-five league in Europe so he can gain the points needed to play in England.

It has been revealed that abada may be a name some fans are familiar with. After starting out in his native Israel with Maccabi Petah Tikva, he moved to Celtic in the summer of 2021 and spent a little under three years with the Scottish club. (Related: Industry News: Summer at Hugging Face...)

According to recent reports, he was a hit at Parkhead, managing 29 goals and 22 assists in 112 games. Abada has found success in the MLS with Charlotte FC too, with 11 goals and seven assists in the 2026 campaign taking him to 24 goals and 11 assists in 97 outings for the club.

Sources indicate that wolves target Abada can play on both wings as he is good with both feet. Without a doubt, his biggest asset is his speed and agility, as it makes him a threat when running at his man or getting the jump on the backline to run in behind. He is technically adept too though, allowing him to cut inside and link up with central players.

Industry observers note that abada has ended the 2026 season in fine goalscoring form as well. He scored a hat-trick off the bench in a 4-0 win over CF Montreal, and two games later, he scored twice in a 3-3 draw against Inter Miami.

After dropping from the Premier League, Wolves don’t really have a weak area in their squad. They have talented players on the wings too, so a deal for Abada isn’t a glaring need.

However, with Enso Gonzalez down the pecking order and Bertrand Traore’s impact limited by injury, a true specialist winger could help Wanderers in their bid to break into the Championship’s top two.

Abada would be strong competition for the likes of Rafiki Remarked, Mateus Mane and Rodrigo Gomes. He can join for nothing and further strengthen Peixoto’s frontline, and at 25, he has the time and room to grow into a potential Premier League player for the Old Gold.
