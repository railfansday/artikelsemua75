Aston Villa suffered a disappointing 0-0 draw at home to relegation-threatened Sunderland, making it three stalemates on the bounce.

After going on an impressive run of form, Aston Villa have dropped off of late, throwing away a lead against Manchester United, drawing at home to Fulham, and most recently failing to score against Sunderland.

Their latest performance and result will be particularly disappointing for Unai Emery, with Villa struggling to create too many clear-cut chances.

The Midlands club created just 1.03 expected goals against Sunderland, creating just one major chance.

Sunderland have given up plenty of opportunities so far this season, conceding 17 goals, but Villa failed to find the back of the net.

There's no doubt Villa missed a creative spark in the final third, with the likes of Johan Manzambi and Emi Buendia struggling to produce the goods.

Emery's treatment of certain players has certainly been interesting so far this season.

Taylor Harwood-Bellis's treatment was one that was disappointing in the opening months of the campaign, but he has started to give the former Southampton defender more minutes.

Alejandro Garnacho is another for Villa, with the Argentinian forward struggling to even make the matchday squad at the moment.

The most confusing decision, however, is the fact that Ibrahim Mbaye are yet to start in the Premier League so far this season.

The 18-year-old has played just 174 minutes for Villa this season in the Premier League, despite the Midlands club paying a whopping £47 million to secure his signature.

There's no guarantee that Mbaye is going to be the answer for Villa in the final third, but he is the type of player who can produce magic to turn draws into wins.

Manzambi was shoved out wide against Sunderland to allow Buendia to play in the middle, but the latter has produced just one goal in eight starts in the Premier League this season.

It could be time for Emery to start showing more faith in Mbaye, with experienced players like Buendia not producing the goods.

After no wins in their last five games in competitions, failing to score in three of those games, Emery needs to make some changes.

It was always going to be a difficult season for Villa with so much change within their squad.

The Midlands club sold so many key players and lots of experienced ones too, targeting younger stars as their replacements.

As a result, things are taking time to gel, and inconsistency has crept in. Despite that, Emery simply isn't getting the best out of his squad at the moment, failing to use certain players for various reasons.

It's unclear why Emery has his favourites and is reluctant to use others, especially when things aren't going too well for them at this stage.
