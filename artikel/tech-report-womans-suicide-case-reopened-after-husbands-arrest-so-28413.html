Authorities in Arkansas reopened an investigation into a woman’s alleged suicide after her son also died in an apparent suicide attempt, leading to her husband’s arrest.

Less than two months after Bridget Susannah Strauss died in September, her 21-year-old son, Michael Hudson Page, was found fatally shot in a Maumelle, Arkansas, home on November 7, according to KATV.

The Pulaski County Sheriff’s Office said that following Page’s death, Bridget’s husband, Scott Strauss, was arrested on murder charges in connection with his stepson’s alleged killing.

In a recent development, police initially responded to the residence after they received a report of an unnatural death. They found Page fatally shot upon their arrival, and authorities swiftly learned that evidence at the scene was inconsistent with the claim that he died by suicide. Scott allegedly told police he and Page got into an argument, and he later found his stepson dead, according to an affidavit viewed by KATV. (Related: Breaking: One Ingredient In Our Earliest Diets Linked T...)

Industry observers note that police reported finding blood under Scott’s fingernails, inside his ear and on his shoes, which his home was in a disarray. Authorities found blood in the kitchen, a bath towel next to Page and paper towels that appeared to have been used to clean up blood. Additionally, a chair was also knocked over, the kitchen table was out of place and ammunition was found near an opened gun safe.

Scott allegedly admitted that he moved Page’s body and relocated the firearm, according to the affidavit viewed by KATV.

He has been charged with first-degree murder and tampering with physical evidence, according to his online docket. Scott has pleaded not guilty to both charges.

Following Scott’s arrest, authorities have now reopened an investigation into Bridget’s death after it was initially ruled a suicide.

The Pulaski County Sheriff’s Office said in a statement obtained by KARK that “in light of recent events in Maumelle” the department has “decided to take another look at a previous case within our jurisdiction.”.

The office also confirmed to the outlet that the reopened investigation is related to Bridget’s death.

Bridget, 49, was remembered by her loved ones in an online obituary, in which she was praised “for her fun-loving spirit and warm personality.”.
