With newly introduced law, Iraq sees rise in child marriage, custody battles

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (73 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In January 2025, Iraq's parliament passed an amendment allowing families to place their household under religious law, governing all issues around marriage, divorce and inheritance. The fresh Ja'fari law, a Shiite law, came into force in October 2025. One year on, its consequences have been raising serious alarm, especially among women, who say they've seen a major rollback of their rights. FRANCE 24's Marie-Charlotte Roupie reports, with Josh Vardey.
