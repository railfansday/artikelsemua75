EXCLUSIVE: Six have joined The Family Disappears, the comedy with thriller undertones starring David Harbour and John Cena. They are: January Jones (Mad Men), Stephen Dorff (Old Henry), Patsy Ferran (A Streetcar Named Desire), Lionel Boyce (The Bear), Eric Rahill (Primetime) and Johnathon Schaech (Greyhound 2).

Character details are being kept under wraps. Also new to the project is Closer Media, which is co-financing alongside studio Fifth Season.

It has been revealed that at the moment in production, The Family Disappears follows an overworked dad who returns from a work trip to find his family has disappeared and a mysterious newcomer has bought and moved into their home. We announced the project in October.

In a recent development, jonatan Etzler, the Swedish filmmaker behind the Saoirse Ronan-led dark comedy Bad Apples, is directing from a script by Brendan O’Hare. Producers are Nick Weidenfeld through Friends Night and Alexis Garcia through CAT5, who together made the comedy Friendship, starring Tim Robinson and Paul Rudd, for Fifth Season and A24. Also producing is Dan Guando for Closer Media. Christopher Slager and Graham Taylor are exec producing for Fifth Season, with Zhang Xin, Jonathan King and Joanna Korshak executive producing for Closer Media. Watch on Deadline.

Jones is repped by Verve, Mosaic and Jackoway Austen Tyerman; Dorff by Mosaic and Hansen, Jacobson, Teller; Ferran by UTA, Independent Talent Group and Goodman, Genow, Schenkman; Boyce by WME and Entertainment 360; Rahill by WME, Rise Management and Schreck Rose Dapello; and Schaech by Link Entertainment.
