Night and evening work lets you earn extra money without giving up your days, from overnight stocking, security, and caregiving shifts to delivery and tutoring gigs you schedule yourself. Before you take an employer’s night shift, ask whether it pays a shift differential, a premium for night hours, since the federal minimum wage and overtime law doesn’t require one.
