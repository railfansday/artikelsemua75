Liverpool need to upgrade their team again soon.

The Anfield outfit have been left behind by their rivals on multiple occasions and, so far, this season has been no different. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

They're still in the Champions League places but it's been made more than clear that several areas of their squad are underperforming.

That is, despite over £600 million being spent on fresh players by Liverpool recently. (Related: Large Language Models: A Fresh Moore's Law?...)

Thanks to the Reds' clear need to change things up at Anfield, their scouts have been sent to eye up new players with Liverpool actually sending their scouts to watch one attacker "every week.".

Gustavo Varela is being eyed for a move by Liverpool.

Liverpool have focused a lot of their recruitment on their attack recently.

Mohamed Salah has exited and, with Luis Diaz and Darwin Nunez both leaving last summer, it was clear that change was afoot.

Last term, Florian Wirtz and Alexander Isak were brought in with Victor Munoz and Bradley Barcola being signed this season.

And, even though all four of these players have had their moments over the last year or so, more change is still needed.

That's why these latest rumours linking Liverpool with Monza sensation Gustavo Varela are rather intriguing.

"Napoli themselves have conducted a survey though not directly with Monza to try to anticipate the competition, which, in addition to Juventus, is fierce," Corriere dello Sport wrote.

"In the Premier League, Tottenham, Brentford, and Sunderland have inquired , while Liverpool sends scouts every week to monitor Varela's performance.

"The Serie A top scorer is also a hot topic abroad, but there's another factor: Benfica has a 20% interest in the future resale and the ability to match any offer that comes to the Monza club, having a preferential route.

Industry observers note that "In other words, if a €40 million offer were to arrive, the Portuguese club could match it (in that case, the outlay would be € 32 million ) and bring home Gustavo Varela. It would still be a huge capital gain for the red and whites, given that they paid €2.5 million for him a few months ago.

"He was close to joining Frosinone, who then preferred to invest in Tomas Bobcek, a mystery item in the Ciociaria's extraordinary start to the season.".

Gustavo Varela would be a strange signing for Liverpool.

Even though Varela may be the Serie A's top goal scorer at the moment, an out-and-out striker is not what Liverpool needs right now.

With Hugo Ekitike set to return from his injury in 2027, the Reds will have two number nines to choose from again.

Right now, Isak is massively underperforming so, whenever Ekitike is back to full fitness, he's likely to be handed a major role by Andoni Iraola.

And, if he can re-find his form from last season, the Reds will be in for a treat.

Last term, he was easily one of Liverpool's best players and, without him as an option this season, it's clear that Liverpool have struggled.

So, even though Varela would be a good option for Liverpool to have, especially since he's only 21 years old, there's just no room for him among Iraola's ranks.

That is, especially since Lewis Koumas has promptly become one of the Spaniard's favourite players to use.

€40 million is also a lot of money for a player who was only signed by Monza for €2.5 million this season.

Here's hoping that Liverpool focus their transfers on other areas of their squad.
