Mauricio Pochettino is gearing up for his first match in charge of Manchester United on Sunday, but work will already be underway behind the scenes on strengthening his squad in the January transfer window.

Man United travel to Anfield on Sunday to face Liverpool lying 19th in the Premier League table, which demonstrates just how bad their beginning to the campaign has been.

A point would be enough to lift the Red Devils out of the bottom three, and that will be the minimum aim for Pochettino as he looks to make a positive start to life as United manager. (Related: Stop treating agents as one-off projects and start runn...)

The 54-year-old will have to do that without two of the club's best attackers, with their first-choice striker Benjamin Sesko likely to be out until 2027.

GIVEMESPORT also revealed on Saturday evening that Marcus Rashford will miss the trip to Merseyside, despite hope that he, Harry Maguire and Luke Shaw would be available for selection for the former Spurs boss.

Reports have suggested that Pochettino has 'always loved' Rashford, having tried to sign him for Tottenham previously, and it will be interesting to see if they can strike up a successful partnership together when he returns. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

The absence of Rashford means that, unless he chooses to start Shea Lacey, the front four is likely to be Matheus Cunha, Bruno Fernandes, Bryan Mbeumo and Amad Diallo at Anfield.

Mbeumo and Cunha have largely shared the centre-forward role in Sesko's absence this term, but neither has been convincing in the role, and it is no surprise that GMS understands a new striker is a priority in the January window.

According to the Telegraph, their US sources claim that Pochettino could ask the club to sign Monaco striker Folarin Balogun, who has been in fine form under the Argentine at international level.

GMS sources understand that Balogun would be 'very open' to making a move to the Premier League in 2027, having come close to joining Everton in the summer after they agreed a £40m deal with Monaco.

A separate report has claimed that the 25-year-old would be ready to listen to an offer from United after Pochettino was confirmed as Michael Carrick's successor, such is the strength of his relationship with the 54-year-old.

If Pochettino wants him, and he is keen to make the switch to Old Trafford, then it increases the likelihood of a January transfer, as long as a suitable deal can be agreed between the Red Devils and Monaco.

It would be a big outlay for United to pay £40m for him mid-season, and perhaps a loan deal with an option or obligation to buy would be a better option for them, if the Ligue 1 giants would give the green light to that.

Balogun, described as being 'insane' by international teammate Christian Pulisic, could be exactly what United need too, given their struggles when the likes of Mbeumo and Cunha have played as a 9 this season.

The former Arsenal forward has scored goals wherever he has been, from Stade Reims to Monaco, and for the United States, and if the likes of Bruno Fernandes are feeding him, you would expect him to score goals in a red shirt.

GMS sources revealed previously that the Red Devils have an interest in Dominic Calvert-Lewin at Leeds United, although the England international is expected to sign a newly introduced contract at Elland Road.

Elsewhere, there have been links to Victor Osimhen of Galatasaray, but that is another move that appears unlikely given his salary and how much it would cost to prise him away from the Turkish giants.

Balogun could be their most realistic option in the winter market, and it will be interesting to see if he is the striker the club and Pochettino decide to make a move on.
