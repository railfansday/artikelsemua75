Chelsea are starting to have serious doubts about Xabi Alonso's position as a result of a miserable recent run of form.

Despite a strong start to life under the Spaniard, Chelsea have now won just three of their last nine games across all competitions, sparking a growing list of concerns.

A 4-1 defeat against bitter rivals Leeds United at Stamford Bridge last time out has done little to calm the situation, and any Blues manager would face pressure after that result.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Alonso must turn around Chelsea's form rapidly or risk coming under fire from all angles.

Sections of the fanbase at Stamford Bridge have already begun to turn on the manager, and while the board are keen to support him long-term, they could soon follow suit.

Alonso has proven his managerial credentials in the past, but further defeats like the one against Leeds will only ramp up the pressure on his position at Chelsea.

Brown told Football Insider on 9 November that Chelsea do not have any significant concerns about Alonso's future in the position, and are backing him to turn things around.

The manager recently explained that the side we see now will not be the same one we see come the end of the season, but should these results continue, he may not oversee that change.

The heavy defeat against Leeds, coming just weeks after they lost against another major rival in Tottenham, has raised upcoming questions about Alonso's role in the dugout.

"Chelsea have been pretty keen to stick by Alonso, but that can only last for so long," Brown told Football Insider.

"Ultimately, the biggest influence on his position will be whether he's winning games or not, so when they're in this kind of form there will always be pressure on him.

"But a 4-1 defeat at home against Leeds would be a disaster for any Chelsea manager, and I expect they now have some serious doubts about his role in that.

"It was a shocking result and won't have gone down well with anybody at Chelsea.

"Chelsea fans have high expectations, so it's no surprise that they've started to turn on him, but I can see the board going that way too if things don't change rapidly.".

Doubts about Alonso's position have already grown amongst the Chelsea fans and hierarchy, and there are now concerns that the players are also facing concerns.

In the wake of a heavy defeat aganist Leeds, influential players are believed to have raised concerns, with discontent growing from within the Chelsea dressing room.

Alonso has only been in the job for a matter of months, but is already understood to be facing significant pressure on his position, and things must change quickly.
