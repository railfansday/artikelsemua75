Clicks is still on track to release the phone with a physical keyboard within the next few months, but a Communicator launch before the end of this year isn’t looking very likely as shipping gets pushed back.

Industry observers note that the Communicator is a fresh take on what an Android phone can be. It was first introduced at CES earlier this year, with the main selling angle being the physically integrated keyboard. Clicks wants to target those who are looking for a second device, and the Communicator fits the bill. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

Last we heard, the Clicks Communicator was on track to launch and ship in December, following a cheery enhancement detailing an upgrade to 12GB of RAM and other changes. However, Clicks posted a upcoming update today stating that the timeline has shifted out a month because of supply chain issues caused by something other than RAM. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

Industry observers note that unfortunately, we’ve encountered a delay with securing a critical supply chain component needed to fabricate our printed circuit board ahead of manufacturing and final assembly. Assembly will now begin in early January, with deliveries expected to start in late January.

In a recent development, it notes that the “critical supply chain component” is the essential fabric used for the tech giant’s printed circuit boards. The blog post dives into some neat details about the component holding things up. The PCBs Clicks is using are made up of more material layers than some competitor boards. But the fabric used to layer those boards is in short supply at this sort of demand, with four manufacturers reportedly unable to meet the company’s required volume.

The post also spells out difficulties caused by troubles in the Middle East, and rising memory costs straining the chain of supply further, even if memory – surprisingly – isn’t the cause of this small delay. Those compounded concerns have pushed production back, and consumers are now told to expect their Clicks Communicator pre-orders to be shipped in late January, at the earliest. Many pre-orders will likely be shipped after that because of further time constraints before Chinese Latest Year.

In an act of good faith, Clicks says it’s including a set of 3.5mm wired headphones in each box for orders placed prior to today.

Sources indicate that A push from December to January isn’t massive, and Clicks still seems to be making solid time on developing a form factor we don’t often see. You can still pre-order the Clicks Communicator starting at $649.
