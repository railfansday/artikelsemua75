Preston North End, the bottom side in the Championship, arrived in east London yesterday unaffected by the occasion and kept West Ham at bay for an hour until Dinos Mavropanos headed equaliser restored parity and saved Scott Parker’s blushes.

Two weeks’ work during the international break had accounted for very little as Preston withstood everything the Hammers and their almost 60,000 fans had to throw at their lowly opponents.

Recent findings suggest that and therein lies the problem. Having surrendered the small, intimidating, sometimes downright hostile atmosphere of Upton Park back in 2016, the distance from the pitch and the wide open spaces of the Olympic Stadium are about as intimidating as a day out at London Zoo for our opponents and their fans who continue to pour scorn on the venue. It just doesn’t strike fear into opponents as it should. Even when we score.

Isolated tiers in the stadium and poor pitch proximity do little to help the atmosphere at the London Stadium – as our opponents are finding out.

Preston fan Tm Mercer, writing in the Lancashire Evening Post, makes it abundantly clear that there was no noticeable crowd reaction to lift the home side, as there should have been once Mavropanos restored parity:.

“Aside from play-off finals, I’m pretty certain it is the first time I’ve been in a stadium for a league match with 60,000-plus through the gate.

Sources indicate that yet, when they equalised, it did not feel like the roar of that many people in such a cavernous ground. Give me somewhere like Gigg Lane, stood in their paddock pitch side, any day!”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

For the uninitiated, Gigg Lane is the home of Bury FC with a capacity of around…..12,000.

Recent findings suggest that nobody is suggesting the Hammers fans were silent. The cursèd accoustics from the soulless bowl and sheer distance from the pitch play right into opponents’ hands. And, ten years after moving in, it’s going to take a lot more than some safe standing and a couple of family zones to fix it.
