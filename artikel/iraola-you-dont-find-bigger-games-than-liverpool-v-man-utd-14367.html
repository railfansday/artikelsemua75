Andoni Iraola believes "you don't find bigger games" than Liverpool v Manchester United as the two Premier League rivals face off on Sunday.

The Reds welcome Mauricio Pochettino's United side to Anfield, looking to close the gap on early leaders Arsenal. (Related: Tech Report: Estate Planning for Early Retirement: The ...)

United won this exact fixture 2-1 last season, ending a nine-game winless run at Anfield in the Premier League (D5 L4).

Recent findings suggest that indeed, Liverpool lost both Premier League meetings with United last season, as many as they had in their previous 18 (W7 D9). They last lost more consecutively between December 2014 and January 2016 (four). (Related: Hawaiian Interisland Awards — Hop Between Islands...)

Just 28% of Liverpool's Premier League points this season have come at Anfield (5/18), the lowest home ratio of any side in 2026-27, and Iraola is eager to see his side win at home against United.

In a recent development, "It is true that you don't find bigger games than this. I feel lucky to be here and privileged to be part of this game," noted Iraola.

"We have to acknowledge the importance of the game and all of its ingredients, such as their new manager, because that will be a challenge for us.

"It is vital for us in lots of different ways because we want to improve our form at home and the first chance, we have to do that is against Manchester United on Sunday.".

Ahead of their clash with United, Iraola revealed Virgil van Dijk, Ryan Gravenberch and Jeremie Frimpong will all be available for the match.

Should he start this match, Van Dijk will be starting a 50th consecutive Premier League game for Liverpool, the fourth time he has had a run of 50+ unbroken starts in the competition – the joint-most by an outfield player along with Sylvain Distin.

Aged 35 years and 137 days, he would also become the oldest outfield player to start 50+ consecutive Premier League games for a single club, overtaking Kevin Davies for Bolton (67 in a row aged 34y 175d).

"If everything goes to plan, they should all be available for the game," Iraola noted about his Dutch trio.

"It is a matter of pain for Ryan Gravenberch, he is managing his situation a little bit but he has trained with us the past two days.

"Jeremie Frimpong is recovering in the same way, the international break helped with that. Virgil van Dijk is also fine to go.".
