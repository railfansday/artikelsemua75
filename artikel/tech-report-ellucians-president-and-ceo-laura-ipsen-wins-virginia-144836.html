RESTON, Va., Nov. 23, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Ellucian, the leading higher education technology solutions provider, today announced that President and CEO Laura Ipsen has been named a 2026 Women in Leadership honoree by Virginia Business. The annual awards recognize accomplished women executives across the Commonwealth for their professional achievements, leadership, civic engagement and commitment to helping others succeed. Honorees are selected by the Virginia Business leadership team.

Recognizing Leadership and Impact.

Recent findings suggest that "I'm deeply honored to be recognized by Virginia Business alongside such extraordinary women leaders, and I'm grateful for what this award represents: the power of leadership to open doors for others," noted Laura Ipsen, President and CEO, Ellucian. "Higher education is at a defining moment, and I've never been more optimistic about what's possible now more than ever as AI powered innovation and human purpose come together. Every day I'm inspired by our team at Ellucian and by the institutions we serve around the world, who are reimagining what higher education can be for the more than 21 million students we support.".

Advancing Innovation for Higher Education.

The recognition follows a significant year for Ellucian under Ipsen's leadership, including the launch of Ellucian Student in April 2026. Ellucian Student is higher education's complete solution that powers the end-to-end student lifecycle, bringing Student, HCM, and Finance together on one AI-native platform to align people, resources, and decisions around student success.
