Bruno Fernandes has revealed how close he came to joining Tottenham under Mauricio Pochettino before his move to Manchester United in 2020.

The Portugal star is set to lead Man Utd out on Sunday in Pochettino’s first game as manager, but they duo could have first worked together in 2019.

The Man Utd captain, then at Sporting, held talks with Tottenham's board seven years ago and was impressed by their plans, including the role Pochettino envisaged for him and the club's training facilities. (Related: Breaking: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for Summa...)

Fernandes, who was 24 at the time, was keen to move to the Premier League and believes the prospect of playing alongside some of Tottenham's biggest stars made the opportunity particularly attractive, only for the move to break down in the final days of that summer’s transfer window. “I met with the Tottenham board, I’ve met with all of them,” he told Sky Sports.

In a recent development, “They highlighted me all the good things they had in their training ground, the way they wanted to play. They showed me also the positions that the manager has told them I could play in the system.”.

Asked whether he liked Pochettino's ideas for him, Fernandes admitted the proposed move appealed to him, particularly because of the players already at the club.
