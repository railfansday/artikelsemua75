John Souttar is struggling to attract suitors as he prepares to leave Rangers permanently in the January transfer window.

The 30-year-old has been cast out under Derek McInnes and has not featured since the manager's arrival during the summer, despite remaining under contract at Ibrox.

He was widely expected to leave during the summer transfer window, but failed to agree terms, and there are now doubts about whether a January move will be forthcoming.

Former Man United chief scout Mick Brown told Football Insider that Souttar will find it difficult to land a latest club as concerns about his fitness have now emerged.

Having not played for Rangers this season, the centre-back last started a competitive game of football in May, when he captained Danny Rohl's side to victory against Falkirk.

However, the lack of top-level interest in Souttar during the summer raised early red flags, and there is a growing chance he may have to see out the saeson on the sidelines at Ibrox. (Related: Compound Interest Calculator: See Your Money Grow...)

Football Insider revealed back in September that Souttar has no chance of returning to the Rangers side under McInnes and has been told to find a new club in January.

Despite failing to agree a move to any of his suitors during the summer window, there was a growing expectation that he would be more open to opportunities in January.

However, after months on the sidelines, any lingering interest in Souttar has faded and it is becoming increasingly difficult for him to find a destination away from Ibrox.

"I watched John Souttar years ago and I've never been convinced by him," Brown told Football Insider.

"He always looks big, heavy and clumsy, so it wasn't a surprise to me when Derek McInnes decided he wouldn't be part of his aims at Rangers.

According to recent reports, "The problem is, which clubs are going to want him? There aren't any top clubs who are going to be interested, to be honest I'd be surprised if Championship clubs wanted him. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

"So he was always going to have to think outside the box a little bit to find a latest club, but he's still struggling to attract interest, and the longer it goes on for, the less likely it becomes.

Recent findings suggest that "Mid-way through the season, you want to sign players who are ready to step in and make a difference. That won't be the case after he's spent months on the sidelines.

Industry observers note that "So it's going to be a real problem for him, where does he end up? Maybe he could stay at Rangers for the rest of the season, but then it's an entire season wasted.".

As previously mentioned, Souttar is going to have to think outside the box when it comes to deciding his next club, with a shortage of interest from top sides in Europe.

Football Insider revealed in September that Greek outfit Aris Thessaloniki were working on a deal to sign Souttar and had hoped to get an agreement over the line.

However, that deal eventually fell through at the end of the Greek window and left Souttar stranded at Rangers, at least until the mid-season window opens.

There are now doubts, though, about whether Aris could return with a latest offer after he has spent months on the sidelines, and he may be forced to look elsewhere yet again.
