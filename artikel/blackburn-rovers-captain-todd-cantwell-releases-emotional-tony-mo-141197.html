Blackburn Rovers travelled to bitter rivals Burnley on Sunday afternoon aiming to claim all three points despite Tony Mowbray's absence, and though they didn't achieve their goal, they undoubtedly made their manager proud.

Blackburn were rocked by the news last week of Mowbray's sudden decision to step back from his role at the helm at Ewood Park, citing health problems as his reasoning.

Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds have since assumed control of the Rovers squad, and heading into Sunday's crucial clash with Burnley in the Championship, the Blackburn squad looked to make their manager proud, and they did.

Industry observers note that burnley took the lead just before the half-hour mark courtesy of a strike from Belgian winger Largie Ramazani. However, the Clarets lead didn't last long, as Andri Gudjohnsen did what he does best and fired home an equaliser just four minutes later. In his celebration, Gudjohnsen held a shirt showing support for Mowbray, along with his Blackburn teammates, to the camera, in a touching tribute to the 62-year-old who previously beat bowel cancer.

Burnley would again take the lead in the 51st minute thanks to Dutch winger Jayden Banel, but Blackburn were insistent on not leaving empty-handed, as Gudjohnsen won a penalty which Todd Cantwell converted with 15 minutes to go.

The game would ultimately end 2-2, as Blackburn have made Burnley wait even longer for a first home win in over 400 days, as the players' support and admiration for Mowbray has grown increasingly evident after full-time.

Blackburn captain Cantwell, who netted the equaliser, joined in his side's celebrations for the first goal and subsequent support of Mowbray, and following the game, the former Norwich City forward has released a message to his gaffer via Instagram.

In a heartfelt message directed towards Mowbray, Cantwell claims that the squad admire and respects the 62-year-old greatly, before going on to acknowledge his impact on the team and everyone at the club.

Blackburn sit 13th in the Championship table after this fixture, and being five points off the play-offs and 11 clear of the relegation zone, have silenced some critics.

As for now, Johnson and Dodds will lead the club forward while Mowbray recovers; as Blackburn are not looking for a permanent successor right now, Mowbray's philosophy and leadership will continue to reign over Ewood Park.

Looking ahead to the summer, Blackburn have numerous contract issues to address sooner rather than later, with 13 players set to leave the club on a free transfer in 2027, including Cantwell.

Along with the Englishman, Balazs Toth, Oladapo Afolayan, Yuki Ohashi, and Harry Pickering also see their current deals expire, though the latter two reportedly have one-year extension clauses in their contracts.

Regardless, attention must turn to these contract situations sooner rather than later at Ewood Park, especially in the case of Cantwell, who has been consistently one of Blackburn's best players over the past few years.

His importance to the club cannot be measured, and it would be a disaster to allow him to leave for nothing, as this is an glitch that must be addressed ASAP.
