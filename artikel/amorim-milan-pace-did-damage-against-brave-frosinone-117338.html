Ruben Amorim explains how he made the most of ‘brave’ Frosinone’s approach to run riot in the spaces and focus on the ‘pace’ in his Milan side for a 5-1 win. ‘The team believe in the proposal.’.

It was a morale-boosting performance from the Rossoneri against a Frosinone side who changed tactics to 3-4-3 with a False 9 for this trip to San Siro.

It left massive gaps in defence that Milan took full advantage of with a counter-attacking masterclass, as after Gabriele Calvani’s own goal, Goncalo Ramos, Ardon Jashari, Alphadjo Cisse and Christian Pulisic ran riot.

The only negative was the Patrizio Masini goal to temporarily make it 2-1 going into the half-time break, while Goncalo Ramos went off with a muscular twinge.

“The main thing is that we have a strategy in the game, and the team believe in the plan,” Amorim told DAZN Italia.

“You know Frosinone as well as I do, they are all about full pressure, I understood that with a lot of points coming to San Siro, they would be really brave. With the pace we have with Rabiot, Moreira, Pulisic and Ramos, we could make transitions.

“We changed a little bit the way we play the game because of our opponents, and it was a good night for us.”.

Goncalo Ramos scored his first goal since October 19, while Jashari hadn’t ever scored in the Milan jersey before this evening.

It has been revealed that “We are building principles and understanding the game. We knew Frosinone are about marking man to man, that’s why Pulisic looked like he was in every position, because we knew they would follow us, and we could create a lot of danger with changing roles,” continued Amorim. “It doesn’t matter who plays, every player in training needs to understand every position on the pitch, so that we can change the characteristics. There are so many things I saw in the game that we need to improve, but we stuck to the plan, and that is something I am pleased with.”. When Ramos went off with a calf twinge, why did Amorim turn to Cisse and not Francesco Camarda?

According to recent reports, “You saw the game, we did not spend many minutes around the box, the space was behind. If we were controlling the game in a different way, Camarda was the right way to play, but Cisse is really good with pace. You saw the trouble he made for the opponent was not holding the ball, it was the runs behind, the passes, not a typical striker,” explained the coach.

“I didn’t notice it was Ramos who scored the goal, it was more notable that it was 2-0, because Milan need lots of goals to control a game, and also Frosinone had to put more men forward at 2-0, so it opened up more spaces for us.”.

Milan have won five of the last six Serie A matches, and consolidated fourth place in the hunt for Champions League qualification.
