Manchester United's new manager Mauricio Pochettino is likely to be able to repair the relationship with club captain Bruno Fernandes in the coming months.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who believes that the Portuguese doesn't have as much power as he thinks he does over his contract situation. (Related: Breaking: Japan debuts a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t conne...)

The 32-year-old has already rejected two offers of a new deal ahead of his current terms expiring next summer, but the club hold a one-year extension option in their favour. (Related: Taco Bell adds ‘Make It Flamin’ Hot’ option for scorchi...)

Wyness believes that the decision regarding Fernandes' future has effectively now been left for Pochettino to make between now and the end of the season.

Fernandes has been heavily criticised for his performances so far this season, but he still has four goals and two assists from nine Premier League games already.

Tensions had flared between the midfielder and previous boss Michael Carrick, but Wyness expects a different story under Pochettino.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Fernandes is still most likely to stay at United, despite rejecting two contract offers already.

Recent reports have suggested that the veteran's wage and length of contract are both up for debate at the moment, as he heads into the twilight of his career.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained how the "noises" coming out of Old Trafford suggest that Fernandes will stay for at least another year.

"Well, when I looked at it, there's actually a year option available to the club. So Fernandes doesn't really have the strength that it might appear on the surface," he mentioned.

Industry observers note that "So it's all going to be on Pochettino deciding, does he want him? And can he mend fences with the player if he wants to? I think he probably will.

"I wouldn't be surprised to see Fernandes staying and the club exercising that option. But again, they're giving Pochettino the chance to make that decision.

"And all the noises coming out are that he will exercise that option and Fernandes will stay at least for the next period of time.".

Football Insider revealed on Saturday that Pochettino is keen to try and lure Fulham left-back Antonee Robinson to Old Trafford this winter.

The left side of defence has been a problem position for United of late, and it is one of their top priorities to be strenghthened in January.

Robinson is a long-term target for the Red Devils, and sources say that Pochettino is also a huge fan of the USA international.

The 29-year-old is high on the Argentine's list of winter targets, and could yet become his first signing as United boss.
