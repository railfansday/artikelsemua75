In a crossover that is sure to delight God of War fans: the game’s cast member Deborah Ann Woll will reprise her role as Faye in Prime Video’s upcoming TV series adaptation headlined by Dave Bautista.

This is not a recasting on God of War, which replaced its lead Kratos with Bautista after the series had filmed for several months with original star Ryan Hurst whose serious injury halted production at the end of June.

In a recent development, I hear Woll was always the producers’ choice for the Faye role. She didn’t get to shoot in the spring as her character does not appear in the episodes that were completed, I hear, so this is a fresh casting. As Deadline reported at the time of Hurst’s exit, God of War, from Sony Pictures Television and Amazon MGM Studios, is starting from scratch with no footage from the original shoot being used. Filming with Bautista is at the moment underway in Vancouver on the series’ two-season order. It began earlier this fall. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

Based on PlayStation’s hugely popular ancient mythology-themed video game, God of War follows father and son Kratos (Bautista) and Atreus (Callum Vinson) as they embark on a journey to spread the ashes of their wife and mother, Faye (Woll). Through their adventures, Kratos tries to teach his son to be a better god, while Atreus tries to teach his father how to be a better human.Faye is the mysterious late wife of Kratos and mother of Atreus whose abrupt passing sets in motion the events of God of War. Along their journey, Kratos and Atreus discover secrets about Faye that make them wonder how well they truly knew her at all.

Woll portrays the character of Faye in the 2022 PlayStation video game God of War Ragnarök and as the lead character in the upcoming God of War Laufey (2027).

In addition to Bautista as Kratos and Vinson as Atreus, on the God of War TV series Woll joins Mandy Patinkin, who plays Odin, Ed Skrein as Baldur, Max Parker as Heimdall, Ólafur Darri Ólafsson as Thor, Teresa Palmer as Sif, Alastair Duncan as Mimir, Jeff Gulka as Sindri, Danny Woodburn as Brok, Sonya Walger as Freya, Island Austin as Thrud, Ben Chapple as Magni, Louis Cunningham as Modi, and Evelyn Miller as Gna.

God of War is co-produced by Sony Pictures Television and Amazon MGM Studios in association with PlayStation Productions and Tall Ship Productions. Ronald D. Moore serves as showrunner, executive producer, and writer. Additional executive producers include Maril Davis, Cory Barlog, Naren Shankar, Matthew Graham, Asad Qizilbash, Jeff Ketcham, Hermen Hulst, Roy Lee, Brad Van Arragon, and Lawrence Trilling. Joe Menosky, Marc Bernardin, Tania Lotia, and Ben McGinnis serve as co-executive producers on the series. Frederick E.O. Toye is directing the first two episodes.
