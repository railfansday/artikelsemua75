Sources indicate that universal Epic Universe is currently celebrating its second holiday season with more holiday decorations than last year and a fresh Dark Universe ride currently under construction. There’s even more gifts from the theme park during this time of merriment! It’s just been confirmed that for the first time ever Epic is going to be added into the Annual Pass mix. The news led to a mix of huge excitement and tons of questions.

In a Universal Parks announcement, the Orlando resort shared that annual passes featuring Universal Epic Universe access are coming in early December. Guests with current annual passes will be able to upgrade their current passes, and those looking to purchase one can do so at this time. Recent findings suggest that universal was all over the comment section trying to answer some of these questions. Most of them seem to point to there being another wave of details on the way, but for now Universal Orlando’s Annual Pass website has been refreshed with current steps to take if you'd like to upgrade.

Industry observers note that universal Orlando Resort will still offer four different types of pass tiers, but the announcement includes a complete re-branding of the system. Those who hold these memberships will be called “Pass Members” rather than “Passholders” and the type names will go from being called Seasonal, Power, Preferred and Premier to Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum. They’re also going completely digital with all physical passes being phased out over time.

According to recent reports, in regards to the Epic Universe upgrade, current Passholders will be given a timeframe for when they can upgrade their passes, and current holders will be able to switch before anyone else. Those who are in their renewal period will also have the option to “pay a price difference of $125 to $410 to renew into a Pass with Universal Epic Universe access, depending on Pass type.” Annual passes right now cost between $475 and $1015, based on that price difference info; the price range might have changed to between $600 to $1425, but all the details are not yet clear to anyone quite yet.
