Strahinja Pavlović did not mince his words when talking about Sérgio Conceição, his former coach at AC Milan during the 2024-2025 season. Guest of the Serbian podcast SS Calciothe defender denounced the particularly strict methods of the Portuguese technician, going so far as to compare them to those of Adolf Hitler. “Conceição banned Nutella and, since then, we have no longer been allowed to eat it. He’s a good person, I like him, but he had Hitler’s methods.”said the Serbian international, now 25 years old. He explained in particular that players had to wear knee pads and elbow pads in training, whatever the temperature.

Pavlović also mentioned that Conceição had assistants responsible for monitoring the players and reporting their actions to him. Three days before a meeting, five or six people had gone to a concert until 11 p.m. Informed of their exit, the coach decided to exclude them from the following match. The Serbian also returned to the Portuguese’s first meeting upon his arrival in Milan: he had deliberately been 40 minutes late, leaving players and managers to wait, in order to show, according to Pavlovic, who was the boss.
