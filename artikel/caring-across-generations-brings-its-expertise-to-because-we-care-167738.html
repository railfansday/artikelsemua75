Co-Founder Ai-jen Poo Joins Network Caregiving Channel to Elevate Family Caregiver Stories During National Family Caregivers Month.

UPCOMING YORK, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Caring Across Generations is contributing its expertise to Because We Care with Richard Lui in recognition of National Family Caregivers Month. Hosted by MS NOW anchor, journalist, filmmaker, author, and caregiving advocate Richard Lui, the series connects personal stories to the broader economic, workforce, and policy challenges shaping the U.S. care systems.

Launched September 9, 2026, as part of MS NOW Membership's original contributor programming, Because We Care with Richard Lui marks MS NOW as the first legacy network to launch a dedicated caregiving channel and community, convening public figures, family caregivers, and national experts to explore the realities of caregiving in America. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Caring Across Generations anchors the initiative by expanding support systems, shifting cultural norms, and advocating for policies that allow everyone to live, work, and age with dignity.

"Caregiving is the invisible foundation of  our economy, workforce, and social structures, as it is the work that makes all other work possible," noted Ai-jen Poo, Executive Director of Caring Across Generations. "Because We Care with Richard Lui amplifies caregiver experiences, reveals the complexities of our care economy, and drives momentum toward equitable policies and cultural change for care.".

Lui draws on over a decade of personal experience caring for both of his parents to fuel his advocacy, journalism, filmmaking, and technology initiatives.

Recent findings suggest that "Caring for my parents revealed how unseen this journey can be," Lui said. "Through Because We Care, we highlight critical personal stories to ensure media spotlighting translates into sustained action.". The series highlights in-depth interviews with prominent figures from Caring Across Generations' Creative Care Council including Emma Heming Willis (caring for Bruce Willis following his frontotemporal dementia diagnosis), Yvette Nicole Brown (caring for her father with Alzheimer's while maintaining an acting career), Lauren Miller Rogen (caring for her mother, Adele, who developed early-onset Alzheimer's), Seth Rogen (supporting Lauren and her family through Alzheimer's), Sen. Andy Kim (caring for his father while raising two young children and serving in the U.S. Senate), Lisa Ling, (who spent over a decade caring for her late father through dementia before he passed away), and others. These conversations explore dementia, career balancing, family dynamics, and public policy.

In its first 60 days, Because We Care with Richard Lui assembled a roster of public figures and national caregiving experts across five interview groupings, generating at least 12 documented editorial and public audio/video placements, including coverage in eight national editorial outlets, alongside syndicated reporting and public excerpts.

This ongoing partnership reinforces a shared goal: connecting audiences with trusted caregiving resources and expanding media coverage of the care economy.

Throughout National Family Caregivers Month, Because We Care with Richard Lui continues to push caregiving to the forefront of national discourse.

ABOUT CARING ACROSS GENERATIONS.

Caring Across Generations is a national organization of family caregivers, professional care workers, disabled people and aging adults working to transform the way care is given and received in the United States. Through cultural storytelling, advocacy, coalition building and policy change, the organization works to make care accessible, affordable and equitable so everyone can live, work and age with dignity. Its work focuses on affordable childcare, aging and disability care, care work, and paid leave. Learn more at caringacross.org. Follow on social: Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube.
