A modder using AI to create The Last of Us Part 2 multiplayer that Naughty Dog never gave us claims Sony can't do much even if it wanted to shut it down.

Sources indicate that earlier this year, Sony put the brakes on one such mod. YouTuber Speclizer unveiled their mod at the start of this year, but two months ago, they announced it was canceled after they received a cease and desist from Sony Interactive Entertainment. However, another has emerged in the form of Twelve Weeks (via IGN), with the developer releasing a mod earlier this week featuring 4v4 team battles.

The creator, Kellie, admitted on Twitter that AI was used in its development, saying, "Claude code was used for most of the project." However, they note that no AI artwork has been used, with the dev explaining she made the artwork herself. (Related: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming Game in 5 D...)

Over on a YouTube video for the mod, the developer's account claims that Speclizer's mod was subject to a cease and desist because it was paywalled, but "mine is released already for free and in open source." However, even if the devs do receive a letter from Sony, it's claimed that "since people have the source code to the full mod already, there ain't much they can do even if they do shut me down.". (Related: Man calls police on his ex for taking back her Xbox acc...)

It has been revealed that naughty Dog was creating The Last of Us Online based on the second game for some time before it was canceled in 2023. Word out of the studio was very favorable, with its director, Vinit Agarwal, saying some who worked on it thought it was "the best multiplayer game they've ever played.".

Industry observers note that the Last of Us had a shockingly great multiplayer mode, strong enough to rival other substantial hitters of the Xbox 360 and PS3 generation. Who knows how the sequel's would've been, but with how much better the gameplay was in The Last of Us Part 2 was, I reckon it could've been great.
