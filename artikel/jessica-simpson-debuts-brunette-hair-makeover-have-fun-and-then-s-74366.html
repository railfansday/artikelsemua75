Jessica Simpson has parted ways with her signature blonde tresses in favor of a darker — and more winter-like — shade.

“They say blondes have more fun,” celebrity hairstylist Ken Paves wrote via his Instagram Stories on Friday, November 20, alongside a selfie featuring Simpson, 46. “She states brunettes have fun and then some.”.

Recent findings suggest that simpson, who reposted the snap onto her account, dressed casually in all-black. As for her glam, the singer’s newly darkened mane was styled in loose, face-framing waves.

While Simpson is often synonymous with her honey-colored hair, she made a major statement late last year when she went brunette.

Sources indicate that “It took a few hours to color because we kept making it darker and darker,” her colorist Rita Hazan told People in December 2025. “I didn’t want to go too dark at once, so we gradually increased the hue to ensure that we got the most perfect tone.”.
