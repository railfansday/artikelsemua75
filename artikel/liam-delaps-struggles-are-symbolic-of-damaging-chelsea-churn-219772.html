Liam Delap came and went. He joined Chelsea in the summer of 2025 and was hailed by Enzo Maresca as the next Harry Kane. Fast forward a year, though, and there was no chance of Delap joining Kane in England’s World Cup squad. The striker’s form was miserable and the only surprise was that Chelsea made a profit when they sold him to Nottingham Forest for £50m last summer.

On one level it looks like good business. Delap’s only season at Stamford Bridge featured one serious hamstring injury, one league goal, one ridiculous red card and not much else. Analysts at other clubs rated him at Ipswich and felt Chelsea was the wrong fit for his direct style of play. The mistake was less the decision not to give Delap another season, no matter the worries over cover for João Pedro, more the call to sign him in the first place.
