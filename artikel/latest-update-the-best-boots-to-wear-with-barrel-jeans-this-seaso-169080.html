Sources indicate that whether or not you’ve jumped on board, barrel jeans remain a favorite silhouette among the fashion set. They’re quirky, fashion-forward, and, yes, undeniably comfortable, qualities that explain their staying power. I’ve been devoted to them since I got my first pair in 2022, and they’ve been my main go-to jean ever since. Now that boot season is here, we set out to find the best styles to wear with them.

Recent findings suggest that our conclusion: ankle boots are the best match for this sculptural cut, because they tuck neatly beneath the curved hem. That stated, not all ankle boots are created equal. A sleek Chelsea boot keeps the proportions clean, while a pointed-toe style adds a bit of length to balance the leg’s volume. If you lean casual, a lug-sole pair gives the look an easy, utilitarian edge. Whatever you choose, aim for a hem that skims the top of the boot, so the curve of the denim stays the star of the outfit.
