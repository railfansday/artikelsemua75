Sources indicate that the short version is that the Fool was wildly overpowered when Mega Crit added her to the early access deckbuilder's beta branch, with a base arrow-shooting mechanic that lets her dish out at least 21 damage in her first turn, and a Repeat Shot card that allows for upwards of 80. She ain't so vicious anymore, because they've just released a "mostly Fool-centric" patch that nerfs a couple dozen or so cards. (Related: Show, Don't Certify: How to Prepare for Your First Data...)

It has been revealed that it's not all nerfs – they've buffed Cho Chorus, Quietus and Show Off, among others, while reworking Tune, Flourescence and Rapid Shot. There's a latest Rare card, Optimism, which exhausts all Sadness in your Hand, while drawing a card for every card exhausted.

In a recent development, some context: Sadness is a special colourless card, earned by way of cards like Mourning Blow. It remains in your hand between turns and can't be played, making it an immediate hindrance, but you can transform it and use it as fuel for certain cards. Just like real sadness! Best of all, you can turn it into Joy, which grants you Energy. Reading about this complex neurophysical alchemy is making me tired. Shall I give the beta build a go? Do I dare to eat a peach? Are you sick of ham-fisted Prufrock references yet?

Read the full patch notes here. The Fool is an interesting creation, eyebrow-raising damage output aside, and I look forward to her addition to the game's main Steam branch. She can play various flavours of music to modify other cards.
