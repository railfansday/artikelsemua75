Sources indicate that iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone Duo have been out for quite some time now, and despite that, it’s still incredibly challenging to order an iPhone Duo in a timely manner. If for whatever reason you’re considering skipping out on this year’s iPhones, here’s what you have to look forward to next year.

Recent findings suggest that 2027 is going to be a relatively big iPhone release year for Apple. First, the firm will be continuing the iPhone 18 lineup after it introduced a split release schedule. Then, in the fall, the company will debut a new round of flagships in celebration of the 20th anniversary of the iPhone. Here’s what to expect. It has been revealed that for the first time, Apple will be introducing its base iPhone in the spring. Upgrades for the iPhone 18 aren’t quite clear, though more RAM is one of the key rumors. Thermal upgrades will likely happen as well, as well as the newly introduced Apple C2 modem.

It’s possible Apple will patch the design to fall more in line with the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro, though we don’t yet know. (Related: OpenAI's AI models boost AMD's upcoming flagship GPU de...)

This phone will of course equip the A20 chip, and it’ll probably start at the same $899 price point that iPhone 17 presently sits at – after it was increased from $799 in September.

This phone will most likely just end up being a chip bump from the A19 to A20 chip, and debut in the Spring alongside the iPhone 18. Earlier this year, reports indicated that Apple would share many components between the iPhone 18 and iPhone 18e, though it isn’t quite clear what that could mean for the devices.

Out of all of the spring iPhone releases, this one is the one to look forward to. After receiving a lukewarm reception with the initial iPhone Air model, Apple went back to the drawing board and decided to modify the design to fit an ultrawide camera into the device.

In a recent development, personally, I don’t think an ultrawide camera is too key – though many people will see one camera on the back of the phone and immediately assume it’s less premium, so it’ll probably end up being an important upgrade for the sake of sales. Plus, ultrawide photos can be fun at times.

In a recent development, it should also have a larger battery, reportedly as large as 3500mAh. That’s a roughly 10% improvement from the original iPhone Air. iPhone Air 2 will likely also jump from Apples C1X modem to the C2.

According to recent reports, for the 20th anniversary of the iPhone, Apple is expected to debut a fully redesigned iPhone to celebrate. It’ll reportedly be much more glassy, feature significantly slimmer bezels, a quad curved display, and ditch the flat edge design of the iPhone 12 and later.

According to recent reports, personally, I’m slightly skeptical that this’ll truly be a replacement to the “Pro” phone for thermal reasons, but time will tell. iPhone Duo seemingly has strong thermals while not featuring an aluminum unibody, so my concern might not be relevant. It’ll offer two, larger display sizes: 6.4-inch and 7-inch. This largely comes from the bezel reduction.

It has been revealed that I wish they prioritized the Telephoto camera more. I need that (whether it be on an Air or a Duo) way more than an Ultrawide.

If you’re holding out for iPhone 18 Pro, this “iPhone 20” is definitely the phone to wait out for.

Recent findings suggest that lastly, Apple will release a successor to its just-revealed iPhone Duo next fall. Of course, you can expect next-generation Apple Silicon with the A21 Pro chip next year, but there aren’t many other substantial changes expected. Apple may bring proper Face ID to the outer display, an improvement from the recently-announced face unlock, but it’s unclear whether this feature will make the engineering cutoff date.
