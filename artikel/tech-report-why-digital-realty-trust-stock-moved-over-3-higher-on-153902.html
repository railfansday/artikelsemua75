The No. 1 news item by far in the tech industry -- and indeed, global business -- was the reinstatement of Nvidia's (NVDA +31.48%) Jensen Huang as CEO. The move by that tech giant's board of directors came mere days after Huang resigned his position following his admissions of extramarital affairs, one of which may have been with a person with ties to Chinese intelligence.
