Marella Cruises‘ Discovery 2 is presently undergoing enhancements in a wet dock in Málaga, Spain.

The ship is set to welcome only adults starting in 2027 with former youth facilities transformed into four latest venues, including Marella’s first private karaoke lounge.

Amplify Karaoke will operate as a private lounge for up to seven guests, which can be booked by the hour.

Other new venues include the Lost Luggage Bureau, a speakeasy venue inspired by the Golden Age of Travel, allowing passengers to take part in two 90-minute shows that include live music, live actors, storytelling and three signature cocktails.

The Marella Discovery 2 will also introduce Red Handed, an escape room set backstage at the fictional Cabaret Rouge in the 1920s, where teams of up to six guests have 60 minutes to steal Lady Ruby Bardot’s diamond necklace before they’re caught red-handed.

Also being added is The Loft, which is described as a calm space for passengers who want to slow down, combining a library, board games, and hosted arts and crafts in a relaxed setting.

Following the enhancements in wet dock, the Marella Discovery 2 is scheduled to operate a winter season in the Canaries and Madeira, offering seven-night cruises from Santa Cruz de Tenerife and Puerto del Rosario to destinations in Spain and Portugal.
