Having fun learning with an app like Duolingo? You might be learning less than you think

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (83 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Recent findings suggest that apps like Duolingo aim to make learning as enjoyable as possible. But enjoyment can also make you feel you are learning more than you actually are. This is the finding of research by Tilburg marketing researchers Robert Smith and Anna Paley. In an experiment with a language-learning app, participants who completed a more enjoyable learning task believed that they learned more than they actually did. The study is published in the Journal of Marketing Research.
