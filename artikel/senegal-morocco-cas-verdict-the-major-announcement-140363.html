Senegal-Morocco: CAS verdict, the major announcement

According to recent reports, the series of the CAN 2025 final between Senegal and Morocco could soon reach its epilogue. While the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) unveiled that it would make its decision in this particularly sensitive matter at the end of November, a precise date is now circulating on social networks.

According to recent reports, on X, Khirch Mohammed claims that information “quasi-official” report an announcement planned for this Wednesday, at 12 p.m. GMT, or 1 p.m. in France. A deadline which could therefore put an end to several months of waiting for the two federations. However, this date was not confirmed by the arbitration body.

Other elements also call for caution. “The only information I have personally: the FRMF has not yet received a decision from the CAS this Friday”wrote journalist Youssef Amz in particular. For the moment, no official communication from the Court of Arbitration for Sport has confirmed the confirmed schedule.

The president of the Confederation of African Football (CAF), Patrice Motsepe, nevertheless suggested that the outcome was near. “The CAS should make its decision in the coming days. Whatever it is, we will respect it,” he declared on the sidelines of the CAF General Assembly on Saturday.

Recent findings suggest that as a reminder, the Senegalese Football Federation contests the decision of CAF to award the victory of the final to Morocco on the green carpet, with a score of 3-0. However, Senegal won 1-0 on the field. The CAS, which heard the different parties on October 8, officially declared that its reasoned sentence was expected at the end of November.
