It has been revealed that if your holiday spread always includes a plate of crispy latkes and a round of dirty martinis, these dirty martini latkes are probably your dream mash-up. This playful party app takes everything you love about the classic Hanukkah potato pancake and gives it a cocktail-inspired twist with buttery green olives, pimento peppers, and a tangy blue cheese and vermouth-spiked sour cream for dipping. The blue cheese isn’t mandatory, but if you love an ice-cold filthy martini with a couple of blue cheese stuffed olives in it, you’ll totally understand my vision. Pair these with other Hanukkah favorites for a festive spread that's equal parts nostalgic and fun.

Recent findings suggest that the latkes:Traditional latkes start with freshly grated potatoes, but frozen shredded hash browns are the secret shortcut here, cutting prep time without sacrificing crispy edges or fluffy centers. The key is squeezing out as much moisture as possible, then fluffing the shreds with a fork so they stay light instead of turning dense. Castelvetrano olives add a rich, buttery flavor, but Spanish queen or manzanilla olives work just as well. If you can find green olives already stuffed with pimento, go ahead and use them—you can skip the extra chopped pimentos altogether. It has been revealed that make ahead & storage:The vermouth-blue cheese dipping sauce can be made up to 7 days ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator, making your day-of party prep that much easier. Leftover latkes can be refrigerated for up to 4 days; just reheat them in a 400° oven or air fryer until hot and crisp again for the best texture.
