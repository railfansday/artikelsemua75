Breaking: Researchers trace shifting pulsation modes in δ Scuti star KIC 4737720

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (65 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Stars in the night sky may look calm and unchanging, but some are in fact constantly "vibrating." Just as a musical instrument can sound several notes at once, a star can pulse in several modes simultaneously. By measuring minute changes in a star's brightness, astronomers can read these stellar vibrations and, in turn, probe the physical processes at work inside it.
