Recent findings suggest that ’Tis the season to embrace all things cozy, and that includes gingerbread. The scent isn’t just wafting from our ovens and favorite coffee shops anymore. It’s showing up in our everyday lives, too, as gingerbread fragrances grow more popular.

Recent findings suggest that in perfume, gingerbread is a gourmand accord: warm ginger, molasses, and cinnamon, usually rounded out with vanilla or woods. Ginger is a clever ingredient. Perfumers often use it as a top note, where it adds sparkle and energy, and it pairs beautifully with pepper, citrus, rich woods, and amber. That’s why a good gingerbread perfume can feel cozy without turning into a candle.
