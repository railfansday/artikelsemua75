Industry observers note that known as being one of Spider-Man's most notorious enemies, the Green Goblin has been a formidable foe for our favorite web-slinger since he first debuted in The Amazing Spider-Man #14. As the alias of many super villains, he has been mostly incarnated as Norman Osbourn, and over the years, the iconic character has been portrayed by the likes of Willem Dafoe, James Franco, Chris Cooper and Dane DeHaan, to name but a few.

According to recent reports, more recently, the character of Norman Osbourn has played an crucial role in 2023's Marvel's Spider-Man 2, and it was clear to see that the storyline was setting him up to become the next Green Goblin. If you have not caught up with the Insomniac saga so far, you had better click off, but in the final moments of Marvel's Spider-Man 2, Harry Osborn falls into a coma following the defeat of Venom. As such, his father, Norman, demands that his assistants create the "G-Serum", which we can safely assume will lead to his transformation into the Green Goblin.

So, at the time of writing, fans have not yet seen Insomniac Games' interpretation of the Green Goblin, with many believing that the transformation will finally take place in Marvel's Spider-Man 3. However, despite rumors suggesting that a third installment is currently in development, it has not been confirmed by neither Insomniac nor Sony.

One such rumor that suggests Marvel's Spider-Man 3 is in development is uncovered concept art for none other than the Green Goblin himself, which was discovered over three years ago and shared to r/GamingLeaksAndRumors. (Related: Three 'e-waste' Nvidia V100s hit 100 tokens per second ...)

It is here that we can see concept art that was highlighted in what many believed to be an Insomniac Games' leak. It depicts the Green Goblin, looking quite fashionable in a black hooded jacket complete with purple accents. His green face and arms can be seen, with the latter being quite muscular, and he has various contraptions strapped to his fitted ensemble. Finishing off with a menacing grin, this Green Goblin looks a lot more fashionable than previous depictions, but no less terrifying. (Related: Breaking: Japan launches a ‘dumbphone’ that doesn’t con...)

According to recent reports, not only that, but the leaked screenshot reads, "Marvel's Spider-Man, the best-selling superhero game of all time, continues in Marvel's Spider-Man 3 with an explosive confrontation with a villain whose story spans all three games." Of course, it is worth taking this leak with a pinch of salt, and nothing has come of it in over three years. However, it did not stop fans from heading to the comments to share their thoughts.

"It's been interesting they've been building Norman up as the significant bad of the whole trilogy," one user commented. "I kind of wish him and Peter had a closer relationship because it's clear it's so one-sided (e.g, Norman indicates s*it about him being like a son to him, but it's clear Peter hates Norman with good reason, of course). Not sure if that'll be a good payoff. Also, I hope he still has the power of Oscorp behind him as the Goblin." Another shared, "I love Venom, but Green Goblin is my favorite of Spider-Man's Rogues Gallery, so, I'm down for this finally, especially this style too.".

For now, we can only hope that the rumors are true and Marvel's Spider-Man 3 is in development behind-the-scenes. If so, we may finally see another epic showdown between Spider-Man and the Green Goblin once more.
