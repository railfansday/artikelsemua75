The most elaborate of the bunch is the Hazelnut Chocolate Shake, which starts with vanilla frozen custard blended with chocolate-hazelnut spread. That’s poured into a crackable dark chocolate shell before being topped with whipped cream, chopped hazelnuts, crunchy wafer flakes, and a Ferrero Rocher chocolate square.

There’s also the new Butterscotch Crème Brûlée Shake, made with butterscotch crème brûlée frozen custard and finished with whipped cream and honeycomb pieces in the drink, giving a little crunch to go along with all that creaminess. It has been revealed that even the bags and cups are getting dressed up for the holidays. Shake Shack's packaging this year turns its original Madison Square Park location into a gingerbread house, complete with wreaths and string lights as a cute little nod to where the chain got its start.
