Robert Pattinson isn’t the only A-lister who has appeared in multiple massive movie franchises.

According to recent reports, adam Driver starred as the lightsaber-wielding antagonist Kylo Ren in the “Star Wars” trilogy and will don a cape once more as Dr. Nathaniel Essex, aka Mister Sinister — a hyperintelligent, shape-shifting telepath and the main villain in Marvel’s upcoming “X-Men” reboot directed by Jake Schreier. And, during his appearance on “Late Night with Seth Meyers” Monday to promote the James Gray film “Paper Tiger,” the Oscar-nominated actor was asked a series of questions by the talk-show host about the upcoming superhero movie. Industry observers note that first, Meyers held up a photo of Mister Sinister — body suit, sharp collar, shredded cape, makeup and all — to which Driver quipped, “[Looks] just like me. This is 6 a.m. when my daughter wakes me up.”. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

He then asked the “Girls” and “Marriage Story” performer how familiar he was with Mister Sinister prior to joining the cast. “Yes. Not to the degree — I was more aware of Magneto. I was a major X-Men fan… My cousin Tony would come over, and he made these Sabretooth costume things with the yellow rubber gloves and put toothpicks out the side, and I would dress as Wolverine.”.

Sources indicate that driver added that his 10-year-old son, who’d previously expressed little to no interest in watching his films, is finally looking forward to one of them. “My son is actually excited about ‘X-Men.’ Plus, at this point, he’s getting older and hasn’t seen anything that I’ve been in, but he’s been to sets. He wants to see me in something,” he said.

Marvel Studios honcho Kevin Feige admitted he’d been courting Driver for quite some time before he finally caved with “X-Men,” though the actor would usually engage in discussions with Marvel before politely declining. “It’s no secret we’d like to [work with Adam],” Feige told the “Happy Sad Confused” podcast. “But it’s a tradition to Zoom with him and then he passes. That’s a long-lived tradition. He couldn’t be more cordial. He is usually upfront about how he is feeling.”.

Industry observers note that schreier’s upcoming “X-Men” reboot will see Driver square off against Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Inde Navarrette as Rogue, Kit Connor as Cyclops, Maya Boyd as Storm, Asa Germann as Angel and Christopher Abbott as Charles Xavier/Professor X. The film will hit theaters May 5, 2028.
