Jamie Lee Curtis is marking a rather somber birthday as the only surviving member of her immediate family.

“The long and winding road. Another circle around the sun!” Curtis wrote via Instagram on Sunday, November 22, marking her 68th birthday. “Today will be the first year that I am the only surviving family member of my immediate family and my immediate second family. The last in the line.”.

Curtis’ older sister, Kelly Lee Curtis, died earlier this year of natural causes, according to the Blaine County coroner’s office. She was in hospice care for the week prior to her death. She was 69.

“A warm aloha to my older sister, Kelly Lee Curtis. She passed away this morning. In her home. In nature. At peace,” Jamie Lee wrote via Instagram at the time, announcing her sister’s death. “She was my first friend and lifelong confidant. She was jaw droppingly beautiful, and a talented actress.”.

On Saturday, Jamie Lee opened up about how the loss of her sister is impacting her on her birthday.

It has been revealed that “The loss of my sister Kelly in June was a real blow and I feel sad today,” Jamie Lee continued in her Saturday post. “I look back at photographs of me as a child, and I look so stunned to be here at birth, and it carried on through much of my young life, always seemingly startled and slightly concerned. I’ve worked through a lot of that discomfort, and I’m so happy to be here and have the life I have. Both personal and creative.”.

She added, “What remains for me is the legacy of other humans who forged a path for me to be here through their actions and examples as I hope I am for my own family and of course friends and, as a public person, the millions of people I’ve involved in my life.”. “I thank my parents and my stepfather and sister and her husband and my extended family and my friends and mostly my husband and my daughters and their families and my young grandson, all of whom I get to see today for this life that I love so very much and I wish you all a happy and healthy Thanksgiving and holiday season,” Jamie Lee concluded on Saturday.

The legendary actress received a slew of support in the comments section, from fans and friends alike.

“I knew your sister a little in college. Not only was she beautiful but also kind. I am sorry for your losses. May their memories be blessings.”.
