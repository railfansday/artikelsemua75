The legendary Speed Racer franchise’s next series will focus on a beloved series icon. Debuting in Japan during the late 1960s, Speed Racer was not only a hit there, but in the United States as well. For generations of viewers, Speed Racer was their first exposure to anime, and it still holds a place in fans' hearts.

Airing for only 11 months between April 1967 and March 1968, Speed Racer would nonetheless become a seminal anime. The series, created by Tatsuo Yoshida, follows the adventures of Speed, Pops Racer, Racer X and other members of the Racer family. Speed Racer hit American airwaves during the Summer of 1967, dubbed and edited for English-speaking audiences. There have been remakes and latest adaptations of Speed Racer over the years, including a live-action film, released in 2008 and directed by the Wachowskis. Speed Racer has also inspired volumes of manga in Japan. (Related: Industry News: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hu...)

In a recent development, recently, independent publisher Mad Cave Studios obtained the rights to produce newly introduced comics based on the Speed Racer franchise. The first offering debuted on Free Comic Book Day in 2025. Written by David Pepose, the series was a hit with fans and critics, and proved there were still plenty of stories left in the Speed Racer franchise. Now, one of Speed’s most important supporting characters steps into the spotlight in a new, four-issue miniseries. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

According to recent reports, today, Mad Cave Studios has revealed Speed Racer: Trixie, and ScreenRant is pleased to bring fans an exclusive first look. The miniseries, which spins out of Mad Cave’s Speed Racer title, will be written by Meghan Fitzmartin and drawn by Pablo Collar. Mad Cave has provided a synopsis for Speed Racer: Trixie, which reads as follows:.

In Speed Racer: Trixie, the head of Turnwell Enterprises is ready to shine in her own miniseries!

When she's not supporting Speed Racer on the track, Trixie's running races of her own—around board members and rival companies! It's been a difficult few years for Turnwell Enterprises, and Trixie's worked hard to rebuild her parents'; manufacturer. But now, an old contract is about to tear down everything she's worked for. A rival CEO wants Turnwell—and Trixie—and he'll sacrifice Speed and anyone else in his way to get it. Trixie's not about to give her hand OR her company—and she's got a few tricks of her own. In a mechanic's jumpsuit or a gala dress, she's the baddest B in the room. The action starts here!

The February-debuting series will speed into shops with a variety of variant covers, shared here.

Recent findings suggest that the creators behind Speed Racer: Trixie are excited to take on the project. Writer Fitzmartin, who has been a fan of the Speed Racer franchise since she was eight years old, had this to say about the book:.

This has been such a dream come true, and I can't wait for Trixie and Lovelace to knock your socks off.

Likewise, artist Collar, a lifelong fan of anime and manga, described Speed Racer: Trixie as “everything I’ve ever wanted.” Collar went on to say:.

Trixie is a character I’ve enjoyed exploring so much and this fits perfectly with my career as an artist.

Fitzmartin and Collar’s passion for the Speed Racer franchise shines through, and ensures fans will enjoy this new book.

In a recent development, despite its relatively short time on the air, Speed Racer is still one of the best-loved anime franchises ever. Its winning combination of action, mystery and heart gave it an appeal that transcended culture and location. Creator Tatsuo Yoshida crafted something special in Speed Racer. The show functioned as a cultural ambassador by shaping Western audiences’ perceptions of anime and Japanese culture as a whole. The numerous remakes, manga adaptations and now Western comic books are a testament to Speed Racer’s longevity.

Yet a racer is only as good as his crew, both on and off the track. Trixie, Speed’s lover and manager, is integral to the franchise, but has rarely taken the spotlight like she will this February. Speed Racer fans are in for a treat, as Meghan Fitzmartin and Pabo Collar explore the life and times of one of Speed’s most trusted allies.

Speed Racer: Trixie #1 is on sale February 17 from Mad Cave!
