Kenneth Taylor has ruled out leaving Lazio in January, insisting he wants to help Gennaro Gattuso’s side return to the Champions League.

According to Il Messaggero, via TuttoMercatoWeb, the Dutch midfielder knows about the interest from Juventus and the Premier League, but has made it clear he isn’t going anywhere.

It has been revealed that “It’s all true, my agents have informed me. But I’ve already noted I’m not even thinking about leaving in January,” Taylor said. “I want to stay here and help Lazio return to the Champions League.”.

The 24-year-old has become one of Serie A’s standout midfielders this season. Tuttosport reported last week that Newcastle have set their sights on him, with Bournemouth and Brentford also after the Dutchman, while Juventus are monitoring his situation.

Lazio have no intention of selling. According to the report, even €40 million might not be enough to convince Claudio Lotito and sporting director Angelo Fabiani to negotiate next summer. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Taylor’s contract already runs until 2030, but he earns only around €1.5m a season, so the club are considering a pay rise to reward his form.

It’s quite a turnaround. Taylor arrived from Ajax in January, and in July Gattuso sent him off the training pitch after a row in pre-season, leaving his future in real doubt. Instead, he has become a key part of Gattuso’s midfield as Lazio sit top of Serie A on 32 points. (Related: Tech Report: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for Su...)

He was outstanding again in Saturday’s 3-0 win over Lecce, producing a back-heel that drew comparisons with Rui Costa and completing a record 23 progressive passes.
