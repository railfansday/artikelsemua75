Recent findings suggest that if your winter wardrobe could use a seasonal refresh, Banana Republic’s Black Friday sale is the perfect opportunity to fill in the gaps in your closet without paying full price. For a limited time, the retailer is offering 40% everything—including apparel, shoes, and handbags. Whether you’re looking to stock up on cold-weather staples, give your office-wear rotation an upgrade, or get a head start on holiday shopping, there’s tons of picks worth adding to cart.

In a recent development, first on the shopping list? The pieces you’ll wear on repeat all winter long. Banana Republic’s selection of elevated essentials includes graphic knitwear, cozy wool sweaters, and luxurious cashmere, all of which will work overtime as temperatures continue to drop. Outerwear is another category worth exploring, with everything from reliable herringbone wool coats to buttery leather jackets and statement-making animal-print layers heavily discounted. Add a pair of tailored flannel trousers to the mix, and you’ve got a timeless winter uniform.

In a recent development, for those who’ve long relied on Banana Republic as a workwear destination, the sale presents an opportunity to overhaul your 9-to-5 wardrobe. Think wintery suede blazers, dressy wool iterations with contrasting lapels, elegant sweater dresses, and goes-with-everything silk shirts. There’s plenty to shop for off-duty occasions, too. Dark-wash denim makes a versatile (and on-trend) weekend staple, while sequin dresses come party-ready for the holiday calendar. Don’t overlook the accessories, either: With shoes and handbags included in the promotion, now’s the time to scoop up classic loafers, knee-high riding boots, and an everyday bag.

Industry observers note that and with the holidays around the corner, it’s also the perfect time to make a dent in your gift list. From cashmere sweaters to oversized scarves, Banana Republic has present options that feel far more expensive than their Black Friday price tags suggest.
