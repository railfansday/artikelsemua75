Roberto De Zerbi has revealed what Destiny Udogie must do to start regularly for Tottenham after an injury disrupted start to the season.

Udogie has started just twice in the Premier League this season and was left out of the matchday squad as Spurs lost 1-0 to Leeds at Elland Road.

He is available for selection against Ipswich this weekend, but it is unclear whether he start, as he has not trained consistently since suffering a knee injury against Manchester United in October. (Related: Leveraging Pre-trained Language Model Checkpoints for E...)

De Zerbi said earlier this season that Udogie’s injury challenges were becoming “problematic” ahead of Spurs’ 2-0 win at home to Crystal Palace.

Asked what the Italian full-back had to do to force his way into the starting lineup, the Spurs boss admitted Udogie had to train with the rest of the squad more frequently.

Recent findings suggest that “He has to work. If I don’t see him on the pitch in training, he can’t play,” De Zerbi noted in his pre-match press conference on Friday afternoon.

Udogie and James Maddison have struggled to train regularly all season, with the latter suffering several setbacks in his recovery from an anterior cruciate ligament injury.

Maddison has not featured since coming off midway through Spurs’ famous 2-0 victory over Chelsea, despite being named in the matchday squad against Palace and Leeds.

De Zerbi admits the 29-year-old is in a similar position to Udogie, in that he must train more regularly before he can rely on him to put a run of starts together.

“He is working very well from last Wednesday. He is working very well every day. Before Leeds, from Chelsea to Leeds game, he worked just three times on the pitch. If you don’t work during the week, you risk injury,” De Zerbi continued.

“In any way, you lose the normal physical condition you need to play in the Premier League.

“The same for Udogie. When they work, when they are fit to work, they have many chances to play, especially Maddison, because he is a special player.”.
