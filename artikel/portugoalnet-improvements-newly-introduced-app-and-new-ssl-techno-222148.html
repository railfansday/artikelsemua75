As part of PortuGOAL’s efforts to constantly evolve and serve lovers of Portuguese football with top-quality and increasingly accessible content, we are replacing the PortuGOAL Android and Apple APPs with a new Progressive Web Application (PWA) as from tomorrow, Tuesday 3 April.

This substantial step forward in phone apps technology loads the content instantly, even in areas of low connectivity, stays up to date at all times, displays the most recent version upon launching, is more efficient than native apps, works on-demand and is always accessible, without taking up a smartphone’s valuable memory or data.
