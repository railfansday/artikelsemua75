Industry observers note that photoVogue is proud to unveil the 100 photographers and video makers selected for our latest Global Open Call, Brave Newly introduced Visions: Creativity as Rebellion. Chosen from an extraordinary 95,758 submissions by 9,160 artists across 155 regions, this digital exhibition celebrates the power of visual storytelling to challenge the status quo and imagine alternative futures.

It has been revealed that in a world saturated with images, true creative rebellion lies in refusing to give us what we already know. Rebellion doesn't always announce itself loudly—while some selected works confront urgent social and political realities, others explore identity, memory, tenderness, and desire through intimate, poetic, or playful lenses. What unites these 100 artists is not a single aesthetic, but a fearless willingness to experiment and question what we have been taught to accept. Follow PhotoVogue on Instagram and check back in the gallery for updates—and thank you to every artist who trusted us with their vision.
