Omar Marmoush has backed his "unbelievable" Tottenham team-mate Luca Williams-Barnett to have a "very substantial future".

The teenager has impressed under Roberto De Zerbi in recent weeks, most notably scoring and assisting in their Premier League win over Ipswich Town last weekend.

Aged 18 years and 51 days, Williams-Barnett became the second-youngest player to score and assist in a Premier League game, behind Michael Owen.

He also became the fourth-youngest player to score on his first Premier League start, behind Daniel Jebbison in May 2021 (17y 276d), Danny Cadamarteri in September 1997 (17y 343d) and Mark Cullen in May 2010 (18y 12d).

Should he start and score against Sunderland on Sunday, he'd be the youngest player to score in his first two starts (18 years, 59 days).

The forward's breakthrough into the Spurs' first team was rewarded with a newly introduced contract, which is reported to be a five-year deal, and Marmoush believes the only way is up for the exciting winger.

"He is unbelievable," Marmoush told Sky sports about Williams-Barnett.
