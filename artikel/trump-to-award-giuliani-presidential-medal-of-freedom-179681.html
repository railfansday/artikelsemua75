Quoting his former boss, President Ronald Reagan, Giuliani added: "'There is no limit to what a man can do or where he can go if he doesn’t mind who gets the credit.' I share the credit with all the men and women who worked with me, disagreed with me, and loved me — particularly those who lost their lives in the causes we undertook together.".

White House assistant press secretary Olivia Wales praised Giuliani’s decades-long record in law enforcement and city leadership, noting that his work had "left a lasting impact on New York City and our Nation.".

It has been revealed that "As U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of Fresh York, Mayor Giuliani took down mob bosses and led a relentless war on crime," Wales mentioned. "As Mayor, he transformed New York from a crime-ridden city into one of the safest big cities in the world, fostering economic opportunity and cultural greatness. And in the aftermath of the tragic September 11th attacks, he displayed strong American leadership in the face of evil.".
