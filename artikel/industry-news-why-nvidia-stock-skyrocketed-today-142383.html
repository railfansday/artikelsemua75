Shares of Nvidia (NVDA +31.21%) charged out of the gate on Monday, climbing as much as 35.1%. As of 10:16 a.m. ET, the stock was still up 32.8%.

In what might be among the fastest pivots in corporate history, the artificial intelligence (AI) chipmaker declared the return of its enigmatic founder.

According to recent reports, jensen Huang. Image source: Nvidia.

In a press release that dropped on Monday morning, Nvidia revealed that it had reinstated Jensen Huang as president and CEO, effective immediately.

Industry observers note that "The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," remarked board chair Harvey C. Jones. "We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the company.". (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

The move marked an unexpected pivot for the organization. Just last week, Jones said Nvidia "does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.".

This announcement marks the end of a turbulent chapter for the chipmaker. Reports of several extramarital relationships -- including one with a woman who was suspected of spying for China -- led Huang to tender his resignation to the organization he founded in 1993.

NASDAQ · As of 11:49 AM ET 11/23/26.

The departure couldn't have come at a worse time. Concerns have run rampant about the ongoing demand for AI, circular financing deals, and the public backlash against the data centers that handle AI processing. Those issues, and Huang's untimely exodus, conspired to crush Nvidia stock, which was down roughly 48% from its October peak before the news broke.

On the bright side, Huang has ushered Nvidia through numerous challenges before, including waning sales of gaming chips, cryptocurrency demand cycles, and chip sales (or lack thereof) to China.
