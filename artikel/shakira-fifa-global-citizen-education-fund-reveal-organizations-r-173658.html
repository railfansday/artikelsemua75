Shakira and Global Citizen have announced that five community-based organizations in Venezuela will receive $450,000 to help children get back to learning following the twin earthquakes that struck the country last June, disrupting schooling for thousands.

Sources indicate that in a video posted on Tuesday (Nov. 24), the Colombian superstar and activist and Global Citizen co-founder and CEO Hugh Evans remarked that with the investment, the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund’s Venezuela initiative is projected to reach more than 13,000 children.

According to recent reports, “When you give a child a safe place to learn, you change their life forever,” Shakira stated. “I’m so happy to announce that the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund is coming to Venezuela, supporting five organizations and reaching more than 13,000 children. To every teacher and community leader making this possible: this is for you.”. (Related: What I do every time I get an IRS notice...)

Evans added: “When we debuted the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund, we made a simple commitment: to get resources directly to organizations doing extraordinary work for children’s education around the world. To the teachers, community leaders and organizations doing this work: thank you. As you undertake the courageous work of rebuilding in Venezuela, and equipping the children in your communities to build their own futures, our hope is that this funding supports and multiplies your efforts for the long term.”. Sources indicate that according to a press release shared with Billboard, the programs will support education continuity, inclusive learning for students with autism, teacher development and sport-based activities, alongside nutrition and health services that help children participate in education. Together, these investments will support 44 schools, train 41 teachers and academic staff and engage 180 parents and caregivers, helping create safer, more inclusive learning environments.

Sources indicate that last July, Shakira and FIFA revealed that they had teamed up for a huge charitable effort to help the thousands of children impacted by the devastating earthquakes that hit Venezuela on June 24. The two magnitude 7.2 and 7.5 quakes that struck less than a minute apart claimed more than 6,500 lives and left many more injured and homeless, with thousands of citizens unaccounted for.

One month later, when a 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck her native Colombia, Shakira committed an initial $1.25 million to help rebuild damaged schools, announcing the pledge during a surprise visit to one of the hardest-hit regions of the country, Quibdó, the capital of the province of Chocó.

In a recent development, previously, in May, the singer announced that she would be donating 100% of the profits from her 2026 FIFA World Cup anthem “Dai Dai” with Burna Boy to the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund, whose board she, and the Weeknd, joined last fall in advance of the summer’s FIFA World Cup tournament. The song spent seven and ten weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Global 200 and Global Excl. U.S. charts, respectively.

According to the press release, the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund — with a combined investment of about $9.55 million across 63 organizations in 21 countries — is projected to reach more than 413,000 children over the next year, including with its latest initiative in Venezuela.

For more information, visit the FIFA Global Citizen Education Fund official website.
