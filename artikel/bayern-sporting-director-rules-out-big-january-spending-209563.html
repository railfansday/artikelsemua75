Bayern Munich sporting director Christoph Freund insisted they are not planning "anything big" in the January transfer window, despite their recent injury crisis.

Vincent Kompany's side were dealt two major blows in their Bundesliga draw with Koln last week after both Harry Kane and Michael Olise went down with injuries.

While Olise's knock is not deemed to be serious, Kane is set to miss at least three months of action after having surgery on his ankle.

Both players missed Bayern's Champions League defeat to Lille on Wednesday, with Serge Gnabry, Ismael Saibari, Alphonso Davies and Manuel Neuer also sidelined for the loss.

Luis Diaz did make his return after a month out, but Bayern's latest-look attack stuttered against Lille.

Konrad Laimer started as a makeshift striker, with Lennart Karl, Aleksandar Pavlovic and Nathaniel Brown featuring behind the Austrian.

Nineteen-year-olds Noah Codjo-Evora and Tim Binder came off the bench, but the Bundesliga champions were only able to accumulate 0.95 expected goals (xG) from their 12 shots.
