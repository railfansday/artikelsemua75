Heather Rae El Moussa and Christine Quinn are set to make their highly anticipated return to “Selling Sunset,” and while both women say the time since they’ve last been on the show has been transformative, it appears they still have unresolved problems to iron out.

Sources indicate that netflix dropped the Season 10 trailer on Nov. 18, and in one heated exchange, Christine and Heather’s conflict comes to a head.

Sources indicate that the pair have been at odds for quite some time, facing multiple tense on-screen moments. In a 2023 E! News interview, Christine branded Heather “a snooze fest,” claiming she’s not interesting enough for TV.

Then, in a July 2026 clip from E!, Heather revealed that prior to filming Season 10, she had not seen or spoken to Christine in four years.

“You literally mentioned you hated me,” Christine pressed in the trailer.

Instead of shying away from the confrontation, Heather fessed up.

“At that point, Christine, I did,” she admitted.

In another scene, Heather opens up to her husband, Tarek El Moussa, sharing insight on the challenging dynamic between the women.

“She’s gone through some sh-t,” Tarek responded. “That previous Christine is still this new Christine.”.

Industry observers note that despite the tension, both women seem to be excited for the upcoming season, which premieres on Dec. 4. (Related: Industry News: Designing Azure silicon on Azure silicon...)

According to recent reports, in a since-expired Instagram Story, Heather mentioned, “The trailer is so good. I watched it like 10 times.”.

Christine meanwhile wrote “We’re SOOO back!” in a comment on a Netflix post sharing a condensed-version of the trailer.

Both women opened up to Netflix’s publication, Tudum, ahead of the new season.

“I finally feel secure enough in who I am to walk back into that world and not lose myself in it,” Heather noted.

The proud mom left The Oppenheim Group after Season 7 to focus on family after welcoming her first child with Tarek—a son named Tristan Jay, now 3. Heather is also a co-parent to her husband’s two older children, Taylor Reese, 16, and Brayden James, 11, whom he shares with his ex-wife, Christina Haack.

Industry observers note that “I want people to see the full picture of who I am now,” she continued. “Not just the dramatic moments. The version of me returning is very different from the one who left.”.

In the interview, Heather teased, “there are conversations that got cut short,” that there are some things she’s “not going to pretend” haven’t happened.

Meanwhile, Christine parted ways with the brokerage at the end of Season 5, after welcoming her son, Christian Georges Dumontet, whom she shares with her estranged husband, Christian Dumontet.

Recent findings suggest that “Motherhood has a way of sharpening your priorities and changing how you move through the world,” she told Tudum. “I’ve become more intentional and much more protective of my peace.”.

With that said, Christine doesn’t initiative to steer clear of all conflict.

According to recent reports, “I’m still ambitious and direct, but I think people will see a version of me that’s more centered and even more sure of herself,” she explained. “I’m hoping this chapter feels fresh and honest.”.

Season 10 will also see the return of agents Bre Tiesi and Amanza Smith. Four newly introduced professionals will join the group, too: Jordyn Taylor, Sasha Lance, Sonni Pacheco, and Tara Burns.

Sources indicate that jason Oppenheim, head of the brokerage, said, “The new agents have already shaken up the office, but in a good way. I absolutely love the new ladies, and they make me excited about the future here at the brokerage and on the show.”.
