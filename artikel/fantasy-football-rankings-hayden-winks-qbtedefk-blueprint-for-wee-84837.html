1. Joe Burrow (at WAS) - 24.3 proj. points.

It has been revealed that burrow is playing like the MVP. He's scored 24.6+ fantasy points in seven straight games, depending on the scoring system. His +0.26 EPA per dropback is fourth, and he's doing it on the highest neutral pass rate (67%) by a wide margin. He's at his mental and physical peak.

(Chart by Hayden Winks/Yahoo Sports).

In a recent development, the Commanders defense has allowed the sixth-most half PPR points per game to QBs (20.8), and opposing offenses pass 59% of the time in neutral situations against Washington (third). Sign me up. The Bengals roll in as 6.5-point road favorites, carrying a 30.5-point implied team total (third).

2. Josh Allen (vs. MIA) - 23.9 proj. points.

Another year of Allen averaging 26.3 fantasy points per game, which somehow is QB2 behind Joe Burrow. The only real change this year is the presence of a real downfield threat in DJ Moore, who scored a post TD and hit on a couple of crossing routes last game now that he's actually healthy.

Recent findings suggest that the Dolphins just placed three key DTs on injured reserve and are quite inexperienced in the back. Allen's history against the Dolphins is exquisite. He's been a top-10 fantasy QB in 9 of 10 outings against them since 2022, with five top-5 games. Buffalo enters as 16.5-point home favorites with a 31.0-point implied team total (second).

3. Dak Prescott (vs. TEN) - 22.2 proj. points.

Prescott is coming off his best performance of the year, hanging 343 passing yards and five touchdowns on the 49ers in Week 10. That's what will happen whenever he's pushed because the defense is failing, as usual. Prescott is currently the QB5 in fantasy, while sitting eighth in EPA per dropback. He's having a great season again.

Industry observers note that the Titans aren't. They've allowed the most passing yards per game (282) and are on the worst three-game stretch of their team's history based on total yards allowed. It's hovering around 1,500 yards. Yikes. They won't have superstar DT Jeffery Simmons, nor S Amani Hooker moving forward. Dallas enters as 8.5-point home favorites with the top implied team total of the week at 31.5 points (first).

Industry observers note that 4. Caleb Williams (vs. NO) - 20.9 proj. points.

Fresh off the bye, Williams is hoping to hit his stride after an injury and difficult schedule slowed him down to open the year. He's the QB12 on a per-game basis (19.8), including his rain/hamstring clunker against the Vikings. He's been the QB4 in passing yards since then but hasn't run as often (9.5 YPG). That hopefully will change after the break.

Sources indicate that week 11 brings a home date against a Saints defense that allows the 22nd-most half PPR points to opposing QBs (17.5 per game). New Orleans can rush the passer fairly well, but it's been terrible at tackling and can be run on at will. The Bears could lean on that here, but there's nothing about the LB and DB rooms to be fearful of. New Orleans is 11th in pass YPG and sixth in rush YPG. The Bears head into Sunday as 11.5-point home favorites with a stout 30-point implied team total (fourth).

It has been revealed that 5. Jayden Daniels (vs. CIN) - 21.3 proj. points.

According to recent reports, daniels is not rushing nearly as often the last month (19.3 YPG) as he typically does, which has kept him in check in fantasy as the QB11. They aren't calling designed QB runs, so we're at the mercy of unstable scrambling production. That can return at any point, however, and this is a matchup that should incentivize just that. The Bengals are weakest at LB by far and there are more scrambles while chasing points. Washington is listed as 6.5-point home underdogs carrying a 24-point implied team total (11th). This is a spot to chase the ceiling. Cincy has allowed the second-most rushing yards per game to QBs versus their opponents' seasonal average.

Industry observers note that 6. Patrick Mahomes (vs. ARI) - 20.4 proj. points.

Sources indicate that mahomes exploded for 348 yards and five passing touchdowns against Atlanta in Week 10, bringing his season-long baseline up to QB6 (20.8 PPG). He has delivered +0.15 EPA per dropback (QB9) across his nine starts while chipping in 11.1 rushing yards per game (28th). I can make the case that the return of Rashee Rice and the addition of Josh Downs have the pass game in the best spot of the year right now.

Recent findings suggest that plus, the Cardinals defense can't stop anyone, allowing the third-most fantasy points to QBs this season. Matthew Stafford turned in three scores despite missing his top receivers last week, and Dak Prescott (QB11), Bo Nix (QB3) and Jared Goff (QB4) have all lit it up since September. It's not a surprise to see the Chiefs projected for 28.5 points (sixth). They may not have Kenneth Walker III (groin) either, which could mean additional throwing production on the margins.

7. C.J. Stroud (vs. IND) - 21 proj. points.

Stroud is the QB4 this season, averaging 274 passing YPG (QB3) with 2.1 TDs (QB6). The Texans have much better WR depth now than before, while still struggling to run the ball with any consistency. Houston is suddenly fifth in neutral pass rate (57%), and the Colts are missing all of their best defenders in CB1 Sauce Gardner, CB2 Charvarius Ward and DT1 DeForest Buckner. Recent fantasy QB1s Dak Prescott (336 yards) and Trevor Lawrence (three scores) have come through against them. Houston enters Thursday night as 12.5-point home favorites sporting a strong 28.5-point implied team total (fifth).

8. Trevor Lawrence (at NYG) - 18.8 proj. points.

Lawrence has been in a groove as fantasy's QB8 (20.7 PPG), generating +0.29 EPA per dropback (QB3) with 21 passing touchdowns across nine starts. The only glitch at times is not having a ton of volume due to game script, as the Jaguars easily lead the NFL in point differential. They're actually 31st all time in that metric through nine games.

Sources indicate that this matchup is expected to be a bit closer, partially because the Giants defense has outkicked expectations. They've still allowed the seventh-most fantasy points to QBs, despite getting breakouts from rookie LB Arvell Reese and CB1 Deonte Banks. They're well coached with solid safety play. Where they've been lit up is against the scramble, as both Tyler Shough and Jayden Daniels have run like crazy. That's unlikely for Lawrence, so a more mediocre output wouldn't be too surprising. Jacksonville travels as 6-point road favorites with an implied team total of 26.25 points (seventh).

9. Bo Nix (vs. LV) - 17.8 proj. points.

According to recent reports, bo Nix has more games below 10.0 fantasy points (three) than he does above 20.0 (two) so far. It's largely the same challenges as in previous seasons, but to his credit, things historically have looked way better in the second half of seasons. I haven't noticed that he's a worse athlete coming off the dislocated ankle. It's more of just decision-making inside the pocket, but hopefully Sean Payton got into his head during this bye week. Hopefully Jaylen Waddle was listening, too!

Recent findings suggest that to be fair, Nix has had the third-hardest schedule to date and the easiest schedule moving forward. This week kicks that off. The Raiders have been horrific on defense in both phases. They've allowed the second-most fantasy points to QBs and the second-most rushing yards to all positions. That's difficult to do! Nix has been the QB17, QB23, QB8 and QB8 against the Raiders historically, but this defense's current six-game streak of allowing a top-13 fantasy QB is more important. Denver is set up well as 10-point home favorites with a 25.25-point team total (eighth).

10. Jared Goff (vs. TB) - 17.9 proj. points.

Goff's weird schedule is behind him after facing the Vikings, traveling outside to Miami and then ending up in Germany. Back in Detroit, Goff should normalize. He was the QB4 from Weeks 1-7 before all that and still is the QB6 in EPA per dropback and QB5 in passing yards per game.

According to recent reports, this is still not an easy matchup, though. The Buccaneers bring the heat, something that has historically bothered Goff. Tampa Bay has an awesome CB duo and more depth along the defensive line, while still being coached by a Super Bowl winner. The Bucs are the fourth-best fantasy QB defense, despite everyone knowing this is a relative pass funnel (32nd in rush YPG). Caleb Williams squeaked by as the QB9 in Week 9, but they haven't allowed a top-10 since Week 2 otherwise. The Lions return home as 7-point home favorites with a solid 25.25-point implied team total (ninth).
