Sources indicate that I'd just stolen my very first airplane in GTA 6 and was happily cruising over the city when I looked out my window and saw an enormous purple building. I banked, slowed down, and flew a bit closer to read the huge sign on the roof.

Oh, hell yes. I was so excited to throw my carefully accumulated three hundred dollars away on poker and slots that I immediately attempted my very first landing. After the explosion and my miraculous resurrection, I rushed back to the casino and crashed again, this time with my body into the locked front doors.

The casino in GTA 6 is closed. Trust me, I tried every door, and the only one that opened was a utility closet.

I was early into my playthrough, still in Chapter 1 of the GTA 6 story missions, so I thought maybe there'll be some mission that unlocks the casino so I can play some poker, try my luck at the slots, and maybe bet on the also-closed dog track—I assume it's a dog track because I've seen dogs in GTA 6, but no horses—that's right next door to the casino.

Nope! I'm now 100% done with the main story and that casino is still closed. And that's not the only business in GTA 6 that isn't open for business. Not even a half-mile away from the casino there's a go-cart track called Hot Skidz sporting a huge "Coming Soon" sign. I saw that early in my game as well, and checked back later. Still closed. I also found a second, smaller casino. Also closed. (Related: Animal control called to rescue alligator that turned o...)

It has been revealed that what a letdown. I managed to get my motorcycle onto the go-cart track, and as I drove glumly around it, alone, having no fun, I perked up a bit. Maybe this just gives me something to look forward to. A hint of more singleplayer content that'll be coming to GTA 6 next year. Hopefully next year.

It came to the game post launch, but only as part of GTA Online. It never opened in singleplayer.

Recent findings suggest that but surely, why include the building at all in GTA 6 singleplayer if it wasn't also going to be in singleplayer? They wouldn't do that to us, would they? They wouldn't open it in GTA 6 Online and not open it in GTA 6 singleplayer.

That would be like putting dozens of dogs in a game and not letting us have our own dog. Madness.
