Sources indicate that with a name like Lootbox RPG, it's not hard to see developer Mario Gaida's turn-based dungeon crawler is a bit of a snapshot of 2019, back when lootboxes were all the rage. But, while lootboxes have gone the way of the DoDo, Lootbox RPG is still going strong with its largest enhancement yet that comes absolutely jam-packed with content!

In a recent development, I really do mean largest too. Just for a taster, the 2.0 enhancement for Lootbox RPG includes five new regions to explore, including an icy spire and sweltering desert pyramid, alongside a host of new bosses to face in these areas.
