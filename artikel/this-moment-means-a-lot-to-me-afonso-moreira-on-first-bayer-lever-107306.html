It has been revealed that afonso Moreira (20) had to wait until his 10th Bundesliga game before opening his account for Bayer Leverkusen. A €29.5m signing from Lyon in the summer, the Portuguese attacker had struggled since switching to Germany, but is slowly finding his feet.

“This moment means a lot to me,” he explained after Leverkusen’s 4-0 victory over Paderborn. “I’ve wait a very long time for it. It was such a good feeling. All the pressure has been lifted.” Moreira opened the scoring in the 14th minute before adding his second shortly after the break.
