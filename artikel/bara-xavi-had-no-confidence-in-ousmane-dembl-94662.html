During his time on the FC Barcelona bench, Xavi Hernandez was obviously not completely convinced by the defensive qualities of Ousmane Dembélé. An anecdote told by Alberto Edjogo, former player and consultant for DAZNin the podcast Offsides. Invited to participate in a private meeting with several analysts, the latter notably questioned Xavi on his choice to have his full-backs defend with the midfielders rather than the wingers. The Spaniard’s response was particularly clear regarding Dembélé: “He has a lot of qualities, but I’m not convinced that he does the pressing or that he prevents the ball from circulating to force the opposing full-back to play inside. I prefer Frenkie de Jong to do it, for example. ».

Sources indicate that A statement which illustrates Xavi’s doubts. The current Netherlands coach also explained that he evaluated each action of his players through a precise analysis system. According to his data, Dembélé then displayed 60% poorly executed actions, but the remaining 40% included particularly dangerous situations: “among those he achieves, 10% are scoring chances”. Since then, the Frenchman has changed dimensions. Now Ballon d’Or, Ousmane Dembélé is playing in a completely different register and has established himself as one of the most decisive players at PSG, also thanks to his constant pressing.
