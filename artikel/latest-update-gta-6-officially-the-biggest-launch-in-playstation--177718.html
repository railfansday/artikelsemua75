Sony has shared a selection of staggering statistics relating to the launch of GTA 6 on PS5.

The game set a new record on PlayStation for the highest number of concurrent players on any game.

PS5 players have logged 683 million hours of GTA 6 playtime, and on average, every owner on PlayStation has played it for 17.4 hours. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

In a recent development, outside of the console's launch and Christmas holidays, the launch of GTA 6 resulted in "the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day". GTA 6 was the first game played on 70% of those newly introduced PS5 systems.

"These are just a few ways we’ve seen our PlayStation community come together around this incredible moment in gaming, and we’re excited to see how you continue to build and share newly introduced memories and experiences in the months ahead," noted content communications manager Tim Turi on the PlayStation Blog.

Sources indicate that the day after release, Rockstar Games declared GTA 6 had sold 32.5 million units across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. With these new stats from PlayStation, we know it's sold over 39 million copies on PS5 alone, as of yesterday.
