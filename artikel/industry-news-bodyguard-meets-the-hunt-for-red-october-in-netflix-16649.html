Industry News: ‘Bodyguard’ Meets ‘The Hunt for Red October’ in Netflix’s 7-Part Political Thriller

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

In 2018, Netflix brought the BBC series Bodyguard to its streaming library. The political thriller centers on David Budd (Richard Madden), a former soldier turned bodyguard who's assigned to protect Home Secretary Julia Montague (Keeley Hawes). It's an arrangement that's ripe with tension, as Budd despises Montague's policies and is struggling with PTSD from his service in Afghanistan. Bodyguard still remains one of Netflix's most popular political thrillers, and the streamer is looking to recapture its success with the upcoming limited series, Trinity.

Trinity will reunite Richard Madden with Bodyguard creator Jed Mercurio, but this time, he isn't playing the action hero. Instead, Madden will be playing U.S. Secretary of Defense Webb Preston, while Gugu Mbatha-Raw (Surface) takes on the lead role of Commander Katherine Decker, an officer serving on the nuclear submarine Kansas. Katherine winds up stumbling onto the secret military program "Trinity", which could launch World War III. As Katherine works with Preston to prevent Trinity's fallout, the two grow closer—but their budding bond is threatened by her fear that he might be "the savior of world peace or its greatest threat," according to Mercurio. At first glance, Trinity bears more than a few similarities to The Hunt for Red October. A race to stop World War III? Check. A deeper look into geopolitics and military structure? Check. Rising tensions inside a submarine? Major check. During an interview on Netflix's official site, Tudum, Jed Mercurio revealed that he had another political thriller in mind when creating Trinity. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

"One of my all-time favorites is No Way Out. I wanted to create an action thriller set around the Pentagon with a high-stakes conspiracy to challenge a heroic naval officer.".

No Way Out centers on naval officer Tom Farrell (Kevin Costner), who enters into a passionate romance with Susan Atwell (Sean Young). What Farrell doesn't know is that Atwell is the mistress of the U.S. Secretary of Defense David Brice (Gene Hackman), and this leads him into a web of subterfuge and murder. It's not hard to see where Mercurio drew inspiration for Trinity, and the looming threat of nuclear war adds an extra wrinkle to the narrative. Given that Bodyguard also had more than a few romantic sparks flying between polar opposites, it wouldn't be hard to imagine a similar dynamic between Mbatha-Raw and Madden's characters, which only adds to the mystery that Mercurio has teased about Preston's true objectives.

In addiiton to Gugu Mbatha-Raw and Richard Madden, Trinity boasts a star-studded cast full of supporting actors. That cast includes Marcia Gay Harden (Mystic River) as ambitious CEO Margaret Vandenburg, Bruce Greenwood (Fall of the House of Usher) as Special Agent Eric Colby, Kirk Acevedo (Arrow) as Captain Jock Campbell, JD Pardo (High Potential) as Lieutenant Commander Tom Flores, and James Remar (It: Welcome to Derry) as President Paul Barnard. While Jed Mercurio had previously worked withMadden on Bodyguard, he noted he was happy to cast Mbatha-Raw in a lead role because he was a fan of her past roles: "I’m a longtime fan of Gugu’s work, so I was honored when she took on the role of Commander Katherine Decker.".

Trinity is heading up a stacked December for Netflix. In addition to Brad Bird's sci-fi noir Ray Gunn and David Fincher's The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, the streamer's also releasing a miniseries adaptation of Pride and Prejudice and the final season of Emily in Paris. It's a very stacked lineup, but Trinity stands out thanks to its creator's previous work on Bodyguard and the presence of talented actors like Madden and Mbatha-Raw. If political thrillers with a sprinkling of romantic drama are up your alley, you'll definitely want to tune in when Trinity debuts.

Trinity premieres on Netflix on December 1, 2026.
