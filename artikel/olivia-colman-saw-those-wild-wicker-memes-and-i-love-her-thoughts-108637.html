According to recent reports, the viral ad hilariously advertised a wicker bench with the simple tagline of “Made from husband material”. Tons of movie fans saw it, but now we know that the Wicker cast was laughing over it, too. Colman also stated this of all the Wicker reactions to E! News:.

I’ve never had such a reaction from a trailer. Friends and family—and I don't know how people find it, but then I'm an idiot about social media—but everyone's gone. ‘I can't wait to see this. Oh my god, so exciting.’ It’s lovely, everyone has really taken to it.

Sources indicate that wicker is based on the short story "The Wicker Husband" by Ursula Wills and adapted by writer/directors Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson. Skarsgård wore an actual wicker suit created by Wētā Workshop.
