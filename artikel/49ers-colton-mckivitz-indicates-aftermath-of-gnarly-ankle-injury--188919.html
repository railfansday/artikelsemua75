San Francisco 49ers offensive lineman Colton McKivitz managed to smile through the pain after suffering a grotesque ankle injury on Sunday Night Football.

McKivitz, 30, reposted a photo from the locker room via his Instagram Story on Monday, November 23, showing the NFL star’s left leg in a cast and wrestling on a pile of towels.

The picture was initially shared by trainer and performance coach Tareq Azim.

It has been revealed that “Gods will is imposed for reasons that all make sense when they are supposed to,” Azim wrote alongside the image. “Blessed to have one of the baddest mad men ever as my team mate and dawg I get the honor to coach. @cmckivitz53 is meant for chaos. Love you 🙏prayers for a [speedy] recovery massive dawg.”.

The 49ers have yet to concern an official revision about McKivitz’s injury status, but head coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters that the team believes his ankle is “broken and dislocated,” on Sunday, November 22.

It is widely expected that McKivitz will miss the remainder of the season.

McKivitz’s injury happened during the second quarter of the 49ers’ game against the Minnesota Vikings in Mexico City on Sunday, when his leg was rolled up during a run by San Francisco’s Christian McCaffrey. The NBC broadcast showed McKivitz’s ankle bent in a horrifying direction before he was carted off the field.

Industry observers note that “That was really crushing,” Shanahan, 46, added on Sunday. “Colton has had an unbelievable year. … It was tough to see Colton go down like that.”. (Related: Industry News: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Hugg...)

McCaffrey, 30, called the injury to his teammate “brutal.”.

“He’s somebody that is one of the heart and souls of this team, a guy who shows up every day with the right attitude,” the running back stated. “He’s the standard, and we’re going to really miss him. We all love him. We’re praying for him. It’s one of the worst parts about this game is stuff like that, but we love him and we’re going to play for him from here on out.”.

McKivitz reposted a series of messages from his teammates via his Instagram Story on Monday.

“Ride or die you’ll be back better than ever Uce,” 49ers offensive lineman Dominick Puni wrote.
