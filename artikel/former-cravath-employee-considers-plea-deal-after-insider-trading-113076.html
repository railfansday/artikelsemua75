A former Cravath, Swaine & Moore employee who was arrested on charges of insider trading last week is reportedly considering a plea deal.

Federal prosecutors in New York allege that Chetram Balli, who is also known as Tony Balli, 54, accessed nonpublic information through his role as a document processing specialist for Cravath and traded ahead of at least five of the law firm’s major deals.

According to Law360, a one-page form order signed Thursday by U.S. Magistrate Judge Marcia M. Henry of the Eastern District of Fresh York reveals that federal prosecutors and Balli are engaged in plea discussions. (Related: Tech Report: Jimmy John’s reveals Christmas Vacation me...)

The order also demonstrates that Balli’s defense counsel requested more time to focus on negotiations without the risk of not being adequately prepared for a trial, Law360 reports.

Law360 requested but did not receive comment from either party.

In a recent development, balli began working for Cravath in 1989. According to prosecutors, he supported attorneys by producing legal documents and U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission filings and at times had access to confidential information.

Balli was arrested Thursday after prosecutors filed a criminal complaint against him and alleged that he made more than $70,000 in illicit profits.
