KJ was indicted in September 2026 for aggravated child molestation, aggravated sexual battery, aggravated sodomy, false imprisonment and three counts of sexual battery against a child under the age of 16. (He was not immediately arrested for the remaining charges in the indictment.).

KJ’s attorney filed a motion ahead of the teenager’s September 2026 bond hearing to transfer the case to juvenile court, which would prevent KJ from being tried as an adult.

In a recent development, “It is difficult to comprehend that a 14- or 15-year-old child can be held in detention for a month or longer simply waiting for a bond hearing,” lawyer Jason Sheffield told Page Six in a statement. “But that detention is a direct consequence of the detective’s decision to arrest him on charges of this magnitude.”.

During KJ’s bond hearing in September 2026, prosecutors allegedly claimed that investigators found evidence of KJ engaging in sexual activity with an unidentified family member under the age of 12.

Recent findings suggest that according to Page Six, the investigators reportedly discovered recordings of “multiple encounters” where KJ used “predatory [and] derogatory” language toward the victim. The incident had not been previously reported, with prosecutors planning to press charges.

KJ ‘s lawyer categorically denied the accusations.

“The characterizations made in court regarding the prior juvenile matter involving KJ Biermann grossly misrepresent both the facts and the ultimate resolution of that case,” Sheffield told TMZ in a statement. “The matter arose from inappropriate, crude behavior involving two middle-schoolers.”.

Sources indicate that us confirmed on October 6, 2026, that KJ pleaded not guilty to all seven charges in the indictment. Later that month, a court motion revealed that any motions are due by November before KJ enters his final plea in February 2027.

KJ’s attorney, Jason Sheffield, filed a special demurrer — a legal response challenging the legal sufficiency of an opposing party’s complaint or claim — in Fulton County Superior Court in November 2026, according to court docs obtained by Us.

Sheffield argued that KJ’s aggravated child molestation charge “fails to identify the physical injury allegedly resulting from the charged conduct” and requested that the state “identify the alleged physical injury with sufficient particularity to permit intelligent preparation of his defense.”.

Arguing against the aggravated sexual battery charge, Sheffield claimed that it “alleges penetration by the accused’s fingers but fails to identify the occurrence with sufficient particularity to distinguish it from the conduct charged elsewhere in the indictment.”.

Sheffield also challenged the aggravated sodomy charge, arguing that it “fails to identify the conduct by which force was allegedly accomplished.”.

As for the three charges of sexual battery against a child under 16, Sheffield argued that two of the counts are ”facially indistinguishable” because “both allege the same offense,” while the third count “identifies a different anatomical area” from the other two but “does not otherwise identify whether the alleged contact constitutes a discrete criminal occurrence or part of the conduct charged elsewhere.”.

Lastly, Sheffield fought the false imprisonment charge, arguing that it “alleges that the accused unlawfully detained the complainant without legal authority but identifies no conduct by which the alleged detention occurred.”.

Sheffield requested a hearing to discuss the complaints outlined in the demurrer and asked that the lead detective in the case testify to answer questions about “the facts and circumstances surrounding the indictment.”.

Sources indicate that us confirmed in November 2026 that prosecutors filed a motion opposing the transfer of KJ’s case to juvenile court. According to court documents obtained by Us, the State of Georgia called KJ’s alleged offenses “incredibly serious” and claimed that the teen had exhibited “sophistication and premeditation.”.

“This behavior has become part of a deeply troubling pattern when it comes to this defendant forcing sexual compliance through a personal relationship,” the docs read. “This defendant has proven to be beyond the rehabilitation offered within the Fulton County Juvenile Court process.”.

In November 2026, a Georgia court granted a motion made by KJ’s three younger siblings in which they sought to stop their forensic interviews from being used in his sexual battery case.
