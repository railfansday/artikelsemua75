Following the departure of Mauricio Pochettino to Manchester United, the United States men's national team are suddenly in the market for a latest manager. Pep Guardiola's name has emerged as an ambitious target for the US, with GIVEMESPORT reporting the interest earlier this month.

It has been revealed that the 55-year-old is at the moment without a job, having left Manchester City in the summer. Sources indicate that as reported by The Telegraph, Guardiola has opted to turn down any offer from the US to take over as the national team manager. The former Man City boss is said to be 'determined' to take a full year off from football, having left the Etihad club at the end of last May.

It has been revealed that guardiola has spent plenty of time in the US of late and previously spent a large part of his sabbatical in the country after he left Barcelona in 2012. He's spoken about his fascination with the US before, and how much he enjoyed living in relative anonymity in an apartment near Central Park.

Even so, the Catalan is expected to turn down any approach from US Soccer. For now, his priority is to take a break from the sport.

According to recent reports, although Guardiola doesn't look likely to accept any managerial jobs in the near future, the same report claims he is interested in working with two FIFA nations in the future. It's claimed that he would like to eventually manage a national team at a major tournament.

It has been revealed that he went as far as to hold talks with Italy earlier in 2026. That move fell through with the 55-year-old not ready to return to football. Italian football federation president Giovanni Malago explained: “Pep was honest. He took a sabbatical, partly for family reasons, but if he’d done anything, he would have done it with us.”.

In a recent development, once a year has passed, though, there are two nations that may well 'appeal' to him. The Telegraph report:.

"He has long held an interest in Brazil and their iconic yellow jersey while the prospect of coaching England also appeals to him.".

It's interesting to note that England actually made inquiries about Guardiola replacing Gareth Southgate before ultimately hiring Thomas Tuchel in 2024. The German has a contract which will take him through to Euro 2028.

According to recent reports, carlo Ancelotti is currently head coach of the Brazil national football team. He will be under contract until the end of the 2030 World Cup, so it's unclear whether Guardiola would get a chanceto take over from either man in the near future.

Of course, that may suit the ex-Man City coach, who likely won't return to football until 2027 at the earliest.
