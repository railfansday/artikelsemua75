The Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic remake has become a somewhat infamous project over the course of the past several years. Revealed in 2021, the remake had the potential to bring a venerated classic to a new generation, but reports of its progress over time started to suggest that things weren't proceeding as smoothly as one might hope.

Five years later, we still haven't seen a second of gameplay footage, but it might be time for that to change. Not only has it been confirmed on multiple occasions that the game is still in development, despite fears to the contrary, but the latest rumor also suggests that a re-reveal is imminent.

Last week, X user 100% Star Wars suggested that the Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic remake "is likely to be revealed at The Game Awards," claiming that they first heard of this plan in January and that it appears to remain in place. Backing up this claim, Insider Gaming's Mike Straw posted about his desire "to replay Knights of the Old Republic" one day later, seemingly teasing the announcement with an offhanded "No reason.".

These teases follow a confirmation of the game's continued progress earlier this year, with the chief creative officer of co-developer Saber Interactive telling IGN that the title was "still in development." At an earlier point, the game was set to be developed by Aspyr Media, but shake-ups during the development cycle eventually brought Saber into the mix.

While it might be too early to count your chickens, a re-reveal at The Game Awards 2026 would make a lot of sense. To start with, the awards ceremony has become a major spot for game announcements, drawing a huge number of viewers every year and featuring plenty of trailers alongside the awards themselves. On top of that, Star Wars is already set to have a strong presence at this year's ceremony, with both Star Wars: Galactic Racer and Star Wars Zero Company receiving TGA 2026 nominations.

Recent findings suggest that at The Game Awards 2025, Star Wars also revealed Fate of the Old Republic, a upcoming RPG that seems set to continue in the tradition of the Knights of the Old Republic games. Director Casey Hudson has clarified that Fate of the Old Republic is early in development, so if the Knights of the Old Republic remake truly is set to make a showing this year, it should ultimately release well before the new game does.

The Game Awards 2026 will be broadcast live on December 10, so fans looking forward to a possible re-reveal won't have to wait long to find out if the rumors of its return are accurate. At this point, the biggest question isn't whether the Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic remake still exists, but whether the long-gestating project will ultimately live up to the high bar set by the original game and deliver a proper way to play an excellent Star Wars RPG.
