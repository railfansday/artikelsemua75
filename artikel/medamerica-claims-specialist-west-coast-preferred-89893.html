This is an exciting opportunity to join The Mutual Risk Retention Group's Claims team, supporting healthcare professionals through the complexities of medical professional liability claims.

We are seeking a curious, confident, and motivated professional who brings strong analytical skills, sound judgment, and a genuine interest in medicine and law. While previous medical professional liability (MPL) experience is valued, it is not required. We welcome candidates with experience in insurance claims, workers' compensation, personal injury, litigation, or related legal and medical fields. Reporting to the Assistant Vice President of Claims, this individual will help manage claims across the Western United States while delivering the personalized, high-touch service that distinguishes The Mutual's approach to supporting its insureds.

The Opportunity: Claims Specialist.

Claims Investigation and Management.

Legal Collaboration and Case Strategy.

Policyholder Relationships and Service.

Required Experience and Competencies.

The ideal candidate is someone who enjoys solving complex problems, asks thoughtful questions, and takes pride in supporting others through challenging situations. You do not need to come from a traditional medical malpractice insurance background to be successful. We value professionals who bring transferable experience, a willingness to learn, and an interest in the intersection of medicine, law, and insurance.

Compensation and Work Environment.

Sources indicate that we are excited to share that the base salary for this position is $95,000 - $125,000, exclusive of benefits or potential bonuses. Final base salary compensation will be determined based on factors such as skills, education, experience, and internal equity.

This is a fully remote position within the United States, primarily supporting claims across the Western region, including California, Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Nevada, Colorado, and Texas.

The position requires occasional business travel, generally one to two trips per quarter, including mandatory quarterly meetings and attendance at court proceedings or other business events as necessary.

This is an opportunity to join a collaborative team that values professional development, thoughtful decision-making, and meaningful relationships with the healthcare professionals it serves.
