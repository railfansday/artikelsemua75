Marking their return from the fortnight interval with a dramatic 6-2 victory at home against Iraklis 1908, Imanol Alguacil’s side have their sights set on mounting a genuine Europa League charge this year. Getting their European adventure underway with a 2-1 win against Polish outfit Jagiellonia on September 16th, Olympiacos are unbeaten in all of their previous 12 consecutive appearances across all competitions. In fact, Alguacil’s men have suffered just a single competitive defeat in front of their own supporters since the start of April.

However, despite enjoying a real domestic flurry, Amorim’s camp have drastically failed to find their feet on the European stage. Including a nightmare 3-0 drumming against Bournemouth on October 22nd, AC Milan have lost three of their first four Europa League showdowns and the find themselves sat outside the knockout stage spots. Looking to kickstart their European charge in midweek, the Rossoneri have suffered just a sole competitive defeat on their travels so far this season.
