When asked about his thought process around making that change to the formation, Blackwell mentioned: “Well we haven’t kept many clean sheets have we.”.

The result justified the extra body he added to the defence to sure it up at the back and he was full of praise for the way his side defended.

Blackwell said: “Ben Heneghan was coming in for his first game and I didn’t want to expose him in one on one situations; I thought he was superb today Ben Heneghan, absolutely superb, led the line.

“I thought Flinty [Josh Flint] did ever so well. Flinty’s first game, Ben Heneghan’s first game – I’ve got to give credit to those guys.”.

He added: “The change of shape was just looking at what I’ve got at the club. Priestley’s [Farquharson] out injured now so we’ve lost another centre-half.

“I just wanted to make sure we were solid and that, as you say, we could get more support up to the strikers because we are not getting enough shots on target.”.

Industry observers note that when faced with a follow up question regarding whether he will stick with this winning formula, Blackwell noted: “I will look at all options. It depends on who we play against and how they play – that could have a substantial bearing on what we do.”.
