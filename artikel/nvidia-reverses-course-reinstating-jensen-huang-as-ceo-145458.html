UPDATED 10:50 EST / NOVEMBER 23 2026.

It has been revealed that artificial intelligence chip giant Nvidia Corp. today announced that Jensen Huang has been reinstated as chief executive, president and board director effective immediately after resigning almost two weeks ago.

The company’s rockstar leather-jacket-wearing CEO faced turmoil earlier this month after it was revealed that he had been embroiled in a scandal involving multiple extramarital affairs, including with an Nvidia executive and a suspected Chinese spy. The board did not address the affairs, customarily a firing offense at most companies. in its statement.

Sources indicate that news of his affair with alleged Chinese spy Alice Huang, an executive of Singapore-based Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd., broke on Nov. 10, reported by The Information. She was reportedly instrumental in helping China earn over $7.5 billion in chip sales through Nvidia, which is at the moment sanctioned by the United States from shipping to the country under trade restrictions. Industry observers note that huang resigned amid reports that Nvidia’s board was in talks with looks to replace him. Today’s announcement marks a major about-face by the firm. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

In today’s announcement, the board stated it voted unanimously to reinstate him to his vacated roles after a series of discussions and that it was “satisfied with his candor and ability to lead.” The board added that it received input from “senior levels of United States government” on its decision, and is “confident” that Huang poses no national security risk.

Huang thanked Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress, who stood in as interim CEO, for taking up the helm in his absence before taking back control of the firm.

“I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” said Huang. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.”.

Nvidia shares surged around 29% following Huang’s reinstatement. The stock had fallen almost 33% after Huang admitted to the affair with Alice Huang earlier in November.

In a recent development, “Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as Nvidia CEO is a stunning latest development, but not an altogether surprising one,” eMarketer Inc. analyst Jacob Bourne told SiliconANGLE. “After more than three decades at the helm, Huang has become so central to Nvidia’s identity and the broader AI trade that his departure fueled uncertainty during an already volatile period for the industry.”.

Recent findings suggest that bourne added that Huang’s return may assuage investor upset and restore confidence in the organization, reflected in the current stock upsurge, but the upheaval echoes the brief ouster of OpenAI Group PBC CEO Sam Altman in 2023. Between Nvidia and OpenAI, removing and then welcoming back CEOs amid disagreement and now scandal raises pertinent questions about corporate governance and how the outsized influence of individual leaders in the AI industry will affect the future.

Recent findings suggest that in its statement, the board added that it formed a committee of independent directors to investigate foreign intelligence targeting Huang. The same committee has retained independent counsel to assist it and intends to reach a conclusion despite his reinstatement.

Huang declared his full support for the investigation and said he would cooperate fully.

Sources indicate that the board also announced the formation of a National Security and Export Control Committee, composed of independent directors, to oversee trade compliance and national security risks. The organization added that it will make certain the committee oversees national security matters and trade controls and is currently searching to fill a fresh chief compliance officer role.
