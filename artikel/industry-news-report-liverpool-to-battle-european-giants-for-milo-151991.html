Left-back has become a persistent matter for Liverpool this season.

The departure of Andy Robertson at the end of his contract in the summer marked the end of an era at Anfield, and there is a clear gap in Andoni Iraola's side.

Milos Kerkez and Kostas Tsimikas have both failed to make the position their own, leading to plenty of speculation that the Reds will move to sign a upcoming left-back either this winter or in the summer.

Several top options are now being linked with Merseyside moves as a result, but Liverpool have just been told how much competition they'll face for one of their top targets.
