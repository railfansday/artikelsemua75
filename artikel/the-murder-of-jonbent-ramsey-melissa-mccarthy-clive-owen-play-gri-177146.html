Netflix has revealed a trailer for its upcoming scripted series surrounding the infamous unsolved killing of JonBenét Ramsey.

Released Tuesday, the trailer opens on the early hours of December 25, 1996, when JonBenét’s parents John (Clive Owen) and Patsy (Melissa McCarthy) wake to find their daughter missing and a ransom note on their kitchen counter. A piano version of “Silent Night” sounds quite ominous as it lingers in the background of the trailer, which zeroes in on investigators’ suspicions regarding the child’s own family. The trailer makes it clear that the slaying itself will just scratch the surface of the story that unfolds in the six-episode series. Watch on Deadline.

The Murder of JonBenét Ramsey, which will premiere just short of the 30th anniversary of the six-year-old beauty queen’s death, centers on one of the most infamous unsolved murder cases in American history, and the devastating personal and public reckoning that followed the death of JonBenét Ramsey on Christmas night in 1996.

Recent findings suggest that in addition to the trailer, which is embedded above, Netflix also released a few photos from the upcoming series. Find those below.

The Murder Of JonBenét Ramsey is co-written by Harrison Query and Tommy Wallach, directed by Anne Sewitsky and showrun by LaGravenese. LaGravenese, Query, Wallach, McCarthy and Sewitsky executive produce with Glasser, Ron Burkle, Bob Yari, David Hutkin for 101 Studios.

The cast includes McCarthy (Patsy Ramsey), Owen (John Ramsey), Emily Mitchell (JonBenét Ramsey), Garrett Hedlund (Det. Steve Thomas), Alison Pill (Det. Linda Arndt), Shea Whigham (Alex Hunter), Owen Teague (Shane Edwards), Clifton Collins Jr. (Det. Tom Trujillo), Angus Caldwell (Burke Ramsey), Jaime Ray Newman (Amelia Hunt), Rory Cochrane (John Eller), Chris Bauer (Chief Tom Koby), Will Patton (Lou Smit), Jeremy Bobb (Pete Hofstrom), John Billingsley (Santa Bill McReynolds), Josh Stamberg (Reed Hunt), Margo Martindale (Nedra Paugh), and Tzi Ma (Dr. Henry Lee).

All six episodes stream December 10 on Netflix.
