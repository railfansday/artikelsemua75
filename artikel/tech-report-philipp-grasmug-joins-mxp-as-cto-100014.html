MarineXchange (MXP) has appointed Philipp Grasmug as its chief technology officer (CTO).

In this role, he will lead MXP’s technology strategy, product engineering and the continued development of the MXP Enterprise Platform, with a strong focus on security, scalability and AI.

Michael Eder, CEO of MXP, welcomed Grasmug to MXP, commenting: “As the leading software partner for the cruise industry, we attract top technology talent and leaders. Philipp’s experience in building and scaling business-critical software comes at exactly the right time as we take the next step in our growth story.”. With more than 20 years of experience in developing complex software products for essential business operations, Grasmug most recently served as CTO of UBIMET Group, where he led 30 engineers and a global weather forecasting platform used by clients in insurance, critical infrastructure and renewable energy.

He brings deep expertise in scalable platforms, data-driven product development, AI and building high-performing engineering teams and he co-founded Reactive Reality, taking virtual try-on technology from concept to market. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

“I am excited to join MXP and its talented team. The MXP Enterprise Platform already powers operations for more than 60 cruise brands, and that gives us a unique foundation to build on,” said Grasmug.

“My focus will be on streamlining and scaling our platform, bringing AI and agentic features into our products in ways that create real value, and making sure our customers can rely on technology that is secure, modern and ready for the future of cruising.”.

This follows the recent announcement of a definitive agreement MXP signed with Deep Wave Maritime Technologies Group.

Under the deal, MXP will broaden the services available to its customers as part of Deep Wave, while continuing to operate under its own brand.
