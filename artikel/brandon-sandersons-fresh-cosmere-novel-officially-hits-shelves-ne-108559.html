Brandon Sanderson has given some thought to what he’d do if he woke up as a teenager with all his adult memories intact: he’d happily pass on the opportunity. “I’ve read too many stories about time travel and it never goes well,” the Mistborn author told Woman’s World. He did, however, get out of bed the next morning and write two chapters of a book about a woman who wakes up on her 21st birthday with an entire lifetime to reconsider. Her name is December, and one of the things she remembers is a plague that the people she loves have yet to live through. She sets out on the River Violet to warn the king’s court, with a priestess and an aspiring fashion designer for company. Getting a royal official to take the word of a 21-year-old seriously is an immediate concern, but the harder one is deciding what to change. December can remember the people she lost, but she also knows whom she fell in love with and the children she had. Saving someone now could cost her the chance to meet somebody else later. Tor Books will publish the U.S. hardcover of Brandon Sanderson’s The Fires of December on Tuesday, December 8. The 384-page edition brings the standalone Cosmere novel to bookstore shelves after an earlier release through Sanderson’s Hoid’s Storybook Collection crowdfunding campaign. Readers who prefer a physical copy can preorder the Tor edition now, while the ebook and audiobook have been available to the public since October 6, with longtime Mistborn narrator Michael Kramer reading the audio edition.

In a recent development, you can start with December. Sanderson groups the book with Tress of the Emerald Sea and Yumi and the Nightmare Painter under the name Hoid’s Travails. The three share a narrator in Hoid, the storyteller who turns up across the Cosmere, but each follows a different lead and tells a complete story. Readers can pick them up in any order, including anyone still working through The Stormlight Archive who’d like to spend a weekend elsewhere in Sanderson’s universe.

It has been revealed that sanderson had already tried two other ideas for a secret project before December came to him. Once he had those opening chapters, he wrote the first draft in about a month and printed a copy for his wife, the first person he wanted to share it with. Those same chapters are now available on his website, along with the reading he gave at Dragonsteel Nexus. It’s a good place to hear Hoid tell this one from the beginning, back when December’s biggest question about the River Violet was whether there really was a demon upstream supplying all that blood.
