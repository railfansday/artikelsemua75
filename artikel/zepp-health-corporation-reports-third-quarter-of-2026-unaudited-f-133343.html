Third Quarter of 2026 Financial Results.

Revenue reached US$68.3 million, representing a year-over-year decrease of 9.9% and a quarter-over-quarter increase of 7.5%. The year-over-year decline was due to the broader macroeconomic environment and a strong comparison base, as well as supply constraints on top-selling offerings, particularly Helio Strap and Balance 3. However, revenue from the Active series increased significantly year-over-year.

In a recent development, gross margin was 35.0%, compared with 38.2% and 37.4% in the same period of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026, respectively. Gross margin faced several headwinds during the quarter, primarily elevated memory and other component costs, as well as foreign currency impacts. While product mix improved year-over-year, supported by latest product introductions including the Active series, this favorable mix benefit was more than offset by higher memory costs within our entry-level price segments. Gross margin for the second quarter of 2026 benefited from tariff refunds that the Firm recorded related to duties previously remitted under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act ("IEEPA"). Excluding the impact of these refunds, gross margin increased sequentially.

Research and Development Expenses.

Research and development expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were US$11.4 million, compared with US$10.8 million and US$11.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026, respectively. Excluding US$0.6 million of foreign currency headwinds, research and development expenses were flat compared with the same period of 2025. We continued to invest in a series of cutting-edge products as well as new technologies, including AI, to maintain our competitive edge against our peers. At the same time, we focused on refined research and development approaches, consistently evaluating resource efficiency to optimize return on investment and productivity.

It has been revealed that selling and marketing expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were US$18.0 million, compared with US$12.0 million and US$18.3 million in the third quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026, respectively. The US$6.0 million year-over-year increase was primarily attributable to US$1.9 million investment across online and offline channels for content refreshes, in-store displays and holiday season readiness, and US$1.5 million of launch campaigns for new products including the Balance 3, Balance Ultra and T-Rex Dual Solar. The remainder of the increase reflected US$1.4 million for marathons, Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc (UTMB) and media sponsorships, and US$1.2 million related to the HYROX and new athletes partnerships.

General and Administrative Expenses.

General and administrative expenses were US$5.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with US$7.0 million and US$6.2 million in the third quarter of 2025 and the second quarter of 2026, respectively. We continued to streamline overhead, maintaining disciplined cost control while improving operating efficiency.

GAAP and adjusted operating expenses[2] for the third quarter of 2026 were US$35.0 million and US$34.2 million, respectively, compared with US$29.8 million and US$28.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, and US$36.0 million and US$34.8 million in the second quarter of 2026. Out of the year-over-year increase of US$5.2 million in operating expenses, US$0.8 million was due to foreign currency impacts. The remaining US$4.4 million was primarily due to higher selling and marketing expenses as mentioned above. On a quarter-over-quarter basis, operating expenses decreased slightly.

GAAP and adjusted operating results[3] were loss of US$11.1 million and loss of US$10.3 million, respectively, compared with loss of US$0.9 million and income of US$0.4 million in the same quarter of 2025. The net operating loss was due to lower gross margin mainly resulting from higher memory and other component costs and foreign currency headwinds, as well as increased investment in marketing and branding activities, but the operating loss narrowed compared with the second quarter of 2026.

GAAP and adjusted net loss attributable to Zepp for the third quarter of 2026 were US$12.6 million and US$11.6 million, respectively, compared with GAAP and adjusted net loss of US$1.6 million and US$0.7 million for the same quarter in 2025. The US$10.9 million year-over-year increase in adjusted net loss was primarily driven by a US$6.0 million increase in selling and marketing expenses, as discussed above, followed by a US$3.2 million unfavorable foreign exchange impact. In addition, higher memory and other component costs resulted in a net US$2.7 million gross profit headwind, partially mitigated by a more favorable product mix. These unfavorable impacts were partially offset by US$1.1 million in savings from general and administrative expenses.

Liquidity and Capital Resources.

As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents and restricted cash were US$99.0 million, compared with US$102.6 million and US$106.3 million as of September 30, 2025 and June 30, 2026, respectively.

In a recent development, the Tech giant recorded inventory of US$68.6 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with US$62.4 million as of June 30, 2026 and US$87.7 million as of September 30, 2025. We will continue to manage the inventory level tightly and working capital closely.

Long-term and short-term debt levels remained flat as of September 30, 2026 compared with June 30, 2026. We remain committed to prudently managing our debt profile. Our primary objective is to maintain overall debt levels broadly stable while actively extending the maturity profile by replacing short-term borrowings with long-term debt. During the third quarter and for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, we successfully converted US$26.6 million and US$56.5 million, respectively, of short-term debt into long-term obligations, and we expect to continue this strategy in the coming quarters, supported by sufficient financial headroom and liquidity capacity. Since the beginning of 2023, the Organization has cumulatively retired US$38.1 million of debt, and will continue to optimize its capital structure.

Share Repurchase Program Update.

The Manufacturer declared in its third quarter 2021 earnings release that the board had authorized a share repurchase program of up to US$20 million through November 2022. On November 21, 2022, the board authorized a 12-month extension of the Company's share repurchase program. On November 20, 2023, the board further authorized the Company to extend its share repurchase program for another 12 months. On November 18, 2024, the board further authorized the Company to extend its share repurchase program for another 24 months. On November 22, 2026, the board further authorized the Company to extend its share repurchase program for another 12 months. Pursuant to the extended share repurchase program, the Company may repurchase its shares in the form of ADSs and/or ordinary shares through November 2027 with an aggregate value equal to the remaining balance under the share repurchase program. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had used US$17.8 million to repurchase approximately 2.4 million ADSs. The Company expects to fund the repurchases under the extended share repurchase program out of its existing cash balance.

For the fourth quarter of 2026, the Organization's management at the moment expects net revenues to be between US$90.0 million and US$95.0 million, which would represent an increase by approximately 6% to 12% from US$85.2 million in the fourth quarter of 2025.

This outlook is based on current market conditions and reflects the Company's current and preliminary estimates of market, operating conditions and customer demand, which are all subject to change.

Zepp Health (NYSE: ZEPP) is the technology firm behind Amazfit, a global sports technology brand. Its wider portfolio includes Zepp Clarity hearing technology and Zepp Aura sleep and recovery services.

The company's vertically integrated technology platform brings together Zepp OS, wearable chipsets and biometric sensors designed in-house, and algorithms for biometric measurement and personalized guidance to enhance product performance and user experiences across devices, software and services.

Industry observers note that zepp Health operates as a global multi-hub organization, bringing together R&D, product, design, brand and business expertise across the Americas, Europe and Asia. These teams co-create and share responsibility for innovation, product performance and global growth.
