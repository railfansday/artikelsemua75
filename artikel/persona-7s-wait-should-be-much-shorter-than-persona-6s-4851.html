The wait between Persona 5 and Persona 6 has been a long one. In September, Persona 5 turned 10, and while we've had our first teaser for Persona 6, it's likely to hit the 11-year mark before we actually see it. There has been enough to keep hardcore series fans happy during that time, with multiple spin-offs as well as remakes of Persona 3 and, soon, Persona 4, but it's been agonizing.

Thankfully, Atlus seems fully aware of this problem and has pledged that it is "working on various things" to make sure we don't have to wait anywhere near as long for the next entry of its beloved JRPG series.

Industry observers note that 11 years is a long time to wait for any sequel; just ask Grand Theft Auto 6 fans, who are now diving into the game after a 13-year wait. For context, there were three years between Persona (which is reportedly also getting the remake treatment) and Persona 2, seven years between Persona 2 and Persona 3, just two between 3 and 4, and then eight between Persona 4 and Persona 5. The long wait for Persona 6 is now finally coming to an end.

The eight years between Persona 4 and Persona 5 were already killer, but 11 is taking things to the next level. It won't be the case for the next entry, though, if Atlus is to be believed.

In a new interview, posted today on the official Sega website, Persona series producer Kazuhisa Wada discussed the future of the franchise, including the work on Persona 6 and beyond.

In a recent development, "The one we're right now working on is Persona 6. While there's still limited information I can share, we want to prove the results of all the challenges we've faced so far through the game itself. With that in mind, we're creating something even better than before, and we're feeling a real sense of accomplishment as we work on it," he said. His following comments were the most interesting, though.

It has been revealed that "And Persona 6 is not the end. We are working on various things to ensure that you don't have to wait as long for the next installment as we have in the past. And in order to make Persona an even bigger IP, we are constantly thinking about creative ideas and developments that will not disappoint your expectations. Please look forward to what's to come from Persona.".

It's clear that he sees the frustration fans have had, waiting so long for the next game, and vows that the company is doing what it can to make development on the next title run more smoothly.

Wada's words are very similar to those that came from Rockstar's leadership in the build-up to Grand Theft Auto 6. The 13-year wait for that game was deemed too long, with Phil Hooker, GTA 6's director of development, saying, "The engine, the tools, and the team we've built during this project will allow us to do things more promptly.".
