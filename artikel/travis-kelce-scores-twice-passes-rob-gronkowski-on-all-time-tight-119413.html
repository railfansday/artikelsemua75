Travis Kelce finally jumped Rob Gronkowski on Sunday afternoon.

Kelce hauled in an 8-yard touchdown early in the Kansas City Chiefs' 34-7 blowout win over the Arizona Cardinals on Sunday. That marked the 109th score of Kelce's career, which moved him to third on the NFL's all-time touchdown list by a tight end and broke a tie with Gronkowski. The longtime Upcoming England Patriots star finished his career with 108 touchdowns.

Now, only Hall of Famers Antonio Gates, with 118, and Tony Gonzalez, with 115, sit ahead of Kelce.

It has been revealed that kelce scored again in the second quarter when Patrick Mahomes found him for a 22-yard touchdown. Kelce jumped up and made the grab between a trio of Cardinals.

That touchdown put the Chiefs up 23-7 at the time.

Kelce finished the game with 74 receiving yards on six receptions in the 27-point win, which pushed the Chiefs to 8-2 on the season. Mahomes went 27-of-39 passing for 324 yards with three touchdowns. He sat early in the fourth quarter with the win all but secured. The Cardinals (3-8) have lost three straight.

Recent findings suggest that sunday was also Kelce's 100th regular-season game at Arrowhead Stadium, which made him the fourth player in team history to reach that mark. Kelce has already broken the NFL record for most yards after catch in league history this season, too, and earlier this fall Mahomes and Kelce surpassed Gates and Philip Rivers for the most yards generated by a tight end and quarterback duo in NFL history.

Kelce has spent his entire career with the Chiefs, who first selected him out of Cincinnati in the third round of the 2013 NFL Draft. He has 13,588 receiving yards in his career, which trails only Gonzalez among tight ends, and he has 586 receiving yards and four touchdowns this season.

While Kelce's career is winding down, he is only nine touchdowns shy of becoming the all-time tight end touchdown leader. That may be tough to pull off with just seven games left in the regular season, but the mark is in reach.
