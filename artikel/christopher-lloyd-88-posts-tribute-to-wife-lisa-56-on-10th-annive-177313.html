Lloyd met Lisa, 56, in 2005 and they tied the knot in 2016.

The actor was previously married four times, first to Catherine Boyd from 1959 until their divorce in 1971, Kay Tornborg from 1974 to 1987, Carol Ann Vanek from 1988 to 1991, and finally Jane Walker Wood, whom he wed in 1992 and divorced in 2005.

Earlier this month, the couple opened up about their marriage ahead of their 10th anniversary together.

In a recent development, “I appreciate his focus and his calmness. Because I’m very outgoing and gregarious. And sometimes he won’t talk. So it’s a balance,” Lisa praised the actor in a joint interview with People.

In a recent development, lloyd added, “Well, Lisa claims I’m kind of calm, even though I may be going crazy inside! But I think I’m pretty easy to live with, get along with.”.

In a recent development, “He reads all day long,” Lisa also shared, noting that Lloyd doesn’t go online. “I’ve been with him almost 22 years. My head’s full with a bunch of stupid stuff that you see on the internet… I watch how contemplative and in control he can be, and I’ve always been more hyper and outgoing than him.”.

In a recent development, lloyd, meanwhile, praised Lisa as being “full of energy and excitement to do this and that. She’s far more naturally social than I am. I’m not a social bug by nature, and it just seems the gel just comes together.”.
