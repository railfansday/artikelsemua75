Dozens of passengers across two separate transatlantic Condor flights became ill, causing a mass casualty incident response at Frankfurt Airport (FRA), as reported by aeroTELEGRAPH. While this is still being investigated, there’s a theory as to what happened, given what all passengers had in common….

On Saturday, November 21, 2026, Condor operated two flights from La Romana, Dominican Republic (LRM), to Frankfurt, Germany (FRA). Both flights were operated by the Airbus A330-900neo, with as many as 620 passengers onboard.

Industry observers note that flight DE3705 took off at 8:03PM on Saturday and landed at 9:23AM on Sunday, while DE3845 took off at 9:14PM on Saturday and landed at 10:35AM on Sunday. The flights had one thing in common, which is that between them, dozens of passengers became ill. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

Sources indicate that the illnesses onboard were so bad that a “MANV 25” response was triggered at Frankfurt Airport. That’s a German emergency response alert for a mass casualty incident — “Massenanfall von Verletzten” (though fortunately there were no fatalities here).

Passengers onboard were screened by medical teams, and one person on each flight was taken to a local hospital, while others are being monitored.

Sources indicate that it’s not uncommon to hear stories of multiple people becoming sick on flights. Sometimes it’s a fume event, sometimes it could be a case of food poisoning (though it doesn’t usually happen that fast), etc. So what’s going on here, that people on two separate Condor flights became ill? (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Well, the passengers on these two flights had one thing in common — they all came off the AidaBella cruise ship in La Romana, and then these flights were sold as part of the cruise package.

Sources indicate that so while this is still being investigated, a norovirus outbreak on the cruise ship is believed to be the likely culprit. I’ve gotta say, an airplane seems like a pretty unpleasant place to come down with a gastrointestinal illness, so I imagine the lavatory situation onboard probably wasn’t great.

Dozens of passengers on two separate Condor flights between the Dominican Republic and Germany around the same time became ill, triggering an emergency response at Frankfurt Airport, which included a couple of people being hospitalized.

While it remains to be seen what caused this, the belief is that it could be some sort of a norovirus outbreak, based on the symptoms, as well as the fact that the passengers had all come off the same cruise ship. I hope everyone makes a quick recovery.
