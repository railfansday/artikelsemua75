Marathon is becoming a whole upcoming game next month with a roguelite PvE mode, a beta for a very Halo-esque team deathmatch mode, a vibey social space and more

  • Top of the order is that PvE mode, which has a name now: Expedition.
  • It has been revealed that the previously promised social space will be coming with this revision too, and it's called Limbo.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that marathon is getting a lot bigger next month. In September, Bungie outlined their plans for the shooter making some major promises, namely the arrival of a PvE mode, and now they've shown off what you can expect from its next season, Symbiosis.

Top of the order is that PvE mode, which has a name now: Expedition. It's a roguelite mode, one that's playable in co-op, though a lack of details at this current time doesn't make it clear as to whether you can run it solo or not. The season's trailer, which you can have a gander at below, gives off the impression that you'll have to complete particular tasks before exfiltrating like you normally do. I'm sure we'll learn more in time. (Related: One Familiar Item Of Clothing Scored Lowest for Intelli...)

It has been revealed that the previously promised social space will be coming with this revision too, and it's called Limbo. Morbidly appropriate! You can hang out here with your buds, head to a mini arena for some impromptu matches (I think), or head to the firing range where you can test out your weapons. You'll also be able to try out the latest runner shell, Bishop, a cooler than you, samurai-sword-wielding robot who can jump real good and do that cool movie thing where it slices a whole bunch of enemies at once in one swing real quick like. (Related: The Hidden Social Cost Of Smoking Few People Talk About...)

What will undoubtedly have some folks going "Halo's back!" is the beginning of a beta test for a team deathmatch mode. The Halo comparison is apt not simply because of the fact that it's Bungie who are making it, but because there's a deep-voiced announcer guy talking about all the sick kills you do. Details are slim on how this will work too. Beyond that, there's a few fresh weapons to flaunt, and maybe most excitingly, big ole alien enemies to take on.

Sources indicate that you've still got a little while to wait for Symbiosis, which is at the moment slated to launch on December 8th. The game will be free to play from then until December 15th, too, obviously in a bid to show sceptics how good it is now (hopefully it is good, for Bungie's sake).
