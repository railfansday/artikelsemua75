📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitwarden is the open source password and secrets management manufacturer trusted by more than 15 million users and over 80,000 businesses worldwide. Our products, including Password Manager, Secrets Manager and Passwordless.dev, help individuals, teams and enterprises secure the credentials that run their lives and their companies. We're a fully remote, growth-equity backed company in a scaling phase, and we're building the finance function to match.

Sources indicate that this isn't a classic FP&A seat. Reporting directly to our CFO, you'll own revenue planning and reporting end to end, combining sales finance and strategic finance into one role. You'll be the person who knows our customer and revenue data cold, turns it into the strategy, and puts it in front of the executive team and the board.

You'll partner closely with Sales, Marketing, Product and RevOps leaders on how we grow: where we invest in go-to-market, how we price and package a multi-product suite, and which drivers actually move revenue.
