Christian Pulisic has contributed to a goal every 87 minutes for Milan this season, while the Rossoneri have scored 28 goals in 12 league games, nearly a club record.

Milan secured a comfortable 5-1 win over Frosinone on Sunday, with Pulisic scoring in the second half.

The American has already scored five Serie A goals this season and, according to Gazzetta, has contributed to a goal every 87 minutes through either a goal or an assist.

He has also scored in each of his last three Serie A appearances. The only time he had a longer scoring streak for Milan was between September and October 2024, when he found the net in four consecutive matches.

Pulisic’s scintillating performances have contributed to Milan’s strong attacking displays, with eight different players scoring in Serie A so far. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

The Rossoneri have scored 28 goals in 12 games; only twice in history have they achieved more: 29 under Nereo Rocco and Lajos Czeizler, who coached Milan in the 1950s.
