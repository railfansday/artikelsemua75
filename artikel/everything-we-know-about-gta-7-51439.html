In a recent development, grand Theft Auto 6 has only just been released, so it might seem a bit early to start thinking about Grand Theft Auto 7. I swear I’m not just writing an “Everything we know about GTA 7” article just so I can stuff a post with the “Everything we know about GTA 7” keyword and have Polygon own “Everything we know about GTA 7” search queries. That would be quite a gambit.

Especially for a search term as competitive as “Everything we know about GTA 7.”. (Related: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers and Ray Tune...)

Recent findings suggest that but the truth is there actually are some infinitesimal snippets of information already making the rounds about GTA 7. It can feel like grasping at straws; again, GTA 6 only just came out. But it’s never too early to start the “GTA 7 when???” conversation in the comments section of every Game Awards and State of Play YouTube stream. Here’s everyth– oh, you know the drill.

According to recent reports, the wait for GTA 6 has been atypically long — for the series, for its developer, and for video games on the whole. It’s been 13 years since GTA 5, eight years since Red Dead Redemption 2. Until now, Rockstar Games hadn’t even released a game for this console generation, which, if timelines for the PlayStation 6 and Xbox’s Project Helix are to be believed, will start winding down in a year or two. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

In a recent development, in an interview ahead of GTA 6’s release, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told Polygon that the studio’s map team started work as early as 2015. 2015! Remember how young we were? Remember how innocent we were? Remember how many fewer grays I had? Not that one? OK.

One of the reasons GTA 6’s development took so long was because, alongside making the game, Rockstar has also been refining the technology and processes needed to make games of this scale in the first place.

According to recent reports, “The engine, the tools, and the team we’ve built during this project will allow us to do things more swiftly,” GTA 6 co-head of development Phil Hooker told Bloomberg. “I feel confident that we can build on top of this and go more sideways than upwards to build more compelling content.”.

Recent findings suggest that “We foundationally changed a lot of stuff on this project,” added Rockstar North co-head Aaron Garbut during that same interview. “But now it’s done, and we’re not going to rip it apart.”.

There’s also the fact that Rockstar employs close to 5,000 people today, more than double it did around the end of development for Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018.

In a sprawling Guardian profile ahead of GTA 6’s release, Nelson suggested the series might eventually move beyond its signature satirical version of the United States, but that the studio still had its eye on America for the time being.

According to recent reports, “Never say never, but there’s still so much to do in America,” Nelson mentioned. “It doesn’t matter where you live … no one experiences the America that we’re portraying here in its fullness.”.

Previous Grand Theft Auto games have been set in major metropolitan areas like Los Angeles (Los Santos) and New York City (Liberty City). But even if Rockstar opts not to return to a previously seen location, there are plenty of cultural American hubs to satirize, including Chicago, Seattle, Boston, Washington, D.C., and New Orleans (a 19-century version of which appeared in Red Dead Redemption 2 as Saint Denis).

According to recent reports, let’s imagine Rockstar does leap straight from GTA 6 to a hypothetical GTA 7, as opposed to pivoting to long-desired sequels to either Red Dead Redemption 2 or Bully. We won’t hear about whatever the studio’s next game is for a long time, according to Strauss Zelnick, CEO of Rockstar parent firm Take-Two Interactive. He all but mentioned as much in an interview with TBPN (on Nov. 19, GTA 6’s release date, no less).

“We at Take-Two just went live with GTA 6 on Nov. 19. Wait, let me check my watch… Oh, it’s still Nov. 19,” Zelnick remarked. “I mean, guys, really? My father-in-law had a line about how whenever he had many great business achievements and a lot of admirers, and if he ever said anything nice, he’d say, ‘Oh, those are yesterday’s headlines.’ But it’s not even yesterday!”.

According to recent reports, before Rockstar even revealed a release date for GTA 6, fans of the series began riffing on GTA 7 in a dedicated subreddit. Yes, it’s intentionally tongue-in-cheek, with winking suggestions for its setting (Harrisburg, PA) and features (“will there be car?”), but it gets at the heart why such a thing can resonate in the modern internet in the first place: GTA 6 took a reallllllllllly long time. And as any Elder Millennial (or their therapists) will tell you, humor is a pretty effective coping mechanism.

According to recent reports, in fact, even GTA 6 has gotten in on the GTA 7 memes. Early in the game, as pointed out by some GTA fans on Reddit, you can see an in-game social media post quipping about the excruciating wait for a game called Race and Chase 7. The original name for Grand Theft Auto’s pitch, back in the ‘90s? Race ‘n’ Chase.
