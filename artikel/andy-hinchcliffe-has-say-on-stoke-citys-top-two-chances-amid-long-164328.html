Andy Hinchcliffe has say on Stoke City’s top two chances amid ‘long season’ claim

Sources indicate that stoke City are currently positioned in 2nd place in the table after 16 fixtures behind Middlesbrough on goal difference. SCFC are one point above Wolves in 3rd following their 1-0 win over Birmingham City last time out.

Sources indicate that the Potters have won 10 matches already this season which is more than any other Championship side. They are back in action on Wednesday night with another home clash against Millwall.

Industry observers note that hinchcliffe, who played for Manchester City, Everton and Sheffield Wednesday, has delivered his verdict on the Staffordshire outfit, as per a report by Sky Sports: “We’re only 16 games into a long season. Mark (Robins) will know winter is really tough for every team, particularly when you’re up near the top and everyone is looking to knock you off your position. They’ll be looking at their performances and adjusting their training because that’s all you can do. Could Stoke possibly be in the mix for a top-two finish? With the way they’re playing and the players they’ve got, who could improve further, very possibly.”. (Related: Industry News: Simple considerations for simple people ...)

It has been a strange campaign for Stoke City so far. They lost their first three league games on the spin against Swansea City, Southampton and Wolves and looked set for yet another disappointing year of mid-table mediocracy.

Recent findings suggest that attendances were low, the club looked stale, fans were frustrated and pressure was growing on head coach Mark Robins. However, things changed after they beat Norwich City 1-0 back on 1st September.

Since their win over the Canaries, the Potters’ fortunes have been transformed. They have lost just once in their last 12 outings and that was to Middlesbrough and they are on a roll.

It has been revealed that supporters have started coming back and there is a big feel-good factor around the Bet365 Stadium for the first time in a while. Ex-Coventry City boss Robins deserves credit for the way he has turned things around and he is a vastly experienced boss in the EFL.

Stoke City swooped to sign Ethan Galbraith and Jack McGlynn in the summer from Swansea City and Houston Dymano respectively. SCFC’s decision to land the pair has worked out well and they have both adapted well to life under Robins. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Recent findings suggest that further up the pitch, George Hirst’s goal against Birmingham City should give him a significant confidence boost. Meanwhile, Justin Devenny has turned out to be a smart addition from Crystal Palace.

The Potters sold key man Sorba Thomas to Hull City in the last transfer window. However, they haven’t missed him at all and are less reliant on just one player now.

Recent findings suggest that stoke City will be in good spirits as they aim to keep their momentum going for as long as possible. Their upcoming opponents Millwall have been out of form of late.
