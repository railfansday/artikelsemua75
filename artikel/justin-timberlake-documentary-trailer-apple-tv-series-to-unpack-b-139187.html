📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that apple TV has confirmed that it will launch “Timberlake,” a newly introduced four-part documentary on Justin Timberlake’s life and career helmed by Academy Award winner Fisher Stevens; Variety first reported the news earlier this month. The series premieres globally on Apple TV on Friday, December 11 with the first two episodes, followed by the third episode on Friday, December 18 and the finale on Wednesday, December 23, 2026.

Industry observers note that judging by the trailer and our source, the doc will get into deeper detail about Timberlake’s upbringing, career, years with ‘NSYNC and, not least, his relationship with Britney Spears and the controversy around the “wardrobe malfunction” that took place when he performed with Janet Jackson at the Super Bowl Halftime Show in 2004. Recent findings suggest that “I’m sure you’re going to go back and see these incredible sweet and goofy things from my childhood,” Timberlake says in a voiceover, as the trailer cuts to a clip of him showing off a Janet Jackson poster on his bedroom wall as a teenager, “and some things that are really hard to talk about,” which then cuts to footage of him and Spears. The trailer reveals several shots of Spears and Jackson, as well as Timberlake with his wife, Jessica Biel, and others.

It has been revealed that “Raw, reflective and revealing, Timberlake sits down to discuss the triumphs that made him a global success, his personal perspective beyond the headlines and the challenges he’s faced behind the scenes,” the announcement reads. “Directed by Academy Award winner Fisher Stevens (“The Cove,” “Beckham”), the series spans three decades of music and popular culture and features never-before-seen archival footage and exclusive interviews with family, friends and collaborators.”. It has been revealed that “Timberlake” is produced for Apple TV by Blackbird Presents Films, Misher Films and Highly Flammable, with Maura Anderson and Stevens producing. Kevin Misher, Keith Wortman, Lee Eisenberg, Michael Rapino, Ryan Kroft, Rick Yorn, Josh Dembling, Johnny Wright and Andre Person serve as executive producers. The series is a Live Nation Studios presentation.
