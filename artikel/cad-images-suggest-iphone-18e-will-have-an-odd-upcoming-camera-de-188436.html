CAD images of the iPhone 18e, said to come from a previously reliable source, have shown a rather odd new design for the camera bump.

If accurate, it would represent a significant change from its predecessor, which simply has a single lens protruding without a surrounding bump or plateau ….

The iPhone 17e was rolled out in March with a design unchanged from last year’s iPhone 16e. While the camera did get some feature enhancements, including the ability to store depth information for portrait photos, the physical camera design remained the same.

It has been revealed that the current model has a single lens protruding directly from the casing with a flush-mounted flashlight next to it. Engadget reports on CAD images showing a very different approach with next year’s iPhone 18e. (Related: How to train a latest language model from scratch using...)

The renders show an almost square camera bump measuring about 28 mm by 35 mm that houses the single rear camera, the LED flash and the rear microphone.

The site doesn’t share the images, but says that the camera bump looks similar to that of the M5 iPad Pro, shown below. (Related: Fine tuning CLIP with Remote Sensing (Satellite) images...)

It’s unclear what might have prompted the change of design, especially as the site’s Filipe Espósito says he’s also seen CAD models for the standard model iPhone 18 or the iPhone Air 2, neither of which adopt it.

Previous reports have suggested that the only spec change with the iPhone 18e over its predecessor will be performance improvements thanks to the A20 chip.
