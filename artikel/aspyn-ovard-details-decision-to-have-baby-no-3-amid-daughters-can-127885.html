Aspyn Ovard is shedding light on her motherhood journey.

Over a week after the Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County star shared her daughter Cove’s terminal cancer diagnosis on the Hulu series premiere, she explained why she decided to grow her family and have a third baby with then-husband Parker Ferris amid their eldest daughter’s health battle.

“I didn’t want my other child to grow up alone,” Ovard—mom to daughters Lola, 5, and Elle, 2, with Ferris—shared in a Nov. 21 YouTube vlog, answering a fan’s question about her decision to have more kids. “I did decide to have another baby. I did do it on kind of a crazy timeline.”.

Sources indicate that “It was a lot to be doing all of that with everything we were going through, but I just knew that that was what was right for our family,” she continued. “I didn’t want a really big age gap, so that was something that I put myself through and did for my family and for my daughters.”.

And while the 30-year-old and Ferris were in the midst of navigating Cove’s diagnosis at the time, Ovard noted that being pregnant ultimately made her stronger.

“That was the one thing I could focus on,” she recalled, “and the one thing that was in my control.”.

Though the reality star admitted that it’s difficult to discuss that time, she shared that pregnancy “kept me going.”.

In a recent development, “I do think that being pregnant saved my life,” Ovard continued. “I don’t know how I would have had the strength to keep going and stay here if I was not growing that baby, so that also was a huge reason why I felt like I needed to do that in that time.”.

The social media personality welcomed daughter Elle in 2024, announcing the birth of her third baby on the same day she filed for divorce from Ferris.

While revealing Cove’s cancer diagnosis for the first time on the premiere episode of the Mormon Wives: OC, Ovard shared that her marriage to Ferris was already struggling when the former couple learned of their daughter’s diagnosis.

Industry observers note that “My marriage was nowhere near in a place to be able to make it through something like this,” she mentioned during an emotional confessional. “We weren't even going to make it through before this happened.”.
