From witches to vampires and werewolves, Robert Eggers has had his fill of the horror genre after more than a decade.

The Werwulf co-writer and director recently explained that he’s ready to break free from “Robert Eggers’ House of Horror” as he hopes to explore other genres with his next project, following Werwulf‘s Christmas premiere in theaters.

In a recent development, “I’m eager not to continue purposely doing Robert Eggers’ House of Horror,” Eggers mentioned during a recent Q&A. “I don’t need to do the Mummy or The Opera. Not to say that I won’t, but I think I need a break.”.

In a recent development, eggers’ comments come amid recent talks with Focus Features over an adaptation of William Shakespeare’s Romeo & Juliet, which he explained would be “a break” from the next horror pic he was planning. Watch on Deadline.

Sources indicate that “I was doing something else,” noted Eggers. “But what happened was, after Werwulf, I was writing another horror movie, and it got so dark again, and I was like, ‘I need a break. So, I’ll do something about two teenagers killing themselves.'”.

In a recent development, following his 2015 feature debut The Witch, Eggers has been praised as a upcoming master of horror with films like The Lighthouse (2019), Nosferatu (2024) and Werwulf, premiering Dec. 25 from Focus Features.

In Werwulf, a man (Aaron Taylor-Johnson) in 14th century England finds him and his family threatened by a ravenous beast haunting the town. The film also stars Lily-Rose Depp, Willem Dafoe, Ralph Ineson and more.
