Nashville’s finest were pushed to the brink during the 9-1-1: Nashville fall finale — and after a kidnapping twist, two beloved characters could be in grave danger.

Warning: Spoilers below from season 2, episode 6 of 9-1-1: Nashville.

“All the main characters are at risk,” showrunner Rashad Raisani exclusively told Us ahead of the Thursday, November 19, episode, teasing that both Fire Captain Don Hart (Chris O’Donnell) and Detective Rogue Kelly (Ryan Phillippe) cannot come out of their predicament “unharmed.”.

During the fall finale, titled, “Tears of the Nile,” Rogue’s season-long foe known as “The Pharaoh” gave him 48 hours to figure out what his cryptic “kill the seven” message meant and how to stop his latest attack on Music City.

According to recent reports, with the help of Nashville’s first responders, Rogue discovered that the “seven” individuals meant seven firstborn children, who all disappeared on the same night after playing a video game about a pharaoh. The vow for the “death of the firstborn” is the 10th and final plague in the Book of Exodus, following the killer’s modus operandi (MO). While the seven children were found safely — after their impending doom was livestreamed and a grain silo filled with water with every newly introduced viewer — the pharaoh was still on the loose. (One of the kids, who were all connected through their church, was able to describe him to a sketch artist, getting them one step closer to finding his identity.). During the final moments of the episode, Don stopped to help a man whose car broke down ahead of celebrating his son firefighter Ryan Hart (Michael Provost) and wife Dr. Sam Hart’s (MacKenzie Porter) pregnancy announcement at his home.

Don was drugged and kidnapped. When he woke up there appeared to be a bomb around his neck and he was looking at the pharaoh, a dark-haired man with a scruffy beard. They weren’t alone, however, as Rogue had also been taken and was tied up across from Don.

Industry observers note that “We warned you what would happen if you kept interfering but you didn’t listen, did you?” pharaoh told Don. “You thought you denied me the death of the firstborn. You just delayed it. And the only question is, which one of you will it be?”.

After pharaoh’s ominous warning, Raisani told Us that fans should be “pretty worried” about the hostage situation.

“This case is going to go to even more twisted places and darker places than it even seems,” he said. “And it’s going to get pretty dark really fast.”.

While the executive producer played coy about how Don and Rogue will escape their captor, Raisani confirmed that “definitely we aren’t killing two [characters],” as Don’s sister-in-law, Cammie Raleigh (Kimberly Williams-Paisley) deals with her own villain in 9-1-1 call center supervisor Marcia Zellner (Camryn Manheim).

Scroll down for a breakdown of what’s to come when 9-1-1: Nashville returns in January — including how much danger Cammie is in with her prank caller and if Phillippe is coming back after this season:.

“It’s two things. One, is that he warns them from episode two on. He says, ‘Anybody who interferes with my prepares, you will be punished. There will be consequences.’ And he’s kind of warned them a couple times,” the showrunner explained. “So now he’s saying, ‘OK, here are the consequences.’”.

Additionally, the serial killer warned, “I’m gonna have my death of a firstborn [plague], and you know, massive surprise, Don and Rogue are both firstborns.” Raisani added, “His will will be done, and so that’s kind of his ultimate blueprint was to say, ‘You guys got my way, and now there’s a price.’”.

“We always knew who this person would be, but we also didn’t want to let anybody look too innocent because it’s a complex story,” Raisani told Us, noting that while fans have been “clever” in their theories — one of which was that Rogue was actually the killer — the mystery of which characters are “not as innocent or as above board as they seem” is still being unraveled.

He pointed out, “There is a web that’s a little bit more complex than what’s being revealed right now.”.

“I didn’t want to be done with him so fast, so that there was no chance on my end that he was going to be the Pharaoh,” Raisani revealed, noting that while someone could die when the show returns, Phillippe’s story as Rogue isn’t done yet.

In a recent development, the executive producer told Us, “I love what Ryan has been on the show and what he’s brought to the show and his energy and his vibe. … He is a bedrock character on the show.”.

Raisani noted that like the 9-1-1 indicates that came before Nashville, adding a key character during season 2 is like “adding a major piece to the puzzle” and helps the writers “figure out what we are missing.”.

“Ryan really has enhanced our show in a great way. So I definitely see him with us for the long haul,” he teased.

“It’s put on hold while his dad’s missing. He’s not going to leave and, you know, head out of town, so it’s put on hold until there is resolution on that,” Raisani told Us in regards to Ryan taking a break from firefighting to join Sam as she undergoes surgery to battle her cancer and hopefully protect her baby in another state.

He reassured Us that the story will be told and it’s a “pretty massive” arc for the married couple.

“[Ryan] is going to step away from the firehouse to be with Sam and and try to get her through this really scary and harrowing journey that she’s on, and they’re going to go through it together,” Raisani stated, confirming that the story line will “resolve near the end of the season.”.

“She has a very specific reason why she hates Cammie so much,” Raisani explained of 9-1-1 call center supervisor Marcia’s vendetta against worker Cammie, which as of the fall finale has included prank calls and threats of harm virtually.

The showrunner warned that Marcia “means to torment her at the deepest levels, which is part of why she picks the phone is the way to be cruel to Cammie because she identifies with the phone more than any other object to help people.”.

Marcia, who has been disguising her voice over the phone and calling Cammie a “hag” while pretending to be a stern but helpful boss at work, is now “tainting” Cammie’s perception of the phone being a lifeline.

“That’s just phase one for what Marcia’s got in store for Cammie,” he teased. “She really does want to take Cammie apart emotionally and mentally, and ultimately physically, and so yes, it’s definitely going to escalate and get more intense.”.

He also hinted that Marcia’s young son, who was killed by a drunk driver, could be the key to their connection.

“Right now, Rogue is an obsessive person. It wasn’t his fault, but his first wife died because he was so obsessed with the last serial killer,” Raisani explained. “This is not a guy who’s got, you know, he’s not on Tinder when there’s a serial killer out there.”.
