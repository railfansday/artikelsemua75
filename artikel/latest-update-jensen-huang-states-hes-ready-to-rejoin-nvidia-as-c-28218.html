Jensen Huang’s resignation as Nvidia CEO shook up the AI market and US economy last week, but now via The Wall Street Journal Huang has spoken out publicly for the first time since stepping down, and he signals he’s ready to return as CEO if asked.

Today Robbie Whelan at The Wall Street Journal published Jensen Huang’s first public statement since departing Nvidia last week.

It has been revealed that “Since my departure, I’ve seen an outpouring of calls from Nvidia’s employees, investors, business partners, and customers for me to return as CEO,” Huang stated in a short statement. “If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the organization, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO.”. According to recent reports, since Huang stepped down, President Trump publicly called on Apple’s Tim Cook to step in as Nvidia CEO. But it’s expected that a new CEO—assuming it’s not Huang—will take much longer for the company to find and Cook is unlikely to want the job.
