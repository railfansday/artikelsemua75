Prime minister also says former Tory premiers who have criticised revival of Brexit debate are ‘stuck in the past’.

Andy Burnham has stated it would be “unthinkable” for the UK to rejoin the EU without holding another referendum, offering his strongest commitment yet to directly consulting the public if he concludes Britain should go back in.

The prime minister is planning to set out the long-term options for closer ties with the bloc next month, with concrete steps coming after the next general election, as he has pledged to stick to Labour’s manifesto red lines.
