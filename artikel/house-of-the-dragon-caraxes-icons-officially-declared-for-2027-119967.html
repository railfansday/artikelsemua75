Industry observers note that house of the Dragon has been a fascinating experiment for the longevity of George RR Martin's wider universe in television form, and for the most part, it has been largely successful. Though the series has lost a bit of momentum as it wore on, and it never reached the lofty heights of the original Game of Thrones, it has done enough to show there's plenty of potential moving forward in new stories.

Industry observers note that having said all of that, the most notable thing that it did was restore faith after a horrendous Season 8, which really could have seen the franchise as a whole die on the spot. If you do count yourself among the many fans of House of the Dragon, there's a new release that celebrates one of its coolest winged beasts, Caraxes. (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

According to recent reports, over on the Iron Studios website, the manufacturer of physical memorabilia has confirmed that pre-orders are now open for Caraxes - House of the Dragon - Icons, a new highly-detailed polystone rendition of Daemon's dragon. The figure clocks in at 14" (H) x 10.9" (W) x 13.3" (D), making it the perfect size for display area at home, without taking up too much space.

The official description for the statue reads as follows:. (Related: Hugging Face Reads, Feb. 2021 - Long-range Transformers...)

"Inspired by the acclaimed HBO series House of the Dragon, Caraxes joins the Icons line from Iron Studios. Known as the Blood Wyrm, Caraxes is one of the most fearsome dragons in the world of Westeros and serves as the mount of Daemon Targaryen.

"This collectible faithfully recreates the dragon's distinctive appearance, featuring his elongated neck, powerful wings, menacing expression, and unique serpentine physique. The detailed sculpt captures the creature's imposing presence, while the themed display base enhances the dramatic presentation inspired by the Targaryen legacy. Expertly crafted and hand-painted, this statue is a must-have for fans of the series and collectors of fantasy-themed collectibles.".

Recent findings suggest that if you aren't familiar with Caraxes' story, he's widely known as the Blood Wyrm thanks to his blood-red scales, serpentine build, and immense ferocity. First claimed by Prince Aemon Targaryen, he spent many years without a rider after his death before being claimed by Daemon, and playing a crucial role throughout the events of House of the Dragon.

By the time of the events of Game of Thrones, some 170 to 200 years later, dragons are seemingly entirely a thing of the past, until Daenerys hatches her three eggs and rules the skies (until she doesn't).

In a recent development, as mentioned earlier, the statue is currently available for pre-order, with an expected release date of April—June 2027. It's also worth noting that the images included in the listing are of the current design, which is still awaiting approval from the licensor. It's also not cheap, coming in at a considerable $399.99, though in this case you are paying for the quality.

As for the final season of House of the Dragon, there's a bit of a wait for that as well, with the final season not expected to debut until 2028 (though we don't have anything more specific than that just yet).

Taking place about 172 years before the events of Game of Thrones, House of the Dragon tells the tale of the rise of the Targaryens, the only family of dragonlords to survive the Doom of Valyria.
