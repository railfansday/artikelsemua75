In Upcoming York City, a lunch reservation at The Plaza is always a good idea. But on Thursday afternoon, there was a particularly good reason to be there. Around 400 guests from the fashion, business, entertainment, and philanthropy worlds arrived at the storied hotel for Citymeals on Wheels’ 39th annual Power Lunch, raising more than $1.3 million to help feed older New Yorkers.

It has been revealed that this year’s honorees included Latest York City police commissioner Jessica Tisch, Imaginary Ventures co-founder Dame Natalie Massenet, and Today co-host Al Roker, each honored for their longtime support of Citymeals. Deborah Roberts, a Citymeals board member and ABC News correspondent, served as emcee.

It has been revealed that A number of familiar faces from the city’s social scene turned out, including Karlie Kloss, Lauren Santo Domingo, Tamara Mellon, and Derek Blasberg. Event chairs Blasberg, Dr. Samantha Boardman, Margo M. Nederlander, and Lizzie Tisch helped bring the afternoon together, alongside Citymeals board co-presidents Anne Cohen and Julie Daum.
