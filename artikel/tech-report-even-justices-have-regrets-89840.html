In June 2023, the court decided Students for Fair Admissions, Inc. v. University of North Carolina, holding that race-conscious admissions at Harvard and the University of North Carolina violated the Constitution. Chief Justice John Roberts’ majority opinion in the case, along with Justice Brett Kavanaugh’s concurrence, cited Justice Sandra Day O’Connor’s 2003 prediction in Grutter v. Bollinger that “25 years from now, the use of racial preferences will no longer be necessary to further” student body diversity.

It has been revealed that but the justices did not account for one thing, according to Brooklyn Law School Professor Yuvraj Joshi: O’Connor’s later misgivings about that statement.
