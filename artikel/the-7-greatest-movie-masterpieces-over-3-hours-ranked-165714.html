The biographical epic follows T. E. Lawrence, a lieutenant in the British Army tasked with uniting the feuding Arab tribes to fight as one force against the Turks as WWI rages on. Ego, eccentricity, and emotional turmoil play as much a part in Lawrence’s journey as his far-famed tactical nous and military exploits. It culminates in one of Hollywood’s most quintessential achievements, an ageless icon of cinematic majesty.
