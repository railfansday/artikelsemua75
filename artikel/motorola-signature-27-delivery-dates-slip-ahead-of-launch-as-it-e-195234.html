Motorola and Best Buy websites are now showing an early December shipping date, despite at least one customer already receiving tracking info.

In a recent development, tons of excitement surrounds Motorola’s upcoming release of the Signature 27, and now retail websites are showing that new preorders won’t be arriving until early December. The original announcement in early November proclaimed a November 19 release date, but Motorola tells 9to5Google:. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

We’re excited by the strong consumer response of the newly introduced motorola signature 27, with demand exceeding our initial expectations. As a result, some orders may experience updated shipping timelines. Our teams are working to meet demand and get devices to customers as quickly as possible.

Recent findings suggest that motorola and Best Buy websites are still showing “Pre-order” buttons, so while you can order one right now, December 8 and December 11 are the current ship dates. Motorola also just confirmed that the Motorola Signature 27 will be officially available in India starting December 9 (via GSMArena), further complicating the supply chain. Recent findings suggest that despite this, one Redditor who preordered states they received a tracking number for the Signature 27 and the free Motorola Watch Ultra preorder, and it’s scheduled to arrive on November 25. The Motorola Signature 27 has been available in Europe since November 16, so it’s clear demand is quite high for Motorola’s biggest premium launch in a long time.

According to recent reports, the Motorola Signature 27 is the most premium phone Motorola has possibly ever launched in North America. The company has shied away from releasing flagship phones here for years, although Motorola’s Razr line of foldable phones often blends premium specs with more mid-range ones. The excitement for the Signature 27 is completely understandable in this case, especially now that Motorola is offering the full seven-year software revision treatment that Samsung and Google do.
