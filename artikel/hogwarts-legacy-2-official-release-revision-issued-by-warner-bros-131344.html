Despite the controversy surrounding its launch, there is no denying that Hogwarts Legacy dominated the gaming sphere in 2023, with the Harry Potter-inspired RPG even going on to become the best-selling game of the year.

As such, it was not long before a sequel was revealed, and over the past three years, Avalanche Software has been hard at work getting ready to send players back to Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. (Related: School sends out minibus to catch absent pupils playing...)

It may have been the best-selling game of 2023, but Hogwarts Legacy did not come without its downfalls, especially as it did not receive any expansions or substantial post-game content drops. As such, fans are feeling mixed over a sequel. Will it expand on the open-world and offer long-term content, or will it be much of the same?

At the time of writing, Hogwarts Legacy 2 does not have a release date, or even a release window, despite previous rumors suggesting that it would coincide with the upcoming launch of the Harry Potter television show, which is set to debut on Christmas Day. However, it is unlikely that Hogwarts Legacy 2 is going to pop up over the next month, so we can safely assume that it will be 2027 at the earliest. (Related: Take-Two CEO is shutting down questions about GTA 7 jus...)

It was back in October that Warner Bros., the publishers behind Hogwarts Legacy, issued a statement on the game's progress, and after Paramount acquired Warner Bros. Discovery, it was confirmed that Warner Bros. Games would merge with Paramount Games Studio to create one giant gaming division.

Sources indicate that it will be this gaming division that works on Hogwarts Legacy 2, but, as mentioned, no further information has been revealed, and fans are yet to see any gameplay or substantial updates. As such, its potential cancellation has been a subject of worry for fans, but according to a letter to shareholders discussing WB's games division, WB's strategy will be to focus on its most successful franchises, with Harry Potter being one of them. "We expect Games to more meaningfully contribute to segment profitability going forward as our pipeline expands," the statement reads, "including the second installment of Hogwarts Legacy.".

With The Game Awards taking place on December 10, we can only hope that this next major gaming event will spare a moment for Hogwarts Legacy 2, and it would be perfect timing in the lead-up to the Harry Potter television show, especially as the two projects are rumored to be working together closely. However, this is mere speculation, and for now, we will have to wait with bated breath to see what comes next for the franchise.

Back in 2023, our very own William Cennamo awarded Hogwarts Legacy an almost perfect 9/10, with his review reading:.

According to recent reports, hogwarts Legacy is a wonderful game filled to the brim with adventures to be had, activities to engage in, and secrets to be found. The school and the surrounding areas have been recreated beautifully, and players will easily lose hours lost in the whimsical splendor of this wizarding world.
