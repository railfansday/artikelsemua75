Recent findings suggest that if you can believe it, Love and Deepspace is almost two-years-old, and Infold Games is celebrating the moment in a few major ways. The Throne of Eros patch brings the game up to version 5.0, and it’s one for fans of all love interests.

For one, this is the first time all five of them are brought together. The update is also adding some important latest features, alongside anniversary events, and more.

Throne of Eros also doubles as the fresh Wish Pool, which is the one that has all of the game’s love interests in a single banner. This is a limited-time affair, of course, but it’s not the only thing you should look forward to here.

There’s also the Memory Growth Bonus, another limited-time event that you can take advantage of to grow your Memories. The even bigger news is that you can target your Wishes through the Xspace Echo, which lets you select five Memories to create your own pool from. You can use this to get any Memories you might not have, of course.

On the topic of Memories, you’ll also be able to unlock newly introduced second anniversary KV Memories, as part of a Yearly Highlights Event that will let you look back on your own journey with the game over the past year.

On the story and narrative side, Throne of Eros adds two upcoming main stories, with new branches for Xavier, and Rafayel. There’s even a narrative-specific event that will run throughout the anniversary period, through which you can earn two daily outfits for each of the five love interests.

You can also expect various other quality of life updates that touch on the Journal, Phone Moments Search, Resources Quick Cleanup, and new Tete-a-Tete that should make interacting with all of them more streamlined.
