In a recent development, picture this. You’re going through the grueling process that is gift shopping. You spend hours, days (but let’s be honest, not weeks because you left this for the last minute) mindlessly walking through stores, reading online gift guides, trying to find the perfect gift for every single person in your life. You check everyone off your list, one by one, until you get to the dreaded name: Dad.

According to recent reports, you ask your dad what he wants and you hear the famous words “I don’t want anything,” the least helpful words in the English language. You ask your mom for help and she tells you to get him socks. You rack your brain trying to remember what he likes, what his interests are and you’re drawing blanks. Your throat is closing up, and you start breaking out in hives as the last store is about to close the night before the holiday. You reach out and, in defeat, pick up tube socks before you black out. Recent findings suggest that if your dad loves a good drink at home, get him this set to make the whole experience feel more luxe. The kit will look like a statement piece on his bar cart while he entertains and makes his world famous Scotch on the rocks.
