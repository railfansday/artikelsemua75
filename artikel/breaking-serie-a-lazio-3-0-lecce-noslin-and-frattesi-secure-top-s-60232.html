Lazio consolidated their Serie A leadership with a thumping 3-0 victory over Lecce, thanks to a Tijjani Noslin double and delightful Davide Frattesi lob.

The Aquile were the surprise outfit of the season, flying high at the top of the table and one of only two sides still unbeaten. Mattia Zaccagni, Nuno Tavares, Josip Sutalo and Adam Marusic were out injured, but Davide Frattesi shook off a knock on international duty to start. Lecce went into the break with one point from four rounds, missing Santiago Pierotti and Ivan Ilic, but Medon Berisha and Joel Monteiro returned to the bench from injury. An early Diogo Leite volley from a corner tested Wladimiro Falcone, while Amar Fatah went on a mazy run to curl just past the far top corner. (Related: Tech Report: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons i...)

Lazio took the lead when Gustav Isaksen got down the right and stood up a cross for the Tijjani Noslin header to break the deadlock.

Lecce replied, Christos Mandas getting down to parry the deflected Konan N’Dri strike and the follow-up from Willem Geubbels too, while Tiago Gabriel nodded just over on a free kick after the restart.

An acrobatic Fatah volley turned the N’Dri cross over the bar, but after all those chances, Lecce found themselves 2-0 down.

It was a well-worked move, Kenneth Taylor gathering the Romano Floriani Mussolini cross and finding Noslin with a cheeky back-heel flick, the Dutchman’s strike bending back Falcone’s glove at the back post from six yards.

Frattesi threatened to make it 3-0 when getting on the end of an Isaksen cross, but this time Falcone was ready, though he was beaten by a moment of magic.

In the final minute, Matteo Cancellieri knocked down a loose ball in midfield to send Frattesi forward, he drew out the goalkeeper, and deposited a perfect lob into the empty net.

Albert Gudmundsson made his comeback after a shoulder injury and had the ball in the net during added time, but only after wandering offside.

Noslin 25, 68 (La), Frattesi 89 (La).
