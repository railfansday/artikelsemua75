Holiday shoppers let AI agents find gifts — but don't trust them with their credit cards

🌐 Source: Qz • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (116 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

It has been revealed that amando Komoda runs a Minneapolis company that sells supplies for dry-aging meat, and he uses an AI agent called Instinct to set up alerts for hard-to-find parts, such as a particular Raspberry Pi chip, so he doesn't have to search for them himself. Often he still buys the part himself. When he does let Instinct pay, he approves the purchase first and gives the agent a credit card number that works only once, so it can't charge anything he hasn't agreed to. "I wouldn't give it access to my best credit card," he told Business Insider. "I've watched enough sci-fi movies that I can't trust it completely.".
