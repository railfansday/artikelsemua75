Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola said his side could “celebrate at half time” during their 4-0 win over Manchester United.

The Reds ran out easy victors against their fierce rivals on Sunday, with United hoping to snap a miserable run of just one win in 12 matches.

They did not get the desired bounce from the arrival of upcoming boss Mauricio Pochettino, who watched his side thrashed at Anfield and go down to ten men in eight minutes after Leny Yoro’s red card, which he bemoaned afterwards.

Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz netted the goals to hand the hosts a straightforward three points that sees them sit fourth, seven points off league leaders Arsenal.

United, meanwhile, are spiralling and remain in the Premier League relegation zone, in 19th place.

Iraola seemed to fire a jab at Pochettino and United in his post-match interview.

"It's a big win, always against United it is a big game,” he said.

“I think you can enjoy it, you can see it coming. It's difficult to enjoy in the Premier League, normally you suffer even if you win, but I think we could celebrate at half time and enjoy it a little bit and have lovely moments.

According to recent reports, "We could have scored more. We missed some clear chances today. In other games you will remember those chances, today we've controlled the game and everything was happening around the box.

"It's a 4-0 [win] that was good for [our confidence], trying to get better results here at home.".
