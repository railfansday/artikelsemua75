🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 27 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (39 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

High-resolution 3D modeling of Viking brooches and the molds used to make them identifies surprisingly few matches between them, indicating the brooches were not mass-produced, as previously thought, but instead made by individual, creative artisans.
