Sources indicate that the Premier League is back this weekend after the November international break and Fantasy Premier League managers will have been keeping a close eye on the action around the world. A number of players pulled out of their countries’ squads with injury concerns and could face a race against time to be fit for a return to league action. Others had been dealing with knocks or longer-term absences before the break and have had more time to work on their fitness ahead of a potential return.

Manchester City and Fulham kick off the weekend’s action on Saturday lunchtime, with Enzo Maresca’s hosts looking to get their season back on track.

That is one of seven matches on Saturday, along with Bournemouth vs Nottingham Forest, Coventry vs Crystal Palace, Tottenham vs Ipswich, Chelsea vs Leeds, Aston Villa vs Sunderland and Newcastle vs Arsenal.

In a recent development, on Sunday, Hull host Brighton before Manchester United head coach Mauricio Pochettino takes his new team to Anfield to face Liverpool. The weekend’s action concludes on Monday night, when Brentford welcome Everton to West London. (Related: Welcome spaCy to the Hugging Face Hub...)

Here, Express Sport takes you through the latest and most vital injury updates ahead of this weekend’s action...

Note: This article will be updated throughout Friday to bring you all the latest news.

Chelsea striker Joao Pedro has been absent since mid-October with a hamstring injury. The Blues have won just one of their three league games in his absence, losing to Tottenham and Sunderland.

Xabi Alonso was asked about the Brazilian’s fitness before the Sunderland reverse and urged patience until after the international break - with defender Maxence Lacroix in a similar situation. “For sure ahead of the Leeds game I will have more updates, but not today," Alonso said.

It has been revealed that manchester City defender Gvardiol was forced off injured in his team’s defeat at Nottingham Forest before the break. However, it wasn’t deemed serious enough to keep him out of international duty.

In a recent development, the former RB Leipzig star played the full 90 minutes for Croatia as they lost to England and beat Czechia. This suggests he should be available for his club on Saturday.

In a recent development, liverpool captain Van Dijk pulled out of the Netherlands squad with a fitness concern. It came after he admitted to playing through a niggle for his club.

His club and international team-mate Ryan Gravenberch has also been dealing with pain of late, missing the victory over Crystal Palace as a result. However, both Dutchmen could be in line to play some part against Manchester United.

It has been revealed that brentford striker Igor Thiago was sent off in the Bees’ defeat against Brighton last time out. He caught opponent Chema Andres with his arm and saw his yellow card upgraded to red after a VAR check.

The Bees appealed the decision but the appeal was unanimously rejected. This means the Brazilian will miss his team’s next three games, beginning with Everton on Monday.

Manchester City centre-back Guehi didn’t emerge for the second half against Nottingham Forest last weekend. Enzo Maresca remarked the player had asked to be withdrawn due to injury and Guehi subsequently pulled out of England’s Nations League squad.

The former Crystal Palace star is a doubt for Saturday’s game, as is fellow defender Abdukodir Khusanov. With that in mind, Gvardiol’s availability feels important.

According to recent reports, summer signing Manzambi was forced off with a calf problem in the latter stages of Aston Villa’s 4-4 draw with Manchester United before the break. He was ruled out of Switzerland’s Nations League double-header after being assessed by national team medics.
