Newcastle United remain keen to see Joelinton become a key part of Matthias Jaissle's side despite recent injury concerns, sources have told Football Insider.

Joelinton's future at Newcastle was the subject of discussion over the summer as Eddie Howe departed along with a slew of stars. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

The 30-year-old was linked with a massive-money switch to Saudi Arabia, but remained at St James' Park following Jaissle's arrival.

An injury in pre-season put paid to any chance of a move, but the Magpies boss has remained certain that he wants Joelinton to be a big part of his plans going forward.

The Brazilian midfielder has been restricted to just five appearances this season because of a lack of fitness, but as I revealed on the latest edition of Football Insider's Transfer Insider podcast, Newcastle are still planning for the future with him in their squad.

There are no current plans to let him leave in the next window despite injury concerns and previous reports.

The 30-year-old's absence has been felt in Jaissle's squad, with his strong performance in their 2-0 win at Crystal Palace last month proving just how influential he can still be.

Even though injury challenges and other midfield additions have contributed to ongoing talk of an upcoming exit, Joelinton remains a valued Magpies player and they want him back fit to start as soon as possible.

Speaking on the Transfer Insider podcast, I explained: "I don’t think there’ll be concerns [around his future]. I think it’ll be disappointing for Newcastle and disappointing especially for Joelinton, who had only recently returned to first-team action after an injury-disrupted start to the Premier League campaign.

"Obviously once he was fit he came straight back into the team for Matthias Jaissle, so I think that demonstrates how important he will be to the Newcastle manager going forward.".

The powerful Brazilian missed the first seven Premier League matches of the campaign and is now sidelined again ahead of Newcastle's home clash against Arsenal this weekend.

Jaissle provided a latest revision on his problem, picked up in training during the international break, ahead of the Gunners' visit, saying: "I don't want to be too precise with the medical update. We will get feedback from the specialists and we will find out how long Joe will be out. We will get an update, and hope he is back soon.".
