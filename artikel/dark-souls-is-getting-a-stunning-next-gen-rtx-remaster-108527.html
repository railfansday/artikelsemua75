It was in September 2011 that the gaming landscape was forever changed thanks to the arrival of Dark Souls, an action RPG by FromSoftware. Despite sounding like your average RPG on the cover, it was not long before it became a worldwide phenomenon, and to this day, it sits at an impressive 8.5 User Score over on Metacritic.

Serving as both the first game in the Dark Souls trilogy, as well as the spiritual successor to FromSoftware's previous title, Demon's Souls, Dark Souls saw players take on the role of the Chosen Undead and travel to the kingdom of Lordran in order to preserve the Age of Fire or bring about the Age of Dark. As with any FromSoftware RPG, Dark Souls was once perhaps known for its punishing combat, but, over the years, it has been remembered for so much more, including a story of survival and hope against the darkest of times, its iconic characters (Solaire, my boy), and a stunning world that still holds up to this day. (Related: Redditor forced to bring desktop PC in checked luggage ...)

Luckily, 2011's Dark Souls received a remaster in 2018 which not only improved the somewhat outdated graphics, but also welcomed a whole new legion of fans to the franchise, especially as a PC port was also part of the remaster.

Sources indicate that as such, PC modders have been working hard over the past couple of years to make Dark Souls even better, and that includes a modder known as ‘gabdeg’. Thanks to them, an RTX Remix Mod for Dark Souls has now been made available, and it aims to bring full path tracing to the beloved FromSoftware title.

The modder has managed to use Dark Souls' own assets to create fresh PBR textures and introduce improved lighting. On top of that, those who download the mod can expect improved effects, water graphics, and even higher frame rates. Using the RTX Remix, Dark Souls will now showcase path tracing, and uses new PBR maps based on the original game assets. Now, each map will have much more depth and detail, especially when it comes to light and shadows, which Dark Souls has a lot of.

In a recent development, however, do not be concerned that this mod changes the overall feel of the game. It managed to retain the magic of the original, and just enhances what was already put there by FromSoftware. As such, it is the perfect beginner mod for those just wanting a little boost to Dark Souls' graphics.

Last but not least, this latest mod introduces high frame rates courtesy of Frame Generation, as well as DLSS 5 NR support and experimental HDR10 support. As with any mod for PC, how well they work will also depend on your computer's limitations, and so bear this in mind when you download gabdeg's mod.

As a work-in-progress compatibility mod, do not expect an unofficial remaster and, over time, more updates will be released in order to make it the ultimate Dark Souls experience on PC.

You can now find the Dark Souls Prepare to Die Edition RTX mod on Nexus Mods, where it is completely free to download for those who already own a copy of Dark Souls on PC.
