I'm not using it as such since I still have more income than expenses in retirement. However I have setup a ARVA in my spreadsheet for if/when that is no longer the case. I think it is a great way to ensure that you spending won't outstrip your assets. I think it would be most appropriate for those that have some flexibility in their spend whose real question isn't "how long will my money last?" but is "how much more can I actually spend?" For us, it provides a way to see how much of our portfolio we likely can afford to donate and gift and still ensure our retirement.I think the key is being comfortable with adjusting your max spend amount each year based on current portfolio balance and expected real returns.Statistics: Posted by WeakOldGuy — Mon Nov 23, 2026 11:11 am.
