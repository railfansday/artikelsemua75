An Alabama man is accused of fatally shooting his wife, stepdaughter and her boyfriend in the head as they slept at home.

James Hanks’ wife, Kimberly Hanks, died at a hospital the afternoon of Monday, November 23, after a few days on life support following the attack, the Pike County Sheriff’s Office stated in a news release issued the same day.

James, 64, is facing capital murder charges in the deaths of Kimberly, his stepdaughter, Elizabeth Dean, and her boyfriend, Micheal Catic, per AL.com.

The investigation stems from Thursday, November 19, when a school resource officer alerted deputies that James’ two step-grandchildren hadn’t been picked up from Goshen High School, the sheriff’s office stated in a Friday, November 20, news release.

While trying to find the students’ family members, deputies reached James at his Pike County home and asked whether he knew where his stepdaughter, Dean, and her boyfriend were, according to authorities.

James told deputies Dean and Catic left the home and “didn’t know where they had gone,” according to the sheriff’s office.

Despite James’ claim, cars belonging to Dean and Catic were parked in the driveway outside of the home, indicating they hadn’t left, the agency stated.

Kimberly worked as an ICU nurse at Troy Regional Medical Center, according to a statement the facility shared with WSFA.

Since deputies were unable to contact or locate Kimberly, Dean and Catic, the sheriff’s office decided to enter the home “to determine whether anyone required emergency medical assistance or protection,” the agency mentioned.

While outside the residence, a Pike County Special Response Team first tried to get James to step outside. The team forced their way into the home when James refused to leave and found him in the living room, where they detained him before searching the residence, according to the sheriff’s office.

Authorities ultimately discovered Kimberly, Elizabeth and Catic had been shot in two bedrooms while they appeared to have been asleep.

Dean and Catic were found dead lying in bed together inside one room with gunshot wounds to their head, the sheriff’s office mentioned.

James’ two stepchildren were placed in the care of other family members as he was taken into custody, according to the sheriff’s office.

A motive for the killings wasn’t specified by law enforcement.

It wasn’t immediately clear whether James had an attorney who could comment on his behalf.
