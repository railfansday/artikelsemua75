Ciara Miller was eliminated from Dancing With the Stars during week four, but the pressure to perform is not far from her mind.

“I have stage fright. I have severe stage fright,” Miller, 30, exclusively told Us at Raising Canes’ Paddock Club suite at the 2026 Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix on Saturday, November 21, while reflecting on her DWTS journey. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

Miller joked, “But that’s OK because I’m medicated,” adding that overcoming her fears in the ballroom is something she takes pride in after competing on season 35 of the ABC series.

In a recent development, “It was also so fun — not fun, the stage fight was not fun,” the reality star told Us. “But to be able to conquer something that is very intimidating that you’re not good at and display that for millions of people to see, I’m proud of myself.”. In a recent development, the Summer House star took a deep breath before adding, “I did it. It’s done.”.

It has been revealed that miller was paired with pro Brandon Armstrong on DWTS. Throughout the season, the pair performed a foxtrot, tango, cha-cha and jazz.

Miller and Armstrong, 32, were sent home following their jazz routine to Mariah Carey’s “Emotions,” which earned them a score of 21 out of 30 from the judges.

According to recent reports, “I’m so grateful for Brandon,” Miller stated during the October 6 episode. “This experience has been the moment of a lifetime, and there’s no one else I would rather have had this moment with.”.

Following her elimination, the Traitors alum had time to reflect a little more on the competition.

In a recent development, “It’s just sad, like, obviously, [I] loved being here. [It was] so fun, such a challenge and it was so nice getting to challenge myself every week and doing latest things,” Miller tearfully revealed during the October 8 episode of the official DWTS companion podcast. “I hate that it’s cut short, and [I’m] just bummed.”.

Through all the ups and downs, Miller stated Armstrong had her back and pushed her to be her very best.

Sources indicate that “Brandon is really so patient, and I don’t take for granted how annoying it is to probably have to basically carry someone around, essentially, and teach them all the things and all the ropes,” she recalled. “Even just having anxiety and that being so annoying sometimes, and he’s so patient and, like, never made me feel bad about it and really did help talk me through everything and, like, allowed me the space to just be, but also offered words [of support and advice.”.

Miller joked that after a few bad experiences with men — her ex West Wilson put her through the wringer after their 2023 split, secretly hooking up with their Summer House castmate and her former friend Amanda Batula in February before becoming an official couple — she was pleasantly surprised to connect with Armstrong as a friend.

I feel like it was everything I needed right on time.”.
