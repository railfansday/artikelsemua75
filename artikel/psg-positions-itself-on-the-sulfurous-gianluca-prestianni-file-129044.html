Times have changed since the Vinicius-Prestianni affair. A little less than a year ago, the Argentinian was at the center of a serious controversy during the Champions League match between Benfica and Real Madrid. The Real Madrid winger accused his opponent of making racist remarks to him, while covering his mouth so that his lips could not be read. Referee François Letexier was forced to stop the match for a few minutes, which ended in a bit of chaos after this affair.

According to recent reports, UEFA still opened an investigation leading to a 6-match suspension of the Benfica player, 3 of which were suspended for comments deemed “discriminatory” And “homophobic“. This affair was even the trigger for a newly introduced law by FIFA. From, “any player covering his mouth during a confrontation with an opponent may be penalized with a red card“. With 12 goals and 6 assists in 20 matches in all competitions, he does a lot of damage from his left side or in the center, where his coach Marco Silva places him most of the time.
