📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Every now and then I come across a tool that continues to impress me long after the novelty wears off. The latest is the Expandable Push Shoe from Bow Products. Compared with other commercial and shop-built push shoes I’ve used, this one stands out for its thoughtful design and ease of use.

As the name implies, the push shoe expands—from 1¾ in. to 6 in. wide. It features three ribs, each with a rubberized gripping surface on the bottom and an adjustable push fin on the rear. The fins slide up and down and stay in position with no need for a locking mechanism. A comfortable, rubberized handle extends up from the center rib.

According to recent reports, as you expand the shoe, the two outer ribs simultaneously move equidistant from and parallel to the center rib. Once it’s adjusted to secure the workpiece and clear the blade, a turn of a knob locks everything in place. An adjustable side support can be attached to either outer rib and dropped to the table for added stability on narrow rips. The adjustable side support works well with 5/4 stock and thinner lumber but doesn’t adjust for 2x material.

It has been revealed that unlike some competing products that require studying the instructions, this one is intuitive and simple to adjust. The hollow design makes it easy to see the blade and position the ribs. I initially bought it to use at my tablesaw, but I’ve found it equally useful at the jointer, router table, shaper, and bandsaw. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Recent findings suggest that as a practicing emergency physician, I’m well-acquainted with the consequences of saw-related hand injuries and know that the best safety product is one that people will actually use. After a few months, I can attest that the Expandable Push Shoe is one of those products. You can find out more and buy one for $80 at bow-products.com.
