Sources indicate that third victory in a row for Galatasaray! After its victories in Lille and against Stuttgart, the Turkish club followed up well by beating Aston Villa (1-0) this Tuesday, in a postponed match on the 5th day of the Champions League.

In a match very poor in scoring opportunities, Galatasaray made the difference at the end of the game thanks to a goal from Deniz Gül (1-0), and came back, with nine points on the clock, to match their evening opponent. Conversely, it is the second defeat in a row for Unay Emery’s men, who started their campaign with three successes.
