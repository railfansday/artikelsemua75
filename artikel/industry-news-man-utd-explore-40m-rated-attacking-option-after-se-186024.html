Recent findings suggest that the Red Devils have endured a difficult start to the campaign, and their attacking problems have become increasingly apparent since Benjamin Sesko’s injury. The Slovenian striker is expected to remain unavailable until January, leaving Pochettino short of reliable options in the final third.
