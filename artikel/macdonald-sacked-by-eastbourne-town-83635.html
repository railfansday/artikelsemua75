But his ousting has drawn an angry reaction from many on social media, with some saying it is a ‘disgraceful’ decision.

"The Committee would like to place on record their sincere thanks to Jude and his management team for their hard work over the last 4½ years, where, following their arrival, they rapidly reshaped the remaining playing squad, and with some exciting new arrivals, just two years later introduced an amazing mid-season recovery to storm up the SCFL Premier Division table to finish runners-up and then win the league play-off final.
