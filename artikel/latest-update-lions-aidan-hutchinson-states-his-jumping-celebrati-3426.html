Detroit Lions edge rusher Aidan Hutchinson commented publicly on Thursday for the first time since suffering a season-ending torn ACL in his right knee.

In an Instagram post, the two-time Pro Bowl defensive end pledged to come back from his injury stronger than ever and to never again jump up to celebrate a big play.

Recent findings suggest that "I honestly can't believe it," Hutchinson wrote in his post. "I've jumped hundreds of times on a football field and never in a million years did I think this would happen to me. But even something like this won't hold me back.".

According to recent reports, "Two things I know for sure," he added. "1. I always come back stronger. 2. That's the last time I go airborne.".

Hutchinson injured his knee on the Lions' first defensive series during their matchup in Munich with the Fresh England Patriots on Sunday.

Following a sack of Patriots quarterback Drake Maye, Hutchinson jumped in celebration and landed awkwardly on his right leg. The star pass rusher immediately fell to the turf and quickly grabbed his right leg, gesturing to the front of his right knee.

Industry observers note that maye reportedly suffered a fractured ankle on the sack, though the Patriots have yet to confirm that injury. Yet Patriots coach Mike Vrabel sounded as if a return was possible for the quarterback this season, likely depending on whether surgery is required. In a recent development, hutchinson finishes his 2026-27 season with six sacks and 24 total tackles in nine games. He was also credited with one pass defended and one forced fumble.
