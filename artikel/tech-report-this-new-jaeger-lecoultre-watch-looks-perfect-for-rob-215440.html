Just a week after Robert Pattinson was announced as the next James Bond, Jaeger-LeCoultre, the Swiss watchmaker for which he's an ambassador, has unveiled a latest Reverso that looks rather well suited to 007.

Recent findings suggest that it's certainly a good match for Pattinson's personal tastes. “I definitely like really thin watches,” he stated in a recent interview. “That's the main thing. In general, I would probably prefer a leather strap.”.

The fresh Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds checks both boxes. Measuring just 7.56mm thick, it has an 18-carat pink-gold case, a silver-colored dial with a texture inspired by stacked coins, and a black alligator strap. Its smaller proportions–40.1mm by 24.4mm—recall the original Reverso of 1931, whose ingenious swiveling case was designed to protect the watch during polo matches.
