Huang is getting by with a little help from his friends.

Industry observers note that let’s say you’re Jensen Huang. You (allegedly) cheated on your wife with an alleged Chinese spy, allegedly had an affair with not one, but two subordinates, and (allegedly!) “terrorized” your employees, a series of indiscretions that led to your ouster from what was once the world’s most valuable organization. (The stock price has recently taken a tumble, for obvious reasons.) And now you’re back, baby — and all your friends are so eager to congratulate you. (Related: Industry News: Block Sparse Matrices for Smaller and Fa...)

Elon Musk, for one, is thrilled.

And why wouldn’t he be? As a fellow leather jacket enthusiast who has a checkered history with women — including those who work for him — it’s clear that he and Huang have a lot in common beyond their positions as tech CEOs. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, too, is a man who can relate to Huang’s struggle.

Gavin Baker, founder and chief investment officer of Atreides, is so impressed by Huang’s rise from the ashes that he’s going to buy a leather jacket of his own.

Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeny chimed in with an oblique reference to Huang’s outerwear.

And then there’s billionaire investor Bill Ackman, a real wife guy if there ever was one — but one who, for better or worse, seems to understand that the market comes first.

Of course, no one has been more effusive than the man who may have orchestrated it all. President Donald Trump posted his lengthy congratulations to Huang on Truth Social. “I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure,” Trump wrote. He commended Nvidia’s board for making the “correct and proper” decision in reinstating Huang — a decision that, the board admits, was made with input “from senior levels of the United States government,” who determined that “Huang’s reinstatement poses no security risk.” Isn’t that great news? (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)

Trump expects Huang to make him proud. “I think you’re going to see some tremendous numbers out of this tech giant,” Trump remarked on CNBC. “I talked to him last night. I said, ‘Jensen, just promise me one thing: You’re going to work hard.’ I spoke to him last night because I knew what was going on.”.

The market’s reaction may be the most jubilant of all. Per Odd Lots host Joe Weisenthal, NASDAQ-100 futures jumped so promptly that trading was halted. Congratulations to everyone who bought the dip, I guess.
