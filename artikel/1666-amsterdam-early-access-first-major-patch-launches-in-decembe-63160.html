Recent findings suggest that panache Digital Games will release the first major content update for the Early Access version of dark third-person narrative action adventure game 1666: Amsterdam in December, the developer announced. It will expand the game’s world, story, and supernatural abilities.

A Latest Region, a New Original, and More Witchcraft.

Arriving during the final moon of the year, the first major enhancement marks the beginning of a series of planned expansions for 1666: Amsterdam. (Related: Google Home speakers get a major Gemini upgrade ‘soon,’...)

Sources indicate that players will be able to explore Ouderkerk, a latest region slightly larger than the game’s currently available area, effectively doubling the world players can explore. Alongside this new location comes another Esbat and a new Original, bringing the next chapter of Noa’s journey to Early Access.

The upgrade will also introduce additional Lux and Nux spells and combos, along with newly introduced jewels and potions, giving players more ways to develop their abilities and approach encounters.

Panache Digital Games will share more details about these additions in the weeks leading up to the December release.

■ Early Access Price Remains Unchanged.

Panache Digital Games has also confirmed that 1666: Amsterdam will remain at its current Early Access price when the December update introduces, with no price increase for the new content.

This first major upgrade is part of the team’s ongoing Early Access roadmap, with further expansions planned as development continues.

1666: Amsterdam is available now in Early Access for PC via Steam and Epic Games Store.
