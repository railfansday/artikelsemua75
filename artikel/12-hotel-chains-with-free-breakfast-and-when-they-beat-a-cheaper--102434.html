12 Hotel Chains With Free Breakfast – and When They Beat a Cheaper Room

Industry observers note that midprice hotel chains such as Hampton by Hilton, Holiday Inn Express, and Drury include a hot breakfast in the room rate. When you compare hotels, add what your family would spend on breakfast out to the cheaper room’s rate, and book the breakfast hotel when its rate is lower than that total.

In a recent development, breakfast out can easily cost a family of four $40 or more a morning. The government’s per diem, the daily allowance federal employees get on work trips, includes $16 to $23 for breakfast alone, depending on where they travel. According to recent reports, A hotel that includes breakfast saves you that cost every morning of your stay. Its rate can run higher than a rival’s and still cost your family less, as long as the difference is smaller than what breakfast out would cost. Industry observers note that these 12 chains include breakfast at most or all of their U.S. hotels, grouped here by hotel family.

Where breakfast costs extra, elite status in the chain’s loyalty program can get you the meal or a credit toward it. At Hilton’s U.S. hotels that don’t include breakfast, Gold and Diamond members can choose a daily food and beverage credit, which covers them and one other guest registered to the room. Marriott Bonvoy’s Platinum Elite status, which takes 50 nights a year to earn by staying, lets members pick points, a breakfast offering, or another gift on arrival, depending on the brand.

Some hotel credit cards come with Gold status, no stays required. For a family of four, though, a chain that includes breakfast for everyone in the room does more than a credit that covers two.

Compare each hotel’s total for the night, taxes included, and add what breakfast out would cost your family to the one that doesn’t serve it. Say breakfast would run a family of four $45, and the choice is $159 a night with breakfast or $139 without. The cheaper room saves $20 but leaves $45 to spend on breakfast, so the breakfast hotel comes out $25 ahead each morning, or $125 over five nights.

In a recent development, the breakfast hotel stops saving you money once the gap between the two rates is bigger than your breakfast bill. Against a $109 room plus that $45 breakfast, the same $159 hotel costs $5 more a night.

Industry observers note that before you book, check that the rate you picked includes breakfast and that the serving hours fit your prepares. Drury serves breakfast from 6 to 9:30 a.m. on weekdays and 7 to 10 a.m. on weekends, so a family on the road by 6 a.m. would miss it.

On longer stays, a room with a kitchen lets you make lunch and dinner too.

Find what your family spent the last time you ate breakfast out, on a receipt or your card statement, and plug that figure into every hotel comparison on the trip.
