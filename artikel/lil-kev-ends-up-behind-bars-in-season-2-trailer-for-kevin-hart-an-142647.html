Lil Kev is in massive trouble. After leading cops on a slow-speed chase, 12-year-old Kev ends up in the slammer (juvenile detentation actually) in the official trailer for Season 2 of the Kevin Hart animated series Lil Kev.

In a recent development, set in 1993 North Philadelphia, Lil Kev follows twelve-year-old Kev (Hart) as his enormous ambition and wild imagination fuel one scheme after another. Raised by his fiercely protective mother Nancy (Wanda Sykes), he navigates the chaos created by his recovering father Henry (Gerald ‘Slink’ Johnson), cynical older brother Robert (Jamar Malachi Neighbors), and ex-con uncle Richard Jr. (Deon Cole). Through schoolyard rivalries, neighborhood hustles, and family setbacks, Kev turns every disaster into a story.
