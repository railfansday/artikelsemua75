Take MIDI note 69 as A at 440 Hz, and compare two ways to interpret a major third above it. With a 5:4 ratio the upper pitch is 550 Hz (386.314 cents up). With twelve-tone equal temperament the upper pitch is 554.37 Hz (400 cents up). In this illustration either mapping can associate the upper pitch with note 73. Industry observers note that run this Python 3 example to generate two synthetic WAVs. Both use the same root, level and duration; only the upper frequency changes. Start playback at a low level.

Industry observers note that if the receiver only uses equal temperament, the WAV is the reliable way to hear your original pitch choices. Live can also approximate a tuned clip using its Retune Set option, but it may move or drop notes. Do it in a copy and label the approximation.
