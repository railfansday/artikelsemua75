La Gazzetta dello Sport write that Amorim has been coaching two teams since the summer: one which is excellent in the league with some ups and downs, and another that is frankly terrible when the Europa League anthem players. (Related: Breaking: GTA 6 players who used PS5 Latest Zealand tri...)

In a recent development, milan score 2.33 goals per game in the league on average, compared to just 1 per game in Europe. All four of those goals in the UEL came in the win over RB Salzburg too: they have drawn a blank in the other three.

Industry observers note that looking a bit deeper, Milan have less possession in Europa League matches (53%, compared to 59% in the league) but shoot 12.5 times per game, more than in Serie A. In Europe they get into the box more often: 26 touches in the opponent’s box compared to 23.

According to recent reports, so, why do they score so much less despite being more threatening? Simply put, they shoot worse: the Rossoneri have a very high conversion rate in Serie A (26.4%) which plummets to 10% in Europe. This means that in the UEL it takes 10 shots to score a goal, while in the league it only takes four.

According to recent reports, it’s not all mathematics. Anyone who has watched Milan in Europe have noticed their difficulties. At Bournemouth, they were never in the game, against Ferencvaros they collapsed, against Benfica they deserved to lose against a fellow contender.

In a recent development, with just hours to go before the match against the best defence in the competition, another test awaits. However, Amorim must find a way to concede less too. In Europe, Milan concede 1.92 xG per game, while in the league it’s just 1.28.

It has been revealed that this all leads to a discussion about line-ups, and Amorim has rotated a lot outside of league action. The right defender has always been Filippo Terracciano or Fikayo Tomori, never Mario Gila. Without the Spaniard, there’s a noticeable difference.

Recent findings suggest that in midfield, Luka Modric has never started, and Adrien Rabiot started only against Salzburg and Ferencvaros. Up front, Diego Moreira started only against Bournemouth. Life, with or without those three, changes completely.

Industry observers note that amorim already knows he won’t have Modric against Olympiacos, but he can strategy with Rabiot and Moreira, concocting a strong side despite the injury issues. He’ll ask for overtime tonight and on Monday in Cagliari, knowing his side will then have almost a week to rest.
