Recent findings suggest that it’s now official: Michael Carrick has been sacked by Manchester United. The Red Devils have not yet announced the name of his successor, but the entire English press is unanimous in affirming that the current United States coach, Mauricio Pochettino, is the lucky one. To snatch the 54-year-old Argentinian from the American Federation, MU will pay no less than €9 million. An XXL sum for a coach far from having convinced the football world since 2019 and the Champions League final played with Tottenham against Liverpool. Pochettino was also able to perfectly capitalize on his market rating to hit the jackpot. Fired by Spurs in 2019, when he had four years left on his contract, the former defender received €14.5 million in severance pay.

Recruited by Paris Saint-Germain in January 2021 to succeed Thomas Tuchel, Pochettino was fired one year from the end of his lease, in July 2022, in exchange for a check for €10 million. Coming to the rescue of Chelsea during the summer of 2023, the technician only lasted one season, but his ouster cost the Blues €12 million. Between his termination fees and the amount that MU will pay to buy him back, the native of Murphy cost the clubs who hired him very dearly (€45.5 million). And the bill is likely to rise further if he were to be dismissed from his duties at Old Trafford in the future. It is indeed a safe bet that the Mancunian leaders will offer him a salary equal to or greater than the €5.4 million per year guaranteed by his upcoming contract (running until 2030) signed with the American Federation.
