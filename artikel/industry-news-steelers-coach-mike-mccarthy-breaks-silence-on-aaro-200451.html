Mike McCarthy has broken his silence after Aaron Rodgers was shockingly cut from the team after he publicly slammed the Pittsburgh Steelers head coach.

“It was a process that went on throughout this week,” McCarthy, 63, told reporters on Wednesday, November 25, when asked what initially led to Rodgers, 42, being benched as the starting quarterback ahead of the Steelers’ highly-anticipated Friday, November 27, matchup against the Denver Broncos.

Recent findings suggest that mcCarthy, who previously coached Rodgers during the quarterback’s time at the Green Bay Packers, explained, “We got to a point, we’d talked about some things, communication, things like that, and there was an ultimatum made [that was] clearly out of his job description. So it was my decision to bench him.”.

After having “private conversations” with Rodgers, he was informed that backup quarterback Mason Rudolph would be stepping in for Friday’s game.

Rodgers addressed the coach’s decision during an appearance on the The Pat McAfee Show on Wednesday, where he proceeded to claim that McCarthy is “unhealthy.”.

The four-time NFL MVP alleged that McCarthy had fallen asleep during team meetings, missed multiple team walkthroughs and was unwilling to connect with the team’s defensive players.

Rodgers also claimed that his longtime coach had yelled, “One, two, three, Packers!” when breaking a huddle during the Philadelphia Eagles game, hinting that McCarthy wasn’t aware of what team he was coaching at the time.

“The Philadelphia situation at halftime was very emotional,” McCarthy remarked in his Wednesday press conference, referring to Rodgers’ allegations against him. “There’s conversations prior to that. [It was an] embarrassing moment, but I just think we work a lot of hours, so definitely a difference of opinion from what I was told about the comments.”.
