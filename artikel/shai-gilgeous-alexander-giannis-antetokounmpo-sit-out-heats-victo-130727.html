Sunday's game between the Oklahoma City Thunder and Miami Heat was missing some serious star power.

The Heat ruled out Giannis Antetokounmpo before tip-off with a groin injury. The missed game was the first for Antetokounmpo since the former two-time MVP joined the Heat in the offseason. (Related: Latest Update: One Year Since the “DeepSeek Moment”...)

In a recent development, the Thunder, meanwhile, ruled out reigning two-time MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander with a lower back contusion and neck strain. The long-term outlook for both stars wasn't immediately clear. Both players started the day listed as questionable. (Related: Breaking: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming G...)

Miami beat the Thunder 123-113 behind Bam Adebayo's triple-double of 24 points, 15 rebounds and 12 assists.

Antetokounmpo first surfaced on the injury report with his groin injury on Saturday before being ruled out on Sunday. It's not clear when he sustained it.

The Thunder were playing the first game of a back-to-back, but OKC head coach Mark Daigneault said pregame that the schedule didn't play a factor in the decision for Gilgeous-Alexander to sit. He cited "a really hard foul" of Gilgeous-Alexander in Friday's victory over the Pelicans.

"It wouldn't be wise for us to put him on the court tonight regardless, based on how he's feeling," Daigneault remarked.

Gilgeous-Alexander joins a long list of injuries to critical Thunder players. Aday Mara (shin contusion), Alex Caruso (hip), Jaylin Williams (back) and Nikola Topić (hip) also were out against the Heat, joining Chet Holmgren (knee), Cason Wallace (shoulder) and Jaylen Williams (ankle), who are all sidelined with long-term injuries.

According to recent reports, the Thunder started Ajay Mitchell, Jared McCain, Micah Peavy, Kenrich Williams and Isaiah Hartenstein against the Heat. Bobby Portis Jr. stepped into the starting lineup for Miami in place of Antetokounmpo.

Antetokounmpo and Gilgeous-Alexander are both playing at an All-NBA level this season. In 15 games, Antetokounmpo is averaging 28.2 points, 12.6 rebounds, 5.5 assists and 1.3 blocks while shooting 60.2% from the field. The Heat entered Sunday at 9-6, in sixth place in the East.

Sources indicate that gilgeous-Alexander is averaging 31.7 points, 4.5 assists and 4.1 rebounds through 16 games while shooting 48.3% from the floor and 37.3% from 3. The injury-ravaged Thunder entered Sunday at 10-6, in eighth place in the West.
