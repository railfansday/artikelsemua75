Napoli coach Massimiliano Allegri notes Antonio Vergara’s injury might be related to the ‘greater pressure’ the Italian talent is facing this season and insists the Partenopei must be ‘realistic’ in their next Champions League game against Manchester City.

Vergara suffered a fresh muscle injury on Saturday night, during Napoli’s 1-0 win over Torino.

“He got injured, and I don’t know how long we’ll be without him,” Allegri remarked at a post-match press conference via TuttoNapoli.

“It’s very unfortunate; he was very down. I think the injury may be related to what he’s going through: this year he has different responsibilities, the national team, and greater mental pressure. Sometimes these injuries are caused by those things too.”.

Vergara, 23, has made ten appearances across all competitions this season with Napoli, scoring one goal and providing one assist.

However, he has already missed four club games due to a calf injury.

His latest injury is to the flexor of his left thigh, and according to Il Mattino, it’s unclear whether the versatile attacker will play for his club again in 2026.

“It’s a very important match and a great one to play in,” Allegri remarked.

According to recent reports, “We have to be realistic; our Champions League campaign will be decided against Club Brugge, Sabah and Viking. We had to beat Bodo, and we did. Tuesday will be very vital and difficult, but we need to sort out the league, and the home match against Club Brugge will be crucial.”.
