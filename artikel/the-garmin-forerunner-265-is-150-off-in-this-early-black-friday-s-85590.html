If you’re an avid runner, fitness watch features like step and heart rate tracking are just the beginning, and Garmin is leading the charge when it comes to more advanced, comprehensive metrics. The right running watch can be more than a gadget, but an essential training tool—and right now, one of the best is $150 cheaper thanks to Amazon’s early Black Friday sale. The Garmin Forerunner 265 is now 33% off, taking it down to $309.99 (originally $459.99).

Lifehacker Senior Health Editor Beth Skwarecki ran the Forerunner 265 through its paces for us, and says the device completely transformed her runs. The 46mm watch is the larger counterpart to the 42mm 265S (also 33% off), with a sharp, colorful AMOLED screen, physical buttons, and top-tier GPS and heart rate tracking. It also tracks heart rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate. Realistic race time predictions, Training Readiness and Training Status scores, customizable data screens, and daily suggested workouts help runners strategy their training.
