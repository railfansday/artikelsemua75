According to recent reports, no Wicked, no Moana, no problem, because Lionsgate is saving the Thanksgiving week box office with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping which is posting $21.25M in previews, the second best ever for the franchise behind 2013’s Hunger Games: Catching Fire which lit $25.2M in its previews. Sunrise‘s money is currently the second-best previews YTD after Sony’s Spider-Man: Brand Fresh Day ($72M, $360M) and ahead of Universal’s The Odyssey ($17.6M previews, $123.5M opening).

Other previews that Sunrise on the Reaping is beating include Universal’s Wicked from two years ($19.2M; $112.5M opening); for the longest time we heard that the latest feature take of a Suzanne Collins novel was outstripping the presales of that Ariana Grande and Cynthia Erivo female skewing musical. It’s also ahead of Lionsgate’s Michael ($12.6M previews, $97.2M opening), The Devil Wears Prada 2 ($10M, $76.7M opening) and light years ahead of the previous Hunger Games: Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes ($5.75M previews, $44.6M opening) which had its campaign impacted by the 2023 actors strike, not to mention the Collins novel about the origins of Coriolanus Snow wasn’t a fan-fave. Sunrise on the Reaping was beloved by fans in its early story about the Woody Harrelson mentor character, Haymitch Abernathy. Industry observers note that when it comes to the all-time preview record for a Lionsgate title, that belongs to Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2 which made $30.4M from midnight shows leading to a $141M opening back in mid-November 2012. It has been revealed that now, Sunrise on the Reaping‘s advance money was minted with $5M via early access PLF showtimes on Wednesday, with another $16.25M collected tonight per sources. Catching Fire‘s back in the day began warming up with 10PM Thursday night reveals. Note Wicked‘s previews were comprised of Amazon promotional screenings, Wednesday night PLF shows and an $8M Thursday. (Related: The Partnership: Amazon SageMaker and Hugging Face...)

With a great preview start, it’s fair to say that a $100M opening is well in store for the net $150M priced Sunrise on the Reaping, especially with a 95% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, which is the best ever in the franchise ahead of Catching Fire (89%), Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (89%), Hunger Games (81%), Mockingjay Part 2 (71%), and Mockingjay Part 1 (66%).

Overseas, industry projects have risen from a $70M forecast to $86M opening.

Sources indicate that critics aren’t hot on the fifth Hunger Games movie from director Francis Lawrence (the original 2012 movie was helmed by Gary Ross) with an RT score of 68% fresh. However, even if Katniss was shooting Cupid’s arrows at critics, they’d still play hard to get, for the best reviews in the series were a a while ago with Catching Fire (90% certified fresh) and Hunger Games (85% certified fresh). Ever since the Mockingjays (both at 70%), critics’ love for Hunger Games has waned with Songbirds and Snakes being the worst reviewed in franchise at 64%.

The Hunger Games franchise through its five previous movies has minted $3.3 billion at the global box office.
