Bruno Fernandes has revealed that he put contract talks with Manchester United on hold after being offered a newly introduced deal.

Sources indicate that speaking to Sky Sports ahead of the match against Liverpool, the United captain explained: “No, there’s no negotiations ongoing, but I’ve seen people saying that he’s asked for more money, he’s asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester. It has been revealed that “Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I’ve always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it’s time for me to move on, I will move on. According to recent reports, ““I’m not going to be the player that I’ve seen many in this time at Manchester United. They’re going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, ‘Bruno, it’s time to part ways’, I’m going to be the first one walking out the door.

“Yeah, the club offered me a fresh contract, so I think that, from their side, highlights that they want me to stay. And as you noted, there are no negotiations going on.

“We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most crucial thing now is that the club gets back to win games.”.

Fernandes’ current deal expires at the end of the season, but Manchester United have the option to extend it by another year.
