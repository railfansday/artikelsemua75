Why Is SoftBank Stock Down Today?

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Industry observers note that softBank Group shares sank as much as 16.16% in Tokyo on Friday, leading a broad drop in Asian technology stocks after Anthropic’s record Nasdaq debut. The stock was trading at ¥3,013, down 14.81%, by early afternoon. If the losses hold, it would be SoftBank’s worst day in more than two years. Investors are worried about SoftBank’s heavy exposure to OpenAI, Anthropic’s main rival, in which it has invested $64.6 billion. Anthropic’s strong debut has raised fresh questions about OpenAI’s position and whether huge AI spending will pay off. SoftBank shares had already fallen 30% in October and are down 31% so far this month. Japanese chip stocks also slid, with Tokyo Electron down 7.19%, Lasertec falling 6.75% and Renesas Electronics losing 6.71%, pulling the Nikkei 225 lower. The selling spread to tech shares in South Korea, Taiwan and China.
