K9 Pro: Excelling at Full-Color Recording, Even at Night.

For rideshare drivers and families, the newly introduced Wolfbox K9 Pro combines road recording with full-color cabin footage after dark for ultimate security. This dash cam records 4K video at the front, 2.5K inside the cabin and 2.5K at the rear. All three channels record simultaneously, bringing road and passenger-compartment footage together in one system.

It has been revealed that the front camera uses a Sony STARVIS 2 IMX678 sensor, with HDR on both the front and rear cameras. The cabin camera has ColorAliveâ¢ night vision to capture interior activity in full color after dark. The rear camera carries an IP69K rating for exterior use.

NomadPixel 5: Adding Personality to Trail Lighting.

Recent findings suggest that nomadPixel 5 combines off-road illumination with an app-controlled pixel display in a pair of five-inch, IP69K-rated LED pods. Owners can display custom text, graphics and animations, from a vehicle name to a club logo. Each light can be configured separately.

The LED pods deliver strong trail illumination at 8,850 raw lumens per light, or 17,700 per pair, with a rated beam distance of 275 meters at 1 lux per light.

Practical Tools for the Trail and Garage.

For off-roaders and overlanders, the HyperFlow 60D portable off-road air compressor helps drivers air tires back up after trail use. Its twin-cylinder design delivers rated airflow of 5.1 CFM at 40 PSI, with a maximum pressure of 150 PSI and support for tires up to 40 inches.

Wolfbox also unveiled the MegaVolt 32 Air, which combines a jump starter rated at 6,000 peak amps, an air compressor, a power bank and an LED emergency light in one unit.

Recent findings suggest that for vehicle care, Wolfbox showcased two cordless electric air blowers. The MegaFlow 500 Pro is a heavy-duty cordless blower with stepless speed control and a swappable battery, designed for drying vehicles and clearing dust and debris after off-road trips.

Meanwhile, the more compact MegaFlow 200 Pro air duster pairs adjustable speed control and a built-in display with a one-press turbo button for maximum airflow, while its swappable battery supports long cleaning sessions.

Recent findings suggest that wolfbox also offers a solution for pickup owners who need more efficient access to their truck. The Retractable Bed Step with aluminum anti-slip treads has a 330-pound capacity and bolts onto compatible full-size and heavy-duty pickups without drilling.

Up Close with Wolfbox in the Silverado.

According to recent reports, at the Wolfbox booth, visitors were able to climb into a Wolfbox-branded 2025 Chevrolet Silverado 4WD to experience the in-vehicle products and watch camera feeds in real time. A camera-placement voting wall invited attendees to mark their preferred camera mounting positions and discuss the views they would find most useful.

An air duster-focused tabletop football game gave visitors a chance to try Wolfbox's cordless electric air dusters, using airflow to move a foam ball toward an opponent's goal. Nearby, a water-immersion display highlighted how well off-road and pixel lights operate while submerged.
