Home Alone is making a comeback at the perfect time.

The Home Alone franchise began in 1990 with John Hughes and Chris Columbus at the helm to bring the story of an eight-year-old Kevin McCallister to life. Kevin defends his home from burglars after his family forgets to bring him along on a Christmas vacation. The original movie led to six movies across theaters, television, and now streaming. The 1990 installment and its sequel continue to dominate the charts around December, and the love for the franchise is seemingly never-ending.

Industry observers note that macaulay Culkin is set to return as Kevin McCallister in a new Home Alone reboot, with Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg developing the project for Disney. Puck News reported the latest development after Culkin appeared in a McDonald's holiday ad promoting a limited-time Home Alone meal and reprising his character. Culkin hasn't portrayed the character since the 1992 sequel Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, but it appears that the actor wants to bring even more Christmas joy. In a recent development, rogen and Goldberg are producing under the Point Grey banner, and they reportedly just closed the deal to work alongside Culkin to produce a script. Disney has yet to comment on the recent reports. It was previously reported that Culkin was interested in returning around July 2026, as the actor reportedly organized a meeting with Disney's studio head, David Greenbaum. It appears that Culkin's visit has led to Disney wanting its most beloved holiday movie to return.

Four Home Alone movies were released after Culkin's final movie in 1992, with director Chris Columbus also exiting the franchise alongside the actor. Home Alone 3, Home Alone 4: Taking Back the House, Home Alone: The Holiday Heist, and Home Sweet Home Alone all failed to launch and were complete flops with critics who felt that they did not feel as charming and lacked the magic that was certainly felt in the first two movies. Culkin previously returned to screens as McCallister for another advertising role in 2018 to promote Google Assistant. The actor has always had a deep appreciation for his character. However, after the tragic passing of Catherine O'Hara, his on-screen mother in the Home Alone movies, the actor has expressed much regret over their "unfinished business" to appear together on-screen again and often referred to O'Hara as his "mama" in touching tributes after her death.

O'Hara's character, Kate McCallister, is the matriarch of the McCallister family and was responsible for some of the most iconic moments in arguably the biggest and most-beloved holiday movies in history. With both Rogen and Culkin at the helm, a tribute to the beloved and iconic actress, as well as John Heard, who portrayed Kevin's father, Peter, and sadly died in 2017, will certainly be shown in the long-awaited Home Alone reboot.

At the time of writing, it's unclear when Culkin will officially return to theaters in Disney's Home Alone reboot.
