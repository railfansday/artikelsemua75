SAN FRANCISCO, Nov. 19, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Anduin today opened its Investor Portal to all. Developed with leading asset managers and already live with their investors, the fully white-labeled portal connects fundraising, onboarding, reporting, and ongoing servicing in one experience, supported by AI-powered workflows.

Built on Anduin's intelligent operating platform and fully integrated with Fund Subscription and Investor Data Management, the portal carries investor records, contacts, and permissions from onboarding into ongoing servicing. AI-powered workflows help teams and fund administrators distribute documents across entities and contacts, while recording communications and profile updates for an audit trail.

Engagement analytics show how investors interact with documents, videos, and pages, helping investor relations teams tailor communications and inform conversations ahead of future fundraises.

"An investor portal should do more than check a box. We believe that every interaction the GP has with its investors is an opportunity to deepen that relationship," said Eliot Hodges, CEO of Anduin. "By putting AI to work across connected data and workflows, we're helping firms reduce manual work, deliver best-in-class investor experiences, and scale their operations without adding complexity.". Anduin's migration experts use a programmatic approach to transfer investor data and documents from legacy portals accurately and efficiently, reducing the burden on internal teams. The portal has supported the migration of more than 50,000 investor records and documents. It also connects with existing infrastructure through APIs, including CRM systems such as DealCloud and Salesforce.

To learn more or request a demonstration, visit anduintransact.com/anduin-investor-portal.

Sources indicate that anduin Transactions is the end-to-end investment management technology partner for private markets, connecting marketing, fundraising, onboarding, reporting, and ongoing servicing through one unified platform. Trusted by firms managing more than $7 trillion in aggregate AUM, Anduin has supported more than $265 billion in capital committed and over 76,000 unique investors. Visit anduintransact.com.
