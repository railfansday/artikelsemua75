Manchester United are reportedly keeping a close eye on Atalanta defender Giorgio Scalvini, with Liverpool also interested in securing the highly-rated Italian international ahead of the 2027 transfer window.

According to a TEAMtalk report, Manchester United and Liverpool are both assessing their central defensive options, with Scalvini emerging as a potential long-term addition. Chelsea are also monitoring his situation, while Newcastle United have maintained their interest in the defender for several years.

The Atalanta star has previously attracted attention for his impressive physical attributes, technical ability, and composure in possession. His versatility could also make him an attractive option for clubs looking to strengthen their defensive departments.

Scalvini’s progress was interrupted by an ACL injury in 2024, followed by a serious shoulder problem the following year. However, he has successfully overcome those setbacks and is once again demonstrating the qualities that made him such an exciting prospect.

His performances have also earned him an increasingly important role with the Italian national team, where he is developing an understanding alongside Inter Milan defender Alessandro Bastoni.
