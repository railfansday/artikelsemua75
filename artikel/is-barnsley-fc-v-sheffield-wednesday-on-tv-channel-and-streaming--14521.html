Industry observers note that sheffield Wednesday have had troubles of their own and recently changed managers, but are on a considerably stronger footing than their South Yorkshire rivals. The Owls occupy fifth in the table, six points adrift of league leaders Huddersfield Town.

Dave Challinor secured his first win as the club’s boss last weekend, as the Owls claimed a 3-0 victory over Plymouth Argyle.

He said: “I think we've got loads to do. To come here, score three goals, keep a clean sheet, at times maybe luck more than judgement, certainly in the first half, with some of the opportunities we gave away, it is amazing.”.

Here are the key TV and streaming details as Barnsley prepare to entertain Wednesday.

Kick-off at Oakwell is scheduled for 8pm on Friday, November 20.

Industry observers note that yes - those without a ticket for the debry will be able to watch the action unfold at home. The action will be shown live on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Football. Subscriptions can be purchased online.

It has been revealed that supporters can stream the action via the official Sky Sports app or on NOW TV with a valid subscription. There will also be live audio commentary on BBC Radio Sheffield.
