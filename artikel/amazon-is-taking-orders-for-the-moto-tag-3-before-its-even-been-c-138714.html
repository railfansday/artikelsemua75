Motorola’s unannounced Moto Tag 3 has been listed for sale online, just five months after the Moto Tag 2 went on sale in the US, and before any official announcement of a follow-up. The listing on Amazon UK was spotted by X user @matteventu, and shows the tracker in a “Pantone Lucent White” color option, with a price of £24.96 (about $33) and a 1-4 month shipping estimate. (Related: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for Summarization u...)

According to recent reports, A second image shared by leaker Evan Blass also indicates a vibrant purple version of the upcoming tracking tag, which is described as “Pantone Indigo Blue” on a separate Amazon Italy listing. Both are new color options, with the Moto Tag 2 only currently available in orange or a grey-ish beige. While the Moto Tag 3 name suggests this is a follow-up to the Moto Tag 2, there don’t appear to be any major hardware differences between the two trackers.

Both use a replaceable CR2032 battery, claim to provide more than 500 days of battery life, and support UWB in Android Find Hub, among other similarities. The XT2561-1 model number seen on the back of the Moto Tag 3 images also matches that of the Moto Tag 2. That could mean that the Moto Tag 3 name is an error and these are just latest color options for the Tag 2.

We’ve asked Motorola for clarification and will patch if we hear back.
