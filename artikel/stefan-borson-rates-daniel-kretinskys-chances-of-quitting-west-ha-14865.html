Daniel Kretinsky's future should not be questioned despite Scott Parker's latest comments at West Ham United.

That is according to former Manchester City financial adviser Stefan Borson, who exclusively told Football Insider that there is nothing to read into at the London Stadium.

The Hammers are yet to claim victory under Parker, but he has only been in charge for the 3-3 draw with Cardiff City and 2-1 loss to Blackburn Rovers.

Talk in East London has already turned to the January transfer window, and what majority owner Daniel Kretinsky has planned behind the scenes.

Speaking exclusively to Football Insider, Borson does not believe there should be any reason to worry about Kretinsky seeking an exit, though.

It has been revealed that "No, no, not at this stage, no. He's been appointed [by Daniel Kretinsky]," he explained.

"He's been appointed. He wouldn't have been appointed if it wasn't with Kretinsky's blessing. I don't think there's much there [about Kretinsky's future].".

Industry observers note that his comments are likely to raise concerns, though. There are just a handful of weeks remaining until the January transfer window opens.

If West Ham's form continues as it is, they won't be playing Premier League football next season, and that could be massively destructive.

It has been revealed that parker's squad is far from perfect. That's clear for everyone to see, especially in their last two Championship clashes.

Taty Castellanos' future is also a major talking point, with the latest news that the centre-forward wants to leave in January.

Therefore, a newly introduced striker will be needed at the London Stadium, but that won't come cheap, especially when clubs know West Ham's situation.
