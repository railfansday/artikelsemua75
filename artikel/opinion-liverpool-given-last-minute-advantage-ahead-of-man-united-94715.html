Liverpool are right now fifth in the Premier League table.

This is the same position that got Arne Slot sacked last term with the club performing no better than they did under the Dutchman.

Another £150 million has been spent on players by Richard Hughes and Andoni Iraola with Julian Ward now looking to add even more talent to their ranks in January.

However, before all of that can even happen, they still have a fair few games left that they need to get results from.

Sunday's game against Manchester United is one of the biggest of Liverpool's season so far.

The Red Devils have now got Mauricio Pochettino as their manager and will be expecting a bit of a new manager bounce ahead of the clash.

But, Liverpool seem to have just been handed a huge advantage that they did not expect.

Marcus Rashford is likely to miss Sunday's game against Liverpool.

Manchester United have actually been in worse form than Liverpool this term.

Right now, Pochettino's men are 19th in the Premier League and only have eight points to their name.

They've not managed to win any of their last five games and, this season, hardly any of their players have looked like a threat.

However, they always manage to step things up against the Reds as they managed to prove last term.

But, some luck has actually gone Liverpool's way this time around as, according to a fresh revision from GiveMeSport, Marcus Rashford is highly likely to miss out on the clash. (Related: Large Language Models: A Fresh Moore's Law?...)

"Newly introduced Manchester United manager Mauricio Pochettino has suffered a blow ahead of his first match in charge against Liverpool on Sunday, as GIVEMESPORT understands that Marcus Rashford remains sidelined and won't be fit for the trip to Merseyside," they wrote.

"There was anticipation that Harry Maguire, Luke Shaw and Rashford might all return to face Liverpool after various injury issues, and while there is no enhancement on the former two yet, GMS understands that Rashford won't be available.

"The fact that the 29-year-old remains sidelined with his ongoing hip glitch could also raise doubts over his availability for the trip to Sporting Lisbon on Wednesday in the Champions League.".

Marcus Rashford missing out on Sunday's game is great news for Liverpool.

None of Man United's attackers have really looked that scary this season.

However, of the lot, Rashford is the one who feels like he could make the most difference.

Last term, he was in fine form for Barcelona and, even though he wanted to exit Old Trafford for the Camp Nou, that didn't end up happening.

He took this on the chin and has tried his best to perform for his boyhood club again.

But, since his return to Manchester, things just haven't gone to blueprint.

And, since Liverpool have the vast majority of their first-team squad available to choose from, they're now the clear favourites to win.

Indeed, other players from United's squad could end up stepping up but, in reality, this should be a game that Liverpool manages to win rather easily, if they don't, some serious questions will need to be asked. (Related: Breaking: Deploy Hugging Face models easily with Amazon...)
