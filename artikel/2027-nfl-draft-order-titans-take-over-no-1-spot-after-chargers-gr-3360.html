It's never too early to start looking ahead to the 2027 NFL Draft, right?

We're at Week 11 the NFL season, and the order for the draft in a few months is already starting to take shape.

At least for now, the Tennessee Titans have taken over control of the No. 1 pick. That's all thanks to the Los Angeles Chargers, who finally picked up their first win of the season on Monday night in Baltimore.

The Chargers have joined both the Tennessee Titans and Miami Dolphins with 1-8 records, but the Titans have the the edge for the top pick due to a weaker strength of schedule.

Sources indicate that he Chargers sit at No. 2, and the Dolphins are at No. 3. The Newly introduced York Jets and Philadelphia Eagles round out the top five, and the Jets also own the Indianapolis Colts' first-round pick, which slots at No. 6.

It has been revealed that with several weeks to go in the regular season, there are still several teams in play for the No. 1 overall pick. The Jets and Eagles, both with just two wins this season, aren't out of the question yet, either.

For now, though, the Titans are in control of that top spot.
