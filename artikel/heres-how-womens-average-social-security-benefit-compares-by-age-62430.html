Sources indicate that as of December 2025, the average monthly benefit for women was $1,872.43, according to the Social Security Administration (SSA). Men received an average of $2,282.28, which creates a monthly gender gap of $409.85.

Your benefit depends on your personal earnings history and when you claim, but the population averages show a consistent difference between genders. Data as of December 2025, according to the SSA.

The difference in these benefits between men and women reflects historical variations in lifetime earnings and years spent in the paid workforce. The SSA calculates your benefit based on your average wages across your 35 highest-earning years, where earlier wages are adjusted for inflation.

According to recent reports, then, checks are adjusted up or down depending on your current age and your full retirement age (FRA). This ranges from 66 to 67, depending on your birth year, and it's 67 for everyone born in 1960 or later.

Industry observers note that longevity affects how far these dollars must stretch. A woman who is 65 today is expected to live about 21 more years, versus about 18 more years for a man. This means that women's lower average benefits typically have to support a longer retirement.

Recent findings suggest that A 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment (COLA) will take effect in 2027. That adjustment adds about $73 a month on top of the average benefit for 70-year-old women.

Sources indicate that married women often have the option to claim a spousal Social Security benefit while their partner is alive. This is worth up to 50% of the benefit the partner qualifies for at their FRA. You may receive a spousal benefit only if it is larger than your own retirement benefit and only if your partner is already claiming Social Security.

After your spouse dies, you may be entitled to a survivor benefit. This is worth up to 100% of the amount they qualified for or were receiving at the time of their death. Again, you only receive this amount if it's worth more than your own retirement benefit.
