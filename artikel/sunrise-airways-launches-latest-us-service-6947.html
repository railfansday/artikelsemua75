Sunrise Airways Launches Latest U.S. Service

Haiti’s Sunrise Airways this week released a new route to the U.S. Northeast.

Recent findings suggest that the carrier on Tuesday operated its inaugural flight between Cap-Haïtien and Upcoming York-JFK. Flights will operate twice per week going forward.

It has been revealed that sunrise is one of only two airlines currently connecting the U.S. and Haiti. It also serves Boston, Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and Miami from Cap-Haïtien.

American Airlines resumed service to the country earlier this month after a nearly two-year hiatus brought on by rising gang violence in and around Port-au-Prince. It is now operating daily nonstop flights between Miami and Cap-Haïtien.
