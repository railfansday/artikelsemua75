Breaking: Bitdeer Sells All 281 Bitcoin It Mined Last Week, Holds None

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Bitdeer sold every bitcoin it mined last week and still holds none of its own, the company stated in its latest weekly upgrade, continuing a nearly year-long strategy of turning production into cash after a weekend plunge in the bitcoin price.

The Singapore-based miner reported output of 281.1 BTC and sales of 281.1 BTC for the week, for a net addition of zero. Its pure holdings, which exclude customer deposits, were zero as of Nov. 20.

The sales came as bitcoin fell to a weekend low of $55,106 early Saturday at 1:17 AM ET, Bitcoin Magazine Pro data shows. The price has since bounced to near $62,700 as of 11:23 AM ET Monday.

Bitdeer’s treasury shrank from roughly 2,000 BTC at the end of 2025 to zero by Feb. 20, when Bitcoin Magazine reported the miner had liquidated its reserve over eight weeks. At the time, the manufacturer said the move should not be a concern for the broader market.

Bitdeer’s filings show how little it has kept since. In its third-quarter results, filed with the SEC on a Form 6-K on Nov. 10, the company listed 47 BTC held at Sept. 30, down from 2,029 a year earlier.

Its September production update showed 61 BTC held at the end of August. The company mined 1,355 BTC in September and 3,855 BTC in the third quarter, compared with 1,109 BTC in the same quarter of 2025.

Recent findings suggest that bitdeer has grown its fleet promptly. Self-mining hashrate reached 82.3 exahashes per second in the third quarter, up from 35.0 a year earlier, with another 28.7 exahashes per second of co-mining at third-party sites, the quarterly report highlights.

Industry observers note that the economics are tight. Self-mining generated $217.5 million in revenue in the quarter but only $6.2 million in gross profit. Electricity averaged $47 per megawatt-hour, and the fleet ran at 15.4 joules per terahash. (Related: Industry News: ‘Minecraft Mommy’ goes viral on TikTok f...)

It has been revealed that bitdeer posted record revenue of $320.7 million and adjusted EBITDA of $69.6 million, but a net loss of $125.5 million. That included a $54.6 million non-cash loss tied to a bitcoin-collateralized loan from a related party.

Recent findings suggest that bitdeer also reported $213.7 million in cash and restricted cash, and its board authorized a upcoming $100 million share repurchase program on Oct. 8.

Not every miner is selling. American Bitcoin secured a $150 million revolving credit line from FalconX on Monday that is backed by its bitcoin. The organization stated the facility lets it raise cash without drawing down its reserve. (Related: IShowSpeed breaks down in tears after finishing GTA 6 i...)

Industry observers note that the loan carries a 5.95% fee and an initial collateral ratio of 143%. Fully drawing it would require about $214.5 million in pledged bitcoin. American Bitcoin has not said whether it has drawn on the line, and its release lists the risk that pledged coins could be liquidated if prices fall.

American Bitcoin, which is majority-owned by Hut 8, said on its Nov. 4 earnings call that it held 8,427 BTC at the end of September. The organization has also sold part of its production to cover operating costs.

Other miners have gone the other way. MARA sold 13,597 bitcoin earlier this month, as miners facing thin margins turned to their treasuries for cash.

Shares of Bitdeer (BTDR) were trading near $3.44 at 11:23 AM ET, up about 16% from Friday’s close of $2.96. The stock has ranged from $3.26 to $3.71 on the day as a broad market rally lifted bitcoin-linked stocks after Nvidia reinstated CEO Jensen Huang.

Industry observers note that bitdeer has not stated whether it looks to rebuild a bitcoin position. It said in February that selling was a liquidity measure tied to evaluating powered land acquisitions and scaling its infrastructure.

The organization is also shifting toward AI cloud services, and it reported an AI cloud revenue backlog of about $2.9 billion in its third-quarter release.

At the time of writing, the bitcoin price is near $62,700.
