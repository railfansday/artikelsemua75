It has been revealed that gloomwood, the Victorian survival horror immersive sim (or, wait, I'm getting reports that we shouldn't call it an immersive sim, apparently) by Dillon Rogers is a game that is quite happily inspired by its predecessors such as Thief, Resident Evil and Dishonored; you could call it 'Thief with guns', if you really wanted to. (Sorry, I've just seen that the domain for Gloomwood's site used to be 'residenthievil.com'. Wonderful). Now, that last inspiration has come full circle, as publisher Latest Blood Interactive's latest enhancement post confirms that the newest member of the team is none other than former Dishonored designer Julien Eveillé.

In addition to his work at Arkane on Dishonored and its sequel, Deathloop, and even Marvel's Blade, Eveillé also developed his own indie horror, 2024's short and sweet Threshold. It's quite a legacy to add to this team, and his hiring is described by New Blood as an example of how "finding level designers who are up to the task of working on Gloomwood is not easy". The post also mentions that Eveillé will be added alongside "our new devs", implying that he's not the only new addition to the team.

Industry observers note that other than these new hires, the revision is set to include quite a bit to change up Gloomwood and make it bigger. The highlight is the First National Bank level, which is described as "our largest and most intricately designed level yet", with the screenshots showing off a grand and imposing, yet claustrophobic in places, that makes the act of going to the bank even more intimidating than usual. The Bank is joined by the new stun prod weapon, which is self-explanatory, and the new Rat and Fishdog transformations, which let you sneak through crawlspaces and swim through dirtied waters respectively. That sneaking will be made more difficult by the addition of alarms and blood pools that both alert surrounding guards to your presence, though the new Crow King transformation offers a new, acid-belching way to take down any guards that come running. Industry observers note that the update is tentatively scheduled for "before the end of the year", but Newly introduced Blood aren't setting down a specific release date. Rogers himself will also be going through back surgery very soon, for which I wish him a gentle recovery and, hopefully, as easy a return to work as possible.
