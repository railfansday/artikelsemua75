Massimiliano Allegri believes Napoli's Champions League clash with Manchester City will not determine how their European campaign ends.

Napoli are 27th in the 2026-27 league phase after winning just one of their four games – a 4-3 victory against Bodo/Glimt.

The Partenopei have lost five of their last seven group stage/league phase matches in the Champions League, as many defeats as they suffered at this stage of the competition across their previous 23 combined.

Napoli have also been shown more cards than any other team in the Champions League this season (13 – 12 yellows, one red), including five in their defeat to Porto last time out.

With league phase games against Club Brugge, Sabah and Viking still to come, Allegri insisted there is no added pressure ahead of their trip to Etihad Stadium.

"I just stated that it won’t be decisive, because mathematically, even if we win, six points won’t be enough to qualify," said Allegri.

According to recent reports, "So, I will only believe that we've qualified once we have achieved it on the final matchday. It's a matter of numbers. We definitely need at least three wins, mathematically.

"We know how difficult it will be, because we're playing against one of the strongest teams in Europe, even after 10 years of winning in the Premier League and a Champions League victory and with so many changes of players.

"It’s a upcoming group, but they have extraordinary qualities. It's a Champions League match against one of the favourites to go on and win it.

"As always, we'll need great technique when we have the ball and when we don't. We'll have to do our best to create the conditions to counter Man City.".

All five previous meetings between City and Napoli have been in the group/league phase of the Champions League – after failing to win home and away against them in 2011-12 (D1 L1), the Citizens have won each of the last three.

City are unbeaten at home to Napoli (W2 D1), with the most recent one of those being a 2-0 win in the league phase of this competition last season.
