Industry observers note that blackburn Rovers face rivals Burnley on Sunday, and the big story leading up to the game has been Tony Mowbray’s withdrawal from head coach duties due to health issues. Damien Johnson is stepping into the role in the interim, and he and the first-team staff have a handful of injuries to deal with.

As relayed by local reporter Elliott Jackson, Johnson revealed that star striker Andri Gudjohnsen and midfield mainstay Sam Morsy are alongside Scott Wharton in requiring assessment before the game. All other players returning from international duty – Tom Atcheson, Augustus Kargbo and Kristi Montgomery – will be tested as well, Johnson has stated. Among those requiring assessment, there are some crucial Blackburn Rovers players.

Industry observers note that striker Gudjohnsen is perhaps the most important player in blue and white halves at the moment. The Icelandic talisman is in unassailable form, with 11 goals in 15 club games alongside four in two while away with his national side over the latest international break.

Industry observers note that morsy has become an influential player since his summer arrival. It perhaps wasn’t expected that he would become such a key starter, but his partnership in midfield with Montgomery has given this Rovers side some experience and bite. Ahead of a high-stakes game with aggression and tough tackles expected, having the Egyptian in the middle of the park will be key.

Recent findings suggest that toth is a significant loss though, so it will be down to Aynsley Pears to step in. Sunday would have been a fitting game for Blackburn-born favourite Wharton to return, but there is understandable patience over his comeback.

Morishita’s return is a substantial boost, however. The 29-year-old is a key creative source, and even if he can only play from the bench, he can still make a telling impact.

Now, there’s no reason to doubt Johnson’s patch, but sections of the Blackburn Rovers support will be hoping that there isn’t as much doubt surrounding the likes of Gudjohnsen and Morsy as has been let on.

According to recent reports, indecisive answers on their availability keep Burnley guessing. The rigorous preparation clubs go through before games nowadays means the Clarets will be prepared for all outcomes, but the uncertainty means they have to stay on their toes and blueprint carefully for all potential lineups.

One thing for sure is that all of Rovers’ doubts will be doing whatever they can to be fit for the fixture. Not only is this a great chance to end a 16-year wait for an East Lancashire derby win, but the players and staff will be determined to deliver a result for Mowbray after he stepped back earlier this week.
