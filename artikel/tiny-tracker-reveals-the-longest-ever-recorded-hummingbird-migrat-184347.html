Tiny tracker reveals the longest ever recorded hummingbird migration

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (55 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Small though they may be, hummingbirds are no slouches when it comes to long-distance flight. These tiny birds can migrate thousands of miles across countries and continents. And now researchers have revealed the longest hummingbird migration to date—an impressive 6,526-kilometer journey (4,055 miles) from Alabama to Alaska in just 38 days.
