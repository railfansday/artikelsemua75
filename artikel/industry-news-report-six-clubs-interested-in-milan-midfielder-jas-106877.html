A number of clubs are interested in signing Ardon Jashari from AC Milan, according to a report from his native Switzerland.

Jashari has once again found himself playing largely as a back-up under Ruben Amorim this season, after performing the role of being Luka Modric’s deputy during his debut campaign with the Rossoneri under Massimiliano Allegri. (Related: Nebraska voters appear to reject online sports betting ...)

Recent findings suggest that given that over €30m was invested to sign him from Club Brugge, a bigger role and impact was perhaps foreseen by all parties. Thus, Jashari’s situation is one to monitor, and there is certainly no shortage of interested parties.

According to information from 4-4-2.ch, clubs in both Serie A and the Premier League are seriously considering a move for the 24-year-old midfielder, who started last night in Switzerland’s 4-0 win over Scotland and impressed.

Roma, Juventus, Atalanta and Como are the four Italian clubs that are looking at the Swiss international. From England, it is Aston Villa and Nottingham Forest that are monitoring his situation closely.

Industry observers note that jashari is under contract until 2030, so Milan have leverage, and they have not yet received a concrete offer. However, this could change as January gets closer. The Rossoneri still highly value Jashari, even though he hasn’t yet established himself as a regular starter.

A permanent sale would therefore require a fee in the range of €35-40m. Milan paid Club Brugge a similar sum for him a year ago and they have no intention of making a loss, given his age and potential.
