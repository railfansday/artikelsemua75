Roberto De Zerbi has a huge selection dilemma on his hands after a number of his players starred during Tottenham's 2-0 win against Ipswich on Saturday.

Teenager Luca Williams-Barnett stole the headlines after a stunning display on his first competitive start for the club, and he has now made it impossible for De Zerbi to take him out of the XI against Sunderland next weekend.

Conor Gallagher was also one of the most impressive performers on the day as Spurs downed the Tractor Boys, while Pedro Porro showed why he is world-class with his display against Gary O'Neil's men.

Meanwhile, Marcos Senesi, who joined Spurs on a free transfer from Bournemouth in the summer, was drafted into the starting line-up by De Zerbi for the visit of the Tractor Boys. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

He replaced Micky van de Ven, who dropped to the bench, and partnered Jan Paul van Hecke in a new-look back four which included Destiny Udogie and Pedro Porro.

In a recent development, it was the Argentine's third Premier League start for Tottenham, and he didn't put a foot wrong. He was handed an 8/10 rating by Flashscore and did so much good work for De Zerbi's side.

In a recent development, senesi won six duels, two tackles, made eight clearances and three recoveries. Alongside his defensive work, the 29-year-old was as impressive on the ball.

Indeed, Senesi had a 91 per cent passing accuracy, completing 93 successful passes. He had 118 touches, made 10 progressive passes with seven of those into the final third.

He often found himself in possession high up the pitch and helped Tottenham progress the ball with Ipswich defending in a low block. With Van Hecke and Senesi, Spurs have two of the best defensive ball players in the Premier League - and it was a formula that worked against Ipswich. Recent findings suggest that speaking in his post-match press conference, De Zerbi was full of praise for Senesi's performance. "I'm very lucky, because our squad is not just 11 very good players," he stated.

Sources indicate that "We have more than 20 very good players. I changed six players, compared to the last game, but not because I have to give gifts to the players, because they deserve to play. Now, maybe the toughest period of the Premier League starts, and we have to be ready to play changing many players, because we need energy, we need motivation, we need something from every one of us.".

In a recent development, marcos Senesi is a very good player. He's unlucky, because there is Micky van de Ven and JP Van Hecke but his level is top.

According to recent reports, now, De Zerbi has a conundrum going into Tottenham's trip to Sunderland next up. Does he stick with Van Hecke and Senesi? Or does he recall Van de Ven to the XI and drop Senesi again?

Basing it off Senesi's performance against Ipswich, he should keep his spot in the team. The ex-Bournemouth man deserves to start back-to-back league matches for the first time in a Spurs shirt and has that position locked for the time being, though Van de Ven may feel hard done by.

In a recent development, van de Ven hasn't done much wrong this season but may be suited to matches against the so-called 'Big Six' where his attributes could be a better fit. Sunderland are not one of them and are struggling near the bottom of the table, so Senesi and Van Hecke should start as the back-two again.

As well as at centre-back, De Zerbi has competition for places in several other areas. None more so than on the wing after Luca Williams-Barnett marked his first Tottenham start with a goal and an assist in the win against Ipswich. He bundled home from close range to give Spurs the lead in the first half before turning provider for Dominic Solanke to net his side's second.

Elsewhere, Conor Gallagher impressed in Sandro Tonali's absence, while James Maddison kept the ball ticking in midfield and Porro was back to his best on the right side of the back four.
