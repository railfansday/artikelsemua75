Loved ones, friends, and therapists are among the types of confidants people turn to when in distress. Many people suffering from trauma, depression, or anxiety can benefit from a supportive, compassionate conversation partner with whom they can talk things out. Ideally, this involves in-person, human interaction. Yet many people seek solace online, where they end up bonding with artificial conversation partners—some of whom are apparently better listeners than we are, leading to a closer relationship. How can we replicate this dynamic? Research explains.

Recent findings suggest that tobias Kleinert et al. (2026) examined the preference for AI or real people within emotionally engaging interaction in terms of establishing interpersonal closeness.[i] Their study used 492 participants interacting online through a version of the “Fast Friends Procedure” designed to facilitate rapid relationship building. Interactions included pre-generated responses by human partners or a large language model. (Related: Restaurant owners left in tears after discovering their...)

Recent findings suggest that kleinert et al. found that after emotionally engaging chats involving “deep-talk” interactions, people felt closer to AI than to human partners, but only when AI was labelled as human. They explain that this impact stems from AI’s higher amount of self-disclosure, which enhanced the perception of closeness. When the AI partner was accurately labeled as artificial, it reduced relationship building, which Kleinert et al. believe was likely due to a lower motivation to engage, evidenced by shorter responses and decreased feelings of closeness.

Kleinert et al. note that people feeling closer to AI after emotionally engaging interactions appears counterintuitive because AI lacks the capacity to experience emotion-related bodily sensations. Yet they recognize this deficit could give AI an advantage when it comes to responding. Some people are reluctant to share personal information about emotionally charged topics because they fear the reaction of the listener—who might fail to express empathy or even repurpose the information maliciously. The fact that AI cannot experience emotions makes it a safer partner with whom to discuss emotionally charged topics. Kleinert et al. also note that AI-generated responses included higher levels of self-disclosure, which prompted reciprocity and increased perceived interpersonal closeness.

Fortunately, because most people in crisis still prefer to speak to a real human being rather than a chatbot when they have a choice, there are ways we can capitalize on AI’s knack for non-judgmental listening.

In a recent development, kleinert et al. note that unlike AI-generated chats, emotional conversations between real people have many purposes, one of which is relationship building. When seeking to develop intimacy or elicit sensitive information, humans interact with both innate and learned social skills. AI lacks such discernment. Kleinert et al. note that in some cases, going overboard with high self-disclosure may be perceived as unnatural, depending on the situation. Over-disclosure may also be potentially dangerous when revealing personal information to complete strangers or untrustworthy persons and may be seen as unprofessional in the workplace. AI is less able to appropriately adjust to different social contexts and situations due to its lack of actual social experience. (Related: How to Budget for a Wedding Without Going Broke...)

Recent findings suggest that considering these findings, the goal is to listen and respond in a fashion that conveys our understanding and appreciation for what another person is feeling and expressing authenticity and empathy in a fashion that AI cannot replicate. Appropriate responses include soft, steady eye contact, open body language, leaning forward, and nodding. We must also resist the urge to interrupt, allowing someone the freedom to speak, which is often cathartic in and of itself.

Be a good listener, an empathetic ear, and an understanding partner in order to build trust with others through sharing your humanity.
