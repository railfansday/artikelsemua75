Unai Emery claimed Alejandro Garnacho was excluded from Aston Villa's Champions League squad against Galatasaray due to picking the "best players to be here with us".

Villa were below par against Galatasaray, falling to a 1-0 defeat at RAMS Park through substitute Deniz Gul's 77th-minute header.

And Garnacho failed to make the travelling party to Istanbul, despite Emery naming two back-up goalkeepers and opting to fill only 11 of the 12 available options on the bench.

The Argentine is on a season-long loan from Chelsea, with his deal including an obligation to buy if certain conditions were met, but he has made just seven appearances in all competitions, with none of those coming from the start.

Garnacho has also failed to make the previous two Premier League squads – both games Villa drew – and Emery seemingly confirmed the winger was not in his immediate looks.

"The players who were here are who I think are the best players to be here with us," said Emery.

Villa have lost successive games in major European competition for the first time since a run of three in April/May 2024 in the Conference League.

It is also the first time since November 2024 that they have failed to score in successive outings in major European competitions.
