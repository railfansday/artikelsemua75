“Leave With Me,” the emotional upcoming solo single released by LISA of BLACKPINK, tops this week’s new music poll.

Listeners voted in a poll published Friday (Nov. 20) on Billboard, choosing LISA’s latest track as their favorite newly introduced music.

“Leave With Me” topped new releases from TOMORROW X TOGETHER, Gracie Abrams and more on the poll, and it rose above the Grand Theft Auto VI album, which features songs by a number of stars: Cardi B, Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey, Playboi Carti, Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen, Tame Impala and many others — including BLACKPINK member JENNIE (with “Power Trip”). At the poll’s closing time on Sunday, LISA’s new ballad had a landslide victory, bringing in more than 84% of the vote.

“Leave With Me” plays over the end credits of LISA’s Always Lalisa documentary about the rise of her career of a solo artist outside of girl group BLACKPINK. The doc, directed by Sue Kim, is now available to stream exclusively on YouTube Premium.

The song, officially released this week, was co-written by LISA, Feli Ferraro, Tyler Spry, Ali Tamposi and Shintaro Yasuda.

“The only way forward is through,” she sings, leading up to the chorus: “I’ma take yesterday, kiss it goodbye/ Wipe my eyes dry, hold my head high/ I leave with me/ Check the clock, yeah, it’s my time/ Spread my wings, fly, me on my side/ You know that there’s no hard feelings/ Every wrong and right, put me right where I’m supposed to be/ No hard feelings/ And you can come or stay, either way, I’ma leave with me.”.

Prior to its release, LISA had surprised fans with the live debut of the then-unknown “Leave With Me” during opening night of her six-show “Viva La Lisa” residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas.

Trailing behind “Leave With Me” on the poll are TOMORROW X TOGETHER’s “Angel Bite,” with 8% of the vote, and the multi-artist Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, with 2% of the vote.
