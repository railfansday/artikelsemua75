The NFL is back and so are the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab Packs!

Loaded with top performers and fantasy stars, the Yahoo Fantasy x Arena Club Slab packs will be available each week during the regular season for collectors and fans to digitally rip.

For those new to Arena Club, it is a new way of collecting. As the premier tech-powered marketplace and grading platform, Arena Club brings the physical experience of card collecting into the digital age. Customers can purchase and open virtual packs of real, pre-graded cards ("slabs"), buy and sell on the marketplace and manage their card showrooms all in one place. In a recent development, yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs contain graded NFL trading cards curated by Arena Club. Fans can purchase a slab pack, each containing a graded card of an NFL player, via the Arena Club platform. This week, we're upping the ante with grails of some of the most collectible players — and top fantasy performers — being available as chases in both tiers of packs. For the Silver ($70) tier, each grail is worth roughly 35-60x of the pack price. In the Gold ($200) tier, each grail has an estimated value of 25-40x the pack price.

In a recent development, the Yahoo Fantasy Slab Packs will go on sale on at kickoff of the Colts-Texans game (8:15 p.m. ET, Thursday) and be available until the conclusion of the Monday night game (or until they sell out) every week through the end of the season.
