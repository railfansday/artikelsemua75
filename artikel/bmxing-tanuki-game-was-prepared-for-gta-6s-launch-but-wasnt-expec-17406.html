November is a huge month for video games, with not only the release of the Ocarina of Time remake but also the long-awaited (and highly anticipated) arrival of GTA 6 this week.

In a recent development, while most games decided to avoid this month, one other title that has been released over the past week is TANUKI: Pon's Summer. If you are wondering why a game starring a BMX-riding tanuki decided to go up against these two juggernauts, it seems the Zelda news caught it by surprise. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Although it was prepared for GTA 6's arrival, Nintendo only announced the release of Ocarina's remake in September, and then introduced it at the start of November. Here's what Denkiworks co-founder and game director Liam Edwards had to say about this during an interview with Games Industry:. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

"While we originally decided on October to launch, due to marketing opportunities with first-party platforms, we decided that the sweet spot would be just clear of GTA 6's initial launch period. What I don't think we could have accounted for was Nintendo shadow-dropping the release of Ocarina of Time [on November 5].".

According to recent reports, he further notes how there's "probably a fair bit more of an overlap with "Switch 2 Zelda players" for Tanuki than there would be for GTA 6". Despite this, there's hope the title can still find "long-term" success through "word-of-mouth" style marketing, and that maybe some players will pick up the "BMXing Tanuki game" once they're done with Nintendo and Rockstar's games.

We gave Tanuki: Pon's Summer seven out of ten stars in our NL review, describing it as a cosy game you can get lost in.
