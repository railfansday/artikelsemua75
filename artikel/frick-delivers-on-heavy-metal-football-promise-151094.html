Mario Frick joined Aberdeen after leaving Luzern.

If "heavy metal football" is what Aberdeen did, indeed, serve up at Kilmarnock on Saturday, Dons supporters will be eager to see more of this type of concert. (Related: Coffee shop owner refused to serve TikTok reviewer afte...)

Latest head coach Mario Frick used the phrase in the build-up to his first game in charge and his team did not disappoint. (Related: Build A Racetrack codes (November 2026)...)

The 4-2 Rugby Park victory was bombastic, chaotic and end-to-end - just one of six goal-laden Scottish Premiership games on Saturday.

The four Aberdeen strikes - two from Kevin Nisbet and one each from Brad Lyons and Findlay Marshall - showed Aberdeen's attackers at their best with fine finishing.

In the work in progress column, though, is defending, with the visitors lapsing badly for Killie goals scored by Greg Kiltie and Tyreece John-Jules.

All in all, it was an ideal start for former Luzern boss Frick.

Aberdeen's Frick starts with win in six-goal thriller at Kilmarnock.

Watch fresh Aberdeen boss Frick score against Scotland.

Frick was not a complete unknown to Scottish football when he was appointed during the international break - but the reference point was somewhat obscure.

Playing for Liechtenstein at Hampden in 2010, Frick gave the visitors a shock second-half lead.

The end of that game was reminiscent of the kind of heavy metal the head coach was talking about pre-match as Stephen McManus headed Scotland's winner after Kenny Miller's leveller.

Speaking after his Premiership bow, the former Luzern boss described himself as "very happy, very proud" of his fresh team.

"With only three training session, you can see so many attacking patterns in that first half, which was amazing," he told BBC Scotland.

Industry observers note that "We struggled the first few minutes with some players that hadn't played in a long long time and the 1-0 lead helped. First half was very good, the second half, complicated, because Kilmarnock played very well. We were very lucky, but at the end, I think it is a deserved victory and we are very happy.".

When asked if he was happy with the players trying to show his style, Frick replied: "Yes, for sure, after two training sessions, I saw a lot of it in the first half.

"It is a good start for us, but it is just the beginning.".

'Just the beginning' for Mario Frick.

Former Scotland midfielder Derek Ferguson took in the match at Rugby Park and was impressed with the visitors.

"As an attacking force, I thought they were good to watch," the former Rangers player said.

"They've got a substantial game on Tuesday night, against Rangers, so Rangers will need to be at their best, on their toes if they're going to get a result.

"It was the perfect result, obviously, for the manager coming in.

According to recent reports, "There was more energy, more legs, more running power. They were winning a lot of the duels.

"It's something to build on, but they've got to work on that defence. They've got to be more solid if they're going to go forward.".

Next up Ibrox and a season's third meeting with Rangers.

The two previous encounters, in the Premiership and League Cup semi-final, finished 1-0 to the Ibrox side, with the winning goal coming in the second half both times.

Meetings of Aberdeen and Rangers are typically just as fraught as the Rugby Park game on Saturday.

So, more heavy metal? It probably won't be smooth jazz.
