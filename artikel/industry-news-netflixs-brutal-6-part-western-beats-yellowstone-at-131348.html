However, American Primeval arguably takes things much further, largely because of its historical setting. Unlike the more modern world of Yellowstone, where the Duttons still have to contend with law enforcement, government officials, and legal bureaucracy, the Utah Territory depicted in American Primeval is a largely lawless frontier where formal authority has little control over the men fighting for power and land.

In American Primeval, violence is normalized, with little regard for human life or legal consequences.
