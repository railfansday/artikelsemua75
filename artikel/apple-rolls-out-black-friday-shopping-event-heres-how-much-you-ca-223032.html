Industry observers note that as in past years, Apple’s Black Friday Shopping Event offers you a gift card when you buy an eligible product. According to recent reports, apple’s newest products are excluded from the Black Friday Shopping Event. This includes the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone Duo, MacBook Pro with PROLED, HomeView, and Apple TV 4K (4th generation).

According to recent reports, the gift card you receive with a purchase can’t be applied to the product’s purchase price. Instead, you can use it toward a future purchase at Apple, including hardware, services like Apple Music and Apple TV, and Apple Store purchases.

The Apple Black Friday Event runs from today through November 30 at 11:59 p.m. PT. It’s available on Apple’s website and in Apple Stores. You cannot combine it with any other discounts, such as Apple’s Education Store or Veterans and Military Store. Apple’s holiday return window is also currently active, so you have until January 8, 2027, to return any purchases made.
