Alejandro Balde has been one of the most linked players with Liverpool since the summer transfer window slammed shut.

Before the 2026-27 season fully kicked off, nobody realised that the Reds would already be needing a newly introduced left-back.

Fans, pundits and journalists all thought that Milos Kerkez would step up to the plate and make a huge improvement on his game now that Andy Robertson has left.

However, the opposite seems to have happened with neither Kerkez nor Kostas Tsimikas meeting expectations.

This poor form has led to many transfer rumours emerging and now, yet another left-back has been added to Liverpool's shortlist.

Liverpool are in the running for Daniel Svensson.

As mentioned, left-back has been a major problem area for Liverpool this term.

The rest of their back four have performed fairly well with some such as Jeremy Jacquet and Ronald Araujo even being considered as Liverpool's signings of the season.

Balde felt like the most likely candidate to make the switch, especially since Barcelona only want around €40 million to allow him to leave. (Related: Latest Update: One Year Since the “DeepSeek Moment”...)

However, with so many other clubs also vying for the transfer of the Spaniard, they need to have alternative options to explore, just in case.

So, that's why this latest update from FussballNews is rather exciting.

"According to information from fussball-news.de, Liverpool and Arsenal from the Premier League are showing the greatest interest, while Manchester United and Leeds United are also keeping a close eye on the player," they wrote. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

"It is stated that the scouting team of the 'Red Devils' observed him in the Champions League game against Real Betis and produced a report with the rating 'transferable/signable'".

"According to information from fussball-news.de, the management of Borussia Dortmund is still committed to keeping Svensson – who has a long-term contract running until June 2029 – and does not want to let him leave.

Industry observers note that "Whether the club completely closes its doors depends on the conditions put on the table. While the club doesn't consider the player untouchable, it is reluctant to let him go unless suitable conditions are met and plans to strengthen its commitment through a salary increase.

"Borussia Dortmund are hoping for at least €45 million if a sale goes through. Should competition intensify, the total package, including add-ons, could potentially reach €50 million, with performance-related bonuses and sell-on clauses likely to be decisive factors in any deal.

Recent findings suggest that "Premier League clubs are reportedly prepared to test his situation with a €30 million offer for the Swedish player. However, the player is said to be happy in Dortmund and not planning a hasty move.".

Daniel Svensson would be a smart transfer for Liverpool.

Liverpool's defence has obviously been one of their main areas of focus when improving their ranks over the last couple of years.

After Araujo and Jacquet were brought in, most people thought that they'd be set for a while.

However, with Araujo's future still undecided and their left-backs failing to perform, it feels like Liverpool are going to have to focus a lot of their attention on their defence again.

Of all the left-backs linked with a Liverpool move, Svensson and Balde are the two most likely deals to be sanctioned with the former of the two regularly linked with a move in the past.

Both players are young and have a lot of their careers left ahead of them and, in the current transfer market, the €40 million - €50 million they they'd cost seem to be fairly cheap.

Obviously, neither of them have played in the Premier League before so, both players would be risky purchases but, looking back at their performances from the last couple of seasons, it feels like they'd both fit straight into Iraola's looks and help Liverpool out massively.
