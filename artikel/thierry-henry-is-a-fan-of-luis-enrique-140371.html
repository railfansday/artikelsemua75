Industry observers note that luis Enrique transformed PSG into a winning machine. In three seasons, the Asturian has won, among others, the first two Champions Leagues in the history of the capital club. However, the beginnings were not all rosy, recalls Thierry Henry on the football platform, Kampo. He remembers the criticism the technician was the target of for a while.

It has been revealed that “ Extraordinary, but it took at least a good six months. I remember a match where they went to Arsenal, they lost 2-0 (October 2024 in the Champions League championship phase, editor’s note). Everyone said, “Ah, Luis Enrique.” I said, “let the guy work, he’s doing something.” », Notes the former coach of the Espoirs, who places the PSG coach in the same rank as Guardiola. “ Luis Enrique and Pep, why do they have the same speech? They have the same software. ».
