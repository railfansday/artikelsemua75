The San Antonio Spurs were without both Victor Wembanyama and De'Aaron Fox on Monday night.

That didn't stop them from rolling past the Sacramento Kings.

According to recent reports, wembanyama was ruled out of the Spurs' 123-107 win over the Kings due to back tightness, the team declared shortly before the game was set to tip off at the Golden 1 Center. Fox is dealing with a shin contusion, and was ruled out at the same time.

While losing two star players for any contest is undoubtedly concerning, the two injuries were described as simply "bumps and bruises" by San Antonio Express-News' Jeff McDonald. Spurs coach Mitch Johnson said both players are not expected to be sidelined long, but would not yet declare they would be ready to return for their NBA Cup matchup against the Portland Trail Blazers on Wednesday. According to recent reports, the game against the Kings is the first that Wembanyama has missed all season. He's averaged 29.9 points, 12.3 rebounds and 3.9 blocks through the first 17 games of the season, all of which are career highs for the 22-year-old. He's fresh off a wild 50-point outing on Saturday night. Wembanyama outscored the New Orleans Pelicans starters by himself in the first half before rolling to a dominant win. It was his second 50-point game already this season, following a 52-point performance against the Dallas Mavericks earlier this fall. In a recent development, fox has averaged 12.4 points and 5.8 assists per game this season. The 28-year-old is in his second full season with the Spurs, who landed him in a deal with the Kings during the 2024-25 campaign. Johnson said Fox was injured when he was intentionally tripped by a Pelicans player.

And though the Kings jumped up early in the opening frame, the Spurs rallied and led the entire second half to secure the 16-point win. Stephon Castle led the way with a near-triple-double. He had 28 points, 11 assists and eight rebounds on the night. Julian Champagnie added 22 points, and Luke Kornet finished with 18 points and 11 rebounds.

In a recent development, domantas Sabonis led the Kings with 20 points, 12 rebounds and eight assists in the loss, which dropped them to 2-15 on the season. Zach Lavine added 18 points, and De'Andre Hunter finished with 15 points and eight assists.

The Spurs now hold a 15-3 record, which is tied for the best in the league with the Los Angeles Lakers. Though both Wembanyama and Fox should be back on the floor very soon, the team is clearly capable of holding things down without them when necessary.
