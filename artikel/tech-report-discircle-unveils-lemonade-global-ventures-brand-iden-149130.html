Discircle builds the Lemonade Global Ventures brand identity on a gold lemon globe, Zero Sugar discipline, and a black and acid yellow system.

Sources indicate that the whole system starts with one image: a lemon cast in gold, its pitted peel catching the light on pure black. Discircle, a studio from Curitiba, built the identity for Lemonade Global Ventures, an investment fund founded by entrepreneurs, backing profitable businesses with proven cash generation. The mark fuses the lemon with a globe: a calculated elliptical profile, its longitudinal lines reading as citrus segments and as earth meridians at the same time. Gold carries the value, black the weight, acid yellow the shouting. The studio calls the approach Zero Sugar, paired with Squeeze: growth demands sour decisions, an appetite for risk, and no easy formulas. The type does the talking in a tight grotesque with close tracking. Headlines run large and blunt: HOW TO MATURE on the book cover, Investment Perspective across the report, Squeeze the Extraordinary beside the steel squeezer. Small caps do the quiet work underneath: MADE WITH BRAZILIAN LEMONS, POTENTIAL, CONCENTRATED, GROWTH REPORT. The LGV monogram sits beside the line drawn lemon globe. The grid stays strict. Report A runs on three columns, Diagnosis, Strategy, Execution, each with the same measure, and the same three moves return as L1, L2, L3: structural diagnosis and expansion vectors, organization and leverage, consolidation and scale. Each stage gets a white oval and a pill label at the bottom, AGO, SET, OUT. Type sits on generous black space, so a few lines read louder than a full page.

The finishes stay physical. The gold lemon is a solid object, peel texture and all. The same mark turns into a gold lapel pin, its meridian lines cut into polished metal, on a black suit lapel. Report A pairs an acid yellow front and a grey board back, the letters L, G and V large at the corners, with inside pages showing the paper grain. The squeezer in the closing frame is a real steel object, ribbed and reflective.

It has been revealed that the system stretches without thinning. Brazil becomes a map of gold lemons under the line Tropical Capital. The Lemonade Makers wall lists fifteen names, from Martin Luther King Jr. to Oscar Wilde, separated by small yellow lemons, with the line Lemonade Makers squeeze the real greatness running underneath. The book How to Mature carries the same headline and mark, and the report keeps the same discipline.
