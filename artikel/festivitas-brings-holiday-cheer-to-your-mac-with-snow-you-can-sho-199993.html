It has been revealed that back in 2024, developer Simon Støvring launched Festivitas, an app that brings a ton of holiday cheer to your Mac. This year, the app is getting even more whimsical and delightful with new snowfall features.

In a recent development, for those unfamiliar, Festivitas lets you hang lights of different colors and patterns from your Mac’s menu bar and dock. It has a bunch of different customization options, so you can tweak the lights to match your preferences. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

Last year, the app added a new snowfall effect alongside those Christmas lights, plus Shortcuts integration and a dedicated iPhone and iPad app with customizable widgets.

This year’s Festivitas update expands that snowfall feature. Now, the snow that falls from the top of your Mac’s screen can pile up at the bottom of your screen, and you can use your mouse to shovel it away. This joins the existing ability to adjust snow amount, flake size, fall speed, and wind direction.
