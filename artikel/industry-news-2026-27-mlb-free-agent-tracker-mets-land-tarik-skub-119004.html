Tarik Skubal is going to be a Newly introduced York Met. The two-time Cy Young winner is joining the Mets on a nine-year, $450 million contract that will keep him in Flushing through 2035, according to multiple reports. That contract breaks the record for both total value for a pitcher (excluding Shohei Ohtani) and AAV.

Now, the rest of the free agent class, including solid options like Jazz Chisholm Jr., Tyler Rogers, Randy Arozarena and Freddy Peralta, are racing against the clock before the expected lockout after the CBA expires at 11:59 p.m. on Dec. 1.
