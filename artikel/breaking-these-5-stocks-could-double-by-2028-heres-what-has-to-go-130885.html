It has been revealed that when you're investing in individual stocks, you're always looking for a few that have the potential to double in a couple of years. Not every investment will pan out, but a few big winners will help lead to strong overall performance. Let's look at five growth stocks that have the potential to double by 2028 and what would need to happen for them to achieve that.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Despite its size, SpaceX (SPCX -7.69%) has clear potential to double in the next two years. For this to happen, it's going to have to grow rapidly and continue to hit important technological milestones, like it did when it recently caught the upper stage of its Starship rocket.

In a recent development, much of SpaceX's growth in the next two years will come from its infrastructure-as-a-service business. To rapidly grow this business, it's going to add a lot of compute capacity while maintaining its strong payback periods. If it can navigate permitting issues and industry bottlenecks and hit its growth targets of adding 10 gigawatts of power next year, and do that again in 2028, the stock could double. NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Industry observers note that the market is shifting toward custom artificial intelligence (AI) chips and inference, which plays right into Broadcom's (AVGO -12.19%) wheelhouse. With the stock trading at a forward price-to-earnings (P/E) multiple of just 11 times fiscal 2028 analyst estimates (ending October 2028), it has a substantial opportunity to double in the next two years. For this to happen, the manufacturer is going to have to maintain solid hardware gross margins and not lose significant share in the application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) market to cheaper competitors in future chip iterations. It's also going to have to see large customers Anthropic (ANT -8.50%) and OpenAI thrive, as much of its future projected custom chip revenue is coming from these two customers and Alphabet (GOOGL -2.70%) (GOOG -2.59%).

Trading at a huge discount to larger cybersecurity rivals while growing promptly, SentinelOne (S -4.58%) stock could easily double from continued growth and multiple expansion. Right now, the stock trades at a forward price-to-sales (P/S) multiple of 6 times 2027 estimates compared to 17.5 times for Palo Alto Networks (PANW -5.09%) and 37 times for CrowdStrike (CRWD -5.76%), so the opportunity for significant multiple expansion is there.

The firm has been aggressively making moves to become a strong provider in the AI era, and if this leads to accelerating growth, the stock has a great opportunity to double. Analysts at Wedbush, meanwhile, noted after talking to vendors and resellers that customers were starting to expand with SentinelOne solutions, and the company was winning in the small and medium-sized business (SMB) and mid-market spaces due to its strong abilities and cheaper pricing.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

Beaten to a pulp over the past two years, SoundHound AI (SOUN -10.31%) has the potential to see its stock double in the coming years, with its valuation down to a forward P/S multiple under 6. For this to happen, it will need to stabilize and integrate its recent LivePerson acquisition, hit its target of becoming earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) profitable in the second half of 2027, and see strong adoption of OASYS, its fresh voice, language intelligence, and agentic AI orchestration solution.

In a recent development, the ingredients are all there; it will just be a matter of execution. Right now, the market is betting SoundHound won't be able to pull this off, but if it does, the upside is enormous.

Industry observers note that stepping outside of the tech sector, Dutch Bros (BROS -4.01%) has one of the best opportunities in the consumer space for its stock to double over the next two years. The coffee shop operator has been delivering strong same-store sales growth while it has huge expansion opportunities. However, the stock has been beaten down over the past year, leaving it at a very attractive P/S valuation compared to rival Starbucks (SBUX -1.28%) despite its much longer growth runway.

Some of its initiatives to drive same-store growth include menu expansion, including food; order ahead; its loyalty program; and piloting third-party delivery.
