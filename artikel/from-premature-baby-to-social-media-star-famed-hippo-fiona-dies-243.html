The Cincinnati Zoo in Ohio notes its beloved hippopotamus Fiona has died just days after swallowing a knitted hat that slipped into her enclosure.

According to recent reports, fiona was born premature at the Cincinnati Zoo in 2017 and at first struggled to survive. At birth, she weighed 13 kg (29 lbs), which the zoo mentioned was about half the previous lowest recorded birth weight for her species. She reached 1,000 lbs in 2018.

Recent findings suggest that over the years, Fiona became a favourite among visitors and a star on social media. This video captures moments from her life, from her early days as a calf to celebrating her fifth birthday and receiving treatment just a few days ago.
