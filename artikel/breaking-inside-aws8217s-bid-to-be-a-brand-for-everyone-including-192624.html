  • It’s indicative of the cloud organization’s wider strategic pivot, which aims to win over more non-technical customers and help AWS reach CEO Matt Garman’s goal of becoming a $1 trillion revenue business within five to 10 years.

Its recent campaign for AWS Quick, the manufacturer’s AI-centered workspace, included ads featuring visuals that humans could see, undergirded by text for AI agents to read.
