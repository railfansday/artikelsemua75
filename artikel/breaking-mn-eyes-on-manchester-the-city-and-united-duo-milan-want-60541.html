The Rossoneri are also interested in players whose contracts are expiring within the next 18 months, as was the case with Mario Gila this summer, who signed for the Diavolo one year before his contract with Lazio expires.

Bruno Fernandes is one to watch for the upcoming summer transfer window, with his contract expiring in June 2027 but with an option to extend. United’s attempts to extend his deal have so far been unsuccessful, likely due to the player’s desire to try something else.

According to recent reports, fernandes has played at an incredible level in the Premier League in recent years, winning Player of the Year for 2025-26. So far, he has made 341 official appearances for the Red Devils, scoring 113 goals and providing 111 assists.

Industry observers note that he’s a key player for the Portuguese national team and he would provide leadership that the squad could lose if players like Luka Modric leave. Moreover, his past in Italy – where he had spells at Novara, Udinese and Sampdoria – could help him integrate more quickly and effectively.

In a recent development, fernandes also shares the same agency as Ruben Amorim. The relationship between the two is excellent, and already this summer the Rossoneri had inquired about a possible move for the former Sporting star.

At the other Manchester club, however, there’s an old acquaintance of Italian football who could come back into fashion as early as January: Mateo Kovacic. His contract also expires at the end of the campaign, and he could come in to add depth too.

Industry observers note that given the increasingly certain departure of youngster Christian Comotto, an experienced player like Kovacic – at the right price, in terms of wages – could be useful for Amorim. His situation is being watched, but there will be competition from other clubs, likely in Italy too.
