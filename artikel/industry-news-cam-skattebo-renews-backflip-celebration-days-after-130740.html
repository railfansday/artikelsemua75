New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo vowed in the aftermath of Aidan Hutchinson's season-ending celebration injury that he was done doing backflips. That promise lasted less than four quarters.

Skattebo iced New York's 23-18 win over the Jacksonville Jaguars on Sunday with a run for a first down at the 2-minute warning that allowed the Giants to finish the game in victory formation.

When the play was done, Skattebo stood up and flexed. Then he did a backflip.

"It's hard to see," Skattebo said Wednesday of Hutchinon's injury, per the New York Post. "I'm saying yeah. Mentally, I'm like, 'I'm not doing that.' That ain't going to happen to me.".

Per the Post, Skattebo reserved the right to renew his signature celebration "if we go to the playoffs or something.".

Sunday's game must have fallen under "or something.".

According to recent reports, skattebo doesn't have to look to Hutchinson as an injury reference point. He sustained his own season-ending injury as a rookie, a dislocated ankle that left him in fear that he'd need to have his foot amputated.
