EXCLUSIVE: Paramount+ began rolling cameras on Season 3 of MobLand last week — and now the gangland drama has added a latest recruit.

Gregg Sulkin, the British actor known for Wizards of Waverly Place and who recently joined the cast of the PBS Masterpiece series The Forsytes, will take on a recurring role on MobLand. Details of Sulkin’s character are being kept under wraps for now.

Sources indicate that season 3 will pick up from the finale of Season 2, when the Harrigans faced an existential threat from Johnny Flynn’s diabolical rival mobster Frankie Cooper. Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan, and Helen Mirren are all reprising their roles for the crime family saga. Paramount has been tight-lipped about other returning cast. MobLand’s journey to a Season 3 renewal was not straightforward amid disquiet between Hardy and showrunner Jez Butterworth. The renewal was confirmed by Paramount in September after a meeting in London between Hardy, Butterworth, and series executive producer David C. Glasser, where they hashed things out. Season 2 starred Paddy Considine, Joanne Froggatt, Lara Pulver, Anson Boon, Mandeep Dhillon, Jasmine Jobson, Teddie Allen, Emmett J. Scanlan, Flynn, Ophelia Lovibond, Janet McTeer, and Toby Jones.

Industry observers note that sulkin recently returned to his fan-favorite role of Mason Greyback on Wizards Beyond Waverly Place for its four-part conclusion. He previously starred in Hulu’s The Kollective, Netflix’s The Six Triple Eight, and BBC drama World on Fire. Sulkin is repped by D2 Management and Curtis Brown Group.
