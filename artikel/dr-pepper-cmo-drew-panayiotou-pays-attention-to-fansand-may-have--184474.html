Dr Pepper CMO Drew Panayiotou pays attention to fans—and may have seen your TikTok

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that drew Panayiotou has learned to take time out of every day to see what Dr Pepper fans are up to. Last December, the chief marketing and innovation officer (who previously worked at Disney, Coca-Cola, and Google) became aware that a creator named Romeo Bingham had posted a Dr Pepper jingle to TikTok and invited the company to get in on it. The video went viral, earning 151.3 million views and 13.5 million likes. Panayiotou quickly met with Dr Pepper’s brand and social media teams—and creative agency Deutsch—and within about a month they had licensed Bingham’s song for an ad that ran during the NCAA football championship. CBS News, People, and other media covered the TikTok-to-ad journey, netting more than 4 billion impressions and increasing the brand’s social mentions by 46%. A few months later, Panayiotou and his team turned fans’ almost obsessive love of the limited-time flavor Creamy Coconut—which saw 6.3 million engagements on social media during the first three months after it returned to market in April—into “It’s a Pepper Fling.” The campaign featured an absurdist four-part, short-form vertical soap opera about a woman becoming infatuated with the flavor, which drew more than 56 million views across all socials, the company notes. The campaign’s success was a full-circle moment for the brand’s social listening approach, since Creamy Coconut itself had started as a response to the interactive “dirty soda” trend that had fans on TikTok spiking their Dr Pepper with cream, syrup, and lime. “We’ve created a brand that is participatory at its core,” Panayiotou says. “We don’t build it from a conference room. We build it from fan behavior.”.
