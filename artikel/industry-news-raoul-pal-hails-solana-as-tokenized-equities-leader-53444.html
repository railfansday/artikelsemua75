Tokenized equities are rapidly becoming a major competitive landscape among blockchain networks. Speaking at the 2026 Breakpoint conference, Raoul Pal praised Solana as the leader in tokenized stock volume, highlighting the network’s immense trading volume.

At the Solana Breakpoint conference this week, the CEO of the Real Vision financial platform recognized the explosive growth of the tokenized stocks market on the SOL network.

According to recent reports, the celebrated macro investor stated the total value of tokenized equities on Solana has increased from $10 million to over $2 billion in just 15 months. He viewed this massive growth as a strong connection between traditional stock markets and the Solana network.

In a recent development, in recent months, the global tokenized stocks market has delivered notable results on the blockchain infrastructure. According to the latest RWA.xyz report, the global tokenized stock market has reached $3.66 billion, with Solana leading the race in market value, as Raoul noted.

Trading volume in tokenized equities on Solana surged to $2 billion, a 2000% increase from $10 million 15 months ago. This clearly indicates that the network has surpassed its competitors, including BNB Chain and Ethereum, to become the global leader in the tokenized stock market.

Multiple tokenized stock products are already operating on Solana, including those for Johnson & Johnson, Exxon Mobil, Salesforce, Uber, Disney, and several others.

Amid the rapid rise of the tokenized stock market, institutional investor participation is also surging.

Industry observers note that earlier this month, Securitize confirmed the debut of its tokenized stocks on Solana. The move signals that major institutions are turning to blockchain infrastructure to integrate tokenized stocks into supported DeFi services and blockchain applications.
