One of Avengers: Doomsday's heroes has been getting ready for his return off-screen.

In a recent development, shang-Chi's debut expanded the MCU by introducing audiences to Ta Lo, a hidden dimension connected to his mother's family. The mystical location played a significant role in his origin story, as he confronted his father, Wenwu, and ultimately inherited the Ten Rings. However, his first solo movie ended without fully exploring what he could do with his latest powers. (Related: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming Game in 5 D...)

With Avengers: Doomsday approaching its theatrical release, latest details have emerged about what the superhero may have been up to during his absence. Simu Liu, who first portrayed Shang-Chi in 2021's Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, has teased how the character could have developed and improved his abilities ahead of his highly anticipated comeback.

Industry observers note that during an interview on the Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday Official Podcast, Liu joined hosts Iman Vellani, who plays Ms. Marvel, and Frank Alvarez to discuss his character. The actor also spoke about what has transpired with the hero between his 2021 solo film and the upcoming crossover movie. (Related: Industry News: Director of Machine Learning Insights...)

Recent findings suggest that vellani theorized that he could have spent some time at Kamar-Taj with Wong, learning more about the origins of the Ten Rings. While Liu considered that option, he came up with his own idea that would reconnect the character with his family history. According to the actor, Shang-Chi probably returned to Ta Lo after the events of his first movie. The events that occurred during his initial visit to the mystical dimension left him very little time to explore his mother's home or develop relationships with the relatives who still live there.

Liu believes that revisiting Ta Lo could provide Shang-Chi with a chance to strengthen his connection to his mother's side of the family, like his aunt Ying Nan, portrayed by Michelle Yeoh. It could also allow him to learn more about the traditions and abilities associated with the realm itself.

Industry observers note that aside from the character's personal connections, Liu explained that Shang-Chi would likely be training so he could better control the Ten Rings. Although he defeated the Dweller-in-Darkness, the actor believes his victory did not necessarily mean he had mastered everything. He further elaborated, stating that Shang-Chi's victory was the beginning of his arc. Liu explained that he would have understood the need to practice and become more comfortable with his abilities before confronting the events of Avengers: Doomsday.

Avengers: Doomsday will be released in theaters on December 18, 2026.
