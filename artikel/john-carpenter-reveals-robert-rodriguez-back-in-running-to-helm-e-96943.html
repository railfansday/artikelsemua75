After 45 years, John Carpenter is still finding the right director to break an old cult classic out of lockup.

Having recently tapped Zack Snyder to write and direct a reimagining of Escape from Upcoming York, Carpenter noted he’s been considering another candidate, despite being told Synder is “on fire” during a recent podcast appearance.

“He’s not anymore, but he’s trying to get back,” Carpenter said on the Dropping Names podcast. “We’ll see. He’s a nice man. I liked him.”.

In a recent development, the comment came after Snyder’s The Last Photograph premiered at TIFF in September to poor reviews, mostly over the film’s excessive violence and offensive content. Additionally, his Rebel Moon films at Netflix were critically panned. Watch on Deadline.

According to recent reports, carpenter added, “We have a backup: Robert Rodriguez wants to. He a latest story he wants to do. So, we’ll see.”.

Meanwhile, after co-writing and directing the original, Carpenter is returning to compose the score once again. “I demanded to be the composer … we’re gonna remake the score,” he noted.

Rodriguez was previously attached to a remake of Escape from Newly introduced York in 2017, with the hunt for a director leading to several candidates over the years, including Radio Silence.

In Escape from Latest York, special forces operative-turned-prisoner Snake Plissken (Kurt Russell) has 24 hours to rescue the president of the United States from the island of Manhattan, which has been converted into the country’s only maximum security prison. The 1996 sequel Escape from L.A. followed.
