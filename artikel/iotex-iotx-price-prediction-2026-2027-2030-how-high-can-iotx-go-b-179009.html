Sources indicate that not every blockchain is built for finance or NFTs. Some are trying to connect blockchain directly to the physical world.

IoTeX is one of those projects.

According to recent reports, ioTeX is a Layer-1 blockchain designed to connect real-world devices, sensors, and machines to decentralized networks. The project operates at the intersection of IoT, AI, and blockchain, aiming to create a trusted data layer that securely transmits real-world information to decentralized applications. IoTeX is expected to strengthen its position in the Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network (DePIN) sector as it expands its ecosystem throughout 2026.

Recent findings suggest that one of its biggest strengths is the Burn Drop mechanism, which reduces the circulating supply as latest devices join the network. A portion of IOTX is permanently burned, while the remaining tokens are distributed to long-term stakers, encouraging users to hold their tokens.

The expansion of W3bstream is another major catalyst, allowing real-world data from smart devices to connect with leading blockchains such as Ethereum, Solana, and Polygon.

At the same time, enterprise partners deploying DePIN infrastructure must purchase and lock IOTX, helping reduce the available supply. With newly introduced developer grants expected to bring more projects to the network, higher on-chain activity could further support IOTX’s price growth throughout 2026.
