The Switch version of The Wolf Among Us Remastered will launch via Nintendo eShop on November 29 for $24.99, Telltale Games revealed.

While originally scheduled to launch on October 29 alongside the PlayStation 5, Xbox Series, Xbox One, and PC (Steam, Epic Games Store) versions, the Switch version was delayed for further quality improvements. (Related: Course Launch Community Event...)
