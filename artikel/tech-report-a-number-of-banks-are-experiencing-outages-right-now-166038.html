Recent findings suggest that if you're having trouble accessing your bank right now, you aren't alone. As of Monday afternoon, a number of banks are experiencing outages and interruptions to service.

Sources indicate that that's according to Downdetector, which collects user reports of challenges to different sites and services. As of this article, the site demonstrates notable numbers of user reports with the following banks and credit companies: American Express, Ally, Bank of America, Capital One, Chase, Citi, Huntington Bank, JP Morgan, Navy Federal Credit Union, USAA, US Bank, and TD Bank, Visa, Wells Fargo. Recent findings suggest that there isn't a clear reason why these various companies are currently experiencing problems. The timing is suspicious, of course: These interruptions come after last week's massive Microsoft outage, which took down services like Outlook, Teams, and Microsoft 365. It also comes just days after the FAA unexpectedly grounded all flights in the U.S. There's no evidence there's any link between these events, but the frequency of these developments is concerning on the surface.
