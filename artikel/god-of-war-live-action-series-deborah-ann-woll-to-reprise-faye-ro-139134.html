Deborah Ann Woll is set to play Faye in the “God of War” live-action TV series at Amazon Prime Video, Variety has learned.

Woll previously played Faye in the video game “God of War Ragnarok” and will also lead the upcoming game “God of War Laufey,” which is due out in May 2027.

Recent findings suggest that she is the latest addition to the expansive cast, which also includes: Dave Bautista as Kratos, Callum Vinson as Atreus, Mandy Patinkin as Odin, Ed Skrein as Baldur, Max Parker as Heimdall, Ólafur Darri Ólafsson as Thor, Teresa Palmer as Sif, Alastair Duncan as Mimir, Jeff Gulka as Sindri, Danny Woodburn as Brok, Sonya Walger as Freya, Island Austin as Thrud, Ben Chapple as Magni, Louis Cunningham as Modi, and Evelyn Miller as Gna. In a recent development, woll is the latest actor from the “God of War” games to land a role in the series playing the same character. As previously revealed, Duncan will return in the role of Mimir.

The character Faye is described as “The mysterious late wife of Kratos and mother of Atreus, Faye’s abrupt passing sets in motion the events of ‘God of War.’ Along their journey, Kratos and Atreus discover secrets about Faye that make them wonder how well they truly knew her at all.”. It has been revealed that this is Woll’s latest return to an iconic franchise role in recent years. She also reprised the role of Karen Page in “Daredevil: Born Again” and “The Punisher: One Last Kill” at Disney+ after having played the character across multiple Marvel-Netflix shows between 2015 and 2019. Woll is also known for her work in the hit HBO series “True Blood” and will appear in Season 2 of “The Testaments” at Hulu. In film, she has worked in features like “Queen of the Ring” and “The Cycle.”.

She is repped by Luber Roklin Entertainment and Goodman Genow.

Prime Video has given the “God of War” show a two-season order, with the two seasons set to shoot back-to-back. Filming originally began in Vancouver back in February, but after original series lead Ryan Hurst was injured on set over the summer, production was shutdown until Bautista was brought in and shooting resumed in late October. The series will pick up with the story of the two most recent “God of War” games, in which Kratos must raise his ten-year old son Atreus while battling gods of the Norse pantheon. Per the logline, “Father and son Kratos and Atreus embark on a journey to spread the ashes of their wife and mother, Faye. Through their adventures, Kratos tries to teach his son to be a better god, while Atreus tries to teach his father how to be a better human.”.

In a recent development, ronald D. Moore is the writer, executive producer, and showrunner on “God of War” under his Tall Ship Productions banner.

According to recent reports, frederick E.O. Toye will direct the first two episodes of “God of War.” Maril Davis of Tall Ship also executive produces alongside Cory Barlog, Naren Shankar, Matthew Graham, Asad Qizilbash, Jeff Ketcham, Hermen Hulst, Roy Lee, Brad Van Arragon, and Lawrence Trilling. Joe Menosky, Marc Bernardin, Tania Lotia, and Ben McGinnis serve as co-executive producers on the series. The series is co-produced by Sony Pictures Television and Amazon MGM Studios in association with PlayStation Productions and Tall Ship Productions.
