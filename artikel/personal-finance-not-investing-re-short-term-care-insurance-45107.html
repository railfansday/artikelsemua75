Has anyone had any experience with short term care insurance, good or bad? I have LTC insurance but DW does not. I had asked about funding LTC insurance in a previous post and WoW2012 was nice enough to suggest STC insurance which I had never heard of (we now live in Texas which has it - used to live in Florida which doesn't).
