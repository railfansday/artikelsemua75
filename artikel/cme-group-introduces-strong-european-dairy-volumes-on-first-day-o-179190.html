CHICAGO, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- CME Group, the world's leading derivatives marketplace, today announced that more than 100 dairy contracts of its fresh European dairy contracts exchanged hands in the first day of trading.

Sources indicate that CME Group launched contracts for European Butter, European Skimmed Milk Powder, European Whey Powder and European Liquid Milk on November 23, signifying the first step in regional expansion following its agreement with the European Energy Exchange from earlier this year. A total of 126 contracts traded of European Butter and European Skimmed Milk Powder futures and open interest reached 60 across the two contracts. (Related: Compound Interest Calculator: See Your Money Grow...)

In a recent development, "The launch of our European Dairy contracts is a significant milestone in bringing our U.S. dairy franchise to a global audience," stated John Ricci, Managing Director and Global Head of Agricultural Products. "The first trades of our European Butter and European Skimmed Milk Powder futures highlight the need for participants across Europe to have the tools to manage regional price risk on a single platform.".

CME Group's dairy complex reached a monthly volume record for futures and options with 11,611 contracts in October. In addition, dairy futures and options reached a record open interest on November 3, 2026, with 511,090 contracts. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)
