Latest Update: 'We're seeing in real time the effects that AI is going to have on cybersecurity', expert indicates

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 19 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (98 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that FRANCE 24's Monte Francis speaks with Dr. R. David Edelman, Senior Fellow at the Brookings Institution, also former Director for Cybersecurity on the National Security Council under the Obama administration. He explains that the latest spate of network outages indicates "in real time the effects that AI is going to have on cybersecurity" adding that "the one thing that's tying most of these outages together is that companies and institutions are racing to patch a system that's been identified as vulnerable, and they're patching what themselves are pretty creaky digital systems".
