📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The Nintendo Switch 2 has really picked up the baton of playing older games from their history and run with it, and players can now play all sorts of titles from several major console generations. To access most of them, players need to be signed up to Nintendo Switch Online, as well as grabbing the Expansion Pack that grants access to historical titles.

It has been revealed that at last count, there are over 150 titles on the service, including efforts as early as Super Mario Bros. 3 on the NES, all the way up to games like Super Mario Sunshine and The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker for the GameCube. Now, the latter platform has just received yet another title, as well as some interesting quality-of-life updates that should make the experience a lot nicer to play.

As first reported by GoNintendo, Wave Race Blue Storm has just been added to the Switch Online Expansion Pack, while the Nintendo Classics: GameCube app has also seen a change that is bound to please fans.

If you didn't play it at the time, Wave Race Blue Storm is a jetski racing video game developed and published by Nintendo that came out all the way back in 2001. Though it's a bit of a departure from their usual fare, it really is a lot of fun, and has plenty of arcade-style elements that should keep you coming back for more.

In announcing the move over on Twitter/X, Nintendo of America shared the following post:.

"Bust out your wildest tricks in races against seven other riders and become the ultimate Wave Rave champion in Wave Race Blue Storm, available now on Nintendo Switch 2 with Nintendo GameCube - Nintendo Classics for #NintendoSwitchOnline + Expansion Pack members.".

It has been revealed that fan response to the move was also generally positive, even if they were suggesting other games they wanted, with one saying, "Great! I could never play this one on GameCube but I love of the original Wave Race for the N64. Thanks!".

Recent findings suggest that while another added, "Hahahah, finally I was waiting for Wave Race: Blue Storm to come to NSO. I’m hyped as hell!! Thank you, Nintendo.".

At the same time, the tech giant also confirmed that it will now be possible to turn off the pesky borders that players have disliked so much on classic titles, though only on GameCube releases for the time being. They haven't stated as much yet, but it would be surprising if Nintendo are just planning to stop with that initiative there, so don't be surprised to see it crop up for other consoles in the future.

Industry observers note that to do it for yourself, head to the GameCube app and then click Settings. Right at the bottom you should be able to find a Border Graphic toggle option, and de-selecting it will get rid of the border from your gameplay. There's no word just yet on whether Nintendo is planning to add any other games to the Classics catalog before the year is out, but we can certainly expect plenty more next year, as they continue to expand the offering across multiple generations of hardware.
