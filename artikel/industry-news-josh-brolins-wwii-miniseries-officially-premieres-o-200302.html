It has been revealed that it's been eight years since Josh Brolin last played CIA operative Matt Graver in Sicario: Day of the Soldado, and while there's been talk of a third movie for much of that time, Brolin has since reunited with Sicario director Denis Villeneuve for his adaptation of Dune. Brolin's career has taken him from the American frontier in True Grit to the deserts of Arrakis in the Dune films, but his latest project has more in common with one of HBO's greatest television series, Band of Brothers, and he'll be helping Netflix tell the story of the men whose experiences inspired that series.

According to recent reports, brolin's new documentary series, World War II: Behind Enemy Lines, will officially premiere on Netflix on December 2, with all four episodes available to stream in just five days. The series tells the story of the 101st Airborne's battles, covert missions, and losses in Nazi-occupied Europe, starting with the D-Day landings in June 1944 and ending with V-E Day in May 1945. Think of it as the real-life version of the story many people know from Band of Brothers. (Related: Tiny Agents: an MCP-powered agent in 50 lines of code...)

Similar to the work done by Peter Jackson in bringing World War I footage to color and 3D, this is built from archive film that's been restored to the highest quality and colorized too, while much of it is previously unseen and includes rare first-hand accounts from the men who were there. And in a rare, fascinating move, it also shows the German paratroopers who were defending Normandy before the sea landings.

Recent findings suggest that the series comes from 72 Films, the production company behind Netflix's six-part documentary World War II: From the Frontlines, which was narrated by John Boyega (Star Wars: The Force Awakens) and went on to win two Emmy Awards in 2024. Director Rob Coldstream is back for the upcoming series, with David Glover and Mark Raphael serving as executive producers and Brolin taking over narration duties. (Related: Director of Machine Learning Insights...)

While From the Frontlines covered the war across multiple countries and from the perspectives of those on opposing sides, the fresh series will focus specifically on the experiences of the American soldiers who served with the 101st Airborne Division in Europe.
