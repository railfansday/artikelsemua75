Sources indicate that maven Clinic is the world's largest virtual clinic for women and families on a mission to make healthcare work for all of us. Through Maven Enterprise, the company partners with more than 2,300 employers and health plans to provide end-to-end women's and family health programs spanning fertility and family building, maternity and newborn care, parenting and pediatrics, and menopause and midlife — improving clinical outcomes, reducing healthcare costs, and expanding equitable access to high-quality care at scale. Through its consumer platform, Maven provides direct access to virtual care across 30+ specialties, as well as dedicated hormone and GLP-1 care programs purpose-built for women. Founded in 2014 by CEO Kate Ryder, Maven Clinic has raised more than $425 million from leading healthcare and technology investors including General Catalyst, Sequoia, Dragoneer Investment Group, Oak HC/FT, StepStone Group, Icon Ventures, and Lux Capital. Recognized for innovation and industry leadership, Maven has been named to the TIME100 Most Influential Companies, CNBC Disruptor 50, Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies, and FORTUNE Best Places to Work. Learn more at mavenclinic.com.

Maven is looking for a Director, GTM Operations & Architecture will own the strategy, systems, design, and reporting that power Maven's commercial engine. This role sits at the center of Salesforce administration, accountable for the business requirements and sales processes that drive it, providing the growth team with clean, trusted data and efficient workflows. You will report to the Head of Revenue Operations and partner closely with Salesforce engineering, Commercial Strategy, Sales, and Finance to keep our systems scalable, well-documented, and built for growth. Industry observers note that this is a player-coach role. You will have 1-2 direct reports while retaining end-to-end ownership of a few critical domains. The person in this seat will be the functional owner of our GTM tools and processes: defining what the business needs, translating those needs into system requirements, owning prioritization, and driving projects to completion. We are looking for a builder and an operator. (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

Salesforce & GTM Systems Ownership: The Director will own Salesforce as the system of record for the GTM org, ensuring the platform is configured, automated, and maintained to support the sales process end to end.

Data Quality & Process Governance: The Director is the steward of data quality and process discipline across our GTM systems.

Cross-Functional Operations Support: The Director is a hands-on operator across the broader GTM and Finance functions.

The base salary range for this role is $204,000 - $230,000 per year. You will also be entitled to receive equity and benefits. Individual pay decisions are based on a number of factors, including qualifications for the role, experience level, and skillset.

This role requires active work authorization in the US.

Maven embraces a flexible hybrid work model. Our teams primarily operate from the Fresh York Metropolitan area, NY, and remotely via San Francisco/Bay Area, CA, Seattle, WA. For those in our New York City office, we encourage in-person collaboration by requiring team members to work onsite three days a week  (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday). For those based in Boston, DC, Chicago, Seattle, and San Francisco, we encourage in-person collaboration by requiring team members to attend monthly Work Together Days within these cities. This policy aims to balance remote work flexibility with the benefits of face-to-face interaction.
