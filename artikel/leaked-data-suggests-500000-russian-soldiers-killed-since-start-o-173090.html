Leaked data suggests 500,000 Russian soldiers killed since start of Ukraine invasion

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (80 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

More than 500,000 Russian soldiers have been killed in the war with Ukraine, according to an estimate by a number of media organisations. The figures are based on a huge dataset that was hacked from Russia’s state statistics agency Rosstat by Belarusian activist group Cyber Partisans and a Ukrainian hacker group, Silent Crow. Two independent Russian news outlets Meduza and Mediazona say the death toll may rise to more than 700,000 once missing soldiers are included.
