Firefly might return to the small screen after all.

Sources indicate that there have been many successful sci-fi TV shows over the years, with projects like The Expanse, The 100, Stranger Things, Black Mirror, and many more running for multiple seasons. However, way before the age of streaming, multiple shows with potential were already being cut short, airing a season or two before being canceled. That was the fate that befell Firefly, a gritty Space Western series that was somewhat similar to Star Trek, but with its own flavor of sci-fi. Several years after the show was canceled, it became a cult classic, and a planned revival series continues to move forward in development. On the Revenge Of podcast, Nathan Fillion confirmed that there has been some progress on the Firefly animated sequel series.

According to the actor, who led the short-lived sci-fi series as Captain Malcolm "Mal" Reynolds, while progress on the planned revival has been slower than he expected, the show is gradually moving forward. Fillion added to the hopeful upgrade by claiming that although development has moved in ways he could not have foreseen, he has been told "Yes" more than "No," which is an exciting sign for the sci-fi series' future.
