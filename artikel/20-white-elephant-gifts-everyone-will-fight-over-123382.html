If you’re reading this, there’s probably a holiday gift exchange on your calendar coming up. Maybe your boss organized one for the office, or maybe it’s the doing of that one friend who loves hosting so much they’d throw a party for Arbor Day. Either way, you’re probably wondering: What the heck do I bring to this thing?

According to recent reports, there are a few rules to this game. First, there’s likely a price cap, so we’re keeping everything under $100. (If your white elephant involves luxury gifts, we’d love to be a fly on that very well-appointed wall.) Second, your gift should be something just about anyone would be excited to unwrap. That rules out clothes and shoes with specific sizes, phone cases for whatever iPhone model is right now in circulation, and franchise merch that assumes your giftee has seen every Marvel movie or follows football. Luckily, that still leaves plenty of fun, affordable, crowd-pleasing gifts. We’ve rounded up our favorites below, so pick one, wrap it up, and take all the credit when everyone starts fighting over it.

Who doesn’t like waffles? A waffle-maker that’s easy to use and easy to store is for sure going to be a slam dunk at the gift exchange.

Sources indicate that A cute felt ornament is a great gift that’s sort of holiday-themed, but also makes a fun gift for any occasion. It will take their Christmas tree to the next level—and even if they don’t celebrate Christmas, they can hang this little guy on a doorknob, at their desk, etc.

It has been revealed that jigsaw puzzles have always been a popular pastime, but people (ahem, my mom and sister) have especially taken up puzzling since the pandemic. Piecework makes some of my favorite puzzles, like this quirky peppermint-themed one. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Whether they drink coffee or tea, this leakproof tumbler will no doubt become their go-to drink vessel. Coffee for the morning commute? Check. Hot tea for an afternoon pick-me-up? Check. A glass of wine with dinner? Sure, why not. The Strova has all that covered (literally).

In these cold-weather months, hand cream is a must. This luxurious one from Homecourt smells like cedarwood smoke, sweet cardamom, cinnamon, and white leather.

Everyone wants a home that smells good. Aera’s aroma diffuser will make that happen. Yes, it’s at the upper limit price-wise, but it’s bound to be the gift everyone wants.

Just any old liquor won’t cut it, but Fords Sloe Gin is perfect for the holidays. Whoever gets this bottle will be making Sloe Gin Fizzes all winter long, and if you’re lucky, they’ll invite you over for one.

This rechargeable candle lighter is both practical and artful—it eliminates the need for matches altogether, and it looks classy next to a candle.

Yes, a candle is a classic gift exchange option, but don’t just go for your run-of-the-mill Yankee Candle; get one that will make their home smell like a winter wonderland.

Who wouldn’t want a tiny printer that can instantly print photos from your phone? This one comes with a paper roll so your giftee can get started printing as soon as they open this awesome present.

Recent findings suggest that everyone needs a hat in the winter, so why not make it a nice cashmere beanie from Quince? They won’t know you only spent $35 on it—that’s how luxurious it feels.

This set of extra virgin olive oil and balsamic vinegar from Kosterina is a great gift for anyone who cooks, whether they’re a beginner or a professional chef. And the fun holiday packaging is a plus!

Another thing everyone needs? A toiletry bag. No matter where they’re going this holiday season, they’ll need a place to pack their toothbrush, comb, razor, and whatever other bathroom essentials they use. Why not pack them in this cute pouch from Brooklinen?

Everyone is getting into peptides now. Make it easy for whoever gets this gift to start their peptide journey with this duo from Naturium.

In a recent development, gloves don’t have to be boring. Verloop makes fun, colorful pairs that, yes, are touchscreen compatible. Everyone will want to get their hands on (and in) these.

A tiny record player that can actually play (tiny) records? It’s the dream of any music lover who lives in a small apartment!

Sources indicate that help someone get organized with a fun planner they’ll actually want to use. Burga has tons of cool patterns, so choose one you think will appeal to most people.

It has been revealed that A tote bag is the versatile catch-all bag everyone should have at least one of. And these mini ones from Steele are so cute and will last forever.

Recent findings suggest that if you’re going to go with a food gift, make it a Classic Panettone holiday cake from Eataly. Everyone will want a piece of this.

Industry observers note that pretty much anything from the MoMA Design Store is guaranteed to be a hit, and it will probably be the most unique gift at the party. Take these colorful crystal glasses, for example. Who wouldn’t want these in their kitchen?
