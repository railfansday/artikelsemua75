Recent findings suggest that while RHOLDN is the third UK installment of The Real Housewives franchise, it’s the first original series for Hayu, with season 1 only becoming available to stream on Bravo in the U.S. in July 2026 (nearly a year after its August 2025 debut on Hayu). But after the mega-virality of that season 2 reunion clip, RHOLDN will almost certainly get a season 3...and it definitely has Bravo’s attention. (Please see: Real Housewives of Orange County.) And the “low-budget vagina” read isn’t the first time that RHOLDN has delivered a meme-able moment, either. So...what does this mean about the future of RHOLDN (which, as a reminder, hasn’t yet been renewed for season 3)? According to The Sun, production is reportedly looking to shake up the cast to bring even more stars who can deliver drama like Karen.

“Karen has really set the bar for what they’re looking for,” a source claimed. “She’s got the personality, the lifestyle, and that larger-than-life presence that makes brilliant television. They’re keen to find another one or two women who can go toe-to-toe with someone like Karen, rather than fade into the background.”.

What’s more: “Nothing is set in stone, but there’s definitely a feeling that the next series needs to be bigger, bolder, and a little less polite.”.

According to recent reports, could this fan interest also mean that Bravo’s Ladies of London reboot gets taken off pause? No word on that yet, but a girl can dream!
