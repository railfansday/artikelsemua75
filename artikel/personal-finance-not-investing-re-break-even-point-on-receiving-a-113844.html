Sources indicate that for someone who anticipates earning the maximum earned income amount each year that is taxable by Social Security for their 35 highest earning years, what is the general break even point after collecting benefits at age 67 at which one will receive back in benefits what they paid-in in taxes for those 35 highest earning years?My actual experience - about 4 years.It is fairly easy to calculate this for your own Social security statement. Take the Total Social Security taxes you paid divided by your Personalized Monthly Retirement Benefit Estimate. Mine was surprisingly short - just over 3 years.
