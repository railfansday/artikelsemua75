While many Yellowjackets fans are struggling to cope with the inevitable end, for its series creators, it was the right time to land the plane.

Discussing the fourth and final season with Deadline, Ashley Lyle and Bart Nickerson explained that they didn’t want to “run the risk of overstaying our welcome” with another season when they were reaching the conclusion “more rapidly.”.

Recent findings suggest that “We’d always said that we had pitched a five-season proposal, but we’d always given the caveat that we felt like the show would tell us when it wanted to end, and just in the breaking of this story, it started to feel like we were sort of moving towards the end more rapidly,” explained Nickerson. “And so, then the decision kind of became, ‘OK, do we sort of squeeze a lot into Season 4 or run the risk of overstaying our welcome out of the fifth season?’ We chose to try to squeeze it in, and that ended up really just kind of working out nicely from our perspective.

“The show has always had to be very kind of efficient, with the storytelling. It’s a double ensemble with a lot of story that we’re telling, and so I think we’ve gotten pretty good or comfortable kind of operating a at a certain pace and a certain kind of grammar, for lack of a better word, and so that was what we did,” he added.
