Joe Locke claims he was totally unaware of the surprise post-credits scene at the end of VisionQuest... because it didn't come with his screeners.

It has been revealed that "I didn't know Wanda was in it," Locke said on The View. "I watched the episodes a few months ago because they sent it to me. And then the day that episode came out, I saw everyone online talking about Wanda being back, and I'm like, 'Well, that's not true!' And then I watched and was like, 'Oh, okay, it is true!'".

Don't worry, Joe: It didn't come with our screeners, either. Ruiardh Mollica, who plays Tommy Maximoff in the show, also expressed on his personal Instagram story that he, too, was unaware of the post-credits scene. Either it was a last-minute decision, or Marvel decided to keep it a massive secret for a reason. Either way, the inclusion of the scene sent ticket pre-sales for Avengers: Doomsday sky-high, and made perfect sense for the 'family reunion' aspect of VisionQuest season 1, so it all worked out for the best.
