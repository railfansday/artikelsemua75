According to recent reports, battlestar Galactica really does seem to be coming back to the fore after a number of years in stasis, where fans were serviced with very few true worthwhile adaptations. While things remain reasonably quiet on the television and film front, things are really popping off elsewhere, particularly in physical releases and the world of video games.

In a recent development, master Replicas are continuing to produce statues of some of the more iconic ships in franchise history, and they have looks for plenty more besides. They've even produced renditions of ships that have previously been unfilmed, like the Cerberus, expanding the lore of the series in the process. As for our medium of choice, there have also been recent video game releases that are set within the IP, including one in particular which has received solid feedback from fans to date.

Industry observers note that battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes is a survival fleet-management roguelite that features procedural narrative design and real-time space battles. It was released in May of this year, and it essentially tasks the player with making crucial decisions to keep their crew and ship going, all while pursued by the Cylon Fleet. The ultimate goal is to reconvene with the Battlestar Galactica, and it was very well-reviewed by critics when it debuted. (Related: Tech Report: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons i...)

Happily, though the most recent reviews are more mixed, the general feedback on the game is positive from both longtime fans of the franchise, and those who were going into the experience blind.

"A very enjoyable fight against the inevitable roguelike. While I completed my first run and am a little worried about the meta progression making later runs too easy, I had a lot of fun. For much of the run I felt constantly like I was going to fail, and hit the exact vibe you want in a game like this of just eeking out a win," one review read.

While another added, "This is an impressive RTS with pause that covers both fleet management, missions, and combat. The tutorial is gripping, and stressful, and feels like a real mission rather than contrived situations to teach controls. I loved every minute of it, and this is a far more ‘realistic’ and enjoyable Battlestar Galactica game than some of the disastrous predecessors.".

According to recent reports, "A great roguelite that comes close to scratching that FTL itch! It’s challenging and occasionally repetitive, but rewarding, with new things to discover as you progress. What really stands out is the excellent BSG fan service. I love how the game interprets the Battlestar Galactica universe and captures its atmosphere, with plenty of little details for fans to appreciate. Highly recommended, especially for BSG fans!" a third said.

It has been revealed that battlestar Galactica: Scattered Hopes is out now and there's a free demo you can try out if you're not quite sold on making the full purchase just from the written pitch. The complete game has a standard base price of $24.99 USD, though it has been the subject of regular sales that take 50% off that price or more, likely making it well worth the outlay in the process.
