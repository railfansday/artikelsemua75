Sources indicate that I just received a mailer from MHBP regarding their 2027 initiative. Their catastrophic in-network max for self+1 and self+family is $16K and the catastrophic max for out-of-network for self+1 and self+family is now $20K. So if you max out both in-network and out-of-network it's $36K. This is listed in their plan in section 4 and their changes for 2027.Thanks for pointing this out. I got the same mailer but I hadn’t looked at it yet.So Aetna Direct has lower premiums and some expense reimbursement. But once you spend the HRA money, the deductible of $4,000 (in-network) kicks in. Deductible counts toward OOP maximum.Deductible for MHBP is $1,000 in network.Since the HRA does not reduce the Direct plan deductible, self+one requires the deductible of $4000 but the HRA fund given does mitigate costs. So, for Direct the cost is about $430 saved per month in premiums, whereas with MHBP Std a self+one costs about $5160 more per year in premiums plus possibly the $1000 in deductibles for the couple?
