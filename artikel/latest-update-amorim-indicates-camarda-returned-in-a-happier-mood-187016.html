During his press conference earlier today, Ruben Amorim spoke about Francesco Camarda and noted that the youngster appeared much happier after returning from international duty.

It hasn’t been an easy season for Camarda so far, getting a mere 119 minutes on the pitch, after initially believing that he would get a lot more. Luckily, he managed to boost his confidence during the international break, scoring for Italy’s U21 side against France.

In a recent development, he could perhaps use that to turn a corner, though everything depends on how much playing time Ruben Amorim is willing to give him. The feeling is that there isn’t loads up for grabs, which is why the loan rumours are heating up too.

During the press conference ahead of Milan-Frosinone earlier this afternoon, Amorim was asked about Camarda and what the next steps are for him. He confirmed that everything is up in the air, but also said that the youngster is now in a much better mood.

“I was a young player myself once too. A young player wants everything really badly, which is a good thing. That noted, he needs time to adapt, it’s really hard to play for a club like Milan. You just said there is pressure on Ramos, imagine what it’s like for Camarda.

“I don’t know what will happen in January, but he needs to be focused on these games. Everything can change in just a few weeks.

“I’m really happy with how he has been since he returned from international duty, I heard that Baldini spoke with him at length, and he returned in a happier mood. I understand the question about January, but January is so far away right now,” he stated.

It seems Silvio Baldini really did do wonders with his man-to-man talk with Camarda during the break.
