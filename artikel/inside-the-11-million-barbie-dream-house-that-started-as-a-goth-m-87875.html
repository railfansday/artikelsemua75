A real-life Barbie dream house is turning heads in Hudson, WI, where an eye-catching pink Victorian has gone under contract after hitting the market for $1.1 million in June 2026.

With its pink walkway, bubble gum–colored staircase, and vibrant interior, this seven-bedroom, 5,189-square-foot home is anything but ordinary.

But behind its playful Barbie-inspired appearance is a spacious historical residence built in 1900 that is packed with character, original details, and incredible versatility.

In a recent development, “An everyday buyer interested in a classic Victorian home might think this is a bit much,” says listing agent Jennifer R. Martin of Edina Realty Inc. “It has a lot of great qualities. I would say to anyone not to let the color deter you. Painting is easy, and it’s not that expensive to make changes.”.

It has been revealed that right now a successful short-term rental, the home is near the St. Croix River and could be used for multigenerational living or converted into a single-family home. (Related: Obsessed fan breaks into YouTuber’s home demanding marr...)

“The sizing alone of the house is incredible,” Martin states. “It’s enormous and goes on forever. It has two separate kitchens, so it could also be turned into a duplex.”. (Related: Tech Report: Animal control called to rescue alligator ...)

It is part of a collection of rose-hued homes operated by Fleetwood Rentals, which hosts everything from birthday parties to bachelorette weekends.

Recent findings suggest that “The seller’s clientele is looking for a party weekend destination,” she notes. “It’s one block from the riverfront and two blocks away from downtown.”.

It has been revealed that located 30 minutes from the Minneapolis/St. Paul airport, the property last changed hands for $614,000 in 2020. It offers “outstanding income potential,” the listing states.

“When the seller bought it, right away she had it painted in dark charcoal,” Martin indicates. “Her vision at that time was to have a Goth mansion that would be similar to Disney’s Haunted Mansion. It was right around the time the 'Barbie' movie was coming out, so she decided to take advantage of that.”.

The multilevel home also has formal and casual dining spaces and expansive gathering areas. Original details include woodwork, built-in cabinetry, vaulted ceilings, and dual staircases.

"I envisioned the next buyer to be either another Airbnb owner or a large family who would un-pinkify it," Martin says with a laugh. "It's a great house with a lot of great qualities. I told potential buyers not to let the color deter you.".

Recent findings suggest that the living room has pink walls, drapes, and couches. The formal dining room has built-in cabinetry and a fireplace. The light-filled kitchen, with an enormous breakfast bar, is a surprise with its white cabinetry and a copper farmhouse sink.

The bubble gum–painted grand staircase leads to the colorful sleeping quarters. Four bedrooms are on the second level and three more are on the third floor. The primary suite has a walk-in closet, a soaking tub, a separate glass shower, and a dual-sink vanity.

Industry observers note that other highlights include a home theater, a hot tub, a 14-foot swim spa, and a patio designed for entertaining. The fenced lot comes with a private patio, a two-car garage, and a driveway with parking for 12 vehicles.

The property is conveniently located near boutique shopping, riverfront parks, and the marina.

Industry observers note that "Hudson is a riverfront town with tons of history," Martin claims. "People love to come here for bachelorette parties and places to go out to dinner. It also has a lot of charming, fun shops.".
