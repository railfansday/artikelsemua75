📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that in 1973, The Rolling Stones were coming off one of the greatest album runs in rock and roll history, a four-album stretch that cemented them as the World’s Greatest Rock and Roll Band, culminating in 1972’s Exile on Main Street and its legendarily hedonistic accompanying tour. Rather than take time off to victory lap following this iconic period, they headed to Jamaica to record their third album of the ’70s, released in 1973 as Goats Head Soup — which topped the Billboard 200 and spawned a Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 single with breakup ballad “Angie,” but drew middling lukewarm and is now viewed by many fans as the beginning of the end of the band’s glory days.
