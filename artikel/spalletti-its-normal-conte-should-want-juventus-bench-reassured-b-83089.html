Spalletti: ‘It’s normal Conte should want Juventus bench, reassured by McKennie’

Luciano Spalletti shrugged off Juventus controversy around Weston McKennie and Manchester United, Antonio Conte and his Venezia history. ‘I find it absolutely normal Conte should want this bench.’.

It kicks off at the Allianz Juventus Stadium in Turin on Sunday at 11.30 GMT (12.30 CET).

The Bianconeri injury crisis continues to drag on, with Pierre Kalulu, Kenan Yildiz, Nico Gonzalez, Manuel Locatelli, Khephren Thuram and Kamil Grabara out of action.

Zeki Celik, Francisco Conceicao, Edon Zhegrova and Andrea Cambiaso have shaken off knocks and are available.

“We will evaluate who can start, Conceicao needs a couple of shoves before he can feel good, because that’s always the case after an injury like that, you need to realise that you don’t feel pain anymore,” said Spalletti in his press conference.

Nick Woltemade has impressed recently when working in partnership with Randal Kolo Muani, with some reports Juventus could even try to keep him beyond the current loan from Newcastle United.

“He is pretty energetic in tight spaces, as he can find his way out of trouble. You might assume his physical strength was a greater priority, but recently he has shown an improvement in his practicality. He can handle duels well.”.

Wes McKennie made some comments on international duty with Mauricio Pochettino’s USMNT suggesting he wasn’t surprised by transfer speculation linking him with Manchester United.

In a recent development, “We spoke as soon as that quote emerged, but I don’t think he was alluding to the possibility of leaving, it was just a general look at all players. I have a great rapport with him, when he returned to Turin, he immediately came to speak with me, and everything is fine.”. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

Although the results did begin to improve towards the break for international duty, the reports continue to swirl that Spalletti’s position on the Juventus bench is not safe. (Related: Twitch streamer’s PS5 catches fire after they pause GTA...)

The situation was not helped by Antonio Conte and France boss Zinedine Zidane confessing they coveted his job.

“I find it absolutely normal that a free agent like Conte should want to sit on an important bench like Italy, Milan, Manchester United or Juventus, so I really don’t understand the reason for all this noise,” shrugged Spalletti.

“When I was between jobs, I would go to watch games and training sessions to learn new things, I studied to find solutions for my next role. I did it with total respect for my present and future, then waited for the right moment to return.”.

There is certainly a long career behind him, as tomorrow will be the 600th Serie A match in his career.

“I am preparing with the same emotion as I did for my first in Serie A. There were moments during this journey that marked my career as a coach. I’m very happy to have had this tour in football, because it was my life and what I always wished for.

“I learned to be happy even when things are not going in my favour, and I hope to keep going for as long as possible.”.

Juventus missed out on Champions League football last season and run the genuine risk of doing it again this term after a rough start, so do they have time to catch up?

“I do think we have that possibility, but our objective is to become something,” replied the coach.

“If we do that, then we can get good results and a strong position in the table. We have to rely on our work, we want to create habits for constant improvement.”.

Among the worst experiences of his coaching career was Venezia, when Spalletti was fired twice in one season.

“I have fond memories of a melancholic but splendid city. Those were my first seasons, I had strong Presidential and directorial figures, the team was struggling, so I was fired, and brought back.

“I want to congratulate Davide Nicola, as wherever he goes in his career, he brings a solution to problems and fuels enthusiasm. He plays a very vertical style of football, and is starting to pick up points. They’ll try to strangle us and then send strong strikers on the attack like Akor Adams, so balance is essential,” concluded Spalletti.
