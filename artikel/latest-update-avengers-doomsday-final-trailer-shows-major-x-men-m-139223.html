The final trailer for Avengers: Doomsday is here.

In perhaps the best look yet at the upcoming Marvel superhero mashup (below), the footage is largely focused on Captain America (Chris Evans) and his return from domestic bliss with Peggy Carter (Hayley Atwell) into trying to save the world once again.

There’s also some upcoming footage of Doctor Doom (Robert Downey Jr.) as he tells the heroes, “All of you have lived stolen lives, now you must give them back.”.

While Thor (Chris Hemsworth) is urging the heroes to “put aside your petty squabbles” while admitting, “I don’t even know who half of you are.” Related Stories Movies Bassam Tariq Indicates Marvel's Scrapped 'Blade' Reboot Taught Him "Life's Too Short to Make Sh** Films" Hollywood Flashback When Kevin Feige Got Off to a Less-Than-Incredible Start at Marvel.

But the moment fans are buzzing about most has Cyclops (James Marsden) assembling the X-Men — in a scene that recalls the famous circle-up shot in 2012’s The Avengers — and declaring, “To me, my X-Men.”.

According to recent reports, the film from directors Joe Russo and Anthony Russo is set to hit theaters Dec. 18.

The film has a sprawling cast, ranging from stars of the original 2000s X-Men movies such as Patrick Stewart and Ian McKellen, to Black Panther‘s Letitia Wright and Winston Duke, to more recent MCU additions, such as Shang-Chi‘s Simu Liu and Fantastic Four: First Steps‘ Pedro Pascal, Vanessa Kirby, Ebon Moss-Bachrach and Joseph Quinn.

There’s also Captain America actor Anthony Mackie and Ant-Man star Paul Rudd, in addition to Thunderbolts players Sebastian Stan, Florence Pugh, Wyatt Russell and David Harbour.
