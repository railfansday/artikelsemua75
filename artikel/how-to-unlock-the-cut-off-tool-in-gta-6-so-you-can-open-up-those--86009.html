Have you noticed that there's these yellow cargo containers all over Leonida? Did you know that you can actually get inside of them to get rare weapons, explosives, vehicles, and so much more! It's yet another one of those hidden aspects of GTA 6 that you can't help but go out of your way to find more of, once you know to start looking.

Recent findings suggest that if you want to break into yellow Scoop containers in GTA 6, you're going to need to get a vital piece of equipment first: the Cut-Off Tool. Below I'm going to walk you through how to unlock the GTA 6 Cut-Off Tool and explain how it can be used to break into those Scoop containers.

According to recent reports, the Cut-Off Tool is one of the main pieces of GTA 6 equipment that you'll unlock as you play through the story. While you'll likely come across those yellow Scoop containers in your travels, you won't be able to open any of them until you've played a key GTA 6 mission in Chapter 3. Meet the Heders is the third mission of that chapter, and it'll send you down to a dockyard. Once you're these, you'll automatically be assigned the equipment and can use it freely outside of the mission. (Related: These are the Senate Democrats backing Schumer and Van ...)

You need to locate yellow Scoop shipping containers. These can be in the most unlikely of places – on random beaches, next to gang hideouts, in random parking lots – all across Leonida. Once you've located one, walk up to its front doors and press Triangle/Y. This will prompt Jason or Lucia to equip the Cut-Off Tool. You'll need to squeeze R2/RT and keep the power within the pink segment of the semi-circle for a short while until you cut through the lock.
