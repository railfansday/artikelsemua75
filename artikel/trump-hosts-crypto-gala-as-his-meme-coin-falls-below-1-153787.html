U.S. President Donald Trump on Sunday hosted his third cryptocurrency gala for top holders of his meme coin, $TRUMP, which has plummeted to below $1 a coin after reaching a high of $75 the day before Trump's second inauguration, on Jan. 19, 2025.

The dinner, held at the Trump National golf club in Sterling, Virginia, comes as some Democrats say they will investigate potential conflicts of interest and accuse Trump of using the presidency to boost his personal wealth. (Related: Trump keeps losing in court. Here’s how he gets what he...)

At Sunday's dinner, Trump maintained that his support for cryptocurrencies was to boost American economic competitiveness.

Recent findings suggest that "I'm doing it for the United States ... If we don't do it, China's going to do it," he told attendees, The Guardian reported. The event also featured speeches from boxing champion Floyd Mayweather, "Shark Tank" investor Kevin O'Leary and NBA champion Tristan Thompson.

A total of 185 "wallets" met the criteria for entry to the gala, although the leaderboard on the $TRUMP coin club website listed just 130 wallets. The wallets listed on the leaderboard held $8.6 million in $TRUMP tokens, a sharp decline from the May 2025 gala, which featured 220 attendees holding a combined $148 million. Crypto billionaire Justin Sun's wallet alone held $18.5 million at the 2025 event — more than the combined holdings of this year's attendees. It has been revealed that eligibility was based on time-weighted holdings of Trump's meme coins from Sept. 30 to Nov. 16, according to the organizer's website. The top 29 holders were invited to a VIP reception with the president, with the top four receiving an 18-karat, Trump-branded gold watch.

More than half of the wallets that qualified for the event were linked to crypto exchanges that bar U.S.-based people, according to data gathered by consumer advocacy group Public Citizen for Reuters, suggesting they belong to foreign nationals.

The $TRUMP token has earned the Trump family at least $635 million, according to the president's June financial disclosure. Including the meme coins, the Trump family has earned $2.3 billion from its four main crypto-related businesses since the president's second term began, through to April of this year, according to a Reuters report.

Industry observers note that this is despite the ventures' falling value, which, according to the report, they invest little to no money in. Trump's stake in a fifth crypto-linked venture, Trump Media & Technology Group, is worth about $430 million as of Friday's close.

According to recent reports, bitcoin climbed back to over $61,000 on Monday in line with the U.S. stock futures bounce, after an AI-related stock sell-off spilled over into other riskier assets last week.

Bitcoin fell as low as $55,130 on Saturday, its lowest level since September 2024. The sell-off wiped out $1.8 billion in leveraged crypto positions, largely from bets on prices rising. Leveraged buyers were forced out as prices fell, then returned after the price stabilized.

Industry observers note that the world's largest cryptocurrency is still trading at less than half of its October 2025 value. U.S. spot bitcoin ETFs lost $1.24 billion last week, their third week of outflows, according to SoSo Value data cited by FxPro.
