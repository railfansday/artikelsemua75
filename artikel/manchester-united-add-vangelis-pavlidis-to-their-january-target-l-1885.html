Manchester United have included Benfica marksman Vangelis Pavlidis on their list of January targets, according to a report from CaughtOffside.

While Mauricio Pochettino might have only been appointed as Michael Carrick’s replacement last week, the newly introduced Man United boss is expected to be heavily backed during the January transfer window.

Alongside facing a major defensive reset at Old Trafford, Pochettino is reportedly eager to bring in attacking reinforcements.

With Slovenian international Benjamin Sesko ruled out for the remainder of 2026 due to an extended shin injury, CaughtOffside are suggesting that the Manchester United hierarchy are monitoring Pavlidis.

Since completing a permanent switch to the Portuguese giants back in the summer of 2024, Pavlidis has embarked on a record-breaking run and has underlined his status as one of Europe’s most clinical number nines. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Leading the Primeira Liga Golden Boot chase, the former AZ Alkmaar striker has opened up the 2026/27 season by registering 11 goals in as many domestic appearances.

While the Benfica hierarchy might have made it clear that they have no interest in cashing in on the 27-year-old during the January transfer window, CaughtOffside are reporting that United have placed Pavlidis on their shortlist of striking targets.

Marking their return from November’s international break this weekend astonishingly sat in the Premier League’s drop zone, Pochettino’s tenure at Old Trafford will officially get underway on Sunday afternoon when they travel to historic rivals Liverpool.
