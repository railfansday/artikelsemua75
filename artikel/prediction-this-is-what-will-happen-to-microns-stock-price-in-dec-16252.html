Recent findings suggest that micron Technology (MU -12.23%) has made investors significantly richer in 2026, with shares of the memory specialist up 123% as of this writing. However, the stock has run into rough weather in the second half of the year.

According to recent reports, micron stock has lost 32% of its value since the beginning of July. Micron has been affected by concerns about rising competition from China and a pullback in artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure spending due to higher interest rates, which could constrain funding. That's why Micron investors will now be eagerly awaiting Micron's fiscal 2027 first-quarter report, which is expected in the second half of December. I will take a closer look at the state of the memory market to determine whether this high-flying AI stock can regain its mojo.

According to recent reports, market research firm Counterpoint Research notes that the memory supercycle continued in the third quarter of 2026. The dynamic random-access memory (DRAM) market's revenue increased by an incredible 5x over the prior-year period to $200 billion. A massive price increase drove this phenomenal jump, while bit shipments increased by only 4% year over year. (Related: How Much Does a Will Cost? Complete Price Guide...)

Counterpoint points out that Micron controlled 21% of the DRAM market last quarter. So, Micron's DRAM revenue in calendar Q3 should be $42 billion, based on Counterpoint's analysis. It is worth noting that Micron's fiscal Q1 ends in early December, so calendar Q3 overlaps with two months of its fiscal Q1.

Sources indicate that but even if that's the case, Micron's DRAM business is on track to surge by a significant margin over the $10.8 billion in revenue it generated from this segment in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Micron has called for $61.5 billion in revenue for the current quarter, implying a 4.5x increase over last year. The tech giant derives nearly three-fourths of its revenue from the DRAM segment, suggesting it is on track to exceed $40 billion in DRAM revenue.

Also, the manufacturer is forecasting an increase of almost 8x in earnings per share from the year-ago period. Memory prices are still growing exponentially, as the limited supply of these chips forces buyers to compete for available capacity, thereby putting Micron in a position to command higher prices. As a result, the company should be able to achieve the phenomenal increase in earnings per share (EPS) that it expects.

Industry observers note that however, a lot will depend on the firm's guidance for the stock to accelerate again. There is good news for investors on this front as well.

Counterpoint Research expects memory prices to remain strong in 2027 as well. In fact, it isn't the only source that's expecting a bump in memory prices next year. TrendForce, for instance, is forecasting a 70%-140% increase in the price of AI-specific high-bandwidth memory (HBM) next year. The firm estimates that spending on memory and storage chips will account for 68% of the capital expenditure of the top nine cloud service providers (CSPs) globally, up from 47% this year.

According to recent reports, specifically, TrendForce expects a 50% increase in the capex of the top nine CSPs in 2027 to $1.38 trillion. That means memory and NAND flash storage revenue could hit $940 billion next year, up from an estimated $433 billion in 2026. As Micron is among the major manufacturers of DRAM and NAND flash, it seems well-placed to sustain healthy revenue and earnings growth next year.

Sources indicate that in fact, as the memory market is poised to more than double in 2027, there is a chance of the organization exceeding the 133% EPS increase to $176.50 that analysts are expecting for fiscal 2027. However, even that is a sizable jump, warranting a premium valuation for this semiconductor stock, which is trading at just 10 times earnings.

So, there are several signs suggesting that Micron stock could surge in December. The stock is extremely cheap, on track to deliver exponential growth, and could outpace Wall Street's expectations. All these factors could boost investor confidence in the stock and send it flying next month.
