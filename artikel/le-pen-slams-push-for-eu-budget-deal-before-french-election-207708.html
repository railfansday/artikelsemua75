Le Pen slams push for EU budget deal before French election

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (84 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that EU leaders locked horns over the bloc's budget at a summit meeting in Brussels on Thursday, as far-right leader Marine Le Pen slammed an "underhanded" push to lock in the long-term spending looks before French presidential elections next year. European Council head Antonio Costa turned up the pressure upon arrival at the budget talks, where heavyweight Germany is pressing for cuts as well as burden-sharing on money for Ukraine. FRANCE 24's Dave Keating reports from Brussels.
