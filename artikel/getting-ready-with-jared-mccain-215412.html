📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Recent findings suggest that jared McCain loves a routine—several routines, actually, which is why there’s a whiteboard in his bathroom, covered with a bulleted list of his daily to-dos (journaling, affirmations, bone broth, and lymphatic jumps, among them). That’s all separate from the 22-year-old’s bedtime and wake-up regimens (silk eye mask, silk pillowcase, silk bonnet, magnesium, and mouth tape, then a multistep skincare process, respectively). And this doesn’t even include many of the things his 7.7 million social media followers have come to love: Jared hanging out in de-puffing eye patches. Jared wandering around wearing Gen Z’s favorite pimple patches. Jared flashing his latest bright-colored manicure.

As he moves through his Oklahoma City home, where he lives with three of his best friends, it’s clear that none of the above is purely performative, as is so often the case with internet celebrities. “I do a lot of skincare because I sweat a lot,” he says as he works his way through what looks like the entire Hailey Bieber–founded Rhode skincare line. “I really like to take care of my face.”. (Related: Deep Learning over the Internet: Training Language Mode...)

In a recent development, click through the videos on his TikTok or Instagram page and you’ll likely see that face illuminated by LED lights. “One of my favorite parts about my house is my red light showerhead,” he notes. “Red light just creates this vibe and gives me health benefits. It’s number one in my life.” It’s also on his whiteboard three times.

According to recent reports, jared first broke into virality as a starting guard on Duke University’s basketball team. And while his talent on the court would lead him to the NBA—he now plays for the Oklahoma City Thunder—it was something else that captured the wider world’s attention. “I started painting my nails during COVID, and that’s when I also started on TikTok,” he explains. “I played with the polish on and I had a really good game, so I thought, Hold on, this might be the upcoming thing.” (He now has brand partnerships with Sally Hansen and Ulta Beauty.). (Related: Understanding BigBird's Block Sparse Attention...)

Sources indicate that “I didn’t really care what other people thought about it,” he indicates of any negative feedback he got at the time. He now keeps manicure inspiration cataloged on a Pinterest board—“most of the time, I’m feeling colorful; I love bright, bright colors”—and his Oklahoma City–based nail artist Yvie McCrary often sends him ideas to prep for his next appointment.

In a recent development, “In the NBA, there can be a lot of scrutiny, so I try to take care of my mental health,” he adds. In his downstairs “energy room,” he flicks on an LED panel (of course) for his daily yoga flow. “I love finding peace,” he claims. “Meditation allows me to assess what’s going on in my life.” It’s yet another habit he uses to work through any unfavorable comments or haters he comes across: “It’s funny at this point. I’ve gotten used to it by now. It’s a blessing to even be talked about.”.

Not that everything Jared does is 100 percent zen. When he’s not indulging in self-betterment practices, he’s posting TikTok dances and lip syncs from the Thunder’s locker room, sometimes with special appearances from his teammates.

According to recent reports, “Entertaining has always been so fun to me,” he states. “That’s kind of what basketball is, too. Growing up, I thought I was going to be on the Disney Channel or a pop star. I always end up singing some Olivia Dean.” His plea for the singer to bring him onstage during her Art of Loving Tour stop at Madison Square Garden in Newly introduced York City got nearly 2 million views (sadly, she did not invite him up onstage).

He releases into a rendition of Olivia’s “A Couple Minutes” with surprisingly impressive vocals. “The song could make me cry right now,” he confesses. One of his roommates later jokes, “We need a mute button”—something Jared’s fans have never asked for and likely never will.
