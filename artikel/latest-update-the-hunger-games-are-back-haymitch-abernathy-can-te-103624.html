Haymitch Abernathy: Reluctant leader archetype Haymitch Abernathy, played by Woody Harrelson in the original films and Joseph Zada in Sunrise, is an unconventional leader. He would rather be drinking away his sorrows than changing the fate of Panem—but sometimes management is thrust upon us. Abernathy excels at strategic planning. In the original books and movies, he is able to guide Peeta and Katniss through the games two times while organizing a rebellion right under the Capitol’s nose. This comes through very careful planning and deception. In the real world, however, business leaders need to be able to communicate a clear vision and how to execute it. Abernathy is rough around the edges—and his tough exterior allows him to work with a diverse range of difficult colleagues to get results. You could call him a people-first strategist. In addition, Abernathy got the vain people of the Capitol to empathize with Katniss and Peeta and send them lifesaving supplies like medicine and water.
