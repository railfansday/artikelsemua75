Despite Tony Mowbray stepping back from his role as head coach of Blackburn Rovers, the side has maintained form with four points in their last two games, including a 1-0 win over Watford on Tuesday night. The points have the East Lancashire side looking up rather than down, sitting in 12th and only two points off the Championship play-offs.

It has been revealed that club captain Todd Cantwell has stepped up impressively for Rovers, netting in each of the last two games to lead by example in a challenging time at Ewood Park. It’s not been easy for the 28-year-old over the last few years, but he is really showing his worth in his third season at the club.

Nathan Jones‘ Charlton Athletic returned to winning ways as they dismantled Lincoln City 4-0, a result that saw both Chris Cohen and Tom Shaw lose their jobs. There’s no hiding that the Addicks will be in and around the Championship relegation fight this season, so beating those sides that will also be down there is of the utmost importance.

Sources indicate that the victory displayed just what the Addicks can do, and they will be eager to produce a similar performance this weekend.
