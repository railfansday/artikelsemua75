The 21-year-old responsible for hacking Rockstar Games and leaking in-development GTA 6 footage in 2022 has pleaded guilty to a number of offenses.

It has been revealed that the GTA 6 leaker was initially sentenced to an indefinite stay at a hospital due to his severe autism, but was more recently deemed fit for trial and moved to a regular prison earlier this January. On Monday, at the Woolwich Crown Court in South East London, he pleaded guilty to hacking Rockstar Games and a number of other companies, including Uber and EE/BT, in cyber attacks estimated to have caused £10m in damages.

According to recent reports, at the time of his Rockstar hack - when he was between the ages of 16 and 17 - the leaker was already under police protection at a Travelodge hotel to keep him from rival hackers he had allegedly stolen Bitcoin from. Somewhat surprisingly, he then managed to hack the multi-billion dollar game maker with nothing but an Amazon Fire Stick, the hotel room's TV and a bluetooth-connected keyboard and mouse. Industry observers note that as the BBC reports, he also managed to break into the developer's Slack channels, before threatening to release the GTA 6 source code if Rockstar failed to contact him within the day. The leaker has yet to be sentenced. (Related: Industry News: Retrieval Augmented Generation with Hugg...)

Those 2022 leaks were quite substantial, too, revealing about an hour of unfinished gameplay and clueing the world in on Lucia's existence - the series' first female protagonist in years. Of course, those 2022 leaks shouldn't be confused with 2026's, which saw Cyberleek spill nuggets of nearly finished clips onto the internet, despite Rockstar's best efforts to keep GTA 6 a closely guarded secret.

Recent findings suggest that "We recently suffered a network intrusion in which an unauthorised third-party illegally accessed and downloaded confidential information from our systems, including early development footage for the next Grand Theft Auto," Rockstar mentioned at the time, before it had officially unveiled GTA 6. "At this time we do not anticipate any disruption to our live game services, nor any long-term effect on the development of our ongoing projects. We are extremely disappointed to have any details of our next game shared with you all in this way.".

Four years later, GTA 6 is finally around the corner - you're probably already aware considering the marketing frenzy Rockstar's been drumming up.
