You're probably describing at least half of the horror genre when you list Silent Hill and Fatal Frame as two of your main inspirations, but Ugly Within has cut through the crowded space with a reveal at The Horror Game Awards 2026.

It is in development for PS5, Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, with no release window confirmed yet. Catch it in action in the trailer above.

It has been revealed that you play as two characters: Shu, a boy who can see into the past, and Reiko, an exorcist. It sounds like you'll be looking into a family history and confronting the ghost that has haunted it. Shu is a more stealthy character who avoids the supernatural and solves puzzles, while Reiko confronts the ghosts and exorcises them.

Industry observers note that shu and Reiko face the horrors of the Kamata estate in very different ways. Shu survives by staying unseen, moving through the house in silence and piecing together its secrets through visions of what once happened within its walls. Reiko meets the darkness head-on, confronting the entities that haunt the estate and driving them out through exorcism. But Shu's gift comes with a cruel limit: his visions reveal what happened to the people who came before, yet nothing he sees can be undone.
