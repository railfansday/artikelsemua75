📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After years of dominating Netflix's top 10 list, Squid Game finally came to an end last year with its third and final season. But the story may not be over after all, as series creator Hwang Dong-hyuk says he might one day return to the franchise.

In a recent development, "As far as I’m concerned, someday I’ll get back to Squid Game," stated Hwang at the Doha Film Festival (Variety). "When I have a great idea, I’ll make another one." However, Hwang pointed out that he doesn't actually own Squid Game. "Unfortunately, it’s not mine… Netflix owns the IP, so it’s not my decision how to expand.".

Following a desperate man named Seong Gi-hun, who, alongside 455 other players strapped for cash, enters a deadly game to win a hefty prize, the dystopian series swiftly became a hit over at Netflix. The first season debuted to a near-perfect Rotten Tomatoes score, and the show is still Netflix's most-watched series ever, per Tudum. (Related: Smart People Can Still Be Bad At Maths — These 3 Skills...)

The idea for the series came to Hwang when he faced financial troubles of his own. "When I first wrote the script, the beginning point of my creative process was when I was poor," he said. "I didn’t have much money and imagined: What if it was me? Would I enter this kind of competition and try to win the prize? I was so broke in 2009. I was so broke. I only had like $5 in my account.".

Industry observers note that however, after the huge success of the first season, Hwang noted he felt the pressure to make a second and third season just as good. "There was pressure from Netflix," added Hwang, "We were always getting questions: 'Where is Season 2? Season 3?' It was a nightmare.” However, Hwang said he never tries to "make something that people like," adding, "I always try to make what I like to make while at the same time hoping and praying people like what I like to make. If I lose that mindset, I’ll go crazy.". (Related: Industry News: Politics Takes A Toll On Your Health — W...)

Netflix already released the American reality show Squid Game: The Challenge based on the series. Soon after Squid Game season 3, the streamer also announced a US remake from Gone Girl director David Fincher, which was hinted at in the Squid Game season 3 ending featuring Cate Blanchett as the fresh recruiter. However, reports suggest that the show may now be dead.
