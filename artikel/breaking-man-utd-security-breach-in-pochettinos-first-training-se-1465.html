Sources indicate that the start of the Mauricio Pochettino era at Manchester United has not gotten off to the best start. Indeed, there was a huge ‘security breach’ during his first training session at Carrington.

It has been revealed that the Argentine was declared as Michael Carrick's replacement this week, but has a massive job on his hands from the very off. Indeed, the Red Devils travel to Anfield to face Liverpool in Pochettino's first game back in the Premier League.

Man Utd right now sit 18th in the league, winning just one game from their opening 10 before Carrick was sacked. As such, there is plenty of work to do on the training pitch if Pochettino wants to turn things around. (Related: Distributed Training: Train BART/T5 for Summarization u...)

Pochettino arrived on Wednesday, taking a tour of the training ground before later fulfilling club media obligations that evening. He's been so busy, in fact, the club have got permission for his first press conference to start 30 minutes late on Friday. (Related: Deep Learning over the Internet: Training Language Mode...)

Today, Thursday 19 November, the 54-year-old was able to take his first training session. However, in what has been described as an 'embarrassing security breach' by Daily Mail journalist Chris Wheeler, a drone captured was going on.

Around 12 minutes of the work at Carrington was shared online, allowing fans a detailed glimpse of what the newly introduced Man Utd boss has got his players working on. Indeed, the footage clearly indicates Pochettino and his coaching staff working with the players.

As reported by the Daily Mail, Manchester United have refused to comment on the matter.

The Red Devils are stated to regularly use their own drones to film training sessions, but pu up signs six years ago banning public use of them near the training ground, in case they could reveal top-secret information to their opponents.

The footage was posted on YouTube by an account called CC Overview, which insists that the airspace over Carrington is unrestricted and the drone was operating legally within Civil Aviation Authority rules.

It said that the video was taken half an hour into Pochettino’s training session and clearly shows the 54-year-old instructing the United squad, along with his coaching team of long-term assistant Jesus Perez, first-team coach Miguel D’Agostino, goalkeeper coach Toni Jimenez, his son and fitness coach Sebastiano Pochettino and conditioning coach Silvia Tuya.

Sources indicate that it's noteworthy that Tuya is involved. Her presence appears to have confirmed that she will be part of Pochettino's staff. This is a historic appointment as she becomes the first female to work within United’s senior backroom staff. She arrives having left her job with the United States men's national team.

Speaking to club media in his first interview, Pochettino explained how he aims to turn things around at Old Trafford, stating: "The culture is the most vital. Create the habits, the principles, and I think that is what we want to create.

"We need to [be] looking forward and to find a way to create the best habits and the culture that makes us to be close to winning every single game.

In a recent development, "The players need to express their talent. We need to create the environment and the platform for the players to perform. That is the most notable thing and that is our priority now – it's to work with them and try to take the pressure.".

This security breach is an inauspicious way to start things off for Pochettino. He'll hope it isn't a sign of more trouble to come.
