November's full moon is often called the Beaver Moon because it rises around the time the industrious animals are hard at work building dams and preparing for the winter months, according to the Old Farmer's Almanac.

It is also set to be a spectacular supermoon, which reaches peak illumination while within 90% of perigee — the closest point to Earth in the moon's 27-day elliptical orbit.
