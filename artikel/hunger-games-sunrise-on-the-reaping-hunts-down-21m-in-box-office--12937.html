The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is ready for battle in its opening weekend at the box office.

It has been revealed that the sixth film in the Lionsgate franchise grossed $21 million in Thursday previews before hitting 4,018 North American theaters on Friday. This gives it the second-best previews figure for the franchise, behind only the $25.2 million that 2013’s The Hunger Games: Catching Fire collected and besting the $19.7 million that the original Jennifer Lawrence-led film The Hunger Games earned when launching the franchise in 2012. The first Hunger Games went on to gross $152.5 million domestically in its debut frame, while Catching Fire holds the top-grossing launch in the series by opening with $158.1 million. Industry observers note that lionsgate had projected The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping to gross north of $80 million, but other sources expected that it would open much higher and potentially earn between $100 million and $130 million, which now appears the more likely outcome. Adapting author Suzanne Collins’ prequel novel that was published last year, director Francis Lawrence‘s movie carries a production budget of roughly $150 million as it revitalizes the franchise in telling a story set 24 years before the events of the original 2008 book. It has been revealed that in the new film, Joseph Zada stars Haymitch Abernathy, providing the backstory for the character whom Woody Harrelson brought to life in the first four movies. The cast for Sunrise on the Reaping also includes Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Jesse Plemons, Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin and Maya Hawke.

The Hunger Games franchise’s five films have surpassed $3.3 billion at the global box office, with the series coming off of its lowest-performing entry with 2023 prequel The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. Starring Tom Blyth, Rachel Zegler and Hunter Schafer, that one disappointed in opening to $44 million domestically before ending its run with $166.4 million domestically and a global sum of $361.8 million.

Sunrise on the Reaping holds a 67 critical score on Rotten Tomatoes as it lags behind the franchise’s first four movies but bests the 64 percent approval for Songbirds & Snakes. In his THR review, chief film critic David Rooney wrote that the new film “delivers on emotion and brutal spectacle.”.

The weekend’s competition includes Amazon MGM Studios’ I Play Rocky going wide after two frames in limited release for Peter Farrelly’s origin story of the 1976 Oscar-winning Rocky. Also fighting for attention spans this weekend is the highly anticipated video game Grand Theft Auto 6, which rolled out Thursday after a 13-year wait since the franchise’s last title.
