Franchise Business Review recognition reflects veteran owners' satisfaction with the print and marketing franchise.

Recent findings suggest that DENVER, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- AlphaGraphics, a leading franchisor of printing and marketing solutions, has been named one of Franchise Business Review's (FBR) 2026 Top Franchises for Veterans, earning a place among just 50 brands recognized based on independent franchise owner feedback. In a recent development, the annual list highlights franchise systems that earned strong satisfaction ratings from military veterans who own and operate their businesses. FBR analyzed survey data from more than 2,000 veteran franchise owners, evaluating their overall satisfaction with their brands and their likelihood of recommending them to others.

"Veterans bring leadership, accountability and a commitment to serving others that translate well to business ownership," said Fortidia Chief Commercial Officer & Country Manager Jon Visser. "Our responsibility is to give them the training, tools and ongoing support to put those strengths to work. This recognition carries particular meaning because it reflects the experiences of veterans who have invested in AlphaGraphics and are building businesses within our network.". Fortidia is the parent company of AlphaGraphics, supporting the brand's growth and franchise network.

From Military Leadership to Business Ownership.

For Clayton Authement Jr., a military veteran who owns AlphaGraphics locations in Beaumont and Carrollton, Texas, franchise ownership provides an opportunity to apply his leadership experience while helping businesses in his communities succeed.

Sources indicate that "Military service teaches you to take responsibility, support your team and follow through on your commitments," Authement noted. "Those principles guide how I work with our employees and customers every day. With AlphaGraphics, I can bring my own leadership approach to the business while drawing on a network of people and resources to help me make informed decisions and keep moving forward.".

Recognition Rooted in Franchise Owner Feedback.

AlphaGraphics franchisees were surveyed on 33 benchmark questions covering critical aspects of franchise ownership, including leadership, training and support, operations, franchisor-franchisee relations and financial opportunity.

In a recent development, "Anyone looking at franchising after military service faces a crowded field, and it isn't always obvious which brands back up their pitch," said FBR President Michelle Rowan. "Veteran discounts and perks are easy to advertise. What really matters is how the relationship plays out after the paperwork is done. Every year, we survey thousands of veteran franchisees independently and pass their candid feedback along to people researching their next move. The franchises on this year's list earned the strongest satisfaction marks from the veterans running them, which makes them smart places to start for veterans stepping into business ownership.".

The recognition adds to AlphaGraphics' other FBR accolades in 2026, including its selection as a Top Franchise, Most Profitable Franchise and Top 100 Franchise for Women.
