2027 Will Officially Be The Best Year Ever For Horror

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that alien: Isolation 2 is a rare treat that would’ve been difficult to predict. Indeed, the fact that Alien: Isolation is receiving a sequel more than a decade later is a testament to how profoundly influential it’s been for the horror genre. There’s no doubt that the Alien/Aliens franchise is well-established in horror, but Alien: Isolation is arguably the one Alien video game to most faithfully and authentically represent that horror.

In a recent development, alien: Isolation 2 had a playable demo featured at Gamescom in August, and it may not be much longer before the game is fully released. Regardless, thanks to how many heavy hitters it could boast, 2027 is swiftly taking shape as one of the best years for horror games in a while.

Industry observers note that alien: Isolation 2 has massive moon boots to fill. It’ll be neat to see whether stealth has been significantly improved, for instance, and whether the sequel is as long as its predecessor (one of the biggest critiques of Alien: Isolation to this day being that it’s exceedingly long). (Related: Industry News: Designing Azure silicon on Azure silicon...)

It has been revealed that as to whether or not Alien: Isolation 2 will have a physical edition, Creative Assembly’s response in a Dev Q&A video was incredibly encouraging: “Staying true to the analogue nature of Alien is one of our core pillars. Alien: Isolation 2 will have a physical edition.” This guarantees that the studio is at least eyeing a 2027 release window, considering that PlayStation’s shift toward an all-digital future goes into full effect in early 2028, though it’s possible that it’ll only have physical editions for the consoles that accommodate it by the time it actually launches.

Assuming that Alien: Isolation 2 isn’t delayed, this highly anticipated sequel could be horror’s crown jewel in 2027. 12 years after the launch of the first game, 2027 will be a celebration of the stealth-oriented, first-person-perspective horror games that Alien: Isolation and its ilk directly inspired, as well as other unique horror games in general.

Recent findings suggest that launching in 2027 are The Lost Wild, a dinosaur survival horror game whose game director was Alien: Isolation’s lead designer, and Ontos, a psychological horror game from Amnesia: The Dark Descent and Soma’s Frictional Games. Both are horror. Plus, there are plenty of indie horror games that may not already be on your radar, such as the low-poly Resident Evil and Dark Souls-esque game False Oath.

Of course, Resident Evil Veronica being slated for 2027 ensures that the year will be thrilling nonetheless. Resident Evil Veronica ironically has the lightest weight on its shoulders of any Resident Evil remake to come before it (let alone any remake that follows it). That said, Resident Evil Veronica is much more of a black sheep and may not have as many fans in its corner as the Resident Evil 4 remake or Resident Evil Requiem did, perhaps because Resident Evil Code: Veronica is likely the mainline entry that the fewest fans have played.

Sources indicate that beginning with the launch of the Silent Hill 2 remake, Silent Hill has given itself the unenviable task of releasing a new game every year—Silent Hill 2’s remake in 2024, Silent Hill f in 2025, and Silent Hill: Townfall in 2026. There hasn’t been any confirmation regarding whether or not Bloober’s Silent Hill 1 remake will launch in 2027, but it would certainly align with the franchise’s newfound annual release schedule.

Industry observers note that it’d be sad if Silent Hill misses out on such a fantastic year for horror, especially when the series’ installments have been fairly great lately. Moreover, fans can look forward to ILL and Until Dawn 2, with Until Dawn 2 being particularly exciting for anyone who adored the original Until Dawn and feels as though Supermassive’s choice-driven games still haven’t exceeded its golden standard.
