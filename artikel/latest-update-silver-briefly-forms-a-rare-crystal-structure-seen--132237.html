Latest Update: Silver briefly forms a rare crystal structure seen in radioactive polonium

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (81 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that how atoms are arranged in a material shapes many of its properties. The same carbon atoms, for example, can form diamond and graphite depending on how they are arranged, giving rise to materials with vastly different chemical and physical properties. Now, researchers at Hokkaido University have discovered a fresh crystal structure of silver with unusually high chemical reactivity. The findings, published in Nano Letters, could open new avenues for exploring materials with desirable properties.
