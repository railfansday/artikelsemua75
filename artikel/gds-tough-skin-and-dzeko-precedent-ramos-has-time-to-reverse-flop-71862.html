Goncalo Ramos has struggled to find the back of the net with the AC Milan shirt so far, with the Rossoneri hoping that he will eventually find his groove.

According to recent reports, after years of struggling in the No.9 position, following the departure of Olivier Giroud, Gerry Cardinale said enough is enough and spent over €70m on Ramos this summer. However, at least so far, it seems that wasn’t the right solution after all. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Sources indicate that the Portuguese striker has only scored three goals for the Rossoneri so far, despite featuring in every single game except one. Meanwhile, Alphadjo Cisse has eight goals to his name and cost almost ten times less than the No.9.

It has been revealed that as highlighted by Gazzetta dello Sport, if we were to conduct a survey today, Ramos would be the undisputed leader of seasonal flops for many. The fact that Milan are even considering investing another €50m+ in another striker says a lot.

Of course, such a move shouldn’t be seen as the Rossoneri demonstrating their lack of confidence in the striker. On the contrary, since Cardinale wants to aim for the Scudetto, having two very strong strikers would only be natural.

Still, Ramos needs to step up his goalscoring either way. The hard work he puts in every game, while recognised by the fans, isn’t enough as the usual “that’s not what a striker should do” always follows that praise. The old cliché is true: for strikers, scoring goals is the most key thing.

Recent findings suggest that as the pink newspaper continues, given their characteristics and quality on the ball, Edin Dzeko is a good precedent for Ramos to cling to. The Bosnian arrived in Rome from Manchester City and struggled a bit in his first season (eight league goals), causing a headache for everyone involved.
