Ethan Ampadu has become instrumental in how Leeds United play under Daniel Farke.

Since joining the Whites in July 2023, the Welshman has worked his way up the pecking order, earning the captain's armband at Elland Road.

He has become one of the most consistent players at Farke's disposal, whether he's deployed the middle of the park of the centre of defence.

Ampadu already has 138 appearances for Leeds, and he can do just about everything, everything but shoot accurately, many would say.

If Farke could have a whole team of Ampadu's, he'd likely take that option.

He's a prime example of what the youngsters at Thorp Arch should be striving to be, and Farke may already have another talent at his disposal.

Jayden Lienou is a name that an increasing number of supporters are becoming aware of, with multiple appearances on the first-team bench.

The 18-year-old Welshman is also involved with Craig Bellamy's Wales team, but in both, he's yet to be given his debut by either manager.

Ampadu was forced to seek several loan deals before landing on his feet at Elland Road, and history might be repeating itself.

According to Tom Collomosse, several Championship clubs are keen on signing Lienou on loan in the January window, leaving Leeds with a decision to make.

Lienou would definitely benefit from a loan move in January, but it remains to be seen if those at Thorp Arch also agree.

Mick Brown has told Football Insider that Dan James could push to leave Leeds, though Farke is not open to that, it is believed.

Jaka Bijol's future has been a big talking point, and the same can be mentioned about Ilia Gruev and Lukas Nmecha, who are struggling for minutes.
