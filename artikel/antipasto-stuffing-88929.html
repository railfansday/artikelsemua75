Recent findings suggest that stuffing has a place on every Thanksgiving table. In fact, if it didn’t show up, then I would personally cancel the holiday. Because it’s my favorite part of the holiday, I had to make an Italian-American iteration. Now that I’ve tried this antipasto stuffing, I’ve decided that there’s just no going back to the original. Seeded semolina bread, pieces of salami, briny olives, savory sun-dried tomatoes, and small bits of fresh mozzarella add SO much more flavor and texture to the oftentimes bland (sorry) classic.

Recent findings suggest that storage:I’m doubtful there will be leftovers, but in case there are, you can store them in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Bake, uncovered, in a 350° oven until heated through.
