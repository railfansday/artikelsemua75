In a recent development, founded in 2007, Neurelis set out to rethink how critical treatments could reach people living with epilepsy and other neurological conditions. Chambliss had seen firsthand the difference rapid treatment could make for people living with epilepsy, while also recognizing the practical challenges associated with existing treatment options. Where others saw long-standing drug-delivery barriers, he saw an opportunity to rethink how care could be delivered when every second counts.

Sources indicate that that conviction led Chambliss to start Neurelis and assemble a team committed to solving a challenge that had persisted in the epilepsy community for years. After more than a decade of navigating complex formulation, clinical and regulatory hurdles, the organization reached a significant milestone in 2020 with FDA approval of their diazepam nasal spray for seizure clusters. Neurelis has since expanded the treatment's reach, including to children as young as 2 years old, while growing into a fully integrated neuroscience company focused on addressing unmet needs in epilepsy and other neurological disorders. Through it all, Chambliss has kept patients and caregivers at the center, measuring progress not only through business growth, but through the lives Neurelis can impact.
