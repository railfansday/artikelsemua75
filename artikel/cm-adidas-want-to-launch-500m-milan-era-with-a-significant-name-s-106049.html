AC Milan and Adidas will be back together again from 2028, and the German brand want to launch the new era with a bi bang.

As Calciomercato.com write, Milan will return to being an Adidas club again, as they have been for three previous spells. The two brands became so closely linked above all by the successes of the 2000s under Carlo Ancelotti, before PUMA took over in 2018. In a recent development, the contract with Adidas – which will begin in the summer of 2028 – is quite a sensational deal for the industry and for the club’s final years in terms of sporting results. It will bring in €50m per season for 10 seasons, the highest in Serie A by some margin.

Ten years ago, the Rossoneri signed a €14m per season contract with PUMA, which was renewed in 2022 to reach around €25m per season.

According to recent reports, adidas have already invested heavily in Milan and they want to strengthen the ‘alliance’ further. A lot depends on building a squad capable of playing a leading role in both Serie A and the Champions League.

Recent findings suggest that for this reason, Adidas are hoping to help Milan land a world-class player. Napoli midfielder Scott McTominay could gain momentum in the coming months as an idea, as the former Manchester United player is one of Adidas’s most important players worldwide.

Bruno Fernandes is another name to keep an eye on. The Manchester United captain left Italy many years ago as a promising youngster and now wants to return as a top player. The Portuguese is a possible candidate, even if he is currently twinned with Nike.

Industry observers note that last but not least – in terms of consideration and feasibility – is Real Madrid’s Endrick. Milan are ready to make him one of the symbols of their newly introduced project and have a bid of €60m plus bonuses ready to try to break down Los Blancos’ wall.

Industry observers note that jose Mourinho is the main obstacle between the Rossoneri and the Brazilian star, but Cardinale is unwavering and ready to do everything possible to bring him to Milanello as early as January. The money from Adidas could help land Endrick, who is with Upcoming Balance at present.
