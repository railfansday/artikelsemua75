EXCLUSIVE: Artist and filmmaker Allison Janae Hamilton has set her next project, a narrative short titled Holler, starring Edwina Findley and musician Valerie June. The film will be produced under Hamilton’s newly minted production company, Swampwife Pictures.

Recent findings suggest that set against the rural landscape of West Tennessee, the film follows a hard-living Memphis nightclub owner who returns to her family’s land when signs of a once-in-a-generation ecological event begin to emerge. Called home to perform a ritual demanded by her lineage, she must confront both the ancestral responsibilities she has inherited and the person she left behind without saying goodbye. Hamilton wrote and directed the contemporary Western, which stars Findley and June alongside Sheria Irving and Aysia Moorer, with Jaselle Martino (Abdo and Saneya) of Chaconia Pictures producing in collaboration with Hamilton’s Swampwife Pictures. Antonino D’Ambrosio of La Lutta Productions (Roberta) is an Exec Producer. Watch on Deadline.

Hamilton is a 2026 Guggenheim Fellow. Her previous short film, Venus of Ossabaw, debuted in March 2026 at Telfair Museums in Savannah, Georgia. She is currently adapting the short into a feature. Hamilton is also in development on Floridaland, another feature script she has penned. The project was selected as part of the Sundance Institute’s 2026 Screenwriters Intensive.

“The elements that these films have in common form the cornerstone of what I want to do with Swampwife, which is to tell stories rooted in the land, driven by character, and that expand our ideas of genre,” Hamilton told Deadline.

“I want Swampwife to be known for stories that feel like stepping into another world, one of richly layered visuals and emotionally resonant characters. Swampwife gives all my storytelling interests a single home.”.

The Holler creative team also includes cinematographer Nikita Carpenter, who previously collaborated with Hamilton on Venus of Ossabaw; production designer Freyja Bardell, whose recent credits include the feature film Is God Is (2026) and Kendrick Lamar’s Not Like Us music video; and casting director Damian Bao, known for his work on The Man I Love, Forge, Love Me, Look Into My Eyes, Port Authority, Goldie, and American Honey.

Holler will debut in Spring 2027 as part of Hamilton’s forthcoming solo exhibition, A Slow Drag for the Conjure Women, at the Institute of Contemporary Art, Philadelphia (ICA).
