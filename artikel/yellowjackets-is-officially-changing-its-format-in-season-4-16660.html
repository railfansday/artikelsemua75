Spoilers ahead for Yellowjackets season 4, episode 1, "Hivemind"!Yellowjackets has finally returned, and the thriller TV show is full of more backstabbing and brutality than ever in the season 4 premiere, "Hivemind." Now that Yellowjackets is back on Paramount+ for its fourth and final season, the series seems intent on taking significant swings and keeping the audience in suspense about the girls' rescue for as long as possible.

The Yellowjackets survivors have been dropping like flies in the present-day timeline, and the teens in the 1990s were on the precipice of rescue, with Natalie (Sophie Thatcher) making contact with the satellite phone at the end of season 3. After ending on such a jaw-dropping cliffhanger, it's been agony waiting for the series to be back on our screens, but it seems that Yellowjackets has a few more twists to pull before the show signs off for good.

It has been revealed that the season 4 premiere sets Yellowjackets on an intense path for its final installment, which has yet to answer how the girls really got out of the woods. After "Hivemind," it looks like audiences will have to wait a little longer to find out what happened before the Yellowjackets were rescued, as Yellowjackets is officially breaking into three separate timelines for season 4.

Yellowjackets follows three timelines in season 4: the adults in the present day, the 1990s post-rescue in New Jersey, and the 1990s pre-rescue in the wilderness. The series initially captured the attention of audiences thanks to its dual timeline, following the adult survivors in the 2020s while slowly revealing the darkness of their past in the wake of the plane crash in the '90s. However, now, Yellowjackets is upping the ante with a third timeline, prolonging the mystery of the wilderness. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

It has been revealed that it was established from the beginning that the girls were stranded in the woods for 19 months, so viewers knew it would take a long time for them to get back to Fresh Jersey. While it's an ambitious undertaking to split the story into three timelines, it's a relief that the series is tackling the fallout of the girls' initial homecoming and is giving the audience some breathing room after spending so many seasons in the woods. However, the season 4 premiere jumps ahead to the investigation and hospital visit when the girls have already left the woods, skipping the most key part: how they escaped. It's an emotionally wrought debut for season 4, as the audience gets to see the survivors' parents and witness the shock of getting back to civilization after the characters spent so long in their own insular society. At the same time, the adults are at each other's throats, and Melissa (Hilary Swank) takes a big risk by moving next door to Shauna (Melanie Lynskey).

While the stakes remain high in every timeline on Yellowjackets, our biggest questions reside in the wilderness with the girls who haven't been rescued yet. The timeline that will chronicle what happened between Nat's distress call and the rescue is sure to hold more secrets than the audience knows. The identity of the pit girl in Yellowjackets was revealed in the season 3 finale, "Full Circle," so one of the biggest remaining mysteries in Yellowjackets is the discovery of the potentially drastic actions of the girls out there.

In a recent development, every aspect of Yellowjackets season 4 is sure to hold the viewers' attention, as the present-day events are some of the most unpredictable. However, as the final installment unfolds, it will be most exciting to find out the answer to the question that has been plaguing us since episode 1, and we won't be satisfied until the rescue is officially revealed.

Yellowjackets season 4 premieres on Paramount+ on November 20th, 2026.
