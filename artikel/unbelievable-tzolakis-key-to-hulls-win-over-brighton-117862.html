Oli McBurnie conceded Hull City "rode their luck" during their 2-0 win over Brighton, but he lauded goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis for his "unbelievable" display.

Hull ended an eight-game winless run in the Premier League, but they were put through their paces to claim all three points.

Tzolakis made a string of impressive saves to deny Diego Gomez, Georginio Rutter and Pascal Gross during the first half, with Lewis Dunk also repelled after the break.

A late turning point came when Brighton defender Costinha saw red for a second bookable offence, with Hull's threat on the counter-attack eventually proving fruitful in stoppage time.

McBurnie bundled home in the 92nd minute before tucking in his second following Mohamed Belloumi's cross to cap an eventful ending.

But Hull's matchwinner hailed Tzolakis as the key man for the Tigers.

Recent findings suggest that "It doesn't feel like that to be fair. We haven't lost a game at home. They are a top team, and we knew it was going to be tough," McBurnie told Sky Sports.

"We rode our luck, and we have the massive man [Tzolakis] to keep the ball out of the net, so that's what he's done.

"He [Tzolakis] was unbelievable. He has been all season for us to be fair to him. We give him a bit of stick about his gear, but he's been good in net.

"He was fantastic, kept us in the game today. We know if we are always in games we fancy ourselves getting chances and that's what we did.". (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Hull scored two 90th-minute goals in the same Premier League game for the first time.

McBurnie's brace was his first in the competition on his 107th appearance, helping Hull remain unbeaten at home in the Premier League this season (W2 D4).

It is their longest unbeaten start at home in a league campaign since 2015-16 (first eight), and Tzolakis insisted it was a team effort rather than an individual one.

Industry observers note that "So many saves but two goals from Oli [McBurnie]. It was a team effort and everyone helped each other," Tzolakis added. (Related: Industry News: Hyperparameter Search with Transformers ...)

"We are a unit here like a team. We play for each other. We saw a very good team effort, and we deserved to take the three points.

"In the first half we suffered a lot, but we managed the second half and scored two goals in transition, two goals from Oli and that's it.".

Brighton, meanwhile, missed the chance to close the gap to Premier League leaders Arsenal, who were held to a 2-2 draw by Newcastle United on Saturday.

The Seagulls have now lost two of their last three Premier League games against newly promoted opposition (W1), while it was their third top-flight defeat due to winning goals scored in the 90th minute or later in 2026, their most such losses in a single calendar year.

McBurnie's first goal came just two minutes and 33 seconds after Costinha's dismissal.

"For 65 minutes we revealed a good performance, then we changed our intensity, levels and standards," Fabian Hurzeler told BBC Match of the Day.

"In the Premier League they can punish you, especially after a red card. Overall, a frustrating game but we have to keep going. We will analyse it. That's the process we follow.

"I always emphasise staying humble. We have to outwork the opponent, otherwise the opponents are too good in the Premier League and that's what we have to do better.".
