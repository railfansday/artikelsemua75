We promoted developers into architects without informing them

Yesterday, I was debugging a performance problem with our Google Forms addon. It turned out my colleague had vibe coded the logging part, so that he could monitor the new installs every week. Monitoring new customers is the most important part of running a business, so he is absolutely right in doing that. Except, he was logging the user’s email and Google has severely restricted how much detail you can get about the user since last December. So, AI had written a series of try/catch statements:.

In our case, the right fix is simple. Log the timestamp, show the welcome screen first and tell marketing what they can’t get on install. But AI will never make that call for you, because it doesn’t know your consumers and it won’t push back on your requirements. That is the architect’s job. If you are a developer, the coding job is going away. But it doesn’t mean your job is going away. Non-developers who don’t understand architecture and design will end up with bad performance and unmaintainable database tables. So, stop missing the coding and become an architect. Learn to push back on requirements, design the datamodel, constrain AI to it and review the code the way an architect reviews a developer’s code. You got promoted. Nobody told you, so I am telling you. (Related: Latest Update: Simple considerations for simple people ...)
