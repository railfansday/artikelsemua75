Industry News: X’s latest revision for creators focuses on vertical video

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

X announced fresh creation tools Wednesday, many of them focused on short-form vertical video. The updates are part of the platform’s effort to compete with social networks such as YouTube and Instagram as the format continues to grow in popularity.

It has been revealed that the tools are designed to help creators make professional-quality vertical videos, share original content more easily, and earn more. They also give creators more ways to record, edit, and publish content directly on the platform without relying on third-party apps. In a recent development, A key addition is an upgraded media editor that lets creators produce polished vertical videos without leaving X. Recording features include the ability to capture video in segments and use green-screen effects, the company remarked.

It has been revealed that the editor also offers a range of postproduction tools. Creators can convert horizontal videos to vertical format, automatically generate captions, trim footage, add text overlays, choose custom thumbnails, and adjust photos before publishing. (Related: How to generate text: using different decoding methods ...)

Thanks to a recently announced update, users can now add up to 20 photos and videos to a single post, which audiences can swipe through horizontally. X claims the feature is intended for sharing event recaps, behind-the-scenes moments, and product launches.

Availability of the upcoming features varies by region, X said.

Recent findings suggest that earlier this month, the organization introduced a clipping feature that lets people take snippets from longer videos, add their own commentary, and post the clips without leaving X. Clips credit and link to the original video, plus the person who creates a clip and the original poster can earn from its views.

Another feature, “React with Video,” launched earlier this year. Similar to TikTok’s “stitch” feature, this lets creators respond to posts with video commentary while displaying the original post in the background.

It has been revealed that X has also introduced new earning opportunities and updates to its creator monetization programs in recent months. These include the “Video Kickstart Bonus,” an invitation-only pilot that rewards selected creators for publishing original vertical videos under three minutes long and without watermarks.
