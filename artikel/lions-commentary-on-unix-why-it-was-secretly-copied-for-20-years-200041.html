John Lions was teaching operating systems at the University of Latest South Wales in Australia.

What's the best way toteach operating systems?

They need to study the Unix source.But it's tough going.

In May 1976, Lions prepared lecture notes explaining the kernel source code of AT&T's Unix Version 6. These became his Unix commentary.

To understand an OS,we need to readthe actual code.

I've written a guideto the Unix kernel.We'll use it asour textbook.

By 1977, the notes were circulating among other schools and research institutions with Unix licenses [1][4]. Even Bell Labs wanted copies and eventually took over distribution [2].

Early on, Unix source could be studied in class. But at USENIX in June 1979, AT&T declared new Version 7 license terms restricting courses that disclosed or discussed source code. Distribution of Lions' book was tightly controlled as well [5][6].

I am altering the license.Pray I don't alter itany further.

Destroy Lions' book.No more teachingUnix source code.

There is no escapefrom our operating system.

He's teaching from the Unix source code!Seize the books!Run!

I find your interestin source code disturbing.

Just use the system calls.Learn a little theory.Leave the internals to us.

Theory isn't enough.I need to see the code.

This code is our only hopeof understanding an OS.
