Lawmakers from both parties are demanding answers about the Nvidia CEO’s reinstatement and the administration’s handling of national security risks.

Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as Nvidia’s chief executive is “exhibit A” of the “political corruption and rot at the heart of the Trump administration’s massive tech and AI policy”, Chris Van Hollen, the Maryland Democrat vying to become the next Senate majority leader, told reporters Monday.

Van Hollen said he intends to focus on Huang’s “efforts to increase and accelerate the export of high-end chips to China, how he’s been accomplishing that, how he’s been getting his way with the administration” when the reinstalled Nvidia CEO appears before the Senate banking committee on 3 December.
