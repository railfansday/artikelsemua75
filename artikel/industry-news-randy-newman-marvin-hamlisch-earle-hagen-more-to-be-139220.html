Industry News: Randy Newman, Marvin Hamlisch, Earle Hagen & More to Be Honored by Society of Composers & Lyricists

🌐 Source: Billboard • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 3 min read (502 words, 📚 Deep Dive)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Randy Newman is set to receive the Society of Composers & Lyricists’ 2026 Lifetime Achievement Award on Dec.
  • Past SCL Lifetime Achievement Award honorees include Stephen Schwartz, Charles Fox, Giorgio Moroder, Quincy Jones, Danny Elfman, Bill Conti, Lalo Schifrin, Philip Glass, Alan Menken and Ginny Mancini.
  • Newman’s many honors include seven Grammys, three Primetime Emmys and two Academy Awards.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Randy Newman is set to receive the Society of Composers & Lyricists’ 2026 Lifetime Achievement Award on Dec. 9 at the 37th Annual SCL Holiday Dinner in Los Angeles. The organization will also posthumously induct composers Marvin Hamlisch and Earle Hagen into its Hall of Fame.

Past SCL Lifetime Achievement Award honorees include Stephen Schwartz, Charles Fox, Giorgio Moroder, Quincy Jones, Danny Elfman, Bill Conti, Lalo Schifrin, Philip Glass, Alan Menken and Ginny Mancini.

Newman’s many honors include seven Grammys, three Primetime Emmys and two Academy Awards. He was inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2002 and the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2013.

Sources indicate that hamlisch won three Oscars, four Grammys, four Primetime Emmys, a Tony and two Golden Globes. He shared the Pulitzer Prize for Drama for the 1975 Broadway smash A Chorus Line. He died in 2012 at age 68.

Recent findings suggest that hagen composed the theme songs for dozens of TV highlights, including The Andy Griffith Show, The Dick Van Dyke Show and That Girl. Hagen won a Primetime Emmy in 1968 for his work on I Spy. He was inducted into the Television Academy Hall of Fame in 2011, and died in 2008 at age 88.

The 37th Annual SCL Holiday Dinner will be held at the Skirball Cultural Center in Los Angeles on Dec. 9 from 5:30 to 10 p.m.

The SCL will present additional honors at its holiday parties in Latest York, Chicago and Nashville.

Jon Batiste will receive the 2026 SCL Ambassador Award at the SCL New York City Holiday Party at The Cutting Room on Dec. 1 from 6:30 to 10:30 p.m.

In a recent development, sen. Dick Durbin (D-Ill.) will receive the SCL Excellence in Advocacy Award at the SCL Chicago Holiday Party at iO Theater – The Clybourn on Dec. 3 from 7 to 10 p.m.

It has been revealed that composer and film director Brent McCorkle will receive 2026 SCL Trailblazer Award at the SCL Nashville Holiday Party at Ocean Way Studios on Dec. 5 from 6 to 9 p.m.

Sources indicate that the SCL Excellence in Advocacy Award recognizes leading advocates who champion the rights of music creators. Sen. Marsha Blackburn (R-Tenn.) received the award in 2025.

The SCL Trailblazer Award was created to recognize and acknowledge a select group of composers and lyricists whose contributions have pioneered scoring for film, television and other visual media in Nashville. Past recipients include Steve Dorff, Steve Schnur, Brian D. Siewert and Paul Mills.

In making the announcements, Ashley Irwin, president of the SCL, said: “The SCL Lifetime Achievement Award and these other special honors were created to recognize and acknowledge a select group of music creators with significant contributions to our profession and music community.
