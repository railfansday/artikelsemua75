Xabi Alonso has broken his silence after footage emerged online showing Cole Palmer losing his temper with Joao Pedro following Chelsea's 4-1 home defeat to Leeds United on Saturday afternoon.

The Blues have now slipped to eighth in the Premier League table after failing to hold on to the lead handed to them by summer signing Emmanuel Emegha, who opened the scoring after just 25 minutes. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Chelsea were soon blown out of the water and handed their third defeat in four matches, though, as Noah Okafor struck twice, while Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Brenden Aaronsen also got in on the act to hand the Lilywhites their first league win at Stamford Bridge since December 1999.

The inquest into what went wrong, and why confidence has seemingly deserted Chelsea in recent weeks, continues. While the defence has borne the brunt of much of the criticism, with only three teams in the league having conceded more goals this season, Palmer has not covered himself in glory either, following up a largely fruitless creative display with his actions at full-time.

At full-time, Chelsea players went to applaud those fans who had stayed until the end, but Palmer could be spotted seething as he made a bee-line to the tunnel.

Recent findings suggest that his decision to avoid clapping the home support prompted coach Willie Isa and injured teammate Joao Pedro to intervene and try to change his mind. Careful to mask his words with his hand, Palmer strutted up to teammate Pedro with a raised eyebrow, continuing to look angry as he refused to reconsider. Watch the incident below:.

This weekend's result was made even more damaging by the fact Moises Caicedo had to be taken off through injury in the first-half. The Blues may find some solace in the form of back-to-back fixtures against relegation candidates Nottingham Forest and Crystal Palace, but they then face the daunting task of turning their form around against Liverpool and Manchester City.

Alonso - who now finds himself under growing pressure in the Stamford Bridge hot seat - was asked about Palmer's full-time actions, to which he responded to the media (watch below):.

According to recent reports, “We were disappointed, the fans were disappointed. We understand their frustration because expectations are high, and we feel sorry for them. We are in a fragile moment, but we need to work together and stick together. There is still a long way to go.”.

It would seem the Spaniard was eager to stay away from the controversy, having been more concerned about trying to turn his side's form around.

On Chelsea struggling to respond to setbacks, he added: “That's our job, to find solutions and learn from bad experiences like today. We need to improve our ability to overcome difficult moments during games. It's a very key part of our progression.”.
