Report: Juventus, Milan and Napoli on alert as Chelsea open door to Palestra exit

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Chelsea are prepared to let Marco Palestra leave as Xabi Alonso pushes to sign Jules Koundé from Barcelona, and Juventus, Milan and Napoli are all interested in bringing the full-back back to Italy.

According to El Nacional, Alonso is in favour of signing Koundé soon, even in January. The Chelsea coach is willing to let the Italian leave, as he has offers to return to Serie A from clubs such as Juventus, AC Milan and SSC Napoli, and bring in Koundé in exchange. Barcelona are reportedly open to selling Koundé for around €50m after his role was reduced under Hansi Flick.

It’s been a difficult start in London for Palestra. The full-back was named Serie A Defender of the Year for 2025/26, on loan at Cagliari, but has struggled for minutes at Chelsea. The Blues paid Atalanta around €60m for him in the summer.

According to recent reports, A thigh injury delayed his Chelsea debut until October, and El Nacional puts him at 202 minutes across six matches. He made his first Premier League start in a 2-0 win over Manchester United, but Alonso prefers Josh Acheampong at right-back and uses Palestra from the bench before Malo Gusto.

The 21-year-old would have no shortage of options in Serie A, where Juventus, Milan and Napoli are all looking for reinforcements on the flanks.
