Recent findings suggest that the Sussexes are in for a very British Christmas. After moving back in late August—following more than six years in California—Prince Harry and Duchess Meghan are ready to spend the holidays in England.

Per a newly introduced People report, the Duke and Duchess of Sussex are planning to celebrate both Thanksgiving and Christmas at their new home in the Cotswolds with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet. Industry observers note that “Traveling back and forth would be too much for everyone,” the source added. “She doesn’t want to add any more pressure right now.”.

Recent findings suggest that it’s been a while since Harry and Meghan spent the holidays in the United Kingdom. In 2017, the engaged couple joined Harry’s royal family for Christmas at Sandringham. And in 2018, as a newly married couple, they attended the traditional Christmas Day church service at St. Mary Magdalene Church at Sandringham.

It has been revealed that in the years that followed, as the Sussexes stepped back from their senior royal posts, they spent their holidays in either Canada or the United States, which means this will be their little children’s first true British Christmas. We know they might not make it to town for the festivities (as they are trying to keep a low profile), but there really is nothing quite like a London Christmas.

The source told People that Meghan and Harry “really enjoy the privacy” of their new home. “They can go out for lunch or dinner, and people give them space,” the insider said. “With the kids back in school, they’ve been making time for each other and enjoying those outings together.”.
