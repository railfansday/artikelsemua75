Boeing Weighs More Layoffs As 737 MAX Output Boost Slows

🌐 Source: Simpleflying • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (94 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Industry observers note that on November 19th, Boeing declined to tell one of its largest unions whether further layoffs will follow a round planned for next month, even as the tech giant struggles to lift 737 MAX output from 42 jets a month to 47, a rate it had expected to reach this past summer. The company confirmed this month that it would cut 5% of the roughly 50,000 employees in its commercial airplanes division, and now expects to reach 47 jets a month in early 2027, according to Reuters.
