Today's biggest winners and losers in the stock market. On this episode of Stock Movers: - Nvidia (NVDA) shares are higher even though news broke this morning that a pair of Chinese firms installed Nvidia Corp's powerful Blackwell semiconductors in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang.
