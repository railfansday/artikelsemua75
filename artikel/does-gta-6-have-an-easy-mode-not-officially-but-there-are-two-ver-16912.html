Does GTA 6 have an 'easy mode'? Not officially, but there are two very key settings you can change to make the game less difficult

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Sources indicate that by now, you've probably heard that you cannot use cheats in GTA 6 until you complete the main story. Not only that, but enabling cheat codes also locks story progression and deactivates the ability to save. Basically, you can use cheats to have a quick crime spree and blow off some steam, then it's back to normalcy. Understandably, some gamers are a little put out with this change.

But, for anyone looking to have a slightly easier time with GTA 6 than the dev's intended experience, I come with good news: you can make the game a little bit easier by toggling two options in the game's Settings menu. If you open Settings, and then navigate to Gameplay, you'll find 'Accessible Combat' and 'Reduced Vehicle Damage' toggles. You can activate both to make GTA 6's more difficult elements a little bit easier to work through.

In a recent development, 'Accessible Combat' decreases enemy health and makes them deal less damage to your character — the kind of rebalancing that you'd expect from a traditional difficulty slider. 'Reduced Vehicle Damage', as you'd expect, lowers the damage taken by your vehicle and allows your ride to endure more punishment. This applies to gunfire, environmental threats, and impact damage.

It has been revealed that toggle these settings comes with a warning from Rockstar, though. A prompt notes that enabling these modifiers will "significantly change the balance and experience of the gameplay". I've been playing around with these settings and whilst they are not a foolproof 'God Mode' by any means, you can absolutely see a difference in the game when they're enabled: Jason and Lucia take a lot more punishment from melee damage and gunshots, and I had to really try to get Jason's old banged up pickup into even the 'slightly smoking' bracket of disrepair. Your mileage may vary, but these settings, from what I can tell so far, certainly do make the game easier. (Related: Primary vs Contingent Beneficiary: What's the Diff...)

Given that cheats disable trophies and progression, these settings might be something to keep in mind if you're trying to find a smoother path through the main story of GTA 6. It's a credit to Rockstar's accessibility teams that these have been included at all - alongside the 23 other gameplay accessibility options, the 12 display options, the 70 UI options, the 24 camera options, and the 14 audio options. "We create inclusive, intuitive, and immersive experiences for every player," noted Rockstar's associate UI director, Sam Grosset, in materials provided to Eurogamer ahead of the game's launch. "Accessibility is considered from the outset, and designing for diverse needs enables us to create better games for everyone. We wanted every player in GTA 6 to have options to play the game how they like. Whether it's selecting a larger font size, opting for a more minimal screen experience, or making a controls tweak to sprinting... the choices are open to all.".

We also learned yesterday that Rockstar has made GTA 6 missions "harder to fail" by making them more open-ended, and giving players more ways to figure out how to do things in their own way.
