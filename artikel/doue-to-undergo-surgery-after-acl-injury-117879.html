Paris Saint-Germain confirmed Desire Doue has ruptured his anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) in his right knee and will undergo surgery.

Doue was on target as PSG registered a 3-2 win over Nice in Ligue 1 on Saturday but was then withdrawn before the break after appearing to twist his right knee awkwardly in a challenge with Sofiane Diop.

Luis Enrique confirmed after the match that it was a "serious injury" and PSG have said Doue will undergo surgery before the end of November.

No timeline has been set for his return yet, but some reports suggest he could miss six months, which would see him miss the end of PSG's season.

He would also miss France's Nations League quarter-final against Spain in March.

Doue has been an influential figure for PSG this season with 10 direct goal involvements (six goals, four assists) in all competitions – only Ousmane Dembele (11) has more.

The 21-year-old also leads all PSG players for chances created (31) and total shots (45), while he is averaging a goal every 160.8 minutes.

He will now, however, join long-term absentee Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, who is also out with an ACL injury, in the treatment room.
