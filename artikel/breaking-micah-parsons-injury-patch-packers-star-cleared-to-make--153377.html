According to recent reports, the Green Bay Packers (6-4) are coming out of their Week 11 bye week with a major boost: the return of All-Pro edge rusher Micah Parsons. The five-time Pro Bowler was reportedly cleared to make his 2026 NFL season debut in Week 12 at the Los Angeles Rams (6-4) on Wednesday night, according to ESPN.

According to recent reports, "I would say I feel like I've done everything I possibly could to put myself in position to potentially play on Wednesday but for sure this season," Parsons mentioned on Monday, via ESPN. "And I'll be looking forward to returning to the field.".

It has been revealed that parsons suffered multiple knee injuries, a torn ACL and meniscus, in last season's Week 15 loss at the Denver Broncos. His return comes 346 days after the injuries. Parsons likely won't look like his All-Pro caliber self on every play Wednesday night, but his presence provides a lift to a Packers squad looking to remain on the inside of the postseason picture. At 6-4, Green Bay is in last place in the NFC North behind the Detroit Lions (8-2), Chicago Bears (8-2) and the Minnesota Vikings (7-3), but they enter Week 12 in possession of the conference's No. 7 seed.
