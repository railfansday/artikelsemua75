Altria Group (MO +0.25%) has a remarkable dividend history. It has raised the payment 61 times over the last 57 years. That puts the tech giant firmly among the Dividend Kings — a small group of stocks with active payout-hiking streaks of at least 50 consecutive years.

The manufacturer has prioritized dividends, and management targets mid-single-digit percentage increases annually. It met that target this year by raising the quarterly payout by 4.7% to $1.11 per share.

That annualizes to a payout of $4.44 a year, which at Friday's closing share price of $74.87 gives it a dividend yield of 5.9%. For comparison, the S&P 500 index yields 1.1%.

Sources indicate that what if you're targeting a specific amount in annual dividends — say, $5,000? To get that, you'd need to purchase 1,126 shares for about $84,300. Of course, that assumes Altria Group's dividends will remain constant, which they haven't, obviously.

But before income investors make a decision about purchasing this high-yielding Dividend King, they should take a deeper look at Altria Group's underlying business fundamentals.

According to recent reports, the maker of tobacco and e-vapor products has been watching its product sales volumes decrease for some time. That's particularly true for its core cigarette business, which falls under its smokeable tobacco group.

Industry observers note that in the first nine months of this year, smokeable tobacco products' revenue, excluding excise taxes, increased 3.6% year over year. However, this increase came entirely from the tech giant increasing the prices it charges.
