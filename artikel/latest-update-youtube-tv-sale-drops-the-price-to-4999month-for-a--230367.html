YouTube TV isn’t cheap, but a sale around Black Friday cuts the cost of the service down to just $49.99/month for a limited time, with a couple of strings attached.

According to recent reports, the cost of YouTube TV generally lands at $83/month following the most recent price hikes, but there are ways to cut that down. New packages such as “Sports + News” drop the price by a fair bit, with the “Entertainment” package being the most affordable at $55/month.
