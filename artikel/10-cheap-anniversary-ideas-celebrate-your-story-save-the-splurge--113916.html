Mark your anniversary with something that’s about the two of you, such as a return to your first-date spot, your wedding menu cooked at home, or the year’s traditional gift made by hand. Each costs far less than a hotel weekend, so keep that for a milestone year like your fifth or tenth.

Recent findings suggest that at the $168.35 average nightly rate the data firm CoStar reported for the first week of November 2026, two hotel nights cost about $337 before taxes, meals, or gas. The ideas that make an anniversary feel major do one of two things. They revisit your history as a couple, or they have you try something new together.

Trying something newly introduced has research behind it. In a 2000 study led by psychologist Arthur Aron, couples who spent seven minutes on a new, exciting task together reported a bigger rise in relationship quality than couples given a routine one.
