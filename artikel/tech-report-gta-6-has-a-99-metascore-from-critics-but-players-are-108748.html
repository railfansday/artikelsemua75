Grand Theft Auto 6 might be one of the best-reviewed games of all time, but you wouldn't know it from its Metacritic user score.

Recent findings suggest that while critics have given Rockstar's latest a Metascore of 99, its user score has dropped to 5.6 out of 10 at the time of writing, which Metacritic classifies as "mixed or average". Of the 54,000+ ratings left, 44 percent are "negative". (Related: Breaking: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons in a...)

The score had previously dropped into the red, but has since recovered a little. (Related: Tech Report: Score TP-Link Archer with full Wi-Fi 7 sup...)

Some negative reviews raise genuine complaints, such as the game's 30FPS cap on console, while others take glitch with how much of the story focuses on Jason and Lucia's relationship instead of the crime-spree antics of past games.

According to recent reports, "Expectations might've killed this game," states one 0/10 review. "The hype that something otherworldly would come to be flopped. The hyperrealism makes it feel slow, mundane and boring. Its a tecnichal [sic] advancement for sure but for the wrong reasons. Goodlooks zero personality type stuff. Its not as edgy as the rest of the gta games. And thats exactly my problem like is not funny. The game gets carried by its visuals and radio stations. Certain limitations make it feel like not gta and fresh features make it feel like not gta. In the end it feels like a life simulator and the point of gaming is to escape from reality.".

In a recent development, "Genuinely hated almost every minute of this experience," wrote another. "After years of waiting and an absurd amount of hype, GTA VI somehow managed to leave me more frustrated than entertained. The excessive sexual content gets exhausting, and instead of adding anything meaningful to the experience, it often feels like Rockstar is desperately trying to shock its audience.

Industry observers note that "The story is painfully disappointing, the writing fails to keep me invested, and I honestly couldn't care less about what happens to most of these **** gameplay isn't doing the game any favours either. Glitches, bugs, annoying inconsistencies, and frustrating moments constantly get in the way of actually enjoying the experience. It's ridiculous that a game of this scale, backed by a studio with Rockstar's resources, can feel this rough. I expected a polished experience, not a glorified debugging ****.".

Others point to "woke" and "DEI" agendas.

Industry observers note that "A major step down in the writing department compared to previous instalments coupled with the obvious DEI-fication, unfun realism and the resulting player freedom restrictions leave a sour taste in the otherwise detailed gorgeous open world," noted one reviewer. Another unhappy player wrote: "Shameful DEI hijacked cuck simulator, the most overrated game of all time and it's not even close.".
