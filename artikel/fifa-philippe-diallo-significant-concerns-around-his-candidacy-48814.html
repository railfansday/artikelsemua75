Philippe Diallo is launching into a major battle. The president of the French Football Federation (FFF) has decided to run for the presidency of Fifa, with the ambition of succeeding Gianni Infantino next March. Already supported by UEFA, the French leader is now seeking to convince the various federations. With this in mind, he will travel to Abuja, Nigeria, on Saturday, where the General Assembly of the Confederation of African Football (CAF) will be held.

Recent findings suggest that A trip far from trivial. Philippe Diallo knows it, the African continent constitutes particularly favorable terrain for Gianni Infantino. The outgoing president benefits from significant support, which his opponent intends to try to change. But this strategy is far from unanimous. On social networks, Romain Molina strongly criticized the first choices of the French candidate and the way in which his entourage intends to campaign. It has been revealed that the independent journalist, although an opponent of Gianni Infantino, does not mince his words. According to him, Philippe Diallo and certain UEFA officials are completely wrong in their approach to African football. “Either Philippe Diallo is very, very poorly advised, or has he not understood the dynamics of Fifa and international football? His start to the campaign is a sketch since everyone, including several UEFA leaders, is focusing on Africa, which is nonsense, believing that the French-European networks will help (getting 15 votes in Africa would be a victory because it would allow us not to lose the election there). Ideas from another time, with the thought of bringing in well-known people (Africans in particular) to support his campaign and go to large countries with a French-speaking majority (which is the worst possible idea given the arrogance associated with France and UEFA, an elitist association which does not care about “others”)”he believes. The journalist continues his charge by directly implicating the entourage of the president of the FFF. “How can you be so disconnected from reality? However, is this surprising given the recycled “advisors” around him? These people have NO idea of ​​the reality of the federations affiliated to Fifa, and even less of the current dynamics within the confederations/sub-confederations. » A particularly severe first warning for Philippe Diallo, who will have to convince well beyond his European supporters if he wants to shake Gianni Infantino.
