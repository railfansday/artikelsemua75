Chicago-based mortgage lender Rate on Tuesday unveiled the launch of EquitySimple, a fixed-rate home equity loan designed to help homeowners consolidate debt, finance home improvements and cover major expenses.

The product gives borrowers a lump-sum payment with a fixed interest rate and predictable monthly payments and is structured as a second mortgage, allowing homeowners to tap their equity without refinancing their existing first mortgage or giving up a lower interest rate. The launch comes as consumers face rising debt burdens and homeowners hold substantial equity. Recent findings suggest that rate noted that 68% of its fixed-rate home equity loans funded between Jan. 1 and Nov. 19 carried interest rates below 8%, with a median rate of 7.88%. The company cautioned that historical rates do not guarantee current pricing, which varies based on borrower and property characteristics.

“Too many homeowners are paying credit card interest rates above 20% while sitting on significant equity they’ve worked hard to build,” Victor Ciardelli, founder and CEO of Rate, said in a statement. “We’re committed to helping our members build the good life around home, health and wealth, but financial stress can get in the way.”.

Sources indicate that equitySimple uses a flat fee that covers lender and third-party costs, including appraisal and title expenses. Borrowers are responsible for applicable state and local taxes, as well as prepaid interest. Rate said it discloses the loan’s rate, payment and fees before borrowers commit, and it does not add charges at closing.

The firm is also offering a digital application process, with the option for borrowers to work directly with a loan officer or move between the two channels. Rate remarked loan officers can help borrowers evaluate whether using home equity to consolidate unsecured debt is appropriate, given that the home serves as collateral for the upcoming loan.

According to recent reports, eligible borrowers may be able to close in as little as 14 days, compared with an industry average of about 40 days, according to Rate. The company clarified that the timeline is not guaranteed and depends on property, credit and documentation requirements.

It has been revealed that “We’ve spent 26 years helping people get into their homes. EquitySimple is about helping them use the value they’ve built in those homes to find relief,” stated Adam Boyd, president of consumer lending at Rate.

EquitySimple is available in 49 states and Washington, D.C. The product is not at the moment available in Texas.
