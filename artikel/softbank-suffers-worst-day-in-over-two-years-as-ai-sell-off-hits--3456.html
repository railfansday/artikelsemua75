📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Shares in Japan's SoftBank Group closed 14.48% lower on Friday, marking their worst day since August 2024, as a broader sell-off swept through Asian technology stocks following the highly anticipated market debut of artificial intelligence startup Anthropic.

The sharp decline underscores growing investor concerns over SoftBank's aggressive bets on artificial intelligence, particularly its exposure to OpenAI, as markets question whether massive investments in AI infrastructure will generate sufficient returns. The group is one of the biggest backers of OpenAI, Anthropic's rival, having invested a total of $64.6 billion in the ChatGPT maker.

It has been revealed that softBank's heavy exposure to OpenAI as well as its ownership of chip designer Arm has made its shares particularly sensitive to shifts in sentiment toward the AI sector. The stock lost 30% in October and is down 31% so far this month.

Japanese semiconductor stocks also tumbled, with Tokyo Electron closing 7.06% lower, Lasertec shedding 9.21% and Renesas Electronics losing 7.02%.

The sell-off extended to South Korea, where Nvidia suppliers SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics fell 5.92% and 7.01%, respectively, pressuring the benchmark Kospi, which ended 5.21% lower.

Elsewhere in the region, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co, or TSMC, closed 4.76% lower in Taiwan.

According to recent reports, as of the last hour of trading, Chinese tech giant Tencent was down 2.01%, while memory chipmaker CXMT fell 5.31% to a fresh record low, while Baidu dropped 4.79%. AI chipmaker Enflame, dubbed as one of China's "four little dragons" fell 7.16%.

The declines in Asia come after the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite fell more than 2% overnight following Anthropic's highly anticipated Wall Street debut.
