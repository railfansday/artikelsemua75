"Don't you love New York in the fall?" Joe from You've Got Mail writes in an email about the changing seasons on the Upper West Side. After experiencing five autumns in the Substantial Apple, I can answer that question with a resounding yes. And with nearly a month of the season left (winter doesn't officially start until December 21), I'm not ready to move on just yet. It's hard not to adore the pumpkin-covered stoops and the last of the golden Central Park trees that are a part of my daily commute. And when the city you call home is the backdrop for classic fall films—like When Harry Met Sally and Autumn in New York—there's always just a moment when you envision yourself as the main character in a romcom, headphones in, playing There She Goes by The La's in the background.

Ahead, discover the five style essentials I rely on every fall and initiative to keep wearing until the first official day of winter.

According to recent reports, fall is practically synonymous with cozy comfort—and to me, that means a chunky knit sweater. Whether it be cotton, cashmere, or wool, the blanket-like jumper is one of my consistent go-tos, slipped on for movie nights and elevated dinners alike.

One of my favorite ways to wear a chunky knit sweater is over a flowing mini dress and paired with knee-high riding boots (more on that next). For a preppier take, I slip on cigarette jeans, tube socks, and soft loafers.

Falconeri Ultrasoft Cashmere Funnel Neck Sweater.

It has been revealed that earlier sunsets make me crave rich color palettes, so every fall, I gravitate towards moody wine-red accents. The amarino-cherry hue stands out thanks to the striking energy of red combined with earthy brown. Not to mention, I’ve taken a few notes from Miu Miu, Chanel, and Hermès, who’ve also signed off on the head-turning color.

Versace Signature Cat-Eye Sunglasses.

Anthropologie Minimal Claw Clips.

One of the easiest ways to spice up a fall outfit is with edgy, lug-sole boots. I love pairing my moto footwear with color-blocked barn jackets, houndstooth mini skirts, and oversized blazers. When I’m feeling adventurous, I reach for rugby shirts, bloomers, and even drapey sweatpants for early-morning coffee runs.

The over-extended neckline accentuates the collarbones, elevating my most laid-back fall looks.
