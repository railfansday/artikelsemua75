Industry News: 3 Weeks To Go: Cathay Pacific Set To Open First New York Lounge Amid T6 JFK Switch

📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that cathay Pacific has flown out of John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)'s Terminal Eight for almost 30 years, yet in a few weeks' time it will be shifting over to Terminal Six. To mark the move, the Hong Kong carrier has looks to open its first lounge in Fresh York. The official moving date is set to be December 15.

The shift to Terminal Six could cause friction for passengers hoping to connect onto a domestic flight within the US. American Airlines, Cathay's oneworld partner, is keeping its focus squarely on Terminal Eight. Such passengers will have to make the journey between the terminals. In a recent development, cathay first confirmed the move back in 2024. It stated that it would open a lounge during the move with a notable size at 10,000 square feet (930 square meters). Previously, premium passengers flying with Cathay Pacific have been directed to the Soho Lounge or the Greenwich Lounge operated by its partners in Terminal Eight. The former facility is available to Cathay first class ticket holders, and Cathay Diamond and oneworld Emerald members, along with one guest. The latter could be accessed by business class passengers. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

Sources indicate that british designer Ilse Crawford was involved in the ideation for the lounge. It will include a noodle bar, shower facilities, along with every type of modern power outlet. The lounge will also have a separate premium area created for first class and Cathay Diamond members.

In a recent development, simple Flying has contacted Cathay for further comment. Cathay Pacific Senior Vice President, Americas Chris van den Hooven mentioned during the original 2024 announcement:.

“This represents a huge milestone in Cathay Pacific’s 28 years of connecting New York with our home, Hong Kong and from 1 July it will be 20 years since we commenced our regular non-stop service. ​ It has long been our ambition to provide our customers a signature Cathay Pacific airport experience at JFK.".

Along with being the first Cathay lounge in Latest York, it will also be the airline's second in the United States, with the first being at San Francisco International Airport (SFO), pictured above. Despite the opening date now just three weeks away, information on the exact features of the lounge is still lacking in detail. Cathay notes it will be at the "forefront of our lounge proposition.".

In a recent development, the best indication of what premium passengers flying into New York can expect is revealed by a look at Cathay's SFO lounge, which opened in December 2011. The Instagram post embedded below reveals some of what passengers can find in this facility:.

Cathay notes that this lounge gives passengers the ability to enjoy an "enticing range of food options", freshen up with the shower facilities, and stay productive in the work spaces. The showers include plush towels and premium amenities, while the working facilities have dividers to stop you from getting distracted by the busy airport atmosphere. Also included are luggage storage, disability access, and newspapers and magazines to pass the time before your flight.

The upcoming lounge will mark Cathay's entrance into the newly opening Terminal Six. The facility has been built by JFK Millennium Partners. It forms part of a redevelopment by the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey that has cost $19 billion.

Other carriers that will operate there include the Star Alliance airlines, Lufthansa, SWISS, Austrian Airlines, and Brussels Airlines. The move is particularly significant for Cathay as it will be the only oneworld partner operating from the upcoming terminal, dislocating it from American Airlines. Passengers hoping to connect onto an American Airlines flight will now need to take the AirTrain between terminals, so they must make sure to allow additional time.
