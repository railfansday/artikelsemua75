📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

While retailers have been developing agents that can shop on behalf of customers, Target’s latest shopping assistant is not that—yet.

Sources indicate that in October, Target rolled out a beta version of its AI assistant, Ask Target, within its website and mobile app. The assistant finds items based on shopper preferences, shares product facts, compares items, summarizes reviews, and checks inventory availability. “We really are taking a phased approach in terms of AI and the types of problems it’s going to solve for our guests,” Purvi Shah, svp of user experience at Target, told ADWEEK.

Ask Target is now available to all shoppers after initially testing the feature with Target employees.
