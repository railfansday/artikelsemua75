Recent findings suggest that fiona the hippo has died at the Cincinnati Zoo in the US after swallowing a visitor's hat which was accidentally thrown into her enclosure. She was nine years old.

"Despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome," the zoo said. "The team performed necessary supportive care today in an attempt to save her, but it was not successful and she did not recover.". It has been revealed that fiona was born in 2017 prematurely, with the hippo becoming a social media sensation. At the time of her birth, she only weighed 29 pounds (13 kilograms) and was in a fight to survive.

It has been revealed that social media users watched Fiona's daily activities as grew up. She was named after the character in the "Shrek" film series.

Fiona's death was met with reactions from her fans and political leaders.

Second lady Usha Vance, the wife of Vice President JD Vance, posted a photo of their kids face-to-face with Fiona at her enclosure.

"We are so sad to hear the news about Fiona," Vance noted. Vice President Vance hails from the greater Cincinnati area and the US second family has a home there.

Cincinnati Mayor Aftab Pureval lauded Fiona as "a symbol of perservance and joy" in the city.

Ohio Governor-Elect Amy Acton remarked: "Fiona captured the hearts of millions and became one of Cincinnati's most beloved icons.".

"Her story brought joy, hope and a little extra magic to our state," Acton added.

It has been revealed that ohio Senator Bernie Moreno stated: "Fiona wasn't just Cincinnati's hippo, she was Ohio's fighter. Born tiny, premature and against every odd, she grew up and brought great joy to millions of us.".
