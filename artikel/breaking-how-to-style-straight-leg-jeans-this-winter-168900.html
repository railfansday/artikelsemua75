According to recent reports, all denim is timeless, but straight-leg jeans might be the most trend-resistant silhouette of the bunch. That said, they’re trending! The classic style has been making a comeback (or perhaps it never really went away), cropping up in the fall collections of Dior, Stella McCartney, Toteme, and Balenciaga. Amid the revolving door of denim trends, from ultra-baggy shapes to the much-debated return of the skinny jean, there’s something about the simplicity of a straight leg that feels very much like an aesthetic homecoming.

Industry observers note that with their clean lines and easy proportions, straight-leg jeans are a true wardrobe chameleon. They’re a key part of the model off-duty look when worn with a white tee, leather jacket, and retro sneakers, but they also look chic dressed up with a pump or heeled boot. Their streamlined silhouette lends itself well to winter layering too, balancing out both oversized knits and heavy coats. And while the shape is relatively straightforward, there’s plenty of room for interpretation: a faded blue pair can feel laid back, while a dark-wash style can look almost as polished as trousers; a bone-colored pair works a palette cleanser, while something warm and brown brings softness to the look.
