📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Pony.ai’s robotaxi fare revenue nearly quintupled year over year in the September quarter, while the firm-wide operating loss narrowed to $68.7 million from $69.7 million.

Sources indicate that in its Nov. 18 filing, Pony.ai mentioned fare revenue rose nearly 400% year over year. Revenue from its broader robotaxi services business grew 165.4% to $17.8 million from $6.7 million, helped by both passenger fares and higher vehicle deliveries to joint deployments in China and overseas.

In a recent development, in a November service enhancement, separate from its September-quarter figures, Pony.ai said the previously separate operating zones in central Guangzhou and Nansha formed one contiguous area of more than 1,000 square kilometers as of Nov. 17.

Riders can use its PonyPilot app to book trips between Nansha and central Guangzhou, where Pony.ai holds a citywide commercial permit. The area it measured in November covers the connected operating zones where the company states those bookings are available.

In a recent development, CEO James Peng mentioned PonyPilot now lets riders book across Nansha, Haizhu, Tianhe, Huangpu and Panyu. He said the wider network had increased trip distances and the average price per ride in Guangzhou.

On the November earnings call, CFO Leo Wang reported that Pony.ai’s fleets in Guangzhou and Shenzhen had earned enough in fares to more than cover the vehicle costs Pony.ai includes in its calculation.
