Standard Chartered News: Zodia Custody Data Breach Exposes Client & Staff Details

🌐 Source: Coinpedia • 📅 26 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (403 words, 📖 Standard)
💡 Key Takeaways
  • Zodia disclosed that a breach exposed limited organization details and some client and employee contact information.
  • The organization mentioned its investigation had found no unauthorized transactions so far.
  • The incident raises phishing concerns as exposed names and email addresses could aid impersonation attempts.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Zodia disclosed that a breach exposed limited organization details and some client and employee contact information.

The organization mentioned its investigation had found no unauthorized transactions so far.

The incident raises phishing concerns as exposed names and email addresses could aid impersonation attempts.

A crypto custodian can keep private keys offline and still face a security headache. The Zodia Custody data breach exposed limited client and employee information, putting contact details, not confirmed crypto assets, at the centre of the incident. Zodia detected attempts to probe its systems on November 23 and temporarily took offline software connecting institutional clients directly to its platform. But, the service has since been restored. CEO Richard Clark confirmed the breach on November 26.

In a recent development, the company determined on November 24 that an unauthorized party had accessed limited information. This included organization details and, for a small number of clients and employees, business contact names and email addresses. (Related: Tech Report: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farmin...)

In a recent development, but, that’s enough to create another problem. Exposed names paired with working email addresses can help attackers craft convincing phishing messages impersonating Zodia or its staff.

Industry observers note that however, the company said its investigation had found no unauthorized transactions so far. Its custody model keeps primary cryptographic keys offline and requires multiple approvals for withdrawals.

There is no confirmation in the disclosed information that client holdings or wallet keys were accessed.

Industry observers note that zodia has notified affected clients and regulators. The incident comes weeks after Standard Chartered increased its stake to 100%, bringing the custody business into its digital asset operations.

Standard Chartered shares listed in London rose 2.6% on November 26, the day the breach was reported. That market move doesn’t answer the questions facing affected customers.

Industry observers note that the Zodia Custody data breach makes targeted impersonation a practical concern. Clients should treat unexpected messages requesting withdrawal changes or transfer approvals with suspicion, and verify requests through contact details already held on file.

The investigation’s findings still remain limited. But, how many people were really affected, and whether additional information was accessed or not. Also, how the intrusion occurred have still not been disclosed yet.
