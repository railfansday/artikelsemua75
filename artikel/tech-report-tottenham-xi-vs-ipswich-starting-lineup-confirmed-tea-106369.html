Sources indicate that spurs sorely lacked a creative threat against Leeds. But despite naming Maddison on the bench at Elland Road, De Zerbi didn't bring him on in search of a goal, raising concerns about his fitness.

Maddison has struggled to train consistently since the start of the season and came off midway through Spurs’ 2-0 win over Chelsea with a suspected groin problem.

Recent findings suggest that the 29-year-old has not featured since that historic victory at Stamford Bridge, despite being available for selection. After working on his fitness throughout the international break, he returns to the starting lineup here.

Mathys Tel is expected to be out for up to three weeks with an Achilles injury sustained following a heavy tackle from Leeds defender Jayden Bogle.

There is a fresh injury concern for De Zerbi, though, as Sandro Tonali has missed out on the matchday squad altogether, with the reason for his absence presently unconfirmed.
