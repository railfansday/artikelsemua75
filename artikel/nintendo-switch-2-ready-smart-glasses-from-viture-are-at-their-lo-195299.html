Viture’s smart display glasses offer official Switch 2 compatibility with the Mobile Dock Mini, and these Black Friday deals even come with free bonus controllers and other accessories.

One of the most annoying things about playing Ocarina of Time right now is that everyone in my home is at a different point in their save file. This makes it tough to be in the same room with others and not spoil something for them, but plugging in a pair of Viture glasses makes this much easier since no one else can see the screen inside the glasses I’m wearing. According to recent reports, you can get your own pair of Viture glasses with the Switch 2 Mobile Dock Mini for as low as $375. This pack comes with Viture Pro 2 glasses, which sport a pair of 120Hz micro-OLED displays with 1,600-nit peak brightness and built-in myopia adjustment diopters. They also weigh just 63g, making them comfortable for long gaming sessions.

The Mobile Dock Mini (on sale for $99 on its own) in the pack comes with a magnetic mount that attaches to the back of the Switch 2. This Dock lets you plug your Viture glasses in and also charges the Switch 2, thanks to the built-in 10,000mAh battery. That delivers more than double the Switch 2’s battery life, so you can clear several temples in Ocarina before even thinking of stopping to charge.

In a recent development, most of Viture’s glasses are on sale and have extra free gifts, which you can see in the image above. This includes the organization’s high-end Viture Beast model, which is on sale for $489 ($60 off). That’s the lowest price ever for the glasses. According to recent reports, viture Beast upgrades the experience with a wider field of view (58 degrees), native 3DoF spatial tracking, and 9-level electrochromic lens dimming. Native spatial tracking means that you can pin the virtual monitor in space and move around as if it were a TV mounted on a wall. Beast doesn’t have diopters, so you’ll need to order prescription lenses if you need myopia adjustment.

The biggest discount of all is on Viture Luma Pro, which is down to $339 ($160 off). Luma Pro is Viture’s “Goldilocks” pair with excellent image quality and RGB lighting along the frames for some extra style, and even includes myopia adjustment diopters. Pair any of these Viture glasses with the Mobile Dock Mini for Switch 2 compatibility, making them the only pair of smart glasses with official Switch 2 support.

Lastly is Viture Luma Ultra, down to $449 ($150 off). Luma Ultra sports the best image quality of all Viture glasses and includes myopia-adjusting diopters. These are the only pair that support 6DoF tracking when using the Viture Spacewalker app thanks to the cameras on the front, but there’s no native 3DoF support like Beast has, so this functionality is limited to content you view through the app.
