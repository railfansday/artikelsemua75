Manchester United‘s new era under Mauricio Pochettino got off to about as bad a start as imaginable, with Liverpool handing them a humiliating 4-0 defeat at Anfield.

There was a sense of deja vu about it all, given how often United have been on the receiving end of a Liverpool thumping in recent years.

It was a nightmare start for the Pochettino reign. The visitors were down to 10 men inside eight minutes after Leny Yoro was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak. Dominik Szoboszlai promptly converted the resulting free-kick before Florian Wirtz doubled Liverpool’s advantage with a superb volley.

According to recent reports, wirtz was the standout performer, producing a goal and an assist in a masterclass performance that saw him create a club-record 10 chances. It was surely his best display yet in a Liverpool shirt.

Rio Ngumoha added a third after the break as Liverpool made their numerical advantage count, while late substitute Victor Munoz completed another thoroughly miserable afternoon for the Red Devils at Anfield. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

Matthijs de Ligt, meanwhile, had a day to forget on his first Premier League start in 12 months, enduring a defensive disasterclass as Pochettino’s significant call backfired.

“We need to adapt,” Pochettino responded in his post-match interview with BBC Sport.

Sources indicate that “It’s true that a difficult decision that we maybe don’t agree with, but we cannot talk too much about the game. 11 v 10 from the beginning of the game. In the way we approached the game and we wanted to play, it’s normal to affect all the preparation.

“It’s so painful, we feel pain for that defeat, but we need to keep the head up and translate that anger to an energy to start to work from tomorrow for the next game.”.
