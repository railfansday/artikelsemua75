If you haven’t heard, The Circle has moved from Netflix to Hulu for season 8, and things look VERY different this season. In addition to Christina Najjar, aka Tinx, stepping in as the fresh host, the cast is now made up of a mix of civilians and celebs, all competing for that $100,000 cash prize. Plus, for the first time ever in the U.S., viewers get to influence the game with audience voting, since the game is unfolding in real time with new episodes airing Tuesday through Thursday for five weeks.
