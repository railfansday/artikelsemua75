AC Milan had plenty of reasons to smile after some good performances in Belgium’s game against Italy on Sunday night.

According to recent reports, with the final Nations League games underway, Milan hoped their players would perform well to return to Milanello with confidence, and it’s fair to say they did. Indeed, Italy’s trip to Belgium provided that.

It has been revealed that starting with Diego Moreira, the winger performed excellently in the opener of the international break and he’s followed that up with a goal – his first for his country at a senior level – in the second game. On a fast counterattack, Moreira was there to finish the chance on his left foot and it’s another contribution for him this season.

That wasn’t the only thing to take from the game, though. After not being included in the squad for the first game, Alphadjo Cisse was named on the bench by Roberto Mancini and he made an impact when the Italian gave him a chance to.

According to recent reports, coming on in the 62nd minute – with the Azzurri 3-1 down – it was hoped that he could provide a spark for the visitors to get back into the game and he certainly did. Although Pio Esposito missed it, the youngster won a penalty in the 91st minute and he had a goal disallowed, showing what he can do on the senior stage.
