In a recent development, google unveiled on November 23, 2026 that it is starting to roll out Google Wallet as an opt-in connection for Personal Intelligence in Search’s AI Mode. Drive and Gemini app connections are planned for the weeks after the announcement, bringing spending, document and chat context to personalized Search responses.

The new and planned connections expand the existing Gmail, Calendar and Photos sources. AI Overviews and AI Mode will also produce more responses based on recent search context, so someone researching a trip can continue in a new thread without repeating earlier details. Together, the changes push Search toward carrying context across tasks instead of treating each query as self-contained. (Related: Transformer-based Encoder-Decoder Models...)

Recent findings suggest that with Wallet connected, Google indicates AI Mode can help owners map financial milestones, identify ways to reduce everyday spending and optimize cash-back categories, travel perks and reward points using cards linked through Wallet. The connection is read-only: AI Mode can read account information but cannot move, manage or invest the user’s money. (Related: How to train a newly introduced language model from scr...)

Sources indicate that google indicates Drive support will draw context from documents, spreadsheets and PDFs, with a personalized tax-season preparation plan as one example. The forthcoming Gemini app connection will share context in both directions, allowing an idea explored in Gemini to provide the foundation for an AI Mode response and vice versa. Search history could already be connected to Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app; the new option will bring Gemini app context into AI Mode.

Google began rolling out Wallet in the Gemini app on November 13, 2026 for all U.S. Gemini app users across web, Android and iOS. That earlier launch means the November 23 development extends Wallet’s role into Search rather than representing its first Gemini integration.

Industry observers note that connections sit under Connected Apps in Search Personalization. Owners decide whether to opt in and can turn individual connections on or off at any time. Google also looks a Personalization icon below tailored AI Mode responses that will show the context used; people can correct details directly in a thread or disable personalization through Search Settings.

Google’s March 17, 2026 expansion made Personal Intelligence available in AI Mode in the U.S. and started rolling it out to free-tier Gemini and Gemini in Chrome owners. The connected experiences are limited to personal Google accounts rather than Workspace business, enterprise or education accounts, placing this expansion in Google’s consumer AI business.

On data use, Google indicates Gemini and AI Mode do not train directly on a user’s Gmail inbox or Photos library. They do train on limited information, including specific prompts and model responses, to improve functionality over time. For those sources, the distinction is between referencing personal material to answer a request and training directly on the underlying inbox or photo collection.

Google’s January 14, 2026 beta introduction described remaining risks: testing had not eliminated inaccurate responses or over-personalization, including mistaken connections between unrelated topics. The model could also misread timing or relationship changes and infer interests without understanding the reason behind an activity.

The update makes Search more continuous across financial planning, document-based projects and research, while giving those documented failure modes a wider set of personal inputs. That makes the planned context display and correction tools part of the core product: the value of each new connection depends on whether users can see what Search used and fix it when the inference is wrong.
