Amazon Kindle Paperwhite, 16GB: For the entrepreneur who has time to read.

This Kindle Paperwhite has a seven-inch glare-free display, 16GB of storage and battery life of up to 12 weeks. For an entrepreneur, this gift could allow them to read a biography on a flight, return to a novel after closing the shop or finally start the book a friend recommended. Think twice before buying if the person is devoted to paper books or has never expressed an interest in e-readers. This Kindle is $70 off for Black Friday, making it $130 total.

reMarkable Paper Pro bundle with Marker Plus and black leather Book Folio: For the handwritten thinker.
