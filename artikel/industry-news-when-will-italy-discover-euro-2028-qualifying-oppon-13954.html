After missing out on a spot in the Nations League quarter-finals, Italy’s next match will be the first of their EURO 2028 qualifying campaign during the March international break: Here is everything you need to know about the upcoming draw and qualifying process.

Italy will begin their EURO 2028 qualifying campaign during the next international break, which officially kicks off on March 22, 2027.

The Azzurri will learn their EURO 2028 qualifying opponents when the draw takes place in Belfast on Sunday, December 6.

In a recent development, as Italy finished within the top three of the top tier of Nations League groups, they will be among the seeded nations in the EURO 2028 qualifying draw. Joining the Azzurri in the top pot will be France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Greece, Germany, Spain, England, Portugal, Denmark, Norway and Croatia. Each nation that qualifies at the top of their group, along with the eight-best runners up, will qualify directly for the final tournament.

The four remaining runners up along with the best non-qualified group winners from the 2026-27 Nations League, will be involved in the qualification play-offs for EURO 2028, which will take place in March, 2028.

Recent findings suggest that the number of nations competing in the play-offs depends on the number of host nations that qualify. Between two to four additional spots will be up for grabs in the play-offs, depending on how many of the host nations – England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic or Ireland – qualify directly for the final tournament.
