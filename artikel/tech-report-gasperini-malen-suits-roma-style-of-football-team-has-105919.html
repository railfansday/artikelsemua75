Recent findings suggest that gian Piero Gasperini sees Roma ‘confidence’ growing after their 5-1 victory in Parma, and explains why four-goal hero Donyell Malen has been so unstoppable in Serie A. ‘He fits with our style of football.’.

The Giallorossi maintained their two-point gap from Serie A leaders Lazio thanks to what ultimately turned into a comfortable jaunt at the Stadio Tardini this evening, with Malen scoring four goals around the Rodrigo Mora effort. Nesta Elphege had temporarily managed to draw Parma level on the counter-attack, but it spurred Roma into a strong response.

“I am very happy, because there is always some uncertainty when players go off and return at the last minute,” Gasperini told DAZN Italia. (Related: Industry News: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crash...)

“We wanted to see if we could pick up where we left off, and the result doesn’t reflect how difficult today’s match was. Parma were very determined, started strong, but over the course of the 90 minutes we took more control, albeit not as much as the result would suggest.”.

The Giallorossi have 36 goals in the opening 12 Serie A rounds, more than any other team in the last 76 years, since Inter and Milan got 38 and Juventus 37, all in 1950/51.

Malen alone is responsible for 12 of those goals, but if we look at the calendar year since his arrival from Aston Villa in January, the Dutchman has 26 Serie A goals in 2026.

That is an all-time club record, jointly with Pedro Manfredini in 1960, but it is still only November 21 and there are a lot more games to be played.

In a recent development, “We can always improve, both individually and as a team, but undoubtedly this side is growing. We conceded a bad goal at a tough moment of the match, but I saw a strong reaction from the team, and that indicates the confidence is there.

“When we are faced with a negative or unlucky moment, we have the confidence to keep playing and get over it.”.

Malen has scored three hat-tricks in 2026 for Roma, and curiously the last player to achieve this in Serie A was also under Gasperini, namely Josip Ilicic for Atalanta.

“Malen was already a strong striker in the Netherlands, Borussia Dortmund and Aston Villa, you don’t play at that level without quality. Perhaps I played him where he expresses himself in the best way.

In a recent development, “There are a lot of good strikers in Serie A, including young talents, and of course Lautaro Martinez, but right now Malen is extraordinary. He combines so well with our team, we perhaps get more out of Malen than we would do with a striker who is stronger on paper, but doesn’t fit as well with our style of football,” concluded Gasperini.
