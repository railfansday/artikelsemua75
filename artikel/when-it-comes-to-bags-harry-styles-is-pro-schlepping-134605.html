After completing his Together, Together residency at Upcoming York’s Madison Square Garden last month, Harry Styles deserved a little R&R. Where did the singer choose to spend that little smidgen of time off before he traveled to Australia for his next leg? Rome, Italy, of course.

Styles was spotted around one of his favorite cities, most recently grabbing lunch with the architect and designer Matidia Pallini at the beginning of this weekend. (His fiancé, Zoë Kravitz, meanwhile, has been holding things down back in Manhattan.).

Naturally, Styles’s latest outing served up a perfectly casual-cool outfit. A sleek khaki trench coat? Check! Classic straight-leg jeans and brown leather loafers? Check-check. The whole outfit felt modern, yet discrete—except for that substantial, honking bag. The large black leather Chanel maxi flap bag, with silver hardware, immediately caught our eye. (Related: AprielGuard: A Guardrail for Safety and Adversarial Rob...)

It has been revealed that designed by Mathieu Blazy, the XXL Chanel flap has been one of the designer’s biggest hits while helming the French label. Everyone is going crazy for the huge, unstructured bags, which retail for over $13,000. Fellow stars like A$AP Rocky and Kendall Jenner have been carting them around their shoulder, too. Others, like Rosalía, find their capacious carry-alls elsewhere. (Related: Industry News: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons...)

It’s not hard to understand why the luxurious maxi bag is such a huge hit. It fits a lot, for one, making it a utilitarian and practical choice for schlepping one’s belongings around town. (For that reason, it also makes it a great travel bag.).

It has been revealed that harry Styles is clearly pro-schlepping: Despite once carrying mini bags, he’s been seen wearing the very same Chanel style in a leopard-printed version, making the case for carting around ginormous bags that can carry, well, just about anything. What do you think Styles has in his spacious (but not ludicrously capacious) bag, exactly? Some prototypes of his Pleasing merch or products? An extra microphone, for when he’s ready to burst out into song? Use your imagination, Stylers.
