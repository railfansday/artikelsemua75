‘That’s for you, Tony’: Blackburn’s Andri Gudjohnsen dedicates derby goal to Tony Mowbray

Blackburn’s Andri Gudjohnsen paid a touching tribute to the absent Tony Mowbray after scoring in Sunday’s ferocious East Lancashire derby. (Related: School sends out minibus to catch absent pupils playing...)

The club unveiled last week that Mowbray was stepping down as Blackburn manager due to health problems.

Damien Johnson and Mike Dodds have taken charge on an interim basis, leading Rovers for their trip to fierce rivals Burnley.

And after falling behind to Largie Ramazani’s effort in the 27th minute, Gudjohnsen fired home just four minutes later to put the visitors on level terms.

The Icelandic forward celebrated by dedicating his goal to his former manager, holding up a shirt that read: “We’re with you, Tony.”.

Gudjohnsen, who is Blackburn’s top scorer with 10 goals this term, then looked into the camera and said: “That’s for you, Tony!”.

Blackburn players also warmed up wearing shirts in Mowbray’s honour. (Related: GTA 6 players report game-breaking crashes on PS5 and X...)

Rovers fans in the away end at Turf Moor could be heard chanting Mowbray’s name during the match.

Mowbray was diagnosed with bowel cancer in early 2024 and had to stand down from his role at Birmingham.

He was later given the all-clear after a spell at West Brom last year, he joined Blackburn for a second spell in June.

Mowbray had sparked something of a resurgence at Ewood Park, with Blackburn finding themselves just three points off the play-offs in 13th going into the weekend despite starting the season among favourites for relegation.

However, he has been forced to step away from work again due to his health.

“Unfortunately, due to health complications, I have to step back from my role as Head Coach of Blackburn Rovers,” Mowbray said in a statement.

“I’m immensely proud of this group of players. Their dedication and professionalism have given us some memorable performances and wins, and I’ve loved working with them.

Industry observers note that “Thank you to my coaching staff for all your hard work and for the support you’ve given me. It’s been a pleasure working alongside you.

“To the fans, thank you for the way you’ve supported me and the team this season. I’ve always tried to give you a team that you can be proud of. Your support has meant a great deal to me.

“I’m grateful to the Owners and Suhail [Shaikh, Blackburn COO] for giving me the opportunity to lead this great club once again.

Industry observers note that “I’d ask that you give me and my family privacy at this time. Thank you for your understanding.”.

Mowbray previously managed Blackburn for five years between 2017 and 2022, and has also held roles at the likes of Sunderland, Coventry, Middlesbrough and Celtic.
