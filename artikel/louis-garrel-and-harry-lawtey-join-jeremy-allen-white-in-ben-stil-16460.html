EXCLUSIVE: Louis Garrel and Harry Lawtey are set to join Jeremy Allen White in the Laika Live Action and A24 World War II survival thriller Airman directed by Ben Stiller.

Based on the Latest York Times bestseller The Lost Airman: A True Story of Escape from Nazi-Occupied France by Seth Meyerowitz, the film recounts the harrowing and remarkable untold story of Arthur Meyerowitz, who was shot down over Nazi-occupied France and evaded Gestapo pursuers for months before attempting a daring escape. The book was published in 2016 by Penguin.

Sources indicate that the script is by Ben Shattuck and Stiller. Three-time Oscar winner Emmanuel Lubezki has been tapped as director of photography, Christian Goldbeck as production designer, Małgorzata Karpiuk as costume designer and Geoffrey Richman as editor.

Industry observers note that laika Live Action will lead production alongside A24, which will also handle domestic distribution and international sales. The film is a Le Grisbi Productions and Storyteller Productions production, in association with Red Hour Films and Nine Stories. Watch on Deadline. It has been revealed that stiller will produce for his Red Hour Films as will John Lesher for his Le Grisbi Productions, PJ van Sandwijk for his Storyteller Productions, Travis Knight and Matt Levin for Laika, and A24. Executive producers are Maryse Knight, Jeremy Kipp Walker and Zac Locke for Laika, Michael Lesslie and Marie Savare for Storyteller, Jake Gyllenhaal for Nine Stories and Miki Emmerich, Richard Whelan, Meyerowitz, Dan Bodansky and James Dixon. Gian Pablo Antonetti serves as co-producer for Le Grisbi.

It has been revealed that “We’re thrilled to partner with A24, Ben and Jeremy on a film that embodies our commitment to bold, filmmaker-driven cinema,” mentioned Matt Levin, Laika’s President of Live Action Film & Series. “Ben is an extraordinary talent with a singular creative vision, and we couldn’t be more excited to bring this incredible true story to life together.”.

Stiller is represented by WME, VIE and Sloane, Offer, Weber & Dern. White is represented by WME. Garrel is represented by UTA and Agence Adequat. Lawtey is represented by CAA, B-Side and Goodman, Genow, Schenkman, Smelkinson & Christopher.
