Industry observers note that no matter your feelings on how festive is too festive during the holiday season (for the record, we’re pro decorating your home like a European Christmas market from November to February), there’s one universal rule when it comes to beauty looks during this time that we can all agree on: The holidays were meant for glitter. And this year, we’re leaning into a more whimsical take on looking all shiny and bright. Say hello to “stardust” makeup.

Industry observers note that this is more than just putting on a glitter eyeshadow or some tinsel in your hair. “Stardust” evokes a warm, nostalgic feeling and sprinkles some magic along the way. “Adding a pop of shimmer is such an act of self-celebration, and it feels really good,” indicates Donni Davy, celebrity makeup artist and founder of Half Magic. “It’s like decorating your face the same way you buy something beautiful for your home. It’s a feel-good thing that also sparks conversation and joy in other people and inspires other people to do the same.”. (Related: Industry News: Judge blocks construction of Trump’s arc...)

“[It]’s something magical that gives us the same feeling we had as kids opening presents,” adds celebrity makeup artist Alexandra French. “I’m always letting my inner child play when creating these looks. I’ll never not be whimsical.". (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

Recent findings suggest that the most obvious way to add some sparkle is with the eyes. But for that “stardust” effect, there’s a delicate art to it. “Stardust eyes are all about creating that dreamy, ethereal twinkling effect,” notes Davy. “It's not about packing on a ton of glitter; it’s more about these delicate flashes of sparkle that catch in the light in a really magical way.”.

The key to the “stardust” effect is in the application. Compared to traditional glitter or gems, Davy says that stardust eyes are more subtle. “It’s almost like a dusting that catches the light in a totally unexpected and very flattering way.”.

Recent findings suggest that for Davy, she loves starting with a soft wash of color (pale pink, lavender, or champagne are her personal picks) on the lids, like Half Magic’s Duo-Chrome Eyeshadow in Blushing Lizard. You can also try Saie’s viral Supersuede Baked Eyeshadow in Bellini or K-beauty’s Hince Chill Wave for the same shimmer effect. Apply shadow with your fingers, she instructs, focusing on the center of the lid and then blending out. “You want the sparkle to look almost random, rather than perfectly placed,” she notes. “If you want a bit of a grungier look, you can use a brush to apply some underneath your eye as well.”.

For a more ethereal look, build up the shine. She recommends adding some color to the inner and outer waterline corners for added dimension and again all over your eyelids. “You’re going to feel like yourself, but just sparkly,” Davy states.

Sources indicate that but for those who want to go full glam, pack it all on. Think Alix Earle’s Halloween costume as a “gynoid.” Inspired by Japanese illustrator and artist Hajime Sorayama’s “sexy robot” illustrations, French leaned all the way in with silver.

It has been revealed that “I always noted for silver eye makeup to look truly metallic you need to layer different levels of textures and finish it off with a high-shine sparkle,” French claims. “I wanted the eyes to feel like this mask that sparkles and looks alive the way his paintings feel so alive. I wanted people to stop in their tracks when they saw it from across the room.”.

Here, she layered the Urban Decay Chrome Melt Eye Shadow in Temper Tantrum on top of the MAC Dazzleshadow Liquid Eyeshadow for that bold metallic eye. Then she topped it off with Question Beauty Silver Mirror Glitter (her pro tip: Use eyelash glue for application with this) to elevate the metallic look and get something with a more reflective sheen.

But no matter how intense you go with your eye look, both Davy and French say you can never go wrong with sparkle. “It’s a simple way to add a little something to your everyday makeup,” says French.

Why stop at just the eyes? This holiday season, experts say to sprinkle some “stardust” all over. For the body, Davy likes shimmer on the collarbones, shoulders, and décolletage. “Especially if you’re wearing something that indicates a little skin,” she states. Try Half Magic’s Glitterpuck, which she says is a water-based formula made for zero glitter fallout, or we love the Marc Jacobs Money Shot Highlighter and Eva NYC’s Lightweight Silver Hair & Body Glitter Shimmer Spray for an iridescent shine. French recommends using the One/Size On ’Til Dawn Mattifying Waterproof Setting Spray mixed with glitter. She also recommends using the Fenty Beauty Diamond Bomb All-Over Diamond Veil for some body sparkle and more. “It’s the best,” she says. “I use it in the hair as well.”.

It has been revealed that and everyone’s nails have been getting the stardust treatment as early as October, with Taylor Swift wearing a faint, gold “fairy dust”shimmer on her nails at the 2026 Academy Museum Gala. If shadow or body glitter doesn’t speak to you, stardust nails might be the perfect fit.

Sources indicate that “I love depth and dimension, and that’s exactly what this is,” says celebrity nail artist Tom Bachik. “The way this differs from just applying a cat eye or a chrome powder is by actually adding them all. It’s all about layering to get that Stardust effect. You want to have a nice shimmer and iridescence, and chunky glitter, and even a few rhinestones thrown in for good measure.”.

Inspired by Christmas, Bachik wanted to play off gold with a pink iridescent shimmer for Selena Gomez’s latest mani. It’s a subtle pop of color that gives that golden sparkle some added depth. He used the Apreś Nails Maisie Neutral Coffin Gelx Tips before painting on BS-101 and Cosmic Chrome in Etherea from his own collection. Then he used a 3-D gel for that raised gold detail for a nice added touch, and to capture all that holiday spirit. “People love a little sparkle and this is the time of year that actually gives you permission to wear it,” he states.

Industry observers note that so go get your whimsy on this holiday season. It’ll make this time just a little bit more special.
