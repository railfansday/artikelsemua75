Industry observers note that blackburn Rovers’ key midfielder Sam Morsy was absent from their 2-2 draw with Burnley after sustaining a knock in training. However, he is aiming to return to Championship action on Wednesday night against a managerless Watford.

The experienced Egyptian has been an ever-present in Rovers’ side this term since signing in the summer. Morsy has featured on 14 occasions in the league, but is facing a race to be fit in time. On his fitness, caretaker boss Damien Johnson told the Lancashire Telegraph: “Sam was touch and go for the weekend and is similar. He is still being assessed this morning. We will have more news on that after training.”.

It has been revealed that meanwhile, Tom Atcheson is under concussion protocols after coming off early against Burnley, following a hit to the head. The academy graduate has also been a regular feature with his impressive performances at the heart of defence seeing him keep his place in the Northern Ireland national team. Recent findings suggest that blackburn Rovers have been spearheaded by the sensational form of Andri Gudjohnsen, who is only behind Will Lankshear in the goalscoring charts with 11 to his name. But, it has been a collective effort to see them sit in 13th place.

Rovers were showing signs of real promise prior to Tony Mowbray stepping away from the dugout, most notably an impressive 2-1 victory over West Ham before the international break, with Morsy and Atcheson firmly part of that.

Morsy has filled the role as the anchor of Rovers’ midfield admirably and has shown his worth as one of the Championship’s finest midfield operators at 35. (Related: What Is Considered Upper Middle Class? Income and Net W...)

Atcheson meanwhile is at the opposite end of his career, but has been just as influential. He usually plays on the right-hand side of the back three and impresses with his desire to orchestrate attacks from deep.

Both Morsy and Atcheson are vital cogs in Blackburn’s machine and they will be desperate to have the pair available for a favourable-looking encounter against a troubled Watford side.

Hoping to close the gap to the play-off places to just two points with a victory, Blackburn will certainly fancy their chances with the opponents registering a solitary victory in their last five outings.

Morsy’s experience has been vital for midfield partner Kristi Montgomery who is enjoying his breakout campaign at Ewood Park, and his quality on the ball is still there for all to see at 36.

Nonetheless, Moussa Baradji stepped up to fill his shoes last time out, while Gady Beyuku also strengthened his case for a starting berth in place of Atcheson so Johnson has a wealth of options should either of his key players be unavailable.
