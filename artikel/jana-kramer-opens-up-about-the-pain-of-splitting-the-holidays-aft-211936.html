Jana Kramer is reminding divorced parents that it’s OK to feel a variety of emotions as they navigate the holidays with or without their kids.

“I think it’s hard because no matter what, it’s not normal for a mom to wake up on Christmas and not have their kids,” Kramer, 42, exclusively shared with Us before the release of her new Lifetime holiday movie, Love on the Christmas List. “This is the year that I don’t get to see my kids on Christmas morning, and I don’t know which is worse. I don’t know whether it’s worse to not have them in the morning or to say bye to them on Christmas Day.”.

Kramer shares daughter Jolie, 10, and son Jace, 7, with ex-husband Mike Caussin. She also welcomed son Roman in November 2023 with husband Allan Russell.

It has been revealed that “Last year was the first year that I didn’t have a full, complete meltdown. I just kind of had a little cry,” Kramer continued. “I don’t beat myself up for that anymore because I just think about how it’s not supposed to be a normal thing. I’m a mom who wants her kids every day, and I want to wake up to them running down the stairs on Christmas morning. And this year, I don’t get that.”.

The former One Tree Hill actress finds comfort in knowing her two oldest kids have a nice time at Caussin’s house. As many parents can relate to, however, Kramer wishes her time wasn’t cut short.

“I do know that they’re having a beautiful morning at their dad’s, and I’ll be ready to open the door for them and have a great rest of the day with them at noon on,” she noted. “But I think for any divorced parent that splits on Christmas Day, you dread the morning of not having them, or you dread the noon on Christmas Day having to say goodbye. And to this day, I don’t know which one’s worse.”.

Instead of “doomsdaying it,” Kramer has vowed to make the most of the time she does have with her kids. She’s also filled with gratitude for the healthy relationship she has with Caussin, 39, after their public divorce in 2021.

Industry observers note that “I think it’s progress on both or all of our ends, you know,” she said. “But you just want your kids to be happy, and you know that they are. That’s the win right there.”. (Related: Industry News: Train a Sentence Embedding Model with 1B...)

Before Santa arrives for Christmas, Kramer is celebrating Thanksgiving with plenty of food, family and gratitude.

This holiday season, the actress stars in a new Lifetime movie Love on the Christmas List, which is part of It’s a Wonderful Lifetime.

“I would say this is very similar to all the moms that are on the hunt for the best toy for their kids,” Kramer teased about the film. “For this movie, it’s about whether you do what you need to do, whether it’s for your job or for your child, and in the middle of that, you might find a little romance along the way.”.

That love interest may just be actor Ignacyo Matynia, who plays a widowed dad also on a quest to find a special gift for his daughter.

“He’s wonderful. He’s great,” Kramer noted of her costar. “I’ve never met him before, but he’s such a good actor. We had a lot of fun together.”.

With the holiday movie season officially underway, the host of iHeartRadio’s “Whine Down” podcast is grateful that her movies are part of so many family celebrations. She’s also happy to be in a place to experience the magic all around her.

In a recent development, “I think for a long time, I’ve felt unsteady and just like an anxious heart, and I just feel like my children are happy and healthy right now,” she told Us. “I’m healthy, I’m happy. I’ve got a beautiful little family, and I’m really grateful for that because I know that there are a lot of people going through a lot of things, and whether it’s with health or sad hearts, and I just feel for the first time, I feel a very peaceful heart, and that makes me happy.”.
