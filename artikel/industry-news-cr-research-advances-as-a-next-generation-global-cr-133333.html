Driving Clinical Development Leadership Through Global Expansion and Innovation.

SEOUL, South Korea, Nov. 22, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Founded in 1997, C&R Research (KOSDAQ: 359090) pioneered Korea's contract research organization (CRO) industry. With over 2,800 clinical projects, the manufacturer is expanding internationally while positioning AI and healthcare data as latest engines of growth. It has been revealed that as drug development grows more complex and regulatory requirements vary across markets, integrated global capabilities have become essential. Through its operations in the United States, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, C&R Research has conducted multi-regional clinical trials (MRCTs) in more than 25 countries, supporting Korean companies in global trials and international companies in Korea and Asia.

With extensive experience in global regulatory affairs, including U.S. FDA requirements, C&R Research has strengthened its international presence. Global inbound contracts in 2025 reached 2.6 times the 2023 level. In 2026, the manufacturer was recognized among the Global Top 50 CROs and Asia-Pacific Top 25 CROs.

Combining Clinical Expertise with AI and Healthcare Data.

It has been revealed that AI and healthcare data are central to C&R Research's future competitiveness. As technology transforms clinical trial design, patient recruitment and data analysis, digital capabilities have become a key differentiator for CROs.

Recent findings suggest that C&R Research is enhancing its services by integrating AI and healthcare data with its clinical expertise. It is digitizing trial operations through eClinical solutions, exploring AI applications to improve efficiency, and expanding the use of healthcare data in clinical research. By leveraging the expertise of its affiliates Trial Informatics and Mediplexus, the firm aims to deliver integrated clinical development services powered by technology and data.

Sustainable Growth Through Global Reach and Innovation.

C&R Research plans to broaden its global network and strengthen its AI and healthcare data initiatives, while meeting the clinical quality and operational standards expected by global pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies across diverse markets.

Approaching its 30th anniversary in 2027, C&R Research aims to become a global CRO that pairs clinical expertise with technological innovation to help bring innovative therapies to patients faster and more efficiently.
