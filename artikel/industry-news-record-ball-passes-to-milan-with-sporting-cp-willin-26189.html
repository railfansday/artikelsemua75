A report from Portugal has claimed that Sporting CP will not stand in Goncalo Inacio’s way if he wishes to join AC Milan even in January.

After the attempt in the summer which failed, Milan seem ready for round two of their Inacio pursuit. The Rossoneri received a ‘no’ from Sporting towards the end of August but are determined to launch a fresh bid for the Portuguese international in the winter transfer window. According to recent reports, the Lions’ captain is someone that Amorim known well and believes will fit both his systems (the three and four-man defence) and his style of play. The idea is for Inacio to come in and start immediately, to bolster the rearguard.

Sources indicate that gerry Cardinale – the owner of AC Milan – still has the aim of providing Amorim with the best players to lead the team back to the Champions League next season. Meanwhile, Milan know in advance that the centre-back’s release clause is set at €60m.

As Record report, from Sporting’s side they are clear: the Lisbon club are willing to listen to offers and to let Inacio leave if they receive a significant sum in the winter transfer window.

This season the Alcochete academy graduate was an undisputed starter under Rui Borges and will be under Paulo Fonseca too. Despite being called up by Jorge Jesus to the national team, Inácio has yet to play any minutes under the new coach.

The time might be right because of Inacio’s desire to pursue other opportunities, as well as the fact that he already worked with Amorim at Sporting between 2020 and 2024, where they won three league titles, two League Cups, one Portuguese Cup and one Super Cup together.
