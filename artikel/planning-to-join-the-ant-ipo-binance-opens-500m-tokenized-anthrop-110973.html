Industry observers note that the ANT IPO campaign uses an allocation system based on Binance Alpha Points. Users with more than zero Alpha Points receive a subscription quota of $20,000. Higher balances unlock substantially larger limits. Users holding more than 40 points qualify for $100,000, while those exceeding 100 points receive $200,000.

Sources indicate that the highest tier requires more than 200 Alpha Points and unlocks a $500,000 subscription quota. Binance also offers an additional $50,000 quota to eligible users holding its Trenchers Badge.

It has been revealed that these figures are the maximum subscription limits, not guaranteed allocations. The campaign uses an oversubscription model, meaning successful applicants could receive full or partial allocations.

Recent findings suggest that participants must commit USDC through a non-custodial smart contract. Unused funds will return to their Binance Wallet after allocation calculations finish. The subscription also carries a 5% partner service fee charged by xStocks. Binance Wallet does not charge an additional participation fee.

Recent findings suggest that binance has set an indicative subscription price between $70 and $78 per token. This matches Anthropic’s proposed IPO pricing range in its registration filing. Anthropic plans to offer 825 million Class A shares under the proposed listing. The manufacturer has applied to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

It has been revealed that at the upper end of its proposed price range, the offering implies a valuation of approximately $1.41 trillion. At $70 per share, the implied valuation falls to $1.26 trillion.

The final offering price will depend on demand, valuation conditions, and arrangements between the issuer and underwriters.

Sources indicate that the distribution process begins after the application window closes. Successful participants will receive their tokenized exposure after the underlying issuance is completed.
