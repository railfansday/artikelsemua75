The Levi’s Black Friday Sale is easy money. The Don of Denim has been the default brand for quality jeans—as well as every other wardrobe staple—for a century and a half, and it’s consistently been a go-to for bang-for-your-buck goods. So the fact that Levi’s is doing a Black Friday sale is basically like hitting the NOS switch on your neon-green ‘95 Eclipse, the only difference being that you’re actually guaranteed to come out with a win.

Recent findings suggest that from brushed flannel shirts fit for a lumberjack to heirloom-level outerwear to, of course, legendary five-pocket blue jeans, Levi’s is doling out a grip of Black Friday deals with sitewide discounts up to 40% off. The best part? “Free shipping?” Good guess, but no. Free shipping is lumped in with some of the best Black Friday menswear deals going, but the actual “best part” is that you don’t even have to wait until Friday to snag the deals. The Levi’s Black Friday Sale starts today. Jumping the gun like that would end up poorly if this were a good ol’ fashioned illegal street race. In this case, it gets you kitted out in fresh gear.
