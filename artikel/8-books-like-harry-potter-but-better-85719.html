It is an undeniable fact that the Harry Potter novels are among the most influential book series of all time. With over 600 million copies sold worldwide, J.K. Rowling’s fantasy novels have made an indelible mark on the genre, shaping both literature and global culture. That said, just because it’s the best-selling book series of all time doesn’t mean it’s the best book series of all time.

There have been several great fantasy books released over the decades that share similarities with Harry Potter but deliver much better stories and experiences. Not all of them have achieved comparable levels of fame and success, but their quality and significance to the genre are undeniable. With that in mind, here’s our selection of books that are like Harry Potter, but better.

Le Guin’s A Wizard of Earthsea is a classic fantasy novel that’s widely credited with establishing the magical academy setting later used in the Harry Potter series. Set in the titular archipelago, the book follows Ged, a young and impulsive mage who accidentally unleashes a shadow into the world. Haunted by this shadow, Ged sets off on a journey, mastering his powers and gaining deeper wisdom to confront it. (Related: Grandma who accidentally invited a stranger to Thanksgi...)

A Wizard of Earthsea may have a few cosmetic similarities to Harry Potter, but the novel exists on a whole other level, taking a philosophical approach to magic that’s about a lot more than magic duels and good vs. evil. A pioneering work of modern fantasy, the book won the 1969 Boston Globe-Horn Book Award and was one of the final recipients of the Lewis Carroll Shelf Award in 1979. It’s had a strong influence on the fantasy genre ever since and was followed by five more books that collectively make up the Earthsea Cycle. According to recent reports, the debut novel of Irish playwright Derek Landy, Skulduggery Pleasant (also known as Skulduggery Pleasant: Scepter of the Ancients or simply The Scepter of the Ancients), is the first book of the novel series. A genre-bending urban fantasy mystery, the book introduces the young Stephanie Edgley, who inherits her late uncle’s estate and soon finds herself targeted by the forces of a dangerous necromancer. Thankfully, she gets some magical help from the titular mage, a skeleton detective/wizard.

Industry observers note that skulduggery Pleasant was quite favorably received at the time of its publication and went on to win the Red House Children's Book Award and the Hampshire Book Award, as well as several other accolades. It’s an engaging dark fantasy that easily rivals Harry Potter for entertainment, blending horror, mystery, and fantasy, with a relatable protagonist and a fascinating cast of interesting characters. The novel has since spawned a massive series that includes 27 books, the latest, A Small Matter of Impending Catastrophe, released in 2025.

One of the books most commonly recommended to fans of Harry Potter, Percy Jackson & the Olympians: The Lightning Thief is the first novel in Rick Riordan’s bestselling Camp Half-Blood Chronicles franchise. The book introduces readers to 12-year-old Percy Jackson, the demigod son of the Greek god Poseidon, who discovers the secret of his parentage just in time to be caught in the middle of a potential war. His only option to prevent this catastrophe is to head out on a cross-country quest alongside his friends, Annabeth Chase and Grover Underwood.

Industry observers note that witty, energetic, and highly endearing, The Lightning Thief is a fun take on Greek mythology that takes audiences into the fascinating world of ancient gods and monsters. It’s an engaging coming-of-age fantasy novel and a great entry point into the Camp Half-Blood Chronicles, which has since evolved into a massive world. The novel was adapted as a 2010 film by Chris Columbus and more recently adapted for the Disney+ series Percy Jackson and the Olympians, co-created by Riordan.

Sources indicate that the first novel of Eoin Colfer’s book series, Artemis Fowl is a young adult fantasy novel centered on the titular character, a 12-year-old criminal mastermind. With the assistance of his loyal bodyguard Butler, Artemis sets out to capture an elf police officer, setting off a major hostage crisis in the fairy world. The book was followed by ten sequels split across two cycles.

Industry observers note that since its publication in 2001, Artemis Fowl has been highly acclaimed by critics and very popular with readers, developing a sizable fanbase. Combining a sharp sense of humor with intelligent plotting, the book and the series it spawned are easily among the best of 21st-century fantasy, with the series selling more than 21 million copies around the world. A film based on the novel was released in 2020 to highly negative reviews, but though that adaptation was a failure, an animated series is currently in development.

First published in Germany in 2003, Inkheart is a young adult fantasy novel by Cornelia Funke that’s the first book in the series. The novel follows Meggie, a young girl who lives with her father Mortimer, a bookbinder. A visit by a strange man named Dustfinger upends Meggie's and Mortimer’s lives, and Meggie learns that her father has a rare ability to conjure things out of books by reading aloud from them.

A fascinating fantasy book that’s partly a coming-of-age story and partly a celebration of storytelling, Inkheart was quite favorably received at the time of its publication. The novel became a finalist for the 2004 BookSense Book of the Year Award for Children's Literature and won other accolades as well. The book was followed by three sequels, 2005’s Inkspell, 2007’s Inkdeath, and 2023’s The Colour of Revenge, and a film adaptation was released in 2008.

Recent findings suggest that A cozy fantasy romance novel written by Sangu Mandanna, The Very Secret Society of Irregular Witches centers on young witch Mika Moon. The story is set in a world where witches live in secrecy and isolation. Born (and orphaned) in India but now living in the UK, Mika deals with her loneliness by making fake roleplay videos where she “pretends” to be a witch, but when someone recognizes her magic as real, Mika finds herself caring for three young witches living with non-magical adults in a place called Nowhere House.

Sangu Mandanna's earlier books before The Very Secret Society of Irregular Witches were all middle-grade and young adult fiction, and the novel is her adult debut. As such, the book is suited to a more mature audience than the average Harry Potter reader, but it is a great read for adults who grew up dreaming of Hogwarts. There’s no magical school, and the conflicts of the book are more interpersonal than world-ending, but it’s a delightful read that’s as cozy and warm as a Studio Ghibli movie.

According to recent reports, lev Grossman’s The Magicians is the first book of the new adult fantasy trilogy of the same name. The novel is centered on Quentin Coldwater, a young high school graduate who grew up reading a Chronicles of Narnia-esque book series set in a magical world called Fillory. After he is accepted into a secret magical college, Quentin discovers that Fillory and its magic are real — and a lot more dangerous than he thought.

Recent findings suggest that often compared to Harry Potter, The Magicians’ first and most obvious distinction is that its magical school is a university, where magical studies involve a lot less wand-waving and a lot more actual studying. Beyond that, the book and its sequels explore significantly more complex and delightfully weird concepts that are highly engaging for fans of modern fantasy. A TV adaptation premiered on Syfy in 2015, and The Magicians was followed by two sequels, 2011’s The Magician King and 2014’s The Magician’s Land, as well as two comic book series.

It has been revealed that written by Philip Pullman, His Dark Materials is a dark fantasy trilogy that consists of 1995’s Northern Lights (published as The Golden Compass in North America), 1997’s The Subtle Knife, and 2000’s The Amber Spyglass. The first book introduces the central character, Lyra Belacqua, a young child from a universe where people’s souls manifest outside their bodies as animal companions. Lyra’s adventures bring her into conflict with the theocratic rulers of her world, the Magisterium, as she learns more about the nature of the mysterious multiversal substance known as Dust.

Combining parallel universes and dark matter with fantasy concepts and a compelling coming-of-age story, His Dark Materials is a class apart from other fantasy books. A true fantasy masterpiece, the trilogy has earned critical acclaim and won several accolades, including the Carnegie Medal. The books have inspired multiple adaptations, including a 2007 film and a BBC-HBO TV series, and the original trilogy was followed by four short works and a sequel trilogy, the latest of which is 2025’s The Rose Field.
