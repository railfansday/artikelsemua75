Electron microscopy reveals the headquarters of poxviruses

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 23 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (87 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

In a recent development, mpox remains a smoldering threat, with recent clusters in Brazil, Thailand and Austria and ongoing hot spots across multiple African countries. Other poxviruses also endanger human or animal health. A new study by the Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA) uncovers the inner workings of the poxvirus core: how its gene-copying machines sit parked in dormant pairs, ready to print viral instructions and release them into the host cell through dedicated export portals embedded in the core shell.
