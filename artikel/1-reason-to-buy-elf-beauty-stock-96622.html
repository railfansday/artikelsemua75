Sources indicate that e.l.f. Beauty (ELF -5.01%) has been on a tear. This top cosmetics stock closed at $111.21 on Nov. 17, up 53% from a year earlier.

After a run like that, investors might be hesitant to buy it in fear of buying at the top before a pullback. But the best reason to buy it is the business's momentum in a challenging consumer spending environment.

NYSE · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/20/26.

According to recent reports, e.l.f. just posted its 31st straight quarter of net sales growth. Net sales jumped 51% year over year in the September-ended quarter. Sales were inflated by the addition of Rhode, the beauty brand e.l.f. bought in August 2025. Excluding Rhode, sales grew about 35% year over year.

Recent findings suggest that management expects the strong results to continue. "With this momentum, we are raising our full-year net sales guidance to 22% to 24%," CEO Tarang Amin said. This works out to organic growth — which excludes the impact of acquisitions — of 9% to 11% for the fiscal year, up from the previous guidance of 6% to 7%.

Industry observers note that the risk is that e.l.f. relies on a small number of retailers for a large share of its sales. It sells affordable makeup and skin care through retailers such as Target, Walmart, and Amazon. A loss of just one major store that sells its brands could dent the company's sales and momentum. According to recent reports, still, the stock looks reasonably priced. It trades at about 27 times next year's consensus earnings estimate, while analysts expect earnings to grow about 15% a year over the next several years. That's a premium, but analysts have raised their long-term growth estimates for this company that has grown sales for nearly eight years straight.

I believe patient investors buying today could be well rewarded, especially as Amin believes the organization is in the "early days of international expansion.".
