Famitsu has published its estimated physical game software data for Japan for the week of November 9, 2026 to November 15, 2026.

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 remained the top selling game with an additional 86,097 retail copies sold.

On the hardware side, Switch 2 sold 42,402 units, the PlayStation 5 family sold 11,136 units, the Switch family sold 3,754 units, and the Xbox Series family sold 707 units.
