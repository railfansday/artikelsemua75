Local measurements reveal global quantum properties in a constant number of experimental runs

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (78 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

A quantum device with more qubits is harder to verify. Describing a general quantum state takes an exponential number of parameters, and reconstructing it in full—a procedure known as state tomography—becomes impractical as the system grows. Yet most experiments do not need the full description. They need to know a few things: Is the state close to the target? Are the qubits entangled? And does the system carry the resources needed for quantum computation?
