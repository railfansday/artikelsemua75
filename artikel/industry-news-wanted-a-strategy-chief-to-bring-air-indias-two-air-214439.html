It has been revealed that indiGo hired Air India Express' former chief as its strategy officer in March. Now Air India is creating a strategy role of its own. It looks like both airlines agree the job is worth having.

Air India is creating a latest group strategy officer position as part of its efforts to get full-service carrier Air India and its low-cost arm Air India Express working together more closely, sources remarked.

Rival IndiGo made a similar move earlier this year, naming former Air India Express managing director and CEO Aloke Singh as its chief strategy officer. Singh ran Air India Express for five years before joining IndiGo.

The upcoming strategy job is one of two group-level roles being added. The other is a chief of the training academy, who will be responsible for bringing the group’s flying academy, flight training organizations and technical training under one roof.
