Industry observers note that charlie Cox revealed in an interview with Virgin Radio UK that he told his agents to ask around about getting him an audition to play James Bond in the franchise’s upcoming 26th installment, which is being directed by Denis Villeneuve and produced at Amazon MGM Studios. Unfortunately, the feedback Cox got back was that he was too old for the part.

“Can I say something? I asked to audition for that,” Cox revealed about Bond. “Do you know what they told me? ‘Too old.’ But Rob is the same age as me! He does look good for it, I have to be fair. Next [month] I am going to be 44… how is 44 too old?” Related Stories Pink Announces 2027 Sphere Residency in Las Vegas.

Robert Pattinson landed the role of James Bond at 40 years old. Reports had surfaced during the casting process that producers were looking for an actor in his thirties to take over the role of 007 from Daniel Craig, who played Bond across five movies and 15 years. Craig’s last 007 movie was 2021’s “No Time to Die.”.

“I thought it was going to be Jack Lowden or maybe Callum Tuner,” Cox noted about the upcoming Bond actor, referring to the list of final contenders that reportedly also included the likes of Nicholas Hoult and Josh O’Connor. “What an amazing career [Pattinson] has carved out. Some of the stuff that guy has done… worked with directors who have gone on to be huge, huge auteurs but when he worked with them it was their first film or stuff.”.

Industry observers note that pattinson issued a statement upon being announced as the newly introduced James Bond actor that read: “James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime… To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”. (Related: Latest Update: How to train a newly introduced language...)

The fresh James Bond movie is expected to go into production next year.
