📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

Hong Kong stocks fell sharply on Friday, with the Hang Seng Index sliding to an intraday low of 22,333, its weakest level in 18 months. Technology shares led the decline, pushing the Hang Seng Tech Index down more than 4% to around 3,500, while market turnover topped HK$145 billion. Weak quarterly results from Chinese internet companies drove much of the selling. NetEase plunged nearly 18% to HK$153.70 after reporting lower adjusted profit of RMB 8.4 billion and a rare dip in gaming revenue. Kuaishou sank about 12% to HK$20.30 after its adjusted net profit dropped 84.16% to RMB 790 million and it swung to a net loss. Tencent and Baidu also fell, but Alibaba bucked the trend, rising 4% to HK$82.45. Financials and property stocks added to the pressure, with HSBC losing 3.3% to HK$129 and Sun Hung Kai Properties slipping to HK$107. The drop followed a broad AI-led selloff on Wall Street overnight.
