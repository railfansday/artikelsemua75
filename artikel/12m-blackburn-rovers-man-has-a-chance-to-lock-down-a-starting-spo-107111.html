Blackburn Rovers face Burnley in a Lancashire derby on Sunday. It’s a substantial occasion at Ewood Park, not only because of the rivalry, but because it marks Rovers’ first game since Tony Mowbray stepped back as head coach due to health complications earlier this week.

Recent findings suggest that damien Johnson and Mike Dodds will lead the side on an interim basis, but the temporary bosses will not be able to call upon star right-back Ryan Alebiosu. Summer signing Gady Beyuku replaced Alebiosu when he hobbled off against Stoke City. And, he played the full 90 minutes against West Ham while the medical staff waited for a verdict on Alebiosu’s blow. (Related: Breaking: “Determined” woman completes 7 marathons in a...)

Now that we know how long Blackburn Rovers’ go-to right-back will be out for, Beyuku has a real chance to make the starting role his own.

Most clubs have at least two players to cover in every position, and that is the case at Blackburn Rovers. With Alebiosu out, Beyuku is the only natural senior right-back available for selection, paving the way for him to come into the side.

According to recent reports, A reported fee of around £1.2m was paid to sign the 20-year-old from Modena in the summer, so there is clear belief in his potential. It was widely expected that Beyuku would be cover and competition for Alebiosu, but the injury thrusts him into the limelight.

He’s shown signs of growing into Championship football early on as well. His second-half against West Ham was the best we’ve seen from Beyuku to date, offering a lively presence up and down the right-hand side.

The Frenchman saw to his duties at the back with 10 defensive contributions, but he was forward-thinking and frequently involved in possession like Alebiosu as well. Beyuku completed 26 of his 29 passes, nine of which were line-breaking.

He was dispossessed twice, but this does come with the territory of being a progressive right-back who is still developing and settling into a newly introduced league (all stats via FotMob).

Recent findings suggest that alebiosu’s shoes are substantial ones to fill. It might not have been planned that Beyuku would assume a starting role this early into his Blackburn Rovers, given his age and need to grow into Championship football.

It means that, despite the fact he needs to deliver, there should be an element of patience.

If Johnson and Dodds feel Beyuku needs to be taken out of the side, there are options to consider. Kristi Montgomery has a key role in midfield alongside Sam Morsy but he has covered at right-back before. Matty Litherland is working his way back from injury too, while Lewis Miller is a longer-term absentee.

Industry observers note that beyuku will have the firm backing of the Rovers faithful. A strong display against arch-rivals Burnley this Sunday would be a sure-fire way for the youngster to endear himself to the fans and announce himself in Championship football.
