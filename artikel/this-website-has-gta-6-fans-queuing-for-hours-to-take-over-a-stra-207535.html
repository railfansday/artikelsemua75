A GTA 6 player has created a website that allows anyone to play Rockstar Games’ latest release for free by remotely taking control of their PS5.

While GTA 6 is right now available on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S, those without either console have been left waiting for Rockstar to bring the game to PC.

However, a website called PlayMyPS5 is giving players another way to experience Vice City without having to purchase the game or a console, while attempting to get the internet to complete its story together. (Related: Deep Learning over the Internet: Training Language Mode...)

PlayMyPS5 broadcasts gameplay directly from a physical PS5, allowing visitors to join a public queue and remotely control the console through their web browser.

Each player is given five minutes to play before control is handed over to the next person in line. Players have 20 seconds to claim their turn, while remaining idle for 30 seconds will bring their session to an early end.

The website supports controllers, keyboard and mouse, and mobile touchscreen controls. Players can also watch others explore Vice City and communicate through a live chat while waiting for their turn.

There are no downloads, payments, or registration requirements to join the queue, and players can rejoin once their session ends.

The project was created after its developers realized their PS5 spent much of its time sitting unused. Now, they’re encouraging visitors to work together to complete every GTA 6 story mission.

The creators are also attempting to raise $993 to purchase a second PS5, allowing more people to play simultaneously and reducing queue times.
