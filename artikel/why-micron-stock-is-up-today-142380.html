Micron Technology (MU +17.62%) stock is up 20% as of 9:40 a.m. ET on Monday, Nov. 23, after Nvidia (NVDA +30.77%) revealed it is reinstating its CEO.

The S&P 500 (^GSPC +4.44%) and the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC +6.13%) are up 5.2% and up 7%, respectively.

NASDAQ · As of 11:47 AM ET 11/23/26.

Nvidia confirmed on Monday that its board has reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. The announcement shocked the market, given that just last week the board was insisting his return was not an option. Nvidia's stock soared on the news, up 26.2% so far this morning.

Industry observers note that nvidia is a key partner for Micron, whose advanced high bandwidth memory (HBM) is a key component in the firm's AI chips. The return of Jensen Huang helps calm fears that his absence would lead to a slowdown in the AI market and thus, a slowdown in Micron's business. In a recent development, last Wednesday, CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said the company has already sold 75% of its 2027 supply and that customer talks have moved into 2028. And on Sunday, analysts at Citi (C +3.46%) estimated that even if Nvidia-related financing deals dried up completely, global demand for memory would only fall slightly.

That's reassuring, but I still have concerns about memory prices in the long term and what that will do to Micron's earnings.
