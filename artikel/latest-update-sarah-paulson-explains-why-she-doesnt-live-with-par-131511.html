Sarah Paulson is pulling back the curtain on her living arrangement with longtime partner Holland Taylor.

“We met when I was already 40 and she was already 72,” Paulson, 51, said on the Monday, November 16, episode of “The Run-Through With Vogue” podcast. “Stories had been written and ways in which we moved through the world had been decided and she’s a really independent person.”.

Paulson and Taylor, 83, first crossed paths in 2005 at a party and later reconnected, confirming their romance one decade later. During Monday’s episode, Paulson recalled a moment “early on” in their relationship where she bought a house and “worried” whether it was atypical for her and Taylor not to be residing together.
