Nick Cannon revealed Friday (Nov. 20) that he’s been hospitalized amid his battle with lupus, and he’s asking fans to keep him in their prayers.

“#LupusWarrior,” Cannon wrote in his caption, soundtracked by Donny Hathaway’s “A Song for You.” The accompanying photo finds the father of 12 undergoing a series of medical tests while lying in a hospital bed and wearing a PPE mask.

According to recent reports, he added in the photo: “This [world emoji] can definitely take a lot out of you. Pray for me y’all!”.

According to recent reports, cannon posted a clip to his Instagram Story, as he seemingly prepared to undergo a CT scan. “Have you had a CT before?” a medical professional can be heard asking him off-camera.

Billboard has reached out to a rep for Cannon for further information about his hospitalization.

According to recent reports, the Wild ‘n Out creator was diagnosed with the autoimmune disease back in 2012. According to Mayo Clinic, “Lupus is a condition in which the body’s immune system attacks its own tissues and organs. This is called an autoimmune condition. Lupus causes swelling and irritation, called inflammation, that may affect joints, skin, kidneys, blood cells, brain, heart and lungs.”.

Among his myriad business commitments, the 46-year-old has been traveling for his Wild ‘n Out Live: The Next Generation tour, which continues Saturday (Nov. 21) at Houston’s Toyota Center. It’s still unknown if he will appear at the show. (Related: Tech Report: 3D Asset Generation: AI for Game Developme...)

In September, Cannon revealed he was set to give fans an inside look at his chaotic family life through a Netflix docuseries, which is set to air this winter.

“People have been fascinated with my lifestyle for quite some time, so instead of letting them make up stuff, I’m gonna give you everything,” he told Us Weekly.
