Arsenal goalkeeper David Raya will be unavailable for Tuesday’s UEFA Champions League game against Borussia Dortmund after suffering an injury in his side’s 2-2 draw against Newcastle United on Saturday.

Industry observers note that speaking on the injury, Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta said: “He is not fit for tomorrow, unfortunately, but it doesn’t look too serious. We need some more tests tomorrow, but it is quite encouraging with the pain that he was showing immediately after the action – we were all quite worried, but it seems to be better than what we expected.”. Recent findings suggest that fellow Spanish keeper Kepa Arrizabalaga, who came on in the Newcastle game, is expected to start tomorrow. However, Arteta didn’t give a straightforward answer when asked who will play between the sticks on Tuesday. “We always assess every situation to make the right call, and it is quite clear what we are going to do,” he added.

Before the weekend cameo, Kepa only played against Ipswich in the Carabao Cup, where Arsenal won 4-2.
