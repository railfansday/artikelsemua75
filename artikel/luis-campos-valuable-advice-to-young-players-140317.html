According to recent reports, in today’s football, young talents have more opportunities to be thrown into the deep end early. A precocity which can sometimes pay off. But in most cases, hopefuls often experience more complicated moments during which they can show impatience. This Wednesday, Luis Campos sent them a very useful message on his X account.

“Talent requires hard work, discipline and opportunities to grow. Train hard. Ask questions. When your coach corrects you, listen to what you can learn from it. You can follow his advice while continuing to believe in yourself. Be patient. If you keep looking at who was selected before you, you risk not realizing how far you have already come. In football, as in life, some things take time. How you react when you’re not accepted or when something doesn’t go as planned can make all the difference. » The message got through.
