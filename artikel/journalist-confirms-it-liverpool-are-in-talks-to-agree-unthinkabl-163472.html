Starting with one deal that felt unthinkable a couple of years ago.

Recent findings suggest that essentially no one at Liverpool though Alisson Becker would get a upcoming contract back in 2024. You can see that quite clearly: they signed Giorgi Mamardashvili and then stored him at Valencia for a year.

According to recent reports, the plan was always for Mamardashvili to take over as no.1, potentially last season. That didn't happen and Liverpool still haven't made him the first-choice between the sticks.

It has been revealed that the Reds plainly laid out the plan in the summer, though. Lucca Brughmans signed for the future, with the Club Brugge goalkeeper joining after this season - he was supposed to be Mamardashvili's understudy post-Alisson.

However, Alisson is enjoying another brilliant season. It's also though that he wants to remain with the club, mainly because he outright said that publicly in front of reporters.

Instead? Alisson looks set to remain the first choice goalkeeper for at least another couple of years. A move that looked unthinkable as Liverpool signed two latest goalkeepers is now on the brink of reality.
