Latest transfer news and rumours.

Bruno Fernandes has revealed that talks over a new Manchester United contract have ended.

The club captain is said to have rejected two offers from Man Utd to extend his current deal, which is set to expire at the end of the season though the club have the option to extend by an extra 12 months. (Related: Taco Bell adds ‘Make It Flamin’ Hot’ option for scorchi...)

But the decision has been made to put discussions on hold as he believes the club's priority should be returning to winning ways.

"Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, demonstrates that they want me to stay,” he told Sky Sports.

“And there are no negotiations going on.

I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations.

"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side.

“I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most notable thing now is that the club gets back to win games.".

AC Milan are reportedly considering a move for Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes, who has rejected another new contract offer at Old Trafford.

According to Corriere dello Sport, Milan are keeping an eye on Fernandes’ situation at United, with the possibility of a move being explored should circumstances allow.

The midfielder’s contract status could make him an attractive target for the Rossoneri, although no formal negotiations or agreement have been reported at this stage.

Inter Milan are weighing up a summer move for Cristhian Mosquera.

Per reports in Italy, the Nerazzurri feel a move might be feasible, with Mosquera having been pushed down the Arsenal pecking order since Ezri Konsa’s arrival.

They think he could be available for around £25m.

Ismaila Sarr is ready for a “fresh start” under new Crystal Palace boss Thomas Frank and says he has moved on from his failed summer transfer to Liverpool.

It has been revealed that the Reds earmarked the Senegal international as a potential replacement for Mohamed Salah this summer. Crystal Palace, though, refused to entertain the prospect of selling a forward who netted nine league goals the previous season, despite the player being keen on the move.

He was unable to feature in the early days of Pierre Sage’s brief managerial reign due to a groin injury, which continues to niggle him, though he has now managed five appearances this season.

He has now broken his silence on the summer transfer saga, though, throwing his support behind fresh head coach Frank as he confirmed he is fully committed to helping lift Palace off the floor of the Premier League.

"Liverpool is one of the biggest clubs in world football and, when a club like that shows an interest in you, of course it's something you take seriously,” Sarr told BBC Sport.

Sources indicate that "I don't think any player would be indifferent to that kind of opportunity. It was an interesting possibility, and naturally, I considered what it could mean for my career.

According to recent reports, "Today, that chapter is behind me. I've always believed that you can't move forward if you're constantly looking in the rear-view mirror.

"I'm a Crystal Palace player, I'm proud of what we've achieved together, and my focus is on getting back on the pitch and helping the team.".

Virgil van Dijk is unsure where he will be playing his football next season, with his Liverpool contract due to expire in the summer.

The Dutchman, 35, has plied his trade at Anfield since 2018 and is now a two-time Premier League champion.

He will be out of contract in June, though, and has revealed in a newly introduced interview with Sky Sports that it remains unclear whether he will extend his stay. He is, however, clear that he expects to continue his playing career for several more years.

Per reports from BBC Sport, talks regarding a fresh deal are underway but an agreement is not close, and no resolution is expected before the new year. From January, Van Dijk will be free to sign pre-contract agreements with clubs outside England.

“Whatever's going to happen after this season, we'll see,” he told Sky Sports.

Bayern Munich have opened the door to selling Jamal Musiala, or renegotiating his contract, amid concerns he may struggle to get back to his best form after being diagnosed with a fainting condition.

Musiala, 23, left the footballing world deeply concerned in August when he collapsed on-pitch twice in the span of just a few days, during pre-season friendlies against Heidenheim and RB Leipzig.

Industry observers note that he later revealed in a statement that he had been diagnosed with a “neurological dysfunction”, adding: “(These episodes) are currently simply part of my everyday life. I am receiving the best possible medical care in this regard, and I am very optimistic... there is no additional health risk beyond this.”.

Musiala has since appeared in four league matches, three cup ties, and one Champions League match, tallying two goals and two assists in those appearances.

Bayern Munich’s decision-makers, though, are beginning to worry that Musiala, one of Bayern’s best-paid players, may not be able to replicate the form of his youth.

The club has opened the door to selling the former Chelsea youngster Musiala in upcoming transfer windows, though they acknowledge it may be hard to find a buyer given the player’s condition.

Arsenal are making a ‘serious’ move for Olimpia midfielder Eduardo Delmas.

The Paraguay international has yet to leave his home nation but has reportedly caught the eye of the Gunners’ scouts, and they have bene making enquiries regarding his quality and availability.

At 19, Delmas might come as a long-term investment, with reports in Paraguay saying the Gunners are very interested in a deal.

Despite his meagre years, the player has already made his senior international debut, notching an assist in a 4-1 friendly defeat of Singapore earlier this month.

Crystal Palace have confirmed the arrival of Bernardo Cueva from Chelsea.

The Mexican was the Blues’ set-piece coach, but he has now moved to Selhurst Park to become a more general first-team coach under new boss Thomas Frank.

Mykhailo Mudryk has revealed that he wanted to join Arsenal.

The Tottenham winger, on loan from Chelsea, admitted that before he signed for the Blues in January 2023, he had wanted to move to north London.

Before he made the move to Stamford Bridge, he had been heavily linked with a move to Mikel Arteta’s side, but ultimately, he decided to join Graham Potter’s Blues instead.

Now, he has declared that his real desire was a move to Arsenal, which never materialised.

Arsenal are searching for a latest left winger, and a surprise target of theirs is Brentford’s Kevin Schade.

Teamtalk reports that the Gunners have registered an interest in the German, liking in particular his versatility across the frontline.
