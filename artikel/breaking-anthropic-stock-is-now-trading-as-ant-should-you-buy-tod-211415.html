Anthropic (ANT +1.08%), the company behind the popular AI chatbot Claude, began trading last week under the ticker symbol ANT following the largest initial public offering (IPO) in history.

It has been revealed that after a strong first day, when investors bid the newly introduced stock up from its IPO price of $83 to about $111 per share, it has been falling since. Three trading days after its debut, the stock has dropped below its IPO price and traded at around $78 per share.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/25/26.

So, does that make this exciting new stock a buy, given that it's cheaper now?

Industry observers note that I would say no. Even investors determined to add this stock to their portfolio should wait. That's because the initial pop in the share price, followed by a downward trend, is nothing newly introduced. In fact, IPO stocks have a long history of disappointing early investors. In a recent development, but IPO tech stocks then fall, on average, 3% after three months and 14% after six months, according to that same research. So waiting six months, until the stock finds its footing beyond the initial IPO excitement, is your best bet.
