According to recent reports, then the next thing I know, I’m in LA, and I take my kids to a parade, and then I get back on a plane, and I fly back to New York, and I’m on SNL again, and I’m doing Drunk Uncle, also, because the Brewers are in town. It was a very SNL 24 hours. I didn’t know it was happening, and then all of a sudden, I’m on the other side doing an interview the next week, going like, yeah, that place is singularly insane and wonderful.
