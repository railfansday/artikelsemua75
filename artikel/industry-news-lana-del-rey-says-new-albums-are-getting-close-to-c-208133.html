Lana Del Rey is giving fans an update on her long-awaited fresh music.

In a Thanksgiving Day Instagram post on Thursday (Nov. 26), the alt-pop singer-songwriter revealed that her upcoming country album, Stove, and its companion album, Spyda, are “definitely getting close to wrapping up.”.

Industry observers note that “The albums are coming along so well. I’m happy with them,” Del Rey wrote alongside a photo gallery. “Taking time cooking but definitely gonna be out at the perfect time considering how much music is on both of them. Definitely getting close to wrapping up although did take so many unexpected turns over the years. Can’t say I saw it coming but what came out of it feels meant to be — it makes me wonder where I would even go after this.”. (Related: Industry News: Train a Sentence Embedding Model with 1B...)

Although Del Rey didn’t reveal a release date for either project, she offered insight into the albums’ evolution.

“It’s an unusual coupling of albums that explain where I started when I announced everything 4 years ago to my perspective now on my internal web of people and their reactions to my happiness and physical growth and success,” she wrote. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Del Rey also offered insight into the albums’ introspective themes, referencing what may be a song titled “You’ve Got a Friend.” She described its lyrics as containing substantial “internal dialogue” about the events of the past several years, the lessons she’s learned, and whether everything happens on “God’s time.”.

“Makes me feel like there’s no way the things that happen could be random – one thing that comes to mind is that little quote… You can have it all, but you can’t have it all at the same time,” she wrote.

The “Summertime Sadness” singer also revealed that the physical editions will be an notable part of the projects.

“One of the more notable things actually with this album is how beautiful the physical copies are, the copper plates alone took half a year to make,” she wrote.

It has been revealed that del Rey concluded her post with what could be a tease of new lyrics. “All because I met Happy, who would’ve thought it would make you so mad?” she wrote alongside a musical score emoji.

The singer-songwriter’s Thanksgiving post also included a message of gratitude and reflection.

Recent findings suggest that “Sending love to absolutely everyone today,” she wrote alongside a video of herself hugging a tree and driving through a city filled with lit palm trees. “Thinking of the history of our country and trying to energetically honor so many of the early traditions and culture of the people who were first on this beautiful land. My heart is always with you.”.

In a recent development, she added, “Also grateful to my family who have continued to spend time together for our gatherings, old and new. It’s the glue that keeps me together in this crazy game we call life. Hope we can find one thing to feel grateful for.”.

In mid-October, Del Rey teamed up with Zach Bryan on “Lies,” featured on his eight-song EP, Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs. She also released “First Light,” the theme song for the James Bond video game of the same name.

Recent findings suggest that the artist’s upcoming albums will mark her first full-length releases since Did You Know That There’s a Tunnel Under Ocean Blvd, which debuted at No. 3 on the Billboard 200 in March 2023.
