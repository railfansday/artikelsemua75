Though, we'd be genuinely shocked if Liverpool was his ultimate destination. The Red part of it, anyway. Tanaka is now 28 and not really of the kind of quality that the Reds would ignore his age to get a deal done.

There's no long-term investment here, in other words, and the short-term wouldn't be good enough to justify a fee. Really the only players in that age bracket that Liverpool have signed within the past 10 years are Wataru Endo and Thiago.

It has been revealed that both were special cases. Endo was needed because the squad lacked a specialist defensive midfielder at a time when Liverpool really needed one. And Thiago was needed because he was Thiago.

Tanaka ticks neither box. If he were completely free, things would change but there's no chance Liverpool are considering potential free transfers in 2028. Instead, we imagine this is actually agents driving up interest to get Leeds moving on a contract.
