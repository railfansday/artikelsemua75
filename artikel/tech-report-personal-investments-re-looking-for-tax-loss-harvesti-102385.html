The way I evaluate potential wash sales is looking for the replacement shares. If you sold all the shares purchased within the last 30 days and you don't re-purchase those share for another 30 days, then there are no replacement shares for the lost basis to be added to.I meant more with the rules around dividends and how long you need to hold them for them to be capital gains vs ordinary income.Completely separate from the wash sale rules. To keep the qualified status of the dividends you need to hold the shares for 61 days during a 121 day holding period. This holding period starts 60 days prior to the ex-div date and ends 60 days after the ex-div date. The date the stock is purchased doesn't count as a hold day. The day the stock was sold does count as a hold day. How it relates to TLH is don't do TLHing if the tax value of the loss is less than the tax value of that qualified status of the dividends.Statistics: Posted by WeakOldGuy — Sun Nov 22, 2026 2:28 am.
