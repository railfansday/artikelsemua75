Sunderland may have to spend up to £75million in January to help fend off the threat of relegation.

That is according to former Everton chief executive Keith Wyness, speaking exclusively to Football Insider, who is concerned that Regis Le Bris' squad doesn't have enough depth to balance their domestic and European aspirations. (Related: British Airways Avios — Short Nonstop Domestic Flights...)

The Black Cats have won just one of their four games that have followed a Europa League assignment so far this season, and that came against Chelsea last time out. (Related: Breaking: Democrat Hakeem Jeffries nominated to be firs...)

Wyness believes that the Wearsiders simply must bring in a new striker and a winger this winter, and feels that they both need to be high-quality additions.

Le Bris' men are still in the relegation zone despite winning before the international break, and face a difficult trip to Aston Villa this weekend.

They will already be without both Brian Brobbey and Granit Xhaka for that clash, after the pair picked up injuries while away with their countries.

Everton’s former chief Wyness – who served as CEO at Goodison Park between 2004 and 2009 and now runs a football consultancy advising elite clubs – believes that Sunderland may have to pay a premium in order to land their top targets in January.

The Scottish businessman expects selling clubs to drive the prices up for their players, knowing that Sunderland are in desperate need of reinforcements.

Speaking on the latest edition of Football Insider's Inside Track podcast, Wyness explained why he has always "worried" for Sunderland this season.

According to recent reports, "I think there is money there. With the Europa League money, they've got some leeway, and I think they're going to have to open the purse strings to a degree," he said.

Industry observers note that "I think they're going to have to be looking at a winger and a striker, certainly at the very least. And I think they could be looking at having to spend up to £75m.

It has been revealed that "Sunderland will be one of those clubs that other clubs will know are going to need to spend. That's never a great position to go into the January window, when clubs know that you are desperate to get things going, so they're going to have to play that in a very canny way.

Industry observers note that "Hopefully the injury situation calms down before January, but I don't see that really happening, and so I think they're going to have to spend. I've always worried about them having Thursday and Sunday in terms of Europe.".

Football Insider revealed earlier this week that Sunderland are in an open race with rivals Newcastle to sign Sheffield United midfielder Sydie Peck.

The pair of North East rivals are both clear admirers of the England U21 international, who is heading towards the end of his contract in 2028.

Sources say that neither club is close to striking a deal at the moment, with Sheffield United unwilling to sell in the middle of the season.

However, it is expected to be a different story next summer, with the Blades running out of time to cash in on their star man.
