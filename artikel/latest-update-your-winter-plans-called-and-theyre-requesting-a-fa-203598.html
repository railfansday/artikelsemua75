It has been revealed that the holiday season is officially in full swing: travel plans are locked in, Thanksgiving prep is underway, and the search for the perfect gifts has begun. As for what to wear through it all? Few pieces capture the charm of the season quite like the Fair Isle knit.

Industry observers note that it’s the sweater that conjures up weekends upstate, holiday market strolls, and afternoons spent curled up with a cup of hot cocoa. But its appeal goes well beyond a picturesque fantasy. Packed alongside your ski essentials, thrown on for Friendsgiving, or dressed up for the holiday office party, the nostalgic motif brings cheeriness to the everyday—making it a welcome addition in any cold-weather rotation. The patterned knit has quite the history, too. Rooted in the knitting traditions of its namesake Scottish island, it found a fashionable following in the 1920s, thanks in part to the Prince of Wales wearing one on the golf course. A century later, fresh silhouettes and clever layering give the familiar favorite a new point of view. Below, discover how we’re styling the Fair Isle knit for a season’s worth of festive prepares. The table is set, the pumpkin pie is in the oven, and the first “on my way” text has popped on your screen. Now all that’s left is to get dressed. For a minimalist take on the oft-busy pattern, pair Dôen’s black crewneck with tailored suit pants and lady like kitten heels. Consider it your hostess with the mostest uniform.

Nothing gets you in the festive spirit quite like a stroll through the local holiday market: the music is playing, the apple cider is flowing, and there’s always room for one more ornament in your collection. To brave the chill, pile on winter’s best textures—a cozy knit, chocolate corduroys, and a sumptuous leather and shearling jacket.

The group chat has finally settled on the ski destination. Whether it’s Aspen, Vail, or Park City a Fair Isle knit deserves a spot in your suitcase. Consider it the starting point for your aprés ski ensemble—because the slopes are only half the fun.

Hop in the car—we’re going on a roadtrip! From long walks down leaf-covered roads to discovering local gems, a Fair Isle knit feels right at home for a weekend upstate. Style a festive red cardigan with nothing more than the essentials—think rinse-wash jeans, a baseball cap, and a pair of tall Wellies.

The Fair Isle knit has a place beyond your holiday looks. For a day that starts with early meetings and ends with late night drinks, give Altuzarra’s textured knit a city spin with a sleek black pencil skirt and knee-high boots.
