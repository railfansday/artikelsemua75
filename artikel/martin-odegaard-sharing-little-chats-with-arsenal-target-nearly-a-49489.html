Arsenal and Norway captain Martin Odegaard has undoubtedly spoken to Antonio Nusa about a potential move to North London.

That is according to experienced former Aston Villa, Everton and Tottenham scout and expert on Norwegian football, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The Gunners are thought to be leading the race to sign the 21-year-old, who has burst onto the transfer wish lists of several European giants this season.

Nusa had already been recognised as a strong talent but he has taken a step up at RB Leipzig this season and has already set his own career-record tallies for both goals and assists in a single campaign after just 15 games.

The winger is under contract until 2029 and is expected to leave the German side in the coming months, but it remains to be seen if a move will take place in January.

Leipzig have reportedly been intent on keeping the Norway international until the summer, but an offer the right size may well change that stance. (Related: Summer at Hugging Face...)

Industry observers note that on paper, Nusa is a no-brainer of a signing for Arsenal. He is a left-sided winger, which Mikel Arteta's team desperately needs, and he has the pace that they seem to lack. (Related: Industry News: The Reformer - Pushing the limits of lan...)

And after a summer window that saw the Gunners miss out on several top targets for that position, there is growing demand to strengthen Arteta's options.

"If it's the time to sell and the way the boy's playing, you're going to get £80million for him," King told Football Insider.

"To be honest, you look at the crazy figures they're talking about Yamal at Barcelona. I mean, this kid isn't far off him. He's got the Arsenal captain [Odegaard], who's his captain in the national team.

"I can't imagine that they haven't had a little chat about the Emirates [Stadium], or what's it like in London, or whatever.".

Although Odegaard largely features on the right of the Arsenal midfield, he is given the licence to roam where ever the game needs him.

And should Nusa arrive and be deployed on the left, Arteta will be sure to get the pair combining as much as possible.

The pair have demonstrated on several occasions that they have a excellent understanding of each other, and could be a deadly combination to exploit.

And off the pitch, there is no doubting that a presence like Odegaard's will certainly make what will likely be a tough transition easier for Nusa.
