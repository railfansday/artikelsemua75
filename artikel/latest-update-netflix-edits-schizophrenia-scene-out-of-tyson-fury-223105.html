“I want to clarify a statement I made in the original version of the Tyson Fury documentary,” a statement from Paris Fury noted. “I recalled a conversation from many years ago when discussing Tyson’s condition and I used the word ‘schizophrenic.’ While Tyson did show some symptoms associated with schizophrenia, he doesn’t have this condition and has never received a diagnosis.” Watch on Deadline. A Netflix spokeswoman mentioned the scene has been removed from the doc, which dropped today.

“Following new information shared by the family relating to Tyson’s official mental health diagnosis, we have worked alongside them to revision the documentary accordingly ahead of launch,” she added.

Following the schizophrenia comments, which were also included in a Netflix press release about the doc on Monday, the British Boxing Board of Control stated it had been examining whether what was thought to be Fury’s diagnosis would get in the way of his long-anticipated fight with fellow British boxer Joshua.

That fight has now been cleared to take place on December 11 in Cardiff and could prove to be one of Netflix’s most lucrative live sporting events.
