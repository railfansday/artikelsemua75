According to recent reports, good morning, everyone, and thank you for joining us. In the first half of FY 2027, we were and still are navigating challenging markets. We updated full year expectations in September and we are maintaining that full year guidance. In a tough market, we have focused to protect profitability, actioning on what we can control. Our September enhancement was a difficult message. Since then, the first half has come in where we mentioned it would. Distributor destocking has stabilized, and October trading and the Q3 order book are in line with the board expectations.

In a recent development, adjusted EBITDA was at EUR 10 million, broadly in line with the strong first half last year, with underlying EBITDA of EUR 10.6 million, only 1% down from last H1, even though group volumes were 5% lower. That came from decisive management action. We maintained our pricing discipline, we cut costs by EUR 1.8 million through tight cost control of overheads and further R&M operational productivity gains.
