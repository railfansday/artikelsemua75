90 Day Fiancé: The Other Way stars Kenny Niedermeier and Armando Niedermeier-Rubio are setting the record straight on why they chose to share Hannah’s medical emergency on reality TV.

It has been revealed that “We invited you guys into our lives — into our family, into every part of us — and we did that with the intention of showing you our lives as they really are — the good, the bad, the ugly — simply reality,” Armando, 37, said in a YouTube video posted on Saturday, November 21. “Reality TV shouldn’t be just about the crazy arguments and fights. We know that sometimes, that’s what draws people in and captures people in, but we want people to know this is our real life.”. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Recent findings suggest that although Kenny, 63, and Armando have been part of the 90 Day Fiancé franchise for several years, they never experienced something quite like Hannah’s health scare. (Armando welcomed Hannah with his late ex-wife in 2012.).

In July, Hannah, 14, was airlifted from Mexico to another hospital in Arizona after her face became “droopy.” Hannah, who has since been released, later learned through a scan that there was some blood in her brain.

According to recent reports, “We understand that some people might believe that certain things should remain private, and we do respect that,” Armando shared in his latest YouTube video. “But at the same time, sharing our journey has always been a part of us and what we’ve signed up for at the end of the day. What happened with Hannah, that’s a very significant part of our lives.”. (Related: My Journey to a serverless transformers pipeline on Goo...)

According to Kenny, any footage shot inside the hospital was captured by family members. In addition, every picture posted on social media was approved by Hannah.

Sources indicate that “We’re not sharing this because it’s dramatic television or because we want to turn something so personal into a story line,” Armando added. “We’re sharing it because it’s something we’ve been living through. It’s something that has changed our lives and something that many of you have gently been asking about and concerned about.”.

Both Kenny and Armando mentioned they had a candid discussion with Hannah about how much she wanted to share with TV viewers.

According to the couple, Hannah supported documenting her story, including the struggles Armando faced to see his daughter in the hospital because of necessary documents.

“Getting her father into the country to be with her was very traumatic for her,” Kenny noted. “She wanted people to be aware that this is an glitch and that fathers and other family members should be with their sick parents and a border shouldn’t be between them and stopping them from being together.”.

As viewers learn more information during the current season of 90 Day Fiancé: The Other Way, both Kenny and Armando want to make it clear that they are grateful for all the love, care and support they received from fans and strangers across the nation.

She’s our entire world and what happened to her affected our entire family,” Armando remarked while holding back tears. “To the people who have been there since the beginning, to those who joined our journey along the way and to those who have shown our daughter so much love without even having met her, from the bottom of my heart, thank you so much.”.
