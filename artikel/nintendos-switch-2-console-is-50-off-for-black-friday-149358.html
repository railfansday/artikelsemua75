Those hoping to save some cash on a Nintendo Switch 2 can do so starting today, November 22nd, through Saturday, December 5th, giving you plenty of time to think about it after Cyber Monday has passed. For a limited time, Nintendo has knocked $50 off one of its two current console bundles, temporarily rolling back the $50 price increase that went into effect in early September.

In a recent development, it’s tough to predict which bundle will be most popular for your household. The Mario Kart World bundle runs $499.99 (previously $549.99) and includes the console, a digital copy of the aforementioned game, and three months of Nintendo Switch Online. Then there’s the Nintendo Switch Sports Resort bundle for $529.99 (price unchanged for Black Friday), which includes the more recent minigame-filled title instead.
