For the most part, Netflix's live-action One Piece series is what has catapulted the franchise into never-before-seen mainstream popularity. That stated, arguably well before the Netflix live-action could come around, the introduction of Gear Five in the anime and the Wano Arc's jaw-dropping animation was already pushing many towards giving One Piece a chance or finally catching up to all the new good stuff. Sources indicate that in a recent post on X, Netflix officially confirmed that One Piece's Wano Arc is finally making its way to the platform on December 15 across multiple regions, including the United States. The final batch of One Piece's Whole Cake Island Arc (Episodes 878–891) only just dropped on Netflix on November 15, 2026. And in just 4 weeks, the widely loved Wano Arc will also finally be available on Netflix as well, finally rounding out its One Piece offerings.

Recent findings suggest that for the longest time, One Piece's Wano Arc was only available on Crunchyroll. Netflix has gradually been licensing One Piece's anime since 2020, rolling out arc after arc starting right from the beginning. As of the Egghead Arc, the anime's newest episodes began making their way to Netflix a week after their release.

But still, even though Netflix had the newest episodes, its One Piece collection was still largely incomplete, missing a good chunk in between, including the Wano Arc, which concluded with Episode 1085 around this time, three whole years ago, and first aired seven years ago, kicking off in July 2019.

Granted, in view of One Piece's newest arcs, it's hard to still call Wano One Piece's best arc. Be it in terms of storytelling or animation, the Egghead Arc easily surpasses Wano with its masterful portrayal of Kuma and Bonney's backstory or even the more refined depiction of Gear Five.

Even so, this does not take away from the fact that the Wano Arc is still One Piece's most revolutionary arc. The grand scale of Wano's story, its distinct art direction by the late Tatsuya Nagamine, and its discernible boost in quality all make the arc a major turning point for One Piece's anime.

Sources indicate that wano set the bar higher, and trying to surpass that bar is what has given fans such spectacular anime adaptations of the Egghead and Elbaph arcs. As such, the Wano Arc holds a special place for many, especially with how Gear Five famously broke the internet with its anime debut. And finally, fans can revisit this nostalgic moment and more when One Piece's Wano Arc finally makes its way to Netflix on December 15.
