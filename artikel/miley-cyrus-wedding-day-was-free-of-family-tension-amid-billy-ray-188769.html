Miley Cyrus and Maxx Morando wanted their wedding to be all about love, and that’s exactly what they got, a source exclusively tells Us.

The couple’s Saturday, November 21, nuptials were a family affair, reuniting Miley’s parents, exes Tish Cryus and Billy Ray Cyrus, but there was “no family tension with everyone being together under one roof,” the insider says.

Tish, 59, walked Miley, 34, down the aisle during the Palm Springs, California, ceremony, while the bride shared a special dance with her father, 65, according to Miley’s social media recap of her major day.

Behind the scenes, Tish and Billy Ray, who were married from 1993 to 2022, had their respective significant others by their side — which only added to the positive vibes. (Tish married Dominic Purcell in 2023, while Billy Ray has been dating Elizabeth Hurley since early 2025.).

“Everyone got along really well, and Billy Ray and Elizabeth were great,” the source shares. “Elizabeth is super outgoing and was mingling with everyone throughout the weekend.”.

The insider tells Us that the Bedazzled actress, 61, “really helped bring the family together, and everyone genuinely likes her.”.

Tish and Billy Ray’s youngest daughter, Noah Cryus, as well as Tish’s eldest daughter, Brandi Cyrus, whom Billy Ray adopted when they got married, were also on hand to toast Miley and Morando, 28, when they got hitched.

Elizabeth’s son, Damian Hurley, whom she shares with late ex Steve Bing, was among the guests as well.

When it comes to Elizabeth, the Cyrus bunch “appreciate how happy she makes Billy Ray and can see that he’s in a really good place,” the source says.

“Miley was also really happy to have her dad there and be able to share the special weekend with him,” the insider adds.

Us has reached out to the Cyrus family for comment.

Miley has had her ups and downs with dad Billy Ray, following his split from Tish in 2022.

When Miley won her first Grammy in 2024, she thanked her mom and Morando, but didn’t mention the “Achy Breaky Heart” crooner.

The father-daughter duo — who previously costarred on Disney Channel’s Hannah Montana and were thick as thieves for decades — have since mended fences.

“My dad and I have had our challenges over the years,” Miley wrote in a March 2025 statement. “Now, in my 30s, family is my priority above all else. I’m at peace knowing bridges have been built and time has done a lot of healing. Grateful for the good health and love that flows through my family.”.

Eight months later, Miley celebrated a relationship milestone with Morando after he proposed and her dad was one of many people who publicly congratulated her. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

Sources indicate that “So happy you and MaxxAgazillion found each other!!!! Congratulations! Happy to see you both so happy!!! love Dad ❤️,” Billy Ray wrote via Instagram in December 2025 after Us confirmed that Morando had proposed one month prior.

Morando and Miley began dating in 2021 after meeting on a blind date. One year after they got engaged, the couple noted, “I do” at the late Frank Sinatra‘s former Twin Palms estate.

Miley confirmed the news via Instagram, sharing black-and-white photos from her glamorous ceremony. “M + M 🤍 11.21.26,” she captioned the snaps.

Two days after she got hitched, the “Flowers” singer celebrated her 34th birthday.

“Waking up on my 34th birthday feeling so grateful,” Miley wrote via Instagram on Monday, November 23. “Thank you for all the wishes & to everyone who made this weekend a memory we’ll cherish for a lifetime and beyond. 🤍.”.

Both Tish and Billy Ray also celebrated their daughter’s birthday with respective social media tributes — both of which included photos from Miley’s romantic nuptials.

Industry observers note that “I always forget how many memories we make in just one year. So many amazing moments being your Mom, But nothing will ever compare to walking you down the aisle on your wedding day,” Tish gushed. “I love you so very much and Maxxy too ….. Happy 34th Birthday Little 🤍🤍🤍 @miley.”. (Related: Industry News: Showcase Your Projects in Spaces using G...)
