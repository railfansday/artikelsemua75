📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

It has been revealed that every time we hear a upcoming figure, whether it be estimated or confirmed, regarding GTA 6 sales, it becomes even more unbelievable. The confirmed number for launch day was 32 million. Now we know that, by November 22, that figure has comfortably cleared 40 million in only three days.

Industry observers note that in a new post celebrating the launch of GTA 6 on the PlayStation Blog, PlayStation revealed 39 million copies of GTA 6 had been sold on PS5 alone by November 22. The Xbox numbers haven’t been shared, but naturally that means we’re well past 40 million, and probably even 50 million. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

Industry observers note that playStation didn’t just share GTA 6’s three-day sales numbers. It has also broken down just how long we’ve collectively spent in the game so far. 683 million hours between the 39 million of us who played on PS5 during those first three days, averaging almost 17.5 hours each. (Related: Industry News: I Asked About Dirty Dishes. You Delivere...)

683 million is a hard number to quantify, especially when it comes to hours. It becomes both a little easier to get your head around, and a lot harder to believe, when you turn it into years. 683 million hours is almost 28.5 million days, which is close to 80,000 years. Since this particular count stopped on November 22, you can assume we have sailed past that 80,000-year benchmark now.

It has been revealed that at the peak of GTA 6’s launch, there were more people playing the game at one time than any other game in PlayStation’s history. Let me make that a little clearer. Not just PS5, and not omitting free-to-play games like Fortnite. It set the highest concurrent player count for PlayStation across all of its consoles, and includes any game that has ever been released on the platform.

It is, without a doubt, not just the biggest video game launch of all time, but the biggest launch in entertainment history. GTA 6 made more money than any movie ever within seconds of its release thanks to the tens of millions of pre-orders placed, and the cultural footprint it has already left is remarkable.

Industry observers note that it’s incredible to think just how little of this game we have seen too, despite spending a combined 80,000 years together exploring its map. A few easter eggs have been discovered - the GTA 5 true crime podcast easter egg, and the Looney Tunes-style painted tunnel - but we’re going to be finding stuff Rockstar has hidden in this game for tens of thousands more in-game years.
