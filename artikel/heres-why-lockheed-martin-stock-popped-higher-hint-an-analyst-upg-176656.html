According to recent reports, lockheed Martin (LMT +2.92%) stock rose by more than 3% in early trading on a day when the S&P 500 index opened lower. The increase occurred after a Mizuho analyst started coverage of aerospace and defense companies and named Boeing (BA +0.24%) and Lockheed Martin as two of its favorite picks in the sector. Lockheed Martin also looks a good value. As the Mizuho analyst notes, Lockheed and others are well positioned in areas such as munitions (including missiles and interceptors) and unmanned systems, which should do well regardless of which administration guides the defense budget.

Moreover, there's real evidence of positive developments in the working relationships between the Department of Defense and defense companies, and most notably with Lockheed Martin.

Over the last few years, defense companies (including Lockheed and Boeing's defense business) have incurred multi-billion-dollar losses and charges on fixed-price development programs, but Lockheed recently outlined how the latest long-term agreements it made for PAC-3 missiles and F-35 fighter aircraft contain four key provisions that will help mitigate risk and encourage investment. It's a good sign, and if it continues, then defense stocks could rerate substantially.
