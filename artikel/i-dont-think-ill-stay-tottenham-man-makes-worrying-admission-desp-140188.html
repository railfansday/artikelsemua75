Tottenham manager Roberto De Zerbi has made a surprising admission about his coaching future, suggesting he could step away from football management far earlier than expected despite holding a contract in North London until June 2031.

Speaking in a candid interview with French broadcaster Canal+ (relayed via Foot Mercato), the 47-year-old Italian revealed that the all-consuming nature of top-flight management has left him contemplating an early retirement, even though he only signed a five-year deal with Tottenham in March 2026.

De Zerbi explained that he no longer views winning trophies as the only measure of success.

Sources indicate that “I don’t think I’ll stay in football much longer. Your life revolves around football, but life isn’t just that.

“That time hasn’t come yet. For me, being a coach at this level is already a dream. I don’t measure dreams by the titles won, but more by the human impact you have. Tottenham failed to win any of their opening six Premier League matches, scoring just three goals during that period.

However, Tottenham’s recent form suggests the manager’s methods are beginning to produce results.

Spurs have now won four of their last five Premier League matches, including an impressive victory over Chelsea at Stamford Bridge.
