Industry observers note that YYForce announced on November 24, 2026, the launch of Six Seven, a humanoid robot built for hotel concierge service and planned for deployment at Novotel Singapore on Stevens. The hotel team named the robot Six Seven, or 67.

Within the lobby, Six Seven is designed to field questions through natural conversation, drawing on real-time information about the hotel and its surroundings through YYForce’s Workforce OS software. Guests could ask for restaurant details, directions to venues, opening hours, menus or the day’s activities. The concierge brief also covers neighborhood navigation, attractions, transportation options and tourism recommendations across Singapore. Sources indicate that open-ended guest inquiries and interactive storytelling, including engagement with visitors and families, are also part of the intended service. That is a less structured assignment than a predefined delivery route: the concierge must interpret varied requests and supply relevant hotel or travel information.

It has been revealed that YYForce remarked Isaac Sim supplied the physical AI simulations and synthetic training used to develop and validate Six Seven. NVIDIA Jetson runs AI inference, meaning trained models process information on the robot itself. An October 22, 2026, announcement described Workforce OS as a modular architecture intended to work across different robot types. Its scope includes computer vision, mapping and navigation, manipulation, natural-language interaction and coordination of multiple robots.

Sources indicate that the roadmap’s first phase called for real-time navigation, guest-facing conversations and front-desk integration using Jetson edge modules and Isaac ROS. Subsequent phases targeted robotic-arm manipulation, beverage service and coordinated robots for banquets. Six Seven’s unveiled duties sit within the concierge portion of that broader roadmap, whose later stages add physical-service tasks.

“Six Seven’s deployment at Novotel Singapore on Stevens will show that humanoid robotics can deliver genuine warmth, accuracy, and operational value on the hotel front line,” remarked Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Mike Fu. He also connected the rollout to hotels’ efforts to improve guest experiences and address persistent frontline staffing challenges.

Industry observers note that the planned concierge assignment builds on an existing hotel collaboration. On October 13, 2026, YYForce, Novotel Singapore on Stevens and Mercure Singapore on Stevens announced a partnership covering a 772-room precinct with restaurants and event spaces. The hotels contribute hospitality expertise and an evaluation environment, while YYForce provides robotics capabilities and technical support.

The confirmed first phase of that agreement assigned specialized robots to deliveries, food-and-beverage logistics, scheduled cleaning and basic concierge support. The concierge work was limited to guest directions and basic enquiries, while any check-in assistance required separate assessment and approval. Six Seven’s broader conversational brief adds a more extensive guest-facing role to that plan.

According to recent reports, in a separate October 29, 2026, announcement, YYForce mentioned 11 specialized service robots were being commercially deployed at the dual-branded complex under a 24-month agreement. Their work covers internal hotel logistics, guest-room deliveries and movement between floors. Elevator integration lets the robots call lifts across the multi-tower property, while connections to hotel telephone systems enable automated in-room guest notifications.

Recent findings suggest that the hotel partners’ evaluation framework calls for actual costs, productivity and guest-service outcomes to be reviewed after rollout. Proposed measures include reliability, tasks completed per labor hour, colleague workload and guest feedback. Six Seven would extend that operational challenge to open-ended lobby conversation, with its practical value tied to service consistency and manageable staff workloads as well as its ability to answer guests.
