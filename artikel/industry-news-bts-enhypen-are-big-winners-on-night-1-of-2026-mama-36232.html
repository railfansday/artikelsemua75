Industry observers note that BTS and ENHYPEN were the significant winners on night 1 of the 2026 MAMA Awards, which were held Friday (Nov. 20) at the Kyocera Dome Osaka, in Japan. Night 2 of the awards is set for Saturday (Nov. 21). The awards are also streaming live worldwide on CJ ENM’s Mnet Plus platform.

ENHYPEN won fans’ choice of the year, favorite MCD Stage, and also ranked in the top 10 for fans’ choice male. (Related: At historic summit, Xi and Putin share global stage wit...)

It has been revealed that both groups have topped the all-genre Billboard 200 album chart this year. BTS led for three weeks in April with Arirang. ENHYPEN was on top for one week in September with The Sin: Bliss.

Yeonjun of TOMORROW X TOGETHER won best male artist. The group also made the top 10 for Fans’ Choice Male.

Sources indicate that soyeon of i-dle won best female artist. That group also made the top 10 for Fans’ Choice Female.

ALPHA DRIVE ONE and TUIDE were co-winners for best fresh artist.

According to recent reports, music released between Oct. 1, 2025, and Sept. 30, 2026 was eligible for this year’s awards.

Performers on night 1 included Street World Fighter: Directors’ War, ILLIT, Panic, ALLDAY PROJECT, ALPHA DRIVE ONE, BABYMONSTER, CL, ENHYPEN, Hearts2Hearts, INI, LNGSHOT, MODYSSEY, NCT WISH, RESCENE, TREASURE and TUIDE.

The following performers are set to take the stage on night 2: &TEAM, NAYEON (TWICE), HWASA, BIGBANG, CORTIS, IVE, izna, KiiiKiii, KO1KEYZ, LE SSERAFIM, WOODZ and ZEROBASEONE.

Key winners from night 1 include:.

Fans’ Choice of the Year with Visa: ENHYPEN.

Worldwide KConers’ Choice: ZeroBaseOne.

Best Breakthrough Artist: NCT Wish.

Favorite MCD Stage with Mnet Plus: ENYPEN.

Visa Super Stage Artist: Treasure.

Q100 Favorite Mega Artist: NCT Wish.
