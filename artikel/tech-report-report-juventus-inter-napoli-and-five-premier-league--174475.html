Gustavo Varela has caught the eye of some of Europe’s biggest clubs with his performances in a Monza shirt, including almost half of the Premier League according to Tuesday’s reports.

Varela, who joined Monza for a fee reported to be worth around €2.5m during the summer transfer window, has gone on to net 11 goals from his first 11 matches in Serie A, and with cup appearances included, his tally for the season stands at 13 goals from 12 outings. He was the star of the show again on Monday night, netting a brace, including an excellent backheel flick in an eventual 3-0 win over Fiorentina in Serie A.

Varela, who earned his first call-up to the Portugal national team squad for the November international break, has not gone unnoticed with his performances. (Related: Latest Update: Towers of Fortune codes (November 2026)...)

Tuesday’s reports from Gianluca Di Marzio claim that Inter are particularly keen and that the Nerazzurri have been monitoring Varela’s performances since his goal against them during the opening round of 2026-27 fixtures.

Napoli, who are expected to go after a newly introduced striker to replace Lorenzo Lucca in January, are also mentioned to be interested. Gianluca Di Marzio adds that Arsenal and Barcelona have also sent scouts to watch Varela in action this season.

Recent findings suggest that the Corriere dello Sport, meanwhile, claims that an entirely different list of clubs are interested in the 21-year-old. The Corriere dello Sport agrees that Napoli are interested, but also points to Juventus and Premier League quartet Liverpool, Tottenham, Sunderland and Brentford.

The Corriere dello Sport also adds that Benfica, who sold Varela just a few months ago, have the right to match any offer that Monza receive.
