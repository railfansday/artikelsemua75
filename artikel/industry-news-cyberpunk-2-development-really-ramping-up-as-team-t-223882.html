2027 will likely be a pivotal year for Cyberpunk 2 as it rockets towards full production, with CD Projekt Red anticipating significant expansion to the sequel's development team.

In other words, we can probably assume Cyberpunk 2 will leave early production in the relatively near future — and that's a major deal, because it means that the bones of the project are basically locked in place.

CDPR's Karolina Gnaś, who maintains relationships with the organization's investors, reiterates that "good progress" is being made with Cyberpunk 2 — with further comments suggesting that there have been no internal delays.

The thing is, we're probably not going to see the Cyberpunk 2077 successor until The Witcher 4 is out, which seems a rather daunting prospect.
