📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

The newest Hunger Games movie, Sunrise on the Reaping, had a gigantic opening weekend that exceeded expectations. Some theorized it could struggle as people stayed home to playGTA 6, which released one day prior, but that doesn't appear to have been the case. At the domestic box office, Sunrise on the Reaping earned $120 million for its opening weekend, with $105 million more from international markets for a total of $225 million over its first three days, according to Deadline. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lYXtL5TreOE The franchise's second prequel, after 2023's The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, Sunrise on the Reaping tells the story of the 50th Hunger Games, where Haymitch Abernathy emerged victorious. Joseph Zada plays him; Woody Harrelson played Haymitch in the original Hunger Games films. Sunrise on the Reaping also stars Ralph Fiennes as a younger President Snow, Jesse Plemons as a younger Plutarch, and Elle Fanning as a younger Effie. With $120 million for its domestic opening, Sunrise on the Reaping is now just the second prequel film ever to start with $100 million or more domestically. The current top prequel is 2016's Star Wars movie Rogue One, which made $155 million for its domestic opening. Some had theorized that Sunrise on the Reaping could suffer as people stayed home to play GTA 6 instead. Director Francis Lawrence stated, "Hopefully, people find time for both." It sure seems like he was right about that. For comparison, The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes debuted to just $44.6 million for its opening weekend in North America, so Sunrise on the Reaping is doing much better. Sunrise on the Reaping is also Lionsgate's biggest post-COVID movie and its first film that opened to $100 million or more since 2015's The Hunger Games: Mockingjay: Part 2 ($102.6 million).
