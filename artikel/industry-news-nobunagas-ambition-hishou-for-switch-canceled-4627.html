According to recent reports, publisher Koei Tecmo and developer Kou Shibusawa have canceled the previously announced Switch version of historical simulation game Nobunaga’s Ambition: Hishou. The game will now only release for PlayStation 5, Switch 2, and PC via Steam when it introduces in Japan and Asia this winter.

Here is an overview of the game, via Koei Tecmo:.

What is it that warriors fight for?

Recent findings suggest that each warrior of the Warring States period had their own reasons for riding into battle—be it territory, social standing, the sake of humanity, or their own ambition. This title focuses on those reasons, with battles between evenly matched warriors and an exploration of the bonds between ally and enemy.

Enjoy a full revival of the city development portion of domestic affairs for the first time in nearly 20 years. Freely develop your own castle town, depicted in beautifully evolved graphics.

Industry observers note that experience story mode for the first time in series history. Both series fans and newcomers to the Warring States and historical simulation games can enjoy this story focused on the life of Oda Nobunaga.
