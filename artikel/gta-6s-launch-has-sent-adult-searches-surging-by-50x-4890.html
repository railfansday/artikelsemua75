In a recent development, gamers are a predictable bunch. Over the last couple of years, we've seen a rise in "gooner games," and players, male, female, and non-binary, have been thirsting on main more and more. More power to you. Grand Theft Auto 6 looks set to take this all to the next level.

We've seen people are genuinely worried about falling in love with the game's protagonist, Lucia, but they just can't keep their hands to themselves; in the first 24 hours since GTA 6 rolled out, Google searches for terms like "Lucia Naked" and "GTA 6 Sex" have increased by a factor of 10.

Grand Theft Auto 6 is going to be the biggest media event of our lifetimes. It broke records for pre-orders, records for review scores, and records for Twitch viewership numbers. It also looks like it's going to break records for player horniness.

It has been revealed that since its launch, some of our older Grand Theft Auto 6 articles have seen a huge increase in traffic. Namely, one in which the infamous Cyberleek showed off a naked Lucia parachuting in Vice City and another in which the PlayStation Store revealed that the game would feature "sex scenes". That got me wondering: what are these GTA 6 fans really searching for?

Using Google Trends data, I had a look at a handful of Grand Theft Auto 6 search terms related to these articles, and unsurprisingly, they're being searched a lot.

Recent findings suggest that searches for "Lucia Naked" are up 350 percent over the previous week, searches for "Jason Naked" are up over 160 percent, and searches for "GTA 6 Sex" are up over 1,200 percent versus this time last week. Searches for "GTA 6 Prostitutes" have seen the biggest spike, with a more than 5000 percent increase week-over-week. Players are clearly keen for a little extracurricular GTA 6 activity.

It's a mixture of good news and bad news for these thirsty players. GTA 6 does officially feature full-frontal nudity, including a full nudist resort, which coincidentally has also seen a more than 5000 percent increase in search over the last week; however, it is dropping the series staple, sex workers. Rockstar noted it implemented everything it needed to successfully tell Jason and Lucia's "lovers on the run" story, and evidently, prostitutes weren't one of those things.
