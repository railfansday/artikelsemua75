In a recent development, it’s crazy watching months and months and then years of expenses go down over several days. But, I believe in looking so that you’re used to it. It’s not the first correction, recession or whatever this will end up being. It’s not the last. Might as well strap in for the ride. What I always tell my spouse during times like this. “If we can hang on till this thing os over, we will look much better on the other side.”Markets are fascinating to me. So much paper wealth (not meaning mine, but the totality of global stocks) can drop in value so quickly, when a few days ago, they were highly coveted and prices were up and to the right. But suddenly there aren't bids to hold the prices up at the old levels. The drops are so much more impressive than the gains, because they are faster, and because losing a dollar invokes more reaction than gaining one. My reaction is to look and laugh most of the time. I went to the gym this afternoon, and there were two groups of men having locker room and sauna conversations about the stock market gyrations lately. There's a good chance that I have more money than both groups combined, and I decided not to join in for that topic. They'll tell me about their bitcoin or MU, and I'll be honest. LOLA few years ago, my wife was sitting next to a few colleagues discussing their stock bets and crypto purchases. She sat there quietly as she knew better than to get involved. Finally, one of them asked her if she is in the market. She said “yes.” They said, “What do you like?” She said “S&P 500” and that was it. No one asked her further questions, with them probably thinking to themselves; She will never beat the market like that. Trouble averted. BTW, she says S&P 500 because we learned it is easier than saying US Total Stock and then explaining what that is.Statistics: Posted by EnjoyIt — Fri Nov 20, 2026 10:42 pm.
