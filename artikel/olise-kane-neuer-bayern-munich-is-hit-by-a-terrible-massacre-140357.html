According to recent reports, nothing seems to spare Bayern Munich. Already faced with a long list of absentees, the Bavarian club saw two upcoming executives leave the pitch due to injury this Friday evening against Cologne: Harry Kane and Michael Olise. Hit in the ankle after contact with Joël Schmied in the area, the English striker had to give up his place in the 20th minute of play. For his part, the French international was the victim of contact with Tom Krauß in the first period. Despite an attempt to continue the match, he was unable to return after the break.

Olise’s situation also arouses particular concern. According to the German press, the former Crystal Palace player left the stadium on crutches, a sign which raises questions about the seriousness of his injury. For the moment, no official diagnosis has been communicated concerning the Frenchman, any more than for Kane even if Max Eberl affirmed that the Bavarian striker was suffering from a torn syndesmosis (which could keep him away from the field for 3 months, introduces Sky Sports Germany). Bayern could therefore lose two of their main offensive assets as they enter a particularly busy period in their calendar.
