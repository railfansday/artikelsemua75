So many cozy indies got a mention too, including Milki Delivery, Tanuki: Pon’s Summer, and of course Dressmaker (which will be featured during AGDQ 2027 and I’m excited to see how a dressmaking game can be speedrun.).

The way that GTA 6 is dominating every airwave and will continue to do so for at least a few weeks longer is probably isolating for folks who don’t have any interest in that game. It is the game of the moment and deserves this level of discussion, as tedious as it can be sometimes. But I wanted to make a space for folks to have a different discussion, and I was surprised at how swift and voluminous of a response I got. I wanted to share that here, to break up the GTA 6 stream for just a little bit.
