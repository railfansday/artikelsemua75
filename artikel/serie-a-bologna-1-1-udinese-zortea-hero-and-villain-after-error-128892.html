Nadir Zortea was hero and villain for Bologna, as he scored the opener,  then his error gifted Nicolò Zaniolo the Udinese equaliser.

Recent findings suggest that the Friulani sacked coach Kosta Runjaic after a disastrous run of just one point in eight rounds, then Paolo Zanetti made his debut with a 2-1 loss at Venezia before the break for international duty. Nicolò Bertola and Idrissa Gueye were still out, with Keinan Davis, David Alaba and Alessandro Zanoli not fully fit either.

Industry observers note that the Rossoblu were revitalised under Raffaele Palladino, winning five of their last six games, a run broken only by Inter at San Siro. He left out Torbjorn Heggem as punishment after critical comments in the press, with Juan Miranda, Emil Holm, Jay Enem, Oussama El Azzouzi and Ukko Happonen injured. It was an aggressive start from Udinese, as Lukasz Skorupski was forced into tricky saves on Nicolò Zaniolo from the edge of the area, and a Sandi Lovric header.

However, they failed to make the most of the pressure, and eventually went behind just before half-time.

Niccolò Cambiaghi got down the left and Jens Odgaard couldn’t control the low cross, but did manage to flick it on for Nadir Zortea to turn in from six yards. It was the wing-back’s first goal in the Bologna jersey. (Related: Animal control called to rescue alligator that turned o...)

Oumar Solet made a goal-saving challenge to stop Federico Bernardeschi tapping in the Cambiaghi roll across from the left, while Arthur Theate’s attempt was pushed over the bar by Maduka Okoye.

Zortea had scored the opener for Bologna, but also gave away the Udinese equaliser with a terrible first touch, allowing Zaniolo to steal it in the final third and go clear on goal.

Okoye nearly returned the favour when Rahim Alhassane got his head to a cross before the goalkeeper with an almighty leap, but nodded it away from the net.

Udinese wasted a double opportunity when first a Vakoun Bayo volley was scuffed from 12 yards, then James Abankwah sliding in couldn’t quite redirect the wayward effort at the back post.
