I had this problem back in the 2010s after my refinanced loan was sold to Bayview Mortgage. Twice I sent RESPA letters demanding an explanation, but the matter was never resolved. I was eventually convinced there was no solution short of legal action, but because interest rates were so low at the time, I decided the interest savings weren't worth the hassle and invested the extra into stocks instead. That turned out OK (That organization changed their name once or twice since then, probably trying to shake their online reputation. My second run-in with them involved being placed in pre-foreclosure "counseling" despite having never missed a payment!) So, be happy if this can indeed be resolved with a phone call. I've learned not to expect much from mortgage servicers. Sometimes it is best to leave well enough alone, as unsatisfying as that may be.
