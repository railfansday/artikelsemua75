Bruno Fernandes has ended up on AC Milan’s radar ahead of the summer, according to reports, with a free transfer potentially on the cards.

Sources indicate that ever since the summer, when the BBC were the first to report on Milan’s interest, Fernandes has been brought up by various outlets as an option for the Rossoneri. Having just turned 32, Fernandes is still at a good age and he’s showing that with Manchester United so far this season. In 14 appearances, he has bagged six goals and three assists, so it’s no surprise that many clubs are monitoring his situation given the expiring contract.

Industry observers note that as reported by both Corriere dello Sport (via MilanNews) and Tuttosport (via MilanNews) this morning, Milan are seriously evaluating the idea of signing him. It’s still too early to talk about negotiations, but he’s definitely on their radar and will likely remain there. (Related: Industry News: Average Retirement Savings by Age: Are Y...)

Industry observers note that of course, if he does end up leaving on a free transfer, there will be no shortage of suitors so Milan would likely have to table a hefty contract offer. The club owner, Gerry Cardinale, seems more willing to do that now, though, than he did just a year ago.

One potential problem is that Manchester United have confirmed that they are discussing a renewal with the player. The CEO Omar Berrada showered the Portuguese star with praise, as cited by Fabrizio Romano, so it seems a stay in Manchester cannot be ruled out either.
