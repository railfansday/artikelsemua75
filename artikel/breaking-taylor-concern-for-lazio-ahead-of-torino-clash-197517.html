Lazio midfielder Kenneth Taylor is a doubt for his side’s upcoming Serie A clash away against Torino, as he was unable to take part in Gennaro Gattuso’s training session on Thursday according to the latest updates.

Recent findings suggest that the Netherlands international has been a key player in Lazio’s impressive start to the 2026-27 season, starting in all 12 games of his side’s unbeaten run. The Biancocelesti are one of two teams, along with city rivals Roma, who remain unbeaten after 12 league matches and they presently sit alone at the top of the Serie A table with 30 points from their first 12 games of the season.

In a concerning upgrade on Thursday, however, it was confirmed that Taylor did not take part in Lazio’s training session due to muscular fatigue.

This means that he is a risk for his side’s upcoming Serie A match away against Torino, although his condition is being monitored and he will be checked on a regular basis before Saturday’s match in Turin.

Fisayo Dele-Bashiru is the likely candidate to join Davide Frattesi and Reda Belahyane in midfield if Taylor is unable to play.
