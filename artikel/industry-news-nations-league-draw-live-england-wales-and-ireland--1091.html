So we know that England topped Group 1C on 15 points with a superior goal difference to Spain, who they beat 3-2 in Madrid last Sunday.

Of their potential quarter-final opponents, Belgium finished second to France in 1A, just one point behind after losing 1-0 at home and 4-1 away to the group winners.

The Netherlands ended second to Germany in 1B, with the exact same set of six results but an inferior goal difference.

Having been in the same group as Spain, England's pool of opponents is the other three runners-up.

That means Belgium, the Netherlands or Norway await in the last eight.

The Nations League quarter-finals will be played over two legs in March, with England at home in the second leg thanks to topping Group 1C.

England knocked Norway out in the quarter-finals of the World Cup earlier this year, after extra time, while Belgium lost to eventual winners Spain at the same stage.

The Netherlands were meanwhile stunned by Morocco on penalties in the round of 32.

So, the main event is the draw for next year's quarter-finals, which will take place in March.
