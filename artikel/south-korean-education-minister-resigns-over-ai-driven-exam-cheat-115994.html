South Korean education minister resigns over AI-driven exam cheating scandal

🌐 Source: France24 • 📅 22 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (49 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

South Korea Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned Sunday over an AI-assisted cheating scandal that erupted days before the high-stakes Suneung university entrance exam. The Suneung concluded "without any major incidents" last week, Choi said, but he admitted to a failure to "prepare adequately in advance".
