DoorDash vs. Etsy: Which Consumer Favorite Stock Is a Better Buy for 2027?

In a recent development, should everyday investors prioritize the rapid growth of DoorDash (DASH +0.35%) or the specialized marketplace niche of Etsy (ETSY -0.62%)? Both companies serve distinct corners of the digital economy as they eye 2027. (Related: Should I Quit My Job? A Decision Framework...)

According to recent reports, doorDash dominates local logistics by connecting consumers with restaurants and retailers through a vast delivery network. Etsy operates a global creative marketplace focusing on handmade and vintage items. While both rely on a two-sided platform model, their growth trajectories and valuation profiles differ significantly for those looking at the next few years. (Related: How to Start a Side Business While Working Full-Time...)

It has been revealed that doorDash operates a local commerce platform connecting merchants, consumers, and Dashers, who are independent contractors providing delivery services. The company facilitates on-demand delivery for restaurants, grocers, and other retail businesses through its two-sided platform. In its latest annual report, the company noted that it operates in more than 40 countries and ended the year with more than 56 million monthly active people.

In a recent development, in the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue reached about $17.1 billion, representing revenue growth of approximately 35% compared to the prior 12 months. During this period, the firm generated net income of around $1.1 billion. This shift to profitability highlights the increasing scale of its marketplace and the growth of membership programs like DashPass.

According to recent reports, as of its December 2025 balance sheet, the company's debt-to-equity ratio was approximately 0.3x, calculated by dividing total debt by shareholders' equity. Its current ratio, which compares short-term assets to short-term liabilities, was roughly 1.4x. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow reached about $2.2 billion, though stock-based compensation represented roughly 43% of operating cash flow. This compensation level inflates reported cash generation because it is a non-cash expense added back in the cash flow statement.

Etsy runs a global two-sided marketplace for creative entrepreneurs, connecting millions of active buyers and sellers interested in unique or handmade goods. According to its latest annual report, the marketplace serves roughly 86.5 million active buyers and 5.6 million active sellers, many of whom are home-based businesses. This specialized focus helps distinguish the tech giant from broader competition among retail stocks.

In a recent development, in the 12 months ended Sept. 30, 2026, revenue was around $2.9 billion, a figure representing revenue growth of approximately 2.1% over the prior 12 months. The company reported net income of about $987.5 million for the period, resulting in a net margin of roughly 34%. This high level of net profitability reflects the lean nature of its marketplace business model.

As of its December 2025 balance sheet, Etsy reported a debt-to-equity ratio of nearly -2.8x, which means total liabilities exceed shareholder equity. The organization maintains a current ratio of approximately 1.4x, indicating it has sufficient liquid assets to cover short-term obligations. In the fiscal year ended Dec. 31, 2025, free cash flow was about $678 million, while stock-based compensation represented roughly 35% of operating cash flow. This non-cash add-back inflates reported cash generation on the cash flow statement.

DoorDash faces intense competition from local on-demand delivery companies and large-scale retailers. The company is also subject to regulatory and operational risks regarding the classification of its Dashers as independent contractors. Furthermore, the business manages risks involving cybersecurity threats and the financial pressure of maintaining rapid international expansion while relying on third-party payment processors.

Etsy competes with a wide variety of online and offline retail venues, including major platforms like Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN -1.39%) and eBay Inc. (EBAY -2.51%). The organization's growth depends on its ability to attract and retain both buyers and sellers while managing dependencies on infrastructure providers like Google Cloud from Alphabet Inc. (GOOG -1.58%). Legal risks concerning intellectual property and the complexities of international tax regimes also remain key factors, alongside the pending divestiture of Depop.

Etsy appears to be the more conservatively valued option based on its Forward P/E, while DoorDash commands a higher P/S ratio, measuring market capitalization relative to sales over the past twelve months.

Valuation metrics include those sourced from Financial Modeling Prep (FMP) and may differ from those of other data providers.

One of the advantages of Etsy is that individual sellers on the platform are passionate about what they sell and are known for their attention to detail and high customer service. A company with passionate users often has passionate shareholders, too. Metrics such as active buyers and growth in gross merchandise sales per active buyer are improving as the company benefits from personalization and AI initiatives. Etsy's capital-light model means it doesn't need to allocate significant capital to manufacturing facilities or inventory.

In a recent development, still, top-line growth isn't very strong. The company is expected to see a slight $12 million rise in revenue this fiscal year to $2.895 billion. Yet that will come with a more than 550% jump in net income to $1.059 billion, a level Wall Street analysts see as sustainable in the year ahead.

Recent findings suggest that originally a prepared-food delivery service, DoorDash has been finding success by expanding into latest business verticals, such as delivering electronics, apparel, and groceries. Groceries, in particular, are expected to grow twice as fast as meal delivery for the company in the coming years. International expansion, wth the acquisition of the U.K. based Deliveroo, is helping the company expand as well. Advances in efficiency and speed, and improvements that help customers build order carts in the app, are improving sales, too.

For the current fiscal year, 2026, revenue is expected to rise 30% to $17.8 billion, with net income improving about 20% to $1.13 billion.

According to recent reports, so, which is the better buy? Top-line growth makes DoorDash tempting, but the remarkable profit margin of Etsy, coupled with its low forward P/E ratio, is too good to ignore.
