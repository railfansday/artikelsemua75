Sources indicate that introducing a fresh form factor, this is also the most evolved version of Nothing’s transparent design language. Built around an aluminium case with squared edges and a cool metallic finish, Ear (note) introduces machined metal and brutalist forms into Nothing’s transparent design language. Clear windows are inspired by the contours of topographic maps, revealing depth and dimensionality within the case. The TALK button and a new RECORD button draw on the controls on a retro Walkman, both positioned to be easy to reach and to provide tactile feedback when activated.

Speaking to Bloomberg, Nothing has confirmed that its fresh AI product is “Ear (note).” This builds on a pair of earbuds by turning the charging case into an AI-powered recorder.

The case has a “Talk” button, like Ear (3), which can be used to record your own voice, where a record button enables the ability to “record and transcribe conversations.” Ear (3a)’s ability to record audio from the earbuds will also carry over to Ear (note), giving the product the ability to record both conversations in person and over the phone.

Nothing is entering a crowded market here with products from Plaud, Amazon, and countless others already offering AI recording/transcribing products, but building in earbuds is indeed a unique selling point.

Nothing Ear (note) is launching on December 8, though pricing is unclear. Pei’s comments suggest a subscription will be used in some capacity.
