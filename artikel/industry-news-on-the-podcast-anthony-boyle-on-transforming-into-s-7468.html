According to recent reports, on a crisp mid-November morning, Anthony Boyle arrived to the podcast studio wearing a leather jacket, or as our Head of Editorial Content, Chloe Malle, called it “Jensen’s jacket”—a reference to the signature leather jacket donned by disgraced Nvidia founder Jensen Huang. There has been much chatter in Vogue offices about the correspondence between his leather jacket uniform and the fact that he was having an affair. “He’s a top shagger,” Boyle joked, “it’s an homage to him.”.

This coincidence felt topical given the big tech moment we are in as a culture, as well as the fact that Boyle came on the podcast to speak with Chloe and our Deputy Editor Taylor Antrim about his role as Sam Bankman-Fried in the new series The Altruists, which is out today on Netflix. Boyle stars alongside Julia Garner, who plays Bankman-Fried’s on-again, off-again girlfriend and business partner, Caroline Ellison. The two of them portray the nerdy genius couple that made it big during the cryptocurrency boom and then met their spectacular undoing after being caught stealing billions from customers. Unlike many of his previous roles—which had him portraying mustachioed Irishmen in period garb—Boyle spoke about the source material he mined in service of his transformation into this awkward American mastermind. “There was a lot of footage of him. The lad loved giving interviews,” Boyle explained, “Even at the collapse [of the company], when all the lawyers were like, ‘Stop giving interviews,’ he was like, ‘Just one more…’”.

Boyle once attended a Los Angeles warehouse party with some “crypto lads” as a form of character study. “I thought there was something really interesting in that no one had ever looked at these guys. These were guys who were in their mothers’ basement, and they suddenly became billionaires. Suddenly they had power, and they had money, which in America makes you a god. If you have money in Ireland, it makes you a wanker,” he laughed, “I do think he truly believed in effective altruism, but, you know, the best laid intends of mice and men gang aft agley.”.

They also discussed several of his other roles from his Broadway debut in Harry Potter and the Cursed Child to his beloved performance in Say Nothing. He reminisced about his years living in midtown Manhattan, including a “great little Irish bar in Port Authority.” During his rare moments of downtime, he’s been known to watch reality television. “Anything with ‘love’ in the title, I’ll watch.”.
