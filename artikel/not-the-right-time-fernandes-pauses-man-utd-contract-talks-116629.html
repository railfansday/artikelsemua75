Bruno Fernandes has scored in each of his past two games for Manchester United.

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes says he has been offered a fresh contract by the club but feels it "is not the right time" for discussions about his future.

The 32-year-old midfielder is out of contract at the end of the season, though the club have the option to trigger a 12-month extension until 2028.

He was left out of the side under previous manager Michael Carrick but indicates he has no issues at the club and has chosen to stay at Old Trafford despite offers elsewhere in recent seasons.

He told Sky Sports:, external "The club offered me a fresh contract, so I think that, from their side, reveals that they want me to stay.

"There's no negotiations ongoing, but I've seen people saying that 'he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years'. If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here. (Related: Simple considerations for simple people building fancy ...)

"I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

"Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here.". (Related: Industry News: Summer at Hugging Face...)

United chief executive Omar Berrada recently spoke about Fernandes' contract situation, saying: "It's true that we're having conversations about his latest contract, but I prefer to keep that private.".

Fernandes has scored 75 Premier League goals since signing from Sporting in 2020 and last season set a record for the most assists in a Premier League season.

His form has tailed off this season in a poor start for the club as a whole.

United are 19th in the Premier League table going into Mauricio Pochettino's first game in charge against rivals Liverpool on Sunday afternoon.

"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season," Fernandes mentioned.

"We came in at the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations.

"The most important thing now is that the club gets back to winning games.".

Fernandes was dropped for the Premier League defeat by Bournemouth last month after being critical of United's approach in their heavy defeat against Como in the Champions League.

But Fernandes says he did not fall out with Carrick, who was sacked on 12 November.

Recent findings suggest that "The manager came to speak to me before the Bournemouth game, to say: 'Look, I'm not going to start you tomorrow. There's nothing to do with the words of Como, I know people will make it around that. I just want to tell you that we need to change something, I need to make a change, performances have not been great, so I just want you to know that there's nothing to do with that'," Fernandes said.
