According to recent reports, grand Theft Auto 6 is mere days away, and Rockstar is understandably gearing up for its significant launch. With GTA 6 already projected to sell billions within its first year, it could very well be the biggest game launch of all time, surpassing the likes of Skyrim and, of course, GTA V. It's an exciting time to be a Grand Theft Auto fan, especially one who has been waiting 13 years for the release of a latest mainline game, as the tension and elation surrounding GTA 6 are undeniably palpable at this point.

To build up the hype around it even more and to keep people busy as they preload GTA 6's enormous file size, Rockstar has decided to hand out a handful of freebies to fans. There are no weird loopholes you have to jump through to get them, as Rockstar has, thankfully, elected not to make them difficult to download.
