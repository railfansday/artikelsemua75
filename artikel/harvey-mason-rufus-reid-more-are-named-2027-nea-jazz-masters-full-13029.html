It has been revealed that harvey Mason has watched his son Harvey Mason Jr. become CEO of the Recording Academy and one of the most visible people in the music field. But jazz fans know Mason Sr. as a celebrated drummer, composer and producer in this own right. And now he’s a Jazz Master, so declared by The National Endowment for the Arts.

The Jazz Masters Fellowship is the nation’s highest honor in jazz. The Arts Endowment, in collaboration with the DC Jazz Festival, will honor this year’s five recipients in a tribute concert on Monday, April 5, at Arena Stage in Washington, D.C., and streamed online.

“Through their remarkable artistry, leadership and dedication to nurturing future generations, the 2027 National Endowment for the Arts Jazz Masters have worked to advance the distinctly American art form of jazz,” Mary Anne Carter, chairman of the Arts Endowment, stated in a statement. “We look forward to partnering with the DC Jazz Festival to celebrate their contributions and the enduring power of jazz.”.
