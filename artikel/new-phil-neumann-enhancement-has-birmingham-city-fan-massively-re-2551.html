Birmingham City's league-leading start to the Championship season has been the culmination of many key factors, none more so than the consistency displayed by Chris Davies' side.

A 10th-placed finish last term gave the Blues boss plenty of foundations to build from, even if the season as a whole was one of mixed fortunes in terms of both results and performance levels.

Birmingham have scored three or more goals in four of their last six matches whilst also conceding the fewest number of goals in the Championship thus far - 14.

Those within Davies' rearguard have earned plenty of praise for their contributions towards such exploits, but in the case of German centre-back Phil Neumann, it has also led to exit talk.

Neumann, 29, has been an ever-present alongside Birmingham captain, Christoph Klarer, so far this season, following on from an intriguing first campaign in English football after leaving Hannover 96. (Related: Fine-Tune Wav2Vec2 for English ASR in Hugging Face with...)

The German youth international had previously been the subject of interest from clubs such as Sassuolo, Hull City and Borussia Mönchengladbach, but recent reports have suggested that Nottingham Forest, Fiorentina, Coventry City, Ipswich Town and the Tigers are plotting a potential January move for the towering figure.

Given the nature of modern football, Neumann has since revealed that he is aware of such speculation, but, to the delight of Blues fans, stated that his main aim is to return his current employers to the Premier League. (Related: Scaling up BERT-like model Inference on modern CPU - P...)

Sources indicate that "Of course I hear about those kinds of reports, but I don't pay much attention to them. I have my advisors for that, people I trust who handle those matters," he told Transfermarkt.

It has been revealed that "My full focus is on Birmingham City and what we've set out to achieve as a team. There's nothing more crucial to me than doing everything I can to achieve our goals with the club.

Sources indicate that "We have a big task ahead of us, and that's where I'm concentrating all my energy. Everything else is secondary at the moment.".

Neumann's total of 12.1 defensive contributions per 90 is right now the seventh-highest figure of any Championship player and the largest amongst Birmingham's squad at present, which comes on top of his brace against Wrexham in August that sealed the first of eight victories so far for Davies' side.

Such metrics only back up the defender's commitment to the promotion cause at St. Andrew's @ Knighthead Park, and Football League World's Birmingham City fan pundit, Jason Moore, is one of many supporters who have been reassured by Neumann's words.

"Massively relieved," Moore told FLW.

"I sort of expected him to have that mindset because his family's settled here, he's settled here, he's performing really well.

"I get the interest because he's performing well. But, I expected that he'd just knuckle down and focus on the club. It's a massive relief.

"He's been an absolute rock. I honestly believe we've got the best centre-back partnership in the league. I don't think there's many that come close to potentially touching either of them," he added.

It has been revealed that "To have him focused and just wanting to keep performing for the club and wanting to go for promotion is just a massive relief. Touchwood, he stays focused, and we can achieve great stuff this season.".

Neumann may have never played top-flight football in his career so far, but with the defender in his prime years, he is very much on the cusp of earning those stripes in one form or another.

Given Birmingham's lofty short and long-term ambitions, it is no surprise that the defender wants to aid those plans whilst continuing to be valued as a first-team regular, which wouldn't necessarily be guaranteed if a January move was sanctioned.

Moore recently told FLW that the Second City outfit should only consider offers if they were between the £15m and £20m ballpark, but if Neumann continues his remarkable partnership with Klarer over the next 31 matches, there is every chance Tom Wagner could land over 10 times' as much cash.
