Recent findings suggest that new arrivals are landing in the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog this month, but at the same time, you can also expect a few departures from that gaming library. Fortunately, the number of games leaving soon are on the smaller side, as there'll be only three exits in total. Unfortunately, one of those games that you're running out of time to play through the Game Catalog is a massive and pretty fun fantasy title, so get those hours in while you can. PlayStation Plus Game Catalog games leaving soon Granblue Fantasy Relink Granblue Fantasy Relink Somerville Fade to Silence This month's biggest departure is Granblue Fantasy Relink, a massive fantasy game with a storybook art style, cinematic boss fights, and a large roster of characters who all possess unique abilities to master. First released in February 2024, Granblue Fantasy Relink is built around real-time combat and flashy team-up mechanics, making for a visually striking experience overall. While the story isn't much to write home about, it's the gameplay and worldbuilding where this Granblue spin-off shines, and it got even better earlier this year with the Endless Ragnarok expansion. In comparison, Somerville is a story-rich puzzle game that's oozing atmosphere with its slick art direction, and Fade to Silence is a single-player and co-op survival-crafting adventure set in a post-apocalyptic world that is feeling the chill of an eternal winter. While the Game Catalog is going to be a little poorer without those three titles, it is getting some great games this month. Death Stranding 2: On the Beach is the headline act, and it'll be joined by several more titles like Atomfall, Dragon Quest 3 HD-2D Remake, and Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii. All PlayStation Plus subscribers can also grab a trio of freebies between now and November 30: Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider 4-6 Remastered, and Monster Train 2.
