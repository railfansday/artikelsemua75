The Week 11 slate is the smallest of the bunch in 2026. In all, including Thursday's demolition of the Colts by Houston, there are 13 games this week, thanks to a grand total of six teams on the bye. On top of it being small in stature, there are four games with double-digit spreads, including games where the Chiefs and Bills are more than two-touchdown favorites. That combination makes it a pretty tricky slate to wager on.

According to recent reports, but that's where we come in. While you're out dealing with more key items, we've been in the weeds following the latest injury updates and other storylines heading into the weekend and have formulated our picks for Week 11. Instead of frantically doing your own research in between your comings and goings, you can just sit back, relax, and take advantage of our last-minute picks, which include five best bets.

Note: Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, Los Angeles (Rams), Latest England and Seattle are on the bye in Week 11.

All lines via DraftKings; All times ET.

I think Dallas is one of those teams that plays up or down to their opponent. So while it may have gone toe-to-toe with the 49ers last week, I wouldn't be surprised in the slightest to see the 1-8 Titans hang around. In fact, I'm betting they will. Tennessee is a respectable 3-1 ATS on the road this season. Conversely, AT&T Stadium hasn't been much of an advantage for Dallas, as it is 1-3 ATS at home.

Over their last three games, the Titans offense has also been pretty solid, averaging 5.8 yards per play. That's the sixth-highest in the NFL over that stretch. A large part of that success has been thanks to the ground game, as they are averaging 5.4 yards per carry. The only team better than them is the 49ers, who just ran for 214 yards and three touchdowns against this Dallas defense in Week 10. The Cowboys' defense is showing no signs of improvement, as their 6.1 yards allowed per play are the sixth-highest in the NFL season. At the very least, the backdoor will be left wide open. Projected score: Cowboys 30, Titans 23The pick: Titans +8.5.

Recent findings suggest that los Angeles finally got a win, snapping an eight-game losing streak to start the season and an 11-game skid dating back to last year (including playoffs). This moment in the winner's circle, however, will be short-lived. With Geno Smith off the injury report, that only strengthens my faith that the Jets will take down the Chargers on Sunday. I assume Smith plays, and with him under center, the Jets have been solid. (Related: Predicting and detecting failures: How Azure strengthen...)

Industry observers note that moreover, I thought New York's defense largely hung around against Buffalo last week before the wheels fell off in the second half. Justin Herbert is not Josh Allen, nor is Omarion Hampton James Cook, so the Jets' defense should be able to keep this offense in check for four quarters. New York also has the rest advantage after LA played in Baltimore on Monday night.

The Chargers have yet to record an ATS win (0-4) at home this season.

Projected score: Jets 24, Chargers 20The pick: Jets +3.

The Jaguars are a freight train right now. Over their three-game winning streak, Jacksonville is averaging 38.3 points per game. For the season, they're averaging 6.0 yards per play (tied for fifth-highest in the NFL), a 46.8% conversion rate on third down (third-highest) and 8.7 yards per pass attempt (second-highest). Trevor Lawrence is playing at an MVP level, while running back Bhayshul Tuten is in the midst of a full-blown breakout. Over the winning streak, he's rushed for 221 yards and three touchdowns, while adding 80 yards and a touchdown on five receptions.

Meanwhile, Jacksonville's defense is about to get a boost this week as two of their trade deadline acquisitions (safety Jessie Bates III and defensive tackle Jordan Davis) are all set to make their debuts after Liam Coen gave them Week 11 off to get acclimated.

While Newly introduced York does boast a defense that is allowing just 305.7 total yards per game this season (fifth-best in the NFL), the Jaguars feel like an entirely different animal to overcome. I also believe Jacksonville's defense, which is allowing 4.7 yards per play over the last three games (tied for fifth-lowest in the NFL), can contain a J.J. McCarthy-led Giants offense that still gives me pause.

When you look at the trends, the Jaguars have given bettors confidence to back them on the road this season. As the away team, Jacksonville is 3-1 ATS with a 9.5-point average margin of victory, the third-highest in the NFL.

Projected score: Jaguars 33, Giants 21The pick: Jaguars -6.

Fernando Mendoza actually played pretty well given the circumstances, in my opinion. The No. 1 overall pick was making his first career start against one of the toughest defenses in the NFL: the Seahawks. Naturally, he looked like a rookie quarterback at times, completing 17-of-29 for 143 yards, no touchdowns, and no interceptions. He was also sacked six times. That stated, he showed some flashes, including a touchdown run on his first possession of the afternoon. While Mendoza has a bright future in front of him, his baptism by fire will continue in Week 11 as he and the Raiders travel to Denver to face a Broncos team that was on the bye in Week 10.

Sources indicate that denver is 3-0 coming off a bye under coach Sean Payton, and its pass rush could prove to be the difference in this game and keep Las Vegas outside of the number. This season, Denver is second in the NFL in pressures (159) and tied for the ninth-most sacks in the league (26), according to TruMedia. At home, this feels like a recipe for Mendoza to be under siege early and often, allowing Bo Nix and the Broncos offense to simply take advantage of what has been a poor Vegas defense. Coming into Week 11, the Raiders are tied for the most points per game allowed (28.9) in the NFL.

Projected score: Broncos 27, Raiders 16The pick: Broncos -10.5.

There are a lot of big spreads this week, so we're naturally going to see some teams creep through the back door. I think New Orleans could be one of them. The Saints are tied for the second-most pass attempts per game (38.1) in the NFL this season. That number jumps to a league-high 39.5 when they're on the road. That should only continue in this matchup against Chicago as they try to keep up with Ben Johnson and Caleb Williams on the other side.

The Saints' passing attack is also getting a midseason boost with the return of first-round rookie Jordyn Tyson. Over the first two games of his career, the No. 8 overall pick has looked like the real deal, including an 11-catch, 80-yard outing last week in the loss to Carolina. Paired with Chris Olave, that's a nice one-two punch for Tyler Shough to get us to a cover. Speaking of covers, that's all New Orleans has done this season, as they're the only team in the NFL without an ATS loss on the road so far in 2026 (3-0).

Projected score: Bears 30, Saints 21The pick: Saints +11.5.
