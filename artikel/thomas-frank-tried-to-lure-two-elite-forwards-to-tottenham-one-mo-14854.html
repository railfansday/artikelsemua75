Crystal Palace boss Thomas Frank was one of few managers to get the best out of Richarlison but he wanted to lure two forwards to Tottenham Hotspur instead.

That is according to former senior Tottenham scout, Bryan King, speaking exclusively to Football Insider.

The 29-year-old scored eight goals under the Dane in the first half of last season, before he was dismissed in February for his team's terrible form. (Related: Leading House Democrat targets Trump’s inner circle for...)

Richarlison is now looking for a route away from Tottenham, having been left out of Roberto De Zerbi's Premier League squad following several failed exits.

The striker has just six months left on his contract in North London and has not appeared in a competitive game for the senior squad since the opening day.

There was not a lot of good about Frank's time at Tottenham, but he did seem to get Richarlison firing.

The striker was coming off the back of a season in which he had scored just five goals, but he immediately emerged as a key man for his newly introduced manager.

Richarlison netted a brace on the Premier League's opening weekend, and would register contributions against Manchester City, Manchester united, Arsenal and Liverpool.

It was not enough to save Frank, but the pair's reunion at Palace could be a good move for all parties.

In a recent development, "I'll tell you who's gone quiet. Richarlison at Tottenham," King told Football Insider. "I wouldn't take him anywhere, but there again, Frank seemed to believe in him.

"Well, he did get a tune out of him. And I would imagine he wasn't very happy with Spurs, to be honest.

Recent findings suggest that "To not get [Ivan] Toney and miss out on Bryan Mbeumo. That's what Tottenham needed and those were the two he wanted. One went to Man United for £70m.".

Richarlison's wage demands ultimately saw moves to Everton, Trabzonspor and Vasco da Gama fall through in the summer.

It is unclear if anything would change for Palace, but after six months in the cold, the striker's stance may have softened.

There is certainly respect for Frank from the 29-year-old, too, which could play a role in potential negotiations.

Posting after the Dane was sacked in February, Richarlison wrote: "Thanks mister for everything!!!".
