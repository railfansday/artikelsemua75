Fresh from making his Germany debut, Brajan Gruda recorded a goal and assist as RB Leipzig brushed aside struggling Union Berlin on Matchday 10 of the 2026/27 Bundesliga season.

Leipzig’s newly-capped star assisted Rômulo for the go-ahead goal after Michel Aebischer equalised following Antonio Nusa’s converted spot kick. Gruda then struck Leipzig’s third of the afternoon in a 5-1 rout of the Berlin club, which places Leipzig in fourth, just three points behind leaders Borussia Dortmund. In a recent development, raving about Gruda, Demichelis noted: “I love the lad, and have a good relationship with him – both as a player and as a person,” Leipzig’s head coach explained. “He played better in the second half than in the first, but there’s still a lot of work ahead of him. We need to help him a bit and give him some space.

“He’s a great talent, but there’s much more at stake than that.”.

One of Jürgen Klopp’s seven winners after scoring two goals and providing two assists in his first two appearances for the German national team, Gruda is set to make a permanent move to the Saxony club for €25m due to a clause in the player’s loan from Brighton and Hove Albion.
