Newcastle United are likely among several European giants keeping tabs on Ethan Nwaneri's situation in the 2026-27 campaign.

The Arsenal gem is on loan at Borussia Dortmund this term, and he was expected to be the latest Englishman to shine in the Bundesliga.

It hasn't quite worked out for the 19-year-old, though, just a few months after his very disappointing six months at Marseille in 2025-26.

His same struggles are emerging at the Signal Iduna Park, and the Gunners are clearly becoming concerned by his lack of minutes.

According to the Daily Mail (24 November), the Premier League leaders are closely monitoring the situation ahead of the January transfer window.

Arsenal included a recall clause in Nwaneri's contract, a clause that was initially included in case an injury crisis emerged at the Emirates Stadium.

While Bukayo Saka's injury has left Mikel Arteta short in the final third, Nwaneri's lack of minutes is a far more pressing matter this season.

He has started just four games in the Bundesliga, and a further 87 minutes across four Champions League matches, and 19 minutes in the DFB Pokal.

Recalling him and sending him elsewhere seems like the best option for all parties, and Matthias Jaissle's Magpies could benefit greatly.

Newcastle have held previous interest in Nwaneri, and it would be a surprise if Newcastle didn't reignite that in the January window.

He'd fit in perfectly with the likes of Harvey Barnes, Yoane Wissa, Anthony Elanga and Matias Fernandez-Pardo - sharp, quick, creative, and dangerous.

He may be struggling for minutes, but his underlying stats when he does manage to get onto the pitch are certaintly a good sign.

In the Bundesliga, the 19-year-old is registering 1.34 shots on target per 90 minutes, an 84.1 per cent pass completion rate, and completes 2.14 dribbles [FotMob].

He ranks in the top 87 percentile for dribbling success in the division, and with 1.07 interceptions per 90 minutes, he ranks in the top 88th percentile.

Nwaneri continues to prove himself in small bursts, and he could be the perfect addition to the Newcastle squad for the second half of the season.
