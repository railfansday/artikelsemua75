A Louisiana coffee shop co-owner claims she refused to serve a TikTok creator who returned to her Baton Rouge cafe after posting two negative reviews.

Sources indicate that victoria Arroyo co-owns Pura Vida Coffee Bar, a cafe near Louisiana State University. TikTok creator Fabian Beaucoudray had criticized the shop’s coffee and food in earlier videos, and Arroyo included clips from those reviews in her response.

Recent findings suggest that in one review she also posted to Snapchat, Beaucoudray said she went back to give the cafe a second chance. She took issue with paying $3 for what she described as five small pieces of potato alongside a $10 sandwich.

Beaucoudray posted about being refused service on November 19. She had returned to the cafe with a friend, according to a recap from TikTok creator thenameisblossom. Beaucoudray wrote in the caption of her own video that she had “never seen a company take a bad review this serious.”.

Sources indicate that arroyo said she was working the register when she recognized Beaucoudray and decided to turn her away. According to Arroyo, Beaucoudray had left both times without mentioning her complaints to staff. Arroyo said that she left the shop without a chance to address the problems before the videos went up.

Arroyo tagged Beaucoudray in her November 20 response and addressed her directly in the caption. “I am genuinely sorry if you were embarrassed today, that was never my intention,” she wrote.

Arroyo’s response passed one million views, while some commenters defended her decision, and others noted the videos made them want to visit the shop.

It has been revealed that another TikTok food reviewer drew a public response after criticizing the coffee at a Phoenix bakery. In Atlanta, Keith Lee went viral after his family was refused service at The Real Milk and Honey.
