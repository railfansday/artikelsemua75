First-Ever NCAA Football Game in Puerto Rico Will Air on CBS Sports Network December 16.

NEW YORK and SAN JUAN, Puerto Rico, Nov. 24, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- TransPerfect, the title sponsor and owner of the inaugural TransPerfect San Juan Bowl, today revealed that the game will be televised nationally on CBS Sports Network and streamed on the CBS Sports app. Scheduled for Wednesday, December 16, 2026, at Hiram Bithorn Stadium in San Juan, the historic matchup will mark the first NCAA Division I Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) bowl game played in Puerto Rico.
