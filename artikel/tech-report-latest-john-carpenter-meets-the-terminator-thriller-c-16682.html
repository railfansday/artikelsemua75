Adria Arjona plays Celeste Cross in Onslaught, which also stars Dan Stevens, Eric Wareheim, Reginald VelJohnson, Michael Biehn, Alex Pereira, Drew Starkey and Rebecca Hall.

Simon Barrett wrote the script for the film, which takes place in the same universe as Wingard's 2014 thriller The Guest. The director and screenwriter also served as producers with Aaron Ryder, Andrew Swett, Jeremy Platt and Alexander Black.

Recent findings suggest that aside from having an underwhelming box office return, Onslaught also received mixed reviews from critics, who gave it a 59% on Rotten Tomatoes. Reviewers claimed the film relied on too many clichés with an incoherent premise. On the other hand, some of the more positive reviews called Onslaught intriguing, entertaining and intense, and praised Arjona's performance.

The audience score on Rotten Tomatoes is virtually the same at 60%.

Onslaught is one of A24's more recent films, alongside other 2025 releases like The Drama, Mother Mary, Backrooms, Tony, Primetime and the hit documentary You Can See Everything about Elizabeth Holmes.

Before Onslaught, Wingard had directed the blockbusters Godzilla vs. Kong and Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire, which grossed $470 million and $572 million, respectively. He decided not to return for the upcoming Godzilla x Kong: Supernova in order to direct Onslaught. Wingard has also helmed Blair Witch, Death Note and You're Next, among other projects.

Onslaught will be released on HBO Max on Tuesday, December 22, 2026.
