American Plans Return to Israel, Partners With Israir

🌐 Source: Airlinegeeks • 📅 20 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 2 min read (320 words, 📖 Standard)

  • American has not flown to Israel since the Hamas attack on southern Israel in October 2023.
📑 Quick Jump: 1. Overview • 2. Key Details • 3. Closing Notes

After a three-year hiatus, American Airlines is preparing to resume service to Israel.

The carrier said Friday that it will restart flights from Latest York-JFK to Tel Aviv on March 27, 2027. Flights will operate daily using Boeing 777-200ER aircraft.

American has not flown to Israel since the Hamas attack on southern Israel in October 2023. It remained on the sidelines even as rivals United and Delta resumed service to Ben Gurion International Airport, though they too were forced to temporarily pause flights during fighting between Israel and Iran. Israeli officials had questioned why American was slow to reconnect the country, citing the importance of air links between Tel Aviv and New York. The airline did not offer an explanation beyond concern for the safety of its passengers and crew. (Related: Anthropic gave $40M to an AI safety group. Most of the ...)

American also said Friday that it has formed a commercial partnership with Tel Aviv-based low-cost carrier Israir. The agreement will expand in phases over time, American said; in the first phase, Israir will place its code on American’s flights between JFK and Tel Aviv.

The carriers will also work to implement an interline agreement that would give Israir customers access to American’s network across the U.S., Canada, Latin America, and the Caribbean from JFK.

“American looks forward to building a relationship with Israir that will give customers the convenient, well-connected travel options they desire,” American Senior Vice President of Alliances Anmol Bhargava mentioned in a news release. “As we prepare to resume service between Newly introduced York and Tel Aviv next spring, this partnership will help us deliver a stronger customer proposition in the market and create more opportunities for Israir’s travelers to connect beyond our JFK gateway.”.

According to recent reports, israir operates five weekly flights between JFK and Tel Aviv using Airbus A330-200 aircraft. Newly introduced York is its only U.S. destination; most of its network is concentrated in the Near East and Europe.
