It has been revealed that raya has been Arsenal’s first-choice goalkeeper since September 2023 after a short settling in period after his move from Brentford. Remember when Mikel Arteta tried telling us he had two number ones and that it’d be a straight battle between Raya and Aaron Ramsdale every week? That didn’t last long. Sources indicate that since making his league debut for Arsenal, Raya has only missed three games in the top flight. Two were against his parent club Brentford in his debut season, when he was still on loan and therefore ineligible. The third was on the final day of last season, when Arsenal were already home and dry as Premier League champions.

It has been revealed that in other words, when Arsenal need Raya, Raya plays. But depending on the results of his scan, they might have to do without him for a bit.

Kepa Arrizabalaga will be on standby and ready to step up in his absence, with Illan Meslier next in line after.

They normally only get their chances in cup competitions, which can give some insight into how Arsenal do in Raya’s absence.

But for the duration of his Arsenal career so far, how have Arsenal done when Raya has been playing and when he hasn’t?
