The Fastest Shift in FMCG and Retail on Record.

In 2024, shopping with AI was a curiosity: a few hundred million people used AI at all. In the fourth quarter of 2026, three billion people use AI, and more than half of consumers shop with AI. The change took 24 months and reached fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) and retail first.

FMCG and Retail Now Run AI Agents. Their Product Data Is Not Ready.

Shoppers moved to AI first. Brands and retailers are now building AI agents to meet AI shoppers, on product data that was never made for AI agents.

In a recent development, the same product carries a different description in every system. An AI agent that matches on words can pick the wrong size, the wrong pack or the wrong maker. The barcode is the one identifier that the brand, the retailer and the AI agent already share.

It has been revealed that "We built gtinfetch.ai as the AI agentic shopping barcode engine, the largest AI-agent-native source of verified barcodes, for every consumer AI agent that buys," said Matthew Keddy, Chief Executive Officer of WeFetch AI Corp. "A shopper's AI query starts as text, and it turns into a sale more reliably when the answer is anchored to a barcode. With 84 million barcodes and 30 million in monthly AI agent traffic, we connect our customers to the sale.".
