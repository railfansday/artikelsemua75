NASA Hubble's latest view of NGC 4394 raises the bar

🌐 Source: Phys • 📅 24 Nov 2026 • ⏱️ 1 min read (74 words, ⚡ Quick Read)

Sources indicate that A brilliant galaxy with a distinct galactic bar takes center stage in this NASA/ESA Hubble Space Telescope image. The galaxy, called NGC 4394, is in the constellation Coma Berenices (Berenice's Hair). At about 55 million light-years away, NGC 4394 is considered relatively nearby for a galaxy. It's one of more than 1,000 galaxies that make up the Virgo Cluster, which is the galaxy cluster closest to us.
