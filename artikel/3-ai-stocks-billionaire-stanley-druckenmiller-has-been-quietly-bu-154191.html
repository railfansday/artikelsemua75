Billionaire hedge fund manager Stanley Druckenmiller has seemingly turned bearish on stocks, saying recently at the TomorrowX Summit in Austin that he was largely sitting out of the market and just looking to "not lose money.".

However, while he was a net seller of stocks in Q3, he was quietly adding upcoming positions in three artificial intelligence stocks: Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX +4.35%), Palantir Technologies (PLTR +11.06%), and AppLovin (APP +7.36%).

Let's look at what may have attracted Druckenmiller to these three AI stocks.

Space Exploration Technologies — or SpaceX — is an exciting stock, with the potential to disrupt multiple industries and see rapid growth. While CEO Elon Musk has made some pretty fanciful predictions, such as the company eventually becoming worth more than Earth itself, that hyperbole doesn't take away from what is ultimately a huge opportunity.

In a recent development, with the manufacturer recently catching the upper stage of Starship, it has now demonstrated that it can recapture both stages of the rocket, positioning it for the beginning of rapid rocket reuse. That would completely turn the economics of space on its head and give SpaceX an enormous cost advantage not only for launching more Starlink satellites but also for the future launch of orbital data centers. (Related: Breaking: AI for Game Development: Creating a Farming G...)

In the interim, SpaceX should see significant growth in its terrestrial AI infrastructure business, where it is achieving strong payback periods and growing rapidly. Meanwhile, it also looks as if it wants to take aim at the U.S. mobile market with its Starlink satellite service, and its Cursor AI platform is also growing quickly.

Recent findings suggest that while Druckenmiller was busy selling down positions in hardware companies, he mentioned at the conference that investors should focus on the AI application layer. As such, perhaps it should be no surprise that he was adding shares of Palantir to his portfolio.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

Palantir has become the ultimate AI-applications-layer stock, with its AI platform almost akin to an AI operating system. The company has been seeing rapid growth as customers adopt its platform to make AI models more useful in the real world.

Its platform does this by gathering information from a variety of sources and populating an ontology that it then links to physical assets, business processes, and concepts. This significantly reduces potential AI hallucinations and puts guardrails in place to help AI models perform at their best.

The strength of the company's business can be seen not just in its rapid revenue growth but also in the significant growth it's seeing with existing customers. Over the past 12 months, the company's net dollar retention was an astonishing 165%, which essentially means clients who have been with the company for over a year increased their spending with Palantir by an average of 65%, net of any churn or contraction.

To me, there is no better testament to how good the tech giant's solution is than this metric.

AppLovin is another organization benefiting from using AI to drive growth. The company is primarily an adtech solutions provider to the mobile gaming industry, and it has seen rapid revenue growth and margin expansion since the launch of its Axon-2 solution in 2023.

NASDAQ · As of 4:00 PM ET 11/23/26.

It has been revealed that the stock was beaten down following a slight earnings miss in Q2, as its model didn't improve at its typical rate, but the company bounced back in Q3, with revenue surging 55%. Meanwhile, it sees the transition to a new model that performs better than past ones with less data as a key future driver, as it helps it reach newer customers and verticals.

Given AppLovin's growth and valuation (it has a forward P/E of just 19 times), it's easy to see what Druckenmiller likes about the stock.
