Brajan Gruda scored two goals and provided two assists in his first two appearances for the German national team against Serbia and the Netherlands over the recent international break and with four assists in seven Bundesliga games for RB Leipzig in 2026/27, the 22-year-old’s stock continues to rise.

According to recent reports, on loan from Premier League side Brighton and Hove Albion, Gruda’s move to Leipzig will become permanent in the new year if he plays every match between now and Hoffenheim on 16 January. Sources indicate that that ultimately means that Leipzig can command any fee they wish for their rising star. With interest from Liverpool and Bayern Munich, and with no intention of selling, BILD estimate Leipzig’s valuation of Gruda to be around €100m.

With impressive numbers across his first two Germany appearances, Gruda was naturally one of seven winners in Jürgen Klopp’s November squad.
