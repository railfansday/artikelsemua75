In a recent development, democratic Rep. Jimmy Gomez is resigning from Congress after a House Ethics Committee report determined he allegedly made “unwanted sexual advances” and “sexual contact” with a congressional aide.

“I have strong disagreements with the committee’s report and its conclusions. Among other significant deficiencies and omissions, the report does not include key portions of my testimony that are essential to a full and fair understanding of what occurred,” Gomez, 51, said in a statement to CNN on Monday, November 23. “I also have serious concerns about the process. In my interview, I answered the Committee’s questions only to see my statements selectively quoted, disparaged and disregarded throughout the final report, to which I was given no opportunity to respond.”. The California politician added, “With this in mind, I see no other alternative than to resign, effective December 1, to allow for my staff to close out notable constituent casework and needs.”.

Us has reached out to Gomez’s team for comment regarding the report and his resignation.

According to recent reports, the ethics panel made the Gomez report public on Monday. According to CNN, the report found that Gomez — who lost his bid for re-election this year after serving more than nine years in congress— “failed in his obligation to behave in a manner that reflects creditably on the House, and his conduct was exacerbated by his failure to appreciate the inherent power imbalance between House Members and House staff.”.

In its report, the committee also called for “zero tolerance of unwanted sexual advances toward House staff.”.

MS Now also reported that a woman, identified only as Witness 1 in the report, alleged to the committee that she had more than one physical encounter with Gomez and did not consider them to be consensual.

She described two incidents, which allegedly occurred in 2024, to the committee, claiming the incident was “nonconsensual whatsoever” and noted she remembered “feeling very uncomfortable” and “mortified.”.

Attorneys representing the woman praised the committee for its “thorough and sensitive” handling of the investigation.

“Representative Gomez abused the power given to him by the voters when he violated this young woman, effectively destroying her dreams of a career in public service,” they remarked in a statement on a Monday. “Our client testified truthfully and at great personal risk. She sacrificed her own sense of safety and privacy to help reveal the ongoing misogyny and debauchery that permeates Capitol Hill.”.
