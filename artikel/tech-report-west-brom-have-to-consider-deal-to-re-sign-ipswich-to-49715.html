Tech Report: West Brom have to consider deal to re-sign Ipswich Town man in January transfer window: Opinion

It has been revealed that west Brom will get the chance to bring in some fresh faces this winter ahead of the second-half of the season. WBA are presently in the Championship play-offs along with Stoke City, Wolves, Swansea City, Southampton and QPR after 15 fixtures.

Recent findings suggest that the Baggies were dealt a blow at the start of last month when Noah Galves was ruled out for the rest of the campaign with a serious knee injury. That has left them short of options at full-back and they would benefit from another option to battle with Brayann Pereira.

Step forward Ipswich Town defender Darnell Furlong, who could be made available due to a lack of game time at Portman Road following the Tractor Boys’ promotion to the Premier League. He was on the books at The Hawthorns from 2019 to 2025 and made 247 appearances in all competitions for the Midlands outfit, chipping in with 11 goals from the back.

West Brom could get the opportunity now to try and lure him back in some capacity. They are back in action this weekend with an away trip to Swansea City.

In a recent development, furlong is under contract at Ipswich Town until June 2028. However, he has slipped down the pecking order in Suffolk and has made only three outings so far this term, one one of which has come in the league.

A change of scene to West Brom would make sense for him. He would be able to get some regular game time under his belt at WBA and help their push for promotion under James Morrison.

Furlong, who is from Luton, knows the club inside out already and is a solid operator in the EFL. He knows what it takes to get out of the second tier and would be a useful character to have in and around the dressing room.

Sources indicate that prior to his switch to the Baggies, he rose up the ranks at QPR and played 72 times for their first-team in his early career. He also had spells away from the Hoops at Northampton Town, Cambridge United and Swindon Town to get some experience under his belt.

Sources indicate that west Brom have been impressive this season but they lack depth. If Pereira got injured, WBA wouldn’t have anyone to play on the right hand side of their backline and would probably have to put Callum Styles there which wouldn’t be ideal.

Recent findings suggest that therefore, they need to consider a swoop for Furlong and it wouldn’t be a surprise to see Ipswich Town sanction a deal, either permanently or on loan. He is surplus to requirements with the Tractor Boys and should seek an exit.
