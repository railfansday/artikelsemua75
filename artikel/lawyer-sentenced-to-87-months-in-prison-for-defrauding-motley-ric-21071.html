A former Motley Rice attorney was sentenced Monday in Charleston, South Carolina, to 87 months in prison and ordered to pay more than $2.5 million in restitution for defrauding the law firm through fictitious companies and ‌false settlement claims.

William Christopher Swett, 42, had previously pleaded guilty to four counts of ​wire fraud and four counts of money laundering, according to a story by Reuters. (Related: Compound Interest Calculator: See Your Money Grow...)

A lawyer ​at South Carolina-based ⁠Motley Rice for 12 years, Swett had a severe gambling addiction and substance abuse issues, according to court papers filed on his behalf.

Prosecutors have alleged that Swett profited for years ⁠by creating fictitious legal services companies that received reimbursements from Motley Rice. In addition, he allegedly filed fake personal injury claims for family members and put the proceeds into bank accounts that he controlled.
